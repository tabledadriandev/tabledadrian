# Doenjang Jjigae with Clams & Charred Courgette

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The soul-stew of Korea — funky fermented soybean broth, sweet clams, and vegetables cut chunky enough to matter.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Doenjang — 55 g
- Gochugaru — 5 g
- Anchovy-kombu stock — 700 ml
- Palourde clams, purged — 400 g
- Courgette, 2 cm chunks — 200 g
- Potato, 2 cm dice — 150 g
- Onion — 80 g
- Firm tofu, cubes — 200 g
- Garlic, minced — 8 g
- Green chilli, rings — 1
- Spring onion — 25 g

## Method
1. Char courgette chunks in a dry pan until spotted black — extra depth for a short cook.
2. Whisk doenjang into the stock through a sieve; add gochugaru.
3. Simmer potato and onion in the broth 8 minutes.
4. Add clams, courgette, tofu and garlic; simmer until shells open, 5 minutes.
5. Add chilli and spring onion; simmer 1 minute.
6. Taste — doenjang brands vary wildly; adjust with a further 10 g if shy.

## Plating & Service
A bubbling communal pot with rice; the broth should be cloudy, rustic and unapologetic.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Beef brisket slices replace clams in the inland version; fully vegan with kombu-shiitake stock.
