# Kou Shui Ji, Mouth-Watering Chicken

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Silky poached chicken under layered Sichuan sauce — numbing, fragrant, sweet, sharp — served cold and utterly composed.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chicken legs, boneless, skin on — 500 g
- Ginger — 20 g
- Spring onion — 30 g
- Shaoxing wine — 30 ml
- Sichuan chilli oil with sediment — 50 ml
- Soy sauce — 25 ml
- Black vinegar — 15 ml
- Caster sugar — 10 g
- Sichuan pepper, toasted and ground — 2 g
- Garlic, grated — 6 g
- Poaching stock (reserved) — 40 ml
- Roasted peanuts, crushed — 25 g
- Toasted sesame seeds — 5 g
- Coriander — 8 g

## Method
1. Poach chicken with ginger, spring onion and wine at 85°C for 20–25 minutes to 70°C at the core.
2. Ice-shock 10 minutes for taut skin; reserve 40 ml stock.
3. Whisk chilli oil, soy, vinegar, sugar, Sichuan pepper, garlic and warm stock into a layered sauce.
4. Slice chicken 1 cm thick across the grain.
5. Shingle over sliced cucumber if desired; flood with sauce.
6. Rest 5 minutes for the sauce to seep, then top with peanuts, sesame and coriander.

## Plating & Service
Serve cool, not fridge-cold; sauce pooling around the slices, peanuts added at the last second.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Poached prawns or silken tofu take the same sauce; omit peanuts for nut allergies and add fried shallots.
