# Sweet & Sour Iberico Pork, Pineapple & Aged Vinegar

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Gu lo yuk rebuilt with Iberico and fresh pineapple — crackly cubes, a sauce with actual acidity, zero neon.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Iberico pork shoulder, 3 cm cubes — 600 g
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Shaoxing wine — 15 ml
- Egg — 1
- Potato starch — 120 g
- Neutral oil — 1.5 L
- Fresh pineapple, chunks — 200 g
- Red pepper & green pepper, diamonds — 150 g
- Ketchup — 50 g
- Chinkiang black vinegar — 30 ml
- Rice vinegar — 20 ml
- Caster sugar — 45 g
- Soy sauce — 10 ml
- Water — 60 ml
- Potato starch (sauce) — 5 g

## Method
1. Marinate pork with salt, wine and egg 20 minutes.
2. Simmer ketchup, both vinegars, sugar, soy, water and starch to a bright, glossy sauce.
3. Dredge pork heavily in starch, pressing it on; rest 5 minutes.
4. First fry at 165°C for 4 minutes; rest 3 minutes.
5. Second fry at 195°C for 90 seconds until deeply crisp; sear pineapple and peppers in a dry wok meanwhile.
6. Toss pork, pineapple and peppers through the hot sauce for 10 seconds only — serve before the crust surrenders.

## Plating & Service
High pile, sauce barely coating, extra sauce in a jug for the rice.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Monkfish cheeks or cauliflower (vegan, aquafaba for egg) fry the same; the double-vinegar sauce is the keeper.
