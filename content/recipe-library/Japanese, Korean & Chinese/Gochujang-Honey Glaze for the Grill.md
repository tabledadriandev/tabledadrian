# Gochujang-Honey Glaze for the Grill

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The all-purpose Korean lacquer — brush on anything in the last minutes of grilling for a mahogany, sweet-heat crust.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Gochujang — 120 g
- Honey — 80 g
- Tamari — 40 ml
- Rice vinegar — 30 ml
- Toasted sesame oil — 15 ml
- Garlic, grated — 12 g
- Ginger, grated — 8 g
- Water — 40 ml

## Method
1. Whisk everything except sesame oil in a small pan.
2. Simmer 5–6 minutes, stirring, until it coats a spoon thickly.
3. Finish with sesame oil off the heat.
4. Cool; jar. Holds 3 weeks chilled.
5. To use: brush on in the final 3–4 minutes of grilling only — the honey burns before that.

## Plating & Service
Store in a squeeze bottle at the grill station; thin with 10 ml water per 100 g for a tossing sauce.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Maple syrup for vegan; add 20 ml orange juice for poultry, or 10 g doenjang for a funkier pork version.
