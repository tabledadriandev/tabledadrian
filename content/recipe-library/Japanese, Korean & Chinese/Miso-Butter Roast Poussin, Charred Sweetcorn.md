# Miso-Butter Roast Poussin, Charred Sweetcorn

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Hokkaido flavours on a French bird — miso butter worked under the skin, sweetcorn charred and rolled in the roasting juices.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Poussins — 2 (900 g total)
- Unsalted butter, soft — 80 g
- Shiro miso — 40 g
- Garlic, grated — 6 g
- Lemon zest — 2 g
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Sweetcorn cobs — 3
- Sake — 50 ml
- Tamari — 10 ml
- Spring onion — 20 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Beat butter with miso, garlic, lemon zest and pepper.
2. Loosen the breast and thigh skin; pipe two-thirds of the miso butter beneath. Salt the outside lightly.
3. Rest the birds uncovered in the fridge 2 hours for taut skin (optional but transformative).
4. Roast at 200°C for 35–40 minutes, basting twice with sake, to 71°C in the thigh; rest 10 minutes.
5. Char the corn over flame; cut into wheels and roll in the tray juices, remaining miso butter and tamari.
6. Halve the poussins along the backbone.

## Plating & Service
Half a bird per guest with two corn wheels, tray juices spooned over, spring onion scattered.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Whole chicken: 190°C for ~65 minutes; dairy-free version blends miso with 50 ml olive oil.
