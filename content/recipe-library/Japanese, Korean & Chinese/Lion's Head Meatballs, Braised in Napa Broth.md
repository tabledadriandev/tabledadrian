# Lion's Head Meatballs, Braised in Napa Broth

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Huaiyang refinement — huge, cloud-tender pork meatballs braised under a duvet of napa cabbage in clear broth.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Pork shoulder, 30% fat, hand-chopped — 700 g
- Water chestnuts, minced — 60 g
- Ginger, grated — 12 g
- Spring onion, minced — 25 g
- Shaoxing wine — 25 ml
- Light soy — 20 ml
- Egg — 1
- Potato starch — 15 g
- Fine salt — 6 g
- White pepper — 1 g
- Chicken stock — 900 ml
- Napa cabbage, wide ribbons — 400 g
- Dark soy — 5 ml
- Sesame oil — 5 ml

## Method
1. Hand-chop the pork to a coarse, sticky mince — a processor makes it rubbery.
2. Beat with salt, wine, soy, egg, ginger, spring onion and 40 ml cold water until it slaps glossily; fold in chestnuts and starch.
3. Form 8 large balls (about 100 g), passing each between wet palms 10 times to compact.
4. Sear in an oiled pan at 200°C, rolling for colour, 4 minutes.
5. Line a casserole with half the cabbage; nest the meatballs, cover with remaining cabbage, add stock and dark soy.
6. Braise lidded at a bare simmer 40 minutes to 75°C core.
7. Season the broth, finish with sesame oil.

## Plating & Service
Two meatballs per deep bowl, cabbage beneath, broth ladled clear; a dish of quiet luxury.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 100 g crab to the mince for the banquet version; serve over rice noodles for a fuller main.
