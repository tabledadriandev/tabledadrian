# Siu Yuk, Crispy-Skin Roast Pork Belly, Mustard & Sugar

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Glass crackling over juicy belly, the Hong Kong roast-shop icon — the skin technique is everything.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Pork belly, skin on, one slab — 1.5 kg
- White vinegar — 20 ml
- Coarse salt (skin crust) — 300 g
- Fine salt — 12 g
- Caster sugar — 8 g
- Five-spice — 4 g
- White pepper — 2 g
- Shaoxing wine — 20 ml
- Hot mustard — 30 g
- Caster sugar, to serve — 20 g

## Method
1. Blanch the belly 3 minutes; dry. Pierce the skin hundreds of times with a needle tool or skewers — never through to the fat.
2. Rub the meat side (not skin) with fine salt, sugar, five-spice, pepper and wine.
3. Brush skin with vinegar; refrigerate uncovered, skin-up, 12–24 hours until parchment-dry.
4. Foil-wrap the sides, leaving skin exposed; pack the skin under a 1 cm crust of coarse salt.
5. Roast at 160°C for 60 minutes; remove the salt crust completely.
6. Blast at 240°C for 20–25 minutes until the skin puffs to even crackling — watch and rotate.
7. Rest 15 minutes; cut into 2 cm cubes with a heavy knife through the crackle.

## Plating & Service
Cubes stacked skin-up on a board, mustard and a pinch-bowl of sugar alongside in the roast-shop manner.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Serve offcuts through fried rice; a fennel-seed variant in the rub reads slightly Provençal and suits the Riviera table.
