# Chen Pi Beef, Dried Tangerine Peel & Sichuan Chilli

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Orange-peel beef the Sichuan way — crisp-chewy beef lacquered in a dark, citrus-perfumed, gently numbing glaze.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Beef flank or bavette, 5 mm strips — 600 g
- Soy sauce — 20 ml
- Shaoxing wine — 20 ml
- Potato starch — 70 g
- Neutral oil — 1 L
- Dried tangerine peel (chen pi) — 10 g
- Dried chillies — 8
- Sichuan peppercorns — 3 g
- Ginger, julienne — 12 g
- Garlic, sliced — 10 g
- Caster sugar — 30 g
- Black vinegar — 20 ml
- Dark soy — 8 ml
- Water — 50 ml
- Toasted sesame seeds — 5 g
- Spring onion — 20 g

## Method
1. Soak chen pi in warm water 20 minutes; sliver half, keep the soaking water.
2. Marinate beef in soy and wine 15 minutes; dredge in starch for a dry coat.
3. Fry beef at 180°C in two waves, 2.5 minutes each, until crisp at the edges; drain.
4. Fry chillies, Sichuan pepper, chen pi slivers, ginger and garlic in 20 ml oil, 45 seconds.
5. Add sugar, vinegar, dark soy, water and 30 ml peel-soaking water; reduce to a bubbling glaze, 90 seconds.
6. Return beef; toss 45 seconds until lacquered. Finish with sesame and spring onion.

## Plating & Service
Glossy, dark, citrus-scented pile on a warm platter; the peel slivers are meant to be eaten.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fresh mandarin zest (dried 2 h at 80°C) substitutes for chen pi; duck breast strips are a superb variation.
