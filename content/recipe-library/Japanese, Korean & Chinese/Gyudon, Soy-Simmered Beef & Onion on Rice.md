# Gyudon, Soy-Simmered Beef & Onion on Rice

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The great Japanese comfort bowl — sweet soy-braised beef ribbons over hot rice, onsen egg optional but correct.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Beef ribeye or sirloin, 2 mm slices — 500 g
- Onions, thin crescents — 300 g
- Dashi — 300 ml
- Soy sauce — 60 ml
- Mirin — 60 ml
- Sake — 40 ml
- Caster sugar — 15 g
- Ginger, grated — 10 g
- Cooked short-grain rice, hot — 800 g
- Eggs (onsen, 45 min at 63°C) — 4
- Beni shoga (red pickled ginger) — 30 g
- Spring onion — 20 g

## Method
1. Cook eggs at 63°C for 45 minutes if using; hold warm.
2. Simmer dashi, soy, mirin, sake, sugar and ginger 2 minutes.
3. Add onions; simmer 8 minutes until translucent and sweet.
4. Add beef slices in loose ribbons; simmer 2–3 minutes, skimming once — do not boil hard or it toughens.
5. Rest 2 minutes so the beef drinks the broth.
6. Ladle over hot rice with plenty of liquor; crown with onsen egg, beni shoga and spring onion.

## Plating & Service
Deep donburi bowls, lidded for the walk from galley to table — lifting the lid is the moment.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Thin pork or velveted mushrooms take the same liquor; GF with tamari. The broth-to-rice ratio makes or breaks it — be generous.
