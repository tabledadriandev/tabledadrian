# Korean Fried Chicken, Half Gochujang Half Soy-Garlic

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Yangnyeom two ways from one double-fry — shattering crust under either fiery-sweet or garlic-soy lacquer.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chicken wings or boneless thigh chunks — 1 kg
- Fine salt — 8 g
- White pepper — 2 g
- Potato starch — 140 g
- Plain flour — 40 g
- Baking powder — 4 g
- Cold water — 150 ml
- Neutral oil — 2 L
- Gochujang — 45 g
- Honey — 50 g
- Ketchup — 20 g
- Rice vinegar — 15 ml
- Soy sauce — 45 ml
- Caster sugar — 25 g
- Garlic, minced — 20 g
- Toasted sesame seeds — 8 g

## Method
1. Season chicken with salt and pepper 20 minutes ahead.
2. Sauce one: simmer gochujang, honey, ketchup and vinegar 2 minutes. Sauce two: simmer soy, sugar, garlic and 30 ml water 3 minutes.
3. Whisk 80 g starch, flour, baking powder and cold water to a thin batter; keep 60 g starch for dredging.
4. Dredge chicken in starch, then batter thinly.
5. First fry at 165°C for 6 minutes; rest 5 minutes on a rack.
6. Second fry at 190°C for 2–3 minutes to deep gold and 74°C core.
7. Toss half the chicken in each warm sauce — 5 seconds, then out.

## Plating & Service
Two-tone platter, red one side, amber the other, sesame over both; pickled radish cubes alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF with rice flour and tamari; the soy-garlic side is the crowd-safe option for spice-shy guests.
