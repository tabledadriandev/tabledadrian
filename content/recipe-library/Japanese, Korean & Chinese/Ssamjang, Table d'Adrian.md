# Ssamjang, Table d'Adrian

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The wrap sauce — nutty, funky, sweet — that makes every ssam table sing; ours gets toasted walnuts for depth.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Doenjang — 90 g
- Gochujang — 45 g
- Toasted sesame oil — 20 ml
- Honey or rice syrup — 15 g
- Garlic, grated — 10 g
- Spring onion, minced — 20 g
- Walnuts, toasted and crushed — 25 g
- Toasted sesame seeds — 8 g
- Water — 20 ml

## Method
1. Toast the walnuts at 160°C for 8 minutes; crush coarse.
2. Stir doenjang, gochujang, honey, garlic and water to a thick cream.
3. Fold in sesame oil, spring onion, walnuts and sesame seeds.
4. Rest 30 minutes for the flavours to knit.
5. Serve in a low bowl; keeps 2 weeks chilled.

## Plating & Service
A dab per wrap is the dose — put out small spoons so guests stop drowning their ssam.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: swap walnuts for crushed roasted soybeans or omit; a spoon of minced Riviera anchovy is a sacrilege that works.
