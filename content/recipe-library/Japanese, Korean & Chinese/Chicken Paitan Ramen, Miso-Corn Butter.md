# Chicken Paitan Ramen, Miso-Corn Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A rolling-boil white chicken broth, creamy without cream, finished Sapporo-style with sweetcorn and miso butter.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chicken wings, feet if available, carcasses — 2 kg
- Water — 3.5 L
- Onion — 1
- Garlic — 1 head, halved
- Ginger — 25 g
- Shiro miso (tare) — 80 g
- Tamari — 30 ml
- Toasted sesame oil — 10 ml
- Ramen noodles — 500 g
- Sweetcorn kernels — 200 g
- Unsalted butter — 40 g
- Spring onion — 40 g
- Menma — 80 g
- White pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Blanch bones 5 minutes; rinse. Return to the pot with water and aromatics.
2. Boil hard — an actual rolling boil — 4.5–5 hours, topping up, until the broth is white, emulsified and coats a spoon (target 1.8 L).
3. Blend and strain; season nothing yet.
4. Whisk miso, tamari and sesame oil for the tare.
5. Char corn in a dry pan; beat 20 g miso into the soft butter.
6. Boil noodles 90 seconds. Bowl up: 25 g tare, 400 ml paitan at 95°C, noodles, corn, menma, a disc of miso butter, spring onion, white pepper.

## Plating & Service
The butter must melt in front of the guest — that slow gold slick is the dish's signature moment.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pressure cooker: 2 hours high then blend; dairy-free bowls take chilli oil instead of butter.
