# Octopus Sunomono, Cucumber & Wakame

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A sharp, cold vinegared salad that resets the palate between rich robata courses — essential sequencing for long menus.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Cooked octopus tentacle — 200 g
- Cucumber — 300 g
- Dried wakame — 8 g
- Rice vinegar — 60 ml
- Caster sugar — 18 g
- Usukuchi soy — 10 ml
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Ichiban dashi — 30 ml
- Toasted sesame seeds — 4 g
- Ginger, fine julienne — 8 g

## Method
1. Rehydrate wakame in cold water 8 minutes; squeeze dry and chop roughly.
2. Slice cucumber 2 mm thin, salt with 4 g, rest 10 minutes, then squeeze firmly.
3. Slice octopus into 4 mm coins on the bias.
4. Whisk vinegar, sugar, soy and dashi until dissolved (sanbaizu).
5. Toss cucumber, wakame and octopus in the dressing; chill 10 minutes.
6. Serve topped with ginger julienne and sesame.

## Plating & Service
Small glass bowls, slightly mounded, well chilled; a two-bite palate cleanser.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan without octopus (add 40 g cooked glass noodles); crab or blanched prawns swap in for variety.
