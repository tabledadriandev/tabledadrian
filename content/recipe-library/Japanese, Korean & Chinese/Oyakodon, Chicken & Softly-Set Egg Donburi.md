# Oyakodon, Chicken & Softly-Set Egg Donburi

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Parent-and-child bowl — poached chicken bound in barely-set egg over rice, one pan per portion like the specialists do.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chicken thighs, 2 cm pieces — 500 g
- Onion, thin crescents — 200 g
- Dashi — 320 ml
- Soy sauce — 55 ml
- Mirin — 55 ml
- Caster sugar — 10 g
- Eggs — 8
- Cooked short-grain rice, hot — 800 g
- Mitsuba or spring onion — 20 g
- Shichimi — 1 g
- Nori strips — 2 g

## Method
1. Combine dashi, soy, mirin and sugar; divide the sauce and ingredients into 4 servings (per-pan cooking).
2. Per portion: simmer 80 ml sauce with onion 2 minutes in an 18 cm pan.
3. Add chicken; simmer 5–6 minutes to 74°C.
4. Beat 2 eggs loosely — whites still streaky; pour in a spiral over the chicken.
5. Lid on, 45–60 seconds at a simmer: set at the edge, custardy at the centre.
6. Slide — do not scrape — over hot rice; top with mitsuba, nori and shichimi.

## Plating & Service
The egg should tremble; serve with a spoon and the lid on for the reveal.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Duck and its fat for a richer 'cousin-don'; GF via tamari. Never fully cook the egg — carryover finishes it in the bowl.
