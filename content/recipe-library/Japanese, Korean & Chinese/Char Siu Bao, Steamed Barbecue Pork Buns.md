# Char Siu Bao, Steamed Barbecue Pork Buns

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Cloud-white steamed buns split over glossy, sweet-savoury char siu — nostalgic dim sum that guests order twice.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Bao flour or plain flour — 300 g
- Caster sugar — 40 g
- Instant yeast — 4 g
- Baking powder — 4 g
- Water — 155 ml
- Neutral oil — 12 ml
- Cooked char siu, 1 cm dice — 250 g
- Oyster sauce — 25 g
- Hoisin — 25 g
- Soy sauce — 10 ml
- Caster sugar (sauce) — 15 g
- Potato starch — 8 g + water 40 ml
- Shallot, minced — 30 g

## Method
1. Sweat shallot; add oyster, hoisin, soy, sugar and 60 ml water; thicken with the starch slurry to a thick, glossy binder. Fold in char siu; chill until set.
2. Knead flour, sugar, yeast, water and oil 8 minutes to a silky dough; prove 60 minutes at 26°C to double.
3. Knock back, knead in baking powder, and divide into 12 pieces (about 42 g).
4. Roll discs thick in the centre; fill with 25 g pork mixture and pleat closed.
5. Rest seam-down on parchment squares 20 minutes.
6. Steam from cold: bring to 100°C and steam 10–12 minutes; rest 3 minutes before lifting the lid.

## Plating & Service
Serve 2 per guest in the bamboo steamer, pleats up, with jasmine tea.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Bake at 190°C with egg wash for a golden version; duck leg or jackfruit (vegan, with mushroom sauce) fill equally well.
