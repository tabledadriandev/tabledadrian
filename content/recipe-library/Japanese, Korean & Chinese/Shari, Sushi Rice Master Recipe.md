# Shari, Sushi Rice Master Recipe

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Body-temperature, mirror-glossed sushi rice with balanced awasezu — the platform every sushi course stands on.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Japanese short-grain rice — 500 g
- Cold water — 540 ml
- Kombu — 5 g
- Rice vinegar — 90 ml
- Caster sugar — 45 g
- Fine salt — 15 g

## Method
1. Warm vinegar, sugar and salt to 50°C until dissolved; steep a corner of kombu in it; cool (awasezu).
2. Wash rice to near-clear water; drain 15 minutes, soak in measured water 30 minutes with kombu.
3. Cook: boil hard 7 minutes, minimum flame 12 minutes, rest 15 minutes off heat, lid never lifted.
4. Turn rice out onto a wide wooden or plastic tray; remove kombu.
5. Pour 130 ml awasezu over the paddle onto the rice; cut and fold at 45°, fanning, until grains are glossy and steam stops — about 4 minutes.
6. Rest under a damp cloth at room temperature; use within 4 hours at body temperature. Never refrigerate.

## Plating & Service
Hold beside the pass under damp cloth; hands dipped in tezu (water + splash vinegar) for shaping.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Red-vinegar (akazu) version for tuna-forward menus: swap 50% of the rice vinegar; reduce sugar to 30 g.
