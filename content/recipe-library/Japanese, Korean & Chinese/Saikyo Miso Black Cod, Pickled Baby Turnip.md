# Saikyo Miso Black Cod, Pickled Baby Turnip

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The 48-hour sweet-miso cure that made this dish famous — caramelised lacquer outside, custard flakes within.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Black cod (sablefish) portions — 4 × 160 g
- Shiro (saikyo) miso — 200 g
- Mirin — 60 ml
- Sake — 60 ml
- Caster sugar — 30 g
- Baby turnips — 8
- Rice vinegar — 60 ml
- Water — 60 ml
- Caster sugar (pickle) — 25 g
- Fine salt — 3 g
- Yuzu zest — 1 g
- Micro shiso — 4 g

## Method
1. Boil mirin and sake 2 minutes to burn off alcohol; whisk into miso and sugar; cool.
2. Coat cod fully in the cure; refrigerate 36–48 hours.
3. Slice turnips 2 mm; brine in vinegar, water, sugar and salt at least 2 hours.
4. Wipe (do not wash) the cure from the fish.
5. Grill at 180°C for 8 minutes, then 230°C for 3–4 minutes until mahogany at the edges and 52°C at the core.
6. Rest 2 minutes; finish with yuzu zest.

## Plating & Service
One glossy portion, pickled turnip petals fanned beside; the sweetness needs the acid.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Salmon, sea bass or thick pork chops cure the same way (pork to 62°C); halve the cure time for thin fillets.
