# Sesame Prawn Toast Batons, Sweet Soy Dip

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Old-school Cantonese crunch done properly — airy prawn mousse on brioche-thin bread, tiled in sesame, fried to order.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Raw prawns — 300 g
- Pork back fat or butter — 25 g
- Egg white — 25 g
- Ginger, grated — 6 g
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Caster sugar — 4 g
- White pepper — 1 g
- Potato starch — 8 g
- White sandwich bread, day-old — 6 slices
- White + black sesame seeds — 60 g
- Neutral oil — 1.5 L
- Soy sauce — 30 ml
- Honey — 20 g
- Rice vinegar — 10 ml

## Method
1. Blitz prawns, fat, egg white, ginger, salt, sugar, pepper and starch to a smooth, bouncy mousse; chill 15 minutes.
2. Simmer soy, honey and vinegar 2 minutes for the dip.
3. Spread 45 g mousse in a dome over each crustless slice, right to the edges.
4. Press mousse-side down into the mixed sesame seeds.
5. Fry mousse-side down at 170°C for 2.5 minutes, flip, and fry 1 minute more until deep gold.
6. Drain 1 minute; cut each slice into 3 batons.

## Plating & Service
Stack batons crosswise on paper, dip alongside; serve inside 4 minutes of frying.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken-prawn blend lightens the cost; top with a shiso leaf under the sesame for perfume.
