# Chargrilled Shishito Peppers, Yuzu Kosho Bonito Salt

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A two-minute izakaya opener that lands on deck while guests still hold their aperitif — blistered, smoky, one-in-ten spicy.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Shishito peppers — 250 g
- Grapeseed oil — 15 ml
- Flaky sea salt — 4 g
- Katsuobushi (bonito flakes), finely crumbled — 3 g
- Yuzu kosho (green) — 6 g
- Tamari — 10 ml
- Lemon — 1/2, for zest
- Toasted sesame oil — 3 ml

## Method
1. Blend the flaky salt with crumbled katsuobushi to a coarse bonito salt; reserve.
2. Whisk yuzu kosho, tamari and sesame oil to a loose dressing.
3. Toss shishitos in grapeseed oil.
4. Grill on a robata or heavy plancha at maximum heat (250°C+) for 6–8 minutes, turning, until blistered and 40% charred.
5. Toss the hot peppers through the yuzu kosho dressing off the heat.
6. Pile onto a plate, dust with bonito salt and grate over lemon zest.

## Plating & Service
Serve stacked high in a small ceramic bowl, eaten by the stem with fingers; offer a discard bowl.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Omit bonito and use smoked salt for a vegan version; swap shishitos for padrón peppers when local markets have them.
