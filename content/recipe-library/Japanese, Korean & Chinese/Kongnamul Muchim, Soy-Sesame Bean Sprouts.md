# Kongnamul Muchim, Soy-Sesame Bean Sprouts

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The quiet workhorse banchan — nutty, crunchy soybean sprouts that balance every rich Korean main.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Soybean sprouts — 300 g
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Tamari — 12 ml
- Toasted sesame oil — 12 ml
- Garlic, grated — 4 g
- Spring onion, minced — 10 g
- Toasted sesame seeds — 6 g
- Gochugaru — 2 g (optional)

## Method
1. Rinse sprouts and discard browned tails.
2. Steam in 50 ml water in a lidded pan 5 minutes from cold — do not lift the lid (prevents the raw-bean smell).
3. Drain and cool on a tray to keep the crunch.
4. Toss with salt, tamari, sesame oil, garlic and spring onion.
5. Rest 10 minutes; finish with sesame seeds and gochugaru.

## Plating & Service
Small chilled mound per guest; keeps 3 days and improves overnight.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Mung bean sprouts cook in 3 minutes for a lighter version; add 5 ml fish sauce for depth if not cooking vegan.
