# Miso Caramel, Salted-Butterscotch of the East

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A deep amber caramel rounded with shiro miso — pour over ice cream, mochi, roasted pineapple or the matcha cheesecake.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Caster sugar — 200 g
- Water — 50 ml
- Double cream, warm — 180 ml
- Unsalted butter — 50 g
- Shiro miso — 45 g
- Vanilla pod — 1/2
- Lemon juice — 3 ml

## Method
1. Cook sugar and water without stirring to a deep amber 180°C.
2. Off the heat, whisk in warm cream in three additions — it will roar.
3. Whisk in butter, then return to 105°C for 1 minute.
4. Cool to 60°C; whisk in miso and vanilla seeds (miso added hotter turns bitter).
5. Brighten with lemon juice.
6. Jar; fridge 3 weeks. Warm gently to pour.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm from a small copper pot; it seizes beautifully over cold desserts.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan with coconut cream and plant butter; aka miso for a saltier, deeper profile — reduce to 30 g.
