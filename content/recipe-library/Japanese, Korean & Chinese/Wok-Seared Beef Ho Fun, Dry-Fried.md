# Wok-Seared Beef Ho Fun, Dry-Fried

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Gon chow ngau ho — wide rice noodles seared with beef until every strand carries wok breath, no sauce puddle allowed.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Fresh wide rice noodles (ho fun) — 700 g
- Beef flank, 4 mm slices — 350 g
- Baking soda — 2 g
- Soy sauce (marinade) — 12 ml
- Potato starch — 6 g
- Neutral oil — 50 ml
- Light soy — 25 ml
- Dark soy — 12 ml
- Caster sugar — 6 g
- Bean sprouts — 150 g
- Spring onions, 5 cm — 40 g
- Onion, slivers — 60 g
- White pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Tenderise beef with baking soda 15 minutes; rinse, dry, marinate in soy and starch.
2. Separate the noodles gently with oiled hands; microwave 60 seconds if stiff — never wash them.
3. Whisk both soys and sugar.
4. Sear beef in 25 ml oil in a smoking wok 90 seconds; remove at rare.
5. Add remaining oil; sear onion 30 seconds, then noodles — spread flat, leave 45 seconds to scorch, fold, repeat twice. Tossing, not stirring; a broken noodle is a lost point.
6. Return beef with the soy mix around the wok rim; add sprouts and spring onion, toss 30 seconds, finish with white pepper.

## Plating & Service
Straight from wok to warm oval plate — the smoky aroma dissipates in minutes and cannot be reheated back.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken or plain (vegetable) versions work; the two-soy ratio gives colour without wetness — resist adding liquid.
