# Hamachi Sashimi, Ponzu Gel & Pickled Wasabi Stem

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Cold-chain sashimi with a set ponzu gel that will not flood the plate in a rolling anchorage — precision fish work that reads as fine dining.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Sashimi-grade hamachi (yellowtail) loin — 320 g
- Ponzu (see House Ponzu) — 150 ml
- Agar-agar — 1.6 g
- Wasabi stems or rhubarb batons — 40 g
- Rice vinegar — 50 ml
- Caster sugar — 15 g
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Radish, shaved — 30 g
- Shiso leaves — 8
- Extra-virgin olive oil — 10 ml
- Finger lime or lime zest — 1

## Method
1. Bring rice vinegar, sugar and salt to 60°C to dissolve; pour over finely sliced wasabi stems and pickle 30 minutes.
2. Whisk agar into cold ponzu, bring to a full boil for 60 seconds, cast into a shallow tray and set in the fridge (about 20 minutes).
3. Blend the set ponzu to a smooth, glossy fluid gel; pass through a fine sieve into a squeeze bottle.
4. Trim the hamachi of bloodline and skin; chill the blade and board.
5. Slice against the grain into 5 mm sogigiri slices, 8 per portion, wiping the blade between cuts.
6. Arrange fish on chilled plates, dot with ponzu gel, add pickle, shaved radish and torn shiso.
7. Finish with olive oil beads and finger lime pearls.

## Plating & Service
Chilled black plates, fish slightly overlapping in an arc; serve within 5 minutes of slicing.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Works with sea bream or scallop; for soy-free guests set the gel from yuzu juice, dashi and salt instead of ponzu.
