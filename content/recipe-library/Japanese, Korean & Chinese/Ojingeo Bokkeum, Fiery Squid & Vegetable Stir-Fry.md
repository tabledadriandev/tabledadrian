# Ojingeo Bokkeum, Fiery Squid & Vegetable Stir-Fry

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Scored squid curled through a gochujang wok sauce — fast, hot, and better than any late-night Seoul memory.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Squid, cleaned, scored, 4 cm pieces — 600 g
- Gochujang — 45 g
- Gochugaru — 12 g
- Soy sauce — 20 ml
- Caster sugar — 15 g
- Garlic, minced — 12 g
- Ginger — 6 g
- Mirin — 20 ml
- Neutral oil — 25 ml
- Onion, petals — 120 g
- Carrot, thin bias — 80 g
- Cabbage, squares — 120 g
- Spring onion, batons — 30 g
- Toasted sesame oil & seeds — 8 ml + 5 g

## Method
1. Whisk gochujang, gochugaru, soy, sugar, garlic, ginger and mirin.
2. Blanch squid 30 seconds in boiling water; drain hard (prevents a watery wok).
3. Stir-fry onion and carrot in oil at 250°C for 90 seconds.
4. Add cabbage 60 seconds, then squid and all the sauce.
5. Toss 2 minutes maximum — squid must stay taut, sauce clinging, no puddle.
6. Finish with spring onion, sesame oil and seeds.

## Plating & Service
Served on a hot oval with rice or somyeon noodles alongside to catch the sauce.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Prawns or chicken strips swap in; adjust to guest heat tolerance via gochugaru, not gochujang.
