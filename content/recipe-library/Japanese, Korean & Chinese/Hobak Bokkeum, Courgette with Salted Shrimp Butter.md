# Hobak Bokkeum, Courgette with Salted Shrimp Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Riviera courgettes in the Korean register — quick-sautéed with saeujeot, finished like a beurre monté.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Courgettes — 450 g
- Neutral oil — 15 ml
- Saeujeot (salted shrimp), minced — 12 g
- Garlic, minced — 6 g
- Water — 40 ml
- Unsalted butter — 20 g
- Spring onion — 15 g
- Toasted sesame oil — 5 ml
- Toasted sesame seeds — 4 g
- Gochugaru — 1 g

## Method
1. Cut courgettes into 1 cm half-moons.
2. Sauté in oil at 200°C for 3 minutes until edges catch colour.
3. Add garlic and saeujeot; cook 1 minute.
4. Add water and butter, swirling to an emulsified glaze, 2 minutes — courgettes should be tender with bite.
5. Finish with spring onion, sesame oil, seeds and gochugaru.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm in a shallow bowl as banchan or beside grilled fish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: swap saeujeot for 10 ml soy + 3 g white miso and use olive oil for butter.
