# Robata Ox Tongue, Negi Salsa & Lemon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Gyutan the Sendai way — thin-sliced tongue grilled fast, dressed with a salty spring-onion salsa. Offal that converts sceptics.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Cooked, peeled ox tongue (or raw, pressure-cooked 45 min) — 500 g
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Grapeseed oil — 20 ml
- Spring onions, minced — 80 g
- Toasted sesame oil — 25 ml
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Tamari — 10 ml
- White pepper — 1 g
- Lemon — 1, in wedges
- Shichimi — 1 g

## Method
1. If starting raw: pressure-cook tongue with aromatics 45 minutes, peel while warm, chill pressed.
2. Slice tongue 4 mm across the grain.
3. Combine spring onion, sesame oil, lemon juice, tamari and white pepper for the negi salsa; rest 10 minutes.
4. Oil and salt the slices; grill on a 250°C plancha 60–75 seconds per side until the edges frill and char.
5. Rest 1 minute; spoon salsa generously over.
6. Serve with lemon and shichimi.

## Plating & Service
Overlapping slices on a hot cast-iron plate, salsa across the centre line, lemon at 4 o'clock.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The salsa is equally brilliant on grilled chicken thigh or firm tofu; slice tongue thicker (8 mm) for a chewier, steak-like service.
