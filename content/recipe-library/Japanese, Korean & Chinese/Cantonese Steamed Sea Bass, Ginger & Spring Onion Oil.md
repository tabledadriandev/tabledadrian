# Cantonese Steamed Sea Bass, Ginger & Spring Onion Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The dish that proves restraint is a technique — whole bass steamed to silk, finished with sizzling aromatic oil.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Sea bass, whole, scaled and gutted — 1 × 1.2 kg (or 4 fillets)
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Shaoxing wine — 15 ml
- Ginger — 40 g, half sliced, half fine julienne
- Spring onions — 6, half whole, half fine julienne
- Light soy sauce — 40 ml
- Water — 20 ml
- Caster sugar — 6 g
- Neutral oil — 50 ml
- Coriander sprigs — 10 g

## Method
1. Score the fish to the bone twice per side; season with salt and wine inside and out.
2. Lay on sliced ginger and whole spring onions on a steaming plate.
3. Steam at 100%: whole fish 11–12 minutes (fillets 6–7) until the flesh lifts from the bone at the thickest point.
4. Warm soy, water and sugar to 60°C.
5. Discard steaming juices and aromatics; blanket the fish with the raw julienne.
6. Heat oil to 190°C and pour over the julienne to sizzle; flood the plate with the warm soy.

## Plating & Service
Whole fish presented head-left, sauce lapping the sides; serve with rice spooned into the sauce as the finale.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Turbot, grouper or thick cod behave identically; tamari for GF. Timing is everything — set a timer, not a feeling.
