# Donabe Rice, The Master Pot

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The foundation recipe of the whole East Asian repertoire — glossy, distinct grains with the prized crust at the base.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Japanese short-grain rice (koshihikari type) — 450 g
- Cold water — 495 ml
- Kombu — 3 g (optional)
- Fine salt — 0 g (none — the rice stands alone)

## Method
1. Wash rice in 4 changes of water, rubbing gently, until the water runs nearly clear.
2. Drain in a sieve 15 minutes, then soak in the measured water (1.1:1 by weight) 30 minutes.
3. Add kombu; lid on. Bring to a full boil over medium-high heat (steam rushing from the vent, about 7 minutes).
4. Drop to the lowest flame for 12 minutes exactly.
5. 30 seconds on high to build the okoge crust, then off the heat — rest, lid sealed, 15 minutes. No peeking at any stage.
6. Remove kombu; fold the rice from the bottom with a wet paddle to redistribute moisture.

## Plating & Service
Bring the donabe to the table and open it there — the steam and aroma are part of the service.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Heavy saucepan works: same timings, tight lid, tea towel under the lid for the rest; scale water at 1.1× rice weight always.
