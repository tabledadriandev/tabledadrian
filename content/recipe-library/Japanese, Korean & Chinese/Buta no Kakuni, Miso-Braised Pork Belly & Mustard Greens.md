# Buta no Kakuni, Miso-Braised Pork Belly & Mustard Greens

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Twice-cooked pork belly collapsing under a sweet miso-soy braise — make it the day before and it only improves.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Pork belly, boneless — 900 g
- Ginger — 25 g
- Spring onion tops — 40 g
- Sake — 150 ml
- Water — 900 ml
- Soy sauce — 80 ml
- Aka miso — 30 g
- Caster sugar — 50 g
- Mirin — 60 ml
- Eggs, soft-boiled 6.5 min — 4
- Mustard greens or pak choi — 200 g
- Karashi or hot mustard — 8 g

## Method
1. Sear pork belly fat-side down at 200°C until deeply browned; cut into 5 cm cubes.
2. Blanch cubes 10 minutes in water; discard the liquid (removes scum).
3. Braise with sake, water, ginger and onion tops at a bare simmer, lidded, 2 hours.
4. Add soy, sugar, mirin and miso; simmer uncovered 30 minutes, basting, until the liquor is syrupy and pork yields to a spoon.
5. Bathe the peeled soft eggs in the braise off-heat 20 minutes.
6. Blanch greens 90 seconds; serve pork, halved egg and greens with a dab of karashi.

## Plating & Service
One cube per bowl looks restrained and elegant; glaze spooned over, mustard as a bright pin-point.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chill overnight and lift the fat cap for a lighter service; daikon chunks braised alongside are the classic partner.
