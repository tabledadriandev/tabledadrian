# Kimchi Jeon, Aged-Kimchi Pancake with Makgeolli Cream

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Over-fermented kimchi finds its destiny — a tangy, crisp pancake with a whipped rice-wine dipping cream.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Well-fermented kimchi, chopped — 250 g
- Kimchi brine — 50 ml
- Plain flour — 90 g
- Potato starch — 30 g
- Ice-cold water — 100 ml
- Caster sugar — 5 g
- Neutral oil — 50 ml
- Makgeolli or dry sake — 40 ml
- Soy sauce — 15 ml
- Rice vinegar — 8 ml
- Spring onion — 20 g

## Method
1. Whisk flour, starch, sugar, kimchi brine and water to a thin batter.
2. Fold in kimchi and spring onion.
3. Reduce makgeolli by half; whisk with soy and vinegar for the dip.
4. Heat 25 ml oil at 200°C in a 22 cm pan; ladle in half the batter thinly.
5. Fry 4 minutes to a deep crust, flip, press, and fry 3 minutes, adding oil at the edge.
6. Repeat; rest on a rack and cut into wedges.

## Plating & Service
Serve blistering hot on paper with the dip; excellent with cold beer at crew hour or guest apéro.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 80 g chopped prawn or pork belly to the batter; GF blend flour works with 10 g extra starch.
