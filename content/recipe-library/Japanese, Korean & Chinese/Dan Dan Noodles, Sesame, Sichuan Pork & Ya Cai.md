# Dan Dan Noodles, Sesame, Sichuan Pork & Ya Cai

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Chengdu's numbing noodle — layered chilli-sesame sauce below, crispy pork and preserved greens above, tossed at the table.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Fresh wheat noodles, thin — 500 g
- Pork mince — 300 g
- Ya cai or preserved mustard greens — 60 g
- Neutral oil — 30 ml
- Shaoxing wine — 15 ml
- Dark soy — 5 ml
- Chinese sesame paste (or tahini) — 60 g
- Sichuan chilli oil with sediment — 70 ml
- Light soy — 35 ml
- Black vinegar — 15 ml
- Caster sugar — 10 g
- Garlic, grated — 8 g
- Sichuan pepper, ground — 2 g
- Noodle water — 150 ml
- Crushed roasted peanuts — 25 g
- Spring onion — 25 g

## Method
1. Fry pork in oil hard until crisp and browned, 5 minutes; season with wine, dark soy and ya cai; fry 2 minutes more.
2. Loosen sesame paste with light soy, vinegar, sugar, garlic and chilli oil.
3. Divide the sauce between warm bowls with a pinch of ground Sichuan pepper.
4. Boil noodles 2–3 minutes; reserve water.
5. Loosen each bowl's sauce with 35 ml hot noodle water; add drained noodles.
6. Top with pork, peanuts and spring onion; instruct guests to toss 10 times before eating.

## Plating & Service
Sauce hidden at the bottom, toppings composed — the toss is the guest's job and half the pleasure.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap pork for finely chopped shiitake (vegan); tahini + 5 ml sesame oil mimics Chinese sesame paste faithfully.
