# Ebi Tempura, Matcha Salt

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Straightened, glass-crisp prawns with a barely-there lace batter — the benchmark tempura for a charter tasting menu.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Large prawns, shell on — 12 (about 400 g)
- Weak (cake) flour — 100 g
- Potato starch — 20 g
- Ice-cold sparkling water — 160 ml
- Egg yolk — 1
- Neutral oil — 1.5 L
- Flour for dusting — 20 g
- Matcha — 2 g
- Flaky salt — 6 g
- Lemon — 1

## Method
1. Peel prawns leaving tail fans; press out moisture from the fans with the knife spine.
2. Make 4 shallow belly cuts per prawn and press flat to straighten.
3. Grind matcha with flaky salt.
4. Heat oil to 175°C.
5. Whisk yolk into sparkling water, add flour and starch in one go, and mix with chopsticks 5 seconds — lumps must remain.
6. Dust prawns in flour, drag through batter, and fry 3 at a time for 2 minutes, flicking extra batter over them for lace.
7. Drain vertically on a rack 30 seconds; salt immediately.

## Plating & Service
Serve on folded washi paper within 90 seconds, matcha salt and a lemon cheek beside — no dipping sauce needed.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Use rice flour + potato starch for gluten-free; the same batter carries shiso leaves and lemon slices for a vegetal course.
