# Godeungeo Jorim, Gochujang-Braised Mackerel & Daikon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Oily mackerel braised over sweet daikon in a chilli-soy liquor — a fisherman's dish with real elegance when plated tight.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Mackerel, whole, cut into 4 cm steaks — 800 g
- Daikon, 2 cm rounds — 400 g
- Water — 400 ml
- Soy sauce — 50 ml
- Gochujang — 25 g
- Gochugaru — 15 g
- Caster sugar — 15 g
- Garlic, minced — 15 g
- Ginger, minced — 10 g
- Mirin — 30 ml
- Onion, thick slices — 100 g
- Green chilli & spring onion — 30 g

## Method
1. Salt mackerel lightly 10 minutes; rinse and pat dry.
2. Lay daikon in a single layer in a wide pan; add water and simmer lidded 10 minutes.
3. Whisk soy, gochujang, gochugaru, sugar, garlic, ginger and mirin.
4. Lay mackerel and onion over the daikon; pour the sauce over.
5. Simmer with a dropped lid 15 minutes, basting every 5, until the liquor is reduced and clinging.
6. Top with chilli and spring onion for the final 2 minutes.

## Plating & Service
A daikon round beneath each mackerel steak, sauce spooned over — deep bowls, white rice.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Spanish mackerel, sardines or firm tofu (vegan) braise the same; soften the chilli by halving gochugaru.
