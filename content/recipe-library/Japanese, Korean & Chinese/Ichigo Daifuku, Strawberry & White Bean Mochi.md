# Ichigo Daifuku, Strawberry & White Bean Mochi

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A whole Gariguette strawberry inside sweet white-bean paste inside cloud-soft mochi — spring in three concentric layers.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Strawberries, small and perfect — 8
- Shiro-an (white bean paste) — 240 g
- Glutinous rice flour (shiratamako) — 130 g
- Caster sugar — 60 g
- Water — 190 ml
- Potato starch — 60 g

## Method
1. Hull strawberries; dry completely.
2. Wrap each in 30 g shiro-an, leaving the tip exposed; chill.
3. Whisk rice flour, sugar and water smooth; microwave 60 seconds, stir hard; repeat in 30-second bursts to a translucent, elastic mass (about 2.5 minutes total) — or steam 20 minutes.
4. Turn out onto a starch-dusted tray; dust the top; cool 5 minutes to handleable.
5. Cut 8 pieces; stretch each into a disc, brushing off excess starch.
6. Wrap over the an-strawberry, pinching closed at the base, tip pointing up. Serve within 8 hours.

## Plating & Service
Seam down, one per guest with tea; slice one in half for the cross-section reveal on the platter.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Shop koshian (red bean) works; mango cubes or whole lychees swap for strawberry off-season.
