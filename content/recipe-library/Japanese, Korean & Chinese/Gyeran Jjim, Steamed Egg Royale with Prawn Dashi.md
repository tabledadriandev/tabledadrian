# Gyeran Jjim, Steamed Egg Royale with Prawn Dashi

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Korean steamed egg elevated to a royale — souffléd, trembling, and perfumed with a quick prawn-shell stock.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Eggs — 6 (330 g)
- Prawn shells — 100 g
- Water — 400 ml
- Kombu — 4 g
- Fish sauce — 12 ml
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Caster sugar — 2 g
- Spring onion, minced — 12 g
- Toasted sesame oil — 4 ml
- Gochugaru — 1 g

## Method
1. Roast prawn shells at 200°C for 8 minutes; simmer with water and kombu 15 minutes and strain (yield ~330 ml).
2. Whisk eggs with warm (60°C) stock 1:1 by volume, fish sauce, salt and sugar; pass through a sieve.
3. Divide between 4 ttukbaegi or ramekins.
4. Steam at 90°C: 8 minutes lidded, then stir once gently, and steam 5 more until puffed and just set.
5. Finish with spring onion, sesame oil and a breath of gochugaru.

## Plating & Service
Serve immediately in the cooking vessel while still souffléd — it falls in 90 seconds, so time the walk to the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken stock version for shellfish allergies; add 30 g crab for luxury.
