# Mul Naengmyeon-Style Chilled Buckwheat, Icy Broth & Pear

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Icy beef broth, slippery buckwheat noodles, sweet pear and hot mustard — the most refreshing bowl in the repertoire.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Beef brisket — 400 g
- Water — 1.5 L
- Onion — 1
- Garlic — 15 g
- Ginger — 10 g
- Soy sauce — 20 ml
- Rice vinegar — 40 ml
- Caster sugar — 20 g
- Fine salt — 8 g
- Naengmyeon or thin buckwheat noodles — 400 g
- Nashi pear, thin slices — 150 g
- Cucumber, julienne — 120 g
- Eggs, boiled 9 min — 2, halved
- Hot mustard (gyeoja) — 12 g

## Method
1. Simmer brisket with onion, garlic and ginger 90 minutes, skimming, until tender; rest meat, slice thin.
2. Season the strained broth with soy, vinegar, sugar and salt — it should be bracing, sweet-sour and slightly over-seasoned warm.
3. Chill the broth to slush: freezer trays 2–3 hours, forked into crystals.
4. Boil noodles 3–4 minutes; wash under cold water rubbing off starch; drain into tight nests.
5. Set nests in chilled bowls; pour over the slushy broth.
6. Arrange brisket, pear, cucumber and egg; serve mustard and extra vinegar beside.

## Plating & Service
Frozen bowls, scissors offered to cut the noodles table-side — the proper gesture.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dongchimi (radish water kimchi) brine lightens the broth beautifully if a batch is live; GF with 100% buckwheat noodles.
