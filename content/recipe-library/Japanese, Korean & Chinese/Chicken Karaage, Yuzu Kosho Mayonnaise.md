# Chicken Karaage, Yuzu Kosho Mayonnaise

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Double-fried thigh nuggets that crackle audibly — the crew-favourite that also disappears fastest at guest cocktail hour.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chicken thighs, boneless, skin on — 600 g
- Soy sauce — 30 ml
- Sake — 20 ml
- Grated ginger — 12 g
- Grated garlic — 8 g
- Potato starch — 100 g
- Neutral oil — 1.5 L
- Egg yolk — 1
- Neutral oil for mayo — 120 ml
- Rice vinegar — 8 ml
- Yuzu kosho — 8 g
- Lemon wedges — 4

## Method
1. Cut chicken into 40 g pieces; marinate in soy, sake, ginger and garlic 15 minutes (no longer, or it darkens).
2. Make the mayo: whisk yolk with vinegar, emulsify in oil, season with yuzu kosho.
3. Drain chicken and toss in potato starch until craggy and dry-looking; rest 5 minutes so the starch hydrates.
4. First fry at 165°C for 90 seconds; rest on a rack 2 minutes.
5. Second fry at 190°C for 60–90 seconds until deep gold and 74°C inside.
6. Salt lightly the second they leave the oil.

## Plating & Service
Pile on paper in a bamboo basket, mayo in a dip pot, lemon over the top table-side.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Gluten-free with tamari; boneless wings or oyster mushrooms (vegan, skip mayo egg for soy-milk mayo) fry identically.
