# Bo Zai Fan, Clay Pot Rice with Lap Cheong & Crust

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rice cooked in the serving pot until a golden crust forms, sausage fat basting every grain — Hong Kong winter in one lidded vessel.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Jasmine rice — 400 g
- Water — 480 ml
- Lap cheong — 150 g, thick bias slices
- Chicken thigh, boneless, 3 cm — 300 g
- Light soy (marinade) — 15 ml
- Potato starch — 5 g
- Ginger, julienne — 15 g
- Light soy — 25 ml
- Dark soy — 8 ml
- Caster sugar — 8 g
- Toasted sesame oil — 10 ml
- Neutral oil — 15 ml
- Pak choi — 200 g
- Spring onion — 20 g

## Method
1. Marinate chicken in soy, starch and ginger 20 minutes.
2. Wash rice; add to an oiled clay pot (or heavy casserole) with the water. Boil uncovered until craters form, about 8 minutes.
3. Lay chicken and lap cheong over the rice; lid on, lowest heat, 15 minutes.
4. Drizzle neutral oil down the pot sides; 3 minutes more on medium-low to build the crust (listen for a dry crackle).
5. Rest off heat, lidded, 10 minutes; blanch pak choi meanwhile.
6. Whisk both soys, sugar and sesame oil; pour over at the table, add pak choi and spring onion, and stir up from the crust.

## Plating & Service
Open the lid at the table; scrape the fan jiu crust deliberately — it is the prize, not a fault.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Salted fish + chicken is the venerable variant; shiitake and tofu with mushroom soy make a fine vegan pot.
