# Master Tare, The House Yakitori Glaze

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The living glaze — a soy-mirin reduction fortified with roasted chicken bones that gets deeper with every service.

## Ingredients (serves 16)
- Soy sauce — 300 ml
- Mirin — 300 ml
- Sake — 150 ml
- Caster sugar — 80 g
- Chicken wings or bones, roasted dark — 200 g
- Spring onion tops — 30 g
- Ginger, sliced — 15 g
- Black peppercorns — 3 g

## Method
1. Roast the chicken pieces at 220°C for 20 minutes until deeply browned.
2. Simmer mirin and sake 3 minutes to burn off alcohol.
3. Add soy, sugar, bones, spring onion, ginger and peppercorns.
4. Simmer gently 25 minutes; reduce by a third to a light syrup that coats a spoon.
5. Strain; cool and bottle.
6. After each grill service, strain the used dipping portion back through a boil — the tare matures like a solera.

## Plating & Service
Keep in a tall narrow vessel at the grill for dip-glazing skewers; brush work for larger cuts.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF with tamari; a knob of ginger and 10 g white miso whisked into 100 ml makes an instant donburi sauce.
