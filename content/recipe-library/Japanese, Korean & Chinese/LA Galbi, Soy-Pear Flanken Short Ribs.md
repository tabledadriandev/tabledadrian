# LA Galbi, Soy-Pear Flanken Short Ribs

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Cross-cut short ribs in the classic Korean-American cure — bone-in, lacquered, eaten with fingers and no regrets.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Flanken-cut beef short ribs, 8 mm — 1 kg
- Soy sauce — 90 ml
- Water — 60 ml
- Caster sugar — 35 g
- Nashi pear, grated — 150 g
- Onion, grated — 60 g
- Garlic, grated — 15 g
- Toasted sesame oil — 20 ml
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Rice vinegar — 10 ml
- Spring onion — 20 g
- Toasted sesame seeds — 8 g

## Method
1. Soak ribs in cold water 30 minutes to draw blood; dry thoroughly.
2. Blend soy, water, sugar, pear, onion, garlic, sesame oil, pepper and vinegar; marinate ribs 4–12 hours.
3. Drain well; wipe off pulp (it burns).
4. Grill over the hottest zone (250°C) 2.5–3 minutes per side to a dark, sticky lacquer.
5. Rest 3 minutes; slice between the bones into segments.
6. Shower with spring onion and sesame.

## Plating & Service
Piled on a warm platter, bones on; serve with lettuce, ssamjang and rice — napkins mandatory.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Boneless short rib or lamb ribs cure the same; grill low and slow (170°C, 8 min/side) if flare-ups threaten.
