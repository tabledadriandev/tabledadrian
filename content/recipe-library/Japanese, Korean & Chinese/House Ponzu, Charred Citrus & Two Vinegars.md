# House Ponzu, Charred Citrus & Two Vinegars

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The boat's master citrus-soy — charring the fruit first deepens it from condiment to signature.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Lemons — 2
- Limes — 2
- Orange — 1
- Tamari — 200 ml
- Rice vinegar — 60 ml
- Black vinegar — 15 ml
- Mirin — 60 ml
- Kombu — 5 g
- Katsuobushi — 8 g

## Method
1. Halve all citrus; char cut-side down in a dry pan until deeply caramelised, 4–5 minutes.
2. Simmer mirin 1 minute to burn off alcohol.
3. Juice the charred citrus (target 140 ml); strain.
4. Combine juice, tamari, both vinegars, mirin, kombu and katsuobushi in a jar.
5. Steep 24 hours in the fridge; strain through muslin.
6. Bottle; it improves for a week and holds a month chilled.

## Plating & Service
Decant into a small pouring cruet for the table; the base for tataki, shabu dips, dressings and the ponzu gel.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: drop katsuobushi, double the kombu, add 3 g dried shiitake; yuzu juice (40 ml) replaces half the citrus when available.
