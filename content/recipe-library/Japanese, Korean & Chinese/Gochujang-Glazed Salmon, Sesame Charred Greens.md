# Gochujang-Glazed Salmon, Sesame Charred Greens

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A four-ingredient glaze that behaves like a professional lacquer — weeknight fast, charter elegant.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Salmon fillets, skin on — 4 × 170 g
- Gochujang — 40 g
- Honey — 25 g
- Tamari — 20 ml
- Rice vinegar — 12 ml
- Toasted sesame oil — 10 ml
- Tenderstem broccoli — 300 g
- Garlic, sliced — 8 g
- Neutral oil — 20 ml
- Toasted sesame seeds — 6 g
- Spring onion — 15 g
- Lime — 1

## Method
1. Whisk gochujang, honey, tamari and vinegar; reserve a third for serving.
2. Roast salmon skin-down at 220°C for 6 minutes.
3. Brush thickly with glaze; roast 4–5 minutes more to 50°C core, glazing once again midway.
4. Char broccoli in a ripping wok with neutral oil 3 minutes; add garlic for the last 30 seconds.
5. Dress greens with sesame oil and seeds.
6. Plate salmon over greens; spoon reserved glaze around, spring onion and lime to finish.

## Plating & Service
Glaze should be blistered and mahogany at the edges; a lime cheek per plate cuts the sweetness.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The glaze suits pork chops, aubergine steaks (vegan with agave) and chicken wings equally well.
