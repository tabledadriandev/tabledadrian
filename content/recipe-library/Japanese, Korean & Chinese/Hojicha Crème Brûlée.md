# Hojicha Crème Brûlée

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Roasted-tea smokiness under the mandatory glass lid of burnt sugar — the crossover dessert every charter asks for again.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Double cream — 500 ml
- Whole milk — 100 ml
- Hojicha (loose leaf) — 18 g
- Egg yolks — 7
- Caster sugar — 90 g
- Vanilla — 2 ml
- Fine salt — 1 g
- Demerara or caster sugar (top) — 50 g

## Method
1. Bring cream and milk to 85°C; add hojicha, cut heat, steep 8 minutes; strain, pressing lightly.
2. Reweigh and top up to 580 ml with cream if needed.
3. Whisk yolks, sugar, salt and vanilla; temper in the warm infusion.
4. Pass through a fine sieve; rest 10 minutes and skim the foam.
5. Bake in shallow dishes in a bain-marie at 120°C for 35–45 minutes to a uniform tremble (set at 82°C core).
6. Chill 4 hours. Top with a thin even layer of sugar; torch to dark amber glass. Rest 2 minutes, serve.

## Plating & Service
The tap-crack moment belongs to the guest — serve within 15 minutes of torching, never pre-burnt.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Matcha version: whisk 8 g into the warm custard base; genmaicha steep gives a popcorn note that pairs with miso caramel.
