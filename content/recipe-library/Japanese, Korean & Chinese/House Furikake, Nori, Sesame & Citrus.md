# House Furikake, Nori, Sesame & Citrus

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The rice-seasoning blend that recycles the dashi larder — spent kombu, katsuobushi, sesame and dried citrus zest.

## Ingredients (serves 16)
- Spent kombu and katsuobushi from dashi — 60 g
- Tamari — 15 ml
- Mirin — 15 ml
- Caster sugar — 8 g
- White sesame seeds — 40 g
- Black sesame seeds — 20 g
- Nori — 3 sheets
- Dried lemon or yuzu zest — 3 g
- Flaky salt — 6 g
- Shichimi — 1 g

## Method
1. Chop the spent kombu fine; shred the spent katsuobushi.
2. Cook both with tamari, mirin and sugar over low heat until dry and crumbly, 8–10 minutes.
3. Toast both sesames 3 minutes.
4. Toast nori over a flame 5 seconds a side; scissor into confetti.
5. Dry everything on a tray at 90°C for 10 minutes; cool completely.
6. Toss with zest, salt and shichimi; jar airtight — 1 month.

## Plating & Service
On rice, eggs, avocado, grilled fish, buttered toast; the crew jar empties faster than the guest jar.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: build from fresh toasted kombu without katsuobushi; add 10 g dried shiso (yukari) for a purple, tangy version.
