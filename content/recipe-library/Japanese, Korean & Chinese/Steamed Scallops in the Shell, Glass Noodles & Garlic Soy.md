# Steamed Scallops in the Shell, Glass Noodles & Garlic Soy

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Cantonese banquet classic — scallops steamed on the half-shell over vermicelli that drinks every drop of garlicky juice.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Scallops in the shell — 8
- Mung bean vermicelli — 60 g
- Garlic — 30 g, minced
- Neutral oil — 40 ml
- Light soy sauce — 25 ml
- Caster sugar — 5 g
- Water or scallop juice — 30 ml
- Shaoxing wine — 10 ml
- Spring onion, fine — 20 g
- Red chilli, brunoise — 5 g

## Method
1. Shuck scallops, clean the shells, and soak vermicelli in warm water 10 minutes; snip into 8 cm lengths.
2. Fry half the garlic in oil at 150°C until pale gold; combine with the raw half, soy, sugar, wine and water for the sauce.
3. Nest a coil of noodles in each shell, sit the scallop on top, and spoon over 12 ml sauce.
4. Steam at 100°C for 5–6 minutes until the scallops are just opaque.
5. Scatter spring onion and chilli; flash 5 ml smoking-hot oil over each to sizzle.

## Plating & Service
Serve four shells per pair on a bed of rock salt to keep them level; the noodles are the prize.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Tiger prawns butterflied on the shell take the same treatment; tamari keeps it gluten-free.
