# Wafu Hamburg Steak, Grated Daikon Ponzu

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Japan's beloved hamburg in the light wafu register — juicy beef-pork patty, sharp daikon-ponzu instead of demi-glace.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Beef mince (20% fat) — 400 g
- Pork mince — 200 g
- Onion, minced and sweated — 120 g
- Panko — 30 g
- Milk-free: dashi — 30 ml
- Egg — 1
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Black pepper — 1.5 g
- Nutmeg — 0.5 g
- Neutral oil — 15 ml
- Daikon, grated, drained — 150 g
- Ponzu — 80 ml
- Shiso, chiffonade — 4 leaves
- Spring onion — 10 g

## Method
1. Soak panko in dashi. Sweat onion until translucent; cool fully.
2. Knead minces with salt 1 minute until sticky; add onion, panko, egg, pepper and nutmeg.
3. Form 4 patties, slapping air out between palms; dimple the centres. Chill 15 minutes.
4. Sear in oil at 200°C, 2 minutes per side.
5. Lid on, heat low; cook 6–7 minutes to 71°C core, resting 3 minutes after.
6. Top each with daikon oroshi, flood with ponzu, finish with shiso and spring onion.

## Plating & Service
On a hot plate so the ponzu sizzles as it is poured at the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: swap panko for rice crumbs and check the ponzu; add a soft-fried egg on top for lunch service.
