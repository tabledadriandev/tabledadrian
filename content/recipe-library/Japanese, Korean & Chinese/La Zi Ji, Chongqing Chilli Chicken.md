# La Zi Ji, Chongqing Chilli Chicken

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A mountain of toasted chillies hiding crisp chicken nuggets — the hunt is the fun, the tingle is the point.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chicken thighs, 2 cm dice — 700 g
- Soy sauce — 20 ml
- Shaoxing wine — 20 ml
- Ginger, grated — 10 g
- Potato starch — 60 g
- Neutral oil — 1 L
- Dried facing-heaven chillies — 60 g
- Sichuan peppercorns — 6 g
- Garlic, sliced — 20 g
- Caster sugar — 8 g
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Toasted sesame seeds — 8 g
- Spring onion, rings — 20 g

## Method
1. Marinate chicken in soy, wine and ginger 20 minutes; toss through starch for a thin, dry coat.
2. Snip chillies in half and shake out most seeds.
3. Fry chicken at 170°C for 4 minutes; rest 3 minutes, then re-fry at 195°C for 90 seconds until deeply crisp.
4. Heat 25 ml oil in the wok; toast chillies and Sichuan pepper at medium heat 60 seconds until glossy and fragrant — never black.
5. Add garlic 20 seconds, then chicken, sugar and salt; toss violently 60 seconds.
6. Finish with sesame and spring onion.

## Plating & Service
Serve the full red mountain in a wide bowl; instruct guests to eat the chicken, not the chillies.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cauliflower or firm tofu run the same route; a squeeze of lime at the end is untraditional and excellent.
