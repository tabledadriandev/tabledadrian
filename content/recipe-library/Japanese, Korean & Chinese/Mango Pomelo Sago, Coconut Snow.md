# Mango Pomelo Sago, Coconut Snow

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Hong Kong's great chilled dessert soup — ripe mango cream, pearls of sago, bursts of pomelo — light enough after a banquet.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Ripe mangoes — 3 (700 g flesh)
- Small sago pearls — 60 g
- Coconut milk — 250 ml
- Caster sugar — 50 g
- Water — 100 ml
- Lime juice — 10 ml
- Pomelo segments, torn into vesicles — 150 g
- Coconut cream, frozen and grated — 60 g
- Mint tips — 6

## Method
1. Boil sago in plenty of water 10–12 minutes until nearly clear; stand 5 minutes, then rinse cold. Hold in cold water.
2. Dissolve sugar in the 100 ml water; chill.
3. Blend two mangoes with coconut milk, syrup and lime to a smooth cream; chill hard.
4. Dice the remaining mango small.
5. Fold drained sago into the mango cream.
6. Serve in chilled glasses: cream, diced mango, pomelo vesicles, and a snowfall of grated frozen coconut cream; mint tip on top.

## Plating & Service
Glass service, well chilled, spoons long; the pomelo must be scattered at the last minute to keep its pop.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Grapefruit stands in for pomelo (use less); a spoon of ripe passionfruit sharpens the tropical register.
