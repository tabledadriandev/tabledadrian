# Japchae, Sweet Potato Noodles & Seven Vegetables

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Glassy dangmyeon tossed with vegetables cooked one by one — chewy, glossy celebration noodles that hold beautifully on a buffet.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Sweet potato glass noodles (dangmyeon) — 300 g
- Tamari — 60 ml
- Caster sugar — 30 g
- Toasted sesame oil — 35 ml
- Shiitake, sliced — 150 g
- Spinach — 200 g
- Carrot, julienne — 120 g
- Onion, slivers — 120 g
- Red pepper, julienne — 100 g
- Garlic, minced — 12 g
- Neutral oil — 30 ml
- Spring onion — 25 g
- Toasted sesame seeds — 10 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Whisk tamari, sugar and 20 ml sesame oil for the sauce.
2. Boil noodles 6–7 minutes until chewy-clear; rinse cold, drain, and snip into forkable lengths; toss with a third of the sauce.
3. Cook each vegetable separately in neutral oil with a pinch of salt — onion 2 minutes, carrot 90 seconds, pepper 90 seconds, shiitake 3 minutes with garlic; blanch spinach 30 seconds and squeeze.
4. Warm the noodles through in a wide pan 2 minutes with the remaining sauce.
5. Fold in all vegetables off the heat — tongs, lifting high to keep strands separate.
6. Finish with remaining sesame oil, spring onion, sesame and pepper.

## Plating & Service
Room temperature or just-warm, piled loose and glossy; it sits happily 2 hours, making it the chef's buffet ace.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add seared beef strips for the classic; the noodle is naturally gluten-free — verify the tamari.
