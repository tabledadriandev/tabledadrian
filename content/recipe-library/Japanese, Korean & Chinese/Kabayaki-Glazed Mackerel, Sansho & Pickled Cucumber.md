# Kabayaki-Glazed Mackerel, Sansho & Pickled Cucumber

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Unagi treatment on Riviera mackerel — sweet soy lacquer, blistered skin, numbing sansho against the oily richness.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Mackerel fillets, pin-boned — 8 (600 g)
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Soy sauce — 60 ml
- Mirin — 60 ml
- Sake — 30 ml
- Caster sugar — 25 g
- Cucumber — 150 g
- Rice vinegar — 30 ml
- Caster sugar (pickle) — 10 g
- Sansho pepper — 1 g
- Neutral oil — 10 ml

## Method
1. Salt mackerel 15 minutes; pat dry.
2. Reduce soy, mirin, sake and sugar by half to a glaze.
3. Slice cucumber 1 mm, salt lightly, squeeze, and dress in vinegar and sugar.
4. Oil the skin; grill skin-side up at 230°C for 5 minutes until blistered.
5. Brush with glaze and return 90 seconds; repeat twice more until lacquered.
6. Rest 1 minute; dust with sansho.

## Plating & Service
Two fillets crossed over a rice mound or plain, cucumber pickle in a tight pile — richness, sweetness, acid in one frame.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Identical glaze for eel, salmon belly or king oyster mushrooms (vegan); tamari for GF.
