# Cumin Lamb Stir-Fry, Xinjiang Style

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Silk Road flavours in four minutes of wok time — lamb, whole cumin, chilli and raw onion crunch.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Lamb leg or shoulder, 5 mm slices — 600 g
- Soy sauce — 20 ml
- Shaoxing wine — 15 ml
- Potato starch — 8 g
- Cumin seeds — 8 g
- Gochugaru or coarse chilli flakes — 8 g
- Fennel seeds — 2 g
- Neutral oil — 40 ml
- Garlic, sliced — 12 g
- Onion, thick slivers — 150 g
- Fine salt — 3 g
- Coriander, whole sprigs — 20 g
- Spring onion batons — 20 g

## Method
1. Marinate lamb in soy, wine and starch 15 minutes.
2. Toast cumin and fennel seeds in a dry wok 45 seconds; half-crush with chilli flakes.
3. Sear lamb in oil in a smoking wok, one layer, 60 seconds untouched, then 60 seconds tossing.
4. Add garlic and the spice crush; 30 seconds.
5. Add onion and salt; toss 45 seconds — onion must keep bite.
6. Off heat, fold through coriander and spring onion.

## Plating & Service
On a hot platter, more coriander than seems reasonable; flatbreads or rice both work.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Beef bavette or portobello strips swap cleanly; add 5 g Sichuan pepper for a ma-la drift.
