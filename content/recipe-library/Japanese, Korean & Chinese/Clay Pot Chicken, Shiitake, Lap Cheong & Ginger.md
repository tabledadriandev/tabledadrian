# Clay Pot Chicken, Shiitake, Lap Cheong & Ginger

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A lidded pot of soy-braised chicken, sausage fat perfuming everything — open it at the table for the steam moment.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chicken thighs, bone-in, chopped — 900 g
- Dried shiitake, soaked — 40 g (liquid reserved)
- Lap cheong, bias-sliced — 100 g
- Ginger, thick slices — 30 g
- Garlic — 15 g
- Light soy — 35 ml
- Dark soy — 10 ml
- Oyster sauce — 20 g
- Shaoxing wine — 30 ml
- Caster sugar — 10 g
- Shiitake water — 200 ml
- Potato starch — 8 g
- Neutral oil — 20 ml
- Spring onion — 30 g
- Sesame oil — 8 ml

## Method
1. Marinate chicken with soys, oyster sauce, wine, sugar and starch 30 minutes.
2. Fry ginger and garlic in oil in a clay pot or heavy casserole 1 minute.
3. Add chicken skin-down; sear 5 minutes.
4. Add shiitake, lap cheong and mushroom water; lid on and braise at a low simmer 25 minutes to 80°C in the thigh.
5. Reduce uncovered 5 minutes until the sauce coats.
6. Finish with spring onion and sesame oil; re-lid for the table.

## Plating & Service
Lid lifted in front of guests; serve over rice — or cook rice in the same pot beneath the chicken for the full bo zai fan effect.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skip lap cheong and add smoked bacon lardons in a pinch; dried chilli + black beans push it Hunanese.
