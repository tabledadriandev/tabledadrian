# Wagyu Beef Tataki, Smoked Ponzu & Crispy Garlic

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Seared-raw wagyu with cold-smoked ponzu — a luxury opener that needs only a hot pan and confidence.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Wagyu or top-grade sirloin, trimmed — 350 g
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Grapeseed oil — 10 ml
- Ponzu — 90 ml
- Smoked bonito flakes — 3 g
- Garlic — 3 cloves, 1.5 mm slices
- Neutral oil for frying garlic — 100 ml
- Spring onion, fine rings — 15 g
- Fresh wasabi or paste — 8 g
- Myoga or pink radish, shaved — 20 g

## Method
1. Steep smoked bonito in ponzu 20 minutes; strain (or cold-smoke the ponzu 10 minutes if a smoking gun is aboard).
2. Fry garlic slices from cold oil, bringing to 150°C, until pale gold; drain on paper — they crisp as they cool.
3. Salt and oil the beef; sear in a pan at 250°C for 45 seconds per face, keeping the core raw.
4. Rest 3 minutes, then chill 10 minutes wrapped tightly.
5. Slice 6 mm thick against the grain.
6. Dress with smoked ponzu, scatter garlic chips, spring onion, shaved myoga; serve wasabi beside.

## Plating & Service
Slices shingled on a chilled stone plate, sauce pooled at the base so the crust stays defined.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Duck breast (skin rendered first) is an excellent swap; drop garlic chips for a low-FODMAP table and finish with chive oil.
