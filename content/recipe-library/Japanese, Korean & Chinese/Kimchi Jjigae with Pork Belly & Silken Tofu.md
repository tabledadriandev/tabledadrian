# Kimchi Jjigae with Pork Belly & Silken Tofu

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The reason to over-ferment kimchi on purpose — a bubbling crimson stew that gets deeper the older the kimchi.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Well-aged kimchi, chopped — 400 g
- Kimchi brine — 80 ml
- Pork belly, 1 cm slices — 300 g
- Toasted sesame oil — 15 ml
- Gochugaru — 10 g
- Gochujang — 15 g
- Garlic, minced — 10 g
- Anchovy-kombu stock or water — 700 ml
- Fish sauce — 10 ml
- Caster sugar — 5 g
- Silken tofu, thick slabs — 300 g
- Spring onion — 30 g
- Onion, sliced — 80 g

## Method
1. Sear pork belly in sesame oil in the stew pot until the fat renders, 4 minutes.
2. Add kimchi; fry 5 minutes until it smells sweet and the edges catch.
3. Stir in gochugaru, gochujang and garlic for 1 minute.
4. Add stock, brine, fish sauce, sugar and onion; simmer lidded 20 minutes.
5. Slide in tofu slabs; simmer 5 minutes without stirring hard.
6. Finish with spring onion; taste — old kimchi may need no more seasoning at all.

## Plating & Service
Serve roaring hot in a stone pot or copper pan, rice and two banchan alongside — humble, perfect.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Tuna (canned, added late) replaces pork in the classic variant; vegan with mushroom stock, soy for fish sauce.
