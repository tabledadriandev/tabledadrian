# Smacked Cucumber, Black Vinegar & Garlic

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Craggy smashed cucumber that drinks up a garlicky black-vinegar dressing — the fastest, most refreshing plate on the boat.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Cucumbers — 500 g
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Caster sugar — 8 g
- Chinkiang black vinegar — 25 ml
- Light soy sauce — 15 ml
- Garlic, grated — 8 g
- Toasted sesame oil — 10 ml
- Chilli oil — 10 ml (optional)
- Toasted sesame seeds — 4 g
- Coriander leaves — 5 g

## Method
1. Smack cucumbers with the flat of a cleaver until they crack, then tear/cut into 3 cm craggy pieces.
2. Toss with salt and half the sugar; drain in a sieve 15 minutes.
3. Whisk vinegar, soy, remaining sugar, garlic and sesame oil.
4. Toss cucumbers in the dressing just before serving.
5. Finish with chilli oil, sesame and coriander.

## Plating & Service
Serve cold and slightly heaped, juices left behind in the mixing bowl.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skip garlic and add ginger for low-FODMAP; a pinch of Sichuan pepper oil turns it ma-la.
