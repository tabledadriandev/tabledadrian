# Melon Bingsu, Shaved Milk Snow & Condensed Drizzle

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Charentais melon carved into a bowl of milk snow — Korean shave-ice logic with the Riviera's best fruit.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Whole milk — 400 ml
- Condensed milk — 90 g + 40 g to drizzle
- Honey — 15 g
- Fine salt — 1 g
- Charentais or cantaloupe melon — 1 large
- Lime — 1, zest and juice
- Mint, chiffonade — 4 g

## Method
1. Whisk milk, 90 g condensed milk, honey and salt; freeze flat in a tray, forking to crystals every 45 minutes, 3–4 hours (or freeze solid and shave/blitz).
2. Halve the melon in zigzag cuts; scoop balls and chill; reserve the shells.
3. Season the melon balls with lime juice.
4. Pack the shaved milk snow into the chilled melon shells.
5. Bury and crown with melon balls.
6. Drizzle condensed milk; finish with lime zest and mint.

## Plating & Service
The melon-shell presentation lands at the table with two spoons per shell — built for sharing photos and sharing.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan with coconut milk + coconut condensed milk; strawberry-black sesame is the winter variant in a glass.
