# Galbi Jjim, Braised Short Ribs with Chestnut & Jujube

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Celebration short ribs — glossy, spoon-soft, sweetened with pear and studded with chestnuts, built a day ahead.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Beef short ribs, bone-in, 5 cm — 1.6 kg
- Water — 1 L
- Soy sauce — 120 ml
- Caster sugar — 40 g
- Nashi pear, grated — 200 g
- Onion, grated — 100 g
- Garlic — 25 g
- Ginger — 15 g
- Mirin or cheongju — 60 ml
- Cooked chestnuts — 120 g
- Dried jujubes — 8
- Carrot, large roll-cut — 200 g
- Daikon, 3 cm chunks — 250 g
- Toasted sesame oil — 12 ml
- Pine nuts — 10 g

## Method
1. Soak ribs 1 hour in cold water; blanch 8 minutes, rinse, and trim surface fat.
2. Blend soy, sugar, pear, onion, garlic, ginger and mirin.
3. Braise ribs in the sauce plus water, lidded, at a bare simmer 90 minutes.
4. Add daikon, carrot and jujubes; braise 40 minutes more.
5. Add chestnuts; reduce uncovered 20 minutes, basting, until the sauce naps a spoon and ribs wobble on the bone.
6. Rest, ideally chill overnight and de-fat; reheat gently, finish with sesame oil and pine nuts.

## Plating & Service
One rib per bowl with a spoonful of everything, sauce lacquered over; serve with white rice only.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pressure cooker: 45 minutes high, then reduce; omit pine nuts for nut-free and finish with sesame instead.
