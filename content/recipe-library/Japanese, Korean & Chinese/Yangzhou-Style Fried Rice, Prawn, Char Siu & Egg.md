# Yangzhou-Style Fried Rice, Prawn, Char Siu & Egg

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The gold-standard fried rice — cold rice, screaming wok, each grain lacquered in egg with sweet pork and prawn.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Day-old cooked jasmine or short-grain rice — 700 g
- Raw prawns, diced — 150 g
- Char siu or ham, small dice — 120 g
- Eggs — 3
- Frozen peas — 80 g
- Spring onion — 30 g
- Neutral oil — 45 ml
- Light soy — 18 ml
- Shaoxing wine — 15 ml
- Fine salt — 4 g
- White pepper — 1 g
- Toasted sesame oil — 5 ml

## Method
1. Break the cold rice apart with wet hands — no clumps enter the wok.
2. Sear prawns in 15 ml oil 60 seconds; remove at half-cooked.
3. Scramble eggs loosely in 15 ml oil; remove at wet-set.
4. Remaining oil to smoking; rice in — toss and press 2 minutes until grains hiss and jump.
5. Wine around the rim, then soy, salt, char siu, peas and prawns; toss 90 seconds.
6. Return eggs, breaking them through; finish with spring onion, white pepper and sesame oil.

## Plating & Service
Mounded in a domed bowl and turned out — the classic presentation; serve within 2 minutes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The formula holds anything: crab and asparagus, chicken and shiitake, or plain egg-spring onion; day-old rice is non-negotiable.
