# White-Cut Chicken, Two Sauces

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bai qie ji — chicken poached off the boil to absolute silk, served cool with ginger-scallion oil and a soy-shallot dressing.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Whole chicken, best quality — 1 × 1.4 kg
- Water — 3 L
- Ginger — 40 g
- Spring onions — 4
- Fine salt — 25 g
- Shaoxing wine — 30 ml
- Ginger, minced (sauce one) — 30 g
- Spring onion, minced — 40 g
- Neutral oil — 60 ml
- Light soy (sauce two) — 40 ml
- Poaching stock — 20 ml
- Caster sugar — 5 g
- Shallot, fried, & its oil — 20 g
- Coriander — 8 g

## Method
1. Bring water, ginger, spring onion, salt and wine to a boil; submerge the chicken, return to a bare simmer 5 minutes.
2. Cut the heat, lid tight, and let it sit 35–40 minutes to 68°C in the deepest thigh.
3. Plunge into iced water 15 minutes — this sets the signature gelatinous skin.
4. Sauce one: cover minced ginger and spring onion with 190°C oil; salt.
5. Sauce two: warm soy, stock, sugar and fried shallot oil.
6. Dry the bird, chop through the bone into neat pieces, and rebuild on the platter.

## Plating & Service
Room temperature, skin intact and glistening; both sauces in bowls — guests alternate.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Poussins take 20 minutes' residual poach; the stock becomes tomorrow's congee — waste nothing.
