# Soy-Mirin Braised Turbot Wing, Ginger & Burnt Leek

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Nitsuke braising on the king of flatfish — gelatinous wing meat lacquered in its own reduced cooking liquor.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Turbot wings or thick tranches — 4 × 220 g
- Water — 300 ml
- Sake — 150 ml
- Soy sauce — 70 ml
- Mirin — 70 ml
- Caster sugar — 20 g
- Ginger, sliced — 25 g
- Leeks — 2
- Neutral oil — 10 ml
- Shiso or chervil — 4 g

## Method
1. Pour boiling water over the turbot pieces for 10 seconds; refresh and rub away any scales or slime (shimofuri).
2. Char whole leeks in a dry pan until blackened; peel to the soft heart and cut into 4 cm barrels.
3. Bring water, sake, soy, mirin, sugar and ginger to a simmer in a wide pan.
4. Add fish skin-up with a dropped paper lid (otoshibuta); simmer briskly 10–12 minutes, basting.
5. Add leeks for the final 4 minutes.
6. Lift fish carefully; reduce the liquor to a light syrup and spoon over.

## Plating & Service
One wing per shallow bowl, leeks beside, liquor glossing everything; finger bowls — this is bone-sucking territory.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skate, halibut or dorade steaks braise identically; add 20 g gochugaru butter at the end to drift it Korean.
