# Kung Pao Chicken, Charred Peanuts & Heaven-Facing Chillies

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Gong bao ji ding with the proper litchi-flavour balance — sugar and vinegar in tension, tingling heat, wok breath.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chicken thighs, 2 cm dice — 600 g
- Soy sauce — 15 ml
- Shaoxing wine — 15 ml
- Potato starch — 10 g
- Dried chillies, snipped — 12
- Sichuan peppercorns — 3 g
- Raw peanuts — 80 g
- Neutral oil — 50 ml
- Garlic, sliced — 12 g
- Ginger, sliced — 10 g
- Spring onion, 2 cm batons — 50 g
- Caster sugar — 20 g
- Black vinegar — 20 ml
- Soy sauce (sauce) — 20 ml
- Water — 30 ml
- Potato starch (sauce) — 4 g

## Method
1. Marinate chicken in soy, wine and starch 15 minutes.
2. Fry peanuts in the oil at 150°C until pale gold; drain.
3. Whisk sugar, vinegar, soy, water and starch for the sauce.
4. Heat the wok to smoking; sear chicken in the peanut oil, undisturbed 60 seconds, then toss 2 minutes.
5. Push aside; bloom chillies and Sichuan pepper 20 seconds, then garlic and ginger.
6. Add sauce; it thickens in 30 seconds — toss everything with spring onion and peanuts and leave the pan.

## Plating & Service
Serve the moment the sauce turns glossy; rice essential — the sauce is the reward.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Prawns or diced pressed tofu swap in; cashews for the peanut-allergic (changes allergen to nuts).
