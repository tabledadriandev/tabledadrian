# Dak Galbi, Gochujang Chicken with Sweet Potato & Perilla

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Chuncheon's one-pan chicken — fiery gochujang glaze, chewy rice cakes, sweet potato, all scraped from the same hot steel.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chicken thighs, 3 cm pieces — 700 g
- Gochujang — 60 g
- Gochugaru — 12 g
- Soy sauce — 25 ml
- Caster sugar — 18 g
- Garlic, grated — 15 g
- Ginger — 8 g
- Toasted sesame oil — 15 ml
- Sweet potato, 1 cm half-moons — 250 g
- Cabbage, 4 cm squares — 250 g
- Tteok (rice cakes) — 150 g
- Neutral oil — 25 ml
- Perilla or basil leaves — 10
- Toasted sesame seeds — 6 g

## Method
1. Whisk gochujang, gochugaru, soy, sugar, garlic, ginger and sesame oil; coat chicken 30 minutes.
2. Soak rice cakes in warm water 15 minutes if stiff.
3. Heat oil in a wide pan at 220°C; add sweet potato 3 minutes.
4. Add chicken with all its paste; sear 4 minutes without stirring, then turn.
5. Add cabbage and rice cakes; stir-fry 8–10 minutes until chicken reaches 74°C and the paste toasts onto every edge.
6. Tear in perilla off the heat; finish with sesame.

## Plating & Service
Straight from the pan to a trivet at the table; offer fried rice made in the crusty pan as a second act.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Halloumi or extra sweet potato for vegetarian; drop gochugaru by half and it stays family-friendly.
