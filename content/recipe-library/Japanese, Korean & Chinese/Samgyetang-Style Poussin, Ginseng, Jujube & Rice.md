# Samgyetang-Style Poussin, Ginseng, Jujube & Rice

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The restorative whole-bird soup, scaled to individual poussins — garlic, ginseng and jujube in a milky, gentle broth.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Poussins — 4 small (400 g each)
- Glutinous rice, soaked 1 hour — 120 g
- Fresh or dried ginseng root — 12 g
- Garlic cloves, whole — 24
- Dried jujubes — 8
- Ginger — 10 g
- Water — 2 L
- Fine salt — 8 g
- White pepper — 1 g
- Spring onion, fine — 30 g
- Toasted sesame oil — 8 ml

## Method
1. Rinse soaked rice; stuff each poussin with rice, 4 garlic cloves and a jujube; close the cavity with a skewer or leg-lock.
2. Set birds snug in a pot with remaining garlic, jujubes, ginseng and ginger.
3. Cover with water; bring to a simmer, skim, and cook gently (90°C) 70 minutes until legs loosen and rice is swollen.
4. Season the broth with half the salt only — the table seasons the rest.
5. Lift each bird into a deep bowl; ladle broth over.
6. Serve with a salt-pepper dip, spring onion and a drop of sesame oil.

## Plating & Service
One bird per lidded bowl, opened at the table for the aromatic steam; small spoons for the rice inside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken legs + rice cooked loose make a fast crew version; add 5 g wolfberries with the jujube for colour.
