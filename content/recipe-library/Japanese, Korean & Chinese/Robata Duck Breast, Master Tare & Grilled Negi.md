# Robata Duck Breast, Master Tare & Grilled Negi

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Yakitori logic applied to a whole duck breast — slow skin render, tare lacquer, blushing slices over sweet charred onion.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Duck breasts — 2 large (700 g)
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Tare (soy 120 ml, mirin 120 ml, sake 60 ml, sugar 40 g, duck trim) — 340 ml
- Negi or thick spring onions — 8
- Grapeseed oil — 10 ml
- Sansho pepper — 1 g
- Yuzu zest — 1 g
- Watercress — 40 g

## Method
1. Simmer the tare ingredients with duck skin trim 20 minutes; reduce to a light syrup and strain.
2. Score duck skin in a tight crosshatch; salt both sides.
3. Render skin-down in a cold pan brought to medium (170°C) for 10–12 minutes until the skin is thin and glass-crisp.
4. Grill flesh-side 2 minutes, then lacquer with tare 3 times over the coals, 3 minutes total, to 54°C core.
5. Rest 6 minutes; grill oiled negi segments until charred and jammy.
6. Slice duck 8 mm, dust with sansho and yuzu zest.

## Plating & Service
Fanned slices over negi, a final brush of warm tare, watercress dressed in the board juices.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF with tamari tare; the same method suits pigeon (48°C core) and pork presa (62°C).
