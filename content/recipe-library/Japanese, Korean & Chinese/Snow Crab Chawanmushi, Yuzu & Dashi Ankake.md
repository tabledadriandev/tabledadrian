# Snow Crab Chawanmushi, Yuzu & Dashi Ankake

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A trembling savoury custard hiding sweet crab, under a glossy dashi glaze — silk in a cup, served in any sea state.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Eggs — 3 (165 g)
- Ichiban dashi, cooled — 450 ml
- Usukuchi soy — 12 ml
- Mirin — 10 ml
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Picked snow crab — 120 g
- Shiitake, thin slices — 40 g
- Dashi for ankake — 150 ml
- Potato starch — 6 g
- Yuzu zest — 1 g
- Mitsuba or chervil — 4 sprigs

## Method
1. Whisk eggs without aerating; blend with 450 ml dashi, soy, mirin and salt (1:2.7 egg-to-dashi) and pass twice through a fine sieve.
2. Divide crab and shiitake between 4 cups; pour custard to 1 cm below the rim and lid with foil.
3. Steam at exactly 85°C for 22–25 minutes until just set with a central wobble.
4. Simmer the ankake dashi, season lightly, and thicken with starch slurried in 15 ml water to a nappe glaze.
5. Spoon 30 ml glaze over each custard.
6. Finish with yuzu zest and mitsuba.

## Plating & Service
Serve in the steaming cup on a saucer with a small spoon; warn guests it is lava-hot beneath the glaze.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Replace crab with chicken and ginkgo for the classic; vegan version sets 100% shiitake-kombu dashi with 4 g agar instead of egg.
