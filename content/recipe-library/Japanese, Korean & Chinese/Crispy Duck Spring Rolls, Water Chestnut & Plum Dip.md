# Crispy Duck Spring Rolls, Water Chestnut & Plum Dip

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Confit duck and water-chestnut crunch in a shattering cigar — a canapé that pairs dangerously well with Champagne.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Confit or roast duck leg meat, shredded — 300 g
- Water chestnuts, diced — 80 g
- Shiitake, diced and sautéed — 60 g
- Spring onion — 30 g
- Hoisin — 30 g
- Soy sauce — 10 ml
- Five-spice — 1 g
- Toasted sesame oil — 6 ml
- Spring roll pastry, 20 cm squares — 12
- Flour paste (flour 15 g + water 20 ml)
- Neutral oil — 1.5 L
- Plum jam — 60 g
- Rice vinegar — 15 ml
- Ginger, grated — 4 g

## Method
1. Mix duck, water chestnut, shiitake, spring onion, hoisin, soy, five-spice and sesame oil; chill 20 minutes.
2. Warm plum jam with vinegar and ginger; blend smooth for the dip.
3. Place 35 g filling in a low bar across each wrapper corner; roll tight, folding in the sides, sealing with flour paste.
4. Rest seam-down 10 minutes.
5. Fry at 175°C for 4–5 minutes to an even deep gold.
6. Drain upright 1 minute; cut on a hard bias.

## Plating & Service
Stack like firewood over the dip; serve within 5 minutes of frying.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Shredded chicken or pure mushroom-cabbage (vegan) versions roll identically; bake at 220°C, oiled, for a lighter finish.
