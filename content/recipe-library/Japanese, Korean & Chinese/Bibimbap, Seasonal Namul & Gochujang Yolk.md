# Bibimbap, Seasonal Namul & Gochujang Yolk

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Five vegetables, each seasoned as its own dish, over crusted rice with a gochujang-honey sauce — order inside a bowl.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Cooked short-grain rice, hot — 800 g
- Spinach — 200 g
- Soybean sprouts — 200 g
- Carrot, julienne — 120 g
- Shiitake, sliced — 150 g
- Courgette, half-moons — 150 g
- Toasted sesame oil — 40 ml
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Garlic, minced — 10 g
- Soy sauce — 20 ml
- Gochujang — 60 g
- Honey — 20 g
- Rice vinegar — 10 ml
- Eggs — 4
- Neutral oil — 30 ml
- Toasted sesame seeds — 10 g

## Method
1. Season each vegetable separately: blanch spinach and sprouts (dress with sesame oil, salt, garlic); sauté carrot, courgette (salt) and shiitake (soy) each alone, keeping colours clean.
2. Whisk gochujang, honey, vinegar and 10 ml sesame oil to a pourable sauce.
3. Oil 4 hot stone or cast-iron bowls (or one wide pan) with sesame oil; press in the rice and leave on medium heat 5 minutes to crust.
4. Fry eggs sunny, edges lacy, yolks liquid.
5. Arrange vegetables in spokes over the rice; centre the egg.
6. Serve the sauce beside; the guest stirs everything violently — encourage it.

## Plating & Service
Stone bowls still crackling as they land; the mix-it-yourself ritual is the dish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add bulgogi beef or keep it temple-style vegan (no egg, maple for honey); GF with tamari and certified gochujang.
