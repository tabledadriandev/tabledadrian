# Ginger-Scallion Oil, The Master Condiment

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Two aromatics, hot oil, salt — the Cantonese answer to salsa verde; poached chicken, steamed fish and plain rice all bow to it.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Spring onions, minced fine — 100 g
- Young ginger, minced fine — 60 g
- Fine salt — 8 g
- Neutral oil — 150 ml
- Toasted sesame oil — 5 ml (optional)

## Method
1. Combine spring onion, ginger and salt in a deep heatproof bowl (it will surge).
2. Heat oil to 190°C — a haze, not smoke.
3. Pour the oil over in two additions, stirring between — it must roar and foam.
4. Stir in sesame oil if using.
5. Rest 30 minutes; jar. Fridge, 1 week — the first 3 days are the best.

## Plating & Service
Serve at room temperature in a spoonful dose; fold through hot rice or noodles for an instant course.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 5 ml light soy at serving (not storage); a Riviera version with lemon zest folded in after cooling is superb on grilled fish.
