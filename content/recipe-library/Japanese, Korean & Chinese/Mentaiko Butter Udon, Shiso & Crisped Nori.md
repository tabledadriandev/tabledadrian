# Mentaiko Butter Udon, Shiso & Crisped Nori

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Cod-roe butter emulsified over fat chewy udon — five ingredients, carbonara logic, Tokyo late-night soul.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Frozen sanuki udon — 4 portions (720 g)
- Mentaiko or tarako sacs — 80 g
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Egg yolks — 2
- Usukuchi soy — 10 ml
- Udon cooking water — 60 ml
- Shiso, chiffonade — 6 leaves
- Nori, torn and dry-toasted — 2 sheets
- Lemon zest — 1 g
- White pepper — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Scrape mentaiko from its membrane; reserve a spoonful for garnish.
2. Boil udon from frozen 60–90 seconds until just loosened; reserve cooking water.
3. Off the heat, toss udon with butter until glossy.
4. Working off the boil (below 70°C — roe scrambles), fold in mentaiko, yolks, soy and cooking water to a clinging pink emulsion.
5. Season with white pepper.
6. Bowl up; top with reserved roe, shiso, crisped nori and lemon zest.

## Plating & Service
Twirled high in warm (not hot) bowls, garnish at 12 o'clock; eat immediately, it waits for no one.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Smoked cod roe from a French traiteur substitutes for mentaiko; spaghetti works when udon runs out — nobody complains.
