# Yukhoe, Korean Beef Tartare with Nashi Pear & Pine Nuts

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sweet pear threads under sesame-slick raw beef and a cold yolk — Korea's tartare, plated with EMP restraint.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Beef fillet, trimmed, very fresh — 320 g
- Nashi pear — 1 (200 g)
- Tamari — 20 ml
- Toasted sesame oil — 15 ml
- Caster sugar — 8 g
- Garlic, grated — 4 g
- Spring onion, minced — 10 g
- Egg yolks — 4
- Pine nuts, toasted and crushed — 15 g
- Black pepper — 1 g
- Flaky salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Freeze the fillet 30 minutes for clean cutting; julienne into 4 mm matchsticks.
2. Julienne pear and hold in iced lemon water.
3. Whisk tamari, sesame oil, sugar, garlic and pepper.
4. Fold beef through the dressing with spring onion just before service.
5. Drain pear and nest on chilled plates; mound beef over.
6. Crown with a yolk, pine nuts and flaky salt.

## Plating & Service
Chilled plates, served immediately; instruct guests to break the yolk and toss at the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cured yolk (45 min in soy) travels better in heat; sirloin works if fillet is unavailable — never pre-mix more than 5 minutes ahead.
