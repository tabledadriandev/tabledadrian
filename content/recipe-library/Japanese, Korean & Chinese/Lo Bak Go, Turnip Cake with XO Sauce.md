# Lo Bak Go, Turnip Cake with XO Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Steamed daikon cake seared to a caramel crust and dressed with house XO — the dim sum sleeper hit, make-ahead friendly.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Daikon radish, grated — 900 g
- Rice flour — 180 g
- Wheat starch — 30 g
- Lap cheong (Chinese sausage), fine dice — 80 g
- Dried shrimp, soaked and chopped — 25 g
- Dried shiitake, soaked and diced — 30 g
- Neutral oil — 40 ml
- White pepper — 1.5 g
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Caster sugar — 8 g
- XO sauce — 60 g
- Spring onion — 15 g

## Method
1. Simmer grated daikon in its own juice 15 minutes until translucent; reserve daikon and 250 ml liquid (top up with water).
2. Fry lap cheong, dried shrimp and shiitake in 20 ml oil 3 minutes.
3. Whisk rice flour, wheat starch, salt, sugar, pepper with the warm daikon liquid to a batter; fold in daikon and the fried mix.
4. Pour into an oiled 20 cm tin; steam at 100°C for 60 minutes until a skewer comes out clean.
5. Cool fully, ideally overnight, then unmould and cut 2 cm slabs.
6. Sear in remaining oil at 210°C, 3 minutes per side, to a deep crust.
7. Top each slab with warm XO and spring onion.

## Plating & Service
Three slabs per plate, XO spooned over the crust at the pass.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: omit sausage/shrimp, double shiitake, dress with chilli crisp instead of XO.
