# Jjimdak, Soy-Braised Chicken with Glass Noodles

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Andong's braised chicken — dark soy broth, vegetables and chewy sweet-potato noodles in one generous pot.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chicken thighs and drumsticks, bone-in — 1.2 kg
- Water — 500 ml
- Soy sauce — 100 ml
- Caster sugar — 35 g
- Rice syrup or honey — 25 g
- Garlic, sliced — 20 g
- Ginger — 10 g
- Dried red chillies — 2
- Carrot, roll-cut — 150 g
- Potato, chunks — 250 g
- Onion, petals — 150 g
- Sweet-potato glass noodles (dangmyeon) — 120 g
- Spring onion — 30 g
- Toasted sesame oil — 10 ml

## Method
1. Blanch chicken pieces 5 minutes; rinse (clearer braise).
2. Simmer chicken with water, soy, sugar, syrup, garlic, ginger and chillies, lidded, 20 minutes.
3. Add potato and carrot; simmer 12 minutes.
4. Soak noodles in warm water 20 minutes meanwhile.
5. Add onion and drained noodles; simmer uncovered 6–8 minutes until noodles are glossy and the sauce has body.
6. Finish with spring onion and sesame oil.

## Plating & Service
One deep, communal dish at the centre of the table; tongs and rice bowls all round.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Make it fiery with 20 g gochugaru at step 2; boneless thigh cuts cooking to 25 minutes total.
