# Mapo Tofu with Aged Doubanjiang & Beef

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Silken tofu suspended in a crimson, numbing sauce — proper má and là, finished with a snow of fresh Sichuan pepper.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Silken-firm tofu, 2 cm cubes — 600 g
- Beef or pork mince — 150 g
- Doubanjiang (aged) — 40 g
- Fermented black beans, rinsed — 10 g
- Gochugaru or Sichuan chilli flakes — 8 g
- Neutral oil — 40 ml
- Garlic, minced — 12 g
- Ginger, minced — 8 g
- Chicken stock — 300 ml
- Soy sauce — 10 ml
- Caster sugar — 4 g
- Potato starch — 10 g + water 30 ml
- Sichuan pepper, toasted and ground — 2 g
- Spring onion greens — 20 g

## Method
1. Steep tofu cubes in just-salted 80°C water — keeps them intact and warm.
2. Fry the mince crisp in oil, 3 minutes; push aside.
3. Fry doubanjiang 90 seconds until the oil stains red; add black beans, chilli flakes, garlic and ginger for 30 seconds.
4. Add stock, soy and sugar; simmer 2 minutes.
5. Drain tofu into the sauce; simmer 3 minutes, folding — never stirring hard.
6. Thicken with starch slurry in two additions to a loose, glossy cloak.
7. Bowl it; finish with ground Sichuan pepper and spring onion.

## Plating & Service
A wide bowl over a warmer, rice served in; the surface should shimmer with red oil.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fully vegan with shiitake mince and mushroom stock — flavour barely changes; dial Sichuan pepper to the table's tolerance.
