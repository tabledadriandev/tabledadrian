# Bossam, Poached Pork Belly with Oyster-Radish Kimchi

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Aromatic poached belly, sliced warm, wrapped in cabbage with a fresh oyster-radish kimchi — Korea's most refined pork dish.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Pork belly, skinless, one piece — 1 kg
- Water — 2 L
- Doenjang — 30 g
- Onion — 1
- Garlic — 20 g
- Ginger — 20 g
- Black peppercorns — 5 g
- Coffee (instant) — 2 g
- Daikon, julienne — 300 g
- Gochugaru — 20 g
- Fish sauce — 15 ml
- Caster sugar — 12 g
- Fresh oysters — 8
- Napa cabbage leaves, blanched — 12
- Ssamjang — 60 g

## Method
1. Simmer belly with water, doenjang, onion, garlic, ginger, peppercorns and coffee at 90°C, lidded, 70 minutes to 78°C core.
2. Rest the pork in its liquor 15 minutes.
3. Toss daikon with gochugaru, fish sauce and sugar; fold in oysters just before service.
4. Blanch cabbage leaves 60 seconds; refresh and drain.
5. Slice pork 8 mm thick while warm.
6. Serve pork, oyster-radish kimchi, cabbage leaves and ssamjang for wrapping.

## Plating & Service
Slices shingled on a warm board, kimchi vivid beside, leaves fanned — assembly happens in guests' hands.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skip oysters for shellfish allergies (the radish kimchi stands alone); pork shoulder poaches leaner in the same bath.
