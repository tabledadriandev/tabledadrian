# Black Sesame Panna Cotta, Miso Caramel & Sesame Brittle

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Grey-black, silken and nutty — an Italian technique carrying Japanese flavours, with a shard of brittle for violence.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Double cream — 400 ml
- Whole milk — 200 ml
- Caster sugar — 70 g
- Black sesame paste (or 80 g seeds, ground to butter) — 70 g
- Gelatine leaves (gold) — 3 (5 g)
- Vanilla — 2 ml
- Fine salt — 1 g
- Caster sugar (brittle) — 100 g
- Mixed sesame seeds — 40 g
- Miso caramel — 90 g

## Method
1. Soak gelatine in iced water 5 minutes.
2. Warm cream, milk, sugar, salt and sesame paste to 80°C, whisking smooth; blend 20 seconds for a perfect grey.
3. Squeeze in gelatine; add vanilla; pass through a sieve.
4. Pour into 6 glasses or moulds; set 4 hours minimum.
5. Brittle: dry caramel to 175°C, stir in seeds, pour thin on a mat, cool and crack.
6. Serve with warm miso caramel and brittle shards.

## Plating & Service
In the glass with caramel poured table-side, brittle standing vertically — monochrome and dramatic.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: coconut cream + oat milk with 2 g agar (boil 90 seconds); white sesame + honey version reads gentler.
