# Scallop Carpaccio, Brown Butter Ponzu & Finger Lime

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Raw Normandy scallops meet nutty warm butter and citrus pop — a Franco-Japanese handshake that defines the Riviera charter style.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Extra-large diver scallops — 8 (320 g)
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Ponzu — 50 ml
- Finger lime pearls — 20 g (or lime segments, chopped)
- Chives, minced — 6 g
- Flaky salt — 2 g
- Espelette pepper — 0.5 g
- Radish, paper-thin — 20 g
- Extra-virgin olive oil — 10 ml

## Method
1. Chill plates and slice each scallop horizontally into 4 coins; arrange in a rosette and refrigerate.
2. Cook butter to 150°C until hazelnut-brown; cool 2 minutes.
3. Whisk warm brown butter into ponzu to a split, glossy dressing.
4. Spoon dressing over the scallops so the residual warmth barely blooms the surface.
5. Finish with finger lime, radish, chives, olive oil, salt and Espelette.

## Plating & Service
Serve on glass plates over crushed ice on deck; must go from fridge to guest inside 3 minutes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Sea bream or thin-sliced raw langoustine take the same dressing; use miso-brown-butter for a richer winter cut.
