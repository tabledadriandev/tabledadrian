# Oi Kimchi, Same-Day Cucumber Kimchi

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Crunchy stuffed cucumber kimchi ready in two hours — the fresh, fizzy banchan you can produce mid-charter without a fermentation fridge.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Small cucumbers — 600 g
- Coarse salt — 25 g
- Gochugaru — 25 g
- Garlic, grated — 12 g
- Ginger, grated — 6 g
- Spring onion — 30 g
- Carrot, fine julienne — 40 g
- Fish-free dashima stock or water — 30 ml
- Caster sugar — 10 g
- Toasted sesame seeds — 5 g

## Method
1. Quarter cucumbers lengthwise, leaving 1 cm attached at the base; salt inside and out and rest 40 minutes.
2. Rinse briefly and pat dry.
3. Mix gochugaru, garlic, ginger, sugar and stock to a loose paste; fold in spring onion and carrot.
4. Stuff the paste between the cucumber quarters with gloved hands.
5. Rest at room temperature 2 hours, then refrigerate.
6. Serve within 3 days, scattered with sesame.

## Plating & Service
Cut into 4 cm lengths and stand upright in a small bowl; serve cold beside anything grilled.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 10 ml fish sauce for a deeper traditional profile; chives replace spring onion in a softer version.
