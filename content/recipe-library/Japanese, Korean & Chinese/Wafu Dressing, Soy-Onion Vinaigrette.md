# Wafu Dressing, Soy-Onion Vinaigrette

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Grated onion melted into soy and rice vinegar — the Japanese steakhouse dressing that flatters leaves, tomatoes and cold fish alike.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Onion, finely grated — 80 g
- Tamari — 60 ml
- Rice vinegar — 60 ml
- Grapeseed oil — 90 ml
- Toasted sesame oil — 15 ml
- Caster sugar — 10 g
- Ginger, grated — 6 g
- Dijon or karashi — 3 g
- Black pepper — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Grate the onion and rest it 10 minutes (mellows the bite).
2. Whisk onion, tamari, vinegar, sugar, ginger and mustard.
3. Stream in both oils, whisking to a loose emulsion.
4. Rest 1 hour before first use; shake before each service.
5. Keeps 10 days chilled — the onion sweetens over time.

## Plating & Service
Dress leaves at the last second; superb over ripe Riviera tomatoes with shiso, or cold poached chicken.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Blend until smooth and creamy for a thicker version; add 15 g grated apple for a yakiniku-style dip.
