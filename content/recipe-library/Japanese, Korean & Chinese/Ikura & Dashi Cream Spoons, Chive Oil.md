# Ikura & Dashi Cream Spoons, Chive Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> One perfect cold spoonful — warm dashi cream under cold salmon roe — to open a tasting menu with a salt-pop crescendo.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Ikura (salmon roe) — 80 g
- Double cream — 100 ml
- Ichiban dashi — 60 ml
- White soy (shiro shoyu) — 8 ml
- Chives — 20 g
- Grapeseed oil — 60 ml
- Yuzu zest — 0.5 g
- Nori, micro-julienne — 1 g

## Method
1. Blanch chives 10 seconds, refresh, dry hard, and blend with oil at 60°C; strain through a coffee filter.
2. Warm cream, dashi and white soy to 70°C; blend 20 seconds to aerate and season.
3. Rinse ikura briefly in cold dashi if brined hard; drain on paper.
4. Pour 18 ml warm cream into each ceramic spoon.
5. Top with 10 g ikura, 3 drops chive oil, yuzu zest and nori.

## Plating & Service
Line spoons on a slate; serve as guests are seated — one motion, one bite.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Trout roe or smoked cod roe emulsion both work; dairy-free with whipped silken tofu warmed in dashi.
