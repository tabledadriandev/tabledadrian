# Yaki Onigiri, Grilled Rice with Miso & Bonito Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Crackly soy-brushed grilled rice triangles, one glazed miso, one bonito-butter — the smell alone sells the next charter.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Cooked short-grain rice, warm — 600 g
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Soy sauce — 30 ml
- Mirin — 15 ml
- Shiro miso — 25 g
- Caster sugar — 5 g
- Unsalted butter, soft — 30 g
- Katsuobushi, crumbled — 4 g
- Toasted sesame seeds — 5 g
- Neutral oil — 15 ml
- Shiso leaves — 4

## Method
1. With wet, salted hands press rice into 8 firm triangles (75 g each) — compact edges, tender centres.
2. Rest 10 minutes to set (or chill up to a day).
3. Mix soy with mirin; separately mix miso with sugar and 10 ml water; beat butter with katsuobushi.
4. Oil a plancha or grill at 220°C; grill triangles 4 minutes per face until a golden crust forms — do not move them early.
5. Brush half with soy glaze, half with miso; grill 60 seconds per side more until caramelised and crackly.
6. Serve the bonito butter melting on top, sesame and shiso over.

## Plating & Service
Stacked on a cedar board, butter still sliding; napkins, fingers, no cutlery.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fill centres with umeboshi or salmon flakes before shaping; vegan version drops butter for sesame oil brushed at the end.
