# Salmon Shioyaki, Daikon Oroshi & Charred Lemon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Salt-grilled salmon done the ryokan way — blistered skin, clean flesh, grated daikon doing the work of a sauce.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Salmon fillets, skin on — 4 × 170 g
- Fine sea salt — 10 g
- Sake — 20 ml
- Daikon — 300 g
- Lemons — 2
- Grapeseed oil — 10 ml
- Shiso leaves — 4
- Flaky salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Brush salmon with sake, then salt evenly on both sides; rest uncovered in the fridge 40 minutes.
2. Pat dry. Grate daikon finely and drain in a sieve 10 minutes without pressing.
3. Halve lemons; char cut-side down in a dry pan until deeply caramelised.
4. Oil the skin only; grill skin-side to the heat at 220°C for 7–8 minutes until the skin crackles.
5. Turn for a final 2 minutes to 50°C core; rest 2 minutes.
6. Serve with a snowball of daikon and the charred lemon.

## Plating & Service
Skin up, always; daikon mounded on a shiso leaf beside, lemon squeezed table-side.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Mackerel and sea bream love the same cure; a drop of tamari on the daikon deepens it (adds soy).
