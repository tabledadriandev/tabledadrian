# Pork & Ginger Xiao Long Bao

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Soup dumplings with a collagen-set jelly that liquefies in the steam — the high-wire act of the dim sum repertoire.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chicken wings & pork skin — 400 g
- Water — 800 ml
- Ginger — 20 g
- Spring onion — 30 g
- Pork shoulder, minced — 250 g
- Soy sauce — 18 ml
- Shaoxing wine — 12 ml
- Caster sugar — 5 g
- White pepper — 1.5 g
- Toasted sesame oil — 8 ml
- Plain flour — 200 g
- Warm water (45°C) — 105 ml
- Black vinegar + ginger julienne, to serve — 60 ml + 15 g

## Method
1. Simmer wings, pork skin, half the ginger and spring onion in water 2.5 hours; strain, reduce to 250 ml, chill until firmly jelled, then dice 3 mm.
2. Beat pork with soy, wine, sugar, pepper, remaining grated ginger and sesame oil until tacky; fold in an equal weight of jelly dice. Keep very cold.
3. Knead flour and warm water 8 minutes to a smooth, supple dough; rest 30 minutes under cloth.
4. Roll a rope, cut 9 g pieces, and roll to 8 cm discs — centre thicker than the rim.
5. Cup 20 g filling; pleat 16–18 folds, twisting closed with a tiny 'nipple'.
6. Steam on parchment at 100°C for 8 minutes exactly.
7. Rest 1 minute before serving — the soup is scalding.

## Plating & Service
Serve in the steamer with vinegar-ginger dip and spoons; coach guests: lift, bite the skirt, drink the soup.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 20 g crab to the filling for luxury; freeze extras raw on trays and steam from frozen +2 minutes.
