# Tteokbokki, Gochujang-Glazed Rice Cakes & Fish Cake

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Seoul street heat in a refined register — chewy rice cylinders in a glossy, layered gochujang sauce that actually reduces.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Cylindrical rice cakes (tteok) — 500 g
- Fish cakes (eomuk), sliced — 150 g
- Anchovy-kombu stock — 500 ml
- Gochujang — 55 g
- Gochugaru — 10 g
- Soy sauce — 15 ml
- Caster sugar — 25 g
- Garlic, minced — 10 g
- Onion, slivers — 80 g
- Eggs, boiled 8 min — 2, halved
- Spring onion, batons — 30 g
- Toasted sesame seeds — 5 g

## Method
1. Soak rice cakes in warm water 15 minutes if not fresh.
2. Whisk gochujang, gochugaru, soy, sugar and garlic into the stock; bring to a simmer.
3. Add rice cakes and onion; simmer 8–10 minutes, stirring against sticking.
4. Add fish cakes; simmer 3 minutes until the sauce reduces to a heavy gloss that coats each cylinder.
5. Rest 2 minutes — the sauce tightens further.
6. Serve with halved eggs, spring onion and sesame.

## Plating & Service
A shallow bowl with the sauce at lava-flow thickness; toothpicks or forks, deck-party style.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan with kombu-shiitake stock and no fish cake (add fried tofu puffs); throw in mozzarella under a salamander for the K-drama version.
