# Agedashi Tofu in Smoked Dashi

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Custard-soft tofu in a shattering potato-starch crust, sitting in hot smoked dashi — humble ingredients, luxury effect.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Silken-firm tofu — 400 g
- Potato starch (katakuriko) — 60 g
- Neutral oil for frying — 700 ml
- Kombu — 8 g
- Smoked bonito flakes or smoked salt + katsuobushi — 12 g
- Water — 500 ml
- Mirin — 30 ml
- Usukuchi soy sauce — 30 ml
- Fresh ginger, grated — 10 g
- Spring onion, sliced — 15 g
- Daikon, grated — 80 g

## Method
1. Press tofu between trays with a light weight for 15 minutes; cut into 8 blocks.
2. Cold-infuse kombu in the water 30 minutes, bring to 60°C and hold 45 minutes; remove kombu, bring to 85°C, add smoked bonito, cut the heat and steep 3 minutes before straining.
3. Season the dashi with mirin and soy; keep at 80°C.
4. Heat oil to 180°C.
5. Dust tofu heavily in potato starch, shaking off excess just before frying.
6. Fry 3–4 minutes until the crust is crisp and pale gold; drain on a rack 1 minute.
7. Pour 100 ml hot broth into each warmed bowl, sit tofu in it crust-up, and top with daikon, ginger and spring onion.

## Plating & Service
Deep bowls, broth only halfway up the tofu so the top stays crisp; serve immediately with spoons.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: replace bonito with 5 g dried shiitake in the dashi. Gluten-free: use tamari.
