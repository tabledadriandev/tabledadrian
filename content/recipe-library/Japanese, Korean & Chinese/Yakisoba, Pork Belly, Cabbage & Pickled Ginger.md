# Yakisoba, Pork Belly, Cabbage & Pickled Ginger

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Festival noodles with a proper homemade sauce — sweet, tangy, savoury — and enough char to taste of the matsuri stall.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Fresh yakisoba or ramen noodles — 600 g
- Pork belly, thin slices — 250 g
- White cabbage, squares — 250 g
- Onion, slivers — 100 g
- Carrot, matchsticks — 80 g
- Neutral oil — 25 ml
- Worcestershire sauce — 45 ml
- Oyster sauce — 25 g
- Ketchup — 20 g
- Soy sauce — 15 ml
- Honey — 12 g
- Beni shoga — 40 g
- Aonori — 2 g
- Katsuobushi — 3 g

## Method
1. Whisk Worcestershire, oyster sauce, ketchup, soy and honey.
2. Loosen noodles in hot water 30 seconds; drain hard.
3. Crisp the pork belly in oil in a wide pan at 230°C, 3 minutes.
4. Add onion and carrot 90 seconds, then cabbage 90 seconds — vegetables must keep bite.
5. Add noodles; press flat and leave 60 seconds to catch and char, then toss.
6. Sauce in; toss 60 seconds until it caramelises on the noodles. Top with beni shoga, aonori and katsuobushi.

## Plating & Service
On oval plates, bonito flakes waving in the heat — serve immediately while they dance.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Prawns or extra cabbage instead of pork; the katsuobushi is optional for vegetarians but the beni shoga is not negotiable.
