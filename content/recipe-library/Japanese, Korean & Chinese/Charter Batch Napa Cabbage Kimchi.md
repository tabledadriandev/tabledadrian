# Charter Batch Napa Cabbage Kimchi

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A one-cabbage master batch, fermented in a lidded tub in the walk-in — the backbone condiment for stews, fried rice and banchan spreads.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Napa cabbage — 1.5 kg (1 head)
- Coarse sea salt — 120 g
- Water — 1.5 L
- Gochugaru — 70 g
- Fish sauce — 40 ml
- Saeujeot (salted shrimp), minced — 20 g
- Garlic — 30 g
- Ginger — 15 g
- Glutinous rice flour — 15 g + water 150 ml
- Caster sugar — 15 g
- Daikon, julienne — 200 g
- Spring onion — 60 g

## Method
1. Quarter the cabbage through the core; dissolve salt in water and brine the quarters 6 hours, turning hourly, until leaves bend without snapping.
2. Rinse three times; drain cut-side down 1 hour.
3. Cook rice flour with 150 ml water to a porridge; cool.
4. Blend porridge, gochugaru, fish sauce, saeujeot, garlic, ginger and sugar; fold in daikon and spring onion.
5. Glove up and paint paste between every leaf.
6. Pack tightly into a tub, press out air, surface-cover with film and lid loosely.
7. Ferment 36–48 hours at 20°C until it fizzes gently, then refrigerate; peak flavour from day 5.

## Plating & Service
Cut across the quarter into 3 cm pieces and re-form the stack on the plate; serve cold.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: swap fish sauce and saeujeot for 30 ml soy sauce + 5 g kombu powder. Use the over-ripe tail end for jjigae.
