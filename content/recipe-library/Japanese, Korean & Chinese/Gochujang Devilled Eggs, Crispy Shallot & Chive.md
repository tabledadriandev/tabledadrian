# Gochujang Devilled Eggs, Crispy Shallot & Chive

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A canapé bridge between Western cocktail hour and the Korean table — silky gochujang yolk cream with fried-shallot crunch.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Eggs — 6
- Gochujang — 20 g
- Kewpie-style mayonnaise — 60 g
- Rice vinegar — 5 ml
- Toasted sesame oil — 5 ml
- Caster sugar — 3 g
- Shallots — 2, in 1.5 mm rings
- Neutral oil — 150 ml
- Chives — 6 g
- Toasted sesame seeds — 3 g

## Method
1. Boil eggs 9 minutes 30 seconds from a rolling boil; ice-shock and peel.
2. Fry shallot rings from cold oil to 150°C until gold; drain and salt.
3. Halve eggs; sieve yolks and beat with mayonnaise, gochujang, vinegar, sesame oil and sugar to a smooth cream.
4. Pipe back into the whites with a star tip.
5. Top with shallots, chives and sesame just before service.

## Plating & Service
On a chilled platter with the pick-up end facing guests; garnish no more than 10 minutes ahead.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fold in 30 g chopped kimchi (well squeezed) for texture; yuzu kosho replaces gochujang for a Japanese cousin.
