# Salt & Pepper Squid, Five-Spice & Charred Lime

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Cantonese crispy squid with a wok-toasted salt-and-five-spice hit — fried hard, seasoned in the air, gone in minutes.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Squid tubes & tentacles, cleaned — 500 g
- Shaoxing wine — 15 ml
- Potato starch — 90 g
- Plain flour — 30 g
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Five-spice — 2 g
- White pepper — 2 g
- Sichuan pepper, ground — 1 g
- Neutral oil — 1.5 L
- Garlic, sliced — 15 g
- Red chilli, rings — 1
- Spring onion — 20 g
- Limes — 2, halved

## Method
1. Score tube interiors in a crosshatch; cut into 3×4 cm pieces. Toss with wine; drain well.
2. Toast salt, five-spice, both peppers in a dry wok 1 minute; reserve.
3. Char lime halves cut-side down in a dry pan until blackened.
4. Dredge squid in starch-flour mix, shaking off excess.
5. Fry at 190°C in 3 waves, 90 seconds each, until pale gold and curled; drain.
6. Flash-fry garlic and chilli 20 seconds in 10 ml oil in the wok; return squid, dust in the spiced salt, toss with spring onion for 20 seconds.

## Plating & Service
Serve immediately in a paper-lined bowl, charred lime for squeezing over.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Rice-flour dredge for GF; the same treatment flatters prawns and oyster mushrooms.
