# Char Siu Pork Neck, Honey-Lacquered

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Proper Cantonese barbecue from a yacht oven — pork neck for marbling, maltose-honey lacquer for the mirror crust.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Pork neck (collar), whole strips — 900 g
- Hoisin — 45 g
- Light soy — 30 ml
- Dark soy — 10 ml
- Oyster sauce — 20 g
- Shaoxing wine — 25 ml
- Caster sugar — 30 g
- Red fermented tofu (optional) — 15 g
- Five-spice — 3 g
- Garlic, grated — 10 g
- Maltose or honey — 60 g
- Hot water — 20 ml

## Method
1. Whisk hoisin, soys, oyster sauce, wine, sugar, fermented tofu, five-spice and garlic; marinate pork 12–24 hours.
2. Rest meat at room temperature 30 minutes; reserve marinade and boil it 2 minutes for basting.
3. Roast on a rack over a water tray at 200°C for 20 minutes.
4. Baste; roast 15 minutes more at 180°C to 68°C core.
5. Loosen maltose with hot water; brush on and blast at 240°C (or grill) 4–5 minutes until lacquered with charred tips.
6. Rest 8 minutes; slice 1 cm thick.

## Plating & Service
Glossy slices over steamed rice or as the centrepiece of a Cantonese spread; spoon resting juices over.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pork belly or boneless chicken thighs work; the burnt edges (char siu bristle) are a chef's snack by right.
