# Fresh Green Yuzu Kosho

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Two ingredients and salt, fermented into Japan's most electric condiment — a rice-grain dose lifts fish, meat and broth.

## Ingredients (serves 20)
- Green chillies (mild-medium, e.g. serrano/jalapeño mix) — 100 g
- Yuzu zest (or 2:1 lime-grapefruit zest) — 40 g
- Yuzu juice — 15 ml
- Fine sea salt — 14 g (10% of solids)

## Method
1. Deseed chillies (gloves) and chop roughly.
2. Zest the citrus, avoiding all pith.
3. Pound chilli, zest and salt in a mortar to a coarse, wet paste — a processor works in pulses.
4. Loosen with the juice.
5. Jar with headspace; leave at cool room temperature 5–7 days, stirring daily, until it smells rounded and fizzy-bright.
6. Refrigerate; matures for 3 months, keeps for 12.

## Plating & Service
Serve in a thimble dish beside grilled fish, robata meats or clear soups — guests need a rice-grain amount, tell them so.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Red version with ripe chillies and the same 10% salt; stir 5 g into mayonnaise or beurre blanc for instant house sauces.
