# Tempura Courgette Flowers, Prawn & Yuzu Mousse

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Riviera market flowers filled with a bouncy prawn mousse, fried in lace batter — the Franco-Japanese signature of the boat.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Courgette flowers — 8
- Raw prawn meat — 250 g
- Egg white — 25 g
- Fine salt — 3 g
- Yuzu juice — 8 ml
- Potato starch — 8 g
- Weak flour — 100 g
- Ice-cold sparkling water — 170 ml
- Neutral oil — 1.5 L
- Ten-tsuyu (dashi 150 ml, soy 30 ml, mirin 30 ml) — 210 ml
- Grated daikon — 60 g

## Method
1. Blitz half the prawns with egg white, salt and starch to a smooth farce; fold in the rest, hand-chopped, plus yuzu juice.
2. Pipe 30 g mousse into each flower through the petal opening; twist petals closed. Chill 15 minutes.
3. Warm ten-tsuyu components together; keep at 60°C.
4. Heat oil to 170°C.
5. Whisk flour into sparkling water with 5 lazy strokes — keep it lumpy.
6. Dip flowers, fry 2 at a time 3 minutes, turning once, until the mousse reaches 68°C.
7. Drain vertically 1 minute; salt the stems only.

## Plating & Service
One flower upright per plate, daikon beside, broth poured at the table so the batter stays crisp.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fill with whipped tofu-shiso farce for vegetarian; rice-flour batter keeps it gluten-free.
