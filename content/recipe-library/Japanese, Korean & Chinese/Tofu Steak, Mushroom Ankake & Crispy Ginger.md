# Tofu Steak, Mushroom Ankake & Crispy Ginger

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A vegan main with real gravity — seared tofu under a glossy mushroom-dashi glaze and a haystack of fried ginger.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Extra-firm tofu — 600 g
- Potato starch — 40 g
- Neutral oil — 60 ml
- Dried shiitake — 15 g
- Kombu — 6 g
- Water — 500 ml
- Fresh shiitake & shimeji — 250 g
- Tamari — 30 ml
- Mirin — 25 ml
- Potato starch (ankake) — 8 g
- Ginger, fine julienne — 30 g
- Spring onion — 15 g

## Method
1. Cold-infuse dried shiitake and kombu in water 4 hours (or 60°C for 1 hour); strain — this is the stock.
2. Press tofu 20 minutes; cut into 4 thick steaks and dust with starch.
3. Fry ginger julienne in 40 ml oil from cold to 160°C until golden; drain.
4. Sear tofu in the ginger oil at 200°C, 3 minutes per side, to a proper crust.
5. Sauté fresh mushrooms hard; add stock, tamari and mirin, simmer 3 minutes, and thicken with slurried starch to a nappe glaze.
6. Sauce over the tofu; crown with crispy ginger and spring onion.

## Plating & Service
One steak per warm plate, ankake flooding around, ginger tall on top for height.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 10 g yuzu kosho to the glaze for heat; the same ankake dresses seared salmon or chicken for the omnivores.
