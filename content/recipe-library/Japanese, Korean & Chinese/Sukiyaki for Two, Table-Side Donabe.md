# Sukiyaki for Two, Table-Side Donabe

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Paper-thin beef cooked at the table in a sweet soy warishita, dipped in raw yolk — pure occasion cooking for a couple's night.

## Ingredients (serves 2)
- Ribeye or sirloin, frozen 1 hour, sliced 2 mm — 350 g
- Beef fat or neutral oil — 15 g
- Soy sauce — 90 ml
- Mirin — 90 ml
- Sake — 45 ml
- Caster sugar — 30 g
- Water — 90 ml
- Firm tofu, grilled, cubed — 150 g
- Shiitake — 6
- Enoki — 100 g
- Chinese cabbage — 200 g
- Shirataki noodles, rinsed — 150 g
- Spring onions, bias-cut — 3
- Egg yolks, freshest — 2

## Method
1. Combine soy, mirin, sake, sugar and water for the warishita.
2. Arrange all raw items on a platter like a painting — this is half the dish.
3. At the table, melt beef fat in a donabe or cast-iron pan on a burner at medium-high.
4. Caramelise a few beef slices 20 seconds a side; splash with warishita and eat first, dipped in yolk.
5. Add vegetables, tofu and shirataki in sections; pour warishita halfway up and simmer 8–10 minutes.
6. Feed in remaining beef slice by slice — never boil it past pink.

## Plating & Service
Cook it yourself at the table on an induction plate; each guest gets a yolk bowl for dipping.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pasteurised yolks for cautious guests; swap beef for thin pork shoulder or pure mushroom-tofu (vegan, kombu warishita).
