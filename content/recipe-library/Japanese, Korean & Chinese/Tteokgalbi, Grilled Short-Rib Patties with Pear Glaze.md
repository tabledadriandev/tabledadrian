# Tteokgalbi, Grilled Short-Rib Patties with Pear Glaze

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Royal-court 'rice-cake ribs' — hand-chopped short rib pressed into patties, grilled under a soy-pear lacquer.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Beef short rib meat, hand-chopped fine — 600 g
- Pork mince — 150 g
- Soy sauce — 35 ml
- Caster sugar — 18 g
- Nashi pear, grated — 80 g
- Garlic, grated — 10 g
- Ginger — 5 g
- Toasted sesame oil — 15 ml
- Spring onion, minced — 25 g
- Panko — 20 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Honey — 20 g
- Neutral oil — 15 ml
- Pine nuts, crushed — 12 g

## Method
1. Beat chopped rib meat and pork with soy, sugar, half the pear, garlic, ginger, sesame oil, spring onion, panko and pepper until tacky.
2. Form 8 thick oval patties around soaked skewer pairs (or free-form); chill 20 minutes.
3. Simmer remaining pear with honey and 10 ml soy to a glaze.
4. Sear patties in oil at 210°C, 3 minutes per side.
5. Glaze and grill 2 minutes more per side to 71°C, lacquering twice.
6. Rest 3 minutes; dust with pine nuts.

## Plating & Service
Two patties per plate over grilled spring onions; glaze brushed to a mirror at the pass.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: swap panko for cooked rice (30 g); a lamb version with 2 g cumin is quietly brilliant.
