# Yu Xiang Aubergine, Fish-Fragrant & Silken

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> No fish, all fragrance — aubergine collapsed to silk in Sichuan's hot-sweet-sour-garlicky master sauce.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Aubergines — 600 g, in finger-length batons
- Fine salt — 8 g
- Neutral oil — 60 ml
- Doubanjiang — 35 g
- Garlic, minced — 18 g
- Ginger, minced — 12 g
- Spring onion, minced — 30 g
- Caster sugar — 22 g
- Black vinegar — 25 ml
- Soy sauce — 18 ml
- Water — 120 ml
- Potato starch — 6 g + water 20 ml
- Dried chillies — 4
- Sichuan pepper oil — 5 ml

## Method
1. Salt aubergine batons 20 minutes; squeeze and dry hard (less oil absorbed, better colour).
2. Shallow-fry in oil at 190°C in two waves, 3 minutes each, until bronzed and creamy; drain.
3. In 15 ml of the oil, fry doubanjiang and dried chillies until red, 60 seconds.
4. Add garlic, ginger and half the spring onion, 30 seconds.
5. Add sugar, vinegar, soy and water; return aubergine and simmer 2 minutes.
6. Thicken with slurry to a mirror glaze; finish with Sichuan pepper oil and spring onion.

## Plating & Service
Glossy tangle in a shallow bowl; jasmine rice non-negotiable.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 100 g pork mince at step 3 for the classic; air-fry the aubergine (200°C, 10 min, oiled) for a lighter build.
