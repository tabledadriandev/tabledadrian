# Haemul Jjim, Steamed Seafood in Chilli-Soy Bean Sprout Tangle

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A dramatic pile of prawns, clams and squid steamed over crunchy sprouts in a clinging ma-la-adjacent Korean chilli sauce.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Prawns, shell on — 8
- Clams, purged — 400 g
- Squid, scored pieces — 300 g
- Mussels — 300 g
- Soybean sprouts — 400 g
- Gochugaru — 30 g
- Gochujang — 20 g
- Soy sauce — 30 ml
- Caster sugar — 15 g
- Garlic, minced — 20 g
- Ginger — 10 g
- Mirin — 30 ml
- Anchovy stock — 150 ml
- Potato starch — 12 g + water 40 ml
- Spring onion & perilla — 40 g
- Toasted sesame seeds — 6 g

## Method
1. Whisk gochugaru, gochujang, soy, sugar, garlic, ginger, mirin and stock.
2. Steam sprouts in a lidded wide pan with 50 ml water, 3 minutes; drain and reserve the pan.
3. Layer clams and mussels in the pan, then squid and prawns; pour the sauce over.
4. Lid on; steam-braise 6–8 minutes until shells open.
5. Return sprouts; thicken with the starch slurry, tossing 1 minute until every strand is coated.
6. Finish with spring onion, perilla and sesame.

## Plating & Service
A towering share-plate at the table centre; supply shell bowls, picks and plenty of napkins.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Lobster halves make it a flagship dish; keep heat civilised by cutting gochugaru to 15 g.
