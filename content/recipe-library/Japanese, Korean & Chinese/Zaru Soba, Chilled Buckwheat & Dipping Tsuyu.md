# Zaru Soba, Chilled Buckwheat & Dipping Tsuyu

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Iced buckwheat noodles with a deep mentsuyu dip — the most elegant possible answer to a 35°C afternoon on deck.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Dried soba (high buckwheat %) — 400 g
- Mirin — 80 ml
- Soy sauce — 80 ml
- Water — 240 ml
- Katsuobushi — 10 g
- Kombu — 5 g
- Spring onion, fine rings — 30 g
- Fresh wasabi — 10 g
- Nori, fine shreds — 3 g
- Toasted sesame seeds — 5 g
- Ice — plenty

## Method
1. Simmer mirin 1 minute; add soy, water and kombu, bring to 85°C, add katsuobushi, cut heat, steep 5 minutes and strain. Chill hard.
2. Boil soba in unsalted water per packet minus 30 seconds, stirring the first minute.
3. Drain, then wash under cold running water, rubbing to remove surface starch — this step defines the dish.
4. Shock in iced water 1 minute; drain thoroughly.
5. Mound on slatted trays or over ice; scatter nori.
6. Serve tsuyu in cups with spring onion, wasabi and sesame for guests to add; dip a third of each mouthful only.

## Plating & Service
Offer the sobayu (cooking water) at the end to whisk into the leftover tsuyu and drink — the traditional close.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> 100% buckwheat soba for GF (boil gently, it is fragile); a splash of yuzu in the tsuyu modernises it.
