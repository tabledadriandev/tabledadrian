# Siu Mai of Pork, Prawn & Shiitake

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Open-topped dumplings with a coarse, juicy bite and a shiitake back-note — dim sum service made galley-friendly.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Pork shoulder, hand-minced coarse — 250 g
- Raw prawns, diced — 120 g
- Dried shiitake, soaked, fine dice — 30 g
- Spring onion — 15 g
- Soy sauce — 15 ml
- Shaoxing wine — 10 ml
- Toasted sesame oil — 8 ml
- Caster sugar — 5 g
- White pepper — 1 g
- Potato starch — 10 g
- Thin wonton wrappers, yellow — 20
- Goji berries or carrot brunoise — 10 g

## Method
1. Beat pork with soy, wine, sugar and pepper 2 minutes until sticky.
2. Fold in prawns, shiitake, spring onion, sesame oil and starch; chill 20 minutes.
3. Form a circle with thumb and forefinger; press a wrapper in and pack with 22 g filling.
4. Squeeze the waist to a corseted cylinder, flatten the base and smooth the exposed top.
5. Dot each with a goji berry.
6. Steam at 100°C for 8–9 minutes to a core of 74°C.

## Plating & Service
Serve in the steamer with black vinegar and chilli oil; brush tops with the steaming juices for shine.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> All-prawn version for no-pork guests; wrap in blanched cabbage for gluten-free.
