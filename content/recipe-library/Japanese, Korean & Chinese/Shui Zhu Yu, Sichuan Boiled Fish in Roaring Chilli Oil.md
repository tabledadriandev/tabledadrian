# Shui Zhu Yu, Sichuan Boiled Fish in Roaring Chilli Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Velveted white fish poached in ma-la broth over crisp sprouts, finished with a pour of smoking chilli oil at the table.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Firm white fish fillets (bass, cod), 5 mm slices — 600 g
- Egg white — 25 g
- Potato starch — 15 g
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Shaoxing wine — 15 ml
- Soybean sprouts — 250 g
- Celery, batons — 100 g
- Doubanjiang — 45 g
- Neutral oil — 100 ml
- Garlic — 20 g
- Ginger — 15 g
- Chicken stock — 800 ml
- Dried chillies — 15
- Sichuan peppercorns — 5 g
- Spring onion & coriander — 30 g

## Method
1. Massage fish slices with salt, wine, egg white and starch; rest 15 minutes (velveting).
2. Blanch sprouts and celery 60 seconds; bed them in a deep serving bowl.
3. Fry doubanjiang, half the garlic and ginger in 30 ml oil until red; add stock and simmer 5 minutes; season.
4. Poach fish slices in the broth at 90°C for 90 seconds — no boiling.
5. Pour fish and broth over the vegetables; top with remaining raw garlic, dried chillies and Sichuan pepper.
6. Heat 70 ml oil to 200°C and pour over the aromatics table-side — it must roar.
7. Finish with spring onion and coriander.

## Plating & Service
The pour is the theatre: do it in front of guests, then serve with rice and tongs for the fish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Thin-sliced chicken breast velveted the same way works; halve the chillies and it remains deeply flavourful for gentler tables.
