# Dubu Kimchi, Seared Tofu with Kimchi-Pork Stir-Fry

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Warm tofu slabs against hot, funky stir-fried kimchi and pork belly — the definitive anju plate for a night anchorage.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Firm tofu — 400 g
- Aged kimchi, chopped — 250 g
- Pork belly, thin-sliced — 150 g
- Neutral oil — 20 ml
- Gochujang — 15 g
- Caster sugar — 8 g
- Kimchi brine — 30 ml
- Toasted sesame oil — 8 ml
- Spring onion — 20 g
- Toasted sesame seeds — 5 g

## Method
1. Cut tofu into 1.5 cm slabs; steam 5 minutes at 100°C to warm and firm.
2. Render pork belly in oil at 200°C for 3 minutes until edges crisp.
3. Add kimchi; stir-fry 4 minutes until it smells sweetly caramelised.
4. Add gochujang, sugar and brine; cook 2 minutes to a glossy tangle.
5. Finish with sesame oil and spring onion.
6. Blot tofu dry and sear one side in the same pan 90 seconds for colour.

## Plating & Service
Arc the tofu slabs around a hot mound of kimchi-pork; scatter sesame; guests build tofu-plus-kimchi bites.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan without pork (add 80 g shiitake, seared hard); serve tofu unseared and chilled in summer.
