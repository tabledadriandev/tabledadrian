# Scallop & Chive Dumplings, Chilli Crisp Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Pan-steamed scallop potstickers napped in a spoon of chilli crisp mounted with butter — dim sum with a French finish.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Scallops, diced — 250 g
- Raw prawn paste — 80 g
- Chinese chives, minced — 60 g
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Caster sugar — 4 g
- White pepper — 1 g
- Toasted sesame oil — 6 ml
- Round dumpling wrappers — 20
- Neutral oil — 20 ml
- Water — 80 ml
- Chilli crisp — 30 g
- Unsalted butter — 30 g
- Rice vinegar — 10 ml

## Method
1. Fold scallops, prawn paste, chives and seasonings together; chill 20 minutes.
2. Fill wrappers with 18 g each; pleat into crescents.
3. Heat oil in a lidded pan at 200°C; set dumplings flat-side down and fry 2 minutes to a gold base.
4. Add water, lid on, and steam 5 minutes until wrappers turn translucent.
5. Lid off; crisp the bases 1 minute more.
6. Warm chilli crisp with butter and vinegar to a loose, spoonable emulsion; nap over the dumplings.

## Plating & Service
Serve base-up to show the crust, sauce spooned across at the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap scallop for minced chicken thigh for a leaner run; brown the butter first for extra depth.
