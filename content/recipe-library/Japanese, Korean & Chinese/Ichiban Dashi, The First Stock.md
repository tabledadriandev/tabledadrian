# Ichiban Dashi, The First Stock

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The two-ingredient foundation of the Japanese repertoire — temperature discipline in, crystal umami out.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Soft or filtered water — 1 L
- Kombu (hidaka or rausu) — 20 g
- Katsuobushi — 30 g

## Method
1. Wipe kombu with a dry cloth — never wash; the white bloom is flavour.
2. Cold-soak kombu in the water 30 minutes (or overnight chilled).
3. Bring slowly to 60°C and hold 45 minutes (or gently to 80°C over 10 minutes in a hurry); remove the kombu before any boil.
4. Bring to 85°C; add katsuobushi all at once and cut the heat.
5. Steep exactly 3 minutes; strain through muslin without pressing.
6. Use within 24 hours; it does not freeze with dignity.

## Plating & Service
This is the invisible ingredient in chawanmushi, soups, ankake and tamagoyaki — make it daily like coffee.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Niban (second) dashi: re-simmer the spent kombu and flakes 10 minutes for braising stock; vegan dashi = kombu + 10 g dried shiitake, cold, overnight.
