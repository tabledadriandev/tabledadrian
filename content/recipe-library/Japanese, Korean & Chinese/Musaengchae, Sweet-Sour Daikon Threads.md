# Musaengchae, Sweet-Sour Daikon Threads

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Crunchy julienned radish with gentle chilli warmth — the acid-bright thread that ties a banchan spread together.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Daikon radish — 500 g
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Caster sugar — 20 g
- Rice vinegar — 35 ml
- Gochugaru — 6 g
- Garlic, grated — 3 g
- Spring onion, fine — 10 g
- Toasted sesame seeds — 4 g

## Method
1. Julienne daikon into 2 mm threads (mandoline).
2. Salt and rest 15 minutes; squeeze firmly in a cloth.
3. Toss with sugar, vinegar, gochugaru and garlic.
4. Rest 10 minutes for the colour to bloom.
5. Finish with spring onion and sesame.

## Plating & Service
Serve cold in small nests; keeps 4 days and gets better.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Carrot or kohlrabi mixes in for colour; drop garlic for low-FODMAP service.
