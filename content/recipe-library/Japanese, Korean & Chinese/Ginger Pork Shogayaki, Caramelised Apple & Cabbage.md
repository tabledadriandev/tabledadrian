# Ginger Pork Shogayaki, Caramelised Apple & Cabbage

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Weeknight Japan elevated — thin pork in a gingery soy glaze sweetened with sautéed apple instead of sugar.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Pork loin or shoulder, sliced 4 mm — 600 g
- Potato starch — 15 g
- Neutral oil — 25 ml
- Ginger, grated — 30 g
- Garlic, grated — 5 g
- Soy sauce — 50 ml
- Sake — 40 ml
- Mirin — 40 ml
- Apple, grated — 100 g
- Apple, 1 cm dice — 100 g
- White cabbage, shaved — 250 g
- Spring onion — 15 g

## Method
1. Whisk soy, sake, mirin, grated apple, ginger and garlic for the sauce.
2. Dust pork slices lightly with starch (keeps them tender and thickens the glaze).
3. Caramelise the diced apple in 10 ml oil until bronzed; reserve.
4. Sear pork in batches at 220°C, 60 seconds per side, without crowding.
5. Return all pork; pour in the sauce and reduce 90 seconds to a clinging glaze.
6. Fold in apple dice; serve immediately over shaved cabbage.

## Plating & Service
A loose tangle of glazed pork over cold crisp cabbage — the temperature contrast is the point.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF with tamari; chicken thigh or pressed tofu slices behave the same.
