# Gamja Jorim, Soy-Butter Glazed Baby Potatoes

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sticky, lacquered baby potatoes with a butter-mounted soy glaze — banchan that behaves like a French side dish.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Baby potatoes, halved — 500 g
- Neutral oil — 20 ml
- Soy sauce — 45 ml
- Water — 150 ml
- Honey — 30 g
- Caster sugar — 10 g
- Garlic, sliced — 10 g
- Unsalted butter — 20 g
- Toasted sesame seeds — 5 g
- Spring onion, rings — 10 g

## Method
1. Steam potatoes 10 minutes until just tender at the edge but firm-centred.
2. Brown them in oil in a wide pan at 190°C, cut-side down, 4 minutes.
3. Add garlic 30 seconds, then soy, water, honey and sugar.
4. Simmer uncovered 10–12 minutes, rolling the pan, until the liquid reduces to a clinging glaze.
5. Mount in the butter off the heat.
6. Finish with sesame and spring onion.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm or room temperature in a shallow bowl, glaze pooled beneath.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan with olive oil instead of butter; add 3 g gochugaru with the garlic for a spicy version.
