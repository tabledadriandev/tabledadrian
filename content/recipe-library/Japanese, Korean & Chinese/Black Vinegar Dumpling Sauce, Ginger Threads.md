# Black Vinegar Dumpling Sauce, Ginger Threads

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The dim sum table's quiet essential — aged-vinegar depth, soy salinity, a thatch of young ginger.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Chinkiang black vinegar — 90 ml
- Light soy sauce — 45 ml
- Caster sugar — 8 g
- Toasted sesame oil — 8 ml
- Young ginger, hair-fine julienne — 25 g
- Chilli oil — 10 ml (optional)
- Water — 20 ml

## Method
1. Dissolve sugar in vinegar, soy and water.
2. Add sesame oil; do not emulsify — the beads belong.
3. Soak ginger julienne in iced water 5 minutes; drain (crisper, milder threads).
4. Divide sauce between dishes; float ginger on top.
5. Offer chilli oil separately.

## Plating & Service
One saucer per two guests, refreshed between courses; ginger threads renewed each time.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> For xiao long bao, go vinegar-heavy: 3:1 vinegar to soy; a drop of orange juice softens it for delicate palates.
