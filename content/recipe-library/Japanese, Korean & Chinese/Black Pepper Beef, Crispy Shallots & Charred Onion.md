# Black Pepper Beef, Crispy Shallots & Charred Onion

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Cantonese steakhouse energy — cubes of seared fillet rolled in a coarse black-pepper glaze with sweet charred onion.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Beef fillet or sirloin, 3 cm cubes — 600 g
- Soy sauce — 15 ml
- Potato starch — 8 g
- Neutral oil — 35 ml
- Black peppercorns, coarse-crushed — 8 g
- Butter-free glaze: oyster sauce — 25 g
- Dark soy — 8 ml
- Caster sugar — 10 g
- Shaoxing wine — 20 ml
- Water — 40 ml
- Onion, thick petals — 150 g
- Garlic, sliced — 10 g
- Crispy shallots — 25 g
- Spring onion — 20 g

## Method
1. Marinate beef in soy and starch 15 minutes; bring to room temperature.
2. Whisk oyster sauce, dark soy, sugar, wine and water.
3. Char onion petals in a dry smoking wok 2 minutes; reserve.
4. Sear beef in oil in a single layer, undisturbed 90 seconds, then toss 60 seconds — medium-rare.
5. Add garlic and crushed pepper 20 seconds; pour in the sauce and reduce 45 seconds to a cling.
6. Return onions; toss, top with crispy shallots and spring onion.

## Plating & Service
Serve on a hot cast-iron platter so it sizzles at the table — steak-frites drama, Cantonese grammar.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Venison or portobello quarters swap in; halve the pepper for delicate palates, never the char.
