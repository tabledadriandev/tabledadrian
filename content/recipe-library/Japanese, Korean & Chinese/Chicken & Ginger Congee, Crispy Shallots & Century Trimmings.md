# Chicken & Ginger Congee, Crispy Shallots & Century Trimmings

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rice cooked past surrender into silk — the great restorative breakfast, dressed with texture and heat at the table.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Jasmine rice — 150 g
- Chicken legs, bone-in — 500 g
- Water or light chicken stock — 1.8 L
- Ginger, julienne — 30 g
- Fine salt — 8 g
- Neutral oil — 10 ml
- Crispy shallots — 30 g
- Spring onion — 25 g
- Toasted sesame oil — 10 ml
- Tamari — 15 ml
- White pepper — 1.5 g
- Coriander — 10 g

## Method
1. Freeze the washed rice 2 hours or overnight — fractured grains break down twice as fast.
2. Bring stock to a boil with chicken legs and half the ginger; skim.
3. Add rice and oil; simmer at a lazy bubble, stirring every 10 minutes, 75–90 minutes until the grains have burst to velvet.
4. Lift the chicken at 30 minutes (once at 80°C at the bone); shred and reserve, returning bones to the pot until the end.
5. Season with salt; the congee should pour like double cream — thin with hot water as needed.
6. Serve topped with chicken, raw ginger, shallots, spring onion, sesame oil, tamari and white pepper.

## Plating & Service
Deep bowls, toppings in a line so guests calibrate their own; coriander for those who want it.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Plain congee with just ginger for delicate mornings; fish slices poached in the bowl (2 minutes) make a dinner version.
