# Sichuan Wontons in Chilli Oil, Hong You Chao Shou

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Slippery pork wontons in a sweet-fiery chilli oil bath — Chengdu street food scaled to a white-tablecloth bowl.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Pork shoulder, minced — 300 g
- Egg white — 20 g
- Ginger, grated — 8 g
- Shaoxing wine — 10 ml
- Fine salt — 4 g
- White pepper — 1 g
- Chicken stock, cold — 50 ml
- Thin square wonton wrappers — 24
- Sichuan chilli oil with sediment — 60 ml
- Soy sauce — 25 ml
- Black vinegar — 15 ml
- Caster sugar — 8 g
- Garlic, grated — 5 g
- Spring onion + toasted sesame — 15 g + 4 g

## Method
1. Beat pork with salt, egg white, ginger, wine and pepper; work in cold stock in 3 additions until glossy and emulsified.
2. Fill wrappers with 14 g; fold into triangles, then pinch the two corners together (ingot fold).
3. Whisk chilli oil, soy, vinegar, sugar and garlic; divide between warm bowls.
4. Poach wontons in barely-simmering water (95°C) 5–6 minutes until they float and turn translucent.
5. Lift straight into the sauce with 20 ml poaching water per bowl.
6. Finish with spring onion and sesame.

## Plating & Service
Six per bowl, sauce visible at the base; toss table-side for aroma.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Prawn-pork mix for sweetness; serve in clear chicken broth instead of chilli oil for spice-averse guests.
