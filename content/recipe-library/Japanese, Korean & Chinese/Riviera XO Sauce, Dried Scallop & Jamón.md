# Riviera XO Sauce, Dried Scallop & Jamón

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The umami bomb of the Cantonese pantry, rebuilt with Spanish ham — spoon it on eggs, rice, noodles, steamed fish, everything.

## Ingredients (serves 16)
- Dried scallops (conpoy) — 80 g
- Dried shrimp — 50 g
- Jamón or cured ham, fine dice — 80 g
- Shallots — 150 g
- Garlic — 60 g
- Red chillies, fresh — 40 g
- Dried chilli flakes — 15 g
- Neutral oil — 400 ml
- Caster sugar — 15 g
- Light soy — 15 ml
- Shaoxing wine — 30 ml

## Method
1. Steam soaked (overnight) scallops and shrimp 30 minutes; shred the scallops to threads, chop the shrimp.
2. Blitz shallots, garlic and fresh chilli to a coarse rubble — separately, and not to paste.
3. Fry shallots in the oil at 140°C for 10 minutes to pale gold; add garlic 5 minutes; lift both out.
4. Fry scallop threads and shrimp in the same oil 12–15 minutes at 130°C until they crisp to a golden 'floss'.
5. Return everything with ham, chilli flakes, sugar, soy and wine; cook 10 minutes at a lazy sizzle.
6. Cool in the oil; jar, oil covering the solids. Fridge, 1 month+.

## Plating & Service
Serve at room temperature in a small jar with a spoon — it outranks caviar on fried rice.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No pork: double the ham out for extra conpoy; heat level scales with the dried chilli only, so season boldly there.
