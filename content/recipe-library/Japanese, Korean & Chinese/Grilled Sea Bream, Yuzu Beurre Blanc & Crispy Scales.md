# Grilled Sea Bream, Yuzu Beurre Blanc & Crispy Scales

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Where the boat's two languages meet — dorade grilled à la japonaise, sauced with a yuzu-sharpened beurre blanc.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Sea bream fillets, skin on, scales reserved — 4 × 160 g
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Grapeseed oil — 15 ml
- Shallots, minced — 40 g
- White wine — 100 ml
- Rice vinegar — 30 ml
- Yuzu juice — 20 ml
- Cold unsalted butter, cubed — 150 g
- Neutral oil for scales — 300 ml
- Baby pak choi — 4
- Yuzu zest — 1 g
- Chives — 5 g

## Method
1. Rinse and dry the scales; fry at 190°C for 60–90 seconds until they puff and crisp. Salt and reserve.
2. Reduce shallots, wine and vinegar to 40 ml.
3. Salt fillets 15 minutes ahead; dry hard.
4. Grill skin-side down at 220°C for 6 minutes, pressing flat the first 30 seconds; kiss the flesh side 30 seconds — 50°C core.
5. Off the boil, whisk cold butter into the reduction cube by cube; finish with yuzu juice (do not exceed 60°C or it splits).
6. Steam pak choi 2 minutes; plate fish over, sauce around, scales on the skin, zest and chives last.

## Plating & Service
Shallow warm bowls, sauce poured around never over the skin, the fried scales adding a second texture of the same fish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Turbot or John Dory substitute cleanly; lime + a drop of tamari mimics yuzu in a pinch.
