# Seafood Tempura Course, Ten-Tsuyu & Momiji Oroshi

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A composed tempura service — prawns, scallop, and vegetables fried in waves so every piece hits the table at its peak.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Large prawns — 8
- Scallops, halved — 4
- Baby courgette, batons — 120 g
- Sweet potato, 5 mm coins — 120 g
- Shiso leaves — 4
- Weak flour — 160 g
- Potato starch — 30 g
- Egg yolk — 1
- Ice-cold sparkling water — 260 ml
- Neutral oil — 2 L
- Dashi — 200 ml
- Soy sauce — 40 ml
- Mirin — 40 ml
- Daikon, grated — 100 g
- Dried chilli, ground — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Warm dashi, soy and mirin for ten-tsuyu; hold at 60°C.
2. Squeeze grated daikon lightly and blend with chilli for momiji oroshi.
3. Straighten prawns with belly cuts; dry all items thoroughly.
4. Heat oil to 170°C for vegetables; whisk a loose, lumpy batter with yolk and sparkling water at the last second.
5. Fry sweet potato 3 minutes, courgette 2 minutes; hold on a rack in a 90°C oven, door cracked.
6. Raise oil to 180°C; fry scallops 90 seconds, prawns 2 minutes, shiso (batter one side) 20 seconds.
7. Serve immediately in frying order.

## Plating & Service
Angled on folded paper in a lacquer tray, sauce and oroshi in separate bowls; fry in front of guests if the galley allows.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Rice-flour batter for GF; a vegetable-only run (maitake, lotus root, aubergine) makes an elegant vegan course with kombu-only tsuyu.
