# Grilled Swordfish, Shiso Salsa Verde & Sudachi

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Meaty swordfish off the plancha with an all-shiso salsa — clean, green, and built for lunch on the sun deck.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Swordfish steaks — 4 × 170 g
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Grapeseed oil — 15 ml
- Shiso leaves — 20
- Flat-leaf parsley — 20 g
- Capers, rinsed — 12 g
- Shallot, minced — 20 g
- Rice vinegar — 12 ml
- Extra-virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- Dijon mustard — 4 g
- Sudachi or lime — 2
- Radish, shaved — 30 g

## Method
1. Hand-chop shiso, parsley and capers; stir with shallot, vinegar, mustard and olive oil. Rest 15 minutes.
2. Salt swordfish 15 minutes ahead; dry and oil.
3. Sear on a 240°C plancha: 2.5 minutes, quarter-turn for cross-hatch, 1 minute more.
4. Flip for 90 seconds only — pull at 52°C; it carries to a just-set, juicy centre.
5. Rest 2 minutes; spoon salsa over the warm fish.
6. Finish with shaved radish and sudachi halves.

## Plating & Service
One steak, salsa dragged across, citrus on the side — minimal, confident plating.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Tuna or chicken paillard love this salsa; basil substitutes when shiso runs out, at 60% quantity.
