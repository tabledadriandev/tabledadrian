# Prawn & Spring Onion Pajeon, Cho-Ganjang

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A lacy, crisp-edged seafood pancake cut into batons for elegant sharing — the rainy-day Korean classic, tightened for service.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Spring onions — 150 g
- Raw prawns, chopped — 150 g
- Plain flour — 100 g
- Potato starch — 40 g
- Ice-cold water — 180 ml
- Egg — 1
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Neutral oil — 60 ml
- Soy sauce — 30 ml
- Rice vinegar — 15 ml
- Caster sugar — 4 g
- Gochugaru — 1 g
- Toasted sesame seeds — 3 g

## Method
1. Whisk soy, vinegar, sugar, gochugaru and sesame for the cho-ganjang dip.
2. Whisk flour, starch, salt and ice water to a thin batter; keep cold.
3. Halve spring onions lengthwise and cut to pan width.
4. Heat 30 ml oil in a 24 cm pan at 200°C; lay onions in a raft, pour over half the batter, and stud with prawns.
5. Fry 4 minutes until the base is deep gold; drizzle beaten egg over, flip, and fry 3 minutes more, pressing flat.
6. Add remaining oil at the rim for the final minute to crisp. Repeat for a second pancake.
7. Rest 1 minute on a rack, then cut into batons.

## Plating & Service
Serve on a board, dip central; the edge pieces go first — cut generously.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Squid or oysters swap for prawns; use a GF flour blend + starch for coeliac guests.
