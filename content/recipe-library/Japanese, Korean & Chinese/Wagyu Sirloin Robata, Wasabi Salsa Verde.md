# Wagyu Sirloin Robata, Wasabi Salsa Verde

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rare-grilled wagyu against a bright wasabi-herb salsa — richness and cut in the same mouthful, built for warm evenings.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Wagyu sirloin (BMS 6+) — 600 g
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 40 g
- Chives — 15 g
- Capers, rinsed — 15 g
- Anchovy fillets — 2
- Fresh wasabi, grated — 10 g
- Rice vinegar — 10 ml
- Extra-virgin olive oil — 80 ml
- Radishes — 6
- Flaky salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Hand-chop parsley, chives, capers and anchovy; fold in vinegar and olive oil, then wasabi last. Do not blend — keep it coarse.
2. Temper the beef 30 minutes; salt just before grilling.
3. Grill over the hottest coals or a 250°C plancha: 90 seconds per side for a 3 cm steak, rendering the fat edge 60 seconds.
4. Rest 5 minutes on a warm rack.
5. Slice 8 mm against the grain; season the cut faces with flaky salt.
6. Serve with salsa verde and shaved radish.

## Plating & Service
Slices in a single row on a warm stone, salsa in a line alongside — sauce under, never over, wagyu.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Anchovy out + 5 ml tamari in for pescatarian-free tables; the salsa also flatters grilled tuna belly.
