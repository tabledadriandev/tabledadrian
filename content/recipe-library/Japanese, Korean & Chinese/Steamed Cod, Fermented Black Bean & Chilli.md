# Steamed Cod, Fermented Black Bean & Chilli

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Thick cod loins under a pungent douchi-chilli dressing, steamed to a tremble — ten minutes, restaurant depth.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Cod loins — 4 × 160 g
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Shaoxing wine — 15 ml
- Fermented black beans (douchi), rinsed and chopped — 20 g
- Garlic, minced — 10 g
- Ginger, minced — 10 g
- Red chilli, minced — 8 g
- Caster sugar — 5 g
- Tamari — 15 ml
- Neutral oil — 35 ml
- Spring onion, julienne — 30 g
- Coriander — 8 g

## Method
1. Season cod with salt and wine; rest 10 minutes.
2. Fry black beans, garlic, ginger and chilli in 15 ml oil at 160°C for 90 seconds; add sugar and tamari off the heat.
3. Spread the paste over each loin on a steaming plate.
4. Steam at 100°C for 7–8 minutes until the flakes just separate (48–50°C core).
5. Top with spring onion; sizzle over the remaining 20 ml oil at 190°C.
6. Finish with coriander and the plate juices spooned back over.

## Plating & Service
Serve on the steaming plate or slide onto warm plates with jasmine rice; juices are half the dish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Halibut, turbot or firm tofu slabs (vegan) steam identically — adjust to thickness, 1 minute per extra cm.
