# Ginger & Spring Onion Lobster, Cantonese Wok Toss

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The banquet showstopper — lobster flash-fried in shell, glossed in ginger-scallion sauce that demands finger bowls.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Live lobsters — 2 × 700 g
- Potato starch — 40 g
- Neutral oil — 700 ml
- Ginger, thick julienne — 50 g
- Spring onions, 5 cm batons — 80 g
- Garlic, sliced — 15 g
- Shaoxing wine — 30 ml
- Light soy — 25 ml
- Oyster sauce — 15 g
- Caster sugar — 6 g
- Chicken stock — 120 ml
- Potato starch (sauce) — 5 g + water 20 ml
- White pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Dispatch lobsters swiftly (knife through the head); separate tails, claws and knuckles; crack claws; cut tails through the shell into medallions.
2. Dust the cut faces in starch.
3. Flash-fry lobster pieces in 700 ml oil at 190°C for 90 seconds until the shells blaze red; drain all but 20 ml oil.
4. Fry ginger 60 seconds until fragrant and frizzled; add garlic and half the spring onion.
5. Return lobster; splash wine down the wok wall, then soy, oyster sauce, sugar and stock.
6. Lid on 3 minutes; thicken with slurry, toss with remaining spring onion and white pepper.

## Plating & Service
Rebuilt on the platter head-to-tail, sauce lacquering every shell; crackers, picks and hot towels for the guests.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Identical treatment for langoustines (60 seconds fry) or crab; a knob of butter at the end is heresy that works.
