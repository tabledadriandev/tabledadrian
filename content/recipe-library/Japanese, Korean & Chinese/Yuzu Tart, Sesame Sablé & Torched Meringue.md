# Yuzu Tart, Sesame Sablé & Torched Meringue

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Lemon tart's sharper, more perfumed cousin on a black-sesame sablé — the pâtisserie course of the East Asian menu.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Plain flour — 200 g
- Icing sugar — 70 g
- Cold butter — 120 g
- Black sesame seeds, ground — 25 g
- Egg yolk — 1
- Yuzu juice — 120 ml
- Lemon juice — 30 ml
- Eggs — 3 + 2 yolks
- Caster sugar — 150 g
- Butter (curd) — 150 g
- Egg whites — 90 g
- Caster sugar (meringue) — 160 g
- Yuzu zest — 2 g

## Method
1. Sablé: rub flour, icing sugar, ground sesame and butter to sand; bind with yolk. Rest 1 hour, roll 3 mm, line a 22 cm ring, freeze 20 minutes.
2. Blind-bake at 160°C: 20 minutes weighted, 8 unweighted, to an even biscuit brown.
3. Curd: whisk juices, eggs, yolks and sugar over a bain-marie to 82°C, thick and glossy.
4. Off heat, cool to 60°C; blend in butter cube by cube.
5. Pour into the shell; bake 8 minutes at 130°C until just set with a central shiver. Chill 2 hours.
6. Swiss meringue: whites and sugar to 55°C, whip to stiff peaks; pipe and torch to bronze.
7. Finish with yuzu zest.

## Plating & Service
Clean hot-knife wedges, meringue peaks scorched; serve cool with nothing else on the plate.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Straight lime-lemon works when yuzu is scarce (add zest of 1 mandarin); skip meringue and dust matcha for a starker finish.
