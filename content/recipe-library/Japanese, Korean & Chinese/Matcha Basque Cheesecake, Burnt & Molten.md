# Matcha Basque Cheesecake, Burnt & Molten

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> San Sebastián technique, Uji soul — a scorched crust over a barely-set matcha heart, no base, no fuss.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Cream cheese, room temperature — 600 g
- Caster sugar — 180 g
- Eggs — 4
- Double cream — 280 ml
- Ceremonial-adjacent matcha — 18 g
- Cornflour — 15 g
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Vanilla — 3 ml

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 230°C fan; line a 20 cm springform with two rough crossed sheets of parchment, creases and all.
2. Beat cream cheese and sugar smooth — no air, just smooth.
3. Add eggs one at a time on low speed.
4. Sift matcha and cornflour together; slake with 80 ml of the cream to a paste, then blend in with remaining cream, salt and vanilla.
5. Pass the batter through a sieve; pour into the tin.
6. Bake 28–33 minutes: mahogany top, dramatic wobble at the centre (core about 65°C).
7. Cool fully in the tin; serve at cool room temperature, never fridge-cold.

## Plating & Service
Torn parchment on, rustic wedges cut with a hot knife; a pour of miso caramel or plain cream beside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Hojicha powder (20 g) for a toastier version; bake 5 minutes longer for clean slices at a buffet.
