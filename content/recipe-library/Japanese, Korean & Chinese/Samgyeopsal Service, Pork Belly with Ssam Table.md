# Samgyeopsal Service, Pork Belly with Ssam Table

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Thick-cut belly grilled crisp at the table with a full ssam spread — the most social ten minutes in Korean food.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Pork belly, skinless, 1 cm slabs — 800 g
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Butterhead & perilla leaves — 24
- Ssamjang — 100 g
- Garlic, raw slices — 20 g
- Green chillies — 2
- Spring onion, fine julienne — 60 g
- Rice vinegar — 10 ml
- Caster sugar — 4 g
- Toasted sesame oil — 15 ml
- Flaky salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Dry the belly slabs thoroughly; season with salt and pepper.
2. Dress the spring onion julienne with vinegar, sugar and a pinch of gochugaru (pa-muchim).
3. Mix sesame oil with flaky salt as a dip.
4. Grill belly on a 230°C plancha 4–5 minutes per side until deeply bronzed and crisp-edged.
5. Snip into bite-size pieces with kitchen scissors, table-side.
6. Serve with leaves, ssamjang, raw garlic, chilli and the two dips; demonstrate the one-bite wrap.

## Plating & Service
Everything in small bowls around a central grill or hot plate; the chef cuts, guests build.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap in pork jowl or thick halloumi slabs; lettuce-only wraps keep it strictly low-carb.
