# Robata Duck Hearts, Sansho Honey Glaze

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Skewered duck hearts, blushing inside with a numbing-sweet lacquer — the izakaya deep cut that makes a menu feel fearless.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Duck hearts, cleaned — 400 g
- Tamari — 30 ml
- Sake — 20 ml
- Honey — 25 g
- Ground sansho pepper — 2 g
- Grated garlic — 5 g
- Grapeseed oil — 10 ml
- Fine salt — 3 g
- Spring onion, 3 cm batons — 4
- Lemon — 1/2

## Method
1. Halve hearts, trim vessels, and marinate 10 minutes in tamari, sake and garlic.
2. Reduce the drained marinade with honey to a syrup; add sansho off the heat.
3. Thread hearts and spring onion batons alternately on skewers; oil and salt lightly.
4. Grill over the hottest zone (250°C) 90 seconds per side.
5. Brush with glaze and flash 30 seconds more per side — centres should stay rosé (about 58°C).
6. Rest 2 minutes; brush once more.

## Plating & Service
Serve on a hot stone or cast-iron plate, lemon alongside; two skewers per guest.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken hearts or firm shiitake work on the same glaze; add 1 g chilli flakes for heat-lovers.
