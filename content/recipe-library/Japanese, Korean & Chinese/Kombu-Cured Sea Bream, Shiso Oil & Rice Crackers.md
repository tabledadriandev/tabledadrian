# Kombu-Cured Sea Bream, Shiso Oil & Rice Crackers

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Kobujime curing tightens the bream and loads it with glutamate — 3 hours of patience for a slice that tastes twice as much of the sea.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Sea bream fillets, skinned — 320 g
- Kombu sheets — 4 large (30 g)
- Sake — 30 ml
- Fine salt — 3 g
- Shiso leaves — 12
- Grapeseed oil — 100 ml
- Cooked sushi rice — 80 g
- Neutral oil for frying — 400 ml
- Ponzu — 40 ml
- Pickled ginger, julienne — 20 g

## Method
1. Spread cooked rice thinly on baking paper and dry at 90°C for 2 hours; break into shards and fry at 200°C for 20 seconds until puffed. Salt lightly.
2. Wipe kombu with sake to soften.
3. Salt the bream lightly, sandwich between kombu sheets, wrap tightly and cure in the fridge 3 hours (up to 6).
4. Blanch shiso 5 seconds, refresh, squeeze, and blend with grapeseed oil at 60°C for 2 minutes; strain through muslin overnight or in a coffee filter.
5. Unwrap the bream — it should be firm and slightly translucent — and slice 4 mm thick.
6. Dress plates with ponzu, lay fish over, dot with shiso oil, add ginger and rice crackers.

## Plating & Service
Six slices per chilled plate, crackers stacked separately so guests build crisp bites.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Any firm white fish cures the same way; skip the crackers and serve over chilled somen for a lunch course.
