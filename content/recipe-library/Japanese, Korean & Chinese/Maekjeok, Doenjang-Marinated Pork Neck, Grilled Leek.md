# Maekjeok, Doenjang-Marinated Pork Neck, Grilled Leek

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The pre-gochujang classic — pork neck in a nutty doenjang cure, grilled over high heat until burnished.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Pork neck (collar), 2 cm steaks — 800 g
- Doenjang — 50 g
- Soy sauce — 15 ml
- Honey — 25 g
- Mirin — 30 ml
- Garlic, grated — 15 g
- Ginger — 8 g
- Toasted sesame oil — 15 ml
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Baby leeks — 8
- Neutral oil — 10 ml
- Spring onion, julienne — 30 g

## Method
1. Whisk doenjang, soy, honey, mirin, garlic, ginger, sesame oil and pepper.
2. Massage into the pork; marinate 2–6 hours.
3. Scrape off excess marinade; rest meat at room temperature 20 minutes.
4. Grill at 220°C, 4–5 minutes per side to 63°C core with dark caramel edges (doenjang burns — keep it moving).
5. Grill oiled baby leeks alongside until soft and striped.
6. Rest 4 minutes; slice thick against the grain.

## Plating & Service
Rustic slices with leeks and a pile of dressed spring onion; lettuce leaves optional but welcome.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken thighs or thick portobellos (vegan, maple for honey) take the same marinade beautifully.
