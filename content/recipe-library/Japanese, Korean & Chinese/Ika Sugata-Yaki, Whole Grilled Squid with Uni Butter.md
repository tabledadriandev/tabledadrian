# Ika Sugata-Yaki, Whole Grilled Squid with Uni Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Whole squid grilled in its own geometry, sliced back into rings at the table, gilded with sea-urchin butter.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Whole squid, 300 g each, cleaned, bodies intact — 4
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Grapeseed oil — 20 ml
- Uni (sea urchin) — 60 g
- Unsalted butter, soft — 60 g
- Tamari — 8 ml
- Lemon juice — 8 ml
- Espelette or shichimi — 1 g
- Lemon — 1
- Chives — 6 g

## Method
1. Push tentacles back inside the tubes or skewer alongside; pat everything bone-dry.
2. Sieve uni into soft butter with tamari and lemon juice; roll in parchment, chill.
3. Score the tubes in a shallow 1 cm crosshatch on one side only.
4. Oil and salt; grill at 240°C, 2.5 minutes per side — the tubes should just turn opaque and bounce back to the touch.
5. Rest 1 minute; slice into 2 cm rings, keeping the squid's shape.
6. Melt a 15 g coin of uni butter over each and finish with chives, shichimi and lemon.

## Plating & Service
Reassembled squid on a hot plate per guest, butter melting into the score lines as it lands.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No uni? Whip 20 g white miso into the butter instead; cuttlefish takes an extra minute per side.
