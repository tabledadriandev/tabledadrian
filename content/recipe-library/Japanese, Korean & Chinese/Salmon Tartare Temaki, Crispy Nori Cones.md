# Salmon Tartare Temaki, Crispy Nori Cones

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> DIY hand-rolls with tempered nori that stays glass-crisp — an interactive canapé moment for the aft deck.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Sashimi-grade salmon — 300 g
- Nori sheets — 4, halved
- Sushi rice, seasoned, warm — 240 g
- Soy sauce — 20 ml
- Toasted sesame oil — 8 ml
- Yuzu juice — 10 ml
- Chives, minced — 10 g
- Ikura — 40 g
- Wasabi — 8 g
- Toasted sesame seeds — 6 g
- Neutral oil — 5 ml

## Method
1. Pass nori halves over a naked flame 2 seconds per side, brush the merest film of oil, and re-crisp at 140°C for 3 minutes; keep in an airtight box.
2. Dice salmon into 5 mm brunoise, keeping it over ice.
3. Fold soy, sesame oil, yuzu juice and chives through the salmon just before service.
4. Roll each nori half into a cone; spoon in 30 g warm rice.
5. Top with 35 g tartare, a pearl of wasabi and a spoon of ikura.
6. Finish with sesame and serve upright immediately.

## Plating & Service
Stand cones in a holed cedar block or shot glasses filled with black rice; guests eat in three bites.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Gluten-free with tamari; swap salmon for avocado + pickled shiitake for a vegan cone.
