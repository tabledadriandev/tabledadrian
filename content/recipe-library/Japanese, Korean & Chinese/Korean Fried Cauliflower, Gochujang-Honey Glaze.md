# Korean Fried Cauliflower, Gochujang-Honey Glaze

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> KFC crunch, zero meat — double-fried cauliflower in a glossy sweet-heat glaze that survives ten minutes on a buffet.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Cauliflower — 600 g, in 4 cm florets
- Plain flour — 60 g
- Potato starch — 90 g
- Baking powder — 3 g
- Cold sparkling water — 160 ml
- Neutral oil — 1.5 L
- Gochujang — 50 g
- Honey or agave — 40 g
- Soy sauce — 15 ml
- Rice vinegar — 15 ml
- Garlic, grated — 6 g
- Toasted sesame seeds — 6 g
- Spring onion — 15 g

## Method
1. Simmer gochujang, agave, soy, vinegar and garlic 3 minutes to a syrupy glaze.
2. Whisk flour, 60 g starch, baking powder and sparkling water to a batter; keep remaining starch for dusting.
3. Dust florets in starch, then coat in batter.
4. First fry at 165°C for 3 minutes; rest 3 minutes on a rack.
5. Second fry at 190°C for 90 seconds until deeply crisp.
6. Toss through warm glaze in a large bowl — 3 seconds, no soaking.
7. Finish with sesame and spring onion.

## Plating & Service
Pile in a warm bowl and serve with picks; glaze should crackle, not drip.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Same method for wings (fry 6 + 3 minutes to 74°C); agave keeps it vegan, tamari + rice flour keeps it GF.
