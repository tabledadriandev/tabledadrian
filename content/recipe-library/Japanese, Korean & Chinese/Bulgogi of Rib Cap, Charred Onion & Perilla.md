# Bulgogi of Rib Cap, Charred Onion & Perilla

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Pear-sweetened soy marinade on the most marbled cut on the carcass, flashed hard so it caramelises, not stews.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Beef rib cap or ribeye, sliced 3 mm — 700 g
- Nashi pear, grated — 120 g
- Onion, grated — 60 g
- Soy sauce — 70 ml
- Caster sugar — 20 g
- Toasted sesame oil — 20 ml
- Garlic, grated — 12 g
- Ginger, grated — 6 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Neutral oil — 20 ml
- Onions, thick petals — 150 g
- Spring onion — 20 g
- Perilla or lettuce leaves — 12
- Toasted sesame seeds — 6 g

## Method
1. Blend pear, grated onion, soy, sugar, sesame oil, garlic, ginger and pepper; marinate the beef 2–4 hours (not overnight — pear enzymes over-tenderise).
2. Drain the beef well; pat surfaces dry-ish for browning.
3. Char onion petals in a dry wok; reserve.
4. Sear beef in 3 waves in a screaming wok (250°C), 60–90 seconds each — deep colour, still juicy.
5. Return everything with the charred onion; toss 30 seconds.
6. Finish with spring onion and sesame; serve with perilla leaves for wrapping.

## Plating & Service
A sizzling platter, leaves stacked beside — guests wrap, no cutlery required.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF with tamari; the marinade works on chicken thigh, portobello (vegan) and thin pork shoulder.
