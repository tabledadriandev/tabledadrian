# Tonkatsu of Iberico Pork, Yuzu Cabbage & House Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Panko-armoured Iberico with a fruity homemade tonkatsu sauce — the katsu standard, upgraded with proper pork.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Iberico pork loin steaks — 4 × 180 g
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Plain flour — 60 g
- Eggs, beaten — 2
- Panko, coarse — 150 g
- Neutral oil — 1.5 L
- White cabbage — 300 g
- Yuzu juice — 15 ml
- Grapeseed oil — 20 ml
- Ketchup — 60 g
- Worcestershire sauce — 40 ml
- Oyster sauce — 15 g
- Caster sugar — 10 g
- Dijon mustard — 5 g
- Toasted sesame seeds — 5 g

## Method
1. Simmer ketchup, Worcestershire, oyster sauce, sugar and mustard 3 minutes; cool.
2. Shave cabbage hair-thin; hold in iced water 10 minutes, spin dry, and dress with yuzu juice and grapeseed oil at service.
3. Salt pork; snip the fat seam every 2 cm to prevent curling.
4. Flour, egg, then press firmly into panko.
5. Fry at 170°C for 5–6 minutes to golden and 62°C core; rest 3 minutes on a rack.
6. Cut into 2 cm bars against the grain, reassemble, and sprinkle sesame over the cabbage.

## Plating & Service
Sliced katsu leaning on a cabbage cloud, sauce in a jug — never pre-sauced, the crust is sacred.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken thigh or celeriac steaks (vegan, aquafaba for egg) fry identically; GF panko available — verify the Worcestershire.
