# Black Sesame Goma-ae, Charred Baby Spinach

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The banchan-adjacent Japanese classic, deepened with a fast char before dressing in black sesame paste.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Baby spinach — 400 g
- Black sesame seeds — 40 g
- Tamari — 15 ml
- Caster sugar — 8 g
- Mirin — 10 ml
- Rice vinegar — 5 ml
- Toasted sesame oil — 4 ml
- Flaky salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Toast black sesame in a dry pan at medium heat 3 minutes until fragrant and popping.
2. Grind in a suribachi to a coarse, oily paste; work in tamari, sugar, mirin and vinegar.
3. Sear spinach in a dry, screaming-hot wok (30 seconds) so edges char but leaves stay vivid.
4. Cool spinach on a tray, then squeeze firmly into a log and cut into 4 cm lengths.
5. Fold through the sesame dressing just before serving; finish with sesame oil and salt.

## Plating & Service
Serve in small mounds in individual bowls, cold or at room temperature, as part of a spread of small plates.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> White sesame + white miso gives a sweeter profile; green beans or chrysanthemum greens swap in cleanly.
