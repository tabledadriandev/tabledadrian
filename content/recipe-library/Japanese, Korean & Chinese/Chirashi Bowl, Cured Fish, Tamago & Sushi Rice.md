# Chirashi Bowl, Cured Fish, Tamago & Sushi Rice

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Scattered sushi as an elegant service — three cured fish, sweet tamago dice, and glossy seasoned rice built in layers.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Sushi rice, cooked and seasoned — 700 g
- Salmon, sashimi grade — 200 g
- Tuna, sashimi grade — 200 g
- Sea bream — 150 g
- Soy sauce — 40 ml
- Mirin — 20 ml
- Yuzu juice — 10 ml
- Eggs — 3
- Caster sugar — 15 g
- Ikura — 60 g
- Cucumber, fine dice — 100 g
- Pickled ginger — 40 g
- Nori, shredded — 3 g
- Toasted sesame seeds — 8 g
- Wasabi — 12 g

## Method
1. Cure the bream slices in kombu (or 5 ml soy + salt pinch) 30 minutes; zuke-marinate the tuna in soy-mirin 15 minutes; slice salmon fresh with yuzu at service.
2. Beat eggs with sugar; cook a thin rolled omelette in layers, pressing between additions; cool and dice 1 cm.
3. Season rice while warm; hold at room temperature under damp cloth — never refrigerate.
4. Slice all fish into clean 3 cm pieces, wiping the blade.
5. Layer rice into wide bowls; scatter cucumber and half the sesame through the surface.
6. Compose fish, tamago, ikura, ginger and nori over; finish with sesame and wasabi.

## Plating & Service
Build in front of guests if possible — the scatter is the show; soy in droppers, not poured over.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Any impeccable fish works — scallop, amberjack, cured mackerel; vegan chirashi with avocado, pickled shiitake and tamago-style tofu.
