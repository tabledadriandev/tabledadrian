# Sake-Steamed Sea Bass & Clams, Kombu Butter Broth

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A one-pan sakamushi — bass and clams steamed in sake over kombu, the broth mounted with cold butter at the finish.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Sea bass fillets, skin on — 4 × 150 g
- Palourde clams, purged — 500 g
- Kombu — 10 g
- Sake — 150 ml
- Water — 100 ml
- Unsalted butter, cold — 50 g
- Usukuchi soy — 10 ml
- Ginger, julienne — 10 g
- Spring onion, fine — 20 g
- Baby spinach — 100 g
- Lemon — 1/2

## Method
1. Lay kombu in a wide sauté pan; add sake, water and ginger and bring to a bare simmer.
2. Salt the bass lightly; set skin-up on the kombu with the clams around.
3. Lid on; steam at a gentle simmer 8–10 minutes until clams open and bass hits 52°C.
4. Lift fish and clams to warm bowls; discard any closed clams; wilt spinach in the broth 30 seconds.
5. Remove kombu; whisk in cold butter and soy off the boil to a light emulsion; correct with lemon.
6. Pour the broth around the fish.

## Plating & Service
Deep warm bowls, clams circling the bass, broth poured at the table from a small pot.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free: finish with 20 ml olive oil instead of butter; monkfish or turbot behave beautifully here.
