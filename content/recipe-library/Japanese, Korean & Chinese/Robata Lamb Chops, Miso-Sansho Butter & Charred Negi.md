# Robata Lamb Chops, Miso-Sansho Butter & Charred Negi

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Riviera lamb over binchotan heat, glossed with a numbing miso butter — East-West grill work at its most persuasive.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Lamb cutlets, frenched — 12 (900 g)
- Fine salt — 8 g
- Grapeseed oil — 15 ml
- Unsalted butter, soft — 80 g
- Aka miso — 25 g
- Sansho pepper, ground — 1.5 g
- Garlic, grated — 5 g
- Lemon juice — 8 ml
- Baby leeks or negi — 8
- Tamari — 10 ml
- Shichimi togarashi — 1 g

## Method
1. Beat butter with miso, sansho, garlic and lemon; roll in parchment and chill.
2. Salt lamb 30 minutes ahead; oil lightly.
3. Grill baby leeks whole at 230°C until blackened outside, soft inside (8 minutes); dress with tamari.
4. Grill chops 2.5 minutes per side over the hottest zone to 54°C core.
5. Rest 4 minutes under foil with a 10 g coin of miso butter melting on each pile.
6. Dust with shichimi at the pass.

## Plating & Service
Three chops interlocked per plate, leeks alongside, butter pooling into the resting juices.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free: whisk miso and sansho into 40 ml warm olive oil instead; ribeye or swordfish steaks take the same butter.
