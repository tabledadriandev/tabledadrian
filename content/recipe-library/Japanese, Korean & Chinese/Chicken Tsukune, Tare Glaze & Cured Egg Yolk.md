# Chicken Tsukune, Tare Glaze & Cured Egg Yolk

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Springy yakitori meatballs lacquered in tare, dragged through a jammy cured yolk at the table — the izakaya classic, refined.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chicken thigh, minced twice — 450 g
- Chicken skin, finely chopped — 50 g
- Spring onion, minced — 30 g
- Grated ginger — 10 g
- Egg white — 30 g
- Potato starch — 15 g
- Fine salt — 5 g
- White pepper — 1 g
- Tare (soy 100 ml, mirin 100 ml, sake 50 ml, sugar 30 g) — 280 ml
- Egg yolks — 4
- Soy sauce for curing yolks — 60 ml
- Sansho pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Cure the yolks in soy sauce for 45 minutes; drain gently.
2. Reduce the tare ingredients by half at a low simmer (about 15 minutes) to a light syrup.
3. Beat minced chicken, skin, aromatics, egg white, starch, salt and pepper vigorously 2 minutes until tacky.
4. With wet hands form 12 ovals around soaked bamboo skewers.
5. Poach skewers 3 minutes at 90°C to set, then grill at 220°C, brushing with tare 3 times, about 6 minutes until lacquered and 74°C in the centre.
6. Dust with sansho and serve with the cured yolk alongside for dipping.

## Plating & Service
Three skewers per person on a cedar board, yolk in a dimpled dish, extra tare in a dropper.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Gluten-free with tamari tare; swap chicken for duck leg mince and glaze with the same tare plus 5 g yuzu juice.
