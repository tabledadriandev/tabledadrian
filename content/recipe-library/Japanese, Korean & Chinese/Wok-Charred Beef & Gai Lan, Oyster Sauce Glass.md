# Wok-Charred Beef & Gai Lan, Oyster Sauce Glass

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Beef and broccoli, done with wok hei conviction — velveted sirloin, jade-green gai lan, a sauce that barely exists.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Sirloin, 4 mm slices across the grain — 500 g
- Baking soda — 3 g
- Soy sauce — 15 ml
- Shaoxing wine — 15 ml
- Potato starch — 10 g
- Gai lan or tenderstem — 350 g
- Neutral oil — 40 ml
- Garlic, sliced — 12 g
- Ginger, julienne — 10 g
- Oyster sauce — 35 g
- Dark soy — 5 ml
- Caster sugar — 6 g
- Water — 60 ml
- Potato starch (sauce) — 4 g
- White pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Massage beef with baking soda 15 minutes; rinse, dry, then marinate in soy, wine and starch 15 minutes.
2. Whisk oyster sauce, dark soy, sugar, water, starch and pepper.
3. Blanch gai lan 90 seconds; drain hard.
4. Sear beef in a smoking wok in two waves, 60 seconds each, barely moving it; remove.
5. Fry garlic and ginger 20 seconds; return beef and gai lan.
6. Sauce in, 30 seconds to gloss, out of the pan.

## Plating & Service
Stems aligned, beef draped over, sauce at a shine not a puddle.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Velveted chicken or king oyster mushroom slabs swap in; the baking-soda step is what buyers of 'restaurant texture' are tasting.
