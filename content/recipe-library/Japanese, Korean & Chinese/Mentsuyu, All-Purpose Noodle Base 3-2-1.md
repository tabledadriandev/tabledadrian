# Mentsuyu, All-Purpose Noodle Base 3-2-1

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> One bottled concentrate, four dishes a day — dilute for dipping, splash for broths, straight for glazing.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Soy sauce — 200 ml
- Mirin — 200 ml
- Sake — 100 ml
- Katsuobushi — 15 g
- Kombu — 8 g
- Dried shiitake — 5 g
- Caster sugar — 10 g

## Method
1. Simmer mirin and sake 2 minutes.
2. Add soy, sugar, kombu and shiitake; bring to 85°C.
3. Add katsuobushi; cut the heat and steep 5 minutes.
4. Strain through muslin without pressing.
5. Bottle; refrigerate up to 1 month.
6. Dilute: 1:1 with water for dipping (zaru soba), 1:3 for hot kake broth, 1:4 for oden or nimono.

## Plating & Service
Label the bottle with the dilution chart — any crew member can then produce a correct noodle course.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: omit katsuobushi, double shiitake and kombu; the concentrate also seasons tamagoyaki and simmered vegetables.
