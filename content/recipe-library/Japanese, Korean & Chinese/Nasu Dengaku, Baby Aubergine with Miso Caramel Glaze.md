# Nasu Dengaku, Baby Aubergine with Miso Caramel Glaze

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Blowtorched miso glaze over creamy aubergine — a vegan small plate that meat-eaters fight over.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Baby aubergines — 4 (about 480 g)
- Neutral oil — 30 ml
- Red (aka) miso — 60 g
- White (shiro) miso — 30 g
- Mirin — 45 ml
- Sake — 30 ml
- Caster sugar — 25 g
- Toasted sesame seeds — 8 g
- Spring onion, fine rings — 10 g
- Shiso, chiffonade — 4 leaves

## Method
1. Halve aubergines lengthwise and score the flesh in a 1 cm diamond pattern.
2. Brush with oil and roast cut-side down at 200°C for 12 minutes until collapsing.
3. Simmer both misos, mirin, sake and sugar 4 minutes to a spreadable glaze.
4. Spread 15 g glaze over each half.
5. Grill at 230°C (or blowtorch) 2–3 minutes until blistered and bubbling.
6. Scatter sesame, spring onion and shiso.

## Plating & Service
Two halves per plate, glaze-side up, eaten with a spoon straight from the skin.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap aubergine for thick leek segments in winter; add 5 g yuzu juice to the glaze for lift.
