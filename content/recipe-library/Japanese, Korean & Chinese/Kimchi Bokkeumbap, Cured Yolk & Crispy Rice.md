# Kimchi Bokkeumbap, Cured Yolk & Crispy Rice

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Day-old rice, over-ripe kimchi and rendered pork fat, pressed until it crackles — finished with a grated cured yolk.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Day-old cooked short-grain rice — 700 g
- Aged kimchi, chopped — 300 g
- Kimchi brine — 50 ml
- Pork belly or bacon, lardons — 120 g
- Neutral oil — 20 ml
- Gochujang — 20 g
- Soy sauce — 12 ml
- Toasted sesame oil — 12 ml
- Egg yolks, soy-cured 45 min — 4 (plus 4 for frying, optional)
- Spring onion — 25 g
- Toasted sesame seeds — 6 g
- Nori, crumbled — 2 g

## Method
1. Render the lardons in oil until crisp, 4 minutes.
2. Fry kimchi in the fat 3 minutes until its edges caramelise.
3. Add gochujang; toast 30 seconds.
4. Break in the cold rice; toss, then press flat and leave 2 minutes at 220°C to crust. Repeat the press twice.
5. Season with soy, brine and sesame oil around the pan edge.
6. Bowl it; grate a cured yolk over each portion, finish with spring onion, sesame and nori.

## Plating & Service
Serve in warm shallow bowls with crust shards visible; a fried egg on top for lunch service never hurts.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan with shiitake lardons and no yolk; the older and sourer the kimchi, the better the dish — save the best fresh batch for banchan.
