# Tuna Tataki, Charred Spring Onion Ponzu

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Ten seconds a side over binchotan-hot steel gives a mahogany crust on ruby tuna — theatre you can do to order.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Yellowfin tuna loin, centre-cut — 400 g
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Grapeseed oil — 10 ml
- Spring onions — 6
- Ponzu — 80 ml
- Toasted sesame oil — 5 ml
- Grated ginger — 8 g
- Toasted white sesame seeds — 6 g
- Daikon, finely grated and drained — 60 g
- Micro coriander — 4 g

## Method
1. Char 4 whole spring onions on a dry pan at 240°C until blackened in patches; slice finely and steep in the ponzu with sesame oil and ginger for 15 minutes.
2. Season the tuna block with salt and oil; rest 10 minutes at room temperature.
3. Heat a heavy pan until smoking (around 250°C); sear the tuna 10–15 seconds per face — 4 faces — then plunge into iced water for 30 seconds.
4. Pat dry, wrap tightly in cling film and chill 10 minutes to set the shape.
5. Slice into 8 mm slices with a single pulling stroke.
6. Spoon the charred-onion ponzu over, top with daikon oroshi, sesame and the remaining spring onion, sliced raw.

## Plating & Service
Fan slices on a long plate, sauce spooned across the centre so the rare interior stays visible.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap tuna for swordfish or venison loin; use tamari-based ponzu to keep it gluten-free (check your soy).
