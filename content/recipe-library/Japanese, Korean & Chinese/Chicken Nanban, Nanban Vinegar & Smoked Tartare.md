# Chicken Nanban, Nanban Vinegar & Smoked Tartare

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Kyushu's fried-then-soaked chicken with a smoky egg tartare — sweet, sharp, rich; a guaranteed guest favourite.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chicken thighs, boneless — 700 g
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Plain flour — 50 g
- Eggs, beaten — 2
- Neutral oil — 1.5 L
- Soy sauce — 50 ml
- Rice vinegar — 60 ml
- Caster sugar — 40 g
- Dried chilli — 1
- Eggs, hard-boiled — 3
- Kewpie-style mayonnaise — 90 g
- Cornichons, minced — 25 g
- Shallot, minced — 20 g
- Smoked paprika — 1 g
- Chives — 6 g

## Method
1. Simmer soy, vinegar, sugar and chilli 2 minutes for the nanban sauce; keep warm.
2. Chop boiled eggs; fold with mayonnaise, cornichons, shallot, smoked paprika and chives for the tartare.
3. Salt chicken, dust in flour, drag through beaten egg.
4. Fry at 170°C for 7–8 minutes to 74°C, until the eggy crust is deep gold.
5. Dunk the hot chicken in nanban sauce for 20 seconds per side.
6. Slice thickly and spoon tartare generously over.

## Plating & Service
Sliced over shredded cabbage, tartare across the middle, extra nanban in a jug.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swordfish nanban is superb; lighten by grilling instead of frying and halving the sauce sugar.
