# Sundubu Jjigae, Seafood & Silken Tofu Stew

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Curds of barely-set tofu in a chilli-red seafood broth, an egg cracked in at the boil — comfort with drama.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Extra-soft (sundubu) tofu — 700 g
- Neutral oil — 20 ml
- Toasted sesame oil — 10 ml
- Gochugaru — 20 g
- Garlic, minced — 12 g
- Onion, fine dice — 80 g
- Anchovy-kombu stock — 600 ml
- Fish sauce — 15 ml
- Clams, purged — 300 g
- Prawns — 8
- Courgette, dice — 100 g
- Eggs — 4
- Spring onion — 25 g

## Method
1. Bloom gochugaru, garlic and onion in both oils over low heat 2 minutes — do not scorch.
2. Add stock and fish sauce; simmer 5 minutes.
3. Add clams and courgette; simmer until the shells open, 4 minutes.
4. Spoon in the tofu in big soft curds; add prawns and return to a gentle boil for 3 minutes.
5. Crack an egg into each portion (or the pot) and cut the heat — it poaches in the residual 90°C heat.
6. Finish with spring onion and a thread of sesame oil.

## Plating & Service
Individual stone bowls preheated in a 220°C oven so the stew arrives still boiling; warn the guests.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pork-kimchi or pure mushroom versions use the same base; skip the egg for dairy/egg-free tables.
