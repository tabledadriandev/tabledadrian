# Goma Dressing, Roasted Sesame & White Miso

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The creamy sesame dressing that makes salad disappear — roasted seeds ground fresh, rounded with miso and kewpie logic.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- White sesame seeds — 80 g
- Kewpie-style mayonnaise — 60 g
- Shiro miso — 20 g
- Rice vinegar — 30 ml
- Tamari — 15 ml
- Caster sugar — 12 g
- Toasted sesame oil — 10 ml
- Water — 40 ml

## Method
1. Toast sesame seeds at medium heat, moving constantly, 4–5 minutes until deep gold and popping.
2. Grind while warm in a suribachi or spice grinder to a coarse butter.
3. Whisk in miso, mayonnaise, vinegar, tamari, sugar and sesame oil.
4. Loosen with water to a double-cream consistency.
5. Rest 30 minutes; adjust — it should be nutty first, sweet second, sharp third.

## Plating & Service
Bottle for the pass: over charred cabbage, cold chicken, blanched greens, or as the shabu-shabu dip.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan with plant mayo; add 10 g grated onion + 5 ml soy for a wafu steak-salad version.
