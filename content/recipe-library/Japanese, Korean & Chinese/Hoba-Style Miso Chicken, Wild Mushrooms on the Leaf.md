# Hoba-Style Miso Chicken, Wild Mushrooms on the Leaf

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Mountain-style hoba miso translated to the boat — chicken and mushrooms roasted on a leaf under a bubbling miso cap.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chicken thighs, boneless, skin on — 4 × 180 g
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Aka miso — 70 g
- Shiro miso — 30 g
- Sake — 30 ml
- Mirin — 30 ml
- Caster sugar — 15 g
- Mixed wild mushrooms (girolles, shiitake, shimeji) — 300 g
- Spring onion — 30 g
- Hoba, banana leaf or baking parchment — 4 sheets
- Grapeseed oil — 15 ml
- Sansho — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Whisk both misos with sake, mirin and sugar to a thick sauce.
2. Salt chicken; render skin-down in oil at 180°C for 8 minutes until deeply crisp; rest.
3. Sear mushrooms hard in the same fat, 3 minutes.
4. Lay leaves on a tray; spread 30 g miso sauce on each, pile mushrooms, and top with the chicken, sliced, skin up.
5. Roast at 210°C for 8 minutes until the miso bubbles and catches at the edges — chicken to 74°C.
6. Scatter spring onion and sansho; serve on the leaf.

## Plating & Service
The leaf goes straight onto a wooden board per guest — bubbling, aromatic, rustic-luxe.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan with king oyster steaks in place of chicken; scallops need only 4 minutes in the oven.
