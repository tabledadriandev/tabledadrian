# Date & Cacao Sea Bars with Espresso and Olive Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> No-bake, no-melt watch fuel — dense date-cacao bars that hold their shape in a warm cockpit pocket where chocolate would surrender.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Medjool dates, pitted — 400 g
- Almonds, toasted — 200 g
- Walnuts, toasted — 100 g
- Cacao powder — 40 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 30 ml
- Espresso, cooled — 30 ml
- Vanilla paste — 4 g
- Fine sea salt — 3 g
- Sesame seeds, toasted — 20 g
- Cacao nibs — 30 g

## Method
1. Blitz almonds and walnuts to coarse rubble; set a third aside.
2. Add dates, cacao, oil, espresso, vanilla and salt; process to a dough that holds when pinched.
3. Fold in reserved nut rubble and cacao nibs by hand — texture matters.
4. Press very firmly into a lined 20 × 20 cm tin; sesame over, pressed in.
5. Chill 2 hours; cut into 12 bars; wrap individually in parchment.
6. Store chilled or in the coolest locker.

## Plating & Service
Stacked in a tin by the chart table; one bar is a genuine watch-length energy dose.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Keeps 3 weeks chilled, 2 months frozen; nothing melts, nothing crumbs. Blender-only recipe — makeable on a rolling boat when the oven is off-limits.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: sunflower + pumpkin seeds and 20 g extra dates. Decaf: orange zest instead of espresso. Roll into balls and dust with cacao for apéro-adjacent petit fours.
