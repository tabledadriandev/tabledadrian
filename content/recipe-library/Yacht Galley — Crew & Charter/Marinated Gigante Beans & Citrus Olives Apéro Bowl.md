# Marinated Gigante Beans & Citrus Olives Apéro Bowl

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A jar that lives in the fridge and gets better all week — decanted into bowls the second an unexpected tender arrives with guests.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Cooked gigante or butter beans — 480 g
- Mixed olives — 300 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 120 ml
- Orange zest — 4 g
- Orange juice — 40 ml
- Lemon zest — 3 g
- Red wine vinegar — 20 ml
- Garlic, sliced — 10 g
- Fennel seeds, cracked — 4 g
- Chile flakes — 2 g
- Bay leaves — 2
- Rosemary sprig — 1
- Dried oregano — 3 g
- Flaky sea salt — 4 g

## Method
1. Warm oil with garlic, fennel seeds, chile, bay and rosemary to 60 °C — just fragrant, no colour; cool 10 minutes.
2. Whisk in orange juice, both zests, vinegar, oregano.
3. Fold in beans and olives; season with flaky salt.
4. Marinate at least 4 hours, ideally 2 days, jarred in the fridge.
5. Serve at room temperature with a slotted spoon.

## Plating & Service
Shallow bowls with cocktail picks and good bread for the marinade oil — apéro deployed in 90 seconds.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Keeps 10 days chilled and improves for the first three — the standing 'guests appeared' solution. The strained oil becomes tomorrow's salad dressing; nothing wasted.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 150 g feta cubes (no longer vegan) or artichokes. Swap orange for preserved lemon for a sharper jar.
