# Pear & Ginger Sticky Toffee Puddings with Rum Butterscotch

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Individual date-and-pear sponges drowned in rum butterscotch — the cold-night, end-of-season dessert that empties conversation from the crew mess.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Medjool dates, chopped — 250 g
- Boiling water — 250 ml
- Bicarbonate of soda — 5 g
- Unsalted butter, soft — 110 g
- Light brown sugar — 180 g
- Eggs — 3 (180 g)
- Plain flour — 250 g
- Baking powder — 10 g
- Ground ginger — 6 g
- Stem ginger, minced — 40 g
- Pears, ripe, 1 cm dice — 300 g
- Fine sea salt — 4 g
- Double cream — 300 ml
- Dark brown sugar — 180 g
- Unsalted butter (sauce) — 120 g
- Dark rum — 40 ml
- Flaky sea salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Pour boiling water over dates with the bicarb; stand 10 minutes; mash roughly.
2. Beat soft butter and light brown sugar; add eggs one by one.
3. Fold in flour, baking powder, ground ginger, fine salt; then dates (with liquid), stem ginger, pears.
4. Divide between 10 buttered dariole moulds or a deep muffin tray, three-quarters full.
5. Bake at 175 °C fan for 22–26 minutes until springy (internal 96 °C).
6. Butterscotch: melt dark brown sugar, butter and cream together; boil 3 minutes; off heat add rum and flaky salt. Turn out puddings, spike the bases with a skewer, drown in sauce.

## Plating & Service
Inverted onto warm plates, sauce flooding the well, cold pouring cream or vanilla ice cream against the heat.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Sponges freeze for 2 months and rewarm from frozen (microwave 90 sec or oven 12 min); sauce keeps 2 weeks chilled. Dessert for any number, any night, on 15 minutes' notice.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Alcohol-free: swap rum for 5 g vanilla. GF: 1:1 blend. Swap pear for apple + 2 g cinnamon in October.
