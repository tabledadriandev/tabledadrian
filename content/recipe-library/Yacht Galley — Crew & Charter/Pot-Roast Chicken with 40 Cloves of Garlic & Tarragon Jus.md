# Pot-Roast Chicken with 40 Cloves of Garlic & Tarragon Jus

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Two birds, one heavy pot, and garlic melted to sweet cream — Sunday-lunch gravitas with almost no active cooking.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Whole chickens — 2 × 1.5 kg
- Garlic cloves, unpeeled — 40 (200 g)
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Shallots, halved — 200 g
- White wine — 250 ml
- Chicken stock — 500 ml
- Tarragon — 12 g
- Thyme — 5 g
- Bay leaves — 2
- Double cream — 100 ml
- Dijon mustard — 15 g
- Lemon — 1
- Fine sea salt — 16 g
- Black pepper — 3 g

## Method
1. Season birds inside and out with salt the night before; refrigerate uncovered.
2. Brown chickens on all sides in butter and oil in a huge cocotte, 10 minutes; remove.
3. Add shallots and garlic, colour 4 minutes; deglaze with wine.
4. Return birds breast-up with stock, thyme, bay; lid on, oven at 160 °C fan for 70–85 minutes to 72 °C in the thigh... pull at 70 °C breast.
5. Rest birds 15 minutes. Crush half the garlic into the juices; reduce by a third.
6. Finish jus with cream, mustard, tarragon, lemon juice; carve and sauce.

## Plating & Service
Carve at the table; whole soft garlic cloves squeezed onto bread is the ritual.

> [!note] Galley notes
> One pot, no basting, and the lid keeps the oven clean on a heel. Carcasses become tomorrow's stock; leftover meat is the week's sandwich programme.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free: finish jus with 20 ml olive oil instead of cream. Add 600 g baby potatoes to the pot at minute 30 for a true one-pot.
