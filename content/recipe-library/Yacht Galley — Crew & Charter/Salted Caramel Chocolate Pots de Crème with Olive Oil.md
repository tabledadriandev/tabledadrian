# Salted Caramel Chocolate Pots de Crème with Olive Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Deep bittersweet custards baked in a bain-marie and finished like a Ligurian secret — oil, salt, nothing else needed.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Dark chocolate (70%) — 220 g
- Double cream — 500 ml
- Whole milk — 250 ml
- Caster sugar — 120 g
- Egg yolks — 8 (160 g)
- Vanilla paste — 5 g
- Fine sea salt — 3 g
- Extra virgin olive oil (peppery) — 20 ml
- Flaky sea salt — 2 g
- Crème fraîche — 120 g

## Method
1. Dry caramel: melt sugar to deep amber (185 °C); off heat, whisk in warmed cream and milk carefully until smooth.
2. Pour the hot caramel cream over chopped chocolate; stand 2 minutes; stir to a gloss.
3. Whisk yolks with vanilla and fine salt; temper in the chocolate mix slowly.
4. Strain; skim foam. Fill 8 small pots or ramekins.
5. Bake in a deep bain-marie (water to the custard line), covered loosely with foil, at 140 °C for 30–35 minutes — set edges, trembling centres (82 °C).
6. Chill 4+ hours; serve with crème fraîche, olive oil threads, flaky salt.

## Plating & Service
Pot on a saucer, quenelle of crème fraîche, oil pooled in the dip, three salt flakes — restraint is the garnish.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Bakes the day before and holds 3 days — service is opening the fridge. Bain-marie forgiveness makes it the most reliable 'fine-dining' dessert a moving galley can promise.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free: coconut cream + oat milk, skip crème fraîche. Spike the caramel with 20 ml dark rum. Espresso version: dissolve 4 g instant coffee into the milk.
