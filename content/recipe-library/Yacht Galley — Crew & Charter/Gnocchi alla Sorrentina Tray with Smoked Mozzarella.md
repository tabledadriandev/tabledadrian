# Gnocchi alla Sorrentina Tray with Smoked Mozzarella

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Shelf-stable gnocchi, tinned tomatoes and a bag of scamorza become molten comfort — 30 minutes from locker to bubbling tray.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Potato gnocchi (vacuum-packed) — 1.5 kg
- Olive oil — 60 ml
- Garlic, sliced — 20 g
- Chile flakes — 2 g
- Chopped tomatoes — 1.2 kg
- Tomato paste — 40 g
- Caster sugar — 6 g
- Basil — 30 g
- Smoked mozzarella (scamorza), diced — 300 g
- Fior di latte mozzarella, torn — 200 g
- Parmesan, grated — 80 g
- Dried oregano — 3 g
- Fine sea salt — 10 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 220 °C fan.
2. Fry garlic and chile in oil 1 minute; add tomatoes, paste, sugar, oregano, salt; simmer 12 minutes to a thick sugo; tear in half the basil.
3. Blanch gnocchi in salted boiling water until they float (90 seconds); drain.
4. Fold gnocchi through the sugo with the scamorza.
5. Tip into a deep tray, top with fior di latte and Parmesan.
6. Bake 12–15 minutes until blistered and molten; rest 5 minutes, finish with basil.

## Plating & Service
Serve bubbling in the tray with a bitter-leaf salad; strings of smoked cheese are the point.

> [!note] Galley notes
> All main components live in the dry stores/fridge for weeks — a slam-night rescue meal for 10. Assembles ahead and bakes on demand; leftovers reheat shockingly well.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: GF gnocchi (blanch 60 sec only). Add 300 g Italian sausage to the sugo for the meat table. Vegan: plant mozzarella, still excellent.
