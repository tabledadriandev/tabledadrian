# Charred Courgette, Butter Bean & Preserved Lemon Salad with Feta

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Plancha-striped courgettes and creamy butter beans that shrug off an afternoon in the sun — the workhorse salad of every beach setup.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Courgettes, 1 cm diagonal slices — 1 kg
- Olive oil — 90 ml
- Butter beans, cooked — 720 g
- Preserved lemon rind, minced — 30 g
- Lemon juice — 40 ml
- Garlic, grated — 8 g
- Honey — 15 g
- Chile flakes — 3 g
- Dried oregano — 4 g
- Feta — 200 g
- Mint — 15 g
- Dill — 10 g
- Toasted pine nuts — 40 g
- Fine sea salt — 10 g

## Method
1. Brush courgette slices with 50 ml oil and salt; char on a 250 °C plancha or grill, 2–3 minutes per side, to deep bar marks.
2. Dressing: whisk remaining oil, lemon juice, preserved lemon, garlic, honey, chile, oregano.
3. Toss warm courgettes and butter beans with the dressing — warm vegetables drink it deeper.
4. Rest 20 minutes (or up to 2 days chilled).
5. Fold in herbs; crumble feta and scatter pine nuts at service.

## Plating & Service
Low and wide on a platter, feta visible in slabs and rubble, herbs bright on top.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Fully dressed it improves for 48 h — batch double on grill day. Beans make it substantial enough to be the vegetarian main when the BBQ is all meat.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: olives instead of feta. Nut-free: toasted seeds. Swap courgette for grilled aubergine or peppers as the market dictates.
