# Charter Tiramisu Tray with Amaretto & Espresso Salt

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> One deep tray, zero oven, twelve portions — the dessert that must be made yesterday, which is exactly what a busy galley wants to hear.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Mascarpone — 750 g
- Egg yolks — 6 (120 g)
- Caster sugar — 140 g
- Double cream — 300 ml
- Amaretto — 50 ml
- Espresso, strong, cooled — 500 ml
- Savoiardi (ladyfingers) — 400 g
- Cocoa powder — 30 g
- Dark chocolate, grated — 40 g
- Instant espresso powder — 2 g
- Flaky sea salt — 1 g
- Vanilla paste — 4 g

## Method
1. Whisk yolks and sugar over a bain-marie to 70 °C and ribbon stage, 5 minutes; cool, whisking.
2. Beat mascarpone with vanilla until smooth; fold in the yolk sabayon.
3. Whip cream to soft peaks; fold in.
4. Mix espresso with amaretto; dip savoiardi 2 seconds per side — count it, sog is the enemy.
5. Layer in a 30 × 22 cm dish: biscuits, half the cream, biscuits, remaining cream; smooth.
6. Chill 12–24 hours. Serve dusted thick with cocoa, grated chocolate, and a pinch of espresso powder crushed with flaky salt.

## Plating & Service
Big spoonfuls into shallow bowls at the table — a tray dessert served with confidence, not apology.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Needs the overnight set, so it schedules itself: build after dinner service, serve tomorrow. Holds 3 days; the espresso salt garnish wakes up day-two portions.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Alcohol-free: swap amaretto for 30 ml more espresso + 3 g almond extract. Egg-safe version: skip sabayon, whip mascarpone with 80 g icing sugar and cream.
