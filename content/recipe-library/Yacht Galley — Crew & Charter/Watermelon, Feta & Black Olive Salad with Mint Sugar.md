# Watermelon, Feta & Black Olive Salad with Mint Sugar

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The 40 °C day rescue — icy watermelon, salty feta and a mint sugar that makes guests ask what the trick is (it's just mint and sugar).

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Watermelon, chilled, 3 cm chunks — 1.5 kg
- Feta, broken into slabs — 300 g
- Kalamata olives, pitted, torn — 120 g
- Red onion, shaved paper-thin — 80 g
- Lime juice — 40 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 50 ml
- Mint leaves — 20 g
- Caster sugar — 15 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Flaky sea salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Soak shaved onion in iced water 10 minutes; drain and dry — takes the aggression out.
2. Pound 15 g mint with the sugar to a green, fragrant sand.
3. Chill a platter in the freezer 10 minutes.
4. Arrange watermelon, tuck in feta slabs, olives and onion.
5. Dress with lime and oil; scatter mint sugar, remaining mint leaves, salt flakes, pepper.
6. Serve within 15 minutes, ice-cold.

## Plating & Service
On the frozen platter, feta in visible slabs — never crumbled to dust; the mint sugar goes on in front of guests.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Components prep hours ahead and hold in the fridge; only assembly is last-minute. Zero heat, zero pans — the galley stays closed at the hottest hour.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: swap feta for salted avocado + extra olives. Add cucumber and it stretches to 12. A few drops of ouzo in the dressing for the adults-only table.
