# Seared Halloumi & Peach Panzanella with Torn Sourdough

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Stale bread, ripe fruit and squeaky cheese in a bowl of dressing that only gets better — panzanella logic tuned for a hot deck.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Day-old sourdough, torn — 400 g
- Olive oil — 110 ml
- Halloumi, 1 cm slabs — 450 g
- Peaches or nectarines, wedged — 500 g
- Heritage tomatoes, chunked — 600 g
- Red onion, shaved — 100 g
- Red wine vinegar — 40 ml
- Honey — 15 g
- Dijon mustard — 8 g
- Basil — 20 g
- Mint — 10 g
- Capers — 25 g
- Fine sea salt — 10 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Toss torn bread with 40 ml oil and salt; bake at 200 °C fan for 10–12 minutes until golden outside, chewy inside.
2. Salt the tomato chunks in a colander over a bowl 15 minutes — keep every drop of juice.
3. Dressing: whisk tomato juices, vinegar, honey, mustard, 50 ml oil.
4. Sear halloumi in 20 ml oil, 2 minutes per side, to deep gold; tear into rough pieces.
5. Toss bread, tomatoes, peaches, onion and capers with the dressing; rest 15 minutes so the bread drinks.
6. Fold in halloumi and herbs just before serving.

## Plating & Service
A generous tumble in a wide bowl — rustic on purpose; the bottom-of-the-bowl bread is the prize.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Uses up the bread bin and the softest tomatoes — provisioning arithmetic disguised as a composed salad. Holds 2 hours on a buffet, improving for the first one.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: skip halloumi, add 480 g white beans and extra capers. Swap peaches for strawberries in June, figs in September.
