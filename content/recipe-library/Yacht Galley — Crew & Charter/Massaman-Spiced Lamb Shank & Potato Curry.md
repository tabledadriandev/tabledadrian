# Massaman-Spiced Lamb Shank & Potato Curry

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Braised-shank gravitas meets massaman warmth — a special-occasion crew dinner that the oven cooks while you run a beach setup.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Lamb shanks — 8 (3.2 kg)
- Massaman paste: dried red chiles 20 g, shallots 100 g, garlic 30 g, galangal 25 g, lemongrass 50 g, coriander seed 8 g, cumin 5 g, cardamom 3 g, cinnamon 3 g, cloves 1 g, shrimp paste 10 g
- Coconut milk — 1.2 l
- Fish sauce — 50 ml
- Palm sugar — 50 g
- Tamarind paste — 40 g
- Waxy potatoes, halved — 900 g
- Onions, wedges — 300 g
- Roasted peanuts — 100 g
- Neutral oil — 50 ml
- Makrut lime leaves — 4
- Jasmine rice — 640 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Toast whole spices; soak chiles 15 minutes; blitz all paste ingredients with 30 ml water until fine.
2. Brown shanks hard in oil; set aside.
3. Crack 200 ml coconut cream until split; fry paste 8 minutes until dark and fragrant.
4. Add remaining coconut milk, fish sauce, palm sugar, tamarind, lime leaves; return shanks.
5. Braise lidded at 150 °C fan for 2 hours; add potatoes, onions and half the peanuts; braise uncovered 45–60 minutes more until the meat waves off the bone.
6. Skim, season; rest 15 minutes; remaining peanuts over.

## Plating & Service
One shank per bowl over rice, potatoes tucked in, sauce generous, peanuts last.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Day-ahead dish — reheats whole at 140 °C in its pot and tastes deeper for it. Paste freezes in cubes; shanks swap to beef short rib without changing a number.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: omit peanuts, finish with crispy shallots. Chicken-leg version braises in 75 min. Heat lives in the chile count.
