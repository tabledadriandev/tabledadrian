# Everything-Bagel Seeded Crackers with Whipped Feta Dip

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Cracker sheets snapped like slate plus a two-minute whipped feta — the apéro that pretends a bakery and a mezze bar collided on deck.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Plain flour — 250 g
- Wholemeal flour — 100 g
- Olive oil — 60 ml
- Water — 140 ml
- Fine sea salt — 6 g
- Sesame seeds — 25 g
- Poppy seeds — 15 g
- Dried onion flakes — 12 g
- Dried garlic flakes — 10 g
- Flaky sea salt — 5 g
- Feta — 300 g
- Greek yogurt — 120 g
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Honey — 15 g
- Olive oil (dip) — 25 ml

## Method
1. Mix flours, fine salt, 60 ml oil and water to a firm dough; rest 15 minutes.
2. Combine sesame, poppy, onion, garlic and flaky salt — the everything mix.
3. Roll dough between parchment to 2 mm (thinner = crisper); brush with water, carpet with the seed mix, roll once to embed.
4. Score into rough shards; dock with a fork.
5. Bake at 180 °C fan for 20–25 minutes until deep gold; cool completely on the tray.
6. Dip: blitz feta, yogurt, lemon and honey until whipped; ripple with oil.

## Plating & Service
Cracker shards standing in a jar, dip swooshed in a shallow bowl with an oil moat.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Crackers keep 2 weeks airtight — bake on a cool morning, deploy for days. The dough rolls out on any flat surface; no proving, no yeast, no drama.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan dip: whipped silken tofu + white miso. GF crackers: 1:1 blend + 20 ml extra oil. The everything mix upgrades focaccia and eggs too.
