# Eggs Royale with Brown-Butter Hollandaise & Dill

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The benchmark charter breakfast — brown butter takes the hollandaise from hotel-brunch to EMP territory.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Eggs — 4, plus 3 yolks (60 g)
- Smoked salmon — 160 g
- English muffins — 2 (140 g)
- Unsalted butter — 200 g
- White wine vinegar — 30 ml
- Shallot, minced — 20 g
- Black peppercorns — 2 g
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Dill fronds — 6 g
- White vinegar (for poaching) — 30 ml
- Fine sea salt — 3 g
- Cayenne — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Brown the butter over medium heat to 150 °C until nut-fragrant with amber solids; keep warm at 50–60 °C.
2. Reduce vinegar, shallot and peppercorns to 15 ml; strain.
3. Whisk yolks with the reduction over a bain-marie to 65 °C — thick ribbon stage, about 4 minutes.
4. Off heat, drizzle in brown butter whisking constantly; season with lemon, salt, cayenne. Hold at 50 °C max 30 minutes.
5. Poach eggs in barely simmering water (92 °C) with vinegar, 3 minutes; drain on cloth.
6. Toast split muffins at 200 °C for 4 minutes; layer salmon, egg, and nappe with hollandaise. Finish with dill.

## Plating & Service
One muffin half per plate, sauce just cloaking the egg; a blade of dill and a single grind of pepper.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Poach eggs to 2 minutes the night before, shock, hold in iced water; re-warm 1 minute at service — 12 covers become easy. Hollandaise is a flat-calm job; in a swell switch to soft-scrambled.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap salmon for wilted spinach and confit tomato (vegetarian Florentine). GF: serve on rösti instead of muffin.
