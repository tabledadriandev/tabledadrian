# Warm Chocolate & Olive Oil Fondant Puddings

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Frozen-ready fondants with a molten heart — bake from the freezer in 16 minutes whenever the table demands a finale.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Dark chocolate (70%) — 250 g
- Unsalted butter — 180 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 30 ml
- Eggs — 4 (240 g)
- Egg yolks — 4 (80 g)
- Caster sugar — 160 g
- Plain flour — 90 g
- Cocoa powder — 15 g
- Fine sea salt — 3 g
- Butter + cocoa for moulds — 30 g + 15 g
- Crème fraîche — 160 g
- Flaky sea salt — 1 g

## Method
1. Butter 8 dariole moulds twice; dust with cocoa; freeze the moulds.
2. Melt chocolate and butter to 45 °C; whisk in olive oil.
3. Whisk eggs, yolks and sugar to a thick ribbon, 4 minutes.
4. Fold chocolate into the ribbon; then flour, cocoa, salt — minimum strokes.
5. Fill moulds to 1 cm below the rim; chill 1 hour (or freeze up to a month).
6. Bake at 200 °C fan: 12–14 minutes from chilled, 15–16 from frozen — risen, edges set, centre with a 3 cm wobble. Rest 60 seconds; turn out.

## Plating & Service
Unmould onto warm plates, crème fraîche quenelle, three flakes of salt — break it open at the table for the lava shot.

> [!note] Galley notes
> The freezer batch is the entire strategy: dessert for 2 or 8 on 16 minutes' notice, all season. Test-bake one on a calm day to calibrate your oven — then trust the number.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: 1:1 blend or 60 g almond flour. Praline heart: push a square of gianduja into each before baking. Serve with basil ice cream if the freezer allows.
