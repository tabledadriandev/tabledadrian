# Duck Breast à l'Orange with Chicory & Potato Fondant

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Cold-pan rendered duck, a bitter-orange gastrique and fondants basted to mahogany — the crew's anniversary-night showpiece.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Duck breasts — 6 × 200 g
- Fine sea salt — 12 g
- Oranges — 4 (juice 300 ml, zest 6 g)
- Caster sugar — 60 g
- Sherry vinegar — 40 ml
- Chicken stock — 300 ml
- Grand Marnier — 20 ml
- Unsalted butter — 140 g
- Large waxy potatoes — 900 g
- Thyme — 4 g
- Garlic, crushed — 15 g
- Chicory heads, halved — 3 (350 g)
- Honey — 15 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Score duck skin in a tight crosshatch; salt 1 hour ahead.
2. Fondants: cut potatoes into 5 cm cylinders; brown both faces in 80 g butter; add stock halfway up, thyme, garlic; oven at 180 °C for 30 minutes until a knife drops through.
3. Gastrique: dry caramel from sugar to amber; add vinegar (careful), orange juice and zest; reduce by half; add stock, reduce to nappe; finish with Grand Marnier and 30 g cold butter.
4. Ducks skin-down in a cold pan; render over medium 8–10 minutes until the skin is glass; flip 2 minutes; rest to 54 °C core.
5. Caramelise chicory cut-side down in 30 g butter with honey, 4 minutes; season.
6. Slice duck; sauce the plate, never the skin.

## Plating & Service
Three slices per plate, fondant, chicory half, gastrique in a crescent; zest threads on the duck.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Sauce and fondants hold; ducks are a 12-minute à-la-minute finish — plan it for a flat anchorage evening. Render fat into a jar: tomorrow's roast potatoes thank you.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap Grand Marnier for orange juice reduction (alcohol-free). Chicory too bitter for the table? Roasted carrots with the same honey butter.
