# Thermos Ramen with Miso-Sesame Broth & Soft Egg

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Night-watch ramen engineered for a flask: broth in the thermos, noodles and garnish in a tub, assembled in the cockpit in 90 seconds.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Chicken stock — 1.8 l
- White miso — 90 g
- Tahini or neri goma — 50 g
- Soy sauce — 40 ml
- Mirin — 30 ml
- Garlic, grated — 12 g
- Ginger, grated — 20 g
- Toasted sesame oil — 15 ml
- Ramen noodles — 540 g
- Eggs — 6
- Spring onions — 60 g
- Sweetcorn — 200 g
- Nori sheets — 3
- Chile oil — 20 ml
- Cooked chicken or pork, shredded — 400 g

## Method
1. Simmer stock with garlic and ginger 10 minutes.
2. Whisk miso, tahini, soy and mirin with a ladle of hot stock; return to the pot — do not boil after miso goes in (hold ≤90 °C).
3. Boil eggs 6 minutes 30 seconds; shock, peel, halve.
4. Cook noodles 1 minute under packet time; rinse briefly, toss with sesame oil so they hold without clumping.
5. Pack: broth into preheated flasks; noodles, meat, corn, spring onion into tubs; eggs and nori separate.
6. To serve: noodles and garnish into a bowl (or the tub), broth over, egg and nori on top, chile oil to taste.

## Plating & Service
For table service, assemble in deep bowls with the egg halves face-up and nori standing.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Broth holds 4 days and scales by the litre; the flask system means hot food on deck with zero open pans in a seaway. Undercooked noodles finish in the hot broth — never pack them soft.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: mushroom-kombu stock, tofu instead of chicken/egg. GF: rice noodles + tamari.
