# Sticky Tamarind Pork Meatballs with Coconut Rice & Pickles

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Meatballs glazed like caramel shrimp — batchable, freezable, and the pickles do the heavy lifting against the heat of a July galley.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Pork mince — 1.5 kg
- Shallots, minced — 80 g
- Garlic — 25 g
- Lemongrass, minced — 40 g
- Fish sauce — 50 ml
- Egg — 1 (60 g)
- Panko — 80 g
- White pepper — 2 g
- Tamarind paste — 50 g
- Palm or brown sugar — 90 g
- Soy sauce — 40 ml
- Lime juice — 30 ml
- Water — 150 ml
- Jasmine rice — 700 g
- Coconut milk — 300 ml
- Carrot & daikon (or cucumber), julienned — 400 g
- Rice vinegar — 100 ml
- Caster sugar — 40 g
- Coriander & mint — 30 g
- Neutral oil — 40 ml
- Fine sea salt — 10 g

## Method
1. Pickles: dissolve caster sugar and 8 g salt in vinegar with 100 ml water; add julienne; 30 minutes minimum.
2. Mix pork with shallot, garlic, lemongrass, fish sauce, egg, panko, white pepper; roll 40 g balls; chill 20 minutes.
3. Rice: cook jasmine with coconut milk + 500 ml water and 2 g salt, 12 minutes, rest 10.
4. Brown meatballs in oil in batches over medium-high, 6 minutes, until 70 °C core.
5. Glaze: simmer tamarind, palm sugar, soy, water 3 minutes; add meatballs, toss 3–4 minutes to a lacquer; finish with lime.
6. Serve over coconut rice with drained pickles and herbs.

## Plating & Service
Five balls per bowl, glaze dripping, pickles bright on top, herbs torn at the last moment.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Meatballs freeze raw or cooked; glaze keeps 2 weeks. On a rolling night, tuck balls + pickle + herbs into baguettes — instant bánh-mì handhelds, zero cutlery.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken or plant-mince swap 1:1 (add 20 g extra panko). GF: tamari + GF crumbs.
