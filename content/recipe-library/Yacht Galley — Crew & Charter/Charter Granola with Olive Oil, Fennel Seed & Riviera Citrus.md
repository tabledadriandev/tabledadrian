# Charter Granola with Olive Oil, Fennel Seed & Riviera Citrus

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> One tray, three weeks of guest breakfasts — the fennel-citrus profile says 'yacht in Antibes', not hotel buffet.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Rolled oats — 500 g
- Almonds, roughly chopped — 150 g
- Pistachios — 100 g
- Pumpkin seeds — 80 g
- Sesame seeds — 40 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml
- Maple syrup — 140 ml
- Orange zest — 8 g
- Lemon zest — 4 g
- Fennel seeds, cracked — 6 g
- Dried apricots, sliced — 150 g
- Candied orange peel — 60 g
- Fine sea salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 150 °C fan.
2. Toss oats, nuts and seeds with olive oil, maple, zests, fennel seed and salt.
3. Spread 1.5 cm deep on two lined trays; press down lightly for clusters.
4. Bake 30–35 minutes, rotating once, stirring edges to centre at 20 minutes, until deep gold.
5. Cool completely on the tray without stirring — clusters set as they cool.
6. Fold in apricots and candied peel; store airtight.

## Plating & Service
Serve in a glass jar with yogurt, cold milk and fresh figs on the breakfast buffet.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Keeps 3 weeks airtight — bake on turnaround day and breakfast is 60% done for the whole charter. No morning oven competition.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: certified GF oats. Nut-free: swap nuts for sunflower seeds and coconut flakes. Lower sugar: cut maple to 90 ml and add 5 g vanilla.
