# Confit Duck Leg Cassoulet Rapide with Garlic Crumb

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Tinned confit and good beans collapse a three-day cassoulet into one evening — southwest France from the provisioning locker.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Confit duck legs (tinned/vac) — 8 (1.6 kg)
- Toulouse or garlic sausages — 400 g
- Smoked bacon lardons — 200 g
- Duck fat (from the confit) — 40 g
- Onion, diced — 250 g
- Carrot, diced — 200 g
- Celery, diced — 100 g
- Garlic — 30 g
- White beans, cooked — 1.2 kg
- Chopped tomatoes — 400 g
- White wine — 150 ml
- Chicken stock — 600 ml
- Thyme — 5 g
- Bay leaves — 2
- Panko or coarse breadcrumbs — 120 g
- Parsley — 15 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Brown sausages in duck fat; slice thick. Render lardons.
2. Sweat onion, carrot, celery 10 minutes; add two-thirds of the garlic, 2 minutes.
3. Deglaze with wine; add beans, tomatoes, stock, thyme, bay, lardons, sausage; simmer 15 minutes and season.
4. Transfer to a deep dish; nestle in duck legs skin-up.
5. Bake at 180 °C fan for 40 minutes until the skin crisps and the beans blip.
6. Toss crumbs with remaining garlic, parsley and 20 g duck fat; scatter on and bake 10 minutes more to a golden crust.

## Plating & Service
Break the crust at the table; one leg per person over a trench of beans.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Confit tins keep a year — this is the celebration dinner you can always produce mid-crossing. Assembles to step 4 a day ahead; the oven does the rest while you plate starters.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: GF crumbs or none — the beans crust naturally. Lighter: chicken legs roasted separately, half the lardons.
