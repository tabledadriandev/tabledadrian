# Freezer-Stash Chicken, Leek & Tarragon Pot Pie Filling

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A velouté-bound pie filling banked in the freezer by the kilo — mid-season insurance that becomes dinner under any lid you like.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Chicken thighs, boneless, 3 cm — 1.8 kg
- Leeks, sliced and washed — 500 g
- Celery, sliced — 150 g
- Button mushrooms, quartered — 300 g
- Unsalted butter — 90 g
- Plain flour — 70 g
- White wine — 150 ml
- Chicken stock — 900 ml
- Double cream — 200 ml
- Dijon mustard — 20 g
- Tarragon, chopped — 12 g
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Frozen peas — 250 g
- Fine sea salt — 14 g
- Black pepper — 3 g

## Method
1. Brown chicken in 30 g butter in batches; set aside.
2. Soften leeks, celery and mushrooms in remaining butter, 8 minutes.
3. Stir in flour, 2 minutes; deglaze with wine; whisk in stock gradually to a smooth velouté.
4. Return chicken; simmer 15 minutes until tender and the sauce coats.
5. Finish with cream, mustard, tarragon, lemon, peas; season; cool fast in shallow trays.
6. To serve: fill dishes, lid with puff or mash, bake at 200 °C fan for 25 minutes (40 from frozen filling).

## Plating & Service
Individual pots with burnished lids, or one crew trough with mash and buttered greens.

> [!note] Galley notes
> This IS the freezer plan: 3 kg of filling in flat bags = three future dinners with zero thinking. Also serves lidless over rice on rough nights — no oven required.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: cornflour velouté, mash lid. Dairy-free: olive oil roux, oat cream. Swap chicken for turkey or rabbit if provisioning surprises you.
