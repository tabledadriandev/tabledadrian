# Congee of the Night Watch with Ginger, Spring Onion & Crispy Shallots

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The gentlest food on a rough sea — a thermos-friendly rice porridge that settles stomachs at 04:00 and converts skeptics by 04:10.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Jasmine rice — 250 g
- Chicken stock — 2.2 l
- Fresh ginger, julienned — 30 g
- Garlic, smashed — 10 g
- Spring onions, sliced — 60 g
- Eggs — 8
- Light soy sauce (GF tamari) — 40 ml
- Toasted sesame oil — 15 ml
- Crispy fried shallots — 50 g
- White pepper — 2 g
- Coriander — 10 g
- Fine sea salt — 4 g

## Method
1. Rinse rice until water runs nearly clear; freeze 1 hour (fractures the grain, halves cooking time) or skip if planning ahead.
2. Bring stock to 95 °C with ginger and garlic; add rice.
3. Simmer very gently 60–90 minutes, stirring every 15 minutes, until the grains burst into a silky porridge — adjust with water to a pourable nappe.
4. Season with tamari, salt and white pepper.
5. Soft-boil eggs 6 minutes 30 seconds; shock and peel.
6. Serve bowls topped with halved egg, spring onion, coriander, crispy shallots and a few drops of sesame oil.

## Plating & Service
Deep bowls or wide-mouth thermos flasks; toppings in ramekins so each watch dresses their own.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Made in one pot and holds at 80 °C for hours — the definitive rough-weather breakfast. Thin with hot stock as it sits; scale to 12 with 350 g rice and 3 l stock.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: mushroom stock, top with fried tofu instead of egg. Luxury version: fold in 200 g picked crab at service.
