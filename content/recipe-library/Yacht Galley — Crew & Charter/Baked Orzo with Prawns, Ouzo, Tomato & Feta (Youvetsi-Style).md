# Baked Orzo with Prawns, Ouzo, Tomato & Feta (Youvetsi-Style)

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> One deep tray, no stirring: orzo drinks the ouzo-tomato broth while the prawns roast on top — Aegean saganaki logic for eight.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Orzo — 500 g
- Raw prawns, peeled, tail-on — 700 g
- Olive oil — 70 ml
- Onion, diced — 200 g
- Fennel, diced — 200 g
- Garlic — 20 g
- Chile flakes — 2 g
- Ouzo — 50 ml
- Chopped tomatoes — 800 g
- Vegetable or fish stock — 900 ml
- Dried oregano — 4 g
- Feta — 200 g
- Lemon zest — 4 g
- Dill — 15 g
- Fine sea salt — 10 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 200 °C fan.
2. Sweat onion and fennel in 50 ml oil 8 minutes; add garlic and chile, 1 minute; deglaze with ouzo, reduce out.
3. Stir in orzo to coat, then tomatoes, hot stock, oregano and salt; transfer to a deep tray.
4. Bake uncovered 20 minutes until orzo is nearly tender and saucy.
5. Toss prawns with 20 ml oil and lemon zest; seat on top with crumbled feta.
6. Bake 7–8 minutes until prawns are just pink (55 °C core); rest 5, shower with dill.

## Plating & Service
Tray to table; the orzo should be loose like a risotto, tightening as it lands.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Base assembles ahead to step 3 and bakes when the crew sits — prawns go on at the last minute so they never rubberise on the reheat. Feeds 12 with 700 g orzo and 1.2 l stock.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: white beans instead of prawns, skip feta or almond feta. Swap ouzo for white wine if the bar is locked.
