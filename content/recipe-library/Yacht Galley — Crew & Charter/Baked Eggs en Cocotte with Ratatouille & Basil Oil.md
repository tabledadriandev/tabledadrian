# Baked Eggs en Cocotte with Ratatouille & Basil Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Yesterday's ratatouille becomes today's guest breakfast — six ramekins in a bain-marie, zero pan-watching during service.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Aubergine, 1 cm dice — 200 g
- Courgette, 1 cm dice — 200 g
- Red pepper, 1 cm dice — 150 g
- Onion, diced — 100 g
- Garlic, sliced — 10 g
- Olive oil — 60 ml
- Chopped tomatoes — 400 g
- Thyme — 3 g
- Eggs — 6
- Double cream — 60 ml
- Basil — 20 g
- Parmesan, grated — 40 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Sauté aubergine in 30 ml oil over high heat 5 minutes; add onion, pepper, courgette and garlic, 5 minutes more.
2. Add tomatoes, thyme and salt; simmer 10 minutes to a thick stew. (Or use 700 g leftover ratatouille.)
3. Blitz basil with 30 ml oil and a pinch of salt; strain for a bright green oil.
4. Heat oven to 180 °C. Divide ratatouille between 6 buttered ramekins; crack an egg into each, spoon 10 ml cream over each yolk, dust with Parmesan.
5. Bake in a bain-marie (water halfway up) 12–14 minutes until whites set, yolks tremble.
6. Rest 2 minutes; dress with basil oil.

## Plating & Service
Ramekin on a saucer with grilled baguette soldiers and the basil oil pooling at the rim.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Ratatouille batches 3× and freezes — cocottes assemble in 5 minutes for any cover count. Bain-marie keeps things level and forgiving underway.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: swap egg for 60 g white beans per pot and cream for coconut cream. Add 30 g goat's cheese under the egg for richer service.
