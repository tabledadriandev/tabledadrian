# Grilled Lamb Chop 'Scottadito' Platter with Salsa Verde & White Beans

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> 'Burn-your-fingers' chops eaten straight off the platter — a beach barbecue that feels like Rome, backed by creamy beans doing the quiet work.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Lamb cutlets, frenched — 16 (1.6 kg)
- Olive oil — 100 ml
- Garlic — 20 g
- Rosemary — 8 g
- Lemon zest — 4 g
- Fine sea salt — 14 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 60 g
- Mint — 20 g
- Capers — 30 g
- Anchovy fillets — 20 g
- Dijon mustard — 10 g
- Red wine vinegar — 25 ml
- Cannellini beans, cooked — 720 g
- Sage — 4 g
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Black pepper — 3 g

## Method
1. Marinate cutlets in 40 ml oil, half the garlic, rosemary, lemon zest and salt — 1 to 12 hours.
2. Salsa verde: chop parsley, mint, capers, anchovy fine; whisk with mustard, vinegar and 40 ml oil to a spoonable sauce.
3. Warm beans in 20 ml oil with remaining garlic and sage; crush a quarter of them for creaminess; season with lemon juice and salt.
4. Grill cutlets over fierce coals or a 260 °C plancha, 2–3 minutes per side to 54 °C — hard char, pink centre.
5. Rest 3 minutes on the bean platter so the juices season the beans.
6. Spoon salsa verde over everything.

## Plating & Service
Beans as the bed, chops fanned bone-up for grabbing, salsa verde generous — no plates required, napkins essential.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Marinade and salsa verde hold 3 days; beans reheat in one pan. The cook window is 6 minutes — the ideal 'guests just swam back' protein.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan platter: same beans + salsa verde under thick grilled courgette and aubergine. Swap cutlets for pork chops (4 min/side, 62 °C).
