# Coconut Chickpea & Spinach Curry for the Long Haul

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Locker-stable, allergy-flexible and beloved — the curry that covers vegans, dairy-free guests and hungry deckhands in one pot.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Chickpeas, cooked — 960 g
- Coconut milk — 800 ml
- Onions, sliced — 400 g
- Garlic — 25 g
- Ginger — 30 g
- Neutral oil — 50 ml
- Black mustard seeds — 5 g
- Cumin seeds — 5 g
- Ground turmeric — 5 g
- Ground coriander — 8 g
- Garam masala — 8 g
- Chopped tomatoes — 400 g
- Frozen spinach — 450 g
- Tamarind paste — 15 g
- Caster sugar — 8 g
- Fine sea salt — 12 g
- Basmati rice — 700 g

## Method
1. Pop mustard and cumin seeds in hot oil 30 seconds; add onions, cook 10 minutes to gold.
2. Add garlic, ginger, turmeric, coriander; fry 2 minutes.
3. Add tomatoes; cook 5 minutes until the oil splits out.
4. Add chickpeas, coconut milk, salt and sugar; simmer 15 minutes.
5. Fold in spinach until wilted through (3 minutes from frozen); finish with tamarind and garam masala.
6. Serve over basmati cooked by absorption.

## Plating & Service
Big pot, rice alongside, lime wedges and yogurt (or coconut yogurt) for the table.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Every component survives a month without a market — the crossing workhorse. Freezes 3 months, feeds 10 for pocket change, and thickens into a wrap filling on day three.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 600 g roast squash or 700 g prawns (3-min simmer) for variety. Low-FODMAP: skip onion/garlic, use garlic-infused oil and top with chives.
