# Smoked Haddock Kedgeree with Soft-Boiled Eggs & Curry Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The great maritime breakfast — one pot of spiced rice that feeds guests at 08:00 and crew at 08:30 from the same pan.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Smoked haddock (undyed) — 600 g
- Basmati rice — 400 g
- Whole milk — 400 ml
- Bay leaves — 2
- Unsalted butter — 80 g
- Onion, finely sliced — 200 g
- Madras curry powder — 12 g
- Mustard seeds — 4 g
- Turmeric — 3 g
- Eggs — 6
- Flat-leaf parsley — 15 g
- Coriander — 10 g
- Lemon — 1 (juice 30 ml, zest 3 g)
- Fine sea salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Poach haddock in milk with bay at 80 °C for 8 minutes; lift out, flake, reserve the milk.
2. Boil eggs 6 minutes 30 seconds from 100 °C; shock, peel, halve — yolks should be fudgy.
3. Rinse rice; cook by absorption with 550 ml water and 50 ml poaching milk, 12 minutes, rest 10.
4. Fry onion in butter 8 minutes to deep gold; add curry powder, mustard seeds and turmeric, cook 1 minute.
5. Fold rice and flaked haddock through the curry butter over low heat until steaming (75 °C).
6. Finish with herbs, lemon juice and zest; seat the egg halves on top.

## Plating & Service
A big copper pan to the table; mango chutney and extra lemon alongside.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Everything but the eggs reheats beautifully — cook once, serve two sittings. Holds in one pot in a swell; scale to 12 with 600 g rice and 900 g fish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap haddock for hot-smoked salmon or mackerel from provisioning. Dairy-free: poach fish in water, use olive oil instead of butter.
