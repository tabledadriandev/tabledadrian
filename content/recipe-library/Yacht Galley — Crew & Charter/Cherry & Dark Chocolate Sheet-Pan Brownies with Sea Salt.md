# Cherry & Dark Chocolate Sheet-Pan Brownies with Sea Salt

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The crew tin's crown jewels — fudgy squares studded with cherries that survive four days at sea and taste better chilled from the fridge.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Dark chocolate (70%) — 300 g
- Unsalted butter — 250 g
- Caster sugar — 200 g
- Light brown sugar — 150 g
- Eggs — 4 (240 g)
- Vanilla paste — 6 g
- Plain flour — 140 g
- Cocoa powder — 40 g
- Fine sea salt — 4 g
- Dark cherries, pitted (fresh, frozen or jarred, drained) — 300 g
- Dark chocolate chunks — 100 g
- Flaky sea salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 170 °C fan; line a 30 × 22 cm tin.
2. Melt 300 g chocolate with butter to 45 °C.
3. Whisk eggs with both sugars and vanilla 3 full minutes to a pale ribbon — this builds the shiny crust.
4. Fold in the chocolate, then flour, cocoa and fine salt in minimum strokes.
5. Fold in cherries and chocolate chunks; spread; flaky salt over.
6. Bake 26–30 minutes — a skewer should come out with fudgy crumbs, never clean. Cool completely in the tin before cutting into 12.

## Plating & Service
Squares in the crew tin; for guests, warm 10 seconds and plate with crème fraîche and a cherry.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Keeps 5 days tinned, 2 months frozen (better texture from frozen than most things fresh). One bake covers crew dessert, watch snacks and an emergency guest plate.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: 1:1 blend + 10 min rest before baking. Swap cherries for raspberries or candied orange. Add 4 g espresso powder to deepen without tasting of coffee.
