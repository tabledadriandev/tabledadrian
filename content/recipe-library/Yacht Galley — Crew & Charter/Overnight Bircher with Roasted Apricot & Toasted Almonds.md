# Overnight Bircher with Roasted Apricot & Toasted Almonds

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Zero-morning-labour crew and guest breakfast: assembled at midnight after service, perfect at 07:00 before the first swim.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Rolled oats — 320 g
- Whole milk — 500 ml
- Natural yogurt — 250 g
- Apple, coarsely grated — 200 g
- Apricots, halved — 400 g
- Honey — 40 g
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Flaked almonds — 60 g
- Chia seeds — 30 g
- Vanilla paste — 5 g
- Fine sea salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Roast apricot halves with 20 g honey at 190 °C for 12–15 minutes until caramelised at the edges; cool.
2. Toast almonds at 170 °C for 6 minutes until pale gold.
3. Mix oats, milk, yogurt, grated apple, chia, vanilla, remaining honey, lemon juice and salt.
4. Refrigerate at least 6 hours (overnight) at 3 °C; loosen with a splash of milk if thick.
5. Layer bircher into glasses, top with roasted apricots and almonds.

## Plating & Service
Serve chilled in glass tumblers or coupes with the apricots glistening on top; drizzle with roasting juices.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Triple the base for 12 crew + guests and hold 3 days chilled; it travels in any sea state — spoonable, no spills, no morning pans.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: oat milk and coconut yogurt, maple instead of honey. GF: certified GF oats. Swap apricots for roasted plums in late season.
