# Provençal Pan Bagnat for the Tender Ride

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Nice's pressed tuna sandwich — engineered to be made hours ahead, weighted, and eaten with wet hands on a moving tender.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Round crusty rolls or small boules — 8 (960 g)
- Tinned tuna in olive oil — 480 g
- Eggs — 6
- Tomatoes, sliced — 400 g
- Cucumber, thin rounds — 250 g
- Red onion, shaved — 100 g
- Black olives (Niçoise), pitted — 120 g
- Anchovy fillets — 30 g
- Basil leaves — 20 g
- Red wine vinegar — 30 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml
- Dijon mustard — 12 g
- Garlic — 6 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Boil eggs 9 minutes; shock, peel, slice.
2. Whisk vinegar, mustard, garlic, salt and oil to a sharp vinaigrette.
3. Split rolls; pull out a third of the crumb. Brush both faces generously with vinaigrette.
4. Layer: tomato (salted), tuna, egg, cucumber, onion, olives, anchovy, basil; more vinaigrette.
5. Lid, wrap each tightly in parchment then film; weight under a tray with tins for 1–4 hours in the fridge.
6. Halve through the wrap just before handing out.

## Plating & Service
Served in the paper, cut face up — the layers pressed into a terrine-like cross-section.

> [!note] Galley notes
> The pressing IS the recipe: built at breakfast, perfect at the noon beach drop, still good at 15:00. No mayo, no wilt, no cold chain drama on the tender.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: chickpeas mashed with capers and the same vinaigrette instead of tuna/egg/anchovy. GF: sturdy GF rolls, pressed more gently.
