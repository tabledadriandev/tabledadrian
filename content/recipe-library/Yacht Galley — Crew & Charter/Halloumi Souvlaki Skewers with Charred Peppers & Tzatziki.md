# Halloumi Souvlaki Skewers with Charred Peppers & Tzatziki

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The vegetarian skewer that holds the grill next to the lamb without apology — halloumi chars, never melts, never falls in the coals.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Halloumi, 3 cm cubes — 800 g
- Red & yellow peppers, 3 cm — 500 g
- Red onion, petals — 250 g
- Courgette, thick half-moons — 300 g
- Olive oil — 80 ml
- Lemon juice — 50 ml
- Dried oregano — 8 g
- Honey — 20 g
- Smoked paprika — 4 g
- Greek yogurt — 400 g
- Cucumber, grated, squeezed — 250 g
- Garlic, grated — 8 g
- Mint — 10 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g
- Wooden skewers, soaked — 16

## Method
1. Tzatziki: fold squeezed cucumber, garlic, mint, 20 ml oil and a pinch of salt into the yogurt; chill.
2. Marinade: whisk remaining oil, lemon, oregano, honey, paprika, salt.
3. Thread skewers alternating halloumi, peppers, onion, courgette; brush heavily with marinade.
4. Grill over hot coals or a 250 °C plancha, 2–3 minutes per side, until the cheese is bar-marked and the vegetables blistered.
5. Rebrush with marinade off the grill.
6. Serve over the tzatziki, remaining marinade spooned across.

## Plating & Service
Skewers stacked log-cabin style over a tzatziki-swooshed platter; lemon and warm pitas alongside.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Skewers build in the morning and travel flat in trays to the beach; the marinade doubles as the finishing sauce. Nothing raw = no cooler-chain anxiety.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: extra-firm tofu pressed 30 min, same marinade. Add lamb cubes on parallel skewers — same marinade, 8 min cook.
