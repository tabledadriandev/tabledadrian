# Heatproof Freekeh Tabbouleh with Pomegranate & Preserved Lemon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A tabbouleh built to survive four hours on a beach table — sturdy grains and a dressing that gets better as it warms.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Cracked freekeh — 400 g
- Vegetable stock — 900 ml
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 120 g
- Mint, chopped — 30 g
- Spring onions — 60 g
- Cucumber, seeded, diced — 300 g
- Cherry tomatoes, quartered — 400 g
- Pomegranate seeds — 150 g
- Preserved lemon rind, minced — 25 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml
- Lemon juice — 60 ml
- Pomegranate molasses — 20 ml
- Ground allspice — 2 g
- Fine sea salt — 10 g

## Method
1. Simmer freekeh in stock 18–20 minutes until tender with bite; drain, spread on a tray to cool fast.
2. Whisk oil, lemon juice, molasses, preserved lemon, allspice, salt.
3. Salt the cucumber lightly, drain 10 minutes.
4. Fold freekeh with half the dressing while barely warm — grains drink it in.
5. Just before service, fold in herbs, vegetables, pomegranate and remaining dressing.

## Plating & Service
A wide, shallow platter so the jewels show; extra molasses zig-zagged over.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Dressed grains hold 2 days; herbs and tomatoes go in last so nothing slimes in the heat. The salad that carries every BBQ, buffet and beach drop of the season.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: swap freekeh for quinoa (12 min). Add 200 g feta for a fuller lunch. Winter: roasted squash instead of tomato.
