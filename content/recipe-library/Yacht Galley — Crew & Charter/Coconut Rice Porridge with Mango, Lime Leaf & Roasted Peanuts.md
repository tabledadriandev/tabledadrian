# Coconut Rice Porridge with Mango, Lime Leaf & Roasted Peanuts

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sticky-rice-and-mango logic rebuilt as a breakfast porridge — fragrant, dairy-free, and calm in any sea.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Jasmine rice — 300 g
- Coconut milk — 800 ml
- Water — 500 ml
- Palm sugar or light brown sugar — 60 g
- Makrut lime leaves — 4
- Fine sea salt — 4 g
- Ripe mangoes, sliced — 500 g
- Lime juice — 20 ml
- Lime zest — 3 g
- Roasted peanuts, crushed — 60 g
- Coconut flakes, toasted — 25 g

## Method
1. Rinse rice twice. Combine with water, 600 ml coconut milk, lime leaves and salt.
2. Simmer gently 25–30 minutes, stirring often, until the rice is swollen and the porridge creamy.
3. Dissolve in the sugar; remove lime leaves. Adjust with water to a loose ribbon.
4. Warm remaining 200 ml coconut milk with a pinch of salt for a pouring cream.
5. Bowl the porridge, fan mango over, dress with lime juice and zest, pour the coconut cream, finish with peanuts and coconut flakes.

## Plating & Service
The white porridge, gold mango and cream pour want a dark bowl; serve warm, not hot.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Reheats perfectly with a splash of water — make at 05:00, serve two sittings. No dairy to worry about in a warm galley; peanuts in a jar for allergy-flex service.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: toasted pumpkin seeds. Lower sugar: 30 g sugar and let the mango carry it. Swap mango for caramelised pineapple in winter provisioning.
