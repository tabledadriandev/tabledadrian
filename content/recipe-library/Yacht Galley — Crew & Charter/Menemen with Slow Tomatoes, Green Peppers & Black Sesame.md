# Menemen with Slow Tomatoes, Green Peppers & Black Sesame

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Softly-set Turkish scramble where the tomatoes do the work — a gentle, forgiving pan that scales from 2 to 12 without drama.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Ripe tomatoes, grated (skins discarded) — 600 g
- Green peppers (charleston or padrón type), sliced — 200 g
- Onion, finely diced — 120 g
- Olive oil — 45 ml
- Eggs — 9 (540 g)
- Aleppo pepper — 4 g
- Dried oregano — 2 g
- Black sesame seeds — 6 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 10 g
- Fine sea salt — 5 g
- Caster sugar — 3 g

## Method
1. Sweat onion and green pepper in olive oil over medium heat 6 minutes until soft.
2. Add grated tomato, salt, sugar and oregano; simmer 8–10 minutes until thick and jammy, oil pooling at the edges.
3. Reduce heat to low, pour in lightly beaten eggs.
4. Fold slowly, 3–4 minutes, to large barely-set curds — pull the pan off at 70 °C; carryover finishes it.
5. Scatter Aleppo pepper, parsley and black sesame.

## Plating & Service
Straight from the pan with warm simit or grilled bread; the texture should be spoonable, not sliceable.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Tomato base holds 3 days chilled — reheat and add eggs to order. Low, wide pan stays stable underway; a proper rough-weather egg dish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 80 g crumbled beyaz peynir or feta at the end. Vegan: fold in 400 g silken tofu instead of eggs, cook 2 minutes only.
