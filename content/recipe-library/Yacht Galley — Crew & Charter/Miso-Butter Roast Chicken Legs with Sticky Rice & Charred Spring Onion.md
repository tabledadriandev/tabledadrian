# Miso-Butter Roast Chicken Legs with Sticky Rice & Charred Spring Onion

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Four pantry ingredients turn roast chicken into something guests ask about — and the crew fights over the pan juices.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Chicken legs, whole — 8 (2.4 kg)
- White miso — 80 g
- Unsalted butter, soft — 80 g
- Honey — 30 g
- Soy sauce — 30 ml
- Garlic, grated — 15 g
- Ginger, grated — 20 g
- Rice vinegar — 20 ml
- Sushi rice — 640 g
- Spring onions — 150 g
- Toasted sesame seeds — 10 g
- Neutral oil — 15 ml
- Fine sea salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Beat miso, butter, honey, soy, garlic and ginger to a paste; loosen skin and massage half beneath, rest over.
2. Rest chicken uncovered in the fridge 2–12 hours to dry the skin.
3. Roast at 200 °C fan on a rack over a tray, 40–45 minutes to 85 °C internal, basting once at 30 minutes.
4. Rinse and cook sushi rice (1:1.1 water, 12 minutes, rest 10); season with vinegar and salt.
5. Char whole spring onions in oil on a 240 °C plancha 3 minutes; slice thickly.
6. Rest chicken 8 minutes; whisk resting juices into the tray drippings for a pan sauce.

## Plating & Service
Leg over a mound of rice, pan sauce spooned over, spring onions and sesame to finish.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Miso butter keeps 2 weeks and works on corn, salmon and cabbage — batch it. Legs are forgiving: 15 minutes late off the oven still eats perfectly. Scale to 12 on two racks, rotate at half time.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free: swap butter for 60 ml neutral oil. GF: tamari. Swap legs for a whole spatchcocked bird at 55 min.
