# Frozen Yogurt Bark with Berries, Honey & Toasted Seeds

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The freezer snack that answers 'something sweet but not dessert' at 15:00 in a 35 °C anchorage — snapped into shards straight from the tray.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Greek yogurt (full fat) — 800 g
- Honey — 80 g
- Vanilla paste — 5 g
- Lemon zest — 3 g
- Mixed berries — 300 g
- Pumpkin seeds, toasted — 40 g
- Sunflower seeds, toasted — 30 g
- Coconut flakes, toasted — 25 g
- Dark chocolate (70%), chopped — 60 g
- Flaky sea salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Whisk yogurt with 60 g honey, vanilla and lemon zest.
2. Spread 1 cm thick on a lined tray that fits your freezer flat.
3. Press in berries; scatter seeds, coconut and chocolate; drizzle remaining honey; a few salt flakes.
4. Freeze flat 4+ hours until solid.
5. Snap into rough shards; bag and return to the freezer.
6. Serve within 5 minutes of leaving the freezer.

## Plating & Service
Shards in a frosted bowl passed around the deck — napkins, not plates.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Keeps a month frozen in a bag flat against the freezer wall — near-zero volume. High-protein and colder than ice cream feels; the crew's official swim-break snack.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: thick coconut yogurt + maple. Mango + lime + chile flakes version for the heat-lovers. Skip chocolate before it's even a question — someone will ask for double.
