# Peruvian-Style Roast Chicken with Ají Verde & Charred Corn

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Smoke-dark marinade, a green sauce people drink, and corn off the plancha — deck dinner with serious personality.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Chicken, spatchcocked — 2 × 1.4 kg
- Soy-free marinade: smoked paprika 12 g, cumin 10 g, dried oregano 4 g, black pepper 3 g, garlic 30 g, red wine vinegar 40 ml, olive oil 50 ml, honey 20 g, fine sea salt 16 g
- Coriander — 80 g
- Jalapeños or green chiles — 50 g
- Mayonnaise — 150 g
- Lime juice — 40 ml
- Parmesan, grated — 40 g
- Garlic (sauce) — 8 g
- Corn cobs — 6 (1.2 kg)
- Unsalted butter — 50 g
- Lime wedges — 2 limes
- Fine sea salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Blitz marinade ingredients; massage into the spatchcocked birds; 4–24 hours at 4 °C.
2. Ají verde: blitz coriander, chiles, mayo, lime, Parmesan, garlic, salt until smooth and pale green; chill.
3. Roast birds skin-up on racks at 200 °C fan, 45–55 minutes to 72 °C thigh, tenting if darkening too fast.
4. Boil corn 4 minutes, then char on a 250 °C plancha or grill, rolling, 4 minutes; butter and salt.
5. Rest chicken 10 minutes; cut into eighths through the bone.
6. Pile over corn, ají verde in bowls, limes everywhere.

## Plating & Service
Butcher-block service on deck: chicken heaped, corn stacked, green sauce centre stage.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Marinade and sauce both live 5 days chilled — this is a plan-ahead flex with a 10-minute service window. Leftover ají verde upgrades eggs, fish and crew sandwiches all week.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Sauce vegan: plant mayo, skip Parmesan, add 10 g nutritional yeast. Swap chicken for thick cauliflower steaks (25 min) for the plant table.
