# Rough-Weather Beef & Barley Thermos Stew

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Built for a Force 7: a spoon-thick stew that pours into wide-mouth flasks and feeds the watch without a single plate leaving the galley.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Beef chuck, 3 cm dice — 1.5 kg
- Plain flour — 40 g
- Olive oil — 50 ml
- Onions, diced — 400 g
- Carrots, thick coins — 400 g
- Celery, sliced — 150 g
- Swede, 2 cm dice — 300 g
- Pearl barley — 200 g
- Tomato paste — 40 g
- Red wine — 200 ml
- Beef stock — 2 l
- Bay leaves — 3
- Thyme — 5 g
- Worcestershire sauce — 20 ml
- Fine sea salt — 12 g
- Black pepper — 3 g

## Method
1. Toss beef in seasoned flour; brown hard in oil in batches — deep crust, no grey meat.
2. Sweat onions, carrots, celery 8 minutes; add tomato paste, 2 minutes.
3. Deglaze with wine; return beef, add stock, bay, thyme.
4. Simmer lidded at a bare tremble (95 °C) for 90 minutes.
5. Add barley and swede; cook 40 minutes more until barley is plump and beef yields to a spoon.
6. Season with Worcestershire, salt, pepper; rest 15 minutes — it thickens as it stands.

## Plating & Service
Deep mugs or 500 ml thermos flasks with buttered baps wrapped in foil for the cockpit.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Holds 4 days chilled, freezes 3 months, and actually improves overnight. Barley keeps it spoonable at any angle of heel — the definitive rough-weather main. Pressure cooker: 35 minutes total.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: swap barley for 250 g potatoes and use GF flour. Swap beef for lamb neck + 5 g rosemary. Vegan: 800 g mushrooms + 480 g butter beans, mushroom stock.
