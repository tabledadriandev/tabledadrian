# Rosewater Rice Pudding Brûlée with Cardamom & Pistachio

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Stovetop nursery comfort finished with a cracking torched lid — rough-weather-friendly right up until the brûlée, which waits for calm.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Pudding or arborio rice — 220 g
- Whole milk — 1.2 l
- Double cream — 250 ml
- Caster sugar — 110 g
- Green cardamom pods, cracked — 8
- Vanilla paste — 4 g
- Rosewater — 8 ml
- Orange zest — 3 g
- Fine sea salt — 2 g
- Demerara sugar — 60 g
- Pistachios, chopped — 50 g
- Dried rose petals (optional) — 1 g

## Method
1. Rinse rice once. Combine with milk, cream, caster sugar, cardamom, vanilla, zest, salt.
2. Simmer at the barest blip, stirring every few minutes, 40–50 minutes until the rice is tender and the pudding thickly creamy — it loosens nothing as it cools, so stop slightly loose.
3. Remove cardamom pods; stir in rosewater off the heat — always off the heat, always less than you think.
4. Portion into 8 shallow ramekins; chill 2+ hours (or serve warm without the lid).
5. Brûlée: an even layer of demerara; torch to a glass crack (or 3 minutes under a 270 °C grill).
6. Rest 2 minutes; pistachios and rose petals on the glass.

## Plating & Service
Ramekin on a saucer — the spoon-crack is the service moment; garnish only after the sugar sets.

> [!note] Galley notes
> The pudding itself is one-pot, stir-and-forget, and holds 3 days chilled — brûlée only the portions you serve tonight. Warm, lidless, in mugs, it's also the night watch's favourite.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: oat milk + coconut cream, maple instead of caster in the pudding. Skip rosewater for orange blossom or plain vanilla. Nut-free: toasted coconut.
