# Steak Haché au Poivre with Pommes Purée for the Crew Table

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bistro steak-frites energy at burger cost — hand-formed haché, proper pepper sauce, and purée that would pass a Bocuse inspection.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Beef chuck mince (20% fat) — 1.4 kg
- Fine sea salt — 14 g
- Black peppercorns, cracked — 12 g
- Neutral oil — 30 ml
- Unsalted butter — 180 g
- Shallots, minced — 60 g
- Cognac — 50 ml
- Beef stock — 300 ml
- Double cream — 200 ml
- Green peppercorns in brine, drained — 25 g
- Dijon mustard — 10 g
- Floury potatoes — 1.6 kg
- Whole milk — 200 ml
- Chives — 10 g

## Method
1. Season mince with 10 g salt; form 8 × 175 g pucks, 2.5 cm thick, handling minimally; chill 30 minutes.
2. Steam or simmer potatoes until collapsing; pass through a ricer; beat in 120 g butter then hot milk to a glossy purée; season.
3. Press cracked black pepper onto both faces of each haché.
4. Sear in oil + 30 g butter over high heat, 3 minutes per side for rosé (55 °C core) — work in two pans.
5. Rest steaks; in one pan soften shallots, flame with Cognac, add stock, reduce by half.
6. Add cream, green peppercorns, Dijon; reduce to nappe, mount with 30 g butter, season.

## Plating & Service
Haché on a sweep of purée, sauce over, chives; a sharp green salad cuts the richness.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Pucks freeze raw between parchment — pull, thaw, sear; sauce base holds 3 days. Restaurant-night morale for 8 at a fraction of provisioning a côte de boeuf. Calm-day pan work.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Lighter: crème fraîche instead of double cream. Swap purée for baked jacket potatoes in rough weather (oven-stable).
