# Green Coast Smoothie Bowl with Bee Pollen & Coconut Crunch

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Charter-guest wellness bowl — thick enough to hold its topping architecture through a photo session on the aft deck.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Frozen banana — 400 g
- Frozen mango — 200 g
- Baby spinach — 60 g
- Avocado — 100 g
- Coconut milk — 200 ml
- Lime juice — 20 ml
- Fresh ginger, grated — 8 g
- Coconut flakes — 40 g
- Pumpkin seeds — 30 g
- Cashews, chopped — 30 g
- Maple syrup — 15 g
- Bee pollen — 10 g
- Kiwi, sliced — 100 g

## Method
1. Toast coconut flakes, pumpkin seeds and cashews with maple syrup at 160 °C for 7–8 minutes; cool to a crunch.
2. Blend frozen banana, mango, spinach, avocado, ginger, lime and coconut milk to a thick soft-serve texture — add liquid 20 ml at a time, no more.
3. Chill four bowls in the freezer 10 minutes.
4. Spoon the blend into bowls, smoothing the surface.
5. Arrange kiwi, coconut crunch and bee pollen in neat bands.

## Plating & Service
Serve immediately in chilled bowls with long spoons; the bands of topping should stay distinct.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Pre-portion fruit into freezer bags per service — blitz-and-plate takes 4 minutes even at anchor swing. Blender must be gimballed or braced in a swell.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: skip bee pollen (not vegan) and use agave. Nut-free: drop cashews, double pumpkin seeds. Protein: add 60 g vanilla whey for crew.
