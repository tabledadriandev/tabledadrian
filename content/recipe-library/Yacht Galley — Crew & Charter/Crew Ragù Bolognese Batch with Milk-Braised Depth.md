# Crew Ragù Bolognese Batch with Milk-Braised Depth

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> One Sunday pot, four future dinners — a slow milk-and-wine ragù portioned for the freezer like galley currency.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Beef mince (15%) — 1 kg
- Pork mince — 500 g
- Pancetta, fine dice — 150 g
- Onion, fine dice — 300 g
- Carrot, fine dice — 200 g
- Celery, fine dice — 150 g
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Tomato paste — 80 g
- White wine — 250 ml
- Whole milk — 400 ml
- Chopped tomatoes — 800 g
- Beef stock — 400 ml
- Bay leaves — 2
- Nutmeg — 1 g
- Tagliatelle — 900 g
- Parmesan — 120 g
- Fine sea salt — 14 g

## Method
1. Render pancetta in oil; add soffritto (onion, carrot, celery) and sweat 12 minutes.
2. Brown beef and pork in the same pot in batches, breaking to a rubble; season.
3. Stir in tomato paste, 3 minutes; deglaze with wine, reduce out.
4. Add milk; simmer until nearly absorbed, 15 minutes — it tenderises and sweetens.
5. Add tomatoes, stock, bay, nutmeg; simmer part-lidded at a bare blip 2.5 hours, topping with water if it catches.
6. Season; toss half with tagliatelle and pasta water, cool and portion the rest.

## Plating & Service
Tight nests of tagliatelle, ragù folded through (never dolloped), Parmesan snow.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Yields ~3 kg: tonight's pasta, one lasagne base, one freezer stash. Portion flat in bags — defrosts in 20 minutes in a sink of seawater on passage days.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: GF pasta or over polenta. Dairy-free: skip milk, add 100 ml extra stock — different but honest. Lamb + 5 g fennel seed makes a southern variant.
