# Ham Hock & Gruyère Breakfast Galette with Fried Sage

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Buckwheat galettes bretonnes with slow-cooked ham hock — rustic France plated with fine-dining restraint for the aft-deck table.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Buckwheat flour — 250 g
- Water — 500 ml
- Egg — 1 (60 g), plus 6 for filling
- Fine sea salt — 5 g
- Unsalted butter — 80 g
- Cooked ham hock, picked — 300 g
- Gruyère, grated — 180 g
- Crème fraîche — 90 g
- Sage leaves — 18
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Chives — 8 g

## Method
1. Whisk buckwheat flour, water, 1 egg and salt; rest batter 2 hours (or overnight) at 4 °C.
2. Fry sage leaves in 30 g butter at 170 °C for 30 seconds until glass-crisp; reserve leaves and butter.
3. Heat a 28 cm crêpe pan to 200 °C; ladle 90 ml batter, swirl thin, cook 90 seconds.
4. Flip, crack an egg into the centre, spread the white with the back of a spoon, ring with 50 g ham and 30 g Gruyère.
5. Lid 2 minutes until the white sets; fold four edges in to frame the yolk.
6. Finish with crème fraîche, sage butter, fried sage, chives and pepper. Repeat — one galette at a time.

## Plating & Service
Square-folded galette centred on a warm plate, yolk exposed, three fried sage leaves standing in the crème fraîche.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Batter and picked hock hold 3 days — service is 4 minutes per galette, so cap at 8 covers solo or run two pans. Calm-water dish; the flip is unforgiving in a beam sea.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian: spinach and mushrooms instead of hock. Dairy-free: olive oil, skip cheese, finish with rocket and lemon.
