# Provençal Tomato Tartine with Whipped Goat Curd & Za'atar

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Market tomatoes at their August peak on grilled levain — the no-cook-feel breakfast that clears the pass in minutes.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Heritage tomatoes, mixed sizes — 600 g
- Levain or sourdough, thick slices — 6 (360 g)
- Fresh goat curd or soft chèvre — 250 g
- Crème fraîche — 50 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- Za'atar — 10 g
- Garlic clove — 1 (5 g)
- Basil leaves — 12
- Flaky sea salt — 4 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Honey — 15 g

## Method
1. Slice large tomatoes 1 cm thick, halve the small ones; salt lightly and drain on a rack 10 minutes to concentrate.
2. Whip goat curd with crème fraîche and honey until light.
3. Grill bread at 220 °C, 3 minutes per side, until char-edged; rub once with the garlic clove.
4. Spread each slice thickly with whipped curd.
5. Shingle tomatoes over, dress with olive oil, za'atar, basil, flaky salt and pepper.

## Plating & Service
On a long board, cut on the bias, extra oil and za'atar in ramekins for guests.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Everything preps ahead except the grill — 6 minutes from order to table for 12. Tomatoes drain rather than soak the bread, so tartines survive a buffet in the sun.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: whipped silken tofu with 10 ml lemon instead of curd. GF: grilled GF boule. Winter: roasted tomatoes at 160 °C for 40 minutes.
