# Vitello Tonnato Platter with Crispy Capers & Lemon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Cold rosé veal under a silk tuna sauce — the most elegant thing you can serve at 35 °C, and every gram of it is made yesterday.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Veal topside or eye round — 1.2 kg
- Fine sea salt — 14 g
- Olive oil — 60 ml
- White wine — 200 ml
- Carrot, onion, celery — 300 g
- Bay, peppercorns — 4 g
- Tinned tuna in oil, drained — 250 g
- Anchovy fillets — 20 g
- Egg yolks — 2 (40 g)
- Dijon mustard — 10 g
- Lemon juice — 50 ml
- Neutral oil — 200 ml
- Capers — 60 g
- Neutral oil (frying) — 100 ml
- Rocket — 80 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Salt veal 2 hours ahead. Sear all over in olive oil.
2. Braise-poach: add wine, vegetables, aromatics and water to cover two-thirds; lid on at 140 °C fan until the core hits 58 °C, 40–55 minutes.
3. Cool the veal in its liquid, then wrap and chill overnight — this is what makes it carvable.
4. Tonnato: blitz tuna, anchovy, yolks, mustard, half the lemon; stream in neutral oil to a pourable silk; adjust with braising liquid, lemon, salt.
5. Fry capers (dried well) in 100 ml oil at 180 °C, 60–90 seconds until they blossom; drain.
6. Slice veal 2–3 mm; shingle across a platter; nap with tonnato; capers, rocket, pepper, lemon.

## Plating & Service
Full-platter shingle with the sauce covering all but the edges — the reveal is the carving, done in the galley.

> [!note] Galley notes
> A designed-ahead dish: nothing happens on service day but slicing and saucing — the charter-lunch ace when the tender schedule is chaos. Sauce keeps 3 days.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Classic swap: rare roast beef or even poached chicken breast work under the same sauce. Egg-free tonnato: build on 150 g mayonnaise.
