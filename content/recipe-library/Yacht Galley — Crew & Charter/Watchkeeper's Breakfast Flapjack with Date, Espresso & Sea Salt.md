# Watchkeeper's Breakfast Flapjack with Date, Espresso & Sea Salt

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A bar built for the 04:00–08:00 watch — slow oats, dates and a whisper of espresso, cut to fit a foulie pocket.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Rolled oats — 400 g
- Unsalted butter — 200 g
- Light brown sugar — 100 g
- Golden syrup — 100 g
- Medjool dates, chopped — 200 g
- Espresso, brewed — 40 ml
- Ground espresso beans — 4 g
- Pecans, chopped — 100 g
- Sunflower seeds — 60 g
- Cinnamon — 3 g
- Flaky sea salt — 4 g
- Fine sea salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 170 °C fan; line a 30 × 20 cm tin.
2. Melt butter, sugar and syrup at low heat; stir in espresso liquid and grounds.
3. Fold in oats, dates, pecans, seeds, cinnamon and fine salt until every oat is coated.
4. Press firmly into the tin — compression is what stops crumbling on deck.
5. Bake 25–30 minutes to golden at the edges; scatter flaky salt.
6. Cool completely in the tin (2 hours) before cutting into 12 bars.

## Plating & Service
Stack in the crew mess tin; wrap individually in parchment for watch handovers.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Keeps 10 days airtight, 2 months frozen — one bake covers a Corsica delivery. Eats one-handed with a harness clipped on; no crumb blizzard in the cockpit.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: coconut oil and maple syrup 1:1. Nut-free: double sunflower seeds. Decaf watch: swap espresso for orange zest and 30 g cocoa nibs.
