# Apricot & Almond Galette with Honey and Thyme

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A free-form tart that celebrates imperfection — market apricots on frangipane in a crust that shatters, rustic enough to forgive a rolling galley.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Plain flour — 250 g
- Cold unsalted butter — 180 g
- Iced water — 60 ml
- Caster sugar — 100 g
- Fine sea salt — 4 g
- Ground almonds — 100 g
- Unsalted butter, soft (frangipane) — 80 g
- Egg — 1 (60 g)
- Almond extract — 2 g
- Apricots, halved, stoned — 800 g
- Honey — 40 g
- Thyme leaves — 2 g
- Egg (wash) — 1
- Demerara sugar — 20 g
- Crème fraîche — 200 g

## Method
1. Pastry: rub 180 g cold butter into flour, 20 g sugar and salt to hazelnut rubble; bind with iced water; disc, chill 1 hour.
2. Frangipane: beat soft butter with 80 g sugar; add egg, ground almonds, almond extract.
3. Roll pastry to a 35 cm rough circle on parchment; spread frangipane leaving a 5 cm border.
4. Shingle apricot halves cut-side up, tight; fold the border over pleating as it comes.
5. Egg-wash the crust; demerara over everything.
6. Bake at 190 °C fan for 40–45 minutes until the base is bronzed (lift and check); warm honey with thyme, brush over the fruit; rest 20 minutes.

## Plating & Service
Whole to the table on its parchment-lined board; wedges with cold crème fraîche.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Pastry disc and frangipane hold 3 days chilled — assemble and bake to the evening's rhythm. 'Rustic' means a lurch during folding is a style choice, not a failure.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap apricots for plums, figs or peaches all season with zero changes. GF: 1:1 blend + 10 g extra butter. Dairy-free: plant butter and skip crème fraîche.
