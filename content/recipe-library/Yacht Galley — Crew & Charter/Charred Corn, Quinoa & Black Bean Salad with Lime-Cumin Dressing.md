# Charred Corn, Quinoa & Black Bean Salad with Lime-Cumin Dressing

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The indestructible BBQ salad — no leaves, no mayo, nothing that dies in the sun, and it's secretly the protein dish for the vegans.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Quinoa — 400 g
- Water — 700 ml
- Corn cobs — 4 (800 g)
- Black beans, cooked — 480 g
- Red pepper, diced — 250 g
- Red onion, fine dice — 120 g
- Jalapeño, minced — 20 g
- Cherry tomatoes, halved — 300 g
- Coriander — 30 g
- Lime juice — 80 ml
- Olive oil — 80 ml
- Ground cumin, toasted — 6 g
- Maple syrup — 15 g
- Smoked paprika — 3 g
- Fine sea salt — 12 g

## Method
1. Rinse quinoa; simmer in the water with 4 g salt, 12 minutes; rest lidded 5; fluff and cool on a tray.
2. Char corn on a 250 °C plancha or grill, rolling, 6–8 minutes until blackened in patches; cut off the kernels.
3. Whisk lime, oil, cumin, maple, paprika and remaining salt.
4. Fold quinoa, corn, beans, pepper, onion, jalapeño and half the dressing.
5. Add tomatoes, coriander and remaining dressing just before service.

## Plating & Service
Heaped in a wide bowl with lime wedges; it looks as good after two hours on the beach as it did leaving the galley.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Fully dressed it holds 48 h chilled — make the day before every beach drop. No dairy, no leaves: heat-proof, cooler-bag-proof, allergy-card-proof.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 300 g crumbled feta or grilled halloumi where dairy's welcome. Swap quinoa for pearl couscous (not GF) or rice.
