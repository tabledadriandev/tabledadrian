# Tray-Baked Frittata with Courgette, Mint & Goat's Cheese

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> One gastronorm tray feeds the whole crew hot, warm or cold — the frittata that survives a Force 6 lunch.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Eggs — 16 (960 g)
- Courgettes, coarsely grated and squeezed — 500 g
- Goat's cheese — 180 g
- Parmesan, grated — 60 g
- Double cream — 100 ml
- Spring onions, sliced — 80 g
- Mint, chopped — 12 g
- Lemon zest — 4 g
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Fine sea salt — 6 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 170 °C fan; oil and line a 30 × 20 cm tray.
2. Salt grated courgette 10 minutes, squeeze hard — you want under 350 g after squeezing.
3. Sauté courgette and spring onion in olive oil 4 minutes to drive off moisture.
4. Whisk eggs with cream, Parmesan, mint, zest, salt and pepper; fold in courgette.
5. Pour into the tray, dot with goat's cheese, bake 25–30 minutes until just set (centre 74 °C).
6. Rest 10 minutes before cutting into 10 squares.

## Plating & Service
Warm with dressed rocket for brunch, or cold squares straight from the fridge for watch changes.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Keeps 4 days chilled and eats cold with one hand — cut, wrap, done. Doubles into two trays for 20 portions; set trays crosswise to the roll so custard bakes level.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free: omit cheeses and cream, add 150 g roasted peppers and 20 ml extra oil. Swap courgette for 400 g roasted squash in shoulder season.
