# Miso-Maple Trail Mix with Coconut & Wasabi Peas

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sweet-salty-umami deck fuel in a jar by the companionway — the snack that stops the biscuit tin being raided before dinner.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Almonds — 200 g
- Cashews — 150 g
- Peanuts — 150 g
- Pumpkin seeds — 100 g
- Coconut flakes — 60 g
- White miso — 30 g
- Maple syrup — 60 ml
- Neutral oil — 20 ml
- Toasted sesame oil — 10 ml
- Sesame seeds — 20 g
- Wasabi peas — 100 g
- Dried cranberries — 100 g
- Flaky sea salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 150 °C fan.
2. Whisk miso, maple, both oils until smooth.
3. Toss nuts and pumpkin seeds through the glaze; spread on a lined tray.
4. Roast 18–20 minutes, stirring at 10, adding coconut and sesame for the final 5 — watch the coconut.
5. Cool completely on the tray; it crisps as it cools.
6. Break up; toss with wasabi peas, cranberries, flaky salt; jar airtight.

## Plating & Service
A big kilner jar with a scoop, living wherever the crew passes most often.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Keeps 3 weeks airtight and survives any sea state — the anti-seasickness nibble that isn't crackers. One tray per week is about right for a crew of eight.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: all seeds + roasted chickpeas. Skip wasabi peas for delicate palates; add 3 g togarashi instead.
