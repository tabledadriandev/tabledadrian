# Crew Porridge with Apple, Tahini & Toasted Buckwheat

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The default sea-day breakfast: one pot, ten bowls, and a tahini-apple finish that stops porridge fatigue by week six of the season.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Rolled oats — 500 g
- Water — 1 l
- Oat milk — 600 ml
- Fine sea salt — 5 g
- Apples, coarsely grated — 300 g
- Cinnamon — 4 g
- Tahini — 80 g
- Maple syrup — 60 ml
- Buckwheat groats — 80 g
- Flaxseed, ground — 30 g
- Lemon juice — 15 ml

## Method
1. Toast buckwheat in a dry pan over medium heat 5 minutes until biscuity; set aside.
2. Bring water, oat milk and salt to 95 °C; rain in oats, stirring.
3. Simmer 8–10 minutes, stirring, to a creamy porridge; fold in half the grated apple, cinnamon and flax.
4. Loosen tahini with maple, lemon juice and 30 ml warm water to a pourable ribbon.
5. Bowl up; top with remaining fresh apple, tahini ribbon and toasted buckwheat.

## Plating & Service
Crew mess service straight from the pot; toppings central so everyone builds their own.

> [!note] Galley notes
> One pot, 15 minutes, zero oven — the passage-morning standard. Leftovers set into slices: pan-fry squares next day in a little oil. Scale by 50 g oats per extra head.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: certified GF oats and buckwheat. Protein: stir 100 g soy protein or top with yogurt. Winter: swap apple for poached prunes.
