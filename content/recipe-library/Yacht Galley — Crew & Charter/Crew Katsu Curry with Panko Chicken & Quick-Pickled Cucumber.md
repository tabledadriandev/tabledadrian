# Crew Katsu Curry with Panko Chicken & Quick-Pickled Cucumber

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The crew-poll winner every season: crisp panko chicken over a silky curry sauce that batches, freezes, and never sees a jar of paste.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Chicken breasts, butterflied — 1.4 kg
- Plain flour — 150 g
- Eggs — 4 (240 g)
- Panko — 300 g
- Neutral oil for shallow frying — 400 ml
- Onions, sliced — 400 g
- Carrots, chopped — 300 g
- Garlic — 20 g
- Ginger — 25 g
- Mild curry powder — 25 g
- Garam masala — 6 g
- Plain flour (sauce) — 50 g
- Chicken stock — 1.2 l
- Soy sauce — 40 ml
- Honey — 25 g
- Cucumber — 300 g
- Rice vinegar — 60 ml
- Caster sugar — 20 g
- Jasmine rice — 700 g
- Fine sea salt — 12 g

## Method
1. Pickle: slice cucumber thin, toss with vinegar, sugar, 4 g salt; chill.
2. Sauce: sweat onion and carrot in 40 ml oil 10 minutes; add garlic, ginger, curry powder, 2 minutes; stir in 50 g flour, then stock gradually.
3. Simmer 20 minutes; blitz smooth, season with soy, honey, garam masala.
4. Cook rice by absorption (1:1.5, 12 minutes, rest 10).
5. Pane chicken: flour, egg, panko, pressing crumbs on.
6. Shallow-fry at 175 °C, 4 minutes per side to gold, internal 74 °C; rest on a rack, slice thick.
7. Rice, sliced katsu, sauce over the chicken, pickles alongside.

## Plating & Service
Slice on the bias and fan over rice; sauce in a channel, never drowning the crumb.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Sauce freezes in 1 l tubs; chicken panes ahead and holds chilled 24 h — service is a 10-minute fry. In heavy weather bake the cutlets at 220 °C for 18 min instead of frying.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: aubergine or sweet potato katsu, vegetable stock. GF: GF flour, GF panko, tamari.
