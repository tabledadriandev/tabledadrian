# Za'atar Roast Salmon with Freekeh, Charred Lemon & Green Tahini

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A side of salmon roasted whole over smoky freekeh — guest-dinner elegance that's genuinely a one-tray, two-pot operation.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Salmon side, skin-on, pin-boned — 1.4 kg
- Za'atar — 25 g
- Olive oil — 90 ml
- Honey — 15 g
- Lemons — 3
- Cracked freekeh — 400 g
- Vegetable stock — 900 ml
- Onion, diced — 200 g
- Cinnamon — 2 g
- Tahini — 100 g
- Parsley — 30 g
- Coriander — 20 g
- Garlic — 10 g
- Iced water — 90 ml
- Pistachios, chopped — 50 g
- Fine sea salt — 12 g

## Method
1. Soften onion in 30 ml oil; add freekeh and cinnamon, toast 2 minutes; add stock and 6 g salt, simmer lidded 20 minutes, rest 10.
2. Blitz tahini, herbs, garlic, juice of 1 lemon, 4 g salt and iced water to a pale-green pourable sauce.
3. Heat oven to 220 °C fan. Rub salmon with 40 ml oil, za'atar, honey and salt.
4. Halve remaining lemons; set on the tray cut-side down beside the salmon.
5. Roast 12–15 minutes to 48–50 °C at the thickest point — glossy and just set.
6. Fork the freekeh open with remaining oil; rest salmon 3 minutes.

## Plating & Service
Freekeh on a long platter, salmon lifted whole on top, green tahini in ribbons, pistachios and charred lemon to finish.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Sauce and freekeh hold 3 days; the fish is a 15-minute flash before service. Same template swaps to whatever the fish market lands — one recipe, all season.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: swap freekeh for quinoa (15 min). Swap salmon for whole sea bass (roast 20 min) or roasted aubergine halves (vegan).
