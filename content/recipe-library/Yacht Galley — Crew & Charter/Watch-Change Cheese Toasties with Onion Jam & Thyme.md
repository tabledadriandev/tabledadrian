# Watch-Change Cheese Toasties with Onion Jam & Thyme

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The 02:00 handover institution — a slow-griddled toastie with batch onion jam that makes the incoming watch forgive everything.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Sourdough or pain de mie slices — 12 (600 g)
- Mature cheddar, grated — 250 g
- Gruyère or Comté, grated — 150 g
- Onions, thinly sliced — 500 g
- Unsalted butter — 100 g
- Light brown sugar — 30 g
- Balsamic vinegar — 30 ml
- Thyme leaves — 3 g
- Dijon mustard — 20 g
- Fine sea salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Onion jam (batch, keeps 2 weeks): cook onions in 40 g butter with salt over low heat 25 minutes to deep gold; add sugar, balsamic, thyme; reduce to jam, 5 minutes.
2. Butter the OUTSIDES of all bread slices with remaining butter; mustard inside.
3. Build: cheese, onion jam, cheese (cheese both sides welds it shut).
4. Griddle over medium-low heat, pressed, 4–5 minutes per side — low and slow is the law; the cheese must melt before the bread burns.
5. Rest 1 minute; halve diagonally.

## Plating & Service
Halves stacked on a board at the companionway, cut faces steaming — eaten standing, always.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Onion jam is the make-ahead; the toastie is a 10-minute night-cook job any crew member can execute. Nothing drips, nothing needs a plate — clip on and eat.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add ham or leftover roast beef between the cheese layers. GF: sturdy GF loaf, slightly lower heat. Kimchi instead of onion jam for the brave.
