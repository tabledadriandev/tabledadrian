# Prawn, Mango & Rice Noodle Salad with Nuoc Cham

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bright, cold and pulling in four directions at once — sweet mango, hot chile, sour lime, salty fish sauce — with zero flame beyond a 3-minute poach.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Raw prawns, peeled — 800 g
- Rice vermicelli — 400 g
- Mango, underripe-firm, julienned — 400 g
- Cucumber, julienned — 300 g
- Carrot, julienned — 200 g
- Beansprouts — 150 g
- Mint — 20 g
- Coriander — 25 g
- Thai basil — 15 g
- Roasted peanuts, crushed — 80 g
- Fish sauce — 60 ml
- Lime juice — 80 ml
- Caster sugar — 50 g
- Water — 60 ml
- Garlic, minced — 10 g
- Bird's-eye chile, minced — 8 g
- Crispy shallots — 40 g

## Method
1. Nuoc cham: dissolve sugar in warm water; add fish sauce, lime, garlic, chile; balance to bright and punchy.
2. Poach prawns in well-salted 90 °C water 2–3 minutes until just pink; shock in iced water; halve lengthwise.
3. Soak or boil vermicelli per packet (usually 3 minutes); rinse ice-cold; snip with scissors into forkable lengths.
4. Toss noodles with a third of the nuoc cham.
5. Fold in mango, cucumber, carrot, sprouts and half the herbs.
6. Top with prawns, remaining herbs, peanuts, crispy shallots; remaining dressing at the table.

## Plating & Service
A high, loose tangle on a big platter, prawns on the summit — dress finally in front of guests so nothing sits soggy.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Every element preps ahead cold and assembles in 3 minutes — no galley heat at noon. Undressed components hold 24 h; nuoc cham keeps a week and lifts everything on board.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: crispy tofu instead of prawns, soy-lime dressing instead of fish sauce. Nut-free: crispy shallots only. Green papaya instead of mango when you find it.
