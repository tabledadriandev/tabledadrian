# Crab, Fennel & Citrus Salad Cups with Avocado

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> White crab folded with shaved fennel and citrus, served in lettuce cups — cold, precise, and gone in two bites on a hot afternoon.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- White crab meat, picked — 500 g
- Brown crab meat — 60 g
- Fennel bulb, shaved paper-thin — 250 g
- Oranges — 2 (300 g)
- Pink grapefruit — 1 (250 g)
- Avocados, diced — 300 g
- Lime juice — 40 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- Dijon mustard — 5 g
- Tarragon, chopped — 6 g
- Chives — 8 g
- Espelette pepper — 1 g
- Little gem or baby cos leaves — 24 (350 g)
- Fine sea salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Segment the citrus over a bowl, saving 40 ml of juice; halve the segments.
2. Dressing: whisk saved citrus juice, lime, mustard, oil, salt.
3. Shave fennel on a mandoline into iced water 10 minutes; drain and spin dry.
4. Fold white crab with brown crab, half the dressing, tarragon and Espelette — gently, keeping the flakes whole.
5. Dress fennel, citrus and avocado with the rest.
6. Fill chilled lettuce cups: fennel-citrus base, crab crown, chives.

## Plating & Service
Cups on a platter over crushed ice, assembled no more than 15 minutes ahead; a final thread of oil and Espelette.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Crab mix and dressing hold separately for a day; assembly is the only service task. On-ice platter service keeps it safe through a long lunch in the sun.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap crab for poached prawns or, for a budget crew version, flaked hot-smoked trout. Grapefruit-shy guests: all orange plus 10 ml extra lime.
