# Anchovy & Gruyère Palmiers from the Freezer

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Puff pastry, anchovy butter, a tight roll — sliced from the freezer into instant apéro spirals that taste like a Riviera bar.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- All-butter puff pastry — 500 g
- Anchovy fillets — 40 g
- Unsalted butter, soft — 60 g
- Dijon mustard — 15 g
- Gruyère, finely grated — 150 g
- Thyme leaves — 3 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Egg (wash) — 1

## Method
1. Mash anchovies into the soft butter with mustard, thyme and pepper.
2. Roll pastry to 40 × 30 cm; spread the anchovy butter edge to edge; carpet with Gruyère, pressing it in.
3. Roll both long edges inward to meet in the middle (the palmier heart); wrap; freeze 30 minutes (or weeks).
4. Slice 1 cm; lay flat, spaced, on lined trays; egg-wash.
5. Bake at 200 °C fan for 18–22 minutes, flipping at 14, until caramelised both sides.
6. Cool 5 minutes — they crisp as they set.

## Plating & Service
Standing in a tumbler or scattered on a slate with the evening's first pour.

> [!note] Galley notes
> The frozen log is the point: slice-and-bake 6 or 60 at whim, always fresh, never stale tins of crackers. Log keeps 2 months.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian: tapenade or sun-dried tomato paste instead of anchovy. Sweet turn: cinnamon sugar, no cheese, same fold.
