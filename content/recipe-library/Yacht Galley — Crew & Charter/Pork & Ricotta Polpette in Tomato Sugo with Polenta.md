# Pork & Ricotta Polpette in Tomato Sugo with Polenta

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Ricotta-lightened meatballs that stay tender through a double reheat — the make-ahead main that mothers a tired crew.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Pork mince — 1.2 kg
- Ricotta — 250 g
- Panko or fresh crumbs — 100 g
- Whole milk — 60 ml
- Egg — 2 (120 g)
- Parmesan, grated — 80 g
- Garlic — 20 g
- Fennel seeds, cracked — 5 g
- Lemon zest — 4 g
- Parsley — 20 g
- Olive oil — 60 ml
- Chopped tomatoes — 1.2 kg
- Basil — 20 g
- Quick polenta — 400 g
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Fine sea salt — 16 g
- Chile flakes — 2 g

## Method
1. Soak crumbs in milk 5 minutes. Mix pork, ricotta, soaked crumbs, eggs, 50 g Parmesan, half the garlic, fennel, zest, parsley, 10 g salt; roll 50 g balls; chill 20 minutes.
2. Brown polpette in 40 ml oil in batches — colour, not cooked through.
3. Sugo: remaining garlic and chile in 20 ml oil, 1 minute; tomatoes and salt; simmer 10 minutes.
4. Return polpette; simmer gently 15 minutes to 72 °C core; tear in basil.
5. Polenta: whisk into 1.6 l simmering salted water; cook 5–8 minutes; beat in butter and remaining Parmesan.
6. Polenta pooled, polpette and sugo over.

## Plating & Service
Soft polenta first, five balls per bowl, sugo generous, basil and Parmesan last.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Polpette freeze raw or in sauce; sugo is the house base. Polenta feeds many for pennies and pours around any schedule — set leftovers, then grill polenta squares tomorrow.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: GF crumbs (polenta already GF). Beef/veal mix works; turkey + 20 g extra ricotta stays juicy. Dairy-free: skip ricotta/Parmesan, add 60 g grated onion.
