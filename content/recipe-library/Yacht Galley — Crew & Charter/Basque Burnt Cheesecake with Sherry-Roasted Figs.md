# Basque Burnt Cheesecake with Sherry-Roasted Figs

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Deliberately scorched, cracked and proud — the cheesecake that forgives a temperamental boat oven and photographs like San Sebastián.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Full-fat cream cheese — 900 g
- Caster sugar — 250 g
- Eggs — 5 (300 g)
- Double cream — 400 ml
- Cornflour — 20 g
- Vanilla paste — 6 g
- Lemon zest — 4 g
- Fine sea salt — 4 g
- Figs, halved — 500 g
- Pedro Ximénez or sweet sherry — 60 ml
- Honey — 30 g
- Thyme — 2 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 220 °C fan. Line a 23 cm springform with two overlapping sheets of parchment, pleats and all, standing 5 cm proud.
2. Beat cream cheese and sugar smooth, 2 minutes; add eggs one at a time.
3. Whisk cornflour into 100 ml of the cream; add with remaining cream, vanilla, zest, salt; the batter should pour like thick paint.
4. Pour in; bake 45–50 minutes until mahogany-dark on top and still swaying at the centre like a jelly.
5. Cool fully in the tin — it sinks and sets; chill 4 hours but serve cool, not cold.
6. Figs: roast with sherry, honey and thyme at 210 °C for 12 minutes until jammy; spoon alongside.

## Plating & Service
Torn-parchment presentation on a board, cut with a hot knife; figs and their sherry syrup over each slab.

> [!note] Galley notes
> The burnt top is the finish — no water bath, no crack anxiety, no décor. Bakes a day ahead; figs swap for whatever fruit the market surrendered.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Alcohol-free: orange juice + 10 ml balsamic on the figs. Lighter: 700 g cream cheese + 200 g Greek yogurt. Serve solo with just flaky salt — it needs nothing.
