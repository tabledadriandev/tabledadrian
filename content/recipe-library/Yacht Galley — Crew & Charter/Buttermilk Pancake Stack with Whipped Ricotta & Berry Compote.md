# Buttermilk Pancake Stack with Whipped Ricotta & Berry Compote

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A charter-morning crowd-pleaser: the batter rests while you set the deck table, the compote is made days ahead.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Plain flour — 300 g
- Baking powder — 12 g
- Bicarbonate of soda — 3 g
- Caster sugar — 40 g
- Buttermilk — 420 ml
- Eggs — 3 (180 g)
- Melted butter — 50 g
- Vanilla paste — 5 g
- Ricotta — 250 g
- Icing sugar — 20 g
- Lemon zest — 3 g
- Mixed berries — 300 g
- Maple syrup — 90 ml
- Fine sea salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Simmer 200 g berries with 30 ml maple syrup 8 minutes to a loose compote; cool, fold in remaining whole berries.
2. Whip ricotta with icing sugar and lemon zest until light; chill.
3. Whisk dry ingredients; separately whisk buttermilk, eggs, butter, vanilla.
4. Combine wet into dry with 8–10 strokes — lumps are correct; rest 15 minutes.
5. Cook 80 g ladles on a 180 °C plancha or pan, 2 minutes per side, flipping when bubbles burst and stay open.
6. Hold pancakes in a single layer at 90 °C while you finish the batch.

## Plating & Service
Stack three per guest, quenelle of whipped ricotta, spoon of compote, thread of maple syrup at the table.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Batter holds 24 h chilled; compote keeps a week. For 12 covers run two pans — a plancha at anchor does the whole charter in 15 minutes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: 1:1 GF flour blend plus 2 g xanthan. Lighter: yogurt instead of ricotta. Add 3 g ground cardamom to the batter for a Nordic accent.
