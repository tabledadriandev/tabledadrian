# Corsican Wild Herb & Sausage Pasta al Forno

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A baked rigatoni scented with rosemary, fennel and brocciu-style fresh cheese — the après-crossing dinner when the boat smells of maquis.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Rigatoni — 800 g
- Coarse pork sausages (herby), skinned — 700 g
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Fennel seeds — 5 g
- Rosemary, chopped — 5 g
- Onion, diced — 250 g
- Garlic — 20 g
- White wine — 150 ml
- Chopped tomatoes — 800 g
- Chile flakes — 2 g
- Ricotta or brocciu — 400 g
- Lemon zest — 4 g
- Pecorino, grated — 120 g
- Mint — 10 g
- Fine sea salt — 12 g

## Method
1. Brown sausage meat in oil, breaking into rubble; add fennel seed, rosemary, onion; cook 8 minutes.
2. Add garlic, 1 minute; deglaze with wine; add tomatoes, chile, salt; simmer 15 minutes.
3. Boil rigatoni 3 minutes under packet time in salted water; drain, keep 250 ml water.
4. Fold pasta, sauce and pasta water together; season.
5. Into a deep tray; dot heavily with ricotta beaten with lemon zest; cap with pecorino.
6. Bake at 200 °C fan for 20 minutes until the edges crisp; rest 8 minutes, mint over.

## Plating & Service
Crater the tray at the table so everyone sees the ricotta pockets; edges to the deckhands who tied up fastest.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Assembles ahead to step 5 and bakes on demand; leftovers reheat as fried-pasta squares. Casual enough for crew, dressed enough for an informal guest night.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian: 500 g mushrooms + 5 g extra fennel seed instead of sausage. GF: GF rigatoni, bake 15 min only.
