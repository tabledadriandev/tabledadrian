# Provisioning Puttanesca with Tinned Cherry Tomatoes & Colatura

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Entirely from the dry stores locker — the 20-minute crossing dinner that tastes like you provisioned in Naples this morning.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Spaghetti or linguine — 700 g
- Tinned cherry tomatoes — 800 g
- Anchovy fillets in oil — 40 g
- Garlic, sliced — 25 g
- Chile flakes — 3 g
- Black olives (Gaeta or Kalamata), torn — 150 g
- Capers, rinsed — 50 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml
- Colatura di alici (or extra anchovy) — 10 ml
- Tomato paste — 25 g
- Dried oregano — 3 g
- Parsley — 20 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Bring 6 l water with 40 g salt to a rolling boil.
2. Melt anchovies in 70 ml oil with garlic and chile over low heat, 3 minutes — no colour on the garlic.
3. Add tomato paste, fry 1 minute; add tinned cherry tomatoes, olives, capers, oregano; simmer 10 minutes, crushing half the tomatoes.
4. Cook pasta to 2 minutes under packet time; transfer to the sauce with 200 ml pasta water.
5. Finish cooking in the sauce 2 minutes, emulsifying to a gloss; season with colatura off heat.
6. Toss with parsley and remaining raw oil.

## Plating & Service
Twirl into warm bowls with the sauce clinging, a last thread of oil and parsley on top.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Every ingredient lives 6+ months in the locker — the ultimate zero-fresh-provisions main. One pot + one pan, 20 minutes for 8; double the sauce and freeze half.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: skip anchovy/colatura, add 10 g white miso for depth. GF: GF spaghetti — finish 1 min only in sauce so it holds.
