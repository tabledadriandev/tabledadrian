# Green Goddess Chicken & Avocado Platter with Little Gems

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Herb-dense dressing over pulled roast chicken and avocado — a composed platter that reads spa-lunch but eats like a proper meal.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Chicken breasts — 1.2 kg
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Fine sea salt — 12 g
- Greek yogurt — 200 g
- Mayonnaise — 100 g
- Anchovy fillets — 15 g
- Basil — 30 g
- Tarragon — 10 g
- Chives — 15 g
- Parsley — 20 g
- Lemon juice — 50 ml
- Garlic — 6 g
- Little gem lettuce — 4 (400 g)
- Avocados — 400 g
- Cucumber, smashed — 300 g
- Radishes — 100 g
- Toasted seeds (pumpkin/sunflower) — 50 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Roast chicken breasts rubbed with oil and salt at 190 °C fan to 70 °C core, 20–25 minutes; rest 10, then pull into thick ropes.
2. Green goddess: blitz yogurt, mayo, anchovy, all herbs, lemon, garlic to a thick pale-green dressing; season.
3. Quarter the gems through the root; smash and tear cucumber; halve radishes.
4. Toss warm chicken with a third of the dressing so it drinks it in.
5. Platter: gems and cucumber down first, chicken, avocado fans, radish.
6. Remaining dressing over in ribbons; seeds and pepper to finish.

## Plating & Service
A long white platter, dressing visibly generous; serve slightly cool, not fridge-cold.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Dressing keeps 4 days and rescues everything (fish, eggs, crudités); chicken can be yesterday's roast. Assembly-only service at lunch — galley stays cold.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: chickpeas + grilled courgette instead of chicken; plant yogurt/mayo, capers for anchovy. Add soft-boiled eggs to stretch to 10.
