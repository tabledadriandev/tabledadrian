# Tandoori-Spiced Leg of Lamb with Mint Raita & Kachumber

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Yogurt-marinated butterflied leg blasted hot — carved on deck with charred edges, pink centre, and sauces that do the talking.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Lamb leg, butterflied — 2.2 kg
- Greek yogurt (marinade) — 300 g
- Garlic — 30 g
- Ginger — 40 g
- Kashmiri chile powder — 15 g
- Ground cumin — 10 g
- Ground coriander — 10 g
- Garam masala — 10 g
- Turmeric — 5 g
- Lemon juice — 60 ml
- Mustard oil or neutral oil — 40 ml
- Fine sea salt — 20 g
- Greek yogurt (raita) — 400 g
- Mint — 25 g
- Cucumber — 300 g
- Tomatoes — 300 g
- Red onion — 150 g
- Coriander leaves — 20 g
- Lime juice — 30 ml

## Method
1. Slash the lamb 2 cm deep all over. Marinade: yogurt, garlic, ginger, all spices, lemon, oil, salt — massage in; 6–24 hours at 4 °C.
2. Bring to room temp 1 hour. Oven to 240 °C fan.
3. Roast on a rack 20 minutes to char, then 170 °C for 35–50 minutes to 54 °C core for rosé.
4. Rest loosely tented 20 minutes (rises to ~58 °C).
5. Raita: blitz mint into yogurt with a pinch of salt; grate in half the cucumber, squeezed.
6. Kachumber: dice remaining cucumber, tomato, onion; dress with lime, salt, coriander.
7. Carve across the grain in thick slices.

## Plating & Service
Whole on a board, carved at the rail; raita, kachumber and flatbreads down the table.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Marinade day-before is mandatory anyway — so the cook day is just fire and carving. Leftovers are the best wraps of the season; bones flavour tomorrow's dal.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cook through to 70 °C for guests who won't do pink — still juicy thanks to the yogurt. Swap lamb for a whole spatchcocked chicken (45 min total).
