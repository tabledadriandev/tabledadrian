# Grand Aioli Deck Platter with Poached Cod & Market Vegetables

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Provençal flag planted on the aft deck — one golden aioli, cold poached cod and a mountain of vegetables that only improves as it sits in the sun.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Cod loin — 1 kg
- Court bouillon: water 2 l, white wine 200 ml, bay 2, peppercorns 4 g, salt 20 g
- Egg yolks — 3 (60 g)
- Garlic, crushed to cream — 20 g
- Dijon mustard — 10 g
- Lemon juice — 30 ml
- Olive oil (mild) — 300 ml
- Baby potatoes — 800 g
- Green beans — 400 g
- Carrots, peeled, halved — 400 g
- Eggs — 8
- Baby fennel or radishes — 300 g
- Cherry tomatoes — 300 g
- Flaky sea salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Aioli: whisk yolks, garlic, mustard, half the lemon; add oil in a thread to a thick, wobbling emulsion; season with remaining lemon and salt.
2. Bring court bouillon to 80 °C; slide in cod, poach 8–10 minutes to 52 °C; cool in the liquid, then chill.
3. Boil potatoes 15 minutes; carrots 6; beans 3 — all in salted water, all drained and cooled separately.
4. Boil eggs 8 minutes 30 seconds; shock, peel, halve.
5. Break the cod into large petals.
6. Arrange everything in groups around the aioli bowl; flaky salt over.

## Plating & Service
One vast board or platter, aioli central, everything within reach — no individual plating, that's the point.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Every element cooks in the morning and holds chilled; the platter assembles in 5 minutes when the swimmers surface. Room-temp service means zero timing pressure.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: aioli from 60 ml aquafaba, skip cod/eggs, add chickpeas and artichokes. Swap cod for salt cod (soaked 24 h) for the classic.
