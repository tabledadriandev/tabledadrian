# Halloumi, Avocado & Zhoug Breakfast Flatbreads

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Seared halloumi, green zhoug and yogurt flatbreads made from two-ingredient dough — big, fresh, and forgiving of a busy pass.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Self-raising flour — 300 g
- Greek yogurt — 280 g
- Fine sea salt — 5 g
- Halloumi, 1 cm slabs — 450 g
- Avocados — 300 g
- Lime juice — 25 ml
- Coriander — 40 g
- Parsley — 20 g
- Green chiles — 20 g
- Garlic — 8 g
- Ground cardamom — 1 g
- Cumin — 2 g
- Olive oil — 80 ml
- Honey — 15 g
- Cherry tomatoes, halved — 200 g

## Method
1. Blitz coriander, parsley, chiles, garlic, cardamom, cumin, 60 ml oil and a pinch of salt to a coarse zhoug.
2. Mix flour, yogurt and salt to a soft dough; rest 10 minutes; divide into 6 and roll to 3 mm ovals.
3. Dry-fry flatbreads in a screaming pan (240 °C) 90 seconds per side until blistered; stack under a cloth.
4. Sear halloumi in 20 ml oil, 2 minutes per side, to deep gold; drizzle with honey off the heat.
5. Smash avocado with lime and salt.
6. Build: avocado, halloumi, tomatoes, zhoug over each flatbread.

## Plating & Service
Open-faced on boards, zhoug in a bowl for the brave; fold-and-eat encouraged.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Zhoug keeps a week and lifts eggs, fish and crew wraps all season. Dough needs no yeast or proving — flatbreads for 12 in under 30 minutes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: swap halloumi for seared king oyster mushrooms with the same honey-less glaze. GF: GF self-raising blend works, roll gently.
