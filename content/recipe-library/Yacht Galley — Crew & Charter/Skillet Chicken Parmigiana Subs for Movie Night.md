# Skillet Chicken Parmigiana Subs for Movie Night

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Crisp cutlets, molten mozzarella, garlic-buttered rolls — the morale bomb deployed on the first night off in three weeks.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Chicken breasts, butterflied to 8 escalopes — 1.4 kg
- Plain flour — 120 g
- Eggs — 4 (240 g)
- Panko — 280 g
- Parmesan — 120 g
- Dried oregano — 5 g
- Neutral oil — 300 ml
- Chopped tomatoes — 800 g
- Garlic — 25 g
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Basil — 20 g
- Caster sugar — 5 g
- Mozzarella, sliced — 400 g
- Sub rolls — 8 (640 g)
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Fine sea salt — 14 g

## Method
1. Sugo: half the garlic in olive oil, 1 minute; tomatoes, sugar, salt; simmer 12 minutes; tear in basil.
2. Pane escalopes: flour, egg, then panko mixed with 60 g Parmesan and oregano; press well.
3. Shallow-fry at 175 °C, 3 minutes per side to deep gold (72 °C core); rack-rest.
4. Top each cutlet with sugo, mozzarella, remaining Parmesan; grill at 240 °C for 3–4 minutes until molten and spotted.
5. Garlic butter: remaining garlic mashed into soft butter; spread on split rolls, toast 2 minutes.
6. Load cutlets into rolls; more sugo, more basil.

## Plating & Service
Halved on the diagonal, wrapped in parchment, eaten on the sofa — plates optional, napkins mandatory.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Cutlets pane ahead and freeze raw; sugo is the house batch. Fry-and-grill takes 15 minutes for 8 — the highest morale-per-minute dish in the book.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Aubergine parm subs for the vegetarian (roast slices instead of frying). GF: GF rolls + crumbs. Extra sugo jar always — someone will ask.
