# Chilled Soba & Cucumber Salad with Sesame-Miso Dressing

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Cold noodles engineered for the hottest day of the season — nutty, sharp, and structurally sound in a cooler bag for hours.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Soba noodles — 600 g
- Cucumbers, julienned — 500 g
- Carrot, julienned — 200 g
- Radishes, shaved — 100 g
- Edamame, podded — 250 g
- White miso — 50 g
- Tahini or peanut butter — 60 g
- Rice vinegar — 50 ml
- Soy sauce — 40 ml
- Maple syrup — 20 g
- Toasted sesame oil — 25 ml
- Ginger, grated — 15 g
- Iced water — 60 ml
- Sesame seeds — 15 g
- Coriander — 15 g
- Chile oil — 15 ml

## Method
1. Cook soba 4–5 minutes; rinse under cold running water, rubbing, until totally cold and starch-free — this is the whole game.
2. Dressing: whisk miso, tahini, vinegar, soy, maple, sesame oil, ginger; loosen with iced water to double-cream consistency.
3. Salt cucumber julienne lightly; drain 10 minutes and squeeze.
4. Toss soba with two-thirds of the dressing; fold in cucumber, carrot, radish, edamame.
5. Chill 30 minutes; finish with remaining dressing, sesame, coriander, chile oil.

## Plating & Service
A low mound in a wide bowl with chopsticks and spoons; chile oil rippled on top, extra on the side.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Holds dressed for 24 h without collapsing — the beach-cooler carbohydrate. Nut allergies: it's tahini-based unless you chose peanut; label the cooler box accordingly.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: 100% buckwheat soba + tamari. Add shredded poached chicken or seared salmon to convert to a main. Swap coriander for shiso if the market blesses you.
