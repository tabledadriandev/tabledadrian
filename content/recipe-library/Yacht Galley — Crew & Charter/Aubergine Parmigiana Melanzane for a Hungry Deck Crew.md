# Aubergine Parmigiana Melanzane for a Hungry Deck Crew

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Roasted, not fried, so the galley stays sane — layers collapse into that dense, sliceable parmigiana that's even better cold at midnight.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Aubergines, 1 cm rounds — 2 kg
- Olive oil — 150 ml
- Chopped tomatoes — 1.2 kg
- Garlic, sliced — 25 g
- Basil — 40 g
- Caster sugar — 8 g
- Fior di latte mozzarella, drained, torn — 500 g
- Parmesan, grated — 180 g
- Eggs, beaten — 2 (120 g)
- Dried oregano — 4 g
- Fine sea salt — 14 g
- Black pepper — 3 g

## Method
1. Salt aubergine rounds 20 minutes; pat dry. Brush with 120 ml oil and roast at 220 °C fan on trays, 20 minutes, until supple and golden.
2. Sugo: garlic in 30 ml oil 1 minute; tomatoes, sugar, oregano, salt; simmer 15 minutes; tear in half the basil.
3. Beat eggs into 100 g Parmesan (this sets the layers).
4. Layer a deep tray: sugo, aubergine, mozzarella, Parmesan-egg drizzle — three rounds — ending with sugo and remaining Parmesan.
5. Bake at 180 °C fan for 30–35 minutes until burnished; rest 30 minutes.
6. Finish with remaining basil.

## Plating & Service
Warm (not hot) squares with grilled bread; cold wedges are legitimate watch food.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Better made in the morning for the evening — the rest sets it solid for a moving boat. Freezes in portions; roasting instead of frying halves oil use and pan-watching.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add layers of 300 g cooked ham for the meat table. Vegan: skip egg, plant mozzarella, 30 g nutritional yeast in the sugo.
