# Smoky Baba Ganoush & Muhammara Mezze Board with Grilled Flatbreads

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Two anchor dips — fire-blistered aubergine and walnut-pepper — that turn a bag of vegetables and flatbreads into a full deck lunch.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Aubergines — 3 (1.2 kg)
- Tahini — 90 g
- Lemon juice — 60 ml
- Garlic — 12 g
- Roasted red peppers — 400 g
- Walnuts, toasted — 150 g
- Pomegranate molasses — 40 ml
- Breadcrumbs — 40 g
- Chile flakes (Aleppo) — 5 g
- Ground cumin — 5 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 120 ml
- Flatbreads — 8 (640 g)
- Cucumber, radish, carrots — 600 g
- Pomegranate seeds — 80 g
- Parsley & mint — 20 g
- Fine sea salt — 12 g

## Method
1. Burn aubergines whole over a gas flame or under a 270 °C grill, turning, 15–20 minutes until collapsed and charred all over; drain in a colander 20 minutes.
2. Scoop the flesh; chop, don't blend; fold with tahini, 40 ml lemon, half the garlic, 40 ml oil, salt — baba ganoush should be smoky and loose.
3. Muhammara: blitz peppers, walnuts, breadcrumbs, molasses, remaining garlic, cumin, chile, 20 ml lemon and 40 ml oil to a coarse rust-red paste; season.
4. Grill flatbreads 1 minute per side; cut into shards.
5. Cut vegetables into batons and coins.
6. Board: dips swooshed in wide bowls, oil pooled, pomegranate and herbs over, everything else in reach.

## Plating & Service
Abundance over symmetry — dips central, warm bread stacked, vegetables in tumbles; refresh rather than replate.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Both dips improve over 3 days chilled — the standing apéro/lunch solution for any guest schedule. The aubergine burn can happen on the BBQ while it heats for something else.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: GF flatbreads, skip crumbs in muhammara (add 20 g extra walnuts). Add grilled halloumi or lamb skewers to convert the board into a bigger lunch.
