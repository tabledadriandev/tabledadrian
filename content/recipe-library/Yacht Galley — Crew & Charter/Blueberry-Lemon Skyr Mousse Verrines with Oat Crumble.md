# Blueberry-Lemon Skyr Mousse Verrines with Oat Crumble

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> High-protein skyr whipped to a mousse over jammy blueberries — a patisserie-adjacent breakfast that holds two days in the fridge.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Skyr — 600 g
- Double cream — 200 ml
- Icing sugar — 60 g
- Lemon zest — 6 g
- Lemon juice — 30 ml
- Vanilla paste — 5 g
- Blueberries — 400 g
- Caster sugar — 50 g
- Cornflour — 8 g
- Rolled oats — 100 g
- Plain flour — 40 g
- Light brown sugar — 40 g
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Fine sea salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Crumble: rub butter into oats, flour, brown sugar and salt; bake at 170 °C for 12–15 minutes, stirring once, until golden; cool.
2. Compote: simmer 300 g blueberries with caster sugar and lemon juice 5 minutes; whisk in cornflour slurry, boil 1 minute, fold in remaining raw berries; chill.
3. Whip cream with icing sugar and vanilla to soft peaks.
4. Fold skyr and lemon zest into the cream — light but holding.
5. Layer verrines: compote, mousse, chill 1 hour; crumble on top at service only.

## Plating & Service
Glass verrines on a tray with the crumble scattered at the last second for contrast.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Compote and mousse hold 48 h; crumble 2 weeks airtight. Build 16 on prep day — grab-and-serve for early risers, spill-proof at anchor swing.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: GF oats and flour. Lighter: all-skyr, no cream (denser set). Swap blueberries for blackcurrants when provisioning finds them.
