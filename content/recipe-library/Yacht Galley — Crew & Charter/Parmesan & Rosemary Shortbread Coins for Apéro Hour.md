# Parmesan & Rosemary Shortbread Coins for Apéro Hour

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A slice-and-bake log in the freezer means warm cheese biscuits fifteen minutes after anyone says 'apéritif'.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Plain flour — 200 g
- Parmesan, finely grated — 150 g
- Cold unsalted butter, cubed — 150 g
- Egg yolk — 1 (20 g)
- Rosemary, minced — 4 g
- Walnuts, fine-chopped — 50 g
- Cayenne — 1 g
- Flaky sea salt — 3 g
- Fine sea salt — 2 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Rub (or pulse) flour, Parmesan, butter, rosemary, cayenne, fine salt and pepper to damp sand.
2. Add yolk and walnuts; press just until it holds.
3. Roll into two 4 cm logs; wrap; chill 2 hours or freeze.
4. Slice 8 mm coins; space on lined trays; flaky salt on top.
5. Bake at 170 °C fan for 15–18 minutes to pale gold at the edges.
6. Cool 10 minutes on the tray — they set as they cool.

## Plating & Service
Piled in a bowl beside the sundowners; they disappear at a rate of six per guest.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Logs keep 2 months frozen — slice and bake from frozen (+2 minutes) whenever guests linger. The galley smells like a boulangerie for the price of 18 oven-minutes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: skip walnuts. Swap rosemary for thyme + lemon zest, or Parmesan for aged Comté. GF: 1:1 GF flour blend works — handle gently.
