# Miso-Glazed Aubergine Steaks with Ginger Rice & Smacked Cucumber

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Nasu dengaku scaled to trays — lacquered aubergine that gives the plant-based table the same drama as a glazed short rib.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Aubergines, halved lengthwise — 4 large (1.6 kg)
- Neutral oil — 60 ml
- White miso — 120 g
- Mirin — 60 ml
- Sake or water — 40 ml
- Caster sugar — 40 g
- Rice vinegar — 20 ml
- Ginger, grated — 25 g
- Jasmine or sushi rice — 640 g
- Cucumbers — 400 g
- Garlic, grated — 8 g
- Soy sauce — 25 ml
- Toasted sesame oil — 15 ml
- Chile flakes — 2 g
- Sesame seeds — 15 g
- Spring onions — 40 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Score aubergine flesh in a deep diamond crosshatch; brush with oil, salt lightly.
2. Roast cut-side down at 200 °C fan for 20 minutes until collapsing.
3. Glaze: whisk miso, mirin, sake, sugar and vinegar over low heat 2 minutes to a gloss.
4. Flip aubergines, paint thickly with glaze; grill at 240 °C for 4–6 minutes until blistered and lacquered.
5. Rice: cook with the ginger stirred through the water (1:1.1, 12 minutes, rest 10).
6. Smack cucumbers with a rolling pin, tear, dress with garlic, soy, sesame oil, chile; drain 5 minutes.
7. Finish aubergine with sesame and spring onion.

## Plating & Service
One glazed half over ginger rice, cucumber tumbled alongside, glaze pooling in the crosshatch.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Glaze keeps a month and lifts salmon, cabbage and pork chops. Aubergines par-roast in the afternoon; the 5-minute grill finish is service-proof.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: tamari. Not vegan-required? A 6-minute egg on top makes it a crew favourite. Swap aubergine for thick courgette in a pinch (roast 12 min).
