# Crew Breakfast Burrito Tray with Chorizo, Potato & Manchego

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Foil-wrapped, one-handed crew fuel — make Sunday, reheat all week, eat on the passerelle between guest services.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Flour tortillas (25 cm) — 10 (600 g)
- Eggs — 12 (720 g)
- Cooking chorizo, diced — 300 g
- Waxy potatoes, 1 cm dice — 500 g
- Onion, diced — 150 g
- Red pepper, diced — 150 g
- Manchego, grated — 180 g
- Black beans, drained — 240 g
- Smoked paprika — 4 g
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Hot sauce — 30 ml
- Coriander — 15 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Roast potatoes with olive oil, paprika and salt at 210 °C for 25 minutes until crisp.
2. Fry chorizo 5 minutes until its oil runs; add onion and pepper, cook 5 minutes more.
3. Soft-scramble the eggs in the chorizo pan over low heat, 4 minutes, slightly underdone.
4. Fold together potatoes, chorizo mix, eggs, beans and coriander.
5. Fill tortillas (about 180 g each), add Manchego and hot sauce, roll tight and wrap in foil.
6. Serve warm, or chill/freeze; reheat foiled at 180 °C for 15 minutes (25 from frozen).

## Plating & Service
Halve on the bias and stack cut-side up for the crew mess, hot sauce bottle alongside.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Freezer-friendly for 6 weeks — the ultimate rough-weather handheld: no plate, no fork, no spill. Batch of 20 fits one hotel pan.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian: swap chorizo for 250 g mushrooms fried hard with 6 g smoked paprika. GF: corn tortillas, double-wrapped so they don't split.
