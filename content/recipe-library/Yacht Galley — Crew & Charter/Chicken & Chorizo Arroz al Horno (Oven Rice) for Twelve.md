# Chicken & Chorizo Arroz al Horno (Oven Rice) for Twelve

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Paella's oven-baked cousin: no stirring, no socarrat anxiety, one roasting tray of saffron rice for a full crew mess.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Chicken thighs, boneless, halved — 1.4 kg
- Cooking chorizo, thick coins — 400 g
- Bomba or paella rice — 800 g
- Chicken stock, hot — 2 l
- Saffron — 0.4 g
- Smoked paprika — 10 g
- Onion, diced — 300 g
- Red pepper, strips — 300 g
- Garlic — 25 g
- Chopped tomatoes — 400 g
- Butter beans, cooked — 480 g
- Olive oil — 60 ml
- Rosemary — 4 g
- Lemons — 2
- Fine sea salt — 14 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 200 °C fan. Bloom saffron in 100 ml hot stock.
2. Brown chorizo then chicken in oil in the roasting tray on the hob; set meat aside.
3. Soften onion and pepper 8 minutes in the rendered fat; add garlic, paprika, tomatoes; cook 4 minutes to a sofrito.
4. Stir in rice to coat; add all stock, saffron, beans, rosemary, salt; nestle chicken and chorizo back in.
5. Bake uncovered 30–35 minutes — no stirring — until stock is absorbed and the top is freckled.
6. Rest 8 minutes under a cloth; serve with charred lemon halves.

## Plating & Service
Tray to table, lemon squeezed over at the last second; scrape the crusty edges for whoever earned them.

> [!note] Galley notes
> The no-babysit paella: once it's in the oven you're free for guest service. Sofrito can be made 3 days ahead in bulk. Rough weather: rice trays behave better than any pan on the hob.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Seafood version: swap chicken for 800 g prawns + mussels added at minute 20. Vegan: double beans, artichokes, veg stock.
