# Spiced Chickpea Crunch with Lime & Chaat Masala

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Roasted-glass chickpeas with a sour-salty chaat hit — protein that masquerades as crisps and costs almost nothing.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Chickpeas, cooked, drained — 720 g
- Neutral oil — 40 ml
- Ground cumin — 5 g
- Smoked paprika — 5 g
- Ground coriander — 4 g
- Turmeric — 2 g
- Chaat masala — 8 g
- Cayenne — 1 g
- Lime zest — 3 g
- Lime juice — 15 ml
- Fine sea salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Dry chickpeas obsessively on cloth — 10 minutes of patting; loose skins off. Dryness = crunch.
2. Roast plain (no oil) at 200 °C fan for 20 minutes on a lined tray, shaking once.
3. Toss with oil, cumin, paprika, coriander, turmeric, cayenne, salt.
4. Roast 15–20 minutes more until they rattle like gravel on the tray.
5. Kill the oven; leave them inside 20 minutes with the door cracked — the drying finish.
6. Toss with chaat masala, lime zest and juice while warm.

## Plating & Service
Bowls at apéro or zip bags in the cockpit pocket — they hold crunch for 4–5 days airtight.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Two tins of chickpeas become a week of snacks for pennies; the double-roast + oven-rest is the anti-sog insurance a humid boat needs.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Flavour flexes: za'atar + lemon, or rosemary + Parmesan (not vegan). Rain them over salads and soups as GF croutons.
