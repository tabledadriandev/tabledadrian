# Blackened Fish Tacos with Charred Pineapple Salsa & Lime Crema

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The beach-day headliner: spice-crusted fish off the plancha, salsa with plancha-scorched pineapple, tortillas by the stack.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Firm white fish fillets (mahi, cod, sea bream) — 1.2 kg
- Blackening rub: smoked paprika 12 g, cumin 8 g, garlic powder 6 g, dried oregano 4 g, cayenne 3 g, black pepper 3 g, fine sea salt 12 g
- Neutral oil — 50 ml
- Pineapple, 1 cm rings — 500 g
- Red onion, fine dice — 120 g
- Jalapeño, minced — 20 g
- Coriander — 30 g
- Lime juice — 60 ml
- Soured cream — 250 g
- Lime zest — 4 g
- Corn tortillas — 24 (720 g)
- Red cabbage, shaved — 300 g
- Fine sea salt — 4 g

## Method
1. Char pineapple rings on a 250 °C plancha 2 minutes per side; dice; mix with onion, jalapeño, half the coriander, 30 ml lime, salt.
2. Crema: whisk soured cream with lime zest and 20 ml lime juice.
3. Pat fish dry; press the blackening rub on both sides.
4. Sear in oil on the plancha, 2–3 minutes per side, until the crust is near-black and the fish flakes (55 °C); rest 2 minutes, chunk.
5. Toast tortillas 15 seconds per side; hold in a cloth.
6. Build: crema, cabbage, fish, salsa, coriander; remaining lime over.

## Plating & Service
Taco-bar style on deck — components in bowls, tortillas doubled, everyone builds three.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Rub jars up for the season; salsa and crema hold a day. Fish cooks in one 6-minute plancha round for 8 — the definition of beach-club service with a two-burner setup.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: blackened cauliflower steaks + cashew crema. Swap pineapple for mango if that's what the market had.
