# Açaí Deck Bowl with Grilled Pineapple & Buckwheat Crunch

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Charter-standard açaí done properly — thick, glossy, and layered with a toasted buckwheat crunch that keeps its snap in the heat.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Frozen açaí purée — 400 g
- Frozen banana — 300 g
- Frozen blueberries — 150 g
- Apple juice — 100 ml
- Pineapple, peeled, 2 cm rings — 300 g
- Coconut oil — 15 g
- Buckwheat groats — 60 g
- Coconut flakes — 30 g
- Cacao nibs — 20 g
- Maple syrup — 25 ml
- Lime zest — 2 g
- Almond butter — 40 g
- Fine sea salt — 1 g

## Method
1. Toast buckwheat, coconut flakes and cacao nibs with 10 ml maple and salt at 160 °C for 8 minutes; cool crisp.
2. Grill pineapple rings on a 220 °C plancha in coconut oil, 2 minutes per side, until caramelised; dice.
3. Blend açaí, banana, blueberries and apple juice to a thick, spoon-standing purée.
4. Divide between chilled bowls.
5. Top with grilled pineapple, buckwheat crunch, a ripple of almond butter, remaining maple and lime zest.

## Plating & Service
Chilled coupe bowls, toppings in ordered bands; serve within 5 minutes before the sun wins.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Crunch keeps 2 weeks airtight; fruit pre-bagged per service. A 4-minute blender job per sitting — run two rounds for 8+ covers rather than overloading the jug.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: tahini instead of almond butter. Protein: 50 g plant protein into the blend. Off-season: swap pineapple for roasted plums.
