# Huevos Rancheros with Charred Salsa Roja & Refried Black Beans

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Every component batch-makes ahead, so the morning is just eggs and assembly — big flavour with small galley footprint.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Corn tortillas — 12 (360 g)
- Eggs — 6
- Plum tomatoes — 500 g
- White onion — 150 g
- Jalapeños — 30 g
- Garlic, unpeeled — 15 g
- Chipotle in adobo — 20 g
- Black beans, cooked — 480 g
- Cumin, ground — 3 g
- Olive oil — 60 ml
- Lime — 1 (juice 25 ml)
- Feta or cotija — 100 g
- Coriander — 15 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Char tomatoes, onion quarters, jalapeños and garlic under a 250 °C grill 8–10 minutes until blackened in patches.
2. Peel garlic; blitz everything with chipotle, salt and lime to a coarse salsa; simmer 5 minutes in 15 ml oil.
3. Mash beans with cumin and 100 ml water over medium heat 5 minutes to a rough purée; season.
4. Fry tortillas in oil 30 seconds per side until leathery-crisp; drain.
5. Fry eggs sunny-side up over medium heat, 3 minutes, edges lacy.
6. Layer: two tortillas, beans, egg, warm salsa, cheese, coriander.

## Plating & Service
One egg per stacked plate for brunch, or double up for crew; lime wedges and extra salsa on the table.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Salsa and beans hold 4 days and freeze — reheat while the pan does eggs. In a swell, scramble instead of fry and serve as loaded tacos.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: swap eggs for 300 g scrambled tofu with 2 g turmeric; skip cheese. Add shredded leftover roast pork for a crew-lunch remix.
