# Sicilian Caponata with Burrata & Grilled Ciabatta

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sweet-sour aubergine stew served at room temperature with cold burrata — the make-ahead lunch that peaks on day two, exactly when you need it.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Aubergines, 3 cm dice — 1 kg
- Olive oil — 150 ml
- Celery, sliced — 200 g
- Red onion, diced — 200 g
- Chopped tomatoes — 400 g
- Green olives, pitted — 120 g
- Capers, rinsed — 50 g
- Raisins — 60 g
- Pine nuts, toasted — 50 g
- Red wine vinegar — 60 ml
- Caster sugar — 40 g
- Basil — 20 g
- Burrata — 4 × 125 g
- Ciabatta — 500 g
- Fine sea salt — 12 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Salt aubergine 20 minutes; dry. Fry in 100 ml oil in batches over high heat until deeply golden and creamy inside; drain.
2. Blanch celery 2 minutes; drain.
3. Soften onion in 30 ml oil; add tomatoes, simmer 10 minutes.
4. Add celery, olives, capers, raisins; dissolve sugar in the vinegar and add; simmer 5 minutes to agrodolce balance.
5. Fold in aubergine gently; simmer 3 minutes; rest off heat. Cool to room temperature — never serve hot; ideally rest overnight.
6. Grill oiled ciabatta; serve caponata with torn burrata, pine nuts, basil and 20 ml raw oil.

## Plating & Service
Caponata spread low on a platter, burrata torn over at the table so the cream runs into the agrodolce.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Improves for 3 days chilled (serve at room temp) — the definition of make-ahead deck food. Burrata is the only cold-chain item; everything else shrugs at the heat.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: skip burrata, finish with whipped tahini. Add swordfish chunks seared separately for a Siracusa-style main. GF: polenta crostini instead of ciabatta.
