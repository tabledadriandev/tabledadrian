# Smoked Paprika & Manchego Popcorn for Movie Night

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Corn kernels take no locker space and turn into a mountain — the cheapest, fastest crowd-pleaser on the boat.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Popcorn kernels — 150 g
- Neutral oil — 40 ml
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Smoked paprika — 6 g
- Garlic powder — 3 g
- Manchego, finely grated — 80 g
- Cayenne — 0.5 g
- Fine sea salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Heat oil with 3 kernels in a large lidded pot over medium-high; when they pop, add the rest in one layer.
2. Lid on, shake every 20 seconds until pops slow to 2 seconds apart, about 4 minutes; tip into the biggest bowl aboard.
3. Melt butter; stir in paprika, garlic powder, cayenne — 30 seconds off heat so the spice blooms, never burns.
4. Drizzle over the popcorn in three additions, tossing between each.
5. Salt, then shower with Manchego while still warm so it clings.

## Plating & Service
One enormous communal bowl plus paper cones for the bunks — deck cinema protocol.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Kernels store for a year; the whole job is 10 minutes and one pot. Batch two rounds rather than overfilling — scorched popcorn haunts a galley for days.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: olive oil instead of butter, 15 g nutritional yeast for the cheese. Sweet watch: butter + 30 g honey + flaky salt.
