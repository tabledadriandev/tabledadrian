# Whole Grilled Sea Bream with Charred Lemon, Oregano & Ladolemono

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Fish over flame with the ladolemono whisked in a jar — the beach BBQ centrepiece that needs exactly five ingredients and confidence.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Whole sea bream, gutted, scaled — 3 × 600 g
- Olive oil — 100 ml
- Lemons — 4
- Dried Greek oregano — 8 g
- Garlic, sliced — 10 g
- Bay leaves — 6
- Flaky sea salt — 12 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 15 g

## Method
1. Dry the fish inside and out — dry skin is non-negotiable for release. Slash each side three times to the bone.
2. Stuff cavities with lemon slices, garlic, bay; rub skins with 30 ml oil and salt.
3. Grill over medium-hot coals (or 240 °C plancha) 8–10 minutes per side without touching — it releases when ready. Core 55 °C at the shoulder.
4. Halve remaining lemons; char cut-side down 4 minutes.
5. Ladolemono: shake 70 ml oil, juice of the charred lemons, oregano, salt and pepper in a jar to a cloudy emulsion.
6. Rest fish 3 minutes; spoon over half the sauce, parsley over.

## Plating & Service
Whole fish on a board, filleted at the table in front of guests; remaining ladolemono in the jar for the rice or bread.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Three fish feed six with sides; the fishmonger does the knife work at provisioning. If the grill's out (wind), a 220 °C oven for 20 minutes gets 90% there.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Any whole fish — loup de mer, dorade royale, snapper. Add a fennel salad and it's a full deck lunch; swap bream for halloumi slabs for vegetarians on the same grill.
