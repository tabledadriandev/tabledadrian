# Cinnamon Apple Dutch Baby with Calvados Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> One blender, one skillet, maximum drama — the puffed pancake arrives at the table still climbing the pan walls.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Eggs — 4 (240 g)
- Whole milk — 160 ml
- Plain flour — 130 g
- Caster sugar — 30 g
- Vanilla paste — 4 g
- Fine sea salt — 2 g
- Unsalted butter — 70 g
- Apples, 5 mm slices — 300 g
- Light brown sugar — 40 g
- Cinnamon — 4 g
- Calvados — 25 ml
- Icing sugar — 10 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml

## Method
1. Heat oven to 210 °C fan with a 26 cm cast-iron skillet inside for 10 minutes.
2. Blend eggs, milk, flour, caster sugar, vanilla and salt 20 seconds; rest while the pan heats.
3. Sauté apples in 30 g butter with brown sugar and cinnamon 4 minutes until glossy; add Calvados, flame or reduce 1 minute; remove half for topping.
4. Drop 25 g butter into the screaming-hot skillet, swirl, add the pan apples, pour in the batter.
5. Bake 16–18 minutes without opening the door — it should balloon and bronze.
6. Beat remaining 15 g butter into the reserved warm apples; spoon over, add lemon and icing sugar.

## Plating & Service
Straight to the table in the skillet on a thick board; it deflates in 90 seconds, so let guests see the puff.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Batter holds overnight chilled (better, even). One skillet serves 4; run two skillets staggered for 8. Needs a hot, level oven — an anchorage dish, not a passage dish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skip Calvados for apple juice reduction (kid-friendly). Savoury version: omit sugar/apple, bake with Gruyère and serve with ham.
