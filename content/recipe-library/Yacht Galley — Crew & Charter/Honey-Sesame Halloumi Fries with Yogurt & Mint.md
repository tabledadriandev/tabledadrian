# Honey-Sesame Halloumi Fries with Yogurt & Mint

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Golden batons, hot honey, cold yogurt — the snack that makes the swim platform smell like a Limassol beach bar.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Halloumi — 750 g
- Cornflour — 50 g
- Smoked paprika — 4 g
- Neutral oil — 200 ml
- Honey — 50 g
- Chile flakes — 2 g
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Sesame seeds, toasted — 15 g
- Greek yogurt — 250 g
- Mint, chopped — 8 g
- Lemon zest — 2 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Cut halloumi into 1.5 × 8 cm batons; pat completely dry.
2. Toss in cornflour mixed with paprika; shake off excess.
3. Shallow-fry at 180 °C in two batches, 2 minutes per side, to a deep golden shell; drain on a rack.
4. Warm honey with chile and lemon juice to 60 °C.
5. Stir mint and lemon zest through the yogurt.
6. Stack the fries, pour the hot honey over, sesame on top; yogurt alongside.

## Plating & Service
A jenga stack on a small board, honey glossing down the sides — serve within 5 minutes while the squeak lasts.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Batons cut and dust ahead; the fry is 8 minutes for 8 people. Halloumi's shelf life makes this a permanent option weeks from provisioning.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Bake option: 220 °C, 12 minutes, oiled tray — 85% as good, zero oil pot. Vegan: extra-firm tofu pressed hard, same coat and fry.
