# Whole Roasted Cauliflower Shawarma with Tahini & Pomegranate

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A burnished whole cauliflower carved at the table like a roast — the vegan main that doesn't feel like an apology.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Cauliflowers, whole — 2 (1.6 kg)
- Olive oil — 90 ml
- Shawarma spice (cumin 8 g, coriander 6 g, paprika 6 g, turmeric 3 g, cinnamon 2 g, allspice 2 g)
- Garlic, grated — 15 g
- Lemon juice — 50 ml
- Maple syrup — 20 g
- Tahini — 120 g
- Iced water — 80 ml
- Pomegranate seeds — 120 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 20 g
- Pine nuts, toasted — 40 g
- Pomegranate molasses — 20 ml
- Fine sea salt — 12 g

## Method
1. Trim cauliflower bases flat, keeping leaves; simmer whole in well-salted water 8 minutes; drain 10 minutes.
2. Whisk oil, shawarma spice, garlic, 20 ml lemon, maple and 8 g salt; brush all over, into every crevice.
3. Roast at 190 °C fan for 50–60 minutes, basting twice, until a skewer slides in and the crust is deep bronze.
4. Tahini sauce: whisk tahini with remaining lemon, 4 g salt and iced water until pale and pourable.
5. Rest 5 minutes; carve into wedges.
6. Dress with tahini, pomegranate seeds, molasses, parsley and pine nuts.

## Plating & Service
Whole to the table on a board, carved in front of guests; sauce pooling beneath the wedges.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Par-simmer and spice-brush in the afternoon; final roast is hands-off during service. Leftover wedges become tomorrow's grain-bowl hero. Two cauliflowers ride one oven shelf.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: skip pine nuts, add toasted pumpkin seeds. Serve with flatbread + pickles as a build-your-own shawarma night for crew.
