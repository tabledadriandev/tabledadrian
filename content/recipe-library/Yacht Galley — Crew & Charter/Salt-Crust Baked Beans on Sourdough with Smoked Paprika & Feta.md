# Salt-Crust Baked Beans on Sourdough with Smoked Paprika & Feta

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Proper from-scratch beans that live in the fridge all week — the crew breakfast that tastes better on day three.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Dried cannellini beans, soaked overnight — 500 g
- Onion, diced — 200 g
- Carrot, fine dice — 100 g
- Celery, fine dice — 80 g
- Garlic — 15 g
- Olive oil — 45 ml
- Chopped tomatoes — 800 g
- Tomato paste — 40 g
- Smoked paprika — 8 g
- Maple syrup — 25 ml
- Red wine vinegar — 20 ml
- Bay leaves — 2
- Sourdough — 500 g
- Feta — 150 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Simmer soaked beans in fresh water with bay at 95 °C for 40–50 minutes until tender; drain, reserving 200 ml liquor.
2. Sweat onion, carrot, celery in oil 8 minutes; add garlic and paprika, 1 minute.
3. Add tomatoes, paste, maple, vinegar, beans and liquor; simmer 20 minutes to a glossy, thick sauce; season.
4. Grill thick sourdough slices at 220 °C, 3 minutes per side.
5. Ladle beans over toast, crumble feta, extra paprika oil to finish.

## Plating & Service
Crew mess: pot on the table, toast stacked, feta in a bowl — self-service.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Makes 2.5 kg of beans; holds 5 days chilled, freezes 3 months. Reheats in one pot in any weather — spoon over toast, rice or a jacket potato.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: skip feta or use almond feta. Tinned shortcut: 3 × 400 g cannellini, simmer 15 minutes only. Add merguez for a hunt-breakfast version.
