# Roasted Squash, Sage & Brown Butter Risotto for a Crowd

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Half-baked, half-stirred: the oven method that gives real risotto texture without 25 minutes chained to the hob mid-service.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Carnaroli or arborio rice — 640 g
- Butternut squash, 2 cm dice — 1 kg
- Olive oil — 50 ml
- Unsalted butter — 120 g
- Sage leaves — 20
- Onion, fine dice — 200 g
- Garlic — 15 g
- White wine — 200 ml
- Vegetable stock, hot — 2 l
- Parmesan, grated — 140 g
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Nutmeg — 0.5 g
- Fine sea salt — 12 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Roast squash with oil and salt at 210 °C fan for 25 minutes until caramelised; mash half, keep half whole.
2. Brown 80 g butter with sage to 150 °C until nutty; lift out crisp leaves; reserve both.
3. Sweat onion in 40 g butter 6 minutes; add garlic and rice, toast 2 minutes; deglaze with wine.
4. Add 1.6 l hot stock, cover, into a 180 °C oven for 18 minutes.
5. Return to the hob: stir hard, adding remaining stock, 4–5 minutes to all'onda — rice al dente, sauce flowing.
6. Beat in mashed squash, brown butter, Parmesan, lemon, nutmeg; fold in whole squash; rest 2 minutes lidded.

## Plating & Service
Flat plates, risotto shaken to a wave, crisp sage and Parmesan over; it must spread, not stand.

> [!note] Galley notes
> The oven stage frees you for 18 minutes of plating starters — the only way to run risotto for 8+ solo. Squash and brown butter prep the day before.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: oil for butter, 25 g white miso for Parmesan. Spring version: peas + asparagus, mint instead of sage.
