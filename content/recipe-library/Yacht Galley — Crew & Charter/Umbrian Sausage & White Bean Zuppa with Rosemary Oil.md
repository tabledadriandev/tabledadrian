# Umbrian Sausage & White Bean Zuppa with Rosemary Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A thick, thermos-ready hillside soup — the rough-weather dinner that still feels like Italy, not like rations.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Italian sausages, skinned — 600 g
- Olive oil — 80 ml
- Onion, diced — 250 g
- Carrot, diced — 200 g
- Celery, diced — 150 g
- Garlic — 25 g
- Chile flakes — 2 g
- Rosemary — 8 g
- Cannellini beans, cooked — 1.2 kg
- Chopped tomatoes — 400 g
- Chicken stock — 1.8 l
- Cavolo nero, ribboned — 300 g
- Ditalini or broken spaghetti — 200 g
- Parmesan rind — 1 (optional)
- Fine sea salt — 12 g
- Black pepper — 3 g

## Method
1. Brown sausage meat in 30 ml oil, breaking up; add soffritto; sweat 10 minutes.
2. Add garlic and chile, 1 minute; add beans, tomatoes, stock, Parmesan rind; simmer 20 minutes.
3. Blitz 500 ml of the soup and stir back — body without cream.
4. Add pasta; cook 9–10 minutes. Add cavolo nero for the last 4 minutes; season.
5. Warm remaining 50 ml oil with rosemary to 60 °C; infuse 10 minutes, strain.
6. Serve with the rosemary oil zig-zagged over.

## Plating & Service
Deep bowls or 500 ml flasks; grilled bread if the sea allows plates.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Stop before the pasta and the base freezes 3 months — add pasta on reheat so it never blows out. Spoon-standing thickness is deliberate: it stays in the bowl on a heel.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: skip sausage, add 8 g smoked paprika + 300 g mushrooms. GF: GF pasta or 300 g potatoes instead.
