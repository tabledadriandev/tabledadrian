# Asparagus & Ricotta Breakfast Tart with Lemon Thyme

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> All-butter puff, a whipped ricotta base and spring asparagus — a brunch centrepiece that's 80% assembled the night before.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- All-butter puff pastry sheet — 500 g
- Ricotta — 300 g
- Egg yolks — 2 (40 g)
- Parmesan, grated — 50 g
- Lemon zest — 4 g
- Lemon thyme leaves — 4 g
- Asparagus, trimmed — 500 g
- Olive oil — 25 ml
- Egg (wash) — 1
- Pine nuts — 30 g
- Honey — 10 g
- Fine sea salt — 5 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 200 °C fan with a heavy tray inside.
2. Roll pastry to 35 × 25 cm on parchment; score a 2 cm border, dock the centre.
3. Whip ricotta with yolks, Parmesan, zest, half the thyme, salt and pepper; spread inside the border.
4. Toss asparagus in oil and salt; lay in tight parallel rows over the ricotta; scatter pine nuts.
5. Egg-wash the border; slide onto the hot tray, bake 25–30 minutes until the base is bronzed and crisp.
6. Rest 5 minutes; finish with honey, remaining thyme and pepper.

## Plating & Service
Cut into 8 rectangles at the table; serve with a lemon-dressed leaf salad for brunch service.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Assemble fully (minus egg wash) and hold chilled overnight — morning is wash-and-bake. Serves warm or room temp on a buffet, so it forgives delayed guests.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: dairy-free puff, whipped white-bean purée instead of ricotta. Swap asparagus for roasted courgette ribbons in high summer.
