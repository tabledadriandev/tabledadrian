# Golden Gazpacho with Charentais Melon & Basil Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Yellow tomatoes and melon blitzed to cold silk — the deck lunch opener that requires no flame in a heatwave.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Yellow tomatoes, ripe — 1 kg
- Charentais or cantaloupe melon — 400 g
- Yellow pepper — 200 g
- Cucumber, peeled — 250 g
- Shallot — 40 g
- Garlic — 5 g
- Sherry vinegar — 25 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 100 ml
- Basil — 25 g
- Fine sea salt — 10 g
- White pepper — 1 g
- Iced water — 100 ml

## Method
1. Blitz basil with 50 ml oil and a pinch of salt; strain through fine mesh for a green oil.
2. Blend tomatoes, melon, pepper, cucumber, shallot, garlic, vinegar and salt on high 2 minutes until completely smooth.
3. Stream in remaining 50 ml oil to emulsify; adjust body with iced water.
4. Pass through a chinois for silk; season — cold food needs more salt and acid than you think.
5. Chill to 4 °C, minimum 2 hours.
6. Serve in chilled glasses or bowls with basil oil beads and a crack of white pepper.

## Plating & Service
Chilled coupe glasses on deck before lunch, or bowls with diced melon and cucumber as garnish for the table.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Holds 3 days and actually improves overnight — blend a triple batch on prep day. Serve in tumblers for the beach cooler: a drinkable course, zero cutlery.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Classic red version: red tomatoes + 80 g bread (not GF). Add a chiffonade of jamón for the omnivores. Spike with 20 ml fino sherry for apéro hour.
