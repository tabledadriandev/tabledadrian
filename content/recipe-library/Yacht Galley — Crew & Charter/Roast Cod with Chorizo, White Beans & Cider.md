# Roast Cod with Chorizo, White Beans & Cider

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A one-pan Basque-ish braise where the chorizo fat seasons everything — fish-counter flexible and 30 minutes end to end.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Cod loins — 8 × 150 g
- Cooking chorizo, coins — 300 g
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Onion, sliced — 250 g
- Fennel, sliced — 200 g
- Garlic — 20 g
- Smoked paprika — 6 g
- Dry cider — 200 ml
- White beans, cooked — 960 g
- Chicken stock — 300 ml
- Cherry tomatoes — 250 g
- Bay leaf — 1
- Parsley — 20 g
- Lemon — 1
- Fine sea salt — 10 g

## Method
1. Brown chorizo in oil in a wide sauté pan; add onion and fennel, soften 8 minutes.
2. Add garlic and paprika, 1 minute; deglaze with cider, reduce by half.
3. Add beans, stock, tomatoes, bay; simmer 8 minutes and season.
4. Season cod; nestle into the beans.
5. Lid on, poach-braise 8–10 minutes at a gentle simmer until the cod flakes at 52 °C.
6. Rest 2 minutes; lemon juice and parsley over everything.

## Plating & Service
Shallow bowls: beans first, cod lifted whole on top, paprika-stained broth spooned around.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Bean base holds 3 days — fish drops in to order, guest or crew. Any white fish works; in rough weather serve in mugs as a chunky stew with the cod flaked through.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: skip fish/chorizo; add 6 g smoked paprika + 400 g roasted peppers, finish with almond picada. Halal: swap chorizo for smoked paprika + 20 ml extra oil.
