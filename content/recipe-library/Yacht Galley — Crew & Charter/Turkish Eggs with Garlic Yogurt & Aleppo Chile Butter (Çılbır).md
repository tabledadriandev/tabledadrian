# Turkish Eggs with Garlic Yogurt & Aleppo Chile Butter (Çılbır)

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Warm yogurt, poached eggs and sizzling chile butter — five ingredients that eat like a tasting-menu course at 08:00.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Greek yogurt — 400 g
- Garlic, finely grated — 8 g
- Eggs — 8
- Unsalted butter — 70 g
- Aleppo pepper (pul biber) — 6 g
- Sweet paprika — 2 g
- Dill — 6 g
- Mint leaves — 4 g
- White vinegar — 30 ml
- Fine sea salt — 4 g
- Sourdough, grilled — 200 g

## Method
1. Whisk yogurt with garlic and salt; bring to room temperature over a 40 °C bain-marie — warm, never hot, or it splits.
2. Poach eggs at 92 °C with vinegar for 3 minutes; drain on cloth.
3. Foam butter to 140 °C, pull off heat, stir in Aleppo pepper and paprika — it should fizz, not burn.
4. Pool warm yogurt in shallow bowls, seat two eggs per bowl.
5. Spoon over the chile butter; scatter dill and mint. Serve with grilled sourdough.

## Plating & Service
Wide shallow bowls; break the yolks at the table so gold, white and red marble together.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Yogurt base and pre-poached eggs both hold overnight — final assembly is 4 minutes for 8 covers. In rough weather serve in mugs with the bread as a dipper.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: serve with charred GF flatbread. Vegan: 63 °C-style silken tofu rounds over cashew yogurt, chile oil instead of butter.
