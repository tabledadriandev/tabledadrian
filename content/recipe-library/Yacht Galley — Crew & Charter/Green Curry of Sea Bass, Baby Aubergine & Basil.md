# Green Curry of Sea Bass, Baby Aubergine & Basil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Paste pounded from scratch and fish poached gently in the coconut broth — a guest-table green curry with real bite and perfume.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Sea bass fillets, skinless, 4 cm pieces — 1.2 kg
- Green bird's-eye chiles — 30 g
- Large green chiles — 60 g
- Lemongrass — 60 g
- Galangal or ginger — 30 g
- Garlic — 25 g
- Shallots — 80 g
- Coriander root/stem — 20 g
- Makrut lime zest — 3 g
- Shrimp paste — 10 g
- White peppercorns — 2 g
- Coconut milk — 1 l
- Fish sauce — 45 ml
- Palm sugar — 30 g
- Baby aubergines, quartered — 400 g
- Makrut lime leaves — 6
- Thai basil — 30 g
- Jasmine rice — 640 g
- Lime juice — 20 ml

## Method
1. Pound (or blitz) chiles, lemongrass, galangal, garlic, shallots, coriander root, lime zest, shrimp paste and peppercorns to a fine paste.
2. Crack 200 ml thick coconut cream in a wok over medium heat until the oil splits, 4 minutes.
3. Fry the paste in the split cream 5–6 minutes until fragrant and darker.
4. Add remaining coconut milk, palm sugar, fish sauce, lime leaves; simmer 5 minutes; add aubergine, cook 6 minutes.
5. Slide in sea bass; poach at a bare simmer 4–5 minutes until just flaking (52 °C).
6. Off heat: lime juice and basil. Taste — hot, salty, sweet, in that order.

## Plating & Service
Over jasmine rice in wide bowls, basil crown, extra chiles for the heat-seekers.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Paste keeps 10 days chilled or 3 months frozen in ice-cube trays — pound once, curry six times. Fish goes in only at service; the base holds warm for an hour without harm.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken thigh version simmers 12 min. Vegan: skip shrimp paste + fish sauce, use 15 g white miso + salt, tofu and bamboo shoots.
