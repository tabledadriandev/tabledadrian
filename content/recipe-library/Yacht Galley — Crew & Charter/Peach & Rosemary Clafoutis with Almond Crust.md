# Peach & Rosemary Clafoutis with Almond Crust

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Batter over fruit, one dish, forty minutes — the harvest-season dessert that bakes itself while the mains go out.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Peaches, ripe, thick wedges — 700 g
- Eggs — 4 (240 g)
- Caster sugar — 120 g
- Ground almonds — 80 g
- Cornflour — 30 g
- Double cream — 250 ml
- Whole milk — 150 ml
- Vanilla paste — 6 g
- Rosemary, very finely minced — 1.5 g
- Unsalted butter — 30 g
- Amaretto (optional) — 20 ml
- Icing sugar — 15 g
- Fine sea salt — 2 g
- Flaked almonds — 30 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 180 °C fan; butter a 28 cm ceramic dish and dust with 20 g of the sugar.
2. Arrange peach wedges in one snug layer.
3. Whisk eggs and remaining sugar 2 minutes; whisk in ground almonds, cornflour, salt, then cream, milk, vanilla, rosemary, amaretto.
4. Pour the batter around (not over) the fruit; scatter flaked almonds.
5. Bake 35–40 minutes until puffed, gold, and just set at the centre (a knife comes out clean-ish).
6. Rest 15 minutes — it settles and slices; dust with icing sugar.

## Plating & Service
Warm, spooned from the dish at the table with cold crème fraîche or vanilla ice cream.

> [!note] Galley notes
> One bowl, one whisk, one dish — the lowest-washing-up dessert with genuine guest polish. Batter holds 24 h chilled; fruit swaps with whatever the market softened.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Classic: cherries, unpitted (flavour) or pitted (peace). Swap rosemary for lemon thyme or nothing. Dairy-free: coconut cream + oat milk.
