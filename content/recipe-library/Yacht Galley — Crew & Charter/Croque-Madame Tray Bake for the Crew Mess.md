# Croque-Madame Tray Bake for the Crew Mess

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Ten croques on one sheet pan with a proper Mornay — bistro comfort scaled for a hungry deck team.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Pain de mie slices — 20 (900 g)
- Cooked ham — 400 g
- Gruyère, grated — 300 g
- Unsalted butter — 50 g
- Plain flour — 40 g
- Whole milk — 500 ml
- Dijon mustard — 20 g
- Nutmeg, grated — 1 g
- Eggs — 10
- Chives — 8 g
- Fine sea salt — 5 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Make the Mornay: melt butter, cook flour 2 minutes, whisk in milk gradually; simmer 5 minutes to nappe, season with Dijon, nutmeg, salt; fold in 100 g Gruyère.
2. Lay 10 bread slices on a lined sheet pan, spread with Mornay, layer ham and 100 g Gruyère, lid with remaining bread.
3. Spread tops with remaining Mornay and Gruyère.
4. Bake at 200 °C fan for 15–18 minutes until bronzed and bubbling.
5. Meanwhile fry eggs in batches at medium heat, whites set, yolks liquid (about 3 minutes).
6. Seat one egg on each croque, scatter chives, grind pepper.

## Plating & Service
Serve straight from the pan with cornichons and a sharply dressed little gem salad.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Assemble the whole tray the night before and hold chilled — 18 minutes from oven-on to crew fed. In a swell skip the fried egg and it's a one-handed croque-monsieur.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap ham for wilted spinach and roasted mushrooms (vegetarian). GF: sturdy GF sandwich loaf, GF flour in the Mornay.
