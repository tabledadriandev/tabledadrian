# Crew Quesadilla Rounds with Leftover Roast & Pickled Jalapeño

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The official afterlife of every roast on board — crisped in a dry pan, cut in wedges, gone before the pan cools.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Flour tortillas (25 cm) — 8 (480 g)
- Leftover roast meat, shredded — 400 g
- Mature cheddar or Comté, grated — 300 g
- Mozzarella, grated — 150 g
- Pickled jalapeños, chopped — 60 g
- Spring onions — 50 g
- Coriander — 15 g
- Ground cumin — 3 g
- Smoked paprika — 3 g
- Soured cream — 150 g
- Lime — 1
- Hot sauce — 20 ml
- Fine sea salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Toss shredded meat with cumin, paprika, salt and a splash of water.
2. Mix both cheeses with jalapeños, spring onion and coriander.
3. On half of each tortilla: cheese, meat, more cheese (cheese on both sides is the glue); fold.
4. Dry-fry over medium heat (200 °C pan), 2–3 minutes per side, pressing, until shatter-crisp and molten.
5. Rest 1 minute; cut into three wedges each.
6. Serve with soured cream whisked with lime, and hot sauce.

## Plating & Service
Wedges shingled on a board around the lime crema — stackable, passable, one-handed.

> [!note] Galley notes
> This is the leftover programme: any roast, any cheese, 15 minutes. In weather, quesadillas beat plates — nothing slides, nothing spills, everyone's fed between tacks.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian: black beans + corn instead of meat. GF: corn tortillas, fold gently. Breakfast mode: scrambled egg + chorizo at 07:00.
