# Muffuletta-Style Pressed Picnic Loaf with Olive Salad

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> One whole loaf, hollowed, loaded, pressed overnight — sliced into wedges that survive a tender, a beach and a rockpool expedition.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Round sourdough boule — 1 (900 g)
- Mixed olives, chopped — 250 g
- Giardiniera or pickled vegetables, chopped — 200 g
- Capers — 30 g
- Roasted red peppers — 200 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml
- Red wine vinegar — 25 ml
- Dried oregano — 4 g
- Garlic, grated — 8 g
- Mortadella — 200 g
- Salami Milano — 150 g
- Coppa or ham — 150 g
- Provolone, sliced — 250 g
- Basil leaves — 15 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Olive salad: mix olives, giardiniera, capers, half the peppers, oil, vinegar, oregano, garlic, pepper; rest 30 minutes (or 3 days).
2. Slice the top off the boule; hollow both halves, leaving a 2 cm wall.
3. Spoon a third of the olive salad (with its oil) into the base, pressing into the crumb.
4. Layer: provolone, mortadella, peppers, salami, basil, coppa, provolone — pressing as you go; top with remaining olive salad.
5. Lid on; wrap the whole loaf tightly in film; press under a tray weighted with tins, 4–24 hours chilled.
6. Slice into 10 wedges through the wrap with a serrated knife.

## Plating & Service
Wedges in parchment, cut faces showing the strata — hand out like slices of savoury cake.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Built the night before beach day — zero morning work and it improves under the press. No mayo, no lettuce: nothing to wilt or weep in a cooler bag.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian: grilled aubergine and courgette replacing the meats, double provolone. The olive salad alone keeps 2 weeks — batch it for sandwiches all season.
