# One-Tray Harissa Chicken Thighs with Chickpeas, Lemon & Olives

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Fifteen minutes of prep, one sheet pan, and a pan sauce that makes itself — the definition of morale food after a double charter day.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Chicken thighs, bone-in, skin-on — 2 kg
- Harissa — 60 g
- Olive oil — 60 ml
- Chickpeas, drained — 720 g
- Red onions, wedges — 400 g
- Lemons, quartered — 2 (250 g)
- Green olives, crushed — 150 g
- Garlic, whole cloves — 30 g
- Cumin, ground — 6 g
- Coriander seed, ground — 5 g
- Honey — 20 g
- Chicken stock — 150 ml
- Coriander leaves — 20 g
- Fine sea salt — 12 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 210 °C fan.
2. Rub thighs with harissa, half the oil, cumin, coriander, honey and salt — ideally 4+ hours ahead.
3. Toss chickpeas, onions, lemons, garlic and olives with remaining oil on two sheet pans; seat chicken on top, skin up.
4. Roast 35 minutes until skin is burnished.
5. Pour stock into the pan corners (not over the skin); roast 8–10 minutes more — thighs to 85 °C internal for silky meat.
6. Rest 5 minutes; squeeze the roasted lemons over, shower with coriander.

## Plating & Service
Straight from the tray with couscous or flatbread; spoon the pan juices over everything.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Marinate in zip bags on prep day — the tray goes in whenever the crew surfaces. Scales linearly; leftovers strip into tomorrow's wraps. Chickpeas make it a complete one-tray meal.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap chicken for cauliflower steaks + extra chickpeas (vegan). Milder table: yogurt on the side rather than less harissa.
