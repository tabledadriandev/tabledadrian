# Riviera Antipasti Board with Marinated Vegetables & Whipped Ricotta

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A guest-arrival platter built almost entirely from jars, cheese and 25 minutes of roasting — abundance with a prep list that fits on a Post-it.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Courgettes, ribboned — 400 g
- Red peppers — 400 g
- Olive oil — 120 ml
- Red wine vinegar — 30 ml
- Garlic — 10 g
- Chile flakes — 2 g
- Ricotta — 400 g
- Lemon zest — 4 g
- Honey — 20 g
- Artichokes in oil, drained — 300 g
- Mixed olives — 250 g
- Sun-dried tomatoes — 150 g
- Taralli or grissini — 300 g
- Basil & mint — 20 g
- Toasted hazelnuts, crushed — 50 g
- Flaky sea salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Blister whole peppers at 240 °C for 25 minutes; steam in a covered bowl, peel, tear into ribbons.
2. Char courgette ribbons on a dry 240 °C plancha, 1 minute per side.
3. Marinate peppers and courgettes separately: oil, vinegar, garlic, chile, salt — minimum 1 hour, up to 3 days.
4. Whip ricotta with 30 ml oil, lemon zest and a pinch of salt until mousse-light; hollow a well, fill with honey.
5. Build the board: whipped ricotta central, marinated vegetables, artichokes, olives, sun-dried tomatoes in groups; hazelnuts over the ricotta.
6. Herbs torn over everything; taralli stacked at the edges.

## Plating & Service
One oversized board, colours alternating, everything touchable — refill gaps rather than rebuild.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Marinated vegetables improve for days — batch on turnaround and the arrival platter takes 10 minutes at anchor. Doubles as apéro hour for charter guests every single evening.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: whipped tofu-cashew instead of ricotta. Add bresaola and mortadella for the full carne version. GF: crudités + GF crackers.
