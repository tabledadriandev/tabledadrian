# Coconut Panna Cotta with Roasted Pineapple & Lime Caramel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> An agar-set coconut panna cotta that shrugs at heat — unmoulds cleanly on a 30 °C evening and covers vegan, GF and dairy-free cards at once.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Coconut milk — 800 ml
- Coconut cream — 200 ml
- Caster sugar — 120 g
- Agar-agar powder — 4 g
- Vanilla paste — 6 g
- Lime zest — 4 g
- Fine sea salt — 2 g
- Pineapple, peeled, 2 cm wedges — 600 g
- Light brown sugar — 60 g
- Lime juice — 40 ml
- Coconut oil — 15 g
- Toasted coconut flakes — 25 g
- Mint tips — 8

## Method
1. Whisk agar into 200 ml of the coconut milk cold; rest 5 minutes.
2. Combine all coconut milk/cream, caster sugar, vanilla, zest, salt and the agar mix; bring to a full simmer and hold 2 minutes, whisking — agar must boil to activate.
3. Pour into 8 lightly oiled dariole moulds; set at room temp 30 minutes, then chill 2 hours.
4. Roast pineapple with brown sugar and coconut oil at 220 °C fan for 18–20 minutes until caramelised at the edges.
5. Deglaze the tray with lime juice, scraping to a quick caramel; spoon over the pineapple.
6. Unmould (a fingertip press releases the vacuum); pineapple and caramel alongside, coconut flakes, mint.

## Plating & Service
A clean white dome, amber pineapple and its lime caramel in a crescent, flakes for texture.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Agar sets firm in heat where gelatine weeps — the tropical-anchorage insurance policy. Moulds hold 3 days chilled; pineapple roasts in any oven window.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Gelatine version (not vegan): 8 g leaf gelatine, silkier wobble. Swap pineapple for roasted mango or charred banana. Add 30 ml dark rum to the caramel for the adults.
