# Charred Broccoli & Anchovy Orecchiette with Pangrattato

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Puglia's cucina povera at 20 knots — broccoli cooked to a sauce, anchovy depth, and crunchy crumbs standing in for cheese.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Orecchiette — 700 g
- Broccoli or sprouting broccoli — 900 g
- Anchovy fillets — 45 g
- Garlic, sliced — 30 g
- Chile flakes — 4 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 120 ml
- Lemon — 1 (zest 4 g, juice 25 ml)
- Coarse breadcrumbs or panko — 120 g
- Capers, chopped — 30 g
- Parsley — 20 g
- Fine sea salt — 10 g

## Method
1. Pangrattato: fry crumbs in 40 ml oil with capers over medium heat 4 minutes to deep gold; salt; drain on paper.
2. Cut broccoli small; boil in the (salted) pasta water 4 minutes until soft; lift out.
3. Cook orecchiette in the same water to 2 minutes under packet time.
4. Meanwhile melt anchovies with garlic and chile in 60 ml oil over low heat, 3 minutes.
5. Add broccoli to the anchovy pan, crushing with the spoon into a rough sauce; loosen with 200 ml pasta water.
6. Toss in pasta, finishing 2 minutes; emulsify with remaining oil, lemon zest and juice; crumbs over each bowl.

## Plating & Service
Bowls with a thick cap of pangrattato — added at the table, never in the pan, or the crunch dies.

> [!note] Galley notes
> One pot + one pan, locker-stable backbone, 20 minutes for 8. Pangrattato jars up for a week and rescues any pasta, soup or roast veg going forward.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: 15 g white miso instead of anchovy. GF: GF orecchiette + GF crumbs. Add Italian sausage rubble for the hungriest watch.
