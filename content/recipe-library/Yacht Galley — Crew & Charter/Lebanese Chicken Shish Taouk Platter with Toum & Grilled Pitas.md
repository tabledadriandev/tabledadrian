# Lebanese Chicken Shish Taouk Platter with Toum & Grilled Pitas

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Yogurt-tender chicken skewers and a fierce whipped garlic toum — the platter that turns a beach BBQ into a Beirut street corner.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Chicken thighs, boneless, 4 cm — 2 kg
- Greek yogurt — 200 g
- Lemon juice — 80 ml
- Garlic — 70 g
- Tomato paste — 30 g
- Smoked paprika — 8 g
- Ground cumin — 6 g
- Ground coriander — 6 g
- Cinnamon — 2 g
- Olive oil — 60 ml
- Fine sea salt — 20 g
- Neutral oil (toum) — 350 ml
- Iced water — 30 ml
- Pitas — 10 (600 g)
- Pickled turnips & cucumbers — 300 g
- Parsley — 20 g
- Sumac — 4 g

## Method
1. Marinade: yogurt, 50 ml lemon, 20 g garlic (grated), tomato paste, all spices, olive oil, 14 g salt; massage into chicken; 4–24 hours.
2. Toum: blitz remaining 50 g garlic with 6 g salt; with the motor running, alternate thin streams of neutral oil and drops of remaining lemon + iced water until a stiff white cloud forms.
3. Thread chicken onto flat skewers, snug but not crushed.
4. Grill over hot coals or a 250 °C plancha, 3–4 minutes per side, to 74 °C with charred edges.
5. Flash pitas on the grill; halve.
6. Platter: skewers over grilled pitas (they catch the juices), pickles, parsley, sumac; toum in a bowl.

## Plating & Service
Skewers still on the metal, laid across the pita bed; guests slide, smear toum, wrap and repeat.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Toum keeps 4 weeks chilled and converts sandwiches, fish and vegetables all season — make the full amount. Chicken marinates overnight; beach cook time is 8 minutes a round.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: serve over rice, skip pita. Vegan platter: same marinade (coconut yogurt) on cauliflower + mushrooms; toum is already vegan.
