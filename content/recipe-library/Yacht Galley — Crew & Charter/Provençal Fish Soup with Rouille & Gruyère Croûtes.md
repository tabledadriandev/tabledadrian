# Provençal Fish Soup with Rouille & Gruyère Croûtes

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A proper soupe de poissons from the frames and heads the fishmonger gives away — Marseille in a bowl for the cost of the saffron.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Mixed rockfish / fish frames & heads, gills removed — 2 kg
- Olive oil — 80 ml
- Onion — 250 g
- Fennel — 250 g
- Leek — 150 g
- Garlic — 40 g
- Tomato paste — 60 g
- Chopped tomatoes — 400 g
- White wine — 250 ml
- Water — 2.5 l
- Orange zest — 4 g
- Saffron — 0.4 g
- Thyme, bay — 6 g
- Cayenne — 1 g
- Baguette — 200 g
- Egg yolk — 1 (20 g)
- Roasted red pepper — 50 g
- Olive oil (rouille) — 120 ml
- Gruyère, grated — 100 g
- Fine sea salt — 14 g

## Method
1. Sweat onion, fennel, leek in oil 10 minutes; add half the garlic and the tomato paste, 3 minutes.
2. Add fish and frames; crush and stir 5 minutes until they break down.
3. Deglaze with wine; add tomatoes, water, zest, saffron, herbs; simmer 40 minutes, skimming.
4. Remove bay; blitz everything, bones included, then pass hard through a moulin or fine sieve — press every drop.
5. Season with salt and cayenne; hold at 85 °C.
6. Rouille: pound remaining garlic, yolk and pepper; emulsify with oil to a stiff mayonnaise; season.
7. Toast baguette slices; serve soup with croûtes, rouille and Gruyère for guests to float.

## Plating & Service
The ritual is the service: croûte, rouille smear, cheese, submerge — brief the guests, then let them build.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Freezes beautifully after step 5 — make 5 l whenever frames are free and bank charter-grade soup. Strain patience decides the quality; everything else is forgiving.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Rouille without egg: stir garlic-pepper paste into 120 g mayonnaise. GF: GF baguette. Add 400 g mussels at service for a bouillabaisse gesture.
