# Dark Chocolate, Cherry & Pistachio Watch Cookies

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Frozen dough pucks baked eight at a time — warm cookies appear on demand all season without ever making 'cookie day' a thing.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Unsalted butter, browned and cooled — 180 g
- Light brown sugar — 160 g
- Caster sugar — 60 g
- Egg — 1 (60 g)
- Egg yolk — 1 (20 g)
- Vanilla paste — 6 g
- Plain flour — 280 g
- Bicarbonate of soda — 4 g
- Fine sea salt — 4 g
- Dark chocolate (70%), chopped — 220 g
- Dried sour cherries — 120 g
- Pistachios, roughly chopped — 100 g
- Flaky sea salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Brown the butter to 150 °C until nutty; cool to room temperature (still liquid).
2. Whisk butter with both sugars 1 minute; add egg, yolk, vanilla; whisk 30 seconds.
3. Fold in flour, bicarb, fine salt; then chocolate, cherries, pistachios.
4. Scoop 70 g pucks; chill 24 hours (flavour) or freeze (logistics).
5. Bake spaced at 175 °C fan: 12–14 minutes from chilled, 16 from frozen — edges set, centres soft.
6. Flaky salt on landing; rest 10 minutes on the tray.

## Plating & Service
Warm, on a board, with cold milk or short espresso — watch handover currency.

> [!note] Galley notes
> The freezer bag of pucks is a season-long institution: any crew member can bake 4 at 22:00. Dough keeps 2 months frozen; never bake the whole batch at once.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: skip pistachios, add 40 g oats. Swap cherries for candied orange + 2 g fennel seed for a Sicilian mood. GF: 1:1 blend + 15 min rest before scooping.
