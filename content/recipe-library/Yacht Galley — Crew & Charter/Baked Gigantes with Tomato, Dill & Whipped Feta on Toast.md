# Baked Gigantes with Tomato, Dill & Whipped Feta on Toast

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A vast tray of Greek baked beans that costs pennies, keeps for days, and converts the most carnivorous deckhand.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Dried gigantes or butter beans, soaked 24 h — 700 g
- Olive oil — 120 ml
- Onions, diced — 300 g
- Carrot, grated — 150 g
- Celery, fine dice — 100 g
- Garlic — 25 g
- Chopped tomatoes — 800 g
- Tomato paste — 50 g
- Honey — 20 g
- Dried oregano — 5 g
- Dill, chopped — 25 g
- Feta — 250 g
- Greek yogurt — 80 g
- Sourdough — 600 g
- Fine sea salt — 12 g
- Black pepper — 3 g

## Method
1. Simmer soaked beans in fresh water 50–60 minutes until just tender; drain, keep 300 ml liquor.
2. Sweat onion, carrot, celery in 80 ml oil 10 minutes; add garlic, paste, oregano, 2 minutes.
3. Add tomatoes, honey, liquor, beans and salt in a deep tray.
4. Bake uncovered at 180 °C fan for 60–75 minutes, stirring twice, until the sauce is thick and the top blistered.
5. Blitz feta with yogurt until smooth.
6. Fold half the dill through the beans; serve on grilled sourdough with whipped feta and remaining dill.

## Plating & Service
Family-style tray with a stack of charred bread; olive oil at the table.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Better on day two and freezes perfectly — a meatless crew main that stretches across lunch and late-watch toast toppers. Tinned shortcut: 4 × 400 g butter beans, bake 40 min.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: skip feta or almond feta. GF: serve over rice. Add 250 g cooking chorizo to half the tray for the meat-eaters.
