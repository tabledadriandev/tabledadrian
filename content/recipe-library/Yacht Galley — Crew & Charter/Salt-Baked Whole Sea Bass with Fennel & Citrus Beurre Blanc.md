# Salt-Baked Whole Sea Bass with Fennel & Citrus Beurre Blanc

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The tableside crack of a salt crust is the best theatre on board — and underneath, the moistest fish you can serve at anchor.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Whole sea bass, gutted, scales on — 2 × 1.2 kg
- Coarse sea salt — 3 kg
- Egg whites — 6 (200 g)
- Fennel fronds & sliced bulb — 200 g
- Lemon, sliced — 1
- Orange, sliced — 1
- Thyme — 8 g
- White wine — 100 ml
- White wine vinegar — 30 ml
- Shallots, minced — 40 g
- Orange juice — 50 ml
- Cold unsalted butter, cubed — 200 g
- Chervil or dill — 8 g
- Black peppercorns — 3 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 200 °C fan. Stuff fish cavities with fennel, citrus slices and thyme.
2. Mix coarse salt with egg whites (and 30 ml water) to wet-sand texture.
3. Bed of salt on a tray, fish on top, remaining salt packed 1 cm thick over each fish, sealing the edges; leave heads exposed if you like the look.
4. Bake 25–30 minutes — probe through the crust: pull at 54 °C at the spine.
5. Beurre blanc: reduce wine, vinegar, shallots, peppercorns to 40 ml; add orange juice, reduce to 60 ml; over low heat whisk in cold butter cube by cube to a warm silk; strain, season, hold at 55 °C.
6. Rest fish 5 minutes; crack the crust at the table, brush away salt, lift fillets clean.

## Plating & Service
Crack tableside, fillet onto warm plates, nappe with citrus beurre blanc and a frond of chervil.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Crust forgives a 10-minute service delay better than any naked fish. Salt is the cheapest ingredient aboard; the drama is free. Beurre blanc holds 30 minutes in a warm bain — never over direct heat.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap bass for dorade or salmon (same timing per kg). Dairy-free table: skip beurre blanc, serve ladolemono (oil, charred lemon, oregano).
