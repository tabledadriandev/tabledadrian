# Tuna Tataki Platter with Ponzu, Avocado & Crispy Garlic

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Thirty seconds of sear, an hour of chill, and a ponzu you made from the citrus bowl — raw-bar polish from a two-burner galley.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Sashimi-grade tuna loin — 800 g
- Neutral oil — 20 ml
- Fine sea salt — 6 g
- Soy sauce — 80 ml
- Yuzu or lime + orange juice — 60 ml
- Rice vinegar — 20 ml
- Mirin — 30 ml
- Kombu — 5 g
- Garlic, sliced paper-thin — 25 g
- Neutral oil (frying) — 80 ml
- Avocados, sliced — 300 g
- Radishes, shaved — 80 g
- Spring onions — 30 g
- Toasted sesame seeds — 10 g
- Micro coriander or shiso — 5 g

## Method
1. Ponzu: combine soy, citrus juices, vinegar, mirin and kombu; steep 1 hour minimum (a day is better); strain.
2. Crispy garlic: fry slices in oil from cold, stirring, until pale gold (about 150 °C, 3 minutes); drain instantly — they darken as they cool.
3. Salt the tuna loin; sear in a smoking pan with 20 ml oil: 15 seconds per face, all four sides — grey rim, raw core.
4. Chill wrapped 1 hour to set (makes slicing clean and keeps it food-safe on deck).
5. Slice 8 mm against the grain with a long wet knife.
6. Shingle over ponzu-pooled platter; avocado, radish, garlic chips, sesame, spring onion, herbs.

## Plating & Service
A black or white platter, slices overlapping like fallen dominoes, ponzu beneath — never over — the fish.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Ponzu and garlic chips keep for weeks; the sear-chill means all knife work happens calm and cold, not à la minute. Freeze the loin 24 h beforehand unless the supplier certifies it.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: tamari ponzu. Swap tuna for salmon or bonito. Cooked-only guests: sear a portion through and slice thick with the same dressing.
