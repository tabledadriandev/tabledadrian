# Piri Piri Spatchcock Chickens for the Beach Grill

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Two flattened birds, one fierce chile baste, and a beach grill — the lunch people remember from the whole charter.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Whole chickens, spatchcocked — 2 × 1.5 kg
- Red chiles (mix hot & mild) — 80 g
- Garlic — 30 g
- Smoked paprika — 12 g
- Dried oregano — 5 g
- Red wine vinegar — 60 ml
- Lemon juice — 60 ml
- Olive oil — 100 ml
- Honey — 20 g
- Bay leaves — 3
- Fine sea salt — 24 g
- Lemons, halved — 3
- Flat-leaf parsley — 15 g

## Method
1. Blitz chiles, garlic, paprika, oregano, vinegar, lemon juice, oil, honey, bay and salt to a loose piri piri.
2. Reserve a third as baste/table sauce (never touches raw chicken); massage the rest into the slashed, spatchcocked birds; 4–24 hours chilled.
3. Grill bone-side down over medium coals (or 200 °C fan oven) 25 minutes.
4. Flip skin-side down over the hotter edge 10–15 minutes, moving to dodge flare-ups, until skin is blistered and thigh hits 74 °C... pull, baste with reserved sauce.
5. Rest 10 minutes; grill lemon halves cut-side down meanwhile.
6. Cut into pieces through the bone with shears; parsley and charred lemon over.

## Plating & Service
Rustic pile on a board, reserved piri piri in a jar, charred lemons for squeezing — paper napkins by the roll.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Marinates the night before; shears—not knives—make beach carving safe. The reserved-sauce discipline keeps food-safety simple far from the galley.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Heat is set by the chile mix — go all-mild for family charters. Same treatment works on a 1.2 kg side of salmon (12 min skin-down, no flip).
