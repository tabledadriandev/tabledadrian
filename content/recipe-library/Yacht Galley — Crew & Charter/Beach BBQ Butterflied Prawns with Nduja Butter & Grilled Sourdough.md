# Beach BBQ Butterflied Prawns with Nduja Butter & Grilled Sourdough

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Shell-on prawns split for maximum butter surface — five minutes over coals and the beach smells like Calabria.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Large shell-on prawns (16/20) — 1.6 kg
- Nduja — 90 g
- Unsalted butter, soft — 180 g
- Garlic, grated — 15 g
- Lemon zest — 5 g
- Lemon juice — 40 ml
- Honey — 10 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 20 g
- Sourdough, thick slices — 8 (480 g)
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Flaky sea salt — 8 g
- Lemons, halved, for the grill — 2

## Method
1. Beat nduja into soft butter with garlic, zest, honey and half the parsley.
2. Butterfly prawns through the back shell, leaving shells on; devein; press open.
3. Brush cut sides with a third of the nduja butter; chill until the fire is ready.
4. Grill shell-side down over hot coals 3 minutes; flip 60–90 seconds until just opaque (internal 55 °C).
5. Grill oiled sourdough and lemon halves alongside, 2 minutes per side.
6. Melt remaining nduja butter; pour over the platter of prawns; lemon, parsley, flaky salt.

## Plating & Service
Shells on, heads on, one giant platter — fingers, bread for the butter, seawater rinse optional.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Nduja butter logs freeze for a month: slice and deploy on steak, corn or fish all season. Prawns prep in the morning; the cook is 5 minutes of actual fire.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No nduja? 15 g smoked paprika + 3 g chile flakes in the butter. Swap prawns for langoustines (split, 4 min shell-down only).
