# Braised Beef Short Rib Rendang with Toasted Coconut

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A slow dry-style rendang that trades constant wok attention for a low oven — deepest flavour on the boat, and it only improves for three days.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Beef short rib, off the bone, 5 cm chunks — 2.2 kg
- Shallots — 300 g
- Garlic — 40 g
- Ginger — 40 g
- Galangal (or extra ginger) — 30 g
- Lemongrass stalks — 4 (80 g)
- Red chiles, deseeded — 60 g
- Ground turmeric — 6 g
- Ground coriander — 10 g
- Coconut milk — 1.2 l
- Desiccated coconut — 80 g
- Makrut lime leaves — 6
- Tamarind paste — 25 g
- Palm sugar — 30 g
- Neutral oil — 50 ml
- Fine sea salt — 16 g
- Jasmine rice — 700 g

## Method
1. Toast desiccated coconut in a dry pan to deep gold (kerisik); pound or blitz to an oily paste.
2. Blitz shallots, garlic, ginger, galangal, bruised lemongrass hearts, chiles, turmeric, coriander to a fine paste.
3. Fry the paste in oil over medium heat 10–12 minutes until it darkens and smells cooked, not raw.
4. Add beef, coconut milk, lime leaves, tamarind, sugar, salt; bring to a simmer.
5. Transfer to a 140 °C fan oven, uncovered, 3–3.5 hours, stirring hourly, until the sauce reduces to a dark clinging paste and the fat splits.
6. Fold in the kerisik for the final 30 minutes; the beef should hold shape but yield completely.

## Plating & Service
Over jasmine rice with cucumber ribbons; the sauce should coat, not pool.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Better on day three and freezes 3 months — cook it before a charter, deploy when the galley is slammed. Oven method means no wok-watching while you run service.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken thigh rendang cooks in 90 min. Vegan: jackfruit + mushrooms, 60 min, same paste. Heat is adjustable entirely via chile count.
