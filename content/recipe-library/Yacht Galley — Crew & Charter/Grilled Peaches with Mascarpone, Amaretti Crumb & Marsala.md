# Grilled Peaches with Mascarpone, Amaretti Crumb & Marsala

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The BBQ's dessert course — fruit on the dying coals while coffee brews, dressed like a Piedmont trattoria in four moves.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Peaches, ripe, halved, stoned — 8 (1 kg)
- Light brown sugar — 40 g
- Unsalted butter, melted — 30 g
- Marsala — 60 ml
- Honey — 30 g
- Mascarpone — 300 g
- Double cream — 100 ml
- Vanilla paste — 4 g
- Icing sugar — 25 g
- Amaretti biscuits, crushed — 100 g
- Toasted flaked almonds — 30 g
- Mint tips — 8

## Method
1. Brush peach cut faces with butter and press into brown sugar.
2. Grill cut-side down over medium coals (or 240 °C plancha) 3–4 minutes until caramel-marked; flip skin-side down 3 minutes to soften through.
3. Warm Marsala with honey to 70 °C; reduce 2 minutes to a light syrup.
4. Whip mascarpone, cream, vanilla, icing sugar to soft billow.
5. Two halves per plate, cut-side up; a spoon of cream in each hollow.
6. Marsala syrup over; amaretti crumb, almonds, mint.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm from the grill — the contrast of hot fruit and cool cream is the whole dessert.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Rides the same fire as lunch: no galley heat, 10 minutes of cook. Cream and syrup hold chilled; amaretti live in the locker for months.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Alcohol-free: orange juice + honey reduction. Swap peaches for apricots (6, whole halves) or pineapple. Vegan: coconut cream whip, maple, skip amaretti or use GF-vegan biscuits.
