# Omelette Soufflée with Comté & Fines Herbes

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A cloud-light Bocuse-school omelette that turns two eggs into theatre — the breakfast that makes charter guests photograph the galley door.

## Ingredients (serves 2)
- Eggs, separated — 6 (approx. 360 g)
- Comté, finely grated — 60 g
- Unsalted butter — 25 g
- Crème fraîche — 20 g
- Chives — 5 g
- Chervil — 3 g
- Tarragon leaves — 2 g
- Fine sea salt — 2 g
- White pepper — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Whisk yolks with crème fraîche, salt, pepper and half the herbs.
2. Whip whites to soft peaks — just holding shape, never dry.
3. Fold whites into yolks in two additions, keeping maximum air.
4. Heat a 24 cm non-stick pan with butter over medium-low (pan at ~160 °C).
5. Pour in the mousse, cook 2 minutes, scatter Comté, then lid on for 3 minutes until barely set and puffed.
6. Fold in half, slide onto a warm plate, shower with remaining herbs.

## Plating & Service
Serve whole to share or halved onto warm plates; the centre should still tremble.

> [!note] Galley notes
> One omelette per two covers — for a full charter run them back-to-back in two pans rather than scaling the pan up. Skip in heavy seas; whipped whites hate a rolling hull.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free: olive oil and omit cheese, finish with confit tomato. Add 40 g smoked salmon inside the fold for a richer service.
