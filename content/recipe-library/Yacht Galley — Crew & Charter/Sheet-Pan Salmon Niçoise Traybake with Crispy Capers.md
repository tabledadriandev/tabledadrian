# Sheet-Pan Salmon Niçoise Traybake with Crispy Capers

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Niçoise logic on one hot tray — the potatoes roast, the salmon flakes, and the dressing is done before the oven beeps.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Salmon fillets, skin-on — 8 × 140 g
- Baby potatoes, halved — 800 g
- Green beans, trimmed — 400 g
- Cherry tomatoes — 300 g
- Black olives (Niçoise), pitted — 100 g
- Capers, drained, dried — 40 g
- Olive oil — 90 ml
- Eggs — 6
- Dijon mustard — 15 g
- Red wine vinegar — 25 ml
- Shallot, minced — 30 g
- Anchovy fillets, minced — 15 g
- Tarragon — 6 g
- Fine sea salt — 10 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 210 °C fan. Toss potatoes with 40 ml oil and salt; roast cut-side down 20 minutes.
2. Boil eggs 7 minutes 30 seconds; shock, peel, quarter.
3. Whisk mustard, vinegar, shallot, anchovy and 40 ml oil into a sharp dressing.
4. Push potatoes aside; add beans, tomatoes, olives and capers to the tray; seat salmon skin-up, brush with 10 ml oil, season.
5. Roast 12–14 minutes until salmon reaches 50–52 °C at the centre — just set, still blushing.
6. Dress the whole warm tray, tuck in egg quarters, finish with tarragon.

## Plating & Service
Serve from the tray on deck; the capers should have gone crisp among the potatoes.

> [!note] Galley notes
> One tray, one dressing jar — crew or guests. Salmon can swap to whatever the fish co-op landed; potatoes and dressing hold from morning prep. Warm salad = no wilt in July heat.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap salmon for tuna steaks (roast 6 min only) or chickpeas + halloumi (vegetarian). Skip anchovy for pescatarian-lite tables.
