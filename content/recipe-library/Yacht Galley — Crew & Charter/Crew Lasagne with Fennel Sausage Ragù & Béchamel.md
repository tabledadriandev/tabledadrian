# Crew Lasagne with Fennel Sausage Ragù & Béchamel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The season's most-requested crew dinner — a deep tray of proper slow ragù that freezes whole and tastes like a night off.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Italian fennel sausage, skinned — 800 g
- Beef mince (15% fat) — 600 g
- Onion, fine dice — 250 g
- Carrot, fine dice — 150 g
- Celery, fine dice — 100 g
- Garlic — 20 g
- Red wine — 200 ml
- Chopped tomatoes — 1.2 kg
- Tomato paste — 60 g
- Whole milk — 1.2 l
- Unsalted butter — 90 g
- Plain flour — 90 g
- Nutmeg — 1 g
- Dried lasagne sheets — 500 g
- Parmesan, grated — 150 g
- Fine sea salt — 12 g
- Fennel seeds — 4 g

## Method
1. Brown sausage and beef hard in batches over high heat; set aside.
2. Sweat onion, carrot, celery 10 minutes; add garlic, fennel seeds, tomato paste, 2 minutes.
3. Deglaze with wine, reduce by half; return meat, add tomatoes and 8 g salt; simmer uncovered 60 minutes to a thick ragù.
4. Béchamel: melt butter, cook flour 2 minutes, whisk in milk gradually; simmer 6 minutes to nappe; season with nutmeg and 4 g salt.
5. Layer in a deep 32 × 26 cm tray: béchamel, pasta, ragù ×4, finishing with béchamel and Parmesan.
6. Bake at 180 °C fan for 40–45 minutes until bubbling and bronzed (core 80 °C); rest 20 minutes before cutting.

## Plating & Service
Clean squares with a dressed bitter-leaf salad; the rest keeps its shape for tomorrow's lunch.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Builds two trays as easily as one — bake one, freeze one raw (bake from frozen at 170 °C, 75 min). Rest time is your friend in a swell: it slices, it doesn't slide.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian: lentil-mushroom ragù (500 g cooked lentils, 500 g mushrooms). GF: GF sheets and cornflour béchamel.
