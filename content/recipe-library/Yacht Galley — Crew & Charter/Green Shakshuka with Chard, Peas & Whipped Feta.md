# Green Shakshuka with Chard, Peas & Whipped Feta

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The verdant sibling of the red pan — chard, peas and green chile with a cool whipped feta that plays hot against cold.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Swiss chard, leaves and stems separated — 400 g
- Frozen peas — 200 g
- Leeks, sliced — 200 g
- Green chile, sliced — 15 g
- Garlic — 15 g
- Olive oil — 50 ml
- Ground coriander — 4 g
- Cumin — 3 g
- Vegetable stock — 100 ml
- Eggs — 6
- Feta — 150 g
- Greek yogurt — 80 g
- Dill — 8 g
- Lemon — 1 (juice 20 ml, zest 3 g)
- Fine sea salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Blitz feta with yogurt and lemon zest until whipped-smooth; chill.
2. Sweat leeks and chard stems in oil over medium heat 6 minutes; add garlic, chile and spices, 1 minute.
3. Add chard leaves, peas and stock; wilt 3 minutes, then blitz one third of the mix and stir back for body. Season with salt and lemon juice.
4. Make 6 wells; crack in eggs, cover, cook on low 6–8 minutes to set the whites.
5. Finish with whipped feta spoonfuls and dill.

## Plating & Service
Pan to the table, whipped feta melting into the green; charred pita alongside.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Green base freezes in 1 kg bags — defrost, wells, eggs, done. Works for a light crew supper too; poach eggs harder if the anchorage is rolly.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: butter beans instead of eggs, almond yogurt whip. Add preserved lemon to the base for a sharper profile.
