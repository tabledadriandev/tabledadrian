# Chicken Caesar Salad Boats with Anchovy Crumb

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Caesar re-rigged for deck service: gem lettuce boats you pick up with your fingers, dressing that clings instead of pooling.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Chicken thighs, boneless — 1 kg
- Olive oil — 60 ml
- Little gem lettuces — 6 (600 g)
- Egg yolks — 2 (40 g)
- Anchovy fillets — 30 g
- Garlic — 8 g
- Dijon mustard — 10 g
- Lemon juice — 40 ml
- Worcestershire sauce — 5 ml
- Parmesan, finely grated — 100 g
- Neutral oil — 150 ml
- Panko — 100 g
- Unsalted butter — 30 g
- Capers — 25 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Season thighs; roast at 210 °C fan for 20–25 minutes to 74 °C with crisp edges; rest, slice thin.
2. Dressing: pound half the anchovies with garlic; whisk with yolks, mustard, lemon, Worcestershire; emulsify with neutral oil; fold in 60 g Parmesan; loosen with 20 ml water to a thick cream.
3. Crumb: fry panko in butter with remaining anchovies (mashed) and capers, 4 minutes to deep gold.
4. Separate gem leaves into boats; keep in iced water 10 minutes, spin very dry.
5. Fill each boat: dressing smear, chicken, more dressing, anchovy crumb, Parmesan and pepper.

## Plating & Service
Boats in rows on a long platter — finger food that eats like a composed salad; extra dressing in a bowl.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Dressing and crumb hold 3 days; boats assemble to order so nothing wilts poolside. No croutons rolling around the deck — the crumb does that job better.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap chicken for grilled prawns or keep it plant-based with roasted chickpeas + vegan dressing (aquafaba, capers for anchovy). GF: GF panko.
