# Slow-Braised Lamb Shoulder Tagine with Apricots & Almonds

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Set it braising after breakfast service and it minds itself all afternoon — sweet-savoury depth that feeds guests Friday and crew Saturday.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Lamb shoulder, 5 cm chunks — 2 kg
- Olive oil — 50 ml
- Onions, sliced — 500 g
- Garlic — 25 g
- Ginger, grated — 25 g
- Ras el hanout — 18 g
- Cinnamon stick — 1
- Saffron — 0.3 g
- Chopped tomatoes — 400 g
- Chicken stock — 700 ml
- Dried apricots — 250 g
- Honey — 30 g
- Chickpeas, cooked — 480 g
- Flaked almonds, toasted — 60 g
- Coriander — 20 g
- Preserved lemon, sliced — 30 g
- Fine sea salt — 14 g

## Method
1. Brown lamb hard in oil in batches; set aside.
2. Soften onions 10 minutes; add garlic, ginger, ras el hanout, cinnamon; fry 2 minutes.
3. Return lamb with tomatoes, stock, saffron and salt; braise lidded at 150 °C fan for 2 hours.
4. Add apricots, honey and chickpeas; braise uncovered 45–60 minutes more until the lamb collapses and sauce is glossy.
5. Skim, adjust seasoning, fold in preserved lemon.
6. Rest 15 minutes; finish with almonds and coriander.

## Plating & Service
Over buttered couscous or plain rice with the almonds scattered at the table; harissa on the side.

> [!note] Galley notes
> A two-service dish by design: guests night one, shredded into crew wraps or pastilla night two. Freezes 3 months; flavour peaks 24 h after cooking. Heavy lidded pot stays put underway.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap lamb for chicken thighs (halve braise time) or squash + double chickpeas (vegan, vegetable stock). Nut-free: toasted seeds.
