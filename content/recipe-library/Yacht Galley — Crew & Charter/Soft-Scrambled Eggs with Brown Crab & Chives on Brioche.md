# Soft-Scrambled Eggs with Brown Crab & Chives on Brioche

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Guest-level eggs: crème-fraîche-soft folds enriched with brown crab meat — a Riviera charter breakfast that reads luxurious with zero last-minute stress.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Eggs — 8 (approx. 480 g)
- Unsalted butter — 40 g
- Crème fraîche — 40 g
- Brown crab meat — 80 g
- White crab meat — 60 g
- Brioche loaf, thick slices — 4 (240 g)
- Chives, finely sliced — 10 g
- Lemon zest — 2 g
- Espelette pepper — 1 g
- Fine sea salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Whisk eggs with salt; rest 5 minutes so the salt dissolves proteins for a silkier set.
2. Toast brioche at 200 °C for 4 minutes until golden; keep warm.
3. Melt butter in a cold non-stick pan, add eggs, set over low heat (induction 4/10).
4. Stir constantly with a spatula, on and off the heat, 5–6 minutes to small soft curds (remove at 65–70 °C internal).
5. Off heat, fold in crème fraîche to stop the cooking, then brown crab meat.
6. Spoon over brioche, crown with white crab, chives, lemon zest and a pinch of Espelette.

## Plating & Service
Serve on warm plates immediately; eggs wait for no one — call service before the final fold.

> [!note] Galley notes
> On a rolling sea, hold the pan low in the sink well; eggs can be cooked to 80% in a bain-marie bowl for stability. Scale to 12 covers in two pans, never one.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: swap brioche for toasted GF sourdough. Lighter: replace crème fraîche with thick yogurt; omit crab for vegetarian and finish with confit tomatoes.
