# Charter Shakshuka with Feta, Harissa & Preserved Lemon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> One heavy pan, six perfect baveuse yolks, and a sauce you can build the night before — the hardest-working breakfast on the boat.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Onion, sliced — 200 g
- Red peppers, sliced — 300 g
- Garlic, sliced — 15 g
- Olive oil — 45 ml
- Cumin seeds, toasted and ground — 4 g
- Smoked paprika — 5 g
- Harissa — 25 g
- Chopped tomatoes (tinned) — 800 g
- Preserved lemon rind, minced — 15 g
- Eggs — 6
- Feta — 120 g
- Coriander leaves — 10 g
- Fine sea salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Sweat onion and peppers in olive oil over medium heat 10 minutes until soft and sweet.
2. Add garlic, cumin, paprika and harissa; fry 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Add tomatoes, salt and preserved lemon; simmer 12 minutes to a thick, spoon-coating sauce.
4. Make 6 wells, crack in the eggs, cover and cook over low heat 6–8 minutes until whites set, yolks soft (about 63 °C).
5. Scatter with crumbled feta and coriander.

## Plating & Service
Bring the pan to the table on a trivet with grilled flatbread; spoon an egg and generous sauce per guest.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Sauce batches beautifully — make 3× and chill up to 4 days or freeze; in rough weather cook eggs firmer (10 min) so the pan can travel. Scales to 12 in two 28 cm pans.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: replace eggs with 400 g butter beans and feta with almond feta. Add merguez for crew who've done a long night watch.
