# Espresso Affogato Bar with Vanilla Gelato & Toppings

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Dessert as a two-minute ritual: the machine pulls shots, guests drown their own gelato — the galley's off-duty finale.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Vanilla gelato or ice cream — 800 g
- Espresso — 8 double shots (320 ml)
- Amaretto or Frangelico (optional) — 60 ml
- Dark chocolate, shaved — 60 g
- Amaretti, crushed — 80 g
- Toasted hazelnuts, chopped — 60 g
- Candied orange peel — 40 g
- Whipped cream — 150 g
- Flaky sea salt — 2 g
- Cocoa powder — 10 g

## Method
1. Freeze 8 small glasses or coupes 30 minutes ahead.
2. Scoop gelato into balls onto a frozen tray earlier in the day; hold in the freezer — service becomes instant.
3. Set the topping bar: chocolate, amaretti, hazelnuts, orange peel, cream, salt, cocoa in small bowls.
4. At the table: two scoops per frozen glass.
5. Pull fresh double shots; pour over tableside (liqueur for those who nod).
6. Guests dress their own from the bar.

## Plating & Service
The pour is the plating — do it in front of each guest so the melt starts on cue.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Pre-scooped tray + topping bowls = a dessert that runs itself while you start the galley close-down. Works for 2 or 12; no baking, no timing, no leftovers.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free: coconut or oat vanilla. Decaf after 22:00 without telling anyone. Winter version: pour over chocolate gelato with a pinch of cinnamon.
