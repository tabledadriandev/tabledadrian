# Deckhand's Bacon & Egg Pide with Kasseri & Pickled Chiles

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Boat-shaped breads loaded with smoked bacon, molten kasseri and just-set eggs — a bakery-morning flex from one batch of dough.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Strong white flour — 500 g
- Instant yeast — 7 g
- Caster sugar — 8 g
- Fine sea salt — 9 g
- Warm water (35 °C) — 320 ml
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Smoked bacon lardons — 250 g
- Kasseri or provolone, grated — 250 g
- Eggs — 8
- Pickled green chiles, sliced — 60 g
- Parsley — 12 g
- Nigella seeds — 4 g
- Egg yolk (wash) — 20 g

## Method
1. Mix flour, yeast, sugar, salt, water and oil; knead 8 minutes to smooth; prove 1 hour at 26 °C until doubled.
2. Render lardons over medium heat 6 minutes until just crisp; drain.
3. Divide dough into 8; roll each to a 25 × 12 cm oval on semolina.
4. Fill centres with cheese and bacon, leaving a 2 cm border; fold and pinch the long edges to form canoes, twisting the ends.
5. Egg-wash the rims, scatter nigella; bake at 240 °C on a preheated steel/tray 10 minutes.
6. Crack an egg into each pide, return 4–5 minutes until whites set; finish with chiles and parsley.

## Plating & Service
Serve whole on boards, cut into thirds crosswise so the yolk runs down the boat.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Shaped pide freeze raw for 6 weeks — bake from frozen +4 minutes. Filling flexes with whatever provisioning left behind; a two-oven morning for 12+ covers.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian: swap bacon for 200 g sautéed spinach + sun-dried tomato. Halal: use beef bacon or omit.
