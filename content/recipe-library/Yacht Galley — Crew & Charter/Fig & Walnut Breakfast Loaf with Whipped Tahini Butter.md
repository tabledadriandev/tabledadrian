# Fig & Walnut Breakfast Loaf with Whipped Tahini Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A dense, honeyed loaf that improves for three days in the bread bin — provisioning-smart baking for long stretches between markets.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Plain flour — 300 g
- Wholemeal flour — 100 g
- Baking powder — 14 g
- Dried figs, chopped — 250 g
- Walnuts, toasted — 120 g
- Eggs — 3 (180 g)
- Greek yogurt — 200 g
- Olive oil — 90 ml
- Honey — 120 g
- Orange zest — 6 g
- Cinnamon — 4 g
- Unsalted butter, soft — 120 g
- Tahini — 50 g
- Fine sea salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 165 °C fan; line a 900 g loaf tin.
2. Soak figs in 100 ml boiling water 10 minutes; do not drain.
3. Whisk eggs, yogurt, oil, honey and zest; fold in both flours, baking powder, cinnamon, salt.
4. Fold in figs with their liquid and the walnuts.
5. Bake 50–55 minutes until a skewer comes out clean (internal 96 °C); cool in the tin 15 minutes.
6. Whip soft butter with tahini and a pinch of salt until pale; chill lightly.

## Plating & Service
Thick slices, lightly grilled, with a generous sweep of tahini butter and extra honey for the table.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Keeps 4 days wrapped, freezes 2 months sliced — bake two loaves on crossing day. One-handed watch food when toasted and folded in a napkin.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: flax eggs (30 g ground flax + 90 ml water), coconut yogurt, maple; olive oil instead of butter with the tahini. Nut-free: pumpkin seeds for walnuts.
