# Braised Pork Belly Adobo with Garlic Fried Rice

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Four pantry liquids and time — the self-saucing braise that survives any galley chaos and gets better every reheat.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Pork belly, skinless, 4 cm chunks — 1.8 kg
- Soy sauce — 120 ml
- Cane or rice vinegar — 120 ml
- Water — 300 ml
- Garlic, smashed — 60 g
- Black peppercorns, cracked — 6 g
- Bay leaves — 5
- Brown sugar — 30 g
- Neutral oil — 40 ml
- Day-old cooked rice — 1.4 kg
- Garlic, minced (rice) — 30 g
- Eggs — 8
- Spring onions — 40 g
- Cucumber, sliced — 300 g
- Fine sea salt — 4 g

## Method
1. Sear pork belly in a dry heavy pot over high heat until the fat renders and the edges brown, 8 minutes.
2. Add soy, vinegar, water, smashed garlic, peppercorns, bay, sugar — do not stir for the first 10 minutes (lets the vinegar cook out).
3. Simmer lidded at a low blip 75–90 minutes until a spoon cuts the belly.
4. Uncover, reduce 10–15 minutes to a glossy, clinging sauce, turning the pork.
5. Garlic rice: fry minced garlic in oil to pale gold; add cold rice, toss hard 4 minutes; season.
6. Fry eggs crisp; serve adobo over rice, egg on top, cucumber alongside.

## Plating & Service
Shallow bowls: rice, three lacquered chunks, sauce spooned over, egg and spring onion crowning.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Almost zero fresh ingredients, one pot, and it reheats better than it starts — the passage main. Make double: day two becomes adobo flakes for breakfast rice.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: tamari. Chicken thigh adobo braises in 40 min. Cut richness with 200 g pineapple chunks in the last 10 minutes.
