# Crew Pad Krapow with Beef, Holy Basil & Fried Eggs

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Fifteen minutes wok-to-table and hotter-than-the-engine-room flavour — the fastest crew dinner in the repertoire.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Beef mince (or hand-chopped skirt) — 1.2 kg
- Garlic — 30 g
- Bird's-eye chiles — 15 g
- Neutral oil — 60 ml
- Oyster sauce — 60 ml
- Fish sauce — 40 ml
- Light soy sauce — 30 ml
- Dark soy sauce — 10 ml
- Caster sugar — 20 g
- Chicken stock — 100 ml
- Holy basil (or Thai basil) — 60 g
- Green beans, 2 cm — 300 g
- Eggs — 8
- Jasmine rice — 640 g
- Lime — 2

## Method
1. Cook rice by absorption; hold under a cloth.
2. Pound garlic and chiles to a rough paste.
3. Heat 30 ml oil in a wok to smoking; fry the paste 20 seconds, add beef in two batches, searing hard and breaking up, 4 minutes.
4. Add beans; stir 1 minute. Season with oyster, fish, both soys, sugar and stock; toss until glossy, 1 minute.
5. Kill the heat; fold through basil until just wilted.
6. Fry eggs in remaining oil at high heat until the edges are bubbled and crisp, yolks liquid.

## Plating & Service
Rice, beef over one side, crispy fried egg on top, lime wedge; yolk broken into the sauce at the table.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Sauce mix pre-batches in a squeeze bottle — dinner for 8 in the time the rice takes. Chile load scales per bowl, not per pan, so one wok feeds all tolerances.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken or tofu swap 1:1. GF: tamari + GF oyster sauce. No holy basil in provisioning? Thai basil + a few torn mint leaves gets close.
