# Seared Beef Tagliata with Rocket, Parmesan & Balsamic Onions

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> One big steak sliced thin feeds eight elegantly — the carving-board lunch that looks lavish and costs less than plated fillets.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Bavette or sirloin, whole — 1.4 kg
- Olive oil — 70 ml
- Fine sea salt — 16 g
- Black pepper, cracked — 4 g
- Red onions, thick rings — 400 g
- Balsamic vinegar — 60 ml
- Honey — 15 g
- Rocket — 150 g
- Parmesan, shaved — 120 g
- Lemon juice — 25 ml
- Rosemary — 4 g
- Flaky sea salt — 4 g

## Method
1. Salt the beef 1–2 hours ahead; bring to room temperature.
2. Balsamic onions: soften onion rings in 20 ml oil 10 minutes; add balsamic and honey, reduce to a sticky glaze, 5 minutes.
3. Sear beef in 20 ml oil on a 260 °C plancha or heavy pan: 3–4 minutes per side with rosemary, basting, to 52 °C core (rare-medium rare).
4. Rest 10 full minutes on a warm board.
5. Slice 5 mm against the grain, holding the fan shape.
6. Board: rocket dressed with lemon and 30 ml oil, beef fanned over, onions tangled through, Parmesan shaved on, flaky salt.

## Plating & Service
Serve on the carving board at the table, juices pooling into the rocket — that's the sauce.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Onions and dressing prep ahead; the beef needs 8 minutes of fire + 10 of rest, which is exactly one drinks-refresh cycle. One steak = less grill anxiety than eight.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free: skip Parmesan, add toasted pine nuts. Swap beef for seared tuna (1 min/side) for a red-meat-free table. Porcini-rub the steak in autumn.
