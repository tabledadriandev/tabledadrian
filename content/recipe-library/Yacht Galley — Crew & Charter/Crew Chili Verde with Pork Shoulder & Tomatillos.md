# Crew Chili Verde with Pork Shoulder & Tomatillos

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A green chile braise that reheats better than it serves fresh — the freezer's most valuable crew dinner by mid-season.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Pork shoulder, 4 cm dice — 2 kg
- Tomatillos (tinned fine), drained — 800 g
- Poblano or green peppers — 400 g
- Jalapeños — 60 g
- White onions — 400 g
- Garlic — 30 g
- Cumin, ground — 10 g
- Coriander seed, ground — 6 g
- Dried oregano — 4 g
- Chicken stock — 800 ml
- Coriander (fresh) — 60 g
- Lime juice — 50 ml
- Neutral oil — 50 ml
- Fine sea salt — 15 g
- Long-grain rice — 800 g

## Method
1. Char poblanos, jalapeños and halved onions under a 250 °C grill 10 minutes until blistered.
2. Blitz charred vegetables with tomatillos, garlic and half the fresh coriander to a coarse salsa.
3. Brown pork in oil in batches over high heat; season with salt, cumin, coriander seed, oregano.
4. Add salsa and stock; simmer lidded at 95 °C for 2 hours until the pork shreds at a nudge.
5. Uncover, reduce 20–30 minutes to a thick, clinging sauce; finish with lime and remaining coriander.
6. Serve over rice cooked by absorption.

## Plating & Service
Deep bowls over rice with lime wedges, sliced radish and warm tortillas for the table.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Freezes flawlessly in 2-portion bags — the passage stash. Doubles as taco/burrito filling and breakfast-egg smother; pressure cooker cuts braise to 45 min.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken thigh version braises in 1 hour. Vegan: swap pork for 1.2 kg jackfruit + 480 g white beans, veg stock.
