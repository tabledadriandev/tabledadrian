# Persian Herb & Butter Bean Stew (Ghormeh-Style) with Dried Lime

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A deep-green stew soured with dried limes — slow-tasting flavour from a genuinely fast, locker-friendly build.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Butter beans, cooked — 1.2 kg
- Flat-leaf parsley — 200 g
- Coriander — 150 g
- Chives or spring onion tops — 100 g
- Fenugreek leaves, dried — 8 g
- Spinach — 200 g
- Onions, diced — 300 g
- Olive oil — 100 ml
- Ground turmeric — 6 g
- Dried limes (limoo amani), pierced — 4
- Vegetable stock — 1.2 l
- Lemon juice — 30 ml
- Basmati rice — 700 g
- Fine sea salt — 14 g
- Black pepper — 3 g

## Method
1. Finely chop all fresh herbs and spinach (or pulse carefully).
2. Fry onions in 50 ml oil to deep gold, 12 minutes; add turmeric, 1 minute.
3. In a second pan, fry the chopped herbs in 50 ml oil over medium heat 10–12 minutes until dark green and fragrant — this step is the dish.
4. Combine onions, herbs, beans, stock and dried limes; simmer 40 minutes, crushing the limes against the pot as they soften.
5. Season with salt, pepper, lemon juice — it should be sour-forward.
6. Serve over basmati with extra lemon.

## Plating & Service
Deep green over white rice; a crushed dried lime in the lucky bowl.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Freezes and improves like all great stews; herbs can be the wilting end of provisioning. Traditionally lamb — this bean version covers vegan guests and Friday fasting crew alike.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Classic: add 800 g lamb shoulder, browned, +90 min simmer. No dried limes? 40 ml extra lemon + 3 g zest gets 80% there.
