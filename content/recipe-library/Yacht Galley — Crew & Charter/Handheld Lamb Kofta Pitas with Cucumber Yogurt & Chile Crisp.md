# Handheld Lamb Kofta Pitas with Cucumber Yogurt & Chile Crisp

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rough-weather engineering disguised as street food: everything lands inside a pita, nothing lands on the sole.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Lamb mince (20%) — 1.5 kg
- Onion, grated and squeezed — 150 g
- Garlic — 20 g
- Cumin — 10 g
- Coriander, ground — 8 g
- Smoked paprika — 6 g
- Cinnamon — 2 g
- Parsley, chopped — 25 g
- Fine sea salt — 16 g
- Greek yogurt — 400 g
- Cucumber, grated, squeezed — 250 g
- Mint — 10 g
- Lemon juice — 25 ml
- Pitas — 10 (600 g)
- Tomatoes, sliced — 300 g
- Chile crisp or harissa oil — 40 g
- Sumac — 4 g

## Method
1. Mix lamb with onion, garlic, spices, parsley and salt; knead 2 minutes until tacky; shape 20 torpedo koftas around your thumb-line; chill 30 minutes.
2. Cucumber yogurt: fold cucumber, mint, lemon and a pinch of salt through the yogurt.
3. Grill koftas on a 240 °C plancha or under a hot grill, 3–4 minutes per side, to 71 °C core with hard char.
4. Flash pitas on the plancha 30 seconds per side; halve and open.
5. Build: yogurt swiped inside, two koftas, tomato, chile crisp, sumac.

## Plating & Service
Wrapped in parchment collars for deck service — one hand for the pita, one for the boat.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Koftas freeze raw in rows; yogurt holds 3 days. Feeds 10 in a 15-minute cook window and the pita format survives a Force 6 cockpit dinner.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Beef or chicken mince swap 1:1. GF: serve as a kofta bowl over rice. Vegan: 960 g mashed chickpea-walnut mix, bake 18 min at 200 °C.
