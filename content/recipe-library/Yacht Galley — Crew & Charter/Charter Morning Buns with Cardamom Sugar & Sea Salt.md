# Charter Morning Buns with Cardamom Sugar & Sea Salt

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Opera-Patisserie discipline in a galley: an overnight-proofed cardamom bun that bakes off while guests drink their first coffee.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Strong white flour — 500 g
- Whole milk — 250 ml
- Fresh yeast — 20 g (or 7 g instant)
- Caster sugar — 70 g
- Egg — 1 (60 g)
- Unsalted butter, soft — 90 g (dough), plus 100 g (filling)
- Ground cardamom — 8 g
- Light brown sugar — 100 g
- Cinnamon — 3 g
- Flaky sea salt — 3 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g
- Pearl sugar — 30 g

## Method
1. Warm milk to 35 °C, dissolve yeast; mix with flour, sugar, egg, fine salt and 4 g cardamom to a shaggy dough.
2. Knead 10 minutes (windowpane stage); work in 90 g soft butter in three additions; knead 3 minutes more.
3. Prove 1 hour at 26 °C until doubled, then chill overnight at 4 °C.
4. Beat 100 g butter with brown sugar, cinnamon and remaining cardamom for the filling.
5. Roll dough to 40 × 50 cm, spread filling, fold in thirds, cut 12 strips, twist and knot each.
6. Prove 90 minutes at 26 °C until puffy; brush with milk, scatter pearl sugar and flaky salt.
7. Bake at 190 °C fan for 18–22 minutes to deep amber (internal 94 °C).

## Plating & Service
Pile warm on a board with strong coffee; the smell does the front-of-house work for you.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Shape and freeze unproved knots on turnaround day; pull 8 the night before, prove at dawn, bake at 07:30. Baking day must be reasonably calm — proving dough forgives a swell, laminating patience doesn't.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap filling for orange zest + 60 g almond paste. Dairy-free: oat milk and plant butter work at a 1:1 swap.
