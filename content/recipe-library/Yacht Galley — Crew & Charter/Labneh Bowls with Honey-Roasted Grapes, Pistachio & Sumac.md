# Labneh Bowls with Honey-Roasted Grapes, Pistachio & Sumac

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Two-day strained labneh with burst roasted grapes — a make-ahead guest breakfast that looks like a magazine cover with 10 minutes of morning work.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Greek yogurt (full fat) — 900 g
- Fine sea salt — 5 g
- Red seedless grapes, on small stems — 500 g
- Honey — 45 g
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Thyme sprigs — 4
- Pistachios, chopped — 60 g
- Sumac — 4 g
- Mint leaves — 8
- Sesame seed flatbread or crackers — 150 g

## Method
1. Two days ahead: stir salt into yogurt, hang in muslin over a bowl at 4 °C for 24–48 hours until thick as soft cheese.
2. Heat oven to 200 °C. Toss grape clusters with 20 g honey, 20 ml oil and thyme.
3. Roast 15–18 minutes until skins split and juices caramelise.
4. Swoosh 120 g labneh per bowl with the back of a spoon.
5. Top with warm grapes and their juices, remaining honey and oil, pistachios, sumac and torn mint.

## Plating & Service
Shallow bowls, grapes still on their stems, flatbread shards standing in the labneh.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Labneh holds a week and doubles as a canapé base; grapes roast in any oven gap. Assembly-only mornings are gold on changeover days.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: labneh from thick coconut yogurt (strain 12 h). Nut-free: toasted seeds. Savoury turn: za'atar and cucumber instead of honey and grapes.
