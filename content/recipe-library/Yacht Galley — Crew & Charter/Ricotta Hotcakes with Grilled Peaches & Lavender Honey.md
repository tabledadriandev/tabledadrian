# Ricotta Hotcakes with Grilled Peaches & Lavender Honey

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Soufflé-adjacent hotcakes with Riviera stone fruit off the plancha — the breakfast that gets requested twice per charter.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Ricotta — 350 g
- Eggs, separated — 4 (240 g)
- Whole milk — 150 ml
- Plain flour — 180 g
- Baking powder — 8 g
- Caster sugar — 30 g
- Lemon zest — 4 g
- Peaches, halved and stoned — 4 (500 g)
- Honey — 60 g
- Dried lavender — 1 g
- Unsalted butter — 40 g
- Crème fraîche — 120 g
- Fine sea salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Warm honey with lavender to 60 °C; infuse 20 minutes, strain.
2. Whisk yolks, milk, ricotta and zest; fold in flour, baking powder, salt.
3. Whip whites with sugar to soft peaks; fold in thirds into the batter.
4. Grill peach halves cut-side down on a 220 °C plancha 3 minutes until char-striped; brush with lavender honey.
5. Cook 70 g hotcakes in butter over medium-low, 2–3 minutes per side, until puffed and golden.
6. Hold at 90 °C in a single layer, never stacked, while finishing.

## Plating & Service
Two hotcakes, a peach half, crème fraîche and a warm thread of lavender honey per plate.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Peaches and honey prep the day before; batter is a morning job (whipped whites don't hold). Calm-anchorage dish — swap to bircher if the boat is moving.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: 1:1 GF blend plus 2 g xanthan. Swap peaches for figs in September. Skip lavender for plain thyme honey if guests are floral-averse.
