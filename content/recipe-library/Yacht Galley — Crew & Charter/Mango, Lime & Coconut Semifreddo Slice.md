# Mango, Lime & Coconut Semifreddo Slice

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Ice-cream elegance without a churner — a loaf-tin semifreddo sliced at the table, engineered for freezers that live at the mercy of the generator.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Ripe mango purée — 400 g
- Lime juice — 40 ml
- Lime zest — 6 g
- Egg yolks — 5 (100 g)
- Caster sugar — 140 g
- Water — 40 ml
- Double cream — 400 ml
- Coconut cream — 100 g
- Vanilla paste — 4 g
- Fine sea salt — 2 g
- Toasted coconut flakes — 30 g
- Fresh mango, diced — 200 g
- Mint — 6 g

## Method
1. Line a 1 kg loaf tin with film, overhanging.
2. Pâte à bombe: boil sugar and water to 118 °C; pour in a thread onto whisking yolks; whisk to a cool, thick, pale ribbon, 5 minutes.
3. Whip cream with coconut cream, vanilla and salt to soft peaks.
4. Fold mango purée, lime juice and zest into the bombe; then fold in the cream in thirds.
5. Pour into the tin, rippling 50 g extra purée through; cover; freeze 6+ hours.
6. Unmould, peel the film; press coconut flakes onto the top; slice 2.5 cm with a hot knife; fresh mango and mint over.

## Plating & Service
Standing slices on chilled plates, diced mango scattered, served semi-soft — 5 minutes out of the freezer is the sweet spot.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Holds 2 weeks frozen and slices for 2 or 10 — the flexible-guest-count dessert. Semifreddo forgives a soft freezer better than churned ice cream ever will.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap mango for raspberry or roasted apricot purée. Dairy-free-ish: all coconut cream (denser). Layer crushed meringue through for a frozen Eton mess.
