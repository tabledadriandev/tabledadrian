# Night Watch Sausage Rolls with Fennel & Apple

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The 03:00 morale weapon — proper butcher-style rolls that bake from frozen, one hand on the wheel, one on the pastry.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Pork sausage meat — 800 g
- Apple, coarsely grated, squeezed — 150 g
- Fennel seeds, toasted, cracked — 6 g
- Onion, fine dice, sweated — 100 g
- Sage, chopped — 4 g
- Panko — 40 g
- Dijon mustard — 15 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- All-butter puff pastry — 640 g
- Egg (wash) — 1
- Sesame or nigella seeds — 8 g

## Method
1. Mix sausage meat with apple, fennel, cooled onion, sage, panko, mustard, salt, pepper; beat 1 minute until tacky.
2. Roll pastry to two 40 × 15 cm strips, 3 mm thick.
3. Pipe or shape the filling in a log down each strip; egg-wash the edge; fold, seal with a fork.
4. Cut each into 6; two snips on top of each; egg-wash, seed.
5. Bake at 200 °C fan for 25–30 minutes to deep bronze (filling 74 °C).
6. Cool 10 minutes — or freeze raw and bake at 190 °C for 35 minutes from frozen.

## Plating & Service
Warm in a napkin-lined tin at watch handover, mustard and ketchup within reach.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Freeze raw by the dozen: the night cook 'bakes' with zero skill required. One-handed, crumb-contained (mostly), and worth more than money at 03:00 in a headwind.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken-thyme or merguez-harissa versions use the same build. Vegetarian: mushroom-chestnut duxelles filling, 22 min bake.
