# Rosemary & Sea Salt Focaccia Squares with Confit Garlic

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> An overnight no-knead dough that forgives everything — one tray becomes apéro snack, sandwich bread and soup companion across two days.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Strong white flour — 600 g
- Water (20 °C) — 480 ml
- Instant yeast — 5 g
- Fine sea salt — 12 g
- Olive oil — 120 ml
- Garlic cloves, peeled — 60 g
- Rosemary leaves — 8 g
- Flaky sea salt — 6 g
- Cherry tomatoes (optional) — 200 g

## Method
1. Night before: stir flour, water, yeast and fine salt to a wet shaggy dough; 4 folds in the bowl; cover, fridge 12–24 hours.
2. Confit: submerge garlic in 80 ml oil; bake at 120 °C for 45 minutes until spreadable (do this alongside anything else in the oven).
3. Pour dough into a 30 × 22 cm tray slicked with 20 ml oil; stretch gently; prove 2–3 hours at room temp until bubbly.
4. Oil fingers, dimple hard to the bottom; press in confit garlic, tomatoes and rosemary; remaining oil and flaky salt over.
5. Bake at 220 °C fan for 22–25 minutes to a deep gold with crisp oiled edges.
6. Cool 15 minutes on a rack — never in the tray.

## Plating & Service
Warm squares in a basket at apéro, or split for crew sandwiches; the confit garlic spreads like butter.

> [!note] Galley notes
> The fridge does the kneading — 10 minutes of actual hands-on across two days. Dough holds 48 h chilled, so you bake to the guests' schedule, not the dough's.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Top with olives + oregano, or grapes + fennel seed for a sweet-savoury version. GF is a different recipe — don't force this one.
