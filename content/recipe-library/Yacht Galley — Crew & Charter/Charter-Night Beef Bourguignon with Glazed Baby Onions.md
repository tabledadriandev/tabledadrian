# Charter-Night Beef Bourguignon with Glazed Baby Onions

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The classic done with discipline — marinated overnight, braised low, garnished à part — because some nights the crew mess deserves Burgundy too.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Beef chuck/shin, 5 cm — 2.2 kg
- Full-bodied red wine — 750 ml
- Onion — 200 g
- Carrot — 200 g
- Celery — 80 g
- Garlic — 25 g
- Bouquet garni (thyme, bay, parsley stalks)
- Plain flour — 50 g
- Tomato paste — 50 g
- Beef stock — 700 ml
- Smoked bacon lardons — 250 g
- Baby onions, peeled — 400 g
- Button mushrooms — 400 g
- Unsalted butter — 80 g
- Caster sugar — 8 g
- Neutral oil — 50 ml
- Fine sea salt — 16 g
- Parsley — 15 g

## Method
1. Night before: marinate beef in wine with the mirepoix, garlic and bouquet at 4 °C.
2. Drain (keep everything); dry beef thoroughly; brown hard in oil in batches.
3. Sauté the marinade vegetables 8 minutes; add paste and flour, 2 minutes; add wine, reduce by a third, then stock and beef.
4. Braise lidded at 150 °C fan for 2.5–3 hours until fork-tender.
5. Garnish: render lardons; glaze baby onions with 40 g butter, sugar, water to cover — 15 minutes to amber; sauté mushrooms in remaining butter.
6. Strain sauce, reduce to nappe, return beef; fold in garnishes; rest 15 minutes, finish with parsley.

## Plating & Service
Low bowls over pommes purée or buttered tagliatelle, garnish visible, sauce like liquid mahogany.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Day-two dish by design: braise on prep day, garnish at service — flavour improves and service stress halves. Freezes 3 months without the garnish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: cornflour slurry instead of flour. Swap beef for lamb shanks (same time) or portobello + chestnuts (vegan, 50 min braise).
