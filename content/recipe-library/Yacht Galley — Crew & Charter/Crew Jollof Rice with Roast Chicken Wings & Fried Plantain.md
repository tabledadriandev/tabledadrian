# Crew Jollof Rice with Roast Chicken Wings & Fried Plantain

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Smoky one-pot jollof with crisp wings on top — party energy for the crew mess and a genuine fight over the pot-bottom rice.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Long-grain parboiled rice — 800 g
- Plum tomatoes, tinned — 800 g
- Red peppers — 300 g
- Scotch bonnet (to taste) — 10–20 g
- Onions — 400 g
- Tomato paste — 90 g
- Neutral oil — 120 ml
- Curry powder — 10 g
- Dried thyme — 4 g
- Smoked paprika — 8 g
- Bay leaves — 3
- Chicken stock — 900 ml
- Chicken wings — 1.5 kg
- Garlic powder — 6 g
- Ripe plantains — 3 (600 g)
- Fine sea salt — 18 g

## Method
1. Blitz tomatoes, peppers, scotch bonnet and half the onions to a smooth pepper base.
2. Fry remaining onions in 60 ml oil; add tomato paste, fry 4 minutes until brick-dark; add pepper base, curry powder, thyme, half the paprika, bay; reduce 15 minutes to a thick stew.
3. Stir in washed rice and stock; lid with foil then the pot lid; cook at the lowest heat 30 minutes — do not stir; rest 10. A toasted bottom layer is a feature.
4. Wings: toss with 20 ml oil, remaining paprika, garlic powder, salt; roast at 220 °C fan 35–40 minutes until crisp (85 °C).
5. Fry 1 cm plantain coins in remaining oil, 2 minutes per side, to caramel; salt.
6. Fork the jollof open; wings and plantain on top.

## Plating & Service
One giant platter, wings piled, plantain around the rim — self-service, loud music optional.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Pepper base freezes by the litre — jollof, stews and eggs all season. The no-stir foil lid means the pot minds itself while you plate guest dessert.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: skip wings, add fried tofu + mushrooms; veg stock. Scotch bonnet whole (not blitzed) for fragrant-not-fierce heat.
