# Sesame-Crusted Tuna Steaks with Soba, Edamame & Yuzu Dressing

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Ninety-second sears and a cold soba base — the elegant hot-day main when the fish co-op hands you loins off the morning boat.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Tuna loin steaks, 3 cm thick — 6 × 160 g
- White sesame seeds — 50 g
- Black sesame seeds — 30 g
- Neutral oil — 30 ml
- Soba noodles — 500 g
- Edamame, podded — 250 g
- Cucumber, ribboned — 200 g
- Radishes, shaved — 100 g
- Yuzu juice (or lime + orange) — 40 ml
- Soy sauce — 50 ml
- Mirin — 25 ml
- Toasted sesame oil — 20 ml
- Honey — 15 g
- Ginger, grated — 12 g
- Spring onions — 30 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Dressing: whisk yuzu, soy, mirin, sesame oil, honey, ginger.
2. Cook soba 4–5 minutes; rinse under cold water until completely cool and slick-free; toss with half the dressing, edamame, cucumber, radish.
3. Season tuna; press mixed sesame onto all faces.
4. Sear in oil in a very hot pan: 60–90 seconds per side — crust golden, centre rare (38–40 °C).
5. Rest 2 minutes; slice 1 cm against the grain.
6. Fan over the soba; remaining dressing and spring onions over.

## Plating & Service
Low bowls, noodles twisted into a nest, five tuna slices showing the ruby core.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Everything but the sear is done cold in advance — 5 minutes of fire for 6 covers, priceless in an August galley. Tuna quality decides everything; if in doubt, cook it through and glaze teriyaki-style.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: 100% buckwheat soba + tamari. Vegan: sesame-crusted seared tofu, same dressing. Watermelon 'tuna' for a party trick starter.
