# Pain Perdu of Day-Old Croissants, Orange Blossom & Honeycomb

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Provisioning-smart luxury: yesterday's croissants become the richest pain perdu on the Côte d'Azur.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Day-old croissants — 6 (330 g)
- Eggs — 4 (240 g)
- Whole milk — 250 ml
- Double cream — 100 ml
- Caster sugar — 60 g
- Orange blossom water — 10 ml
- Orange zest — 4 g
- Unsalted butter — 40 g
- Honeycomb or set honey — 90 g
- Crème fraîche — 120 g
- Icing sugar — 10 g
- Fine sea salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Halve croissants horizontally and dry at 120 °C for 8 minutes so they drink the custard without collapsing.
2. Whisk eggs, milk, cream, sugar, orange blossom, zest and salt.
3. Soak croissant halves 2 minutes per side.
4. Fry in foaming butter over medium heat, 2–3 minutes per side to deep gold.
5. Finish in a 180 °C oven for 5 minutes so the centres set to a soft custard (internal 72 °C).

## Plating & Service
Two halves per plate, crème fraîche quenelle, shards of honeycomb, dust of icing sugar.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Soak trays can be built the night before and held chilled; cook straight to a sheet pan at 190 °C for 14 min when scaling to 12 — no pan-flipping in a swell.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Use brioche or panettone offcuts equally well. Dairy-free: oat milk plus coconut cream, fry in coconut oil.
