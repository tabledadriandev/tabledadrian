# Steel-Cut Oats with Miso Caramel Banana & Black Sesame

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Porridge with a tasting-menu finish — miso caramel adds Noma-ish depth for the cost of two pantry staples.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Steel-cut oats — 300 g
- Water — 900 ml
- Whole milk — 400 ml
- Fine sea salt — 3 g
- Bananas, thick diagonal slices — 400 g
- Caster sugar — 100 g
- Double cream — 80 ml
- White miso — 25 g
- Unsalted butter — 30 g
- Black sesame seeds, toasted — 12 g
- Vanilla paste — 4 g

## Method
1. Toast oats dry in the pot 3 minutes until nutty.
2. Add water and salt; simmer 25 minutes, stirring occasionally, then add milk and vanilla; cook 8 minutes more to a loose, creamy porridge.
3. Dry caramel: melt sugar to deep amber (180 °C), whisk in cream carefully, then butter and miso off heat.
4. Sear banana slices in a dry non-stick pan 1 minute per side; glaze with 40 g of the caramel.
5. Bowl the porridge, seat glazed bananas, ripple more miso caramel, scatter black sesame.

## Plating & Service
Wide bowls, bananas fanned, caramel rippled not stirred — the salt-sweet contrast is the point.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Porridge base holds 3 days chilled (loosen with milk); caramel keeps 2 weeks. One pot + one pan, stable underway; scale to 12 with 400 g oats.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: oat milk, coconut cream and plant butter in the caramel. GF: certified GF oats. Swap banana for roasted pear off-season.
