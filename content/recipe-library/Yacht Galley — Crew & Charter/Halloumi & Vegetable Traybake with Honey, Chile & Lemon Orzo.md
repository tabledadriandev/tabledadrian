# Halloumi & Vegetable Traybake with Honey, Chile & Lemon Orzo

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The meat-free tray the crew never complains about: squeaky halloumi gone golden over honey-charred vegetables.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Halloumi, 1.5 cm slabs — 500 g
- Courgettes, chunks — 400 g
- Red peppers, chunks — 300 g
- Red onions, wedges — 250 g
- Cherry tomatoes — 300 g
- Olive oil — 80 ml
- Honey — 30 g
- Chile flakes — 3 g
- Dried oregano — 5 g
- Lemons — 2
- Orzo — 450 g
- Vegetable stock — 1 l
- Mint — 10 g
- Fine sea salt — 10 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 210 °C fan.
2. Toss vegetables with 60 ml oil, oregano, salt on a large tray; roast 15 minutes.
3. Meanwhile simmer orzo in stock 9 minutes until al dente and saucy; finish with zest and juice of 1 lemon.
4. Whisk honey, chile, 20 ml oil, juice of half a lemon.
5. Seat halloumi among the vegetables, brush everything with the honey-chile glaze; roast 12–15 minutes until the cheese is bronzed.
6. Rest 3 minutes; shower with mint.

## Plating & Service
Lemon orzo on the platter, tray contents over, pan juices last; lemon wedges for sharpness.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Chops ahead into one hotel pan; 35 minutes of oven and one pot of orzo — no à-la-minute anything. Halloumi survives holding on a buffet without weeping like mozzarella would.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: extra-firm tofu brushed with the same glaze. GF: swap orzo for rice or GF pasta. Add butter beans for a bigger protein hit.
