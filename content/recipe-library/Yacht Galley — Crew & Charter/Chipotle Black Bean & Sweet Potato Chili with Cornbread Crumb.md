# Chipotle Black Bean & Sweet Potato Chili with Cornbread Crumb

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The meatless chili that disappears faster than the beef one — smoky, thick, freezer-loyal, and safe for nearly every diet card on board.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Black beans, cooked — 1.4 kg
- Sweet potatoes, 2 cm dice — 900 g
- Onions, diced — 300 g
- Red peppers, diced — 300 g
- Garlic — 25 g
- Olive oil — 60 ml
- Chipotle in adobo, chopped — 45 g
- Ground cumin — 12 g
- Smoked paprika — 10 g
- Dried oregano — 4 g
- Cocoa powder — 8 g
- Chopped tomatoes — 800 g
- Vegetable stock — 600 ml
- Lime juice — 40 ml
- Coriander — 25 g
- Fine sea salt — 14 g

## Method
1. Roast sweet potato with 30 ml oil and salt at 210 °C fan for 20 minutes.
2. Soften onion and pepper in 30 ml oil 8 minutes; add garlic, chipotle, cumin, paprika, oregano, cocoa; fry 2 minutes.
3. Add tomatoes, beans, stock; simmer 25 minutes, crushing some beans against the pot for body.
4. Fold in roasted sweet potato; simmer 5 minutes; season with lime and salt.
5. Rest 10 minutes — chili thickens and settles.
6. Serve with coriander; cornbread or tortilla chips crumbled over.

## Plating & Service
Deep bowls with lime, avocado and whatever the crew hoards (cheese, jalapeños, crema) in the middle of the table.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Freezes 3 months in flat bags and feeds vegans, GF and dairy-free cards without a single substitution — the safest big-batch main on the boat. Thermos-ready in weather.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Meat table: brown 500 g beef with the onions. Toppings do the personalisation — keep the pot universal.
