# No-Bake Lime & Ginger Cheesecake Pots

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Zesty whipped cheesecake over a gingernut rubble, built in glasses — no oven, no water bath, no unmoulding anxiety at anchor.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Gingernut biscuits — 250 g
- Unsalted butter, melted — 90 g
- Full-fat cream cheese — 600 g
- Double cream — 300 ml
- Icing sugar — 130 g
- Limes — 5 (juice 100 ml, zest 10 g)
- Vanilla paste — 4 g
- Stem ginger in syrup, minced — 50 g
- Ginger syrup (from the jar) — 30 ml
- Fine sea salt — 2 g
- White chocolate, grated — 40 g

## Method
1. Crush biscuits to coarse rubble; bind with melted butter and salt; press into the base of 10 glasses; chill 20 minutes.
2. Beat cream cheese with icing sugar, vanilla, lime zest and stem ginger until smooth.
3. Whip cream to soft peaks; fold into the cheese with the lime juice — it thickens as the acid hits.
4. Pipe or spoon over the bases; swirl the tops.
5. Chill 3+ hours; drizzle ginger syrup and finish with white chocolate and extra zest.

## Plating & Service
In the glass, syrup pooling into the swirl — a spoon-through-the-layers dessert, no plating skill required at service.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Fully built 2 days ahead and served straight from the fridge; glasses can't collapse, slide or crack like a whole cheesecake underway.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: GF ginger biscuits. Swap lime for lemon + 20 g curd rippled through. Vegan: plant cream cheese + whipped coconut cream, maple for icing sugar.
