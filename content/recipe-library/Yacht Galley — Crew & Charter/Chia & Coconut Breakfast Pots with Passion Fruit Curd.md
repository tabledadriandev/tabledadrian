# Chia & Coconut Breakfast Pots with Passion Fruit Curd

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Set-and-forget guest pots with a sharp passion fruit curd that cuts the coconut — built during evening service, plated in seconds.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Chia seeds — 90 g
- Coconut milk — 500 ml
- Whole milk — 200 ml
- Maple syrup — 50 ml
- Vanilla paste — 5 g
- Passion fruit pulp — 160 g
- Eggs — 2 (120 g)
- Egg yolks — 2 (40 g)
- Caster sugar — 90 g
- Unsalted butter — 70 g
- Lime juice — 15 ml
- Coconut flakes, toasted — 30 g
- Fine sea salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Whisk chia, coconut milk, milk, maple, vanilla and salt; rest 15 minutes, whisk again to break clumps, portion into 8 glasses and chill 4 hours minimum.
2. For the curd: whisk passion fruit pulp, eggs, yolks, sugar and lime in a bain-marie.
3. Cook to 82 °C, stirring constantly, 8–10 minutes until thickly coating a spatula.
4. Off heat, beat in cold butter cubes; pass through a fine sieve, keeping some seeds back to fold in.
5. Chill curd fully (2 hours), then crown each pot with 25 g curd and toasted coconut.

## Plating & Service
Serve in glass verrines so the two layers show; a few whole passion fruit seeds on top for crunch.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Both elements hold 4 days chilled — build 16 pots on turnaround and breakfast garnish is the only morning job. Spill-proof in any sea state.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: curd becomes passion fruit-mango coulis (blend 200 g mango, 100 g pulp, 30 g sugar). Dairy-free: all coconut milk, coconut oil in the curd.
