# Crew Bibimbap Bowls with Gochujang Beef & Market Vegetables

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> One rice pot, five toppings, everyone builds their own — the crew dinner that quietly empties the vegetable drawer before provisioning day.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Short-grain rice — 700 g
- Beef mince or thin-sliced sirloin — 900 g
- Soy sauce — 60 ml
- Gochujang — 90 g
- Toasted sesame oil — 40 ml
- Honey — 25 g
- Garlic — 20 g
- Rice vinegar — 25 ml
- Carrots, julienned — 300 g
- Courgette, julienned — 300 g
- Spinach — 300 g
- Shiitake or button mushrooms, sliced — 250 g
- Beansprouts — 200 g
- Eggs — 8
- Neutral oil — 50 ml
- Sesame seeds — 12 g
- Spring onions — 40 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Cook rice (1:1.1, 12 minutes, rest 10).
2. Sauce: whisk gochujang, 20 ml sesame oil, honey, vinegar and 20 ml water.
3. Marinate beef 15 minutes in soy, garlic, 10 ml sesame oil; sear hard in a smoking wok 3–4 minutes.
4. Sauté each vegetable separately in neutral oil with a pinch of salt, 1–2 minutes each; dress spinach and sprouts with remaining sesame oil.
5. Fry eggs crisp-edged, yolks soft.
6. Bowls: rice, vegetables in wedges, beef, egg on top, sauce and sesame at the table.

## Plating & Service
Arrange toppings like a colour wheel; the diner smashes and mixes — that's the dish.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Every topping holds 3 days chilled, so tonight's prep is tomorrow's 5-minute lunch. Dolsot-style option: crisp the rice in a hot oiled pan bottom for the crackle.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: five-spice tofu instead of beef and egg. GF: tamari + GF gochujang. Whatever vegetables are wilting — that's the recipe.
