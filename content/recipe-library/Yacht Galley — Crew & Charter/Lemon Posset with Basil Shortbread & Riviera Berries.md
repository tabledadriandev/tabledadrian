# Lemon Posset with Basil Shortbread & Riviera Berries

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Three-ingredient magic that sets itself in the fridge — the charter dessert with the highest elegance-to-effort ratio known to galley science.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Double cream — 900 ml
- Caster sugar — 220 g
- Lemons — 4 (juice 120 ml, zest 8 g)
- Plain flour — 170 g
- Unsalted butter, soft — 110 g
- Icing sugar — 55 g
- Basil leaves, minced — 6 g
- Fine sea salt — 2 g
- Mixed berries — 250 g
- Basil tips — 8

## Method
1. Simmer cream, caster sugar and zest for exactly 3 minutes, stirring — it must reduce slightly.
2. Off heat, whisk in lemon juice; the acid sets it.
3. Strain into 8 glasses; chill 4+ hours until softly set.
4. Shortbread: beat butter, icing sugar, salt; fold in flour and minced basil; press 1 cm thick, chill 20 minutes.
5. Cut into batons; bake at 160 °C fan for 20–25 minutes to pale gold; sugar-dust warm.
6. Serve possets with berries on top and shortbread alongside.

## Plating & Service
Glass verrines, three berries and a basil tip each, shortbread crossed over the rim.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Possets hold 3 days chilled and travel in their glasses — the make-ahead that never fails at anchor or dockside. Shortbread keeps a week in a tin.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Lime + ginger posset variant. GF: almond-flour shortbread or none. Lighter finish impossible — this is cream; portion small, garnish sharp.
