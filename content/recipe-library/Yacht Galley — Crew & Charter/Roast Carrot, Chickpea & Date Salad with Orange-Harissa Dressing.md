# Roast Carrot, Chickpea & Date Salad with Orange-Harissa Dressing

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Blistered carrots and crisped chickpeas under a warm-spiced orange dressing — a salad with enough backbone to sit beside BBQ meat all afternoon.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Carrots, halved lengthwise — 1.2 kg
- Chickpeas, cooked, dried well — 480 g
- Olive oil — 100 ml
- Ground cumin — 8 g
- Ground coriander — 6 g
- Cinnamon — 2 g
- Medjool dates, sliced — 150 g
- Orange juice — 80 ml
- Orange zest — 4 g
- Harissa — 30 g
- Lemon juice — 30 ml
- Maple syrup — 15 g
- Tahini — 60 g
- Mint & coriander leaves — 25 g
- Pistachios or seeds — 50 g
- Fine sea salt — 12 g

## Method
1. Toss carrots with 50 ml oil, cumin, coriander, cinnamon, salt; roast at 210 °C fan for 25–30 minutes until blistered and sweet.
2. Toss chickpeas with 30 ml oil and salt on a second tray; roast 20 minutes until crisp.
3. Dressing: whisk orange juice and zest, harissa, lemon, maple, 20 ml oil, salt.
4. Tahini drizzle: loosen tahini with 40 ml water and a squeeze of lemon.
5. Platter: warm carrots, chickpeas and dates tossed in the dressing.
6. Finish with tahini ribbons, herbs and pistachios.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm or at room temperature, herbs added at the last minute; it's a centrepiece, not a side dish, so give it a big platter.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Everything holds and travels: roast in the morning, dress at the beach — no leaves, no wilt, no mayo. Doubles as tomorrow's grain-bowl base with leftover chicken.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: toasted pumpkin seeds. Add 200 g feta or grilled halloumi where dairy is welcome. Swap carrots for roasted squash from October.
