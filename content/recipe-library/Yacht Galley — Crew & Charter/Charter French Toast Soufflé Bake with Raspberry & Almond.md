# Charter French Toast Soufflé Bake with Raspberry & Almond

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A bread-and-butter-pudding-meets-French-toast tray assembled the night before — the oven works while you run coffee service.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Brioche or challah, 3 cm cubes — 700 g
- Eggs — 8 (480 g)
- Whole milk — 600 ml
- Double cream — 200 ml
- Caster sugar — 120 g
- Vanilla paste — 8 g
- Orange zest — 5 g
- Raspberries — 300 g
- Flaked almonds — 60 g
- Demerara sugar — 40 g
- Unsalted butter — 30 g
- Icing sugar — 15 g
- Fine sea salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Butter a 30 × 22 cm baking dish; fill with brioche cubes and scatter raspberries through.
2. Whisk eggs, milk, cream, caster sugar, vanilla, zest and salt; pour over and press bread down.
3. Cover, refrigerate 4 hours or overnight so the custard soaks fully.
4. Heat oven to 165 °C fan. Scatter almonds and demerara over the top.
5. Bake 40–45 minutes until puffed, deep gold, centre at 75 °C and barely wobbling.
6. Rest 10 minutes; dust with icing sugar.

## Plating & Service
Scoop generous squares at the buffet with crème fraîche and warm maple syrup.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Uses up every stale end of the bread bin; assembles in 15 minutes at midnight. One dish feeds 10 with no à-la-minute cooking — changeover-morning insurance.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: skip almonds, use oat crumble. Swap raspberries for sliced pears and 3 g cardamom in winter. Dairy-free: oat milk + coconut cream.
