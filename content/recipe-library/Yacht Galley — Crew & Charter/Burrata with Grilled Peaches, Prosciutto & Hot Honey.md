# Burrata with Grilled Peaches, Prosciutto & Hot Honey

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Four ingredients and a plancha — the apéro platter that buys the galley an hour of goodwill during a slow lunch service.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Burrata — 4 × 125 g
- Peaches, ripe but firm, quartered — 6 (750 g)
- Prosciutto crudo — 160 g
- Honey — 60 g
- Chile flakes — 3 g
- Red wine vinegar — 10 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 50 ml
- Basil leaves — 12
- Flaky sea salt — 4 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Hot honey: warm honey with chile flakes and vinegar to 60 °C; infuse 15 minutes.
2. Bring burrata to room temperature 30 minutes — cold kills its cream.
3. Grill peach quarters cut-side down on a 240 °C plancha, 2 minutes per face, until caramel-striped but holding shape.
4. Drape prosciutto loosely across a platter, peaks and valleys, never flat.
5. Nestle whole burrate among peaches; tear open at the table.
6. Dress with oil, hot honey, basil, flaky salt, pepper.

## Plating & Service
Tear the burrata in front of guests so the cream floods the peaches — that moment is the dish.

> [!note] Galley notes
> Hot honey keeps a month and upgrades pizza, fried chicken and cheese boards. Total fire time is 4 minutes; everything else is shopping and restraint.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian: skip prosciutto, add toasted hazelnuts. Swap peaches for figs (no grilling, just torn) in September, tomatoes in high summer.
