# Turbot Rôti, Beurre Blanc au Champagne

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Thick tranches of turbot roasted on the bone for gelatinous succulence, with a champagne beurre blanc — the king of flat fish, treated royally.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Turbot tranches on the bone — 4 (about 300 g each)
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Unsalted butter — 30 g (for roasting)
- Thyme — 3 sprigs
- Fine salt — 8 g
- White pepper — 1 g
- Beurre blanc: shallots, minced — 50 g
- Champagne (or crémant) — 120 ml
- Champagne vinegar — 20 ml
- Double cream — 20 ml
- Cold unsalted butter, diced — 180 g
- Chives, sliced — 8 g
- Lemon juice — 5 ml

## Method
1. Salt the tranches 20 min ahead; pat dry. Oven to 200 °C.
2. Reduce shallots, champagne and vinegar to 30 ml. Add cream (a stabiliser), reduce 30 sec.
3. Sear tranches dark-skin side down in oil in an ovenproof pan, 3 min; turn, add butter and thyme.
4. Roast 8–12 min depending on thickness, basting once, to a core of 54 °C; flesh should pull from the bone with a nudge.
5. Rest fish 3 min on a rack.
6. Finish the sauce: over low heat, whisk in cold butter cube by cube, never above 60 °C; season with salt, lemon; add chives.
7. Serve fish with the beurre blanc spooned generously alongside.

## Plating & Service
Tranche centred, sauce pooled to one side, a few chives over; steamed ratte potatoes and sea beet complete it.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap champagne for plain white wine any weeknight. Dairy-free: serve with sauce vierge instead of beurre blanc.
