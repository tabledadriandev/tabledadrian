# Financiers aux Amandes, Beurre Noisette

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Gold-bar almond cakes built on brown butter and egg whites — the pastry section's favourite way to spend leftover whites, and the galley's best coffee companion.

## Ingredients (serves 8, ~20 financiers)
- Unsalted butter — 150 g
- Egg whites — 5 (150 g)
- Icing sugar — 150 g
- Ground almonds — 90 g
- Plain flour — 60 g
- Honey — 15 g
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Vanilla pod — 1/2
- Flaked almonds — 20 g

## Method
1. Brown the butter to deep noisette (solids toast-brown, smells of hazelnut); strain; cool to 45 °C.
2. Whisk icing sugar, ground almonds, flour and salt in a bowl.
3. Stir in the egg whites (unwhipped — just liquid) until smooth.
4. Add honey, vanilla seeds, then the brown butter in a stream.
5. Rest the batter 1 h chilled (up to 3 days — it improves).
6. Pipe into buttered financier or mini-loaf moulds to 3/4; scatter flaked almonds.
7. Bake at 190 °C for 14–18 min until golden with darker edges; the centre should spring back.
8. Cool 5 min; unmould onto a rack.

## Plating & Service
With coffee after dinner, or as the crunch beside a poached-fruit dessert; best within 24 h.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pistachio version: half ground pistachio. Raspberry: press one berry into each before baking. GF: replace flour with rice flour.
