# Salade Lyonnaise, Crisp Bacon & Poached Egg

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bitter frisée, hot bacon vinaigrette and a just-set egg — the bouchon classic that turns a salad into an event.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Frisée (curly endive), pale hearts — 300 g
- Smoked bacon lardons — 180 g
- Baguette, 1 cm cubes — 100 g
- Eggs, very fresh — 4
- Red wine vinegar — 40 ml
- Dijon mustard — 15 g
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Shallot, finely chopped — 40 g
- White wine vinegar (poaching) — 30 ml
- Flat-leaf parsley — 8 g
- Black pepper — 1 g
- Fine salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Wash and thoroughly dry frisée; keep chilled.
2. Fry bread cubes in olive oil until golden, 4 min; season and drain.
3. Render lardons over medium heat 5–6 min until crisp; keep pan and fat.
4. Poach eggs 3 min at a bare simmer with white vinegar; hold on cloth.
5. Off the heat, whisk shallot, Dijon and red wine vinegar into the warm bacon fat to make the dressing.
6. Toss frisée with the warm vinaigrette, lardons and croutons; season.
7. Top each portion with an egg and crack pepper over.

## Plating & Service
Serve immediately in wide bowls while the dressing is warm — the egg goes on last.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: omit croutons or use GF bread. Lighter: use pancetta at 100 g and add shaved radish for crunch.
