# Filet Mignon de Porc, Sauce Charcutière

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Blushing pork tenderloin with the old butcher's sauce — sharp with mustard, gherkins and onion; a forgotten classic worth reviving.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Pork tenderloins, trimmed — 2 (about 450 g each)
- Fine salt — 9 g; black pepper 2 g
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Onion, minced — 120 g
- Dry white wine — 150 ml
- White wine vinegar — 15 ml
- Brown stock — 300 ml
- Tomato paste — 20 g
- Dijon mustard — 20 g
- Cornichons, julienned — 60 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 8 g
- Sugar — 3 g

## Method
1. Salt the tenderloins 30 min ahead; fold and tie the thin tails for even cooking.
2. Sear all over in oil over medium-high heat, 6–8 min total, to deep gold.
3. Finish at 180 °C oven to a core of 60 °C, about 8–10 min. Rest 8 min (carries to 63 °C, juicy and just blush).
4. Sauce: in the pan, soften onion 4 min; add vinegar and wine, reduce by half.
5. Add stock, tomato paste and sugar; reduce to a light nap.
6. Off the boil, whisk in Dijon (it must not boil after); add cornichons and resting juices; season.
7. Slice pork 2 cm thick; sauce over, parsley to finish.

## Plating & Service
Sliced on warm plates over pommes purée or lentils, cornichon julienne visible in the sauce.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Same sauce transforms grilled pork chops or leftover roast pork (the original use). Add 30 g butter to mount if dairy is fine.
