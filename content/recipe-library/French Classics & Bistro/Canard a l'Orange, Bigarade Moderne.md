# Canard à l'Orange, Bigarade Moderne

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Whole roast duck with a proper bigarade — caramel, vinegar and bitter orange in balance, none of the syrupy sweetness that killed the dish's name.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Whole duck — 1 (about 2.2 kg)
- Fine salt — 14 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Oranges — 3 (zest of 1, juice ~200 ml, segments of 1)
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Caster sugar — 40 g
- White wine vinegar — 40 ml
- Grand Marnier — 30 ml
- Brown chicken/duck stock — 400 ml
- Thyme — 4 sprigs
- Bay leaf — 1
- Cornflour — 6 g (slurried in 15 ml water)

## Method
1. Salt the duck inside and out; rest uncovered in the fridge 12–24 h for dry skin. Prick fatty areas; truss.
2. Roast breast-up at 160 °C for 60 min on a rack, then 15 min at 210 °C, basting, until skin is lacquered mahogany; leg core 80 °C, breast will carry to ~62 °C at the crown.
3. Rest the bird 20 min, breast down.
4. Gastrique: cook sugar to a deep amber caramel; stop with vinegar (stand back). Add orange juice, lemon juice and zest; reduce by half.
5. Add stock, thyme, bay and roasting juices (fat poured off); simmer 10 min; strain.
6. Thicken lightly with cornflour slurry to a glossy nap; finish with Grand Marnier; season.
7. Warm orange segments in the sauce 1 min.
8. Carve; spoon sauce over legs and sliced breast.

## Plating & Service
Carve at the table if sea state allows; serve with pommes sarladaises and watercress.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Faster: use 4 duck legs, braised in the sauce 90 min. Bitter Seville oranges (in season) replace the lemon entirely.
