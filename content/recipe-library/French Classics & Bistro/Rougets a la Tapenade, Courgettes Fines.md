# Rougets à la Tapenade, Courgettes Fines

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Crisp-skinned red mullet on ribboned courgettes with a black olive tapenade — small fish, huge Mediterranean voice.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Red mullet fillets, pin-boned, skin on — 8 (about 480 g)
- Black olives, pitted (Niçoise or Kalamata) — 100 g
- Anchovy fillets — 3 (12 g)
- Capers — 15 g
- Garlic — 4 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Courgettes — 3 (450 g)
- Basil — 10 g
- Espelette pepper — 0.5 g
- Fine salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Tapenade: pulse olives, anchovies, capers, garlic and 40 ml oil to a coarse paste; add 10 ml lemon. No salt needed.
2. Ribbon courgettes lengthwise on a mandoline (2 mm); salt lightly, rest 10 min, pat dry.
3. Warm courgette ribbons in a pan with 20 ml oil for 90 sec — just supple; season, add basil.
4. Dry the mullet skin thoroughly; season flesh side.
5. Sear skin-side down in remaining oil over high heat, pressing 10 sec so they stay flat; cook 2–3 min until skin is crisp and the flesh is opaque two-thirds up.
6. Flip for 20 sec only; finish with remaining lemon.
7. Plate courgettes, fish skin-up, tapenade in three neat dots per plate.

## Plating & Service
Two fillets per plate, skin blistered and facing up, Espelette dusted over.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan option for the table: same courgettes and tapenade over grilled aubergine steaks. Sea bream fillets substitute cleanly.
