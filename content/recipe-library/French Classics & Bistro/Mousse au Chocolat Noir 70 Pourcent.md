# Mousse au Chocolat Noir 70 Pourcent

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The French-family mousse — whole eggs, real chocolate, no cream in sight; four ingredients balanced so the chocolate does all the talking.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Dark chocolate, 70% — 200 g
- Unsalted butter — 50 g
- Eggs, very fresh, separated — 6 (yolks 120 g, whites 180 g)
- Caster sugar — 40 g
- Fine salt — 1.5 g
- Espresso (optional) — 20 ml
- Cocoa nibs, to finish — 15 g

## Method
1. Melt chocolate and butter over a bain-marie to 45 °C; stir in espresso; cool to 38 °C.
2. Beat yolks into the chocolate one by one — the mix will thicken and gloss.
3. Whip whites with salt to soft foam; rain in sugar; continue to soft, droopy peaks — NOT stiff (stiff whites make dry mousse).
4. Whisk one-third of the whites into the chocolate to lighten it.
5. Fold in the rest in two additions with a spatula, cutting to the bottom — stop at the last streak.
6. Pour into a large bowl or six glasses; chill 4 h minimum.
7. Finish with cocoa nibs and a few salt flakes.

## Plating & Service
One big bowl passed around the table with a serving spoon is the true French way.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Orange zest or 10 ml Grand Marnier in the base. Uses raw eggs — source impeccably or use pasteurised for vulnerable guests.
