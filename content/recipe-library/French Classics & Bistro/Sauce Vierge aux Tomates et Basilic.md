# Sauce Vierge aux Tomates et Basilic

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The 'virgin' sauce — warm olive oil, raw tomato, herbs and coriander seed; the modern classic that dresses any grilled fish in ninety seconds.

## Ingredients (serves 6, makes ~400 ml)
- Extra virgin olive oil — 150 ml
- Ripe tomatoes, peeled, deseeded, 5 mm dice — 300 g
- Shallot, fine dice — 40 g
- Coriander seeds, toasted, crushed — 3 g
- Basil, chiffonade — 12 g
- Chervil or parsley, chopped — 6 g
- Lemon juice — 25 ml
- Lemon zest — 2 g
- Black olives, slivered (optional) — 30 g
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Espelette pepper — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Warm the olive oil with the coriander seeds to 60 °C — just hand-hot; infuse 5 min off the heat.
2. Stir in shallot; let it soften in the warmth 2 min.
3. Add tomato dice, lemon juice, zest and olives.
4. Season with salt and Espelette.
5. Fold in the herbs at the last second — heat kills them.
6. Serve warm (50 °C), never hot; the tomato must stay fresh, not stewed.

## Plating & Service
Spoon generously over roasted sea bass, grilled scallops, burrata or warm vegetables.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Riviera version: add 15 g capers and swap lemon for orange with fennel-spiced fish. Keeps 24 h chilled — bring back to 50 °C gently.
