# Soupe au Pistou, Summer Beans & Basil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Provence in a bowl — a garden-vegetable soup finished with raw basil pistou stirred in at the table so the perfume hits first.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Fresh white coco beans (or cooked cannellini) — 300 g
- Green beans, cut 2 cm — 200 g
- Courgette, 1 cm dice — 250 g
- Carrot, 1 cm dice — 150 g
- Waxy potato, 1 cm dice — 200 g
- Ripe tomatoes, chopped — 300 g
- Small macaroni — 100 g
- Vegetable stock — 2 L
- Basil leaves — 60 g
- Garlic — 15 g
- Olive oil — 100 ml
- Fine salt — 10 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Simmer coco beans in the stock 20 min (skip if using cooked beans).
2. Add carrot and potato; simmer 10 min.
3. Add courgette, green beans, tomatoes and pasta; simmer 12 min until pasta is just tender. Season.
4. Pound garlic with a pinch of salt in a mortar; add basil and pound to a paste, then work in olive oil to a loose pistou.
5. Rest soup 5 min off the heat; check seasoning.
6. Serve with a generous spoon of pistou swirled into each bowl at the table.

## Plating & Service
Rustic wide bowls; pass extra pistou and grated cheese separately for those who want it.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: use rice-based pasta. Classic Niçois: stir 30 g grated Parmesan into the pistou (no longer vegan).
