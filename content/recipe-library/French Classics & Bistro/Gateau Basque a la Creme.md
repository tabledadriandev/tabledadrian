# Gâteau Basque à la Crème

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Two discs of buttery, almost-cookie dough sealed over rum-scented pastry cream — the Basque country's crest-scored pride, better on day two.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Dough: plain flour 300 g, ground almonds 40 g, baking powder 4 g, salt 3 g, butter (soft) 180 g, caster sugar 160 g, egg 1 (55 g), egg yolk 1 (20 g), lemon zest 4 g
- Cream: whole milk 350 ml, vanilla pod 1/2, egg yolks 4 (80 g), caster sugar 70 g, cornflour 30 g, dark rum 15 ml, butter 15 g
- Egg wash: yolk 1 + milk 10 ml

## Method
1. Dough: cream butter and sugar; add egg, yolk and zest; fold in flour, almonds, baking powder, salt — a soft cookie dough. Chill 2 h minimum.
2. Cream: standard pâtissière (yolks/sugar/cornflour + hot vanilla milk, boil 2 min); finish with butter and rum; chill filmed.
3. Roll two-thirds of the dough to 4 mm; line a buttered 22 cm tin (base + sides). Roll the rest into a 22 cm lid.
4. Fill with the cold cream, leaving a 1 cm border; moisten the border.
5. Cap with the lid; press the edges to seal; trim.
6. Egg-wash; score the traditional cross-hatch (or Basque cross) with a fork.
7. Bake at 175 °C for 40–45 min until deep gold. Cool COMPLETELY in the tin — it sets as it cools.

## Plating & Service
Room-temperature wedges, nothing else — Basques consider accompaniment an insult; it keeps 3 days.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cherry version: black cherry jam (Itxassou) instead of cream. Half-and-half: jam below, cream above — heterodox, delicious.
