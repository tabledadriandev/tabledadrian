# Haricots Verts Amandine

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Fine beans blanched to a squeak and rolled in almond brown butter — the little black dress of French sides.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Fine green beans, topped — 500 g
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Flaked almonds — 40 g
- Shallot, minced — 30 g
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Lemon zest — 2 g
- Fine salt — 12 g (mostly for the blanching water)
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Boil 3 L water with 10 g salt — sea-water salty.
2. Blanch beans 3–4 min: tender but with a whisper of bite. Do not ice if serving immediately — drain hard.
3. Brown the butter with the almonds together over medium heat, 2–3 min, until both are hazelnut-toned.
4. Add shallot to the foaming butter for 30 sec.
5. Off heat, add lemon juice (it will hiss), zest and 2 g salt.
6. Roll the hot beans through the pan to gloss every one; pepper over.

## Plating & Service
A neat parallel bundle or a loose tangle beside trout, chicken or a Sunday roast.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: toasted breadcrumbs or crisp shallots instead. Vegan: olive oil warmed with almonds, plus a pinch of smoked salt.
