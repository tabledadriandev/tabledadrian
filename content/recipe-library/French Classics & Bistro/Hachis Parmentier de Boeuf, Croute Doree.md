# Hachis Parmentier de Bœuf, Croûte Dorée

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> France's shepherd's pie — braised-beef richness under a buttery gratinated mash; the smartest destiny for yesterday's pot-au-feu.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Beef (braised leftovers, shredded, or fresh mince) — 800 g
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Onion, chopped — 200 g
- Carrot, fine dice — 150 g
- Garlic — 10 g
- Tomato paste — 30 g
- Red wine — 150 ml
- Brown stock — 250 ml
- Thyme — 4 sprigs; bay 1
- Floury potatoes — 1.2 kg
- Whole milk, hot — 200 ml
- Unsalted butter — 100 g
- Egg yolk — 1 (20 g)
- Comté, grated — 80 g
- Nutmeg — 0.5 g
- Fine salt — 14 g; black pepper 2 g

## Method
1. Soften onion, carrot and garlic in oil, 6 min. Add mince (or shredded beef); brown 6 min.
2. Add tomato paste, 2 min; deglaze with wine, reduce; add stock, thyme, bay. Simmer 20 min to a moist, not wet, ragoût; season.
3. Steam potatoes 25 min; rice; beat with butter, hot milk, yolk, nutmeg and salt to a firm purée.
4. Spread beef in a gratin dish; pipe or fork the purée over completely.
5. Rake the surface, scatter Comté.
6. Bake at 200 °C for 25–30 min until bronzed and bubbling at the edges.
7. Rest 10 min before serving.

## Plating & Service
Big spoonfuls at the table from the dish, sharp green salad with mustard vinaigrette alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Duck confit version: 500 g shredded confit, no wine, add 5 g thyme. Dairy-light: olive-oil mash and skip the cheese.
