# Œufs en Meurette, Red Wine Jus & Lardons

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Burgundy's great egg dish — poached eggs in a glossy red-wine reduction that tastes like a bœuf bourguignon distilled.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Eggs, very fresh — 4 (plus 2 spare)
- Red Burgundy (or Pinot Noir) — 500 ml
- Brown chicken stock — 300 ml
- Smoked bacon lardons — 120 g
- Button mushrooms, quartered — 150 g
- Pearl onions, peeled — 120 g
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Plain flour — 10 g
- Sugar — 3 g
- White wine vinegar (for poaching) — 30 ml
- Sourdough, sliced — 4 slices (120 g)
- Garlic clove — 1
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Reduce wine by two-thirds with the stock in a saucepan (to about 300 ml total).
2. Brown lardons in a dry pan; remove. Glaze pearl onions with 20 g butter, sugar and 50 ml water, 10 min; add mushrooms, sauté 4 min.
3. Knead 10 g butter with the flour (beurre manié); whisk into the simmering reduction until it coats a spoon. Season.
4. Return lardons, onions and mushrooms to the sauce; keep at 80 °C.
5. Poach eggs 3 min in barely simmering water with vinegar; drain on cloth.
6. Rub toasted sourdough with garlic and butter it.
7. Set each egg on a crouton, spoon the meurette generously over.

## Plating & Service
Shallow bowls, sauce flooding around the crouton; the yolk must run when cut.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: thicken with 5 g cornflour slurry and serve on GF toast. Vegetarian: replace lardons with smoked shiitake and use vegetable stock.
