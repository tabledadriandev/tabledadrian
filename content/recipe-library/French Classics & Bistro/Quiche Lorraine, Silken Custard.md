# Quiche Lorraine, Silken Custard

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Blind-baked to a biscuit snap, filled with a barely-set 3:2 cream custard — no cheese, as Lorraine intended; the wobble is everything.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Plain flour — 250 g
- Cold unsalted butter — 125 g
- Egg yolk — 1 (20 g)
- Cold water — 40 ml
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Smoked bacon lardons — 200 g
- Eggs — 3 (165 g)
- Egg yolks — 2 (40 g)
- Double cream — 300 ml
- Whole milk — 200 ml
- Nutmeg — 0.5 g
- White pepper — 1 g
- Fine salt (custard) — 3 g

## Method
1. Rub flour, salt and butter to coarse sand; bind with yolk and water. Rest 1 h.
2. Roll to 3 mm, line a 24 cm tart ring, dock, rest 30 min in the freezer.
3. Blind-bake at 170 °C with beans 20 min; remove beans, bake 8 min more; brush with a little beaten egg and bake 2 min to seal.
4. Blanch lardons 1 min, then crisp in a dry pan 5 min; drain and scatter over the base.
5. Whisk eggs, yolks, cream, milk, salt, pepper, nutmeg; strain.
6. Pull the oven shelf out, fill the case to the brim, slide in gently.
7. Bake at 140 °C for 35–40 min to a core of 74 °C — centre should tremble like a set panna cotta.
8. Rest 20 min before unringing.

## Plating & Service
Serve barely warm in slim wedges with dressed frisée — never fridge-cold.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian: swap lardons for charred leeks and 80 g Gruyère (a quiche vosgienne cousin). The pastry freezes well raw-in-ring.
