# Coq au Vin de Bourgogne

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Chicken legs braised in reduced red wine with the holy trinity of lardons, pearl onions and mushrooms — marinate overnight and the sauce turns profound.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Chicken legs, whole (or 1 jointed large bird) — 6 (about 1.8 kg)
- Red Burgundy — 750 ml
- Brown chicken stock — 400 ml
- Cognac — 40 ml
- Smoked bacon lardons — 180 g
- Pearl onions, peeled — 250 g
- Button mushrooms — 300 g
- Carrot, chunks — 150 g
- Onion, chunks — 150 g
- Garlic — 15 g
- Tomato paste — 25 g
- Plain flour — 25 g
- Unsalted butter — 50 g
- Thyme — 5 sprigs; bay 2
- Fine salt — 12 g; black pepper 2 g
- Sugar — 5 g

## Method
1. Marinate chicken overnight in wine with carrot, onion, garlic, thyme and bay.
2. Drain (keep everything); pat chicken very dry; season. Boil the wine, skimming, 5 min.
3. Render lardons in a cocotte; remove. Brown chicken hard in the fat, in batches; remove.
4. Brown marinade vegetables 5 min; add tomato paste and flour, cook 2 min.
5. Add Cognac, flambé; add wine and stock. Return chicken and lardons; braise, lid on, at 150 °C oven for 75–90 min until the meat waves on the bone.
6. Meanwhile glaze pearl onions with 25 g butter, sugar and 100 ml water, 12 min; sauté mushrooms in remaining butter until golden.
7. Lift chicken; strain and reduce sauce to coating consistency; season.
8. Reunite chicken, sauce, onions, mushrooms and lardons; simmer 5 min.

## Plating & Service
Serve from the cocotte with pommes purée or buttered tagliatelle; parsley over.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: thicken with 15 g cornflour at the end instead of flour. Alsace variation: use Riesling and finish with cream (coq au Riesling).
