# Tarte aux Fraises, Crème Diplomate

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Crisp sablé, vanilla diplomat cream and market strawberries standing to attention — the patisserie-window tart that tastes as good as it looks.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Sablé: flour 210 g, icing sugar 80 g, ground almonds 30 g, cold butter 120 g, egg 1 (55 g), salt 2 g
- Diplomat: whole milk 350 ml, vanilla pod 1, egg yolks 4 (80 g), caster sugar 70 g, cornflour 30 g, butter 20 g, gold gelatine 1 leaf (2 g), whipped double cream 120 ml
- Strawberries, ripe, even-sized — 700 g
- Strawberry or redcurrant jam — 60 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Pistachios, chopped — 15 g

## Method
1. Sablé: rub dry ingredients with butter to sand; bind with egg; chill 2 h. Roll 3 mm; line a 24 cm ring; freeze 30 min.
2. Blind-bake at 160 °C: 20 min with beans, 10–12 min without, to even gold. Cool.
3. Pâtissière: yolks + sugar + cornflour, hot vanilla milk in, boil 2 min whisking; add butter and bloomed gelatine; chill filmed.
4. Beat the cold pâtissière smooth; fold in whipped cream.
5. Pipe the diplomat into the case in a level spiral.
6. Hull strawberries; halve large ones. Stand them in tight concentric circles, points up.
7. Glaze: warm jam with lemon juice; brush each berry lightly. Pistachios at the rim.

## Plating & Service
Assemble within 4 h of service; cut with a tall thin knife dipped in hot water.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add basil chiffonade under the berries. Raspberry or mixed red-fruit versions use the same architecture.
