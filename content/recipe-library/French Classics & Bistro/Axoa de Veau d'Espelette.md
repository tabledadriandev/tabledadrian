# Axoa de Veau d'Espelette

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Basque market-day stew — hand-chopped veal simmered with peppers and Espelette until every strand is coated in paprika-red juices.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Veal shoulder, hand-chopped into 1 cm dice — 1.2 kg
- Olive oil — 50 ml
- Onions, fine dice — 300 g
- Green peppers, 1 cm dice — 300 g
- Red pepper, 1 cm dice — 150 g
- Garlic, minced — 15 g
- Bayonne ham, diced — 80 g
- Dry white wine — 200 ml
- Chicken stock — 300 ml
- Espelette pepper — 5 g
- Thyme — 3 sprigs; bay 1
- Fine salt — 11 g; black pepper 1.5 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 10 g

## Method
1. Dice the veal by knife — mince turns pasty; 1 cm dice is the dish's signature.
2. Brown veal in oil in batches over high heat; set aside.
3. Soften onions, garlic and ham in the same pot, 6 min.
4. Add peppers; cook 6 min. Stir in half the Espelette.
5. Return veal; add wine, reduce 3 min; add stock, thyme and bay.
6. Simmer, lid ajar, 50–60 min until the veal is tender and the juices are thick and clinging.
7. Season with salt, pepper and remaining Espelette; rest 10 min; parsley over.

## Plating & Service
With steamed potatoes or Basque rice; a fried egg on top turns it into lunch for the crew.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Classic variant uses veal + a little pork. Hotter: finish with fresh chilli — but Espelette's gentle warmth is the point.
