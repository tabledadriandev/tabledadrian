# Pommes Dauphine Soufflées

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Choux paste beaten into dry potato purée, fried into golden clouds — crisp shell, molten centre; the garnish that gets ordered as a course.

## Ingredients (serves 6, ~30 pieces)
- Floury potatoes — 500 g
- Water — 125 ml
- Unsalted butter — 50 g
- Plain flour — 75 g
- Eggs — 2 (110 g)
- Fine salt — 8 g
- Nutmeg — 0.4 g
- White pepper — 0.5 g
- Frying oil — 2 L
- Fleur de sel — 2 g

## Method
1. Steam potato chunks 25 min; rice and spread on a tray to steam off — the purée must be DRY. You need 400 g.
2. Choux: boil water, butter and 3 g salt; beat in flour; dry the panade over heat 2 min.
3. Beat eggs into the panade one at a time until smooth and glossy.
4. Fold panade into the potato (ratio 1 part choux : 2 parts potato); season with salt, nutmeg, pepper.
5. Heat oil to 170 °C. Shape quenelles with two oiled teaspoons.
6. Fry 6–8 at a time, 4–5 min, turning — they puff, roll and bronze.
7. Drain on a rack; fleur de sel immediately. Serve within 10 min.

## Plating & Service
Piled beside magret or a roast; they must crackle audibly when broken.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 40 g Comté + 2 g Espelette to the paste. Pipe and freeze raw; fry from frozen at 165 °C + 2 min.
