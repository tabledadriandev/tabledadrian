# Poireaux Vinaigrette, Hazelnut & Egg Mimosa

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bistro minimalism — leeks poached to a melt, marinated warm so they drink the vinaigrette, showered with sieved egg.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Slim leeks, trimmed — 8 (about 800 g)
- Dijon mustard — 20 g
- Red wine vinegar — 30 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml
- Shallot, minced — 30 g
- Capers, chopped — 15 g
- Eggs — 2
- Toasted hazelnuts, crushed — 30 g
- Chervil or parsley — 6 g
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Tie leeks in two bundles; simmer in well-salted water 18–22 min until a knife slides through with no resistance.
2. Drain cut-side down on a rack 10 min, pressing gently — waterlogged leeks ruin the dish.
3. Whisk mustard, vinegar and salt; stream in oil; add shallot and capers.
4. Halve leeks lengthwise while warm and lay in a dish; spoon over two-thirds of the vinaigrette. Marinate 30 min minimum.
5. Boil eggs 10 min, cool, peel; sieve yolks and whites separately.
6. Serve leeks at room temperature with remaining vinaigrette, egg mimosa, hazelnuts and chervil.

## Plating & Service
Line leeks like sleepers on a flat plate, stripe the mimosa across the centre, nuts and herbs over.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: omit egg, double the hazelnuts and add crisp shallots. Char the poached leeks on a plancha for a smoky register.
