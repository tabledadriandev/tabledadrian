# Baeckeoffe, Three-Meat Riesling Bake

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Alsace's baker's-oven casserole — beef, pork and lamb marinated in Riesling, layered with potatoes and sealed with a dough rope; open it at the table.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Beef chuck, 4 cm cubes — 500 g
- Pork shoulder, 4 cm cubes — 500 g
- Lamb shoulder, 4 cm cubes — 500 g
- Dry Riesling — 600 ml
- Onions, sliced — 400 g
- Leek, sliced — 200 g
- Carrots, sliced — 250 g
- Garlic — 20 g
- Juniper — 8 berries; bay 2; thyme 5 sprigs; cloves 2
- Waxy potatoes, 5 mm slices — 1.5 kg
- Fine salt — 18 g; black pepper 3 g
- Luting dough: flour 200 g, water 100 ml
- Pork trotter or rind (for body, optional) — 200 g

## Method
1. Marinate all three meats overnight in Riesling with onion, leek, garlic, juniper, bay, thyme and cloves.
2. Next day, season the meats (drain, keep marinade). Mix luting dough.
3. In a large lidded baeckeoffe dish (or cocotte), layer: potatoes, meats and vegetables, salt/pepper each layer, finishing with overlapping potatoes.
4. Pour over the marinade with its aromatics; tuck in the trotter.
5. Seal the lid with the dough rope pressed around the rim.
6. Bake at 160 °C for 3.5 h. No peeking — the seal is the oven.
7. Crack the crust at the table; discard trotter; check seasoning of the juices.

## Plating & Service
Serve straight from the dish, deep spoonfuls hitting every layer; a sharp salad and more Riesling.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: seal with foil instead of dough. Two-meat versions work; keep total meat at 1.5 kg.
