# Terrine de Campagne, Pistachio & Armagnac

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A coarse, properly seasoned country terrine that improves for three days — the backbone of any charcuterie board on charter.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Pork shoulder, coarsely minced — 700 g
- Pork back fat, minced — 200 g
- Chicken livers, cleaned — 200 g
- Shallots, finely chopped — 80 g
- Garlic, minced — 10 g
- Armagnac — 40 ml
- Egg — 1 (55 g)
- Pistachios, shelled — 60 g
- Fine salt — 14 g (1.6% of meat weight)
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Quatre-épices — 2 g
- Fresh thyme leaves — 3 g
- Smoked bacon rashers, thin — 200 g
- Bay leaves — 2

## Method
1. Sweat shallots and garlic in a dry pan 3 min; deglaze with Armagnac, flambé, cool.
2. Blitz half the livers to a purée; chop the rest into 1 cm pieces.
3. Mix pork, fat, livers, shallot reduction, egg, pistachios, salt, pepper, quatre-épices and thyme. Fry a test piece, taste, adjust.
4. Line a 1.2 L terrine mould with bacon, leaving overhang. Pack in the farce, fold bacon over, top with bay leaves.
5. Cover with lid or foil; cook in a bain-marie at 150 °C to a core of 68 °C (about 100–110 min).
6. Cool 1 h, then press with a 1 kg weight overnight in the fridge.
7. Mature 2–3 days before slicing.

## Plating & Service
Slice 1.5 cm thick; serve with cornichons, Dijon mustard and grilled sourdough.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: swap pistachios for green peppercorns. For a finer texture, run a third of the mix through the mincer twice.
