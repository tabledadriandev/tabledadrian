# Ris de Veau Poêlés, Câpres et Citron

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sweetbreads pressed, floured and glazed in foaming butter with capers and lemon — crisp shell, custard centre; the connoisseur's plate.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Veal sweetbreads (heart/noix) — 700 g
- Whole milk (soaking) — 500 ml
- Court-bouillon: water 1.5 L, vinegar 30 ml, salt 12 g, bay 1, peppercorns 3 g
- Plain flour — 40 g
- Unsalted butter — 100 g
- Neutral oil — 20 ml
- Capers, rinsed — 30 g
- Lemon — 1 (juice 25 ml, plus segments of half)
- Brown veal jus — 100 ml
- Flat-leaf parsley — 10 g
- Fine salt — 6 g; white pepper 1 g

## Method
1. Soak sweetbreads in milk 4 h (or overnight), chilled; rinse.
2. Poach in barely simmering court-bouillon 7 min; shock in iced water.
3. Peel away membrane and fatty bits while keeping lobes intact.
4. Press between two trays with a 1 kg weight, 2 h chilled — this firms the texture.
5. Cut into 4 portions; season, dust with flour.
6. Cook in oil and 60 g butter over medium-high heat, basting, 4–5 min per side until deeply golden and 65 °C at the core.
7. Rest 3 min. Wipe the pan; brown remaining butter, add capers, lemon juice and jus; boil 30 sec; add parsley.
8. Glaze the sweetbreads in the sauce 30 sec.

## Plating & Service
On a swipe of pea or celeriac purée, lemon segments and capers scattered, sauce spooned around.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: rice flour dust. Grand version: finish with 50 g morels and 50 ml cream added to the sauce.
