# Poulet aux Morilles et Vin Jaune

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Jura's grandest chicken — breasts poached in a morel and yellow-wine cream that tastes of hazelnut, curry spice and forest floor.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Corn-fed chicken breasts, skin on — 4 (about 200 g each)
- Dried morels — 40 g
- Vin jaune (or dry sherry) — 150 ml
- Shallots, minced — 80 g
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Chicken stock — 200 ml
- Double cream — 300 ml
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Fine salt — 8 g
- White pepper — 1 g
- Chives — 6 g

## Method
1. Soak morels in 300 ml warm water 30 min; halve, rinse; strain and reserve 150 ml liquor.
2. Season breasts; colour skin-side down in 30 g butter over medium heat, 6 min, to deep gold; remove.
3. Sweat shallots in remaining butter 3 min; add morels, 3 min more.
4. Deglaze with 120 ml vin jaune; reduce by half. Add stock and morel liquor; reduce by half.
5. Add cream; return chicken skin-up. Simmer gently, uncovered, 15–18 min to a core of 68 °C.
6. Rest chicken; reduce sauce to coat a spoon; finish with remaining 30 ml vin jaune, lemon, seasoning.
7. Slice breasts; sauce and morels over, chives to finish.

## Plating & Service
Fanned breast over the morel cream with fresh tagliatelle or a potato mousseline.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Use guinea fowl for more character. Lighter: half cream, half stock reduction — still glossy from the morel gelatine.
