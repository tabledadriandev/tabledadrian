# Pot-au-Feu, Os à Moelle et Gros Sel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Three cuts of beef, marrow bones and winter vegetables in a limpid broth — France's national dish; serve the bouillon first, ritual intact.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Beef shin, whole piece — 800 g
- Beef chuck — 800 g
- Oxtail or short rib — 600 g
- Marrow bones — 4
- Water — 4.5 L
- Onions, halved, charred — 2 (300 g)
- Cloves — 3
- Carrots — 600 g
- Leeks, tied — 500 g
- Turnips — 400 g
- Celery — 100 g
- Waxy potatoes — 600 g
- Garlic head — 1
- Thyme — 5 sprigs; bay 2; peppercorns 5 g
- Coarse salt — 25 g
- To serve: gros sel, Dijon, cornichons, toasted baguette for the marrow

## Method
1. Start meats (not marrow) in cold water; bring up slowly, skimming relentlessly for the first 30 min.
2. Add charred onions, cloves, garlic, herbs, peppercorns and salt; simmer at 90 °C, lid ajar, 3 h.
3. Add carrots, turnips and celery; simmer 30 min. Add leeks; 25 min more. Cook potatoes separately in salted water (keeps the broth clear).
4. Wrap marrow bones in muslin; poach in the pot the final 20 min.
5. Rest meats in broth 15 min; strain and degrease the bouillon; season.
6. Serve the bouillon first, in cups.
7. Carve meats; plate with vegetables, potatoes, a ladle of broth and the marrow bones.

## Plating & Service
Big platter, condiments in a caddy: gros sel, Dijon, cornichons; marrow spread on toast with salt.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Day two: leftover broth becomes onion soup; leftover beef becomes hachis Parmentier or a salade de bœuf with shallots.
