# Rillettes de Canard, Green Peppercorn

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Slow-cooked duck pulled into its own seasoned fat — make a big batch, it keeps three weeks sealed and rescues any last-minute apéro.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Duck legs — 4 (about 1.2 kg)
- Duck fat — 400 g
- Coarse sea salt — 15 g
- Garlic, crushed — 15 g
- Thyme — 6 sprigs
- Bay leaves — 2
- Black peppercorns — 3 g
- Green peppercorns in brine, rinsed — 15 g
- Nutmeg, grated — 0.5 g
- Sherry vinegar — 10 ml
- Fine salt — to adjust (about 3 g)

## Method
1. Rub duck legs with coarse salt, garlic, thyme and bay; cure 12 h in the fridge.
2. Rinse, pat dry. Submerge in melted duck fat with the aromatics and black peppercorns.
3. Cook at 120 °C oven (fat at ~90 °C) for 3 h until the meat falls off the bone.
4. Lift out legs; strain and reserve the fat. Discard skin and bones.
5. Shred meat with two forks — never a food processor — keeping long fibres.
6. Beat in 150 g of the warm fat, nutmeg, green peppercorns and vinegar; season.
7. Pack into jars, tap out air pockets, cap with a 5 mm layer of fat. Chill 24 h.

## Plating & Service
Serve at cool room temperature with toasted pain de campagne and cornichons.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap duck for pork belly (cook 3.5 h) or rabbit. Add orange zest for a Gascon accent.
