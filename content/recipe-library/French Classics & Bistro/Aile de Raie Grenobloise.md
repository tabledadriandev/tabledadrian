# Aile de Raie Grenobloise

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Skate wing with brown butter, capers, lemon flesh and crisp croutons — the grenobloise garnish makes cheap fish taste expensive.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Skate wings, skinned — 4 (about 280 g each)
- Plain flour — 50 g
- Neutral oil — 40 ml
- Unsalted butter — 120 g
- Capers, rinsed — 40 g
- Lemons — 2 (one cut into 5 mm flesh dice, one juiced ~30 ml)
- White bread, 5 mm cubes — 80 g
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 12 g
- Fine salt — 7 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Fry bread cubes in 30 g butter until evenly golden, 4 min; drain on paper.
2. Season skate; dust with flour.
3. Heat oil in a wide pan; cook wings thick-side first, 5 min, until golden. Turn; cook 3–4 min to a core of 52 °C — flesh should pull from the cartilage.
4. Rest fish on warm plates.
5. Wipe the pan; brown remaining 90 g butter to noisette.
6. Off heat, add capers, lemon dice and juice — it will spit — then parsley and croutons.
7. Spoon the sizzling garnish over the skate at once.

## Plating & Service
Wing fanned on the plate, garnish across the ridge; steamed potatoes or wilted spinach beside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: rice flour and GF croutons. The same treatment flatters hake, cod cheeks or brill.
