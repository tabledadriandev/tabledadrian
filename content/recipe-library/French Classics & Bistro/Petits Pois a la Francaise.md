# Petits Pois à la Française

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Peas braised with lettuce, spring onions and butter into their own sweet emulsion — the old-school side that outclasses any 'pea purée'.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Peas, fresh or best frozen — 500 g
- Little gem lettuce, quartered lengthwise — 2 (about 250 g)
- Spring onions, halved — 100 g
- Unsalted butter — 70 g
- Water or light chicken stock — 100 ml
- Sugar — 5 g
- Fine salt — 5 g
- White pepper — 0.5 g
- Chervil or mint — 6 g

## Method
1. Melt 40 g butter in a sauté pan; add spring onions, cook 2 min.
2. Add lettuce quarters cut-side down; colour nothing — just soften 2 min.
3. Add peas, sugar, salt and the water/stock; cover.
4. Braise gently 8–10 min (3–4 min if frozen peas) until the peas are tender and sweet.
5. Uncover; boil 1 min to reduce the liquor to a few spoonfuls.
6. Swirl in remaining 30 g cold butter to make a glossy emulsion that clings to the peas.
7. Season; tear in chervil or mint.

## Plating & Service
A generous spoonful with its buttery liquor beside roast chicken, lamb or poached salmon.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 60 g bacon lardons at step 1 for the bonne-femme version. Vegan: olive oil start, finish with 20 ml oil and lemon.
