# Coquelet à la Diable, Moutarde et Chapelure

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Spatchcocked poussin lacquered with Dijon and cayenne, crusted in buttered crumbs, with a diable sauce that bites back.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Poussins, spatchcocked — 2 (about 500 g each)
- Dijon mustard — 60 g
- Cayenne — 1.5 g
- Fine breadcrumbs — 90 g
- Unsalted butter, melted — 70 g
- Fine salt — 9 g; black pepper 2 g
- Sauce: shallots, minced — 60 g
- White wine — 100 ml
- White wine vinegar — 30 ml
- Crushed peppercorns — 3 g
- Brown chicken stock — 300 ml
- Tomato paste — 15 g
- Tarragon, chopped — 6 g
- Cold butter — 30 g

## Method
1. Season poussins; roast skin-up at 200 °C for 20 min on a rack.
2. Sauce: reduce shallots, wine, vinegar and crushed pepper to 30 ml; add stock and tomato paste; reduce to 180 ml; strain, mount with cold butter, add tarragon.
3. Brush the part-roasted birds generously with Dijon mixed with cayenne.
4. Press on breadcrumbs mixed with melted butter.
5. Return to the oven 15–20 min at 200 °C until the crust is deep gold and thigh core reads 78 °C.
6. Rest 8 min; halve each bird along the backbone line.

## Plating & Service
Half a bird per plate, diable sauce pooled beside (never over — the crust must stay crisp), watercress to cut.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: use GF panko. Full-size chicken: roast 30 min before crumbing, 25 min after.
