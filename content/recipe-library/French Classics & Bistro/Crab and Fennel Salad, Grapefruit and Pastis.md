# Crab & Fennel Salad, Grapefruit & Pastis

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> White crab bound with a whisper of pastis mayonnaise, shaved fennel and pink grapefruit — Riviera sunshine on a plate.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- White crab meat, picked — 320 g
- Brown crab meat — 60 g
- Egg yolk — 1 (20 g)
- Dijon mustard — 8 g
- Neutral oil — 100 ml
- Pastis — 8 ml
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Fennel bulb — 1 (250 g)
- Pink grapefruit — 1
- Extra virgin olive oil — 30 ml
- Fennel fronds and dill — 8 g
- Espelette pepper — 0.5 g
- Fine salt — 4 g

## Method
1. Make a mayonnaise with yolk, mustard and neutral oil; season with pastis, half the lemon juice, salt and the brown crab meat, sieved.
2. Check the white crab for shell; fold gently through just enough mayonnaise to bind (about 60 g).
3. Shave fennel paper-thin on a mandoline into iced water for 10 min; drain and dry.
4. Segment the grapefruit over a bowl, keeping the juice.
5. Dress fennel with olive oil, remaining lemon, 10 ml grapefruit juice and salt.
6. Plate crab in a ring, fennel salad on top, grapefruit segments around; dust with Espelette.

## Plating & Service
Chilled plates, built to order — the fennel loses life after 15 minutes dressed.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Egg-free: bind crab with 40 g avocado purée instead of mayonnaise. Swap grapefruit for blood orange in winter.
