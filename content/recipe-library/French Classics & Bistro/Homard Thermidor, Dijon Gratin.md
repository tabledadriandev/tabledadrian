# Homard Thermidor, Dijon Gratin

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Lobster folded into a mustard-cognac cream and glazed in the shell — the grand old showstopper, portioned for a two-top on the aft deck.

## Ingredients (serves 2)
- Live lobsters — 2 (600–700 g each)
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Shallots, minced — 60 g
- Button mushrooms, small dice — 100 g
- Cognac — 30 ml
- Dry white wine — 80 ml
- Fish stock — 100 ml
- Double cream — 180 ml
- Dijon mustard — 20 g
- Egg yolk — 1 (20 g)
- Gruyère, grated — 50 g
- Tarragon, chopped — 6 g
- Cayenne — 0.5 g
- Fine salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Dispatch lobsters; blanch 4 min in heavily salted boiling water, then ice. Halve lengthwise; crack claws and cook them 3 min more.
2. Remove all meat; cut into 2 cm pieces. Clean and dry the half-shells.
3. Sweat shallots and mushrooms in 40 g butter, 5 min; add Cognac, flambé; add wine, reduce by half.
4. Add stock and cream; reduce to a thick coating sauce, about 6 min.
5. Off heat, whisk in Dijon, yolk, tarragon, cayenne and salt.
6. Fold in lobster; heap into the shells; top with Gruyère and remaining butter in flecks.
7. Gratinate at 230 °C for 8–10 min until bronzed and bubbling.

## Plating & Service
Two half-shells per plate on rock salt, lemon and a butter-leaf salad; finger bowls out.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Half-portion as a starter for 4. Lighter: skip the yolk, glaze under the grill 2 min instead of baking.
