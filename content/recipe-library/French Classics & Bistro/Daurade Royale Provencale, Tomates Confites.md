# Daurade Royale Provençale, Tomates Confites

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Whole gilt-head bream roasted over confit tomatoes, olives and herbs — a one-tray Provençal main that forgives a rolling sea.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Whole daurade royale, scaled and gutted — 2 (about 700 g each)
- Cherry tomatoes — 500 g
- Black olives, pitted — 80 g
- Garlic cloves, skin on, crushed — 6 (25 g)
- Fennel, thin wedges — 250 g
- Lemon — 1, sliced
- Extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml
- Dry white wine — 100 ml
- Herbes de Provence — 3 g
- Thyme — 6 sprigs
- Basil leaves — 12 g
- Fine salt — 9 g
- Black pepper — 1.5 g

## Method
1. Oven to 200 °C. Toss tomatoes, fennel, garlic and olives with 60 ml oil, herbes de Provence and 4 g salt in a roasting tray; roast 15 min.
2. Score each bream three times per side; season inside and out; stuff cavities with thyme and lemon.
3. Nest the fish on the vegetables; drizzle with remaining oil, pour wine around.
4. Roast 20–24 min to a core of 55 °C behind the head; the eyes will have turned opaque.
5. Rest 5 min on the tray.
6. Tear basil over and spoon the tray juices back over the fish.

## Plating & Service
Serve from the tray, lifting fillets tableside; the juices are the sauce — bread to mop.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap bream for sea bass or red snapper. Add 300 g sliced potatoes under the vegetables (roast 15 min extra) for a fuller meal.
