# Coquilles Saint-Jacques Gratinées, Vermouth Cream

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Scallops baked in their shells under a vermouth velouté and golden crumb — retro in the best way, and pure theatre at the table.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- King scallops, roe off — 12 (about 360 g)
- Scallop shells (or small gratin dishes) — 4
- Button mushrooms, sliced — 150 g
- Shallots, minced — 60 g
- Unsalted butter — 70 g
- Dry vermouth — 80 ml
- Fish stock — 150 ml
- Double cream — 150 ml
- Plain flour — 15 g
- Fine breadcrumbs — 40 g
- Gruyère, grated — 40 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Fine salt — 5 g
- White pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Sear scallops hard in 20 g butter, 45 sec per side; remove (they stay rare inside). Quarter them.
2. In the same pan, sweat shallots and mushrooms in 20 g butter, 5 min.
3. Dust with flour, cook 1 min; add vermouth and stock, whisking, then cream. Simmer 4 min to a coating sauce; season with salt, pepper, lemon.
4. Fold scallops into the sauce off the heat.
5. Fill shells; top with breadcrumbs mixed with Gruyère and remaining 30 g melted butter.
6. Gratinate at 220 °C for 8–10 min until bubbling and golden.

## Plating & Service
Shells on a bed of coarse salt to keep them level, lemon wedge alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: thicken with cornflour and use GF crumbs. Normandy style: swap vermouth for dry cider and add 20 g apple brunoise.
