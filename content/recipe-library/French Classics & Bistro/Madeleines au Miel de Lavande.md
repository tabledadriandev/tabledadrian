# Madeleines au Miel de Lavande

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The humped shell-cakes, perfumed with lavender honey and brown butter — batter rests overnight, bakes in eleven minutes, served while the hump still steams.

## Ingredients (serves 8, ~24 madeleines)
- Eggs — 3 (165 g)
- Caster sugar — 100 g
- Lavender honey — 40 g
- Plain flour — 170 g
- Baking powder — 6 g
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Unsalted butter — 170 g (150 g browned, 20 g for moulds)
- Lemon zest — 4 g
- Whole milk — 20 ml

## Method
1. Brown 150 g butter to noisette; cool to 45 °C.
2. Whisk eggs, sugar and honey 2 min until pale.
3. Fold in flour, baking powder, salt and zest; then the brown butter and milk.
4. Rest the batter at least 4 h, ideally overnight — the thermal shock of cold batter/hot oven creates the hump.
5. Butter and flour madeleine moulds (even non-stick ones); pipe or spoon to three-quarters full; chill the filled tray 20 min.
6. Bake at 210 °C for 4 min, then drop to 180 °C for 6–7 min — the hump rises in the temperature fall.
7. Rap the tray; turn out immediately onto a rack.

## Plating & Service
In a warm napkin within 15 minutes of baking — a madeleine's half-life is measured in minutes, not hours.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Glaze option: dip humps in lemon icing (100 g icing sugar + 20 ml juice), flash 2 min at 200 °C. Orange-blossom honey swaps in cleanly.
