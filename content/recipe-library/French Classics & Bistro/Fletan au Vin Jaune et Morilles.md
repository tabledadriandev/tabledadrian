# Flétan au Vin Jaune et Morilles

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Halibut with the Jura's oxidative vin jaune and morels — the region's legendary chicken sauce, aimed at a noble white fish.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Halibut portions, skinless — 4 (180 g each)
- Dried morels — 30 g (or fresh 200 g)
- Vin jaune (or fino sherry + dry white) — 120 ml
- Shallots, minced — 60 g
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Chicken or fish stock — 150 ml
- Double cream — 250 ml
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Fine salt — 7 g
- White pepper — 1 g
- Chervil — 5 g

## Method
1. Soak dried morels in 250 ml warm water 30 min; lift out, halve, rinse grit. Strain and keep 100 ml soaking liquor.
2. Sweat shallots in 30 g butter 3 min; add morels, cook 4 min.
3. Add 90 ml vin jaune, reduce by half; add stock and morel liquor, reduce by half again.
4. Add cream; simmer to coating consistency, 6–8 min. Season.
5. Season halibut; cook in remaining butter over medium heat, 3–4 min per side, basting, to a core of 52 °C.
6. Finish sauce with the last 30 ml vin jaune (raw, for perfume) and lemon.
7. Rest fish 2 min; sauce over, chervil to finish.

## Plating & Service
Halibut centred, morels arranged around, sauce flooded generously; fresh pasta or steamed potatoes beside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Classic: use a corn-fed chicken breast instead of fish (cook to 68 °C). No vin jaune aboard? 90 ml dry sherry + 30 ml white wine gets close.
