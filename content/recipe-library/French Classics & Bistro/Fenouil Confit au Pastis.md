# Fenouil Confit au Pastis

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Fennel wedges braised slowly in olive oil, pastis and saffron until they collapse into sweetness — the Riviera side for any fish you'll ever roast.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Fennel bulbs, trimmed, cut into sixths through the root — 3 (about 900 g)
- Extra virgin olive oil — 80 ml
- Pastis — 30 ml
- Dry white wine — 80 ml
- Vegetable stock or water — 150 ml
- Saffron — 0.2 g
- Garlic cloves, crushed — 3 (12 g)
- Coriander seeds — 2 g
- Orange zest — 2 strips
- Thyme — 3 sprigs
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Fennel fronds — from the bulbs

## Method
1. Colour fennel wedges in oil in a wide pan, 3–4 min per cut side, to light gold.
2. Add garlic, coriander seeds and thyme; cook 1 min.
3. Add pastis (flambé if you like), then wine; reduce 2 min.
4. Add stock, saffron, orange zest and salt.
5. Cover; braise at a low simmer (or 160 °C oven) 35–40 min until translucent and utterly tender.
6. Uncover; reduce the juices to a few syrupy spoonfuls that coat the fennel.
7. Rest 5 min; shower with chopped fronds.

## Plating & Service
Wedges fanned beside roast sea bass or bream, juices spooned over both fish and fennel.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 50 g black olives at the end. No pastis? 2 g crushed fennel seeds + a squeeze of lemon carry the anise flag.
