# Loup de Mer en Croûte de Sel, Fenouil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Whole sea bass baked in an aromatic salt shell and cracked open at the table — maximum theatre, near-zero fat, impossible to overcook if timed.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Whole sea bass, gutted through the gills, scales ON — 1 (1.6–1.8 kg)
- Coarse sea salt — 2 kg
- Egg whites — 4 (120 g)
- Fennel fronds and slices — 60 g
- Lemon, sliced — 1
- Thyme — 6 sprigs
- Fennel seeds, crushed — 4 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Espelette pepper — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Oven to 220 °C. Stuff the cavity with fennel, lemon slices and thyme.
2. Mix salt with egg whites and fennel seeds until it holds like wet sand.
3. Bed 1 cm of salt on a tray, lay the fish on, and pack the rest over, sealing completely (leave the tail exposed as a doneness window).
4. Bake 25–28 min for a 1.7 kg fish — rule of thumb 15 min per kg plus 2 min; probe through the crust: core 54 °C.
5. Rest 8 min in the crust.
6. Crack the crust at the table, brush away salt, peel back skin and lift the fillets off the bone.
7. Dress with olive oil, lemon juice and Espelette.

## Plating & Service
Fillet tableside onto warm plates; serve with braised fennel and a sharp green salad.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Works with daurade royale or a 2 kg snapper (adjust time). Add pastis-butter sauce for richness if diet allows.
