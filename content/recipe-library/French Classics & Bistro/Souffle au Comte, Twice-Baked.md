# Soufflé au Comté, Twice-Baked

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The service-proof soufflé: bake ahead, then flash in cream to order — it re-rises and arrives bulletproof even in a swell.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Unsalted butter — 60 g (plus 20 g for moulds)
- Plain flour — 60 g
- Whole milk — 350 ml
- Comté, grated — 180 g
- Egg yolks — 4 (80 g)
- Egg whites — 5 (150 g)
- Dijon mustard — 10 g
- Nutmeg — 0.5 g
- Fine salt — 5 g
- White pepper — 1 g
- Double cream — 200 ml
- Fine breadcrumbs — 20 g

## Method
1. Butter six 150 ml dariole moulds and dust with breadcrumbs. Oven to 170 °C.
2. Make a blond roux with butter and flour, 2 min; whisk in milk gradually and cook to a thick béchamel, 4 min.
3. Off heat, beat in 140 g Comté, yolks, mustard, nutmeg, salt and pepper.
4. Whip whites to soft peaks with a pinch of salt; fold in thirds into the base.
5. Fill moulds to 1 cm below the rim; bake in a bain-marie 20 min until risen and just set.
6. Cool 10 min; unmould onto a tray (they will deflate — that's fine). Chill up to 24 h.
7. To serve: set soufflés in a gratin dish, pour cream around, scatter remaining Comté, bake at 200 °C for 12–14 min until re-puffed and glazed.

## Plating & Service
Serve in the gratin dish or plated with the Comté cream spooned over and a bitter-leaf salad.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: swap flour for rice flour and breadcrumbs for polenta. Add 50 g chopped ham between bakes for a non-vegetarian version.
