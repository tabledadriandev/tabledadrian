# Velouté de Champignons, Sherry & Chive Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A silk-smooth mushroom velouté lifted with dry sherry — pours beautifully tableside over a raw mushroom garnish.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Button mushrooms — 600 g
- Chestnut mushrooms — 300 g
- Shallots, sliced — 120 g
- Unsalted butter — 50 g
- Dry sherry (fino) — 60 ml
- Vegetable stock — 900 ml
- Double cream — 200 ml
- Fine salt — 7 g
- White pepper — 1 g
- Chives — 20 g
- Neutral oil — 60 ml
- Firm button mushrooms for garnish — 3, shaved raw

## Method
1. Blitz chives with oil 45 sec, warm to 60 °C for 5 min, strain through muslin; reserve chive oil.
2. Sweat shallots in butter over medium heat 5 min without colour.
3. Add sliced mushrooms and salt; cook 12–15 min until their water has evaporated and they begin to catch.
4. Deglaze with sherry, reduce to a glaze, add stock and simmer 15 min.
5. Add cream, return to a simmer, then blend at high speed 2 min and pass through a chinois.
6. Season with white pepper; adjust consistency with stock to a pouring cream texture.
7. Serve at 80 °C with shaved raw mushroom and drops of chive oil.

## Plating & Service
Arrange raw mushroom shavings in the bowl, pour the velouté at table, finish with chive oil beads.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: swap butter for olive oil and cream for oat cream. Luxe: add 5 g dried porcini to the stock.
