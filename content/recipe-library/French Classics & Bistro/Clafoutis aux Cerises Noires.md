# Clafoutis aux Cerises Noires

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Limousin flan-cake with unpitted black cherries — the stones perfume the batter with almond; serve barely warm, dusted, unapologetically rustic.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Black cherries, stems off, UNPITTED — 500 g
- Eggs — 3 (165 g)
- Caster sugar — 100 g
- Plain flour — 60 g
- Whole milk — 200 ml
- Double cream — 100 ml
- Unsalted butter — 30 g (15 g for the dish)
- Vanilla pod — 1/2
- Kirsch — 20 ml
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Icing sugar — 15 g

## Method
1. Butter a 24 cm ceramic dish; dust with a spoon of the sugar. Oven to 180 °C.
2. Whisk eggs with sugar, salt and vanilla seeds 1 min.
3. Whisk in flour until smooth, then milk, cream, kirsch and 15 g melted butter — a thin crêpe-like batter.
4. Rest the batter 20 min.
5. Arrange cherries in one snug layer; pour the batter over.
6. Bake 35–40 min until puffed, golden, and just set at the centre — a knife comes out clean but moist.
7. Cool 20 min (it will settle — normal); dust with icing sugar.

## Plating & Service
Spooned warm from the dish, cream optional; warn guests about the stones — tradition insists on them.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pitted cherries + 20 g ground almonds recovers the lost almond note. Apricot or plum version = flaugnarde. GF: 40 g rice flour + 20 g almond flour.
