# Vol-au-Vent Forestier, Crème de Madère

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Tall puff cases brimming with mixed mushrooms in a Madeira cream — the old banquet showpiece gone meatless, and better for it.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- All-butter puff pastry — 500 g
- Egg (glaze) — 1 (55 g)
- Mixed mushrooms (girolles, king oyster, chestnut) — 600 g
- Shallots, minced — 80 g
- Garlic, minced — 8 g
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Madeira — 80 ml
- Vegetable stock — 150 ml
- Double cream — 200 ml
- Thyme leaves — 3 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Fine salt — 8 g; white pepper 1 g
- Chervil — 6 g

## Method
1. Roll puff to 8 mm; cut four 10 cm discs. With a 7 cm cutter, score an inner circle 5 mm deep (the lid). Chill 30 min.
2. Glaze tops with beaten egg (not the cut edges — it glues the layers); bake at 200 °C for 22–25 min until towering and bronzed.
3. Release the lids; hollow out any raw layers. Keep warm.
4. Sauté mushrooms in batches in butter over high heat until golden; season each batch.
5. Add shallots and garlic to the last batch, 2 min; return all mushrooms.
6. Add Madeira, reduce by half; add stock, reduce by half; add cream and thyme, simmer to a coating sauce; finish with lemon.
7. Fill the hot cases to overflowing; cap with lids at an angle; chervil over.

## Plating & Service
Centre of the plate, extra sauce around; a watercress salad cuts the richness.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Classic financière: add poached chicken quenelles and olives. Vegan: oat cream, olive oil and vegan puff (check the pack).
