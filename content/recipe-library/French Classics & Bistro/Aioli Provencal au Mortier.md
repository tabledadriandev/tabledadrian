# Aïoli Provençal au Mortier

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Garlic mayonnaise pounded in the mortar, as dense as ointment — the golden heart of the 'grand aïoli' spread of vegetables, eggs and salt cod.

## Ingredients (serves 8, makes ~350 ml)
- Garlic cloves, germ removed — 25 g
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Egg yolks — 2 (40 g)
- Dijon mustard (optional trainer wheels) — 5 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 150 ml
- Neutral oil — 150 ml
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Warm water — 10 ml
- Saffron (optional) — 0.1 g

## Method
1. Pound garlic with salt in a mortar to an absolutely smooth pomade — 3–4 min of honest work.
2. Work in the yolks (and mustard if using) with the pestle.
3. Add the oils drop by drop at first, turning the pestle constantly; after it catches, a thin stream.
4. Blend the two oils — all extra-virgin turns bitter under the pestle.
5. Loosen midway with lemon juice and the warm water; continue to a dense, standing sauce.
6. Season; add saffron bloomed in the warm water for colour if you like.
7. Rest 30 min at room temperature before serving; use same day.

## Plating & Service
Centre of a grand aïoli platter: poached salt cod, boiled potatoes, carrots, green beans, eggs, and raw fennel.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Rouille: add cayenne, saffron and a little cooked potato. Vegan: emulsify aquafaba (40 ml) instead of yolks — lighter but stable.
