# Sauce Tomate Provençale aux Herbes

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The tomato mother à la provençale — slow-roasted depth, olive oil and herbs; jar it and it underwrites a week of gratins, fish and pastas.

## Ingredients (serves 8, makes ~1.2 L)
- Ripe tomatoes — 2 kg (or best quality tinned, 1.6 kg)
- Extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml
- Onion, fine dice — 250 g
- Carrot, fine dice — 100 g
- Celery, fine dice — 60 g
- Garlic, sliced — 20 g
- Tomato paste — 40 g
- Herbes de Provence — 4 g
- Thyme — 4 sprigs; bay 2
- Basil — 20 g
- Sugar — 8 g (to balance acidity)
- Fine salt — 12 g; black pepper 2 g
- Red wine vinegar — 10 ml

## Method
1. Score fresh tomatoes; blanch 20 sec, ice, peel, quarter, deseed; chop the flesh.
2. Sweat onion, carrot, celery and garlic in 60 ml oil, 10 min, no colour.
3. Add tomato paste; cook 2 min. Add tomatoes, herbs, sugar and salt.
4. Simmer uncovered 45–50 min, stirring, until thick and brick-toned.
5. Remove thyme and bay; pass through a moulin for a rustic texture (or blend smooth).
6. Finish with vinegar, pepper, torn basil and remaining raw oil.
7. Jar hot; keeps 1 week chilled or freeze in portions.

## Plating & Service
Base for petits farcis, poached eggs à la provençale, grilled fish, or simply excellent pasta.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 20 g anchovy and olives for a Niçoise register (no longer vegan). Roast the tomatoes first at 160 °C for smoky depth.
