# Côte de Veau Normande, Pommes et Calvados

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Thick veal chop with caramelised apples, Calvados flambé and a cider cream — Normandy's holy quartet on one plate.

## Ingredients (serves 2)
- Veal chops, 3.5 cm, bone-in — 2 (about 350 g each)
- Fine salt — 7 g; white pepper 1 g
- Unsalted butter — 70 g
- Neutral oil — 20 ml
- Firm apples (Cox/Braeburn), 8 wedges each — 2
- Calvados — 50 ml
- Dry cider — 150 ml
- Chicken stock — 100 ml
- Crème fraîche — 120 g
- Thyme — 3 sprigs
- Lemon juice — 5 ml

## Method
1. Salt chops 30 min ahead; temper.
2. Caramelise apple wedges in 30 g butter over medium heat, 5–6 min, until golden but holding shape; reserve.
3. Sear chops in oil and 20 g butter with thyme, 3–4 min per side, basting; finish to a core of 58 °C. Rest 8 min.
4. Pour off fat; add Calvados and flambé.
5. Add cider, reduce by half; add stock, reduce by half again.
6. Stir in crème fraîche; simmer to a coating sauce; season with salt, pepper, lemon.
7. Return apples to warm 1 min; plate chops, apples and sauce together.

## Plating & Service
Chop with the bone angled, apples tumbled beside, sauce over the meat; celeriac purée underneath if you want more comfort.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pork chops take the same treatment (core 62 °C). Skip cream and finish with 20 g butter for a lighter jus normand.
