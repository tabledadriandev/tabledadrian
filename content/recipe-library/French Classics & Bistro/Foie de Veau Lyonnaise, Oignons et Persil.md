# Foie de Veau Lyonnaise, Oignons et Persil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Thick-cut calf's liver seared pink over melting vinegared onions — Lyon's proof that offal, cooked with respect, outclasses steak.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Calf's liver, 2 cm slices, membrane removed — 4 (160 g each)
- Yellow onions, thinly sliced — 500 g
- Unsalted butter — 80 g
- Neutral oil — 20 ml
- Red wine vinegar — 40 ml
- Brown stock — 100 ml
- Fine salt — 9 g; black pepper 2 g
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 15 g
- Rice flour (optional dusting) — 20 g

## Method
1. Cook onions in 40 g butter with a pinch of salt over medium-low heat, 20–25 min, to a soft blond tangle.
2. Add 25 ml vinegar to the onions; reduce until barely moist; add stock, simmer 2 min; keep warm.
3. Pat liver dry; season; dust lightly with rice flour if using.
4. Heat oil and 20 g butter in a heavy pan until foaming; lay in the liver.
5. Cook 90 sec–2 min per side to a core of 54 °C — firm at the edges, springy-rosy at the centre. Do not crowd; work in two rounds.
6. Rest 3 min. Deglaze the pan with remaining vinegar; swirl in the last 20 g butter and the parsley.
7. Plate liver on the onions; spoon the parsley pan-butter over.

## Plating & Service
Onions beneath, liver on top, pan juices over; pommes purée is compulsory in spirit.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cook to 58 °C for guests who want no pink — never beyond, it turns to leather. Lamb's liver: thinner slices, 60 sec per side.
