# Crème Brûlée à la Vanille de Tahiti

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A yolk-rich custard baked to the faintest tremble under a glass-thin burnt sugar lid — crack tests are performed tableside, obviously.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Double cream — 600 ml
- Whole milk — 100 ml
- Vanilla pods (Tahitian) — 1.5
- Egg yolks — 8 (160 g)
- Caster sugar — 90 g
- Fine salt — 1 g
- Demerara or caster sugar, for the crust — 60 g

## Method
1. Split and scrape vanilla into cream and milk; bring to 85 °C; infuse 20 min, pods in.
2. Whisk yolks with 90 g sugar and salt until just combined — don't aerate.
3. Rewarm cream; pour onto yolks, whisking; strain through a fine sieve; rest 10 min and skim the foam.
4. Fill six shallow 150 ml brûlée dishes.
5. Bake in a bain-marie at 120 °C for 35–45 min until set at the edges with a 3 cm tremble at the centre (core 82 °C).
6. Chill uncovered 4 h minimum.
7. To serve: blot any moisture; sprinkle an even 10 g sugar per dish; torch in circles to a dark amber glass. Rest 2 min to harden.

## Plating & Service
On a saucer with a teaspoon — the first tap should sound like ice cracking on a pond.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Infusions: coffee beans (15 g), lavender (2 g, careful), or orange zest. Lighter (technically): half milk, bake 10 min longer.
