# Beurre Café de Paris Maison

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The legendary 20-ingredient steak butter, batched and rolled — slice a coin over anything seared and watch it become a sauce as it melts.

## Ingredients (serves 10, makes ~350 g)
- Unsalted butter, soft — 250 g
- Anchovy fillets, mashed — 12 g
- Dijon mustard — 15 g
- Ketchup — 10 g
- Capers, chopped — 15 g
- Shallot, minced — 30 g
- Garlic, grated — 6 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 10 g; chives 8 g; tarragon 6 g
- Curry powder — 2 g; sweet paprika 2 g
- Worcestershire-style sauce — 8 ml
- Cognac — 10 ml
- Lemon juice — 10 ml; zest 2 g
- Egg yolk — 1 (20 g)
- Fine salt — 4 g; black pepper 1.5 g; cayenne 0.3 g

## Method
1. Sweat shallot and garlic in a teaspoon of the butter 2 min; cool completely.
2. Beat the soft butter until creamy.
3. Add every remaining ingredient including the shallot mix; beat 2 min until uniform and pale.
4. Taste — it should be aggressively seasoned; it will be diluted by the meat's juices.
5. Roll into a 4 cm log in parchment or cling film; twist the ends.
6. Chill 24 h for the flavours to marry (this step is not optional).
7. Keeps 2 weeks chilled, 3 months frozen; slice 1 cm coins to order.

## Plating & Service
A coin melting over entrecôte, chicken paillard, grilled lobster or roasted cauliflower steaks.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skip the yolk to keep it 2 weeks+. Fish version: double the lemon, halve the curry. Vegetarian: omit anchovy, add 8 g white miso.
