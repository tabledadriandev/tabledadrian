# Mont Blanc aux Marrons, Meringue Croquante

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Crisp meringue, unsweetened chantilly and a summit of chestnut vermicelli — the autumn peak of French patisserie, piped to order.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Meringue: egg whites 3 (90 g), caster sugar 90 g, icing sugar 90 g, salt 1 g
- Chestnut cream: sweetened chestnut purée (crème de marrons) 300 g, unsweetened chestnut purée 150 g, soft butter 60 g, dark rum 20 ml
- Chantilly: double cream 300 ml, vanilla 1/2 pod (NO sugar — balance against the sweet chestnut)
- Candied chestnuts, crumbled — 60 g
- Icing sugar — 10 g

## Method
1. Meringue: whip whites and salt to soft peaks; rain in caster sugar to stiff gloss; fold in sifted icing sugar.
2. Pipe six 8 cm domed discs; bake at 100 °C for 90 min until dry and lift-off clean; cool in the oven.
3. Chestnut cream: beat both purées with soft butter and rum until completely smooth; pass through a fine sieve (essential for clean strands); load into a piping bag with a multi-hole 'vermicelli' tip (or fine plain tip).
4. Whip the cream with vanilla to firm peaks.
5. Build: meringue base, a tall dome of chantilly (pipe or spoon), a few candied chestnut pieces hidden inside.
6. Pipe chestnut vermicelli over the dome in loops, covering the cream like a mountainside.
7. Dust the summit with icing sugar — the snowcap.

## Plating & Service
Assembled to order (meringue softens in 2 h); serve on cold plates with strong coffee.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Blackcurrant coulis hidden under the chantilly cuts the richness beautifully. Store meringues airtight up to a week.
