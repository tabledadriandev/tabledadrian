# Asperges Blanches, Sauce Mousseline

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Fat white spears with hollandaise lightened by whipped cream — spring's most elegant plate, gone in eight weeks a year.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- White asparagus, thick spears — 16 (about 1 kg)
- Fine salt — 15 g (for the water)
- Sugar — 10 g (for the water)
- Lemon — 1/2
- Egg yolks — 3 (60 g)
- White wine — 30 ml
- White wine vinegar — 10 ml
- Clarified butter, 45 °C — 180 g
- Double cream, whipped soft — 60 ml
- Cayenne — 0.3 g
- Chervil — 5 g

## Method
1. Peel asparagus from below the tip to the base, twice over — no fibres may remain. Trim 2 cm off the ends.
2. Simmer in water seasoned with salt, sugar and the lemon half, 10–14 min until a knife meets no resistance. Rest in the liquor off the heat.
3. Whisk yolks, wine and vinegar in a bain-marie to a thick sabayon at 65 °C — ribbons should hold 3 seconds.
4. Off heat, whisk in clarified butter in a thin stream; season with salt and cayenne.
5. Fold in the whipped cream just before serving — this is the mousseline.
6. Drain spears on a cloth; serve warm, sauce spooned across the middle, chervil over.

## Plating & Service
Four spears aligned per plate, sauce in a wide ribbon, a few flakes of sea salt on the tips.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Green asparagus: cook 4–6 min only. Dairy-free: dress instead with a warm hazelnut-oil vinaigrette.
