# Consommé de Bœuf, Root Vegetable Brunoise

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Crystal-clear, deeply beefy, with a jewel-cut brunoise — the technique test that never fails to silence a table.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Cold brown beef stock, fat-free — 2 L
- Beef shin, minced, very cold — 400 g
- Egg whites — 4 (120 g)
- Carrot, chopped — 100 g (plus 60 g fine brunoise to garnish)
- Celery, chopped — 60 g (plus 30 g brunoise)
- Leek, chopped — 80 g (plus 40 g brunoise)
- Tomato, chopped — 100 g
- Turnip, brunoise — 40 g
- Thyme — 2 sprigs
- Bay leaf — 1
- Black peppercorns, crushed — 2 g
- Fine salt — 8 g
- Chervil leaves — 4 g
- Sherry (optional) — 20 ml

## Method
1. Mix minced shin, egg whites, chopped vegetables, tomato, thyme, bay and peppercorns — the clearmeat. Chill 20 min.
2. Whisk clearmeat into the cold stock in a tall pot.
3. Bring up over medium heat, stirring gently until 60 °C, then stop stirring and let the raft form.
4. Pierce a chimney in the raft; simmer at a bare tremble (90 °C) for 75 min. Never boil.
5. Ladle through the chimney and strain through wet muslin without pressing.
6. Degrease with strips of kitchen paper; season with salt and a breath of sherry.
7. Blanch the brunoise garnish 90 sec in salted water.
8. Serve consommé at 90 °C over the brunoise, chervil on top.

## Plating & Service
Warmed shallow bowls; pour tableside from a small jug so guests see the clarity.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 20 g cooked vermicelli (no longer GF) or a poached quail egg. Chill and serve jellied in summer with tarragon.
