# Bisque de Langoustine, Cognac & Tarragon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Roasted shells, a proper flambé and a rice-thickened body — the benchmark bisque, with poached tail meat as garnish.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Langoustines, whole — 1.5 kg
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Carrot, mirepoix — 100 g
- Onion, mirepoix — 120 g
- Celery, mirepoix — 60 g
- Tomato paste — 40 g
- Cognac — 60 ml
- Dry white wine — 150 ml
- Fish stock — 1.6 L
- Carnaroli rice — 50 g
- Double cream — 150 ml
- Tarragon — 10 g
- Cayenne — 1 g
- Fine salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Twist off langoustine tails; peel, devein, chill the meat. Reserve heads and shells.
2. Roast shells in oil in a wide pot over high heat 8 min, crushing with a rolling pin end, until deeply toasted.
3. Add mirepoix, cook 6 min; stir in tomato paste and cook 2 min.
4. Add Cognac and flambé; deglaze with wine, reduce by half.
5. Add stock and rice; simmer gently 40 min.
6. Blend shells and all (heavy-duty blender, 2 min), then pass twice through a chinois, pressing hard.
7. Return to heat, add cream, cayenne, salt; hold at 85 °C.
8. Poach tails in the bisque 90 sec before serving; finish with chopped tarragon.

## Plating & Service
Three tails per bowl, bisque poured around, a few tarragon leaves and a thread of cream.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free: omit cream and mount with 30 ml olive oil. Swap langoustine for crab or prawn shells with equal success.
