# Gigot de Sept Heures, Anchois et Ail

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Seven-hour leg of lamb larded with anchovy and garlic until it's carved with a spoon — set it going at breakfast, forget it until dinner.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Leg of lamb, bone-in — 1 (about 2.5 kg)
- Anchovy fillets — 8 (30 g)
- Garlic — 40 g (half slivered, half crushed)
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Fine salt — 20 g; black pepper 3 g
- Dry white wine — 500 ml
- Brown stock — 500 ml
- Carrots, chunks — 300 g
- Onions, chunks — 300 g
- Tomatoes, chopped — 200 g
- Rosemary — 2 sprigs; thyme 6 sprigs; bay 2

## Method
1. Pierce the leg 16 times; push in garlic slivers and half-anchovy pieces. Rub with oil, salt and pepper.
2. Brown the leg all over in a huge cocotte (or a deep roasting tray on the hob), 12 min.
3. Remove; soften carrots, onions and crushed garlic 6 min. Add wine, reduce by half; add stock, tomatoes, remaining anchovies and herbs.
4. Return the leg; the liquid should reach a third of the way up.
5. Cover tightly (foil plus lid); braise at 120 °C for 7 h, turning the leg twice.
6. Rest the leg covered; strain the juices, degrease, and reduce to a light sauce; season.
7. Serve with a spoon — the meat should have surrendered completely.

## Plating & Service
Present whole, then spoon-carve tableside; sauce and vegetables around, flageolets or pommes purée beneath.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Shoulder works (5 h). No anchovies for allergy: use 15 g extra salt-cured capers or simply omit — the garlic carries it.
