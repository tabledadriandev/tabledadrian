# Foie Gras Terrine, Muscat & Toasted Brioche

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Low-temperature terrine kept blushing pink, perfumed with sweet Muscat — a benchmark luxury course that must be made 3 days ahead.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Duck foie gras, whole lobe, top grade — 600 g
- Fine salt — 8 g
- White pepper — 1.5 g
- Sugar — 2 g
- Muscat de Beaumes-de-Venise — 40 ml
- Cognac — 15 ml
- Brioche loaf — 300 g
- Fleur de sel — 2 g
- Fig jam (to serve) — 80 g

## Method
1. Temper the lobe 1 h at room temperature until pliable. Separate the two lobes.
2. Butterfly each lobe and pull out the primary veins with tweezers, following them from the base.
3. Season all surfaces evenly with salt, pepper and sugar; sprinkle with Muscat and Cognac. Marinate 12 h covered, chilled.
4. Press the pieces into a 600 ml terrine, packing to exclude air; temper 30 min.
5. Cook in a bain-marie at 90 °C oven to a core of 46 °C (about 20–25 min) — no higher.
6. Pour off excess fat and reserve; press the terrine with 500 g overnight.
7. Melt reserved fat, pour a 3 mm cap over the surface; mature 2–3 days at 2 °C.
8. Slice with a hot dry knife; serve with toasted brioche, fleur de sel and fig jam.

## Plating & Service
One 1.5 cm slice per guest on a cold plate, brioche in a napkin, jam quenelle beside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap Muscat for Sauternes or a splash of aged Armagnac. GF guests: serve with toasted GF fruit bread.
