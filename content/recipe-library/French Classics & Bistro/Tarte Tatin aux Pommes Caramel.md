# Tarte Tatin aux Pommes Caramel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Apples cooked standing in a deep dry caramel before the pastry lid goes on — the upside-down icon, flipped hot for a lacquered crown.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Firm apples (Braeburn/Cox/Reine des Reinettes) — 8 (about 1.4 kg)
- Caster sugar — 180 g
- Unsalted butter — 90 g
- Vanilla pod — 1/2
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- All-butter puff pastry — 300 g
- Fine salt — 1 g
- Crème fraîche, to serve — 200 g

## Method
1. Peel, halve and core the apples; toss with lemon juice. Roll puff to 4 mm; cut a 26 cm disc; dock; chill.
2. In a 24 cm ovenproof tatin pan, cook the sugar dry to a deep amber caramel.
3. Off heat, whisk in butter, vanilla seeds and salt.
4. Stand apple halves upright on their edges in tight concentric circles — pack more than looks reasonable; they shrink.
5. Cook on the hob over medium heat 15–20 min, until the caramel bubbles up amber and the apples yield at the edges.
6. Cap with the pastry disc, tucking the edge down inside the pan.
7. Bake at 190 °C for 35–40 min until the pastry is deep gold and crisp.
8. Rest exactly 10 min; place a plate over and invert with commitment. Rearrange any strays; spoon stray caramel over.

## Plating & Service
Warm wedges with cold crème fraîche — the temperature contrast is the dessert.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pear tatin: firm Conference pears, add 2 g black pepper to the caramel. Quince + apple in winter is glorious.
