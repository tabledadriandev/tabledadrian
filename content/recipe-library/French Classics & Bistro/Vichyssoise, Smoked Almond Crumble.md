# Vichyssoise, Smoked Almond Crumble

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Ice-cold leek and potato silk with a smoky, crunchy counterpoint — a deck-lunch opener that holds beautifully in the fridge.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Leeks, white part only, sliced — 500 g
- Floury potatoes, peeled, sliced — 350 g
- Unsalted butter — 40 g
- Vegetable stock — 900 ml
- Whole milk — 200 ml
- Double cream — 150 ml
- Fine salt — 8 g
- White pepper — 1 g
- Smoked almonds, chopped — 50 g
- Chives, sliced — 10 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml

## Method
1. Sweat leeks in butter with 3 g salt over low heat 10 min — zero colour.
2. Add potatoes and stock; simmer 18 min until potatoes collapse.
3. Add milk, bring back to 90 °C, then blend 2 min and pass through a chinois.
4. Stir in cream, remaining salt, white pepper and lemon juice.
5. Chill over ice, then refrigerate at least 3 h; it should be served at 4–6 °C.
6. Check seasoning cold (cold mutes salt) and loosen with milk if needed.
7. Garnish with smoked almond crumble and chives.

## Plating & Service
Chilled coupes or glass bowls; the crumble goes on at the last second to stay crisp.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: swap almonds for crisp fried leeks. Vegan: olive oil, oat milk and oat cream work cleanly.
