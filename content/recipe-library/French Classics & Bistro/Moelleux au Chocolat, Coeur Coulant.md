# Moelleux au Chocolat, Cœur Coulant

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The molten-centre chocolate cake timed to the minute — batter holds 24 h in the fridge, so each one bakes to order and erupts on cue.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Dark chocolate, 70% — 200 g
- Unsalted butter — 180 g (plus 20 g for moulds)
- Eggs — 4 (220 g)
- Egg yolks — 2 (40 g)
- Caster sugar — 120 g
- Plain flour — 60 g
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Cocoa powder (moulds) — 15 g
- Icing sugar — 10 g

## Method
1. Butter six 180 ml dariole moulds twice (chill between coats); dust with cocoa.
2. Melt chocolate and butter to 45 °C over a bain-marie.
3. Whisk eggs, yolks and sugar 2 min until pale and slightly thickened.
4. Fold the chocolate into the eggs; then the flour and salt — stop as it homogenises.
5. Fill moulds to 1 cm below the rim. Chill at least 1 h (up to 24 h).
6. Bake from the fridge at 200 °C for 11–13 min: edges set and risen, a 3 cm centre still glossy and trembling.
7. Rest 60 sec; run a thin knife around; invert onto plates with one firm shake.
8. Dust and serve within 2 min.

## Plating & Service
Centre plate, crème fraîche or vanilla ice cream beside — the first spoon must release a slow lava flow.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: swap flour for 40 g cornflour. Praliné heart: freeze 20 g praliné coins and bury one per mould before baking — guaranteed flow.
