# Cabillaud Dugléré, Tomato & White Wine

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Cod poached over tomato, shallot and parsley, the liquor mounted into its own sauce — a 30-minute classic that tastes far older and grander.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Cod loin portions, skinless — 4 (180 g each)
- Ripe tomatoes, peeled, deseeded, diced — 400 g
- Shallots, minced — 80 g
- Garlic, minced — 6 g
- Dry white wine — 150 ml
- Fish stock — 150 ml
- Unsalted butter — 80 g
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 12 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Fine salt — 7 g
- White pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Butter a sauté pan with 20 g butter; scatter shallots, garlic, half the tomatoes and half the parsley.
2. Season cod and set on the bed; add wine and stock; cover with a buttered cartouche.
3. Bring to 85 °C; poach gently 8–10 min to a core of 50–52 °C. Lift cod to a warm plate.
4. Boil the liquor down by two-thirds with the vegetables.
5. Whisk in remaining 60 g cold butter off the boil to a light emulsion.
6. Fold in remaining raw tomato dice and parsley; season with lemon, salt, pepper.
7. Nap the cod with the sauce immediately.

## Plating & Service
Cod glazed with sauce in a shallow bowl, steamed potatoes or rice pilaf on the side.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free: mount the sauce with olive oil. Equally good with hake, halibut or thick pollack.
