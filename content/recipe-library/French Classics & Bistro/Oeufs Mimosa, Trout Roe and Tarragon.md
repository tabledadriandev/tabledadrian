# Œufs Mimosa, Trout Roe & Tarragon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The bistro egg upgraded — silky yolk cream piped high, popped with trout roe; a one-bite crowd-pleaser for apéro or first course.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Eggs — 9
- Egg yolk (raw, for mayonnaise) — 1 (20 g)
- Dijon mustard — 12 g
- Neutral oil — 130 ml
- White wine vinegar — 8 ml
- Crème fraîche — 30 g
- Tarragon, chopped — 6 g
- Chives, sliced — 6 g
- Trout roe — 50 g
- Espelette pepper — 0.5 g
- Fine salt — 4 g

## Method
1. Boil the 9 eggs exactly 9 min 30 sec from boiling water; shock in iced water, peel.
2. Make a mayonnaise: whisk raw yolk, mustard, vinegar; stream in oil; season.
3. Halve eggs; sieve the cooked yolks. Set 12 best white halves aside; finely chop the remaining whites.
4. Beat sieved yolks with 90 g mayonnaise, crème fraîche, tarragon and salt to a smooth cream.
5. Pipe the cream in tall rosettes into the 12 halves using a star nozzle.
6. Scatter chopped whites and chives over, crown each with trout roe, dust with Espelette.

## Plating & Service
Two halves per person on a smear of the yolk cream to stop them sliding; serve well chilled.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian: swap roe for chopped black olives or pickled mustard seeds. Anchovy lovers: fold 10 g anchovy paste into the cream.
