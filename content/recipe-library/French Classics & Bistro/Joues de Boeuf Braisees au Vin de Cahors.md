# Joues de Bœuf Braisées au Vin de Cahors

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Beef cheeks in black Cahors wine until they quiver — the most forgiving luxury braise in the book; collagen does all the work.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Beef cheeks, trimmed of silver skin — 6 (about 1.6 kg)
- Cahors or Malbec — 750 ml
- Ruby port — 100 ml
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Carrots, chunks — 250 g
- Onion, chunks — 250 g
- Celery — 80 g
- Garlic, halved head — 1
- Tomato paste — 40 g
- Brown veal stock — 700 ml
- Thyme — 6 sprigs; bay 2
- Orange zest — 1 strip
- Fine salt — 14 g; black pepper 2 g
- Cornflour — 8 g (if needed)

## Method
1. Marinate cheeks overnight in wine with the vegetables and herbs.
2. Drain, reserving everything; dry cheeks thoroughly and season.
3. Sear cheeks hard in oil, 3 min per side; remove. Brown vegetables 6 min; add tomato paste, 2 min.
4. Boil the wine separately 5 min, skimming; add to the pot with port, stock and zest. Return cheeks — just submerged.
5. Braise, lid on, at 140 °C for 3.5–4 h until a spoon slides through.
6. Rest cheeks in the liquor 20 min; lift out. Strain, degrease and reduce sauce to a glossy nap (slurry the cornflour in only if thin).
7. Glaze cheeks in the sauce over low heat, spooning constantly, 3 min.

## Plating & Service
One lacquered cheek per plate on pommes purée or celeriac, sauce ribboned over, gremolata-fine orange zest if you like.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pork or veal cheeks: 2.5 h. Make 2 days ahead — the sauce sets to a demi-glace and reheats to perfection.
