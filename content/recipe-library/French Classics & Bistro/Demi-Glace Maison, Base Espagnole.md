# Demi-Glace Maison, Base Espagnole

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The brown mother sauce run down to demi-glace — a weekend project that banks a freezer of instant, glossy pan sauces for the whole charter season.

## Ingredients (serves 10, makes ~800 ml)
- Veal bones, chopped — 3 kg
- Neutral oil — 50 ml
- Onion, chunks — 300 g
- Carrot, chunks — 300 g
- Celery — 150 g
- Tomato paste — 80 g
- Plain flour — 60 g
- Red wine — 300 ml
- Water — 5 L
- Thyme — 8 sprigs; bay 3; peppercorns 5 g
- Parsley stalks — 20 g

## Method
1. Roast bones with oil at 220 °C for 45–60 min, turning, to deep mahogany.
2. Add vegetables and tomato paste for the final 20 min.
3. Dust with flour; roast 8 min more (the singé).
4. Transfer all to a stockpot; deglaze the tray with wine and add. Cover with water.
5. Simmer at 90 °C, never boiling, 5–6 h, skimming every 30 min. Add herbs for the final hour.
6. Strain through muslin; chill overnight; lift off the fat cap.
7. Reduce the clear stock by two-thirds to ~800 ml until it coats a spoon and shines.
8. Portion into ice-cube trays; freeze. Each cube = one pan sauce.

## Plating & Service
Melt 2 cubes into any deglazed pan with a knob of butter for an instant restaurant jus.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: omit flour; reduce further for body. Derivatives: + Madeira = sauce madère; + peppercorns/cream = poivre; + red wine/shallots = bordelaise.
