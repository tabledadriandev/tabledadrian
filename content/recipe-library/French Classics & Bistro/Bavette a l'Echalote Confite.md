# Bavette à l'Échalote Confite

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Flank's beefier cousin seared rare with a heap of sweet-sour confit shallots — the cheapest cut on the board and the one chefs order.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Bavette (flank/skirt) — 800 g
- Shallots, sliced into rings — 400 g
- Olive oil — 50 ml
- Red wine vinegar — 30 ml
- Red wine — 100 ml
- Brown stock — 150 ml
- Dijon mustard — 10 g
- Thyme — 3 sprigs
- Fine salt — 10 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Salt the bavette 30 min ahead; bring to room temperature.
2. Cook shallots slowly in 30 ml oil with thyme and a pinch of salt, 18–20 min, stirring, to a soft amber tangle.
3. Add vinegar and wine to the shallots; reduce until sticky; add stock, reduce to a loose marmalade; whisk in Dijon.
4. Sear bavette in remaining oil over the fiercest heat, 2–3 min per side depending on thickness, to a core of 48–50 °C — bavette must stay rare-to-medium-rare.
5. Rest 8 min on a rack; add resting juices to the shallots.
6. Slice thinly ACROSS the grain at a 45° angle — non-negotiable for tenderness.
7. Season slices with pepper and flaky salt; pile shallots over.

## Plating & Service
Sliced and shingled with the shallot confit down the middle; frites or a green salad beside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Onglet or flat-iron work identically. Add 30 g butter to the shallots for a glossier finish if dairy allows.
