# Matelote de Poisson au Riesling

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Alsace's river-fish stew in Riesling cream — firm white fish poached in a winey velouté with glazed onions and mushrooms.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Mixed firm fish (monkfish, pike-perch, trout), portioned — 900 g
- Dry Riesling — 400 ml
- Fish stock — 400 ml
- Shallots, minced — 100 g
- Unsalted butter — 80 g
- Plain flour — 20 g
- Double cream — 150 ml
- Button mushrooms, quartered — 250 g
- Pearl onions — 150 g
- Thyme — 3 sprigs; bay 1
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Fine salt — 9 g; white pepper 1 g
- Chives — 8 g
- Sugar — 3 g

## Method
1. Glaze pearl onions with 20 g butter, sugar and 80 ml water, 12 min; sauté mushrooms in 20 g butter until pale gold.
2. Sweat shallots in 20 g butter; add Riesling, thyme and bay; reduce by a third; add stock.
3. Poach the fish in this liquor at 85 °C by firmness: monkfish 8 min, pike-perch 5 min, trout 4 min. Lift to a warm dish.
4. Strain the liquor; reduce to 400 ml.
5. Beurre manié: knead remaining 20 g butter with flour; whisk in to thicken lightly.
6. Add cream; simmer 3 min; season with lemon, salt, pepper.
7. Return fish, onions and mushrooms; warm 2 min without boiling; chives over.

## Plating & Service
Shallow bowls with buttered noodles or steamed potatoes — an Alsace Sunday in one dish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: cornflour instead of beurre manié. Traditional versions include eel — add it if your fishmonger obliges.
