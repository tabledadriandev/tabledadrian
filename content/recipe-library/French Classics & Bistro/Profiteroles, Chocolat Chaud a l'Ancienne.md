# Profiteroles, Chocolat Chaud à l'Ancienne

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Craquelin-topped choux filled with vanilla ice cream, flooded tableside with hot dark chocolate — the brasserie finale nobody refuses.

## Ingredients (serves 6, ~24 choux)
- Choux: water 90 ml, milk 90 ml, butter 80 g, salt 3 g, sugar 5 g, flour 110 g, eggs 3–4 (180–200 g)
- Craquelin: butter 50 g, demerara sugar 60 g, flour 60 g
- Vanilla ice cream — 750 ml
- Sauce: dark chocolate 70% — 200 g
- Double cream — 150 ml
- Whole milk — 100 ml
- Caster sugar — 30 g
- Unsalted butter — 20 g

## Method
1. Craquelin: rub butter, sugar and flour together; roll 2 mm between papers; freeze; cut 3.5 cm discs.
2. Choux: boil water, milk, butter, salt, sugar; beat in flour; dry the panade 2 min. Cool 3 min; beat in eggs gradually to a slow-falling V.
3. Pipe 3.5 cm mounds; cap each with a craquelin disc.
4. Bake at 180 °C fan 28–32 min WITHOUT opening the door, until deep gold and rigid. Pierce each; dry 5 min in the turned-off oven.
5. Sauce: melt chocolate with cream, milk and sugar to 60 °C; mount with butter.
6. Split choux; fill each with a small scoop of ice cream; re-lid.
7. Stack 4 per bowl; pour the hot sauce at the table.

## Plating & Service
Pyramid of four in a chilled coupe, sauce in a hot pouring jug — the collision of temperatures is the show.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fill with crème pâtissière + top with caramel for choux à la crème. Freeze baked empty shells; refresh 5 min at 160 °C.
