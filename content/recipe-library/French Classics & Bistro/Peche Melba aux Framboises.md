# Pêche Melba aux Framboises

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Escoffier's homage in its pure form — poached peaches, raw raspberry coulis, vanilla ice cream; nothing cooked that shouldn't be, nothing added that needn't be.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Ripe yellow peaches — 4
- Water — 800 ml
- Caster sugar — 240 g
- Vanilla pod — 1
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Raspberries — 300 g (250 g for coulis, 50 g to serve)
- Icing sugar — 30 g
- Vanilla ice cream — 500 ml
- Flaked almonds, toasted — 25 g

## Method
1. Cross-score the peach bases; blanch 30 sec; ice; slip off the skins.
2. Simmer water, sugar and split vanilla 5 min; add peaches; poach at 85 °C for 8–12 min until tender at the stone.
3. Cool peaches in the syrup; halve and stone when cold.
4. Coulis: blend 250 g raspberries with icing sugar and lemon juice; pass through a fine sieve. NEVER cook it — Escoffier's own rule.
5. Chill coupes.
6. Build: two scoops ice cream, two peach halves cut-side down, coulis poured generously over.
7. Fresh raspberries and almonds to finish.

## Plating & Service
Silver coupes if the boat has them; serve at once — this dessert exists for about four minutes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> White peaches in late summer are sublime. Dairy-free: coconut or almond-milk vanilla ice cream carries it well.
