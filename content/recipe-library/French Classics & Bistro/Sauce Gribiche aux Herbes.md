# Sauce Gribiche aux Herbes

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The cooked-egg mayonnaise cousin — chunky with cornichons, capers and herbs; the sauce that resurrects poached meats, asparagus and cold fish.

## Ingredients (serves 6, makes ~400 ml)
- Eggs — 3 (hard-boiled, 9 min 30)
- Dijon mustard — 20 g
- Red wine vinegar — 20 ml
- Neutral oil — 180 ml
- Cornichons, fine dice — 60 g
- Capers, chopped — 30 g
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 10 g
- Tarragon, chopped — 6 g
- Chervil, chopped — 5 g
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Boil eggs 9 min 30 sec; ice, peel. Separate yolks and whites.
2. Mash the cooked yolks with mustard and vinegar to a smooth paste.
3. Whisk in the oil in a thin stream — it emulsifies like a mayonnaise, slightly looser.
4. Dice the egg whites into 4 mm pieces.
5. Fold in whites, cornichons, capers and all the herbs.
6. Season boldly; rest 20 min for the flavours to marry.
7. Keeps 3 days chilled; loosen with a little warm water before serving.

## Plating & Service
Classic with tête de veau; brilliant over poached leeks, roast salmon, or boiled new potatoes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Ravigote (its vinaigrette sister): skip the emulsion, whisk everything into 120 ml oil + 40 ml vinegar. Add anchovy for pot-au-feu leftovers.
