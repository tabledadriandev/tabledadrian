# Saucisse de Toulouse, Aligot de l'Aubrac

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Cheese-pulled aligot ribboning off the spoon under a coiled grilled Toulouse sausage — Aubrac plateau food, pure spectacle at service.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Toulouse sausage, coiled or links — 700 g
- Floury-waxy potatoes (Agria) — 800 g
- Tomme fraîche de l'Aubrac (or young mozzarella + Comté), slivered — 400 g
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Crème fraîche — 100 g
- Garlic, grated — 8 g
- Fine salt — 10 g; white pepper 1 g
- Olive oil — 15 ml
- Red wine jus (optional) — 100 ml

## Method
1. Steam peeled potato chunks 25 min until completely tender.
2. Rice the potatoes twice; return to a heavy pan over low heat with butter, crème fraîche, garlic, salt and pepper.
3. Grill or pan-roast the sausage: prick nowhere, colour in oil over medium heat 12–15 min, turning, to a core of 72 °C. Rest 5 min.
4. Beat the slivered cheese into the hot purée over low heat in three additions, stirring figure-eights with a wooden spoon.
5. Keep beating 5–8 min until the aligot turns glossy and pulls in long elastic ribbons — lift the spoon high to test.
6. Adjust salt; it must be served immediately at 70 °C+.
7. Slice the sausage thickly; warm the jus.

## Plating & Service
Pull the aligot high at the table before mounding; sausage over, jus around. Applause optional but likely.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No tomme fraîche? 300 g low-moisture mozzarella + 100 g Comté gets 90% there. Vegetarian: serve aligot with roasted mushrooms.
