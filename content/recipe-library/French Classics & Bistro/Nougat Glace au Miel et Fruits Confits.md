# Nougat Glacé au Miel et Fruits Confits

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Frozen honey-meringue parfait strung with caramelised almonds and confit fruit — a no-churn ice dessert that slices like nougat de Montélimar.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Honey (lavender or acacia) — 120 g
- Egg whites — 3 (90 g)
- Caster sugar — 30 g
- Double cream — 400 ml
- Almonds and pistachios — 120 g
- Caster sugar (for pralining nuts) — 60 g
- Candied orange and cherries, diced — 100 g
- Kirsch — 15 ml
- Fine salt — 1 g
- Raspberry coulis: raspberries 250 g, icing sugar 25 g, lemon 10 ml

## Method
1. Toast nuts at 160 °C, 8 min; caramelise with 60 g sugar in a pan until amber-coated; cool on a mat; chop coarsely.
2. Macerate candied fruit in kirsch.
3. Heat honey to 118 °C. Whip whites with salt and 30 g sugar to soft peaks; pour the hot honey down the side in a thread, whipping to a glossy, cool meringue (8 min).
4. Whip cream to soft peaks.
5. Fold cream into the honey meringue, then the nuts and fruit.
6. Fill a lined 1 L terrine (or 8 ring moulds); freeze 6 h minimum.
7. Coulis: blend, sieve, chill.
8. Slice 2 cm with a hot knife straight from the freezer; plate on coulis.

## Plating & Service
A slice on a pool of coulis, extra praliné nuts scattered; it holds 10 min on a warm deck — plate fast.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: caramelised sunflower seeds and extra citrus. Serves from the freezer for two weeks — the ultimate make-ahead charter dessert.
