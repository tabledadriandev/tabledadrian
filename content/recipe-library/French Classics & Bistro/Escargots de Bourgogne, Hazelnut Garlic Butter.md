# Escargots de Bourgogne, Hazelnut Garlic Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Classic snail butter sharpened with toasted hazelnut and a hit of pastis — the bubbling dish arrives at the table still hissing.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Cooked Burgundy snails (canned, drained) — 24 (about 250 g)
- Unsalted butter, soft — 200 g
- Garlic, very finely minced — 25 g
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 30 g
- Shallot, minced — 30 g
- Toasted hazelnuts, finely chopped — 30 g
- Pastis — 10 ml
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Black pepper — 1.5 g
- Baguette — 1 (250 g)

## Method
1. Beat butter with garlic, parsley, shallot, hazelnuts, pastis, lemon, salt and pepper. Chill 20 min so it pipes cleanly.
2. Rinse snails, pat dry, and season lightly.
3. Place one snail in each well of two escargot dishes (or shells); pipe butter generously over each, sealing the well.
4. Rest 15 min in the fridge so the butter anchors.
5. Bake at 200 °C for 8–10 min until the butter foams and just starts to brown at the edges.
6. Warm baguette alongside for the last 5 min.

## Plating & Service
Straight from oven to table on a folded napkin; serve with tongs, picks and plenty of bread for the butter.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: omit hazelnuts, add 10 g fine breadcrumbs for texture. No snails aboard? The same butter over roasted king oyster mushrooms is superb.
