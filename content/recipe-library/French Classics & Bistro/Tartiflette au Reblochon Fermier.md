# Tartiflette au Reblochon Fermier

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Potatoes, lardons, onions and a whole Reblochon melted rind-down — Savoie ski-hut food that flattens a crew after a mistral day.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Waxy potatoes — 1.4 kg
- Reblochon fermier, whole — 1 (450 g)
- Smoked bacon lardons — 250 g
- Onions, sliced — 300 g
- Dry white wine (Savoie Apremont style) — 120 ml
- Crème fraîche — 100 g
- Garlic clove — 1 (for the dish)
- Unsalted butter — 20 g
- Fine salt — 8 g; black pepper 2 g; nutmeg 0.3 g

## Method
1. Simmer whole potatoes in salted water 18–20 min until just tender; cool, peel, slice 8 mm.
2. Render lardons 5 min; add onions, cook 8 min to soft gold.
3. Add wine; reduce until nearly dry.
4. Rub a gratin dish with the garlic clove and butter.
5. Fold potatoes with the onion-lardon mix, crème fraîche, pepper and nutmeg; layer in the dish.
6. Halve the Reblochon horizontally; lay both halves rind-UP over the potatoes.
7. Bake at 200 °C for 25–30 min until molten under a bronzed rind.
8. Rest 5 min.

## Plating & Service
Serve from the dish with cornichons, pickled onions and a bitter salad — nothing else needed.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No Reblochon? Use a ripe Camembert plus 80 g Comté. Vegetarian: smoked tofu lardons and vegetarian-rennet cheese.
