# Endives Braisées au Jus d'Orange

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Endives caramelised cut-side down then braised in orange and butter — bitterness tamed into something silky and sweet-sharp.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- White endives, halved lengthwise — 4 large (about 700 g)
- Unsalted butter — 50 g
- Caster sugar — 10 g
- Orange juice — 150 ml
- Orange zest — 3 g
- Light chicken or vegetable stock — 100 ml
- Thyme — 3 sprigs
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Black pepper — 1 g
- Hazelnuts, toasted, crushed (optional) — 20 g

## Method
1. Melt butter with sugar in a wide sauté pan over medium heat.
2. Lay endives cut-side down; caramelise 6–8 min undisturbed to deep amber.
3. Season; add orange juice, zest, stock and thyme.
4. Cover with a cartouche; braise gently 20–25 min, turning once, until a knife slides into the cores.
5. Remove endives; reduce the juices to a glossy glaze, 3–4 min.
6. Roll endives in the glaze; check seasoning.

## Plating & Service
Two halves per plate, glaze spooned over, hazelnuts scattered; superb with duck, pork or seared scallops.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Gratin version: wrap each braised half in ham, cover with Mornay, gratinate 15 min at 200 °C. Vegan: olive oil, and maple instead of sugar.
