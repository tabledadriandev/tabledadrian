# Pommes Anna au Beurre Clarifié

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Concentric potato petals pressed into a butter-crisped cake — inverted at the table to a collective intake of breath.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Waxy potatoes, even cylinders — 1.2 kg
- Clarified butter, melted — 150 g
- Fine salt — 10 g
- White pepper — 1 g
- Thyme leaves — 2 g

## Method
1. Oven to 200 °C. Peel potatoes; trim into even cylinders; slice 2 mm on a mandoline. Do not rinse.
2. Toss slices with 100 g clarified butter, salt, pepper and thyme.
3. Brush a 20 cm ovenproof frying pan (or copper pommes Anna mould) with butter; start a tight rosette of overlapping slices in the centre, spiralling out.
4. Layer the rest in neat overlapping circles, pressing each layer down; pour remaining butter over.
5. Start on the hob, medium heat, 8 min — the base must begin frying.
6. Cover with foil; bake 30 min. Uncover; bake 20 min more.
7. Rest 5 min; pour off excess butter, run a palette knife around, invert boldly onto a board.
8. The crown should be mahogany and lacquered; salt lightly.

## Plating & Service
Cut into wedges like a cake beside roast chicken or beef; the interior stays supple, the outside shatters.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Slip garlic slivers and rosemary between layers. Duck-fat version for magret nights is devastating.
