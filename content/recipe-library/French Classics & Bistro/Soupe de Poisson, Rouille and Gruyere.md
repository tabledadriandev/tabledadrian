# Soupe de Poisson, Rouille & Gruyère

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Marseille harbour classic — whole rockfish blended bones-and-all then passed, with saffron rouille rafts; deeply savoury and sunset-orange.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Mixed small rockfish / soup fish, gutted — 1.5 kg
- Olive oil — 60 ml
- Onion, sliced — 200 g
- Fennel, sliced — 150 g
- Leek, sliced — 100 g
- Garlic — 20 g
- Tomatoes, chopped — 400 g
- Tomato paste — 40 g
- Saffron — 0.4 g
- Orange zest — 1 strip
- Water — 2.2 L
- Baguette — 150 g
- Gruyère, grated — 100 g
- Rouille: egg yolk 20 g, garlic 8 g, saffron 0.1 g, cayenne 0.5 g, olive oil 150 ml, cooked potato 40 g, salt 3 g
- Fine salt — 10 g

## Method
1. Sweat onion, fennel, leek and garlic in oil 8 min. Add tomato paste; cook 2 min.
2. Add fish, tomatoes, saffron, orange zest and water; boil, then simmer 40 min, crushing the fish down at 20 min.
3. Remove zest. Blend everything — bones included — in batches, 2 min per batch.
4. Pass through a moulin then a chinois, pressing hard; season with salt.
5. Rouille: pound garlic, saffron, cayenne and potato; whisk in yolk, then oil drop by drop to a stiff paste; season.
6. Slice and toast baguette croutons at 180 °C, 8 min.
7. Serve soup at 85 °C; guests spread rouille on croutons, float them, and shower with Gruyère.

## Plating & Service
Tureen to table with croutons, rouille and cheese in separate bowls — the ritual is half the pleasure.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No rockfish? Use gurnard, small bream and 300 g prawn shells. GF: serve with toasted GF bread.
