# Sauce Mornay, Béchamel Mère

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The mother béchamel taken to Mornay — one master ratio (60/60/1000) that runs croques, gratins and glazed fish across a whole season.

## Ingredients (serves 8, makes ~1 L)
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Plain flour — 60 g
- Whole milk — 1 L
- Onion — 50 g, studded with 2 cloves
- Bay leaf — 1
- Nutmeg — 0.5 g
- Fine salt — 8 g
- White pepper — 1 g
- Gruyère, grated — 100 g
- Parmesan, grated — 40 g
- Egg yolks — 2 (40 g)

## Method
1. Infuse milk with the studded onion and bay at 80 °C for 10 min; strain.
2. Melt butter; add flour; cook the roux 2 min over medium heat — blond, not brown.
3. Whisk in the hot milk in four additions, beating smooth between each.
4. Simmer gently 8–10 min, stirring the corners, until nappe (coats a spoon at 2 mm).
5. Season with salt, white pepper and nutmeg. This is your béchamel — stop here for lasagne/gratins.
6. For Mornay: off the heat, beat in yolks, then both cheeses until glossy. Do not reboil.
7. Press cling film onto the surface if holding; use within 2 days chilled.

## Plating & Service
Nap over poached eggs, cauliflower, leeks or croques and gratinate at 240 °C until blistered.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: rice flour roux (cook 3 min). Soubise: add 200 g sweated onion purée to the béchamel instead of cheese.
