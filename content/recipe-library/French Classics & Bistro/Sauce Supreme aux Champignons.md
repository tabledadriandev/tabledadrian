# Sauce Suprême aux Champignons

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Velouté — the second mother — reduced with mushroom essence and finished with cream; the sauce that makes poached chicken taste like occasion.

## Ingredients (serves 6, makes ~600 ml)
- Unsalted butter — 50 g
- Plain flour — 50 g
- White chicken stock, hot — 800 ml
- Button mushrooms, sliced — 200 g
- Double cream — 150 ml
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Fine salt — 6 g
- White pepper — 1 g
- Cold butter (finish) — 20 g

## Method
1. Make a blond roux with butter and flour, 2 min.
2. Whisk in hot stock gradually; bring to a simmer.
3. Add mushrooms; simmer very gently 25 min, skimming the skin that forms — this 'dépouillage' refines the sauce.
4. Strain through a chinois, pressing the mushrooms lightly.
5. Add cream; reduce 5 min to a silky nappe.
6. Season with lemon, salt and white pepper; mount with cold butter.
7. Hold at 80 °C, surface filmed with butter, up to 2 h.

## Plating & Service
Ladle over poached or roasted chicken, veal quenelles or steamed asparagus.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Allemande: bind with 2 yolks instead of the final cream (never boil after). Ivory sauce: add 20 g tomato paste for salmon.
