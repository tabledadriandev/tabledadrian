# Petit Salé aux Lentilles du Puy

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Home-cured pork simmered over slate-green Puy lentils — the Auvergne's Sunday lunch; cure the pork three days ahead and the rest cooks itself.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Pork belly, skin on — 800 g
- Pork knuckle or shoulder — 600 g
- Cure: coarse salt 60 g, sugar 20 g, thyme 4 sprigs, bay 2, juniper 6 berries
- Puy lentils — 450 g
- Smoked sausage (Morteau-style) — 1 (350 g)
- Onion, clove-studded — 1 (150 g)
- Carrots, thick rounds — 300 g
- Leek, sliced — 150 g
- Garlic — 12 g
- Bouquet garni — 1
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Dijon mustard — to serve
- Red wine vinegar — 15 ml
- Flat-leaf parsley — 10 g

## Method
1. Rub pork pieces with the cure; refrigerate 48–72 h, turning daily. Rinse well; soak 2 h in cold water.
2. Cover pork with fresh water; simmer at 90 °C, skimming, 1.5 h.
3. Add the whole sausage; simmer 30 min more, until all meats are tender.
4. Meanwhile simmer lentils with studded onion, carrots, leek, garlic and bouquet in 1.2 L of the pork cooking liquor (taste — dilute if too salty), 25–30 min until just tender.
5. Season lentils with pepper and vinegar only (the pork brings salt); discard onion and bouquet.
6. Slice the meats thickly.
7. Serve meats over the lentils, moistened with liquor, parsley over.

## Plating & Service
Big shallow bowls, Dijon on the table; a glass of young Gamay does no harm.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Short on time? Use bought salted pork or smoked ham hock. The lentils alone (cooked in vegetable stock) make a superb vegan main.
