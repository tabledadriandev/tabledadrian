# Tarte Fine aux Pommes, Calvados

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Paper-thin apple fans on crackling puff, glazed with Calvados butter — a 30-minute dessert that looks like an hour of patisserie.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- All-butter puff pastry — 300 g
- Firm apples (Cox or Braeburn) — 4 (about 700 g)
- Unsalted butter, melted — 50 g
- Caster sugar — 60 g
- Calvados — 25 ml
- Apricot jam — 40 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Fine salt — 1 g
- Vanilla ice cream, to serve — 400 ml

## Method
1. Roll puff to 3 mm; cut four 14 cm discs (or one 28 cm); dock ALL over except a 1 cm border. Chill 20 min.
2. Peel, halve, core apples; slice 2 mm; toss with lemon.
3. Fan the slices in tight overlapping circles to the border — pack them; they shrink.
4. Brush apples with half the melted butter; scatter sugar and salt evenly.
5. Bake at 200 °C on a preheated tray 25–30 min until the pastry base is bronzed and the apple edges catch.
6. Warm jam with Calvados and remaining butter; brush the hot tart generously.
7. Rest 5 min — serve warm, not hot.

## Plating & Service
Whole disc per plate, ice cream quenelle dead centre, a last brush of glaze at the pass.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pear + 2 g crushed cardamom works. Vegan: vegan puff, oil instead of butter, glaze with jam only.
