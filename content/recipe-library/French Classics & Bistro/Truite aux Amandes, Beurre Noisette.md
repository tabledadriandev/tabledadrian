# Truite aux Amandes, Beurre Noisette

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Whole trout crisped in butter with toasted flaked almonds — a five-ingredient bistro standard that rewards perfect timing.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Whole trout, gutted, fins trimmed — 4 (about 300 g each)
- Plain flour — 50 g
- Unsalted butter — 120 g
- Flaked almonds — 60 g
- Lemon juice — 30 ml
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 10 g
- Fine salt — 8 g
- Black pepper — 1.5 g
- Neutral oil — 30 ml

## Method
1. Season trout inside and out; dredge in flour, shaking off excess.
2. Heat oil with 40 g butter in one very large (or two) pans over medium-high heat.
3. Cook trout 5–6 min on the first side until the skin is golden and crisp; turn carefully.
4. Cook 4–5 min on the second side to a core of 54 °C at the thickest point.
5. Transfer to warm plates; wipe the pan.
6. Add remaining 80 g butter; when foaming, add almonds and toast 90 sec to noisette stage.
7. Off heat, add lemon juice and parsley; spoon the almonds and butter over the trout.

## Plating & Service
Whole fish per plate, almonds cascading over; buttered green beans and steamed potatoes alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: use rice flour. Nut allergy: finish with capers and croutons instead (grenobloise style).
