# Frites de Bistrot, Double Cuisson

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The double-fry canon — a soft blanch then a hard crisp; hollow-light inside, glass-crisp outside, salted the second they land.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Maris Piper / Bintje potatoes — 1.2 kg
- Frying oil (rapeseed or beef dripping for heresy-free flavour) — 2.5 L
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Fleur de sel — 3 g

## Method
1. Peel; cut into 1 cm × 1 cm batons of even length.
2. Rinse in cold water until it runs clear; soak 30 min; dry COMPLETELY on cloths — water is the enemy of crispness.
3. First fry at 140 °C, in two batches, 6–7 min: cooked through, floppy, no colour.
4. Drain; rest at least 20 min on a rack (or chill uncovered up to 24 h — even better).
5. Second fry at 180 °C, 2–3 min, until deep gold and rigid — they should 'tick' against each other.
6. Drain 10 sec; toss with fleur de sel in a wide bowl.
7. Serve within 3 minutes, never covered.

## Plating & Service
Standing in a cone or napkin-lined pot beside steak, moules or a croque — mayonnaise on the side, French-style.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Duck-fat second fry for special nights. Herb salt: blitz 2 g thyme + rosemary into the fleur de sel.
