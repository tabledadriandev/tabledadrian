# Céleri Rémoulade with Smoked Trout

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Crunchy mustard-dressed celeriac against silky smoked trout — a no-cook starter that plates in five minutes yet looks composed.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Celeriac, peeled — 500 g
- Lemon juice — 30 ml
- Egg yolk — 1 (20 g)
- Dijon mustard — 25 g
- Neutral oil — 150 ml
- White wine vinegar — 10 ml
- Crème fraîche — 40 g
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Smoked trout fillets — 240 g
- Chives, sliced — 10 g
- Tarragon leaves — 4 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Julienne celeriac on a mandoline (2 mm); toss immediately with lemon juice and 3 g salt. Rest 15 min to soften slightly, then squeeze gently.
2. Whisk yolk, mustard and vinegar; stream in oil to a thick mayonnaise. Loosen with crème fraîche; season.
3. Fold celeriac through the rémoulade with chives — it should be generously coated but not drowning.
4. Flake trout into large petals.
5. Taste and sharpen with extra lemon if needed.

## Plating & Service
A neat mound of rémoulade, trout petals leaning against it, tarragon and cracked pepper to finish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian: skip trout, add toasted walnuts and sliced apple. Lighter: replace half the mayonnaise with thick yogurt.
