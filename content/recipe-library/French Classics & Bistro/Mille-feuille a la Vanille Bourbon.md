# Mille-feuille à la Vanille Bourbon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Caramelised puff pressed flat between trays, layered with diplomat cream and assembled on its side — modern architecture, ancient pleasure.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- All-butter puff pastry — 500 g
- Icing sugar — 60 g
- Diplomat cream: whole milk 400 ml, vanilla pods 1.5, egg yolks 5 (100 g), caster sugar 80 g, cornflour 35 g, butter 25 g, gold gelatine 1 leaf (2 g), double cream (whipped) 150 ml
- Fine salt — 1 g

## Method
1. Roll puff to 2.5 mm into a 30 × 40 cm sheet; dock thoroughly; chill 30 min.
2. Bake between two flat trays (parchment above and below) at 180 °C for 30 min.
3. Uncover; dust heavily with icing sugar; return at 210 °C for 6–8 min until glassy caramel. Cool flat under a light tray.
4. Pâtissière: whisk yolks, sugar, cornflour, salt; add vanilla-infused hot milk; boil 2 min whisking; add butter and bloomed gelatine. Chill filmed.
5. Beat the pâtissière smooth; fold in whipped cream — diplomat.
6. Trim the sheet with a serrated knife into eighteen 11 × 3.5 cm rectangles.
7. Pipe diplomat bulbs on 12 rectangles; stack two creamed layers, cap with a bare one.
8. Lay each mille-feuille on its side (cream visible); chill 30 min; dust the top edge.

## Plating & Service
On its side, one per slate-cool plate — cleanest cut in the house, no squashing at first bite.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Praliné diplomat or fresh raspberries between layers. Assemble within 3 h of service — the enemy is soggy puff.
