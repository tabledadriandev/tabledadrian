# Saumon à l'Oseille, Sorrel Cream

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Barely-cooked salmon escalopes under a sharp sorrel cream — the nouvelle-cuisine idea that acidity, not butter, should carry rich fish.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Salmon fillet, skinless, cut into 1.5 cm escalopes — 700 g
- Sorrel leaves, stemmed — 100 g
- Shallots, minced — 60 g
- Dry white wine — 100 ml
- Dry vermouth — 40 ml
- Fish stock — 150 ml
- Double cream — 250 ml
- Unsalted butter — 20 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Fine salt — 6 g
- White pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Reduce shallots, wine and vermouth to 30 ml; add stock, reduce by half.
2. Add cream; simmer to light coating consistency, about 5 min.
3. Chiffonade the sorrel; stir into the sauce off the heat — it will olive instantly and turn the sauce tangy. Season with salt, pepper, lemon.
4. Season escalopes. Heat a dry non-stick pan until hot; brush fish with butter.
5. Cook escalopes 45–60 sec per side only — the centre must stay coral (48 °C).
6. Pool sauce on hot plates, lay salmon on top so it finishes in the sauce's heat.

## Plating & Service
Sauce first, fish on top, a few raw sorrel ribbons; steamed cucumber or ratte potatoes beside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No sorrel? Use spinach plus 25 ml extra lemon. Trout or arctic char escalopes respond identically.
