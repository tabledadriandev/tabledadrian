# Ratatouille Confit Byaldi, Piment Doux

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The shingled, oven-confited ratatouille — vegetables sliced thin, laid over a smoky piperade base and baked low so each ring candies in olive oil.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Courgettes (mixed green/yellow), 2 mm rounds — 500 g
- Aubergine, slim, 2 mm rounds — 400 g
- Roma tomatoes, 2 mm rounds — 500 g
- Base: red peppers, roasted, peeled — 300 g
- Onion, fine dice — 200 g
- Garlic, minced — 15 g
- Ripe tomatoes, chopped — 300 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 120 ml
- Thyme — 6 sprigs
- Basil — 15 g
- Sherry vinegar — 15 ml
- Espelette pepper — 1.5 g
- Fine salt — 12 g

## Method
1. Base: sweat onion and garlic in 40 ml oil, 6 min; add chopped tomatoes and roasted peppers; simmer 20 min; blend smooth with vinegar and 5 g salt.
2. Spread the base 1 cm deep in a wide gratin or paella dish.
3. Shingle alternating rounds of courgette, aubergine and tomato in tight concentric spirals over the base.
4. Brush generously with 60 ml oil; season with remaining salt, thyme leaves and Espelette.
5. Cover with parchment cut to fit; bake at 150 °C for 75 min.
6. Uncover; raise to 190 °C for 15–20 min until the edges caramelise.
7. Rest 10 min; dress with torn basil and the last of the oil.

## Plating & Service
Serve wedges with the pepper base spooned around — a main with chickpea panisse, or a side to roast lamb.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Classic chunky ratatouille: sauté each vegetable separately, combine 20 min. Add goat cheese medallions (no longer vegan) for a richer plate.
