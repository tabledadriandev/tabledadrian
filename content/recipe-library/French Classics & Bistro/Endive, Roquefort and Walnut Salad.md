# Endive, Roquefort & Walnut Salad

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bitter leaves, salty blue and honeyed walnuts in a walnut-oil dressing — a three-minute assembly that tastes like a Paris wine bar.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- White endives — 3 (about 400 g)
- Red endives — 2 (about 250 g)
- Roquefort — 120 g
- Walnut halves — 80 g
- Honey — 15 g
- Walnut oil — 45 ml
- Neutral oil — 15 ml
- Sherry vinegar — 20 ml
- Dijon mustard — 8 g
- Chives — 8 g
- Fine salt — 3 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Toast walnuts at 160 °C for 6 min; while hot, toss with honey and a pinch of salt; cool on parchment.
2. Whisk mustard, vinegar and salt; stream in both oils.
3. Trim endive bases; separate leaves, halving the largest lengthwise.
4. Toss leaves gently with the dressing just before serving.
5. Crumble Roquefort over; add walnuts, chives and a generous grind of pepper.

## Plating & Service
Build tall and loose on chilled plates — leaves should look tumbled, not arranged.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add sliced pear or Comté instead of Roquefort for a milder plate. Vegan: swap cheese for salted candied pecans and add capers.
