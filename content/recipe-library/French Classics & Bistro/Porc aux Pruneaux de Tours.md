# Porc aux Pruneaux de Tours

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Pork medallions with Vouvray-soaked prunes in a light cream — the Touraine classic that pairs sweet and savoury with total assurance.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Pork tenderloin, 3 cm medallions — 700 g
- Agen prunes, pitted — 200 g
- Vouvray or off-dry Chenin — 250 ml
- Fine salt — 8 g; black pepper 1.5 g
- Unsalted butter — 40 g
- Neutral oil — 20 ml
- Shallots, minced — 60 g
- Chicken stock — 150 ml
- Double cream — 150 ml
- Redcurrant jelly — 15 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Chives — 6 g

## Method
1. Soak prunes in the warm Vouvray 1 h (or simmer 5 min).
2. Season medallions; sear in oil and 20 g butter, 2–3 min per side to a core of 60 °C; rest under foil.
3. Sweat shallots in remaining butter 3 min.
4. Add the prune-soaking wine; reduce by half. Add stock; reduce by half again.
5. Add cream and redcurrant jelly; simmer to a light nap; season with salt, pepper, lemon.
6. Add prunes to warm through 2 min; return medallions and juices for 1 min.
7. Finish with chives.

## Plating & Service
Three medallions per plate, prunes between, sauce over; steamed potatoes or wilted chard alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free: reduce the sauce further and mount with 20 g olive oil instead of cream. Also superb with rabbit saddle.
