# Far Breton aux Pruneaux

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Brittany's dense flan-cake studded with rum-soaked prunes — mixes in five minutes, improves overnight, travels anywhere.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Agen prunes, pitted — 250 g
- Dark rum — 50 ml
- Black tea, hot — 100 ml
- Plain flour — 180 g
- Caster sugar — 130 g
- Eggs — 4 (220 g)
- Whole milk — 750 ml
- Vanilla pod — 1
- Unsalted butter — 40 g (25 g melted for batter, 15 g for the dish)
- Fine salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Soak prunes in hot tea and rum 1 h (or overnight); drain, reserving 20 ml liquor.
2. Butter a deep 24 cm ceramic dish generously. Oven to 180 °C.
3. Whisk flour, sugar and salt; add eggs one by one to a smooth thick paste.
4. Loosen gradually with milk, vanilla seeds, melted butter and reserved liquor — a thin batter, like crêpes.
5. Scatter prunes over the dish; pour the batter over.
6. Bake 55–60 min until puffed, deeply browned on top (far must look almost too dark) and just set.
7. Cool fully — it sinks and densifies as it should. Better after 12 h.

## Plating & Service
Room-temperature squares from the dish, no accompaniment — Breton rules; salted-butter caramel if you must.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Plain far (no fruit) is legitimate. Raisin + Armagnac variant for Gascon guests. Never serve warm — texture sets on cooling.
