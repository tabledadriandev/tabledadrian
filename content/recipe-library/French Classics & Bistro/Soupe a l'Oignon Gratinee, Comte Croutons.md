# Soupe à l'Oignon Gratinée, Comté Croutons

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Deeply caramelised onions deglazed in stages give a mahogany broth that eats like a main — the ultimate cold-night charter opener.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Yellow onions, sliced 3 mm — 1.2 kg
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Olive oil — 20 ml
- Plain flour — 20 g
- Dry white wine — 150 ml
- Beef stock (or dark chicken stock) — 1.5 L
- Fresh thyme — 4 sprigs
- Bay leaf — 1
- Baguette, sliced 1.5 cm — 12 slices (200 g)
- Comté, grated — 180 g
- Sherry vinegar — 10 ml
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Melt butter with oil in a heavy pot over medium heat; add onions and 3 g salt. Cook 45–55 min, stirring every 5 min, until deep mahogany — deglaze the fond with 20 ml water whenever it darkens.
2. Stir in flour and cook 2 min to coat.
3. Add wine, reduce by half, then add stock, thyme and bay. Simmer 25 min, skimming.
4. Season with sherry vinegar, remaining salt and pepper; discard herbs.
5. Toast baguette slices at 180 °C for 8 min until dry and golden.
6. Ladle soup into ovenproof bowls, float 2 croutons per bowl, cover generously with Comté.
7. Gratinate under a hot grill (250 °C) 4–5 min until blistered and bubbling.

## Plating & Service
Serve bubbling in lion's-head bowls on underliners with a napkin — warn guests the bowl is hot.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: use gluten-free baguette and swap flour for rice flour. Lighter: skip the gratin, finish with a Comté tuile on the side.
