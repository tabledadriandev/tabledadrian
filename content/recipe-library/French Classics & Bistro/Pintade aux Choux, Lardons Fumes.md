# Pintade aux Choux, Lardons Fumés

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Guinea fowl pot-roasted on a bed of buttered savoy cabbage and smoked lardons — a hunting-lodge dish scaled for the salon table.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Guinea fowl — 1 (about 1.4 kg)
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Smoked bacon lardons — 150 g
- Savoy cabbage, cored, 3 cm ribbons — 800 g
- Onion, sliced — 150 g
- Carrot, thick slices — 150 g
- Garlic — 10 g
- Dry white wine — 150 ml
- Chicken stock — 250 ml
- Juniper berries, crushed — 4 (2 g)
- Thyme — 4 sprigs; bay 1
- Fine salt — 11 g; black pepper 2 g

## Method
1. Season the bird inside and out; brown all over in 40 g butter in a cocotte, 10 min; remove.
2. Render lardons; add onion, carrot, garlic; sweat 5 min.
3. Blanch cabbage 2 min in salted water; drain hard. Add to the pot with juniper, thyme and bay; toss.
4. Add wine and stock; nestle the bird breast-up on the cabbage.
5. Cover; pot-roast at 170 °C for 50–60 min, to a leg core of 78–80 °C. Uncover for the last 10 min to colour.
6. Rest the bird 12 min; meanwhile reduce the cabbage juices over high heat and finish with remaining 20 g butter.
7. Joint the bird; serve over the cabbage.

## Plating & Service
Family-style from the cocotte — legs and sliced breast over glossy cabbage, juices spooned over.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pheasant or a small chicken substitutes directly. Add 300 g halved potatoes to the pot for a one-pot supper (no longer low-carb).
