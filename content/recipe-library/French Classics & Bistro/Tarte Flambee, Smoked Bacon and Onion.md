# Tarte Flambée, Smoked Bacon & Onion

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Alsace's cracker-thin 'flammekueche' — an unleavened dough blasted at maximum heat so the fromage blanc blisters and the edges char.

## Ingredients (serves 6, makes 2 tarts)
- Plain flour — 250 g
- Water — 125 ml
- Olive oil — 25 ml
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Fromage blanc (or 50/50 quark and crème fraîche) — 250 g
- Crème fraîche — 60 g
- Egg yolk — 1 (20 g)
- Nutmeg — 0.5 g
- Smoked bacon lardons, thin — 150 g
- White onion, sliced paper-thin — 200 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Mix flour, water, oil and salt into a firm dough; knead 3 min, rest 30 min (no yeast).
2. Heat oven to its maximum (275–300 °C) with a pizza stone or heavy tray inside for 40 min.
3. Beat fromage blanc with crème fraîche, yolk, nutmeg, pepper and a pinch of salt.
4. Roll each dough half paper-thin (2 mm) into a rough oval on floured parchment.
5. Spread the cream base to 1 cm from the edge; scatter raw onions and lardons.
6. Slide onto the stone; bake 5–7 min until the rim chars in spots and the base is crisp.
7. Slice immediately and repeat with the second tart.

## Plating & Service
Cut into strips and serve on a board straight away — flammekueche waits for no one.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Gratinée version: add 60 g Gruyère before baking. Vegetarian: swap bacon for smoked tofu lardons or sautéed mushrooms.
