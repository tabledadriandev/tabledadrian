# Boudin Noir aux Pommes, Purée Fine

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Black pudding seared until the skin crackles, with caramelised apples and silky mash — bistro soul food, five components, zero pretence.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Boudin noir (best quality) — 600 g, in 8 pieces
- Firm apples, 8 wedges each — 3 (about 450 g)
- Unsalted butter — 90 g
- Caster sugar — 15 g
- Calvados — 30 ml
- Cider vinegar — 10 ml
- Floury potatoes — 700 g
- Whole milk, hot — 150 ml
- Fine salt — 10 g; black pepper 1.5 g
- Watercress — 60 g

## Method
1. Steam potatoes in skins 25–30 min; peel, rice, and beat with 50 g butter and hot milk to a soft purée; season.
2. Caramelise apple wedges in 25 g butter with the sugar, 6–7 min, until amber and just tender.
3. Add Calvados to the apples, flambé; finish with cider vinegar.
4. Prick boudin pieces once; cook in remaining 15 g butter over medium heat, 4–5 min per side, until the skin is taut and crisp and the centre steams (70 °C).
5. Rest 2 min.
6. Season everything; check the purée is glossy and loose.

## Plating & Service
Purée swept across the plate, boudin propped on it, apples between, watercress on top for bite.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Boudin blanc works for milder palates (cook gently — it splits). Swap purée for celeriac-potato mix for less starch.
