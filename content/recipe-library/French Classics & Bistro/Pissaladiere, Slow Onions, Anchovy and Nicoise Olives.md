# Pissaladière, Slow Onions, Anchovy & Niçoise Olives

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Nice's onion tart on a thin olive-oil dough — the onions cook to jam before they ever see the oven; perfect cut into apéro squares.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Strong flour — 300 g
- Water, warm — 180 ml
- Fresh yeast — 8 g (or 3 g dry)
- Olive oil — 60 ml (30 for dough, 30 for onions)
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Yellow onions, thinly sliced — 1.5 kg
- Thyme — 5 sprigs
- Bay leaf — 1
- Anchovy fillets in oil — 20 (about 80 g)
- Niçoise olives — 80 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Mix flour, yeast, water, 30 ml oil and salt; knead 8 min to a smooth dough. Prove 1 h until doubled.
2. Meanwhile cook onions with 30 ml oil, thyme, bay and a pinch of salt over low heat, lid on, 20 min; then lid off 30–40 min, stirring, to a pale-gold jam with no liquid. Cool.
3. Press dough into an oiled 30 × 40 cm tray to 5 mm thickness; prove 20 min.
4. Spread onions to the edges; lattice the anchovies in diamonds and dot olives in the gaps.
5. Bake at 220 °C for 18–20 min until the base is crisp and deep golden underneath.
6. Rest 5 min, grind over pepper, slice.

## Plating & Service
Warm or room temperature in squares — ideal with chilled rosé on the aft deck.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: replace anchovies with strips of slow-roasted red pepper and add 10 g capers to the onions.
