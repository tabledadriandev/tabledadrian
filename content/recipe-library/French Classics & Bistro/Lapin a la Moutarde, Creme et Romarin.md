# Lapin à la Moutarde, Crème et Romarin

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rabbit slathered in two mustards and braised in white wine cream — the dish that defines French home cooking's quiet confidence.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Rabbit, jointed (legs, saddle, shoulders) — 1 (about 1.4 kg)
- Dijon mustard — 70 g
- Wholegrain mustard — 30 g
- Fine salt — 9 g; black pepper 2 g
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Unsalted butter — 30 g
- Shallots, sliced — 120 g
- Garlic — 8 g
- Dry white wine — 250 ml
- Chicken stock — 250 ml
- Double cream — 200 ml
- Rosemary — 2 sprigs; thyme 3 sprigs
- Lemon juice — 10 ml

## Method
1. Season rabbit; brush all over with 50 g of the Dijon.
2. Brown gently in oil and butter (mustard burns fast — moderate heat), 6–8 min; remove.
3. Sweat shallots and garlic 4 min; deglaze with wine, reduce by half.
4. Add stock, rosemary and thyme; return legs and shoulders. Cover; braise at 160 °C for 30 min.
5. Add the saddle pieces; braise 20–25 min more, until juices run clear (leg core 75 °C).
6. Lift rabbit; strain the liquor, add cream, reduce to a coating sauce.
7. Whisk in remaining Dijon and the wholegrain off the boil; season with lemon.
8. Return rabbit to glaze 2 min.

## Plating & Service
Leg and a saddle piece per plate, sauce spooned over, fresh tagliatelle or steamed potatoes beside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken thighs substitute directly (40 min total braise). Add 200 g button mushrooms with the shallots for a fuller garnish.
