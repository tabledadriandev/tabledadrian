# Riz au Lait, Caramel au Beurre Salé

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rice pudding cooked loose and glossy, ribboned with salted butter caramel — bistro nursery food elevated by patience and good vanilla.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Short-grain pudding rice — 180 g
- Whole milk — 1.2 L
- Double cream — 100 ml
- Caster sugar — 70 g
- Vanilla pod — 1
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Caramel: caster sugar 120 g, double cream 100 ml, salted butter 40 g, fleur de sel 2 g

## Method
1. Blanch rice 2 min in boiling water; drain (removes surface starch for a cleaner finish).
2. Bring milk with split vanilla to a simmer; add rice and salt.
3. Cook at the barest simmer, stirring every few minutes, 30–35 min, until the rice is tender and suspended in a loose cream — it thickens as it cools, so stop early.
4. Stir in sugar and the 100 ml cream; cook 3 min more. Remove the pod.
5. Caramel: cook sugar dry to deep amber; whisk in warmed cream (carefully), then butter and fleur de sel; simmer 1 min.
6. Serve the rice warm or chilled, caramel rippled through at the last moment.

## Plating & Service
Low glass bowls, caramel swirled visibly, a pinch of fleur de sel on top.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chilled: loosen with 100 ml extra milk before serving. Dairy-free: oat milk + coconut cream, coconut caramel — different but honest.
