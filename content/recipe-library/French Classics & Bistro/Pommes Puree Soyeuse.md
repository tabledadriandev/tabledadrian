# Pommes Purée Soyeuse

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The haute-cuisine mash — riced twice, mounted with a shameless ratio of cold butter, sieved to silk; the side that upstages the main.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Ratte or Yukon Gold potatoes, even-sized — 1 kg
- Cold unsalted butter, diced — 250 g
- Whole milk — 150 ml
- Fine salt — 12 g
- White pepper — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Simmer potatoes whole, in their skins, in well-salted water 25–30 min until a knife slides through.
2. Drain; peel while hot, holding them in a cloth.
3. Pass through a ricer twice while still steaming.
4. Return to a heavy pan over low heat; dry the purée 2 min, stirring.
5. Beat in the cold butter a handful at a time — the temperature shock creates the emulsion.
6. Heat the milk to 80 °C; beat in gradually to your preferred looseness.
7. Season; for full silk, force through a fine drum sieve (tamis) with a scraper.
8. Hold in a bain-marie up to 1 h, surface dotted with butter.

## Plating & Service
A tight quenelle or a low pool under braised meat — it should slump slowly, never stand stiff.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Civilian ratio: 150 g butter. Olive-oil purée (dairy-free): 120 ml fruity oil and 100 ml potato water instead of butter/milk.
