# Carré d'Agneau en Croûte d'Herbes

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rack of lamb under a green persillade crust, glued with Dijon — a 30-minute main that plates like fine dining every single time.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Racks of lamb, French-trimmed — 2 (8 bones each, ~700 g each)
- Fine salt — 10 g; black pepper 2 g
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Dijon mustard — 40 g
- Fresh breadcrumbs — 90 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 30 g
- Mint — 10 g
- Thyme leaves — 4 g
- Garlic — 8 g
- Unsalted butter, melted — 50 g
- Brown lamb jus/stock, reduced — 150 ml

## Method
1. Salt racks 1 h ahead. Oven to 200 °C.
2. Blitz breadcrumbs, parsley, mint, thyme, garlic and a pinch of salt to a vivid green crumb; mix with melted butter.
3. Sear racks fat-side down in oil until deeply golden, 4–5 min; sear the ends. Cool 5 min.
4. Brush the fat side generously with Dijon; press on the herb crumb firmly.
5. Roast crumb-up 12–16 min to a core of 52–54 °C for rosé.
6. Rest 10 min on a rack.
7. Warm the jus; carve between the bones into double or single chops.

## Plating & Service
Three cutlets propped against each other, jus pooled beside, never over the crust; ratatouille or flageolets alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: use GF panko. Provençal: add 20 g black olive to the crumb; swap mint for basil.
