# Carottes Vichy Glacées

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Carrots glazed 'à blanc' in butter, sugar and their own liquor until mirror-shiny — the technique that teaches glazing for every vegetable after.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Carrots, peeled, 5 mm rounds (or turned barrels) — 600 g
- Unsalted butter — 50 g
- Caster sugar — 12 g
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Water (sparkling, in true Vichy spirit) — to barely cover (~300 ml)
- Lemon juice — 8 ml
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 8 g
- Black pepper — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Lay carrots in a single crowded layer in a wide sauté pan.
2. Add butter, sugar, salt and water to barely cover; press on a paper cartouche with a small hole.
3. Simmer briskly 12–15 min; the water evaporates as the carrots cook.
4. When the liquid is nearly gone, remove the cartouche and roll the carrots in the forming glaze.
5. Cook 2–3 min more, tossing, until each round is lacquered and tender with slight resistance.
6. Brighten with lemon; season; parsley at the end.

## Plating & Service
Shingled or tumbled beside braises and roasts — each piece should shine individually.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Glaze 'à brun': push 2 min further to caramel for roasted meats. Same method: turnips, pearl onions, radishes. Vegan: olive oil + 5 g extra sugar.
