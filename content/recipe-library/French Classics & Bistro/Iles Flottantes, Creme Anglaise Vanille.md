# Îles Flottantes, Crème Anglaise Vanille

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Poached meringue islands adrift on vanilla anglaise under caramel threads — the grandmother dessert that still stops a dining salon.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Crème anglaise: whole milk — 500 ml
- Vanilla pod — 1
- Egg yolks — 6 (120 g)
- Caster sugar — 80 g
- Meringue: egg whites — 6 (180 g)
- Caster sugar — 100 g
- Fine salt — 1 g
- Caramel: caster sugar — 100 g; water 30 ml
- Toasted flaked almonds — 30 g

## Method
1. Anglaise: infuse split vanilla in milk at 85 °C, 15 min. Whisk yolks with 80 g sugar; temper with the milk; return and stir to 83 °C — nappe stage. Strain; chill over ice.
2. Whip whites with salt to soft peaks; rain in 100 g sugar; whip to glossy, firm-but-supple peaks.
3. Poach: barely simmering water (or milk) at 85 °C — scoop large quenelles, poach 90 sec per side. They swell, then settle.
4. Drain the islands on a cloth; chill 30 min.
5. Caramel: cook sugar and water to amber; cool 1 min until it ribbons off a spoon.
6. Pour anglaise into shallow bowls; float an island on each.
7. Thread caramel over in fast zigzags; scatter almonds. Serve within the hour.

## Plating & Service
Wide chilled bowls, caramel spun at the last minute so it stays crisp against the soft island.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Microwave cheat: cook meringue in ramekins 20 sec at 600 W. Nut-free: praline-free, use candied citrus.
