# Petits Pots de Crème au Café

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Silky baked coffee custards in little lidded pots — the make-ahead dessert that holds three days and always tastes like a Paris brasserie.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Whole milk — 350 ml
- Double cream — 250 ml
- Coffee beans, crushed — 40 g
- Espresso — 30 ml
- Egg yolks — 6 (120 g)
- Whole egg — 1 (55 g)
- Caster sugar — 90 g
- Fine salt — 1 g
- Dark chocolate, grated — 15 g

## Method
1. Bring milk, cream and crushed beans to 85 °C; infuse 30 min; strain.
2. Whisk yolks, egg, sugar and salt without aerating.
3. Rewarm the coffee cream with the espresso; pour onto the eggs, whisking; strain twice; skim the foam.
4. Fill six 120 ml pots or ramekins.
5. Bake in a bain-marie at 130 °C for 35–40 min until set with a slight central tremble (core 82 °C).
6. Chill 4 h minimum.
7. Finish with a whisper of grated chocolate.

## Plating & Service
In their pots on a saucer with a tiny spoon — dense, cold, and gone in six bites.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vanilla or chocolate (80 g melted 70%) versions from the same base. Decaf beans work identically for late charters.
