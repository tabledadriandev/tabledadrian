# Omelette aux Fines Herbes, Roulée Baveuse

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The rolled French omelette — pale gold outside, baveuse within, packed with the four fines herbes; the two-minute dish that exposes every chef.

## Ingredients (serves 2, makes 2 omelettes)
- Eggs, very fresh — 6 (about 330 g)
- Unsalted butter — 40 g
- Chives, sliced — 8 g
- Chervil, chopped — 6 g
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 6 g
- Tarragon, chopped — 4 g
- Crème fraîche (optional, for the centre) — 20 g
- Fine salt — 4 g
- White pepper — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Beat 3 eggs per omelette with half the herbs, salt and pepper — 20 strokes, just homogeneous, never frothy.
2. Heat a 20 cm non-stick pan over medium-high; add 20 g butter; when foaming subsides but before browning, pour in the eggs.
3. Stir briskly with a fork (tines up) while shaking the pan, 30–45 sec, until softly scrambled but still glossy-wet.
4. Stop stirring; let the base set 10 sec. Spoon a line of crème fraîche down the centre if using.
5. Tilt the pan; fold the near edge over with the fork, then roll the omelette toward the far edge.
6. Turn out seam-down onto a warm plate; it should be cigar-shaped, unblemished, and baveuse when cut.
7. Repeat; brush with a butter-glossed knife and scatter remaining herbs.

## Plating & Service
One omelette per person with a lemon-dressed leaf salad — serve within a minute of rolling.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 30 g Comté or sautéed mushrooms before rolling. Dairy-free: cook in olive oil, skip the crème fraîche.
