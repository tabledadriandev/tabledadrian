# Confit de Canard, Pommes Sarladaises

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Périgord double act — duck legs cured and slow-poached in fat until spoon-tender, skin crisped to order over garlicky duck-fat potatoes.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Duck legs — 4 (about 300 g each)
- Coarse sea salt — 18 g
- Garlic, crushed — 20 g (plus 10 g for potatoes)
- Thyme — 8 sprigs
- Bay leaves — 3
- Black peppercorns, crushed — 3 g
- Duck fat — 800 g
- Waxy potatoes — 800 g
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 12 g
- Fine salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Rub legs with coarse salt, 20 g garlic, thyme, bay and peppercorns; cure 18–24 h, chilled.
2. Rinse and dry thoroughly. Submerge in melted duck fat.
3. Cook at 120 °C oven (fat ~90 °C) for 2.5–3 h until a skewer meets no resistance at the joint.
4. Cool in the fat; store submerged (keeps 3 weeks) or proceed.
5. Potatoes: peel, slice 4 mm, rinse, dry. Sauté in 60 g of the confit fat over medium heat 20–25 min, turning gently, until golden and tender; add 10 g garlic and parsley for the last 2 min; season.
6. Crisp the legs skin-down in a dry ovenproof pan, then 12 min at 220 °C until the skin crackles; core 75 °C+.

## Plating & Service
Leg propped on a nest of sarladaise potatoes, dressed bitter leaves alongside to cut the richness.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Same cure works for goose or turkey legs (add 1 h). Serve shredded in a salad with orange and frisée as a lighter lunch.
