# Thon Basquaise, Piperade & Espelette

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Seared tuna over slow-stewed peppers, onion and tomato — the Basque coast's marmitako spirit, plated with a rosy centre.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Tuna loin steaks, 3 cm thick — 4 (180 g each)
- Red peppers, sliced — 300 g
- Green peppers, sliced — 200 g
- Onion, sliced — 200 g
- Garlic, sliced — 12 g
- Ripe tomatoes, chopped — 400 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- Sherry vinegar — 15 ml
- Espelette pepper — 3 g
- Thyme — 3 sprigs
- Bay leaf — 1
- Fine salt — 9 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 8 g

## Method
1. Piperade: sweat onion and garlic in 40 ml oil, 5 min; add peppers, thyme, bay, 4 g salt; cook covered 10 min.
2. Add tomatoes and 2 g Espelette; simmer uncovered 15–18 min to a thick, glossy stew. Finish with vinegar.
3. Bring tuna to room temperature 20 min; season with salt and remaining Espelette.
4. Heat remaining oil in a heavy pan until near-smoking; sear tuna 90 sec per side for a rare-to-medium-rare centre (core 38–40 °C).
5. Rest tuna 2 min; slice thickly against the grain.
6. Bed of piperade, tuna slices on top, parsley and a thread of raw oil.

## Plating & Service
Serve slightly rustic in shallow bowls; steamed Basque-style rice or crushed potatoes on the side.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cook the tuna through (55 °C) for cautious guests — the piperade keeps it moist. Swap tuna for swordfish or bonito.
