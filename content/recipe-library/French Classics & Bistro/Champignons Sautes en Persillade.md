# Champignons Sautés en Persillade

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Mushrooms seared hard in a crowd-free pan, hit with garlic-parsley persillade in the final minute — the bistro side that works beside everything.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Mixed mushrooms (chestnut, oyster, girolles), torn or thick-cut — 700 g
- Olive oil — 50 ml
- Garlic, finely minced — 12 g
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 20 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Heat half the oil in your widest pan until shimmering.
2. Add half the mushrooms in one layer; DO NOT move them for 2 min — colour needs contact.
3. Toss; cook 3 min more until golden and squeaky-edged. Season at the END (salt early = steamed mushrooms). Remove; repeat with the second batch.
4. Return all mushrooms; make a space, add a splash of oil and the garlic; cook 45 sec until fragrant, not coloured.
5. Toss through with parsley — the persillade moment.
6. Finish with lemon, salt and pepper.

## Plating & Service
Straight from pan to plate beside steak, roast chicken or folded into an omelette.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Finish with 30 g butter for beurre-persillade richness. Girolles-only version with 20 ml vin jaune is a main event.
