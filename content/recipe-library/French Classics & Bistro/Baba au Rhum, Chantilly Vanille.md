# Baba au Rhum, Chantilly Vanille

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Feather-light yeasted babas drowned to order in warm rum syrup — dry cake to dripping sponge in ninety seconds, finished tableside with the bottle.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Strong flour — 250 g
- Fresh yeast — 12 g (or 5 g instant)
- Whole milk, warm — 60 ml
- Eggs — 4 (220 g)
- Caster sugar — 25 g
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Unsalted butter, very soft — 90 g
- Syrup: water 1 L, caster sugar 450 g, orange zest 2 strips, lemon zest 2 strips, vanilla pod 1
- Dark rum — 150 ml (plus more at table)
- Chantilly: double cream 300 ml, icing sugar 25 g, vanilla 1/2 pod
- Apricot jam (glaze) — 60 g

## Method
1. Dissolve yeast in warm milk. Mix flour, sugar, salt, eggs and the yeast milk; beat with a dough hook 8 min until elastic and slapping the bowl.
2. Beat in the soft butter in three additions until glossy.
3. Pipe into 8 buttered baba or savarin moulds, half full; prove 40 min until doming over the rims.
4. Bake at 180 °C for 18–20 min until deep gold; unmould onto a rack. Dry babas 24 h uncovered (they drink better stale).
5. Syrup: boil water, sugar, zests and vanilla 3 min; cool to 60 °C; add 150 ml rum.
6. Submerge each baba 60–90 sec per side until swollen and heavy; drain on a rack.
7. Brush with warmed apricot jam.
8. Whip the chantilly to soft peaks.

## Plating & Service
Baba split or whole with a chantilly rosette; present the rum bottle and anoint each at the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Savarin: one large ring, centre filled with chantilly and fruit. Alcohol-free: syrup with orange juice + vanilla, no rum.
