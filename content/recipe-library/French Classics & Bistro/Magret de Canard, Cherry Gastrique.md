# Magret de Canard, Cherry Gastrique

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Cold-pan magret rendered glass-crisp, sliced rosy against a sour-cherry gastrique — Gascony by way of a June cherry market.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Duck breasts (magrets) — 2 large (about 400 g each)
- Fine salt — 8 g
- Black pepper — 1.5 g
- Caster sugar — 30 g
- Red wine vinegar — 40 ml
- Red wine — 100 ml
- Brown stock — 200 ml
- Cherries, pitted, halved — 250 g
- Thyme — 3 sprigs
- Cold butter (optional, omit for dairy-free) — 0 g
- Star anise — 1

## Method
1. Score magret skin in a 1 cm diamond pattern without cutting flesh; season both sides.
2. Lay skin-down in a COLD dry pan; set over medium-low heat. Render 12–15 min, pouring off fat twice, until the skin is thin and deeply crisp.
3. Flip; cook flesh side 3–4 min to a core of 54 °C (rosé). Rest 8 min on a rack, skin up.
4. Gastrique: melt sugar to amber; stop with vinegar; add wine, reduce by half.
5. Add stock, star anise and thyme; reduce to a light glaze.
6. Add cherries; simmer 2 min so they soften but hold shape. Season; discard anise.
7. Slice magret 1 cm thick; catch resting juices into the sauce.

## Plating & Service
Fan slices skin-up, cherries and glaze spooned across, fleur de sel on the fat.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Winter: swap cherries for figs or prunes soaked in Armagnac. The rendered fat is gold — keep it for sarladaise potatoes.
