# Rillettes de Saumon aux Deux Façons

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Poached fresh salmon folded with smoked salmon and lemony butter — two textures in one pot, ready in 30 minutes for apéro hour.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Fresh salmon fillet, skinless — 400 g
- Smoked salmon, chopped — 150 g
- Unsalted butter, soft — 80 g
- Crème fraîche — 60 g
- Lemon zest — 4 g
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Chives, sliced — 15 g
- Dill, chopped — 8 g
- Espelette pepper — 1 g
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Court-bouillon: water 1 L, white wine 100 ml, bay 1, peppercorns 3 g

## Method
1. Bring court-bouillon to 80 °C; slide in the fresh salmon and poach 8–10 min to a core of 50 °C. Drain and cool.
2. Beat butter with crème fraîche, lemon zest, juice, Espelette and salt.
3. Flake the poached salmon coarsely into the butter mixture — keep visible flakes.
4. Fold in smoked salmon, chives and dill; do not overwork.
5. Pack into a jar or terrine, smooth the top, chill 2 h minimum.
6. Pull from the fridge 20 min before serving.

## Plating & Service
Serve in the jar with toasted baguette, radishes and lemon; or quenelle onto blinis for canapés.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free: use whipped olive oil emulsion instead of butter/crème fraîche. Swap salmon for hot-smoked trout for a stronger cure note.
