# Filets de Sole Bonne Femme

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Poached sole in a mushroom-wine velouté glazed under the salamander — the Escoffier-era classic every serious chef should own.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Sole fillets, skinned — 8 (about 640 g)
- Fish stock — 300 ml
- Dry white wine — 150 ml
- Shallots, minced — 60 g
- Button mushrooms, sliced — 200 g
- Unsalted butter — 90 g
- Double cream — 150 ml
- Egg yolk — 1 (20 g)
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 8 g
- Fine salt — 6 g
- White pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Butter a sauté pan; scatter shallots and mushrooms. Fold fillets in half, skin-side in, and lay on top; season.
2. Add wine and stock to come halfway up; press on a buttered paper cartouche.
3. Bring to 85 °C on the stove, then poach 5–6 min until the fillets are just opaque (core 50 °C). Lift to a warm dish, cover.
4. Reduce the cooking liquor with the mushrooms by two-thirds over high heat.
5. Add 100 ml cream; reduce to a light coating consistency; season with salt, pepper, lemon.
6. Off heat, beat remaining 50 ml cream with the yolk; whisk in, then mount with 60 g cold butter. Do not reboil.
7. Coat the fillets with sauce and glaze under a hot grill (250 °C) for 60–90 sec until lightly gilded. Parsley over.

## Plating & Service
Two fillets per plate glazed under the sauce, with steamed potatoes turned small or plain rice.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Lighter: skip the glaze and yolk, serve as a simple mushroom cream. Works equally with lemon sole or plaice.
