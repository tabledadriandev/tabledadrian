# Gratin Dauphinois à l'Ail

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> No cheese, no eggs — just potatoes lacquered in garlicky cream that reduces in the oven to a custard; the Dauphiné's quiet masterpiece.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Waxy-floury potatoes (Agria/Desiree) — 1.5 kg
- Double cream — 500 ml
- Whole milk — 250 ml
- Garlic — 15 g (10 g simmered, 1 clove for the dish)
- Unsalted butter — 30 g
- Fine salt — 12 g
- White pepper — 1 g
- Nutmeg — 0.6 g
- Bay leaf — 1
- Thyme — 2 sprigs

## Method
1. Rub a gratin dish with a cut garlic clove; butter it thickly.
2. Peel potatoes; slice 3 mm on a mandoline. DO NOT rinse — the starch binds the cream.
3. Warm cream, milk, sliced garlic, bay, thyme, salt, pepper and nutmeg to 85 °C; infuse 10 min; remove bay and thyme.
4. Layer potatoes flat in the dish, pouring warm cream between layers; finish with cream just reaching the top layer.
5. Press down; bake at 150 °C for 75 min until a knife meets no resistance.
6. Raise to 200 °C for 10–15 min to bronze the top.
7. Rest 15 min — it carves into neat spoonfuls instead of collapsing.

## Plating & Service
Spoon at the table beside roast lamb or bavette; or press overnight, cut into cylinders and re-crisp for plated service.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Savoyard heresy (delicious): 100 g Beaufort between layers. Lighter: half milk, half cream — bake 15 min longer.
