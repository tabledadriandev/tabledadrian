# Chicken Liver Parfait, Sauternes Gelée

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Passed twice for a texture like set silk, capped with a thin sweet-wine jelly — reads as luxury for the cost of chicken livers.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Chicken livers, trimmed — 500 g
- Whole milk (for soaking) — 300 ml
- Shallots, sliced — 100 g
- Garlic — 8 g
- Port — 100 ml
- Madeira — 50 ml
- Brandy — 30 ml
- Eggs — 3 (165 g)
- Unsalted butter, melted, 40 °C — 350 g
- Fine salt — 12 g
- Pink cure #1 (optional, for colour) — 1 g
- Sauternes — 150 ml
- Gold gelatine leaves — 3 (6 g)
- White pepper — 1.5 g

## Method
1. Soak livers in milk 2 h; drain and pat dry.
2. Reduce shallots, garlic, Port, Madeira and brandy to a wet glaze (about 50 g); cool.
3. Blend livers, reduction, eggs, salt, cure and pepper 90 sec; with motor running, stream in melted butter.
4. Pass twice through a fine chinois into a 1 L terrine or 10 small jars.
5. Cook in a bain-marie at 110 °C oven to a core of 64 °C (35–40 min); it should barely tremble.
6. Chill 4 h. Bloom gelatine, warm Sauternes to 60 °C, dissolve gelatine, cool to 20 °C and pour a 3 mm layer over each parfait; set 1 h.
7. Rest 24 h for flavour to bloom.

## Plating & Service
Serve in the jar with toasted brioche and a few sea salt flakes on top of the jelly.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap Sauternes jelly for a tart cherry gelée. Serve with GF toast for coeliac guests — the parfait itself is gluten-free.
