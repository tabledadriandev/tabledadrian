# Garbure Béarnaise au Confit

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Béarn's ham-and-cabbage pot thickened only by its own vegetables, finished with confit duck — a soup that eats like a feast.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Smoked ham hock — 1 (about 1 kg)
- Duck confit legs — 3
- Water — 3 L
- White beans (soaked) — 300 g
- Savoy cabbage, ribbons — 700 g
- Potatoes, chunks — 500 g
- Carrots, thick rounds — 300 g
- Turnip, chunks — 250 g
- Leek, sliced — 200 g
- Onion — 150 g
- Garlic — 20 g
- Thyme — 4 sprigs; bay 2
- Espelette pepper — 2 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Fine salt — to adjust (hock is salty)

## Method
1. Simmer the hock in water with onion, garlic, thyme and bay, skimming, 1.5 h.
2. Add beans; simmer 40 min.
3. Add carrots, turnip, leek and potatoes; simmer 20 min.
4. Add cabbage; simmer 15 min more, until everything is tender and the broth slightly thickened.
5. Lift the hock; shred the meat, discard skin and bone; return meat to the pot.
6. Warm confit legs in a dry pan, skin down, 8 min until crisp; pull into large pieces.
7. Season the garbure with Espelette and pepper; salt only if needed.
8. Serve with confit pieces on top of each bowl.

## Plating & Service
Deep bowls, confit crowning the vegetables; tradition finishes with 'faire chabrot' — a splash of red wine in the last spoonfuls.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Leaner: skip confit, keep just the hock. Vegetarian garbure works with smoked paprika, white miso and olive oil.
