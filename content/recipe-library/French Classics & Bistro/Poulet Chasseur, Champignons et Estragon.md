# Poulet Chasseur, Champignons et Estragon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Hunter's chicken done properly — thighs braised with mushrooms, tomato, white wine and a tarragon finish; weeknight food with old-school depth.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chicken thighs, bone-in, skin-on — 8 (about 1.4 kg)
- Plain flour — 20 g
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Unsalted butter — 40 g
- Button and chestnut mushrooms, quartered — 350 g
- Shallots, sliced — 100 g
- Garlic, minced — 8 g
- Dry white wine — 200 ml
- Brandy — 20 ml
- Brown chicken stock — 300 ml
- Tomatoes, chopped — 250 g
- Tomato paste — 20 g
- Tarragon — 10 g; parsley 8 g
- Fine salt — 10 g; black pepper 2 g

## Method
1. Season thighs, dust with flour; brown skin-down in oil, 8 min, then flip 3 min; remove.
2. Sauté mushrooms in the butter until golden, 6 min; remove half for garnish.
3. Sweat shallots and garlic 3 min; add tomato paste, 1 min; deglaze with brandy and wine, reduce by half.
4. Add stock and tomatoes; return chicken skin-up and any juices.
5. Simmer uncovered (or 180 °C oven) 30–35 min until the sauce is glossy and thighs reach 82 °C.
6. Stir in reserved mushrooms and most of the tarragon; check seasoning.
7. Rest 5 min; shower with parsley and remaining tarragon.

## Plating & Service
Two thighs per plate over pommes purée or buttered noodles, sauce generously over.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: skip the flour dust; reduce the sauce a little longer. Rabbit legs take identically well to this treatment (braise 45 min).
