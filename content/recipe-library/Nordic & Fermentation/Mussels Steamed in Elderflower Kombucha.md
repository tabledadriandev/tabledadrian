# Mussels Steamed in Elderflower Kombucha

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Kombucha replaces white wine: floral acidity, gentle fizz, zero alcohol — the broth is the best part.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Mussels, cleaned, debearded — 1500 g
- Elderflower kombucha — 250 ml
- Shallots, sliced — 60 g
- Celery, fine dice — 40 g
- Butter — 50 g
- Garlic, sliced — 8 g
- Crème fraîche — 60 g
- Dill, chopped — 10 g
- Lemon zest — 2 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. SAFETY: discard any mussel that stays open when tapped before cooking, and any that stays closed after.
2. Sweat shallot, celery and garlic in half the butter over medium heat, 3 minutes, no colour.
3. Raise heat; add mussels and kombucha; lid on.
4. Steam 3–4 minutes, shaking once, until just opened.
5. Lift mussels out; reduce the broth 2 minutes.
6. Whisk in crème fraîche, remaining butter, dill, zest and pepper; do not boil hard after the cream.
7. Pour over the mussels.

## Plating & Service
Deep warm bowls, broth flooded over, dill on top — bread mandatory, spoons non-negotiable.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free: finish with 30 ml olive oil instead of cream; apple-verbena kombucha works when elderflower is out.
