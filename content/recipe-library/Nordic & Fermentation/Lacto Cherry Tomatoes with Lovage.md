# Lacto Cherry Tomatoes with Lovage

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Whole tomatoes turn into bursting sour bonbons and the drained liquid is a crystal-clear fermented tomato water for chilled sauces.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Cherry tomatoes, ripe, pricked once with a pin — 1000 g
- Fine sea salt (non-iodised) — 20 g
- Lovage leaves — 3 g
- Garlic clove, bruised — 5 g
- For tomato-water dressing: strained ferment liquid — 100 ml
- Olive oil — 50 ml
- White balsamic — 5 ml
- Espelette pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. SAFETY: 2% salt; ferment at 21–24°C; total fermentation time 3–4 days (tomatoes move fast in summer); refrigerate immediately at target flavour.
2. Toss pricked tomatoes gently with salt, lovage and garlic.
3. Vacuum-seal at 99% in one layer, or submerge in a jar under a brine-filled bag.
4. Ferment at 21–24°C; burp daily. The bag will inflate quickly by day 2.
5. Taste at day 3: tomatoes should fizz gently on the tongue, sour-savoury; pH 4.0 or below. Stop by day 4.
6. Chill to 2–4°C; keeps 3 weeks. Strain and keep the pale-red tomato water.
7. Whisk tomato water with oil, balsamic and Espelette for a chilled dressing.

## Plating & Service
Serve the cold burst tomatoes on burrata or grilled bread; spoon tomato-water dressing around raw scampi or poached lobster.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Omit garlic for low-FODMAP service; add 2 g fennel seed for an anise register that flatters shellfish.
