# Apple and Lemon Verbena Kombucha

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Crisp orchard acidity with a citrus-herbal top note — the crowd-pleaser of the kombucha fridge.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Filtered water — 1800 ml
- Caster sugar — 130 g
- Green tea leaves — 12 g
- Kombucha SCOBY with 200 ml starter — 1
- Fresh apple juice, cloudy — 200 ml
- Lemon verbena leaves, fresh — 6 g
- Sterilised 3 L jar + swing-top bottles — 1 set

## Method
1. SAFETY: keep 10% starter, pH under 4.5 at the start; ferment 21–26°C; total fermentation time 9–13 days; refrigerate bottles once carbonated and vent daily if held longer than a week.
2. Boil water, dissolve sugar, steep tea 8 minutes at 80°C; strain and cool below 30°C.
3. Ferment with SCOBY and starter under cloth, 7–9 days at 21–26°C, until pleasantly tart.
4. Remove SCOBY and next-batch starter.
5. Stir in apple juice; add verbena leaves; steep 8 hours, chilled; strain.
6. Bottle with 3 cm headspace; condition 2–4 days at 21°C; refrigerate at 2–4°C.
7. Serve within 3 weeks.

## Plating & Service
Serve over ice with a verbena leaf at lunch; also the poaching liquid of dreams for halved apricots (80°C, 8 minutes).

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pear juice + rosemary is the winter sibling; keep residual sugar in mind for diabetic guests.
