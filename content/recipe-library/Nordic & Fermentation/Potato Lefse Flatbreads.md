# Potato Lefse Flatbreads

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Soft Norwegian potato flatbreads cooked on a dry pan — the wrap for hot-smoked fish, the blanket for butter and sugar.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Floury potatoes, peeled — 600 g
- Butter, soft — 50 g
- Double cream — 40 ml
- Fine sea salt — 5 g
- Plain flour (plus more for rolling) — 160 g
- Caster sugar (optional, traditional) — 10 g
- Butter for serving — 40 g

## Method
1. Boil potatoes until tender, 20 minutes; drain and steam-dry; rice while hot.
2. Beat in butter, cream, salt (and sugar); cool completely, ideally overnight — warm mash drinks too much flour.
3. Work in the flour briefly until it just forms a dough — overworking toughens it.
4. Divide into 8; roll each paper-thin (2 mm) on a floured cloth.
5. Cook on a dry pan or griddle at high heat, 60–90 seconds per side, until leopard-spotted.
6. Stack under a cloth as you go — steam keeps them supple.
7. Serve warm, or cool and stack with parchment; they freeze well.

## Plating & Service
Roll around hot-smoked char with horseradish skyr and dill; or butter-and-cinnamon-sugar for the children (and the crew).

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: swap flour for a GF blend + 2 g xanthan (note: current tags assume wheat flour — retag if GF); use them as soft tacos for fried fish.
