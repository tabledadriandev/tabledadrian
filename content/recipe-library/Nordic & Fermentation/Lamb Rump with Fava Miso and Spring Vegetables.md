# Lamb Rump with Fava Miso and Spring Vegetables

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Fava miso rubbed into lamb before roasting and whisked into the jus after — one ferment, two jobs, complete dish.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Lamb rumps, fat scored — 2 × 350 g
- Fava bean miso (larder) — 40 g
- Rapeseed oil — 20 ml
- Butter — 40 g
- Lamb or chicken stock — 200 ml
- Peas, podded — 150 g
- Baby carrots, halved — 150 g
- Spring onions, halved — 80 g
- Mint, small leaves — 5 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Rub lamb with half the miso; rest 2 hours at 2–4°C, then wipe mostly off (it burns) and pat dry.
2. Sear fat-side down in oil from a cold pan, rendering 5–6 minutes to crisp; brown all faces.
3. Roast at 140°C to 54°C core, 15–20 minutes; rest 10 minutes to 58°C — blushing pink.
4. Glaze carrots and spring onions in half the butter with a splash of stock, 5 minutes; add peas for the final 2.
5. Jus: deglaze the sear pan with stock; reduce by half; whisk in remaining miso and butter off the heat; sharpen with lemon.
6. Do not re-boil the jus after the miso.
7. Carve lamb against the grain; season the cut faces.

## Plating & Service
Vegetables scattered first, lamb slices over, jus pulled across at the pass, mint last.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Yellow pea miso for a sweeter register; duck fat on the vegetables if the fridge allows.
