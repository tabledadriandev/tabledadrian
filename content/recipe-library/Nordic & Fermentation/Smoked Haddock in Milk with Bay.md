# Smoked Haddock in Milk with Bay

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Home-smoked haddock poached gently in bay milk — comfort at yacht-lunch level, with the smoking done in-house.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Haddock fillets, skin on, pin-boned — 900 g
- Filtered water — 900 ml
- Fine sea salt — 72 g
- Demerara sugar — 25 g
- Oak chips — 50 g
- Whole milk — 600 ml
- Bay leaves — 2 g
- Black peppercorns — 2 g
- Butter — 40 g
- Chives, sliced — 8 g

## Method
1. SAFETY: 8% brine for 45 minutes; smoke then ALWAYS cook through — this is a light smoke, not preservation; poach to 60°C core (haddock flakes at lower temp than salmon); eat same or next day.
2. Brine fillets 45 minutes at 2–4°C; rinse, pat dry; fridge-dry 2 hours for the pellicle.
3. Smoke at 60–70°C over oak for 25–30 minutes — coloured and perfumed but not fully cooked.
4. Warm milk with bay and peppercorns to 80°C; rest 10 minutes.
5. Slide in haddock; poach at 70–75°C for 8–10 minutes to 60°C core.
6. Lift fish; reduce 200 ml of the poaching milk by half, mount with butter.
7. Nap the fish; shower with chives.

## Plating & Service
Serve on crushed dill potatoes with a poached egg for lunch; the bay milk sauce is the point — spoon generously.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pollock is a budget stand-in; oat cream replaces milk for dairy-free (adjust tags).
