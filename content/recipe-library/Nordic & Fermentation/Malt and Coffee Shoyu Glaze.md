# Malt and Coffee Shoyu Glaze

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> House shoyu married to coffee and dark malt into a lacquer for short ribs, beets and barbecue nights on the aft deck.

## Ingredients (serves 20)
- House shoyu (or good soy sauce) — 300 ml
- Freshly brewed strong coffee — 150 ml
- Barley malt syrup — 120 g
- Apple juice — 150 ml
- Cider vinegar — 40 ml
- Black peppercorns, cracked — 3 g
- Star anise — 2 g
- Cold butter or vegan butter (to mount) — 40 g

## Method
1. This is a finished sauce built on a long ferment — no live fermentation step; keep refrigerated at 2–4°C after making and use within 3 weeks.
2. Combine shoyu, coffee, malt syrup, apple juice, vinegar and spices in a saucepan.
3. Simmer at a bare bubble, 90–95°C, for 20–25 minutes until reduced to about 350 ml and coating the back of a spoon.
4. Strain out spices.
5. For glazing, brush onto proteins or roots in the final 5 minutes of cooking at 180–200°C, in 2–3 coats.
6. For a pan sauce, warm 100 ml and mount with cold butter off the heat.
7. Store in a squeeze bottle, chilled.

## Plating & Service
Lacquer short ribs or glazed beetroot until mirror-shiny; a teaspoon under seared duck sharpens the whole plate.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap malt syrup for maple to reduce gluten (soy remains); add 10 g fermented chilli paste for a spicier deck-barbecue version.
