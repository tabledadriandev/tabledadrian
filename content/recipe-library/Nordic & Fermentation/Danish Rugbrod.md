# Danish Rugbrod

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The dense, seeded rye brick that underpins every smørrebrød — 24 hours of patience, a week of eating.

## Ingredients (serves 16)
- Active rye sourdough starter — 200 g
- Wholegrain rye flour — 400 g
- Cracked rye (or rye chops) — 250 g
- Water, warm — 500 ml
- Fine sea salt — 14 g
- Barley malt syrup — 30 g
- Sunflower seeds — 80 g
- Flax seeds — 60 g
- Pumpkin seeds — 60 g
- Sesame seeds (topping) — 15 g
- Dark beer — 100 ml

## Method
1. Night before: soak cracked rye and all seeds (except sesame) in the beer plus 200 ml of the water.
2. Morning: mix starter, rye flour, remaining 300 ml water, salt and malt syrup into the soaker — a thick, sticky paste, not a kneadable dough.
3. Ferment in the bowl, covered, 3–4 hours at 24–26°C until visibly aerated. Total fermentation time including the overnight soaker: about 16–20 hours.
4. Load into a greased 2.5 L loaf tin (two-thirds full); slick the top with wet hands; scatter sesame.
5. Proof 2–3 hours at 24–26°C until small fissures open on the surface.
6. Bake at 180°C for 80–90 minutes to 96–98°C core.
7. Cool completely, then — critically — wrap and rest 24 hours before slicing (the crumb sets).
8. Keeps 7 days wrapped; slices freeze perfectly.

## Plating & Service
Slice 8 mm thin for smørrebrød; butter edge-to-edge, always.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap beer for coffee for a darker note; seeds are flexible — keep the total at 200 g.
