# Rhubarb Kombucha

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Pink, mouth-watering and aggressively refreshing — doubles as a mother sauce for spring desserts.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Filtered water — 1800 ml
- Caster sugar — 140 g
- White tea leaves — 12 g
- Kombucha SCOBY with 200 ml starter — 1
- Rhubarb stalks, chopped — 300 g
- Extra sugar (for rhubarb syrup) — 60 g
- Sterilised 3 L jar + swing-top bottles — 1 set

## Method
1. SAFETY: 10% starter, pH below 4.5 from day one; ferment 21–26°C; total fermentation time 10–14 days; never use rhubarb leaves (oxalic acid); chill bottles once fizzy.
2. Brew sweet tea (boil water, dissolve 140 g sugar, steep tea 8 minutes at 80°C); cool below 30°C.
3. Primary ferment with SCOBY under cloth, 7–10 days at 21–26°C.
4. Meanwhile make the syrup: barely simmer rhubarb with 60 g sugar and 60 ml water at 90°C for 12–15 minutes; strain, keeping the pink liquor.
5. Remove SCOBY and starter. Blend kombucha with 250 ml rhubarb liquor.
6. Bottle, 3 cm headspace; condition 2–4 days at 21°C; refrigerate at 2–4°C.
7. Serve within 3 weeks, well chilled.

## Plating & Service
A flute of it beside skyr panna cotta is a complete dessert pairing; reduce leftovers into a granita base (freeze, scrape hourly).

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The strained poached rhubarb becomes compote for breakfast; strawberry tops (clean) can join the syrup for depth.
