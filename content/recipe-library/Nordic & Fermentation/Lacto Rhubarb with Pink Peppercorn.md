# Lacto Rhubarb with Pink Peppercorn

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rhubarb's acidity doubled by fermentation — batons stay blush-pink and snappy, made for oily fish and pork.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Rhubarb stalks, forced or field, cut 6 cm batons — 1000 g
- Fine sea salt (non-iodised) — 20 g
- Pink peppercorns, cracked — 2 g
- Vanilla pod, split (optional) — 1 g
- For service: honey or agave — 15 g
- For service: olive oil — 15 ml
- For service: baby red-vein sorrel — 4 g

## Method
1. SAFETY: 2% salt; ferment at 20–23°C; total fermentation time 3–4 days only — rhubarb softens fast; refrigerate promptly. Never ferment or eat rhubarb leaves (oxalic acid).
2. Toss batons with salt, pink pepper and vanilla.
3. Vacuum-seal flat at 99% or weight under brine bag in a jar.
4. Ferment at 20–23°C; burp on day 2.
5. Stop at day 3–4 while batons still snap; pH 3.8 or below (rhubarb starts acidic and drops quickly).
6. Chill to 2–4°C; use within 3 weeks. Reserve the vivid pink juice.
7. Slice on the bias, dress lightly with honey and oil for service.

## Plating & Service
Arrange three glossy batons over grilled mackerel or seared duck; the pink juice makes a striking mignonette for oysters.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skip honey and it is strictly vegan/low-sugar; the juice + 50% sugar syrup makes a brilliant sorbet base.
