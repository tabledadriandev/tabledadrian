# Juniper and Beetroot Gravlax

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A crimson-rimmed cure that looks spectacular carved tableside — beet stains the edge, juniper does the talking.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Salmon fillet, skin on, pin-boned, sushi-grade — 1000 g
- Coarse sea salt — 80 g
- Caster sugar — 60 g
- Raw beetroot, grated — 250 g
- Juniper berries, crushed — 6 g
- Black peppercorns, crushed — 4 g
- Lemon zest — 4 g
- Dill, chopped — 30 g
- Aquavit or gin — 30 ml

## Method
1. SAFETY: use previously frozen (-20°C for 24 h minimum) or certified sashimi-grade salmon to eliminate parasites; cure refrigerated at 2–4°C; total cure time 36–48 hours; cured fish keeps 5 days chilled.
2. Mix salt, sugar, juniper, pepper and zest. This is an 8% salt / 6% sugar cure — firm but not leathery.
3. Rub the flesh with aquavit. Pack the cure evenly over the flesh side; blanket with grated beetroot and half the dill.
4. Wrap tightly in film on a tray; top with a light 1 kg weight.
5. Cure at 2–4°C for 36–48 hours, flipping once at halfway; thicker than 3 cm goes the full 48.
6. Unwrap, rinse very briefly under cold water, pat completely dry.
7. Press remaining fresh dill onto the crimson edge. Rest uncovered in the fridge 2 hours to set the pellicle.
8. Carve in thin diagonal slices off the skin.

## Plating & Service
Carve tableside onto chilled plates with mustard-dill sauce, pickled shallot rings and rye crisps.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap beet for grated horseradish for a white-heat version; trout cures the same in 24–30 hours.
