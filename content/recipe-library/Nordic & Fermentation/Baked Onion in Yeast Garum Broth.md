# Baked Onion in Yeast Garum Broth

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A whole onion, baked to collapse, floating in a clear garum broth — French onion soup rebuilt as Nordic minimalism.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Onions, medium, skin on — 4 (approx. 600 g)
- Rapeseed oil — 20 ml
- Vegetable stock, clear — 800 ml
- Yeast garum (larder) — 30 ml
- Apple balsamic or cider vinegar — 10 ml
- Thyme sprigs — 3 g
- Chives, sliced — 8 g
- Black pepper — 1 g
- Flaky salt — 2 g
- Rye croutons (optional) — 60 g

## Method
1. Rub whole onions with oil; bake at 190°C for 60–75 minutes until utterly soft and caramel juices leak from the base.
2. Rest 10 minutes; trim the root, slip off skins, keeping onions whole.
3. Warm stock with thyme to 90°C for 5 minutes; remove thyme.
4. Season the broth with yeast garum and vinegar — it should taste roasted and deep, like it simmered all day.
5. Seat one onion per warmed bowl; press gently so the petals bloom open.
6. Pour broth around at the table.
7. Finish with chives, pepper, flaky salt and croutons if using.

## Plating & Service
The tableside pour is the theatre; a spoon and the collapsing onion do the rest.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Beef garum instead of yeast garum for carnivores; add a slice of melting Comté for a Franco-Nordic bridge.
