# Cep Mushroom Shoyu

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Shoyu brewed with a heavy dried-cep infusion — forest-floor umami that finishes game sauces and vegetarian broths alike.

## Ingredients (serves 40)
- Dried soybeans — 400 g
- Cracked toasted wheat — 400 g
- Koji-kin spores (shoyu strain) — 2 g
- Dried ceps (porcini) — 80 g
- Filtered water — 1400 ml
- Fine sea salt (non-iodised) — 322 g
- Sterilised 4 L crock — 1

## Method
1. SAFETY: 23% salt on the brine water (1400 ml = 322 g, ~15% in mash); koji growth 28–30°C, never above 42°C; total fermentation time 6–10 months at 18–25°C.
2. Soak and steam soybeans (12 h / 60 min); toast and crack wheat; cool to 30°C, mix and inoculate.
3. Grow koji 44–48 hours at 28–30°C, turning at 24 hours.
4. Steep dried ceps in the water at 80°C for 30 minutes; cool fully, leaving mushrooms in.
5. Dissolve salt into the cep infusion; combine with the koji mash and mushrooms in the crock.
6. Stir daily for month one, then weekly; taste from month 6 — soy depth over a wave of roasted mushroom.
7. Press through muslin overnight; pasteurise at 80°C for 10 minutes.
8. Bottle; refrigerate at 2–4°C, 1 year.

## Plating & Service
Five millilitres deepens a venison jus; splash over grilled maitake with brown butter; season mushroom broths without stock reduction.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dried morels give a smokier luxury version; the strained mushrooms blend into an intense duxelles seasoning paste.
