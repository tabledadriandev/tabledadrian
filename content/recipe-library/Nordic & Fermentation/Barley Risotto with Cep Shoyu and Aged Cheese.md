# Barley Risotto with Cep Shoyu and Aged Cheese

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Pearl barley cooked risotto-style, seasoned with mushroom shoyu instead of stock cubes — chewy, savoury, deeply Nordic.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Pearl barley — 300 g
- Butter — 60 g
- Shallots, minced — 80 g
- White wine — 100 ml
- Hot vegetable stock — 1000 ml
- Cep shoyu (larder) — 25 ml
- Aged hard cheese (Västerbotten or Comté), grated — 80 g
- Fermented cep pieces (larder), chopped — 60 g
- Parsley, chopped — 8 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Toast barley in half the butter with shallot, 3 minutes, until it smells like fresh bread.
2. Deglaze with wine; let it vanish.
3. Add hot stock a ladle at a time, stirring often, 35–40 minutes, until the barley is tender with a proud chew.
4. Season with cep shoyu instead of salt — add gradually, tasting.
5. Off heat, beat in remaining butter and the cheese until creamy (mantecatura).
6. Fold through the chopped fermented ceps.
7. Rest 2 minutes; loosen with a splash of stock — it should spread lazily, not stand.

## Plating & Service
Flat bowls, a final grating of cheese, parsley and pepper — spoon-food with backbone.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: olive oil mantecatura + 20 g white miso for the cheese note; add roast squash in autumn.
