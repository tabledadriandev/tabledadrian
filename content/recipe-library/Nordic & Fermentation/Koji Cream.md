# Koji Cream

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rice koji cooked into a silky, dairy-free 'crème anglaise' with natural sweetness — a chameleon base for sauces and desserts.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Fresh rice koji — 400 g
- Filtered water — 600 ml
- Fine sea salt — 4 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Rice flour (only if needed to thicken) — 10 g
- For savoury version: white miso — 20 g
- For sweet version: vanilla seeds — 1 g

## Method
1. SAFETY: this is a cooked saccharification, not a wild ferment — hold precisely at 60°C for 60 minutes (enzymes work 55–62°C; above 70°C they die); afterwards chill below 4°C within 90 minutes and use within 5 days.
2. Blend koji and water 1 minute until sludgy.
3. Transfer to a bain-marie or immersion-circulator bag at 60°C for 60 minutes, stirring every 15 minutes; it sweetens dramatically as starches convert.
4. Blend on high 2 minutes until completely silky; pass through a chinois.
5. Season with salt and lemon. Split the batch: whisk miso into half (savoury), vanilla into the other (sweet).
6. If loose, simmer briefly to 85°C with rice flour slurry, then re-chill.
7. Store at 2–4°C in squeeze bottles.

## Plating & Service
Pool the savoury cream under roast cauliflower or scallops; pipe the sweet version beside rye crumble and lacto berries.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Replace half the water with oat milk for extra body; savoury version may contain soy if miso does — check tags at service.
