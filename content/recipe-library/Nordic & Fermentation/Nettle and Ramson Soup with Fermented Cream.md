# Nettle and Ramson Soup with Fermented Cream

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Foraged spring greens in a silky emerald soup, soured with cultured cream — the taste of the first warm week of April.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Young nettle tops (gloves on), washed — 200 g
- Ramson (wild garlic) leaves — 60 g
- Butter — 40 g
- Leek, sliced — 120 g
- Potato, diced — 150 g
- Vegetable stock — 800 ml
- Crème fraîche or soured cream — 100 g
- Ramson caper brine or lemon juice — 10 ml
- Fine sea salt — 3 g
- Eggs (optional, soft-boiled 6 min 30 s) — 2
- White pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. SAFETY: pick only young nettle tops before flowering; blanching (step 2) removes the sting entirely; identify ramsons by their garlic smell.
2. Blanch nettles 60 seconds in boiling salted water; shock in ice water; squeeze.
3. Sweat leek and potato in butter, 5 minutes, no colour.
4. Add stock; simmer 12 minutes until potato collapses.
5. Add blanched nettles and raw ramsons; blend immediately on high until velvet — do not simmer after (colour dies).
6. Season with salt, white pepper; keep at 75°C.
7. Whisk crème fraîche with the brine; halve the eggs if using.
8. Bowl the soup; swirl the soured cream; seat half an egg in the centre.

## Plating & Service
Deep bowls, cream swirled at the table — the green should be shocking.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Spinach + garlic clone it off-season; vegan with oil-sweated leeks and oat crème.
