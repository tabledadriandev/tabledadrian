# Plum Wine Vinegar

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Deep ruby vinegar with stone-fruit body — the acid backbone for game sauces and beet dishes all winter.

## Ingredients (serves 30)
- Ripe dark plums, stoned, chopped — 1000 g
- Filtered water — 1000 ml
- Caster sugar — 140 g
- Champagne yeast (optional, for reliability) — 1 g
- Live vinegar mother or unpasteurised cider vinegar — 100 ml
- Sterilised 3 L jar, muslin, band — 1

## Method
1. SAFETY: aerobic ferment under cloth only; 20–26°C; total fermentation time 7–9 weeks; keep fruit submerged during stage one to prevent mould; finished acidity should bite like wine vinegar before shelving.
2. Dissolve sugar in water; add plums (and yeast if using) to the jar.
3. Cloth cover; stir twice daily for 12–14 days of alcoholic fermentation — it should smell like young plum wine.
4. Strain, pressing lightly. Add the vinegar mother to the liquid.
5. Cloth cover again; rest at 20–26°C, stirring weekly, 5–7 weeks.
6. Taste weekly from week 4: stop when vividly sour with a plum-skin tannic grip.
7. Bottle; optionally pasteurise at 70°C for 10 minutes. Keeps 1 year+.

## Plating & Service
Reduce with a spoon of honey into a gastrique for duck; sharpen braised red cabbage; splash into sparkling water as a shrub.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cherries make a luxe variant; use the spent plum pulp in chutney the same day.
