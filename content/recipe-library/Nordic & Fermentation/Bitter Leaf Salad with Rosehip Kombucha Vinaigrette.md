# Bitter Leaf Salad with Rosehip Kombucha Vinaigrette

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Live kombucha stands in for vinegar — gentler acidity that lets bitter leaves and toasted nuts speak.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Mixed bitter leaves (radicchio, endive, frisée), torn — 250 g
- Rosehip kombucha (larder), flat — 60 ml
- Dijon mustard — 8 g
- Maple syrup — 8 g
- Rapeseed oil — 70 ml
- Fine sea salt — 2 g
- Toasted hazelnuts, crushed — 40 g
- Apple, shaved thin — 100 g
- Chives, cut long — 6 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Reduce the kombucha by half in a small pan (to 30 ml) and cool — concentrates acidity without harshness.
2. Whisk reduced kombucha with mustard, maple and salt.
3. Stream in oil to a light emulsion.
4. Wash and spin the leaves bone-dry; chill.
5. Dress leaves gently with three-quarters of the vinaigrette — hands, not tongs.
6. Fold in apple shavings.
7. Plate tall; finish with hazelnuts, chives, pepper and the last vinaigrette.

## Plating & Service
Serve beside rich mains — short ribs, pork belly — as the palate-reset course.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free with toasted seeds; any house kombucha works — elderflower for summer, rhubarb for spring.
