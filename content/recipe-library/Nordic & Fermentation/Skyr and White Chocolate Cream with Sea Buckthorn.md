# Skyr and White Chocolate Cream with Sea Buckthorn

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sharp sea buckthorn curd cutting through a white chocolate–skyr cloud — sweet, sour and Nordic-orange.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- White chocolate, chopped — 150 g
- Double cream — 250 ml
- Skyr, full-fat — 250 g
- Sea buckthorn juice — 150 ml
- Caster sugar — 60 g
- Butter — 40 g
- Egg yolks — 60 g
- Cornflour — 6 g
- White chocolate shards (garnish) — 30 g
- Micro lemon balm — 3 g

## Method
1. Curd: whisk sea buckthorn juice, sugar, yolks and cornflour; cook over low heat, whisking, to 82°C until thick; beat in butter; press film to the surface; chill.
2. Melt white chocolate over a bain-marie at 45°C.
3. Warm 100 ml of the cream to 60°C; emulsify into the chocolate.
4. Cool to 30°C; whisk in skyr.
5. Whip remaining cream to soft peaks; fold in.
6. Chill the cream 2 hours at 2–4°C.
7. Layer in glasses: curd, cream, curd; finish with shards and lemon balm.

## Plating & Service
Serve well chilled with a thin knækbrød tuile if the tin is full — the orange layer should glow.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Passion fruit mimics sea buckthorn on Riviera provisioning; halve sugar and it still balances.
