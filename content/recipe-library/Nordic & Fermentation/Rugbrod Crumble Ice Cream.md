# Rugbrod Crumble Ice Cream

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The old Danish trick: caramelised rye breadcrumbs folded through custard ice cream — malty, toffee-dark, zero waste.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Stale rugbrød, blitzed to coarse crumbs — 120 g
- Brown sugar — 80 g
- Butter — 30 g
- Whole milk — 300 ml
- Double cream — 300 ml
- Egg yolks — 100 g
- Caster sugar — 90 g
- Vanilla pod, seeds — 1 g
- Fine sea salt — 1 g
- Blackcurrant vinegar (finishing) — 10 ml

## Method
1. Toss rye crumbs with brown sugar and melted butter; bake at 170°C for 12–15 minutes, stirring twice, until dark, crisp and caramelised. Cool; break up.
2. Custard: warm milk, cream and vanilla to 80°C.
3. Whisk yolks with caster sugar; temper with the hot dairy; return and cook to 82–84°C, stirring, until it coats a spoon.
4. Strain; add salt; chill fully to 4°C.
5. Churn to soft-serve.
6. Fold through three-quarters of the caramelised crumbs; freeze 2 hours.
7. Serve scoops with remaining crumbs and 2–3 drops of blackcurrant vinegar per plate.

## Plating & Service
A single scoop on a chilled plate, crumb ridge, vinegar drops — the sour-dark finish makes it adult.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The crumbs alone upgrade any dessert; a spoon of rye miso in the custard turns it butterscotch-savoury.
