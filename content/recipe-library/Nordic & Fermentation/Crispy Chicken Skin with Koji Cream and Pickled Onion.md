# Crispy Chicken Skin with Koji Cream and Pickled Onion

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Glass-crisp chicken skin as the cracker, sweet koji cream as the luxury, pickled onion as the whip-crack — one perfect bite.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Chicken skins, scraped clean of fat — 200 g
- Fine sea salt — 2 g
- Savoury koji cream (larder) — 120 g
- Red onion, shaved into petals — 60 g
- Blackcurrant or plum vinegar — 40 ml
- Caster sugar — 10 g
- Water — 40 ml
- Chives, sliced — 5 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Quick-pickle: warm vinegar, sugar and water to dissolve; pour over onion petals; cool 30 minutes.
2. Stretch chicken skins flat between two sheets of parchment on a tray; salt lightly; top with a second tray.
3. Bake at 180°C for 30–40 minutes until amber and rigid; drain on paper. They must snap, not bend.
4. Cool completely; break into 8 handsome shards.
5. Load the koji cream into a piping bag.
6. Pipe three dots per shard just before service (they soften fast).
7. Top each dot with a pickled onion petal; finish with chives and pepper.

## Plating & Service
Serve upright in a bowl of black pebbles or flat on slate — hand around with the first glass of wine.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian route: pipe the cream on rye crisps with the same pickles; duck skin works for a richer shard.
