# Koldskal with Kammerjunker

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Denmark's chilled buttermilk bowl with crisp double-baked biscuits — the only correct dessert above 25°C.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Buttermilk, cold — 800 ml
- Skyr or soured cream — 100 g
- Egg yolks, pasteurised — 40 g
- Caster sugar — 60 g
- Vanilla pod, seeds — 1 g
- Lemon zest and juice — 4 g / 20 ml
- Kammerjunker: flour 150 g, butter 75 g, sugar 50 g, egg 30 g, baking powder 3 g, cardamom 1 g
- Strawberries, sliced — 150 g

## Method
1. Biscuits: rub butter into flour, sugar, baking powder and cardamom; bind with egg; roll into a 3 cm log; chill 30 minutes.
2. Slice into 1 cm coins; bake at 180°C for 10 minutes; halve each horizontally, bake cut-side up 5 more minutes until fully crisp.
3. Whisk yolks, sugar and vanilla until pale and ribboned.
4. Whisk in skyr, then cold buttermilk, lemon zest and juice.
5. Chill hard — minimum 1 hour; it must be icy cold.
6. Whisk before serving (it settles).
7. Bowl, float strawberry slices, pile biscuits on top at the last second.

## Plating & Service
Serve in chilled bowls on deck at the hottest hour — biscuits go on tableside so they crunch.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skip yolks and double the skyr for an egg-free version; raspberries or lacto blueberries by season.
