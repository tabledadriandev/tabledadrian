# Skyr Mousse with Lacto Blueberries

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Airy skyr mousse against fizzy fermented blueberries — a dessert that tastes cultured in every sense.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Skyr, full-fat — 400 g
- Double cream — 200 ml
- Caster sugar — 70 g
- Vanilla pod, seeds — 1 g
- Gelatine leaves — 3 g (approx. 2 leaves)
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Lacto blueberries with juice (larder) — 180 g
- Honey — 15 g
- Rye crumble or crushed knækbrød (optional) — 40 g

## Method
1. Bloom gelatine in iced water 5 minutes.
2. Warm 50 ml of the cream with sugar and vanilla to 60°C; dissolve the squeezed gelatine in it.
3. Whisk the warm mixture into the skyr with lemon juice.
4. Whip remaining cream to soft peaks; fold in thirds into the skyr base.
5. Pipe or spoon into glasses; set 3 hours at 2–4°C.
6. Stir honey into the lacto blueberries and their pink juice.
7. Crown each mousse with berries, juice and a scatter of rye crumble.

## Plating & Service
Serve cold in glass so the purple juice bleeds into the white — no further garnish needed.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Agar (1.5 g, boiled in the cream) replaces gelatine for vegetarians strict about it; lacto plums work in autumn.
