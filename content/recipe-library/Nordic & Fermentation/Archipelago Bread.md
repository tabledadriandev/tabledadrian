# Archipelago Bread

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Finland's dark, sweet buttermilk loaf — no kneading, deep malt flavour, and it slices like velvet for seafood boards.

## Ingredients (serves 14)
- Buttermilk, room temperature — 500 ml
- Fresh yeast — 25 g (or 8 g instant)
- Dark syrup or molasses — 100 g
- Wholegrain rye flour — 200 g
- Strong white flour — 250 g
- Rolled oats — 50 g
- Wheat bran — 30 g
- Fine sea salt — 9 g
- Glaze: syrup 30 g, hot water 30 ml

## Method
1. Whisk yeast and syrup into the buttermilk until dissolved.
2. Stir in all dry ingredients to a thick, sticky batter — no kneading.
3. Scrape into a greased 2 L loaf tin; smooth the top with a wet spatula.
4. Prove covered 45–60 minutes at 24–26°C until risen by half.
5. Bake at 180°C for 60–75 minutes to 95°C core; tent with foil if darkening too fast after 40 minutes.
6. Brush the hot loaf with the syrup glaze — twice — for the signature sticky sheen.
7. Cool completely; rest wrapped 12 hours before slicing thin.

## Plating & Service
The classic base for cold-smoked salmon and soured cream; also devastating with blue cheese.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free with oat drink + 10 ml vinegar; keeps a week wrapped, improving daily.
