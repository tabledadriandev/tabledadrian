# Fermented Cep Tartlets with Aged Cheese

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> One-bite rye tartlets of fermented cep duxelles under a snow of aged cheese — the canapé that tastes like October.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Rye-butter pastry: flour 120 g, rye flour 60 g, cold butter 90 g, egg yolk 20 g, cold water 20 ml, salt 2 g
- Fermented ceps (larder), fine-chopped — 150 g
- Fresh mushrooms, fine-chopped — 150 g
- Shallot, minced — 40 g
- Butter — 30 g
- Double cream — 60 ml
- Thyme leaves — 2 g
- Cep ferment juice — 15 ml
- Aged hard cheese, for grating — 40 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Pastry: rub butters into flours and salt; bind with yolk and water; rest 30 minutes chilled.
2. Roll to 2 mm; line 8 small tartlet moulds; dock; freeze 15 minutes.
3. Blind-bake at 170°C for 15–18 minutes to crisp gold; cool.
4. Duxelles: sweat shallot in butter; add fresh mushrooms, cook dry 6–8 minutes.
5. Fold in fermented ceps, cream and thyme; reduce to a thick, spoonable paste; season with cep juice and pepper.
6. Fill tartlets warm.
7. Grate an unreasonable amount of cheese over each at the pass.

## Plating & Service
Serve within minutes of filling; two per guest disappears anyway.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Shells hold 3 days airtight; vegan with oat cream and 10 g white miso instead of cheese.
