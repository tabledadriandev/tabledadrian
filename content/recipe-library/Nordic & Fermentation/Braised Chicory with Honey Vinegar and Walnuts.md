# Braised Chicory with Honey Vinegar and Walnuts

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bitter leaves glazed in fermented honey vinegar — bitter, sweet, sour and toasty in one caramelised wedge.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chicory (Belgian endive), halved lengthwise — 600 g
- Butter — 50 g
- Fermented honey vinegar (larder) — 40 ml
- Honey — 15 g
- Vegetable stock — 100 ml
- Fine sea salt — 3 g
- Walnuts, toasted, crushed — 40 g
- Thyme leaves — 2 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Melt butter in a wide pan; lay chicory cut-side down; cook over medium 5–6 minutes to deep gold without moving.
2. Season with salt; add honey; let it caramelise 1 minute.
3. Deglaze with honey vinegar; add stock.
4. Cover loosely; braise at a simmer 12–15 minutes until the cores yield to a knife.
5. Uncover; reduce the juices to a glaze, spooning over, 3–4 minutes.
6. Finish with thyme and pepper.
7. Scatter walnuts at the last second so they stay crisp.

## Plating & Service
Cut-side up, glaze brushed over, walnuts clustered in the leaves — pairs with duck, pork or a cheese course.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan with plant butter and agave; radicchio quarters braise the same in 10 minutes.
