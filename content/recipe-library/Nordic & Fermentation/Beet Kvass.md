# Beet Kvass

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A garnet-red, earthy-sour tonic that doubles as instant borscht seasoning and the most dramatic sauce base on board.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Beetroot, scrubbed, cut in 2 cm chunks (skin on) — 500 g
- Filtered water — 1500 ml
- Fine sea salt (non-iodised) — 40 g
- Caraway seeds — 2 g
- Garlic clove, bruised (optional) — 5 g
- Rye bread crust (optional traditional booster) — 30 g
- Sterilised 2 L jar — 1

## Method
1. SAFETY: 2% salt on total weight (2000 g = 40 g); ferment at 18–22°C; total fermentation time 5–7 days; skim any surface film daily; discard if slimy ropes form.
2. Pack beets, caraway, garlic and bread crust (if using) into the jar.
3. Dissolve salt in the water; pour over to cover; weight the solids down.
4. Lid loosely; ferment at 18–22°C.
5. Taste from day 4: earthy, saline, gently sour and faintly fizzy. Stop at day 5–7.
6. Strain; bottle the liquid; refrigerate at 2–4°C. Keeps 1 month. The spent beets can be roasted.
7. Serve as a 60 ml chilled shot, or reduce by half for a glaze.

## Plating & Service
A chilled shot with lemon before a rich lunch; whisk warm reduced kvass with butter into a stunning sauce for roasted duck.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Omit garlic and bread for a cleaner, gluten-free jar (as tagged, without the crust); add ginger for a livelier tonic.
