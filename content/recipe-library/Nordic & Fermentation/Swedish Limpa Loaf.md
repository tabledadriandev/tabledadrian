# Swedish Limpa Loaf

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A soft, sweet rye perfumed with orange, fennel and anise — the bread that makes ham sandwiches taste like Christmas.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Strong white flour — 300 g
- Light rye flour — 200 g
- Warm water (37°C) — 260 ml
- Fresh yeast — 18 g (or 6 g instant)
- Dark syrup or molasses — 60 g
- Rapeseed oil — 30 ml
- Fine sea salt — 9 g
- Orange zest — 6 g
- Fennel seeds, lightly crushed — 3 g
- Anise seeds, lightly crushed — 2 g
- Raisins (optional) — 60 g

## Method
1. Dissolve yeast and syrup in the warm water.
2. Mix in flours, oil, salt, zest and spices; knead 8–10 minutes to a soft, slightly tacky dough; work in raisins at the end.
3. Prove covered 60–75 minutes at 24–26°C until doubled.
4. Shape into a batard; seam down on parchment.
5. Prove 45 minutes until a poked dimple springs back slowly; slash three diagonal cuts.
6. Bake at 200°C for 15 minutes, then 180°C for 25 minutes to 94°C core and a hollow knock.
7. Brush the hot crust with a little syrup dissolved in hot water for shine; cool fully before slicing.

## Plating & Service
Slice for the breakfast board with butter and cheese; it is the correct bread under cured duck ham.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Caraway can replace anise; stays moist 4 days wrapped — day-5 slices make the French toast of your life.
