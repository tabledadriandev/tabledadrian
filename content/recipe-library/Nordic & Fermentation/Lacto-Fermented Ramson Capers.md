# Lacto-Fermented Ramson Capers

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Wild garlic buds fermented into punchy 'capers' — the definitive foraged garnish for cured fish and butter sauces.

## Ingredients (serves 20)
- Ramson (wild garlic) flower buds, unopened — 300 g
- Filtered water — 300 ml
- Fine sea salt (non-iodised) — 12 g
- Mustard seeds — 2 g
- Lemon zest strip — 1 g
- For storage: extra 2% brine as needed — 100 ml
- For service: cold-pressed rapeseed oil — 10 ml

## Method
1. SAFETY: 2% brine on total weight (600 g total, 12 g salt); ferment at 18–21°C; total fermentation time 10–14 days; buds must stay below the brine.
2. Rinse buds, drain, pack into a sterilised 500 ml jar with mustard seed and zest.
3. Dissolve salt in the water, pour over, weight the buds down, lid loosely or airlock.
4. Ferment at 18–21°C, skimming kahm yeast if it forms.
5. Taste at day 10: they should be sour, garlicky and snappy like a caper; pH 3.8 or below. Stop by day 14.
6. Refrigerate at 2–4°C in brine; they hold 3 months and mellow beautifully.
7. Blot dry and toss in a drop of oil before garnishing.

## Plating & Service
Scatter over gravlax, beef tartare or brown-butter skate; chop into remoulade for fried fish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nasturtium seed pods ferment the same way for a peppery version; use the garlicky brine in vinaigrettes.
