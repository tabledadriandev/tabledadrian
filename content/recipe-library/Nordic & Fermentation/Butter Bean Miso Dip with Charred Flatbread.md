# Butter Bean Miso Dip with Charred Flatbread

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> House blond miso whipped into beans — hummus energy, Nordic accent, four-minute assembly once the larder exists.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Cooked butter beans, drained — 400 g
- Butter bean miso with lemon (larder) — 40 g
- Olive oil — 60 ml
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Iced water — 40 ml
- Garlic, small clove — 3 g
- Tahini — 20 g
- Flatbreads — 300 g
- Toasted seeds (pumpkin, sunflower) — 20 g
- Chilli oil or fermented hot sauce — 10 ml

## Method
1. Blend beans, miso, garlic, tahini and lemon on high.
2. Stream in olive oil, then iced water, until whipped and mousse-light, 2 minutes.
3. Taste — the miso should carry all the salt; adjust with lemon only.
4. Char flatbreads directly over flame or in a screaming pan, 1 minute per side.
5. Swoosh the dip in a wide bowl.
6. Dress with a flick of oil and hot sauce.
7. Scatter seeds; serve flatbread torn and warm.

## Plating & Service
Aperitif hour on deck: one big bowl, torn bread, no plates.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF flatbread or vegetable batons; whisk leftovers with stock into an instant sauce for grilled fish.
