# Fermented Honey Vinegar

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Mead brewed then soured — a rounded, waxy-floral vinegar that flatters carrots, chicken and cheese in equal measure.

## Ingredients (serves 25)
- Raw honey — 350 g
- Filtered water, 30°C — 1400 ml
- Champagne yeast — 1 g
- Live vinegar mother or unpasteurised cider vinegar — 120 ml
- Sterilised 2 L jar, muslin, band — 1
- Chamomile flowers (optional, at bottling) — 2 g

## Method
1. SAFETY: aerobic ferment, cloth cover; 20–26°C; total fermentation time 8–10 weeks (2–3 weeks mead + 6–7 weeks acetic); never seal during fermentation — CO2 then oxygen access are both required.
2. Whisk honey into the warm water until fully dissolved (a quick mead 'must' of ~11% sugar).
3. Add yeast; cloth cover; ferment 14–20 days at 20–26°C until bubbling stops and it tastes dry and boozy.
4. Stir in the mother; re-cover.
5. Rest at 20–26°C, stirring weekly; taste from week 5 of this stage.
6. Stop when honeyed on the nose but cleanly sour on the palate.
7. Bottle over chamomile if using; store cool; refrigerate after opening.

## Plating & Service
Glaze heritage carrots with 30 ml vinegar + 30 g butter; deglaze roast chicken pans; drizzle over aged goat cheese.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Not vegan (honey); heather or chestnut honey each give a distinct single-origin character worth labelling.
