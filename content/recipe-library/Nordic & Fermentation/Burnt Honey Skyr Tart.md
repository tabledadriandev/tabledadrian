# Burnt Honey Skyr Tart

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A baked skyr tart — lighter than cheesecake, sharper than custard — glazed with honey taken to the edge of bitter.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Pastry: flour 200 g, cold butter 100 g, icing sugar 40 g, egg yolk 20 g, cold water 20 ml, salt 1 g
- Skyr, full-fat — 500 g
- Double cream — 150 ml
- Caster sugar — 100 g
- Eggs — 110 g
- Cornflour — 15 g
- Lemon zest — 3 g
- Honey — 80 g
- Flaky salt — 1 g
- Thyme leaves (optional) — 1 g

## Method
1. Pastry: rub butter into flour, sugar, salt; bind with yolk and water; rest 30 minutes chilled.
2. Roll to 3 mm; line a 24 cm tart ring; freeze 15 minutes; blind-bake at 170°C for 20 minutes, then 5 uncovered until biscuit-gold.
3. Filling: whisk skyr, cream, sugar, eggs, cornflour and zest until just smooth — do not aerate.
4. Pour into the warm shell; bake at 150°C for 25–30 minutes until set at the edge with a confident wobble at the centre.
5. Cool 2 hours; the centre finishes setting.
6. Burnt honey: boil honey in a deep pan 3–4 minutes until it foams, darkens and smells toasty-bitter; cool 5 minutes until pourable.
7. Glaze the tart in a thin layer; finish with flaky salt and thyme.

## Plating & Service
Slice with a hot knife; serve at cool room temperature — cold mutes the honey's bitterness.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> A spoon of whey caramel under each slice doubles down; GF pastry works — keep the wobble sacred.
