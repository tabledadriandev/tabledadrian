# Fermented Wholegrain Mustard

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Mustard seeds bloomed in live pickle brine — poppy texture, round heat, and it never tastes raw or bitter.

## Ingredients (serves 30)
- Yellow mustard seeds — 150 g
- Brown mustard seeds — 100 g
- Live lacto brine (from cucumber or kohlrabi ferment) — 300 ml
- Cider vinegar — 60 ml
- Honey or agave — 30 g
- Fine sea salt — 5 g
- Turmeric — 1 g
- Dill seed — 2 g

## Method
1. SAFETY: the live brine (already pH < 4) plus vinegar keeps this ferment acid-safe; hold at 20–23°C; total fermentation time 3–5 days, then mature 1 week cold; if brine is dead/pasteurised, add 20 ml extra vinegar.
2. Combine both seeds with brine in a sterilised 750 ml jar; seeds should be covered by 1 cm of liquid.
3. Lid loosely; hold at 20–23°C for 3–5 days, stirring daily; seeds swell and the brine gets gluey (normal — that's mucilage).
4. Blitz one-third of the mixture, then fold back in for a half-smooth, half-popping texture.
5. Season with vinegar, honey, salt, turmeric and dill seed.
6. Jar; refrigerate at 2–4°C and mature 1 week — the harsh edge mellows dramatically.
7. Keeps 4 months chilled.

## Plating & Service
The non-negotiable partner for cured pork and hot dogs off the aft grill; whisk into vinaigrettes and gravlax sauce.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Agave keeps it vegan; splash in 20 ml dark beer at step 5 for a Danish-Christmas register (adds gluten).
