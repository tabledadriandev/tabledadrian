# Rye Bread Miso

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Yesterday's rugbrød reborn as a chocolate-dark miso with coffee and molasses notes — zero-waste and utterly Nordic.

## Ingredients (serves 30)
- Stale dark rye bread, crusts on, torn — 700 g
- Filtered water (to rehydrate) — 350 ml
- Fresh barley koji — 700 g
- Fine sea salt (non-iodised) — 168 g
- Extra salt for the surface — 15 g
- Sterilised 2 L crock — 1

## Method
1. SAFETY: 12% salt on bread+koji weight (1400 g = 168 g); age at 18–24°C; total fermentation time 60–90 days; keep the surface salted, filmed and weighted to stay anaerobic.
2. Toast the rye pieces at 160°C for 20 minutes to deepen flavour; cool.
3. Blitz to coarse crumbs; work in water until the crumb feels like damp sand that clumps.
4. Mix thoroughly with crumbled koji and salt.
5. Pack into the crock in punched-down layers; level, dust with salt, press film to the surface, weight.
6. Age at 18–24°C, checking monthly and skimming any kahm yeast.
7. Ready from day 60: glossy, near-black, tasting of espresso, prune and soy. Best at 90 days.
8. Refrigerate at 2–4°C; keeps 1 year.

## Plating & Service
Rub into short ribs before braising, whisk into brown butter for roasted cabbage, or sweeten with honey as a caramel-adjacent dessert sauce.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Gluten-free bread + rice koji works; a spoon blended with 100 g soft butter makes an instant steak butter.
