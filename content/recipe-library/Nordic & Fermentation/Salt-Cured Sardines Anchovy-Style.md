# Salt-Cured Sardines Anchovy-Style

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Riviera sardines cured hard like Nordic salt fish — after three months they melt into sauces like the finest anchovy.

## Ingredients (serves 20)
- Sardines, rigid-fresh, scaled, gutted, heads off — 1000 g
- Coarse sea salt — 500 g
- Bay leaves — 2 g
- Black peppercorns — 3 g
- Olive oil (for final packing) — 200 ml
- Sterilised wide jar or crock — 1

## Method
1. SAFETY: this is a heavy salt cure (50% of fish weight) — the salt is the preservative, never reduce it; cure refrigerated at 2–4°C throughout; total cure time 90 days minimum before eating; fish must be day-boat fresh (histamine risk otherwise).
2. Butterfly the sardines; remove backbones; pat dry.
3. Lay a 1 cm salt bed in the crock; add a tight fish layer, skin down; season with bay and pepper.
4. Repeat salt/fish layers, finishing with 1 cm of salt.
5. Weight with a plate; refrigerate at 2–4°C. Brine will rise and submerge everything within 2 days — top with a little 20% brine if it doesn't.
6. Cure 90 days. The flesh turns mahogany and smells of proper anchovy.
7. To use: rinse fillets, soak 10 minutes in water, pat dry, pack in olive oil; keeps 3 weeks chilled.

## Plating & Service
Melt a fillet into brown butter for cauliflower; drape on buttered rye with shaved radish; blend into dressings.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fresh anchovies cure the same in 60 days; the used cure-brine seasons tomato sauces (sparingly).
