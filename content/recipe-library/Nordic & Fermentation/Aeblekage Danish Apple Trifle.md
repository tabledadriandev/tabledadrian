# Aeblekage Danish Apple Trifle

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Layers of vanilla apple compote, buttery caramelised crumbs and softly whipped cream — Denmark's answer to the trifle, served cold and unbothered.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Apples, peeled, chopped — 800 g
- Caster sugar — 80 g
- Vanilla pod, split — 1 g
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Water — 40 ml
- Breadcrumbs or crushed rye biscuits — 140 g
- Butter — 60 g
- Brown sugar — 60 g
- Double cream — 250 ml
- Redcurrant jelly or lacto blueberry juice — 30 g

## Method
1. Compote: simmer apples, sugar, vanilla, lemon and water, lidded, 12–15 minutes; mash rustic; cool completely.
2. Toast crumbs with butter and brown sugar in a wide pan over medium heat, stirring, 6–8 minutes until deep gold and crisp; cool on a tray.
3. Whip cream to soft, floppy peaks — no stiffer.
4. Layer in glasses: compote, crumbs, cream; repeat.
5. Chill 1 hour (the crumbs half-soften — that's correct).
6. Finish with a last dust of crumbs.
7. Dot with jelly or a drizzle of lacto blueberry juice.

## Plating & Service
Serve cold in glass with long spoons; it holds 4 hours, making it the perfect prep-ahead charter dessert.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF with GF crumbs; fold 100 g skyr into the cream for tang and structure.
