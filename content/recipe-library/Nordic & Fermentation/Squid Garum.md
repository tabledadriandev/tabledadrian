# Squid Garum

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The closest thing to ancient Roman garum in the larder — oceanic, clean and shockingly elegant in butter sauces.

## Ingredients (serves 40)
- Squid, whole, cleaned weight incl. tentacles, chopped — 1000 g
- Fresh rice koji — 250 g
- Filtered water — 700 ml
- Fine sea salt (non-iodised) — 234 g
- Sterilised 2 L jar — 1
- Muslin for straining — 1

## Method
1. SAFETY: 12% salt on total weight (1950 g ≈ 234 g); hold at 60°C throughout; total fermentation time 6–8 weeks (cephalopods autolyse fast); use impeccably fresh squid; discard on putrid aroma.
2. Chop squid finely, including wings and tentacles; the more surface area, the faster the enzymatic breakdown.
3. Mix squid, koji, water and salt in the jar.
4. Hold at 60°C, lid loose; stir twice weekly. The liquid turns amber and smells of dried scallop by week 4.
5. Taste from week 6: saline, sweet, deeply marine without fishiness.
6. Strain through muslin; do not press. Optionally clarify at 70°C and re-strain.
7. Bottle; refrigerate at 2–4°C for up to 6 months.

## Plating & Service
Three drops finish a shellfish beurre blanc; whisk with olive oil and lemon over raw fish; season squid-ink rice in place of salt.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Mussel meat works the same (still shellfish allergen); for fish-allergy-free umami use the Yeast Garum instead.
