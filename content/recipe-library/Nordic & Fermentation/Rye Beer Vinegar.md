# Rye Beer Vinegar

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Dark Nordic beer soured into a malty, bread-crusted vinegar — the natural acid for smoked meats and rugbrød dressings.

## Ingredients (serves 25)
- Dark rye or dunkel-style beer (open, flat) — 1000 ml
- Filtered water — 200 ml
- Live vinegar mother or unpasteurised cider vinegar — 150 ml
- Barley malt syrup — 20 g
- Sterilised 2 L jar, muslin, band — 1

## Method
1. SAFETY: aerobic ferment under cloth at 20–26°C; total fermentation time 4–6 weeks; the alcohol is already made, so this is a single acetic stage; dilute beer above 6% ABV (acetobacter stalls over ~8%).
2. Pour beer and water into the jar; whisk to flatten remaining carbonation completely.
3. Stir in malt syrup and the mother.
4. Cloth cover; hold at 20–26°C, stirring weekly. A new mother forms on the surface — leave it undisturbed after week 2.
5. Taste from week 3: malt sweetness fades into round, toasty acidity.
6. Stop at week 4–6 when assertively sour; bottle, keeping the mother for the next batch.
7. Optional: pasteurise at 70°C for 10 minutes; otherwise refrigerate.

## Plating & Service
Whisk with mustard and rapeseed oil over warm potato salad; deglaze pans after smoked pork; season braised onions.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Gluten-free beer makes a GF version; stout gives coffee-bitter depth for glazing beef.
