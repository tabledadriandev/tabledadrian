# Cardamom Buns

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Swedish kardemummabullar with freshly crushed cardamom in both dough and butter — the smell alone books the next charter.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Strong white flour — 500 g
- Whole milk, warm (37°C) — 250 ml
- Fresh yeast — 20 g (or 7 g instant)
- Caster sugar — 70 g
- Butter, soft (dough) — 70 g
- Green cardamom seeds, crushed — 8 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g
- Egg — 55 g
- Filling: butter 100 g, brown sugar 90 g, crushed cardamom 6 g, cinnamon 1 g
- Glaze: water 40 ml, sugar 40 g; pearl sugar 20 g

## Method
1. Dissolve yeast in warm milk; mix with flour, sugar, egg, salt and half the cardamom; knead 8 minutes.
2. Knead in soft butter until silky, 4 more minutes; prove covered 60–90 minutes at 24–26°C until doubled.
3. Beat filling ingredients to a paste.
4. Roll dough to 30 × 50 cm; spread filling; fold in thirds like a letter.
5. Cut into 12 strips; twist and knot each into a bun; tray on parchment.
6. Prove 45 minutes until puffy; brush with beaten egg (reserve a little from the dough egg or use milk).
7. Bake at 210°C for 10–12 minutes to deep gold.
8. Boil glaze 1 minute; brush hot buns; hit with pearl sugar and remaining crushed cardamom.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm within hours, with strong coffee — fika is non-negotiable at 10:30.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap filling to cinnamon-forward for kanelbullar; dough freezes shaped — bake from cold, +3 minutes.
