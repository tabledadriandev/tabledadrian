# Tomato Salad with Fermented Tomato Water

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Peak-season tomatoes swimming in their own fermented essence — served ice-cold with warm oil, it's electric.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Heirloom tomatoes, mixed sizes, ripe — 600 g
- Lacto cherry tomatoes (larder), halved — 120 g
- Fermented tomato water (larder) — 120 ml
- Olive oil, best — 40 ml
- Flaky salt — 2 g
- Black pepper — 1 g
- Basil and lovage leaves, small — 6 g
- Shallot, shaved paper-thin — 30 g
- White balsamic — 5 ml

## Method
1. Chill plates and the fermented tomato water to 4°C.
2. Cut large tomatoes into irregular thick slices and wedges; season with half the flaky salt 5 minutes ahead so they weep slightly.
3. Season the tomato water with white balsamic — it should taste like the essence of a tomato sandwich.
4. Arrange tomatoes and lacto halves on the chilled plates.
5. Pour the cold tomato water around, 30 ml per plate.
6. Warm the olive oil to 50°C; spoon over the tomatoes so it slicks the surface.
7. Finish with shallot, herbs, pepper, remaining salt.

## Plating & Service
Serve immediately, cold and glistening — bread to drink the plate is obligatory.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add burrata for a richer course (adds dairy); a drop of green jalapeño-dill hot sauce in the water wakes it further.
