# Whey Caramel with Apple Sorbet

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Leftover skyr whey reduced to a lactic caramel under sharp green-apple sorbet — waste stream to signature dessert.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Whey (drained from skyr or yoghurt) — 1000 ml
- Caster sugar — 100 g
- Double cream — 80 ml
- Butter — 30 g
- Fine sea salt — 2 g
- Green apples, juiced (Granny Smith) — 600 ml
- Caster sugar (sorbet) — 110 g
- Glucose syrup — 30 g
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Apple, brunoise (garnish) — 80 g

## Method
1. Reduce the whey in a wide pan at a steady simmer to 150 ml, 40–50 minutes — it turns golden and smells of dulce de leche.
2. Add 100 g sugar; cook to a deep amber caramel, 8–10 minutes.
3. Off heat, whisk in cream, butter and salt; strain; cool. It should pour like honey.
4. Sorbet: warm 100 ml of the apple juice with sugar and glucose to dissolve; chill; combine with remaining juice and lemon.
5. Churn; freeze 2 hours to scoop.
6. Temper caramel to room temperature for service.
7. Pool caramel, seat the sorbet, scatter raw apple brunoise for snap.

## Plating & Service
Cold sorbet on warmish caramel — the temperature gap is the pleasure; serve the moment they meet.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pear juice sorbet in winter; the whey caramel keeps 3 weeks chilled and belongs on the rugbrød ice cream too.
