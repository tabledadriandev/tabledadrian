# Lacto Cucumber with Blackcurrant Leaf

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The blackcurrant leaf's tannin keeps these half-sours audibly crunchy — the pickle guests ask to take home.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Small ridge cucumbers, whole, blossom end trimmed — 1000 g
- Filtered water — 1000 ml
- Fine sea salt (non-iodised) — 40 g
- Blackcurrant leaves — 4 g
- Dill flower heads — 5 g
- Garlic cloves, bruised — 10 g
- Black peppercorns — 2 g
- Horseradish slice — 8 g

## Method
1. SAFETY: 2% brine on total weight (2000 g = 40 g salt); ferment at 18–21°C; total fermentation time 5–7 days for half-sours; always trim the blossom end (enzymes there soften pickles).
2. Dissolve salt fully in the water.
3. Pack cucumbers upright in a sterilised 2 L jar, layering leaves, dill, garlic, pepper and horseradish.
4. Cover completely with brine, weight down, lid loosely or fit an airlock.
5. Ferment at 18–21°C; the brine will cloud by day 2 — normal. Skim any surface yeast.
6. Taste at day 5: half-sour, fizzy, garlicky, loud crunch; pH 3.9 or below. For full sours go 10–12 days.
7. Refrigerate at 2–4°C in brine; keeps 2 months.

## Plating & Service
Slice thick on smørrebrød or serve whole, ice-cold, beside smoked meats and schnapps.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Oak or vine leaves replace blackcurrant; omit garlic and use the green brine as a low-FODMAP martini seasoning.
