# Rye Miso Glazed Short Ribs

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Four-hour ribs lacquered with rye-bread miso and blackcurrant vinegar — sticky, dark and impossible to serve without seconds.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Beef short ribs, bone-in — 2000 g
- Fine sea salt — 16 g
- Rapeseed oil — 30 ml
- Onion, carrot, celery, rough cut — 300 g
- Dark beer — 300 ml
- Beef stock — 800 ml
- Bay leaves — 1 g
- Rye bread miso (larder) — 70 g
- Blackcurrant vinegar (larder) — 30 ml
- Honey — 30 g
- Horseradish, freshly grated — 15 g

## Method
1. Salt ribs 1 hour ahead. Sear in oil, all faces, to deep brown; remove.
2. Brown the vegetables in the same pot, 6 minutes.
3. Deglaze with beer; add stock, bay and ribs; the liquid should come two-thirds up.
4. Braise covered at 150°C for 3.5–4 hours until the bone twists free.
5. Rest ribs; strain and skim the braise; reduce to 250 ml.
6. Whisk in miso, vinegar and honey; simmer to a glaze that coats a spoon.
7. Glaze the ribs at 220°C, basting every 4 minutes, 12 minutes total, until lacquered.
8. Finish with fresh horseradish grated over at the pass.

## Plating & Service
One rib per person over barley risotto or potato purée, extra glaze in a jug, lacto cucumber on the table to cut the richness.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: use GF beer and pea miso; the same glaze transforms roasted celeriac for vegetarians.
