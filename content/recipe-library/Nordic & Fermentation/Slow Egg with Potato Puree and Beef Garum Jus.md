# Slow Egg with Potato Puree and Beef Garum Jus

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A 63-degree egg in silk potato with a glossy garum jus — three textures of comfort, plated like a secret.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Eggs — 4
- Ratte potatoes, peeled — 400 g
- Whole milk — 150 ml
- Butter — 120 g
- Fine sea salt — 4 g
- Beef garum (larder) — 12 ml
- Reduced mushroom or beef stock — 150 ml
- Cold butter (for the jus) — 25 g
- Chives, minced — 6 g
- Rye crumbs, toasted in butter (optional) — 30 g

## Method
1. Cook eggs in a 63°C water bath for 60 minutes (or 64°C for 45 minutes for a slightly firmer white).
2. Simmer potatoes in salted water until collapsing, 20 minutes; drain and dry 2 minutes.
3. Rice the potatoes; beat in hot milk then butter over low heat to a glossy, pourable purée; season.
4. Jus: reduce the stock to 80 ml; whisk in cold butter; season with beef garum off the heat — no extra salt should be needed.
5. Hold purée and jus at 60°C; crack eggs onto a slotted spoon to drain the loose white.
6. Pool purée in warmed shallow bowls; seat an egg in each.
7. Spoon the garum jus around; finish with chives and buttered rye crumbs.

## Plating & Service
Instruct guests to break the yolk into everything — that's the dish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian with yeast garum in the jus (as tagged, use yeast garum); truffle shavings if the charter demands it.
