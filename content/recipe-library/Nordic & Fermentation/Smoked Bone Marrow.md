# Smoked Bone Marrow

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Marrow brined, smoked and roasted — spoon it over steak, whip it into butter, or serve in the bone with rye toast.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Beef marrow bones, split lengthwise by the butcher — 1200 g
- Filtered water — 1000 ml
- Fine sea salt — 60 g
- Black peppercorns — 2 g
- Beech or oak chips — 50 g
- Flaky salt — 3 g
- Parsley, chopped — 10 g
- Shallot, minced — 30 g
- Capers or ramson capers, chopped — 20 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml

## Method
1. SAFETY: soak-brine at 2–4°C for 12–24 hours (draws blood, seasons); roast until the centre reaches 70°C and the marrow is glossy and just slumping; serve hot, same day.
2. Dissolve salt in water; submerge bones, cut side down; refrigerate 12–24 hours, changing brine once if very bloody.
3. Drain, pat dry.
4. Smoke cut-side up at 90°C over beech for 20 minutes.
5. Roast at 200°C for 8–12 minutes until the marrow is soft, glossy and 70°C at the centre — not melted away.
6. Toss parsley, shallot, capers and lemon into a sharp gremolata.
7. Season marrow with flaky salt; top with gremolata.

## Plating & Service
Serve bones on a board with hot rye toast and the gremolata; or scoop marrow over grilled ribeye at the pass.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Whip cooled smoked marrow 1:1 with butter for a service-ready steak topper; the gremolata alone lifts grilled fish.
