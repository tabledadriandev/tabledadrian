# Fermented Mushroom Broth with Soft Egg

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A clear, dark broth built on fermented cep juice and shoyu — vegetarian but with the gravity of consommé.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Mixed mushrooms (button, chestnut), sliced — 500 g
- Dried ceps — 20 g
- Water — 1200 ml
- Fermented cep juice (from lacto cep ferment) — 60 ml
- Cep shoyu (or house shoyu) — 20 ml
- Leek, white, sliced — 80 g
- Eggs — 4
- Chives — 8 g
- Rapeseed oil — 15 ml
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Roast sliced mushrooms with oil at 220°C for 20 minutes until browned and dry at the edges.
2. Simmer roasted mushrooms, dried ceps, leek and water at a bare tremble, 30 minutes — never a rolling boil (keeps it clear).
3. Strain through muslin without pressing.
4. Season the broth with fermented cep juice and shoyu; it should taste deep, sour-edged, almost meaty.
5. Soft-cook eggs: 6 minutes 30 seconds in boiling water, ice bath, peel.
6. Reheat broth to 80°C.
7. Halve eggs into warm bowls; pour broth around; chives and pepper.

## Plating & Service
Pour the broth at the table from a small jug over the waiting egg — steam and aroma do the introduction.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: swap egg for silken tofu warmed in the broth; add cooked barley to make it a light main.
