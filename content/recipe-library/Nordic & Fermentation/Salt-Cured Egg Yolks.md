# Salt-Cured Egg Yolks

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The simplest larder luxury — amber yolk discs that grate like mimolette over pasta, tartare and buttered greens.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Very fresh egg yolks — 12 (approx. 220 g)
- Fine sea salt — 500 g
- Caster sugar — 250 g
- Neutral oil (finishing wipe) — 10 ml

## Method
1. SAFETY: cure refrigerated at 2–4°C start to finish; total cure + dry time 6–8 days; use clean, uncracked fresh eggs; discard any yolk that breaks or discolours during curing.
2. Whisk salt and sugar together (2:1).
3. Bed half the mix in a tray; press 12 dimples.
4. Lower a yolk into each; cover completely with remaining cure.
5. Cure at 2–4°C for 4 days until firm and jelly-bean bouncy.
6. Brush off cure, rinse fast, pat dry. Dry on a rack in the fridge 2–4 days (or 3 hours in a 50°C dehydrator) until firm enough to grate.
7. Wipe with oil; store airtight, chilled, 1 month.

## Plating & Service
Microplane over hand-cut pasta, steak tartare or grilled asparagus at the table — theatre for zero effort.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 5 g smoked paprika to the cure for a paprika-gold version; the koji-cured cousin in this folder brings more umami.
