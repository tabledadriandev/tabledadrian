# Venison Loin with Lacto Blueberry Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Fermented blueberries give the classic game-and-berry pairing a sour, savoury depth no fresh fruit can reach.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Venison loin, trimmed — 600 g
- Fine sea salt — 5 g
- Black pepper, cracked — 2 g
- Rapeseed oil — 20 ml
- Butter — 50 g
- Juniper berries, crushed — 2 g
- Game or beef stock — 200 ml
- Lacto blueberries with juice — 120 g
- Blackcurrant vinegar — 10 ml
- Celeriac purée: celeriac 400 g, milk 200 ml, butter 30 g, salt 2 g

## Method
1. Purée: simmer diced celeriac in milk with salt until soft, 15 minutes; blend with butter until silk; keep at 60°C.
2. Season venison; sear in oil over high heat, 2 minutes per face, to deep brown.
3. Add butter and juniper; baste 2 minutes; roast at 120°C to 50°C core, about 8–10 minutes.
4. Rest 8 minutes to 54°C — rosy throughout.
5. Sauce: deglaze the pan with stock; reduce by half; add lacto blueberry juice; reduce to coating.
6. Off heat, fold in whole fermented berries and vinegar — keep them intact and barely warmed.
7. Carve into thick medallions.

## Plating & Service
Purée swiped, venison on top, berries and sauce spooned over the meat, not the plate — dark, glossy, precise.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Duck breast substitutes; dairy-free with olive-oil celeriac purée.
