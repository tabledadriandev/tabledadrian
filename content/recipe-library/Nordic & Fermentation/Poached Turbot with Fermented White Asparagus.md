# Poached Turbot with Fermented White Asparagus

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Butter-poached turbot against sour fermented asparagus and a brine-sharpened sauce — a study in white on white.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Turbot fillets, thick, skinless — 4 × 150 g
- Fish stock — 300 ml
- Butter — 120 g
- Fermented white asparagus spears (larder) — 240 g
- Asparagus ferment brine — 30 ml
- White wine — 60 ml
- Shallot, minced — 30 g
- Double cream — 60 ml
- Chervil — 6 g
- White pepper — 1 g
- Fine sea salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Warm fish stock with 60 g butter to 62–65°C in a shallow pan — a butter-emulsion poach.
2. Season turbot lightly; slide into the liquid; poach 8–12 minutes to 52°C core, spooning liquid over.
3. Sauce: reduce wine and shallot to 20 ml; add cream, reduce 1 minute; whisk in 60 g cold butter.
4. Sharpen the sauce with the ferment brine instead of lemon — add gradually, tasting.
5. Warm asparagus spears in the poaching liquid 2 minutes (60°C — keep their snap).
6. Rest fish 2 minutes on a warm cloth.
7. Season with white pepper; sauce through a fine sieve if you want it mirror-smooth.

## Plating & Service
Three spears aligned, turbot on top, sauce flooded around, chervil last — serve on warm, wide-rimmed plates.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Halibut or brill substitute; dairy-free by mounting the reduction with olive oil and skipping cream.
