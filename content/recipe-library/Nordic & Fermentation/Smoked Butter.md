# Smoked Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Cold-smoked cultured butter that makes bread service unforgettable and finishes sauces with a campfire whisper.

## Ingredients (serves 20)
- Cultured butter, best quality, cut in 2 cm slabs — 500 g
- Flaky sea salt — 4 g
- Oak or beech dust for cold smoke — 60 g
- Ice, for the smoking chamber — 1000 g
- Baking parchment — 1 sheet

## Method
1. SAFETY: butter must stay below 15°C or it melts and turns greasy-acrid — cold-smoke only, chamber under 20°C, with the butter set over a tray of ice; total smoke time 2–3 hours; refrigerate immediately after.
2. Lay slabs on parchment on a perforated tray; set that over a tray of ice.
3. Run the cold smoke generator with oak dust; keep the chamber below 20°C (smoke at night or in the walk-in with a pellet tube).
4. Smoke 2 hours for a whisper, 3 for a statement; turn slabs once.
5. Taste an edge — it should be perfumed, not ashy.
6. Knead slabs together briefly with the flaky salt; roll into a log in parchment.
7. Chill at 2–4°C (2 weeks) or freeze (3 months).

## Plating & Service
Serve a cool disc on warm rye at the start of dinner; mount fish sauces with it; melt over hot-smoked char for double smoke.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Smoke plant butter the same way for vegan service; whip smoked butter with 10% brown butter for a hazelnut register.
