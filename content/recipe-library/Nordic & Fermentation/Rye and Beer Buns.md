# Rye and Beer Buns

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Dark, malty dinner buns risen on beer — crisp lids, tender crumb, and the right size for pulled-pork sliders.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Strong white flour — 350 g
- Wholegrain rye flour — 150 g
- Dark beer, room temperature — 300 ml
- Fresh yeast — 15 g (or 5 g instant)
- Barley malt syrup — 40 g
- Rapeseed oil — 30 ml
- Fine sea salt — 9 g
- Rolled oats (topping) — 20 g
- Water (spritz) — 20 ml

## Method
1. Whisk yeast and malt syrup into the beer.
2. Mix in flours, oil and salt; knead 8 minutes to a soft dough.
3. Prove covered 60–75 minutes at 24–26°C until doubled.
4. Divide into 12 × 75 g pieces; shape into tight rounds.
5. Tray, spritz with water, dip tops in oats; prove 40 minutes until pillowy.
6. Bake at 210°C with a splash of water on the oven floor for steam, 16–18 minutes, to deep brown.
7. Cool on a rack — the crusts crackle as they cool.

## Plating & Service
Bread basket with smoked butter; split for sliders of rye-miso pork belly.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Alcohol bakes off but swap beer for malty kvass or water+20 g syrup if preferred; add 40 g walnuts for a cheese-course bun.
