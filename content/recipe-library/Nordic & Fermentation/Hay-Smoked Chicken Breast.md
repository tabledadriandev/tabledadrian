# Hay-Smoked Chicken Breast

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Brined chicken finished under smouldering hay — a haunting meadow smoke you cannot buy in chips.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Chicken breasts, skin on — 6 (approx. 1100 g)
- Filtered water — 1000 ml
- Fine sea salt — 60 g
- Honey — 30 g
- Thyme sprigs — 3 g
- Clean, dry, untreated hay (from a feed store, not roadside) — 100 g
- Butter — 40 g
- Lemon — 60 g

## Method
1. SAFETY: 6% brine for 2 hours maximum; cook chicken to 68°C core, resting to 70°C+; hay must be dry, pesticide-free and food-safe; smoke in a heavy pan with a tight lid, outdoors or under strong extraction; eat within 3 days chilled.
2. Dissolve salt and honey in water with thyme; chill. Brine breasts 2 hours at 2–4°C.
3. Rinse, pat dry; rest uncovered in the fridge 1 hour.
4. Roast at 160°C until 60°C core, about 20–25 minutes.
5. Line a deep roasting tray with hay; light it, let it flame 10 seconds, then smother-lid it to smoulder.
6. Set breasts on a rack over the smouldering hay, lid on, 6–8 minutes; core temperature will finish at 68°C.
7. Rest 5 minutes; brush with melted butter and lemon.

## Plating & Service
Slice thick over yellow pea purée with fermented ceps; the hay perfume should reach the guest before the plate does.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Guinea fowl is even better; hay-smoke a whole butter-basted cauliflower alongside for the vegetarians.
