# Lemon Verbena Cured Halibut

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A short citrus-herbal cure that leaves halibut translucent and barely set — crudo discipline with Nordic perfume.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Halibut loin, skinless, pre-frozen or sashimi-grade — 700 g
- Fine sea salt — 42 g
- Caster sugar — 28 g
- Lemon verbena leaves, chopped (or zest of 2 lemons + 5 g verbena tea) — 10 g
- White pepper, ground — 2 g
- For service: olive oil 30 ml, lemon juice 10 ml, flaky salt 2 g, baby verbena or basil leaves 3 g, lacto green gooseberries, sliced — 60 g

## Method
1. SAFETY: pre-freeze fish at -20°C for 24 h unless certified sashimi-grade; this is a light 6% salt cure for 6–8 hours only, refrigerated at 2–4°C; slice and serve within 48 hours of curing.
2. Blitz salt, sugar, verbena and white pepper to a fragrant green-flecked cure.
3. Coat the loin evenly on all sides; wrap snugly in film.
4. Cure at 2–4°C for 6–8 hours depending on thickness (1 hour per cm as a rule).
5. Rinse 5 seconds, pat scrupulously dry; rest unwrapped 1 hour in the fridge.
6. Slice across the grain at 3 mm — it should be glossy, semi-firm, translucent at the edges.
7. Dress plates at the last second: oil, lemon, flaky salt, herbs and sliced fermented gooseberries.

## Plating & Service
Fan seven slices on a chilled plate, gooseberry slivers between, oil and lemon flicked over at the pass.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Sea bass or turbot cure identically; dust with toasted fennel pollen for a Riviera-Nordic bridge.
