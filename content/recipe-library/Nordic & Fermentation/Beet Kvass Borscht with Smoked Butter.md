# Beet Kvass Borscht with Smoked Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A clean, garnet broth finished with live beet kvass and a melting coin of smoked butter — sour, earthy, quietly dramatic.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Beetroot, peeled, grated — 400 g
- Carrot, grated — 100 g
- Onion, fine dice — 100 g
- Rapeseed oil — 20 ml
- Vegetable stock — 900 ml
- Bay leaf — 1 g
- Beet kvass (larder) — 150 ml
- Fine sea salt — 3 g
- Smoked butter (larder), in 4 cold coins — 40 g
- Dill, chopped — 8 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Sweat onion in oil 4 minutes; add carrot and beet, 5 minutes more.
2. Add stock and bay; simmer 25 minutes until the vegetables are tender and the broth is deep red.
3. Strain for a clear borscht (pressing lightly) or leave rustic.
4. Season with salt; cool the pot off the boil for 2 minutes.
5. Stir in the beet kvass off the heat — never boil it (kills the culture and dulls the colour).
6. Check: it should be vividly sour-earthy; add more kvass rather than vinegar.
7. Bowl at 80°C; float a cold smoked-butter coin in each; dill and pepper over.

## Plating & Service
The butter melts into smoky rivulets at the table — pair with dark rye and cold aquavit.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan with smoked plant butter; add shredded braised beef shin to make it a main.
