# Smoked Mackerel with Bay and Black Pepper

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Fat Atlantic mackerel hot-smoked to bronze — cheap fish, luxury outcome, and the base of a dozen quick plates.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Mackerel fillets, ultra-fresh, pin-boned — 800 g
- Filtered water — 800 ml
- Fine sea salt — 64 g
- Demerara sugar — 30 g
- Bay leaves, torn — 2 g
- Black peppercorns, cracked (half for brine, half for crust) — 6 g
- Beech or oak chips — 60 g
- Lemon — 60 g

## Method
1. SAFETY: 8% brine for 30–40 minutes; mackerel is histamine-prone — keep below 4°C at every stage before smoking; hot-smoke to 63°C internal; eat within 3 days, chilled.
2. Dissolve salt and sugar in cold water with bay and half the pepper; brine fillets 30–40 minutes at 2–4°C.
3. Rinse, pat dry; press remaining cracked pepper onto the skin side.
4. Air-dry on a rack in the fridge 90 minutes for the pellicle.
5. Hot-smoke at 85°C over beech, 30–40 minutes, to 63°C internal; the skin should be tight and golden.
6. Rest 5 minutes; serve warm or chill rapidly.
7. Store at 2–4°C up to 3 days.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm with horseradish skyr and pickled cucumber, or flake into a pâté with lemon and chives for the bread basket.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Herring fillets take 20 minutes of smoke; a 5 g fennel-seed crust instead of pepper suits summer menus.
