# Fermented Carrot and Sea Buckthorn Slaw Base

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sunshine-orange kraut with sea buckthorn's passionfruit sharpness — instant acidity and crunch for rich winter plates.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Carrots, peeled, julienned — 800 g
- Sea buckthorn juice (unsweetened) — 100 ml
- Fine sea salt (non-iodised) — 18 g
- Coriander seed, toasted, cracked — 2 g
- Fresh ginger, fine julienne — 15 g
- For service: rapeseed oil — 20 ml
- For service: toasted sunflower seeds — 30 g
- For service: parsley leaves — 5 g

## Method
1. SAFETY: 2% salt on total weight (900 g mix = 18 g salt); ferment at 20–23°C; total fermentation time 5–7 days; keep packed under liquid.
2. Massage carrots with salt for 3 minutes until dripping; fold in ginger, coriander and sea buckthorn juice.
3. Pack hard into a sterilised 1.5 L jar so brine rises above the vegetables; weight down.
4. Ferment at 20–23°C, pressing down daily with a clean spoon.
5. Taste from day 4; stop at day 5–7 when tangy, tropical-sour, still crunchy; pH 3.8 or below.
6. Refrigerate at 2–4°C; holds 2 months.
7. To serve, drain lightly and toss with oil, seeds and parsley.

## Plating & Service
Serve a tight nest beside fried chicken, meatballs or smoked fish; excellent inside rye-bread sandwiches.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No sea buckthorn? Use 50 ml lemon + 50 ml orange juice; add 2 g chilli flake for heat.
