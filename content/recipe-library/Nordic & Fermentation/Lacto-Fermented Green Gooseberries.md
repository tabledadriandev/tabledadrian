# Lacto-Fermented Green Gooseberries

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Under-ripe gooseberries turn into savoury-sour capsules that cut through oily fish and grilled pork like a Nordic caper.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Green (under-ripe) gooseberries, topped and tailed — 1000 g
- Fine sea salt (non-iodised) — 20 g
- Blackcurrant leaf (optional, for tannin) — 2 g
- Filtered water (only if jar-packing) — 100 ml
- Extra salt for topping brine (2% of water) — 2 g
- For service: dill fronds — 5 g
- For service: cold-pressed rapeseed oil — 15 ml
- For service: flaky salt — 1 g

## Method
1. SAFETY: 2% salt on total weight; ferment at 20–24°C; total fermentation time 5–6 days; keep fruit fully submerged at all times.
2. Toss gooseberries with 20 g salt and the blackcurrant leaf; rest 10 minutes until glossy.
3. Vacuum-seal at 99%, or pack into a sterilised 1.5 L jar and top with the 2% brine (100 ml water + 2 g salt), weighting the fruit under the surface.
4. Ferment at 20–24°C for 5–6 days, burping the bag daily from day 2. Taste from day 3.
5. Stop when the berries are pleasantly sour with a green-apple snap and the brine tastes like tart lemonade; pH 4.0 or lower.
6. Chill to 2–4°C. Store up to 4 weeks; the flavour deepens in week one.
7. To serve, halve berries, dress with oil, dill and flaky salt.

## Plating & Service
Scatter halved berries over grilled mackerel or pork chops; use the brine to sharpen beurre blanc or vinaigrettes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Ripe gooseberries give a sweeter, faster ferment (3–4 days); slice leftover ferment into tartare instead of cornichons.
