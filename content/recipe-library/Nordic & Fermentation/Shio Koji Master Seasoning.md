# Shio Koji Master Seasoning

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A living marinade that seasons, tenderises and browns everything from chicken to cabbage — the most-used jar in the galley.

## Ingredients (serves 30)
- Fresh rice koji (or rehydrated dried) — 300 g
- Filtered water — 450 ml
- Fine sea salt (non-iodised) — 75 g
- Sterilised 1 L jar — 1

## Method
1. SAFETY: salt is 10% of total weight (825 g total = 75 g) — do not reduce below 8% or spoilage organisms can grow; ferment at 20–24°C; total fermentation time 10–14 days.
2. Crumble koji into the jar, add salt, then water; stir hard 1 minute.
3. Lid loosely. Hold at 20–24°C, stirring once daily with a clean spoon.
4. From day 7 the mix smells of banana and sweet miso; grains soften.
5. Done at day 10–14 when a grain crushes easily between fingers and it tastes salty-sweet-savoury.
6. Blend smooth if desired. Refrigerate at 2–4°C; keeps 6 months.
7. Usage: 10% of protein weight as a marinade, 45–90 minutes for fish, 4–12 hours for chicken or pork; wipe off before searing (it burns past 180°C).

## Plating & Service
Not plated alone — brush on vegetables before roasting, whisk into dressings (1:3 with oil), or cure egg yolks in it.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Barley koji gives a darker, maltier version (contains gluten); blend with lemon zest for an instant seafood glaze.
