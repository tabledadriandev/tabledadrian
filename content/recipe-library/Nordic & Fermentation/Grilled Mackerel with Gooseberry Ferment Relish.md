# Grilled Mackerel with Gooseberry Ferment Relish

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The oldest pairing in the North Sea book, rebuilt with fermented gooseberries — sourness with depth against blistered oily fish.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Mackerel fillets, pin-boned, skin on — 8 (approx. 640 g)
- Rapeseed oil — 20 ml
- Fine sea salt — 4 g
- Lacto green gooseberries (larder), chopped — 150 g
- Gooseberry ferment brine — 20 ml
- Shallot, fine dice — 40 g
- Fermented wholegrain mustard (larder) — 15 g
- Dill, chopped — 8 g
- Rapeseed oil (for relish) — 25 ml
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Relish: fold chopped gooseberries, shallot, mustard, dill, 25 ml oil and the brine; rest 15 minutes.
2. Dry mackerel skin thoroughly; score twice; oil and salt both sides.
3. Grill skin-side down over high heat (or heavy pan) 3–4 minutes without moving until the skin is charred and releases.
4. Flip for 30–60 seconds only — the flesh should be just set, 55°C.
5. Rest 1 minute.
6. Taste relish; adjust with pepper and more brine if the fish is very rich.
7. Serve two fillets per person, skin up.

## Plating & Service
Fillets crossed, relish in a generous spoon at the side, charred lemon if the grill is already hot.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Sardines or herring take the same treatment; the relish doubles as a sauce for pork chops.
