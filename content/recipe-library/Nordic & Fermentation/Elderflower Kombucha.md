# Elderflower Kombucha

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The house apéritif for dry charters — floral, dry and fizzy enough to pour in a flute without apology.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Filtered water — 2000 ml
- Caster sugar — 140 g
- Green or white tea leaves — 12 g
- Fresh elderflower heads (or 15 g dried) — 40 g
- Kombucha SCOBY with 200 ml starter liquid — 1
- Lemon zest strips — 3 g
- Sterilised 3 L jar + swing-top bottles — 1 set

## Method
1. SAFETY: starter liquid must bring the sweet tea below pH 4.5 immediately (use 10% mature kombucha); ferment at 21–26°C; total fermentation time 9–14 days (7–10 primary + 2–4 bottle); refrigerate bottles after conditioning and open carefully — pressure is real.
2. Boil water, dissolve sugar, steep tea 8 minutes at 80°C; strain and cool fully to below 30°C.
3. Pour into the jar with starter liquid; float the SCOBY. Cloth cover.
4. Ferment 7–10 days at 21–26°C, tasting from day 6 — target dry, tart, lightly sweet.
5. Remove SCOBY with 200 ml for the next batch. Steep elderflower and lemon zest in the kombucha 12 hours, chilled; strain.
6. Bottle in swing-tops leaving 3 cm headspace; condition 2–4 days at 21°C for fizz.
7. Refrigerate at 2–4°C; open within 3 weeks, chilled, over ice or in a flute.

## Plating & Service
Serve at 6°C in champagne flutes as a zero-proof apéritif, or splash into a beurre blanc reduction in place of wine.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Sugar is largely consumed but not zero — flag for strict diabetic guests; dried lime flower is an off-season substitute.
