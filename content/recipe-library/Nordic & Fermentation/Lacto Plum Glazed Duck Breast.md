# Lacto Plum Glazed Duck Breast

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Fermented plum juice reduces into a sour-bright lacquer that cuts duck fat better than any orange ever did.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Duck breasts, skin scored — 4 × 180 g
- Fine sea salt — 4 g
- Lacto plum juice (from the larder ferment) — 150 ml
- Honey — 25 g
- Duck or chicken stock — 100 ml
- Sherry vinegar — 10 ml
- Cold butter (optional mount) — 20 g
- Lacto plum wedges — 120 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Watercress — 40 g

## Method
1. Salt the breasts; rest 20 minutes at room temperature.
2. Start skin-down in a COLD dry pan; render over medium-low 8–10 minutes until the skin is deep gold and thin.
3. Flip; cook 2–3 minutes to 52°C core. Rest 8 minutes (carryover to 56–57°C, rosy).
4. Pour off fat (save it). Deglaze with plum juice, honey and stock; reduce to a glossy 80 ml.
5. Adjust with vinegar; mount with butter if you want more polish.
6. Warm plum wedges briefly in the glaze — do not cook them soft.
7. Slice duck; glaze the skin with a brush of sauce.

## Plating & Service
Fan slices, plum wedges between, sauce pulled in a comma, watercress for bitterness.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skip honey and mount only with butter for lower sugar; the glaze works identically on pork chops.
