# King Oyster Scallops with Squid Garum Glaze

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Scored king oyster stems seared like scallops and lacquered with squid garum — plant texture, ocean soul.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- King oyster mushrooms, thick stems, cut in 3 cm 'scallops' — 400 g
- Rapeseed oil — 30 ml
- Butter or plant butter — 40 g
- Squid garum — 15 ml
- Mirin or apple juice — 30 ml
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Chives — 6 g
- Black pepper — 1 g
- Dill oil (larder) — 20 ml

## Method
1. Score both faces of each mushroom scallop in a fine crosshatch, 2 mm deep.
2. Sear in oil over high heat, 3 minutes per side, pressing gently, until deep gold and crosshatch opens.
3. Lower heat; add butter; baste 1 minute.
4. Deglaze with mirin; add squid garum; roll the scallops in the reducing glaze 1–2 minutes until lacquered.
5. Finish with lemon and pepper.
6. Rest 1 minute so the glaze sets tacky.
7. Plate with dots of dill oil.

## Plating & Service
Three scallops in a curve, glaze brushed on top, dill oil and chives around — serve immediately while the edges are crisp.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fully vegan with plant butter and yeast garum instead of squid; real scallops love this exact glaze too.
