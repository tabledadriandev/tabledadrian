# Fried Plaice with Fermented Kohlrabi Remoulade

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Danish harbour classic — butter-fried breaded plaice with a remoulade where fermented kohlrabi replaces the usual sweet pickle.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Plaice fillets, skinned — 8 (approx. 700 g)
- Flour — 60 g
- Eggs, beaten — 100 g
- Fine breadcrumbs — 140 g
- Butter — 80 g
- Rapeseed oil — 30 ml
- Remoulade: mayonnaise 120 g, lacto kohlrabi (larder) fine-diced 80 g, fermented mustard 15 g, capers 15 g, curry powder 2 g, dill 8 g, lemon juice 10 ml
- Lemon wedges — 80 g
- Fine sea salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Remoulade: fold all remoulade elements together; rest 20 minutes chilled — the kohlrabi should stay crunchy.
2. Season fillets; pané through flour, egg, crumbs; rest 10 minutes so the crust sets.
3. Heat oil with half the butter to a brisk foam.
4. Fry fillets 2 minutes per side to deep gold, adding remaining butter as you turn.
5. Baste 30 seconds; the fish is done when it flakes at the thickest point (55–58°C).
6. Drain on a rack; salt immediately.
7. Serve at once with remoulade and lemon.

## Plating & Service
Two fillets per plate, remoulade in a proud spoonful, lemon and boiled potatoes with dill — Danish lunch, done properly.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF flour and crumbs work; sole or lemon sole substitute; remoulade doubles for fried mussels.
