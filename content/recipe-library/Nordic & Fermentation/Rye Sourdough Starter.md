# Rye Sourdough Starter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The engine of every rye loaf on board — a week of small stirs builds years of bread.

## Ingredients (serves 20)
- Wholegrain rye flour (total across the week) — 500 g
- Filtered water, 26–28°C (total across the week) — 500 ml
- Clean 1 L jar — 1
- For maintenance: rye flour — 50 g per feed
- For maintenance: water — 50 ml per feed

## Method
1. SAFETY: this is a wild ferment — keep at 24–26°C; total establishment time 6–7 days; it must smell sour-fruity (yoghurt, apple, vinegar); pink or orange streaks or a putrid smell mean discard and restart; a grey liquid layer ('hooch') is harmless — stir it in.
2. Day 1: whisk 100 g rye flour with 100 ml water in the jar; lid loose; hold at 24–26°C.
3. Day 2: rest — bubbles may appear.
4. Days 3–6: every 24 hours discard half, feed with 100 g flour + 100 ml water; the smell moves from cheesy to cleanly sour.
5. Day 6–7: it should double within 6–8 hours of feeding and pass the float test (a spoonful floats in water). It is ready.
6. Maintain: feed daily at room temperature, or weekly from the fridge (feed, rest 2 hours warm, refrigerate).
7. Before baking, give 1–2 feeds at 24–26°C until doubling reliably.

## Plating & Service
Not plated — but the discard makes excellent crispbread and blini batters; never waste it.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Convert to a wheat starter with three consecutive wheat feeds; a spoon of honey rescues a sluggish winter starter.
