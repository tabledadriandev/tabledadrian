# Lacto Green Strawberry Condiment

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Unripe strawberries — usually waste — become a rhubarb-like sour garnish that reads as pure luxury on crudo.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Green/white under-ripe strawberries, hulled — 800 g
- Fine sea salt (non-iodised) — 16 g
- Elderflower head, fresh or 2 g dried — 3 g
- For service: olive oil — 20 ml
- For service: white pepper — 1 g
- For service: small basil leaves — 4 g
- For service: finely diced shallot — 20 g
- For service: strained ferment juice — 30 ml

## Method
1. SAFETY: 2% salt; ferment at 20–23°C; total fermentation time 4–5 days; keep berries submerged and burp daily.
2. Halve strawberries, toss with salt and elderflower.
3. Vacuum-seal at 99% or weight under a bag in a sterilised jar.
4. Ferment at 20–23°C, tasting from day 3.
5. Stop at day 4–5 when sour, green-fruity and lightly effervescent; pH 4.0 or below.
6. Refrigerate at 2–4°C, up to 3 weeks.
7. For the condiment, dice berries, fold with shallot, oil, juice and pepper; finish with basil.

## Plating & Service
Spoon a neat line beside sea bream crudo or seared scallops; the juice can deglaze a fish pan for an instant sour glaze.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Ripe strawberries make a dessert version in 3 days — serve those over skyr ice cream with olive oil.
