# Aebleflaesk with Lacto Apples

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Denmark's crispy pork-and-apples, upgraded: half the apples are lacto-fermented so the pan sauce bites back.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Pork belly slices, 5 mm thick — 500 g
- Fine sea salt — 3 g
- Apples, cored, thick wedges — 300 g
- Lacto-fermented apple or plum wedges (larder) — 150 g
- Onion, sliced — 120 g
- Thyme leaves — 2 g
- Caster sugar — 10 g
- Cider vinegar or ferment brine — 15 ml
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Chives — 6 g

## Method
1. Lay pork slices in a cold, dry, wide pan; render over medium heat, turning, 12–15 minutes until deeply crisp. Salt lightly.
2. Remove pork; keep warm. Pour off all but 2 spoons of fat.
3. Soften onion in the fat, 4 minutes.
4. Add fresh apple wedges and sugar; caramelise 5–6 minutes until gold but holding shape.
5. Off heat, fold in the lacto apples and brine — they should stay tart and alive.
6. Season with thyme and pepper.
7. Return pork to the pan just to reheat.

## Plating & Service
Straight from the pan at the table: pork shingled over the apples, chives thrown on — rye bread mops it up.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Smoked bacon is the classic shortcut; vegetarians get the apple-onion pan with fried halloumi (adds dairy).
