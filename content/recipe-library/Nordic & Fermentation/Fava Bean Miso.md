# Fava Bean Miso

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Broad beans give a miso that is meaty and faintly bitter-green — the one to pair with lamb, morels and spring alliums.

## Ingredients (serves 30)
- Dried split fava beans — 800 g
- Fresh barley koji — 800 g
- Fine sea salt (non-iodised) — 192 g
- Bean cooking liquor — 160 ml
- Savory or thyme sprigs (aged in, then removed) — 3 g
- Extra salt for surface — 15 g
- Sterilised 2.5 L crock — 1

## Method
1. SAFETY: 12% salt (1600 g base = 192 g); age at 18–24°C; total fermentation time 90–120 days; anaerobic pack, monthly inspection, skim kahm, trim or discard on coloured mould.
2. Soak favas 8 hours; simmer 40–45 minutes to a crushable bean; drain and cool to 30°C, reserving liquor.
3. Mash coarsely; mix with koji, salt, herbs and liquor to a stiff paste.
4. Pack in slammed-down layers; level, salt the top, film and weight.
5. Age at 18–24°C, harvesting tamari as it rises.
6. From day 90 taste monthly; target deep umami with a green, almost matcha bitterness. Remove herb stems.
7. Refrigerate at 2–4°C when ready; keeps 1 year.

## Plating & Service
Rub on lamb rump before roasting; whisk into spring-vegetable braises; loosen with mushroom stock for a sauce base.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Note: avoid serving to guests with G6PD deficiency (favism); rice koji version is gluten-free.
