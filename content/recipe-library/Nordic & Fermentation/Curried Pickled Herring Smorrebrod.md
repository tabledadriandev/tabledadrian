# Curried Pickled Herring Smorrebrod

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Danish lunch canon: sweet-sour herring in curry cream on dark rye — ours starts with herring we pickle in-house.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Pickled herring fillets (see method for quick house pickle), sliced — 300 g
- Quick pickle: cider vinegar 200 ml, water 100 ml, sugar 80 g, bay 1 g, allspice 2 g
- Crème fraîche — 80 g
- Mayonnaise — 40 g
- Madras curry powder, toasted — 6 g
- Apple, fine dice — 80 g
- Shallot, fine dice — 30 g
- Lacto cucumber (larder), diced — 60 g
- Dark rye bread slices — 4 (approx. 200 g)
- Butter — 30 g
- Eggs, boiled 8 minutes, quartered — 2
- Dill and cress — 8 g

## Method
1. House pickle (2 days ahead): simmer vinegar, water, sugar and spices 2 minutes; cool fully; submerge fresh salt-matured herring fillets; refrigerate 48 hours at 2–4°C.
2. Toast curry powder in a dry pan 30 seconds until fragrant.
3. Whisk crème fraîche, mayonnaise and curry into a sauce.
4. Fold in sliced herring, apple, shallot and lacto cucumber; rest 20 minutes, chilled.
5. Butter the rye generously, edge to edge.
6. Spoon the curry herring on thickly.
7. Finish with egg quarters, dill and cress.

## Plating & Service
Knife-and-fork open sandwich; serve with cold beer and ceremony — this is lunch, not a snack.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Good jarred herring shortcuts step 1 honestly; skyr can replace crème fraîche for a lighter dressing.
