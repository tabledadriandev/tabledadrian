# Lacto Kohlrabi with Dill Seed

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Juicy, sour kohlrabi batons that behave like a cross between pickle and fresh apple — a crunchy foil for rich mains.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Kohlrabi, peeled, cut into 1 cm batons — 900 g
- Fine sea salt (non-iodised) — 18 g
- Dill seed — 3 g
- Yellow mustard seed — 2 g
- Horseradish, sliced — 10 g
- For service: dill fronds — 5 g
- For service: rapeseed oil — 15 ml
- For service: flaky salt — 1 g

## Method
1. SAFETY: 2% salt by weight; ferment at 20–23°C; total fermentation time 5–7 days; vegetables must remain submerged in their own brine.
2. Toss batons with salt and spices; massage 2 minutes until wet.
3. Pack tightly into a sterilised 1.5 L jar, pressing until brine covers, or vacuum-seal at 99%.
4. Weight down; ferment at 20–23°C.
5. Taste from day 4; stop at day 5–7 when sour and still crisp; pH 3.9 or below.
6. Refrigerate at 2–4°C; keeps 2 months.
7. Serve cold, dressed with oil, dill and flaky salt.

## Plating & Service
Pile beside smoked pork or fried fish as the sharp element; dice into potato salads instead of gherkins.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Turnip or radish work identically; add 1 sliced chilli for a spicier jar aimed at smørrebrød.
