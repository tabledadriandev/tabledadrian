# Prawn Shell Garum

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Every langoustine and prawn shell on board goes into this — a bisque-meets-fish-sauce elixir for shellfish emulsions.

## Ingredients (serves 30)
- Prawn/langoustine shells and heads, fresh — 800 g
- Fresh rice koji — 200 g
- Filtered water — 700 ml
- Fine sea salt (non-iodised) — 204 g
- Tomato paste — 30 g
- Sterilised 2 L jar — 1
- Muslin for straining — 1

## Method
1. SAFETY: 12% salt on total weight (1700 g ≈ 204 g); hold at 60°C for the entire ferment; total fermentation time 8–10 weeks; shells must be same-day fresh, never previously frozen and thawed twice.
2. Roast shells at 180°C for 12–15 minutes until coral-red and fragrant; crush with a rolling pin.
3. Combine shells, koji, water, salt and tomato paste in the jar.
4. Hold at 60°C, lid loose, stirring twice weekly.
5. From week 8 taste: intensely crustacean, sweet-saline, faint roasted tomato.
6. Strain through muslin without pressing; chill and lift any surface fat (reserve — prawn fat butter is superb).
7. Bottle; refrigerate at 2–4°C up to 6 months.

## Plating & Service
Whip 5 ml into hollandaise for grilled lobster; season shellfish rice, bisques and brown-butter sauces at the pass.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Crab shells work equally well; strict shellfish-allergy charters get the Egg White or Yeast Garum instead.
