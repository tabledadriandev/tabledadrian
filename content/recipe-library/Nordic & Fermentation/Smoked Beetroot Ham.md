# Smoked Beetroot Ham

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Whole salt-baked beets brined, smoked and glazed like ham — the vegan centrepiece that omnivores steal from.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Large beetroots, even-sized, scrubbed — 1200 g
- Coarse salt (for baking crust) — 800 g
- Brine: filtered water 1000 ml, fine sea salt 60 g, brown sugar 40 g, bay 1 g, cloves 1 g, black pepper 2 g
- Beech chips — 60 g
- Glaze: maple syrup 60 g, house shoyu or soy 30 ml, cider vinegar 20 ml, Dijon mustard 15 g

## Method
1. SAFETY: this is cooked-vegetable curing, low risk — but brine refrigerated at 2–4°C and use within 5 days of smoking; total process 2 days (bake + overnight brine + smoke).
2. Bake beets buried in the coarse salt at 180°C for 100–120 minutes until a skewer slides in; cool, crack off crust, peel.
3. Simmer brine ingredients 3 minutes; chill fully.
4. Submerge peeled beets in cold brine; refrigerate overnight (12–16 hours).
5. Pat dry; hot-smoke at 90°C over beech for 40 minutes.
6. Whisk glaze; score beets in a ham-like diamond pattern, brush and roast at 200°C for 15 minutes, glazing twice more.
7. Rest 5 minutes; carve in thick slices.

## Plating & Service
Carve at the table with fermented mustard, dill potatoes and lacto cucumber — a Sunday-ham tableau, meat-free.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Celeriac works identically (tag celery); leftover slices sear beautifully for breakfast 'ham' and eggs.
