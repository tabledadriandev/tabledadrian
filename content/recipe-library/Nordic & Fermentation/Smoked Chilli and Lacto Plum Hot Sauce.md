# Smoked Chilli and Lacto Plum Hot Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Alder-smoked red chillies fermented with plum — a brooding, barbecue-ready sauce that loves pork and grilled salmon.

## Ingredients (serves 30)
- Red fresno or ripe jalapeño chillies, stemmed — 400 g
- Lacto-fermented plums with juice (from the larder) — 200 g
- Shallot, chopped — 60 g
- Fine sea salt (non-iodised) — 14 g
- Alder or beech wood chips for smoking — 30 g
- Cider vinegar (post-ferment) — 80 ml
- Date syrup — 20 g
- Smoked paprika (optional bolster) — 3 g

## Method
1. SAFETY: mash ferment at 2.5% salt on chilli+shallot weight (460 g ≈ 12 g; 14 g covers the smoke moisture loss); ferment at 20–24°C; total fermentation time 8–12 days; final blended sauce must reach pH 3.7 or below.
2. Cold-smoke the chillies over alder for 45 minutes below 30°C (or hot-smoke 15–20 minutes at 90°C for a softer texture and faster route).
3. Blitz smoked chillies and shallot with salt to a coarse mash; pack into a sterilised jar under its brine, weighted.
4. Ferment at 20–24°C, pressing down daily; taste from day 7.
5. At day 8–12, blend the mash with lacto plums and their juice, vinegar and date syrup until velvet.
6. Pass through a chinois; verify pH ≤ 3.7.
7. Bottle; refrigerate at 2–4°C, burping weekly; keeps 3 months.

## Plating & Service
Brush onto pork ribs in the final minutes; a stripe under grilled salmon with charred spring onion is a menu staple.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No smoker? Use 5 g smoked paprika and 2 chipotle; apricot lacto-ferment swaps for plum in summer.
