# Almond Rice Cream with Lacto Plum Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Risalamande reimagined for the fermentation larder — cold almond rice cream under a warm, sour lacto-plum glaze.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Pudding rice — 150 g
- Whole milk — 800 ml
- Vanilla pod, split — 1 g
- Caster sugar — 60 g
- Fine sea salt — 1 g
- Almonds, blanched, chopped — 80 g
- Double cream — 250 ml
- Lacto plum wedges with juice (larder) — 200 g
- Honey — 30 g
- Butter — 20 g

## Method
1. Rinse rice; simmer gently with milk, vanilla and salt, stirring often, 35–40 minutes until thick and tender.
2. Stir in sugar; cool completely, film on the surface, then chill.
3. Fold in chopped almonds.
4. Whip cream to soft peaks; fold into the cold rice in two additions — light, not stiff.
5. Chill 1 hour.
6. Sauce: warm lacto plum juice with honey to a light syrup; add plum wedges and butter; swirl until glossy — keep the plums' sourness alive, 2 minutes only.
7. Serve the cold rice cream with the warm sauce over.

## Plating & Service
Deep bowls, sauce poured at the table — the hot-sour against cold-sweet is the whole point.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The Danish Christmas game: hide one whole almond, winner gets a prize; cherry compote is the classic if plums run dry.
