# Hazelnut Miso

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Roasted hazelnuts fermented into a gianduja-savoury paste — devastating with brown butter, celeriac and aged beef.

## Ingredients (serves 25)
- Hazelnuts, skinned — 600 g
- Fresh barley koji — 600 g
- Fine sea salt (non-iodised) — 144 g
- Filtered water — 120 ml
- Extra salt for surface — 12 g
- Sterilised 1.5 L crock — 1

## Method
1. SAFETY: 12% salt on nut+koji weight (1200 g = 144 g); age at 18–22°C; total fermentation time 60–90 days; nut misos are oil-rich — press extra firmly to exclude air and check monthly for rancid smells (discard if present).
2. Roast hazelnuts at 160°C for 12–15 minutes to deep gold; cool completely.
3. Pulse to a coarse rubble — not butter; some texture should survive.
4. Mix with crumbled koji, salt and water to a stiff, clumping paste.
5. Pack hard into the crock, layer by layer; level, salt the surface, film and weight.
6. Age at 18–22°C. Skim kahm if it appears; pour off any tamari and bottle it.
7. Taste from day 60 — roasted, sweet-salty, praline-meets-soy. Stop when it thrills you (by day 90).
8. Refrigerate at 2–4°C; keeps 8 months.

## Plating & Service
Melt a spoon into brown butter for turbot or celeriac; spread thinly inside a game-bird slider; whisk with honey over ice cream.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Almonds or walnuts work at the same ratios; rice koji version is gluten-free — always flag the nut allergen.
