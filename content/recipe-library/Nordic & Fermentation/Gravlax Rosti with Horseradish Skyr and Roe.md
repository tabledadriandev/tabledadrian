# Gravlax Rosti with Horseradish Skyr and Roe

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Crisp potato cake, cold house gravlax, sharp skyr and popping roe — the brunch plate that sells the whole charter.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Waxy potatoes, peeled — 600 g
- Butter, clarified — 60 g
- Fine sea salt — 4 g
- House gravlax (larder), sliced thin — 200 g
- Skyr, full-fat — 150 g
- Fresh horseradish, grated — 12 g
- Lemon juice — 8 ml
- Juniper-cured trout roe (larder) — 40 g
- Dill sprigs — 6 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Coarsely grate potatoes; squeeze absolutely dry in a cloth (this is the whole game); season with salt.
2. Heat half the clarified butter in a 24 cm non-stick pan; press in the potato as a 1.5 cm cake.
3. Fry over medium 8–10 minutes until the underside is deep gold; invert onto a plate.
4. Add remaining butter; slide the cake back; fry 8 minutes more.
5. Whisk skyr with horseradish, lemon and pepper.
6. Rest rösti 2 minutes on a rack (keeps the base crisp); cut into quarters.
7. Top each quarter: skyr first, gravlax draped, roe spooned, dill last.

## Plating & Service
Serve while the rösti still crackles — assemble at the last moment or the steam wins.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Hot-smoked char instead of gravlax for a warm version; dairy-free with whipped tofu-lemon cream.
