# Butter Bean Miso with Lemon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A blond, mild miso with candied-lemon lift — the gateway miso for guests, gorgeous in beurre blanc and vinaigrettes.

## Ingredients (serves 30)
- Dried butter beans — 800 g
- Fresh barley koji — 800 g
- Fine sea salt (non-iodised) — 192 g
- Lemon zest, finely grated (no pith) — 12 g
- Bean cooking liquor — 150 ml
- Extra salt for surface — 15 g
- Sterilised 2.5 L crock — 1

## Method
1. SAFETY: 12% salt on bean+koji weight (1600 g = 192 g); age at 18–24°C; total fermentation time 75–100 days; surface must stay salted, filmed and weighted.
2. Soak beans 12 hours; simmer 50–60 minutes until a bean crushes cleanly; drain, keep liquor, cool to 30°C.
3. Mash beans coarsely; mix with koji, salt, lemon zest and enough liquor for a stiff, fudgy paste.
4. Ball and slam into the crock to exclude air; level, salt, film, weight.
5. Age at 18–24°C; skim kahm, harvest tamari monthly.
6. Taste from day 75: mild, sweet, citrus-bright. Stop by day 100 for a blond profile.
7. Refrigerate at 2–4°C; keeps 1 year.

## Plating & Service
Whisk 30 g into a classic beurre blanc for poached fish; thin with olive oil and lemon juice as a crudité dip.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chickpeas work identically; rice koji keeps it gluten-free — the zest may be swapped for preserved-lemon rind at packing.
