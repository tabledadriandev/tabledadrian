# Fermented Habanero and Sea Buckthorn Hot Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Tropical heat meets Nordic sour berry — a lava-orange sauce with passionfruit lift that guests hoard.

## Ingredients (serves 30)
- Habanero chillies, stemmed, halved — 300 g
- Yellow pepper, chopped — 300 g
- Carrot, chopped — 100 g
- Garlic cloves — 20 g
- Fine sea salt (non-iodised) — 22 g
- Sea buckthorn juice (added post-ferment) — 120 ml
- Cider vinegar (post-ferment, for stability) — 60 ml
- Honey or agave — 25 g

## Method
1. SAFETY: mash ferment at 2.5% salt on vegetable weight (720 g = ~18 g; round to 22 g including brine correction); ferment at 20–24°C; total fermentation time 10–14 days; wear gloves; finished sauce must sit at pH 3.7 or below — verify with strips before bottling.
2. Blitz chillies, pepper, carrot and garlic to a coarse mash with the salt.
3. Pack into a sterilised 1 L jar, press down until brine rises; weight, lid loosely or airlock.
4. Ferment at 20–24°C, pressing the mash under its brine daily for the first 4 days.
5. From day 10 taste: bright, hot, sour and faintly funky. Stop by day 14.
6. Blend the mash with sea buckthorn juice, vinegar and honey until totally smooth; pass through a chinois.
7. Check pH ≤ 3.7; bottle and refrigerate at 2–4°C. Keeps 3 months (it stays mildly alive — burp bottles weekly).

## Plating & Service
Dot on grilled prawns, fried chicken or oysters; whisk a spoon into mayo for a fiery smørrebrød spread.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skip honey for strict sugar-free; mango replaces sea buckthorn in a pinch — adjust vinegar up to hold pH.
