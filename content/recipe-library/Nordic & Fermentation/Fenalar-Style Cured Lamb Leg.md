# Fenalar-Style Cured Lamb Leg

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A small boned lamb leg cured and air-dried the Norwegian mountain way — carved paper-thin, it is pure heather and salt.

## Ingredients (serves 20)
- Lamb leg, boned, trimmed, in one muscle-block — 2000 g
- Coarse sea salt — 300 g
- Caster sugar — 60 g
- Juniper berries, crushed — 8 g
- Black pepper, cracked — 6 g
- Dried thyme — 3 g
- Muslin and butcher's twine — 1 set

## Method
1. SAFETY: salt-box cure 1.5 days per 500 g (6 days for 2 kg) at 2–4°C; then air-dry at 12–15°C, 70–75% humidity until 35% weight loss (5–8 weeks) — weigh and label at hanging; any soft, wet or sulphurous patch means trim wide or discard; slice thin and keep chilled once cut.
2. Mix salt, sugar, juniper, pepper and thyme.
3. Rub the lamb hard on all faces; pack into a tray with remaining cure over and under.
4. Cure 6 days at 2–4°C, flipping daily and draining liquid.
5. Rinse, soak 1 hour in cold water, pat dry; rest uncovered in the fridge 24 hours.
6. Wrap in muslin, tie, and hang at 12–15°C / 70–75% RH.
7. Weigh weekly; at 35% loss (5–8 weeks) the leg is firm with a deep-rose centre.
8. Rest wrapped, chilled, 3 days before the first carve.

## Plating & Service
Shave translucent slices onto a board with butter, crispbread and lacto kohlrabi — aperitif hour, sorted.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> A 700 g lamb rump makes a 3-week 'starter' version; brush with birch syrup before hanging for a sweet edge.
