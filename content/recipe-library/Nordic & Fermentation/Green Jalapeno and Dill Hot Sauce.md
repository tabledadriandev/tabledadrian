# Green Jalapeno and Dill Hot Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A grass-green, herbaceous hot sauce that reads Scandinavian — dill, green chilli and cucumber coolness for fish dishes.

## Ingredients (serves 30)
- Green jalapeños, stemmed — 400 g
- Green pepper, chopped — 150 g
- Cucumber, peeled, seeded — 100 g
- Fine sea salt (non-iodised) — 16 g
- Dill, fronds and stems — 30 g
- White wine vinegar (post-ferment) — 70 ml
- Lime juice — 20 ml
- Rapeseed oil (to emulsify) — 30 ml

## Method
1. SAFETY: 2.5% salt on vegetable weight (650 g ≈ 16 g); ferment at 20–24°C; total fermentation time 7–10 days; gloves on; final sauce pH must be 3.7 or below before bottling.
2. Blitz chillies, pepper and cucumber with salt to a coarse mash.
3. Pack under its own brine in a sterilised jar; weight; lid loose or airlock.
4. Ferment at 20–24°C, pressing down daily; taste from day 6.
5. Stop at day 7–10 while still vividly green-tasting.
6. Blend with dill, vinegar and lime until smooth; stream in oil last for a stable, glossy emulsion; pass through a chinois.
7. Check pH ≤ 3.7; bottle; refrigerate at 2–4°C, 2 months (the green fades slowly — use it).

## Plating & Service
Dot around cured salmon or fried plaice; a spoon in crème fraîche makes an instant dressing for prawn smørrebrød.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Serrano lifts the heat a level; swap dill for lovage for a celery-forward version (tag celery).
