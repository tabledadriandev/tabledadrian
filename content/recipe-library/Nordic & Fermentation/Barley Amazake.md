# Barley Amazake

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Naturally sweet fermented barley porridge — a sugar replacement for glazes, ice creams and breakfast bowls with deep malt character.

## Ingredients (serves 15)
- Cooked pearl barley porridge (from 250 g raw barley) — 700 g
- Fresh barley koji — 350 g
- Filtered water — 350 ml
- Fine sea salt — 2 g
- For glaze use: strained amazake — 200 ml
- For drinking: extra water and grated ginger — 100 ml / 5 g

## Method
1. SAFETY: saccharify at 55–60°C for 8–10 hours — below 55°C souring bacteria can take over, above 65°C enzymes denature; afterwards boil 2 minutes to stabilise, then chill to 4°C; keeps 10 days.
2. Cook barley in 3× water until fully soft; cool the porridge to 60°C.
3. Stir in koji and water; the mix should feel like loose risotto.
4. Hold at 58–60°C (rice cooker on 'keep warm' with lid ajar, or circulator bag) for 8–10 hours, stirring every 2 hours.
5. Done when intensely sweet, like malted honey. Taste at 8 hours.
6. Boil 2 minutes to halt enzymes; blend smooth or leave rustic; add salt.
7. Chill fast in a shallow tray to 4°C.

## Plating & Service
Whisk into whipped skyr for a no-sugar dessert cream, or reduce by half into a lacquer for roast carrots and pork.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Rice koji + rice porridge makes it gluten-free; diabetic guests should treat it as a sugar (high natural glucose).
