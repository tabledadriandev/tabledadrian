# Chanterelles on Toast with Koji Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Foraged apricot-scented chanterelles on sourdough with sweet koji butter — five ingredients doing the work of fifteen.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chanterelles, brushed clean, torn — 400 g
- Butter, soft — 80 g
- Shio koji — 20 g
- Sourdough slices, thick — 4 (approx. 200 g)
- Rapeseed oil — 20 ml
- Shallot, minced — 30 g
- Parsley, chopped — 8 g
- Lemon juice — 8 ml
- Flaky salt and pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Beat the soft butter with shio koji — the koji butter keeps for weeks and belongs on everything.
2. Dry-fry chanterelles in a hot pan 2–3 minutes until their water is gone (never wash them wet into a pan).
3. Add oil and shallot; sauté 2 minutes.
4. Add half the koji butter; toss until foaming and nutty; season with lemon and pepper.
5. Grill or pan-toast the sourdough golden; spread with remaining koji butter while hot.
6. Pile mushrooms on the toasts.
7. Finish with parsley and flaky salt.

## Plating & Service
One loaded toast per plate, pan juices spooned over the edge — serve immediately, knife and fork.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Ceps or girolles by season; vegan with plant butter — the koji carries it.
