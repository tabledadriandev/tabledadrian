# Yeast Garum

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Fresh baker's yeast autolysed into a vegan soy-sauce cousin with roast-chicken depth — indispensable for plant-based menus.

## Ingredients (serves 40)
- Fresh baker's yeast, crumbled — 500 g
- Fresh rice koji — 250 g
- Filtered water — 600 ml
- Fine sea salt (non-iodised) — 162 g
- Sterilised 1.5 L jar — 1
- Muslin for straining — 1

## Method
1. SAFETY: 12% salt on total weight (1350 g = 162 g); hold at 60°C; total fermentation time 3–4 weeks (yeast breaks down fastest of all garums); stir often to prevent settling.
2. Whisk yeast, koji, water and salt into a smooth slurry.
3. Hold at 60°C, lid loose, stirring three times weekly.
4. From week 2 the aroma turns from bready to roasted-meaty (autolysis in action).
5. Taste at week 3: savoury, salty, faintly malty. Stop by week 4.
6. Strain through muslin; press gently this time — yields matter.
7. Bottle; refrigerate at 2–4°C, 6 months.

## Plating & Service
The vegan 'jus' base: whisk 20 ml into 200 ml reduced mushroom stock with 20 g cold vegan butter for a glossy sauce.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 50 g toasted onion during the ferment for a French-onion register; dried yeast works at 40% of the weight, hydrated first.
