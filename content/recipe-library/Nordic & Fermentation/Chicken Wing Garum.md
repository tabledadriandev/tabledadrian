# Chicken Wing Garum

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Roasted wings ferment into a golden 'liquid schmaltz-soy' — the secret spine of our poultry jus and Caesar-style dressings.

## Ingredients (serves 40)
- Chicken wings, chopped through the bone — 1000 g
- Fresh barley koji — 250 g
- Filtered water — 800 ml
- Fine sea salt (non-iodised) — 240 g
- Sterilised 2 L jar — 1
- Muslin for straining — 1

## Method
1. SAFETY: 12% salt on total weight; hold at 60°C for the entire ferment; total fermentation time 10–12 weeks (bones slow it slightly); skim fat weekly; discard if putrid or ammoniacal.
2. Roast wings at 200°C for 35–40 minutes to deep golden; keep every scrap of pan juice.
3. Combine wings, juices, koji, water and salt in the jar; stir.
4. Hold at 60°C, lid loose. Stir twice weekly; lift off the clear fat cap each week and reserve it (fermented chicken fat = gold for roasting potatoes).
5. Taste from week 10: roasted, brothy, salty-sweet.
6. Strain through muslin, drip without pressing, then chill and lift any final fat.
7. Bottle; refrigerate at 2–4°C up to 6 months.

## Plating & Service
Whisk into vinaigrette for charred gem lettuce, brush over rotisserie birds in the last minutes, or round out ramen-style broths.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Duck wings give a gamier garum; keep the skimmed fat for a fermented-fat mayonnaise.
