# Rosehip Kombucha

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Autumn hedgerow in a glass — tangy, apricot-skinned and rose-tinted, with enough acidity to double as a sauce base.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Filtered water — 2000 ml
- Caster sugar — 140 g
- Black tea leaves — 10 g
- Dried rosehips, crushed — 60 g
- Kombucha SCOBY with 200 ml starter — 1
- Orange zest strip — 2 g
- Sterilised 3 L jar + swing-top bottles — 1 set

## Method
1. SAFETY: 10% mature starter to drop pH below 4.5 from day one; ferment 21–26°C; total fermentation time 10–15 days including bottle conditioning; strain rosehips through muslin — their sliver-hairs are irritants.
2. Boil water, dissolve sugar, steep tea 5 minutes at 90°C; strain, cool below 30°C.
3. Jar with starter and SCOBY; cloth cover; primary ferment 7–10 days at 21–26°C.
4. Remove SCOBY + 200 ml starter for the next brew.
5. Add crushed rosehips and orange zest; steep 24 hours chilled; strain through muslin twice.
6. Bottle with 3 cm headspace; condition 3–5 days at 21°C; refrigerate.
7. Serve within 3 weeks.

## Plating & Service
Pour at 6°C alongside game dishes; reduce 300 ml by two-thirds into a sweet-sour glaze for roasted squash.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Hibiscus gives similar acidity year-round; blend 50/50 with sparkling water for a lighter table drink.
