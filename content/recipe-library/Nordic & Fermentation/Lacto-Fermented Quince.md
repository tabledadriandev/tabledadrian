# Lacto-Fermented Quince

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Perfumed autumn fruit turned sour and floral — shave it raw over cheese, game and brown-butter desserts.

## Ingredients (serves 14)
- Quince, ripe, peeled, cored, cut in 2 cm wedges — 1000 g
- Fine sea salt (non-iodised) — 20 g
- Lemon juice (anti-browning, in the bag) — 15 ml
- Bay leaf — 1 g
- For service: honey — 20 g
- For service: walnut oil — 15 ml
- For service: thyme leaves — 2 g
- For service: cracked pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. SAFETY: 2% salt; ferment at 21–24°C; total fermentation time 6–8 days (quince is dense and slow); keep submerged, burp daily.
2. Toss wedges immediately with salt, lemon juice and bay to limit oxidation.
3. Vacuum-seal at 99% in a single layer.
4. Ferment at 21–24°C; the perfume shifts from raw pear-floral to cider-like.
5. Taste at day 6; stop by day 8 when sour, tender-crisp and intensely aromatic; pH 4.0 or below.
6. Refrigerate at 2–4°C; keeps 1 month. Reserve the golden juice for vinaigrettes.
7. For service, shave wedges thinly, dress with honey, walnut oil, thyme, pepper.

## Plating & Service
Drape shavings over aged Comté or roast partridge; the juice sharpens a game jus in seconds.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Firm pears ferment in 4–5 days as a softer alternative; skip honey for a strictly sugar-free garnish.
