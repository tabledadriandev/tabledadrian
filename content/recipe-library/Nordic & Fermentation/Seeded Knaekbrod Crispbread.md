# Seeded Knaekbrod Crispbread

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Snapping seed-heavy crispbread that keeps for weeks — the eternal companion to cured fish and cheese.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Rye flour — 150 g
- Rolled oats — 100 g
- Sunflower seeds — 80 g
- Pumpkin seeds — 80 g
- Flax seeds — 50 g
- Sesame seeds — 40 g
- Rapeseed oil — 50 ml
- Fine sea salt — 6 g
- Boiling water — 200 ml
- Flaky salt (topping) — 3 g

## Method
1. Stir all dry ingredients together.
2. Add oil and boiling water; mix into a stiff, spreadable paste; rest 10 minutes (the flax binds).
3. Divide between two parchment sheets; top with more parchment; roll to 3 mm — thinness is everything.
4. Peel off the top sheets; score into rough rectangles with a pizza wheel; scatter flaky salt.
5. Bake at 170°C for 22–25 minutes, rotating once, until golden and rigid at the centre.
6. Cool completely on the trays — they crisp as they cool.
7. Snap along the scores; store airtight up to 3 weeks.

## Plating & Service
Stand shards upright by the cheese and gravlax boards; crush over salads for seeded crunch.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: buckwheat flour + GF oats; press caraway into the surface for a schnapps-friendly version.
