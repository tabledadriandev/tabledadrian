# Spruce-Cured Venison Loin

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Venison cured with young spruce tips — resinous, cool-forest flavour over deep red meat, carved like bresaola.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Venison loin, trimmed of all silverskin — 800 g
- Coarse sea salt — 64 g
- Caster sugar — 40 g
- Spruce or fir tips, young, chopped (or 4 g rosemary + 2 g juniper) — 20 g
- Black pepper, cracked — 4 g
- Gin — 20 ml
- Muslin — 1

## Method
1. SAFETY: 8% salt cure at 2–4°C for 48 hours; then dry, wrapped in muslin, in the fridge for 7–10 days to ~20% weight loss; use only correctly identified edible conifer tips (spruce/fir — NEVER yew, which is deadly); slice thin, keep chilled, eat within 2 weeks.
2. Blitz salt, sugar, spruce tips and pepper to a green, piney cure.
3. Rub the loin with gin, then coat with the cure on all sides.
4. Wrap tightly in film; cure 48 hours at 2–4°C, flipping once.
5. Rinse quickly, pat dry.
6. Wrap in muslin; rest on a rack in the fridge 7–10 days until firm and 20% lighter.
7. Carve across the grain at 2 mm.

## Plating & Service
Lay slices flat, dot with skyr whipped with horseradish, add lacto blueberries and rye crumbs — a forest on porcelain.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Beef fillet takes the identical cure; Douglas fir tips give a citrus register in spring.
