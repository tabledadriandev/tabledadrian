# Roast Carrots with Pumpkin Seed Miso Dressing

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Blistered carrots, tangy carrot-buckthorn ferment and a green-gold miso dressing — three registers of the same vegetable.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Heritage carrots, scrubbed, halved lengthwise — 600 g
- Rapeseed oil — 30 ml
- Fine sea salt — 3 g
- Pumpkin seed miso — 30 g
- Cider vinegar — 20 ml
- Maple syrup — 10 g
- Water — 20 ml
- Carrot & sea buckthorn ferment (larder), drained — 100 g
- Toasted pumpkin seeds — 30 g
- Carrot tops or parsley, chopped — 8 g

## Method
1. Toss carrots with oil and salt; roast cut-side down at 210°C for 25–30 minutes until blistered and tender.
2. Whisk miso, vinegar, maple and water into a pourable dressing.
3. Warm the roasted carrots 2 more minutes if they've cooled.
4. Swipe dressing across the plate.
5. Pile carrots over; scatter the cold fermented carrot on top for acid crunch.
6. Finish with pumpkin seeds and chopped tops.
7. Season with a last pinch of salt.

## Plating & Service
A shareable platter beside roast chicken or as part of a vegetable table; contrast of hot/cold is the point.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Hazelnut miso version is richer (adds nuts); parsnips work in winter at 200°C for 35 minutes.
