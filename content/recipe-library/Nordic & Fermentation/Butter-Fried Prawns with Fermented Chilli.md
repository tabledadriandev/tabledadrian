# Butter-Fried Prawns with Fermented Chilli

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Shell-on prawns rolled in foaming butter and house hot sauce — ten minutes, one pan, napkins everywhere.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Large prawns, shell on, deveined — 800 g
- Butter — 80 g
- Rapeseed oil — 20 ml
- Fermented habanero & sea buckthorn hot sauce (larder) — 30 ml
- Garlic, sliced — 12 g
- Lemon — 80 g
- Fine sea salt — 3 g
- Dill and parsley, chopped — 10 g
- Good bread for mopping — 200 g

## Method
1. Dry the prawns thoroughly; salt lightly.
2. Heat oil in the widest pan until shimmering; sear prawns 90 seconds per side until the shells blister.
3. Lower heat; add butter and garlic; foam 1 minute, basting.
4. Off heat, add the hot sauce and a hard squeeze of lemon; roll the prawns through the emulsifying pan sauce.
5. Taste — the sauce should be hot, sour, buttery in equal parts.
6. Shower with herbs.
7. Straight to the table in the pan.

## Plating & Service
Pan on a trivet, bread and lemon on the side, finger bowls out — informal, loud, perfect deck lunch.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Langoustines elevate it; the green jalapeño-dill sauce version reads completely different and equally good.
