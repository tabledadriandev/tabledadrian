# Grilled Leeks with Yeast Garum Butter and Hazelnuts

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Blackened whole leeks, steamed in their own jackets, dressed in vegan-umami butter — humble, smoky, luxurious.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Leeks, medium, roots trimmed, whole — 4 (approx. 600 g)
- Butter — 80 g
- Yeast garum — 15 ml
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Toasted hazelnuts, crushed — 40 g
- Chives, sliced — 8 g
- Rapeseed oil — 10 ml
- Flaky salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Rub leeks with oil; grill over high heat (or roast at 250°C), turning, 15–20 minutes until the outer layers are charred black and the centres steam soft — a knife should slide through.
2. Rest 5 minutes wrapped in foil to finish steaming.
3. Melt butter to foaming; take off heat; whisk in yeast garum and lemon.
4. Split leeks lengthwise; peel back the burnt jackets to reveal the silky cores.
5. Season cores with flaky salt.
6. Spoon the garum butter generously into the layers.
7. Shower with hazelnuts and chives.

## Plating & Service
Two halves per plate, cut side up, butter pooling; serve warm, not hot, so the sweetness shows.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan with plant butter; swap hazelnuts for toasted rye crumbs (adds gluten, drops nuts).
