# Lacto Cep Ferment

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Fermented porcini are umami grenades; the exuded juice is arguably the single best pan-sauce seasoning in the Nordic larder.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Fresh ceps (porcini), cleaned, firm, sliced 1 cm — 1000 g
- Fine sea salt (non-iodised) — 20 g
- Thyme sprig — 2 g
- For mushroom butter: strained cep juice — 60 ml
- Cold butter, diced — 120 g
- Lemon juice — 5 ml
- Chives, sliced — 5 g
- Cracked pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. SAFETY: 2% salt by weight; ferment at 21–24°C; total fermentation time 5–7 days; use only firm, worm-free mushrooms and keep everything submerged in exuded liquid.
2. Toss cep slices with salt and thyme; rest 20 minutes until they weep.
3. Vacuum-seal at 99% with all juices, or press into a jar under a weighted bag.
4. Ferment at 21–24°C, burping daily; the aroma shifts from raw fungus to dark stone fruit and soy.
5. Stop at day 5–7 when deeply savoury and sour; pH 4.0 or below. Refrigerate at 2–4°C, up to 1 month.
6. Strain; chop the ceps as a condiment, keep the amber juice as seasoning.
7. For cep butter sauce: warm 60 ml juice to 60°C, whisk in cold butter cube by cube, season with lemon, chives and pepper. Do not boil.

## Plating & Service
Spoon chopped fermented ceps over grilled ribeye or celeriac; the butter sauce naps roasted chicken beautifully.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Button mushrooms work for a milder version; keep the sauce vegan by mounting the juice with 60 ml cold olive oil instead of butter.
