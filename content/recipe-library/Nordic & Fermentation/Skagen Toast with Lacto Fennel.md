# Skagen Toast with Lacto Fennel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Swedish classic sharpened up — fermented fennel replaces half the mayo's job, and trout roe crowns it.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Cold-water prawns, cooked, peeled — 300 g
- Mayonnaise — 60 g
- Crème fraîche — 60 g
- Fermented wholegrain mustard (larder) — 10 g
- Lacto fennel (larder), drained, chopped — 80 g
- Dill, chopped — 10 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- White sourdough or brioche slices — 4 (approx. 160 g)
- Butter — 30 g
- Juniper-cured trout roe (larder) — 40 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Dry the prawns well on paper — wet prawns kill the toast.
2. Fold prawns with mayonnaise, crème fraîche, mustard, lacto fennel, dill, lemon and pepper. Chill 15 minutes.
3. Trim crusts; butter bread on both sides.
4. Pan-fry slices golden, 2 minutes per side.
5. Cool toasts 1 minute so the topping doesn't slide.
6. Pile the skagen high on each toast.
7. Crown with a spoon of trout roe and a dill sprig.

## Plating & Service
One toast per plate with a lemon cheek — serve within minutes, with something cold and sparkling.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF bread works; swap roe for extra pickles if unavailable — but the pop is worth sourcing.
