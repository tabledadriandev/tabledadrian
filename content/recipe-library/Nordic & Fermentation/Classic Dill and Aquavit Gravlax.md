# Classic Dill and Aquavit Gravlax

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The benchmark cure every Nordic menu is judged by — clean, balanced, dill-saturated, carved whisper-thin.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Salmon fillet, skin on, pin-boned, sashimi-grade — 1000 g
- Coarse sea salt — 75 g
- Caster sugar — 75 g
- White peppercorns, crushed — 4 g
- Dill, whole bunch, roughly chopped — 80 g
- Aquavit — 40 ml
- For hovmästarsås: Dijon mustard 40 g, honey 20 g, cider vinegar 15 ml, rapeseed oil 90 ml, chopped dill 15 g

## Method
1. SAFETY: fish must be pre-frozen at -20°C for 24 h or certified sashimi-grade; cure at 2–4°C; total cure time 40–48 hours; consume within 5 days.
2. Mix salt, sugar and pepper (a classic 1:1 cure at 7.5% each).
3. Splash the flesh with aquavit; coat evenly with cure; bury under all the dill.
4. Film-wrap on a tray, weight lightly, cure at 2–4°C for 40–48 hours, flipping once.
5. Rinse 5 seconds, pat dry, rest uncovered 2 hours in the fridge.
6. Whisk the sauce: mustard, honey, vinegar, then oil in a thread, then dill.
7. Carve at 30–45° into 2 mm slices, leaving the grey bloodline behind.

## Plating & Service
Overlap slices on a chilled platter, sauce in a ribbon alongside, warm new potatoes and rye on the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skip honey and use agave in the sauce for vegans at the table (the fish stays fish); fennel fronds + pastis is a Riviera twist.
