# Rhubarb Compote with Skyr Ice Cream

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Tangy skyr ice cream — barely sweet, almost frozen yoghurt with manners — under blush rhubarb and its kombucha syrup.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Skyr, full-fat — 500 g
- Double cream — 200 ml
- Caster sugar — 130 g
- Glucose syrup — 40 g
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Rhubarb, cut 3 cm — 400 g
- Rhubarb kombucha or water — 100 ml
- Caster sugar (compote) — 70 g
- Vanilla pod, split — 1 g
- Pistachios, chopped (optional) — 30 g

## Method
1. Ice cream base: warm cream with 130 g sugar and glucose to 60°C until dissolved; cool.
2. Whisk into the skyr with lemon juice; chill to 4°C.
3. Churn until soft-serve; freeze 2 hours to scoop.
4. Compote: warm kombucha, 70 g sugar and vanilla; add rhubarb; poach at a bare simmer (90°C) 6–8 minutes until tender but intact.
5. Lift rhubarb out; reduce the syrup by half; recombine and cool.
6. Temper ice cream 5 minutes before service.
7. Scoop over the compote; spoon syrup around; pistachios if using.

## Plating & Service
Chilled bowls, compote first, one proud scoop, syrup poured at the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No churner: freeze in a tray, blitz twice at hourly intervals; strawberries replace rhubarb in high summer.
