# Beef Tartare with Beef Garum Emulsion and Rye Crisps

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Garum instead of anchovy and capers from the ramson jar — a tartare that tastes like it was aged, not assembled.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Beef fillet or trimmed sirloin, hand-cut 4 mm dice — 400 g
- Beef garum — 12 ml
- Egg yolk — 20 g
- Dijon mustard — 10 g
- Rapeseed oil — 80 ml
- Shallot, fine dice — 40 g
- Ramson capers, chopped — 25 g
- Chives — 10 g
- Rye bread, sliced 2 mm — 120 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Pickle brine (from lacto cucumber) — 10 ml

## Method
1. SAFETY: use same-day-cut beef from a trusted butcher, kept below 4°C; dice on a chilled board and serve within an hour of cutting.
2. Bake rye slices between two trays at 160°C for 8–10 minutes until glassy-crisp.
3. Emulsion: whisk yolk and mustard; stream in oil; season with beef garum and pickle brine — it should taste like liquid roast beef.
4. Fold beef with shallot, ramson capers, half the chives, pepper and half the emulsion.
5. Taste — the garum should season it fully; add drops more if flat.
6. Quenelle or ring-mould onto chilled plates.
7. Dot remaining emulsion around; stand rye crisps upright; finish with chives.

## Plating & Service
Chilled plates, served immediately; offer extra crisps — guests always want more scoop.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No raw-egg service? Emulsify with 40 g skyr instead; venison tartare takes the same seasoning.
