# Crispy Chicken Bites with Fermented Chilli Glaze

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Shio-koji-marinated thigh, rye-crumb crust, house hot-sauce glaze — deck food with a fermentation degree.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chicken thighs, boneless, skinless, in 3 cm pieces — 600 g
- Shio koji — 60 g
- Flour — 60 g
- Eggs, beaten — 100 g
- Rye breadcrumbs, fine — 120 g
- Neutral oil for shallow frying — 300 ml
- Fermented habanero & sea buckthorn hot sauce — 40 ml
- Honey — 30 g
- Butter — 20 g
- Spring onion, sliced — 20 g
- Sesame seeds, toasted — 8 g

## Method
1. Massage chicken with shio koji; marinate 4–12 hours at 2–4°C (it tenderises and seasons).
2. Wipe off excess koji; pané: flour, egg, rye crumbs.
3. Heat oil to 170°C; fry in batches 5–6 minutes to golden and 74°C core.
4. Drain on a rack, never paper.
5. Glaze: melt hot sauce, honey and butter together; simmer 1 minute to a syrup.
6. Toss the hot bites through the glaze in a large bowl — three flips, no more.
7. Finish with spring onion and sesame.

## Plating & Service
Pile in a paper-lined bowl with picks; extra hot sauce on the side for the brave.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: rice flour + GF crumbs; cauliflower florets take the identical marinade and glaze for a vegetarian bowl.
