# Raspberry and Rosehip Parfait

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A no-churn frozen parfait perfumed with rosehip — hedgerow flavour in a make-ahead slice that survives any service.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Raspberries — 300 g
- Dried rosehips (or 60 ml rosehip kombucha, reduced to 20 ml) — 20 g
- Water — 100 ml
- Caster sugar — 140 g
- Egg yolks — 80 g
- Double cream — 350 ml
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Fine sea salt — 1 g
- Fresh raspberries (garnish) — 100 g
- Rye crumble or crushed knækbrød — 40 g

## Method
1. Steep rosehips in the hot water 15 minutes; strain to 60 ml infusion.
2. Purée raspberries; sieve out seeds; stir in half the infusion and the lemon.
3. Pâte à bombe: boil sugar with remaining infusion to 118°C; whisk onto the whipping yolks in a thin stream; whisk until cool, thick and ribboned.
4. Whip cream to soft peaks.
5. Fold purée into the bombe, then the cream, with the salt.
6. Pour into a lined terrine; freeze 6 hours minimum.
7. Slice 2 cm thick with a hot knife; plate with fresh raspberries and crumble.

## Plating & Service
One clean slice per chilled plate, berries in a line, crumble for contrast — it holds 10 minutes on deck.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Blackberries + a drop of rose water clone the profile; serve with whey caramel when both are in the larder.
