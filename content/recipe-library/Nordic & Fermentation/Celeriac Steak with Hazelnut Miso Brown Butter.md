# Celeriac Steak with Hazelnut Miso Brown Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Salt-baked celeriac cut thick and basted like a ribeye — the hazelnut miso turns brown butter into a sauce with a past.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Celeriac, large, scrubbed — 1200 g
- Coarse salt (baking bed) — 300 g
- Butter — 120 g
- Hazelnut miso — 40 g
- Sherry vinegar — 15 ml
- Toasted hazelnuts, crushed — 40 g
- Thyme — 3 g
- Rapeseed oil — 20 ml
- Flaky salt and black pepper — 3 g
- Parsley, chopped — 8 g

## Method
1. Bake the whole celeriac on the salt bed at 180°C for 45–60 minutes until a skewer meets slight resistance (it finishes in the pan). Cool; peel; cut two 3 cm 'steaks' then halve them.
2. Brown the butter to hazelnut stage (small golden solids), about 4 minutes; off heat, whisk in miso and vinegar. Keep warm at 60°C.
3. Sear steaks in oil over high heat, 3 minutes per side, until mahogany.
4. Baste with 2 spoons of the miso butter and thyme for 1 minute.
5. Rest 2 minutes; season with flaky salt and pepper.
6. Loosen remaining sauce with a splash of water if split.
7. Finish with crushed hazelnuts and parsley.

## Plating & Service
One steak per plate, sauce spooned over generously, hazelnuts clustered on top — knife-and-fork vegetarian main.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: brown 100 ml plant butter carefully; pumpkin seed miso keeps it nut-free (adjust tags).
