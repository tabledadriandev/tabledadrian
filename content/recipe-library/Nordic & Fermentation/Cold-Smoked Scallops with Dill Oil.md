# Cold-Smoked Scallops with Dill Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Barely-cured scallops kissed with cold oak smoke and sliced raw — smoke as seasoning, not as cooking.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Diver scallops, XL, side muscle removed, sashimi-grade — 8 (approx. 400 g)
- Fine sea salt — 12 g
- Caster sugar — 8 g
- Lemon zest — 2 g
- Oak dust for cold smoke — 40 g
- Ice for the chamber — 500 g
- Dill oil: dill 50 g, neutral oil 150 ml
- Flaky salt — 2 g

## Method
1. SAFETY: scallops must be certified sashimi-grade and kept below 4°C at all times; cold-smoke under 20°C over ice for 30–40 minutes only; serve the same day.
2. Blitz dill and oil 3 minutes; warm to 60°C for 5 minutes; strain through muslin into a bowl over ice — vivid green oil.
3. Toss scallops with salt, sugar and zest; cure 20 minutes at 2–4°C; rinse fast, pat dry.
4. Set scallops on a rack over ice in the smoker.
5. Cold-smoke 30–40 minutes below 20°C — the surface turns faintly golden and perfumed.
6. Chill 30 minutes to settle.
7. Slice each scallop horizontally into 3 coins; dress with dill oil and flaky salt.

## Plating & Service
Overlap coins in a ring, pool dill oil at the centre, add a few trout-roe pearls — serve on chilled plates, fast.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The same treatment flatters raw langoustine tails (15 minutes smoke); swap dill for tarragon in high summer.
