# Butter-Roasted Cauliflower with Cep Shoyu

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A whole caramelised cauliflower basted with mushroom shoyu butter — carved at the table like a roast.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Cauliflower, whole, leaves trimmed but base intact — 900 g
- Butter — 100 g
- Cep mushroom shoyu — 25 ml
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Rapeseed oil — 20 ml
- Fine sea salt — 3 g
- Chives, sliced — 8 g
- Toasted rye crumbs — 30 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Steam the whole cauliflower over simmering water 10 minutes; drain and dry 10 minutes — this guarantees a creamy interior.
2. Rub with oil and salt; roast at 200°C for 25–30 minutes until deep gold all over.
3. Melt butter in a small pan to light brown; off heat, add cep shoyu and lemon — it will foam and smell outrageous.
4. Baste the cauliflower with half the butter; roast 5 more minutes.
5. Rest 5 minutes.
6. Reheat remaining shoyu butter gently.
7. Carve into thick wedges; spoon butter over; finish with rye crumbs, chives, pepper.

## Plating & Service
Present whole, carve tableside, sauce from a warm jug — the crumbs go on last for crunch.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan with plant butter; GF with buckwheat crumbs and a tamari-style shoyu.
