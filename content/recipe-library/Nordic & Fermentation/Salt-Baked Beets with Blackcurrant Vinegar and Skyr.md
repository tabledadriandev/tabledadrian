# Salt-Baked Beets with Blackcurrant Vinegar and Skyr

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Concentrated, candy-sweet beets against cold skyr and inky house vinegar — a three-element side that eats like a course.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Beetroots, mixed colours, even-sized — 800 g
- Coarse salt — 500 g
- Blackcurrant vinegar — 40 ml
- Honey — 15 g
- Rapeseed oil — 20 ml
- Skyr, full-fat — 150 g
- Fine sea salt — 2 g
- Dill fronds — 5 g
- Toasted caraway, ground — 1 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Bury beets in coarse salt in a baking dish; bake at 180°C for 75–90 minutes until a skewer glides in.
2. Crack out, cool 10 minutes, rub off skins with paper towel.
3. Cut into irregular chunks while warm.
4. Whisk vinegar, honey and oil; toss the warm beets — they drink the dressing as they cool.
5. Season skyr with fine salt and caraway.
6. Rest beets 10 minutes to marinate.
7. Assemble: cold skyr swiped first, warm beets over, dressing from the bowl, dill and pepper last.

## Plating & Service
Serve slightly warm next to roast lamb, or alone with rye bread as a light lunch.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: whipped tofu or oat crème instead of skyr; plum vinegar works when blackcurrant runs out.
