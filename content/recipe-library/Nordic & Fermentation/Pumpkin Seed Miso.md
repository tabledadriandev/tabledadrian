# Pumpkin Seed Miso

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A nut-free 'nutty' miso — grassy, toasty and forest-green at heart, brilliant with squash, poultry and rye.

## Ingredients (serves 25)
- Pumpkin seeds (pepitas) — 600 g
- Fresh barley koji — 600 g
- Fine sea salt (non-iodised) — 144 g
- Filtered water — 140 ml
- Extra salt for surface — 12 g
- Sterilised 1.5 L crock — 1

## Method
1. SAFETY: 12% salt (1200 g base = 144 g); age at 18–22°C; total fermentation time 45–75 days (seeds ferment faster than beans); anaerobic packing and monthly checks as always.
2. Toast seeds at 150°C for 8–10 minutes until they pop and smell green-nutty; cool.
3. Pulse to coarse meal. Mix with koji, salt and water into a stiff paste.
4. Pack airtight-firm into the crock; level, salt, film, weight.
5. Age at 18–22°C, skimming and tasting monthly.
6. Ready from day 45; peak around day 70 — savoury, grassy, faintly bitter-chocolate.
7. Refrigerate at 2–4°C; keeps 8 months.

## Plating & Service
Whisk with rapeseed oil and cider vinegar into a dressing for roast pumpkin; smear under seared duck breast.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Sunflower seeds swap 1:1; blend 50 g miso with 100 ml warm water for a rapid vegan 'jus'.
