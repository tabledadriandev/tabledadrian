# Lacto Fennel with Anise Hyssop

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Layered anise notes — fresh, fermented and herbal — that make this the house pickle for every fish course aboard.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Fennel bulbs, trimmed, sliced 5 mm on mandoline — 900 g
- Fine sea salt (non-iodised) — 18 g
- Anise hyssop leaves (or tarragon) — 3 g
- Fennel seeds — 2 g
- Lemon zest strips — 2 g
- For service: olive oil — 20 ml
- For service: fennel fronds — 5 g
- For service: lemon juice — 5 ml

## Method
1. SAFETY: 2% salt; ferment at 20–23°C; total fermentation time 5–6 days; keep slices below the brine line at all times.
2. Massage fennel with salt until limp and wet, 3 minutes; fold in herbs, seeds, zest.
3. Pack into a sterilised jar under its own brine and weight, or vacuum-seal at 99%.
4. Ferment at 20–23°C; burp/press daily.
5. Stop at day 5–6: sour, aromatic, with gentle crunch; pH 3.9 or below.
6. Refrigerate at 2–4°C; keeps 6 weeks.
7. Serve dressed with oil, lemon and fronds.

## Plating & Service
Twist into a small mound beside cured salmon or grilled sea bass; also stellar chopped through crab salads.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 30 ml pastis-free orange juice on service for sweetness; celery can replace fennel (tag allergen celery).
