# New Potatoes with Fermented Ramson Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The first potatoes of the season rolled in a butter spiked with fermented wild-garlic capers — spring in one pan.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- New potatoes, small, scrubbed — 600 g
- Fine sea salt (for the water) — 20 g
- Butter, soft — 80 g
- Ramson capers (larder), chopped — 30 g
- Ramson caper brine — 10 ml
- Lemon zest — 2 g
- Dill, chopped — 8 g
- Flaky salt — 2 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Simmer potatoes in well-salted water (20 g per litre) from cold, 15–18 minutes until a knife slips in.
2. Meanwhile beat the soft butter with chopped ramson capers, brine and lemon zest.
3. Drain potatoes; steam-dry in the pan 1 minute.
4. Crush each potato slightly with the back of a spoon — more surface for butter.
5. Off heat, roll potatoes in the ramson butter until glossy.
6. Fold through dill.
7. Finish with flaky salt and pepper.

## Plating & Service
Serve in a warm bowl beside gravlax or hot-smoked char — the brine in the butter means it needs nothing else.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan with plant butter; in winter use chopped lacto kohlrabi instead of ramson capers.
