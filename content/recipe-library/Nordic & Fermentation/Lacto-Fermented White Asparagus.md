# Lacto-Fermented White Asparagus

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Spring spears with a gentle sourness and intact crunch — spectacular shaved raw over butter-poached fish.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- White asparagus, peeled, woody ends removed — 800 g
- Filtered water — 800 ml
- Fine sea salt (non-iodised) — 32 g
- Lemon zest strip — 2 g
- Coriander seeds, toasted — 2 g
- White peppercorns — 1 g
- For service: brown butter or olive oil — 40 g
- For service: chervil — 5 g

## Method
1. SAFETY: this is a 2% brine ferment on TOTAL weight (water + vegetable = 1600 g, so 32 g salt); ferment at 18–21°C; total fermentation time 7–10 days.
2. Dissolve salt in the water. Pack spears vertically into a sterilised 1.5 L jar with zest and spices.
3. Pour brine to cover fully; weight with a glass disc so nothing breaches the surface. Lid loosely or use an airlock.
4. Ferment at 18–21°C. Skim any white kahm yeast promptly (harmless but off-tasting).
5. Taste from day 5. Stop at day 7–10 when sour, faintly funky and still snappy; brine pH 3.8 or below.
6. Refrigerate at 2–4°C in the brine; keeps 6 weeks.
7. Serve spears warmed briefly (60°C, 3 minutes) in brown butter, or shave raw on a mandoline.

## Plating & Service
Lay three warm spears beside poached turbot, spoon over nutty butter and finish with chervil and a splash of the brine.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Green asparagus ferments in 5–6 days; the sour brine makes an electric vegan hollandaise base blended with oil.
