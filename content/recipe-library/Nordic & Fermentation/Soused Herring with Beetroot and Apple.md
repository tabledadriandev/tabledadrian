# Soused Herring with Beetroot and Apple

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bright vinegar-cured herring against sweet beet and sharp apple — a chilled starter that costs little and reads regal.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Fresh herring fillets, pin-boned — 400 g
- Fine sea salt — 24 g
- Cider vinegar — 250 ml
- Water — 125 ml
- Caster sugar — 60 g
- Bay leaf, allspice, mustard seed — 4 g
- Red onion, sliced in rings — 60 g
- Cooked beetroot, diced — 200 g
- Apple, diced — 120 g
- Fermented wholegrain mustard (larder) — 15 g
- Rapeseed oil — 30 ml
- Dill — 8 g

## Method
1. SAFETY: salt herring first — 6% salt (24 g) for 2 hours at 2–4°C — then vinegar-cure; the acid bath must fully submerge the fish for a minimum 24 hours; use pre-frozen herring (-20°C, 24 h) against parasites.
2. Rinse the salted fillets; pat dry.
3. Simmer vinegar, water, sugar and spices 2 minutes; cool completely.
4. Submerge fillets with onion rings; souse 24–48 hours at 2–4°C.
5. Dress beet and apple with mustard and oil.
6. Slice soused herring on the bias.
7. Assemble cold: beet-apple base, herring draped, soused onions and dill over.

## Plating & Service
Chilled plates, a drizzle of the sousing liquid, buttered rye on the side.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Mackerel souses beautifully in 36 hours; add horseradish to the beets for more push.
