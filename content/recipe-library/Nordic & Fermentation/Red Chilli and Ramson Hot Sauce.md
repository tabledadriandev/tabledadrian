# Red Chilli and Ramson Hot Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Spring wild garlic fermented into a scarlet sauce with green-garlic bite — the house sriracha, foraged edition.

## Ingredients (serves 30)
- Red chillies (fresno/cayenne mix), stemmed — 450 g
- Ramson (wild garlic) leaves — 50 g
- Apple, peeled, chopped — 100 g
- Fine sea salt (non-iodised) — 15 g
- Cider vinegar (post-ferment) — 80 ml
- Maple syrup — 20 g
- Rapeseed oil — 20 ml

## Method
1. SAFETY: 2.5% salt on mash weight (600 g = 15 g); ferment at 20–24°C; total fermentation time 8–12 days; confirm pH ≤ 3.7 at bottling; positively identify ramsons (lily-of-the-valley lookalike is toxic) — crush a leaf: it must smell of garlic.
2. Blitz chillies and apple with salt to a coarse mash (reserve ramsons — they go in raw at the end to keep their bite).
3. Ferment the mash under brine, weighted, at 20–24°C, pressing daily.
4. Taste from day 7; stop at day 8–12 when sour and rounded.
5. Blend hot mash with ramson leaves, vinegar and maple until silky; emulsify with oil; pass fine.
6. Verify pH ≤ 3.7; adjust with extra vinegar if needed.
7. Bottle; refrigerate at 2–4°C; use within 2 months, burping weekly.

## Plating & Service
Stripe under crispy pork belly; stir into butter for grilled langoustines; brilliant with fried eggs at crew breakfast.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Off-season, use 30 g spinach + 15 g garlic instead of ramsons; skip maple for keto service.
