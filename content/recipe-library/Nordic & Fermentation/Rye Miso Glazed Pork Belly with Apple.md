# Rye Miso Glazed Pork Belly with Apple

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Slow-pressed belly under a near-black rye miso lacquer with raw apple to slice through — festive Nordic power food.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Pork belly, boneless, skin removed — 1500 g
- Fine sea salt — 12 g
- Chicken stock — 500 ml
- Bay leaves — 1 g
- Rye bread miso — 60 g
- Honey — 40 g
- Cider vinegar — 30 ml
- Apple juice — 60 ml
- Crisp apple, matchsticks — 150 g
- Watercress — 40 g

## Method
1. Salt the belly; rest 1 hour.
2. Braise: submerge in stock with bay, cover, 150°C oven for 3 hours until a spoon-handle slides through.
3. Press: cool between two trays with a 2 kg weight, overnight in the fridge.
4. Whisk miso, honey, vinegar and apple juice into a glaze; simmer 3 minutes to 100 ml.
5. Portion belly into 6 blocks; crisp fat-side down in a dry pan, 4 minutes.
6. Roast at 200°C, glazing every 4 minutes, 12 minutes total, until lacquered and 70°C core.
7. Rest 5 minutes; toss apple matchsticks with a few drops of the glaze.

## Plating & Service
One glossy block per plate, apple stacked on top, watercress beside; spoon the last glaze over at the pass.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Yellow pea miso gives a blonder, sweeter glaze; serve with lacto kohlrabi to cut the richness further.
