# Skyr Flatbreads with Dill Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Two-ingredient-adjacent flatbreads where skyr does the tenderising — blistered in minutes, brushed with dill butter.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Plain flour — 300 g
- Skyr, full-fat — 250 g
- Baking powder — 8 g
- Fine sea salt — 5 g
- Rapeseed oil — 15 ml
- Butter — 60 g
- Dill, chopped — 10 g
- Garlic, grated (optional) — 4 g
- Flaky salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Mix flour, baking powder and salt; add skyr and oil; bring together into a shaggy dough.
2. Knead 2 minutes only — just until smooth; rest 15 minutes under the bowl.
3. Divide into 8; roll each to 3–4 mm ovals.
4. Cook on a dry, very hot pan 90 seconds per side until blistered and puffed in spots.
5. Melt butter with dill (and garlic); brush each bread as it lands.
6. Stack under a cloth.
7. Finish the pile with flaky salt.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm with the butter bean miso dip or torn beside soups; they reheat in 20 seconds on a flame.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Greek yoghurt substitutes; wholemeal flour up to half keeps them supple; skip garlic for low-FODMAP.
