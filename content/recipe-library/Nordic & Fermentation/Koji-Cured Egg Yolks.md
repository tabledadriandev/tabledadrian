# Koji-Cured Egg Yolks

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Yolks cured in shio koji until they grate like aged parmesan — umami gold dust for pasta, tartare and buttered rye.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Very fresh egg yolks — 12 (approx. 220 g)
- Shio koji (blended smooth) — 400 g
- Fine sea salt — 40 g
- Caster sugar — 20 g
- For finishing: neutral oil to wipe — 10 ml

## Method
1. SAFETY: curing pulls water activity down but hygiene matters — use fresh eggs, cure refrigerated at 2–4°C throughout; total cure + dry time 7–9 days; discard any yolk that smells sulphurous or shows discolouration.
2. Whisk salt and sugar into the shio koji.
3. Spread half the cure in a tray; make 12 dimples with the back of a spoon.
4. Nestle a yolk in each dimple; blanket gently with remaining cure.
5. Cure at 2–4°C for 5 days until firm like soft toffee.
6. Lift out, rinse briefly, pat dry. Dry on a rack in the fridge (or dehydrate at 45°C for 4 hours) for 2–4 more days until firm enough to grate.
7. Wipe with a drop of oil; store airtight at 2–4°C up to 1 month.

## Plating & Service
Microplane over beef tartare, buttered asparagus or hand-rolled pasta at the pass — a little snowfall goes far.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> A plain 60/40 salt-sugar cure works without koji (less umami); grate over vegan dishes only if egg allergy is cleared.
