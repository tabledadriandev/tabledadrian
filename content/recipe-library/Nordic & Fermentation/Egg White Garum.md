# Egg White Garum

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Pastry-section egg whites transformed into a light amber amino sauce — vegetarian umami with a whisper of toasted brioche.

## Ingredients (serves 40)
- Fresh egg whites — 1000 g
- Fresh rice koji — 300 g
- Filtered water — 500 ml
- Fine sea salt (non-iodised) — 216 g
- Sterilised 2 L jar — 1
- Muslin for straining — 1

## Method
1. SAFETY: 12% salt on total weight (1800 g = 216 g); hold at 60°C for the full ferment; total fermentation time 6–8 weeks; the 60°C hold also pasteurises the whites over time — still discard on any sulphurous-putrid turn.
2. Whisk whites briefly to loosen; stir in crumbled koji, water and salt.
3. Pour into the jar; the mix looks milky and unpromising — trust the process.
4. Hold at 60°C, lid loose; stir twice weekly. Whites coagulate, then slowly dissolve into amber liquor.
5. Taste from week 6: nutty, brothy, gently eggy in the best way.
6. Strain through muslin twice; the spent curd is discarded.
7. Bottle; refrigerate at 2–4°C, 6 months.

## Plating & Service
Season vegetarian consommés, glaze roasted alliums, or replace fish sauce in dressings for vegetarian charters.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> This is the house 'fish sauce' for vegetarians; not vegan — use Yeast Garum for plant-based service.
