# Rye-Crusted Cod with Yellow Pea Miso Beurre Blanc

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The house miso does the heavy lifting: a beurre blanc with honeyed umami under a shattering rye crust.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Cod loin portions, skinless — 4 × 160 g
- Stale rye bread, blitzed to coarse crumbs — 80 g
- Butter, melted (for crust) — 40 g
- Dijon mustard — 15 g
- White wine — 100 ml
- Shallot, minced — 40 g
- White wine vinegar — 20 ml
- Cold butter, diced (for sauce) — 150 g
- Yellow pea miso — 30 g
- Chives, sliced — 8 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml

## Method
1. Season cod lightly (the miso sauce carries salt); brush tops with mustard.
2. Mix rye crumbs with melted butter; press a 5 mm crust onto each loin. Chill 15 minutes to set.
3. Reduction: simmer wine, vinegar and shallot to 60 ml, about 8 minutes.
4. Roast cod at 180°C for 10–12 minutes to 52°C core; flash under the grill 1 minute to crisp the crust.
5. Meanwhile whisk cold butter into the warm (not boiling) reduction cube by cube at ~80°C.
6. Whisk in the pea miso off the heat; do not boil after this or it turns grainy. Adjust with lemon.
7. Rest fish 2 minutes; core should reach 54–56°C.

## Plating & Service
Pool sauce, set the cod on top crust-proud, scatter chives; charred gem lettuce alongside completes it.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: use toasted buckwheat crumbs; the sauce also loves roasted cauliflower for a vegetarian main.
