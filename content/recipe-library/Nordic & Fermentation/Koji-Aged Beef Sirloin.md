# Koji-Aged Beef Sirloin

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A 3-day koji rub mimics weeks of dry-aging — nutty, funky depth and a crust that browns like a dream.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Beef sirloin, whole piece, well-trimmed — 1200 g
- Shio koji, blended smooth — 150 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g
- Cracked black pepper — 3 g
- Beef dripping or neutral oil — 30 g
- Butter — 40 g
- Thyme sprigs — 4 g
- Garlic clove, crushed — 5 g

## Method
1. SAFETY: age refrigerated at 1–3°C on a wire rack, uncovered airflow, for exactly 48–72 hours; wipe the koji off before cooking (it scorches); cook to at least 52°C core for rare service and rest; total process time 3 days.
2. Coat the sirloin in a thin, even 3–4 mm layer of shio koji.
3. Set on a rack over a tray; age uncovered at 1–3°C for 48–72 hours. The surface dries and smells sweetly of aged beef.
4. Scrape and wipe off all koji with damp paper; pat bone-dry. Season with salt and pepper.
5. Sear in smoking dripping, 2 minutes per face, until deeply bronzed.
6. Roast at 120°C to 50°C core (about 20 minutes); baste with butter, thyme and garlic in the final minutes.
7. Rest 10 minutes to 54–56°C; carve against the grain.

## Plating & Service
Thick slices with fermented cep butter sauce and charred hispi cabbage; the aged crust deserves the table's attention.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Works on duck breast (24 h only); for a leaner plate serve with beet kvass reduction instead of butter sauce.
