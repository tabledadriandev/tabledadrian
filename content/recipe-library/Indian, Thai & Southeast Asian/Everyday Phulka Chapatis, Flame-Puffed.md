# Everyday Phulka Chapatis, Flame-Puffed

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The daily bread, done properly — thin wholemeal rounds finished directly over the flame until they balloon. Ten minutes of practice, a lifetime of use.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- chapati flour (atta) — 300 g
- fine sea salt — 5 g
- warm water — 185 ml
- vegetable oil — 10 ml
- extra atta for dusting — 30 g
- ghee (optional finish) — 20 g

## Method
1. Knead flour, salt, oil and water 8 min to a soft, elastic dough — softer than pasta, firmer than focaccia.
2. Rest 20–30 min under a damp cloth.
3. Divide into 10; ball and keep covered.
4. Roll each to a 2 mm, 15 cm round with even pressure — uneven thickness prevents puffing.
5. Heat a tawa or dry pan to medium-hot. Cook 30–40 seconds until small bubbles rise; flip, 30 seconds more.
6. Move directly onto a high gas flame (tongs) — it should balloon in 5–10 seconds. Flip once on the flame. Electric hob: press gently all over with a cloth on the pan and it will puff.
7. Stack in a cloth, brushing with ghee if using.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm and soft, folded in a cloth-lined basket, replenished in small batches through the meal.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Half plain flour makes rolling easier for beginners but drops the wholegrain virtue. Dough keeps 24 h chilled; bring to room temperature before rolling.
