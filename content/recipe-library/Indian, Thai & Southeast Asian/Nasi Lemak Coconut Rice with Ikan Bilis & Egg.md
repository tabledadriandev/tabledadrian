# Nasi Lemak Coconut Rice with Ikan Bilis & Egg

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Malaysia's national breakfast — pandan coconut rice with the full supporting cast: crisp anchovies, toasted peanuts, egg and cucumber, ready for a spoon of sambal.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- jasmine rice, rinsed — 300 g
- coconut milk — 220 ml
- water — 240 ml
- pandan leaf, knotted — 1
- ginger, sliced — 15 g
- fine sea salt — 6 g
- dried anchovies (ikan bilis) — 60 g
- raw peanuts, skin-on — 80 g
- eggs — 4
- cucumber, thick slices — 150 g
- vegetable oil — 200 ml (for frying)

## Method
1. Cook the rice: coconut milk, water, pandan, ginger and salt; boil, stir once, lid on minimum heat 12 min, rest 10 min.
2. Rinse the anchovies, dry completely — wet fish spits and steams.
3. Fry peanuts in 150 °C oil, 4–5 min to golden-brown, stirring; drain (they crisp as they cool).
4. Raise oil to 175 °C; fry anchovies 2–3 min until glass-crisp and pale gold. Drain.
5. Boil eggs 8 min for just-set yolks; halve. (Or fry, kampung-style.)
6. Fluff the rice; remove pandan and ginger.
7. Compose each plate: rice dome, anchovies, peanuts, egg, cucumber.

## Plating & Service
Traditional in a banana-leaf cone; on board, a neat dome with each element clockwise and sambal at six o'clock.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> With sambal ikan bilis and fried chicken it graduates from breakfast to feast. Peanut-free: use toasted coconut chips for the crunch line.
