# Bún Chả-Style Grilled Pork Patties, Vermicelli & Herb Basket

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Hanoi's smoky lunchtime classic — caramelised pork patties and belly slices dropped into a warm, sweet-sour nước chấm bath with noodles and a garden of herbs.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- pork shoulder mince (20% fat) — 500 g
- pork belly, thin slices — 250 g
- shallots, minced — 40 g
- garlic, minced — 20 g
- fish sauce — 55 ml
- palm or caster sugar — 55 g
- black pepper — 2 g
- kecap-style dark caramel (sugar 20 g cooked amber + 15 ml water) — 35 g
- rice vermicelli, cooked — 400 g
- lime juice — 50 ml
- rice vinegar — 20 ml
- water, warm — 250 ml
- garlic, fine-chopped, extra — 8 g
- bird's-eye chilli, sliced — 1
- green papaya or carrot, pickled ribbons — 100 g
- lettuce, mint, coriander, perilla if found — 200 g

## Method
1. Make the caramel; cool. Mix mince with shallots, half the garlic, 25 ml fish sauce, 15 g sugar, pepper and the caramel. Form 12 flat patties; chill 20 min.
2. Marinate belly slices in 10 ml fish sauce and 10 g sugar.
3. Make the dipping broth: dissolve remaining 30 g sugar in warm water; add remaining fish sauce, lime juice, vinegar, extra garlic and chilli. It should be light, balanced, drinkable.
4. Grill patties and belly over hot coals or a 230 °C plancha, 3–4 min per side, until charred and lacquered.
5. Warm the dipping broth to just-hot; add the pickled ribbons.
6. Divide broth between four bowls; slide the grilled pork in.
7. Serve vermicelli and the herb basket separately.

## Plating & Service
Guests pull noodles and herbs through the pork-laden bowl bite by bite — set the table with chopsticks, spoons and no rush.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken thigh patties work; for a lighter version grill courgette and mushrooms into the same bath. The broth must stay warm, never boiling.
