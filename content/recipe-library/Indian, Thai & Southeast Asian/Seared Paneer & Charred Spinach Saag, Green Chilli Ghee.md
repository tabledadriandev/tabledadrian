# Seared Paneer & Charred Spinach Saag, Green Chilli Ghee

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Palak paneer rebuilt: the spinach is flash-charred not boiled, keeping it vivid green and iron-rich, with crisp-edged pan-seared paneer.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- paneer, cut in 2.5 cm cubes — 400 g
- baby spinach — 500 g
- mustard greens or chard leaves — 100 g
- ghee — 50 g
- onion, finely chopped — 150 g
- garlic, sliced — 15 g
- ginger, chopped — 15 g
- green chillies — 2 (about 10 g)
- cumin seeds — 4 g
- ground coriander — 6 g
- garam masala — 3 g
- double cream — 60 ml
- lemon juice — 15 ml
- fine sea salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Heat a wide dry pan to very hot; char spinach and mustard greens in handfuls, 30–40 seconds per batch, until wilted with scattered dark spots. Spread on a tray to cool fast.
2. Blend greens with one chilli and 50 ml cold water to a coarse, vivid purée. Do not over-blend or it dulls.
3. Sear paneer in 20 g ghee over medium-high heat until golden on two faces, about 4 min; season lightly and set aside.
4. In the same pan, heat 20 g ghee; sizzle cumin seeds 20 seconds, add onion and cook 8 min to soft gold.
5. Add garlic and ginger, cook 2 min; add ground coriander, bloom 20 seconds.
6. Fold in the green purée, salt and 100 ml water; simmer only 4–5 min to keep colour.
7. Add paneer, cream and garam masala; warm through 2 min. Slit the second chilli, fry it 30 seconds in the last 10 g ghee, and spoon this chilli ghee over. Finish with lemon.

## Plating & Service
A shallow bowl of green, paneer edges poking through, the chilli ghee glistening on top; jeera rice or roti alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: use extra-firm tofu and coconut cream, sear in coconut oil. Add blanched broccoli for extra bulk without dulling the colour.
