# Kluai Buat Chi, Bananas Poached in Salted Coconut Milk

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The five-ingredient Thai comfort dessert — just-ripe bananas barely poached in sweet-salty coconut milk; ready in fifteen minutes and better than it has any right to be.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- just-ripe bananas (firm, barely speckled) — 4
- coconut milk — 400 ml
- water — 100 ml
- palm sugar — 60 g
- fine sea salt — 3 g
- pandan leaf (optional) — 1
- toasted sesame seeds — 8 g

## Method
1. Warm the coconut milk, water, palm sugar, salt and pandan to a bare simmer; dissolve fully.
2. Peel the bananas; halve lengthwise, then across — 4 pieces each.
3. Slide the bananas into the barely-simmering milk.
4. Poach 4–6 min — just until heated through and glossy; overcooking turns them to mush and the milk grey.
5. Taste the broth: sweet with a clear salt line — that tension is the dish.
6. Rest 5 min off the heat.
7. Serve warm in small bowls, sesame over.

## Plating & Service
A ladle of fruit and broth per bowl — the right ending after a chilli-heavy Thai spread, when anything heavier would lose.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pumpkin or taro cubes (simmered 12 min first) make classic variants. Chilled next-day leftovers are a legitimate breakfast.
