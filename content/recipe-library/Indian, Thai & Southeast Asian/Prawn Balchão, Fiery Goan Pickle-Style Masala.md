# Prawn Balchão, Fiery Goan Pickle-Style Masala

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Half curry, half pickle — this Goan prawn dish keeps for days and gets better, ideal make-ahead heat for charter lunches.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- raw king prawns, peeled — 500 g
- dried Kashmiri chillies — 10
- cider or coconut vinegar — 70 ml
- garlic — 25 g
- ginger — 20 g
- cumin seeds — 5 g
- black peppercorns — 3 g
- cloves — 3
- cinnamon — 1 stick (4 cm)
- vegetable oil — 50 ml
- onions, finely chopped — 250 g
- tomato purée (concentrate) — 30 g
- jaggery or brown sugar — 15 g
- curry leaves — 12
- fine sea salt — 9 g

## Method
1. Soak chillies in warm vinegar 20 min; toast cumin, peppercorns, cloves and cinnamon 2 min, then blend all with garlic and ginger to a smooth balchão paste.
2. Season prawns with 3 g salt; flash-sear in 15 ml oil over high heat 60 seconds per side, well short of cooked. Remove.
3. Add remaining oil; fry curry leaves 20 seconds, then onions 10 min to deep gold.
4. Add the balchão paste; fry 4–5 min, stirring, until the oil separates and the raw vinegar edge mellows.
5. Add tomato purée, jaggery, remaining salt and 100 ml water; simmer 6 min to a thick, glossy, brick-red masala.
6. Return prawns and juices; simmer 2–3 min until just cooked.
7. Rest 5 min; the pickle character emerges as it cools slightly.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm or room-temperature with coconut rice; a little goes far — offer yogurt alongside for the cautious.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Also outstanding with squid (90-second flash) or diced pork belly (braise 45 min first). Keeps 4 days chilled — flavours deepen.
