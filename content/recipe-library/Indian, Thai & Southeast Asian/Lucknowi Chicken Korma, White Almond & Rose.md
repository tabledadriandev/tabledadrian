# Lucknowi Chicken Korma, White Almond & Rose

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A pale, perfumed royal korma — no tomato, no turmeric — where browned onion paste, almond and a whisper of rose do the heavy lifting.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- chicken thighs, bone-in, skinless — 900 g
- yogurt — 200 g
- blanched almonds — 60 g
- onions, sliced — 300 g
- ghee — 60 g
- green cardamom — 6
- cloves — 4
- cinnamon — 1 stick (4 cm)
- mace blade — 1
- white pepper, ground — 2 g
- garlic-ginger paste — 30 g
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 3 g
- rose water — 5 ml
- double cream — 60 ml
- fine sea salt — 10 g

## Method
1. Fry onions in 40 g ghee over medium heat 15–18 min to an even deep gold; drain and blend with the almonds, yogurt and 50 ml water to a smooth cream.
2. Heat remaining ghee; sizzle cardamom, cloves, cinnamon and mace 30 seconds.
3. Add garlic-ginger paste, 2 min; then chicken and salt, searing gently 6 min without colouring hard — korma stays pale.
4. Bloom chilli powder 15 seconds, then lower the heat and add the onion-almond cream.
5. Simmer very gently, part-covered, 30 min, stirring often; yogurt gravies split if boiled.
6. Add white pepper and cream; simmer 5 min to a silky, pale-gold nappe.
7. Finish off the heat with rose water — add it drop-wise and taste; it should be felt, not smelt.

## Plating & Service
Serve in a shallow bowl with sheermal or basmati, a few toasted almond flakes and a single dried rose petal if you have the garnish kit out.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Lamb korma: braise 90 min before step 6. Cashews for almonds is classical too; skip rose and add saffron water for a different palace.
