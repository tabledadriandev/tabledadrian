# Mangalorean Prawn Ghee Roast, Byadgi Chilli & Curry Leaf

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A dry-style coastal roast where prawns are lacquered in a bright red, tangy chilli-ghee masala — intense, glossy, unforgettable.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- raw king prawns, peeled, tails on — 500 g
- dried Byadgi or Kashmiri chillies — 12
- coriander seeds — 8 g
- cumin seeds — 4 g
- black peppercorns — 3 g
- fenugreek seeds — 1 g
- garlic — 20 g
- tamarind paste — 20 g
- ghee — 70 g
- curry leaves — 20
- jaggery — 10 g
- yogurt — 50 g
- ground turmeric — 2 g
- fine sea salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Marinate prawns in yogurt, turmeric and 3 g salt, 15 min.
2. Toast chillies, coriander, cumin, peppercorns and fenugreek in a dry pan 2–3 min until fragrant; soak in 100 ml hot water 15 min, then blend with garlic and tamarind to a fine paste.
3. Heat 20 g ghee in a wide pan; sear prawns 60 seconds per side over high heat; remove before fully cooked.
4. Add remaining ghee; fry the masala paste over medium heat 8–10 min, stirring constantly, until it darkens brick-red and the ghee separates around it.
5. Add jaggery and remaining salt; fry 1 min more.
6. Return prawns with half the curry leaves; toss 2–3 min until each prawn is thickly lacquered and just cooked.
7. Crisp remaining curry leaves in a corner of the pan and fold through.

## Plating & Service
Pile onto a warm platter, tails up, extra masala scraped over — neer dosa or lemon rice alongside, finger bowls essential.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken ghee roast: use 700 g boneless thigh, cook 15 min in the masala. Halve the chillies for gentler heat; the Byadgi variety is colour-rich but mild.
