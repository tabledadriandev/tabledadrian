# Mì Xào Giòn, Crispy Noodle Nest with Seafood & Ginger Velouté

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A crackling fried-noodle cake flooded at the table with a glossy ginger-laced seafood sauce — Chinese-Vietnamese banquet drama, superb for a final charter night.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- fresh thin egg noodles — 400 g
- raw prawns, peeled — 250 g
- squid, scored — 200 g
- scallops — 150 g
- choy sum or pak choi — 200 g
- carrot, thin slices — 80 g
- shiitake, sliced — 100 g
- garlic — 15 g
- ginger, julienne — 25 g
- chicken stock — 500 ml
- oyster sauce — 30 g
- light soy sauce — 20 ml
- fish sauce — 10 ml
- cornflour — 20 g
- white pepper — 1 g
- vegetable oil — 90 ml

## Method
1. Blanch noodles 60 seconds; drain and dry well on a tray, 15 min.
2. Heat 60 ml oil in a 26 cm pan; press the noodles in as a cake. Fry over medium heat 6–8 min until deep gold and crisp; flip (use a plate) and crisp the other side 5 min. Keep warm at 100 °C.
3. Slurry the cornflour with 40 ml cold water.
4. Stir-fry garlic and ginger in 30 ml oil 30 seconds; add mushrooms and carrot, 2 min.
5. Add seafood; sear 90 seconds. Add greens, 60 seconds.
6. Add stock, oyster sauce, soy, fish sauce, white pepper; at the boil, stream in slurry until the sauce coats a spoon — a light velouté.
7. Slide the noodle cake onto a platter.

## Plating & Service
Pour the seafood sauce over the crackling nest at the table — the hiss is the service. Cut into wedges as it softens.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken and mushroom version for shellfish-free tables; rice noodles fry up gluten-free but need extra drying.
