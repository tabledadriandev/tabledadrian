# Nam Jim Jaew, Toasted Rice & Tamarind Dipping Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Isaan grill sauce — smoky chilli flakes, tamarind, lime and nutty toasted-rice powder; made in ten minutes, it upgrades anything charred.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- jasmine or sticky rice, raw — 20 g
- fish sauce — 50 ml
- lime juice — 40 ml
- tamarind paste — 20 g
- palm sugar — 15 g
- roasted chilli flakes (prik bon) — 8 g
- shallot, fine dice — 30 g
- spring onion, fine rings — 15 g
- coriander, chopped — 10 g
- water — 20 ml

## Method
1. Toast the rice in a dry pan over medium heat, shaking, 8–10 min to a deep gold; grind to coarse sand (khao khua).
2. Warm the water with palm sugar and tamarind to dissolve; cool.
3. Stir in fish sauce and lime juice.
4. Add chilli flakes; rest 5 min so they bleed smoke into the liquid.
5. Fold in shallot, spring onion and coriander.
6. Add the toasted rice powder last — it thickens and turns nutty as it sits.
7. Taste: sour-salty-smoky, sweetness in the back row.

## Plating & Service
Serve in a shallow bowl beside grilled steak, chicken, pork or mushrooms — spoon, dip, repeat. Note the fish sauce for strict vegans and swap accordingly.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Keeps 3 days chilled but add rice powder per service to keep the crunch. Vegan fish-sauce substitutes work fully here.
