# Onion & Spinach Pakoras, Double-Fried with Chaat Masala

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Craggy, lacy gram-flour fritters — sliced onion barely bound, double-fried for shatter, dusted with chaat masala. Sundowner fuel.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- onions, thinly sliced — 400 g
- baby spinach, rough-chopped — 80 g
- gram (chickpea) flour — 150 g
- rice flour — 30 g
- cumin seeds — 4 g
- coriander seeds, crushed — 4 g
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 4 g
- ground turmeric — 2 g
- ginger, grated — 12 g
- green chilli, minced — 1
- fine sea salt — 7 g
- cold water — about 60 ml
- oil for deep frying — 1 L
- chaat masala — 4 g
- lemon — 1

## Method
1. Salt the onions; rest 10 min until they weep — their juice is most of your liquid.
2. Add spinach, ginger, chilli, whole and crushed spices; toss.
3. Rain in both flours; mix with fingers, adding water a spoon at a time until the mix just clings — batter should barely coat, not drown.
4. Oil to 170 °C. Drop loose, craggy clusters (about 40 g); don't compact them.
5. Fry 3–4 min to pale gold; drain. This can be done an hour ahead.
6. Second fry at 185 °C, 60–90 seconds, to deep gold and audibly crisp.
7. Rack-drain; dust immediately with chaat masala.

## Plating & Service
Pile on a paper-lined board with lemon wedges and coriander chutney; serve within minutes of the second fry.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap spinach for shredded kale or julienned potato; a pinch of ajwain aids digestion, as every Indian grandmother will confirm.
