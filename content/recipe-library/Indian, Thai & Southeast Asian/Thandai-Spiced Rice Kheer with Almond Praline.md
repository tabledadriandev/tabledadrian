# Thandai-Spiced Rice Kheer with Almond Praline

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A slow-simmered rice pudding perfumed with the thandai spice set — fennel, cardamom, rose, pepper — finished with shattered almond praline for texture.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- short-grain or basmati rice, rinsed — 80 g
- whole milk — 1.2 L
- caster sugar — 90 g
- green cardamom, ground — 5 pods
- fennel seed, ground — 2 g
- black peppercorn, fine-ground — 0.5 g
- saffron — 0.2 g
- rose water — 4 ml
- almonds — 60 g
- caster sugar (praline) — 60 g
- pistachios, chopped — 20 g
- fine sea salt — 1 g

## Method
1. Simmer the rice in the milk over low heat, stirring often, 40–45 min until the grains collapse and the milk thickens to a loose custard consistency.
2. Steep saffron in a spoon of the hot milk; return.
3. Add sugar, cardamom, fennel, pepper and salt; 5 min more.
4. Off the heat, rose water — drop-wise, tasting.
5. Praline: dry-melt 60 g sugar to amber, fold in almonds, pour onto parchment; cool and crack into shards.
6. Chill the kheer at least 2 h; it sets to a spoonable cream (loosen with cold milk if needed).
7. Serve cold, praline and pistachios on top at the last second.

## Plating & Service
Chilled coupes, praline standing upright, a saffron thread each — humble pudding, plated like it studied in Lyon.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan with oat milk + coconut cream (add 20 min simmer). The praline keeps a week airtight — make extra; it disappears.
