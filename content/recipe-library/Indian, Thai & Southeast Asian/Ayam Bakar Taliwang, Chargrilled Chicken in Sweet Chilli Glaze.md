# Ayam Bakar Taliwang, Chargrilled Chicken in Sweet Chilli Glaze

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Lombok's twice-cooked grilled chicken — first braised in a chilli-shrimp paste bumbu, then charred and basted with kecap manis until burnished.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- chicken legs, whole — 4 (about 1.1 kg)
- dried red chillies, soaked — 10
- bird's-eye chillies — 5 g
- shallots — 80 g
- garlic — 25 g
- candlenuts or macadamias — 30 g
- shrimp paste (terasi), toasted — 8 g
- tomato — 100 g
- palm sugar — 25 g
- tamarind paste — 15 g
- kecap manis — 50 ml
- lime juice — 20 ml
- vegetable oil — 40 ml
- fine sea salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Blend both chillies, shallots, garlic, candlenuts, terasi and tomato to a paste.
2. Fry the paste in oil over medium heat 6–8 min until it darkens and the oil returns.
3. Add palm sugar, tamarind, salt and 250 ml water; slide in the chicken legs.
4. Simmer, turning, 25 min until just cooked and the bumbu has reduced to a thick coat.
5. Stir kecap manis into the pan glaze.
6. Grill the legs over medium-hot coals (or 240 °C grill), basting with the glaze, 8–10 min, turning until charred and lacquered.
7. Rest 5 min; lime juice over.

## Plating & Service
Serve with steamed rice, sliced cucumber and a raw sambal — the char lines and sticky glaze do the visual work.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Poussin halves or a whole spatchcocked bird scale the same way; skip terasi and use miso for a shellfish-free umami base.
