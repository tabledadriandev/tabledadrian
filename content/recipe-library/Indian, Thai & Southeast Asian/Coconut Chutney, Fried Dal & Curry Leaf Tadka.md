# Coconut Chutney, Fried Dal & Curry Leaf Tadka

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The dosa-idli companion — fresh coconut ground with chilli and ginger, finished with a crackling mustard-dal tadka that perfumes the whole bowl.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- fresh or frozen grated coconut — 200 g
- roasted chana dal (dalia) — 30 g
- green chillies — 8 g
- ginger — 12 g
- fine sea salt — 6 g
- lime juice — 15 ml
- cold water — 150–200 ml
- vegetable or coconut oil — 20 ml
- black mustard seeds — 3 g
- urad dal — 5 g
- dried red chilli — 1
- curry leaves — 10
- asafoetida — 0.3 g

## Method
1. Blend coconut, roasted chana dal, green chillies, ginger, salt and lime with enough cold water for a thick, spoonable purée.
2. Taste — clean coconut sweetness with a firm chilli line; adjust salt and lime.
3. Transfer to the serving bowl.
4. Tadka: heat the oil; crackle mustard seeds fully.
5. Add urad dal; fry to gold, 30–40 seconds.
6. Add dried chilli, curry leaves and asafoetida; 15 seconds of noise.
7. Pour the sizzling tadka over the chutney; stir half in, leave half on top.

## Plating & Service
Serve with dosas, appam, idli or grilled fish; it's also a sleeper hit beside tandoori chicken.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 20 g mint or coriander for a green version; desiccated coconut (soaked in warm water 15 min) works mid-Atlantic. Best day one, fine day two.
