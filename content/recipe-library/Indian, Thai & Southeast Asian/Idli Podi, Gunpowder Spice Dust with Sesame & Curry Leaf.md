# Idli Podi, Gunpowder Spice Dust with Sesame & Curry Leaf

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The dry chutney — roasted dals, chillies, sesame and curry leaves ground to a smoky dust; mixed with oil at the table it becomes instant chutney anywhere, anytime.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- urad dal — 60 g
- chana dal — 60 g
- dried red chillies — 12 g
- sesame seeds — 25 g
- curry leaves — 20
- cumin seeds — 4 g
- asafoetida — 0.5 g
- fine sea salt — 8 g
- jaggery — 8 g
- vegetable or sesame oil, to serve — as needed

## Method
1. Dry-roast urad dal over medium-low heat, stirring, 5–6 min to even gold; tip out.
2. Roast chana dal the same way, 5–6 min; tip out.
3. Roast chillies and cumin 90 seconds; then sesame 60 seconds until it pops; then curry leaves until crisp, 60 seconds.
4. Cool everything completely — grinding warm turns it pasty.
5. Grind with salt, asafoetida and jaggery to a coarse, sandy powder — texture between sand and breadcrumbs.
6. Taste; it should be nutty, hot and moreish enough to eat off the palm.
7. Store airtight.

## Plating & Service
Serve in a bowl with a cruet of sesame oil — guests puddle and swirl; classically over idli, dosa or ghee rice, brilliant on avocado toast.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Keeps a month airtight, longer chilled. Add 20 g dried coconut for a Karnataka accent; skip sesame for allergy manifests.
