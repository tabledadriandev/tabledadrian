# Coorg-Style Pandi Pork, Dark Kachampuli Braise

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Pork braised black with berry vinegar standing in for Coorg's kachampuli — sour, dark, and built to age gracefully over a charter week.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- pork shoulder + belly mix, 4 cm dice — 1.3 kg
- black peppercorns — 8 g
- coriander seeds — 10 g
- cumin seeds — 5 g
- dried red chillies — 5
- mustard seeds — 3 g
- fenugreek seeds — 1 g
- balsamic + tamarind mix (2:1) — 50 ml (kachampuli substitute)
- onions, chopped — 300 g
- garlic — 30 g
- ginger — 25 g
- curry leaves — 15
- ground turmeric — 4 g
- vegetable oil — 30 ml
- fine sea salt — 13 g

## Method
1. Dry-roast peppercorns, coriander, cumin, chillies, mustard and fenugreek 3 min until dark and fragrant; grind fine — this dark masala defines the dish.
2. Blend half the onions with garlic and ginger to a paste.
3. Heat oil in a casserole; fry remaining onions 10 min to gold, then the paste for 3 min.
4. Add turmeric and the ground masala; bloom 30 seconds.
5. Add pork and salt; sear 8 min until the meat is coated and edges brown.
6. Add 300 ml water; cover and simmer gently 1.5 h, stirring occasionally.
7. Uncover, add the balsamic-tamarind and curry leaves; reduce 15 min until the gravy is nearly black and clings to the pork.

## Plating & Service
Serve with steamed rice or coconut-laced kadambuttu-style dumplings; it should look dark and glossy, almost lacquered.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Improves for 3 days chilled. Beef short rib works with +30 min braise; keep the final reduction slow so the vinegar rounds rather than sharpens.
