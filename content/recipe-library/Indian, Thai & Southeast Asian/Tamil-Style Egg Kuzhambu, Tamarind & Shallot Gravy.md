# Tamil-Style Egg Kuzhambu, Tamarind & Shallot Gravy

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Jammy-yolked eggs in a punchy tamarind-shallot gravy — the ultimate store-cupboard main when the fridge is down to eggs and aromatics.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- eggs — 8
- small shallots, halved — 250 g
- vegetable or sesame oil — 45 ml
- black mustard seeds — 4 g
- fenugreek seeds — 1.5 g
- curry leaves — 15
- garlic, sliced — 20 g
- tomatoes, chopped — 250 g
- sambar powder or (coriander 8 g + chilli 5 g + cumin 3 g, ground) — 16 g
- ground turmeric — 3 g
- tamarind paste — 30 g
- jaggery — 10 g
- fine sea salt — 10 g
- coriander leaves — 10 g

## Method
1. Boil eggs 7 min from boiling water, shock in iced water, peel; score lightly and flash-fry in 10 ml oil with a pinch of turmeric until blistered gold. Set aside.
2. Heat remaining oil; crackle mustard seeds, add fenugreek and curry leaves, 20 seconds.
3. Add shallots; cook 8 min until golden at the edges. Add garlic, 2 min.
4. Bloom sambar powder and turmeric 20 seconds; add tomatoes and cook 6 min to a pulp.
5. Add tamarind, jaggery, salt and 400 ml water; simmer 12–15 min until the gravy thickens and the oil rings the edge.
6. Halve the eggs and slide them in yolk-up; spoon gravy over and simmer 2 min.
7. Rest 5 min; scatter coriander.

## Plating & Service
Serve over steamed rice or with dosa; make sure each plate gets whole shallots and a yolk-up egg half.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add okra or drumstick-style green beans with the tomatoes; for vegan, swap eggs for pan-seared king oyster mushrooms.
