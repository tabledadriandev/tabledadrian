# Sambal Terasi, Fried Shrimp-Paste Sambal

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The deep, funky classic — chillies, shallots and toasted terasi fried down with palm sugar and tomato; the sambal that makes nasi goreng taste like Java.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- fresh red chillies — 150 g
- bird's-eye chillies — 15 g
- shallots — 80 g
- garlic — 20 g
- terasi (shrimp paste) — 12 g
- tomato, chopped — 150 g
- palm sugar — 20 g
- fine sea salt — 5 g
- lime juice — 15 ml
- vegetable oil — 60 ml

## Method
1. Toast the terasi in foil in a dry pan, 2 min per side, until dry and pungent.
2. Blend or pound chillies, shallots, garlic and terasi to a coarse paste.
3. Heat the oil in a wok over medium heat; fry the paste 6–8 min, stirring, until it darkens and the oil separates back out.
4. Add tomato; fry 5 min more until jammy and the rawness is gone.
5. Season with palm sugar and salt; 2 min.
6. Off the heat, lime juice in.
7. Cool; the flavour settles and rounds within the hour.

## Plating & Service
Serve with nasi lemak, fried chicken, grilled fish or plain rice and cucumber — a little does a lot.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Keeps 10 days chilled under oil. Halve the bird's-eyes for guest-safe heat; it should still make itself known.
