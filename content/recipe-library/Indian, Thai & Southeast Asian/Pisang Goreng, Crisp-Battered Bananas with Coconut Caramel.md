# Pisang Goreng, Crisp-Battered Bananas with Coconut Caramel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Street bananas in a rice-flour batter that stays glassy-crisp, with a salted coconut caramel — five minutes of frying, disproportionate joy.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- firm ripe bananas — 4
- rice flour — 100 g
- plain flour — 50 g
- cornflour — 20 g
- baking powder — 3 g
- fine sea salt — 4 g
- caster sugar — 15 g
- ice-cold sparkling water — about 180 ml
- ground cinnamon — 1 g
- oil for deep frying — 1 L
- coconut cream — 150 ml
- palm sugar — 80 g
- lime — 1

## Method
1. Caramel: melt palm sugar with the coconut cream and 1 g salt; simmer 4–5 min to a light toffee sauce. Keep warm.
2. Whisk all flours, baking powder, remaining salt, sugar and cinnamon; add sparkling water to a thin, just-coating batter. Keep over ice.
3. Halve bananas lengthways.
4. Oil to 180 °C.
5. Dip each piece; fry 3–4 min, turning once, until pale gold and rigid.
6. Rest 2 min on a rack; the crust sets glassy.
7. Optional double-fry 45 seconds at 190 °C for service delays.

## Plating & Service
Three halves per plate, caramel ribboned, lime squeezed over at the last second — the acid flips the whole dish awake. Vanilla ice cream is welcome company.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Jackfruit or pineapple rings fry in the same batter; swap plain flour for more rice flour to go gluten-free (slightly more brittle, arguably better).
