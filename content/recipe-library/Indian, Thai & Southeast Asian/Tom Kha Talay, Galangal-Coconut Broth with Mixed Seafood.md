# Tom Kha Talay, Galangal-Coconut Broth with Mixed Seafood

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Gentler sister of tom yum — a velvet coconut-galangal broth that lets Riviera seafood shine; deceptively simple, entirely about balance.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- mixed seafood (prawns, squid rings, mussels, firm fish) — 600 g
- coconut milk — 600 ml
- chicken or fish stock — 400 ml
- galangal, sliced — 30 g
- lemongrass, bruised — 40 g
- makrut lime leaves, torn — 6
- bird's-eye chillies, bruised — 3
- oyster mushrooms, torn — 150 g
- fish sauce — 35 ml
- lime juice — 45 ml
- palm sugar — 10 g
- cherry tomatoes, halved — 100 g
- coriander leaves — 10 g

## Method
1. Simmer stock with galangal, lemongrass and lime leaves 8 min — infuse hard; these are eaten around, not removed.
2. Add coconut milk and palm sugar; return to a bare simmer. Do not boil hard from here.
3. Add mushrooms; 3 min. Add tomatoes; 1 min.
4. Add seafood by firmness: fish first, 2 min; then squid and prawns, 2 min; mussels until they open.
5. Season with fish sauce and the bruised chillies.
6. Off the heat, add lime juice; taste — sour and salty in balance over a sweet coconut base, heat as an undertone.
7. Rest 2 min for the seafood to settle to succulence.

## Plating & Service
Big bowls, seafood arranged, broth ladled over, coriander scattered; lime wedge and extra fish sauce on the side.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken-only version (tom kha gai) simmers thigh slices 8 min; vegan with tofu, king oysters and soy-based seasoning.
