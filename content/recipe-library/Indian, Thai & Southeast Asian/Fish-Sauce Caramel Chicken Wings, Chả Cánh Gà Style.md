# Fish-Sauce Caramel Chicken Wings, Chả Cánh Gà Style

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Double-cooked wings lacquered in fish-sauce caramel with fried garlic — sticky, salty-sweet, and the first empty plate at any apéro.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- chicken wings, split at the joint — 900 g
- cornflour — 30 g
- fine sea salt — 5 g
- oil for frying — 1 L (or oven method)
- fish sauce — 45 ml
- palm or caster sugar — 60 g
- water — 30 ml
- garlic, chopped — 25 g
- lime juice — 15 ml
- bird's-eye chilli, sliced — 1
- coriander leaves — 10 g
- black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Dry the wings hard; toss with salt and cornflour.
2. Fry at 170 °C, 8–9 min, until cooked and lightly gold; rest 5 min. (Oven route: 220 °C on a rack, 40 min, turned once.)
3. Second fry at 190 °C, 2–3 min, to a deep crunch.
4. Fry the garlic in a small pan with 20 ml of the oil at 150 °C until pale gold, 90 seconds; drain instantly.
5. Caramel: sugar and water to amber (170 °C); off heat, whisk in fish sauce, then lime.
6. Return to low heat 1 min until syrupy.
7. Toss wings through the caramel; out fast; shower with fried garlic, chilli, pepper, coriander.

## Plating & Service
Pile on a platter with a stack of napkins and cold beer — the glaze sets slightly as it cools into a proper lacquer.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Same treatment flatters pork ribs (roast first) and cauliflower florets. Glaze holds a week chilled; re-melt gently.
