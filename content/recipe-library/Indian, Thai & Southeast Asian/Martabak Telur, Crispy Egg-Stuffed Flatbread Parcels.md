# Martabak Telur, Crispy Egg-Stuffed Flatbread Parcels

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The street-theatre stuffed pancake — hand-stretched dough folded around a spiced beef-and-egg custard, griddled crisp and cut into squares.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- plain flour, strong — 250 g
- water — 140 ml
- fine sea salt — 5 g
- vegetable oil — 30 ml + 100 ml for resting
- beef mince — 250 g
- onion, fine dice — 100 g
- spring onions, sliced — 50 g
- garlic, minced — 12 g
- curry powder — 10 g
- white pepper — 2 g
- eggs — 4
- light soy sauce — 10 ml
- oil for the griddle — 40 ml
- cucumber pickle (vinegar 40 ml, sugar 20 g, cucumber 150 g) — 210 g

## Method
1. Dough: knead flour, salt, water and 30 ml oil 8 min until silky. Divide in 4, ball, and submerge in oil to rest 2 h minimum — the oil bath makes the stretch possible.
2. Filling: brown beef with onion and garlic 6 min; add curry powder and white pepper, 1 min; cool. Mix with spring onion, eggs and soy — a loose savoury custard.
3. Oil a large work surface. Flatten one ball and stretch paper-thin to about 35 × 35 cm, lifting and pulling the edges.
4. Ladle a quarter of the filling into the centre square.
5. Fold all four sides over to a sealed parcel.
6. Griddle in 10 ml oil over medium heat 3–4 min per side, pressing, until deep brown and crisp with the egg just set inside.
7. Rest 2 min; cut into squares.

## Plating & Service
Serve stacked with the quick cucumber pickle and sliced bird's-eye in vinegar — rich parcel, sharp sides.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken or a leek-and-egg vegetarian filling work; store-bought spring roll pastry (two sheets, layered) rescues rough seas when stretching is impossible.
