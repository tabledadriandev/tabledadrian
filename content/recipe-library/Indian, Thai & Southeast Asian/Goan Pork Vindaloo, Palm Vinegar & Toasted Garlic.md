# Goan Pork Vindaloo, Palm Vinegar & Toasted Garlic

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> True vindaloo is a sour-hot Portuguese-Goan braise, not a heat contest — this one balances palm vinegar, jaggery and a slow-built chilli paste.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- pork shoulder, cut in 4 cm cubes — 1.2 kg
- dried Kashmiri chillies, seeded — 12
- coconut or palm vinegar (or cider vinegar) — 90 ml
- garlic cloves — 30 g
- ginger — 25 g
- cumin seeds — 6 g
- black peppercorns — 4 g
- cloves — 4
- cinnamon stick — 1 (5 cm)
- black mustard seeds — 3 g
- ground turmeric — 4 g
- vegetable oil — 50 ml
- onions, sliced — 300 g
- jaggery or dark brown sugar — 20 g
- fine sea salt — 12 g

## Method
1. Soak chillies in the vinegar with 50 ml warm water for 20 min.
2. Toast cumin, peppercorns, cloves, cinnamon and mustard seeds in a dry pan 2 min; grind fine.
3. Blend soaked chillies and their liquid with garlic, ginger, ground spices and turmeric to a smooth paste.
4. Massage half the paste into the pork with 6 g salt; marinate 1 h (or overnight chilled).
5. Heat oil in a casserole; cook onions 12 min to deep gold. Add remaining paste and fry 3–4 min until the oil separates.
6. Add pork and marinade; sear 5 min, then add 300 ml water, jaggery and remaining salt.
7. Cover and simmer very gently 1.5 h, stirring occasionally, until the pork is tender and the sauce is dark, thick and sharp-edged. Rest 10 min; the flavour rounds as it sits.

## Plating & Service
Bowl it deep with steamed rice and a cooling kachumber; a day's rest in the fridge makes it even better for charter prep.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Lamb shoulder or firm cauliflower + potato both take the same paste; reduce chillies to 8 and add more jaggery for gentler palates.
