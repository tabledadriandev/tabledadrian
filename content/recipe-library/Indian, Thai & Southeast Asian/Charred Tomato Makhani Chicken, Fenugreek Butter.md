# Charred Tomato Makhani Chicken, Fenugreek Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A makhani built on blistered tomatoes and a kasoori methi beurre noisette — silkier and smokier than any takeaway version, perfect for a charter crowd-pleaser.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- chicken thigh fillets, boneless — 700 g
- full-fat yogurt — 100 g
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 8 g
- garam masala — 6 g
- plum tomatoes — 600 g
- unsalted butter — 80 g
- double cream — 120 ml
- ginger, julienned — 25 g
- garlic, minced — 20 g
- green cardamom pods — 4
- cinnamon stick — 1 (5 cm)
- kasoori methi (dried fenugreek) — 4 g
- honey — 15 g
- fine sea salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Marinate chicken in yogurt, half the Kashmiri chilli, half the garam masala and 4 g salt for at least 30 min (up to 12 h chilled).
2. Blister whole tomatoes under a 250 °C grill for 10–12 min until skins char; blend to a smooth passata.
3. Roast chicken on a rack at 240 °C for 14 min until edges char; rest.
4. In a heavy pan, melt 60 g butter over medium heat with cardamom and cinnamon; when foaming subsides and it smells nutty (about 2 min), add garlic and ginger, cook 1 min.
5. Add remaining chilli powder, bloom 20 seconds, then pour in tomato purée. Simmer 15 min at a gentle bubble until thick and brick-red.
6. Crush kasoori methi between palms into the sauce; add honey, cream and remaining salt. Simmer 3 min.
7. Fold in chicken with its resting juices; simmer 5 min. Finish with remaining 20 g butter swirled in off the heat.

## Plating & Service
Serve in a warm shallow bowl, a swirl of cream, ginger julienne and a few methi flakes on top; naan alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap cream for coconut cream and butter for coconut oil to go dairy-free; paneer or roasted cauliflower replaces chicken for vegetarian.
