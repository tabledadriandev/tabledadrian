# Klepon-Style Pandan Rice Dumplings with Molten Palm Sugar

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Chewy pandan-green glutinous rice balls hiding liquid palm-sugar centres, rolled in salted coconut — one bite, a controlled explosion.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- glutinous rice flour — 200 g
- rice flour — 20 g
- pandan juice (15 g leaves blitzed with 140 ml water, strained; or extract + water) — 150 ml
- fine sea salt — 4 g
- gula melaka or dark palm sugar, chopped fine — 90 g
- desiccated or fresh grated coconut — 100 g
- water for boiling — 2 L

## Method
1. Steam or microwave the coconut with 2 g salt for 1 min (keeps it fresh through service); cool on a plate.
2. Mix both flours with 2 g salt; add warm pandan juice gradually, kneading to a smooth, pliable dough — earlobe-soft, not sticky.
3. Roll into 20 balls; keep covered.
4. Flatten each in the palm, cup it, and fill with a packed half-teaspoon of chopped palm sugar; pinch firmly shut and re-roll — any gap leaks in the pot.
5. Boil in batches: they sink, then float. From floating, give them 2 min more — this melts the core.
6. Lift straight into the coconut; roll to coat while hot and tacky.
7. Rest 5 min — the molten centre is genuinely molten.

## Plating & Service
Serve slightly warm, three per person, with the standing instruction: whole ball, one bite, mouth closed. The table learns fast.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Sweet-potato-enriched dough (add 60 g mash) is a Javanese variant; they're best within 4 hours — the chew stiffens overnight, though 10 seconds of steam revives them.
