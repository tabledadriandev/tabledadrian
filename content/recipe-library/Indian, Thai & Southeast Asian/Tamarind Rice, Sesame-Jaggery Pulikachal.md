# Tamarind Rice, Sesame-Jaggery Pulikachal

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Temple-style puliyodharai — rice dressed in a dark, hot-sour tamarind concentrate with sesame and jaggery. Keeps beautifully; flavour deepens by the hour.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- cooked rice, cooled and separated — 700 g
- tamarind paste — 60 g
- sesame oil — 60 ml
- black mustard seeds — 5 g
- chana dal — 15 g
- raw peanuts — 50 g
- dried red chillies — 4
- curry leaves — 18
- ground turmeric — 3 g
- asafoetida — 0.5 g
- jaggery — 20 g
- sesame seeds, toasted and ground — 15 g
- coriander seeds, toasted and ground — 6 g
- fenugreek seeds, toasted and ground — 1 g
- fine sea salt — 9 g

## Method
1. Heat 45 ml sesame oil; crackle mustard seeds, then fry chana dal and peanuts to gold, 2 min.
2. Add chillies, curry leaves and asafoetida; 30 seconds.
3. Add turmeric, then tamarind, jaggery, salt and 150 ml water.
4. Simmer 10–12 min to a thick, dark, glossy paste (pulikachal) — oil should re-emerge at the edges.
5. Stir in the ground sesame, coriander and fenugreek; 1 min more.
6. Fold two-thirds of the paste through the rice with the remaining 15 ml raw sesame oil; taste and add more paste to preference.
7. Rest 20 min minimum before serving.

## Plating & Service
Serve ambient with papad and yogurt; pack the extra pulikachal in a jar — it keeps a month chilled and rescues any plain rice.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cashews swap for peanuts; a pinch of chilli powder in the paste for more fire. The dish is at its best 4 hours after mixing.
