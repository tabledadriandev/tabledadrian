# Sambal Ijo, Green Chilli & Tomatillo-Style Padang Sambal

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Padang's green sambal — steamed green chillies and shallots crushed coarse and fried in oil; grassy, savoury heat that flatters fried fish and rice.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- large green chillies — 200 g
- green bird's-eye chillies — 15 g
- shallots — 80 g
- garlic — 15 g
- green tomato or tomatillo (or underripe tomato) — 100 g
- fine sea salt — 6 g
- caster sugar — 4 g
- lime juice — 15 ml
- vegetable oil — 70 ml

## Method
1. Steam the chillies, shallots, garlic and green tomato 8–10 min until softened and dull green.
2. Pound to a coarse, flaky crush — sambal ijo is rustic; visible flecks of chilli skin are correct.
3. Heat the oil in a wok over medium heat.
4. Fry the crushed mix 6–8 min, stirring, until the raw smell turns savoury and the oil greens slightly.
5. Season with salt and sugar; 1 min.
6. Off the heat, lime juice.
7. Serve warm or ambient.

## Plating & Service
The classic partner to fried fish, ayam goreng and rendang — its grassy heat cuts rich food better than any red sambal.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Keeps a week chilled. For the full Padang table, serve it beside a red sambal and let guests triangulate.
