# Slow Lamb Shoulder Rogan Josh, Ratan Jot Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Kashmiri-style braise where the colour comes from chilli and alkanet-infused oil, not tomato — a deep, aromatic centrepiece for a cool evening at anchor.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- lamb shoulder, bone-in, cut in 5 cm pieces — 1.4 kg
- mustard oil (or vegetable oil) — 60 ml
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 15 g
- ground fennel seed — 10 g
- dried ginger powder (sonth) — 6 g
- green cardamom pods — 6
- black cardamom pods — 2
- cloves — 5
- cinnamon stick — 1 (6 cm)
- bay leaves — 2
- onions, finely sliced — 300 g
- garlic paste — 20 g
- full-bodied lamb or chicken stock — 500 ml
- fine sea salt — 12 g

## Method
1. Heat mustard oil in a heavy casserole to just smoking (about 190 °C), cool 1 min, then add both cardamoms, cloves, cinnamon and bay; sizzle 30 seconds.
2. Add onions with a pinch of salt; cook over medium heat 15 min to deep gold.
3. Add garlic paste, cook 2 min, then lamb in batches, browning well, about 12 min total.
4. Off the heat, stir in Kashmiri chilli mixed to a slurry with 50 ml water (this blooms colour without scorching). Return to low heat 1 min.
5. Add fennel, dried ginger, salt and stock. Bring to a bare simmer.
6. Cover and braise at 150 °C in the oven for 2–2.5 h until the lamb yields to a spoon.
7. Skim, then reduce the sauce on the hob to a glossy, spoon-coating consistency; adjust salt and a pinch more fennel.

## Plating & Service
Pile into a warmed copper-style dish, bones proudly visible, sauce spooned over; steamed basmati and a sharp onion salad alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Use goat or beef chuck with the same timing; for a lighter version skim all fat and finish with a spoon of yogurt (adds dairy).
