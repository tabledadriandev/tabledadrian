# Massaman Curry of Braised Beef Cheek, Roasted Peanuts & New Potatoes

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Thailand's Persian-influenced curry taken seriously: beef cheeks braised to collapse in a roasted-spice paste with tamarind, palm sugar and peanuts.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- beef cheeks, trimmed, halved — 1.2 kg
- dried long red chillies, seeded — 10
- shallots — 60 g
- garlic — 30 g
- galangal — 15 g
- lemongrass — 25 g
- coriander seeds — 6 g
- cumin seeds — 3 g
- cinnamon — 1 stick (5 cm)
- green cardamom — 4
- cloves — 3
- white peppercorns — 2 g
- shrimp paste — 8 g
- coconut cream — 250 ml
- coconut milk — 600 ml
- roasted peanuts — 80 g
- palm sugar — 40 g
- fish sauce — 40 ml
- tamarind paste — 35 g
- new potatoes, halved — 400 g

## Method
1. Roast shallots, garlic, galangal and lemongrass in a dry pan or 220 °C oven until charred-soft, 10 min. Toast coriander, cumin, cinnamon, cardamom, cloves and peppercorns 2 min; grind.
2. Soak chillies in hot water 15 min; pound with the roasted aromatics, ground spices and shrimp paste to a smooth massaman paste.
3. Crack the coconut cream in a heavy casserole 5 min until oil separates; fry the paste 5 min until deeply fragrant.
4. Add beef cheeks, turning to coat; add coconut milk and 300 ml water.
5. Cover and braise at 150 °C for 2.5 h until a spoon slides through the cheeks.
6. Add potatoes, peanuts, palm sugar, fish sauce and tamarind; simmer uncovered 25 min until potatoes are tender and the sauce is a rich, russet, slightly oily gravy.
7. Rest 15 min; balance again — sweet should be forward but chased by sour and salt.

## Plating & Service
One cheek half per bowl, potatoes and peanuts around, sauce over; jasmine rice and cucumber relish (ajad) cut the richness.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Lamb shanks or short rib braise the same way; chicken thigh version is 40 min total. Cashews swap for peanuts if the manifest requires.
