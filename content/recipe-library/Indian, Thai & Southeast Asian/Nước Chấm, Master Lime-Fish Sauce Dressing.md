# Nước Chấm, Master Lime-Fish Sauce Dressing

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Vietnamese master sauce — fish sauce, lime, sugar and water in proper proportion; drinkably balanced, endlessly adjustable, on the table at every meal.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- fish sauce (good quality, first press) — 60 ml
- lime juice, fresh — 60 ml
- caster or palm sugar — 50 g
- warm water — 180 ml
- garlic, minced fine — 10 g
- bird's-eye chilli, minced — 5 g
- rice vinegar — 10 ml
- carrot, hair-fine julienne (optional) — 20 g

## Method
1. Dissolve the sugar fully in the warm water.
2. Add fish sauce, then lime juice, then vinegar; stir.
3. Taste before aromatics: it should be balanced enough to sip — sweet, sour, salty in conversation, none shouting.
4. Adjust: more water if fierce, more lime if flat, more sugar if harsh.
5. Add garlic and chilli — they float when the balance is right (an old cook's tell).
6. Add carrot julienne if using.
7. Rest 10 min; serve in shallow individual bowls.

## Plating & Service
The dip for rolls, the dressing for bún bowls, the sauce for grilled everything — make double, always.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Keeps 5 days chilled without the garlic (add fresh per service). Vegan: salt + a splash of soy + pineapple juice makes a worthy chay version.
