# Green Curry Paste from Scratch, Pounded Mortar Method

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The master green paste — pounded in strict order so each element breaks down fully; makes enough for three curries and freezes flat.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- green bird's-eye chillies — 25 g
- large mild green chillies — 60 g
- fine sea salt — 5 g
- white peppercorns — 3 g
- coriander seeds, toasted — 6 g
- cumin seeds, toasted — 3 g
- lemongrass, tender core, sliced fine — 45 g
- galangal, chopped — 20 g
- coriander roots and lower stems — 20 g
- makrut lime zest — 5 g
- garlic — 30 g
- shallots — 50 g
- shrimp paste, foil-roasted 2 min per side — 12 g

## Method
1. Grind the toasted coriander, cumin and peppercorns to powder; set aside.
2. Pound both chillies with the salt to a wet pulp — the salt is your abrasive. 3–4 min of honest work.
3. Add lemongrass; pound smooth. It's the most fibrous element, so it goes early.
4. Add galangal, then coriander root, then lime zest, pounding each smooth before the next.
5. Add garlic and shallots; pound to a paste — moisture rises here and the paste loosens.
6. Add the ground spices and shrimp paste; work 2 min to a uniform, fragrant green paste.
7. Rest 10 min; the aroma rounds. (Food processor: same order, minimal water, expect 85% of the glory.)

## Plating & Service
Portion into 60 g pucks — one per curry for four. This paste is a gift to your future self mid-charter.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Keeps 10 days chilled under a film of oil, 3 months frozen. Omit shrimp paste + add 5 g white miso for vegan galley stock.
