# Sangkhaya Fak Thong, Pandan Custard-Steamed Baby Squash

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A whole baby squash becomes the baking dish — filled with pandan-coconut custard and steamed until sliceable; a table-side reveal that costs almost nothing.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- baby kabocha or acorn squash (about 800 g) — 1
- eggs — 4
- coconut milk — 250 ml
- palm sugar — 100 g
- pandan leaves, blitzed with 30 ml water and strained (or 2 drops pandan extract) — 20 g
- fine sea salt — 2 g
- cornflour — 5 g
- coconut cream, to serve — 60 ml

## Method
1. Cut a neat lid from the squash; scrape out seeds and fibres completely. Rinse and dry the cavity.
2. Whisk eggs gently with palm sugar (melted in the warmed coconut milk), pandan water, salt and slaked cornflour — do not aerate; foam becomes holes.
3. Strain the custard twice for silk.
4. Fill the squash to 1 cm below the rim; lid alongside, not on (it traps steam drips).
5. Steam over gentle rolling steam 40–50 min until the custard reads 82 °C at the centre and barely trembles.
6. Cool completely, then chill 3 h — it must be fully cold to slice cleanly.
7. Cut into wedges through squash and custard together.

## Plating & Service
A wedge per plate, green custard against orange flesh, a thread of coconut cream — quietly spectacular.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Individual version: steam the custard in ramekins 18 min inside hollowed mini squash halves. Butternut halves work when kabocha hides.
