# Kashmiri Dum Aloo, Yogurt-Fennel Gravy & Baby Potatoes

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Whole baby potatoes, fried then slow-steamed in a tomato-free yogurt gravy scented with fennel and dried ginger — Kashmiri vegetarian cooking at its most elegant.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- baby potatoes, peeled — 700 g
- vegetable oil — 50 ml
- yogurt, whisked — 250 g
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 10 g
- ground fennel seed — 8 g
- dried ginger powder — 5 g
- green cardamom — 4
- cloves — 3
- cinnamon — 1 stick (4 cm)
- bay leaf — 1
- gram flour — 10 g
- garam masala — 3 g
- fine sea salt — 10 g
- fresh coriander — 10 g

## Method
1. Prick potatoes all over with a skewer; par-boil in salted water 8 min, drain and dry.
2. Fry potatoes in the oil over medium-high heat 8–10 min, rolling, until deep gold and blistered. Set aside; keep 30 ml oil in the pan.
3. Whisk yogurt with gram flour (stabilises it) and the chilli powder slaked in 30 ml water.
4. Sizzle cardamom, cloves, cinnamon and bay in the pan 30 seconds.
5. Off the heat, stir in the yogurt mix; return to low heat, stirring constantly until it simmers and thickens, 5 min.
6. Add fennel, dried ginger, salt, potatoes and 150 ml water; cover tightly and cook on the lowest heat (dum) 20 min.
7. Finish with garam masala; the gravy should be pale brick, clinging, with oil at the rim.

## Plating & Service
Serve with steamed rice or Kashmiri-style bread, coriander over; each potato should be creamy right through.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: coconut yogurt + extra gram flour. Add fried paneer cubes alongside potatoes for a richer plate.
