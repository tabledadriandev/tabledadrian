# Burani Raita, Charred Garlic Yogurt with Cumin Smoke

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Hyderabad's garlic raita — yogurt whipped with slow-fried garlic and toasted cumin; the designated cooling system for biryani and kebab plates.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- thick whole-milk yogurt — 400 g
- garlic, sliced — 20 g
- ghee or oil — 20 g
- cumin seeds — 4 g
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 2 g
- fine sea salt — 5 g
- caster sugar — 3 g
- mint leaves, fine chiffonade — 8 g
- black salt (optional) — 1 g

## Method
1. Whisk the yogurt smooth with salt, sugar and black salt; loosen with 20–30 ml cold water to a thick pouring cream.
2. Fry the garlic slices in the ghee over low heat, 3–4 min, to an even pale gold — one shade darker turns bitter.
3. Add cumin seeds to the same pan for the last 20 seconds.
4. Tip half the garlic-cumin ghee into the yogurt; whisk through.
5. Fold in the mint.
6. Chill 20 min — garlic mellows and infuses.
7. Serve topped with the remaining fried garlic, its ghee, and a dust of chilli powder.

## Plating & Service
A cold, garlicky pool beside biryani, tikka or kebabs — spoon it on like a sauce, not a side salad.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Coconut yogurt converts it for dairy-free. Add grated cucumber (squeezed) for volume; it keeps 2 days chilled.
