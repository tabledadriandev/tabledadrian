# Son-in-Law Eggs, Crispy Fried Eggs in Tamarind Caramel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Jammy boiled eggs deep-fried to a blistered golden skin, drowned in sticky tamarind sauce with fried shallots — sweet, sour and dangerous.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- eggs — 6
- oil for frying — 500 ml
- tamarind paste — 40 g
- palm sugar — 60 g
- fish sauce (or light soy for vegetarian) — 25 ml
- water — 60 ml
- shallots, fine-sliced — 60 g
- bird's-eye chilli, sliced — 1
- coriander leaves — 10 g

## Method
1. Boil eggs 7 min from boiling; shock hard in iced water, peel, and dry completely.
2. Fry the sliced shallots in 150 °C oil, stirring, 4–6 min to even gold; drain fast (they darken after). Keep the oil.
3. Simmer palm sugar, tamarind, fish sauce and water 4–5 min to a glossy, pourable caramel.
4. Raise the oil to 180 °C; lower in the dried eggs.
5. Fry 3–4 min, rolling, until the whites blister gold all over. Stand back — they can spit.
6. Halve the eggs; yolks should be fudgy.
7. Sauce over, then shallots, chilli and coriander.

## Plating & Service
Serve two halves per person on a spoon-friendly dish; the contrast of crisp skin, soft yolk and sticky sauce is the entire argument.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian with soy seasoning; scale to a canapé by using quail eggs (2 min fry). The sauce holds a week chilled.
