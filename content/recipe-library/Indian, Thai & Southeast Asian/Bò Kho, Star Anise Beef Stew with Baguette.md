# Bò Kho, Star Anise Beef Stew with Baguette

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> French pot-au-feu logic, Vietnamese aromatics — beef and carrot in a lemongrass–star anise gravy, mopped with crusty baguette. Perfect rough-weather food.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- beef chuck, 4 cm dice — 1.2 kg
- lemongrass, bruised — 60 g
- star anise — 3
- cinnamon stick — 1 (5 cm)
- five-spice powder — 4 g
- fish sauce — 45 ml
- tomato paste — 40 g
- garlic, minced — 20 g
- ginger, minced — 20 g
- onion, chopped — 200 g
- carrots, thick oblique cuts — 400 g
- coconut water or stock — 900 ml
- annatto or paprika oil — 20 ml
- sugar — 10 g
- fine sea salt — 8 g
- Thai basil + coriander — 20 g
- baguette — 2

## Method
1. Marinate beef 30 min: fish sauce, five-spice, half the garlic and ginger, sugar.
2. Heat annatto oil in a casserole; brown beef in batches, 8 min total.
3. Add onion, remaining garlic and ginger; 3 min. Stir in tomato paste; cook 2 min.
4. Add coconut water, lemongrass, star anise, cinnamon, salt; scrape the fond.
5. Simmer, covered, 1.5–2 h until the beef is yielding.
6. Add carrots; uncovered, 25 min more, until tender and the gravy has light body (mash a carrot chunk in if it needs thickening).
7. Fish out lemongrass and whole spices; check salt-sweet balance.

## Plating & Service
Deep bowls, herbs on top, torn warm baguette for dunking — or ladle over wide rice noodles for phở-style service.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Oxtail version is spectacular (+1 h). Gluten-free with rice instead of bread. The stew improves for 2 days chilled.
