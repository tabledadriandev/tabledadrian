# Sayur Lodeh, Young Vegetable & Coconut Turmeric Stew

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A market-basket coconut stew — vegetables in a turmeric-lemongrass broth that's light enough for lunch on deck yet layered enough to satisfy.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- coconut milk — 500 ml
- vegetable stock — 500 ml
- shallots — 60 g
- garlic — 20 g
- candlenuts or macadamias — 25 g
- fresh turmeric — 12 g
- coriander seeds, toasted — 5 g
- galangal, bruised — 20 g
- lemongrass, bruised — 2 stalks
- makrut lime leaves — 3
- chayote or courgette, batons — 250 g
- green beans — 150 g
- cabbage, squares — 200 g
- carrot, oblique — 120 g
- firm tofu, cubes — 200 g
- palm sugar — 12 g
- fine sea salt — 10 g
- vegetable oil — 30 ml
- bird's-eye chilli, whole — 2

## Method
1. Blend shallots, garlic, candlenuts, turmeric and ground coriander to a paste.
2. Fry the paste in oil 5 min until fragrant and pale gold.
3. Add stock, galangal, lemongrass, lime leaves, whole chillies, palm sugar and salt; simmer 5 min.
4. Add carrot; 5 min. Beans and chayote; 5 min.
5. Add coconut milk and tofu; return to a gentle simmer — do not boil hard.
6. Add cabbage; 4–5 min until just tender with bite.
7. Taste — savoury-sweet, turmeric-gold, the chilli only whispering.

## Plating & Service
Ladle into wide bowls over rice or rice cakes; make sure each bowl gets a whole chilli for those who want to crush it in.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Any young vegetable works — asparagus, fennel, chard stems. Add prawns (3 min) for pescatarian tables; cashews replace candlenuts.
