# Khao Pad Sapparot, Pineapple Fried Rice with Prawns & Cashews

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The banquet fried rice — day-old jasmine tossed with prawns, pineapple, curry powder and cashews, served (why not) in the hollowed fruit.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- cooked jasmine rice, day-old, chilled — 600 g
- raw prawns, peeled — 250 g
- fresh pineapple, 2 cm chunks — 250 g
- eggs — 2
- onion, dice — 80 g
- garlic, minced — 15 g
- mild curry powder — 6 g
- fish sauce — 25 ml
- light soy sauce — 15 ml
- sugar — 6 g
- spring onions, sliced — 30 g
- cashews, toasted — 60 g
- raisins (optional) — 20 g
- vegetable oil — 45 ml
- white pepper — 1 g
- lime + cucumber to serve — 100 g

## Method
1. Break the cold rice apart with wet hands — no clumps.
2. Wok to smoking; 15 ml oil, prawns 90 seconds until barely pink; remove.
3. Remaining oil; eggs in, scramble hard 20 seconds; add garlic and onion, 60 seconds.
4. Rice in; toss and press against the wok 2 min, letting it catch and jump.
5. Curry powder over; toss 30 seconds. Season with fish sauce, soy, sugar, white pepper.
6. Pineapple, prawns, raisins back in; 60 seconds — the fruit should warm, not stew.
7. Off heat: spring onions and cashews.

## Plating & Service
Serve in the hollowed pineapple halves for full charter theatre, cucumber and lime alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken or just egg both work; drop the prawns and fish sauce (use soy) for vegetarian. Cold rice is the only rule that cannot bend.
