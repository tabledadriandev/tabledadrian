# Lamb Saag with Mustard Greens & Brown Onion Masala

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Slow-braised lamb folded through a rough-textured two-green purée — the Punjabi trick of mustard greens adds a horseradish-like lift.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- lamb shoulder, 4 cm dice — 1 kg
- ghee — 50 g
- onions, sliced — 350 g
- garlic-ginger paste — 40 g
- cumin seeds — 5 g
- black cardamom — 1
- ground coriander — 10 g
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 6 g
- ground turmeric — 4 g
- tomatoes, chopped — 250 g
- spinach — 500 g
- mustard greens or rocket — 150 g
- green chillies — 2
- garam masala — 4 g
- fine sea salt — 12 g

## Method
1. Melt ghee in a casserole; sizzle cumin and black cardamom 30 seconds, then brown onions slowly, 15 min, to a deep mahogany.
2. Add garlic-ginger paste 2 min; bloom coriander, chilli and turmeric 20 seconds.
3. Add tomatoes; cook 8 min to a thick masala. Add lamb and salt, sear 6 min in the paste.
4. Add 400 ml water; cover and braise at a low simmer (or 150 °C oven) for 1.5 h until tender.
5. Meanwhile blanch spinach and mustard greens 60 seconds in salted boiling water, shock in iced water, squeeze, and pulse-blend with the chillies to a coarse purée.
6. Uncover the lamb, reduce liquid to a cling, then fold in the green purée; simmer 10 min so greens and gravy marry without dulling.
7. Finish with garam masala and a small knob of raw ghee.

## Plating & Service
Serve family-style with makki-style flatbreads or basmati, julienned ginger fried crisp on top.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken thigh version braises in 35 min; vegan with chickpeas and the same greens, coconut oil instead of ghee.
