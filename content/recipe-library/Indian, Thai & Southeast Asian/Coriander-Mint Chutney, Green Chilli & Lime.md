# Coriander-Mint Chutney, Green Chilli & Lime

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The essential green chutney — herb-dense, hot and sharp, emulsified with a little oil so it stays vivid for days instead of hours.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- coriander, leaves and tender stems — 100 g
- mint leaves — 40 g
- green chillies — 10 g
- ginger — 15 g
- garlic — 5 g
- lime juice — 40 ml
- caster sugar — 8 g
- fine sea salt — 6 g
- cumin, toasted and ground — 2 g
- cold water — 30 ml
- vegetable oil — 20 ml
- ice cube — 1

## Method
1. Chill the herbs; wash and spin-dry.
2. Blend chillies, ginger, garlic, lime, sugar, salt and cumin with the water first — a smooth base before the leaves go in.
3. Add coriander, mint, the ice cube and oil; blitz in short pulses to a thick, pourable purée. The ice fights blade-heat; the oil locks the colour.
4. Taste: hot, sour, salty, faintly sweet — it should slap.
5. Adjust with lime or salt; loosen with water only if needed.
6. Rest 10 min for the flavours to fuse.
7. Store airtight with cling film pressed on the surface.

## Plating & Service
Serve with everything fried in this library, swirl into yogurt for an instant raita-dip, or brush inside sandwiches.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 30 g roasted peanuts or coconut for a thicker dosa-style version; keeps 4 days chilled, freezes perfectly in ice-cube trays.
