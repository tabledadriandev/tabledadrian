# Sambal Oelek, Fresh Pounded Chilli Base

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The mother sambal — nothing but fresh red chillies, salt and vinegar, pounded coarse. Every other sambal starts here; every fridge should hold a jar.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- fresh red chillies (long, medium heat) — 250 g
- red bird's-eye chillies — 20 g
- fine sea salt — 8 g
- rice or cane vinegar — 20 ml
- caster sugar — 5 g
- lime juice — 10 ml
- vegetable oil — 15 ml

## Method
1. Stem the chillies; halve and seed the long ones (keep bird's-eye seeds for heat).
2. Blanch all chillies 60 seconds in boiling water; drain and dry — this brightens colour and tames raw harshness.
3. Pound with the salt in a mortar to a coarse, seedy crush — texture is the signature; a processor pulse works but stop early.
4. Stir in vinegar, sugar and lime.
5. Warm the oil and fold through — it carries flavour and helps keeping.
6. Rest 30 min before first use.
7. Jar; press the surface level and film with oil.

## Plating & Service
The default table condiment for everything in this collection — nasi goreng to grilled fish to fried eggs.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Keeps 2 weeks chilled, 3 months frozen in cubes. Roast the chillies instead of blanching for a smoky variant.
