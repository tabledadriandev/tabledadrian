# Bhel Puri, Puffed Rice Salad with Raw Mango & Tamarind

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bombay beach food — a toss-and-eat salad of puffed rice, chutneys and crunch that must be mixed at the very last moment and eaten faster.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- puffed rice (mamra) — 120 g
- sev — 60 g
- papdi crisps or crushed crackers — 60 g
- potato, boiled, small dice — 150 g
- red onion, fine dice — 80 g
- tomato, seeded, fine dice — 100 g
- raw green mango, fine dice — 60 g
- roasted peanuts — 40 g
- coriander leaves, chopped — 15 g
- tamarind-date chutney — 90 g
- coriander-mint chutney — 50 g
- chaat masala — 4 g
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 2 g
- lime — 1

## Method
1. Crisp the puffed rice in a dry pan over low heat 3–4 min if it has softened at sea; cool fully.
2. Have everything diced, chilled and lined up — bhel is assembly, not cooking.
3. In a wide bowl, toss puffed rice with potato, onion, tomato, mango and peanuts.
4. Add both chutneys, chaat masala and chilli powder; toss exactly until coated.
5. Fold in half the sev and crushed papdi.
6. Taste — sweet, sour, hot, salty should hit in one bite; correct with lime and chutney.
7. Top with remaining sev, papdi and coriander.

## Plating & Service
Serve instantly in paper cones or small bowls with teaspoons — after five minutes it softens and the magic is gone.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pomegranate seeds instead of mango when out of season; skip papdi for gluten-free. Prep all components ahead, toss table-side.
