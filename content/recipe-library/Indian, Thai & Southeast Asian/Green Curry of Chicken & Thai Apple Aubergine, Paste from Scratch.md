# Green Curry of Chicken & Thai Apple Aubergine, Paste from Scratch

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The full ceremony: a pounded-from-scratch green paste, coconut cream cracked in the wok, and a sauce that's vivid, hot and herbaceous — nothing from a jar comes close.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- chicken thighs, sliced — 600 g
- green bird's-eye chillies — 15 g
- large green chillies — 40 g
- lemongrass, sliced — 30 g
- galangal — 15 g
- coriander roots/stems — 15 g
- makrut lime zest — 3 g
- garlic — 20 g
- shallots — 40 g
- shrimp paste, roasted — 8 g
- white peppercorns — 2 g
- coriander seed, toasted — 4 g
- cumin seed, toasted — 2 g
- coconut cream — 200 ml
- coconut milk — 400 ml
- apple aubergines, quartered (or 200 g regular) — 200 g
- fish sauce — 30 ml
- palm sugar — 20 g
- makrut lime leaves — 4
- Thai basil — 20 g

## Method
1. Pound the paste: peppercorns and toasted seeds first, then lemongrass, galangal, coriander root, lime zest, garlic, shallots, both chillies with a pinch of salt, finally shrimp paste — 10–15 min to a fine, fragrant paste (or blitz with minimal water).
2. Simmer the coconut cream in a wok over medium-high heat 4–6 min until it thickens and the oil visibly cracks out.
3. Fry the paste in this cracked cream 3–4 min until it darkens slightly and smells cooked, not raw.
4. Add chicken; seal 3 min in the paste.
5. Add coconut milk, torn lime leaves, palm sugar and fish sauce; simmer 10 min.
6. Add aubergines; simmer 6–8 min until tender but holding shape.
7. Balance: it should lead salty-hot, then sweet, with the sauce loose enough to flood rice. Off the heat, fold in basil.

## Plating & Service
Serve in a deep bowl with jasmine rice, extra basil and a few slivers of red chilli for contrast against the green.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Prawns (3 min) or firm tofu + bamboo shoots make easy swaps; paste keeps 1 week chilled, 3 months frozen — scale it up.
