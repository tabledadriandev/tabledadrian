# Chả Cá-Style Turmeric Fish with Dill & Rice Noodles

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Hanoi's famous tableside fish — turmeric-galangal marinated cod flash-fried with a shocking amount of dill and spring onion, over vermicelli with peanuts.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- cod or monkfish, 4 cm chunks — 600 g
- ground turmeric — 6 g
- galangal or ginger, grated — 20 g
- shallot, grated — 30 g
- fish sauce — 30 ml
- plain yogurt or coconut yogurt — 40 g
- sugar — 8 g
- vegetable oil — 50 ml
- dill, fronds picked, stems chopped — 60 g
- spring onions, 5 cm batons — 80 g
- rice vermicelli, cooked — 400 g
- roasted peanuts, crushed — 40 g
- lime — 1
- nước chấm — 150 ml

## Method
1. Marinate fish 30–60 min: turmeric, galangal, shallot, fish sauce, yogurt (tenderises and helps browning), sugar and 15 ml oil.
2. Bring fish to room temperature; heat a wide pan or tabletop skillet with 20 ml oil until shimmering.
3. Lay fish in one layer; fry 2–3 min undisturbed until a golden crust forms, then turn once, 2 min.
4. Push fish aside; add remaining oil, dill stems and spring onion batons — they should hiss and wilt in 60–90 seconds.
5. Off the heat, bury the fish in the dill fronds; residual heat wilts them to a green tangle.
6. Squeeze half the lime over.
7. Serve fast: vermicelli in bowls, fish and greens over, peanuts and nước chấm to finish.

## Plating & Service
Classically finished on a burner at the table — do the last two steps in front of guests if the sea allows; the dill perfume is theatre.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Salmon or halibut behave identically; tofu takes the same marinade for plant-based. Use coconut yogurt to keep it dairy-free.
