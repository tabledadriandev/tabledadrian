# Khao Soi Gai, Chiang Mai Curried Noodles with Crispy Nest

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Northern Thailand's great noodle dish — a curried coconut broth over egg noodles with a crackling fried-noodle crown and the essential pickle-shallot-lime sideshow.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- chicken legs, jointed — 4 (about 900 g)
- egg noodles, fresh — 500 g (100 g reserved for frying)
- dried red chillies, soaked — 8
- shallots — 50 g
- garlic — 25 g
- ginger — 20 g
- turmeric, fresh (or 4 g ground) — 15 g
- coriander seeds, toasted — 5 g
- black cardamom or 2 green, toasted — 1
- shrimp paste — 6 g
- curry powder, mild — 8 g
- coconut milk — 800 ml
- chicken stock — 400 ml
- fish sauce — 35 ml
- palm sugar — 25 g
- lime — 2
- pickled mustard greens, chopped — 80 g
- banana shallots, sliced — 60 g
- vegetable oil for frying — 300 ml

## Method
1. Pound the paste: chillies, shallots, garlic, ginger, turmeric, ground coriander seed, cardamom seeds, shrimp paste, curry powder.
2. Fry the paste in 30 ml oil in a casserole 4 min; add chicken legs and turn to coat.
3. Add coconut milk and stock; simmer gently 35 min until the chicken is tender and the broth is rich and orange-slicked.
4. Season with fish sauce and palm sugar — it should be rich, salty-sweet with mild heat.
5. Fry the reserved 100 g noodles in 180 °C oil until puffed and golden, 30–60 seconds; drain.
6. Boil remaining noodles 2–3 min; drain and divide between bowls.
7. Ladle chicken and broth over, crown with the crispy nest.

## Plating & Service
Serve with lime wedges, raw shallot, pickled mustard greens and chilli oil on a side plate so each guest builds their own balance.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Beef shin version simmers 2.5 h; tofu + mushroom for meat-free (use vegan fish-sauce alternative). Rice noodles make it gluten-free minus the crispy wheat nest.
