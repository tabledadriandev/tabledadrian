# South Indian Lemon Rice, Peanut & Mustard Seed Tadka

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Day-old rice transformed — a turmeric-lemon tadka with peanuts, chana dal and curry leaves. Picnic-proof: it's designed to be eaten at room temperature.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- cooked basmati or sona masoori rice, cooled and separated — 700 g
- vegetable or sesame oil — 45 ml
- black mustard seeds — 5 g
- chana dal (split chickpeas) — 15 g
- raw peanuts — 50 g
- dried red chillies — 2
- green chilli, slit — 1
- fresh curry leaves — 18
- ginger, minced — 12 g
- ground turmeric — 4 g
- asafoetida — 0.5 g
- lemon juice — 55 ml
- fine sea salt — 8 g
- coriander leaves — 10 g

## Method
1. Heat oil in a wide pan; add mustard seeds and wait for the full crackle.
2. Add chana dal and peanuts; fry 2 min to gold.
3. Add both chillies, curry leaves, ginger and asafoetida; 30 seconds of noisy sizzle.
4. Turmeric in for 10 seconds — no longer, it scorches.
5. Off the heat, add lemon juice and salt; stir into a bright yellow dressing.
6. Fold in the rice gently until every grain is gold — hands or a flat spatula, never stirring hard.
7. Rest 10 min; taste and re-lemon if needed. Coriander through.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm or ambient, mounded with extra peanuts on top — the ideal beach-drop lunch box with yogurt on the side.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cashews for peanut-free service; add 40 g grated coconut for temple-style richness. Rice must be cold and dry — fresh hot rice turns gluey.
