# Andhra-Style Gongura Chicken, Tangy Sorrel Masala

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sorrel stands in for Andhra's gongura leaf, giving this fierce, tangy chicken curry its signature sour green edge — a connoisseur's pick.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- chicken thighs, bone-in, skinless — 900 g
- sorrel leaves (or spinach + 20 ml lemon juice) — 200 g
- vegetable oil — 40 ml
- black mustard seeds — 4 g
- cumin seeds — 4 g
- dried red chillies — 3
- curry leaves — 12
- onions, chopped — 250 g
- garlic-ginger paste — 30 g
- green chillies, slit — 3
- ground coriander — 8 g
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 6 g
- ground turmeric — 3 g
- fine sea salt — 10 g

## Method
1. Wilt sorrel in a dry pan 2–3 min until collapsed; blend to a coarse purée.
2. Heat oil; crackle mustard seeds, then cumin, dried chillies and curry leaves, 30 seconds.
3. Add onions; cook 10 min to golden. Add garlic-ginger paste and green chillies, 2 min.
4. Bloom coriander, chilli powder and turmeric 20 seconds.
5. Add chicken and salt; sear in the masala 6 min until the pieces take colour.
6. Add 150 ml water; cover, simmer 20 min.
7. Stir in the sorrel purée; simmer uncovered 8–10 min until the sauce is thick, dark-green-flecked and clings to the chicken.

## Plating & Service
Serve with plain steamed rice and a spoon of ghee melting over — the classic Andhra way to buffer the heat and sourness.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Lamb version braises 75 min; vegan with black-eyed beans. If using spinach + lemon, add the lemon at the very end.
