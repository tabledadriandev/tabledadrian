# Yakhni-Style Chicken Biryani, Saffron Dum Finish

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A stock-based (yakhni) biryani — rice par-cooked in spiced chicken broth, layered and sealed for a dum steam. Every grain seasoned, no gravy sludge.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- chicken thighs and drumsticks, bone-in — 1 kg
- basmati rice, aged, rinsed and soaked 30 min — 500 g
- yogurt — 150 g
- onions, sliced — 350 g
- vegetable oil or ghee — 90 g
- garlic-ginger paste — 40 g
- green chillies, slit — 3
- cinnamon — 1 stick
- green cardamom — 6
- black cardamom — 1
- cloves — 5
- bay leaves — 2
- cumin seeds — 5 g
- garam masala — 5 g
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 6 g
- mint + coriander leaves — 40 g
- saffron — 0.4 g, steeped in 60 ml warm milk
- fine sea salt — 18 g
- toasted cashews (optional) — 40 g

## Method
1. Fry onions in the oil to deep gold, 15 min; remove two-thirds for layering, leave the rest in the pot.
2. Add whole spices and cumin, 30 seconds; garlic-ginger paste, 2 min; then chicken, chilli powder and 8 g salt. Sear 6 min.
3. Add yogurt off the boil, then 900 ml water; simmer 25 min. Lift the chicken out; strain and reserve the yakhni (broth).
4. Boil the yakhni topped up to 1.2 L with 10 g salt; add drained rice and cook to 70% — a grain should snap with a white core, about 5 min. Drain.
5. Layer in a heavy pot: chicken and a ladle of thick broth, half the rice, fried onions, herbs and chillies, remaining rice.
6. Saffron milk over the top; lid sealed with foil or dough.
7. Dum: 10 min on medium to build steam, then 20 min on the lowest heat (or 160 °C oven). Rest 10 min unopened.

## Plating & Service
Open at the table for the saffron steam; serve with raita and never stir — lift from the bottom in sections so the layers show.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Lamb yakhni version braises 75 min before layering; vegetable biryani uses the same method with root-veg stock. The rest is non-negotiable.
