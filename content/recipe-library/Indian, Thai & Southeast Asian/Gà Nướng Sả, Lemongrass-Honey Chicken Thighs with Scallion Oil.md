# Gà Nướng Sả, Lemongrass-Honey Chicken Thighs with Scallion Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A workhorse marinade — lemongrass, fish sauce, honey — that chars beautifully on grill or oven tray, finished with the green gloss of mỡ hành.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- chicken thighs, boneless, skin-on — 800 g
- lemongrass, tender core, minced — 45 g
- garlic, minced — 20 g
- shallot, minced — 30 g
- fish sauce — 40 ml
- honey — 30 g
- light soy sauce — 15 ml
- lime juice — 20 ml
- black pepper — 2 g
- vegetable oil — 60 ml
- spring onions, thin rings — 50 g
- pinch of salt and sugar for the oil — 2 g each
- roasted peanuts, crushed (optional) — 20 g

## Method
1. Blend lemongrass, garlic, shallot, fish sauce, honey, soy, pepper and 20 ml oil to a rough paste; massage into the chicken. Marinate 2–24 h.
2. Bring to room temperature; heat oven to 220 °C (or grill to medium-hot).
3. Roast skin-up on a rack 22–25 min to 74 °C core, charring at the edges; flash under the grill 2 min if needed for colour.
4. Rest 5 min; add lime juice to the tray juices.
5. Make scallion oil: heat remaining 40 ml oil to 160 °C, pour over the spring onion rings with the salt and sugar; stir 30 seconds — vivid green, just softened.
6. Slice chicken on the bias.
7. Spoon tray juices, then scallion oil over.

## Plating & Service
Serve over broken rice or vermicelli with pickled carrot-daikon and cucumber; peanuts over the top if the manifest allows.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Same marinade transforms pork chops, prawns or tofu steaks; skip honey and add 10 g sugar for a more classic profile.
