# Quick Sriracha-Style Chilli-Garlic Sauce, Riviera Method

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A same-day, cooked version of the fermented classic — red chillies, garlic and vinegar simmered and blended silky; brighter than bottled and free of thickeners.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- red chillies (medium heat, ripe) — 350 g
- garlic — 40 g
- cider or rice vinegar — 80 ml
- caster sugar — 45 g
- fine sea salt — 9 g
- water — 100 ml
- tomato paste (colour and body) — 15 g

## Method
1. Stem the chillies; halve, seeding half of them (calibrates heat while keeping body).
2. Simmer chillies, garlic, water, half the vinegar, sugar and salt, covered, 12–15 min until completely soft.
3. Blend on high 2 full minutes — the silkiness comes from over-blending, not straining.
4. Add tomato paste and remaining vinegar; blend again.
5. Return to the pan; simmer 5 min to a ketchup-adjacent thickness (it thickens further cold).
6. Taste: heat forward, then garlic, sweet-sour close behind.
7. Bottle hot; refrigerate.

## Plating & Service
The house hot sauce — phở, fried rice, eggs, bánh mì, burgers; label the bottle because guests will ask.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Keeps a month chilled. For funk closer to the fermented original, add 10 ml fish sauce (loses vegan tag) or a teaspoon of white miso.
