# Gỏi Gà, Shredded Chicken & Cabbage Salad with Rau Răm

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Vietnam's bright poached-chicken slaw — cabbage, herbs and lime-fish sauce, with peanuts and fried shallots for the crunch dividend. Light course or standalone lunch.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- chicken breasts or leg meat — 400 g
- white cabbage, fine shred — 300 g
- carrot, julienne — 100 g
- red onion, paper-thin — 60 g
- Vietnamese coriander (rau răm) or mint + coriander — 25 g
- lime juice — 50 ml
- fish sauce — 35 ml
- caster sugar — 25 g
- garlic, minced — 8 g
- bird's-eye chilli, minced — 4 g
- rice vinegar — 15 ml
- roasted peanuts, crushed — 40 g
- fried shallots — 25 g
- black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Poach the chicken: submerge in simmering salted water, cut heat to 80 °C, 15–18 min to 72 °C core. Cool in the liquid 10 min; hand-shred along the grain.
2. Soak the red onion in the rice vinegar 10 min to gentle it.
3. Whisk the dressing: lime, fish sauce, sugar, garlic, chilli — sharp and punchy; it must season raw cabbage.
4. Toss cabbage and carrot with half the dressing; rest 5 min to soften slightly.
5. Add chicken, onion and remaining dressing; toss.
6. Fold in the herbs, torn.
7. Top with peanuts, fried shallots and pepper.

## Plating & Service
Serve in a low mound within 20 minutes of dressing — the cabbage should still squeak.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Poached prawns or shredded tofu swap in; add green mango julienne when the fruit basket allows. Prawn crackers underneath for a street-style crunch.
