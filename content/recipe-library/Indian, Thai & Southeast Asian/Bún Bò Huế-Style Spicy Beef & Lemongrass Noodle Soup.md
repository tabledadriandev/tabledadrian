# Bún Bò Huế-Style Spicy Beef & Lemongrass Noodle Soup

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Huế's answer to phở — a lemongrass-saturated broth stained red with annatto-chilli oil, thick rice noodles, and slow-braised shin. Bolder in every direction.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- beef shin — 800 g
- pork hock or belly — 400 g
- beef bones — 1 kg
- lemongrass, bruised — 100 g
- onion, halved and charred — 200 g
- ginger, charred — 50 g
- shrimp paste (mắm ruốc or substitute) — 15 g
- fish sauce — 60 ml
- rock sugar — 20 g
- fine sea salt — 14 g
- annatto seeds (or 4 g sweet paprika) — 8 g
- dried chilli flakes — 8 g
- garlic, minced — 15 g
- vegetable oil — 60 ml
- thick round rice noodles, cooked — 600 g
- lime, banana blossom or shaved cabbage, mint, sprouts — 300 g

## Method
1. Blanch bones, shin and pork 5 min; rinse. Cover with 4 L cold water.
2. Add lemongrass, charred onion and ginger, salt; hold at a bare simmer 2.5–3 h, skimming. Pull pork at 1 h, shin when a skewer slides through (about 2 h); chill both.
3. Whisk shrimp paste with 100 ml broth; settle 10 min, pour the clear liquid back in (grit stays behind).
4. Make the chilli-annatto oil: warm oil to 150 °C with annatto 2 min; strain, then bloom chilli flakes and garlic in it 30 seconds.
5. Season broth with fish sauce and rock sugar; stir in half the chilli oil — it should be spicy, lemongrass-forward, faintly funky.
6. Slice shin and pork thin.
7. Bowl the noodles and meats; ladle boiling broth over, drizzle remaining chilli oil.

## Plating & Service
Serve with lime, shaved crunch and herbs on a communal plate; the red-slicked surface is the visual signature — don't stir it away before it hits the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skip pork and use all-beef for halal service; the shrimp-paste step is optional but is the authentic backbone. Broth freezes well.
