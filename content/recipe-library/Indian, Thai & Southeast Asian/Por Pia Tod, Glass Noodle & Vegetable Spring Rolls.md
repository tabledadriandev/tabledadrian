# Por Pia Tod, Glass Noodle & Vegetable Spring Rolls

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Shatter-crisp Thai spring rolls with a glass-noodle, mushroom and cabbage heart — rolled tight, fried twice, gone instantly.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- spring roll wrappers, 20 cm — 18
- glass noodles, soaked and snipped — 100 g dry
- dried shiitake, soaked, sliced — 40 g dry
- white cabbage, fine shred — 200 g
- carrot, fine julienne — 100 g
- bean sprouts — 100 g
- garlic, minced — 15 g
- coriander stems, chopped — 10 g
- light soy sauce — 25 ml
- white pepper — 2 g
- sugar — 8 g
- cornflour paste (cornflour 15 g + water 20 ml) — 35 g
- vegetable oil — 30 ml + 1 L for frying
- sweet chilli sauce — 120 g

## Method
1. Stir-fry garlic and coriander stems in 30 ml oil, 30 seconds; add mushrooms, cabbage, carrot — 3 min until just collapsed.
2. Add noodles, soy, sugar, white pepper; toss 2 min. Fold in sprouts; off the heat.
3. Spread on a tray to cool completely — hot filling ruptures rolls.
4. Roll: 40 g filling per wrapper, corner towards you, one tight turn, sides in, roll and seal with cornflour paste.
5. First fry at 160 °C, 4 min, pale gold; rest on a rack (can hold 2 h here).
6. Second fry at 185 °C, 60–90 seconds, deep gold and blistered.
7. Rack-drain 2 min — never paper, it steams the base.

## Plating & Service
Stack log-cabin style with sweet chilli sauce and lettuce-mint wraps for the full ritual.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add minced prawn or pork to the filling (cook through at step 1); rolls freeze raw beautifully — fry from frozen at 160 °C +2 min.
