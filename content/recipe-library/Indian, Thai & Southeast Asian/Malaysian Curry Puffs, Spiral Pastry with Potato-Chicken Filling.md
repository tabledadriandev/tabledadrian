# Malaysian Curry Puffs, Spiral Pastry with Potato-Chicken Filling

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Karipap pusing — the spiral-laminated curry puff, flaky in rings, around a dry curry of chicken and potato. Real pastry work; worth every fold.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- plain flour (water dough) — 200 g
- water — 100 ml
- fine sea salt — 4 g
- vegetable oil (water dough) — 20 ml
- plain flour (oil dough) — 120 g
- vegetable oil or ghee (oil dough) — 70 g
- chicken thigh, small dice — 250 g
- potato, 1 cm dice — 250 g
- onion, fine dice — 100 g
- curry powder — 18 g
- chilli powder — 3 g
- sugar — 5 g
- fine sea salt (filling) — 6 g
- water (filling) — 120 ml
- oil for frying — 1 L

## Method
1. Filling: fry onion in 25 ml oil 4 min; add curry and chilli powders, bloom 30 seconds. Add chicken 3 min, potato, salt, sugar, water; simmer 12–15 min until dry. Cool completely.
2. Water dough: knead flour, salt, water, oil 5 min; rest 30 min. Oil dough: rub flour and oil to a soft paste.
3. Divide both into 6. Wrap each oil-dough ball inside a water-dough ball.
4. Roll each into a long tongue, roll up like a cigar; repeat the roll-and-curl once more, then cut each cigar in half to reveal the spiral.
5. Press cut-side up, roll to a 12 cm disc — rings visible.
6. Fill with 30 g curry mix, fold, and crimp a rope edge.
7. Fry at 165 °C, 6–8 min, until the spirals bloom golden. Drain upright.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm in a napkin-lined basket — break one open to show the rings and steam for the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Sardine-sambal and vegetable fillings are classic; they freeze raw (fry from frozen +3 min). Bake at 190 °C for 25 min if the fryer's off-limits.
