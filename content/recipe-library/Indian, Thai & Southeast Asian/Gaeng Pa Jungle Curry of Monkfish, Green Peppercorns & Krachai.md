# Gaeng Pa Jungle Curry of Monkfish, Green Peppercorns & Krachai

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> No coconut, no safety net — a clear, ferociously aromatic broth-curry from the Thai forests, with monkfish standing in for river fish.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- monkfish, 4 cm chunks — 600 g
- dried red chillies, soaked — 8
- fresh red chillies — 10 g
- shallots — 40 g
- garlic — 20 g
- lemongrass — 25 g
- galangal — 15 g
- krachai (fingerroot), julienned (or extra galangal) — 20 g
- shrimp paste — 6 g
- fresh green peppercorns on the strand (or brined) — 15 g
- baby corn, halved — 100 g
- snake beans or green beans — 120 g
- Thai apple aubergines, quartered — 120 g
- fish sauce — 30 ml
- chicken or fish stock — 700 ml
- makrut lime leaves — 4
- holy basil or Thai basil — 20 g

## Method
1. Pound a paste from both chillies, lemongrass, galangal, garlic, shallots and shrimp paste — coarser than a coconut-curry paste is fine.
2. Heat 20 ml oil in a pot; fry the paste 3 min until it catches at the edges and smells fierce.
3. Add stock, fish sauce, torn lime leaves, half the krachai and the peppercorn strands; simmer 5 min.
4. Add beans and baby corn; 3 min.
5. Add aubergine and monkfish; poach at a strong simmer 6–7 min until the fish is just set.
6. Taste — jungle curry is salty and hot with no sugar; adjust fish sauce only.
7. Off the heat, add basil and remaining raw krachai for a top note.

## Plating & Service
Serve like a hot broth-bowl with jasmine rice on the side; warn the table — this one bites.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken thigh, prawns or a mushroom medley all work; soften the heat by halving the fresh chillies, never by adding sugar.
