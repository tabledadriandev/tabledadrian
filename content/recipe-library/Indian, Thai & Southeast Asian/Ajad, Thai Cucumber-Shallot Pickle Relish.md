# Ajad, Thai Cucumber-Shallot Pickle Relish

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The quiet essential — a barely-sweet vinegar relish of cucumber, shallot and chilli that resets the palate between bites of satay, fish cakes and curries.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- rice vinegar — 100 ml
- caster sugar — 70 g
- water — 60 ml
- fine sea salt — 3 g
- cucumber, quartered lengthways, 3 mm slices — 250 g
- shallots, fine-sliced — 50 g
- red chilli, fine rounds — 10 g
- ginger, hair-fine julienne (optional) — 10 g
- coriander leaves — 5 g

## Method
1. Warm vinegar, sugar, water and salt just until dissolved; cool completely — a warm pickle wilts the cucumber.
2. Slice the cucumber thin and even; salt lightly and rest 5 min, then pat dry.
3. Combine cucumber, shallots, chilli and ginger in a bowl.
4. Pour the cooled syrup over 15 minutes before serving — no earlier.
5. Stir once; the vegetables should still snap.
6. Top with coriander.
7. Serve in small individual bowls, a little syrup in each.

## Plating & Service
One small bowl per guest beside satay or fish cakes — it's a condiment with the workload of a palate cleanser.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add crushed roasted peanuts on top for satay service. The syrup keeps a week chilled; cut vegetables fresh each time.
