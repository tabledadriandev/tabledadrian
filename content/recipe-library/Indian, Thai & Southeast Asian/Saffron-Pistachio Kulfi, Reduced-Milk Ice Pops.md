# Saffron-Pistachio Kulfi, Reduced-Milk Ice Pops

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> True kulfi — milk reduced by two-thirds until caramel-sweet on its own, set dense and chewy with saffron and pistachio; no churning, no machine.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- whole milk — 1.5 L
- caster sugar — 90 g
- saffron threads — 0.4 g
- green cardamom, seeds ground — 4 pods
- pistachios, chopped — 60 g
- double cream — 100 ml
- cornflour — 8 g
- rose water (optional) — 3 ml
- fine sea salt — 1 g

## Method
1. Reduce the milk in a wide, heavy pan over medium heat, stirring every few minutes and scraping the sides in, 45–55 min, to about 500 ml — thick, pale gold, sweetly lactic.
2. Steep the saffron in 20 ml of the warm milk; return it.
3. Slake the cornflour in the cream; whisk in and simmer 2 min (insurance against ice crystals).
4. Add sugar, cardamom, salt; dissolve, then cool completely.
5. Stir in half the pistachios and the rose water.
6. Pour into kulfi moulds or small conical cups; freeze 6 h minimum.
7. Unmould by rolling between palms 10 seconds; slice thick coins or serve whole.

## Plating & Service
Serve on chilled plates with remaining pistachios and a few saffron threads — dense enough to eat with knife and fork, as kulfi should be.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Mango version: fold 200 g purée in at step 5. The reduction is the dish — condensed-milk shortcuts taste like it.
