# Bengali Shorshe Salmon, Mustard-Poppy Sauce & Nigella

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bengal's beloved mustard fish translated to salmon — pungent, hot, and ready in twenty minutes with almost no chopping.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- salmon fillets, skin on — 4 × 140 g
- black + yellow mustard seeds — 25 g
- poppy seeds — 10 g
- green chillies — 4
- ground turmeric — 5 g
- mustard oil — 40 ml
- nigella seeds (kalonji) — 3 g
- coconut milk — 150 ml
- fine sea salt — 8 g
- coriander leaves — 10 g
- lime — 1

## Method
1. Soak mustard and poppy seeds in 100 ml warm water for 15 min; blend with 2 chillies, half the turmeric and 3 g salt to a smooth, sharp paste.
2. Rub salmon with remaining turmeric and salt; rest 10 min.
3. Heat mustard oil in a wide pan until it just smokes (about 190 °C); cool 30 seconds — this tames its raw pungency.
4. Sear salmon skin-down 3 min until crisp; remove (it will finish in the sauce).
5. In the same oil, crackle nigella seeds 15 seconds; add the mustard paste and fry 2 min, stirring — do not let it scorch or it turns bitter.
6. Add coconut milk and 100 ml water; simmer 3 min. Slit remaining chillies in.
7. Return salmon skin-up; poach at a bare simmer 5–6 min until just set at the centre (50–52 °C core).

## Plating & Service
Skin-up so it stays crisp, yellow sauce around, coriander and lime at the end — steamed rice is non-negotiable here.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Classic with any firm fish or halved boiled eggs (vegetarian). Reduce mustard seeds to 15 g for a gentler sauce.
