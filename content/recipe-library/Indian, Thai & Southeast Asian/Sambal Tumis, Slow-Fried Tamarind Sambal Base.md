# Sambal Tumis, Slow-Fried Tamarind Sambal Base

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Malaysian workhorse — dried-chilli paste fried patiently until the oil splits, seasoned with tamarind and palm sugar; the engine of nasi lemak and sambal prawns.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- dried red chillies, seeded, soaked — 60 g
- fresh red chillies — 60 g
- shallots — 120 g
- garlic — 25 g
- belacan, toasted — 12 g
- tamarind paste — 35 g
- palm sugar — 35 g
- fine sea salt — 6 g
- vegetable oil — 150 ml
- onion, fine-sliced (optional body) — 100 g

## Method
1. Blend both chillies, shallots, garlic and belacan with 40 ml water to a smooth paste.
2. Heat the oil in a wok over medium-low heat; add the paste — it should sizzle gently, not spit.
3. Fry 20–25 min, stirring every few minutes, as it darkens from scarlet to deep brick and the oil separates (pecah minyak). This patience IS the recipe.
4. Add the sliced onion if using; 5 min until melted in.
5. Season: tamarind, palm sugar, salt. Fry 5 min more.
6. Taste — rounded heat, sour-sweet balance, no raw shallot edge.
7. Cool; jar under its oil.

## Plating & Service
The base for sambal ikan bilis, sambal sotong and nasi lemak service — or straight over fried eggs and rice.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Keeps 3 weeks chilled, months frozen. Fry anchovies or halved boiled eggs directly in a ladleful for instant classics.
