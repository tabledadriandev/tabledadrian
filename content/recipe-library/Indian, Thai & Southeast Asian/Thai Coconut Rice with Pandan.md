# Thai Coconut Rice with Pandan

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Jasmine rice simmered in coconut milk with a knotted pandan leaf — the sweet-scented sidekick to sambals, satays and grilled fish.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- jasmine rice, rinsed until nearly clear — 300 g
- coconut milk — 250 ml
- water — 220 ml
- pandan leaf, knotted (or 1 makrut lime leaf) — 1
- caster sugar — 10 g
- fine sea salt — 5 g
- toasted coconut flakes — 15 g

## Method
1. Combine rice, coconut milk, water, sugar, salt and pandan in a heavy pot.
2. Stir once; bring to a boil over medium-high heat, uncovered.
3. The moment it boils, stir once more, scrape the base, lid on, heat to minimum.
4. Cook 12 min — coconut rice catches more easily than plain, so trust the clock, not your nose... but a toasted base (tahdig-style) is a legitimate bonus.
5. Off the heat, rest 10 min, lidded.
6. Remove pandan; fluff gently — it is stickier than plain jasmine by design.
7. Top with toasted coconut.

## Plating & Service
Mound into a small bowl per guest and invert onto the plate for a neat dome beside rendang, satay or sambal prawns.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> For Thai khao man gai service, cook in chicken stock instead of water. Leftovers fry brilliantly the next day with pineapple and chilli.
