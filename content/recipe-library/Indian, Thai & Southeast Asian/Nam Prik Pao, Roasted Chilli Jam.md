# Nam Prik Pao, Roasted Chilli Jam

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Thailand's black-gold chilli jam — chillies, shallots and garlic charred then fried with tamarind and palm sugar; the secret depth in tom yum and fried rice.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- dried long red chillies, seeded — 40 g
- shallots, thick slices — 120 g
- garlic cloves, peeled — 60 g
- dried shrimp — 25 g
- vegetable oil — 180 ml
- palm sugar — 60 g
- tamarind paste — 40 g
- fish sauce — 25 ml
- fine sea salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Dry-toast the chillies in a wok over medium heat 2–3 min until darkened and brittle; remove.
2. Toast shallots and garlic in the dry wok, turning, 8–10 min until charred in spots and soft; remove.
3. Toast the dried shrimp 2 min.
4. Blend chillies, shallots, garlic and shrimp with 60 ml of the oil to a coarse paste.
5. Fry the paste in the remaining oil over low heat, stirring, 10–12 min — it darkens from red to near-black without burning.
6. Season with palm sugar, tamarind, fish sauce and salt; fry 3 min more to a glossy, spoonable jam under its oil.
7. Cool; jar with the oil on top.

## Plating & Service
A spoon in tom yum, smeared on grilled prawns, stirred through fried rice or eaten shamelessly on buttered toast.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Keeps a month chilled. Vegan: swap shrimp and fish sauce for 15 g fermented soybean paste — different, still excellent.
