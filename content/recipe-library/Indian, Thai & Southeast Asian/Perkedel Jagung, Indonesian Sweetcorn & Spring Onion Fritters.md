# Perkedel Jagung, Indonesian Sweetcorn & Spring Onion Fritters

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Craggy corn fritters, half the kernels crushed for cream and half left whole for pop — with kecap manis and chilli for dunking.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- sweetcorn kernels (fresh or thawed) — 400 g
- shallots, minced — 50 g
- garlic, minced — 12 g
- spring onions, sliced — 40 g
- red chilli, minced — 10 g
- coriander leaves, chopped — 10 g
- ground coriander — 4 g
- white pepper — 1 g
- egg — 1
- plain flour — 60 g
- rice flour — 30 g
- fine sea salt — 6 g
- dried shrimp, minced (optional) — 10 g
- oil for shallow frying — 300 ml
- kecap manis + lime, to serve — 60 ml

## Method
1. Pulse half the corn to a coarse cream; leave the rest whole.
2. Mix all corn with shallots, garlic, spring onion, chilli, coriander leaf and ground, white pepper, salt and dried shrimp if using.
3. Add egg, then both flours — a thick, spoonable batter that just holds a peak.
4. Heat 1 cm oil to 175 °C in a wide pan.
5. Drop heaped tablespoons; flatten slightly. Fry 2–3 min per side to deep gold with lacy edges.
6. Drain on a rack; salt while hot.
7. Serve fast, with kecap manis sharpened with lime.

## Plating & Service
Stack on banana leaf or paper with the dark dip alongside — they vanish at drinks hour.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: swap egg for 30 g extra rice flour + 40 ml water (slightly crisper, less tender). Add crab for a luxury version.
