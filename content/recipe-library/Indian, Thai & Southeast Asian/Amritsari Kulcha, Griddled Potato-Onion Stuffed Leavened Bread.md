# Amritsari Kulcha, Griddled Potato-Onion Stuffed Leavened Bread

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Amritsar's crisp-bottomed stuffed kulcha — leavened dough around a punchy potato-onion masala, pressed and griddled until the base crackles like a cracker.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- plain flour — 350 g
- yogurt — 70 g
- instant yeast — 3 g
- caster sugar — 6 g
- fine sea salt — 7 g
- warm water — 150 ml
- vegetable oil — 20 ml
- potatoes, boiled, riced, cooled — 300 g
- onion, fine dice, squeezed dry — 100 g
- anardana powder — 5 g
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 4 g
- cumin seeds, toasted — 4 g
- coriander leaves — 15 g
- fine sea salt (filling) — 5 g
- butter or ghee — 50 g
- nigella + sesame seeds — 6 g

## Method
1. Make a soft dough: flour, yeast, sugar, salt, yogurt, oil, water; knead 8 min. Prove 90 min to double.
2. Mix filling: potato, onion, anardana, chilli, cumin, coriander, filling salt — taste; it should be sharp and hot.
3. Divide dough into 6; rest 10 min.
4. Money-bag 70 g of filling into each ball; seal, rest 5 min seam-down.
5. Press and roll gently to 14 cm; brush the top with water, scatter nigella-sesame and press in.
6. Cook seed-side up on a medium-hot heavy pan, lid on, 2–3 min until the base is deep brown and crisp; flip 60–90 seconds to blister the top. (Or bake at 280 °C on a steel, 5 min, then flash the base in a pan.)
7. Smash lightly, brush hard with butter.

## Plating & Service
Serve crushed-open with chole and sliced raw onion — the crisp base against the soft crumb is the whole event.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Paneer or cauliflower fillings rotate well; day-old kulchas re-crisp perfectly in a dry pan and make an indecent breakfast with fried eggs.
