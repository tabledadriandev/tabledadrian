# Nam Tok Beef, Grilled Sirloin Waterfall Salad with Toasted Rice

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Isaan's 'waterfall' salad — charred rosé sirloin tossed warm with lime, chilli flakes, herbs and nutty toasted-rice powder. A main-course salad with swagger.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- sirloin steak, 3 cm thick — 600 g
- jasmine or sticky rice, raw — 25 g
- fish sauce — 40 ml
- lime juice — 50 ml
- palm sugar — 8 g
- roasted chilli flakes (prik bon) — 4–6 g
- shallots, fine-sliced — 60 g
- spring onions, sliced — 30 g
- mint leaves — 15 g
- coriander leaves — 15 g
- sawtooth coriander (or extra coriander), sliced — 8 g
- cucumber, thick half-moons — 150 g
- vegetable oil — 10 ml
- fine sea salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Make khao khua: toast the raw rice in a dry pan over medium heat 8–10 min, shaking, until deep gold and popcorn-scented; grind to a coarse sand.
2. Oil and salt the steak; grill or sear over high heat 3–4 min per side to 52–54 °C core. Rest 8 min — the resting juices go into the dressing.
3. Whisk fish sauce, lime juice, palm sugar, chilli flakes and the steak's resting juices.
4. Slice the beef 5 mm against the grain while still warm.
5. Toss beef with the dressing, shallots and spring onions; warm meat drinks the seasoning.
6. Fold in mint, coriander and sawtooth; add half the toasted rice powder.
7. Taste — sour-salty-hot, sweetness barely there.

## Plating & Service
Mound on a platter with cucumber around, remaining rice powder over the top; sticky rice and cold beer make it a meal.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Duck breast or grilled portobello + tempeh give the same pleasure; adjust chilli flakes downward with civilians aboard.
