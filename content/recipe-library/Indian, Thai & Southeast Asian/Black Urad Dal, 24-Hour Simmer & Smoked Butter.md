# Black Urad Dal, 24-Hour Simmer & Smoked Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A dal makhani-inspired slow dal where patience does the work — restaurant-deep flavour from a long, lazy simmer and a final smoked butter emulsion.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- whole black urad dal, soaked overnight — 300 g
- red kidney beans, soaked overnight — 60 g
- water — 1.8 L
- tomato passata — 250 g
- unsalted butter — 90 g
- double cream — 100 ml
- ginger paste — 20 g
- garlic paste — 20 g
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 6 g
- garam masala — 4 g
- kasoori methi — 3 g
- cumin seeds — 5 g
- fine sea salt — 12 g
- smoked sea salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Drain soaked pulses; simmer in the water with 6 g salt, partially covered, 2.5–3 h, mashing occasionally against the pot until beans collapse and the liquid turns creamy. Top up water as needed.
2. In a separate pan, melt 60 g butter over medium heat; add cumin seeds and sizzle 30 seconds until fragrant.
3. Add ginger and garlic pastes, cook 2 min; bloom chilli powder 20 seconds.
4. Add passata and cook 8–10 min until the fat separates at the edges.
5. Stir this tadka into the dal; simmer gently 30 min more, stirring so it never catches.
6. Finish with cream, garam masala, crushed kasoori methi and remaining salt; simmer 5 min.
7. Melt final 30 g butter until nut-brown, season with smoked salt, and swirl over just before serving.

## Plating & Service
Serve in a deep bowl with the smoked butter pooling on top, a thread of cream, and blistered naan for dragging through.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: finish with coconut cream and smoked oil instead of dairy. Pressure cooker cuts simmering to 50 min at high pressure.
