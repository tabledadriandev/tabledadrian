# Instant Lime Pickle, Hot Oil Nimbu Achar

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A same-week lime pickle — blanched lime wedges in a hot mustard-fenugreek oil with chilli and jaggery; achar character without the month on the windowsill.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- unwaxed limes — 500 g
- fine sea salt — 35 g
- ground turmeric — 5 g
- mustard oil (or vegetable oil) — 120 ml
- black mustard seeds — 8 g
- fenugreek seeds — 3 g
- fennel seeds — 6 g
- asafoetida — 1 g
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 18 g
- jaggery — 30 g

## Method
1. Blanch whole limes in boiling water 4 min (softens skins and removes wax bitterness); dry completely.
2. Cut into 12 wedges each, flicking out pips; toss with salt and turmeric.
3. Rest 2 h minimum (overnight is better) — they weep and begin to cure.
4. Heat the mustard oil to just smoking; cool 2 min.
5. Fry mustard, fenugreek and fennel seeds 20 seconds; add asafoetida.
6. Off the heat, stir in chilli powder and jaggery, then the limes and all their brine.
7. Jar; rest at room temperature 2–3 days, shaking daily, then refrigerate.

## Plating & Service
A sharp, hot spoonful beside dal-rice, curd rice or any rich braise — tiny portions, big consequences.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Ready in 3 days, better at 2 weeks, keeps 3 months chilled under its oil. Meyer lemons make a softer, faster version.
