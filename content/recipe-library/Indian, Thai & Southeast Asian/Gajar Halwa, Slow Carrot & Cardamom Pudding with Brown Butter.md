# Gajar Halwa, Slow Carrot & Cardamom Pudding with Brown Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Winter's great Indian dessert — carrots melted slowly in milk, finished in ghee until they candy at the edges; served warm with cold cream for the temperature play.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- carrots, coarse-grated — 800 g
- whole milk — 700 ml
- ghee — 60 g
- caster sugar — 110 g
- green cardamom, ground — 5 pods
- cashews — 30 g
- raisins — 25 g
- almonds, slivered — 20 g
- double cream, cold, to serve — 100 ml
- fine sea salt — 1 g

## Method
1. In a wide pan, cook the carrots in 20 g ghee over medium heat 5 min until their raw smell fades.
2. Add the milk; simmer, stirring often, 35–40 min until fully absorbed and the mixture is thick.
3. Add sugar; it will loosen everything — cook 10 min more until thick again.
4. Push to one side; in the gap, heat remaining ghee and fry cashews, almonds and raisins 2 min until the nuts gild and raisins puff.
5. Fold together; cook 8–10 min more until the halwa darkens, smells caramelised and the ghee glistens at the edges.
6. Cardamom and salt in; 1 min.
7. Rest 5 min.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm in small bowls with a pour of cold double cream — or press into a frame, chill, and pan-sear slices for a plated version.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: coconut oil + oat milk works honestly. Keeps 5 days chilled and reheats beautifully — a make-ahead charter hero.
