# Bhatura, Overnight Yogurt-Ferment Fried Bread

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The chole companion — a lightly fermented yogurt dough that balloons dramatically in hot oil into chewy-crisp domes.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- plain flour — 300 g
- fine semolina — 40 g
- yogurt — 120 g
- caster sugar — 8 g
- baking powder — 4 g
- bicarbonate of soda — 1.5 g
- fine sea salt — 6 g
- warm water — about 70 ml
- vegetable oil — 15 ml + 1.2 L for frying

## Method
1. Mix flour, semolina, sugar, baking powder, bicarb and salt.
2. Add yogurt and 15 ml oil; knead with enough warm water for a soft, smooth dough, 6–8 min.
3. Ferment covered 4 h at room temperature (or overnight chilled) — it should smell faintly tangy and relax fully.
4. Divide into 8; ball and rest 15 min, oiled and covered.
5. Roll each to a 14 cm oval, 4 mm thick — no dusting flour; use oil.
6. Fry one at a time at 185–190 °C: slide in, immediately spoon hot oil over the top and press gently — it balloons in 10–15 seconds. Flip; 30–40 seconds more to pale gold.
7. Drain upright; serve within minutes.

## Plating & Service
Serve tall and puffed beside Punjabi chole — puncture at the table for the steam sigh.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Half wholemeal flour adds character; leftover dough makes excellent fried bread for eggs next morning. They deflate but stay delicious.
