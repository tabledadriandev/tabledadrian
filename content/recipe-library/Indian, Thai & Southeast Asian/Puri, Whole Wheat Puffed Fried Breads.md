# Puri, Whole Wheat Puffed Fried Breads

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Golden fried moons that puff in seconds — a stiff dough, hot clean oil and one confident press are all it takes.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- chapati flour (atta) — 300 g
- fine semolina — 20 g
- fine sea salt — 5 g
- vegetable oil — 15 ml + 1.2 L for frying
- warm water — about 155 ml

## Method
1. Knead flour, semolina, salt, 15 ml oil and water to a stiff dough — firmer than chapati dough; stiffness powers the puff. Rest 15 min only.
2. Divide into 14 small balls; keep covered but do not dust with flour (it burns the oil) — use oil to roll.
3. Roll each to a 10 cm round, 2 mm thick, even as a coin.
4. Heat oil to 185–190 °C — a test scrap should rise instantly and sizzle hard.
5. Slide one puri down the side of the pan; press gently all over with the back of a slotted spoon — it balloons in 5–8 seconds.
6. Flip; 15–20 seconds to pale gold. Never let them brown hard.
7. Drain upright in a bowl lined with a rack.

## Plating & Service
Serve hot and puffed with chole, aloo sabzi or halwa for the classic weekend spread — fry in relay while someone else serves.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 3 g turmeric or 20 g spinach purée to the dough for coloured puris; they hold 20 minutes but the drama is in serving them inflated.
