# Andhra Peanut Chutney, Red Chilli & Garlic

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A rustic, fiery peanut chutney from Andhra — roasted peanuts, red chillies and garlic, loosened with tamarind water; dosa's best friend and a grilled-meat cheat code.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- raw peanuts — 150 g
- dried red chillies — 5
- garlic — 12 g
- tamarind paste — 10 g
- fine sea salt — 6 g
- water — 150–200 ml
- vegetable oil — 25 ml
- black mustard seeds — 3 g
- urad dal — 5 g
- curry leaves — 10
- cumin seeds — 2 g

## Method
1. Dry-roast the peanuts over medium heat 6–8 min, stirring, until golden and popping their skins; cool and rub off most skins.
2. Toast the chillies and cumin 60 seconds in the same pan.
3. Blend peanuts, chillies, cumin, garlic, tamarind and salt with water to a thick, coarse cream — rustic, not silky.
4. Taste: nutty, hot, faintly sour; adjust salt and tamarind.
5. Tadka: heat oil, crackle mustard seeds, fry urad dal to gold, add curry leaves.
6. Pour over the chutney.
7. Rest 10 min; it thickens — loosen with water as needed.

## Plating & Service
Serve with dosa, idli, pakoras — or brush on grilled chicken in the last minute for an Andhra-satay hybrid.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Half peanuts, half roasted chana for a lighter body; sesame-free by nature. Keeps 4 days chilled; loosen and re-salt after day two.
