# Gujarati Kadhi with Charred Okra, Sweet-Sour Yogurt Curry

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A silky, gently sweet-sour yogurt curry thickened with gram flour — light as a soup, comforting as a stew, with crisp charred okra for texture.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- natural yogurt — 500 g
- gram (chickpea) flour — 40 g
- water — 600 ml
- jaggery or brown sugar — 18 g
- ginger, grated — 15 g
- green chilli, minced — 1
- ghee — 30 g
- black mustard seeds — 4 g
- cumin seeds — 4 g
- fenugreek seeds — 1 g
- dried red chillies — 2
- curry leaves — 15
- cinnamon — 1 stick (4 cm)
- okra, halved lengthways — 250 g
- fine sea salt — 9 g

## Method
1. Whisk yogurt, gram flour and water until lump-free (a blender guarantees silk).
2. Pour into a pan with ginger, green chilli, jaggery and 6 g salt. Bring to a simmer over medium heat, whisking constantly — it must not boil unattended or it splits.
3. Simmer gently 15–18 min, whisking often, until it coats a spoon lightly and the raw flour taste is gone.
4. Meanwhile toss okra with a film of oil and 3 g salt; char in a screaming-hot pan or 240 °C oven for 8–10 min until blistered and just tender.
5. Make the tempering: heat ghee until shimmering; add mustard seeds, and when they crackle add cumin, fenugreek, dried chillies, cinnamon and curry leaves for 20 seconds.
6. Pour the sizzling tempering over the kadhi and stir once.
7. Fold in half the okra; keep the rest for the top.

## Plating & Service
Ladle into deep bowls, crown with charred okra and a few crisp curry leaves; steamed rice or khichdi alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: use thick coconut yogurt (simmer more gently). Skip okra for pakora dumplings if seas are calm and you can fry.
