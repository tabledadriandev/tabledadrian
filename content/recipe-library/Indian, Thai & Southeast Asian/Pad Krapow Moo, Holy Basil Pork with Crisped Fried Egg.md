# Pad Krapow Moo, Holy Basil Pork with Crisped Fried Egg

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Thailand's one-plate king — screaming-hot wok, coarse pork, holy basil and a lacy fried egg with a molten yolk. Ready faster than the rice.

## Ingredients (serves 2)
- pork mince (coarse, 12–15% fat) — 400 g
- garlic — 20 g
- bird's-eye chillies — 6–10 g (to nerve)
- long red chilli, sliced — 1
- fish sauce — 25 ml
- oyster sauce — 20 g
- light soy sauce — 10 ml
- palm sugar — 8 g
- chicken stock or water — 50 ml
- holy basil leaves (or Thai basil) — 30 g
- green beans, thin rounds — 80 g
- vegetable oil — 45 ml
- eggs — 2
- jasmine rice, cooked — 350 g

## Method
1. Pound garlic and bird's-eye chillies to a coarse rubble — not a paste; the uneven bits fry into flavour bombs.
2. Get a wok smoking-hot with 20 ml oil; fry the eggs one at a time in hot oil, spooning it over, until the whites are bubbled and crisp-edged, yolks liquid. Set aside.
3. Add 25 ml oil; fry the garlic-chilli 15 seconds until fragrant.
4. Add pork; press flat, let it catch 45 seconds, then break up and stir-fry 2–3 min until browned in spots.
5. Add green beans and the long red chilli, 1 min.
6. Season with fish sauce, oyster sauce, soy and palm sugar; splash in stock and let it sizzle to a gloss, 1 min.
7. Kill the heat; fold in the basil until just wilted — it should perfume, not stew.

## Plating & Service
Rice on the plate, pork over one side, crisp egg on top — yolk pierced at the table into the sauce.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken, duck or minced tofu all take the same treatment; add a spoon of dark soy for colour. The dish lives or dies on wok heat — split into two batches rather than crowd.
