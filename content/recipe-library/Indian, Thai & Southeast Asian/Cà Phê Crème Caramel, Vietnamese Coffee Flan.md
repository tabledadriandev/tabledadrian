# Cà Phê Crème Caramel, Vietnamese Coffee Flan

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bánh flan by way of a French pastry section — a robusta-coffee custard under dark caramel, with the condensed-milk richness of a proper cà phê sữa đá.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- caster sugar (caramel) — 120 g
- water — 40 ml
- whole milk — 350 ml
- condensed milk — 150 g
- strong Vietnamese-style coffee (robusta, or double espresso) — 90 ml
- eggs — 3
- egg yolks — 2
- vanilla — 3 g
- fine sea salt — 1 g
- cold brew or espresso, to serve — 30 ml

## Method
1. Caramel: sugar and water to a deep amber (175–180 °C); divide instantly between six 150 ml ramekins, tilting to coat the bases.
2. Warm the milk with the condensed milk to 60 °C; whisk in the coffee, vanilla and salt.
3. Whisk eggs and yolks gently — no foam; stream in the warm milk.
4. Strain twice; rest 10 min and skim any bubbles.
5. Fill the ramekins; bake in a water bath (water halfway up) at 150 °C for 35–40 min until set with a central tremble (custard 80–82 °C).
6. Chill 4 h minimum, ideally overnight.
7. Run a thin blade around; invert onto rimmed plates with conviction.

## Plating & Service
Serve cold with a spoon of fresh espresso poured around the caramel moat and, for full Saigon credit, a few crushed ice shards on top.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Coconut milk replaces dairy milk credibly (keep the condensed milk or use the coconut version). The robusta bitterness against caramel is the signature — don't use gentle beans.
