# Bánh Xèo, Turmeric Crepes with Prawn, Pork & Bean Sprouts

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sizzling rice-flour crepes — shatteringly crisp, stained gold with turmeric — folded over prawns and pork, eaten wrapped in lettuce and herbs.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- rice flour — 250 g
- cornflour — 25 g
- ground turmeric — 4 g
- coconut milk — 200 ml
- cold water or soda water — 350 ml
- spring onion, thin rings — 30 g
- fine sea salt — 4 g
- pork belly or shoulder, thin slices — 250 g
- raw prawns, peeled, halved lengthways — 250 g
- bean sprouts — 200 g
- onion, sliced — 80 g
- vegetable oil — 60 ml
- lettuce leaves + mint + coriander — 250 g
- nước chấm (lime-fish sauce dip) — 200 ml

## Method
1. Whisk rice flour, cornflour, turmeric, salt, coconut milk and water to a thin cream (single-cream consistency); rest 30 min. Stir in spring onion.
2. Sear pork slices in a 24 cm nonstick pan with 10 ml oil, 2 min; add a share of prawns and onion, 1 min.
3. Raise heat; add 10 ml oil, then a ladle (about 120 ml) of batter, swirling to a thin sheet that hisses violently — the sizzle names the dish.
4. Scatter a handful of bean sprouts on one half; lid on, 2 min.
5. Uncover; cook 2–3 min more until the base is deeply crisp and lacy at the edge. Drizzle a little oil around the rim to help.
6. Fold in half; slide out. Repeat for 4 crepes, holding them in a 100 °C oven uncovered.
7. Cut into sections at the table.

## Plating & Service
Guests wrap shards in lettuce with herbs and drag through nước chấm — messy, interactive, and always the meal people remember.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Filling is a suggestion: mushrooms + tofu, or leftover roast duck both shine. The batter must be thin and the pan hot — thick crepes are the only failure.
