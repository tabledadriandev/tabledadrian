# Appam, Overnight Coconut-Rice Lace Hoppers

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Kerala's bowl-shaped hoppers — crisp lace edges, custardy centre — from a fermented rice-coconut batter swirled in a hot pan.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- raw rice (basmati or idli rice), soaked 4 h — 300 g
- cooked rice — 80 g
- coconut milk — 300 ml
- fresh or desiccated coconut — 50 g
- caster sugar — 15 g
- instant yeast — 2 g
- fine sea salt — 6 g
- warm water — about 100 ml
- vegetable oil for the pan — 15 ml

## Method
1. Blend the drained soaked rice with cooked rice, coconut, 200 ml coconut milk and water until completely smooth — the batter should pour like double cream.
2. Whisk in yeast and sugar; ferment 8–12 h at room temperature until bubbly and slightly sour.
3. Before cooking, whisk in remaining coconut milk and salt; adjust to a thin pouring consistency.
4. Heat a small wok or appam pan (20 cm) over medium heat; wipe with oil.
5. Pour in a small ladle (60 ml) and immediately swirl the pan in a full circle — batter climbs the walls thin, pooling thick at the base.
6. Lid on; cook 2–3 min until the edges are gold lace and the centre is set but tender.
7. Ease out with a flexible spatula; repeat, wiping the pan between hoppers.

## Plating & Service
Serve straightaway in overlapping curves with fish moilee, vegetable stew or just coconut milk and palm sugar.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> A pinch more sugar and a 30-second longer ferment deepen the tang; the batter holds 24 h chilled and actually improves.
