# Cơm Tấm-Style Broken Rice with Grilled Pork Chop & Scallion Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Saigon's plate of broken rice — nutty short grains under a lemongrass-honey pork chop, scallion oil and pickles; a side that eats like a main.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- broken rice (com tam; or jasmine pulsed 2 seconds in a processor) — 300 g
- water — 400 ml
- pork chops, thin-cut — 4 × 150 g
- lemongrass, minced — 25 g
- garlic, minced — 15 g
- fish sauce — 30 ml
- honey — 25 g
- black pepper — 2 g
- vegetable oil — 55 ml
- spring onions, fine rings — 40 g
- pinch salt + sugar for the oil — 3 g
- carrot-daikon pickle — 150 g
- cucumber + tomato — 150 g
- fried egg (optional) — 4
- nước chấm — 150 ml

## Method
1. Marinate the chops 1–12 h: lemongrass, garlic, fish sauce, honey, pepper, 15 ml oil.
2. Rinse the broken rice once; cook with the water — boil, stir, lid on minimum 11 min, rest 10 min. Grains stay small, nutty, slightly clingy.
3. Scallion oil: heat 40 ml oil to 160 °C; pour over spring onions with the salt-sugar pinch.
4. Grill the chops over high heat or a 240 °C plancha, 3 min per side, until charred at the fat and 63 °C core. Rest 4 min.
5. Fry the eggs crisp if using.
6. Fluff the rice; spoon scallion oil generously through and over.
7. Compose: rice, chop sliced or whole, pickles, fresh vegetables, egg.

## Plating & Service
Serve with a small bowl of nước chấm to pour over the rice — the scallion-oil-plus-fish-sauce soak is the entire point.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Grilled chicken or lemongrass tofu replace pork seamlessly; regular jasmine works if breaking rice feels like sacrilege.
