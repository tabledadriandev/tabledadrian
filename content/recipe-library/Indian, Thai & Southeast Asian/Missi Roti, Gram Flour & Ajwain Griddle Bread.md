# Missi Roti, Gram Flour & Ajwain Griddle Bread

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rajasthani gram-flour flatbread — nutty, savoury, flecked with onion, chilli and ajwain; higher protein and lower GI than any plain roti.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- gram (chickpea) flour — 200 g
- chapati flour (atta) — 150 g
- onion, very fine dice — 80 g
- green chilli, minced — 1
- ajwain seeds — 3 g
- cumin seeds — 3 g
- ground turmeric — 3 g
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 3 g
- coriander leaves, chopped — 15 g
- fine sea salt — 8 g
- vegetable oil — 20 ml
- warm water — about 170 ml
- ghee or oil for cooking — 40 g

## Method
1. Mix both flours with all spices, onion, chilli, coriander and salt.
2. Add oil, then water gradually — gram flour drinks unpredictably — to a medium-firm dough. Knead 5 min; rest 20 min.
3. Divide into 8; ball.
4. Roll each to 4 mm, 14 cm — gram flour tears more easily than wheat, so dust and turn often.
5. Cook on a medium tawa 60–90 seconds until spotted; flip.
6. Brush with ghee; cook 60 seconds, flip and ghee again, pressing the edges to crisp.
7. Rest in a cloth 2 min — they soften slightly and improve.

## Plating & Service
Serve with dal, a knob of white butter or thick yogurt, and raw onion — rustic and proud of it.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add dried fenugreek to the dough for methi missi roti; all-gram version (no atta) is gluten-free but roll between parchment sheets.
