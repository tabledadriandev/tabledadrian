# Kapitan Chicken Curry, Nyonya Candlenut & Lime

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Penang's Kapitan curry — brighter than Indian, richer than Thai — candlenut-thickened, tangy with lime and layered with toasted belacan.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- chicken thighs, bone-in, skinless — 900 g
- dried red chillies, soaked — 8
- fresh red chillies — 20 g
- shallots — 100 g
- garlic — 25 g
- galangal — 20 g
- fresh turmeric (or 4 g ground) — 15 g
- lemongrass, sliced — 30 g
- candlenuts or macadamias — 40 g
- belacan, toasted — 8 g
- coconut milk — 400 ml
- tamarind paste — 20 g
- palm sugar — 15 g
- makrut lime leaves — 4
- lime juice — 20 ml
- vegetable oil — 50 ml
- fine sea salt — 10 g

## Method
1. Blend chillies, shallots, garlic, galangal, turmeric, lemongrass, candlenuts and belacan to a fine rempah paste.
2. Fry the rempah in oil over medium heat 8–10 min until fragrant, darker, and the oil splits back out — this step is the curry.
3. Add chicken; turn in the paste 5 min.
4. Add coconut milk, lime leaves, salt and 150 ml water; simmer gently 25 min.
5. Add tamarind and palm sugar; simmer uncovered 8–10 min until the gravy is rich and lightly oiled at the rim.
6. Balance with lime juice — Kapitan should finish tangy and aromatic, not heavy.
7. Rest 10 min before serving.

## Plating & Service
Serve with coconut rice or roti-style flatbread, a lime cheek and fried shallots on top.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Prawn kapitan cooks in 8 min at step 4; swap candlenuts for cashews freely. Macadamias keep the authentic waxy richness.
