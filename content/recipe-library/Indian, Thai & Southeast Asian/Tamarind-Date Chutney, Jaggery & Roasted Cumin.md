# Tamarind-Date Chutney, Jaggery & Roasted Cumin

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The sweet-sour dark chutney (saunth) that completes every chaat — dates for body, tamarind for tang, jaggery for depth, roasted cumin for smoke.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- pitted dates — 150 g
- tamarind paste — 60 g
- jaggery or dark brown sugar — 80 g
- water — 500 ml
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 4 g
- roasted cumin, ground — 5 g
- dried ginger powder — 3 g
- black salt (kala namak) or sea salt — 5 g
- fine sea salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Simmer dates in the water 15 min until fully collapsed.
2. Add tamarind and jaggery; simmer 8–10 min more, mashing.
3. Blend completely smooth; sieve for a glossy, pourable sauce.
4. Return to the pan with chilli, cumin, ginger powder and both salts.
5. Simmer 3–4 min to a single-cream consistency — it thickens further when cold.
6. Taste: sweet first, then sour, warm spice behind; adjust jaggery/tamarind.
7. Cool; jar.

## Plating & Service
Drizzle over chaats, samosas, tikkis and bhel; thin with water for a dipping consistency, keep thick for layering.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Keeps 3 weeks chilled, months frozen. A pinch of fennel powder makes it more Delhi; more ginger, more Bombay.
