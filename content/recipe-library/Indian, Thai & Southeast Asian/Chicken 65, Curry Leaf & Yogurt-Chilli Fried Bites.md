# Chicken 65, Curry Leaf & Yogurt-Chilli Fried Bites

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The cult South Indian bar snack — twice-seasoned fried chicken tossed through a sizzle of curry leaves, garlic and yogurt-chilli glaze.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- chicken thigh, 3 cm pieces — 600 g
- yogurt — 60 g
- egg — 1
- cornflour — 40 g
- rice flour — 30 g
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 10 g
- ground turmeric — 2 g
- garam masala — 4 g
- garlic-ginger paste — 25 g
- fine sea salt — 8 g
- oil for deep frying — 1 L
- curry leaves — 25
- garlic, sliced — 15 g
- green chillies, slit — 3
- extra yogurt — 60 g
- extra Kashmiri chilli — 4 g
- lemon — 1

## Method
1. Marinate chicken 30 min: yogurt, egg, garlic-ginger, 8 g chilli powder, turmeric, garam masala, salt.
2. Add cornflour and rice flour just before frying; the coating should be a thick, clinging batter.
3. Fry at 170 °C in batches 5–6 min to golden; rest 3 min; refry at 185 °C for 60 seconds — audibly crisp.
4. In a wide pan, heat 20 ml of the frying oil; crackle curry leaves, sliced garlic and slit chillies 30 seconds.
5. Whisk extra yogurt with extra chilli powder; pour into the pan — it will sputter and reduce in 60–90 seconds to a clinging glaze.
6. Toss the fried chicken through for 30 seconds only.
7. Out of the pan before it softens; lemon over.

## Plating & Service
Serve in a warm bowl, crisp curry leaves on top — cocktail hour will end it in minutes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cauliflower florets (gobi 65) fry identically for vegetarian; skip the yogurt toss for a dry version that holds longer on a buffet.
