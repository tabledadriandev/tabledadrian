# Wok-Seared Cashew Chicken, Roasted Chilli & Spring Onion Batons

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Gai pad med mamuang done properly — velveted chicken, whole roasted chillies for aroma not violence, and cashews toasted in the wok fat.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- chicken breast, 2 cm dice — 550 g
- cornflour — 15 g
- light soy sauce — 25 ml
- oyster sauce — 25 g
- fish sauce — 10 ml
- palm sugar — 12 g
- chicken stock — 60 ml
- raw cashews — 100 g
- dried red chillies, snipped, seeds shaken out — 6
- garlic, sliced — 15 g
- onion, thick petals — 120 g
- spring onions, 4 cm batons — 60 g
- vegetable oil — 40 ml
- white pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Toss chicken with cornflour, 10 ml soy and white pepper; rest 10 min (a quick velvet).
2. Toast cashews in the dry wok over medium heat 3–4 min to pale gold; remove.
3. Add 15 ml oil; fry the dried chillies 20–30 seconds until darkened and smoky — do not blacken. Remove.
4. Wok to full heat with remaining oil; sear chicken in one layer 90 seconds, then stir-fry 2 min more until golden.
5. Add garlic and onion petals; 1 min.
6. Sauce in: remaining soy, oyster sauce, fish sauce, palm sugar, stock. Toss 1 min to a gloss.
7. Return cashews and chillies with spring onion batons; toss 30 seconds and out.

## Plating & Service
Serve immediately over jasmine rice, chillies visibly lounging on top — guests eat around or into them by choice.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Prawn or crisp tofu versions identical; sunflower seeds for a nut-free table. The dish needs the wok's breath — cook in two rounds for more than 4 covers.
