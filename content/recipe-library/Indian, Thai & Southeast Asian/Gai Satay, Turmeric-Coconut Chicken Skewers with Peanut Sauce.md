# Gai Satay, Turmeric-Coconut Chicken Skewers with Peanut Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Proper satay — thin chicken ribbons in a turmeric-coconut marinade, charred fast, with a from-scratch peanut sauce that owes nothing to a jar.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- chicken thigh, 1 cm ribbons — 600 g
- coconut milk — 100 ml
- ground turmeric — 5 g
- ground coriander — 6 g
- ground cumin — 3 g
- lemongrass, minced — 20 g
- palm sugar — 15 g
- fish sauce — 20 ml
- fine sea salt — 4 g
- roasted peanuts, ground — 120 g
- red curry paste — 30 g
- coconut milk (sauce) — 200 ml
- tamarind paste — 15 g
- palm sugar (sauce) — 20 g
- bamboo skewers, soaked — 16

## Method
1. Marinate chicken 1–12 h: coconut milk, turmeric, coriander, cumin, lemongrass, palm sugar, fish sauce, salt.
2. Sauce: fry curry paste in 50 ml of the sauce coconut milk 2 min; add ground peanuts, remaining coconut milk, tamarind, palm sugar and 100 ml water.
3. Simmer the sauce 8–10 min, whisking, until thick but spoonable; balance salt-sweet-sour.
4. Thread chicken in tight waves onto skewers.
5. Grill over hot coals or a 250 °C plancha, 3 min per side, brushing with leftover marinade — char at the tips is mandatory.
6. Rest 2 min.
7. Loosen sauce with hot water if it has tightened.

## Plating & Service
Fan the skewers around a bowl of warm peanut sauce with cucumber-shallot ajad; serve as the sun drops.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Beef, pork or halloumi all satay well; sunflower-seed butter converts the sauce for peanut allergies without losing body.
