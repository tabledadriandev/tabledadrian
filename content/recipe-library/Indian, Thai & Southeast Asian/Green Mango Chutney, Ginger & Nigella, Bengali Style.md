# Green Mango Chutney, Ginger & Nigella, Bengali Style

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A glossy sweet-hot preserve of underripe mango with ginger and nigella — Bengali aamer chutney logic, calibrated as a chutney-board anchor.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- green (underripe) mangoes, peeled, 1 cm slices — 600 g
- caster sugar — 180 g
- cider vinegar — 60 ml
- water — 150 ml
- ginger, fine julienne — 25 g
- nigella seeds (kalonji) — 4 g
- fennel seeds — 3 g
- dried red chillies, broken — 2
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 3 g
- ground turmeric — 2 g
- fine sea salt — 7 g
- vegetable oil — 20 ml

## Method
1. Heat the oil; sizzle nigella, fennel and broken chillies 20 seconds.
2. Add ginger; 30 seconds. Add mango slices, turmeric and salt; sauté 3 min.
3. Add sugar, vinegar and water; bring to a simmer.
4. Cook gently 20–25 min, stirring more towards the end, until the mango is translucent and the syrup ribbons.
5. Add chilli powder; 2 min.
6. Check the set: a drop on a cold plate should hold a slow line.
7. Jar hot into sterilised jars.

## Plating & Service
Serve beside curries, cold roast meats and cheese; it earns its place at both an Indian thali and a French apéro board.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Firm peaches or Granny Smith apples substitute when green mango hides; halve the sugar for a sharper, fridge-only version (10 days).
