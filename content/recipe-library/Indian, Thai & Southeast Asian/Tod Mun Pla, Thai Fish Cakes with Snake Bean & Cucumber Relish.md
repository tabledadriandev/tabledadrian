# Tod Mun Pla, Thai Fish Cakes with Snake Bean & Cucumber Relish

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Springy-bouncy red-curry fish cakes — the texture comes from slapping the paste — with the essential sweet cucumber ajad alongside.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- firm white fish (cod, whiting), chilled, diced — 500 g
- red curry paste — 50 g
- egg — 1
- fish sauce — 15 ml
- palm sugar — 10 g
- makrut lime leaves, hair-fine chiffonade — 4
- snake beans or green beans, thin rounds — 80 g
- cornflour — 10 g
- oil for shallow frying — 300 ml
- rice vinegar — 60 ml
- caster sugar — 50 g
- water — 40 ml
- cucumber, quarter-moons — 150 g
- shallot, sliced — 30 g
- red chilli, sliced — 1
- roasted peanuts, crushed — 20 g

## Method
1. Make the ajad: dissolve sugar in vinegar and water over low heat; cool, then add cucumber, shallot and chilli.
2. Blitz the cold fish with curry paste, egg, fish sauce, palm sugar and cornflour to a smooth, sticky paste.
3. Slap the paste against the bowl 20–30 times — this develops the classic bounce.
4. Fold in lime leaves and beans.
5. With oiled hands, form 12 patties, 1.5 cm thick.
6. Shallow-fry at 170 °C, 2–3 min per side, until puffed and mahogany.
7. Drain on a rack 2 min.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm, ajad in a bowl alongside with peanuts scattered over the relish — one dunk per bite.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Prawn (half fish, half prawn) gives extra sweetness; the paste can be made 6 h ahead. Fry to order — they deflate as they wait.
