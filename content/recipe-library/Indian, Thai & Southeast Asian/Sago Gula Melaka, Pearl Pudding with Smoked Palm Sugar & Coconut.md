# Sago Gula Melaka, Pearl Pudding with Smoked Palm Sugar & Coconut

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The elegant Malaysian trinity — chilled sago pearls, thick coconut cream and a dark gula melaka syrup — three jugs, one perfect spoonful.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- sago or small tapioca pearls — 180 g
- water — 1.5 L
- gula melaka (palm sugar block) — 180 g
- water (syrup) — 80 ml
- pandan leaf, knotted — 1
- coconut cream — 250 ml
- fine sea salt — 3 g
- lime zest (untraditional, excellent) — 1 g

## Method
1. Rain the pearls into the fully boiling water; stir immediately.
2. Boil 10–12 min, stirring occasionally, until translucent with tiny white centres; off heat, lid on, 5 min to clear fully.
3. Rinse in a sieve under cold water to strip the starch.
4. Press into six lightly-oiled moulds or one bowl; chill 2 h until set.
5. Syrup: melt the gula melaka with 80 ml water and the pandan; simmer 5 min to a loose, smoky-dark syrup. Cool.
6. Season the coconut cream with the salt.
7. Unmould each sago dome; serve with both jugs at the table.

## Plating & Service
Guests drown their own dome — first coconut cream, then syrup; the salted cream against smoky palm sugar is the whole event. Lime zest over, if you dare.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Everything keeps 3 days chilled — the ultimate make-ahead charter dessert. Dark muscovado plus a drop of maple mimics gula melaka at a pinch.
