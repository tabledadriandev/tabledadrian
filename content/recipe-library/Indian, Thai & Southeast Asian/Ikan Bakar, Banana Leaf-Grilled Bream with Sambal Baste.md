# Ikan Bakar, Banana Leaf-Grilled Bream with Sambal Baste

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Whole bream slathered in a belacan-chilli baste and grilled on banana leaf until smoky and lacquered — beach-shack cooking elevated to the aft deck.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- whole sea bream, gutted, scored — 2 (about 1.2 kg)
- dried red chillies, soaked — 8
- fresh red chillies — 20 g
- shallots — 80 g
- garlic — 20 g
- belacan, toasted — 8 g
- fresh turmeric — 12 g
- lemongrass — 25 g
- tamarind paste — 25 g
- palm sugar — 20 g
- coconut milk — 60 ml
- lime — 2
- banana leaves (or foil) — 2 sheets
- vegetable oil — 50 ml
- fine sea salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Blend chillies, shallots, garlic, belacan, turmeric and lemongrass to a paste.
2. Fry the paste in oil 6–8 min until split and fragrant; stir in tamarind, palm sugar, coconut milk and salt. Cool.
3. Dry the fish; rub the paste all over and deep into the score marks. Rest 20 min.
4. Soften banana leaves over a flame 10 seconds a side until glossy.
5. Grill fish on the leaf over medium coals (or 220 °C grill), 8–10 min per side depending on size, basting with leftover paste — the leaf chars and perfumes while protecting the skin.
6. Check at the bone: 58–60 °C, flesh lifting cleanly.
7. Rest 3 min; hard squeeze of lime.

## Plating & Service
Serve on the charred leaf with extra sambal, lime cheeks and rice — let guests lift the cheeks first.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Mackerel, snapper or thick swordfish steaks (6 min/side) all take the baste; foil works when banana leaves don't make the provisioning list.
