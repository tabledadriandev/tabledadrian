# Gỏi Cuốn, Prawn & Herb Fresh Rolls with Peanut-Hoisin Dip

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Translucent rice-paper rolls with prawns showing through like stained glass — the discipline is in the tight roll and the herb-to-noodle ratio.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- rice paper rounds, 22 cm — 12
- raw prawns — 300 g
- rice vermicelli, cooked and cooled — 150 g
- butter lettuce leaves, ribs removed — 12
- mint leaves — 20 g
- coriander sprigs — 20 g
- Thai basil — 15 g
- garlic chives or spring onion greens — 12 strands
- cucumber, fine batons — 100 g
- hoisin sauce — 80 g
- smooth peanut butter — 40 g
- lime juice — 15 ml
- water, hot — 40 ml
- roasted peanuts, crushed — 15 g
- sriracha-style chilli sauce — 10 g

## Method
1. Poach prawns 2 min; shock, peel, and halve lengthways.
2. Whisk the dip: hoisin, peanut butter, lime and hot water to a pourable cream; chilli sauce swirled in; peanuts on top.
3. Set a station: warm water bath, damp board, all fillings within reach.
4. Dip a rice paper 5–8 seconds — it should still feel slightly firm; it keeps softening as you work.
5. Lay lettuce, a small bundle of noodles, cucumber and herbs on the lower third; three prawn halves cut-side up above them (they'll face out).
6. Fold the bottom over, tuck the sides, lay a chive strand with its tail out, and roll tight.
7. Rest seam-down under a damp cloth; roll all 12.

## Plating & Service
Halve on the bias and stand upright, prawn-side out, dip central; serve within the hour, cloth-covered.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Shredded poached chicken, grilled tofu or just avocado + mango all roll well; the tight roll matters more than the filling.
