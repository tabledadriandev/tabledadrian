# Railway Beef Curry, Potato & Coconut Milk

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> An Anglo-Indian braise built for rolling motion — one pot, forgiving timing, and a mellow coconut gravy that pleases every palate on board.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- beef chuck, 4 cm dice — 1.2 kg
- vegetable oil — 45 ml
- onions, sliced — 300 g
- garlic-ginger paste — 35 g
- curry leaves — 15
- cinnamon stick — 1 (5 cm)
- green cardamom — 4
- ground coriander — 12 g
- ground cumin — 6 g
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 6 g
- ground turmeric — 4 g
- tomatoes, chopped — 250 g
- coconut milk — 400 ml
- waxy potatoes, halved — 400 g
- tamarind paste — 15 g
- fine sea salt — 13 g

## Method
1. Heat oil in a heavy casserole; sizzle cinnamon, cardamom and curry leaves 30 seconds.
2. Add onions; cook 12 min to golden-brown. Add garlic-ginger paste 2 min.
3. Bloom coriander, cumin, chilli and turmeric 30 seconds, adding a splash of water if catching.
4. Add tomatoes; cook 6 min. Add beef and salt; turn in the masala 5 min.
5. Add coconut milk and 300 ml water; cover and simmer very gently 1.5 h.
6. Add potatoes; cook uncovered 25–30 min more until both beef and potato are tender and the gravy has body.
7. Finish with tamarind; simmer 3 min and check salt — it should be gently sour behind the coconut.

## Plating & Service
A big lidded pot at the table, rice and green beans thoran alongside; it reheats better than it serves fresh, so make it the day before.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Lamb or goat identical; chicken thigh version cooks in 35 min total. Add green chillies with the onions for more edge.
