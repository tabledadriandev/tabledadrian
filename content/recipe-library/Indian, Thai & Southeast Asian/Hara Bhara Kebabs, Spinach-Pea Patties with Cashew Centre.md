# Hara Bhara Kebabs, Spinach-Pea Patties with Cashew Centre

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Emerald-green griddled patties of spinach, pea and potato with a buried toasted cashew — a vegetarian kebab with real texture, not an apology.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- spinach — 300 g
- peas — 200 g
- potato, boiled and riced — 250 g
- gram flour, toasted — 40 g
- ginger, minced — 15 g
- green chillies, minced — 2
- cumin seeds, toasted and ground — 4 g
- garam masala — 3 g
- amchoor — 4 g
- fine sea salt — 8 g
- cashews, toasted — 16 halves
- vegetable oil — 45 ml
- lemon — 1
- coriander leaves — 15 g

## Method
1. Blanch spinach 45 seconds, shock in iced water, squeeze absolutely dry, chop fine.
2. Pulse peas to a coarse crush; cook in a dry pan 3–4 min to drive off moisture.
3. Mix spinach, peas, potato, toasted gram flour, ginger, chillies, spices, amchoor, salt and coriander into a firm dough. If loose, add more toasted gram flour.
4. Form 16 patties; press a cashew half into each centre and reseal.
5. Chill 15 min to set.
6. Griddle in the oil over medium heat 3–4 min per side until dark green-gold with a crisp face.
7. Rest 2 min; squeeze lemon over.

## Plating & Service
Stack on a slate with mint chutney and lemon wedges — cut one open to show the green interior and hidden cashew.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Bake at 200 °C for 18 min turning once for oil-light service. Swap cashew for a cube of smoked cheese if dairy is welcome.
