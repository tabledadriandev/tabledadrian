# Aloo Tikki Chaat, Crisped Potato Cakes with Yogurt & Two Chutneys

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Delhi street chaat, layered properly: shattering-crisp potato cakes under cold yogurt, tamarind and mint chutneys, and a storm of sev — hot, cold, sweet, sour at once.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- potatoes, boiled and cooled overnight — 600 g
- cornflour — 30 g
- ginger, minced — 12 g
- green chilli, minced — 1
- cumin seeds, toasted — 4 g
- fine sea salt — 7 g
- vegetable oil — 60 ml
- thick yogurt, whisked with 5 g sugar — 250 g
- tamarind-date chutney — 80 g
- coriander-mint chutney — 60 g
- chaat masala — 4 g
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 2 g
- sev (crisp gram noodles) — 50 g
- pomegranate seeds — 60 g
- coriander leaves — 10 g

## Method
1. Grate the cold potatoes coarsely; mix with cornflour, ginger, chilli, cumin and salt.
2. Form 8 tight pucks (about 80 g); chill 15 min.
3. Shallow-fry in the oil over medium heat, 5–6 min per side, pressing gently, until deep gold with a thick crust. Don't rush — the crust is the dish.
4. Whisk the yogurt loose and cold.
5. Plate two tikkis per person; break their tops slightly.
6. Spoon over cold yogurt, then both chutneys in ribbons.
7. Finish with chaat masala, chilli powder, sev, pomegranate and coriander.

## Plating & Service
Serve immediately in shallow bowls — the point is crisp cakes fighting cold toppings; assemble at the last second.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add white chickpeas under the yogurt for a fuller plate. Vegan: coconut yogurt. GF: check the sev or swap for crushed roasted peanuts.
