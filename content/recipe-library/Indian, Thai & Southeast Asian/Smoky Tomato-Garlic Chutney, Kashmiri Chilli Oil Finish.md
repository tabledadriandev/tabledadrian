# Smoky Tomato-Garlic Chutney, Kashmiri Chilli Oil Finish

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A jammy, gently smoky cooked chutney — charred tomatoes, slow garlic and Kashmiri chilli — that behaves like a desi ketchup with actual dignity.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- ripe tomatoes — 700 g
- garlic, sliced — 25 g
- vegetable oil — 50 ml
- black mustard seeds — 4 g
- cumin seeds — 4 g
- curry leaves — 12
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 8 g
- ground coriander — 4 g
- jaggery — 25 g
- tamarind paste — 10 g
- fine sea salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Char the whole tomatoes under a 250 °C grill, turning, 10–12 min until blistered and slumping; chop roughly, keeping the charred skins.
2. Heat 40 ml oil; crackle mustard and cumin, add curry leaves and garlic; fry the garlic slowly to pale gold, 2 min.
3. Bloom the chilli powder and coriander 15 seconds.
4. Add tomatoes, salt, jaggery; cook over medium heat 20–25 min, mashing, until thick, glossy and darkened, with oil pooling at the edges.
5. Add tamarind; 2 min more.
6. Taste — smoke, sweetness, sourness in balance.
7. Finish with the remaining 10 ml oil bloomed with a pinch of chilli powder, poured over the top.

## Plating & Service
Serve with parathas, pakoras, cheese boards or burgers — it outperforms ketchup everywhere ketchup goes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Keeps 2 weeks chilled under its oil layer. Add a diced red pepper with the tomatoes for extra body and sweetness.
