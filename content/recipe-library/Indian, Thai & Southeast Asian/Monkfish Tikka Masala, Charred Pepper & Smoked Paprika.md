# Monkfish Tikka Masala, Charred Pepper & Smoked Paprika

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Meaty monkfish stands up to tandoori charring better than any chicken — a coastal tikka masala built for a yacht galley's grill.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- monkfish tail, trimmed, 4 cm chunks — 600 g
- Greek yogurt — 120 g
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 8 g
- garam masala — 5 g
- ground cumin — 4 g
- lemon juice — 25 ml
- garlic-ginger paste — 30 g
- red peppers — 2 (300 g)
- butter — 40 g
- onion, chopped — 180 g
- tomato passata — 300 g
- smoked paprika — 3 g
- double cream — 100 ml
- kasoori methi — 3 g
- fine sea salt — 9 g

## Method
1. Marinate monkfish in yogurt, half the chilli powder, cumin, half the garam masala, half the garlic-ginger, lemon and 4 g salt; 30–60 min chilled (no longer — acid softens fish).
2. Char whole peppers under a 250 °C grill until blackened; peel, seed and chop.
3. Thread fish onto skewers; grill at 250 °C for 8–10 min, turning once, until charred at the corners and just opaque.
4. Melt butter; cook onion 8 min to gold, add remaining garlic-ginger 2 min.
5. Bloom remaining chilli powder and smoked paprika 20 seconds; add passata and charred peppers, simmer 10 min, then blend smooth.
6. Return to pan with cream, crushed methi, remaining salt; simmer 4 min to a nappe consistency.
7. Slide in the grilled fish and any juices; warm 2 min without stirring hard.

## Plating & Service
Skewer-marks up, sauce beneath, a flick of cream and coriander — serve with lemon rice or naan.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap monkfish for prawns or halibut; coconut cream keeps it dairy-free if you marinate in coconut yogurt.
