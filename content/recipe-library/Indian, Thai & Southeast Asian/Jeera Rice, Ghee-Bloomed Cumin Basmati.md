# Jeera Rice, Ghee-Bloomed Cumin Basmati

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The workhorse pilaf — cumin toasted in ghee until it crackles, then absorbed-method basmati that lands in separate, perfumed grains every time.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- aged basmati rice, rinsed until clear, soaked 20 min — 300 g
- ghee — 35 g
- cumin seeds — 7 g
- green cardamom — 3
- bay leaf — 1
- cinnamon — 1 stick (4 cm)
- water — 480 ml
- fine sea salt — 6 g
- lemon juice — 10 ml
- coriander leaves — 8 g

## Method
1. Melt ghee over medium heat; add cumin, cardamom, bay and cinnamon. Sizzle 30–40 seconds until the cumin darkens a shade and smells toasty.
2. Add the drained rice; fold gently 90 seconds to coat every grain in spiced fat — this keeps them separate.
3. Add water, salt and lemon juice (the acid whitens and separates the grains).
4. Boil hard, uncovered, until the surface shows steam holes and the water level meets the rice, 4–5 min.
5. Lid on, lowest heat, 10 min. No peeking.
6. Off the heat, rest 8 min, still covered.
7. Open, fluff from the edges inward with a fork; coriander through.

## Plating & Service
Serve mounded in a warm bowl — the neutral-aromatic partner to every curry in this collection.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan with neutral oil (lose a little soul); add 60 g fried onions and peas for instant pulao. Ratio to remember: rice 1 : water 1.6 by volume.
