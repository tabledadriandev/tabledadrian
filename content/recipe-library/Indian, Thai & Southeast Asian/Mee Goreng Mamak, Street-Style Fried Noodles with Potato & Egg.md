# Mee Goreng Mamak, Street-Style Fried Noodles with Potato & Egg

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The mamak-stall grease-and-fire classic — yellow noodles, chilli-tomato-soy tangle, boiled potato, egg scrambled through, lime hard at the end.

## Ingredients (serves 2)
- fresh yellow hokkien noodles — 400 g
- boiled waxy potato, thick slices — 150 g
- firm tofu, cubes — 100 g
- eggs — 2
- choy sum or cabbage, chopped — 100 g
- bean sprouts — 80 g
- garlic, minced — 12 g
- chilli paste (fried dried-chilli paste) — 40 g
- tomato ketchup — 30 g
- kecap manis — 25 ml
- light soy sauce — 20 ml
- fish sauce — 8 ml
- vegetable oil — 45 ml
- lime — 1
- roasted peanuts, crushed — 15 g

## Method
1. Get the wok smoking with 25 ml oil; brown the tofu and potato slices 2–3 min. Push aside.
2. Add garlic and chilli paste; fry 30 seconds.
3. Add noodles with kecap manis, soy, ketchup and fish sauce; toss hard 90 seconds, letting the noodles catch in places.
4. Well in the centre, remaining oil, crack in the eggs; scramble 30 seconds, then toss through.
5. Add greens; 60 seconds. Sprouts; 20 seconds — they stay crunchy.
6. Taste: sweet-savoury with a tomato tang and building chilli.
7. Plate; squeeze half the lime over immediately.

## Plating & Service
Serve blazing hot with the second lime cheek and peanuts over — a green-chilli pickle alongside if the fridge has it.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Prawns or shredded chicken drop in at step 3. This is a two-portion wok dish; repeat rather than double.
