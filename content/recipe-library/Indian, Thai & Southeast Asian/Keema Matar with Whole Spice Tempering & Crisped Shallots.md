# Keema Matar with Whole Spice Tempering & Crisped Shallots

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Weeknight-fast lamb mince curry with proper technique — whole spices bloomed in hot fat first, so ten minutes of tempering tastes like an hour of cooking.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- lamb mince (15% fat) — 600 g
- vegetable oil — 30 ml
- cinnamon stick — 1 (5 cm)
- green cardamom pods — 4
- cloves — 3
- cumin seeds — 5 g
- onions, finely chopped — 250 g
- garlic-ginger paste — 30 g
- tomatoes, chopped — 250 g
- ground coriander — 8 g
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 5 g
- ground turmeric — 3 g
- frozen peas — 150 g
- garam masala — 3 g
- fine sea salt — 9 g
- crispy fried shallots — 30 g

## Method
1. Heat oil in a wide pan over medium; drop in cinnamon, cardamom, cloves and cumin. Sizzle 30 seconds until the cardamom puffs.
2. Add onions and a pinch of salt; cook 8–10 min to golden.
3. Add garlic-ginger paste, 2 min; bloom coriander, chilli and turmeric 20 seconds.
4. Add tomatoes, cook 6 min until thick and the fat separates.
5. Add mince, breaking it fine; cook 8 min until no longer grey and starting to brown at the pan base.
6. Add 150 ml water and salt; simmer 12 min until rich but not wet.
7. Fold in peas 3 min, finish with garam masala; scatter crisped shallots at the table.

## Plating & Service
Serve with warm pav-style rolls or basmati, lime wedges and yogurt on the side.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Beef or turkey mince works equally; vegan with fine-chopped mushrooms and brown lentils. A diced potato added with the water makes it aloo-keema.
