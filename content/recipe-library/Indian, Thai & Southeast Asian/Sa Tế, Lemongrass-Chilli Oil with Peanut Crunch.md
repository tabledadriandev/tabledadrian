# Sa Tế, Lemongrass-Chilli Oil with Peanut Crunch

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Vietnamese satế — a crunchy, lemongrass-heavy chilli oil that turbocharges phở, grilled meats and noodle bowls; the jar everyone steals from.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- vegetable oil — 250 ml
- lemongrass, minced fine — 80 g
- shallots, minced — 60 g
- garlic, minced — 40 g
- dried chilli flakes — 25 g
- fresh red chilli, minced — 20 g
- roasted peanuts, chopped — 40 g
- caster sugar — 15 g
- fine sea salt — 8 g
- soy sauce — 10 ml
- sweet paprika (for colour) — 5 g

## Method
1. Heat the oil to 150 °C in a saucepan.
2. Add lemongrass; fry 6–8 min, stirring, until golden and no longer squeaking (its water is gone).
3. Add shallots; 3–4 min to gold. Garlic; 2 min to pale gold — no darker.
4. Off the heat 1 min, then add both chillies and paprika — the residual heat blooms without scorching.
5. Stir in sugar, salt, soy and peanuts.
6. Cool completely in the pan; the solids settle into a crunchy sediment.
7. Jar; stir before each use.

## Plating & Service
A spoonful into phở or over grilled chicken, fried eggs, dumplings — anywhere that needs heat with texture.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Keeps a month chilled. Skip peanuts for allergy boats; toasted sesame steps in. Strain half for a clean chilli oil, keep half crunchy.
