# Methi Murgh, Fresh Fenugreek & Green Chilli

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bitter-sweet fresh fenugreek leaves against browned chicken — an under-travelled北 Indian classic that tastes sophisticated with zero cream reliance.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- chicken thighs, boneless, quartered — 700 g
- fresh fenugreek (methi) leaves, picked — 150 g (or 60 g baby spinach + 8 g kasoori methi)
- vegetable oil — 35 ml
- cumin seeds — 5 g
- onions, sliced — 220 g
- garlic-ginger paste — 30 g
- green chillies, slit — 3
- tomatoes, chopped — 200 g
- ground coriander — 7 g
- ground turmeric — 3 g
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 4 g
- yogurt, whisked — 100 g
- garam masala — 3 g
- fine sea salt — 9 g

## Method
1. Salt the methi leaves lightly, rest 10 min, squeeze out bitter juices, and chop roughly.
2. Heat oil; sizzle cumin 20 seconds, add onions and cook 10 min to gold.
3. Add garlic-ginger paste and chillies; 2 min. Bloom coriander, turmeric and chilli powder 20 seconds.
4. Add tomatoes; cook 6 min until the oil separates.
5. Add chicken and salt; sear in the masala 5 min.
6. Whisk yogurt and stir in off the boil to prevent splitting; return to a gentle simmer with 100 ml water for 15 min.
7. Fold in methi; cook 5 min until the leaves melt into the sauce. Finish with garam masala.

## Plating & Service
Serve in a karahi-style dish with lachha paratha and lemon; the sauce should cling, not pool.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free: replace yogurt with 60 ml coconut cream + 10 ml lemon. Works with turkey thigh or firm white fish (add fish for final 8 min only).
