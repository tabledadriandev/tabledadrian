# Tahu Isi, Stuffed Fried Tofu with Vegetable Crunch & Sambal Kecap

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Street-warung tofu pockets — crisp shells stuffed with sprouts, cabbage and carrot, batter-fried, with a raw chilli-kecap dip that cuts straight through.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- firm tofu, 6 cm triangles — 400 g
- bean sprouts — 100 g
- cabbage, fine shred — 100 g
- carrot, julienne — 80 g
- garlic, minced — 10 g
- white pepper — 1 g
- fine sea salt — 6 g
- plain flour — 120 g
- rice flour — 40 g
- ground coriander — 3 g
- ground turmeric — 2 g
- cold water — 220 ml
- oil for frying — 1 L
- kecap manis — 60 ml
- bird's-eye chillies, sliced — 6 g
- lime juice — 15 ml
- shallot, fine dice — 20 g

## Method
1. Deep-fry the tofu triangles naked at 180 °C, 4–5 min, until golden with a skin; cool. Slit one side to form a pocket.
2. Stir-fry garlic 20 seconds; add cabbage, carrot, sprouts, salt, white pepper — 90 seconds only, still crunchy. Cool.
3. Stuff each pocket generously.
4. Batter: both flours, coriander, turmeric, 4 g salt, cold water — single-cream thickness.
5. Dip stuffed tofu; let excess run off.
6. Fry at 175 °C, 3–4 min, to a craggy gold.
7. Dip: kecap manis, chilli, shallot, lime — stirred, not blended.

## Plating & Service
Halve one to show the striped filling; serve hot with the dark dip and extra bird's-eyes for the fearless.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> A minced-prawn addition to the stuffing is common; tamari + rice flour only for gluten-free. Eat within 15 minutes of frying.
