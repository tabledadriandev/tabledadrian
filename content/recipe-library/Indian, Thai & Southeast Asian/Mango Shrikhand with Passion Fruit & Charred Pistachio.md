# Mango Shrikhand with Passion Fruit & Charred Pistachio

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Strained-yogurt cloud folded with Alphonso mango and cut with passion fruit acidity — a no-cook dessert that plates like a mousse and travels like a dream.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- thick Greek-style yogurt — 600 g
- ripe mango purée (Alphonso if possible) — 250 g
- icing sugar — 60 g
- green cardamom, ground — 3 pods
- saffron — 0.2 g, steeped in 10 ml warm milk
- passion fruit — 3
- pistachios — 40 g
- lime zest — 2 g
- fine sea salt — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Hang the yogurt in muslin over a bowl, chilled, 4–6 h until it loses a third of its weight — thick as mascarpone (skip if using labneh).
2. Toast the pistachios in a dry pan until they smell deep and char at a few edges; chop roughly.
3. Whisk the hung yogurt with icing sugar, cardamom, saffron milk and salt until silken — do not overwork or it loosens.
4. Fold in two-thirds of the mango purée in two strokes — ribbons, not uniformity.
5. Chill 1 h.
6. Quenelle into glasses or bowls; spoon remaining mango around.
7. Passion fruit pulp over, pistachios and lime zest last.

## Plating & Service
Serve cold in glass so the mango ribbons show — after a heavy curry menu it's exactly the right weight of ending.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Coconut yogurt (hung 2 h) converts it; swap mango for roasted apricots off-season. Sugar can drop to 40 g when the mango is truly ripe — hence the honest diabetic-friendly tag.
