# Tandoori-Style Spatchcock Poussin, Mint Yogurt

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> No tandoor needed — a two-stage yogurt marinade and a screaming-hot oven give charred, juicy birds with that unmistakable smoky-red crust.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- poussins, spatchcocked — 2 (about 1 kg total)
- thick Greek-style yogurt — 200 g
- lemon juice — 30 ml
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 12 g
- garam masala — 6 g
- ground cumin — 5 g
- ground turmeric — 3 g
- garlic paste — 20 g
- ginger paste — 20 g
- mustard oil — 20 ml
- fine sea salt — 10 g
- mint leaves — 15 g
- extra yogurt for sauce — 120 g
- chaat masala — 2 g

## Method
1. First marinade: rub birds with lemon juice, 4 g salt and half the chilli powder; rest 20 min so the acid opens the surface.
2. Second marinade: whisk 200 g yogurt with garlic, ginger, remaining chilli, garam masala, cumin, turmeric, mustard oil and remaining salt. Coat birds thoroughly, working under the skin; marinate 4–24 h chilled.
3. Bring to room temperature 30 min before cooking. Heat oven to 250 °C (or as high as it goes) with a rack over a tray.
4. Roast birds skin-up 20–25 min until charred at the edges and 74 °C at the thickest thigh.
5. Rest 8 min under loose foil.
6. Meanwhile blitz mint with 120 g yogurt and a pinch of salt for the sauce.
7. Dust the rested birds with chaat masala and a squeeze of lemon.

## Plating & Service
Halve each poussin, stack over charred lemon halves, mint yogurt in a pool beside; red onion rings and coriander to finish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Works on a BBQ over indirect heat then flashed over coals; swap poussin for a butterflied chicken breast (12 min) or cauliflower steaks for vegetarian.
