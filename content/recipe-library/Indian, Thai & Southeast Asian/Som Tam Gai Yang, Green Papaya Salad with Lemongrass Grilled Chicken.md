# Som Tam Gai Yang, Green Papaya Salad with Lemongrass Grilled Chicken

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The great Isaan duet — pounded green papaya salad and lemongrass-marinated grilled chicken, built as a light main for hot afternoons on deck.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- chicken thighs, boneless, skin-on — 600 g
- lemongrass, minced — 30 g
- garlic — 25 g
- coriander root — 10 g
- fish sauce — 45 ml
- palm sugar — 35 g
- green papaya, fine julienne — 400 g
- cherry tomatoes, halved — 120 g
- snake beans or green beans, 3 cm — 80 g
- dried shrimp — 10 g
- bird's-eye chillies — 4–8 g
- lime juice — 50 ml
- tamarind paste — 10 g
- roasted peanuts — 40 g
- white pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Marinate chicken: pound half the garlic with lemongrass, coriander root and white pepper; mix with 20 ml fish sauce and 15 g palm sugar. Coat chicken; 1–12 h chilled.
2. Grill chicken skin-down over medium coals or pan at 180 °C surface, 8 min; flip 6–8 min to 74 °C core with mahogany skin. Rest 5 min.
3. In a mortar, bruise remaining garlic and the chillies.
4. Add beans; crack them with the pestle. Add dried shrimp and half the peanuts; bruise.
5. Season: remaining fish sauce and palm sugar, lime juice, tamarind. Pound-and-scoop to dissolve.
6. Add papaya and tomatoes; bruise gently with pestle and spoon together — the papaya must stay crunchy.
7. Taste: sour-hot leading, salty, sweet trailing.

## Plating & Service
Salad mounded beside sliced chicken, remaining peanuts over the tam, sticky rice in a basket if you have it.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Green mango or julienned kohlrabi replace papaya at Riviera markets; omit dried shrimp + use vegan seasoning for plant-based tables.
