# Curd Rice, Tempered Yogurt Rice with Pomegranate & Curry Leaf

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> South India's great cooler — soft rice folded through seasoned yogurt with a mustard-seed tadka, jewelled with pomegranate. The antidote to a chilli-heavy menu.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- cooked rice, soft and slightly overdone — 500 g
- whole-milk yogurt — 400 g
- milk — 60 ml
- fine sea salt — 7 g
- vegetable oil — 25 ml
- black mustard seeds — 4 g
- urad dal (split black gram) — 8 g
- dried red chilli — 1
- green chilli, minced — 1
- ginger, minced — 12 g
- fresh curry leaves — 12
- asafoetida — 0.4 g
- carrot, fine grate — 60 g
- cucumber, fine dice — 80 g
- pomegranate seeds — 60 g
- coriander leaves — 8 g

## Method
1. Mash the warm rice lightly with the milk — it should be creamy, not grainy.
2. Cool to room temperature; fold in yogurt and salt (hot rice splits yogurt).
3. Tadka: heat oil, crackle mustard seeds, add urad dal to gold, then both chillies, ginger, curry leaves, asafoetida — 30 seconds.
4. Pour the tadka over the rice; fold through.
5. Add carrot and cucumber.
6. Chill 30 min if time allows — it thickens and settles.
7. Loosen with a splash of milk if needed; top with pomegranate and coriander.

## Plating & Service
Serve cool in small bowls at the end of a spicy spread, or as a standalone light lunch on a hot bay day.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Coconut yogurt version for dairy-free is genuinely good; green grapes stand in for pomegranate. A classic with lime pickle alongside.
