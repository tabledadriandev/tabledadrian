# Penang-Style Assam Fish Curry, Tamarind & Torch Ginger Broth

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Assam pedas — a coconut-free, hot-and-sour tamarind curry that lets a whole fish or thick fillets keep centre stage; sharp, clean and addictive.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- snapper or sea bream fillets, thick — 600 g
- dried red chillies, soaked — 10
- shallots — 80 g
- garlic — 20 g
- belacan, toasted — 8 g
- fresh turmeric — 12 g
- lemongrass, bruised — 2 stalks
- tamarind paste — 45 g
- water — 700 ml
- palm sugar — 20 g
- okra — 150 g
- tomatoes, wedges — 150 g
- laksa leaf or mint — 10 g
- torch ginger bud, sliced (optional) — 1
- vegetable oil — 45 ml
- fine sea salt — 9 g

## Method
1. Blend chillies, shallots, garlic, belacan and turmeric to a paste.
2. Fry the paste in oil over medium heat 6–8 min until the oil returns.
3. Add water, tamarind, lemongrass, palm sugar and salt; simmer 10 min into a light, russet broth.
4. Add okra; 4 min. Add tomatoes; 2 min.
5. Lay in the fish; poach at a gentle simmer 6–8 min until just flaking.
6. Taste — boldly sour, then hot, then a background sweetness; adjust tamarind or sugar.
7. Off the heat, scatter laksa leaf and torch ginger.

## Plating & Service
Serve from the pot with jasmine rice; the broth is thin by design — give everyone a spoon.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Mackerel is the classic and stands up beautifully; prawns take 3 min. For gentler heat, seed the dried chillies before soaking.
