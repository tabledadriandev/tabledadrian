# Sindhi Kadhi-Style Vegetable Curry, Toasted Gram Flour & Tamarind

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A tomato-tamarind curry silked with roasted gram flour instead of yogurt — a vegan-safe, deeply savoury vehicle for whatever vegetables the market gave you.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- gram (chickpea) flour — 50 g
- vegetable oil — 40 ml
- cumin seeds — 4 g
- black mustard seeds — 3 g
- fenugreek seeds — 1 g
- curry leaves — 12
- ginger, grated — 15 g
- green chillies, slit — 2
- ground turmeric — 4 g
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 4 g
- tomatoes, chopped — 250 g
- tamarind paste — 20 g
- mixed vegetables (okra, potato, carrot, green beans, cauliflower) — 600 g
- water — 900 ml
- fine sea salt — 11 g

## Method
1. Cut vegetables to even 3 cm pieces; keep okra whole. Roast potato and cauliflower with a film of oil at 220 °C for 15 min for extra depth.
2. Heat oil in a casserole; crackle mustard, cumin and fenugreek 20 seconds, then curry leaves, ginger, chillies.
3. Add gram flour; toast over medium-low heat 4–5 min, stirring constantly, until biscuit-scented and a shade darker.
4. Bloom turmeric and chilli powder 15 seconds, then whisk in the water gradually to avoid lumps.
5. Add tomatoes and salt; simmer 12 min until the curry has a light velouté body.
6. Add carrots and beans, 8 min; then roasted vegetables and okra, 8 min more, until everything is tender.
7. Finish with tamarind; simmer 3 min — it should be gently sour, savoury and pourable.

## Plating & Service
Ladle over steamed rice in wide bowls; a papad and lime pickle turn it into a proper Sindhi lunch.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add fried gram-flour dumplings for the full classic; swap seasonal vegetables freely — courgette and fennel work beautifully on the Riviera.
