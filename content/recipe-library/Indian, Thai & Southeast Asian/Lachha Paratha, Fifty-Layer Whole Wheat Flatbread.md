# Lachha Paratha, Fifty-Layer Whole Wheat Flatbread

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The coiled, ruffled paratha — laminated by hand with oil and flour dust, then scrunched to release dozens of crisp layers. Bread as knife-skills.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- chapati flour (atta) or half wholemeal half plain — 400 g
- fine sea salt — 8 g
- water — 230 ml
- vegetable oil or melted ghee — 90 ml
- extra flour for dusting — 40 g

## Method
1. Knead flour, salt and water 8 min to a supple dough; rest 30 min under a damp cloth.
2. Divide into 6. Roll one ball paper-thin into a large round — thinner than you think possible.
3. Brush generously with oil, dust lightly with flour (the flour separates the layers).
4. Pleat the whole sheet into a 2 cm accordion strip, then stretch the strip gently longer.
5. Coil the strip into a tight snail, tucking the tail under; rest 10 min. Repeat all.
6. Roll each coil gently to 15 cm — pressing, not grinding, to keep layers alive.
7. Cook on a medium-hot tawa or pan: 90 seconds, flip, brush with oil, 90 seconds, flip again pressing with a cloth until gold-brown and flaky. Off heat, scrunch each paratha between both hands — the layers shatter open.

## Plating & Service
Serve scrunched and steaming in a basket — the ruffles catch dal and curry like nothing else.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Ghee gives the best flavour if dairy is welcome. Stack extras with parchment and freeze; revive on a dry pan, 90 seconds a side.
