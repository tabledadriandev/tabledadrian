# Vegetable Pulao with Whole Garam Masala & Crisped Onions

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A one-pot festive pilaf — vegetables and basmati steamed together over whole spices, finished with fried onions and toasted cashews.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- basmati rice, rinsed, soaked 20 min — 300 g
- vegetable oil — 40 ml
- cumin seeds — 5 g
- cinnamon — 1 stick
- green cardamom — 4
- cloves — 3
- bay leaf — 1
- onion, sliced — 150 g
- garlic-ginger paste — 20 g
- carrot, small dice — 100 g
- green beans, 2 cm — 100 g
- peas — 80 g
- cauliflower, small florets — 120 g
- water — 470 ml
- fine sea salt — 8 g
- mint leaves — 10 g
- cashews, toasted — 30 g
- fried onions — 25 g

## Method
1. Heat oil; bloom cumin, cinnamon, cardamom, cloves and bay 30 seconds.
2. Add sliced onion; fry 8 min to gold. Add garlic-ginger, 90 seconds.
3. Add carrot, beans and cauliflower; sauté 3 min.
4. Add drained rice; fold 90 seconds to coat.
5. Add water, salt and peas; boil until steam holes appear, 4–5 min.
6. Lid on, minimum heat, 10 min; rest off-heat 8 min.
7. Fluff; fold in mint, top with cashews and fried onions.

## Plating & Service
A generous platter with raita and papad turns this into lunch on its own; otherwise the reliable plus-one to any main aboard.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap vegetables with the market — fennel and courgette are excellent here. Add 0.2 g saffron in 20 ml milk for occasion service (adds dairy).
