# Red Curry of Roast Duck, Lychee & Thai Basil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bangkok banquet classic — crisp duck breast against a scratch red curry with bursts of lychee and grape tomato; luxury that comes together fast once the paste is made.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- duck breasts — 2 (about 600 g)
- dried long red chillies, seeded, soaked — 12
- red bird's-eye chillies — 5 g
- shallots — 50 g
- garlic — 25 g
- galangal — 15 g
- lemongrass, sliced — 25 g
- coriander root — 10 g
- makrut lime zest — 3 g
- shrimp paste, roasted — 8 g
- coconut cream — 200 ml
- coconut milk — 400 ml
- fish sauce — 30 ml
- palm sugar — 25 g
- lychees, peeled (fresh or drained tinned) — 150 g
- cherry tomatoes — 120 g
- makrut lime leaves — 4
- Thai basil — 20 g

## Method
1. Pound the red paste: chillies and salt first, then lemongrass, galangal, coriander root, lime zest, garlic, shallots, finishing with shrimp paste — smooth and uniform.
2. Score duck skin; start in a cold pan, skin-down, over medium heat. Render 8–10 min until the skin is deep gold, flip 2 min, then roast at 180 °C to 54 °C core, about 6 min. Rest 8 min.
3. Crack the coconut cream in a wok 5 min until oil separates; fry 4 tbsp of paste 3–4 min.
4. Add coconut milk, torn lime leaves, fish sauce and palm sugar; simmer 8 min to a light, red-slicked sauce.
5. Add lychees and tomatoes; simmer 3 min until the tomatoes just split.
6. Slice the rested duck thickly and lay into the sauce off the boil; 1 min only, to keep it rosé.
7. Fold in basil; check balance — salty, then sweet, gentle sour from tomato.

## Plating & Service
Fan the duck over jasmine rice, sauce and fruit spooned around, basil and fine red chilli on top — charter show-stopper.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pineapple replaces lychee happily; confit duck legs (shredded) or grilled tofu for other services. Extra paste freezes flat in bags.
