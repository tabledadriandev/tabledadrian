# Blistered Yogurt Naan, Cast-Iron Method

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Tandoor results from an upside-down cast-iron pan — a slack yogurt dough slapped onto screaming metal until it bubbles, chars and puffs.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- strong white flour — 350 g
- fine sea salt — 7 g
- caster sugar — 8 g
- instant yeast — 4 g
- yogurt — 90 g
- warm water — 160 ml
- vegetable oil — 15 ml
- ghee or butter, melted — 40 g
- nigella seeds — 4 g
- garlic, fine-grated (optional) — 10 g
- coriander leaves — 10 g

## Method
1. Mix flour, salt, sugar, yeast; add yogurt, water and oil. Knead 8 min to a soft, slightly sticky dough.
2. Prove 60–90 min to double (or overnight in the fridge for better flavour).
3. Divide into 6; ball, rest 15 min under a cloth.
4. Heat a cast-iron pan over maximum flame 5 min — properly ferocious.
5. Roll each ball to a 3–4 mm teardrop; wet one face and press nigella seeds into the other.
6. Slap the wet face onto the pan; it glues. Cook 60–90 seconds until bubbled, then invert the whole pan over the flame (or flash under a 280 °C grill) 30–60 seconds to char the top.
7. Brush immediately with ghee (garlic-infused if using) and scatter coriander.

## Plating & Service
Stack in a cloth-lined basket, ghee-side gleaming; serve within 15 minutes of the pan.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fold grated cheese + chilli inside before rolling for a stuffed version; oven method: 280 °C on a preheated steel, 3 min. Vegan with coconut yogurt and oil.
