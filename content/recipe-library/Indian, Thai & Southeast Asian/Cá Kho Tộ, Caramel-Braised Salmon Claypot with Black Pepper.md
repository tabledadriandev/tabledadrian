# Cá Kho Tộ, Caramel-Braised Salmon Claypot with Black Pepper

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Vietnam's bittersweet fish-sauce caramel turns salmon steaks mahogany — a small, intense claypot dish that makes rice essential.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- salmon steaks, bone-in if possible, 3 cm — 700 g
- caster sugar — 50 g
- water — 30 ml + 150 ml
- fish sauce — 50 ml
- shallots, sliced — 50 g
- garlic, sliced — 15 g
- ginger, julienne — 20 g
- spring onions, whites and greens separated — 40 g
- black pepper, coarse — 3 g
- bird's-eye chilli, whole — 2
- coconut water (or water + 5 g sugar) — 150 ml
- vegetable oil — 15 ml

## Method
1. Make nước màu: melt sugar with 30 ml water over medium heat, without stirring, to a deep amber (170–175 °C); off heat, carefully add 150 ml water. Reset over low heat to dissolve.
2. Season salmon with 10 ml fish sauce; rest 10 min.
3. In a claypot or heavy casserole, warm oil; soften shallots, garlic, ginger and spring onion whites 2 min.
4. Lay in the salmon; pour over the caramel, remaining fish sauce and coconut water — liquid should come halfway up.
5. Simmer uncovered over low heat 20–25 min, spooning the sauce over every 5 min, never flipping.
6. Add whole chillies and half the pepper for the last 5 min; the sauce should be reduced, dark and syrupy.
7. Rest 5 min; finish with remaining pepper and spring onion greens.

## Plating & Service
Serve in the pot, bubbling, with mountains of jasmine rice and a sharp cucumber salad — spoon the black-gold sauce over everything.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Traditional with catfish or mackerel; pork ribs braise the same way (60 min). Watch the caramel colour — blond is bland, black is bitter.
