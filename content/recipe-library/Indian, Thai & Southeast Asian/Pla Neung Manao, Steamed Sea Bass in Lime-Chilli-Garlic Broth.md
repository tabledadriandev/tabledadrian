# Pla Neung Manao, Steamed Sea Bass in Lime-Chilli-Garlic Broth

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Whole steamed bass electrified by a raw lime-garlic-chilli dressing poured over at the last second — clean, fierce, and stunning at the table.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- whole sea bass, gutted, scored — 1.2 kg (or 4 fillets, 140 g each)
- lemongrass, bruised — 30 g
- lime juice — 80 ml
- fish sauce — 50 ml
- garlic, fine-chopped — 25 g
- bird's-eye chillies, fine-chopped — 8–12 g
- palm sugar — 15 g
- chicken stock, hot — 150 ml
- coriander, chopped — 15 g
- Chinese celery or coriander stems — 15 g
- lime, thin slices — 1

## Method
1. Stuff the fish cavity with lemongrass; lay on a heatproof plate that fits your steamer.
2. Steam over rolling boil: whole fish 12–15 min (fillets 6–7 min) until the flesh flakes at the bone (58–60 °C at the spine).
3. Meanwhile dissolve palm sugar in the hot stock.
4. Stir in fish sauce, then lime juice, then garlic and chillies — raw, so their heat stays bright and green-tasting.
5. Taste the sauce: sharply sour, then salty, faint sweetness; it should make you blink.
6. Transfer the fish with its juices to a deep serving plate; pour the hot broth-dressing over.
7. Shower with coriander, celery leaves and lime slices; serve within minutes.

## Plating & Service
Serve centrepiece-style with jasmine rice; spoon broth over each portion — guests will drink what's left.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Any pristine white fish or even shelled prawns (steam 4 min) work; halve the chilli for delicate palates but never skimp the lime.
