# Gado-Gado Platter, Charred Vegetables & Roasted Peanut Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Indonesia's great composed salad — blanched and charred vegetables, tofu, tempeh and jammy eggs under a warm, tamarind-sharpened peanut sauce.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- roasted peanuts — 150 g
- garlic — 10 g
- bird's-eye chilli — 3 g
- palm sugar — 25 g
- tamarind paste — 20 g
- kecap manis — 25 ml
- coconut milk — 150 ml
- fine sea salt — 6 g
- eggs — 4
- tempeh, sliced — 200 g
- firm tofu, sliced — 200 g
- green beans — 150 g
- white cabbage, wedges — 200 g
- cucumber, batons — 150 g
- bean sprouts — 100 g
- new potatoes, boiled, halved — 250 g
- vegetable oil — 40 ml
- prawn crackers or emping (optional) — 40 g

## Method
1. Blend peanuts, garlic, chilli, palm sugar, tamarind, kecap manis, coconut milk, salt and 100 ml water; warm in a pan 5 min to a pourable sauce — it thickens as it sits.
2. Boil eggs 7 min; shock and halve.
3. Pan-fry tempeh and tofu in the oil until golden on both faces, 6–8 min; salt lightly.
4. Blanch beans 2 min and sprouts 20 seconds; shock both.
5. Char the cabbage wedges cut-side down in the tempeh pan, 3 min.
6. Arrange everything in groups on a platter — colour-blocked, not tossed.
7. Sauce half over, half in a jug; crackers on the side.

## Plating & Service
Serve at room temperature — it's a composed landscape, dressed at the table so nothing sogs.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Any vegetable that blanches works: asparagus and fennel are excellent Riviera stand-ins. Omit crackers/use tamari for strict gluten-free; egg-free version stays hearty via tempeh.
