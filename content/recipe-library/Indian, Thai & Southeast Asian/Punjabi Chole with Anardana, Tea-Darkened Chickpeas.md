# Punjabi Chole with Anardana, Tea-Darkened Chickpeas

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Street-style chole gets its signature dark colour from a tea bag in the simmer and its sour edge from pomegranate powder — no cream, all character.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- dried chickpeas, soaked overnight — 300 g (or 700 g cooked)
- black tea bag — 1
- vegetable oil — 40 ml
- cumin seeds — 5 g
- onions, finely chopped — 250 g
- garlic-ginger paste — 25 g
- tomatoes, chopped — 300 g
- ground coriander — 8 g
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 5 g
- anardana (dried pomegranate) powder — 6 g
- garam masala — 4 g
- amchoor (dry mango powder) — 3 g
- green chilli, slit — 1
- fine sea salt — 10 g

## Method
1. Simmer soaked chickpeas with the tea bag and 5 g salt in fresh water for 45–60 min until completely tender; discard tea bag, reserve 300 ml cooking liquor.
2. Heat oil in a wide pan; sizzle cumin seeds 20 seconds.
3. Add onions; cook 10–12 min to a deep brown — darker than usual, this is the flavour base.
4. Add garlic-ginger paste, 2 min; then coriander and chilli powder, blooming 20 seconds.
5. Add tomatoes and cook 8 min until the oil separates.
6. Add chickpeas, liquor, anardana, amchoor and remaining salt; simmer 15 min, crushing a few chickpeas to thicken.
7. Finish with garam masala and the slit green chilli; rest 5 min off the heat.

## Plating & Service
Serve with bhatura or steamed rice, raw onion rings, lemon and coriander — proper Delhi street energy in the saloon.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add spinach in the last 5 min for chole-palak; a spoon of yogurt on top for non-vegans. Tinned chickpeas: simmer 25 min with the tea bag.
