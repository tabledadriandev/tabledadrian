# Bò Lúc Lắc, Shaking Beef with Watercress & Lime-Pepper Dip

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Cubed fillet seared hard and 'shaken' in a garlicky soy-butter glaze, tumbled over peppery watercress — French technique meeting Saigon flavour, very much this chef's lane.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- beef fillet or sirloin, 3 cm cubes — 700 g
- garlic, minced — 20 g
- oyster sauce — 25 g
- light soy sauce — 20 ml
- fish sauce — 10 ml
- caster sugar — 12 g
- dark soy sauce — 5 ml
- vegetable oil — 30 ml
- butter (or 15 ml oil) — 20 g
- red onion, petals — 100 g
- watercress — 120 g
- cherry tomatoes, halved — 100 g
- lime juice — 30 ml
- black pepper, coarse — 3 g
- flaky salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Marinate beef 15 min in oyster sauce, both soys, fish sauce and half the sugar.
2. Mix the dip: lime juice, coarse pepper, flaky salt and remaining sugar in a saucer.
3. Heat a wok or heavy pan to smoking with the oil; add beef in one layer. Leave 60 seconds to crust.
4. Shake and toss 90 seconds more — cubes stay pink inside (about 50 °C core).
5. Add onion petals and garlic; toss 45 seconds.
6. Butter in; shake to a gloss, 20 seconds, and out of the pan immediately.
7. Arrange watercress and tomatoes on a platter; tumble the beef and pan glaze over.

## Plating & Service
Serve with jasmine rice or crusty baguette, the lime-pepper dip alongside — each cube gets a dunk.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Venison or portobello-and-tofu take the same glaze; drop butter for fully dairy-free. Sear in two batches beyond 4 covers.
