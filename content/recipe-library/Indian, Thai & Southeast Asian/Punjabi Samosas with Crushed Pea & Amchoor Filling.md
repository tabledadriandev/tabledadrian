# Punjabi Samosas with Crushed Pea & Amchoor Filling

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Proper short, blistered samosa pastry — the ajwain-flecked dough is half the dish — around a chunky potato-pea filling sharpened with dry mango.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- plain flour — 250 g
- fine semolina — 25 g
- ajwain (carom) seeds — 3 g
- vegetable oil (for dough) — 55 ml
- cold water — about 90 ml
- fine sea salt — 8 g
- potatoes, boiled, broken (not mashed) — 450 g
- peas — 120 g
- cumin seeds — 5 g
- coriander seeds, crushed — 5 g
- ginger, minced — 15 g
- green chilli, minced — 1
- amchoor (dry mango powder) — 5 g
- garam masala — 3 g
- coriander leaves — 15 g
- oil for deep frying — 1 L

## Method
1. Dough: rub 55 ml oil into flour, semolina, ajwain and 4 g salt until it clumps when squeezed; add water gradually to a stiff dough. Rest 30 min under a cloth.
2. Filling: sizzle cumin and crushed coriander in 20 ml oil; add ginger and chilli, 1 min; fold in potatoes, peas, amchoor, garam masala, remaining salt. Cook 3 min; cool completely; add coriander leaves.
3. Divide dough into 6; roll each to an 18 cm oval, halve into two D-shapes.
4. Brush the straight edge with water; fold into a cone, sealing the seam firmly.
5. Fill two-thirds, pleat and press the top shut.
6. Fry from a COLD-ish start: oil at 150 °C, 8–10 min until pale and blistered, then raise to 180 °C for 2–3 min to deep gold — this two-stage fry makes the crust flaky, not bubbly.
7. Drain on a rack, never paper.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm with tamarind and coriander chutneys; they hold 2 hours and re-crisp at 160 °C for 6 min.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 50 g crumbled paneer or keema to the filling for variants; bake-at-200 °C version works (brushed with oil, 25 min) but loses the classic blister.
