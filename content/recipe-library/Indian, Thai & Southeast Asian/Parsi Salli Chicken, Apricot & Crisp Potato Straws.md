# Parsi Salli Chicken, Apricot & Crisp Potato Straws

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sweet-sour Parsi comfort: chicken braised with apricots and vinegar, showered with shoestring potato crunch — a guaranteed table-silencer.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- chicken thighs, bone-in, skinless — 900 g
- dried apricots — 100 g
- cider vinegar — 30 ml
- jaggery or brown sugar — 20 g
- vegetable oil — 40 ml
- onions, finely chopped — 300 g
- garlic-ginger paste — 30 g
- cumin seeds — 4 g
- cinnamon stick — 1 (5 cm)
- ground coriander — 8 g
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 6 g
- ground turmeric — 3 g
- tomatoes, chopped — 200 g
- waxy potato, fine julienne — 250 g
- fine sea salt — 12 g

## Method
1. Soak apricots in 150 ml hot water. Rinse potato julienne, dry thoroughly, and fry in 180 °C oil until golden and crisp, 3–4 min; salt lightly (or use an air fryer at 200 °C, 12 min, tossed in 10 ml oil).
2. Heat oil; sizzle cumin and cinnamon 30 seconds, add onions, cook 12 min to deep gold.
3. Add garlic-ginger paste 2 min; bloom coriander, chilli and turmeric 20 seconds.
4. Add tomatoes; cook 6 min. Add chicken and salt; sear in the masala 5 min.
5. Add apricots with their soaking water plus 100 ml more; cover, simmer 25 min.
6. Uncover; add vinegar and jaggery, and reduce 8–10 min until the gravy is thick and glossy with a sweet-sour balance.
7. Rest 5 min; pile the salli (potato straws) on at the very last second so they stay crisp.

## Plating & Service
Serve straight away, straws towering, with steamed rice or rotli — the contrast of crisp and braise is the dish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Lamb version braises 80 min; prunes can replace apricots. For low-carb, top with crisped shallots instead of potato.
