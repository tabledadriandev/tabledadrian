# Tôm Rim, Caramelised Black Pepper Prawns in Sandpot Glaze

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Ten-minute Vietnamese home cooking — shell-on prawns tossed in a fish-sauce caramel until sticky, with an aggressive crack of black pepper.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- raw king prawns, shell-on, legs trimmed — 700 g
- caster sugar — 40 g
- water — 25 ml
- fish sauce — 35 ml
- shallots, minced — 40 g
- garlic, minced — 15 g
- spring onions, sliced — 30 g
- black pepper, coarse-cracked — 4 g
- bird's-eye chilli, sliced — 1
- vegetable oil — 20 ml
- lime — 1

## Method
1. Dry the prawns thoroughly — moisture fights the glaze.
2. Make caramel: sugar and water to amber (170 °C); off heat, whisk in fish sauce (it will spit) and 30 ml water.
3. Heat oil in a wide pan over high heat; soften shallots and garlic 60 seconds.
4. Add prawns in one layer; sear 90 seconds per side until the shells blush and char at the edges.
5. Pour in the caramel; toss constantly 2–3 min as it reduces to a sticky lacquer that clings to every shell.
6. Add pepper and chilli; toss 30 seconds.
7. Finish with spring onions and a hard squeeze of lime.

## Plating & Service
Serve shell-on with rice, finger bowls and napkins — sucking the glaze off the shells is the point.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Peeled prawns save mess but lose depth (cut caramel time by 1 min). Same glaze works on seared scallops or chicken wings.
