# Aloo Paratha, Green Chilli Potato-Stuffed Flatbread

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Punjabi breakfast king — a spiced potato heart rolled inside wholemeal dough without blowouts; the trick is a dry filling and confident rolling.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- chapati flour (atta) — 350 g
- fine sea salt — 7 g
- water — 200 ml
- potatoes, boiled, riced, fully cooled — 400 g
- green chillies, minced — 2
- ginger, grated — 12 g
- coriander leaves, chopped — 15 g
- cumin seeds, toasted — 4 g
- amchoor — 4 g
- garam masala — 2 g
- fine sea salt (filling) — 5 g
- ghee or oil for cooking — 50 g

## Method
1. Knead flour, salt and water to a soft dough; rest 30 min.
2. Mix riced potato with chillies, ginger, coriander, cumin, amchoor, garam masala and filling salt. It must be dry and cool — moisture is the enemy.
3. Divide dough into 8, filling into 8 balls (about 50 g each).
4. Roll a dough ball to 10 cm; set a filling ball in the centre, gather the edges up like a money-bag and pinch shut, tearing off excess dough at the knot.
5. Flatten seam-side down; dust and roll gently and evenly to 16 cm — rotate constantly, never forcing.
6. Cook on a medium-hot tawa: 60 seconds dry, flip, brush with ghee, 60–90 seconds, flip and ghee again, pressing the edges until both faces are brown-spotted and it puffs.
7. Rest 1 min under cloth.

## Plating & Service
Serve hot with thick yogurt, white butter or lime pickle — breakfast on deck that outclasses any pastry basket.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Grated paneer-and-mint or spiced cauliflower (squeezed dry) stuff the same way. Use oil throughout for vegan.
