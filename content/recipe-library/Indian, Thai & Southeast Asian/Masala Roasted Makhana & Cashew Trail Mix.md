# Masala Roasted Makhana & Cashew Trail Mix

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Fox nuts roasted in ghee with turmeric, chilli and chaat masala — a crunchy, absurdly moreish deck snack that's lighter than any crisp.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- makhana (fox nuts / popped lotus seeds) — 150 g
- cashews — 80 g
- ghee or coconut oil — 30 g
- ground turmeric — 3 g
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 4 g
- chaat masala — 5 g
- fine sea salt — 5 g
- curry leaves — 15
- black pepper, fine — 1 g
- icing sugar (optional, for balance) — 3 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 160 °C (or use a wide dry pan on low).
2. Toss makhana with half the melted ghee; roast 10–12 min, shaking once, until they snap cleanly between fingers.
3. Toast cashews alongside for the last 6 min.
4. Crisp the curry leaves in the remaining ghee, 30 seconds; reserve leaves, keep the ghee.
5. Toss hot makhana and cashews with the curry-leaf ghee, turmeric, chilli, salt, pepper and icing sugar.
6. Add chaat masala and the crisp curry leaves; toss again.
7. Cool 10 min — they crisp further as they cool.

## Plating & Service
Serve in small copper bowls with drinks; keeps a week airtight, so make a big batch on prep day.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan with coconut oil; add roasted chana or coconut chips to stretch it. Skip sugar entirely for strict low-carb.
