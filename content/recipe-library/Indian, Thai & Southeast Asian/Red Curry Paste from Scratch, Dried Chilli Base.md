# Red Curry Paste from Scratch, Dried Chilli Base

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The backbone paste of the Thai repertoire — dried-chilli depth rather than fresh-chilli fire; the base for red, panang and countless marinades.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- dried long red chillies (guajillo work), seeded — 30 g
- dried bird's-eye chillies (optional heat) — 3 g
- fine sea salt — 5 g
- white peppercorns — 3 g
- coriander seeds, toasted — 6 g
- cumin seeds, toasted — 3 g
- lemongrass, sliced — 45 g
- galangal — 20 g
- coriander roots — 15 g
- makrut lime zest — 4 g
- garlic — 35 g
- shallots — 60 g
- shrimp paste, roasted — 12 g

## Method
1. Soak all dried chillies in hot water 20 min; drain hard and chop.
2. Grind the toasted seeds and peppercorns to powder.
3. Pound chillies with salt to a smooth red pulp, 4–5 min.
4. Lemongrass in; pound smooth. Then galangal, coriander root, lime zest in sequence.
5. Garlic and shallots; pound to a glossy paste.
6. Ground spices and shrimp paste last; work until uniform.
7. Rest 10 min. The colour should be deep brick, the smell loud from across the galley.

## Plating & Service
60 g per curry for four; also the base for tod mun fish cakes, satay sauce and grilled-chicken marinades.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chilled 10 days under oil, frozen 3 months. Guajillo + a few árbol chillies replicate Thai chillies convincingly on the Riviera.
