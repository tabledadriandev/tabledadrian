# Malai Kofta, Cashew-Saffron Velouté

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Paneer-potato dumplings poached in a saffron-cashew sauce with EMP-level refinement — the koftas are pan-seared, not deep-fried, so they stay light.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- paneer, grated — 250 g
- potato, boiled and riced — 200 g
- cornflour — 25 g
- raisins, chopped — 20 g
- fine sea salt — 9 g
- ghee — 40 g
- cashews — 80 g
- onion, chopped — 150 g
- garlic-ginger paste — 20 g
- green cardamom pods — 4
- tomato passata — 200 g
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 4 g
- saffron threads — 0.3 g
- double cream — 80 ml
- kasoori methi — 2 g
- honey — 10 g

## Method
1. Soak cashews in 200 ml hot water 20 min; steep saffron in 30 ml warm water.
2. Mix paneer, potato, cornflour, raisins and 4 g salt; roll into 12 tight balls. Chill 20 min.
3. Sear koftas in 20 g ghee over medium heat, rolling gently, 5–6 min to an even gold. Set aside.
4. In the same pan, heat remaining ghee with cardamom; cook onion 8 min to pale gold, add garlic-ginger paste for 2 min.
5. Bloom chilli powder 20 seconds, add passata, cook 6 min. Blend with cashews and their water until completely smooth; sieve for silk.
6. Return sauce to the pan; add saffron water, honey, remaining salt and 150 ml water. Simmer 8 min to a light coating consistency.
7. Add cream and crushed methi; lower koftas in and warm 3–4 min without boiling.

## Plating & Service
Three koftas per bowl, sauce flooded around, a thread of cream, saffron strands and micro coriander on top — proper dinner-party plating.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: firm tofu + potato koftas, coconut cream, maple instead of honey. Sauce doubles beautifully for grilled vegetables.
