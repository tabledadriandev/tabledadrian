# Black Sticky Rice Pudding, Coconut Cream & Palm Sugar Caramel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Purple-black glutinous rice simmered to a chewy, nutty porridge under a cap of salted coconut cream and a dark palm-sugar caramel — humble and hypnotic.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- black glutinous rice, rinsed, soaked 2 h — 250 g
- water — 1.1 L
- pandan leaf, knotted — 1
- palm sugar — 110 g
- fine sea salt — 4 g
- coconut cream — 200 ml
- rice flour — 4 g
- extra palm sugar (caramel) — 40 g
- toasted coconut flakes — 15 g

## Method
1. Simmer the drained rice with the water and pandan, part-covered, 50–60 min, stirring occasionally, until the grains burst open and the liquid turns violet and porridge-thick.
2. Add 110 g palm sugar and 2 g salt; simmer 5 min. It should be loose — it sets firmly as it cools.
3. Salted cream: warm the coconut cream with 2 g salt and the slaked rice flour; 1 min to a pouring sauce.
4. Caramel: melt 40 g palm sugar with 10 ml water to a dark, smoky syrup, 2–3 min.
5. Bowl the warm rice.
6. Flood the coconut cream over one side.
7. Ribbon the caramel; finish with toasted coconut.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm in winter, chilled in summer — it holds both moods; the salt line in the cream is non-negotiable.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add sliced banana between rice and cream for the Thai-street version. Keeps 3 days chilled; loosen with coconut milk on reheat.
