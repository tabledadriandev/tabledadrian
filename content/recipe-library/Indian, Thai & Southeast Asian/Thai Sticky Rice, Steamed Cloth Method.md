# Thai Sticky Rice, Steamed Cloth Method

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Proper glutinous rice — soaked, then steamed over cloth, never boiled — chewy, glossy and made for pinching into balls alongside larb, nam tok and grilled birds.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Thai glutinous (sticky) rice — 350 g
- water for soaking — 1 L
- muslin or clean cotton cloth — 1
- water for steaming — 1.5 L

## Method
1. Soak the rice in cold water 4 h minimum, ideally overnight (short-cut: 2 h in warm 40 °C water). No salt — sticky rice is seasoned by what it accompanies.
2. Drain; rinse once gently.
3. Line a steamer basket with damp muslin; spread the rice in an even 3 cm layer with a well in the centre for steam flow.
4. Steam over fully boiling water, covered, 15 min.
5. Flip the entire rice cake over on the cloth — this is the step everyone skips and shouldn't.
6. Steam 10–12 min more until uniformly translucent and chewy with no chalky centre.
7. Turn into a bamboo basket or bowl lined with the cloth; keep covered — it stays supple for 2 hours.

## Plating & Service
Serve in small lidded baskets or cloth bundles; guests pinch off thumb-sized pieces and press them into sauces — no fork required.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> For mango sticky rice, fold the hot rice with sweetened salted coconut milk (150 ml per 350 g). Never refrigerate uncovered; it turns to stone.
