# Mango Sticky Rice, Salted Coconut Cream & Toasted Mung Beans

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Khao niaow ma muang, done with discipline — warm seasoned sticky rice, cold ripe mango, a salted coconut sauce, and the traditional toasted-mung crunch.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Thai glutinous rice, soaked 4 h — 300 g
- coconut milk — 300 ml
- caster sugar — 70 g
- fine sea salt — 5 g
- pandan leaf (optional) — 1
- ripe mangoes (Nam Dok Mai or Alphonso) — 2 large
- coconut cream (topping) — 120 ml
- rice flour — 4 g
- split mung beans (or sesame seeds) — 20 g

## Method
1. Steam the soaked rice over cloth 15 min; flip the cake, 10 min more, until translucent and chewy.
2. Meanwhile warm 300 ml coconut milk with sugar, 3 g salt and the pandan — do not boil.
3. Fold the hot rice into the sweet coconut milk; cover and rest 20 min — it drinks everything and turns glossy.
4. Topping: warm the coconut cream with 2 g salt and the rice flour slaked in 10 ml water; simmer 1 min to a light, salty-sweet sauce.
5. Toast the mung beans in a dry pan 3–4 min to gold and crackle.
6. Slice the mango cheeks and fan.
7. Plate warm rice beside cold mango; sauce over the rice, mung beans over the sauce.

## Plating & Service
The temperature contrast — warm rice, chilled fruit, salted cream — is the dish; assemble at the last moment.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The soaked rice can be steamed ahead and re-steamed 5 min to order. Off-season: caramelised pineapple stands in for mango with honour.
