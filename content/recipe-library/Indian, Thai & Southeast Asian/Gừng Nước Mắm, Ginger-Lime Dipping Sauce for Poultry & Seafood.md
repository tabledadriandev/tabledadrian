# Gừng Nước Mắm, Ginger-Lime Dipping Sauce for Poultry & Seafood

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The sharper, ginger-loaded cousin of nước chấm — traditionally for poached chicken and steamed shellfish, where its heat and perfume cut cleanly.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- ginger, very fresh, minced to a pulp — 40 g
- garlic, minced — 8 g
- bird's-eye chilli, minced — 5 g
- fish sauce — 50 ml
- lime juice — 45 ml
- caster sugar — 35 g
- warm water — 60 ml
- coriander stems, minced — 5 g

## Method
1. Pound the ginger with the sugar to a wet, fragrant pulp — pounding releases far more than mincing alone.
2. Add garlic and chilli; bruise together 30 seconds.
3. Dissolve with the warm water.
4. Add fish sauce, then lime juice.
5. Taste — this sauce runs hotter, sharper and more gingery than table nước chấm by design.
6. Fold in coriander stems.
7. Rest 10 min; the ginger blooms.

## Plating & Service
Serve with poached or grilled chicken, steamed prawns, clams or oysters — a teaspoon per bite is plenty.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Keeps 3 days chilled. For hải sản service add 5 ml of the shellfish steaming liquor — it ties sauce to plate.
