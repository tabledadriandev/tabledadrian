# Thịt Kho Trứng, Caramel Pork Belly & Jammy Eggs in Coconut Water

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Vietnamese family braise — pork belly and eggs slowly lacquered in fish-sauce caramel and coconut water until sticky, deeply savoury and faintly sweet.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- pork belly, skin on, 4 cm chunks — 1.2 kg
- eggs — 6
- caster sugar — 60 g
- water — 40 ml
- fish sauce — 70 ml
- coconut water — 700 ml
- shallots, halved — 80 g
- garlic, crushed — 20 g
- black peppercorns, cracked — 3 g
- bird's-eye chillies, whole — 2
- spring onion, sliced — 20 g
- fine sea salt — 4 g

## Method
1. Blanch pork 3 min in boiling water; rinse (keeps the braise clear).
2. Boil eggs 7 min, shock, peel.
3. Make the caramel: sugar and 40 ml water to deep amber (170 °C); off heat, add 60 ml warm water carefully.
4. In a casserole, toss pork with the caramel over medium heat 5 min until every piece is bronzed.
5. Add fish sauce, coconut water, shallots, garlic, pepper, salt; the liquid should nearly cover.
6. Simmer, part-covered, 90 min, skimming fat occasionally, until the pork is spoon-tender.
7. Add eggs and chillies for the final 20 min, rolling them for even colour; reduce until the sauce is a loose syrup.

## Plating & Service
Serve in a deep bowl, eggs halved to show the set yolks, sauce spooned over rice; pickled bean sprouts or cucumber cut the richness.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Even better next day — make on prep day and reheat gently. Chicken legs (45 min) or jackfruit + tofu (30 min) take the same caramel.
