# Chettinad Black Pepper Chicken, Roasted Spice Masala

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Fiery, dark and deeply aromatic — the freshly roasted-and-ground masala is the whole point, delivering heat that builds rather than burns.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- chicken thighs, bone-in, skinless — 900 g
- black peppercorns — 8 g
- fennel seeds — 6 g
- coriander seeds — 10 g
- cumin seeds — 4 g
- dried red chillies — 4
- star anise — 1
- desiccated coconut — 20 g
- vegetable oil — 45 ml
- onions, chopped — 250 g
- tomatoes, chopped — 200 g
- garlic-ginger paste — 30 g
- fresh curry leaves — 15
- ground turmeric — 3 g
- fine sea salt — 10 g

## Method
1. Dry-roast peppercorns, fennel, coriander, cumin, chillies and star anise in a pan over medium heat 3–4 min until fragrant and a shade darker; add coconut for the final minute. Cool and grind to a coarse powder.
2. Heat oil in a heavy casserole; fry curry leaves 20 seconds, then onions with a pinch of salt, 10 min to golden.
3. Add garlic-ginger paste; cook 2 min until raw smell fades.
4. Add turmeric and two-thirds of the ground masala; bloom 30 seconds in the oil.
5. Add tomatoes; cook 6–8 min until jammy and the oil separates.
6. Add chicken and salt, turning to coat; cook 5 min, then add 200 ml water, cover, and simmer 20 min.
7. Uncover, reduce until the sauce clings darkly to the chicken, and finish with the remaining masala for a fresh top note.

## Plating & Service
Serve on a rustic platter with lemon wedges, extra fried curry leaves and flaky parotta or plain rice to temper the heat.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Use prawns (reduce sauce first, add for final 4 min) or mushrooms and chickpeas for a vegan take; halve the peppercorns for milder palates.
