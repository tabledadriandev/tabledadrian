# Rasmalai-Style Poached Curd Dumplings in Cardamom Milk

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Fresh chenna kneaded to silk, poached into spongy discs, then steeped in reduced cardamom-saffron milk — the most delicate dessert in the Indian canon.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- whole milk (for chenna) — 1.5 L
- lemon juice — 45 ml
- caster sugar (poaching syrup) — 250 g
- water — 1 L
- whole milk (rabri) — 800 ml
- caster sugar (rabri) — 50 g
- green cardamom, ground — 5 pods
- saffron — 0.3 g
- pistachios + almonds, slivered — 30 g
- cornflour — 5 g
- fine sea salt — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Make chenna: boil 1.5 L milk, add lemon juice off the heat until it splits fully; strain through muslin, rinse briefly, hang 45 min — moist but not wet.
2. Knead the chenna with the cornflour and salt on a cold surface 8–10 min with the heel of the hand until completely smooth and faintly greasy.
3. Roll into 14 balls; flatten to 2 cm discs, no cracks.
4. Boil the syrup (250 g sugar, 1 L water) in a wide pan; slide in the discs, cover, and poach at a rolling simmer 15 min — they double. Cool in the syrup.
5. Rabri: reduce 800 ml milk by half, stirring, 25–30 min; add 50 g sugar, cardamom, saffron and half the nuts.
6. Gently press each disc between palms to remove syrup; steep in the warm rabri.
7. Chill 4 h minimum.

## Plating & Service
Two discs per shallow bowl, rabri flooded around, nuts and a saffron thread over — serve very cold.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The knead (step 2) decides sponge or rubber — commit to the full 8 minutes. Keeps 3 days chilled; flavour peaks on day two.
