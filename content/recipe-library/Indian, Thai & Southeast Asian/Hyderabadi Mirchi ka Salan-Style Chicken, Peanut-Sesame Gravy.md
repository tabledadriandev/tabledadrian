# Hyderabadi Mirchi ka Salan-Style Chicken, Peanut-Sesame Gravy

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The nutty, tangy salan gravy of Hyderabad — usually a chilli side dish — turned into a full chicken main with real depth from triple-toasted seeds.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- chicken thighs, boneless, halved — 700 g
- peanuts — 50 g
- sesame seeds — 25 g
- desiccated coconut — 20 g
- vegetable oil — 45 ml
- black mustard seeds — 4 g
- cumin seeds — 4 g
- curry leaves — 15
- onion, sliced — 200 g
- garlic-ginger paste — 30 g
- ground coriander — 8 g
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 5 g
- ground turmeric — 3 g
- tamarind paste — 20 g
- long green chillies, slit — 4
- fine sea salt — 10 g

## Method
1. Dry-toast peanuts 4 min, then sesame 2 min, then coconut 1 min, each until golden; blend with 150 ml water to a smooth paste.
2. Heat oil; crackle mustard seeds, add cumin and curry leaves, 20 seconds.
3. Add onion; cook 10 min to gold. Add garlic-ginger paste, 2 min.
4. Bloom coriander, chilli powder and turmeric 20 seconds; add chicken and salt, sear 5 min.
5. Add the nut paste and 250 ml water; simmer gently 15 min, stirring — nut gravies catch easily.
6. Add tamarind and the slit green chillies; simmer 8–10 min until the oil beads on the surface and the gravy is spoon-coating.
7. Rest 5 min; the salan should taste nutty first, sour second, hot third.

## Plating & Service
Serve with steamed rice or biryani leftovers; spoon the beaded oil over the top — it carries the aroma.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: swap chicken for whole baby aubergines (slit, fried, then simmered 15 min). Almonds can replace peanuts for allergy routing.
