# Smoky Baingan Bharta, Flame-Roasted Aubergine & Peas

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Aubergine roasted until collapsing over a direct flame gives this rustic mash its campfire soul — a stealth favourite even among meat eaters.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- large aubergines — 2 (about 700 g)
- vegetable oil — 35 ml
- cumin seeds — 5 g
- onion, finely chopped — 180 g
- garlic, minced — 15 g
- ginger, minced — 12 g
- green chillies, chopped — 2
- tomatoes, chopped — 250 g
- ground coriander — 6 g
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 4 g
- frozen peas — 100 g
- garam masala — 3 g
- coriander leaves — 15 g
- fine sea salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Prick aubergines and roast directly over a gas flame or under a 250 °C grill, turning, 15–20 min until the skin is blackened and the flesh fully soft.
2. Rest in a covered bowl 10 min, peel, and chop the smoky flesh roughly; keep any juices.
3. Heat oil; crackle cumin seeds 20 seconds, add onion and cook 8 min to gold.
4. Add garlic, ginger and chillies; 2 min more.
5. Bloom ground coriander and chilli powder 20 seconds, then add tomatoes; cook 6–8 min until jammy.
6. Fold in aubergine and its juices with salt; cook 8 min, mashing coarsely, until it darkens and the oil re-emerges at the edge.
7. Stir through peas for 3 min, finish with garam masala and coriander leaves.

## Plating & Service
Serve hot in a shallow bowl with roti and a wedge of lime — or chilled next day as a dip with papad.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fold in a spoon of yogurt for creaminess (adds dairy); for extra smoke, tip a lit charcoal in a foil cup into the covered pan for 2 min (dhungar method).
