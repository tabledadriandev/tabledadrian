# Kerala Coconut Fish Moilee, Curry Leaf Crackle

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A gentle turmeric-coconut broth that flatters any firm white fish on board — Kerala backwaters by way of the Riviera fish market.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- firm white fish fillets (sea bass, cod, monkfish) — 600 g
- coconut milk — 400 ml
- coconut oil — 30 ml
- onion, sliced — 180 g
- ginger, julienned — 20 g
- garlic, sliced — 12 g
- green chillies, slit — 2
- fresh curry leaves — 20
- black mustard seeds — 4 g
- ground turmeric — 4 g
- black peppercorns, cracked — 2 g
- cherry tomatoes, halved — 120 g
- lime juice — 20 ml
- fine sea salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Season fish with 3 g salt and 2 g turmeric; rest 10 min.
2. Heat coconut oil in a wide sauté pan over medium-high; crackle mustard seeds 20 seconds, then add half the curry leaves — they should spit and crisp.
3. Add onion, ginger, garlic and chillies; cook 6 min until soft but not coloured.
4. Add remaining turmeric and cracked pepper, bloom 20 seconds, then pour in coconut milk and 100 ml water with remaining salt.
5. Simmer gently 5 min — never boil hard or the coconut milk splits.
6. Slide in fish and tomatoes; poach at a bare tremble (about 85 °C) for 6–8 min until just flaking.
7. Finish with lime juice and the rest of the curry leaves fried crisp in a teaspoon of hot oil.

## Plating & Service
Serve from the pan at table with steamed rice or appam, curry leaves scattered over the pale gold broth.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap fish for prawns (4 min poach) or halloumi-style paneer for vegetarian; add okra in step 4 for a fuller stew.
