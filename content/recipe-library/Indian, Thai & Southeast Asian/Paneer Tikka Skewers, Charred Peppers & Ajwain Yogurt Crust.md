# Paneer Tikka Skewers, Charred Peppers & Ajwain Yogurt Crust

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Cubes of paneer in a thick, ajwain-spiked yogurt masala, blasted under high heat until the crust chars — the vegetarian answer to tandoori that no one treats as a compromise.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- paneer, 3 cm cubes — 450 g
- thick Greek-style yogurt — 120 g
- gram flour, toasted — 15 g
- Kashmiri chilli powder — 8 g
- garam masala — 4 g
- ajwain seeds — 2 g
- ground turmeric — 2 g
- garlic-ginger paste — 20 g
- mustard oil — 20 ml
- lemon juice — 20 ml
- red pepper, 3 cm squares — 150 g
- red onion, petals — 120 g
- fine sea salt — 7 g
- chaat masala — 3 g

## Method
1. Toast the gram flour in a dry pan 2 min until nutty — it keeps the marinade clinging.
2. Whisk yogurt with gram flour, chilli powder, garam masala, ajwain, turmeric, garlic-ginger, mustard oil, lemon and salt into a thick paste.
3. Fold in paneer, peppers and onion; marinate 30 min–4 h.
4. Thread onto skewers, alternating paneer and vegetables.
5. Grill at 250 °C (top heat) or over hot coals 10–12 min, turning twice, until the crust chars at the corners.
6. Baste once with any leftover marinade halfway.
7. Rest 2 min; dust with chaat masala and more lemon.

## Plating & Service
Serve on the skewers over sliced red onion with mint chutney — charred edges facing the guest.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Halloumi chars even harder for a salty variant; tofu + coconut yogurt makes it vegan. Same marinade works on mushrooms and baby courgettes.
