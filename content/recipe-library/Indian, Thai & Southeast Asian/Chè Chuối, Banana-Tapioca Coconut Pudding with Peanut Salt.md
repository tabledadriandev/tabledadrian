# Chè Chuối, Banana-Tapioca Coconut Pudding with Peanut Salt

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Warm banana and pearl tapioca in coconut milk with a roasted-peanut-salt finish — Vietnamese chè logic: soft, warm, sweet, salty, crunchy in one spoon.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- small tapioca pearls — 60 g
- water — 600 ml
- coconut milk — 400 ml
- ripe bananas — 4
- caster or palm sugar — 60 g
- fine sea salt — 3 g
- pandan leaf (optional) — 1
- roasted peanuts, crushed — 40 g
- toasted sesame seeds — 8 g

## Method
1. Simmer the tapioca in the water, stirring, 12–15 min until the pearls are translucent with a pinpoint white centre.
2. Add coconut milk, sugar, 2 g salt and pandan; return to a gentle simmer.
3. Slice bananas into thick coins; fold in.
4. Simmer gently 5–6 min until the banana softens and the pudding thickens to a loose porridge.
5. Rest 5 min — the pearls finish clearing off the heat.
6. Mix the remaining 1 g salt into the crushed peanuts with the sesame.
7. Serve warm, peanut-sesame salt generously over.

## Plating & Service
Small warm bowls, crunch on top — or chill it and loosen with coconut milk for a hot-day version; both are canonical.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Taro or sweet potato cubes extend it; sago and tapioca are interchangeable here. Keeps 2 days chilled; the pearls firm but revive with gentle heat.
