# Bánh Mì Thịt Nướng, Grilled Pork Baguette with Pickles & Pâté Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The great sandwich — lemongrass-caramel pork, quick pickles, cucumber, coriander and a swipe of pâté in a shattering baguette. Every component earns its place.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- small baguettes, light and crisp — 4
- pork shoulder, 5 mm slices — 450 g
- lemongrass, minced — 25 g
- garlic, minced — 15 g
- fish sauce — 30 ml
- honey — 20 g
- dark soy sauce — 10 ml
- sesame oil — 8 ml
- black pepper — 2 g
- carrot, julienne — 150 g
- daikon, julienne — 150 g
- rice vinegar — 100 ml
- caster sugar — 60 g
- water — 100 ml
- smooth pork or mushroom pâté — 80 g
- cucumber, long ribbons — 100 g
- coriander sprigs — 20 g
- red chilli, sliced — 1
- Maggi-style seasoning or light soy — 10 ml

## Method
1. Pickle first: dissolve sugar in vinegar and water; submerge carrot and daikon 30 min minimum (keeps 2 weeks chilled).
2. Marinate pork 30 min–12 h: lemongrass, garlic, fish sauce, honey, dark soy, sesame oil, pepper.
3. Grill pork over high heat or a 240 °C plancha, 2–3 min per side, until caramelised at the edges.
4. Crisp the baguettes at 190 °C for 4 min; split lengthways, keeping a hinge.
5. Swipe pâté along one face.
6. Layer: pork, drained pickles, cucumber, coriander, chilli.
7. A few drops of Maggi-style seasoning down the middle; press gently shut.

## Plating & Service
Wrap the lower half in paper and serve while the bread still crackles — deck lunch, no plates required.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cold roast chicken or seared tofu versions are excellent; skip pâté and add avocado for a lighter build. Pickles and marinade are the non-negotiables.
