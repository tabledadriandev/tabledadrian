# Pad Kee Mao Talay, Drunken Noodles with Squid & Holy Basil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Late-night Bangkok in a wok — fat noodles, seafood, green peppercorns, a violent amount of basil and chilli. Loud, fast, unforgettable.

## Ingredients (serves 2)
- fresh wide rice noodles — 400 g
- squid, scored and sliced — 200 g
- raw prawns, peeled — 200 g
- garlic — 20 g
- bird's-eye chillies — 8 g
- long red chilli, sliced — 1
- green peppercorns, fresh strands or brined — 10 g
- baby corn, halved — 80 g
- holy basil (or Thai basil) — 30 g
- oyster sauce — 25 g
- light soy sauce — 15 ml
- fish sauce — 15 ml
- dark soy sauce — 8 ml
- sugar — 8 g
- vegetable oil — 40 ml

## Method
1. Pound garlic and bird's-eye chillies coarsely.
2. Heat the wok to smoking with the oil; fry the garlic-chilli 15 seconds.
3. Add squid and prawns; sear 60–90 seconds until barely opaque.
4. Add noodles, baby corn, peppercorns and the combined sauces with sugar.
5. Toss over full heat 90 seconds until the noodles char at the edges and the sauce clings.
6. Add the long red chilli; 20 seconds.
7. Off the heat, fold through the basil until it collapses into perfume.

## Plating & Service
One pan, one plate, no ceremony — serve blisteringly hot with lime on the side.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken or mushroom versions equal; gluten-free with tamari-style sauces. The name invites a cold lager alongside — oblige.
