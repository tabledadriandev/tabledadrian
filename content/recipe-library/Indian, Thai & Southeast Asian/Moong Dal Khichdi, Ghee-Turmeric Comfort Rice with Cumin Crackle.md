# Moong Dal Khichdi, Ghee-Turmeric Comfort Rice with Cumin Crackle

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rice and yellow moong cooked to a soft, savoury porridge with turmeric and a double ghee-cumin tadka — the dish every Indian grandmother prescribes, and rightly.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- basmati or short-grain rice, rinsed — 150 g
- yellow moong dal, rinsed — 150 g
- water — 1.1 L
- ground turmeric — 4 g
- ginger, minced — 15 g
- fine sea salt — 9 g
- ghee — 40 g
- cumin seeds — 6 g
- dried red chilli — 1
- asafoetida — 0.4 g
- black pepper, cracked — 1 g
- carrot + peas (optional) — 150 g
- lemon — 1
- coriander leaves — 10 g

## Method
1. Dry-toast the moong dal in the pan 2–3 min until faintly nutty.
2. Add rice, water, turmeric, ginger, salt (and vegetables if using).
3. Simmer, part-covered, 25–30 min, stirring occasionally, until rice and dal collapse into a loose, spoonable porridge. Add water freely — khichdi should flow.
4. First tadka: half the ghee with half the cumin, stirred in at 20 min.
5. Final tadka: remaining ghee heated until fragrant with remaining cumin, chilli, asafoetida and pepper; poured over just before serving.
6. Rest 5 min; it thickens — loosen with hot water to taste.
7. Lemon and coriander at the table.

## Plating & Service
Serve in deep bowls with yogurt, papad and lime pickle — the definitive recovery meal after a long charter day.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan with coconut oil; add roasted cauliflower for texture. It sets as it cools — always re-loosen with hot water, never more fat.
