# Walnut & Cinnamon Baklava Rolls, Burma Style

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Phyllo rolled around walnuts on a dowel, then scrunched like a curtain — the crinkles triple the crisp surface area and drink the syrup evenly; faster than layered baklava and just as impressive.

## Ingredients (serves 10, makes 20 rolls)
- Phyllo sheets — 300 g (about 15)
- Unsalted butter, clarified — 180 g
- Walnut halves — 280 g
- Sugar (filling) — 30 g
- Ground cinnamon — 4 g
- Fine sea salt — 1 g
- Sugar (syrup) — 300 g
- Water — 220 ml
- Honey — 40 g
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Rose water — 8 ml
- Pistachio dust — 20 g

## Method
1. Syrup: sugar, water, honey and lemon simmered 8 min (103 °C); add rose water off heat; cool fully.
2. Pulse walnuts with sugar, cinnamon and salt to coarse rubble.
3. Lay one phyllo sheet, butter lightly, and scatter 18 g walnut mix along the near edge.
4. Roll loosely around a thin dowel or wooden spoon handle; scrunch both ends toward the centre like an accordion, then slide off.
5. Pack rolls snugly in a buttered tray; brush tops heavily with remaining butter.
6. Bake at 175 °C for 35–40 min to deep gold.
7. Cool syrup over hot rolls; rest 3 h.

## Plating & Service
Two rolls per plate with pistachio dust and, for dinner service, a quenelle of thick yogurt whipped with honey — the tang against the syrup earns its place.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Hazelnut-chocolate filling (250 g hazelnuts + 30 g cocoa nibs) is the crowd-pleaser heresy. Keep 5 days airtight — they hold better than layered trays.
