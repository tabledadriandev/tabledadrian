# Pocket Pita, Steam-Puffed on a Baking Steel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A pita either balloons or it doesn't — even rolling, full hydration rest, and a 290 °C steel decide it; watch each one inflate like a paper lantern in 90 seconds.

## Ingredients (serves 8, makes 8)
- Strong flour — 400 g
- Wholemeal flour — 100 g
- Instant yeast — 6 g
- Sugar — 8 g
- Fine sea salt — 10 g
- Warm water (35 °C) — 330 ml
- Olive oil — 25 ml

## Method
1. Knead everything 8 min to a soft, slightly tacky dough; bulk prove 60–90 min until doubled.
2. Divide into 8 × 105 g balls; rest covered 20 min — relaxed gluten rolls evenly, and even thickness is what makes the pocket.
3. Heat a steel or upturned tray at maximum oven (280–290 °C) for 45 min.
4. Roll each ball to a 3–4 mm round on light flour, flipping once; no thin patches, no creases.
5. Rest rounds 10 min; bake one or two at a time directly on the steel, 60–90 seconds until fully ballooned, then 30 seconds more.
6. Stack inside a folded towel immediately — steam keeps them supple as they deflate.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm in the towel-lined basket. Halve for pockets, or keep whole for tearing over mezze.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> All-white flour puffs most reliably for beginners. Stovetop: a screaming cast-iron pan, 45 seconds a side, works at anchor when the oven is busy.
