# Crisp Kibbeh Torpedoes, Spiced Lamb & Pine Nuts

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The benchmark of a Levantine kitchen — a thin, crackling bulgur-lamb shell around a juicy hashweh; shape with iced hands and a wet thumb for walls under 4 mm.

## Ingredients (serves 6, makes 18)
- Fine bulgur (#1) — 250 g
- Lean lamb leg, twice-minced, ice cold — 350 g
- Onion, grated and squeezed dry — 80 g
- Fine sea salt — 12 g
- Kibbeh spice (allspice, cinnamon, black pepper 2:1:1) — 6 g
- Iced water — 60 ml
- Lamb shoulder, minced (filling) — 250 g
- Onions, finely diced (filling) — 150 g
- Pine nuts — 40 g
- Pomegranate molasses — 15 g
- Baharat — 4 g
- Neutral oil for deep-frying — 1500 ml

## Method
1. Filling: sweat diced onion in 20 ml oil at medium heat 8 min; add lamb shoulder, brown, season with baharat, 6 g salt, pomegranate molasses. Fold in pine nuts toasted at 160 °C for 6 min. Cool fully.
2. Shell: rinse bulgur, squeeze dry. Knead with cold lean lamb, grated onion, salt, kibbeh spice and iced water for 5 min to a smooth, sticky dough. Pulse half in a processor for finer bind, then re-knead together.
3. With wet hands roll 45 g balls. Drive a wet thumb in, rotating to thin the wall to 3–4 mm.
4. Fill each with 15 g cooled hashweh, pinch closed, and taper both ends to the classic torpedo.
5. Chill 30 min uncovered to set the surface.
6. Deep-fry at 175 °C in batches, 4–5 min, to deep mahogany; rest on a rack 2 min.

## Plating & Service
Stack on a warm platter with lemon wedges and a bowl of cold mint labneh for dipping. Serve within 10 min of frying.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Bake at 220 °C for 18 min brushed with oil for a lighter version. Pumpkin kibbeh (vegan): replace shell lamb with 300 g dry roasted pumpkin purée and add 40 g flour.
