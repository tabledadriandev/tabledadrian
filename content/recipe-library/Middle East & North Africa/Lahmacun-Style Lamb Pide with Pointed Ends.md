# Lahmacun-Style Lamb Pide with Pointed Ends

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The boat-shaped pide with a whisper-thin lamb topping — bake on a scorching stone so the pointed ends char while the middle stays supple enough to roll.

## Ingredients (serves 6, makes 6)
- Strong flour — 500 g
- Instant yeast — 6 g
- Sugar — 8 g
- Fine sea salt — 10 g
- Warm water (35 °C) — 300 ml
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Lamb mince — 350 g
- Onion, grated and squeezed — 100 g
- Red pepper, very fine dice — 80 g
- Tomato paste — 30 g
- Biber salçası — 20 g
- Pul biber — 6 g
- Ground cumin — 3 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 20 g
- Lemon wedges — 6

## Method
1. Knead flour, yeast, sugar, salt, water and oil 8 min to a soft, supple dough; bulk prove 60–90 min at ~26 °C until doubled.
2. Topping: mix raw lamb with onion, pepper, tomato paste, salça, pul biber, cumin, 6 g salt and half the parsley — it should spread like a paste; loosen with 20 ml water if needed.
3. Divide dough into 6; ball, rest 15 min. Heat a stone or steel at 280 °C (max oven) for 45 min.
4. Roll each ball to a 30 × 14 cm oval; spread 80 g topping thinly, leaving a 2 cm border; fold the long edges in and twist the ends to points.
5. Bake in pairs on the stone 6–8 min until the rim blisters and the lamb sizzles.
6. Brush rims with olive oil out of the oven.

## Plating & Service
Slice across in bands, parsley and lemon over the top. Serve with ezme and ayran-style yogurt drink for the full ocakbaşı experience.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Roll thinner with no folded rim for true lahmacun (bake 4 min). Beef works; add 10 g lamb fat or olive oil to keep it juicy.
