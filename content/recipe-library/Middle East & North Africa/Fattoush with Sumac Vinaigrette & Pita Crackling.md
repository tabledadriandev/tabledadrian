# Fattoush with Sumac Vinaigrette & Pita Crackling

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The bread salad where the bread must shatter — pita fried in olive oil and salted like crisps, dressed at the table so nothing slumps.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Pita breads, day-old — 2 (about 120 g)
- Olive oil (for frying pita) — 60 ml
- Baby gem lettuce — 200 g
- Cucumber — 250 g
- Ripe tomatoes — 300 g
- Radishes — 100 g
- Spring onions — 50 g
- Purslane or rocket — 60 g
- Mint leaves — 15 g
- Sumac — 8 g
- Pomegranate molasses — 20 g
- Lemon juice — 40 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil (dressing) — 70 ml
- Fine sea salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Tear pita into rough shards; fry in 60 ml olive oil over medium heat (about 170 °C) until deep gold, 4–5 min. Drain, salt, and dust with 2 g sumac while hot.
2. Whisk lemon juice, pomegranate molasses, remaining sumac, salt, then the 70 ml olive oil into a cloudy vinaigrette.
3. Cut cucumber and tomato into 2 cm chunks; slice radishes paper-thin; slice spring onions on the bias.
4. Tear lettuce; keep everything cold and dry until service.
5. At the table: toss vegetables and herbs with the dressing, add pita last, toss twice, serve immediately.

## Plating & Service
Serve in a wide, shallow bowl so the crisp pita stays on top. Best within 5 minutes of dressing.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Gluten-free: use GF flatbread or omit bread and add 40 g toasted seeds. Add 100 g crumbled feta for a richer lunch salad (then not vegan).
