# Shatta, Fermented Red Chilli Paste of the Levant

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Gaza's table heat — chillies salted and left to ferment on the counter for days, then crushed with vinegar and capped with oil; brighter and livelier than any cooked sauce.

## Ingredients (makes ~400 g)
- Fresh red chillies (medium heat), stemmed — 450 g
- Fine sea salt — 14 g (3% of chilli weight)
- White wine vinegar or cider vinegar — 25 ml
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 40 ml (plus to seal)

## Method
1. Chop chillies roughly (seeds in), toss with the salt, and pack into a sterilised jar, pressing so brine rises.
2. Cover with a loose lid or cloth; ferment at room temperature 4–6 days, stirring daily — expect gentle bubbles and a bright, pickled smell.
3. When pleasingly sour, drain briefly and pulse/pound to a coarse paste.
4. Work in vinegar and lemon; jar and float a 5 mm cap of olive oil.
5. Refrigerate; it matures for a month and keeps three.

## Plating & Service
A dab beside hummus, falafel, fried fish, eggs — anywhere the plate needs voltage. Mix 1:2 with olive oil for a fiery finishing drizzle.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Green shatta: green chillies + 10 g dill. In humid conditions ferment in the fridge door for 10 days instead — slower, same result.
