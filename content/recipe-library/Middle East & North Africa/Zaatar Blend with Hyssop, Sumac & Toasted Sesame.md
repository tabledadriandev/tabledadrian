# Zaatar Blend with Hyssop, Sumac & Toasted Sesame

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Real za'atar is a ratio argument — this one runs herb-heavy and sour, with sesame toasted separately so it stays crisp; make small batches monthly, it fades like ground coffee.

## Ingredients (makes ~160 g)
- Dried za'atar herb (hyssop) or best dried thyme — 60 g
- Dried oregano — 15 g
- Sumac — 45 g
- Sesame seeds — 35 g
- Fine sea salt — 4 g
- Ground cumin — 2 g

## Method
1. Toast sesame seeds in a dry pan over medium heat, 3–4 min, to pale gold; cool completely on a plate.
2. Rub the dried herbs through a coarse sieve, discarding stems — you want soft flakes, not powder.
3. Grind the sieved herbs briefly with salt and cumin in a spice grinder — 2 short pulses; over-grinding makes it dusty and bitter.
4. Fold in sumac and the cooled sesame by hand.
5. Store airtight, dark, up to 6 weeks.

## Plating & Service
Whisk 1:2 with olive oil for manakish and dipping; dust over labneh, eggs, grilled halloumi, roast chicken skin.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Aleppo-style: add 8 g toasted coriander seed and 5 g aniseed. If your sumac is salted, omit the added salt.
