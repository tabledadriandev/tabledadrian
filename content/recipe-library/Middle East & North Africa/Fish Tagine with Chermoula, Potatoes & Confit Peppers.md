# Fish Tagine with Chermoula, Potatoes & Confit Peppers

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Essaouira style — thick fish steaks double-doused in chermoula (half as marinade, half fresh at the end), steam-braised over a raft of potatoes, tomatoes and peppers.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Monkfish or cod loin, thick portions — 1000 g (6 pieces)
- Chermoula (see condiments) — 150 g
- Waxy potatoes, 8 mm slices — 500 g
- Ripe tomatoes, sliced — 300 g
- Red and green peppers, strips — 250 g
- Carrot, thin rounds — 150 g
- Preserved lemon rind, slivered — 30 g
- Purple olives — 80 g
- Olive oil — 60 ml
- Fine sea salt — 10 g
- Water or fish stock — 200 ml
- Coriander — 10 g

## Method
1. Coat fish with 90 g of the chermoula; marinate 1–2 h chilled.
2. Oil the tagine base; layer carrots, then potatoes, salting between layers; add water.
3. Cover and cook gently 20 min until potatoes are half-tender.
4. Lay pepper strips and tomato slices over, then the marinated fish; spoon marinade left in the bowl on top.
5. Cover and steam-braise 15–18 min until fish reaches 55 °C and flakes.
6. Scatter preserved lemon and olives for the final 3 min; finish with remaining fresh chermoula and oil.

## Plating & Service
Bring the tagine to the table lidded; unveil for the steam and aroma. Serve fish over vegetables, juices spooned around, coriander over.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Whole small bream, slashed and stuffed with chermoula, works beautifully (25 min). Prawns added in the last 5 min make it festive (adds shellfish).
