# Khoresh Bademjan, Lamb & Aubergine Stew with Sour Grapes

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Whole fried aubergines laid like sleepers over a tomato-saffron lamb braise, sharpened with ghooreh (sour grapes) or verjus — they must hold their shape yet melt on the fork.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Lamb shoulder, 4 cm cubes — 900 g
- Small aubergines — 800 g (6, halved lengthwise)
- Onion, sliced — 200 g
- Tomato paste — 40 g
- Ripe tomatoes, grated — 300 g
- Turmeric — 4 g
- Ground cinnamon — 1 g
- Neutral oil — 120 ml
- Saffron, steeped in 20 ml hot water — 0.2 g
- Sour grapes (ghooreh) or 60 ml verjus — 80 g
- Fine sea salt — 15 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Water — 700 ml

## Method
1. Salt aubergine halves 20 min; pat dry. Fry in 80 ml oil until deep gold on the cut faces, 8 min; drain on a rack.
2. Brown onion in remaining oil 10 min; add turmeric and cinnamon, then lamb, sealing well.
3. Add tomato paste (fry 2 min), grated tomato, salt, pepper and water; braise covered at 90 °C for 90 min.
4. Lay aubergines over the stew, add sour grapes and saffron water; cover and simmer gently 40–50 min more — do not stir, only baste, so the aubergines stay whole.
5. Uncover for the final 15 min until oil rises and the sauce is thick.

## Plating & Service
Slide the aubergines onto chelow rice, spoon lamb and glossy sauce around. Sabzi khordan (herb plate) and yogurt on the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Verjus at the end replaces hard-to-find ghooreh perfectly. Vegan: chickpeas replace lamb; braise 45 min, same aubergine treatment.
