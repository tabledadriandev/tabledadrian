# Basbousa, Semolina Yogurt Cake Soaked in Lemon Syrup

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The tea-time syrup cake of the eastern Mediterranean — coarse semolina for texture, yogurt for tenderness, cool syrup onto hot cake; an almond crowns each diamond like a wax seal.

## Ingredients (serves 10, one 28×20 cm tray)
- Coarse semolina — 300 g
- Desiccated coconut — 60 g
- Sugar (cake) — 120 g
- Baking powder — 8 g
- Fine sea salt — 2 g
- Greek yogurt — 250 g
- Unsalted butter, melted — 130 g
- Eggs — 50 g (1)
- Vanilla — 4 g
- Blanched almonds — 25 (about 30 g)
- Sugar (syrup) — 280 g
- Water — 200 ml
- Lemon juice — 25 ml
- Orange blossom water — 10 ml
- Tahini (for the tin) — 15 g

## Method
1. Syrup: simmer sugar, water and lemon 8 min; add orange blossom off heat; cool fully.
2. Brush the tin with tahini — the traditional non-stick with a sesame whisper.
3. Whisk semolina, coconut, sugar, baking powder and salt; fold in yogurt, melted butter, egg and vanilla to a thick batter.
4. Spread into the tin; rest 20 min (semolina drinks first).
5. Score diamonds and press an almond into each; bake at 180 °C for 35–40 min until deep gold.
6. Re-cut the lines and pour cool syrup slowly over the hot cake; rest 2 h to absorb.

## Plating & Service
Serve diamonds at room temperature with mint tea or thick coffee; a spoon of clotted cream for the indulgent table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: coconut yogurt, oil, no egg (add 30 ml aquafaba). Rose-pistachio version: rose water syrup and pistachios instead of almonds.
