# Gulf Prawn Skewers with Loomi, Baharat & Lime Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Dried lime (loomi) ground into the marinade gives Gulf-style prawns their haunting fermented-citrus edge — three minutes over coals, basted with lime butter.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Large prawns, peeled, tail on — 600 g
- Ground loomi (dried black lime) — 5 g
- Baharat — 8 g
- Turmeric — 2 g
- Garlic, grated — 10 g
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Fine sea salt — 7 g
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Lime juice and zest — 30 ml juice, 3 g zest
- Coriander, chopped — 10 g
- Green chilli, sliced — 10 g

## Method
1. Butterfly prawns lightly along the back; pat dry.
2. Marinate with loomi, baharat, turmeric, garlic, oil and salt, 30 min chilled — no longer, or the loomi cures them.
3. Thread onto flat skewers, 4 per skewer, keeping them straight.
4. Melt butter with lime juice, zest and chilli; hold warm.
5. Grill over hot coals or a plancha at 230 °C, 90 seconds per side, basting with lime butter, until just opaque at the centre (55 °C core).
6. Rest 1 min, baste once more.

## Plating & Service
Serve on the skewers over warm flatbread that catches the butter, scattered with coriander. Squeeze of lime at the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free: baste with olive oil, lime and chilli. Same treatment is superb on monkfish cheeks or scallops.
