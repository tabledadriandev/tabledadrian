# Balila, Warm Chickpeas with Cumin, Lemon & Garlic Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Hummus before it becomes hummus — whole warm chickpeas in a cumin-lemon broth with raw garlic oil; the most underrated dish on a Beirut breakfast table.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Cooked chickpeas with liquor — 600 g (drained weight 450 g)
- Chickpea cooking liquor — 150 ml
- Ground cumin — 6 g
- Cumin seeds, toasted — 3 g
- Garlic, pounded to paste — 8 g
- Lemon juice — 50 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- Fine sea salt — 7 g
- Tahini (optional swirl) — 30 g
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 8 g
- Aleppo pepper — 2 g
- Toasted pine nuts — 15 g

## Method
1. Warm chickpeas in their liquor with ground cumin and salt to a gentle simmer, 8–10 min, until fully tender and the broth is slightly cloudy.
2. Pound garlic with a pinch of salt; whisk with lemon juice and half the olive oil.
3. Ladle chickpeas with a little broth into a serving bowl; crush a spoonful against the side to thicken.
4. Pour the garlic-lemon oil over while steaming hot.
5. Finish with tahini swirl, remaining oil, cumin seeds, Aleppo pepper, parsley and pine nuts.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm in a deep bowl with flatbread for dredging. This is spoon food — rustic, fast, restorative after a night watch.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Top with a soft-boiled egg for a heartier breakfast (adds eggs). Nut-free: omit pine nuts.
