# Haydari, Whipped Yogurt Meze with Dried Mint Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Thicker and punchier than cacık — strained yogurt beaten with feta and garlic, finished with sizzled dried-mint butter; five minutes of work that tastes like a taverna in Bodrum.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Greek yogurt, strained 4 h (from 600 g) — 450 g
- Feta or Turkish beyaz peynir — 100 g
- Garlic, grated to paste — 6 g
- Dried mint — 5 g
- Unsalted butter — 40 g
- Walnut halves, toasted, chopped — 30 g
- Dill, chopped — 8 g
- Olive oil — 20 ml
- Fine sea salt — 3 g
- Pul biber — 2 g

## Method
1. Strain yogurt in muslin 4 h in the fridge until very thick.
2. Crumble feta and beat with the yogurt, garlic, dill, salt and 3 g of the dried mint until fluffy — a whisk, 1 min.
3. Chill 30 min to set.
4. Melt butter to foaming (about 140 °C), pull off heat, stir in remaining dried mint and pul biber.
5. Spread haydari thickly, pour warm mint butter over, scatter walnuts, thread of olive oil.

## Plating & Service
Serve slightly cool with warm bazlama or crudités; the warm butter over cold yogurt should be poured at the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: finish with toasted pumpkin seeds. Lighter: skip butter, finish with mint-infused olive oil (still excellent, fully vegetarian-lean).
