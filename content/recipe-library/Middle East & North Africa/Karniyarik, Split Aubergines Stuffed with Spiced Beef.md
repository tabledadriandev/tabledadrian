# Karniyarik, Split Aubergines Stuffed with Spiced Beef

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> "Split belly" aubergines — fried whole until creamy, then split and packed with a tomato-pepper beef filling and baked until the two collapse into each other.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Small aubergines — 6 (about 1100 g)
- Neutral oil for frying — 500 ml
- Beef mince (or lamb) — 500 g
- Onion, fine dice — 150 g
- Green pepper, fine dice — 100 g
- Garlic, minced — 10 g
- Ripe tomatoes — 300 g (200 g diced, 100 g sliced)
- Tomato paste — 30 g
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Ground cinnamon — 1 g
- Pul biber — 5 g
- Fine sea salt — 14 g
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 15 g
- Long green peppers — 6 small (90 g)
- Hot water — 200 ml

## Method
1. Peel aubergines in zebra stripes, leaving stems on. Fry whole at 170 °C, turning, 8–10 min until soft and gold all over; drain well and salt lightly.
2. Filling: sweat onion and diced pepper in olive oil 8 min; add garlic, then mince, browning well. Add tomato paste, diced tomato, cinnamon, pul biber and 8 g salt; cook 8 min. Fold in most of the parsley.
3. Lay aubergines in a baking dish; split each lengthwise without cutting through, and press open a pocket.
4. Pack 80 g filling into each; top with a tomato slice and a small green pepper.
5. Whisk remaining salt into hot water with a spoon of the filling's juices; pour around (not over) the aubergines.
6. Bake at 190 °C for 30–35 min until bubbling and the peppers blister.

## Plating & Service
One aubergine per guest with a spoonful of the dish juices, parsley over, and buttered rice pilaf alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Roast (220 °C, 25 min, brushed with oil) instead of frying to lighten. Vegetarian imam bayildi is the sister dish — see its own card.
