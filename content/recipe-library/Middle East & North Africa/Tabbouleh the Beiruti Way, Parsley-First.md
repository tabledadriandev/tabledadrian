# Tabbouleh the Beiruti Way, Parsley-First

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Real tabbouleh is a parsley salad seasoned with bulgur, not the reverse — knife work is the whole technique: dry herbs, sharp blade, no bruising.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Flat-leaf parsley (leaves and fine stems) — 200 g
- Mint leaves — 30 g
- Fine bulgur (#1) — 60 g
- Ripe tomatoes — 300 g
- Spring onions — 60 g
- Lemon juice — 70 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- Fine sea salt — 7 g
- Ground allspice — 1 g
- Black pepper — 1 g
- Baby gem leaves, to serve — 6 leaves

## Method
1. Dice tomatoes 5 mm, salt lightly, drain in a sieve 20 min; reserve 30 ml of the juice.
2. Rinse fine bulgur, then soak it in the reserved tomato juice and 20 ml lemon juice — no cooking — for 20 min until tender.
3. Wash herbs, then dry completely (salad spinner, then towels) — wet herbs make grey tabbouleh.
4. Bunch parsley tightly and slice paper-thin with a razor-sharp knife in one pass; do not chop repeatedly. Slice mint the same way.
5. Slice spring onions finely and rub with the allspice, salt and pepper.
6. Fold everything together with remaining lemon and the olive oil no more than 10 min before serving.

## Plating & Service
Pile into a chilled bowl ringed with baby gem leaves — guests scoop with the leaves. Bright, sharp, glistening.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Gluten-free: swap bulgur for 60 g cooked quinoa (cooled and dried on a tray). Winter version: swap tomato for finely diced fennel and orange.
