# Baghali Polo, Dill & Broad Bean Rice with Lamb Shank

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Lamb shanks braised to wobble in saffron-onion broth, served over dill-and-broad-bean steamed rice — the green-flecked polo perfumes the whole boat as it steams.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Lamb shanks — 6 small or 4 large (2400 g)
- Basmati rice — 500 g
- Broad beans, double-podded — 300 g
- Dill, chopped — 60 g
- Dried dill — 8 g
- Onion, sliced — 250 g
- Garlic — 15 g
- Turmeric — 5 g
- Saffron, steeped in 50 ml hot water — 0.4 g
- Neutral oil — 80 ml
- Fine sea salt — 26 g
- Black pepper — 4 g
- Water — 1200 ml
- Flatbread (for tahdig, optional) — 1 sheet

## Method
1. Brown shanks all over in 40 ml oil; remove. Brown onion 10 min, add garlic and turmeric.
2. Return shanks with water, 12 g salt, pepper and a third of the saffron water; braise covered at 150 °C oven for 3–3.5 h until the meat trembles on the bone. Reduce the braise to a glossy jus.
3. Wash and soak rice; parboil 5 min in heavily salted water (12 g), adding broad beans for the final minute; drain.
4. Fold rice gently with fresh and dried dill.
5. In the oiled rice pot (remaining oil, hot), lay flatbread if using, mound the dill rice, poke steam holes, towel-wrapped lid, steam 40 min on the lowest heat.
6. Dress the top of the rice with remaining saffron water.

## Plating & Service
Mound the emerald polo, crack the tahdig into shards around, set shanks on top and pour over their reduced jus. Yogurt and torshi at the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Frozen baby broad beans work brilliantly. Vegan: serve the polo with saffron-braised celeriac steaks and the same reduced (vegetable) jus.
