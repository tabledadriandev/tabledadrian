# Iskender-Style Kebab, Brown Butter Tomato & Yogurt

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bursa's legend rebuilt: thin-sliced marinated lamb over toasted pide croutons, hot tomato sauce, cold yogurt, and the final pour of foaming brown butter at the table.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Lamb leg, trimmed — 900 g
- Greek yogurt — 350 g
- Onion, juiced (grated and strained) — 60 ml
- Olive oil — 50 ml
- Dried oregano — 3 g
- Pul biber — 6 g
- Fine sea salt — 14 g
- Pide or thick flatbread — 300 g
- Ripe tomatoes, grated — 400 g
- Tomato paste — 25 g
- Garlic, grated — 6 g
- Unsalted butter — 90 g
- Long green peppers, grilled — 120 g

## Method
1. Freeze lamb 45 min, then slice across the grain to 3 mm. Marinate with onion juice, 30 ml oil, oregano, 3 g pul biber and 8 g salt, 2–12 h.
2. Tomato sauce: cook tomato paste in 20 ml oil 2 min, add garlic, grated tomato and 4 g salt; simmer 12 min until spoon-coating.
3. Cut pide into 3 cm squares; toast at 190 °C for 8 min until dry and golden.
4. Sear lamb in a screaming pan (or plancha at 250 °C) in thin single layers, 60–90 seconds per batch, undisturbed for crust.
5. Season yogurt with remaining salt; hold at room temperature.
6. Brown the butter to 150 °C with remaining pul biber.

## Plating & Service
Layer per plate: pide croutons, hot tomato sauce, lamb, a bold spoon of yogurt, then pour the sizzling chilli butter over at the table with grilled green pepper alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken thigh version works marinated the same way. GF: use toasted GF flatbread. The butter pour is the dish — do not skip.
