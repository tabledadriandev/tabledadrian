# Kibbeh bil Sanieh, Baked Kibbeh Cake with Onion Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> All the flavour of fried kibbeh without the à-la-minute stress — two pressed layers of bulgur-lamb around a pine-nut hashweh, scored in diamonds and baked to a lacquered crust.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Fine bulgur (#1) — 350 g
- Lean lamb leg, twice-minced, cold — 500 g
- Onion, grated and squeezed — 100 g
- Kibbeh spice (allspice, cinnamon, pepper 2:1:1) — 8 g
- Fine sea salt — 16 g
- Iced water — 90 ml
- Lamb shoulder mince (filling) — 350 g
- Onions, diced (filling) — 200 g
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Pine nuts — 50 g
- Pomegranate molasses — 20 g
- Baharat — 5 g
- Unsalted butter — 60 g

## Method
1. Filling: fry diced onion in olive oil 10 min to gold; add shoulder mince, brown, season with baharat, 6 g salt and pomegranate molasses; fold in pine nuts toasted at 160 °C. Cool.
2. Shell: rinse and squeeze bulgur; knead with cold lean lamb, grated onion, kibbeh spice, remaining salt and iced water 5 min to a cohesive paste.
3. Butter a 28 cm round tin. Press half the shell mix into an even 1 cm base with wet hands.
4. Spread the cooled filling evenly. Flatten palm-sized patches of remaining shell between wet palms and tile them over, smoothing seams to seal.
5. Score deeply into diamonds, run a knife around the edge, and dot with the butter.
6. Bake at 200 °C for 40–45 min until deep brown and pulling from the tin edges; rest 10 min.

## Plating & Service
Re-cut the diamonds and serve from the tin or plated over cacık-style yogurt, with a sharp cabbage-lemon salad.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Slide a thin layer of labneh between filling and top shell for a molten seam. Beef works; keep the shell meat very lean and very cold.
