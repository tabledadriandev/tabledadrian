# Manti, Tiny Lamb Dumplings with Garlic Yogurt & Mint Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Kayseri pride says forty manti should fit on one spoon — aim for thumbnail parcels; the trinity of hot dumpling, cold garlic yogurt and sizzling paprika-mint oil does the rest.

## Ingredients (serves 6, makes ~120)
- Strong flour — 400 g
- Eggs — 100 g (2)
- Water — 90 ml
- Fine sea salt — 14 g
- Lamb mince, fine — 350 g
- Onion, grated and squeezed — 90 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Pul biber — 8 g
- Greek yogurt — 500 g
- Garlic, grated — 10 g
- Unsalted butter — 70 g
- Dried mint — 5 g
- Sweet paprika — 4 g
- Light chicken stock (for boiling) — 2000 ml

## Method
1. Knead flour, eggs, water and 6 g salt to a stiff dough, 8 min; rest wrapped 45 min.
2. Mix lamb, onion, pepper, 4 g salt and 3 g pul biber.
3. Roll dough (pasta machine to setting 2, ~1 mm) and cut 3 cm squares.
4. Dot each square with 2 g filling; pinch opposite corners into a four-pointed star, sealing seams.
5. Dry on semolina-dusted trays 30 min (or freeze for the pantry).
6. Beat yogurt with garlic and remaining salt; bring to room temperature.
7. Simmer manti in stock at 95 °C, 6–8 min, until they float and the pasta is tender; drain, reserving 50 ml stock to loosen the yogurt.
8. Brown butter to 145 °C; off heat add paprika, dried mint and remaining pul biber.

## Plating & Service
Manti in warm shallow bowls, garlic yogurt spooned over (not under — it must meet hot pasta), then the crackling red-flecked butter. Sumac at the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Freeze uncooked and boil from frozen (+2 min). Pumpkin-walnut filling for a vegetarian manti. Oven method: bake dumplings 15 min at 180 °C, then simmer 5 min in stock for nutty depth.
