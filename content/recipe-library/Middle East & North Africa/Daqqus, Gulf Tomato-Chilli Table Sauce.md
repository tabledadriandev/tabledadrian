# Daqqus, Gulf Tomato-Chilli Table Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Machboos without daqqus is a handshake without eye contact — a quick, loose tomato-chilli-coriander sauce that cuts through spiced rice like a squeeze of lime with attitude.

## Ingredients (makes ~400 g)
- Ripe tomatoes — 500 g
- Garlic cloves — 12 g
- Green chillies — 25 g
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Tomato paste — 15 g
- Ground cumin — 3 g
- Ground loomi (dried lime) — 2 g
- Fine sea salt — 7 g
- Coriander, chopped — 20 g
- Lime juice — 15 ml

## Method
1. Blanch tomatoes 30 seconds; peel and rough-chop.
2. Blitz tomatoes, garlic and chillies to a coarse purée.
3. Warm oil in a saucepan; fry tomato paste 2 min, add the purée, cumin, loomi and salt.
4. Simmer briskly 12–15 min to a loose, spoonable sauce — thinner than passata, thicker than juice.
5. Off heat, stir in coriander and lime. Serve warm or room temperature; keeps 5 days chilled.

## Plating & Service
A bowl beside machboos, murabyan, ouzi and grilled fish — spooned over each plate to taste. Doubles as a poaching sauce for eggs on leftover-rice mornings.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fiery Kuwaiti style: double the chillies and add 2 g black pepper. Roast the tomatoes first for a smokier, thicker daqqus.
