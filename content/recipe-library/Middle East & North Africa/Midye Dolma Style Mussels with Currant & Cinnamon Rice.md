# Midye Dolma Style Mussels with Currant & Cinnamon Rice

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Istanbul's street-corner obsession — mussels stuffed with spiced pilaf and steamed shut, served cold with lemon; fiddly, and worth every minute on a raw bar spread.

## Ingredients (serves 6, makes ~30)
- Large mussels, live — 1200 g
- Short-grain rice — 200 g
- Onion, fine dice — 150 g
- Olive oil — 90 ml
- Pine nuts — 25 g
- Currants — 25 g
- Ground cinnamon — 2 g
- Ground allspice — 2 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g
- Hot water — 250 ml
- Lemons — 2
- Dill, chopped — 8 g

## Method
1. Scrub and debeard mussels. Open each raw with a short knife along the straight edge, keeping the hinge intact; chill.
2. Sweat onion in oil 8 min; add pine nuts to colour lightly, then rice, coating 2 min.
3. Add currants, spices, salt and 150 ml hot water; cook 8 min until water absorbs — rice remains al dente. Cool; fold in dill.
4. Spoon a scant 15 g pilaf into each mussel, close, and pack tightly in layers in a wide pot, hinge down.
5. Weight with a plate, add remaining 100 ml hot water and a squeeze of lemon; steam covered at a low simmer 20–25 min.
6. Cool in the pot, then chill thoroughly.

## Plating & Service
Serve cold, mounded on crushed ice with lemon halves — guests squeeze, slurp and use one shell as a spoon for the next.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: omit pine nuts. Same pilaf stuffs squid tubes (braise 40 min at 95 °C) for a heartier version.
