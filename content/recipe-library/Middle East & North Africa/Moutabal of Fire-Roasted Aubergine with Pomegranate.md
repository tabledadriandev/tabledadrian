# Moutabal of Fire-Roasted Aubergine with Pomegranate

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Aubergines charred to collapse directly over flame, then drained — the smoke is the seasoning; yogurt gives the Damascene silkiness that separates moutabal from baba ghanoush.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Aubergines — 900 g (about 3 large)
- Tahini — 90 g
- Greek yogurt — 60 g
- Lemon juice — 40 ml
- Garlic, grated to paste — 6 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Pomegranate seeds — 50 g
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 5 g
- Sumac — 2 g

## Method
1. Prick aubergines and char directly over a gas flame or under a 250 °C grill, turning, 20–30 min until the skin is blackened and flesh fully collapsed.
2. Rest in a covered bowl 10 min; peel over a sieve, discarding every fleck of skin, and drain flesh 15 min.
3. Chop the flesh with two knives to a rough pulp — never blend; blending turns it gluey.
4. Fold in garlic, salt, lemon juice, then tahini and yogurt; the mix should just hold its shape.
5. Rest 20 min at room temperature for the smoke to bloom; adjust lemon and salt.

## Plating & Service
Spoon into a shallow bowl, create a moat, fill with olive oil, and dress with pomegranate, parsley and sumac. Room temperature only — never fridge-cold.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: omit yogurt and raise tahini to 120 g. For a smokier plated version, fold in 2 g smoked salt and torch the surface before garnishing.
