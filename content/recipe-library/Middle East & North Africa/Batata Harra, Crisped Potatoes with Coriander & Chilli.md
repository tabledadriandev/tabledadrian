# Batata Harra, Crisped Potatoes with Coriander & Chilli

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Twice-cooked potatoes with the garlic-coriander sizzle added off the heat so the garlic stays sweet, not acrid — the mezze everyone fights over.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Waxy potatoes (Charlotte) — 900 g
- Neutral oil for frying — 500 ml
- Olive oil — 50 ml
- Garlic, thinly sliced — 20 g
- Coriander, chopped (stems and leaves) — 40 g
- Aleppo pepper — 8 g
- Smoked paprika — 3 g
- Lemon juice — 30 ml
- Fine sea salt — 8 g
- Coriander leaves, to finish — 5 g

## Method
1. Peel potatoes and cut into 2.5 cm cubes. Simmer in well-salted water from cold to 90 °C, cook 6 min until just tender at the edges; drain and steam-dry 10 min on a tray.
2. Rough up the surfaces with a fork toss — the fluff is the crunch.
3. Fry at 180 °C in batches, 6–7 min, until deeply golden and crisp; drain and salt.
4. In a wide pan, warm olive oil to a gentle sizzle; add garlic and cook 60–90 seconds to pale gold only.
5. Off the heat, add Aleppo pepper, paprika and chopped coriander — it will crackle.
6. Return potatoes to the pan, toss through the flavoured oil with lemon juice.

## Plating & Service
Serve tumbling hot in a shallow bowl, showered with fresh coriander, wedge of lemon on the side. Great beside grilled fish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Oven route: roast the parboiled cubes with 60 ml oil at 220 °C for 35 min. Add 30 g diced red pepper with the garlic for the Beirut street version.
