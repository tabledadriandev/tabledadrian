# Taktouka, Charred Pepper & Tomato Compote

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Zaalouk's green-pepper sibling — flame-roasted peppers melted into spiced tomato until glossy; brilliant under grilled fish or beside kefta.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Green peppers — 400 g
- Red romano pepper — 150 g
- Ripe tomatoes — 500 g
- Garlic, grated — 10 g
- Olive oil — 80 ml
- Ground cumin — 4 g
- Sweet paprika — 5 g
- Fine sea salt — 7 g
- Sugar — 3 g
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Coriander, chopped — 12 g

## Method
1. Char all peppers over flame or under a 250 °C grill until blistered; steam covered 10 min, peel, deseed, and tear into strips.
2. Score, blanch (30 seconds at 100 °C), and peel tomatoes; chop flesh, discarding seeds.
3. Cook tomato with garlic, olive oil, cumin, paprika, salt and sugar over medium heat 15 min until reduced and shiny.
4. Fold in pepper strips; cook 10 min more, crushing lightly — keep some ribbons visible.
5. Finish with lemon and coriander off the heat; rest 30 min.

## Plating & Service
Room temperature in a shallow bowl with a gloss of olive oil. It also earns its place under a seared sea bass fillet as a warm relish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 2 beaten eggs at the end over low heat for a Moroccan-style soft scramble (then contains eggs). Spike with 10 g harissa for heat.
