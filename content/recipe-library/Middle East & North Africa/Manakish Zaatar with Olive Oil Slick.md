# Manakish Zaatar with Olive Oil Slick

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Levant's morning flatbread — dimpled dough flooded with za'atar and oil so it fries in its own topping; the ratio is 1:2 za'atar to oil, always fresh blend.

## Ingredients (serves 6, makes 6)
- Strong flour — 500 g
- Instant yeast — 6 g
- Sugar — 8 g
- Fine sea salt — 9 g
- Warm water — 310 ml
- Olive oil (dough) — 30 ml
- Za'atar blend (see condiments) — 60 g
- Extra virgin olive oil (topping) — 120 ml
- Tomato, sliced, and cucumber to serve — 200 g
- Mint sprigs — 10 g

## Method
1. Knead flour, yeast, sugar, salt, water and 30 ml oil 8 min; prove 60–90 min.
2. Whisk za'atar with the 120 ml oil to a loose slurry.
3. Divide dough into 6; ball, rest 15 min. Heat stone/steel at 260 °C.
4. Press and roll to 18 cm rounds; dimple deeply with fingertips right to the edge — the pools hold the topping.
5. Spread 30 g slurry per round, edge to edge.
6. Bake 7–9 min until the base is gold and the surface bubbles through the green oil.

## Plating & Service
Fold hot around tomato, cucumber and mint, or slice into petals for the mezze table. Breakfast standard with sweet tea.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Half za'atar, half cheese (akkawi/mozzarella) gives the beloved "cocktail" mankousheh (adds dairy). Leftovers crisp into za'atar chips at 180 °C.
