# Arnabit Mekle, Fried Cauliflower with Tahini & Pomegranate

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Cauliflower fried to bronze — nutty and almost meaty — then dressed like the Beirut street classic with lemony tarator and pomegranate seeds.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Cauliflower — 900 g (1 large head)
- Neutral oil for frying — 1200 ml
- Fine sea salt — 8 g
- Ground cumin — 3 g
- Tahini — 100 g
- Lemon juice — 45 ml
- Garlic, grated — 4 g
- Iced water — 80 ml
- Pomegranate seeds — 60 g
- Pomegranate molasses — 15 g
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 8 g
- Toasted pine nuts — 20 g

## Method
1. Cut cauliflower into even 4 cm florets; wash and dry completely.
2. Whisk tahini, lemon, garlic and 3 g salt, loosening with iced water to a pouring cream.
3. Fry florets at 170 °C in two batches, 6–8 min each, until deep golden brown at the edges — pale cauliflower is pointless.
4. Drain on a rack; season immediately with salt and cumin.
5. Assemble while hot: florets over half the tarator, remaining sauce zigzagged on top.

## Plating & Service
Finish with pomegranate seeds, a thread of molasses, parsley and pine nuts. Serve hot or warm — it holds well for buffet mezze.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Oven route: roast with 60 ml oil at 230 °C for 25 min. Nut-free: skip pine nuts, add toasted sesame.
