# Riz bi Shariyeh, Buttered Vermicelli Rice

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The default rice of every Levantine table — vermicelli fried in butter to hazelnut brown before the rice goes in; it must taste toasted, never merely boiled.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Basmati or Calrose rice, rinsed — 400 g
- Broken vermicelli nests (şehriye) — 80 g
- Unsalted butter — 50 g
- Olive oil — 15 ml
- Fine sea salt — 10 g
- Boiling water or light chicken stock — 700 ml
- Pine nuts, toasted (optional) — 20 g

## Method
1. Melt butter with oil in a heavy pot over medium heat.
2. Fry the vermicelli, stirring constantly, 3–4 min to an even deep gold — one shade before it burns is the correct shade.
3. Add drained rice; stir 2 min to coat every grain in the browned butter.
4. Add boiling water and salt; boil uncovered 3 min until holes appear in the surface.
5. Cover tightly on the lowest heat 12 min; rest off heat, lid on, 10 min.
6. Fork gently from the sides inward.

## Plating & Service
Serve in a warm bowl, pine nuts over, beside any stew, kafta or yogurt dish in this library — it is the supporting actor in half of them.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan/dairy-free: all olive oil (add 2 g extra salt). GF: use rice vermicelli, frying only 60 seconds.
