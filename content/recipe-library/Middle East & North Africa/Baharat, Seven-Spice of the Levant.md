# Baharat, Seven-Spice of the Levant

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The backbone of half this library — warm, peppery, faintly sweet; whole spices toasted and ground the same day beat any tin by a nautical mile.

## Ingredients (makes ~90 g)
- Black peppercorns — 20 g
- Allspice berries — 18 g
- Coriander seeds — 14 g
- Cumin seeds — 12 g
- Cassia or cinnamon stick — 10 g
- Cloves — 4 g
- Green cardamom seeds (podded) — 5 g
- Ground nutmeg — 5 g
- Sweet paprika — 8 g

## Method
1. Toast peppercorns, allspice, coriander, cumin, cassia, cloves and cardamom in a dry pan over medium heat, tossing, 2–3 min until fragrant — pull at first wisp of smoke.
2. Cool completely on a plate (grinding warm spices cakes them).
3. Grind to a fine powder in batches.
4. Sieve; regrind what remains.
5. Whisk in nutmeg and paprika. Store airtight, dark, 8 weeks.

## Plating & Service
The default seasoning for kafta, shawarma, kibbeh, dawood basha; 6–10 g per kilo of meat. A pinch in tomato sauces rounds them instantly.

## Gulf variation
For machboos and saloona, add 6 g ground loomi and 4 g turmeric to the finished blend.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Turkish register: more black pepper, no paprika. Toasting is the flavour — never skip it.
