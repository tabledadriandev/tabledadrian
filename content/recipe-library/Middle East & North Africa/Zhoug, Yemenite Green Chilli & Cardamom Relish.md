# Zhoug, Yemenite Green Chilli & Cardamom Relish

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Hot, herbal, haunted by cardamom — the Yemenite salsa verde that wakes up hummus, eggs, grilled anything; keep it coarse and it stays vivid for a week.

## Ingredients (makes ~250 g)
- Green chillies (jalapeño-strength) — 120 g
- Coriander (leaves and stems) — 100 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 30 g
- Garlic cloves — 12 g
- Green cardamom seeds, freshly podded — 2 g
- Cumin seeds, toasted — 3 g
- Black peppercorns — 2 g
- Fine sea salt — 7 g
- Lemon juice — 30 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml

## Method
1. Grind cardamom, cumin and pepper to powder.
2. Deseed half the chillies (leave the rest armed); rough-chop.
3. Pulse chillies, garlic and salt 5–6 times; add herbs in two additions, pulsing to a coarse, flecked relish — visible leaf fragments, not soup.
4. Fold in ground spices and lemon; stream in the oil with 2 final pulses.
5. Rest 1 h. Keeps 7 days chilled under oil; freezes in cubes.

## Plating & Service
Swirled through hummus, spooned on falafel, cilbir eggs, grilled fish, or thinned with oil as a fierce vinaigrette.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Red zhoug: ripe red chillies and 3 g caraway. Milder table: all chillies deseeded plus 30 g green pepper for body.
