# Chermoula Carrot Salad with Orange & Green Olives

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Carrots poached just to the point of yielding, then marinated warm in chermoula so they drink the dressing — a make-ahead mezze that improves for two days.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Carrots — 600 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g
- Chermoula (see condiments) — 90 g
- Orange juice — 40 ml
- Orange zest — 3 g
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 40 ml
- Green olives, pitted, torn — 60 g
- Coriander leaves — 8 g
- Toasted cumin seeds — 2 g

## Method
1. Peel carrots and cut into 1 cm oblique chunks.
2. Simmer in salted water at 95 °C for 8–10 min until just tender — a knife should enter with slight resistance.
3. Drain and, while hot, toss with chermoula, orange juice and zest; the heat opens the spices.
4. Cool to room temperature, then fold in lemon, olive oil and olives.
5. Marinate at least 2 h (better overnight); return to room temperature to serve.

## Plating & Service
Pile loosely in a shallow bowl, scatter coriander and cumin seeds. Part of a trio with zaalouk and taktouka for a Moroccan salad course.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Roast the carrots at 210 °C instead for a sweeter, charred version. Add 30 g toasted almonds for crunch (then contains nuts).
