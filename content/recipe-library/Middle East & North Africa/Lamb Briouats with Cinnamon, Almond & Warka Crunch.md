# Lamb Briouats with Cinnamon, Almond & Warka Crunch

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sweet-savoury triangles in shattering pastry — spiced lamb with almonds and a whisper of cinnamon sugar, the b'stilla flavour in one canapé bite.

## Ingredients (serves 6, makes 18)
- Warka or phyllo sheets — 250 g
- Lamb mince — 400 g
- Onion, fine dice — 120 g
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Ras el hanout — 6 g
- Ground cinnamon — 3 g
- Ground ginger — 2 g
- Fine sea salt — 7 g
- Eggs, beaten — 100 g (2)
- Flaked almonds, toasted — 60 g
- Coriander, chopped — 10 g
- Icing sugar — 10 g
- Neutral oil for frying — 1000 ml

## Method
1. Sweat onion in olive oil 8 min; add lamb, brown well, and season with ras el hanout, 2 g of the cinnamon, ginger and salt.
2. Reduce heat, stir in beaten eggs and cook 2 min to soft curds binding the meat. Fold in almonds and coriander; cool completely.
3. Cut pastry into 8 cm-wide strips. Place 25 g filling at one end; fold corner-over-corner into tight triangles, sealing the last flap with a dab of water.
4. Chill 20 min to set the folds.
5. Fry at 175 °C, 3 min, to deep gold (or bake at 200 °C, 16 min, brushed with oil).
6. Drain; dust immediately with icing sugar mixed with remaining cinnamon.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm, three per person, on a small plate with a few coriander leaves. The sugar-cinnamon dust over hot lamb is non-negotiable.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Seafood briouats: swap lamb for 350 g chopped prawns with chermoula, skip sugar dust. Chicken confit + preserved lemon is the classic alternative.
