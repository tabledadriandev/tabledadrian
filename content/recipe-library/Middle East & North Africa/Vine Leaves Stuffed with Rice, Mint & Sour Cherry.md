# Vine Leaves Stuffed with Rice, Mint & Sour Cherry

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Yalanji with a twist — dried sour cherries in the rice echo the leaves' own tang; rolled tight as cigars and cooked under a plate so none unravel.

## Ingredients (serves 8, makes ~40)
- Brined vine leaves, rinsed — 250 g (about 45 leaves)
- Short-grain rice — 250 g
- Onion, finely diced — 150 g
- Olive oil — 120 ml
- Tomato, grated — 150 g
- Dried sour cherries, chopped — 50 g
- Mint, chopped — 15 g
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 20 g
- Ground allspice — 3 g
- Fine sea salt — 10 g
- Lemon juice — 80 ml
- Water or light vegetable stock — 400 ml

## Method
1. Blanch rinsed vine leaves in simmering water 2 min to soften; drain.
2. Sweat onion in 60 ml olive oil 8 min; stir in rice for 2 min. Add tomato, cherries, herbs, allspice, salt and 100 ml water; cook 5 min until water is absorbed (rice stays half-raw). Cool.
3. Lay each leaf shiny-side down, stem trimmed. Place 15 g filling in a low bar near the stem end.
4. Fold sides in, then roll firmly away from you into a slim cigar — snug, not tight; rice swells.
5. Line a heavy pot with torn leaves, pack rolls seam-down in tight concentric layers.
6. Pour over remaining oil, lemon juice and stock; weight with an upturned plate.
7. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook at the barest bubble (about 95 °C) for 50–60 min until rice is tender and liquid absorbed.
8. Cool in the pot to room temperature before lifting out.

## Plating & Service
Stack in a low pyramid, brush with olive oil for shine, serve with lemon wedges and thick yogurt. Better the next day.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap cherries for 40 g currants and 20 g pine nuts for the Istanbul style. Meat version: replace half the rice with 200 g minced lamb and cook 15 min longer.
