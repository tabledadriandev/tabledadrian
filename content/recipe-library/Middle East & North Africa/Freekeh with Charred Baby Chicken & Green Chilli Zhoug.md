# Freekeh with Charred Baby Chicken & Green Chilli Zhoug

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Smoked green wheat cooked like a risotto in chicken juices under a spatchcocked, fiercely grilled poussin — smoke on smoke, cut by bright zhoug.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Poussins, spatchcocked — 3 (about 1500 g)
- Cracked freekeh, rinsed — 400 g
- Chicken stock — 900 ml
- Onion, fine dice — 150 g
- Olive oil — 80 ml
- Ground cumin — 6 g
- Ground allspice — 4 g
- Ground coriander — 4 g
- Lemon juice and zest — 40 ml juice, 4 g zest
- Fine sea salt — 18 g
- Zhoug (see condiments) — 60 g
- Flaked almonds, toasted — 40 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 10 g

## Method
1. Rub poussins with 30 ml oil, half the spices, zest and 10 g salt; rest 2 h uncovered in the fridge for taut skin.
2. Sweat onion in remaining oil 8 min; add freekeh and remaining spices, toasting 2 min.
3. Add hot stock and 8 g salt; simmer covered at 95 °C for 20 min, then rest 10 min — grains stay nutty, distinct.
4. Grill poussins skin-down over medium coals (or roast at 220 °C) 12 min; flip, 10–12 min more to 74 °C in the thigh, charring the skin properly.
5. Rest birds 8 min over the freekeh pot so juices drip in; fork juices and lemon through the grains.

## Plating & Service
Mound freekeh on a platter, halve the poussins over it, spoon zhoug in stripes, finish with almonds and parsley.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Freekeh is wheat — for GF, use the same method with coarse cornmeal-free sorghum or rice. One large chicken (roast 50 min) replaces the poussins.
