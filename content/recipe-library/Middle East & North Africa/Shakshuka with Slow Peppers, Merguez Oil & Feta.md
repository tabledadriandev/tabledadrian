# Shakshuka with Slow Peppers, Merguez Oil & Feta

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The pepper base is cooked twice as long as most dare — jammy, faintly smoky — then eggs poach in dimples at a whisper of heat so whites set and yolks tremble.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Eggs — 6 (about 360 g)
- Red peppers, sliced — 400 g
- Onion, sliced — 150 g
- Ripe tomatoes, grated — 500 g
- Merguez sausage (optional), sliced — 150 g
- Olive oil — 60 ml
- Garlic, sliced — 12 g
- Harissa — 25 g
- Ground cumin — 5 g
- Sweet paprika — 6 g
- Fine sea salt — 9 g
- Feta — 80 g
- Coriander, chopped — 10 g

## Method
1. If using merguez, render it in a dry 28 cm pan 5 min until its paprika oil releases; remove, leave the oil, top up with olive oil.
2. Cook onion and peppers over medium-low heat 20 min until collapsed and sweet.
3. Add garlic, cumin, paprika, harissa; 2 min. Add tomato and salt; simmer 12 min to a thick, trench-holding sauce.
4. Return merguez. Make 6 dimples; crack an egg into each.
5. Cover and cook at the barest simmer 6–8 min until whites set, yolks liquid (surface ~80 °C, no bubbling).
6. Rest 1 min off the heat, uncovered.

## Plating & Service
Straight from the pan at the table, crumbled feta and coriander over, warm bread for the yolks. Spoons for the sauce; no plates needed.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian: skip merguez, add 3 g smoked paprika. Green shakshuka: swap the base for chard, leek and 100 ml cream.
