# Sayadieh, Spiced Fish over Caramelised Onion Rice

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The fisherman's rice of the Levantine coast — onions taken to near-black stain the rice bronze and bitter-sweet, crowned with cumin-dusted fish and a lemony onion gravy.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Firm white fish fillets (cod, sea bass) — 900 g
- Basmati rice, soaked 30 min — 450 g
- Onions, thinly sliced — 600 g
- Olive oil — 120 ml
- Ground cumin — 10 g
- Ground coriander — 5 g
- Turmeric — 4 g
- Ground cinnamon — 2 g
- Fish or light chicken stock — 850 ml
- Fine sea salt — 16 g
- Lemon juice — 40 ml
- Pine nuts, fried — 30 g
- Coriander leaves — 8 g

## Method
1. Fry onions in 80 ml oil over medium heat 25–30 min to the edge of burnt — mahogany, catching at the pan. Remove a third for garnish.
2. Blitz the rest with the stock; strain. Season this onion stock with half the cumin, coriander, turmeric, cinnamon and 10 g salt.
3. Cook drained rice in the onion stock: boil 2 min, then cover at lowest heat 12 min; rest 10 min.
4. Dust fish with remaining cumin, turmeric and salt. Sear in remaining oil, 3 min skin-side, 2 min flesh-side, to 55 °C core.
5. Deglaze the fish pan with lemon and 50 ml water; reduce 1 min into a pan gravy.

## Plating & Service
Fork the bronze rice onto a platter, lay fish over, pour the lemon gravy across, and finish with reserved crisped onions, pine nuts and coriander.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: skip pine nuts, add fried onion only. Monkfish or halibut hold beautifully; adjust sear to thickness (target 55 °C core).
