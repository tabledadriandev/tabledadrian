# Sabzi Polo ba Mahi, Herbed Rice with Crisp-Skin Fish

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Nowruz plate — rice steamed emerald with four herbs and garlic, beside turmeric-dusted fish seared crisp; a New Year dish that belongs on a spring charter menu.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Sea bass or bream fillets, skin on — 900 g (6 × 150 g)
- Basmati rice — 500 g
- Coriander, chopped — 50 g
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 50 g
- Dill, chopped — 40 g
- Chives, chopped — 30 g
- Garlic, whole cloves — 30 g
- Turmeric — 6 g
- Plain rice flour (dusting) — 30 g
- Neutral oil — 100 ml
- Unsalted butter (optional finish) — 30 g
- Saffron, steeped in 30 ml hot water — 0.3 g
- Fine sea salt — 24 g
- Limes — 3

## Method
1. Wash and soak rice 30 min; parboil 5 min in water with 14 g salt; drain.
2. Fold the drained rice gently with all the herbs.
3. Heat 50 ml oil in the rice pot; mound in herbed rice, tucking whole garlic cloves through it; steam holes, towel-wrapped lid, lowest heat 40 min for a golden tahdig.
4. Dust fish skin with rice flour, turmeric and 6 g salt.
5. Sear skin-down in remaining oil over high heat, pressing 20 seconds, 4 min until glass-crisp; flip 60–90 seconds to 55 °C core. Baste with butter if using.
6. Finish rice with saffron water over the crown.

## Plating & Service
Herb rice mounded with tahdig shards, fish skin-up, charred lime halves. The steamed garlic cloves are prizes — point them out.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Traditional with white fish but salmon (52 °C core) is superb. Dairy-free: skip butter baste. The rice alone, with the garlic, is a complete vegan side.
