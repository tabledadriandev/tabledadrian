# Roast Beetroot Hummus, Goat Labneh & Hazelnut Dukkah

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A magenta showpiece for deck lunches — earthy roast beets against lactic goat labneh and crunchy dukkah, all doable a day ahead.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Raw beetroot — 300 g
- Cooked chickpeas, drained — 240 g
- Tahini — 100 g
- Lemon juice — 45 ml
- Garlic clove — 6 g
- Ground coriander — 3 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g
- Iced water — 60 ml
- Goat labneh (or thick goat yogurt) — 120 g
- Hazelnut dukkah — 30 g
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Dill fronds — 4 g

## Method
1. Wrap beets in foil with a splash of water; roast at 200 °C for 55–60 min until a knife slides through. Cool, peel, chop.
2. Blitz beets, chickpeas, garlic, coriander and salt for 60 seconds to a coarse purée.
3. Add lemon juice and tahini; blend, streaming in iced water until silky and holding soft peaks.
4. Taste — beets need more salt and acid than classic hummus; adjust boldly.
5. Chill 30 min for the colour and texture to set.

## Plating & Service
Spread in a wide crescent, drop spoonfuls of goat labneh into the swoosh, scatter dukkah and dill, finish with olive oil. Serve with seeded crackers.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: swap labneh for coconut yogurt whipped with lemon zest. Nut-free: use a seed-only dukkah (sesame, coriander, cumin, fennel).
