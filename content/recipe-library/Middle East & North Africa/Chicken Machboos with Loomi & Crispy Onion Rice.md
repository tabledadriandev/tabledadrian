# Chicken Machboos with Loomi & Crispy Onion Rice

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bahrain's national dish — chicken braised with dried lime and Gulf baharat, the rice then cooked in that same broth so every grain carries the story; the pierced loomi is the soul of it.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Chicken legs, bone-in — 1600 g
- Basmati rice, soaked 30 min — 500 g
- Onions, sliced — 300 g
- Neutral oil — 70 ml
- Gulf baharat — 12 g
- Turmeric — 4 g
- Ground loomi — 3 g
- Whole dried limes, pierced — 3 (about 22 g)
- Cinnamon stick — 1 (4 g)
- Cardamom pods — 6
- Garlic, grated — 15 g
- Ginger, grated — 15 g
- Tomatoes, chopped — 300 g
- Green chilli, slit — 15 g
- Fine sea salt — 18 g
- Water — 950 ml
- Coriander — 12 g

## Method
1. Fry onions in oil to deep gold, 15 min; remove a third and crisp it further for garnish.
2. Add garlic, ginger, baharat, turmeric and ground loomi to the pot; 1 min.
3. Add chicken, colouring in the spiced onions; add tomatoes, whole limes, cinnamon, cardamom, chilli, salt and water.
4. Simmer covered 40 min until chicken is tender; lift chicken out and keep warm.
5. Add drained rice to the broth (top up to 850 ml if reduced); boil 3 min, then cover on lowest heat 12 min; rest 10 min.
6. Crisp the chicken skin under a 240 °C grill for 5 min while the rice rests.

## Plating & Service
Fluff rice onto a platter, squeeze the softened dried limes over it, set chicken on top with crispy onions and coriander. Daqqus (spicy tomato sauce) alongside if you have it.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Lamb machboos: shoulder chunks, 90 min braise. The loomi is non-negotiable — order it before the season starts.
