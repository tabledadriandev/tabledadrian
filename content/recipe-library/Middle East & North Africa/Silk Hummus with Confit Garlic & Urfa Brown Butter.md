# Silk Hummus with Confit Garlic & Urfa Brown Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Overcooked, peeled chickpeas blitzed hot with iced tahini give a Michelin-smooth emulsion; the smoky Urfa butter makes it read as fine dining on a mezze table.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Dried chickpeas, soaked 12 h — 250 g
- Bicarbonate of soda — 8 g
- Tahini (light, Lebanese-style) — 180 g
- Iced water — 120 ml
- Lemon juice — 60 ml
- Garlic cloves, peeled — 40 g
- Olive oil (for confit) — 150 ml
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Urfa chilli flakes — 6 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g
- Ground cumin — 2 g
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 5 g

## Method
1. Simmer soaked chickpeas with bicarbonate in 1.5 L water, skimming, 60–75 min at a gentle 95 °C until they crush to paste between two fingers; reserve 50 ml cooking liquor.
2. Meanwhile confit the garlic in olive oil at 90 °C for 40 min until fully tender; drain (keep the oil).
3. Rub warm chickpeas in cold water to release skins; discard skins and drain well.
4. Blitz hot chickpeas with confit garlic, salt and cumin in a powerful blender for 90 seconds to a stiff paste.
5. With the motor running, add lemon juice, then tahini, then stream in iced water until the hummus turns pale, glossy and pipeable; adjust with reserved liquor.
6. Brown the butter to 150 °C until nutty, pull off heat, stir in Urfa flakes and 20 ml garlic confit oil.
7. Rest hummus 10 min covered so flavours settle; check salt and acid.

## Plating & Service
Swoosh into a shallow bowl with the back of a spoon, flood the well with warm Urfa butter, scatter parsley. Serve just above room temperature with warm flatbread.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan/dairy-free: replace butter with smoked olive oil warmed with Urfa. Top with braised whole chickpeas and pine nuts for a Beiruti version.
