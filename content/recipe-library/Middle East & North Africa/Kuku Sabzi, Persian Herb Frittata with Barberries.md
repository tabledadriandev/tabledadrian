# Kuku Sabzi, Persian Herb Frittata with Barberries

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> More herb than egg — a deep green Nowruz frittata where the eggs merely bind a kilo of greens; barberries stud it with sour rubies.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Flat-leaf parsley — 150 g
- Coriander — 150 g
- Dill — 80 g
- Spring onion tops or chives — 80 g
- Baby spinach — 100 g
- Eggs — 300 g (6)
- Dried fenugreek leaves — 3 g
- Turmeric — 2 g
- Baking powder — 3 g
- Barberries, rinsed — 30 g
- Walnuts, chopped — 40 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Olive oil — 60 ml

## Method
1. Wash and thoroughly dry all herbs and spinach; chop fine but not minced — a processor pulse works if brief.
2. Whisk eggs with salt, pepper, turmeric, fenugreek and baking powder.
3. Fold in herbs — the mix should look like dressed herbs, barely coated. Fold in barberries and walnuts.
4. Heat 40 ml oil in a 24 cm nonstick ovenproof pan over medium heat; pour in the mix, level, cook 8 min until edges set.
5. Transfer to a 180 °C oven for 15–18 min until the centre springs back.
6. Rest 5 min, invert onto a board, brush with remaining oil for shine.

## Plating & Service
Cut in diamonds, serve warm or room temperature with yogurt and flatbread. Travels beautifully for beach picnics.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: omit walnuts. Bake in muffin moulds at 180 °C for 14 min for canapé rounds. Dairy pairs: a smear of labneh under each diamond.
