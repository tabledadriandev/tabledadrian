# Toum, Cloud-White Lebanese Garlic Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> An egg-free emulsion of pure garlic and oil — the technique is patience: oil in threads, lemon and iced water alternating, until it stands in stiff white peaks like a savoury meringue.

## Ingredients (makes ~500 g)
- Garlic cloves, peeled, germs removed — 100 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g
- Neutral oil (sunflower/grapeseed), fridge-cold — 400 ml
- Lemon juice, cold — 45 ml
- Iced water — 30 ml

## Method
1. Halve cloves and strip out any green germs — they turn toum bitter and hot.
2. In a food processor, run garlic and salt 60 seconds, scraping twice, to a fine wet paste.
3. With the motor running, add oil in the thinnest possible thread — the first 100 ml decides everything.
4. Alternate: 15 ml lemon, then more oil thread, then iced water, continuing until all is incorporated, 8–10 min total.
5. The finished toum is pure white, stiff and fluffy. If it breaks: restart with 30 g cooked potato or 1 spoon of the broken sauce plus new garlic paste, and re-stream.
6. Rest 12 h chilled — rawness mellows dramatically. Keeps 3 weeks.

## Plating & Service
Mandatory with shish taouk and shawarma; brushed on grilling bread; folded 1:3 into mayonnaise for a civilised aioli cheat.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Herb toum: fold in 15 g minced mint post-emulsion. Roasting half the garlic first gives a gentler, sweeter sauce (slightly ivory).
