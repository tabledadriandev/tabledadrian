# Kefta Mkaouara, Meatball Tagine with Eggs & Cumin

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Marrakech's street tagine — cumin-paprika beef meatballs simmered in a punchy tomato sauce with eggs cracked straight in at the end; a one-pot that feeds a crew in style.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Beef mince (or beef-lamb mix) — 800 g
- Onion, grated and squeezed — 100 g
- Ground cumin — 10 g (divided)
- Sweet paprika — 10 g (divided)
- Coriander, chopped — 25 g
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 15 g
- Fine sea salt — 15 g
- Cayenne — 2 g
- Olive oil — 50 ml
- Garlic, minced — 12 g
- Ripe tomatoes, grated — 700 g
- Tomato paste — 25 g
- Eggs — 6 (about 360 g)
- Black pepper — 3 g

## Method
1. Knead mince with onion, 5 g cumin, 5 g paprika, half the herbs, 9 g salt and pepper; roll into 20 g balls.
2. Sauce: warm oil with garlic 1 min; add tomato paste 2 min, then grated tomato, remaining cumin, paprika, cayenne and salt. Simmer 15 min until thick.
3. Nestle meatballs into the sauce; cover, simmer gently 15 min, shaking (not stirring) the pot.
4. Make 6 wells; crack in the eggs. Cover and cook at a low simmer 5–7 min — whites set, yolks molten.
5. Rest 2 min; scatter remaining herbs.

## Plating & Service
Straight to the table in the tagine with khobz — everyone claims an egg and dredges. No cutlery beyond bread required.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skip eggs and serve over couscous for a family platter. A spoon of harissa in the sauce moves it to Tunisia.
