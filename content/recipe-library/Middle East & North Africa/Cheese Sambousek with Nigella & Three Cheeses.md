# Cheese Sambousek with Nigella & Three Cheeses

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Half-moon pastries with a stretchy, salty three-cheese heart — the dough is short enough to shatter but strong enough to crimp; freeze a battalion and fry to order.

## Ingredients (serves 6, makes 20)
- Plain flour — 400 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g
- Olive oil — 60 ml
- Softened butter — 40 g
- Warm water — 160 ml
- Akkawi or mozzarella, grated — 200 g
- Feta, crumbled — 120 g
- Ricotta — 80 g
- Egg — 50 g (1)
- Nigella seeds — 6 g
- Dried mint — 3 g
- Neutral oil for frying — 1200 ml

## Method
1. Rub flour with salt, oil and butter to fine crumbs; add water and knead 3 min to a smooth, firm dough. Rest wrapped 30 min.
2. If using akkawi, soak 1 h in cold water to desalinate, then squeeze. Mix cheeses with egg, nigella and dried mint.
3. Roll dough to 2 mm; cut 9 cm circles.
4. Place 18 g filling on each, wet the edge, fold to a half-moon and rope-crimp the seam tightly.
5. Chill 20 min (or freeze for the pantry).
6. Fry at 170 °C for 3–4 min until golden and blistered; drain on a rack.

## Plating & Service
Pile onto a warm plate and serve within minutes — the cheese pull is the show. A cucumber-mint yogurt dip on the side.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Bake at 200 °C for 15 min with egg wash for a lighter canapé. Meat sambousek: use the sfeeha lamb filling, cooked and cooled first.
