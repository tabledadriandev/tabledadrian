# Kunefe, Molten Cheese in Crisp Kadayif with Lemon Syrup

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The dessert that must be eaten in its first three minutes — shredded pastry pressed around desalinated cheese, cooked in a pan until both faces are copper, syruped while molten; theatre and timing.

## Ingredients (serves 6, one 24 cm pan)
- Kadayif (shredded phyllo), thawed — 350 g
- Unsalted butter, clarified — 160 g
- Fresh mozzarella + akkawi (or all mozzarella), sliced — 350 g
- Ricotta — 60 g
- Sugar — 300 g
- Water — 220 ml
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Orange blossom water — 8 ml
- Pistachios, ground — 40 g

## Method
1. Soak salty cheese (if using akkawi) 2 h in cold water, changing twice; pat dry.
2. Syrup: sugar, water, lemon, 8 min at a simmer; orange blossom off heat. Keep warm — künefe takes warm syrup, unlike baklava.
3. Chop kadayif roughly; work the clarified butter through with your hands until every strand glistens.
4. Press half firmly into a 24 cm nonstick pan. Layer cheese and ricotta, leaving a 2 cm border; cap with remaining kadayif, pressing hard.
5. Cook over medium heat 8–10 min, rotating the pan, until the underside is deep copper; invert onto a plate and slide back to colour the second side 8 min.
6. On the heat, ladle 150 ml warm syrup over — it will hiss and soak.

## Plating & Service
Slide onto a warm platter, shower with pistachio, cut wedges immediately — the cheese must pull in ropes. Extra syrup in a jug.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Individual künefe in 10 cm blini pans (4 min per side) for plated service. Cream (ashta) filling instead of cheese for the Beirut morning version.
