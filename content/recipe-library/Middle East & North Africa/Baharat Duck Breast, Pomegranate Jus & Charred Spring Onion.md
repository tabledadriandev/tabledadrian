# Baharat Duck Breast, Pomegranate Jus & Charred Spring Onion

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A plated fine-dining main from the mezze pantry — baharat-cured duck rendered glass-crisp, with a pomegranate molasses jus that behaves like a gastrique; charter dinner-party artillery.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Duck breasts — 4 (about 800 g)
- Baharat — 10 g
- Fine sea salt — 10 g
- Sugar — 4 g
- Pomegranate molasses — 40 g
- Pomegranate juice — 150 ml
- Chicken jus or reduced stock — 150 ml
- Sherry-style vinegar — 10 ml
- Spring onions — 12 (150 g)
- Olive oil — 20 ml
- Pomegranate seeds — 40 g
- Picked coriander — 4 g

## Method
1. Score duck skin in a fine crosshatch; rub flesh side with baharat, salt and sugar. Cure uncovered in the fridge 4–12 h.
2. Start skin-down in a cold pan; render over medium-low heat 12–14 min, pouring off fat, until the skin is deep amber and crisp.
3. Flip for 2 min; then roast at 180 °C to 54 °C core (about 4–6 min). Rest 8 min skin-up.
4. Jus: reduce pomegranate juice by two-thirds; add molasses and jus, reduce to spoon-coating; sharpen with vinegar and a pinch of salt.
5. Char whole spring onions in a dry pan with a little duck fat, 3–4 min, seasoning lightly.
6. Slice duck across at a low angle, seasoning the cut faces.

## Plating & Service
Fan the duck over the charred onions, sauce pulled across the plate, pomegranate seeds and coriander placed, not scattered. A saffron-parsnip purée completes the restaurant plate.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The cure and jus suit venison loin (52 °C core) in winter. Sweeter table: add 5 g honey to the jus and serve with jeweled rice instead.
