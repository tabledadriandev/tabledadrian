# Tarator, Lemon Tahini Sauce Three Consistencies

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The mother sauce of the Levant — tahini seizes with lemon, then blooms open with iced water; master the three stops (dip, drizzle, dressing) and half this library plugs into it.

## Ingredients (makes ~380 g)
- Tahini, well stirred — 200 g
- Lemon juice — 60 ml
- Garlic, grated to paste — 5 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g
- Ground cumin — 1 g
- Iced water — 90–150 ml (by consistency)
- Flat-leaf parsley, minced (optional) — 8 g

## Method
1. Whisk tahini with garlic, salt and cumin.
2. Add lemon juice — the mixture will seize and stiffen to a paste; this is correct.
3. Whisk in iced water 30 ml at a time; it loosens, lightens and turns pale ivory.
4. Stop at your consistency: 90 ml = thick dip (falafel), 120 ml = spoon-coating drizzle (cauliflower, fish), 150 ml = pourable dressing (salads, shawarma).
5. Rest 15 min; it thickens slightly — adjust water, salt and lemon.

## Plating & Service
Keeps 5 days chilled. The base for samke harra (warmed gently), fatteh, arayes, and every fried vegetable aboard.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Green tarator: blend with 30 g parsley and 15 g dill. Yogurt-tahini (1:1) is gentler for children's tables (adds dairy).
