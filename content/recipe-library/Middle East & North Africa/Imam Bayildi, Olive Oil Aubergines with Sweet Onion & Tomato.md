# Imam Bayildi, Olive Oil Aubergines with Sweet Onion & Tomato

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The imam fainted — from pleasure or the olive oil bill — at this vegan main: aubergines braised low with a marmalade of onions, tomato and garlic, served at room temperature like all great zeytinyağlı dishes.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Small aubergines — 6 (about 1100 g)
- Extra virgin olive oil — 180 ml
- Onions, thinly sliced — 500 g
- Garlic, sliced — 20 g
- Ripe tomatoes — 400 g (300 g diced, 100 g sliced)
- Sugar — 10 g
- Fine sea salt — 14 g
- Ground allspice — 2 g
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 20 g
- Dill, chopped — 8 g
- Lemon juice — 25 ml
- Hot water — 150 ml

## Method
1. Peel aubergines in stripes; deep-score a pocket lengthwise. Salt with 4 g and rest 20 min; pat dry.
2. Brown aubergines all over in 100 ml of the oil over medium heat, ~10 min; set in a braising dish, pocket up.
3. Cook onions in remaining oil, low, 20 min to sweet translucence; add garlic 3 min, then diced tomato, sugar, allspice and 8 g salt; 8 min more. Fold in most of the parsley.
4. Press the onion mixture generously into and over each pocket; top with tomato slices.
5. Add hot water with remaining salt and lemon around them; cover and braise at 170 °C for 40–45 min until utterly tender.
6. Cool in the dish, basting once — never serve hot; flavour peaks at room temperature the next day.

## Plating & Service
One glistening aubergine per plate, spoon of the braising oil-juice around, dill and parsley over. Crusty bread and a young rosé.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 40 g currants and 30 g pine nuts to the onions for the Istanbul meyhane version (adds nuts). Halved, it becomes a starter for 12.
