# Kebab Karaz, Aleppo Sour Cherry Meatballs on Bread

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Aleppo's haute street food — lamb meatballs glazed in a sweet-sour cherry sauce over toasted flatbread; served historically at weddings, it plates like a modern bistro dish with zero adjustment.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Lamb mince (15% fat) — 800 g
- Onion, grated and squeezed — 90 g
- Baharat — 7 g
- Fine sea salt — 13 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Sour cherries, pitted (frozen or jarred, drained) — 450 g
- Pomegranate molasses — 25 g
- Sugar — 25 g (adjust to cherry tartness)
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Ground cinnamon — 1 g
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Flatbreads — 3 (about 200 g)
- Pine nuts, toasted — 30 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 10 g

## Method
1. Knead lamb with onion, baharat, 9 g salt and pepper; roll 25 g balls. Chill 30 min.
2. Brown in oil over high heat, 4 min, rolling; remove.
3. In the same pan: cherries, sugar, pomegranate molasses, cinnamon, remaining salt and 150 ml water. Simmer 10 min, crushing a third of the cherries.
4. Return meatballs; simmer gently 15 min until the sauce is a glossy, deep-crimson glaze and meatballs are 70 °C.
5. Balance with lemon — it should hover exactly between sweet and sour.
6. Toast flatbread pieces at 190 °C for 6 min.

## Plating & Service
Bread shards on the platter, meatballs and cherry glaze over so the bread half-soaks, then pine nuts and parsley. Serve immediately, spoon-first.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fresh sour cherries in June are transcendent (add 50 ml water). GF: serve over rice. Beef works; keep the fat at 15%.
