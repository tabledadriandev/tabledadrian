# Amba, Fermented-Style Mango & Fenugreek Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Iraqi mango condiment that electrifies sabich and shawarma — sour, funky, mustard-yellow; this quick version fakes the ferment with lime, salt and time.

## Ingredients (makes ~450 g)
- Firm, underripe mangoes, peeled, 1 cm dice — 500 g
- Fine sea salt — 12 g
- Neutral oil — 30 ml
- Garlic, minced — 10 g
- Fenugreek seeds, ground — 4 g
- Mustard seeds — 4 g
- Ground turmeric — 5 g
- Ground cumin — 4 g
- Sweet paprika — 3 g
- Cayenne — 2 g
- Lime juice — 50 ml
- White wine vinegar — 30 ml
- Water — 100 ml
- Sugar — 10 g

## Method
1. Toss mango with salt; rest at room temperature 4–12 h (this stands in for the traditional brining) — do not rinse.
2. Sizzle mustard seeds in oil until they pop; add garlic 30 seconds, then fenugreek, turmeric, cumin, paprika and cayenne.
3. Add mango with its brine, water, sugar and vinegar; simmer 15 min until the fruit slumps.
4. Crush half the mango against the pan; the sauce should be spoonable with soft chunks.
5. Finish with lime; cool. Rest 24 h — the fenugreek funk needs a night to bloom. Keeps 3 weeks chilled.

## Plating & Service
Ribboned over fried aubergine and egg sandwiches, shawarma, grilled fish, or stirred into yogurt as an instant sauce for masgouf.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Smoother table sauce: blend fully and thin with water. Heat lovers: leave the cayenne whole-chilli instead (10 g sliced bird's eye).
