# Ras el Hanout Grilled Quail with Grape & Almond Salad

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Spatchcocked quail take the full perfume of ras el hanout without the hours a tagine needs — nine minutes over coals, honey-lemon glaze, and a sharp grape salad doing the work of a sauce.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Quail, spatchcocked — 8 (about 1200 g)
- Ras el hanout — 14 g
- Garlic, grated — 10 g
- Lemon juice and zest — 40 ml, 3 g
- Olive oil — 60 ml
- Honey — 25 g
- Fine sea salt — 12 g
- Red grapes, halved — 200 g
- Toasted almonds, cracked — 50 g
- Rocket — 80 g
- Mint leaves — 8 g
- Sherry-style vinegar — 15 ml

## Method
1. Rub quail with ras el hanout, garlic, zest, 30 ml oil and 9 g salt; marinate 2–12 h.
2. Whisk honey with half the lemon juice for the glaze.
3. Grill skin-down over medium-hot coals 5 min, pressing flat with a spatula; flip 3–4 min to 65 °C in the thigh.
4. Brush with honey-lemon glaze in the final minute per side — sooner and it burns.
5. Rest 4 min. Toss grapes, rocket, mint and almonds with remaining oil, lemon, vinegar and salt.

## Plating & Service
Two quail per plate over the grape salad, resting juices spooned over. Finger bowls and zero cutlery formality — quail is hand food.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Poussin halves work (18–20 min). Nut-free: toasted sunflower seeds. Winter: swap grapes for sliced blood orange.
