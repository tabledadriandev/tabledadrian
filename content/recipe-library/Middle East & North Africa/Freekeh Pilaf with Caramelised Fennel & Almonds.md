# Freekeh Pilaf with Caramelised Fennel & Almonds

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Smoked green wheat has more to say than any rice — here it plays against sweet caramelised fennel and toasted almonds; the pilaf that upgrades any grilled fish or lamb plate.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Cracked freekeh, rinsed — 350 g
- Fennel bulbs, 1 cm wedges — 400 g (fronds reserved)
- Onion, fine dice — 120 g
- Olive oil — 70 ml
- Ground allspice — 3 g
- Ground cinnamon — 1 g
- Ground cumin — 3 g
- Vegetable stock — 800 ml
- Fine sea salt — 12 g
- Flaked almonds, toasted — 50 g
- Lemon zest and juice — 3 g, 25 ml
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 10 g

## Method
1. Caramelise fennel wedges in 40 ml oil over medium heat, 10–12 min, until deeply golden on the cut faces; season and set aside.
2. In the same pot, sweat onion in remaining oil 6 min; bloom the spices 30 seconds.
3. Add freekeh, toasting 2 min in the spiced oil.
4. Add hot stock and salt; simmer covered at 95 °C for 18–20 min until tender with a gentle chew; rest 10 min.
5. Fold in fennel, lemon zest and juice.

## Plating & Service
Loose mounds with almonds, parsley and fennel fronds over. Pairs with joojeh kebab, roast fish, or a spoonful of labneh for lunch.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Not GF (freekeh is wheat) — swap for sorghum or rice with 2 g smoked paprika to fake the smoke. Add 80 g green olives for a briny version.
