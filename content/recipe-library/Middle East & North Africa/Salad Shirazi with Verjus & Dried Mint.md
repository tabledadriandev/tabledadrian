# Salad Shirazi with Verjus & Dried Mint

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Persia's daily salad — everything in 5 mm dice, dressed with verjus for a rounder acidity than lime alone; the palate-cleanser every rich khoresh needs.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Persian cucumbers — 300 g
- Firm ripe tomatoes — 300 g
- Red onion — 100 g
- Verjus (or 30 ml lime juice + 10 ml water) — 40 ml
- Lime juice — 20 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 45 ml
- Dried mint — 3 g
- Fresh mint, sliced — 6 g
- Sumac — 2 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Deseed tomatoes; dice flesh 5 mm. Dice cucumber and onion the same — uniformity is the discipline of this salad.
2. Rinse the diced onion in cold water 1 min and dry — keeps it sweet.
3. Whisk verjus, lime, oil, dried mint, salt and pepper.
4. Dress the vegetables no more than 10 min before serving; fold in fresh mint.
5. Taste — it should be bracing, almost a chilled relish.

## Plating & Service
Serve in a small chilled bowl, dusted with sumac, alongside rice dishes and grills — a spoonful with every bite.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 100 g diced radish for crunch in summer. For a lunch bowl, fold in 200 g cooked chickpeas and double the dressing.
