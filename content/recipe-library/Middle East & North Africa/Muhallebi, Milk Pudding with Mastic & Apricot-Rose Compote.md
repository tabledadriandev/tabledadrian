# Muhallebi, Milk Pudding with Mastic & Apricot-Rose Compote

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Levant's panna cotta — milk set with starch alone, perfumed with mastic and orange blossom; it should tremble, barely holding its shape, under a sharp apricot compote.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Whole milk — 800 ml
- Double cream — 100 ml
- Cornflour — 55 g
- Sugar — 90 g
- Mastic crystals, ground with a pinch of sugar — 0.5 g
- Orange blossom water — 10 ml
- Fine sea salt — 1 g
- Dried apricots, quartered — 150 g
- Water (compote) — 150 ml
- Honey — 30 g
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Rose water — 5 ml
- Pistachios, slivered — 25 g

## Method
1. Whisk cornflour with 150 ml of the cold milk to a slurry.
2. Heat remaining milk, cream, sugar and salt to a simmer; whisk in the slurry and cook, whisking constantly, 3–4 min until thick and glossy (it must boil briefly to cook out the starch).
3. Off heat, whisk in ground mastic and orange blossom water.
4. Pour into 6 glasses or shallow bowls; press film onto the surface; chill 4 h.
5. Compote: simmer apricots, water and honey 10–12 min until syrupy; finish with lemon and rose water; cool.

## Plating & Service
Spoon compote over each pudding, scatter pistachio slivers. Serve cold in the glass — a make-ahead dessert that survives any sea state.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: oat milk + coconut cream, same starch. No mastic? Use 3 g vanilla — different, still lovely. Top with sour cherries in season.
