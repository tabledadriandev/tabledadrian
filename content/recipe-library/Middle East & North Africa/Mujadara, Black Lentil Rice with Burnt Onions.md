# Mujadara, Black Lentil Rice with Burnt Onions

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Peasant food that outclasses its company — the onions are taken past golden to the frontier of burnt, half stirred through, half piled on top; cumin and lentils do the rest.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Green or brown lentils — 250 g
- Basmati rice, rinsed — 250 g
- Onions, thinly sliced — 600 g
- Olive oil — 100 ml
- Ground cumin — 7 g
- Ground coriander — 3 g
- Ground allspice — 2 g
- Fine sea salt — 13 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Water — 700 ml (plus lentil water)
- Lemon wedges — 1 lemon
- Greek yogurt or vegan yogurt, to serve — 200 g

## Method
1. Simmer lentils in unsalted water 15 min — tender outside, firm centre; drain, reserving liquid.
2. Fry onions in the oil over medium-high heat, 20–25 min, stirring less as they darken, to deep mahogany with frizzled dark edges. Salt lightly; reserve half.
3. In the onion pot, bloom cumin, coriander and allspice 30 seconds; add rice, coating in the onion oil.
4. Add lentils, salt, pepper and 700 ml liquid (reserved lentil water topped up). Boil 3 min; cover on lowest heat 14 min.
5. Rest 10 min; fork through half the onions.

## Plating & Service
Mound on a platter, crown with the reserved frizzled onions, lemon wedges around, cold yogurt on the side. Room temperature it might be even better.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Coarse bulgur instead of rice for the northern Lebanese version (adds gluten). A cucumber-tomato salad turns it into a complete vegan dinner.
