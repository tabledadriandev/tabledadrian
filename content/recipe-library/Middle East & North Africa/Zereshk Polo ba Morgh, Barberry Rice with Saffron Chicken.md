# Zereshk Polo ba Morgh, Barberry Rice with Saffron Chicken

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Persian wedding plate — saffron-braised chicken beside chelow strewn with butter-glossed barberries; the berries are only ever warmed, never cooked, or they blacken and turn acrid.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Chicken legs — 1500 g (6 pieces)
- Basmati rice — 500 g
- Barberries (zereshk), picked over, rinsed — 60 g
- Unsalted butter — 80 g
- Onion, sliced — 200 g
- Turmeric — 4 g
- Tomato paste — 25 g
- Saffron, ground, steeped in 60 ml hot water — 0.5 g
- Sugar — 15 g
- Fine sea salt — 24 g
- Neutral oil — 70 ml
- Water — 400 ml
- Slivered pistachios — 20 g

## Method
1. Brown onion in 40 ml oil; add turmeric, then chicken, colouring all sides. Add tomato paste, 10 g salt, water and a third of the saffron water; braise covered at 90 °C for 50–60 min until tender. Reduce the sauce to a glaze consistency.
2. Rice: wash basmati until water runs clear; soak 30 min in salted water. Boil in 2 L water with 12 g salt for 5–6 min to al dente; drain.
3. Layer rice back into the oiled pot in a mound, poke steam holes, wrap the lid in a towel; steam on lowest heat 35–40 min.
4. Fold a ladle of cooked rice with half the remaining saffron water for the golden garnish rice.
5. Melt butter with sugar over the gentlest heat; add barberries and warm 60–90 seconds until glistening and plump — off the heat at first sizzle.
6. Finish chicken with the last saffron water and 2 g salt.

## Plating & Service
White rice mounded, saffron rice crowning it, barberries cascading down, chicken and its reduced saffron sauce alongside; pistachios over all.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No barberries? Dried cranberries soaked and quartered, plus 10 ml lemon. Dairy-free: warm berries in oil with sugar.
