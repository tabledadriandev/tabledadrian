# Advieh, Persian Rice Spice with Rose & Cardamom

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Persia's answer to garam masala, but perfumed rather than hot — rose petals and cardamom over a cinnamon base; a pinch over steaming chelow is the fastest route to Tehran.

## Ingredients (makes ~70 g)
- Cinnamon stick — 18 g
- Green cardamom seeds — 8 g
- Cumin seeds — 8 g
- Coriander seeds — 8 g
- Dried rose petals — 10 g
- Ground turmeric — 6 g
- Ground nutmeg — 3 g
- Black peppercorns — 4 g
- Dried ginger — 5 g

## Method
1. Toast cinnamon, cardamom, cumin, coriander and peppercorns gently, 2 min, until aromatic; cool fully.
2. Grind with the dried ginger to a fine powder.
3. Grind rose petals separately, briefly — they should stay slightly flaky.
4. Combine all with turmeric and nutmeg.
5. Store airtight and dark; at its best within 6 weeks.

## Plating & Service
Advieh-e polo: a pinch forked through finished rice or tahchin. Advieh-e khoresh: bloom 3–4 g in the stew's frying stage (gheymeh, fesenjan).

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Khoresh version: add 4 g ground loomi and drop the rose. Dust over roasted almonds with a little sugar and salt for cocktail hour.
