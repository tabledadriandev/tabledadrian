# Khobz, Moroccan Semolina Crust Round

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The tagine's official cutlery — a low, wide round with a semolina-sanded crust, docked so it bakes dense-crumbed and strong enough to scoop daghmira without folding.

## Ingredients (serves 8, makes 2 rounds)
- Strong flour — 400 g
- Fine semolina — 200 g (plus 40 g for dusting)
- Instant yeast — 7 g
- Sugar — 8 g
- Fine sea salt — 11 g
- Warm water (35 °C) — 380 ml
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Sesame or nigella seeds (optional) — 8 g

## Method
1. Knead flour, semolina, yeast, sugar, salt, water and oil 8 min to a firm, elastic dough.
2. Bulk prove 45 min.
3. Divide in two; shape into tight balls, then flatten to 20 cm discs, 2.5 cm thick, on semolina-dusted trays.
4. Dust tops with semolina, cover, and prove 40 min until pillowy.
5. Dock each loaf: prick deeply with a fork in a ring pattern, and score a shallow ring around the circumference. Scatter seeds.
6. Bake at 220 °C for 22–25 min until hollow-sounding, golden, and sandy-crusted.

## Plating & Service
Cool 20 min; cut in wedges. Every tagine, zaalouk and glass of mint tea on the table assumes its presence.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> 100% semolina gives a more golden, rustic crumb (add 20 ml water). Add 5 g aniseed to the dough for the festive version.
