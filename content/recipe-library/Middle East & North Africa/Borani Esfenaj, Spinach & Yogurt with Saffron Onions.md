# Borani Esfenaj, Spinach & Yogurt with Saffron Onions

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Wilted spinach folded through thick yogurt while barely warm so it stays emerald — the saffron-fried onion crown makes it plate-worthy.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Spinach (mature leaf) — 500 g
- Greek yogurt, strained 2 h — 400 g
- Onion, thinly sliced — 150 g
- Olive oil — 50 ml
- Garlic, sliced — 8 g
- Turmeric — 1 g
- Saffron, steeped in 15 ml hot water — 0.15 g
- Fine sea salt — 7 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Walnuts, toasted, chopped — 25 g

## Method
1. Fry onion in olive oil over medium heat 12–15 min to deep gold; remove half and dress it with the saffron water for garnish.
2. Add garlic and turmeric to the pan, 1 min, then spinach in handfuls; wilt fully, 4–5 min.
3. Tip into a sieve and press out all liquid; chop roughly and cool to just-warm.
4. Fold spinach mixture into the strained yogurt with salt, pepper and lemon.
5. Chill 30 min; re-season (yogurt swallows salt).

## Plating & Service
Spoon into a shallow bowl, swirl the surface, top with saffron onions and walnuts. Room temperature with warm barbari bread.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Beetroot borani: swap spinach for 400 g roasted grated beets. Vegan: use thick oat or coconut yogurt and add 5 ml extra lemon.
