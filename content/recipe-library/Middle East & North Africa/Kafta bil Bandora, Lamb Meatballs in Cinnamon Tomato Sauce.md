# Kafta bil Bandora, Lamb Meatballs in Cinnamon Tomato Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Herb-heavy kafta seared then finished gently in a cinnamon-scented tomato sauce — a one-pan main that tastes of a Damascus home kitchen.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Lamb mince (15–20% fat) — 900 g
- Onion, grated and squeezed — 120 g
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 40 g
- Baharat — 8 g
- Ground cinnamon — 3 g
- Fine sea salt — 14 g
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Ripe tomatoes, grated — 700 g
- Tomato paste — 30 g
- Garlic, sliced — 12 g
- Sugar — 4 g
- Aleppo pepper — 4 g
- Pine nuts, toasted — 25 g
- Mint leaves — 5 g

## Method
1. Knead lamb with onion, parsley, baharat, 2 g of the cinnamon and 9 g salt for 2 min until tacky; roll into 40 g torpedoes.
2. Sear in olive oil over high heat, 2 min per side, to a deep crust — inside stays raw. Remove.
3. In the same pan, soften garlic 1 min; add tomato paste, cook 2 min, then grated tomato, sugar, remaining cinnamon and salt.
4. Simmer the sauce 12 min until thickened and brick-red.
5. Return kafta with any juices; simmer gently, covered, 12–15 min to 70 °C core.
6. Rest off the heat 5 min; the sauce should coat a spoon.

## Plating & Service
Serve over vermicelli rice, sauce spooned generously, finished with pine nuts, Aleppo pepper and mint.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Bake as one layer with sliced potatoes and the sauce at 190 °C for 45 min for kafta bil sanieh. Beef or a lamb-beef mix both work.
