# Lamb Guvec, Clay-Pot Lamb with Summer Vegetables

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Everything raw into the pot, sealed, and forgotten at low heat — the vegetables braise the lamb in their own juices; zero-stress galley cooking that tastes like an Anatolian village.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Lamb shoulder, 3 cm cubes — 1000 g
- Aubergine, 3 cm chunks — 300 g
- Courgette, 3 cm chunks — 250 g
- Green beans, halved — 200 g
- Long green peppers, chunks — 150 g
- Ripe tomatoes, chopped — 500 g
- Baby onions, peeled — 200 g
- Garlic cloves, whole — 20 g
- Tomato paste — 40 g
- Olive oil — 70 ml
- Dried oregano — 4 g
- Pul biber — 6 g
- Ground allspice — 3 g
- Fine sea salt — 16 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 12 g

## Method
1. Toss lamb with tomato paste, half the oil, oregano, pul biber, allspice and salt.
2. Layer in a clay pot or heavy casserole: lamb first, then onions, garlic, beans, aubergine, courgette, peppers, tomatoes on top. Pour over remaining oil. No added liquid.
3. Seal with a double layer of foil, then the lid.
4. Bake at 160 °C for 2.5 h without peeking — the seal is the technique.
5. Uncover, raise to 200 °C for 15 min to colour the top and concentrate juices.
6. Rest 10 min; check salt of the juices.

## Plating & Service
Serve from the pot at the table over rice or with crusty bread, parsley strewn over. The juices are the sauce — spoon deep.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Winter version: swap courgette/beans for 400 g potato and 200 g quince. Vegan güveç: double vegetables, add 250 g chickpeas, reduce cook to 100 min.
