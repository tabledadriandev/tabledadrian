# Maqluba, The Upside-Down Rice with Aubergine & Lamb

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The flip is the theatre — layered lamb, fried aubergine and spiced rice inverted at the table into a standing cake; a confident 10-second flip beats a hesitant one.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Lamb shoulder, 4 cm cubes — 900 g
- Basmati rice, soaked 30 min — 500 g
- Aubergines — 700 g
- Tomatoes, sliced — 200 g
- Onion, quartered — 150 g
- Neutral oil for frying — 700 ml
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Baharat — 10 g
- Turmeric — 4 g
- Ground cinnamon — 3 g
- Cardamom pods — 6
- Bay leaves — 2
- Fine sea salt — 20 g
- Flaked almonds, fried — 50 g
- Greek yogurt, to serve — 300 g

## Method
1. Brown lamb in olive oil; cover with 1.5 L water, add onion, cardamom, bay and 10 g salt; simmer at 90 °C for 60 min until tender. Strain — keep 800 ml broth, season it with baharat, turmeric and cinnamon.
2. Slice aubergine 1.5 cm, salt 20 min, pat dry, and fry at 180 °C until deep gold; drain.
3. In a heavy 26 cm pot, lay tomato slices over the oiled base, then the lamb, then overlapping aubergine up the sides.
4. Drain rice and spoon it over, levelling gently; pour the hot spiced broth down the side until it sits 1 cm above the rice.
5. Boil 5 min, then cover and cook at the lowest heat (rice at ~95 °C) for 25 min; rest off heat, still covered, 20 min — the rest sets the cake.
6. Run a palette knife around the edge, place the platter on top, grip and invert in one decisive motion. Wait 2 min before lifting the pot.

## Plating & Service
Lift the pot at the table; crown with fried almonds. Serve wedges with cold yogurt and a sharp cucumber salad.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken maqluba: use thighs, 35 min braise. Add a cauliflower layer (fried florets) for the fuller Jerusalem version. Nut-free: omit almonds.
