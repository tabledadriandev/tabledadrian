# Cilbir, Poached Eggs on Garlic Yogurt with Aleppo Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Ottoman palace breakfast — warm (never cold) garlic yogurt under trembling poached eggs and scarlet chilli butter; temperature control is the whole dish.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Eggs, very fresh — 4 (about 240 g)
- Greek yogurt — 400 g
- Garlic, grated to paste — 5 g
- Fine sea salt — 5 g
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Aleppo pepper — 8 g
- Smoked paprika — 1 g
- White wine vinegar — 20 ml
- Dill fronds — 6 g
- Sourdough, grilled, to serve — 200 g

## Method
1. Beat yogurt with garlic and salt; set the bowl over a pan of 50 °C water, stirring, until it reaches blood temperature (37–40 °C) — warm, never hot, or it splits.
2. Bring 2 L water with vinegar to 90 °C (bare shivers, no boil).
3. Crack each egg into a small sieve to drop the loose white, then slide into the water; poach 3 min for set whites, liquid yolks.
4. Brown the butter lightly to 140 °C; off heat, stir in Aleppo pepper and paprika — it should foam scarlet.
5. Spoon warm yogurt into shallow bowls, nest eggs on top, blot briefly, then spoon over the chilli butter.

## Plating & Service
Scatter dill, serve immediately with grilled sourdough soldiers. The yolk, yogurt and butter must merge into one sauce at the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: serve with grilled GF bread or crisped potatoes. Lighter: replace half the butter with olive oil warmed with the chilli.
