# Slata Mechouia, Tunisian Grilled Vegetable Salad

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Everything over live fire — peppers, tomatoes, onion, garlic — then hand-chopped and dressed with caraway and good oil; smoke is the main ingredient.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Green peppers — 400 g
- Ripe tomatoes — 400 g
- Onion, unpeeled — 150 g
- Garlic cloves, unpeeled — 15 g
- Green chilli — 20 g
- Ground caraway — 3 g
- Ground coriander — 3 g
- Fine sea salt — 7 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- Lemon juice — 25 ml
- Black olives — 50 g
- Capers, rinsed — 15 g

## Method
1. Grill peppers, tomatoes, onion, garlic and chilli over hot coals (or under a 250 °C grill), turning, until charred all over — tomatoes 8 min, peppers and onion 15–20 min.
2. Steam everything in a covered bowl 10 min; peel, deseed peppers and tomatoes, squeeze garlic from its skins.
3. Chop everything together on a board to a coarse, juicy rubble — never a processor.
4. Season with caraway, coriander, salt and lemon; beat in olive oil.
5. Rest 1 h at room temperature for the smoke to settle in.

## Plating & Service
Spread on a plate, strew olives and capers, final thread of oil. Classic with grilled fish or tinned-tuna crostini for aperitivo.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Traditional finish adds tuna and hard-boiled egg on top. Stir into pasta with its oil for an off-menu crew meal.
