# Zeytinyagli Enginar, Artichokes Braised in Olive Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Aegean olive-oil braise served cold — artichoke bottoms cradling sweet peas and carrots, glazed in their own lemony cooking oil; elegance from four vegetables.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Globe artichokes — 6 large (yielding ~600 g bottoms)
- Lemons — 2 (juice ~80 ml, plus water bath)
- Extra virgin olive oil — 150 ml
- Onion, fine dice — 120 g
- Carrot, 5 mm dice — 150 g
- Waxy potato, 5 mm dice — 150 g
- Peas (fresh or frozen) — 120 g
- Sugar — 8 g
- Fine sea salt — 9 g
- Water — 300 ml
- Dill, chopped — 10 g
- Celery heart, fine dice — 40 g

## Method
1. Turn the artichokes: snap off leaves, trim to the bottom, scoop the choke with a spoon; hold in lemon water.
2. Sweat onion, carrot and celery in 60 ml olive oil over medium heat 6 min without colour.
3. Add potato, sugar, salt, 40 ml lemon juice and water; nestle in artichoke bottoms cup-side up, spooning some vegetables into each.
4. Cover with a paper cartouche, simmer gently at 90–95 °C for 25 min.
5. Add peas and remaining olive oil, cook 6 min more until artichokes are tender to a knife tip.
6. Cool completely in the liquor — they season as they cool. Chill overnight if possible.

## Plating & Service
Serve at cool room temperature, each bottom mounded with vegetables, spoon over the emulsified oil-lemon liquor, shower with dill.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 60 g broad beans in season. For a starter plate, set one artichoke on lemon-dressed leaves with the liquor as vinaigrette.
