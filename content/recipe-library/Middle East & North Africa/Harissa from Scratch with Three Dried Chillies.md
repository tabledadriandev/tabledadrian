# Harissa from Scratch with Three Dried Chillies

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Real Tunisian harissa is dried chillies, garlic, spice, salt and oil — nothing else; three chilli varieties give depth (fruit, smoke, heat) instead of one-note fire.

## Ingredients (makes ~350 g)
- Dried guajillo or ñora chillies (fruity, mild) — 60 g
- Dried ancho chillies (dark, raisiny) — 30 g
- Dried árbol or bird's eye chillies (heat) — 10 g
- Garlic cloves — 30 g
- Caraway seeds — 6 g
- Coriander seeds — 6 g
- Cumin seeds — 4 g
- Fine sea salt — 10 g
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml (plus more to seal)

## Method
1. Stem and deseed all chillies (gloves advised); toast 60 seconds in a dry pan until pliable and fragrant.
2. Soak in hand-hot water 25–30 min until fully soft; drain and squeeze well — wet chillies make loose harissa.
3. Toast and grind caraway, coriander and cumin.
4. Pound or process chillies with garlic, ground spices and salt to a dense, slightly fibrous paste — scrape and work it; texture should be like sun-dried tomato paste.
5. Work in lemon juice, then the oil in a stream.
6. Jar, smooth the surface, and float 10 mm of oil on top. Refrigerated, it improves for a week and keeps a month.

## Plating & Service
The engine of ojja, chraime, fish couscous and the octopus in this library. Loosen with hot broth as a table sauce; whisk into vinaigrettes at 1:10.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Rose harissa: pound in 4 g dried rose petals and 5 ml rose water at the end. Smoked: char one fresh red pepper and work it in (shortens shelf life to 10 days).
