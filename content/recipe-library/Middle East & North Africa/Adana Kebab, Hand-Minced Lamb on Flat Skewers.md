# Adana Kebab, Hand-Minced Lamb on Flat Skewers

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> No binder, no breadcrumbs — just hand-chopped lamb with tail-fat-level richness, red pepper and salt, kneaded to tackiness so it grips the wide skewer over live coals.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Lamb shoulder with good fat cover — 1000 g
- Lamb breast or extra fat trim — 200 g
- Red pepper, very finely grated, squeezed dry — 80 g
- Pul biber (Aleppo pepper) — 12 g
- Biber salçası (hot pepper paste) — 20 g
- Fine sea salt — 16 g
- Sumac onions: onion 200 g + sumac 6 g
- Flatbreads (lavash) — 6 (about 350 g)
- Ripe tomatoes, halved — 300 g
- Long green peppers — 150 g
- Olive oil — 20 ml

## Method
1. Chill meat and fat to near-freezing. Hand-chop with a heavy knife (or coarse-grind once, 8 mm plate) to a rubbly mince — never fine.
2. Knead with pepper, pul biber, salça and salt for 5–6 min until sticky and pale; it must cling upside-down to your palm. Chill 1 h.
3. With wet hands, mould 180 g per flat skewer, pressing ridges with finger and thumb along the length; keep 1.5 cm thickness.
4. Chill skewers 20 min to set.
5. Grill over hot coals, 2 min per face, 8–10 min total, pressing nothing — fat dripping and flaring is correct. Grill tomatoes and green peppers alongside; oil them lightly.
6. Rest 2 min laid on the flatbreads so they catch the juices.

## Plating & Service
Slide kebabs onto the juice-stained bread with charred tomato, peppers and a heap of sumac onions. Ezme on the side is mandatory.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No skewers? Shape into flat sausages and cook on a screaming-hot plancha. Beyti: roll the cooked kebab in lavash, slice, top with tomato sauce and yogurt.
