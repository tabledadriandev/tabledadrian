# Hazelnut Dukkah with Coriander & Wild Fennel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Egypt's crunch condiment — everything toasted separately to its own point, then crushed (never ground) so it stays rubble; the bread-oil-dukkah ritual sells itself at aperitivo.

## Ingredients (makes ~220 g)
- Hazelnuts — 100 g
- Sesame seeds — 60 g
- Coriander seeds — 25 g
- Cumin seeds — 15 g
- Fennel seeds — 8 g
- Black peppercorns — 4 g
- Dried mint — 2 g
- Flaky sea salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Roast hazelnuts at 160 °C for 10–12 min until the skins split; rub off skins in a towel and cool.
2. Toast sesame in a dry pan to gold, 3 min; set aside. Toast coriander, cumin, fennel and pepper together 2 min; cool.
3. Crush the seeds and spices in a mortar (or 3 pulses in a grinder) to a coarse, sandy rubble.
4. Crush hazelnuts separately to split-pea size.
5. Combine everything with mint and salt. Store airtight 4 weeks — the nuts date it.

## Plating & Service
Serve in a bowl beside good olive oil and warm bread: dip bread in oil, then dukkah. Also over labneh, hummus, roast squash, seared tuna.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Classic uses roasted chickpeas instead of half the nuts (budget version, also great). Pistachios + rose petals make the showpiece jar.
