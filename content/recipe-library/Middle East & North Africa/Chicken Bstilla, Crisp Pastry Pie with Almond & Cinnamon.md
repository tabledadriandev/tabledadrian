# Chicken Bstilla, Crisp Pastry Pie with Almond & Cinnamon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The great sweet-savoury pie of Fez — saffron chicken, lemony egg curd and cinnamon almonds in shattering pastry, dusted with icing sugar; the course that makes guests put phones down.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Chicken thighs, bone-in — 1200 g
- Onions, sliced — 400 g
- Olive oil — 80 ml
- Ground ginger — 5 g
- Saffron, steeped in 30 ml hot water — 0.4 g
- Ground cinnamon — 8 g (divided)
- Fine sea salt — 14 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Coriander and parsley, chopped — 30 g
- Eggs — 300 g (6)
- Lemon juice — 30 ml
- Blanched almonds — 200 g
- Icing sugar — 50 g (divided)
- Orange blossom water — 10 ml
- Warka or phyllo — 300 g
- Melted butter — 100 g

## Method
1. Braise chicken with onions, 60 ml oil, ginger, saffron, 2 g cinnamon, salt, pepper and 200 ml water, 45 min. Shred meat; discard bones.
2. Reduce the braising liquid to 150 ml; over low heat, stir in beaten eggs and lemon, cooking to a thick, dryish curd, 8–10 min. Fold in herbs and chicken.
3. Fry almonds in remaining oil to gold; grind coarsely with 30 g icing sugar, 4 g cinnamon and orange blossom water.
4. Butter a 26 cm pan; overlap pastry sheets like petals with generous overhang, buttering each.
5. Layer: half the almond mixture, all the chicken-egg filling, remaining almonds. Fold the overhang over, cap with two buttered sheets, tucking edges under.
6. Bake at 180 °C for 35–40 min until deep gold; invert onto a tray and bake 10 min more for an even base.

## Plating & Service
Dust with remaining icing sugar and lattice with cinnamon lines. Serve wedges warm — the sugar-cinnamon crust against saffron chicken is the whole argument.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pigeon or duck confit is the grand original. Seafood bstilla (vermicelli, prawns, chermoula) skips the sugar entirely.
