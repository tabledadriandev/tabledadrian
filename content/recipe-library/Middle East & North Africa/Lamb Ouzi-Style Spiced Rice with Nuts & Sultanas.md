# Lamb Ouzi-Style Spiced Rice with Nuts & Sultanas

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The banquet centrepiece of the Gulf — slow-roasted spiced lamb collapsed over baharat rice with fried nuts and sultanas; built for the moment the platter lands and conversation stops.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Lamb shoulder, bone-in — 2200 g
- Gulf baharat — 16 g
- Turmeric — 4 g
- Ground cinnamon — 3 g
- Garlic, grated — 20 g
- Fine sea salt — 26 g
- Olive oil — 60 ml
- Basmati rice, soaked — 600 g
- Onion, fine dice — 200 g
- Cardamom pods — 8
- Cloves — 3
- Bay leaves — 2
- Chicken or lamb stock — 1000 ml
- Flaked almonds — 50 g
- Pine nuts — 30 g
- Sultanas — 60 g
- Neutral oil (for nuts) — 60 ml
- Flat-leaf parsley — 10 g

## Method
1. Rub lamb with baharat, turmeric, cinnamon, garlic, 16 g salt and olive oil; rest overnight.
2. Roast covered with 300 ml water at 150 °C for 3.5–4 h until shreddable; uncover the last 20 min at 220 °C for colour. Rest; skim and keep juices.
3. Rice: fry onion in a little of the nut oil; add cardamom, cloves, bay, drained rice, 10 g salt; add stock plus lamb juices to 1.5× rice volume.
4. Boil 3 min, cover on lowest heat 12 min, rest 10 min.
5. Fry almonds, pine nuts, then sultanas (they balloon in seconds) in the oil at 160 °C.
6. Pull lamb into generous pieces.

## Plating & Service
Rice mountain, lamb draped over, nuts and sultanas raining down, parsley last. Serve with cucumber-mint yogurt and a sharp salad.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Traditionally wrapped in warka for individual parcels — line ramekins with pastry, fill, bake 20 min at 190 °C. Nut-free: fried onion and sultanas only.
