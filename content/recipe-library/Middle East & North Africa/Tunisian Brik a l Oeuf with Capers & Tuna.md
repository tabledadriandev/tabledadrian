# Tunisian Brik a l Oeuf with Capers & Tuna

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The theatre of the runny yolk inside crackling pastry — folded and fried in under two minutes per brik; guests cut in and the yolk floods the tuna.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Warka/brick pastry sheets — 4 (about 120 g)
- Eggs — 4 (about 240 g)
- Tuna in olive oil, drained — 160 g
- Capers, rinsed — 20 g
- Red onion, fine dice — 50 g
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 12 g
- Harissa — 20 g
- Ground coriander — 2 g
- Fine sea salt — 4 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Neutral oil for frying — 800 ml
- Lemon wedges — 4

## Method
1. Mix tuna, capers, onion, parsley, harissa, coriander, salt and pepper.
2. Heat oil in a wide pan to 180 °C — hot enough to set pastry before the egg overcooks.
3. Lay one pastry sheet flat; spoon a quarter of the filling slightly off-centre, make a well, and crack in an egg.
4. Fold into a half-moon, pressing edges; lift immediately with a slotted spatula into the oil.
5. Fry 60–75 seconds per side, basting the top, until blistered gold — the yolk must stay liquid.
6. Drain 30 seconds on a rack; repeat, working one at a time.

## Plating & Service
Serve instantly with a lemon wedge — the brik waits for no table. Warn guests about the yolk; it's the best part.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap tuna for 120 g mashed potato with capers for a vegetarian brik. A pinch of ras el hanout in the filling deepens it.
