# Harees-Style Cracked Wheat & Chicken Porridge with Baharat Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Ramadan's great comfort — wheat and chicken beaten together until they become one silken, savoury porridge; the restraint (salt, wheat, chicken) is the luxury, the baharat butter the only flourish.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Whole cracked wheat (harees wheat or coarse bulgur), soaked overnight — 350 g
- Chicken legs, bone-in — 1200 g
- Water — 2000 ml
- Fine sea salt — 16 g
- Cinnamon stick — 1 (4 g)
- Cardamom pods — 4
- Unsalted butter or ghee — 70 g
- Gulf baharat — 5 g
- Ground cinnamon (to finish) — 1 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Simmer chicken with water, cinnamon stick, cardamom and half the salt, skimming, 45 min. Lift out; strip meat, discard skin and bones, return meat to the broth.
2. Add drained wheat; simmer very gently at 90 °C, stirring every 15 min from the base, for 90–105 min until wheat has burst and thickened.
3. Remove cinnamon and cardamom. Beat vigorously with a wooden paddle (or brief burst with a stick blender) until the mixture is a smooth, stretchy porridge — chicken fibres should vanish into it.
4. Adjust salt and consistency with hot water; it should slump slowly off a spoon.
5. Melt butter with baharat and pepper until foaming.

## Plating & Service
Pool into warm shallow bowls, flood the centre with baharat butter, dust with cinnamon. Serve with dates and strong tea for the full majlis effect.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Lamb harees: neck fillet, 2.5 h. Dairy-free: finish with olive oil warmed with the spices.
