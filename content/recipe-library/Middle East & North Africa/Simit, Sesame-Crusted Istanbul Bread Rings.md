# Simit, Sesame-Crusted Istanbul Bread Rings

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The street ring dipped in grape molasses water before its sesame armour — that dip is what bakes the crust deep mahogany and faintly sweet; twist two ropes for the authentic spiral.

## Ingredients (serves 8, makes 8)
- Strong flour — 600 g
- Instant yeast — 6 g
- Fine sea salt — 12 g
- Sugar — 10 g
- Warm water (35 °C) — 360 ml
- Olive oil — 25 ml
- Grape molasses (pekmez) — 60 g
- Water (for dip) — 120 ml
- Sesame seeds — 200 g

## Method
1. Knead flour, yeast, salt, sugar, water and oil 8–10 min to a smooth, medium-firm dough; prove 60 min.
2. Toast sesame seeds in a dry pan to pale gold; spread on a tray.
3. Whisk pekmez with the 120 ml water in a wide bowl.
4. Divide dough into 16 × 60 g pieces; roll each to a 40 cm rope. Twist pairs together, join the ends into a 12 cm ring, and roll the join smooth.
5. Dip each ring fully in the molasses water, then press both faces into sesame until upholstered.
6. Prove 20 min on trays; bake at 230 °C with a splash of water in a tray below for steam, 15–18 min, to deep reddish brown.

## Plating & Service
Serve barely warm, stacked on a dowel or piled in a basket, with feta, honey, olives and tea — the Istanbul breakfast trinity.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No pekmez? Use 40 g honey + 20 g dark sugar in the dip (not vegan then). Day-old simit toasts into the best croutons for fattoush.
