# Zaalouk, Smoky Moroccan Aubergine & Tomato Salad

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A cooked salad that eats like a confit — aubergine and tomato collapsed together with cumin and paprika until jammy, brightened at the end with lemon and fresh coriander.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Aubergines — 700 g
- Ripe tomatoes, grated (skins discarded) — 400 g
- Garlic, grated — 12 g
- Olive oil — 90 ml
- Ground cumin — 5 g
- Sweet paprika — 6 g
- Cayenne — 1 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g
- Lemon juice — 30 ml
- Coriander, chopped — 15 g
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 10 g
- Green olives, slivered — 40 g

## Method
1. Char aubergines whole over flame or under a 250 °C grill until blackened and soft, 20 min; peel and chop the flesh.
2. In a wide pan, warm 60 ml olive oil with garlic 1 min; add grated tomato, cumin, paprika, cayenne and salt.
3. Simmer briskly 10 min until the tomato darkens and the oil separates.
4. Add aubergine; cook, mashing with a fork, 12–15 min until thick enough to hold a trench.
5. Off heat, beat in lemon juice, herbs and remaining oil.
6. Rest at least 1 h; serve at room temperature.

## Plating & Service
Spread in a shallow tagine dish, dimpled, with olive slivers and coriander; khobz or crusty bread to scoop.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Roast (rather than char) the aubergine at 220 °C for a milder version. A spoon of harissa folded in turns it Tunisian.
