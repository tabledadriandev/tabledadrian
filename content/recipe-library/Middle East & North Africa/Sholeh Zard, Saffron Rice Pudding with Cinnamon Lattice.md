# Sholeh Zard, Saffron Rice Pudding with Cinnamon Lattice

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Persia's golden votive pudding — rice cooked to surrender in saffron water and sugar, no dairy at all, decorated with cinnamon lines and almonds; served cold, it tastes like a temple smells.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Short-grain rice, rinsed — 200 g
- Water — 1600 ml
- Sugar — 280 g
- Saffron, ground, steeped in 60 ml hot water — 0.6 g
- Rose water — 25 ml
- Slivered almonds — 40 g
- Neutral oil or vegan butter — 30 g
- Ground cardamom — 1 g
- Ground cinnamon (décor) — 4 g
- Slivered pistachios (décor) — 20 g

## Method
1. Simmer rice in the water, partially covered, 45–50 min, stirring occasionally, until the grains have burst and the pot looks like loose porridge.
2. Add sugar, oil, cardamom and half the almonds; cook gently 20 min more, stirring — sugar loosens it before it re-thickens.
3. Stir in saffron water and rose water; simmer 15–20 min until the pudding coats a spoon thickly and glows amber-gold.
4. Pour 1.5 cm deep into shallow bowls or one platter; cool, then chill 3 h — it sets to a soft, spoonable jelly.
5. Decorate with cinnamon lines (use a paper stencil for lattice), pistachios and remaining almonds.

## Plating & Service
Serve cold. Traditionally shared out to neighbours as a vow of thanks — on charter, that means the crew mess gets a tray too.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: décor with cinnamon and dried rose petals only. A knob of butter (30 g) instead of oil adds gloss for non-vegan tables.
