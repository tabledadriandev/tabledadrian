# Shish Barak, Lamb Dumplings Poached in Garlic-Mint Yogurt

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Little lamb tortellini of the Levant, poached directly in a stabilised yogurt sauce — the dumplings thicken the sauce with their starch as they cook; finish with sizzled garlic-mint oil.

## Ingredients (serves 6, makes ~50)
- Plain flour — 350 g
- Water — 170 ml
- Fine sea salt — 14 g
- Lamb mince — 300 g
- Onion, fine dice — 80 g
- Baharat — 4 g
- Pine nuts, toasted, chopped — 25 g
- Olive oil — 50 ml
- Greek yogurt — 900 g
- Cornflour — 15 g
- Egg white — 30 g (1)
- Chicken stock or water — 300 ml
- Garlic, minced — 15 g
- Dried mint — 6 g
- Fresh coriander — 10 g

## Method
1. Dough: knead flour, water and 5 g salt 6 min; rest 30 min.
2. Filling: fry onion in 20 ml oil, add lamb, baharat and 5 g salt; cook 8 min, fold in pine nuts, cool.
3. Roll dough to 1.5 mm; cut 5 cm circles. Fill with 5 g each, fold to half-moons, then pinch the corners together around a fingertip — tiny hats.
4. Bake dumplings at 190 °C for 10 min to light gold (traditional, and it stops them bursting).
5. Whisk yogurt with cornflour, egg white and stock; bring to a simmer, stirring one direction, 5 min. Season with remaining salt.
6. Poach dumplings in the yogurt at a gentle simmer 12–15 min until tender.
7. Sizzle garlic in remaining oil to pale gold; add dried mint off heat; swirl over the pot.

## Plating & Service
Deep bowls, dumplings and plenty of sauce, the garlic-mint oil pooled on top, coriander over. Vermicelli rice or nothing — the sauce is the carbohydrate's excuse.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Freeze baked dumplings; poach from frozen (+4 min). Nut-free: omit pine nuts. A pinch of Aleppo pepper in the finishing oil is welcome heresy.
