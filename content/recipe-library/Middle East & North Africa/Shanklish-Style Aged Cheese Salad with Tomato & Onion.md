# Shanklish-Style Aged Cheese Salad with Tomato & Onion

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A Riviera-workable homage to the fermented Syrian cheese — aged goat cheese rolled in za'atar and Aleppo pepper, crumbled over a sharp tomato-onion chop.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Aged crottin or dry goat cheese — 250 g
- Za'atar blend — 10 g
- Aleppo pepper — 6 g
- Ripe tomatoes — 350 g
- Red onion — 80 g
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 12 g
- Mint, sliced — 6 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Fine sea salt — 4 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Roll the cheese firmly in a mix of za'atar and half the Aleppo pepper, pressing the crust on; chill 30 min.
2. Dice tomatoes 8 mm, discarding watery seeds; dice onion 4 mm and rinse briefly.
3. Toss tomato, onion, parsley and mint with lemon, salt, black pepper and 40 ml oil.
4. Spread the salad on a plate; crumble the crusted cheese over in rough nuggets.
5. Finish with remaining oil and Aleppo pepper.

## Plating & Service
Serve at room temperature with warm flatbread — guests fold cheese and salad together. Arak or pastis alongside is traditional and correct.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> True shanklish (if sourced) needs only the salad. For a milder plate use fresh goat cheese and double the za'atar crust.
