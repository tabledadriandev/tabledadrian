# Musakhan, Sumac Chicken on Onion-Soaked Taboon Bread

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Palestine's national dish — chicken roasted with staggering amounts of sumac and olive-oil-melted onions, served on bread that soaks the pan like a savoury sponge.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Chicken legs, whole — 1800 g (6)
- Onions, sliced — 800 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 150 ml
- Sumac — 40 g
- Ground allspice — 4 g
- Ground cinnamon — 2 g
- Fine sea salt — 16 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Taboon or thick flatbreads — 3 (about 400 g)
- Pine nuts — 40 g
- Lemon — 1 (wedges)
- Flat-leaf parsley — 10 g

## Method
1. Season chicken with 10 g salt, pepper, allspice, cinnamon and 10 g of the sumac; rest 1 h.
2. Stew the onions in 120 ml olive oil over low heat, 30–35 min, until utterly soft and sweet but not browned; season with remaining salt and 20 g sumac off the heat.
3. Roast chicken skin-up at 210 °C for 35–40 min until skin is bronzed and the joint reaches 78 °C; reserve pan juices.
4. Fry pine nuts in the remaining 30 ml oil to gold.
5. Lay flatbreads on an oven tray, spread thickly with the sumac onions and half the pan juices; warm at 180 °C for 8 min.
6. Set chicken on the onion bread, spoon over remaining juices, dust with the last 10 g sumac.

## Plating & Service
Serve the whole tray to the table — bread torn and eaten from under the chicken, scattered with pine nuts, parsley and lemon wedges.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Roll leftovers into musakhan wraps and griddle them for lunch. GF: use gluten-free flatbread; the onions carry the dish.
