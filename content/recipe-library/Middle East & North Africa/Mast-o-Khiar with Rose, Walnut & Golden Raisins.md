# Mast-o-Khiar with Rose, Walnut & Golden Raisins

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Persian answer to tzatziki, but perfumed — rose petals, raisins and walnuts turn a yogurt side into something guests photograph.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Greek yogurt — 500 g
- Persian or Lebanese cucumbers — 250 g
- Golden raisins — 40 g
- Walnut pieces, toasted — 40 g
- Dried mint — 4 g
- Fresh dill, chopped — 8 g
- Dried rose petals — 3 g
- Garlic, grated — 3 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g
- Ice water — 20 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 15 ml

## Method
1. Soak raisins in warm water 10 min; drain.
2. Dice cucumber 5 mm (do not grate — it waters out); salt with 2 g and drain 10 min.
3. Whisk yogurt with garlic, dried mint, remaining salt and ice water to a light cream.
4. Fold in cucumber, raisins, dill, most of the walnuts and most of the rose petals.
5. Chill 30 min for the mint to bloom; adjust salt.

## Plating & Service
Serve cold in a footed bowl, strewn with the reserved walnuts and rose, a thread of olive oil. Beside any kebab or spooned over tahdig.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Turn it into abdoogh khiar (chilled soup) by whisking in 200 ml ice water and serving over ice with torn flatbread. Nut-free: omit walnuts, add 20 g toasted pistachio-free seeds.
