# Tunisian Fish Couscous with Harissa Broth

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sfax-style — a red, harissa-warmed broth built on fish bones, grouper steaks poached in it at the last, the grain drinking the brick-coloured liquor; the Mediterranean answer to bouillabaisse.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Grouper, bream or cod steaks — 900 g (6)
- Fish bones and heads, rinsed — 500 g
- Medium couscous — 450 g
- Olive oil — 80 ml
- Onion, chopped — 150 g
- Garlic, crushed — 15 g
- Tomato paste — 50 g
- Harissa — 40 g
- Ground caraway — 4 g
- Ground coriander — 4 g
- Turmeric — 3 g
- Potatoes, quartered — 300 g
- Carrots, batons — 200 g
- Green peppers, quartered — 150 g
- Fine sea salt — 20 g
- Water — 1800 ml
- Lemon — 1

## Method
1. Sweat onion and garlic in 40 ml oil; add tomato paste and harissa, frying 3 min to a dark brick.
2. Add spices, fish bones and water; simmer 25 min, then strain, pressing. Season with 12 g salt.
3. Return broth; add potatoes and carrots, 15 min; peppers, 10 min more.
4. Steam couscous over the broth twice, raking with 200 ml salted water between; finish with remaining oil.
5. Season fish steaks with salt; slide into the barely-simmering broth (90 °C) for 8–10 min to 55 °C core.
6. Check broth: it should be spicy, faintly sour; adjust with lemon.

## Plating & Service
Grain in wide bowls, fish and vegetables on top, broth ladled generously, lemon wedge each. Extra harissa for the brave.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Octopus (pre-braised) or prawns can join the fish. GF: serve the same broth over rice or millet.
