# Zurbian, Yemeni Layered Lamb & Potato Rice

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Aden's biryani cousin — yogurt-marinated lamb and potatoes steamed under saffron rice in sealed layers; less chilli, more cardamom and rose than its Indian relative.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Lamb shoulder, 4 cm cubes — 1400 g
- Greek yogurt — 250 g
- Garlic, grated — 20 g
- Ginger, grated — 20 g
- Zurbian spice: ground cardamom 4 g, cumin 5 g, coriander 5 g, turmeric 4 g, cinnamon 3 g, clove 1 g, black pepper 3 g
- Tomatoes, chopped — 250 g
- Green chilli — 10 g
- Fine sea salt — 24 g
- Potatoes, thick wedges — 500 g
- Basmati rice, soaked — 600 g
- Neutral oil or ghee — 90 ml
- Saffron, steeped in 40 ml hot water — 0.4 g
- Rose water — 8 ml
- Fried onions — 80 g
- Coriander and mint — 20 g
- Flaked almonds, fried — 40 g

## Method
1. Marinate lamb in yogurt, garlic, ginger, spice mix, tomatoes, chilli and 12 g salt, 4–24 h.
2. Tip lamb and marinade into a heavy pot with 40 ml oil; cook covered at a gentle simmer 75 min. Add potatoes; 20 min more — sauce should be thick.
3. Parboil rice in heavily salted water (12 g) 5 min; drain.
4. Layer rice over the lamb; drizzle saffron water, rose water and remaining oil; scatter half the fried onions and herbs between and on top.
5. Seal with foil and lid; steam on lowest heat (or 160 °C oven) 30–35 min.
6. Rest 10 min; fold layers together only twice at the table.

## Plating & Service
Serve from the pot or inverted onto a platter, crowned with remaining fried onions, almonds and herbs. A green chilli-coriander sahawiq alongside is traditional.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken zurbian: legs, 40 min base cook. Nut-free: skip almonds. The rose is signature — do not omit.
