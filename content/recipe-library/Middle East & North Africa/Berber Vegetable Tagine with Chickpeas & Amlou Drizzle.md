# Berber Vegetable Tagine with Chickpeas & Amlou Drizzle

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Vegetables stacked in the tagine like a tent — steam does the cooking, no stirring allowed; a thread of almond amlou at the end brings unexpected Atlas-mountain richness to a vegan main.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Waxy potatoes, thick slices — 400 g
- Carrots, batons — 300 g
- Courgettes, logs — 300 g
- Red pepper, wide strips — 200 g
- Ripe tomatoes, sliced — 300 g
- Cooked chickpeas — 300 g
- Onion, sliced — 150 g
- Garlic, sliced — 12 g
- Olive oil — 80 ml
- Ras el hanout — 8 g
- Ground cumin — 4 g
- Sweet paprika — 5 g
- Fine sea salt — 13 g
- Water — 200 ml
- Almond butter + argan or olive oil + honey-alternative (amlou: 40 g + 20 ml + 10 g maple)
- Coriander — 12 g

## Method
1. Whisk 60 ml oil with ras el hanout, cumin, paprika, garlic and salt into a marinade; toss each vegetable in it separately.
2. Layer in the tagine: onion base, then potatoes, carrots, chickpeas, courgettes, peppers, tomato slices as the roof.
3. Add water down the side and the remaining oil over the top.
4. Cover; cook on the lowest flame (heat diffuser) or 170 °C oven for 55–60 min. No peeking before 45 min.
5. Amlou drizzle: whisk almond butter, argan oil and maple to a pourable ribbon.
6. Rest 5 min; test the potatoes — every layer should be tender from steam alone.

## Plating & Service
Uncover at the table; thread the amlou over, coriander last. Crusty bread and preserved-lemon yogurt (for non-vegans) alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: skip amlou, finish with green olives and preserved lemon. Winter build: squash, turnip, fennel, prunes.
