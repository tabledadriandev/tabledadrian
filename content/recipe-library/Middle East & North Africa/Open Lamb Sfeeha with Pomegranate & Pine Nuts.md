# Open Lamb Sfeeha with Pomegranate & Pine Nuts

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Baalbek-style open meat pies — raw spiced lamb pressed onto soft dough and blasted in a hot oven so the meat roasts in its own fat; two bites of perfection.

## Ingredients (serves 6, makes 18)
- Strong flour — 350 g
- Instant yeast — 5 g
- Sugar — 8 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g
- Olive oil — 35 ml
- Warm water (35 °C) — 200 ml
- Lamb mince (15% fat) — 350 g
- Onion, grated and drained — 100 g
- Tomato, grated and drained — 100 g
- Tahini — 30 g
- Pomegranate molasses — 25 g
- Baharat — 5 g
- Pine nuts — 30 g
- Greek yogurt (in filling) — 40 g

## Method
1. Knead flour, yeast, sugar, salt, oil and water 8 min to a supple dough; prove covered at ~26 °C for 60 min until doubled.
2. Mix lamb with onion, tomato, tahini, yogurt, pomegranate molasses, baharat and 6 g salt — the tahini and yogurt keep it juicy.
3. Divide dough into 30 g balls; rest 10 min, then flatten to 9 cm discs on oiled trays.
4. Press 30 g of filling onto each disc right to the rim, dimpling with fingertips; stud with pine nuts.
5. Bake at 250 °C (stone or steel preferred) for 8–10 min until the rims blister and the meat sizzles.
6. Brush rims with olive oil out of the oven.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm, stacked casually, with lemon wedges and a bowl of yogurt. They reheat perfectly at 180 °C for 4 min.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Aleppo version: add 30 g finely diced red pepper and 10 g biber salçası to the meat. Beef works; keep 15% fat minimum.
