# Kousa Mahshi, Stuffed Courgettes in Mint-Garlic Broth

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Pale Lebanese courgettes hollowed to 4 mm walls, filled loosely with rice and lamb (it swells), and simmered in a tomato broth spiked at the end with sizzled garlic and dried mint.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Small pale courgettes (or round) — 1400 g (about 12)
- Short-grain rice, rinsed — 200 g
- Lamb mince — 350 g
- Ground allspice — 4 g
- Ground cinnamon — 2 g
- Fine sea salt — 16 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Olive oil — 60 ml
- Tomato passata — 500 g
- Water or light stock — 700 ml
- Garlic, minced — 15 g
- Dried mint — 6 g
- Lemon juice — 30 ml

## Method
1. Trim courgette tops; core with a manakra or apple corer to 4 mm walls, keeping bases intact. (Save cores for fritters.)
2. Mix rice, lamb, allspice, cinnamon, 8 g salt, pepper and 20 ml oil.
3. Fill each courgette two-thirds only — rice needs room to swell.
4. Lay snugly in a wide pot, openings inward. Pour over passata, water and 8 g salt; weight with a plate.
5. Bring to a simmer, then cook gently at 90–95 °C, covered, 50–60 min until a skewer passes easily and rice is tender.
6. Sizzle garlic in remaining oil to pale gold, add dried mint off heat, and stir into the broth with lemon; 5 min more.

## Plating & Service
Serve three per bowl with plenty of broth, spoon and fork. The broth is half the dish — bread for what remains.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: fill with rice, chickpeas, tomato and herbs; simmer 45 min. Mixed pots with stuffed vine leaves layered beneath are traditional and better.
