# Bazlama, Yogurt Griddle Breads Puffed in a Skillet

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Anatolian village bread — yogurt in the dough gives a moist, tangy crumb that puffs in a dry skillet with leopard spots; no oven, no stone, perfect for the galley.

## Ingredients (serves 6, makes 6)
- Strong flour — 500 g
- Instant yeast — 7 g
- Sugar — 10 g
- Fine sea salt — 10 g
- Greek yogurt — 150 g
- Warm water (35 °C) — 230 ml
- Olive oil — 20 ml
- Melted butter, to brush — 40 g
- Dried oregano or za'atar — 3 g

## Method
1. Knead flour, yeast, sugar, salt, yogurt, water and oil 8 min to a soft, smooth dough.
2. Prove 60 min until doubled.
3. Divide into 6 balls; rest 15 min, then roll to 16 cm rounds, 8 mm thick.
4. Prove on the counter 15 min — this second rise is what makes them balloon.
5. Cook in a dry cast-iron skillet over medium heat (surface ~220 °C), 2–3 min per side, flipping when big bubbles rise; press edges gently to chase the puff around.
6. Brush hot with melted butter and a pinch of oregano; stack in a towel.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm and floppy for wrapping kebabs, or split like muffins for breakfast with honey and clotted cream.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: coconut yogurt and oil brush. Knead in 40 g crumbled feta and dill for a stuffed-crumb version.
