# Three-Cheese Pide with Egg & Nigella

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Black Sea boat filled with molten cheese and a just-set egg cracked in for the final two minutes — carve at the table and stir the yolk through while it steams.

## Ingredients (serves 6, makes 3 large)
- Strong flour — 500 g
- Instant yeast — 6 g
- Sugar — 8 g
- Fine sea salt — 10 g
- Warm milk (35 °C) — 180 ml
- Warm water — 130 ml
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Mozzarella, grated — 250 g
- Feta, crumbled — 150 g
- Kaşar or gruyère, grated — 100 g
- Eggs — 3 (about 180 g)
- Egg yolk + 10 ml milk (wash) — 28 g
- Nigella seeds — 5 g
- Unsalted butter, melted — 30 g
- Pul biber — 3 g

## Method
1. Knead flour, yeast, sugar, salt, milk, water and oil 8 min; prove 60–90 min until doubled.
2. Mix the three cheeses. Heat a baking steel at 270 °C.
3. Divide dough in 3; roll each to a 35 × 16 cm oval. Spread cheese down the centre, fold edges into a boat, twist the ends hard, and crimp.
4. Egg-wash the rims; scatter nigella.
5. Bake 8–10 min until rims are bronzed and cheese bubbles; crack an egg into each boat and bake 2–3 min more — whites set, yolks liquid.
6. Brush rims with melted butter; dust the cheese with pul biber.

## Plating & Service
Slice into bands at the table, dragging each slice through the yolk. Best 3 minutes out of the oven, so bake as guests sit.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 100 g cooked spinach under the cheese. Sucuk coins on top before baking turn it carnivorous.
