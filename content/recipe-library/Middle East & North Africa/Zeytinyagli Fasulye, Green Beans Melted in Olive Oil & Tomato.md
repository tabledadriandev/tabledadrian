# Zeytinyagli Fasulye, Green Beans Melted in Olive Oil & Tomato

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Turkish olive-oil braise philosophy: cook green beans well past crisp-tender until silky and sweet, then serve them cold — patience replaces technique.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Flat green beans (or fine beans) — 700 g
- Onion, sliced — 180 g
- Ripe tomatoes, grated — 350 g
- Tomato paste — 15 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 120 ml
- Sugar — 8 g
- Fine sea salt — 9 g
- Water — 200 ml
- Garlic, sliced — 10 g
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Dill, chopped — 8 g

## Method
1. Top and string beans; cut into 5 cm lengths.
2. Sweat onion in 80 ml of the olive oil over medium heat 8 min without browning; add garlic 1 min.
3. Add tomato paste, grated tomato, sugar and salt; cook 5 min.
4. Add beans and water, press a paper cartouche on top, and braise at the gentlest simmer (90 °C) for 40–45 min until utterly tender — no bite left.
5. Off heat, add remaining oil and lemon; cool in the pan.
6. Chill overnight; the dish is designed for tomorrow.

## Plating & Service
Serve cool, not fridge-cold, mounded with its juices and a shower of dill. Lemon wedge and crusty bread mandatory.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Same method suits leeks (zeytinyağlı pırasa) with 100 g diced carrot. A pinch of pul biber in the sweat adds gentle heat.
