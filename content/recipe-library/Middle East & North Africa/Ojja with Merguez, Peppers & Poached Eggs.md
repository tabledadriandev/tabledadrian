# Ojja with Merguez, Peppers & Poached Eggs

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Tunisia's fiercer shakshuka — harissa-forward, rough-cut peppers, merguez rendering its paprika fat into the sauce, eggs barely set; brunch with authority.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Merguez sausages, thick coins — 300 g
- Green peppers, rough 2 cm cut — 250 g
- Ripe tomatoes, chopped — 500 g
- Garlic, sliced — 12 g
- Harissa — 35 g
- Tomato paste — 20 g
- Ground caraway — 3 g
- Ground coriander — 3 g
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Fine sea salt — 7 g
- Eggs — 5 (about 300 g)
- Flat-leaf parsley — 8 g
- Crusty bread — 250 g

## Method
1. Render merguez coins in a wide pan over medium heat 5 min; remove, leaving the red fat.
2. Add olive oil and peppers; fry 8 min until blistered and softening.
3. Add garlic, tomato paste, harissa, caraway and coriander; fry 2 min.
4. Add tomatoes and salt; simmer 10–12 min to a loose, rugged sauce — ojja is wetter than shakshuka.
5. Return merguez; crack eggs into gaps, cover, and cook at a low simmer 5–6 min.
6. Rest 1 min; break the yolks into the sauce as you serve — that is the ojja way.

## Plating & Service
Pan to table, parsley over, bread torn and dealt like cards. Strong coffee after.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Prawn ojja: swap merguez for 300 g prawns added in the last 4 min (adds shellfish). Vegetarian: brown 150 g halloumi cubes instead.
