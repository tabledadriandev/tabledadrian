# Lamb Tagine with Prunes, Sesame & Fried Almonds

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The celebration tagine — lamb collapsed in saffron-ginger onions, prunes glazed separately in cinnamon and honey so they lacquer instead of dissolving; sesame and fried almonds for the final jewelled crunch.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Lamb shoulder, 5 cm pieces, bone-in preferred — 1600 g
- Onions, grated — 350 g
- Garlic, grated — 12 g
- Ground ginger — 6 g
- Saffron, steeped in 30 ml hot water — 0.3 g
- Ground cinnamon — 4 g (divided)
- Olive oil — 60 ml
- Fine sea salt — 15 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Water — 500 ml
- Prunes, pitted — 250 g
- Honey — 50 g
- Blanched almonds — 60 g
- Sesame seeds, toasted — 15 g
- Neutral oil (for almonds) — 100 ml

## Method
1. Toss lamb with onion, garlic, ginger, 2 g cinnamon, salt, pepper and olive oil.
2. Set in a heavy pot over medium heat, uncovered, 10 min, turning — the onions must sweat, not brown.
3. Add water and saffron; braise covered at the barest simmer (or 150 °C oven) 2–2.5 h until the meat leaves the bone at a nudge.
4. Prunes: simmer with honey, remaining cinnamon and 100 ml of the braise liquid, 12–15 min, until glossy and swollen.
5. Reduce the lamb sauce uncovered to a rich, clinging consistency; return prunes gently.
6. Fry almonds in oil at 160 °C to gold, 3 min; drain.

## Plating & Service
Lamb centre-plate, prunes clustered on top, sauce spooned over, then sesame and hot fried almonds. Bread, always.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Apricots + orange blossom water (5 ml) replace prunes for a lighter register. Beef cheeks braise magnificently in this sauce, 3 h.
