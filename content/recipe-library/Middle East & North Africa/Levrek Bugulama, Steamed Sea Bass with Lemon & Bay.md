# Levrek Bugulama, Steamed Sea Bass with Lemon & Bay

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Bosphorus fish-house cure for excess — sea bass steam-braised over vegetables with lemon, bay and good oil; ten honest minutes of cooking, clean as sea air.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Sea bass fillets, skin on — 640 g (4 × 160 g)
- Waxy potatoes, 5 mm slices — 300 g
- Celery heart, sliced — 80 g
- Carrot, thin rounds — 100 g
- Shallots, sliced — 80 g
- Cherry tomatoes, halved — 150 g
- Lemon — 1 (half juiced ~25 ml, half sliced)
- Bay leaves — 2
- Black peppercorns, cracked — 2 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- Fine sea salt — 9 g
- Water or light fish stock — 200 ml
- Dill — 8 g

## Method
1. Layer potatoes, carrot, celery and shallots in a wide shallow pan; season with 5 g salt, add water and 30 ml oil.
2. Cover and simmer 12 min until potatoes are nearly tender.
3. Season fillets with remaining salt; lay skin-up over the vegetables with tomatoes, lemon slices, bay and peppercorns.
4. Cover and steam-braise at a gentle simmer 7–9 min until the fish reaches 54–56 °C and flakes reluctantly.
5. Off heat, add lemon juice and remaining oil, tilting the pan to emulsify the liquor into a light sauce.

## Plating & Service
Fish over its vegetables in warm shallow bowls, broth spooned around, dill over everything. A dish that flatters the fish and the guest's waistline equally.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Any white fish or even salmon works; adjust to 56 °C core. Add 50 ml raki to the broth for the full meyhane perfume (burns off).
