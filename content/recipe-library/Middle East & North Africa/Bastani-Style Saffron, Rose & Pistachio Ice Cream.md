# Bastani-Style Saffron, Rose & Pistachio Ice Cream

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Persian bastani's trademark is frozen clotted-cream shards folded through a saffron-rose custard — they hit the palate as cold butter-cream flakes; salep is faked here with a whisper of cornflour stretch.

## Ingredients (serves 8, makes ~1.2 L)
- Whole milk — 500 ml
- Double cream — 350 ml (plus 100 ml for the frozen shards)
- Egg yolks — 90 g (5)
- Sugar — 150 g
- Cornflour — 8 g
- Saffron, ground, steeped in 30 ml hot milk — 0.5 g
- Rose water — 20 ml
- Fine sea salt — 1 g
- Pistachio kernels, chopped — 70 g

## Method
1. Shards first: whip the 100 ml cream to soft peaks, spread 3 mm thick on parchment, freeze hard, then snap into flakes; hold frozen.
2. Whisk yolks, sugar, cornflour and salt. Heat milk and 350 ml cream to 80 °C; temper into the yolks, return, and cook stirring to 83 °C until it coats a spoon.
3. Stir in the saffron infusion; chill the custard fully (ideally overnight — the colour deepens).
4. Add rose water just before churning (it fades if added early).
5. Churn to soft-serve; fold in pistachios and the frozen cream shards in the last turns.
6. Freeze 4 h to scoop.

## Plating & Service
Two scoops between thin wafers for the Tehran "bastani sandwich", or a single quenelle with rose petals and pistachio for plated service.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 40 g sour cherry compote ripple for acid balance. No churner: freeze in a tray, beating every 45 min, three rounds.
