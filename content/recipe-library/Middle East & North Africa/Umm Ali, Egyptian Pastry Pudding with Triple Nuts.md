# Umm Ali, Egyptian Pastry Pudding with Triple Nuts

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Egypt's bread pudding built on crisp puff pastry instead of stale bread — soaked in sweet spiced milk, capped with cream, and grilled until the peaks scorch; ten minutes of work, standing ovation.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- All-butter puff pastry — 400 g
- Whole milk — 900 ml
- Double cream — 250 ml (150 ml in, 100 ml on top)
- Sugar — 130 g
- Vanilla — 4 g
- Ground cinnamon — 2 g
- Ground cardamom — 1 g
- Fine sea salt — 1 g
- Flaked almonds — 50 g
- Pistachios, chopped — 40 g
- Hazelnuts, chopped — 40 g
- Sultanas — 50 g
- Desiccated coconut — 25 g

## Method
1. Bake the puff pastry sheet at 200 °C for 20–22 min until fully risen, deep gold and dry; cool and break into rough shards.
2. Toast all nuts at 160 °C for 6–8 min.
3. Heat milk with 150 ml cream, sugar, vanilla, spices and salt to a simmer.
4. Layer pastry shards, most of the nuts, sultanas and coconut in a 26 cm baking dish; pour the hot milk over and rest 5 min to soak.
5. Pour remaining cream over the surface in ribbons.
6. Grill at 240 °C for 8–10 min until the peaks are scorched bronze and the cream bubbles.

## Plating & Service
Serve hot with a big spoon, straight from the dish, remaining nuts over — half custard, half crisp, entirely gone in minutes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Croissants (day-old, toasted) replace puff beautifully. Lighter: halve the cream and finish with flaked almonds only.
