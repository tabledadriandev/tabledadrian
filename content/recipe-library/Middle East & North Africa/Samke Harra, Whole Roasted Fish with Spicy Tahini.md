# Samke Harra, Whole Roasted Fish with Spicy Tahini

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Tripoli's masterpiece — whole fish roasted then cloaked in a warm chilli-coriander tahini that must be emulsified with the fish's own roasting juices.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Whole sea bass or bream, scaled and gutted — 1800 g (2 fish)
- Fine sea salt — 16 g
- Olive oil — 80 ml
- Lemon, sliced — 1 (100 g)
- Coriander, chopped — 50 g
- Garlic — 25 g
- Green chilli — 25 g
- Red chilli — 15 g
- Ground cumin — 4 g
- Tahini — 200 g
- Lemon juice — 80 ml
- Warm water — 150 ml
- Walnuts, toasted, chopped — 50 g
- Pine nuts, toasted — 25 g

## Method
1. Score fish three times per side; season inside and out with salt, stuff cavities with lemon slices, rub with 40 ml oil. Rest 20 min.
2. Roast on a rack at 210 °C for 22–28 min until 58 °C at the bone; collect any juices.
3. Meanwhile pound (or pulse) garlic, chillies and half the coriander with 4 g salt to a coarse paste; fry gently in remaining oil with cumin, 3 min, until fragrant.
4. Whisk tahini with lemon juice — it will seize — then loosen with warm water and the roasting juices to a warm, pourable sauce.
5. Fold the chilli paste into the tahini; heat gently to 65 °C, never boiling (it splits).
6. Rest fish 5 min; peel back the top skin.

## Plating & Service
Lay the fish on a warm platter, spoon the harra sauce generously over the flesh, and finish with walnuts, pine nuts and remaining coriander. Vermicelli rice alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Works with thick cod or halibut fillets (roast 12 min at 200 °C). Nut-free: finish with fried coriander and lemon zest only.
