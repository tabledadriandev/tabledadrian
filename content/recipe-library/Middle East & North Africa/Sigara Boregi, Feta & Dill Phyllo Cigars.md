# Sigara Boregi, Feta & Dill Phyllo Cigars

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Shatter-crisp cigars that fry in 3 minutes from frozen — the make-ahead canapé that has never once come back to the galley uneaten.

## Ingredients (serves 6, makes 18)
- Yufka or phyllo sheets — 270 g
- Feta — 300 g
- Ricotta — 80 g
- Egg yolk — 18 g (1)
- Dill, chopped — 12 g
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 10 g
- Lemon zest — 3 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Flour paste (flour + water 1:1) — 40 g
- Neutral oil for frying — 1000 ml
- Nigella seeds — 4 g
- Honey (optional, to serve) — 30 g

## Method
1. Mash feta with ricotta, yolk, herbs, lemon zest and pepper — leave it rubbly, not smooth.
2. Cut phyllo into long triangles about 12 cm at the base. Keep under a barely damp cloth.
3. Lay 20 g filling in a line along the base, fold in the edges, roll to a tight cigar, and seal the tip with flour paste.
4. Rest seam-down 10 min (or freeze on a tray for later — fry from frozen, add 1 min).
5. Fry at 175 °C in batches, turning, 2.5–3 min until evenly deep gold.
6. Drain on a rack, scatter nigella seeds while glistening.

## Plating & Service
Stack log-cabin style on a warm plate; a saucer of honey with pul biber alongside converts sceptics. Serve within 10 min.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Spinach version: replace ricotta with 150 g squeezed cooked spinach. Bake at 200 °C for 15 min brushed with oil for a lighter service.
