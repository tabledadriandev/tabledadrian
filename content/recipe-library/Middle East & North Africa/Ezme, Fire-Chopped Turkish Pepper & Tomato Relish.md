# Ezme, Fire-Chopped Turkish Pepper & Tomato Relish

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Everything chopped to a wet, glistening rubble by hand — a Gaziantep kebab-house staple that doubles as the sharpest thing on a grilled-meat table.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Ripe tomatoes — 400 g
- Red romano pepper — 120 g
- Green chilli — 20 g
- Red onion — 80 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 25 g
- Garlic clove — 4 g
- Tomato paste — 20 g
- Pomegranate molasses — 25 g
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 50 ml
- Pul biber (Aleppo pepper) — 6 g
- Dried mint — 2 g
- Fine sea salt — 7 g

## Method
1. Deseed tomatoes (keep flesh only) and chop with a heavy knife to 3 mm confetti; drain in a sieve 10 min.
2. Chop pepper, chilli, onion, parsley and garlic to the same fineness — a mezzaluna helps; never a food processor, which foams it.
3. Beat tomato paste with pomegranate molasses, lemon, oil, pul biber, dried mint and salt into a dressing.
4. Fold everything together; the texture should be a spoonable salsa that mounds.
5. Rest 30 min at room temperature; drain excess liquid once more and re-season before serving.

## Plating & Service
Spread on a small flat plate with a rim of olive oil, dusted with pul biber. Compulsory beside Adana kebab and warm pide.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> For a smoky ezme, char the pepper and chilli over flame first. Heat scales with the chilli — swap in 5 g biber salçası for a deeper, milder version.
