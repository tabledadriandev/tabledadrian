# Chermoula, The Green Marinade of the Maghreb

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Fish's best friend from Tangier to Sfax — coriander-forward, cumin-warm, soured with lemon and preserved lemon; pound it coarse for marinades, blend it smooth for plating.

## Ingredients (makes ~280 g)
- Coriander (leaves and stems) — 90 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 40 g
- Garlic cloves — 15 g
- Ground cumin — 8 g
- Sweet paprika — 8 g
- Ground ginger — 2 g
- Cayenne — 1 g
- Preserved lemon rind — 20 g
- Lemon juice — 40 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 100 ml
- Fine sea salt — 7 g
- Saffron, steeped in 10 ml hot water (optional) — 0.1 g

## Method
1. Pound garlic with salt to a paste in a large mortar (or pulse in a processor).
2. Add herbs in handfuls, pounding/pulsing to a coarse, wet rubble — never a smooth purée for marinade use.
3. Work in cumin, paprika, ginger, cayenne and the preserved lemon, minced.
4. Stream in lemon juice, saffron water, then olive oil.
5. Rest 30 min. Keeps 4 days chilled under a film of oil; freezes perfectly in cubes.

## Plating & Service
Marinade for fish tagine and grilled sardines (30 min–2 h); dressing for the carrot salad; spooned fresh over anything charred.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Red chermoula: double paprika, add 20 g tomato paste — better for lamb. No preserved lemon? Add zest of 1 lemon and 2 g extra salt.
