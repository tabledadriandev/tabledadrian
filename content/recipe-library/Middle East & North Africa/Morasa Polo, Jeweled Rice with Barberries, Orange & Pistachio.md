# Morasa Polo, Jeweled Rice with Barberries, Orange & Pistachio

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The wedding rice — five "jewels" prepared separately (each has its own tolerance for heat) and set over saffron chelow at the last moment so every gem keeps its colour and bite.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Basmati rice — 600 g
- Fine sea salt — 42 g (mostly parboil)
- Neutral oil — 70 ml
- Saffron, steeped in 60 ml hot water — 0.5 g
- Barberries — 50 g
- Orange peel, fine julienne — 60 g (2 oranges)
- Sugar — 60 g
- Carrots, fine julienne — 200 g
- Slivered pistachios — 50 g
- Slivered almonds — 50 g
- Golden raisins — 50 g
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Ground cardamom — 1 g

## Method
1. Blanch orange julienne three times from cold water to remove bitterness; then candy gently in 40 g sugar + 60 ml water, 10 min; drain.
2. Sauté carrot julienne in 20 g butter with remaining sugar and cardamom, 5 min, to glossy ribbons.
3. Soak raisins 10 min; warm barberries in 20 g butter with a pinch of sugar 60 seconds only.
4. Toast both nuts at 150 °C for 6 min.
5. Cook chelow: wash, soak, parboil rice 5 min in salted water; drain, steam in the oiled pot with towel-wrapped lid 40 min. Stain a quarter of the finished rice with saffron water.
6. Fold a third of every jewel through the white rice with remaining butter, melted.

## Plating & Service
Mound the rice, cap with the saffron quarter, and arrange the remaining jewels in deliberate stripes down the slopes. Serve beside joojeh or lamb shank — applause is standard.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: oil instead of butter throughout. Prep every jewel a day ahead; assemble in 5 minutes at service.
