# Grilled Octopus, Harissa Butter & Preserved Lemon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Octopus braised to tenderness in aromatic liquor, chilled to set, then charred hard and glossed with harissa butter — North African fire meets Riviera plating.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Octopus, cleaned — 1500 g
- Onion, halved — 150 g
- Bay leaves — 2
- Coriander seeds — 5 g
- Orange peel — 1 strip (6 g)
- Fine sea salt — 12 g
- Unsalted butter — 80 g
- Harissa (see condiments) — 40 g
- Preserved lemon rind, fine dice — 25 g
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 10 g
- Lemon juice — 20 ml

## Method
1. Simmer octopus with onion, bay, coriander seeds, orange peel and salt in water at 90–95 °C (never boiling) for 60–75 min, until a knife slides into the thickest tentacle.
2. Cool in the liquor 30 min, then drain, portion into tentacles, oil lightly and chill uncovered 2 h — a dry, cold surface chars best.
3. Beat butter with harissa and half the preserved lemon.
4. Grill tentacles over fierce heat (250 °C plancha or coals), 2–3 min per side, until blistered and crisp at the tips.
5. Off the heat, roll the tentacles in the harissa butter to glaze; add lemon juice.

## Plating & Service
Curl tentacles on warm plates, spoon over remaining glaze, scatter preserved lemon and parsley. A few leaves of rocket dressed in the plate juices complete it.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free: emulsify harissa into warm olive oil instead of butter. The braise liquor, reduced, makes a stellar base for a seafood couscous broth.
