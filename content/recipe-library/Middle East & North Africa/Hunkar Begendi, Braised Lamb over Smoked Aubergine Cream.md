# Hunkar Begendi, Braised Lamb over Smoked Aubergine Cream

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> "The Sultan approved" — a slow tomato-braised lamb served over hünkar's real secret: a smoked aubergine béchamel mounted with kaşar cheese, silky as pommes purée.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Lamb shoulder, 4 cm cubes — 1200 g
- Onion, diced — 200 g
- Garlic, sliced — 12 g
- Tomato paste — 40 g
- Ripe tomatoes, grated — 400 g
- Olive oil — 50 ml
- Dried oregano — 3 g
- Fine sea salt — 18 g
- Water — 500 ml
- Aubergines — 900 g
- Unsalted butter — 50 g
- Plain flour — 40 g
- Whole milk, warm — 350 ml
- Kaşar or gruyère, grated — 80 g
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Flat-leaf parsley — 8 g

## Method
1. Brown lamb in oil in batches; remove. Sweat onion 8 min, add garlic and tomato paste, 2 min.
2. Return lamb with grated tomato, oregano, 10 g salt and water; braise covered at 150 °C oven (or bare simmer) for 2–2.5 h until fork-soft, uncovering the last 30 min to reduce to a clinging sauce.
3. Char aubergines whole over flame until collapsed, ~20 min; peel meticulously, drain 15 min, chop, and toss with lemon.
4. Make a blond roux: butter and flour, 3 min over medium. Whisk in warm milk to a thick béchamel, 5 min.
5. Beat in the aubergine, cooking 5 min, then cheese and remaining salt off the heat — taste; it should be smoky first, cheesy second.

## Plating & Service
A wide swoosh of begendi, the dark lamb and its sauce in the centre, parsley over. White tablecloth food from a one-pot tradition.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: thicken with 20 g rice flour. The begendi under grilled prawns or roast cauliflower is a menu unlock.
