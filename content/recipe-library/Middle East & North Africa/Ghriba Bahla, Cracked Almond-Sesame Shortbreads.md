# Ghriba Bahla, Cracked Almond-Sesame Shortbreads

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Morocco's crackled tea cookie — an almond-sesame dough that must fissure across the dome as it bakes; the cracks are the proof of a properly soft, barely-bound dough.

## Ingredients (serves 12, makes ~26)
- Ground almonds — 250 g
- Sesame seeds, toasted and half-ground — 80 g
- Icing sugar — 130 g
- Baking powder — 5 g
- Fine sea salt — 2 g
- Lemon zest — 4 g
- Eggs — 100 g (2)
- Neutral oil — 30 ml
- Orange blossom water — 12 ml
- Icing sugar (coating) — 60 g
- Blanched almonds (tops) — 26 (about 30 g)

## Method
1. Whisk ground almonds, sesame (half whole, half ground), icing sugar, baking powder, salt and zest.
2. Beat eggs with oil and orange blossom water; fold in to a soft, sticky dough — it should barely hold a ball. Rest 20 min.
3. With damp hands, roll 22 g balls; drop each into icing sugar and coat heavily.
4. Set on parchment 4 cm apart; press a blanched almond into each crown — the press starts the crack.
5. Bake at 170 °C for 16–18 min until risen, fissured and pale gold at the cracks; centres stay soft.
6. Cool on the tray 10 min (they set as they cool).

## Plating & Service
Pile beside mint tea poured from height. Chewy centre, crisp shell, snowline of sugar in every crack.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Coconut ghriba: replace sesame with 80 g desiccated coconut. Naturally gluten-free — flag it for the table that asks.
