# Arayes, Grill-Charred Pita Stuffed with Spiced Lamb

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Raw kofta pressed thin inside pita, grilled until the fat crisps the bread from within — the best meat-to-crunch ratio in the Levant, ideal for a deck barbecue.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Pita breads — 3 large (about 200 g)
- Lamb mince (20% fat) — 500 g
- Onion, grated and drained — 80 g
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 20 g
- Tomato, grated and drained — 60 g
- Baharat — 6 g
- Ground cinnamon — 1 g
- Aleppo pepper — 4 g
- Fine sea salt — 9 g
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Tahini — 80 g
- Lemon juice — 35 ml
- Cold water — 60 ml

## Method
1. Knead lamb with onion, tomato, parsley, spices and salt for 2 min until tacky.
2. Halve pitas into pockets. Spread 90 g of filling inside each half in an even 8 mm layer, right to the edges, pressing flat.
3. Whisk tahini with lemon, water and a pinch of salt to a pourable sauce.
4. Brush both faces of each araye with olive oil.
5. Grill over medium-hot coals or a plancha at 200 °C, 5–6 min per side, pressing gently, until bread is deeply charred and meat reads 70 °C at the core.
6. Rest 2 min, then cut into wedges.

## Plating & Service
Fan the wedges cut-side up on a board, drizzle tahini sauce across, scatter parsley. Serve with pickled chillies.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Beef or a 50/50 lamb-beef blend works; add 40 g crumbled feta to the filling for richness. GF: use gluten-free flatbread folded over the filling.
