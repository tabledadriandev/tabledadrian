# Couscous Royale-Style Lamb, Chicken & Merguez

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The maghrebi feast via Marseille — three meats, one saffron broth, hand-rolled-style steamed grain; a proper royale is logistics, so this card sequences everything to land together.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Lamb shoulder, large chunks — 800 g
- Chicken legs, halved — 900 g
- Merguez sausages — 500 g
- Medium couscous — 600 g
- Olive oil — 80 ml
- Onions, chopped — 250 g
- Tomatoes, grated — 400 g
- Carrots, batons — 300 g
- Turnips, wedges — 250 g
- Courgettes, logs — 300 g
- Chickpeas, cooked — 300 g
- Ras el hanout — 12 g
- Turmeric — 4 g
- Saffron, steeped — 0.3 g
- Fine sea salt — 26 g
- Water — 2500 ml
- Harissa, to serve — 60 g

## Method
1. Brown lamb in 40 ml oil in the couscoussier base; add onion, tomato, ras el hanout, turmeric, saffron, 14 g salt and water. Simmer 60 min.
2. Add chicken; simmer 30 min. Add carrots and turnips, 15 min; courgettes and chickpeas, final 15 min.
3. Meanwhile steam couscous twice over the broth (15 min each), raking with 250 ml salted water between; finish with remaining oil and salt.
4. Grill or pan-sear merguez separately, 8 min — never in the broth, or it turns everything orange and greasy.
5. Rest meats in a little broth; strain and skim the remainder.

## Plating & Service
Grain mounded on the grandest platter aboard, meats arranged by kind, vegetables between, broth in a tureen; harissa loosened with hot broth in a small bowl.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Scale down to chicken + merguez only for weeknights. GF: serve over steamed millet or corn couscous.
