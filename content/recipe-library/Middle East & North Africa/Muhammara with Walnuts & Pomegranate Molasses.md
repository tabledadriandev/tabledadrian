# Muhammara with Walnuts & Pomegranate Molasses

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Aleppo classic — sweet roasted peppers, bitter walnuts and sour pomegranate in tension; texture should be spoonable rubble, never a smooth dip.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Red romano peppers — 500 g
- Walnut halves — 150 g
- Fresh breadcrumbs — 50 g
- Pomegranate molasses — 40 g
- Aleppo pepper (pul biber) — 8 g
- Ground cumin — 3 g
- Garlic clove — 5 g
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Olive oil — 60 ml
- Fine sea salt — 6 g
- Walnuts, chopped, to garnish — 20 g
- Mint leaves — 4 g

## Method
1. Roast peppers at 230 °C for 30–35 min until blistered and collapsing; steam in a covered bowl, then peel and deseed. Drain 15 min.
2. Toast walnuts at 160 °C for 8 min; cool completely.
3. Pulse walnuts, breadcrumbs, garlic, Aleppo pepper, cumin and salt to coarse sand.
4. Add peppers, pomegranate molasses and lemon; pulse to a textured paste — stop while grainy.
5. Stream in olive oil in short pulses. Rest 1 h chilled; serve at room temperature.

## Plating & Service
Spread in a rough disc, dimple with a spoon, dress with olive oil, chopped walnuts and mint. Pairs with grilled meats or warm pide.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Gluten-free: replace breadcrumbs with 30 g fine GF crumbs or omit and reduce oil. A drop of espresso deepens it for lamb pairings.
