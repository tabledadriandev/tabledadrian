# Warm Chickpea Fatteh with Toasted Pita & Pine Nut Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Layered à la minute — hot chickpeas over crisp pita under cool garlic-tahini yogurt, crowned with foaming pine nut butter; a study in temperature contrast.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Cooked chickpeas with their liquor — 500 g
- Pita breads — 2 (about 120 g)
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Greek yogurt — 400 g
- Tahini — 60 g
- Garlic, grated — 8 g
- Lemon juice — 30 ml
- Fine sea salt — 8 g
- Ground cumin — 4 g
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Pine nuts — 40 g
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 6 g
- Aleppo pepper — 3 g

## Method
1. Tear pita into bite pieces, toss with olive oil, and bake at 190 °C for 8–10 min until golden and completely crisp.
2. Whisk yogurt with tahini, garlic, lemon, 5 g salt; keep at cool room temperature (fridge-cold kills the dish).
3. Warm chickpeas in their liquor with cumin and remaining salt to a bare simmer; keep them soupy.
4. Melt butter over medium heat with pine nuts, cooking to 150 °C until butter is nut-brown and pine nuts golden.
5. Assemble immediately: pita in a wide bowl, hot chickpeas with a little liquor, yogurt spooned over, then the foaming pine nut butter and Aleppo pepper.

## Plating & Service
Serve in the centre of the table within 3 minutes of assembly, spoon in the bowl — the half-soggy/half-crisp interface is the point.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 200 g braised lamb shoulder shreds between chickpeas and yogurt for a main. GF: use toasted GF flatbread or crisped rice cakes.
