# Maamoul, Date-Filled Semolina Shortbread

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Eid and Easter cookie both — semolina dough rested overnight until it loses all grit, wrapped around a mahleb-scented date paste; pale on top, sandy in the crumb, never browned.

## Ingredients (serves 12, makes ~30)
- Fine semolina — 300 g
- Plain flour — 100 g
- Unsalted butter, melted — 200 g
- Sugar — 40 g
- Mahleb, ground (or 2 g almond extract) — 3 g
- Instant yeast — 2 g
- Orange blossom water — 15 ml
- Rose water — 10 ml
- Water — 40 ml
- Fine sea salt — 2 g
- Soft date paste (or pitted dates, blended) — 350 g
- Ground cinnamon — 2 g
- Neutral oil (for date paste) — 15 ml
- Icing sugar — 30 g

## Method
1. Day one: rub semolina, flour, sugar, salt, yeast and mahleb with the melted butter; cover and rest at room temperature 8–12 h — the semolina must fully hydrate in the fat.
2. Knead date paste with cinnamon and oil until pliable; roll into 12 g balls.
3. Day two: work orange blossom, rose water and water into the dough until it holds together when squeezed — knead minimally.
4. Take 20 g dough, cup it around a date ball, seal, and press into a floured ma'amoul mould (or crimp with fork tines); tap out.
5. Bake on parchment at 180 °C for 16–18 min — bases pale gold, tops still ivory. They firm as they cool.
6. Cool fully; dust with icing sugar.

## Plating & Service
Pyramid on a small plate with coffee. They keep 2 weeks airtight and improve for the first three days.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pistachio or walnut filling: 200 g nuts + 60 g sugar + 10 ml rose water (dust these heavily with icing sugar to mark them). Dairy-free: ghee-style vegan butter works.
