# Ghormeh Sabzi, Persian Herb Stew with Dried Lime

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Iran's most beloved khoresh — a full kilo of herbs fried dark until almost black-green, then braised with lamb, kidney beans and pierced dried limes; the frying, not the braising, makes the dish.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Lamb shoulder or leg, 3 cm cubes — 900 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 400 g
- Coriander — 300 g
- Chives or spring onion tops — 200 g
- Dried fenugreek leaves — 8 g
- Fresh spinach — 100 g
- Onion, diced — 200 g
- Turmeric — 5 g
- Neutral oil — 100 ml
- Dried limes (loomi), pierced — 4 (about 30 g)
- Red kidney beans, soaked overnight — 200 g
- Fine sea salt — 16 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Lime juice — 20 ml

## Method
1. Wash, thoroughly dry and finely chop all fresh herbs and spinach.
2. Fry the herbs with fenugreek in 60 ml oil over medium heat, stirring, 20–25 min until deep forest green and aromatic — undercooked herbs taste grassy; this step cannot be rushed.
3. In a heavy pot, brown onion in remaining oil 8 min; add turmeric, then lamb, sealing all over.
4. Add drained kidney beans, fried herbs and 1 L water; simmer covered at 90 °C for 90 min.
5. Add pierced dried limes, salt and pepper; simmer 60–75 min more until lamb collapses, beans are creamy and the oil rises green at the edges.
6. Press the limes against the pot to release their dark sourness; adjust with lime juice.

## Plating & Service
Serve over chelow rice with tahdig; the stew should be thick, almost black-green, with a slick of green oil. Torshi pickles alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Beef chuck works (same timing). Vegan: mushrooms and double beans, braise 75 min — still profound thanks to the fried herbs.
