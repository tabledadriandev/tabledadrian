# Kabab Koobideh, Twin Skewers of Grated-Onion Lamb

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The test of a Persian grill cook — minced lamb bound only by onion juice, salt and patient kneading; it must hug the flat skewer through the first crucial minute over coals or fall into the fire.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Lamb shoulder, 20% fat, twice-minced cold — 1000 g
- Onion, finely grated, lightly squeezed — 180 g
- Fine sea salt — 15 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Turmeric — 2 g
- Sumac — 6 g
- Unsalted butter, melted (baste) — 40 g
- Saffron, steeped in 15 ml hot water — 0.15 g
- Tomatoes, halved — 400 g
- Lavash or chelow rice, to serve — 400 g
- Raw onion wedges — 100 g

## Method
1. Knead lamb with grated onion, salt, pepper and turmeric for a full 8–10 min until the paste is sticky, pale and stretches without tearing. Chill 2 h — cold fat is structural.
2. Divide into 6 × 190 g portions. With a wet palm, mould each along a wide flat skewer, pinching waves with two fingers; seal the ends firmly.
3. Mix melted butter with saffron water for basting.
4. Grill over hot coals, starting with 45 seconds per side twice in quick succession to set the crust — this is when they fall if under-kneaded.
5. Continue turning every minute, 8–9 min total, basting once, until juicy at 70 °C with charred ridges. Grill tomato halves alongside.
6. Rest 2 min on lavash.

## Plating & Service
Slide off with a fold of bread; serve two ribbons of kebab per guest with charred tomato, raw onion, sumac and rice or lavash. Butter-saffron gloss at the last second.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> 50/50 lamb-beef is standard in Tehran. No grill: shape into flat patties and cook on a cast-iron plancha at maximum heat, 3 min per side.
