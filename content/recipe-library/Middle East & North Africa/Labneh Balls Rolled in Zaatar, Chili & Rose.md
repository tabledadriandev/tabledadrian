# Labneh Balls Rolled in Zaatar, Chili & Rose

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A 48-hour make-ahead that looks like a jeweller's tray — three coatings, one strained yogurt; keeps a week under oil for spontaneous aperitivo hours.

## Ingredients (serves 8, makes 24 balls)
- Full-fat Greek yogurt — 1000 g
- Fine sea salt — 10 g
- Za'atar blend — 25 g
- Aleppo pepper flakes — 15 g
- Dried rose petals, crushed — 8 g
- Toasted sesame seeds — 15 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 300 ml
- Lemon zest — 4 g
- Thyme sprigs — 4
- Black peppercorns — 3 g

## Method
1. Whisk salt into yogurt. Spoon into a muslin-lined sieve over a bowl, twist closed, weight with a plate, and strain in the fridge 36–48 h until clay-firm.
2. Roll the labneh into 20 g balls with lightly oiled palms; set on baking paper and chill uncovered 2 h to form a skin.
3. Roll a third of the balls in za'atar, a third in Aleppo pepper mixed with sesame, a third in rose petals.
4. Layer gently into a sterilised jar with thyme, lemon zest and peppercorns; cover completely with olive oil.
5. Mature 24 h before serving; keeps refrigerated 7 days. Bring to room temperature to serve.

## Plating & Service
Lift balls onto a slate with some of their infused oil, alternating colours, with warm bread and crisp radishes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Goat or sheep yogurt gives a sharper, cheese-like result. Dairy-free: strain thick coconut yogurt 24 h and reduce salt to 6 g.
