# Dawood Basha, Pomegranate Meatballs with Baby Onions

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Named for an Ottoman pasha — small lamb meatballs braised with whole caramelised baby onions in a pomegranate-tomato glaze that turns lacquer-dark.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Lamb mince — 800 g
- Onion, grated and squeezed — 100 g
- Baharat — 7 g
- Fine sea salt — 13 g
- Pine nuts — 40 g
- Baby onions or shallots, peeled — 350 g
- Olive oil — 50 ml
- Tomato paste — 40 g
- Pomegranate molasses — 50 g
- Ripe tomatoes, grated — 300 g
- Water — 350 ml
- Ground cinnamon — 2 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 10 g
- Pomegranate seeds — 40 g

## Method
1. Knead lamb with grated onion, baharat and 8 g salt; roll 25 g balls around 2–3 pine nuts each.
2. Brown meatballs in half the oil over high heat, 3 min, rolling for colour; remove.
3. Caramelise whole baby onions in remaining oil, 8–10 min, to deep gold.
4. Add tomato paste, cook 2 min; add grated tomato, pomegranate molasses, cinnamon, remaining salt and water.
5. Return meatballs; simmer gently, half-covered, 30 min until onions are translucent-sweet and the sauce is glossy and dark.
6. Rest 5 min; the sauce should coat the back of a spoon.

## Plating & Service
Serve over rice or with warm bread, strewn with parsley and fresh pomegranate seeds — the raw seeds against the dark glaze lift the whole dish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: omit pine nuts from centres. A splash (30 ml) of red grape juice deepens the glaze without wine.
