# Firin Sutlac, Burnished Baked Rice Pudding

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Istanbul pudding-shop classic — stovetop rice pudding finished under fierce top heat until the surface scorches like crème brûlée's rustic cousin; serve cold with that dark skin intact.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Short-grain rice — 90 g
- Water — 250 ml
- Whole milk — 1000 ml
- Sugar — 140 g
- Cornflour — 25 g
- Egg yolk — 18 g (1)
- Vanilla — 3 g
- Fine sea salt — 1 g
- Double cream — 50 ml
- Hazelnuts, ground, to serve — 20 g
- Ground cinnamon — 1 g

## Method
1. Simmer rice in the water until absorbed, 10 min.
2. Add 850 ml of the milk, sugar and salt; simmer gently, stirring often, 25–30 min until the rice is fully soft and the pot creamy.
3. Whisk cornflour into remaining cold milk; stir in and cook 3 min to thicken.
4. Off heat, temper in the yolk beaten with cream and vanilla.
5. Ladle into six ovenproof ramekins set in a water bath (hot water halfway up).
6. Bake under a 250 °C grill (or top heat) 10–15 min until the surfaces are deeply blistered and mottled dark brown.
7. Cool, then chill 4 h — the contrast of scorched skin and cold cream beneath is the dessert.

## Plating & Service
Serve in the ramekin, a pinch of cinnamon and ground hazelnuts on the rim. No garnish on the burnished skin — it earned its place.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF by nature. Mastic version: 0.3 g ground mastic with the vanilla. Dairy-free: oat milk + coconut cream; grill time shortens by 3 min.
