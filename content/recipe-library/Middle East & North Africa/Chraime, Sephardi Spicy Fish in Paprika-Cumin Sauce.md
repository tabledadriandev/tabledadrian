# Chraime, Sephardi Spicy Fish in Paprika-Cumin Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Friday-night fish of Libyan and Tunisian Jewish tables — thick white fish poached in a brick-red sauce that is mostly good paprika, garlic and oil; fast, ferocious, elegant.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Thick white fish steaks (cod, halibut, grouper) — 1000 g (6)
- Olive oil — 90 ml
- Garlic, thickly sliced — 25 g
- Sweet paprika — 15 g
- Hot paprika or cayenne — 3 g
- Ground cumin — 6 g
- Ground caraway — 2 g
- Tomato paste — 40 g
- Water — 350 ml
- Fine sea salt — 11 g
- Lemon juice — 25 ml
- Long red chilli, sliced — 15 g
- Coriander, chopped — 12 g

## Method
1. Warm olive oil in a wide sauté pan; fry garlic gently to pale gold, 2 min.
2. Off the heat, stir in both paprikas, cumin and caraway — blooming off-heat keeps them from scorching.
3. Return to heat with tomato paste, frying 2 min; add water, salt and chilli. Simmer 8 min to a loose, vivid sauce.
4. Season fish; slide into the sauce, spooning it over. Cover and poach at 85–90 °C, 10–12 min to 55 °C core.
5. Finish with lemon; the sauce should be slick with red oil at the edges.

## Plating & Service
One steak per shallow bowl, sauce spooned around, coriander over, challah or couscous alongside. Serve hot or at room temperature — it holds beautifully.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 200 g sliced red pepper with the garlic for body. Salmon works but halve the cumin. Leftover sauce is tomorrow's ojja base.
