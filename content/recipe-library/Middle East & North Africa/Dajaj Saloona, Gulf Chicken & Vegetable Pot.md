# Dajaj Saloona, Gulf Chicken & Vegetable Pot

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The everyday stew of Emirati kitchens — chicken and vegetables in a loose, turmeric-stained tomato broth soured with loomi; light where machboos is rich, and a natural crew-lunch hero.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Chicken thighs, bone-in, skinned — 1300 g
- Onion, diced — 180 g
- Garlic, grated — 12 g
- Ginger, grated — 12 g
- Neutral oil — 50 ml
- Gulf baharat — 10 g
- Turmeric — 5 g
- Dried limes, pierced — 2 (15 g)
- Tomatoes, chopped — 400 g
- Tomato paste — 25 g
- Potatoes, chunks — 300 g
- Carrots, chunks — 200 g
- Courgette, chunks — 200 g
- Green chilli, slit — 10 g
- Fine sea salt — 15 g
- Water — 900 ml
- Coriander — 12 g

## Method
1. Sweat onion in oil 8 min; add garlic, ginger, baharat and turmeric, 1 min.
2. Add chicken, turning in the spices; add tomato paste, tomatoes, dried limes, chilli, salt and water.
3. Simmer, half-covered, 25 min.
4. Add potatoes and carrots, 15 min; courgette, final 10 min — vegetables should be tender, broth loose and vivid.
5. Press the limes against the pot for sourness; adjust salt.

## Plating & Service
Ladle into deep bowls over white rice or with flatbread, coriander scattered. It should eat like a spiced, restorative soup-stew.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fish saloona: firm fish chunks in the last 8 min only. Vegan: chickpeas and squash, 25 min total simmer.
