# Fesenjan, Chicken in Walnut & Pomegranate Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The regal khoresh of northern Iran — walnuts ground fine and simmered for hours release their oil and thicken pomegranate molasses into a sauce the colour of dark chocolate; sweet-sour balance is a personal signature.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Chicken thighs, bone-in, skinned — 1200 g
- Walnut halves — 350 g
- Pomegranate molasses — 120 g
- Onion, fine dice — 200 g
- Neutral oil — 50 ml
- Turmeric — 3 g
- Ground cinnamon — 2 g
- Fine sea salt — 14 g
- Water — 800 ml
- Sugar or honey (to balance) — 0–20 g
- Saffron, steeped in 20 ml hot water — 0.2 g
- Pomegranate seeds — 60 g
- Walnut halves, toasted, to garnish — 20 g

## Method
1. Toast walnuts at 150 °C for 8 min (pale gold only — dark walnuts turn bitter); grind to a fine, oily meal.
2. Sweat onion in oil 10 min; add turmeric and cinnamon, then brown the chicken lightly.
3. Add ground walnuts and water; bring to a bare simmer.
4. Cook uncovered at 85–90 °C, stirring every 15 min from the bottom, for 90 min — the sauce will darken from beige to milk chocolate as walnut oil releases.
5. Add pomegranate molasses, salt and saffron water; simmer 40–60 min more until the sauce is deep brown, glossy, and the oil beads on top.
6. Balance: taste and adjust with sugar (if too sour) or more molasses (if flat). Chicken should be falling from the bone.

## Plating & Service
Serve in a warm dish over saffron chelow, scattered with pomegranate seeds and toasted walnuts. The sauce should mound, not pour.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Classic with duck legs (braise 2.5 h) for charter dinners. Vegan: butternut and mushrooms, 50 min braise — the sauce carries it.
