# Rose & Pistachio Lokum, Turkish Delight from Scratch

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Real lokum is a starch-and-sugar marathon — an hour of stirring while the mass turns from cloudy to glassy amber-pink; cut with an oiled knife and it stays cloud-soft for weeks.

## Ingredients (makes ~40 pieces, 20 cm tin)
- Sugar — 450 g
- Water (syrup) — 175 ml
- Lemon juice — 12 ml
- Cornflour — 110 g
- Water (starch phase) — 400 ml
- Cream of tartar — 2 g
- Rose water — 25 ml
- Natural red colour (beet or pomegranate reduction) — 5 ml
- Pistachio kernels, lightly toasted — 80 g
- Icing sugar — 60 g
- Cornflour (coating) — 40 g
- Neutral oil (tin and knife) — 10 ml

## Method
1. Cook sugar, 175 ml water and lemon juice to 118 °C; hold warm.
2. In a heavy pot, whisk cornflour and cream of tartar into 400 ml cold water; cook over medium heat, whisking, until it becomes a thick, translucent paste.
3. Stream the hot syrup into the starch paste in four additions, whisking each smooth.
4. Now the marathon: simmer on the lowest heat, stirring every 2–3 min with a silicone spatula, 60–70 min, until deep golden, glassy and pulling from the pot sides in a single elastic mass.
5. Off heat, beat in rose water, colour and pistachios.
6. Scrape into the oiled tin lined with oiled parchment; level. Set uncovered at room temperature 12 h — never the fridge.
7. Turn out, cut with an oiled knife into 2.5 cm cubes, and tumble in sifted icing sugar + cornflour.

## Plating & Service
Stack in a rough pyramid with coffee, or box as the guest-departure gift — homemade lokum is remembered longer than the itinerary.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Orange blossom + toasted almond, or mastic + no colour, rotate well. Under-stirred lokum weeps; if in doubt, give it ten more minutes.
