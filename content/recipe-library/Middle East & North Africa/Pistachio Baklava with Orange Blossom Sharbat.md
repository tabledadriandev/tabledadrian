# Pistachio Baklava with Orange Blossom Sharbat

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The two commandments: clarify the butter, and pour cool syrup over hot pastry (never both hot) — that is what makes baklava crisp for days instead of soggy by dinner.

## Ingredients (serves 12, one 30×20 cm tray)
- Phyllo sheets — 400 g (about 20 sheets)
- Unsalted butter, clarified — 220 g
- Green pistachio kernels — 300 g
- Sugar (filling) — 30 g
- Ground cardamom — 1 g
- Fine sea salt — 1 g
- Sugar (syrup) — 350 g
- Water — 250 ml
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Orange blossom water — 12 ml
- Pistachios, finely ground, to finish — 30 g

## Method
1. Syrup first: dissolve sugar in water, add lemon, simmer 8–10 min to a light syrup (103 °C); off heat add orange blossom water. Cool completely.
2. Grind pistachios with filling sugar, cardamom and salt to coarse sand.
3. Brush the tray with clarified butter. Layer 10 phyllo sheets, buttering each with a light, complete coat.
4. Spread the pistachio layer evenly; press gently.
5. Layer the remaining 10 sheets, buttering each; butter the top generously.
6. Cut all the way through into diamonds with a sharp knife — cutting after baking shatters it.
7. Bake at 170 °C for 45–50 min until evenly deep gold to the centre layers.
8. Pour the cool syrup evenly over the hot baklava — it will crackle. Rest uncovered 4 h minimum.

## Plating & Service
Lift diamonds cleanly, crown each with ground pistachio. Serve with black coffee — the bitterness is the pairing.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Walnut-cinnamon (300 g walnuts + 3 g cinnamon) for the Levantine register. Dairy-free: high-quality ghee-style coconut oil works but loses the noisette note.
