# Kashk-e Bademjan, Persian Aubergine & Whey Dip

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Caramelised aubergine mashed with tangy kashk, crowned with three sizzled garnishes — fried mint, saffron onions and walnuts; the Tehran teahouse classic plated for the salon.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Aubergines — 800 g
- Olive oil — 100 ml
- Onions, sliced — 250 g
- Garlic, sliced — 15 g
- Turmeric — 3 g
- Kashk (Persian whey; or crème fraîche + 10 ml lemon) — 120 g
- Dried mint — 6 g
- Neutral oil (for mint oil) — 30 ml
- Walnuts, chopped — 40 g
- Saffron threads, steeped in 20 ml hot water — 0.2 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Peel aubergines in stripes, cut in 3 cm chunks, salt with 4 g, rest 15 min, pat dry.
2. Fry aubergine in 70 ml olive oil over medium-high heat until deeply golden and collapsing, 12–15 min; set aside.
3. In the same pan, fry onions in remaining oil to deep gold, 15 min; remove a third for garnish and toss it with half the saffron water.
4. Add garlic and turmeric to the pan onions, cook 2 min, return aubergine with 100 ml water; simmer 10 min and mash coarsely.
5. Stir in two-thirds of the kashk, salt, pepper; cook 5 min to a thick, spoonable mash.
6. Bloom dried mint in 30 ml oil at 120 °C for 30 seconds — no longer, it burns to bitterness.
7. Toast walnuts at 160 °C for 6 min.

## Plating & Service
Spread warm in a shallow bowl; ribbon over remaining kashk, drizzle mint oil, cluster saffron onions and walnuts at the centre. Warm flatbread alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: use a cashew cream soured with lemon in place of kashk. Grill the aubergines whole over flame first for a smoky version.
