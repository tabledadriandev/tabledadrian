# Piyaz, White Bean Salad with Tahini & Sumac Onions

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Antalya version — creamy white beans dressed in loosened tahini rather than plain vinaigrette, with sumac-cured onions cutting through; a protein-rich mezze that anchors a vegetarian spread.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Cooked white beans (cannellini), drained — 500 g
- Red onion — 120 g
- Sumac — 6 g
- Fine sea salt — 7 g
- Tahini — 80 g
- White wine vinegar — 35 ml
- Lemon juice — 25 ml
- Cold water — 70 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 50 ml
- Ripe tomatoes, cut in wedges — 200 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 20 g
- Pul biber — 3 g

## Method
1. Slice onion into fine half-moons; massage with sumac and 2 g salt, rest 15 min to cure and soften.
2. Whisk tahini with vinegar, lemon and remaining salt — it will seize; loosen with cold water to single-cream consistency, then whisk in 30 ml olive oil.
3. Fold beans gently through the tahini dressing; rest 20 min so they drink it in.
4. Fold in tomatoes, most of the parsley and half the onions.
5. Adjust salt and acid — beans blunt both; be generous.

## Plating & Service
Mound on a platter, crown with remaining sumac onions, parsley, pul biber and the last of the oil. Room temperature, beside köfte or grilled fish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Classic Antalya adds quartered boiled eggs on top (then not vegan). Butter beans make a plusher, more luxurious version.
