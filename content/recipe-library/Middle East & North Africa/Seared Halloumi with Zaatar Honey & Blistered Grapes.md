# Seared Halloumi with Zaatar Honey & Blistered Grapes

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Halloumi seared hard in a dry pan, then glazed at the table with warm za'atar honey — salty, sweet, herbal; a three-minute mezze that looks composed.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Halloumi — 450 g
- Seedless red grapes, on small stems — 200 g
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Honey — 60 g
- Za'atar blend — 8 g
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Thyme leaves — 2 g
- Aleppo pepper — 2 g
- Toasted sesame seeds — 5 g

## Method
1. Slice halloumi into 1.5 cm slabs; pat completely dry.
2. Blister the grape clusters in a dry pan over high heat, 2–3 min, until skins split; set aside.
3. Warm honey with za'atar, lemon and 15 ml water to a pourable glaze (about 60 °C); keep warm.
4. Heat a heavy pan with olive oil until shimmering (around 200 °C); sear halloumi 90 seconds per side to a deep crust.
5. Plate immediately — halloumi toughens as it cools — and pour the warm za'atar honey over.

## Plating & Service
Shingle the golden slabs, lean grape clusters against them, spoon glaze over, finish with thyme, Aleppo pepper and sesame. Serve with forks and urgency.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap grapes for grilled apricot halves in summer. Vegan: the glaze is superb over seared aubergine steaks; use maple instead of honey.
