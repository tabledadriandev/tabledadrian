# Joojeh Kebab, Saffron-Yogurt Chicken with Grilled Tomato

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Persia's favourite grill — chicken stained gold in saffron, yogurt and onion juice, basted with saffron butter; the lime-onion marinade tenderises without masking the saffron.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Chicken thigh fillets — 1100 g
- Greek yogurt — 120 g
- Onion, grated and strained (juice) — 80 ml
- Lime juice — 50 ml
- Saffron, ground, steeped in 40 ml hot water — 0.4 g
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Fine sea salt — 14 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Unsalted butter — 50 g
- Tomatoes — 6 medium (500 g)
- Limes, halved, to serve — 3
- Sumac — 4 g

## Method
1. Cut chicken into 4 cm pieces. Marinate with yogurt, onion juice, lime juice, half the saffron water, oil, salt and pepper — 4 to 24 h chilled.
2. Melt butter with the remaining saffron water for the baste.
3. Thread chicken snugly on flat skewers; skewer tomatoes separately.
4. Grill over medium-hot coals (or 230 °C grill), turning every 2–3 min and basting with saffron butter, 12–14 min to 72 °C core with golden char.
5. Grill tomatoes until blistered and slumping, 6–8 min.
6. Rest 3 min with a final baste.

## Plating & Service
Slide off skewers over chelow or lavash, blistered tomato alongside, sumac dusted over, charred lime halves for squeezing.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free: marinate in onion juice, lime, saffron and oil only; baste with saffron oil. Poussin halves marinated the same way, roasted at 220 °C for 30 min, plate beautifully.
