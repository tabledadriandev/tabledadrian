# Shish Taouk, Toum-Marinated Chicken Skewers

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Yogurt-lemon-garlic marinade with tomato paste for colour — the paste's sugars caramelise where flame licks; serve with extra toum for the full Beirut grill-house effect.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Chicken thigh fillets — 1000 g
- Greek yogurt — 150 g
- Lemon juice — 50 ml
- Garlic, grated — 20 g
- Tomato paste — 30 g
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Sweet paprika — 6 g
- Ground white pepper — 3 g
- Dried oregano — 2 g
- Fine sea salt — 12 g
- Toum (see condiments) — 80 g
- Flatbreads, to serve — 300 g
- Sumac onions (sliced onion + 4 g sumac) — 120 g

## Method
1. Cut chicken into 3.5 cm cubes, trimming excess fat but keeping some — it bastes the skewer.
2. Whisk yogurt, lemon, garlic, tomato paste, oil, paprika, white pepper, oregano and salt; massage into chicken. Marinate 4–12 h chilled.
3. Thread snugly onto flat skewers, alternating orientation for an even cylinder.
4. Grill over medium-hot coals or a 220 °C plancha, turning quarter-turns every 3 min, 12–14 min total, to 72 °C core with charred edges.
5. Rest 4 min tented under flatbread — the bread steams and catches juices.

## Plating & Service
Slide skewers onto the juice-soaked bread, dollop toum, scatter sumac onions. Pickles and fries in paper for authenticity.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free: replace yogurt with 60 ml olive oil + 20 ml lemon. Breast works if brined 30 min in 3% salt water first.
