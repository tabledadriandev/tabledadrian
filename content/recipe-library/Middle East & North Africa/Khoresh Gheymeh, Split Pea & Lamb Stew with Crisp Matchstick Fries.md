# Khoresh Gheymeh, Split Pea & Lamb Stew with Crisp Matchstick Fries

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Tomato-saffron lamb with yellow split peas and dried lime, buried at the last moment under a haystack of crisp matchstick potatoes — the contrast is childhood joy, refined.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Lamb shoulder, 3 cm cubes — 800 g
- Yellow split peas — 180 g
- Onion, fine dice — 200 g
- Tomato paste — 60 g
- Dried limes, pierced — 3 (about 22 g)
- Turmeric — 4 g
- Ground cinnamon — 1 g
- Saffron, steeped in 20 ml hot water — 0.2 g
- Neutral oil — 60 ml
- Fine sea salt — 16 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Water — 900 ml
- Waxy potatoes — 400 g
- Oil for frying potatoes — 700 ml

## Method
1. Brown onion in oil 10 min; add turmeric, then lamb, sealing well.
2. Fry tomato paste with the meat 3 min until dark; add water, dried limes, cinnamon and pepper. Braise gently 75 min at 90 °C.
3. Rinse split peas; add and simmer 35–45 min until tender but intact — overcooked peas turn the stew to porridge.
4. Season with salt and saffron water; reduce uncovered to a thick, oil-slicked stew, pressing the limes to release their sour depth.
5. Fries: cut potatoes into 3 mm matchsticks, rinse, dry completely; fry at 175 °C until golden and rigid, 4–5 min; salt.
6. Rest the stew 10 min; it should mound on rice.

## Plating & Service
Ladle over chelow, then crown generously with the matchstick fries at the very last second — they must arrive crisp.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Gheymeh bademjan: add fried aubergine quarters with the salt stage. Vegan: mushrooms replace lamb; use 240 g split peas.
