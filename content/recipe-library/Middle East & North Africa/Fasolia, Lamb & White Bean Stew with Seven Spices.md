# Fasolia, Lamb & White Bean Stew with Seven Spices

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Sunday stew of the Levant — lamb neck braised until spoonable with white beans in a tomato broth; humble, deep, and better on day two for crew or family charters.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Lamb neck or shoulder, 4 cm cubes — 800 g
- Dried white beans, soaked overnight — 300 g
- Onion, diced — 180 g
- Garlic, sliced — 15 g
- Tomato paste — 50 g
- Ripe tomatoes, grated — 300 g
- Olive oil — 50 ml
- Lebanese seven-spice (baharat) — 10 g
- Bay leaves — 2
- Fine sea salt — 16 g
- Water — 1400 ml
- Lemon juice — 25 ml
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 10 g
- Coriander, chopped — 10 g

## Method
1. Brown lamb in olive oil in a heavy pot over high heat, in batches; set aside.
2. Sweat onion 8 min in the same pot; add garlic and tomato paste, cooking 2 min until brick-dark.
3. Return lamb with grated tomato, baharat, bay and water; simmer at 90 °C, covered, 60 min.
4. Add drained soaked beans; continue at a gentle simmer 50–60 min until beans are creamy and lamb collapses. Salt only in the last 30 min (earlier toughens beans).
5. Crush a ladle of beans against the pot to thicken the broth; finish with lemon and half the herbs.

## Plating & Service
Serve in deep bowls over vermicelli rice, remaining herbs on top. A raw onion wedge and pickled chillies on the side, Beirut style.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: skip lamb, use 2 g smoked paprika and vegetable stock; still a complete meal. Green bean version (loubieh) uses 700 g flat beans, 40 min total.
