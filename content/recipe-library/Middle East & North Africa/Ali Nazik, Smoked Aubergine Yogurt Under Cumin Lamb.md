# Ali Nazik, Smoked Aubergine Yogurt Under Cumin Lamb

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Gaziantep's gentle giant — warm smoked-aubergine yogurt beneath a sizzling scatter of cumin-butter lamb; the temperature and texture contrast is the entire seduction.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Aubergines — 900 g
- Greek yogurt — 300 g
- Garlic, grated — 8 g
- Fine sea salt — 13 g
- Lamb leg or shoulder, hand-diced 1 cm — 700 g
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Olive oil — 20 ml
- Ground cumin — 6 g
- Pul biber — 6 g
- Biber salçası — 15 g
- Long green pepper, diced — 80 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 10 g

## Method
1. Char aubergines whole over flame until collapsed; peel scrupulously, drain 20 min, chop to a coarse pulp.
2. Warm the pulp in a pan 3 min to drive off moisture; off heat, fold in yogurt, garlic and 6 g salt. Hold just-warm (40 °C) — never hot.
3. Pat lamb dry. Sear in oil in a wide pan at maximum heat, single layer, 3 min undisturbed for crust.
4. Add butter, green pepper, salça, cumin, 4 g of the pul biber and remaining salt; toss 3–4 min until lamb is glossy, 60 °C core, and the butter is stained red.
5. Rest the pan 1 min so juices settle into the butter sauce.

## Plating & Service
Spread the warm aubergine yogurt in a ring, spoon the lamb and every drop of its red butter into the centre, finish with parsley and remaining pul biber. Flatbread optional; spoons essential.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken thigh or portobello-halloumi (vegetarian) versions work over the same base. Lighter: fold labneh instead of full yogurt and finish lamb with oil only.
