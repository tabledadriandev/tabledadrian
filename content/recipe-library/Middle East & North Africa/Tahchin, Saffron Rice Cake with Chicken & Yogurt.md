# Tahchin, Saffron Rice Cake with Chicken & Yogurt

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The whole dish is tahdig — parboiled rice bound with saffron yogurt and egg yolk, layered with braised chicken, and baked until the outside is amber glass; it cuts like a gâteau.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Basmati rice — 500 g
- Chicken thigh fillets — 600 g
- Onion, sliced — 150 g
- Greek yogurt — 300 g
- Egg yolks — 72 g (4)
- Saffron, ground, steeped in 60 ml hot water — 0.6 g
- Neutral oil — 90 ml
- Unsalted butter — 40 g
- Turmeric — 2 g
- Fine sea salt — 22 g
- Barberries, warmed in 20 g butter with 8 g sugar — 40 g
- Slivered pistachios — 25 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Braise chicken with onion, turmeric, 6 g salt and 150 ml water, 30 min; shred coarsely, reducing juices to 30 ml.
2. Parboil washed, soaked rice in salted water (12 g salt) 5 min to al dente; drain and cool slightly.
3. Whisk yogurt, yolks, saffron water, remaining salt and pepper; fold in the rice until every grain is stained gold.
4. Heavily oil a 24 cm nonstick ovenproof dish (60 ml oil, hot). Press in half the rice firmly, layer the chicken and its glaze, cap with remaining rice, pressing flat. Dot with butter.
5. Cover with foil; bake at 190 °C for 75–85 min until the sides show deep amber through the glass (or a knife at the edge reveals crust).
6. Rest 10 min, run a knife around, and invert bravely onto a platter.

## Plating & Service
Present whole — the amber dome gets its ovation — then cut wedges. Scatter buttered barberries and pistachios over the top.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian: layer with sautéed spinach and caramelised onion instead of chicken. Individual tahchins in muffin tins bake in 45 min at 200 °C.
