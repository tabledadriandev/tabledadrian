# Chicken Tagine with Preserved Lemon & Cracked Green Olives

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The daghmira is the point — onions melted with saffron and ginger into a thick, spoon-coating sauce that clings to the chicken; preserved lemon goes in late so it perfumes rather than dominates.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Chicken legs, bone-in — 1800 g (6)
- Onions, thinly sliced — 500 g
- Garlic, grated — 15 g
- Ground ginger — 6 g
- Turmeric — 4 g
- Saffron, steeped in 30 ml hot water — 0.3 g
- Ground white pepper — 3 g
- Olive oil — 70 ml
- Fine sea salt — 14 g
- Preserved lemon — 60 g (rind of 1½, plus 15 g pulp)
- Cracked green olives, rinsed — 150 g
- Coriander, chopped — 15 g
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 10 g
- Water — 250 ml

## Method
1. Rub chicken with garlic, ginger, turmeric, white pepper, 10 g salt and 30 ml oil; marinate 2–12 h.
2. In a tagine or heavy casserole, brown chicken skin-side lightly in remaining oil; remove.
3. Cook the onions in the same pot, low, 15 min until collapsing. Return chicken, add saffron water and water.
4. Cover and braise at the gentlest simmer (or 160 °C oven) for 50 min, turning once.
5. Remove chicken; crush the onions into the juices and reduce to a thick daghmira, 8–10 min.
6. Return chicken with preserved lemon rind (slivered), pulp, olives and half the herbs; 10 min more, uncovered, basting to glaze.

## Plating & Service
Serve in the tagine, herbs strewn over, with khobz for the sauce — never rice in Morocco; bread is the utensil.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 200 g potato quarters under the chicken for a fuller one-pot. Guinea fowl elevates it for formal dinners (same timing).
