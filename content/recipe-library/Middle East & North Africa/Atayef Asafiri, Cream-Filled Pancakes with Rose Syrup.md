# Atayef Asafiri, Cream-Filled Pancakes with Rose Syrup

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Ramadan's little birds — yeast pancakes cooked on one side only (the bubbled face stays open), half-sealed around clotted cream, dipped in pistachio; assembled to order, never ahead.

## Ingredients (serves 8, makes ~24)
- Plain flour — 250 g
- Fine semolina — 50 g
- Sugar — 20 g
- Instant yeast — 5 g
- Baking powder — 4 g
- Fine sea salt — 2 g
- Warm water — 400 ml
- Ashta (clotted cream) or mascarpone whipped with 50 ml cream — 400 g
- Orange blossom water — 8 ml
- Pistachios, finely chopped — 60 g
- Sugar (syrup) — 250 g
- Water (syrup) — 180 ml
- Lemon juice — 12 ml
- Rose water — 10 ml

## Method
1. Whisk flour, semolina, sugar, yeast, baking powder, salt and water to a crepe-thin batter; rest 45 min until foamy.
2. Syrup: simmer sugar, water and lemon 8 min; rose water off heat; cool.
3. Perfume the cream with orange blossom water.
4. Cook on a dry nonstick pan over medium heat: pour 40 mm rounds, cook 90 seconds until the top is matte and covered in burst bubbles — never flip. Stack under a towel.
5. While supple, pinch each pancake into a half-moon sealed only halfway — an open cone.
6. Pipe cream into the opening; dip the exposed cream in pistachio.

## Plating & Service
Arrange like a flight of green-tipped shells, syrup zigzagged or served alongside for dipping. Eat within the hour — the pancake must stay tender.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fully seal around walnut-cinnamon-sugar and deep-fry at 175 °C for the crisp syrup-soaked version. Unfilled pancakes freeze well between parchment.
