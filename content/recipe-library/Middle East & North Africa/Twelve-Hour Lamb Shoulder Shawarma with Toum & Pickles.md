# Twelve-Hour Lamb Shoulder Shawarma with Toum & Pickles

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The vertical spit reimagined for a yacht oven — shoulder cured overnight in shawarma spices, cooked at 120 °C until it shreds, then blasted for crisp edges.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Lamb shoulder, bone-in — 2500 g
- Fine sea salt — 30 g
- Ground cumin — 12 g
- Ground coriander — 10 g
- Sweet paprika — 10 g
- Turmeric — 5 g
- Ground cinnamon — 4 g
- Ground cloves — 1 g
- Black pepper — 5 g
- Garlic, grated — 30 g
- Lemon juice — 60 ml
- Olive oil — 60 ml
- Toum — 150 g
- Flatbreads, pickled turnips, sumac onions to serve — 500 g

## Method
1. Whisk salt, all spices, garlic, lemon and oil to a wet rub; massage into the scored shoulder. Cure uncovered in the fridge 12–24 h.
2. Bring to room temperature 1 h. Set on a rack over a tray with 200 ml water below.
3. Cook at 120 °C for 10–12 h (overnight) until probe-tender at 92–94 °C internal; baste twice with tray juices if awake.
4. Rest 30 min loosely covered; skim the tray juices.
5. Before service, blast at 240 °C for 12–15 min to crisp the exterior.
6. Shred with forks, dressing the meat in its skimmed juices.

## Plating & Service
Pile onto a warm platter with flatbreads, toum, pickled turnips and sumac onions — guests build their own. The crisp-fat shards go to whoever carved.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken thigh shawarma: same rub, stack thighs in a loaf tin, roast at 200 °C for 50 min, slice and crisp in a pan.
