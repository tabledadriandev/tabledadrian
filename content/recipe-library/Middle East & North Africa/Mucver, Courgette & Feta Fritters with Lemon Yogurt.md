# Mucver, Courgette & Feta Fritters with Lemon Yogurt

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The secret is violence to the courgette — salt it, wring it dry, and the fritters fry lacy-edged instead of soggy; batter thin, pan hot.

## Ingredients (serves 6, makes 18)
- Courgettes — 600 g
- Fine sea salt — 10 g (divided)
- Spring onions, sliced — 60 g
- Dill, chopped — 12 g
- Mint, chopped — 8 g
- Feta, crumbled — 150 g
- Eggs — 100 g (2)
- Plain flour — 80 g
- Baking powder — 4 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Pul biber — 3 g
- Olive oil for shallow frying — 150 ml
- Greek yogurt — 200 g
- Lemon zest and juice — 4 g zest, 20 ml juice

## Method
1. Coarsely grate courgettes, toss with 6 g salt, rest 15 min, then wring in a towel until you extract at least 150 ml liquid.
2. Mix courgette with spring onion, herbs, feta, eggs and pul biber.
3. Fold in flour, baking powder, pepper and remaining salt just to combine — overmixing toughens.
4. Heat 5 mm olive oil in a wide pan to 175 °C. Drop 40 g dollops, flatten to 1 cm.
5. Fry 2.5–3 min per side to deep gold with crisp lace edges; drain on a rack.
6. Stir lemon zest and juice into yogurt with a pinch of salt.

## Plating & Service
Shingle the fritters on a warm platter, lemon yogurt in a bowl at the centre. Serve hot or warm — they hold 20 min in a 90 °C oven.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: swap flour for 60 g chickpea flour (adds a lovely nuttiness). Add 100 g grated carrot for colour and sweetness.
