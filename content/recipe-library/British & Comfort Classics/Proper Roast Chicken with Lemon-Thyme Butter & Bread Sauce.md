# Proper Roast Chicken with Lemon-Thyme Butter & Bread Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Butter under the skin, lemon in the cavity, and the forgotten glory of clove-scented bread sauce — the roast chicken to judge all others by.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Whole free-range chicken — 1.8 kg
- Unsalted butter — 100 g, softened (80 g bird, 20 g sauce)
- Thyme — 8 sprigs (leaves from 4, whole 4)
- Lemon — 1, halved
- Garlic — 1 head, halved across
- Onion — 1 (150 g), studded with 4 cloves
- Whole milk — 400 ml
- Bay leaf — 1
- White sourdough crumbs — 90 g
- Freshly grated nutmeg — 0.5 g
- Chicken stock — 300 ml
- White wine — 100 ml
- Fine sea salt — 8 g; black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Bring the bird to room temperature 45 minutes. Heat oven to 200°C.
2. Beat 80 g butter with thyme leaves, zest of the lemon and 3 g salt. Loosen the breast skin; spread butter beneath. Push lemon halves, garlic and whole thyme into the cavity. Salt the skin all over.
3. Roast breast-up 20 minutes, then at 180°C for 55–65 minutes, basting twice, until the thigh reads 74°C.
4. Bread sauce: infuse milk with the clove-studded onion and bay at 80°C for 20 minutes. Remove onion; whisk in crumbs and simmer 5 minutes. Finish with 20 g butter, nutmeg, salt.
5. Rest the chicken breast-down 20 minutes under loose foil.
6. Jus: skim the tray, deglaze with wine, add stock, reduce by half, strain; add resting juices.
7. Carve: legs first, then breasts off the crown, sliced thick.

## Plating & Service
Carved bird on a warm platter, bread sauce in a bowl, jus in a boat — with roast potatoes and buttered greens for the full Sunday.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: skip bread sauce for celeriac purée. Herb swap: tarragon butter turns it French; sage keeps it firmly British.
