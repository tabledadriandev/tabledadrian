# Crispy Fish Butty with Sauce Gribiche

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The fish-finger sandwich, reinvented for the sundeck — panko-crusted haddock goujons, a French gribiche standing in for tartare, soft brioche rolls.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Haddock or cod loin — 600 g, in 3 cm-wide goujons
- Plain flour — 60 g
- Free-range eggs — 2, beaten, plus 2 hard-boiled for gribiche
- Panko breadcrumbs — 150 g
- Neutral oil — 1 L, for frying
- Brioche rolls — 4 (320 g)
- Cornichons — 40 g, chopped
- Capers — 20 g, chopped
- Dijon mustard — 10 g
- Olive oil — 90 ml
- White wine vinegar — 12 ml
- Tarragon and parsley — 12 g, chopped
- Baby gem lettuce — 1 head (100 g), shredded
- Fine sea salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Gribiche: mash the hard-boiled yolks with mustard and vinegar; whisk in olive oil to a rough emulsion. Fold in chopped whites, cornichons, capers and herbs. Season.
2. Season goujons; pané through flour, beaten egg, then panko, pressing crumbs on.
3. Heat oil to 175°C.
4. Fry goujons in two batches, 3–4 minutes, until deep gold; drain on a rack and salt immediately.
5. Split and toast the brioche rolls cut-side down in a dry pan, 60 seconds.
6. Build: gribiche on both cut faces, shredded gem below, three goujons per roll, more gribiche over.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm, halved on the diagonal, with extra gribiche and lemon wedges; paper-lined baskets keep it casual-luxe.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: rice-flour and GF-panko crumb, GF rolls. Oven option: 220°C for 12 minutes on a preheated oiled tray.
