# Beetroot-Cured Salmon with Horseradish Cream

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A 36-hour cure that paints the salmon a jewel-magenta rim — spectacular sliced thin on a sharing board, zero à-la-minute stress.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Salmon fillet, skin on, pin-boned — 600 g
- Raw beetroot — 250 g, coarsely grated
- Coarse sea salt — 100 g
- Caster sugar — 80 g
- Gin — 30 ml
- Juniper berries — 6, crushed
- Dill — 20 g, chopped, plus fronds to serve
- Orange — 1, zested
- Crème fraîche — 150 g
- Fresh horseradish — 20 g, finely grated
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Mix beetroot, salt, sugar, gin, juniper, dill and orange zest to a wet cure.
2. Lay salmon skin-down on a double layer of cling film; pack the cure over the flesh completely.
3. Wrap tightly, set in a tray, weight with a board and 1 kg. Refrigerate 36 hours, flipping once at 18 hours.
4. Unwrap, rinse briefly under cold water, pat thoroughly dry. Rest uncovered in the fridge 2 hours to tack up.
5. Stir horseradish and lemon juice into crème fraîche; season with pepper. Chill.
6. Slice the salmon at a shallow angle, 2–3 mm thin, leaving the skin behind.

## Plating & Service
Shingle slices across a chilled plate, dot with horseradish cream, scatter dill fronds and cracked pepper. Brown bread optional.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Alcohol-free: replace gin with 30 ml cold water and extra juniper. Cured salmon keeps 4 days wrapped; slice to order.
