# Roast Turkey Crown with Sage-Onion Stuffing & Cranberry

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Crown-only roasting solves the dry-breast curse — butter beneath the skin, stuffing baked separately for crunch, sharp cranberry to cut through.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Turkey crown, bone in — 2.5 kg
- Unsalted butter — 150 g, softened (120 g bird, 30 g stuffing)
- Sage — 15 g, chopped (5 g butter, 10 g stuffing)
- Lemon — 1, zested
- Onions — 400 g, fine dice
- Fresh white breadcrumbs — 200 g
- Free-range egg — 1
- Chicken stock — 600 ml (100 ml stuffing, 500 ml gravy)
- White wine — 150 ml
- Plain flour — 20 g
- Cranberries — 300 g; caster sugar — 100 g; orange — 1, juice and zest
- Fine sea salt — 12 g; black pepper — 3 g

## Method
1. Beat 120 g butter with 5 g sage, lemon zest and 4 g salt; work under the crown's skin and over the top. Rest at room temperature 45 minutes.
2. Roast at 190°C for 40 minutes, then 160°C for 60–80 minutes to a 68°C core (carryover to 72°C). Baste twice. Rest 30 minutes under foil.
3. Stuffing: soften onions in 30 g butter 10 minutes; mix with crumbs, 10 g sage, egg, 100 ml stock, salt and pepper. Bake in a buttered dish at 190°C for 30 minutes until crisp-topped.
4. Cranberry: simmer cranberries, sugar, orange juice and zest 10–12 minutes until just collapsed. Cool.
5. Gravy: set the roasting tray over heat; stir flour into the juices, cook 2 minutes, deglaze with wine, whisk in 500 ml stock. Simmer 8 minutes; strain, season, add resting juices.
6. Carve the crown in thick slices across the breast.

## Plating & Service
Platter service: sliced crown, stuffing squares, cranberry in a bowl, gravy in a boat — with all the trimmings orbiting.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Brine the crown (5% salt, 4 hours) for insurance. GF: cornflour gravy, GF-crumb stuffing.
