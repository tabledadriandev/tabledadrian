# Grilled Asparagus, Poached Egg & Brown Butter Hollandaise

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> English asparagus season on a plate — char from the grill, a nutty noisette hollandaise, and a yolk to bind it all.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Asparagus spears — 500 g, trimmed
- Olive oil — 20 ml
- Free-range eggs — 4, plus 3 yolks
- Unsalted butter — 170 g
- White wine vinegar — 30 ml (15 ml reduction, 15 ml poaching)
- Shallot — 25 g, minced
- Black peppercorns — 5, crushed
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Parmesan — 20 g, shaved
- Flaky sea salt — 3 g
- Chervil — 5 g

## Method
1. Brown the butter: melt 170 g over medium heat, cook 4–5 minutes until hazelnut-scented; strain off 140 g of the clear noisette, keeping some toasted solids aside. Hold at 50°C.
2. Reduce 15 ml vinegar with shallot and peppercorns to 10 ml; strain.
3. Whisk yolks with the reduction over a bain-marie at 65–70°C to thick ribbons, 4–5 minutes; slowly whisk in the warm brown butter. Season with lemon juice and salt.
4. Toss asparagus in oil and salt; grill on a screaming-hot plancha or griddle 3–4 minutes, turning, until charred and just tender.
5. Poach eggs in barely simmering water with 15 ml vinegar for 3 minutes; drain on cloth.
6. Loosen hollandaise with a teaspoon of warm water if needed; fold in the reserved brown solids.

## Plating & Service
Asparagus stacked loosely, egg on top, hollandaise spooned over, Parmesan shavings and chervil to finish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add crisp Parma ham shards, or keep it meat-free with toasted hazelnuts. Out of season: use purple sprouting broccoli.
