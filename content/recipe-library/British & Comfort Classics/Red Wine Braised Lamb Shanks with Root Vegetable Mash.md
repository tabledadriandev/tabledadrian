# Red Wine Braised Lamb Shanks with Root Vegetable Mash

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Shanks braised until the bone twists free, glazed in their own reduced liquor over a swede-carrot-potato mash — winter anchoring at its best.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Lamb shanks — 4 (about 1.6 kg)
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Onions — 250 g, chunky
- Carrots — 350 g (150 g braise, 200 g mash)
- Celery — 60 g, sliced
- Garlic — 5 cloves (25 g)
- Tomato purée — 40 g
- Red wine — 500 ml
- Lamb or beef stock — 600 ml
- Rosemary — 2 sprigs; bay — 2
- Redcurrant jelly — 20 g
- Swede — 300 g, diced
- Floury potatoes — 500 g, diced
- Unsalted butter — 80 g
- Fine sea salt — 9 g; black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Season shanks; brown on all sides in oil in a deep casserole, 10 minutes. Remove.
2. Colour onions, 150 g carrots and celery 8 minutes; add garlic and tomato purée, 2 minutes.
3. Deglaze with wine; reduce by half. Add stock, rosemary, bay and jelly.
4. Return shanks; braise covered at 150°C for 2.5–3 hours, turning once, until the meat slumps from the bone.
5. Mash: simmer swede 10 minutes, add 200 g carrots and potatoes, cook 15 minutes more; drain well, mash with butter, season.
6. Strain and reduce the braising liquor by half to a glossy sauce; return shanks to glaze, basting, 5 minutes.

## Plating & Service
Shank standing bone-up on the mash, sauce poured over until it floods the plate rim; gremolata or parsley optional but welcome.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> DF: olive-oil mash. Add a strip of orange zest to the braise for warmth. Sauce freezes; shanks reheat better than fresh.
