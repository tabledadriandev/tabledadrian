# Root Vegetable Wellington with Chestnut & Sage

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A centrepiece that owes nothing to meat — roasted roots and chestnut farce in crisp pastry, carving into a mosaic cross-section.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Butternut squash — 400 g, 2 cm batons
- Carrots — 250 g, halved lengthways
- Parsnips — 250 g, quartered lengthways
- Olive oil — 50 ml
- Cooked chestnuts — 200 g, crumbled
- Chestnut mushrooms — 300 g, minced
- Shallots — 80 g, minced
- Garlic — 3 cloves (15 g)
- Sage — 10 g, chopped; thyme — 3 g
- Soy sauce — 15 ml
- Fresh breadcrumbs — 60 g
- Vegan puff pastry — 500 g
- Plant milk — 40 ml, with 10 ml oil for glazing
- Fine sea salt — 8 g; black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 200°C. Roast squash, carrots and parsnips in 30 ml oil with salt, 25 minutes, until tender and edged gold. Cool.
2. Farce: cook mushrooms, shallots and garlic in 20 ml oil over high heat 10 minutes until dry; add chestnuts, herbs, soy and breadcrumbs. Season; cool.
3. Roll pastry to a 30 x 40 cm sheet on paper. Spread half the farce down the centre third.
4. Lay the roots lengthways in alternating rows; press remaining farce over and around into a firm loaf.
5. Fold pastry over, seal seam and ends underneath; chill 30 minutes.
6. Glaze with the milk-oil mix; score chevrons; salt the top.
7. Bake at 200°C for 40–45 minutes until deep gold. Rest 10 minutes before slicing thickly.

## Plating & Service
Thick slices showing the vegetable mosaic, vegan onion gravy poured at the table, braised red cabbage beside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: swap chestnuts for cooked green lentils. Add a layer of wilted spinach under the roots for colour.
