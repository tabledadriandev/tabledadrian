# Cauliflower Cheese Soup with Crispy Sage

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sunday's favourite side reimagined as a velvet soup — roasted florets for depth, sharp Cheddar melted in at the end.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Cauliflower — 800 g, in florets
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Onion — 150 g, sliced
- Garlic — 2 cloves (10 g)
- Unsalted butter — 60 g (30 g soup, 30 g sage)
- Vegetable stock — 800 ml
- Whole milk — 200 ml
- Mature Cheddar — 120 g, grated
- English mustard — 5 g
- Sage leaves — 12
- Freshly grated nutmeg — 0.5 g
- Fine sea salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 200°C. Roast two-thirds of the cauliflower with oil and a pinch of salt for 25 minutes until deeply golden.
2. Meanwhile, sweat onion and garlic in 30 g butter over low heat 8 minutes.
3. Add raw cauliflower and stock; simmer 12 minutes until tender. Add roasted florets and milk; simmer 3 minutes.
4. Blend at full speed 90 seconds; pass through a sieve for silk.
5. Off the boil, whisk in Cheddar, mustard and nutmeg until melted. Season. Do not reboil.
6. Foam 30 g butter in a small pan; fry sage leaves 30–45 seconds until crisp. Drain on paper.

## Plating & Service
Warm bowls, soup poured at the table, crispy sage and a few drops of the sage butter on the surface.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: oil-roast everything, oat milk, and 20 g white miso plus nutritional yeast instead of cheese. Add a Stilton crumble for punch.
