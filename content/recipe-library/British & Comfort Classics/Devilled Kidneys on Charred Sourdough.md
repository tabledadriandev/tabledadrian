# Devilled Kidneys on Charred Sourdough

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A Victorian club-breakfast revival — kidneys flashed hard and napped in a cayenne-mustard cream that begs for good bread.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Lamb kidneys — 400 g, halved, cores snipped out
- Plain flour — 20 g
- Cayenne pepper — 2 g
- Smoked paprika — 3 g
- English mustard — 10 g
- Worcestershire-style sauce — 15 ml
- Sherry — 40 ml
- Chicken stock — 100 ml
- Double cream — 60 ml
- Unsalted butter — 40 g
- Sourdough — 4 thick slices (200 g)
- Flat-leaf parsley — 10 g, chopped
- Fine sea salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Soak kidneys in cold salted water 10 minutes; drain and pat completely dry.
2. Toss kidneys in flour seasoned with cayenne, paprika and salt; shake off excess.
3. Char sourdough over a flame or in a searing-hot dry pan; keep warm.
4. Heat 30 g butter in a wide pan until foaming subsides; sear kidneys cut-side down 90 seconds, flip for 60 seconds. Remove — they must stay pink.
5. Deglaze with sherry, reduce by half, add mustard, Worcestershire sauce and stock; reduce 2 minutes to a glaze.
6. Stir in cream and remaining butter; simmer 60 seconds until coating consistency.
7. Return kidneys with their juices for 30 seconds only — no longer, or they toughen.

## Plating & Service
Spoon kidneys and sauce over the charred toast, finish with parsley and a crack of pepper. Serve immediately — this dish waits for no one.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Substitute chicken livers (identical timing). GF: dust with rice flour and use GF bread.
