# Beer-Battered Onion Rings with Smoked Paprika Salt

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Thick-cut sweet onion in a lager batter that shatters — the burger's rightful escort, seasoned with smoked paprika salt.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Large sweet onions — 2 (500 g), in 1.5 cm rings
- Plain flour — 150 g, plus 50 g for dusting
- Cornflour — 50 g
- Baking powder — 5 g
- Cold lager — 280 ml, fridge-cold
- Fine sea salt — 6 g
- Flaky sea salt — 5 g
- Smoked paprika — 4 g
- Neutral oil — 2 L, for frying

## Method
1. Separate onion rings; soak in ice water 10 minutes (crisper, milder), then dry completely.
2. Grind flaky salt with smoked paprika.
3. Heat oil to 180°C.
4. Whisk flour, cornflour, baking powder and fine salt; whisk in cold lager until just smooth.
5. Dust rings in flour, drag through batter, and fry in batches 2–3 minutes until puffed and golden — never crowd; the temperature must hold.
6. Drain on a rack; hit with paprika salt immediately.

## Plating & Service
Stacked in a tower or tumbled beside a burger — sauce optional; the crunch is the event.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: rice flour + cornflour with GF beer. Buttermilk-soak version fries up more tender but heavier — pick your church.
