# The Ultimate Bacon Butty with Tomato-Chilli Jam

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The bacon sandwich taken seriously: dry-cured rashers rendered crisp, soft white bloomer, and a sticky homemade tomato-chilli jam instead of ketchup.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Dry-cured smoked back bacon — 12 rashers (480 g)
- White bloomer loaf — 8 thick slices (400 g)
- Ripe tomatoes — 400 g, chopped
- Red chilli — 1 (10 g), minced
- Red wine vinegar — 50 ml
- Light brown sugar — 80 g
- Garlic — 1 clove (5 g), grated
- Fresh ginger — 10 g, grated
- Fine sea salt — 3 g
- Olive oil — 15 ml
- Watercress — 40 g

## Method
1. For the jam: combine tomatoes, chilli, vinegar, sugar, garlic, ginger and salt in a saucepan.
2. Simmer over medium-low heat 30–35 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thick, glossy and jammy. Cool — it thickens further.
3. Lay bacon in a cold, dry pan; bring to medium-high heat and render 3–4 minutes per side until crisp at the fat, tender at the eye.
4. Toast the bread cut-side only, briefly, in the bacon fat with the olive oil — one side soft, one side golden.
5. Spread jam thickly on the soft side of four slices.
6. Pile on three rashers each, add a handful of watercress, and cap with the second slice, toasted-side out.
7. Press gently and halve on the diagonal.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm within minutes, jam-side details oozing, with extra jam in a pot alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The jam keeps 2 weeks chilled — make double. GF: use GF bloomer. Add a fried egg to upgrade to the full "double-yolker" butty.
