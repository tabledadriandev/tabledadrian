# Beer-Battered Haddock with Triple-Cooked Chips & Crushed Peas

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The national dish done to restaurant spec — glass-crisp lager batter, thrice-cooked chips with shattering edges, and minted crushed peas.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Haddock fillets, skinned — 4 x 180 g
- Plain flour — 200 g, plus 50 g for dusting
- Cornflour — 50 g
- Baking powder — 6 g
- Cold lager — 330 ml, fridge-cold
- Floury potatoes (Maris Piper) — 1.2 kg, cut into 2 cm chips
- Neutral oil or beef dripping — 2.5 L
- Frozen petits pois — 400 g
- Unsalted mint — 8 g, chopped
- Olive oil — 25 ml
- Lemon — 2, in wedges
- Malt vinegar — to serve
- Fine sea salt — 10 g

## Method
1. Chips, cook one: simmer chips in salted water 8–10 minutes until almost falling apart; drain, steam-dry, freeze on a tray 30 minutes.
2. Cook two: fry at 130°C for 8 minutes until blond and blistered; drain, return to the freezer tray.
3. Batter: whisk flour, cornflour, baking powder and 4 g salt; whisk in ice-cold lager just to combine — lumps are fine. Use within 15 minutes.
4. Heat oil to 180°C. Dust fish in flour, drag through batter, and lower in away from you, holding the tail 3 seconds so it sets.
5. Fry fish 6–7 minutes, turning once, to deep gold and 55°C core. Drain on a rack; salt.
6. Cook three: fry chips at 190°C for 4–5 minutes until deep amber. Salt hard.
7. Peas: boil 3 minutes, drain, crush roughly with olive oil, mint and salt.

## Plating & Service
Fish over chips on warm plates (never stacked long), peas in a mound, lemon and malt vinegar on the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: rice flour plus cornflour batter with GF beer. Cod, hake or pollock swap in evenly — adjust frying by thickness, not species.
