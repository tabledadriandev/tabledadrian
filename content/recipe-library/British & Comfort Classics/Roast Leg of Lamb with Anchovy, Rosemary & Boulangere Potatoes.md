# Roast Leg of Lamb with Anchovy, Rosemary & Boulangere Potatoes

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Anchovies melt into the lamb as pure seasoning — no fishiness, just depth — while the potatoes roast underneath drinking every dripping.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Leg of lamb, bone in — 2.2 kg
- Anchovy fillets — 10 (40 g)
- Garlic — 6 cloves (30 g), slivered
- Rosemary — 6 sprigs
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Waxy potatoes — 1.5 kg, sliced 3 mm
- Onions — 300 g, thinly sliced
- Chicken or lamb stock — 600 ml, hot
- Unsalted butter — 0 g (kept dairy-free; use 20 ml extra oil)
- White wine — 150 ml
- Fine sea salt — 10 g; black pepper — 3 g
- Lemon — 1, juiced (30 ml)

## Method
1. Heat oven to 220°C. Pierce the lamb 20 times; push in anchovy halves, garlic slivers and rosemary tips. Rub with 20 ml oil, salt lightly (anchovies season), pepper well.
2. Layer potatoes and onions in a deep tray, seasoning between layers; pour over hot stock and wine, drizzle with 20 ml oil.
3. Set a rack directly over the potatoes; sit the lamb on top.
4. Roast 20 minutes at 220°C, then 90–100 minutes at 160°C to a core of 55°C for pink.
5. Rest the lamb under foil 25 minutes; keep potatoes in the oven at 200°C to crisp the top layer.
6. Squeeze lemon over the lamb before carving; carve parallel to the bone in long slices.
7. Spoon any board juices back over the potatoes.

## Plating & Service
Bring the tray and the carved lamb to the table together — the potatoes, imbibed with lamb fat and stock, are the real star.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 15 g Dijon under the oil rub. Leftovers make superb shepherd's pie. GF and DF as written.
