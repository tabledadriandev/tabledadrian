# Venison Burger with Juniper & Beetroot Relish

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Lean venison needs added pork fat and a firm hand on doneness — juniper in the mix and a sweet-sharp beetroot relish keep it wild in the right way.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Venison mince — 550 g
- Pork back fat or fatty pork mince — 150 g
- Juniper berries — 6, finely crushed
- Thyme leaves — 3 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g; black pepper — 2 g
- Cooked beetroot — 250 g, coarsely grated
- Red onion — 100 g, minced
- Cider vinegar — 30 ml; light brown sugar — 30 g
- Horseradish cream — 30 g
- Crème fraîche — 60 g
- Brioche buns — 4 (340 g)
- Watercress — 50 g
- Neutral oil — 15 ml

## Method
1. Relish: simmer beetroot, onion, vinegar and sugar with a pinch of salt 12–15 minutes until sticky. Cool.
2. Mix venison, pork fat, juniper, thyme and pepper with light hands; shape 4 x 175 g dimpled patties. Chill 20 minutes.
3. Stir horseradish into crème fraîche.
4. Salt patties; sear in oil over high heat 3 minutes per side to a core of 57–60°C — past medium, venison turns to liver.
5. Rest 4 minutes under foil.
6. Toast buns; spread horseradish cream on both faces.
7. Build: watercress, patty, a generous spoon of beetroot relish, lid.

## Plating & Service
With game chips or celeriac fries; the relish pot on the table for topping up.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No venison? Use 50/50 beef chuck and venison sausagemeat. DF: swap crème fraîche for mustard mayo.
