# Proper Yorkshire Puddings

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Equal volumes of egg, flour and milk, a long rest, and violently hot fat — four rules, sky-high puddings, no exceptions.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Plain flour — 140 g
- Free-range eggs — 4 (about 200 ml)
- Whole milk — 200 ml
- Fine sea salt — 3 g
- Beef dripping or neutral oil — 60 g
- White pepper — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Whisk flour and salt with the eggs to a thick paste, then whisk in milk until smooth and pourable. No lumps.
2. Rest at room temperature at least 1 hour — overnight in the fridge is better (bring back to room temp).
3. Heat oven to 220°C. Divide dripping between a 12-hole muffin tin; heat 10 minutes until smoking.
4. Re-whisk the batter; pour into a jug.
5. Working at the open oven, fill each hole two-thirds — the batter must scream on contact.
6. Bake 20–25 minutes without opening the door, until towering, bronzed and crisp.
7. Serve within minutes; they wait for no gravy.

## Plating & Service
Straight to the table in a cloth-lined bowl beside the roast — one per guest is a lie; make twelve for six.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Giant version: four Yorkshire in a deep bun tray, 25 minutes. DF: use oil and swap milk for water-milk mix (crispier, less tender).
