# Butternut, Sage & Brown Butter Risotto

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Comfort in a bowl — half the squash melts into the rice, half roasts for texture, and brown butter with crisp sage finishes it like autumn itself.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Carnaroli or arborio rice — 320 g
- Butternut squash — 700 g (400 g diced 1 cm, 300 g grated)
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Onion — 150 g, fine dice
- Celery — 40 g, fine dice
- Dry white wine — 120 ml
- Hot vegetable stock — 1.3 L
- Unsalted butter — 90 g (30 g mantecatura, 60 g browned)
- Parmesan (or vegetarian hard cheese) — 80 g, grated
- Sage leaves — 16
- Freshly grated nutmeg — 0.3 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g; black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 200°C; roast the diced squash in 20 ml oil with salt, 22–25 minutes, until caramelised.
2. Soften onion and celery in 20 ml oil, 6 minutes. Add rice; toast 2 minutes until the edges turn translucent.
3. Add wine; stir until absorbed. Add the grated squash.
4. Ladle in hot stock one addition at a time, stirring, 17–18 minutes, until the rice is al dente and flowing.
5. Meanwhile brown 60 g butter with the sage until nutty and the leaves crisp; separate leaves onto paper.
6. Off heat: beat in 30 g butter, Parmesan and nutmeg; rest 2 minutes lidded. Adjust to all'onda (wave) consistency with stock; season.
7. Fold in half the roasted squash.

## Plating & Service
Flat bowls, risotto spread with a tap, remaining roasted squash on top, brown butter spooned over, crisp sage last.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: olive oil mantecatura, 20 g white miso for the cheese. Add crumbled amaretti for the Lombard wink, or Gorgonzola for punch.
