# Sage & Onion Stuffing Balls

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Baked as balls, not slab — maximum crisp surface, moist sausage-sage centres, and they roll obligingly into any gravy.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Sausage meat — 400 g
- Onions — 300 g, fine dice
- Unsalted butter — 40 g
- Fresh white breadcrumbs — 150 g
- Sage — 12 g, chopped
- Thyme leaves — 3 g
- Lemon — 1, zested
- Free-range egg — 1
- Chicken stock — 60 ml
- Fine sea salt — 5 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Olive oil — 15 ml

## Method
1. Soften onions in butter over medium-low heat, 10 minutes, until sweet; cool 10 minutes.
2. Mix onions with sausage meat, breadcrumbs, sage, thyme, lemon zest, egg, stock, salt and pepper — light hands.
3. Test-fry a nugget; adjust seasoning.
4. Roll 18 balls (about 45 g each); chill 20 minutes.
5. Heat oven to 190°C. Space balls on an oiled tray; brush tops with oil.
6. Bake 30–35 minutes, turning once, until bronzed and 72°C at the core.

## Plating & Service
Piled in a warm bowl within reach of the gravy boat — three per guest vanishes; make eighteen for six.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian: swap sausage for 250 g cooked chestnuts and 150 g sautéed mushrooms, plus one extra egg. Apricot version for festive tables.
