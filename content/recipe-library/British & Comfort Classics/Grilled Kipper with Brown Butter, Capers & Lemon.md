# Grilled Kipper with Brown Butter, Capers & Lemon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> An old-school breakfast for guests who know — butterflied smoked herring under nutty butter with capers doing the seasoning.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Kippers (butterflied smoked herring) — 4 (about 800 g)
- Unsalted butter — 100 g
- Capers — 30 g, rinsed
- Lemon — 2 (1 juiced, 1 in wedges)
- Flat-leaf parsley — 15 g, chopped
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Cayenne pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Preheat the grill to high; line a tray with foil.
2. Lay kippers skin-side down; dot with 20 g of the butter.
3. Grill 5–6 minutes without turning, until the flesh is hot through and the edges catch.
4. Meanwhile, melt remaining 80 g butter in a small pan over medium heat; cook 3–4 minutes, swirling, until the solids turn hazelnut brown.
5. Off heat, add capers (they will sizzle), lemon juice, cayenne and parsley.
6. Warm four plates — kippers go cold fast.

## Plating & Service
One kipper per warm plate, brown butter and capers spooned nose to tail, lemon wedge and black pepper. Brown bread and butter on the side for those who want it.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Equally good with smoked mackerel fillets (3 minutes under the grill). DF: use olive oil warmed with capers and lemon instead of butter.
