# Chestnut & Mushroom Nut Roast with Onion Gravy

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A nut roast with actual structure and savour — chestnuts, toasted hazelnuts and miso-caramelised mushrooms, sliced thick under onion gravy.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Chestnut mushrooms — 400 g, minced
- Cooked chestnuts — 200 g, crumbled
- Hazelnuts — 150 g, toasted, roughly chopped
- Olive oil — 50 ml
- Onions — 550 g (150 g roast, 400 g gravy)
- Celery — 60 g, fine dice
- Garlic — 3 cloves (15 g)
- White miso — 20 g; soy sauce — 15 ml
- Cooked green lentils — 200 g
- Fresh breadcrumbs — 80 g
- Ground flaxseed — 20 g, whisked with 60 ml water
- Sage — 8 g; thyme — 4 g
- Plain flour — 15 g; dark ale or stock — 500 ml
- Fine sea salt — 8 g; black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Cook mushrooms in 20 ml oil over high heat until dry, 10 minutes; add miso, stir 1 minute.
2. Soften 150 g onion, celery and garlic in 15 ml oil, 8 minutes.
3. Combine mushrooms, onion mix, chestnuts, hazelnuts, lentils, crumbs, flax gel, soy, herbs, salt and pepper; the mix should hold when squeezed.
4. Pack firmly into a lined 900 g loaf tin; brush with oil.
5. Bake at 180°C, covered with foil 40 minutes, uncovered 25–30 minutes until firm and browned. Rest 15 minutes before turning out.
6. Gravy: caramelise 400 g sliced onions in 15 ml oil, 25 minutes; stir in flour, add ale or stock gradually; simmer 10 minutes, season.

## Plating & Service
Thick slices with roast potatoes, greens and a flood of onion gravy — it earns its place at the centre of any Sunday table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: GF crumbs and cornflour gravy. Cranberry layer through the middle for festive service. Slices freeze and pan-fry brilliantly.
