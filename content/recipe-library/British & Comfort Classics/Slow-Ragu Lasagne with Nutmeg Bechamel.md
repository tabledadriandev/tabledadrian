# Slow-Ragu Lasagne with Nutmeg Bechamel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A three-hour beef-and-pork ragù, silk béchamel and proper sheets — built a day ahead so it cuts in clean, proud squares.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Beef chuck mince — 600 g; pork mince — 400 g
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Onion — 200 g; carrot — 150 g; celery — 80 g — all fine dice
- Garlic — 4 cloves (20 g)
- Tomato purée — 50 g
- Red wine — 250 ml
- Chopped tomatoes — 800 g
- Whole milk — 1.2 L (200 ml ragù, 1 L béchamel)
- Bay — 2
- Unsalted butter — 80 g; plain flour — 70 g
- Freshly grated nutmeg — 1 g
- Dried egg lasagne sheets — 300 g
- Parmesan — 120 g, grated
- Fine sea salt — 12 g; black pepper — 3 g

## Method
1. Brown the minces hard in oil in a wide pot, breaking them up, 10 minutes; remove. Soften soffritto 10 minutes in the fat.
2. Add garlic and tomato purée, 2 minutes; return meat, deglaze with wine, reduce 5 minutes.
3. Add tomatoes, 200 ml milk, bay, salt; simmer part-covered on lowest heat 2.5–3 hours, stirring occasionally, until thick and glossy.
4. Béchamel: cook butter and flour 2 minutes; whisk in 1 L warm milk gradually; simmer 6 minutes. Season with nutmeg and salt.
5. Build in a 25 x 35 cm dish: béchamel smear, sheets, ragù, béchamel, Parmesan — four rounds, finishing with béchamel and a heavy Parmesan lid.
6. Rest 30 minutes (or chill overnight). Bake at 180°C for 45 minutes until bronzed and bubbling.
7. Rest 20 minutes before cutting — non-negotiable for clean squares.

## Plating & Service
A tall square per plate with dressed bitter leaves; nothing else — the lasagne is the event.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fresh pasta sheets: blanch 30 seconds first. Make it a day ahead; it reheats better than it debuts (160°C, 30 minutes).
