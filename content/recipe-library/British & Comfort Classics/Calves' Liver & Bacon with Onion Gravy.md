# Calves' Liver & Bacon with Onion Gravy

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Ninety seconds a side — that is the entire dish. Blushing liver, crisp bacon and a patient onion gravy made before the pan gets hot.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Calves' liver, sliced 1 cm — 600 g
- Whole milk — 200 ml (optional soak)
- Smoked back bacon — 8 rashers (320 g)
- Onions — 400 g, thinly sliced
- Unsalted butter — 50 g
- Plain flour — 15 g, plus 30 g for dusting
- Red wine — 100 ml
- Beef stock — 400 ml
- Sherry vinegar — 10 ml
- Sage leaves — 8
- Olive oil — 20 ml
- Fine sea salt — 6 g; black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Optional: soak liver in milk 30 minutes for a milder profile; pat completely dry.
2. Gravy: cook onions in 30 g butter with a pinch of salt over medium-low, 25 minutes, to deep gold. Stir in 15 g flour, deglaze with wine, add stock; simmer 10 minutes to coating. Finish with sherry vinegar.
3. Crisp the bacon in a dry pan, 3–4 minutes per side; keep warm.
4. Dust liver in seasoned flour, shaking off all excess.
5. Heat oil and 20 g butter until foaming hard; fry liver 90 seconds per side with the sage — mahogany outside, rose-pink inside. Do not crowd; two batches.
6. Rest the liver 2 minutes; it finishes to a blush.

## Plating & Service
Liver overlapping on mash or celeriac purée, bacon across, crisped sage and a full ladle of onion gravy over the lot.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: cornflour for dusting and thickening. Lamb's liver is the honest weeknight swap — same timing, stronger flavour.
