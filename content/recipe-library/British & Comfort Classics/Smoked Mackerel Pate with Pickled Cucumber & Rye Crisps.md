# Smoked Mackerel Pate with Pickled Cucumber & Rye Crisps

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Three textures in three minutes of assembly — rich oily fish whipped light, sharp quick pickles, and shatter-crisp rye.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Smoked mackerel fillets — 300 g, skinned
- Cream cheese — 120 g
- Crème fraîche — 60 g
- Horseradish cream — 20 g
- Lemon — 1, zest and 20 ml juice
- Dijon mustard — 5 g
- Cucumber — 200 g, ribboned
- White wine vinegar — 40 ml
- Caster sugar — 15 g
- Dill — 8 g
- Dark rye bread — 150 g, sliced 3 mm
- Olive oil — 15 ml
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 160°C. Brush rye slices with oil, bake on a rack 10–12 minutes until completely crisp. Cool.
2. Whisk vinegar and sugar with a pinch of salt; toss with cucumber ribbons and half the dill. Pickle 15 minutes; drain.
3. Flake mackerel, checking for bones.
4. Pulse two-thirds of the fish with cream cheese, crème fraîche, horseradish, mustard, lemon zest and juice until smooth.
5. Fold in the remaining flaked fish for texture; season with black pepper (rarely needs salt).
6. Chill 30 minutes to firm.

## Plating & Service
A generous quenelle of pâté, a tangle of pickled cucumber, rye crisps standing upright, dill fronds over.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: serve with seeded GF crackers. Lighter: all crème fraîche, no cream cheese. Works with hot-smoked trout or salmon too.
