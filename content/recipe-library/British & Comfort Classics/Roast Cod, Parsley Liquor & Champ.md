# Roast Cod, Parsley Liquor & Champ

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The East End pie-shop's green liquor, poured around a thick roast cod loin instead of eels — heritage flavours, fine-dining execution.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Cod loin portions, skin on — 4 x 170 g
- Olive oil — 25 ml
- Unsalted butter — 100 g (30 g fish, 70 g champ)
- Fish or chicken stock — 300 ml
- Double cream — 80 ml
- Flat-leaf parsley — 60 g, leaves and stalks separated
- Garlic — 1 clove (5 g)
- Cornflour — 8 g, slaked in 15 ml water
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Floury potatoes — 900 g, peeled
- Spring onions — 70 g, sliced
- Whole milk — 100 ml
- Fine sea salt — 8 g; white pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Champ: boil potatoes 20 minutes; rice. Warm milk with spring onions 3 minutes; beat in with 70 g butter. Season.
2. Liquor: simmer parsley stalks and garlic in stock 5 minutes; strain. Add cream, thicken with slaked cornflour, simmer 2 minutes.
3. Blanch parsley leaves 20 seconds; refresh, squeeze, and blend into the warm (not boiling) sauce until vivid green. Season with salt, white pepper, lemon juice. Hold at 70°C.
4. Salt the cod skin; rest 10 minutes, pat dry.
5. Sear skin-down in oil over medium-high, 4–5 minutes, pressing flat at first. Flip, add 30 g butter, baste 60–90 seconds to a 52°C core.
6. Rest the fish 2 minutes.

## Plating & Service
A mound of champ, cod skin-up on top, the green liquor flooded around the plate — a few drops of the basting butter over the skin.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Traditionalists: poach the cod instead. Haddock, hake or pollock all work. Liquor must stay green — never boil after blending.
