# Summer Pudding with Creme Fraiche

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> No oven, pure alchemy — white bread pressed overnight with barely-cooked berries until it turns crimson and slices like a terrine.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Mixed summer berries — 900 g (raspberries 350 g, redcurrants 200 g, blackcurrants 100 g, strawberries 250 g, halved)
- Caster sugar — 140 g
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Crème de cassis — 20 ml (optional)
- White bread, day-old, medium-sliced — 10 slices (about 400 g), crusts off
- Crème fraîche — 200 g
- Mint — 6 leaves

## Method
1. Warm currants with sugar and 30 ml water, 3–4 minutes until they burst; add raspberries and strawberries off the heat with lemon juice and cassis. Cool 10 minutes; strain, keeping fruit and juice separate.
2. Line a 1.2 L pudding basin with cling film, overhanging.
3. Dip bread slices in the juice, one side only; line the basin dipped-side out, overlapping with no gaps — a full circle of stained walls.
4. Pack the fruit in with half the remaining juice; seal with juice-dipped bread.
5. Fold over the film; press with a saucer and a 500 g weight. Refrigerate 12–24 hours.
6. Unmould onto a lipped plate; paint any pale patches with reserved juice.

## Plating & Service
Cut wedges with a serrated knife; crème fraîche and mint alongside, remaining juice in a small jug for flooding.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Brioche version is richer but softer — classic white holds better. Frozen berry mixes work off-season; thaw fully and drain first.
