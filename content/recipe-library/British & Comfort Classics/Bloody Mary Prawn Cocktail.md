# Bloody Mary Prawn Cocktail

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The retro classic with a bar-cart backbone — vodka, celery and a horseradish-spiked Marie Rose that actually wakes up the prawns.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Cooked cold-water prawns, peeled — 320 g
- Raw king prawns, shell on — 8 (240 g)
- Olive oil — 15 ml
- Mayonnaise — 100 g
- Tomato ketchup — 40 g
- Vodka — 15 ml
- Worcestershire-style sauce — 8 ml
- Hot sauce — 4 ml
- Fresh horseradish — 8 g, grated
- Lemon — 1, half juiced (15 ml), half in wedges
- Baby gem lettuce — 2 heads (200 g), shredded
- Celery — 60 g, fine brunoise
- Avocado — 1 (150 g), diced
- Smoked paprika — 1 g

## Method
1. Whisk mayonnaise, ketchup, vodka, Worcestershire sauce, hot sauce, horseradish and lemon juice into a Marie Rose. Chill.
2. Sear the king prawns in oil over high heat 90 seconds per side until pink and lightly charred; season and cool slightly.
3. Fold the peeled prawns and celery through two-thirds of the sauce.
4. Layer shredded gem into chilled coupes, then avocado, then the dressed prawns.
5. Nap with remaining sauce.
6. Hang two charred king prawns off each glass rim; dust with paprika.

## Plating & Service
Serve in stemmed glasses on crushed ice with lemon wedges and brown bread and butter alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Alcohol-free: omit vodka, add 5 ml extra lemon and a pinch of celery salt. Swap prawns for white crab meat for a lighter luxury version.
