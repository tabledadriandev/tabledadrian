# Leek, Cheddar & Jersey Royal Breakfast Frittata

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A make-ahead deck breakfast that holds beautifully warm or at room temperature — sweet leeks, waxy potatoes, sharp Cheddar.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Free-range eggs — 8
- Jersey Royal or waxy new potatoes — 300 g, sliced 5 mm
- Leeks — 300 g, sliced 1 cm
- Unsalted butter — 40 g
- Olive oil — 15 ml
- Mature Cheddar — 120 g, grated
- Crème fraîche — 60 g
- Thyme — 3 sprigs, leaves picked
- Chives — 8 g, sliced
- Fine sea salt — 4 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Simmer potato slices in salted water 8 minutes until just tender; drain well.
2. Heat oven to 180°C. Sweat leeks in butter and oil with thyme in a 24 cm ovenproof pan over medium-low heat, 8–10 minutes, until silky.
3. Whisk eggs with crème fraîche, salt, pepper and two-thirds of the Cheddar.
4. Layer potatoes through the leeks in the pan; pour over the egg mixture.
5. Cook on the hob over low heat 4 minutes to set the base.
6. Scatter remaining Cheddar; bake 12–14 minutes until just set with a slight tremble at the centre.
7. Rest 5 minutes before releasing the edges.

## Plating & Service
Cut into generous wedges, scatter chives, and serve with dressed leaves and toasted sourdough for the hungry.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add hot-smoked salmon flakes or crisped bacon. DF: skip cheese and crème fraîche, add 10 g nutritional yeast and extra herbs.
