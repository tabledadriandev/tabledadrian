# Millionaire's Shortbread

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Three disciplined layers — sandy shortbread, chewy salted caramel cooked to exactly 112°C, and tempered-look chocolate that snaps clean.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Plain flour — 230 g
- Caster sugar — 75 g
- Unsalted butter — 320 g (170 g shortbread, 150 g caramel)
- Fine sea salt — 4 g
- Condensed milk — 397 g tin
- Golden syrup — 60 g
- Light brown sugar — 100 g
- Vanilla extract — 4 ml
- Flaky sea salt — 3 g
- Dark chocolate, 55–65% — 200 g
- Milk chocolate — 50 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 170°C; line a 23 cm square tin.
2. Shortbread: rub flour, caster sugar, 170 g butter and 2 g fine salt to a dough; press evenly into the tin, dock with a fork.
3. Bake 22–25 minutes until pale gold; cool.
4. Caramel: melt 150 g butter, condensed milk, syrup and brown sugar; cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, 8–10 minutes to 112°C — thick, golden, pulling from the sides. Add vanilla and 2 g fine salt.
5. Pour over the base; level; scatter half the flaky salt. Chill 1 hour until firm.
6. Melt dark chocolate to 45°C, cool to 31°C, then spread over the caramel; pipe thin lines of melted milk chocolate and feather with a skewer. Remaining flaky salt over.
7. Set at cool room temperature; cut with a hot dry knife into 12 bars.

## Plating & Service
Stacked on a slate or boxed for beach picnics — clean strata visible on every cut face.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: GF flour blend shortbread. Stir 30 g crushed pretzels into the caramel for salt-crunch chaos (adds gluten twice over).
