# Treacle-Glazed Gammon with Fried Duck Egg & Pineapple Chutney

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Gammon, egg and pineapple — the café classic reclaimed with a treacle-mustard glaze and a proper spiced chutney instead of a tinned ring.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Unsmoked gammon joint, boneless — 2 kg
- Onion — 150 g, halved; carrot — 100 g; bay — 2; black peppercorns — 8
- Cloves — 12, whole
- Black treacle — 60 g
- English mustard — 20 g
- Dark brown sugar — 40 g
- Fresh pineapple — 400 g, 1 cm dice
- Red chilli — 1 (10 g), minced
- Cider vinegar — 60 ml
- Light brown sugar — 60 g
- Ginger — 10 g, grated
- Duck eggs — 6
- Neutral oil — 30 ml
- Watercress — 60 g

## Method
1. Cover gammon with cold water; add onion, carrot, bay and peppercorns. Simmer gently 1 hour 40 minutes (50 min/kg); core 68°C. Cool 15 minutes in the liquor.
2. Chutney: simmer pineapple, chilli, vinegar, light brown sugar and ginger 25–30 minutes until thick and glossy. Cool.
3. Heat oven to 200°C. Strip the skin, leaving an even fat layer; score diamonds, stud crossings with cloves.
4. Mix treacle, mustard and dark sugar; brush half over the fat.
5. Roast 25–30 minutes, re-glazing twice, until mahogany and blistered. Rest 20 minutes.
6. Fry duck eggs in oil over medium heat, basting, 3 minutes — crisp lace edges, molten yolks.
7. Carve gammon 8 mm thick.

## Plating & Service
Two slices per plate, duck egg riding on top, a generous spoon of chutney and watercress alongside — chips are permitted and encouraged.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Boil-in-cider version: replace half the water with dry cider. The chutney keeps 2 weeks and makes any cold cut sing.
