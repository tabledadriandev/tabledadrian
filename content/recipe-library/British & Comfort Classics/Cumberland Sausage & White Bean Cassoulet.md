# Cumberland Sausage & White Bean Cassoulet

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A Franco-British handshake — herby sausages and smoked bacon slow-baked into garlicky beans under a golden crumb.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Cumberland sausages — 8 (600 g)
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Smoked bacon lardons — 150 g
- Onion — 200 g, diced
- Carrot — 150 g, diced
- Celery — 60 g, diced
- Garlic — 4 cloves (20 g), sliced
- Tomato purée — 30 g
- Chopped tomatoes — 400 g
- Cooked haricot or cannellini beans — 600 g (drained)
- Chicken stock — 400 ml
- Thyme — 4 sprigs; bay — 2; rosemary — 1 sprig
- Coarse sourdough crumbs — 60 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 10 g, chopped
- Fine sea salt — 7 g

## Method
1. Brown sausages in oil in a wide casserole, 8 minutes; remove. Render lardons 4 minutes.
2. Soften onion, carrot and celery 8 minutes; add garlic and tomato purée, 2 minutes.
3. Add chopped tomatoes, beans, stock and herbs; season lightly and simmer 5 minutes.
4. Nestle the sausages in; scatter the crumbs over.
5. Bake uncovered at 170°C for 50–55 minutes until the crumb is deep gold and the beans have drunk the stock to a stew.
6. Rest 10 minutes; the beans keep thickening.

## Plating & Service
Serve from the pot with parsley over — bitter leaves dressed sharp on the side, nothing else needed.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: skip crumbs or use GF bread. Add confit duck leg for the full cassoulet fantasy. Vegetarian: plant sausages and smoked paprika instead of bacon.
