# Pan-Fried Sea Bass with Brown Shrimp Butter & Samphire

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Crisp-skinned bass with a nut-brown butter full of sweet shrimp and capers — a 15-minute main that tastes of the British coast.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Sea bass fillets, skin on — 4 x 160 g
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Unsalted butter — 90 g
- Brown shrimp, peeled — 150 g
- Capers — 25 g, rinsed
- Samphire — 200 g, picked
- Lemon — 1, 30 ml juice plus zest
- Flat-leaf parsley — 10 g, chopped
- New potatoes — 500 g, halved
- Fine sea salt — 5 g; black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Simmer new potatoes in salted water 15 minutes; drain, crush lightly with a little olive oil.
2. Dry the bass skin thoroughly; score lightly and salt the skin only.
3. Heat oil in a heavy pan over medium-high. Lay fillets in skin-down, pressing flat 20 seconds with a spatula.
4. Cook 4 minutes on the skin — 90% of the cooking — until the flesh turns opaque at the edges. Flip for 30 seconds; remove.
5. Wipe the pan; add butter and cook to hazelnut brown, 2–3 minutes.
6. Off heat, add shrimp, capers, lemon juice and zest — they warm, never fry.
7. Blanch samphire 60 seconds in unsalted boiling water (it is naturally salty); drain.

## Plating & Service
Crushed potatoes as the base, samphire tangled over, bass skin-side up, shrimp butter spooned generously around; parsley last.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No samphire? Use fine beans or spinach and season normally. Works with bream, hake or John Dory — adjust skin time by thickness.
