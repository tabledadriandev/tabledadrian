# Blueberry Drop Scones with Lemon Curd Cream

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Scotch pancakes, stacked and unapologetic — bursting blueberries inside, sharp lemon curd folded through soft cream on top.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Self-raising flour — 250 g
- Caster sugar — 40 g
- Fine sea salt — 2 g
- Free-range eggs — 2
- Whole milk — 250 ml
- Unsalted butter — 40 g, melted, plus 20 g for the pan
- Blueberries — 200 g
- Lemon curd — 100 g
- Double cream — 150 ml
- Lemon — 1, zested
- Icing sugar — 10 g, to dust

## Method
1. Whisk flour, sugar and salt. In a jug, beat eggs with milk and 40 g melted butter.
2. Pour wet into dry and whisk to a smooth, thick batter — do not overmix. Rest 10 minutes.
3. Whip cream to soft peaks; ripple through the lemon curd and half the zest. Chill.
4. Heat a heavy pan or griddle over medium heat; grease lightly with butter.
5. Drop 60 ml batter per scone; stud each immediately with 6–8 blueberries.
6. Cook 2 minutes until bubbles pierce the surface and edges set; flip and cook 90 seconds more.
7. Keep warm in a folded cloth while you cook the rest, re-buttering the pan each batch.

## Plating & Service
Stack three per plate, crown with lemon curd cream, remaining zest and a light dusting of icing sugar.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap blueberries for raspberries or banana. DF: oat milk, vegan butter, and whipped coconut cream with curd made on oil.
