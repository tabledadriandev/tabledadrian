# Bramley Apple & Hazelnut Overnight Oats

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bircher, but make it British — sharp Bramley compote layered through cinnamon oats, for guests who trained before breakfast.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Jumbo rolled oats — 200 g
- Natural yoghurt — 250 g
- Whole milk — 250 ml
- Chia seeds — 20 g
- Ground cinnamon — 3 g
- Honey — 30 g
- Bramley apples — 400 g, peeled and diced
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Water — 30 ml
- Hazelnuts — 60 g, toasted and crushed
- Eating apple — 1 (150 g), julienned
- Vanilla extract — 3 ml

## Method
1. Simmer Bramley apple with lemon juice, water and 15 g honey over low heat 8–10 minutes to a rough, sharp compote. Cool.
2. Stir oats, chia, cinnamon and vanilla into the yoghurt and milk with remaining 15 g honey.
3. Layer oats and half the compote in four jars or glasses.
4. Chill overnight, or a minimum of 4 hours — the oats must fully soften.
5. Before service, loosen with a splash of milk if thick.
6. Toast and crush the hazelnuts fresh on the day for maximum aroma.

## Plating & Service
Top each jar with remaining compote, a nest of julienned fresh apple and the crushed hazelnuts. Serve cold with long spoons.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: oat milk, coconut yoghurt, maple instead of honey. GF: certified GF oats. Nut-free: toasted pumpkin seeds.
