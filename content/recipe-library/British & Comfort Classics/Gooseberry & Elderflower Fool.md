# Gooseberry & Elderflower Fool

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Two flavours born a hedge apart — tart gooseberries barely sweetened, rippled through cream so the sharpness still bites.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Gooseberries — 450 g, topped and tailed
- Caster sugar — 90 g
- Elderflower cordial — 40 ml
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Double cream — 300 ml
- Greek yoghurt — 100 g
- Icing sugar — 15 g
- Vanilla extract — 3 ml
- Shortbread or ginger thins — 4, to serve (omit for GF or use GF biscuits)

## Method
1. Simmer gooseberries with caster sugar and 20 ml water, 8–10 minutes, until they burst and thicken.
2. Off heat, stir in elderflower cordial and lemon juice; taste — it should make you blink. Chill completely.
3. Whip cream with icing sugar and vanilla to soft peaks; fold in the yoghurt.
4. Reserve 4 spoonfuls of compote; ripple the rest through the cream in two lazy folds — streaks, not uniform pink.
5. Layer into glasses, finishing with the reserved compote.
6. Chill 1 hour.

## Plating & Service
A biscuit balanced across each glass, served cold on a hot afternoon — the fool is summer's mic-drop.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Rhubarb, damson or blackcurrant rotate through the seasons at identical ratios. Lighter: all yoghurt, no cream — call it a yoghurt fool and dodge the purists.
