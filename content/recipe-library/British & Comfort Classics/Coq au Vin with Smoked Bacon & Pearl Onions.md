# Coq au Vin with Smoked Bacon & Pearl Onions

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The bistro classic that belongs in every comfort repertoire — thighs and drumsticks braised in a whole bottle of red, garnished the proper way.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chicken thighs and drumsticks, bone in — 1.4 kg
- Fine sea salt — 8 g; black pepper — 2 g
- Plain flour — 30 g
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Smoked bacon lardons — 180 g
- Pearl or silverskin onions, peeled — 250 g
- Button mushrooms — 250 g
- Garlic — 4 cloves (20 g), smashed
- Tomato purée — 20 g
- Full-bodied red wine — 750 ml
- Chicken stock — 300 ml
- Brandy — 30 ml
- Thyme — 5 sprigs; bay — 2
- Flat-leaf parsley — 12 g, chopped

## Method
1. Season chicken; dust with flour. Brown in oil in a wide casserole, skin-side first, 8–10 minutes total; remove.
2. Render lardons 4 minutes; brown pearl onions and mushrooms in the fat 5 minutes; remove half the garnish for later.
3. Add garlic and tomato purée, 1 minute; deglaze with brandy, then wine, scraping the fond. Reduce 10 minutes.
4. Return chicken with stock, thyme and bay; braise covered at 160°C for 55–60 minutes until the meat waves on the bone.
5. Lift chicken; reduce the sauce on the hob to a glossy coat, 8–10 minutes. Season.
6. Return chicken and reserved garnish; simmer 5 minutes together.

## Plating & Service
Serve in shallow bowls over buttered tagliatelle or pomme purée, parsley over, the sauce almost black at the edges.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> White version: dry Riesling and crème fraîche stirred in at the end. GF: cornflour dusting. Better made a day ahead.
