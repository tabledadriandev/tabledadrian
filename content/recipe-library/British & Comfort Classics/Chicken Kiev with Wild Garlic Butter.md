# Chicken Kiev with Wild Garlic Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The retro dinner-party bomb, rebuilt properly — a sealed pocket of wild-garlic butter that gushes on first cut, in a double panko shell.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chicken breasts, skin off — 4 x 200 g
- Unsalted butter — 150 g, softened
- Wild garlic — 30 g, finely chopped (or 15 g parsley + 2 garlic cloves)
- Lemon — 1, zested
- Fine sea salt — 7 g; black pepper — 2 g
- Plain flour — 80 g
- Free-range eggs — 3, beaten
- Panko breadcrumbs — 180 g
- Neutral oil — 1.5 L, for frying
- Lemon wedges — to serve
- Watercress — 60 g

## Method
1. Beat butter with wild garlic, lemon zest, 2 g salt; roll into 4 x 35 g cylinders in cling film; freeze 30 minutes until hard.
2. Cut a deep horizontal pocket into the thick side of each breast; insert a butter cylinder and press the flesh sealed. Season.
3. Pané: flour, egg, panko — then egg and panko a second time, pinching the seam closed with crumbs. Chill 30 minutes (critical).
4. Heat oil to 170°C and oven to 180°C.
5. Fry Kievs 4 minutes, turning, until golden all over.
6. Transfer to a rack; bake 10–12 minutes to a core of 70°C beside the butter pocket.
7. Rest 3 minutes — but serve fast; the pressure inside is the point.

## Plating & Service
Whole Kiev per plate over crushed new potatoes and watercress; cut at the table so the green butter floods out.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No wild garlic? Basil-garlic or tarragon butter. GF: rice flour and GF panko. Butterflied thigh version is forgiving for crew meals.
