# Lemon Posset with Shortbread Thins

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Three ingredients set themselves into silk — the highest reward-to-effort dessert in the British canon, with a buttery thin for scooping.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Double cream — 600 ml
- Caster sugar — 150 g, plus 30 g for shortbread
- Lemons — 3 (zest of 2, 90 ml juice)
- Plain flour — 120 g
- Unsalted butter — 80 g, soft
- Fine sea salt — 1 g
- Raspberries — 120 g, to serve

## Method
1. Simmer cream, 150 g sugar and lemon zest gently for 3 minutes — it must bubble to activate the set. Do not walk away; it climbs the pan.
2. Off heat, whisk in lemon juice; the cream visibly thickens.
3. Strain into six 120 ml glasses; chill at least 4 hours until softly set.
4. Shortbread: rub butter, flour, 30 g sugar and salt to a dough; press 5 mm thick, chill 20 minutes, cut thin fingers.
5. Bake at 160°C for 14–16 minutes until pale gold; sugar-dust while warm.
6. Rest possets at room temperature 10 minutes before serving — cold numbs the lemon.

## Plating & Service
Glass on a plate, two shortbread thins across, a few raspberries — restraint is the luxury here.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: serve with GF biscuits (posset itself is GF). Lime-basil or blood-orange versions follow identical ratios: 600 ml cream, 150 g sugar, 90 ml citrus.
