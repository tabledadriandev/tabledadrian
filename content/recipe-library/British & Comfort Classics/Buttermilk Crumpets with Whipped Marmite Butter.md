# Buttermilk Crumpets with Whipped Marmite Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Proper holes-all-the-way-through crumpets from a loose yeasted batter, finished with an umami butter that melts into every crater.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Strong white flour — 250 g
- Buttermilk — 200 ml, at 30°C
- Warm water — 150 ml, at 35°C
- Fresh yeast — 10 g (or 4 g dried)
- Caster sugar — 5 g
- Fine sea salt — 5 g
- Bicarbonate of soda — 3 g, dissolved in 30 ml warm water
- Neutral oil — 20 ml, for rings and pan
- Unsalted butter — 120 g, softened
- Marmite — 12 g
- Chives — 5 g, finely sliced

## Method
1. Whisk flour, yeast, sugar and salt with buttermilk and water to a thick, smooth batter. Cover; prove 60–75 minutes at room temperature until very bubbly.
2. Whisk in the bicarb solution; rest 10 minutes — the batter should be pourable like thick cream. Adjust with 15–30 ml water if stiff.
3. Whip butter with Marmite 3 minutes until pale and mousse-like; fold in chives. Set aside at room temperature.
4. Heat a heavy pan to low-medium (a drop of batter should sizzle gently). Oil pan and four 9 cm rings.
5. Pour 80 ml batter per ring. Cook 8–10 minutes untouched until holes open across the surface and the top sets matte.
6. Lift rings, flip crumpets, kiss the tops 60 seconds only.
7. Repeat with remaining batter, re-oiling rings each round.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm, two per person, with a generous quenelle of Marmite butter melting into the holes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Marmite-sceptics: whip the butter with honey and sea salt instead. Batter keeps 24 h chilled — re-whisk and let warm before cooking.
