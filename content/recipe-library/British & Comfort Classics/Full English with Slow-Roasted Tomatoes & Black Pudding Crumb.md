# Full English with Slow-Roasted Tomatoes & Black Pudding Crumb

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The charter-boat full English: every element cooked separately and properly, with a crisp black pudding crumb that seasons the plate instead of a sad fried slice.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Cumberland sausages — 8 (about 500 g)
- Dry-cured back bacon — 8 rashers (320 g)
- Black pudding — 150 g, crumbled
- Panko breadcrumbs — 30 g
- Vine tomatoes — 4, halved (400 g)
- Chestnut mushrooms — 300 g, thick-sliced
- Free-range eggs — 4
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Thyme — 4 sprigs
- Sourdough — 4 thick slices (200 g)
- Flaky sea salt — 4 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 130°C. Season tomato halves, drizzle with 15 ml oil and thyme, roast cut-side up 40 minutes until concentrated but holding shape.
2. Fry crumbled black pudding in a dry pan over medium heat 5 minutes until crisp; add panko and 20 g butter, toss 2 minutes until deep brown. Drain on paper; season.
3. Roast sausages on a tray at 190°C (raise oven after tomatoes) for 18–20 minutes, turning once, until 72°C core.
4. Lay bacon in a cold pan, bring to medium-high; render 3–4 minutes per side until the fat is glass-crisp.
5. Sauté mushrooms in the bacon fat over high heat 5–6 minutes without crowding; season only at the end.
6. Toast sourdough in the pan in 20 g butter until golden both sides.
7. Fry eggs gently in remaining 20 g butter, basting the whites, 2–3 minutes — set whites, liquid yolks.
8. Season eggs with flaky salt and pepper just before serving.

## Plating & Service
Warm plates essential. Anchor with toast, arrange elements clockwise, egg on the mushrooms, and shower the whole plate with black pudding crumb.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: use gluten-free bread and GF black pudding. Lighter: poach the eggs and grill the sausages; skip the crumb.
