# Champ with Burnt Spring Onion Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Irish mash with a modern edge — half the spring onions steeped in milk, half charred black and blended into the butter.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Floury potatoes — 1 kg, peeled, even chunks
- Spring onions — 120 g (60 g sliced, 60 g whole)
- Whole milk — 150 ml
- Unsalted butter — 120 g (90 g mash, 30 g burnt-onion butter)
- Fine sea salt — 8 g
- White pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Char the whole spring onions in a dry, screaming pan (or over flame) until blackened in patches and soft, 4–5 minutes.
2. Blend charred onions with 30 g soft butter and a pinch of salt to a smoky green-black butter.
3. Simmer potatoes in salted water 18–20 minutes; drain and steam-dry 3 minutes.
4. Warm milk with the sliced spring onions 3 minutes to infuse.
5. Rice the potatoes; beat in 90 g butter cube by cube, then the onion milk. Season with salt and white pepper.
6. Serve with a crater in each mound, the burnt spring onion butter melting in the middle.

## Plating & Service
A proud mound per plate or one family bowl — every fork should drag through the smoky butter pool.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Colcannon: fold in 200 g blanched kale. Vegan: olive oil and oat milk; the burnt onions still carry it.
