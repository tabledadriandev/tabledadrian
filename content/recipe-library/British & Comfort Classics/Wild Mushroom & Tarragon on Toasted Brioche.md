# Wild Mushroom & Tarragon on Toasted Brioche

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Creamed mushrooms done with restraint — high heat, a splash of Madeira, and tarragon lifting it out of the ordinary.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Mixed mushrooms (chestnut, oyster, girolle) — 600 g, torn
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Olive oil — 20 ml
- Shallots — 80 g, minced
- Garlic — 2 cloves (10 g), grated
- Madeira — 50 ml
- Crème fraîche — 120 g
- Tarragon — 8 g, chopped
- Chives — 6 g, sliced
- Brioche loaf — 4 thick slices (240 g)
- Fine sea salt — 4 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Heat a wide pan until smoking. Add oil and 30 g butter, then mushrooms in a single layer — in two batches if needed.
2. Leave 2 minutes untouched to colour, then toss and cook 3 minutes more until golden and dry. Season each batch.
3. Lower heat; add remaining butter, shallots and garlic; sweat 2 minutes.
4. Deglaze with Madeira; reduce to almost nothing.
5. Stir in crème fraîche; simmer 2 minutes to a coating consistency.
6. Toast brioche slices until deep gold on both sides.
7. Off heat, fold tarragon through the mushrooms; adjust seasoning.

## Plating & Service
Pile mushrooms generously over the brioche, letting the cream soak the edges; finish with chives and pepper.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: sourdough, olive oil and oat crème fraîche. Add a poached egg or shaved Parmesan for a heartier brunch.
