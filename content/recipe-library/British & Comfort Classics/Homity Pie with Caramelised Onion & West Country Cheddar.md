# Homity Pie with Caramelised Onion & West Country Cheddar

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The wartime open pie of potato, leek and cheese, upgraded with slow caramelised onions and a crisp wholemeal crust — vegetarian comfort with history.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Wholemeal flour — 150 g
- Plain flour — 150 g
- Unsalted butter — 150 g, cold, diced, plus 40 g for filling
- Cold water — 70 ml
- Waxy potatoes — 600 g, 2 cm dice
- Onions — 400 g, thinly sliced
- Leeks — 250 g, sliced
- Garlic — 3 cloves (15 g), grated
- Double cream — 100 ml
- Mature Cheddar — 200 g, grated
- Flat-leaf parsley — 15 g, chopped
- Thyme leaves — 3 g
- Egg — 1, beaten
- Fine sea salt — 8 g; black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Rub flours, 150 g butter and 3 g salt to crumbs; bind with cold water. Chill 30 minutes.
2. Caramelise onions in 20 g butter over medium-low heat, 25 minutes, until deep gold.
3. Boil potatoes 8 minutes until just tender; drain. Sweat leeks and garlic in 20 g butter 6 minutes.
4. Combine potatoes, onions, leeks, cream, two-thirds of the Cheddar, herbs, salt and pepper.
5. Roll pastry to 4 mm; line a 24 cm tart tin. Blind-bake at 180°C with beans 15 minutes; remove beans, 5 minutes more.
6. Pile in the filling, mounding proudly; scatter remaining Cheddar. Egg-wash exposed pastry.
7. Bake at 190°C for 30–35 minutes until bronzed. Rest 10 minutes.

## Plating & Service
Warm wedges with a sharply dressed watercress and apple salad — the pie is rich; the salad is the counterweight.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 100 g crumbled Stilton for punch. Vegan: olive-oil pastry, oat cream, vegan cheese — still excellent.
