# Wild Mushroom & Ale Suet Pudding

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A steamed suet crust hiding a dark, alely mushroom braise — the vegetarian answer to steak-and-kidney that converts committed carnivores.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Mixed mushrooms (chestnut, portobello, dried porcini) — 700 g fresh + 20 g dried
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Onion — 200 g, diced
- Carrot — 100 g, small dice
- Celery — 50 g, diced
- Garlic — 3 cloves (15 g)
- Tomato purée — 25 g
- Brown ale — 300 ml
- Mushroom soaking water + vegetable stock — 300 ml
- Miso paste — 15 g; Marmite — 5 g
- Thyme — 4 sprigs
- Cornflour — 12 g, slaked
- Self-raising flour — 300 g; vegetable suet — 150 g
- Cold water — 160 ml; fine sea salt — 9 g

## Method
1. Soak porcini in 200 ml hot water 20 minutes; chop, keep the liquor.
2. Sear fresh mushrooms in oil in batches over high heat until golden and dry, 10 minutes; remove.
3. Soften onion, carrot and celery 8 minutes; add garlic and tomato purée, 2 minutes. Return mushrooms with porcini.
4. Add ale, porcini liquor plus stock, miso, Marmite and thyme; simmer 20 minutes. Thicken with slaked cornflour to a rich gravy; season and cool.
5. Crust: mix flour, suet and 4 g salt; bind with cold water. Roll two-thirds to line a buttered 1.2 L pudding basin; fill with the cold braise; lid with the rest, sealing the edge.
6. Cover with pleated baking paper and foil, tied. Steam 2 hours, topping up water.
7. Rest 10 minutes; run a knife around and invert boldly onto a lipped plate.

## Plating & Service
The turn-out is the show — carve wedges at the table as the gravy escapes; buttered greens alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan as written if suet is vegetable-based and no butter used on the basin (oil it). Add chestnuts in winter.
