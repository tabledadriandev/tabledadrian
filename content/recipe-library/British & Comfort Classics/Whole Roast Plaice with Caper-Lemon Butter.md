# Whole Roast Plaice with Caper-Lemon Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A whole flatfish roasted on the bone stays juicier than any fillet — theatrical for two, and the frame makes tomorrow's stock.

## Ingredients (serves 2)
- Whole plaice, gutted, dark skin scored — 1 (about 800 g)
- Olive oil — 20 ml
- Unsalted butter — 70 g
- Capers — 20 g, rinsed
- Lemon — 1 (30 ml juice), plus 1 in wedges
- Flat-leaf parsley — 10 g, chopped
- Cherry tomatoes on the vine — 200 g
- Thyme — 3 sprigs
- Flaky sea salt — 4 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 200°C with a large tray inside.
2. Dry the fish well; rub both sides with oil and salt.
3. Lay the plaice dark-side up on the hot tray; add tomatoes and thyme alongside.
4. Roast 14–16 minutes until the flesh lifts cleanly from the bone at the thickest point (55°C core).
5. Meanwhile, cook butter to nut-brown, 3 minutes; off heat add capers, lemon juice and parsley.
6. Rest the fish 3 minutes on the tray.

## Plating & Service
Serve the whole fish on a platter, roasted tomatoes around, caper-lemon butter spooned over at the table; lift the top fillets, remove the frame, and share the underside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Lemon sole, brill or small turbot roast identically (adjust 2 minutes per 200 g). DF: olive oil, capers and lemon warmed together instead of butter.
