# Seared Scallops, Pea Puree & Black Pudding Crumb

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The gastropub holy trinity — sweet scallop, earthy pudding, fresh pea — plated cleanly enough for a tasting menu.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- King scallops, roe off — 12 (about 360 g)
- Black pudding — 120 g, crumbled
- Panko breadcrumbs — 20 g
- Frozen petits pois — 300 g
- Unsalted butter — 70 g (20 g purée, 50 g basting)
- Vegetable stock — 100 ml, hot
- Mint leaves — 6 g
- Lemon — 1 (15 ml juice plus wedges)
- Olive oil — 20 ml
- Pea shoots — 15 g
- Fine sea salt — 4 g
- White pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Fry black pudding in a dry pan over medium heat 5 minutes until crisp; add panko and toss 2 minutes until deep brown. Drain, season, keep warm.
2. Boil peas 3 minutes in salted water; blend hot with stock, 20 g butter and mint for 60 seconds. Sieve, season with salt, white pepper and 5 ml lemon juice. Keep warm.
3. Pat scallops bone-dry; salt one side only.
4. Heat oil in a heavy pan until just smoking. Lay scallops in clockwise, salted-side down; sear 90 seconds untouched until deeply caramelised.
5. Flip in the same order, add 50 g butter, and baste 45–60 seconds. Finish with 10 ml lemon juice.
6. Rest scallops 1 minute on a warm plate.

## Plating & Service
Three spoonfuls of purée per plate, a scallop on each, crumb scattered over and between, pea shoots to lift.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: use GF black pudding and skip panko. Vegetarian route: sear king oyster mushroom coins the same way.
