# Cornish Fish Stew with Saffron & Cider

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bouillabaisse logic, Cornish larder — saffron and dry cider carry hake, mussels and prawns in a fennel-tomato broth.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Hake or cod fillet — 400 g, 4 cm chunks
- Mussels — 500 g, scrubbed, debearded
- Raw king prawns, shell on — 8 (240 g)
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Fennel — 200 g, sliced; fronds reserved
- Onion — 150 g, sliced
- Celery — 50 g, sliced
- Garlic — 4 cloves (20 g), sliced
- Saffron — 0.3 g, bloomed in 30 ml hot water
- Dry cider — 250 ml
- Chopped tomatoes — 400 g
- Fish stock — 500 ml
- Orange — 1, one strip of zest
- Waxy potatoes — 300 g, 2 cm dice
- Fine sea salt — 7 g; cayenne — 1 g

## Method
1. Soften fennel, onion and celery in oil over medium heat, 8 minutes; add garlic, 1 minute.
2. Add cider; reduce by half. Add tomatoes, stock, saffron water, orange zest, potatoes, salt and cayenne.
3. Simmer 15 minutes until the potatoes are just tender and the broth has body.
4. Season the hake; slide into the broth, 2 minutes.
5. Add mussels and prawns; cover and simmer 3–4 minutes until the shells open and prawns blush. Discard any closed mussels.
6. Rest 2 minutes off the heat; check salt — shellfish season the broth themselves.

## Plating & Service
Deep bowls, shells up and proud, broth ladled over, fennel fronds scattered; grilled sourdough rubbed with garlic alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add rouille and it goes full Marseille. Swap cider for white wine. Monkfish holds best if the service window is long.
