# Salmon & Dill Fishcakes with Watercress Mayonnaise

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Big flakes of salmon, not paste — bound with just enough mash, crumbed and shallow-fried, with a peppery green mayo.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Salmon fillet — 500 g
- Floury potatoes — 400 g, peeled
- Unsalted butter — 30 g
- Dijon mustard — 12 g
- Lemon — 1, zest and 20 ml juice
- Dill — 12 g, chopped; chives — 8 g
- Capers — 20 g, chopped
- Plain flour — 60 g; free-range eggs — 2, beaten; panko — 140 g
- Neutral oil — 100 ml
- Mayonnaise — 100 g
- Watercress — 60 g (30 g blanched for mayo, 30 g to serve)
- Fine sea salt — 7 g; black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Roast salmon at 180°C for 12–14 minutes to just-set; cool and flake large.
2. Boil potatoes 20 minutes; rice, beat in butter, cool.
3. Fold potato with mustard, lemon zest and juice, dill, chives, capers, salt and pepper; fold in salmon gently, keeping flakes whole.
4. Shape 8 cakes (3 cm thick); chill 30 minutes until firm.
5. Watercress mayo: blanch 30 g watercress 20 seconds, refresh, squeeze; blend into mayonnaise. Season.
6. Pané cakes through flour, egg, panko.
7. Shallow-fry in oil over medium heat, 3–4 minutes per side, until deep gold and hot through (a skewer to the centre should come out warm).

## Plating & Service
Two cakes per plate, a rocket of watercress, mayo quenelled beside, lemon wedge — minted peas if the table wants more.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Half smoked haddock makes them smokier. GF: rice flour and GF crumbs. Bake option: 200°C, 18 minutes, oiled tray.
