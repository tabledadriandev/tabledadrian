# Piri-Piri Spatchcock Chicken with Charred Corn

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Flattened bird, fierce marinade, hard roast — the deck lunch that smells like a Lisbon churrasqueira and carves in four minutes.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Whole chicken — 1.6 kg, spatchcocked (backbone out, pressed flat)
- Red bird's-eye chillies — 4 (20 g)
- Red pepper — 100 g, chopped
- Garlic — 5 cloves (25 g)
- Smoked paprika — 8 g; dried oregano — 3 g
- Red wine vinegar — 40 ml
- Lemon — 2 (60 ml juice; 1 in halves for charring)
- Olive oil — 80 ml
- Fine sea salt — 9 g; black pepper — 2 g
- Corn cobs — 4, husked
- Unsalted coriander leaves — 10 g

## Method
1. Marinade: blend chillies, red pepper, garlic, paprika, oregano, vinegar, 40 ml lemon juice, 60 ml oil and 5 g salt.
2. Slash the chicken legs and breast to the bone; massage in two-thirds of the marinade. Rest 2–24 hours chilled.
3. Heat oven to 210°C (or a covered BBQ to medium-hot). Roast the bird skin-up on a rack over a tray, 40–45 minutes, basting twice with the tray drippings, to a 74°C thigh.
4. Rest 10 minutes under loose foil.
5. Char corn in a dry pan or over coals, turning, 8 minutes; roll in remaining oil and salt. Char the lemon halves cut-down, 3 minutes.
6. Warm the reserved marinade with 20 ml lemon juice as the serving sauce (it must not touch raw chicken).

## Plating & Service
Quarter the bird on a board, corn stacked beside, sauce drizzled, charred lemon for squeezing, coriander over.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dial heat with chilli count. Butterflied poussins: 25 minutes, one per guest. Corn becomes charred hispi in cooler months.
