# Crispy Whitebait with Tartare Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Seaside pub in a paper cone — whole tiny fish in a peppery flour crust, eaten head-and-all with a proper chunky tartare.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Whitebait — 400 g
- Plain flour — 100 g
- Cornflour — 40 g
- Cayenne pepper — 2 g
- Smoked paprika — 3 g
- Fine sea salt — 5 g
- Whole milk or water — 60 ml, for dampening
- Neutral oil — 1.5 L, for frying
- Mayonnaise — 120 g
- Cornichons — 40 g, chopped
- Capers — 20 g, chopped
- Shallot — 20 g, minced
- Flat-leaf parsley and tarragon — 10 g, chopped
- Lemon — 2 (1 juiced, 1 in wedges)

## Method
1. Stir cornichons, capers, shallot, herbs and 15 ml lemon juice into the mayonnaise; season. Chill.
2. Heat oil to 180°C.
3. Whisk flour, cornflour, cayenne, paprika and salt.
4. Moisten whitebait barely with milk, then toss through the seasoned flour in batches, shaking off excess in a sieve.
5. Fry in three batches, 2–3 minutes each, until pale gold and rigid; the oil must recover to 180°C between batches.
6. Drain on a rack; salt immediately while hot.

## Plating & Service
Pile into a paper-lined bowl or cone, tartare in a pot, lemon wedges and a final dust of cayenne.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: swap plain flour for rice flour. Same treatment works for squid rings or strips of lemon sole.
