# Baked Beans from Scratch on Charred Sourdough

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Proper beans — haricots braised in a smoky tomato-treacle sauce that makes the tinned version a distant memory.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Cooked haricot beans — 500 g (drained weight)
- Onion — 150 g, finely diced
- Celery — 50 g, finely diced
- Garlic — 2 cloves (10 g), grated
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Tomato passata — 400 g
- Black treacle — 20 g
- Cider vinegar — 20 ml
- Smoked paprika — 5 g
- English mustard powder — 3 g
- Bay leaf — 1
- Vegetable stock — 200 ml
- Sourdough — 4 thick slices (200 g)
- Fine sea salt — 4 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 150°C. In a casserole, sweat onion and celery in oil over medium heat 8 minutes; add garlic for 1 minute.
2. Stir in paprika and mustard powder; toast 30 seconds.
3. Add passata, treacle, vinegar, bay, stock and beans; season and bring to a simmer.
4. Cover and bake 45 minutes, stirring once — the sauce should turn glossy and cling to the beans. Uncover for the last 10 minutes if loose.
5. Rest 5 minutes; discard bay, adjust salt and vinegar balance.
6. Char sourdough over a flame or in a scorching dry grill pan; rub lightly with olive oil.

## Plating & Service
Beans ladled generously over the charred bread, a thread of olive oil and cracked pepper on top.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Not vegan? Finish with grated Cheddar or top with a fried egg. GF: serve on GF toast or jacket potatoes. Beans improve overnight.
