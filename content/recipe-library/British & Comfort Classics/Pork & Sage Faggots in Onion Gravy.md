# Pork & Sage Faggots in Onion Gravy

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Old-school offal meatballs — pork shoulder and liver bound with sage crumbs, braised in gravy until they barely hold together.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Pork shoulder mince — 500 g
- Pork or chicken liver — 200 g, blitzed to a coarse purée
- Smoked bacon — 100 g, minced
- Onions — 500 g (100 g minced for the mix, 400 g sliced for gravy)
- Fresh white breadcrumbs — 80 g
- Free-range egg — 1
- Sage — 8 g, chopped; mace — 1 g; allspice — 1 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g; black pepper — 3 g
- Unsalted olive oil — 30 ml
- Plain flour — 15 g
- Dark ale — 150 ml
- Beef stock — 500 ml
- Worcestershire-style sauce — 10 ml

## Method
1. Mix minces, liver, bacon, 100 g minced onion, breadcrumbs, egg, sage, mace, allspice, salt and pepper; beat 1 minute until sticky. Chill 20 minutes.
2. Shape 12 faggots (about 75 g each) with wet hands.
3. Brown in oil in a wide casserole, gently, 6 minutes total; remove.
4. Gravy: cook 400 g sliced onions in the fat 20 minutes to deep gold; stir in flour, deglaze with ale, add stock and Worcestershire sauce. Simmer 5 minutes.
5. Nestle faggots into the gravy; cover and braise at 170°C for 35 minutes until 72°C core and the gravy has body.
6. Rest 5 minutes, basting once.

## Plating & Service
Three faggots per plate on buttery mash with peas, gravy over everything — humble food, correctly proud.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Traditional caul-fat wrap improves the shape if the butcher obliges. GF: GF crumbs and cornflour. Freezes well in its gravy.
