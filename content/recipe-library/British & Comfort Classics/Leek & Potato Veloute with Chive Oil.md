# Leek & Potato Veloute with Chive Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The humblest British soup, blended to silk and finished with a vivid green oil — proof technique beats luxury ingredients.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Leeks, white and pale green only — 400 g, sliced
- Floury potatoes — 300 g, peeled, 2 cm dice
- Unsalted butter — 50 g
- Onion — 100 g, sliced
- Vegetable stock — 800 ml
- Double cream — 100 ml
- Fine sea salt — 5 g
- White pepper — 1 g
- Chives — 40 g
- Neutral oil — 80 ml
- Crème fraîche — 40 g, to finish

## Method
1. For the chive oil: blitz chives with oil in a blender 60 seconds, warm to 60°C for 5 minutes, then strain through muslin. Chill.
2. Sweat leeks and onion in butter with a pinch of salt over low heat, lidded, 10 minutes — no colour.
3. Add potatoes and stock; simmer 15 minutes until the potato collapses.
4. Add cream; blend at full speed 90 seconds until completely smooth.
5. Pass through a fine sieve; season with salt and white pepper.
6. Reheat gently to 80°C — do not boil after blending or it dulls.

## Plating & Service
Pour into warm bowls at the table, spoon a swirl of crème fraîche and ring with chive oil.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: olive oil for butter, oat cream for dairy. Serve chilled as a summer vichyssoise. Top with crisp leek frizzles for texture.
