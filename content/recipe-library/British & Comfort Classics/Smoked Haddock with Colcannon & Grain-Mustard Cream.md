# Smoked Haddock with Colcannon & Grain-Mustard Cream

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Poached smoked haddock over buttery kale-flecked colcannon with a mustard cream and a poached egg — pure rainy-night solace, refined.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Undyed smoked haddock — 4 x 170 g portions
- Whole milk — 500 ml
- Bay leaf — 1; black peppercorns — 5
- Floury potatoes — 1 kg, peeled
- Curly kale or Savoy cabbage — 250 g, shredded
- Spring onions — 60 g, sliced
- Unsalted butter — 100 g
- Double cream — 150 ml (50 ml colcannon, 100 ml sauce)
- Wholegrain mustard — 20 g
- Free-range eggs — 4
- White wine vinegar — 15 ml
- Chives — 8 g; fine sea salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Boil potatoes 20 minutes; rice, and beat in 80 g butter and 50 ml cream.
2. Blanch kale 3 minutes; drain, squeeze, and sauté with spring onions in 20 g butter 2 minutes. Fold into the mash; season.
3. Poach haddock in milk with bay and peppercorns at 80°C for 6–7 minutes until it flakes.
4. Sauce: strain 150 ml poaching milk into a pan, add 100 ml cream, reduce 3–4 minutes; whisk in mustard. No salt needed.
5. Poach eggs in simmering water with vinegar, 3 minutes.
6. Warm four bowls; taste everything once more.

## Plating & Service
A bed of colcannon, haddock lifted whole on top, poached egg, mustard cream spooned over, chives to finish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap haddock for smoked cod. Lighter: skip the egg and halve the cream. Colcannon must be aggressively buttery — that is the dish.
