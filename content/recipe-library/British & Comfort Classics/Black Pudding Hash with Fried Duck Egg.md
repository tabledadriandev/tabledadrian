# Black Pudding Hash with Fried Duck Egg

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rich Lancashire black pudding against sweet apple and crisp potato, with a deep-orange duck yolk as the sauce.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Black pudding — 300 g, thick coins
- Waxy potatoes — 500 g, 2 cm dice
- Eating apples — 2 (300 g), 2 cm dice
- Red onion — 150 g, sliced
- Olive oil — 45 ml
- Thyme — 4 sprigs, leaves picked
- Duck eggs — 4
- Cider vinegar — 15 ml
- Watercress — 50 g
- Fine sea salt — 4 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Parboil potatoes in salted water 6 minutes; drain and steam-dry.
2. Heat 30 ml oil in a wide pan over medium-high; fry potatoes 10–12 minutes until golden and crisp-edged.
3. Add onion and thyme; cook 4 minutes. Add apple; cook 3 minutes until caramelised at the edges but holding shape.
4. Push the hash aside; add black pudding coins and fry 2 minutes per side until crusted. Break roughly into the hash.
5. Deglaze with cider vinegar; toss once and season.
6. Fry duck eggs in remaining 15 ml oil over medium heat, basting, 3 minutes — duck whites need slightly longer to set.

## Plating & Service
Hash into warm bowls, duck egg on top, watercress tumbled alongside — its pepperiness cuts the richness.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: use gluten-free black pudding. Swap duck eggs for hen eggs (2–3 minutes). Add sage instead of thyme for a deeper autumn note.
