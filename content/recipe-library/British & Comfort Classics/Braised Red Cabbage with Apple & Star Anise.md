# Braised Red Cabbage with Apple & Star Anise

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sweet-sour, glossy and better on day two — the ruby side that cuts through every roast, pie and glazed ham on the menu.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Red cabbage — 1 kg, cored, finely sliced
- Red onion — 200 g, sliced
- Bramley or sharp apples — 300 g, grated
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Red wine vinegar — 80 ml
- Port or red wine — 100 ml
- Dark brown sugar — 60 g
- Star anise — 2
- Cinnamon stick — 1
- Cloves — 3
- Bay leaf — 1
- Fine sea salt — 7 g
- Redcurrant jelly — 20 g

## Method
1. Soften onion in oil in a wide casserole over medium heat, 5 minutes.
2. Add cabbage, apple, vinegar, port, sugar, spices, bay and salt; toss thoroughly.
3. Cover and braise at 150°C (or lowest hob heat) for 75–90 minutes, stirring twice, until yielding and deeply stained.
4. Uncover; stir in redcurrant jelly and reduce any loose liquid over medium heat 5 minutes until glossy.
5. Fish out the whole spices and bay.
6. Rest 10 minutes — or cool and reheat tomorrow, when it peaks.

## Plating & Service
A deep bowl beside the roast; it partners pork belly, venison pie and glazed gammon with equal loyalty.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Alcohol-free: apple juice for port. Add orange zest for brightness or a knob of butter at the end for gloss (no longer vegan).
