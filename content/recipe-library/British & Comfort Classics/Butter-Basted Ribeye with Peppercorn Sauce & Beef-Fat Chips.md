# Butter-Basted Ribeye with Peppercorn Sauce & Beef-Fat Chips

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Steak-night liturgy: a thick shared ribeye basted in foaming thyme butter, brandied peppercorn sauce, and chips fried in beef fat.

## Ingredients (serves 2)
- Ribeye, 4 cm thick — 1 x 600 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g; black pepper — 2 g
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Garlic — 3 cloves (15 g), smashed
- Thyme — 5 sprigs
- Green peppercorns in brine — 20 g, drained
- Brandy — 30 ml
- Beef stock — 150 ml
- Double cream — 100 ml
- Dijon mustard — 5 g
- Floury potatoes — 700 g, in 2 cm chips
- Beef dripping — 1 kg (or 1.2 L oil)

## Method
1. Salt the steak all over; rest at room temperature 45 minutes.
2. Chips: simmer in salted water 8 minutes until nearly collapsing; drain, steam-dry, chill. Fry in dripping at 130°C for 7 minutes; drain. Hold.
3. Heat a heavy pan until smoking. Sear the ribeye 90 seconds per side to build crust.
4. Add butter, garlic and thyme; baste continuously, flipping every minute, to a core of 50–52°C (about 4–6 minutes more). Rest 10 minutes on a rack.
5. Sauce: pour off fat, add brandy (flame off), scrape the fond, crush in half the peppercorns; add stock, reduce by half, add cream, mustard and whole peppercorns, simmer to coating. Season.
6. Final fry: chips at 190°C, 4–5 minutes, to deep amber; salt hard.
7. Slice the steak 1 cm thick against the grain; pour over any resting juices.

## Plating & Service
Sliced steak on a warm board, sauce in a copper pot, chips in a pile — serve with a sharp watercress salad.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> DF: skip cream — reduce stock with peppercorns and mount with olive oil. Côte de boeuf for four: same method, 55 minutes resting-basting rhythm.
