# Smoked Haddock Kedgeree with Soft-Boiled Egg

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Anglo-Indian brunch royalty — the haddock poaching liquor becomes the rice cooking stock so nothing is wasted and every grain tastes of smoke.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Undyed smoked haddock — 500 g
- Whole milk or water — 500 ml
- Basmati rice — 280 g, rinsed
- Onion — 150 g, finely diced
- Ghee or olive oil — 40 g
- Madras curry powder — 8 g
- Ground turmeric — 3 g
- Cardamom pods — 4, cracked
- Bay leaf — 1
- Free-range eggs — 4
- Flat-leaf parsley — 15 g, chopped
- Coriander — 10 g, chopped
- Lemon — 1, in wedges
- Fine sea salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Poach haddock in barely simmering milk (or water) with the bay leaf at 80°C for 6 minutes. Lift out, flake into large pieces, strain and reserve 450 ml liquor.
2. Boil eggs from cold: bring to boil, cook exactly 6 minutes, refresh in iced water, peel and halve.
3. Sweat onion in ghee over medium heat 6–8 minutes until translucent. Add curry powder, turmeric and cardamom; toast 60 seconds.
4. Stir in rice to coat, add reserved liquor and salt, cover and simmer on lowest heat 12 minutes.
5. Rest off heat, lid on, 5 minutes; fork through gently to separate grains.
6. Fold in flaked haddock and most of the herbs, warming 2 minutes.
7. Check seasoning — smoked fish carries salt, so taste before adding more.

## Plating & Service
Pile into warm shallow bowls, crown with the jammy egg halves, scatter remaining herbs and serve lemon wedges alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap haddock for hot-smoked salmon or trout; make it lighter with brown basmati (add 8 minutes and 60 ml extra liquid).
