# Spotted Dick with Vanilla Custard

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The steamed suet pudding of school-dinner legend, done with citrus-soaked currants and a custard that restores its dignity.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Self-raising flour — 250 g
- Beef or vegetable suet — 125 g
- Caster sugar — 80 g, plus 70 g for custard
- Currants — 150 g
- Sultanas — 50 g
- Lemon — 1, zested; orange — 1, zested
- Whole milk — 150 ml (dough), plus 300 ml for custard
- Fine sea salt — 2 g
- Double cream — 150 ml
- Egg yolks — 5
- Vanilla pod — 1, split
- Unsalted butter — 10 g, for the basin

## Method
1. Soak currants and sultanas in 30 ml hot water with both zests, 15 minutes; drain.
2. Mix flour, suet, 80 g sugar and salt; fold in the fruit.
3. Bind with 150 ml milk to a soft, dropping dough.
4. Scrape into a buttered 1.2 L pudding basin; cover with pleated baking paper and foil, tied with string.
5. Steam 1 hour 40 minutes in a lidded pan, water halfway up the basin, topping up as needed.
6. Custard: warm 300 ml milk, cream and vanilla; whisk yolks with 70 g sugar; temper, then stir over low heat to 82°C.
7. Rest the pudding 5 minutes; invert onto a lipped plate.

## Plating & Service
Thick wedges drowned in hot custard — nostalgia weaponised; serve with zero irony.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian as written with vegetable suet. Steam in a log shape wrapped in paper and foil for café-style slices. Add 30 ml rum to the fruit soak.
