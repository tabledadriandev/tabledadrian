# Classic Sherry Trifle with Raspberry & Vanilla Custard

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Built in glass so the strata show — sherried sponge, sharp raspberry, thick vanilla custard and a cloud of softly whipped cream.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Madeira or sponge cake — 300 g, in 3 cm cubes
- Amontillado sherry — 90 ml
- Raspberry jam — 100 g
- Fresh raspberries — 400 g
- Whole milk — 400 ml; double cream — 550 ml (150 ml custard, 400 ml topping)
- Egg yolks — 7
- Caster sugar — 90 g
- Cornflour — 20 g
- Vanilla pod — 1, split
- Flaked toasted almonds — 30 g (optional; omit for nut-free)
- Icing sugar — 15 g

## Method
1. Custard: warm milk, 150 ml cream and vanilla to steaming. Whisk yolks, caster sugar and cornflour; temper with the hot milk; return and stir over low heat to 84°C until thick. Press cling film onto the surface; cool.
2. Spread sponge cubes with jam; layer into a 2.5 L glass bowl.
3. Soak with sherry; crush 200 g raspberries over and let them bleed in, 10 minutes.
4. Spoon the cooled custard over in an even layer; chill 1 hour.
5. Whip 400 ml cream with icing sugar to soft, drooping peaks; billow over the custard.
6. Chill at least 2 hours — overnight deepens everything.

## Plating & Service
Crown with remaining raspberries (and almonds if cleared); serve deep spoonfuls reaching every layer.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Alcohol-free: orange juice with 5 ml vanilla. Swap raspberry for poached pear and chocolate custard in winter.
