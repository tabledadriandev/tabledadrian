# Roast Tomato Soup with Cheese Toastie Soldiers

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Childhood's greatest double act, made honest — slow-roasted tomatoes blended with their caramelised juices, dunked with molten Cheddar soldiers.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Ripe vine tomatoes — 1.2 kg, halved
- Olive oil — 50 ml
- Garlic — 4 cloves (20 g), unpeeled
- Onion — 150 g, sliced
- Celery — 40 g, sliced
- Caster sugar — 8 g
- Vegetable stock — 400 ml
- Basil — 10 g
- Red wine vinegar — 10 ml
- Sourdough — 8 slices (400 g)
- Mature Cheddar — 160 g, grated
- Unsalted butter — 40 g, softened
- Fine sea salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 160°C. Roast tomatoes cut-side up with garlic cloves, 30 ml oil, sugar and salt for 40 minutes until collapsed and edged with caramel.
2. Sweat onion and celery in remaining oil over low heat 8 minutes.
3. Squeeze roasted garlic from its skins into the pan; add tomatoes with all tray juices and the stock. Simmer 10 minutes.
4. Add basil; blend 90 seconds and pass through a sieve. Season with salt and vinegar.
5. Butter the bread on the outsides; sandwich the Cheddar inside. Toast in a dry pan over medium heat, pressed, 3 minutes per side until deep gold and molten.
6. Rest toasties 1 minute; cut into soldiers.

## Plating & Service
Soup in deep bowls, toastie soldiers stacked alongside for dunking, a thread of olive oil and torn basil on the surface.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: skip the toastie cheese for a vegan melt; the soup itself needs no dairy. Add a pinch of chilli flakes when roasting for warmth.
