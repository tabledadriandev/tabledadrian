# Duck-Fat Roast Potatoes

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rough-shaken edges, smoking duck fat, and no crowding — the roast potato at maximum crunch-to-fluff ratio.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Floury potatoes (Maris Piper) — 1.5 kg, peeled, in fist-halves
- Duck fat — 120 g
- Fine sea salt — 12 g (8 g water, 4 g finish)
- Garlic — 6 cloves (30 g), unpeeled, smashed
- Rosemary — 3 sprigs
- Flaky sea salt — 4 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 200°C with the duck fat in a large sturdy tray.
2. Simmer potatoes in salted water 9–10 minutes until the outsides soften; drain.
3. Shake violently in the colander until every piece is furred and ragged — this is the crust.
4. Tip carefully into the smoking fat; turn each piece to coat, cut-side down, leaving space between all.
5. Roast 30 minutes untouched, then turn; add garlic and rosemary and roast 25–30 minutes more until deeply golden and hollow-sounding.
6. Drain 30 seconds on paper; flaky salt while hot.

## Plating & Service
A warm serving bowl, never lidded; the garlic cloves squeeze out sweet for those who know.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian/vegan: olive oil or rapeseed at the same temperatures. Semolina dusting after shaking adds even more shatter.
