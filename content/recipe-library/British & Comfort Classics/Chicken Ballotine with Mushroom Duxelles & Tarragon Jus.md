# Chicken Ballotine with Mushroom Duxelles & Tarragon Jus

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Roast chicken refined to a cylinder — thigh-wrapped duxelles poached then crisped, carving into flawless rounds for plated service.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chicken legs, boned, skin on — 4 (about 900 g)
- Chestnut mushrooms — 400 g, minced
- Shallots — 60 g, minced
- Garlic — 2 cloves (10 g)
- Thyme leaves — 2 g
- Unsalted butter — 60 g (20 g duxelles, 20 g basting, 20 g jus)
- Madeira — 60 ml
- Chicken stock — 500 ml
- Tarragon — 8 g
- Double cream — 40 ml
- Olive oil — 20 ml
- Fine sea salt — 7 g; black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Duxelles: cook mushrooms, shallots, garlic and thyme in 20 g butter over high heat, 10–12 minutes, until dry. Deglaze with 30 ml Madeira; reduce to nothing. Season; cool.
2. Lay each boned leg skin-down on cling film; season, spread duxelles, and roll into a tight cylinder, twisting the ends hard. Double-wrap; chill 30 minutes.
3. Poach ballotines at 68–70°C for 35–40 minutes to a 65°C core (a probe through the film is fine).
4. Jus: reduce remaining Madeira by half; add stock, reduce to 150 ml. Add cream and tarragon; mount with 20 g butter. Season.
5. Unwrap ballotines; pat dry. Sear in oil and 20 g butter over medium-high, rolling, 3–4 minutes until the skin is bronzed and crackling.
6. Rest 4 minutes; carve each into 4 thick coins.

## Plating & Service
Coins overlapping on pomme purée or celeriac, jus poured tableside, a tarragon tip on each round.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Stuff with wild mushrooms and chestnuts at Christmas. No sous-vide setup needed — a steady thermometer and a deep pan suffice.
