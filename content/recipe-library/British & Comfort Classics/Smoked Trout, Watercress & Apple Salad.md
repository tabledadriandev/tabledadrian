# Smoked Trout, Watercress & Apple Salad

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> No pans, all balance — hot-smoked trout, peppery leaves, sweet-sharp apple and a cider-mustard dressing; the light opener before a big roast.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Hot-smoked trout fillets — 300 g
- Watercress — 120 g
- Green apple (Granny Smith) — 1 (160 g), fine julienne
- Fennel — 100 g, shaved paper-thin
- Radishes — 60 g, sliced thin
- Cider vinegar — 25 ml
- Wholegrain mustard — 10 g
- Honey — 8 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- Dill — 6 g
- Lemon — half (15 ml juice)
- Flaky sea salt — 2 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Whisk cider vinegar, mustard and honey; stream in olive oil to emulsify. Season.
2. Hold apple julienne and shaved fennel in water with the lemon juice to stay crisp; drain and dry just before serving.
3. Flake the trout into large, generous pieces, discarding skin.
4. Toss watercress, fennel, apple and radish with two-thirds of the dressing — lightly, hands only.
5. Taste a leaf; adjust salt and acidity.
6. Fold the trout through at the last second so it stays in whole flakes.

## Plating & Service
Build tall and airy on chilled plates, spoon remaining dressing around, finish with dill and cracked pepper.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap trout for hot-smoked salmon or peeled prawns. Add a spoon of horseradish crème fraîche underneath if dairy is welcome.
