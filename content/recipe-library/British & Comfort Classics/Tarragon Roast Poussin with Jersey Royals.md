# Tarragon Roast Poussin with Jersey Royals

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A whole bird per guest always lands — tarragon butter under the skin, Jersey Royals roasting in the drippings below.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Poussins — 4 (about 450 g each)
- Unsalted butter — 120 g, softened
- Tarragon — 15 g, chopped (10 g butter, 5 g jus)
- Lemon — 2, zested and quartered
- Garlic — 4 cloves (20 g), smashed
- Jersey Royal potatoes — 800 g, scrubbed, halved if large
- Olive oil — 25 ml
- White wine — 150 ml
- Chicken stock — 250 ml
- Fine sea salt — 9 g; black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 200°C. Beat butter with 10 g tarragon, lemon zest and 3 g salt.
2. Loosen each poussin's breast skin; spread butter beneath. Push a lemon quarter and garlic clove into each cavity; salt the skins.
3. Toss potatoes with oil and salt in a large roasting tray; set a rack over and sit the birds on top.
4. Roast 40–45 minutes to a 74°C thigh, shaking the potatoes at halftime — they crisp in the dripping butter.
5. Rest birds 10 minutes; give potatoes 5 more minutes at 220°C if pale.
6. Jus: deglaze the rack drippings in a pan with wine, add stock, reduce by half; finish with 5 g tarragon and resting juices.

## Plating & Service
One bird per plate atop the buttery Royals, jus around; finger bowls out — poussin is best attacked by hand at the end.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> DF: olive oil-tarragon rub under the skin. Swap tarragon for lemon-thyme. One 1.6 kg chicken serves the same four with 70 minutes roasting.
