# Toad in the Hole with Caramelised Shallot Gravy

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The batter must hit smoking-hot fat around already-browned sausages — that is the whole secret to the rise.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Pork sausages — 8 (600 g)
- Beef dripping or neutral oil — 40 g
- Plain flour — 140 g
- Free-range eggs — 4
- Whole milk — 200 ml
- English mustard powder — 3 g
- Thyme leaves — 2 g
- Shallots — 300 g, halved lengthways
- Unsalted butter — 20 g
- Plain flour — 15 g (gravy)
- Red wine — 100 ml
- Beef stock — 400 ml
- Redcurrant jelly — 10 g
- Fine sea salt — 7 g

## Method
1. Batter: whisk 140 g flour, eggs, milk, mustard powder and 2 g salt smooth; rest 1 hour minimum at room temperature.
2. Heat oven to 220°C. Brown sausages in half the dripping in a 25 x 30 cm metal dish on the hob, 6–8 minutes; remove.
3. Gravy: caramelise shallots in butter over medium heat, cut-side down, 10 minutes; dust with 15 g flour, deglaze with wine, add stock and jelly, simmer 10 minutes to coating. Season.
4. Add remaining dripping to the dish; heat in the oven 5 minutes until smoking.
5. Working fast: sausages back in, batter poured around (it must sizzle violently), thyme scattered.
6. Bake 25–28 minutes without opening the door, until risen, crisp and deeply golden.

## Plating & Service
Straight to the table in the dish; cut generous quarters and drown in shallot gravy, with greens alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Herb the batter with sage for depth. Vegetarian: use plant sausages and oil — batter behaves identically.
