# Dry-Aged Beef Burger with Ogleshield & Bacon Jam

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Coarse-ground dry-aged chuck, seasoned only on the outside, smashed-edge crust, raclette-style Ogleshield melting over — the burger done right.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Dry-aged beef chuck, 20% fat, coarsely minced — 720 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g; black pepper — 2 g
- Ogleshield (or raclette) cheese — 160 g, sliced
- Smoked streaky bacon — 200 g, diced
- Onion — 150 g, minced
- Espresso or strong coffee — 30 ml
- Dark brown sugar — 40 g
- Cider vinegar — 25 ml
- Brioche buns, seeded — 4 (340 g)
- Mayonnaise — 60 g
- Dijon mustard — 10 g
- Baby gem lettuce — 1 head (100 g)
- Pickled gherkins — 60 g, sliced
- Neutral oil — 15 ml

## Method
1. Bacon jam: render bacon over medium heat 8 minutes; add onion, cook 8 minutes; add sugar, coffee and vinegar and simmer 10–12 minutes to a sticky jam. Cool.
2. Divide beef into 4 x 180 g pucks handling minimally — no kneading, no seasoning inside. Chill 20 minutes.
3. Whisk mayonnaise and mustard.
4. Heat a heavy pan or plancha to smoking with oil. Salt and pepper the pucks all over.
5. Sear 3 minutes, pressing edges lightly for crust; flip, top with cheese, cook 2–3 minutes to 55°C core (medium-rare-plus). Tent 2 minutes — the cheese finishes melting.
6. Toast bun halves cut-side down in the beef fat, 45 seconds.
7. Build: mustard mayo both faces, gem below, patty, bacon jam over the cheese, gherkins, lid.

## Plating & Service
Skewer, halve for the photo, and serve with triple-cooked chips and extra jam. Napkins mandatory.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: GF buns. No Ogleshield? Mature Cheddar mixed 50/50 with raclette. The jam keeps 2 weeks — make double.
