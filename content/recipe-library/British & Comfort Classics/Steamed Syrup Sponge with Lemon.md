# Steamed Syrup Sponge with Lemon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Golden syrup pooling down a feather-light steamed sponge — the inversion moment is worth the 100-minute wait.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Golden syrup — 120 g, plus 40 g to serve
- Lemon — 1, zest and 20 ml juice
- Unsalted butter — 175 g, soft, plus 10 g for the basin
- Caster sugar — 175 g
- Free-range eggs — 3
- Self-raising flour — 175 g
- Baking powder — 3 g
- Whole milk — 40 ml
- Fine sea salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Butter a 1.2 L pudding basin; pour 120 g golden syrup and the lemon juice into the base.
2. Beat butter and sugar until pale, 4 minutes; add eggs one at a time.
3. Fold in flour, baking powder, salt and lemon zest; loosen with milk to a soft dropping batter.
4. Spoon over the syrup; level without pressing.
5. Cover with pleated baking paper and foil; tie with string and make a handle.
6. Steam 1 hour 40 minutes, water halfway up, lid on, topping up with boiling water as needed.
7. Rest 5 minutes; free the edges and invert onto a lipped plate — hold nerve, lift slowly.

## Plating & Service
Extra warmed syrup over the summit, custard or cold cream circling — serve in wedges while it steams.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Jam roly-poly energy: swap syrup for raspberry jam. Microwave emergency version: 8 minutes at 800 W — decent, not divine.
