# Halibut with Mushy Peas, Brown Butter & Malt Vinegar Pearls

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Chip-shop flavours plated for a tasting menu — pearl-white halibut, marrowfat purée, and malt vinegar set into caviar-like pearls.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Halibut portions, skinned — 4 x 160 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g
- Olive oil — 25 ml
- Unsalted butter — 90 g (60 g browned, 30 g peas)
- Marrowfat peas, soaked overnight with 5 g bicarb — 250 g dry weight
- Frozen petits pois — 150 g
- Mint — 6 g
- Malt vinegar — 100 ml
- Agar-agar — 1.6 g
- Very cold neutral oil — 300 ml, in a tall chilled glass
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Pea shoots — 15 g

## Method
1. Pearls: whisk agar into malt vinegar, boil 60 seconds, cool 2 minutes to 55°C. Drip from a squeeze bottle into the cold oil — spheres set as they sink. Strain and rinse gently.
2. Rinse soaked marrowfats; simmer in fresh water 40–50 minutes until collapsing; drain.
3. Blitz half the marrowfats with petits pois (blanched), mint and 30 g butter; fold back into the whole peas. Season; hold warm.
4. Salt halibut 10 minutes ahead; pat dry.
5. Sear in oil over medium-high, 3 minutes on the presentation side; flip, add 60 g butter, brown it while basting 2 minutes to a 50°C core. Add lemon juice off the heat.
6. Rest fish 2 minutes.

## Plating & Service
A spoon of mushy peas dragged across the plate, halibut on top, brown butter around, malt pearls scattered like roe, pea shoots to finish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No agar? Reduce malt vinegar with 20 g sugar to a gastrique instead. Cod loin substitutes at the same core temperature.
