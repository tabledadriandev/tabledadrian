# Knickerbocker Glory with Raspberry Ripple & Honeycomb

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The tall-glass seaside spectacular rebuilt with homemade honeycomb and a proper raspberry ripple — spoons of absurd length obligatory.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Vanilla ice cream — 500 g (8 scoops)
- Raspberries — 300 g
- Caster sugar — 130 g (30 g coulis, 100 g honeycomb)
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Golden syrup — 40 g
- Bicarbonate of soda — 5 g
- Double cream — 200 ml
- Icing sugar — 15 g
- Strawberries — 150 g, quartered
- Ripe peach — 1 (150 g), diced
- Dark chocolate — 30 g, melted
- Fan wafers — 4

## Method
1. Honeycomb: line a tray. Melt 100 g sugar with golden syrup over medium heat without stirring; cook to amber (150°C). Whisk in bicarb — it erupts — and pour immediately. Cool 30 minutes; smash.
2. Coulis: blitz 200 g raspberries with 30 g sugar and lemon juice; sieve.
3. Whip cream with icing sugar to soft peaks.
4. Chill four tall sundae glasses.
5. Build: coulis, scoop, fruit, honeycomb shards, cream — twice over, rippling coulis down the glass walls.
6. Crown with whole raspberries, a drizzle of melted chocolate, more honeycomb and the wafer.

## Plating & Service
Serve instantly on saucers with long spoons — the layers must still be distinct when they hit the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: skip wafers, GF honeycomb is naturally fine. Mango-passionfruit version for the tropics. Honeycomb keeps 3 days airtight — humidity is its assassin.
