# Salt-Baked Beetroot, Goat's Curd, Honey & Thyme

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Beets baked in a salt crust concentrate to something almost meaty — against cool goat's curd and thyme honey, this is elegant simplicity.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Mixed beetroot (red, golden, candy) — 600 g, scrubbed, similar size
- Coarse sea salt — 500 g
- Plain egg whites — 2 (70 g)
- Thyme — 8 sprigs
- Goat's curd or soft goat cheese — 200 g
- Honey — 30 g
- Sherry vinegar — 20 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 40 ml
- Orange — 1, zested
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Baby watercress — 20 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 190°C. Mix salt with egg whites and leaves from 4 thyme sprigs to wet-sand texture.
2. Bed beetroot on a thin salt layer in a small tray; pack the rest over to encase. Bake 70–80 minutes until a skewer slides in easily through the crust.
3. Rest 10 minutes, crack off the crust, and rub away the skins with a cloth while warm.
4. Warm honey with remaining thyme sprigs to 60°C for 5 minutes; infuse off heat.
5. Cut beets into irregular wedges; dress warm with sherry vinegar, 25 ml olive oil and pepper.
6. Season the curd lightly with orange zest and a pinch of salt.

## Plating & Service
Swipe curd across the plate, tumble the warm beets, thread the thyme honey over, finish with watercress and remaining oil.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: whipped almond ricotta and maple instead of honey (drop the salt-crust egg white; wrap beets in foil instead). Add toasted seeds for crunch.
