# Buttermilk Fried Chicken Burger with Fennel Slaw

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> An overnight buttermilk brine, a craggy double-dredged crust, and a bright anise slaw — the fried chicken sandwich, tuned for a superyacht galley.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chicken thighs, boneless, skin off — 4 large (600 g)
- Buttermilk — 300 ml
- Fine sea salt — 8 g
- Hot sauce — 10 ml
- Plain flour — 200 g
- Cornflour — 60 g
- Smoked paprika — 5 g; garlic powder — 4 g; white pepper — 2 g
- Neutral oil — 1.5 L
- Fennel — 200 g, shaved
- White cabbage — 150 g, shredded
- Mayonnaise — 70 g; Dijon mustard — 8 g; cider vinegar — 15 ml
- Honey — 15 g
- Brioche buns — 4 (340 g)
- Pickled gherkins — 60 g, sliced

## Method
1. Brine thighs in buttermilk, 5 g salt and hot sauce, 4–24 hours chilled.
2. Slaw: toss fennel and cabbage with mayonnaise, mustard, vinegar and a pinch of salt; chill 20 minutes.
3. Dredge: whisk flour, cornflour, paprika, garlic powder, white pepper, 3 g salt. Drizzle in 30 ml of the brine and rub to create craggy clumps.
4. Press each thigh firmly into the dredge, back through the brine, and into the dredge again.
5. Fry at 170°C, 7–8 minutes, to deep gold and 74°C core. Drain on a rack; brush lightly with honey while hot.
6. Toast the buns; build with slaw below, chicken, gherkins on top.

## Plating & Service
Halve to show the crust-to-juice ratio; serve with skinny fries and remaining slaw in a bowl.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: rice flour and cornflour dredge, GF buns. Fiery version: whisk 20 g cayenne butter over the fried chicken, Nashville-style.
