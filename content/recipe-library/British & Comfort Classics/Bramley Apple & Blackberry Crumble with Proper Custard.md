# Bramley Apple & Blackberry Crumble with Proper Custard

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sharp Bramleys collapsing under a rubble of buttery oat crumble, with a crème anglaise thick enough to insult tinned custard forever.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Bramley apples — 900 g, peeled, 3 cm chunks
- Blackberries — 250 g
- Caster sugar — 160 g (80 g fruit, 80 g custard)
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Ground cinnamon — 2 g
- Plain flour — 180 g
- Jumbo oats — 60 g
- Demerara sugar — 100 g
- Unsalted butter — 140 g, cold, diced
- Fine sea salt — 2 g
- Whole milk — 300 ml; double cream — 200 ml
- Egg yolks — 6
- Vanilla pod — 1, split (or 8 ml extract)

## Method
1. Heat oven to 190°C. Toss apples with 80 g caster sugar, lemon juice and cinnamon in a 2 L dish; scatter blackberries through.
2. Crumble: rub flour, butter and salt to coarse rubble; toss in oats and demerara — squeeze a few clumps for texture.
3. Top the fruit loosely (never pressed); bake 40–45 minutes until deep gold with juices bubbling at the edge.
4. Custard: warm milk, cream and vanilla to steaming. Whisk yolks with 80 g sugar; temper with the hot milk.
5. Return to a clean pan; stir constantly over low heat to 82°C — coats the spoon, holds a line.
6. Strain into a warm jug.
7. Rest the crumble 10 minutes; molten fruit takes no prisoners.

## Plating & Service
Deep bowls, generous custard poured at the table — the crackle of crust into sharp fruit is the whole point.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: GF flour and certified oats. Vegan: plant butter and an oat-milk cornflour custard. Rhubarb takes over in spring.
