# Eton Mess with Macerated Strawberries & Black Pepper

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A mess assembled with intent — chewy-centred meringue, cream whipped soft, and strawberries woken up with balsamic and a crack of pepper.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Egg whites — 3 (about 100 g)
- Caster sugar — 200 g
- Strawberries — 600 g, hulled, quartered
- Icing sugar — 25 g
- Balsamic vinegar — 10 ml
- Black pepper — 1 g, coarsely cracked
- Double cream — 400 ml
- Vanilla extract — 4 ml
- Mint leaves — 6, fine ribbons

## Method
1. Meringue: whisk whites to soft peaks; add caster sugar a spoon at a time to a stiff gloss. Spread in rough clouds on paper.
2. Bake at 110°C for 80–90 minutes until crisp outside, marshmallow inside; cool in the switched-off oven.
3. Macerate two-thirds of the strawberries with icing sugar, balsamic and pepper, 30 minutes.
4. Blitz the remaining third into a quick sauce.
5. Whip cream with vanilla to soft, floppy peaks — stiff cream kills the mess.
6. Just before service: shatter meringue over the cream, fold twice with berries and half the sauce — visible streaks, not uniformity.

## Plating & Service
Heap into coupes, remaining sauce and mint over — serve within 10 minutes while the meringue still crunches.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Roasted rhubarb or mango-lime versions rotate by season. Shop meringue is forgivable at sea; homemade is the difference.
