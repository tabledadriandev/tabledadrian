# Breakfast Hash of Cumberland Sausage & Sage

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> One-pan crew-and-guest pleaser: crisped sausage nuggets, golden potatoes and sage, each portion crowned with a runny fried egg.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Cumberland sausages — 6 (450 g), skins removed
- Waxy potatoes — 600 g, 2 cm dice
- Onion — 150 g, sliced
- Red pepper — 150 g, 2 cm dice
- Olive oil — 45 ml
- Sage leaves — 12
- Garlic — 2 cloves (10 g), sliced
- Smoked paprika — 4 g
- Free-range eggs — 4
- Flat-leaf parsley — 10 g, chopped
- Fine sea salt — 4 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Parboil potato dice in salted water 6 minutes; drain and steam-dry thoroughly.
2. Heat 30 ml oil in a wide heavy pan over medium-high. Pinch sausage meat into rough nuggets and brown hard, 5–6 minutes; remove.
3. Add potatoes to the fat; fry 10–12 minutes, turning occasionally, until golden and crusted.
4. Add onion and pepper; cook 5 minutes until softened and catching colour.
5. Return sausage with garlic, paprika and sage; toss 2 minutes until the sage crisps.
6. In a separate pan, fry eggs in 15 ml oil, basting, 2–3 minutes — set whites, liquid yolks.
7. Season the hash boldly; fold through parsley.

## Plating & Service
Serve the hash in warm shallow bowls straight from the pan, one egg per portion, extra pepper over the yolk.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian: swap sausage for 300 g halloumi cubes added at step 5. Add a splash of hot sauce or brown-sauce vinaigrette for punch.
