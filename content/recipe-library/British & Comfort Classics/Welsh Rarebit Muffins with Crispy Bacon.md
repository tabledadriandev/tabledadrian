# Welsh Rarebit Muffins with Crispy Bacon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The cheese toastie's grown-up cousin — ale-spiked Cheddar sauce grilled onto English muffins with shards of crisp bacon.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- English muffins — 4, split (280 g)
- Mature Cheddar — 200 g, grated
- Brown ale — 60 ml
- Unsalted butter — 25 g
- Plain flour — 20 g
- English mustard — 8 g
- Worcestershire-style sauce — 10 ml
- Egg yolk — 1
- Smoked streaky bacon — 8 rashers (240 g)
- Chives — 6 g, sliced
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Cook bacon in a cold pan brought to medium-high heat until deeply crisp, 6–8 minutes; drain and snap into shards.
2. Make a roux with butter and flour, 2 minutes over medium heat. Whisk in ale gradually; simmer 2 minutes to a thick paste.
3. Off heat, beat in Cheddar, mustard and Worcestershire sauce until smooth. Cool 5 minutes, then beat in the egg yolk.
4. Preheat grill to high. Toast the muffin halves cut-side up until pale gold.
5. Spread the rarebit mixture thickly to the edges of each half.
6. Grill 3–4 minutes until blistered, bronzed and bubbling.
7. Rest 1 minute — the topping is molten.

## Plating & Service
Two halves per plate, bacon shards spiked into the rarebit, chives and black pepper over.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian: skip bacon, top with a poached egg. Swap ale for cider and Cheddar for Caerphilly for a lighter tang.
