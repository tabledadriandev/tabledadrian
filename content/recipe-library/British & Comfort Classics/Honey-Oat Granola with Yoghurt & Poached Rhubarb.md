# Honey-Oat Granola with Yoghurt & Poached Rhubarb

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Clumpy, deeply toasted granola against blush-pink forced rhubarb — make a big jar and breakfast service runs itself.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Jumbo rolled oats — 300 g
- Flaked almonds — 80 g
- Pumpkin seeds — 60 g
- Sesame seeds — 20 g
- Honey — 90 g
- Olive oil — 60 ml
- Vanilla extract — 5 ml
- Fine sea salt — 3 g
- Rhubarb — 400 g, in 6 cm batons
- Caster sugar — 60 g
- Orange — 1, juice and zest (about 80 ml juice)
- Greek yoghurt — 500 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 150°C. Warm honey, oil, vanilla and salt until pourable.
2. Toss oats, almonds and both seeds with the honey mixture until evenly coated.
3. Press onto a lined tray in an even 1.5 cm layer. Bake 35–40 minutes, rotating once, without stirring — this builds clusters.
4. Cool completely on the tray, then break into clumps. Store airtight.
5. For the rhubarb: dissolve sugar in orange juice and zest in a wide pan; lay in rhubarb in one layer.
6. Poach at a bare simmer 4–5 minutes until just tender but intact. Cool in the syrup.

## Plating & Service
Layer yoghurt, rhubarb batons with a spoon of syrup, and a generous handful of granola in glass coupes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: maple syrup and coconut yoghurt. GF: certified GF oats. Out of rhubarb season, use poached plums or roasted figs.
