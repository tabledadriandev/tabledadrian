# Stilton & Walnut Tart with Pickled Pear Salad

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A blue-cheese custard tart with a walnut pastry, cut by quick-pickled pear — cheese course and starter in one.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Plain flour — 180 g
- Walnuts — 60 g, finely ground, plus 30 g toasted to serve
- Unsalted butter — 120 g, cold, diced
- Egg yolk — 1, plus 2 whole eggs and 1 yolk for the custard
- Cold water — 20 ml
- Stilton — 150 g, crumbled
- Double cream — 200 ml
- Whole milk — 100 ml
- Thyme leaves — 2 g
- Pears — 2 (300 g), thinly sliced
- White wine vinegar — 60 ml; caster sugar — 30 g
- Rocket — 60 g
- Fine sea salt — 4 g

## Method
1. Rub flour, ground walnuts, butter and 3 g salt to crumbs; bind with 1 yolk and water. Chill 30 minutes.
2. Roll to 3 mm; line a 23 cm tart ring. Chill 20 minutes. Blind-bake at 180°C with beans 18 minutes; remove beans, bake 8 minutes more until sandy.
3. Warm vinegar and sugar until dissolved; pour over the pear slices. Pickle 20 minutes; drain.
4. Whisk 2 eggs, 1 yolk, cream, milk, thyme and a little pepper; fold in Stilton.
5. Pour into the case; bake at 150°C for 25–30 minutes until just set with a central tremble.
6. Rest 15 minutes — serve warm, not hot.

## Plating & Service
A slim wedge with rocket and pickled pear tossed in a little of the pickle liquor and olive oil, toasted walnuts over.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Milder: use half Stilton, half soft goat cheese. Nut-free: plain shortcrust and pumpkin seeds in the salad.
