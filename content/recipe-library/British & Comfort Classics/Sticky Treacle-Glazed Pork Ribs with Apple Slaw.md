# Sticky Treacle-Glazed Pork Ribs with Apple Slaw

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Low-oven ribs lacquered with black treacle and cider vinegar — British BBQ language — with a mustardy apple slaw for crunch.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Pork spare ribs — 1.6 kg, in racks
- Fine sea salt — 8 g; black pepper — 2 g
- Smoked paprika — 6 g; ground fennel seed — 3 g
- Dry cider — 200 ml
- Black treacle — 60 g
- Tomato ketchup — 60 g
- Cider vinegar — 50 ml (30 ml glaze, 20 ml slaw)
- English mustard — 10 g
- Worcestershire-style sauce — 10 ml
- White cabbage — 300 g, shredded
- Green apples — 2 (320 g), julienne
- Spring onions — 40 g, sliced
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Honey — 10 g

## Method
1. Rub ribs with salt, pepper, paprika and fennel. Lay in a deep tray with the cider; cover tightly with foil.
2. Bake at 150°C for 2.5 hours until the meat pulls back from the bone.
3. Glaze: simmer treacle, ketchup, 30 ml vinegar, mustard and Worcestershire sauce with 50 ml rib tray juices, 5 minutes, to a thick lacquer.
4. Raise oven to 210°C. Brush ribs heavily with glaze; roast uncovered 15 minutes, glazing twice more, until sticky and charred at the edges.
5. Slaw: whisk 20 ml vinegar, oil and honey; toss with cabbage, apple and spring onion; season. Do this last so it stays crisp.
6. Rest ribs 5 minutes; cut between the bones.

## Plating & Service
Pile ribs on a board, remaining glaze brushed over, slaw in a bowl — finger bowls and no cutlery pretensions.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Finish over coals for real smoke. GF: check the Worcestershire-style sauce. Swap treacle for date molasses if that's the galley shelf.
