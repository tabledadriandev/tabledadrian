# Chicken Liver Parfait with Ale-Onion Jam

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Silk-smooth parfait cooked gently in a bain-marie, with a dark ale-onion jam that out-performs any chutney from a jar.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Chicken livers, trimmed — 400 g
- Free-range eggs — 2
- Unsalted butter — 250 g (200 g melted for parfait, 50 g clarified to seal)
- Shallots — 80 g, sliced
- Garlic — 1 clove (5 g)
- Brandy — 40 ml
- Port — 40 ml
- Thyme — 3 sprigs
- Fine sea salt — 7 g
- Onions — 400 g, thinly sliced
- Brown ale — 150 ml
- Light brown sugar — 50 g
- Cider vinegar — 30 ml
- Sourdough — 6 slices, toasted (300 g)

## Method
1. Simmer shallots, garlic and thyme in brandy and port until reduced to 30 ml; discard thyme.
2. Heat oven to 110°C with a deep bain-marie inside at 70°C water temperature.
3. Blend livers, eggs, reduction and salt for 60 seconds; with the motor running, stream in 200 g melted butter at 45°C.
4. Pass through a fine sieve into a terrine or six 100 ml ramekins; cover with foil.
5. Cook in the bain-marie 35–45 minutes to a core of 64°C — barely set with a slight wobble.
6. Cool 30 minutes, then seal the surface with clarified butter; chill at least 6 hours.
7. Jam: cook onions in a little butter with a pinch of salt over low heat 20 minutes; add sugar, ale and vinegar and reduce 15 minutes to a dark, sticky jam.

## Plating & Service
Parfait in its pot or as a thick quenelle, warm jam alongside, hot sourdough toast wrapped in a napkin.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The parfait keeps 4 days sealed. Swap ale jam for spiced fig chutney. GF: serve with toasted GF baguette.
