# Chicken, Cider & Tarragon Casserole

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> One-pot ease with a cream-cider sauce that tastes like Normandy met Somerset — the weeknight casserole that still impresses guests.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chicken thighs, bone in, skin on — 8 (1.3 kg)
- Olive oil — 25 ml
- Smoked bacon lardons — 120 g
- Leeks — 250 g, thick slices
- Celery — 60 g, sliced
- Garlic — 3 cloves (15 g), sliced
- Dry cider — 400 ml
- Chicken stock — 250 ml
- Dijon mustard — 15 g
- Crème fraîche — 150 g
- Tarragon — 10 g, chopped
- Eating apple — 1 (150 g), wedges
- Cornflour — 10 g, slaked in 20 ml water
- Fine sea salt — 7 g; black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Season chicken; brown skin-side down in oil in a casserole 6–8 minutes until deeply golden; remove.
2. Render lardons 3 minutes; soften leeks, celery and garlic 5 minutes.
3. Deglaze with cider; reduce by a third. Add stock and mustard.
4. Return chicken skin-up, skin above the liquid line. Braise uncovered at 170°C for 40–45 minutes — the skin stays crisp.
5. Lift chicken; whisk slaked cornflour into the simmering liquor, then crème fraîche. Simmer 3 minutes; season.
6. Caramelise apple wedges in a dry pan 2 minutes per side; fold into the sauce with tarragon.
7. Return chicken to the pot for service.

## Plating & Service
To the table in the casserole with mash or crusty bread — crisp skin up, sauce spooned around, never over the skin.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> DF: finish with 50 ml extra cider reduced hard instead of crème fraîche. Swap tarragon for thyme if the aniseed divides the table.
