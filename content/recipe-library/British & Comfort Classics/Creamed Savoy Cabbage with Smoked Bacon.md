# Creamed Savoy Cabbage with Smoked Bacon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The steakhouse side Britain forgot it invented — ribboned savoy folded through reduced cream and smoky lardons, still with bite.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Savoy cabbage — 700 g, cored, in 2 cm ribbons
- Smoked bacon lardons — 120 g
- Unsalted butter — 20 g
- Shallots — 60 g, sliced
- Garlic — 1 clove (5 g), grated
- Double cream — 200 ml
- Freshly grated nutmeg — 0.5 g
- Fine sea salt — 4 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Lemon juice — 5 ml

## Method
1. Blanch cabbage in salted boiling water 2 minutes; drain hard and press dry.
2. Render lardons in a wide pan over medium heat, 5 minutes, until crisp.
3. Add butter, shallots and garlic; soften 3 minutes.
4. Add cream; reduce 2–3 minutes until it coats a spoon.
5. Fold in the cabbage; toss 2 minutes until hot through and glossed, never stewed.
6. Season with nutmeg, salt, pepper and a whisper of lemon juice.

## Plating & Service
Into a warm bowl beside roast chicken, pork or a pie — serve immediately while the ribbons still stand.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian: skip bacon, add 3 g smoked paprika with the shallots. Hispi or kale take the same treatment.
