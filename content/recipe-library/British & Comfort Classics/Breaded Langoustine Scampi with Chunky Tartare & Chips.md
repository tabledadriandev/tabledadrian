# Breaded Langoustine Scampi with Chunky Tartare & Chips

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Real scampi — whole langoustine tails, not reformed paste — in a light crumb with proper chips and a tartare heavy on the cornichons.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Langoustine tails, peeled (or large king prawns) — 500 g
- Plain flour — 70 g
- Free-range eggs — 2, beaten
- Panko breadcrumbs — 150 g
- Lemon — 2 (1 zested into crumbs, 1 in wedges)
- Neutral oil — 2 L
- Floury potatoes — 1 kg, in 2 cm chips
- Mayonnaise — 120 g
- Cornichons — 50 g, chopped
- Capers — 25 g, chopped
- Shallot — 25 g, minced
- Tarragon and parsley — 12 g, chopped
- Dijon mustard — 8 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Chips: simmer in salted water 8 minutes; steam-dry, chill. Fry at 140°C for 6 minutes; drain and hold.
2. Tartare: fold cornichons, capers, shallot, herbs and mustard through the mayonnaise with a squeeze of lemon. Chill.
3. Pat tails bone-dry; season. Pané through flour, egg, and lemon-zested panko.
4. Fry tails at 180°C in two batches, 2–2.5 minutes, until golden and just cooked — they overcook in seconds. Drain; salt.
5. Final chip fry at 185°C, 4–5 minutes; salt hard.
6. Reunite everything while screaming hot.

## Plating & Service
Paper-lined baskets or warm plates: scampi piled on chips, tartare in a pot, lemon wedge — malt vinegar available, not imposed.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: rice flour and GF panko. Monkfish cheeks make a worthy scampi if langoustines are scarce.
