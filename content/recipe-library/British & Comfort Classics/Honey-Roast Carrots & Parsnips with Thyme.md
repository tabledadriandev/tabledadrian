# Honey-Roast Carrots & Parsnips with Thyme

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Roasted hard first, glazed last — honey added too early burns; added late it lacquers.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Carrots — 600 g, halved lengthways
- Parsnips — 600 g, quartered lengthways, cores intact
- Olive oil — 50 ml
- Honey — 40 g
- Thyme — 6 sprigs
- Cider vinegar — 10 ml
- Flaky sea salt — 5 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 200°C with a large tray inside.
2. Toss carrots and parsnips with oil, fine salt and half the thyme.
3. Spread on the hot tray cut-side down, uncrowded; roast 30 minutes untouched.
4. Turn; roast 10 minutes more until caramelised at the edges and tender at the core.
5. Warm honey with vinegar and remaining thyme leaves; pour over and toss.
6. Return to the oven 4–5 minutes only, until glossed and sticky.

## Plating & Service
Piled on a warm platter, tray juices dripped over, flaky salt and pepper at the last.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: maple syrup for honey. Add a spoon of wholegrain mustard to the glaze beside gammon or sausages.
