# Chicken, Leek & Tarragon Pot Pie

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Thigh meat stays succulent under the lid, and tarragon-mustard cream turns a homely pie quietly luxurious.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chicken thighs, boneless, skinless — 700 g, 4 cm pieces
- Olive oil — 20 ml
- Unsalted butter — 40 g
- Leeks — 350 g, 1 cm slices
- Smoked bacon lardons — 100 g
- Plain flour — 35 g
- White wine — 100 ml
- Chicken stock — 350 ml
- Double cream — 120 ml
- Dijon mustard — 12 g
- Tarragon — 8 g, chopped
- All-butter puff pastry — 320 g
- Egg — 1, beaten
- Fine sea salt — 6 g; black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Brown chicken in oil over high heat 4–5 minutes; remove. Render lardons 3 minutes in the same pan.
2. Add butter and leeks; soften 6 minutes. Stir in flour; cook 2 minutes.
3. Deglaze with wine; add stock gradually, stirring smooth. Simmer 5 minutes.
4. Return chicken; add cream and mustard. Simmer 5 minutes until the sauce coats. Off heat, add tarragon; season. Cool 20 minutes.
5. Fill four 350 ml pie dishes (or one 1.2 L dish). Heat oven to 200°C.
6. Roll pastry to 4 mm; cut lids with 2 cm overhang. Egg-wash rims, crown the pies, crimp, slit, and egg-wash the tops.
7. Bake 25–30 minutes (35 for one large) until puffed and deep gold.

## Plating & Service
Individual pies straight down on the plate, greens and buttered new potatoes served family-style.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 150 g sautéed mushrooms, or swap tarragon for thyme. Lighter: crème fraîche for cream, filo scrunch instead of puff.
