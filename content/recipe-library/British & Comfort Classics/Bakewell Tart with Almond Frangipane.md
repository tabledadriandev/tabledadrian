# Bakewell Tart with Almond Frangipane

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Derbyshire's finest — raspberry jam under a just-set frangipane, flaked almonds for crunch, no fondant icing in sight.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Plain flour — 220 g
- Unsalted butter — 280 g (130 g pastry, 150 g frangipane), soft/cold as needed
- Icing sugar — 40 g, plus 10 g to dust
- Egg yolk — 1; cold water — 25 ml
- Caster sugar — 150 g
- Free-range eggs — 3
- Ground almonds — 150 g
- Plain flour — 25 g (frangipane)
- Almond extract — 3 ml
- Raspberry jam — 160 g
- Flaked almonds — 40 g
- Fine sea salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Pastry: rub 220 g flour, 130 g cold butter, icing sugar and salt to crumbs; bind with yolk and water. Chill 30 minutes.
2. Line a 23 cm tart ring at 3 mm; chill 20 minutes. Blind-bake at 180°C with beans 18 minutes; remove beans, 5 minutes more.
3. Frangipane: beat 150 g soft butter with caster sugar until pale; add eggs one at a time, then ground almonds, 25 g flour and almond extract.
4. Spread jam over the base; pipe or spoon frangipane over in an even layer.
5. Scatter flaked almonds. Bake at 170°C for 35–40 minutes until golden and springy at the centre.
6. Cool 30 minutes in the ring; dust with icing sugar.

## Plating & Service
Room-temperature wedges with crème fraîche or thick cream — the jam line should read clean against the frangipane.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cherry jam and kirsch nod to the original pudding. GF: GF flour blend in both layers works — frangipane barely notices.
