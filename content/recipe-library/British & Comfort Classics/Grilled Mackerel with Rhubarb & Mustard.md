# Grilled Mackerel with Rhubarb & Mustard

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Oily fish demands acid — sharp poached rhubarb does what gooseberries did for our grandmothers, with a mustard dressing to bridge them.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Mackerel fillets, pin-boned — 8 (about 640 g)
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Rhubarb — 300 g, in 5 cm batons
- Caster sugar — 40 g
- Orange juice — 80 ml
- Star anise — 1
- Wholegrain mustard — 15 g
- Cider vinegar — 15 ml
- Honey — 8 g
- Watercress — 80 g
- Fennel — 100 g, shaved
- Flaky sea salt — 4 g; black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Poach rhubarb: dissolve sugar in orange juice with star anise; add rhubarb in one layer and simmer 3–4 minutes until just tender. Cool in the syrup.
2. Dressing: whisk mustard, vinegar, honey and 25 ml olive oil with 15 ml of the rhubarb syrup.
3. Preheat the grill to maximum; line a tray with oiled foil.
4. Score mackerel skin at 2 cm intervals; rub with remaining oil and salt.
5. Grill skin-up, 4–5 minutes without turning, until the skin blisters and the flesh flakes.
6. Toss watercress and shaved fennel with half the dressing.

## Plating & Service
Two fillets skin-up per plate, rhubarb batons alongside, salad in a loose pile, remaining dressing around.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Out of rhubarb season: use quick-pickled gooseberries or apple. Also excellent with sardines or herring, 3 minutes under the grill.
