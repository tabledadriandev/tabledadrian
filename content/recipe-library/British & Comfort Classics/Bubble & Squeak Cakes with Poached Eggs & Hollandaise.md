# Bubble & Squeak Cakes with Poached Eggs & Hollandaise

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Leftovers dressed for the aft deck — crisp-edged cabbage-potato cakes under a sharp, lemony hollandaise.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Floury potatoes (Maris Piper) — 600 g, peeled
- Savoy cabbage — 250 g, shredded
- Spring onions — 60 g, sliced
- Unsalted butter — 180 g (30 g for cakes, 150 g clarified for hollandaise)
- Free-range eggs — 4, plus 3 yolks
- White wine vinegar — 30 ml (15 ml for reduction, 15 ml for poaching)
- Shallot — 30 g, minced
- White peppercorns — 6, crushed
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Rice flour — 30 g, for dusting
- Fine sea salt — 5 g
- Chives — 8 g, sliced

## Method
1. Boil potatoes in salted water 18 minutes; drain, steam-dry 5 minutes, crush roughly.
2. Blanch cabbage 2 minutes; drain, squeeze dry. Fold into potato with spring onions, 30 g butter and salt. Shape into 8 cakes; chill 15 minutes, then dust with rice flour.
3. For the hollandaise: reduce 15 ml vinegar with shallot and peppercorns to 10 ml; strain.
4. Whisk yolks with the reduction over a bain-marie at 65–70°C until thick ribbons form, 4–5 minutes. Slowly whisk in 150 g warm clarified butter; season with lemon juice and salt. Hold warm, not hot.
5. Fry cakes in a film of butter over medium heat 4 minutes per side until deep golden and crusted.
6. Poach eggs in barely simmering water with 15 ml vinegar, 3 minutes; drain on cloth.
7. Season eggs; check the hollandaise's consistency — loosen with warm water if needed.

## Plating & Service
Two cakes per plate, egg on top, hollandaise spooned over generously, chives to finish. Break the yolk at the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add flaked hot-smoked salmon between cake and egg. DF/lighter: skip hollandaise, serve with a mustard vinaigrette.
