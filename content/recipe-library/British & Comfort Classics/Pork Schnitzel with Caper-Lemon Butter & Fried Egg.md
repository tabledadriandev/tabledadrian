# Pork Schnitzel with Caper-Lemon Butter & Fried Egg

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Plate-wide crisp pork in a wavy soufflé crumb — shaken, not pressed, in the pan — Holstein-style with an egg and caper butter.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Pork loin steaks — 4 x 170 g, fat trimmed
- Plain flour — 80 g
- Free-range eggs — 3 (2 for pané, plus 4 for frying)
- Fine white breadcrumbs — 180 g
- Neutral oil — 400 ml
- Unsalted butter — 80 g (30 g pan, 50 g sauce)
- Capers — 30 g, rinsed
- Lemon — 2 (40 ml juice; wedges to serve)
- Flat-leaf parsley — 10 g, chopped
- Fine sea salt — 7 g; black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Bat each steak between cling film to 5 mm with a flat mallet. Season both sides.
2. Pané: flour, beaten egg, then crumbs — lay the crumbs on loosely, never pressing; the crust should billow.
3. Heat 400 ml oil with 30 g butter in a wide pan to 170°C (a crumb fizzes instantly).
4. Fry one schnitzel at a time, 90 seconds per side, swirling and spooning fat over so the crust puffs into waves. Drain on a rack; salt. Hold at 90°C in the oven.
5. Fry 4 eggs in a separate pan in a little of the schnitzel fat, 2–3 minutes.
6. Sauce: brown 50 g butter, 2–3 minutes; off heat, add capers, lemon juice and parsley.

## Plating & Service
Schnitzel overhanging the plate, egg on top, caper-lemon butter spooned around, lemon wedge and potato-cucumber salad beside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken or veal swap directly. GF: rice flour and GF crumbs. Skip the egg for the classic Wiener-style with just lemon.
