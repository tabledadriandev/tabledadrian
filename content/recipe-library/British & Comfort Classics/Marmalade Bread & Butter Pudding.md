# Marmalade Bread & Butter Pudding

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Day-old brioche, bitter orange marmalade and a vanilla custard that soaks for twenty minutes before baking — burnished peaks, molten valleys.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Brioche loaf, day-old — 350 g, sliced 1.5 cm
- Unsalted butter — 60 g, soft
- Seville orange marmalade — 120 g, plus 30 g to glaze
- Sultanas — 60 g, soaked in 30 ml hot tea or whisky
- Whole milk — 350 ml
- Double cream — 250 ml
- Free-range eggs — 3, plus 2 yolks
- Caster sugar — 80 g
- Vanilla pod — 1, split
- Orange — 1, zested
- Demerara sugar — 20 g
- Fine sea salt — 1 g

## Method
1. Butter the brioche slices; spread with marmalade and cut into triangles.
2. Shingle into a buttered 2 L dish, scattering drained sultanas between layers.
3. Custard: warm milk, cream and vanilla to steaming. Whisk eggs, yolks, caster sugar, salt and orange zest; pour on the hot milk, whisking.
4. Strain the custard over the bread; press gently and soak 20–30 minutes.
5. Scatter demerara. Bake at 160°C in a bain-marie 40–45 minutes until just set with golden peaks.
6. Warm 30 g marmalade with a splash of water; brush over the top.
7. Rest 10 minutes.

## Plating & Service
Big spoonfuls at the table — crisp corner and soft middle in every serving, cold cream in a jug.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Croissants or panettone happily replace brioche. Chocolate-cherry version: swap marmalade for dark chocolate and kirsch cherries.
