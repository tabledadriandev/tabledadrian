# Luxury Fish Pie with Cheddar Mash & Soft Egg

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Three fish, prawns and jammy eggs under a Cheddar-crusted mash — the dish every returning charter guest requests by name.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Salmon fillet — 300 g, 3 cm chunks
- Undyed smoked haddock — 300 g, 3 cm chunks
- Cod or pollock — 200 g, 3 cm chunks
- Raw king prawns, peeled — 200 g
- Whole milk — 600 ml
- Bay leaf — 1
- Unsalted butter — 130 g (60 g sauce, 70 g mash)
- Plain flour — 60 g
- Dijon mustard — 12 g
- Double cream — 100 ml
- Free-range eggs — 4
- Flat-leaf parsley and dill — 20 g, chopped
- Floury potatoes — 1.4 kg, peeled
- Mature Cheddar — 120 g, grated
- Fine sea salt — 10 g; white pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Poach salmon, haddock and cod in milk with bay at 80°C for 4 minutes; lift out. Strain and keep the milk.
2. Boil eggs 6 minutes 30 seconds; refresh, peel, quarter.
3. Sauce: cook 60 g butter and flour 2 minutes; whisk in the poaching milk gradually. Simmer 4 minutes; finish with mustard, 50 ml cream, herbs, salt, white pepper.
4. Boil potatoes 20 minutes; pass through a ricer with 70 g butter and 50 ml cream. Season.
5. Fold fish, raw prawns and eggs gently into the sauce in a 2 L dish — do not break the fish.
6. Pipe or fork the mash over; scatter Cheddar.
7. Bake at 190°C for 30–35 minutes until golden with the sauce bubbling at the edges. Rest 10 minutes.

## Plating & Service
Serve at the table in the dish with buttered peas or wilted spinach — the resting time keeps portions intact.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: cornflour-thickened sauce. Lighter: all-milk sauce, olive-oil mash. Scallops instead of prawns for a splurge.
