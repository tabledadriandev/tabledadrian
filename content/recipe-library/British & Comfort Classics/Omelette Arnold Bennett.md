# Omelette Arnold Bennett

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Savoy's open-faced smoked haddock omelette, glazed under a hot grill — pure hotel-breakfast theatre for two.

## Ingredients (serves 2)
- Undyed smoked haddock — 250 g
- Whole milk — 300 ml
- Bay leaf — 1
- Free-range eggs — 6
- Unsalted butter — 50 g (20 g roux, 30 g omelette)
- Plain flour — 20 g (or rice flour for GF)
- Double cream — 60 ml
- Mature Cheddar — 60 g, grated
- Parmesan — 20 g, grated
- Egg yolk — 1
- Chives — 8 g, sliced
- White pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Poach haddock in milk with bay at 80°C for 6 minutes; flake, reserve 200 ml milk.
2. Make a roux with 20 g butter and flour, 2 minutes; whisk in reserved milk gradually. Simmer 4 minutes to a thick sauce.
3. Off heat, beat in cream, half the Cheddar, and the extra yolk. Fold in flaked haddock. Season with white pepper only.
4. Preheat grill to maximum.
5. Beat 6 eggs lightly with a pinch of salt. Heat 30 g butter in a 24 cm ovenproof pan until foaming; add eggs.
6. Stir with a spatula over medium heat until softly scrambled underneath but still wet on top, about 90 seconds. Stop stirring; cook 30 seconds to set the base.
7. Spoon the haddock sauce over the surface, scatter remaining Cheddar and Parmesan.
8. Grill 2–3 minutes until glazed, golden and souffléd at the edges.

## Plating & Service
Serve straight from the pan at the table with chives scattered over — the centre should tremble.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: rice-flour roux. Swap haddock for hot-smoked trout; add a pinch of cayenne to the glaze for bite.
