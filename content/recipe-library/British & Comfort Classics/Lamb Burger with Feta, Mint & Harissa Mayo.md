# Lamb Burger with Feta, Mint & Harissa Mayo

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Lamb shoulder mince loves big Mediterranean seasoning — feta melts into pockets, harissa mayo brings the heat, mint keeps it fresh.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Lamb shoulder mince, 20% fat — 700 g
- Ground cumin — 4 g
- Ground coriander — 3 g
- Garlic — 2 cloves (10 g), grated
- Feta — 120 g, in 1 cm cubes
- Mint — 12 g, chopped (8 g patties, 4 g garnish)
- Fine sea salt — 6 g; black pepper — 2 g
- Mayonnaise — 80 g
- Rose harissa — 25 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Red onion — 100 g, fine rings
- Cucumber — 150 g, ribbons
- Pitta or brioche buns — 4 (320 g)
- Olive oil — 20 ml

## Method
1. Mix lamb with cumin, coriander, garlic, 8 g mint, salt and pepper — light hands, no overworking.
2. Fold in feta cubes; shape 4 patties slightly wider than the buns (they shrink). Dimple the centres. Chill 20 minutes.
3. Whisk harissa and lemon juice into the mayonnaise.
4. Toss onion rings and cucumber ribbons with a pinch of salt; rest 10 minutes, then squeeze lightly.
5. Cook patties in oil over medium-high, 4 minutes per side to 70°C core — lamb wants medium, not rare.
6. Rest patties 3 minutes; toast the buns or pittas.
7. Build: harissa mayo, cucumber, patty, onion, remaining mint.

## Plating & Service
Serve with lemon-dressed leaves or spiced wedges; extra harissa mayo in a pot for the brave.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> DF: skip feta, add chopped preserved lemon. GF: GF buns or lettuce wraps. Grill over coals when the beach BBQ calls.
