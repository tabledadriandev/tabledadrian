# Sunday Roast Sirloin with Yorkshire Puddings & Red Wine Gravy

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The centrepiece Sunday: rosy sirloin rested properly, sky-high Yorkshires from a rested batter, and a gravy built on the roasting tray.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Sirloin of beef, boned and rolled — 1.5 kg
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- English mustard — 15 g
- Plain flour — 140 g (for Yorkshires)
- Free-range eggs — 4
- Whole milk — 200 ml
- Beef dripping — 60 g
- Shallots — 100 g, halved
- Carrot — 100 g, chunked
- Celery — 50 g, chunked
- Red wine — 300 ml
- Beef stock — 500 ml
- Redcurrant jelly — 15 g
- Fine sea salt — 10 g; black pepper — 3 g

## Method
1. Yorkshire batter first: whisk flour, eggs, milk and 2 g salt until smooth; rest at room temperature at least 1 hour.
2. Bring beef to room temperature 1 hour; rub with oil, salt and pepper. Heat oven to 240°C.
3. Sear the joint hard in a roasting tray on the hob, all sides, 6–8 minutes total. Brush with mustard; bed on shallots, carrot and celery.
4. Roast 15 minutes at 240°C, then 55–65 minutes at 160°C to a core of 52°C for medium-rare. Rest under loose foil 25 minutes (carryover to ~56°C).
5. Raise oven to 220°C. Divide dripping between a 12-hole tin; heat 10 minutes until smoking. Pour in batter and bake 20–22 minutes without opening the door.
6. Gravy: set the roasting tray over heat, deglaze with wine, reduce by half, add stock and jelly; simmer 10 minutes, strain, season, and mount with any resting juices.
7. Carve the beef 5 mm thick against the grain.

## Plating & Service
Family-style: sliced beef on a warm platter, Yorkshires piled high, gravy in a boat — send duck-fat potatoes and greens alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: cornflour-thickened gravy and skip Yorkshires (or use a GF blend). Cook to 60°C core for medium if guests insist.
