# Bourbon-Treacle Glazed Beef Short Ribs

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Four-hour ribs lacquered in bourbon and black treacle — American low-and-slow with a British molasses accent; forks only.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Beef short ribs, bone in — 2 kg (4 large ribs)
- Fine sea salt — 10 g; black pepper — 3 g
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Onions — 250 g, chunky
- Carrots — 200 g, chunky
- Celery — 60 g
- Garlic — 6 cloves (30 g)
- Tomato purée — 40 g
- Bourbon — 120 ml (80 ml braise, 40 ml glaze)
- Beef stock — 800 ml
- Black treacle — 50 g
- Cider vinegar — 25 ml
- Dijon mustard — 10 g
- Bay — 2; thyme — 4 sprigs

## Method
1. Season ribs; sear hard in oil on all faces in a casserole, 10 minutes. Remove.
2. Colour vegetables 8 minutes; add garlic and tomato purée, 2 minutes. Deglaze with 80 ml bourbon.
3. Return ribs; add stock, bay and thyme. Braise covered at 140°C for 4 hours until sliding from the bone.
4. Lift ribs carefully; strain the liquor, skim, and reduce by two-thirds.
5. Glaze: whisk treacle, 40 ml bourbon, vinegar and mustard into 200 ml of the reduction; simmer to a lacquer.
6. Brush ribs; blast at 220°C for 10 minutes, re-glazing twice, until sticky and burnished.

## Plating & Service
One rib per plate, bone on, over white bean mash or grits; the remaining reduction as gravy, pickled red onions to cut.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Alcohol-free: strong black tea plus 10 ml vinegar replaces bourbon. Make a day ahead; chill in the liquor and de-fat cold.
