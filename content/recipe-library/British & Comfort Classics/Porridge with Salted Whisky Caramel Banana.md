# Porridge with Salted Whisky Caramel Banana

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Scottish comfort with a nightcap — creamy pinhead-oat porridge topped with bananas rolled in a quick whisky caramel.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Pinhead (steel-cut) oatmeal — 160 g
- Whole milk — 500 ml
- Water — 500 ml
- Fine sea salt — 3 g
- Caster sugar — 100 g
- Unsalted butter — 40 g
- Double cream — 80 ml
- Whisky — 30 ml
- Bananas — 3, thick-sliced (330 g)
- Flaky sea salt — 2 g
- Toasted oat flakes — 20 g, for crunch

## Method
1. Toast pinhead oats in a dry saucepan 2 minutes until nutty.
2. Add milk, water and fine salt; bring to a simmer, then cook gently 18–20 minutes, stirring often, until creamy but with bite.
3. Meanwhile, melt sugar in a wide pan over medium heat to a deep amber caramel, about 6 minutes — do not stir, just swirl.
4. Off heat, whisk in butter, then cream (it will bubble), then whisky. Return to low heat 1 minute until smooth.
5. Add banana slices to the warm caramel; turn gently to coat, 60 seconds.
6. Loosen porridge with a splash of milk if needed; it should settle softly, not stand in peaks.

## Plating & Service
Ladle porridge into warm bowls, spoon over bananas and caramel, finish with flaky salt and toasted oats.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan/DF: oat milk, vegan butter, coconut cream. Skip the whisky for guests who prefer — a drop of vanilla works.
