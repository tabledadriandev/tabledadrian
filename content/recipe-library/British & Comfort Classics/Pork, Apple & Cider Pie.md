# Pork, Apple & Cider Pie

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> West Country in a dish — pork shoulder braised in dry cider with sage, sweet apple folded in at the end so it keeps its shape under the lid.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Pork shoulder — 1.2 kg, 4 cm dice
- Plain flour — 40 g
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Onions — 250 g, diced
- Celery — 60 g, diced
- Dry cider — 450 ml
- Chicken stock — 300 ml
- Wholegrain mustard — 15 g
- Sage — 6 g, chopped; bay — 2
- Eating apples — 2 (300 g), thick wedges
- Unsalted butter — 20 g
- Double cream — 80 ml
- All-butter puff pastry — 375 g
- Egg — 1, beaten
- Fine sea salt — 9 g; black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Toss pork in seasoned flour; brown in oil in batches in a casserole.
2. Soften onions and celery 8 minutes; return pork with sage and bay.
3. Add cider and stock; braise covered at 150°C for 2 hours until tender.
4. Caramelise apple wedges in butter 3 minutes per side; set aside.
5. Reduce the braise liquor to coat; stir in mustard and cream, simmer 3 minutes, season. Fold in apples; cool completely.
6. Fill a 1.5 L pie dish. Roll pastry to 4 mm, egg-wash the rim, lid and crimp; slit and egg-wash the top.
7. Bake at 200°C for 30–35 minutes until puffed and deep gold.

## Plating & Service
Crack the lid at the table; serve with buttered greens and colcannon for full West Country effect.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 100 g black pudding chunks with the apples for depth. DF: skip cream, mount with 20 ml cider vinegar and extra stock.
