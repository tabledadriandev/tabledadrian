# Treacle Tart with Clotted Cream

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Golden syrup cut with lemon and brown crumbs in a crisp shell — humble ingredients, precision execution, national treasure status.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Plain flour — 220 g
- Unsalted butter — 130 g, cold, diced
- Icing sugar — 40 g
- Egg yolk — 1, plus 1 whole egg for the filling
- Cold water — 25 ml
- Golden syrup — 450 g
- Fresh brown breadcrumbs — 130 g
- Double cream — 60 ml
- Lemons — 2, zest and 40 ml juice
- Ground ginger — 2 g
- Fine sea salt — 2 g
- Clotted cream — 200 g, to serve

## Method
1. Pastry: rub flour, butter, icing sugar and salt to crumbs; bind with yolk and water. Chill 30 minutes.
2. Roll to 3 mm; line a 23 cm tart ring, leaving overhang. Chill 20 minutes.
3. Blind-bake at 180°C with beans 18 minutes; remove beans, 6 minutes more until sandy. Trim the overhang.
4. Warm golden syrup to pouring (40°C); stir in breadcrumbs, cream, lemon zest and juice, ginger and the beaten whole egg.
5. Rest 10 minutes so the crumbs swell; pour into the case.
6. Bake at 160°C for 30–35 minutes until just set with a faint centre-wobble.
7. Cool at least 45 minutes — it slices warm, not hot.

## Plating & Service
Slim wedges (it is rich beyond appearances) with a defiant spoonful of clotted cream melting alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Lattice top for the traditionalists. Extra 10 ml lemon juice keeps the sweetness honest. Serves cold from the fridge for midnight raids.
