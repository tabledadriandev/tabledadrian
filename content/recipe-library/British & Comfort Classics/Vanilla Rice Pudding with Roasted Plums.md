# Vanilla Rice Pudding with Roasted Plums

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Oven-baked until a freckled skin forms — dividing the table into skin-fighters and skin-avoiders — with burgundy roasted plums bleeding into the cream.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Pudding rice — 100 g
- Whole milk — 700 ml
- Double cream — 150 ml
- Caster sugar — 60 g, plus 40 g for plums
- Vanilla pod — 1, split and scraped
- Unsalted butter — 20 g
- Freshly grated nutmeg — 0.5 g
- Fine sea salt — 1 g
- Ripe plums — 6 (about 500 g), halved, stoned
- Star anise — 1
- Orange — 1, juice (about 80 ml) and zest

## Method
1. Heat oven to 150°C. Butter a 1.5 L baking dish.
2. Stir rice, milk, cream, 60 g sugar, vanilla (pod and seeds) and salt in the dish; dot with butter, dust with nutmeg.
3. Bake 90–100 minutes, stirring once at 30 minutes then leaving alone so the skin builds — the rice should be tender in a thick, wobbling cream.
4. Plums: toss with 40 g sugar, star anise, orange juice and zest in a snug tray; roast at 190°C (after the pudding, or alongside at the top) 18–20 minutes until slumped and syrupy.
5. Rest the pudding 10 minutes; fish out the vanilla pod.
6. Loosen with a splash of warm milk if it has tightened.

## Plating & Service
A ladle of pudding, skin fairly distributed, three plum halves over and their spiced syrup drizzled to bleed crimson into the white.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Hob version: 35 minutes gentle stirring — creamier, skinless. Vegan: oat milk and coconut cream; roast the plums in maple.
