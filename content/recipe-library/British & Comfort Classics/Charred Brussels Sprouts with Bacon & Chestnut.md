# Charred Brussels Sprouts with Bacon & Chestnut

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sprouts converted by fire — halved, seared cut-face down until black-edged, tossed with bacon fat, chestnuts and a balsamic hit.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Brussels sprouts — 600 g, trimmed, halved
- Smoked bacon lardons — 120 g
- Olive oil — 25 ml
- Cooked chestnuts — 120 g, halved
- Garlic — 2 cloves (10 g), sliced
- Balsamic vinegar — 15 ml
- Honey — 8 g
- Fine sea salt — 4 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Render lardons in a wide pan over medium heat, 5 minutes, until crisp; lift out, keep the fat.
2. Add oil; heat until shimmering. Lay sprouts cut-side down in one layer.
3. Sear 4–5 minutes untouched until deeply charred; toss and cook 3 minutes more until just tender.
4. Add garlic and chestnuts; toss 90 seconds.
5. Return bacon; deglaze with balsamic and honey, coating everything, 60 seconds.
6. Season — go easy on salt; the bacon votes early.

## Plating & Service
Hot bowl, charred faces up, pan syrup scraped over — the sprout dish that gets requested in July.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: skip bacon, use 20 g smoked almonds and 5 ml extra balsamic — then it's also nut-heavy, so flag allergens. Chilli flakes welcome.
