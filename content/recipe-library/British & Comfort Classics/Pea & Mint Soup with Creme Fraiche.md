# Pea & Mint Soup with Creme Fraiche

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Five-minute simmer, violently green — the trick is blending the peas barely cooked and chilling the base fast if serving cold.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Frozen petits pois — 600 g
- Unsalted butter — 30 g
- Spring onions — 80 g, sliced
- Garlic — 1 clove (5 g)
- Vegetable stock — 750 ml, hot
- Mint leaves — 12 g
- Baby spinach — 40 g
- Crème fraîche — 80 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Fine sea salt — 5 g
- White pepper — 1 g
- Pea shoots — 20 g, to garnish

## Method
1. Sweat spring onions and garlic in butter over medium heat 3 minutes without colour.
2. Add hot stock and bring to a rolling boil.
3. Tip in the peas; return to the boil and cook only 3 minutes.
4. Add mint and spinach; immediately transfer to a blender.
5. Blend on full power 90 seconds; pass through a fine sieve, pressing well.
6. Season with salt, white pepper and lemon juice. Reheat to 80°C max, or chill over ice for cold service.

## Plating & Service
Warm (or chilled) bowls, a spoonful of crème fraîche dragged through the surface, pea shoots and a crack of pepper.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: olive oil and coconut yoghurt. Add ham hock shreds or crispy bacon for the classic pea-and-ham register.
