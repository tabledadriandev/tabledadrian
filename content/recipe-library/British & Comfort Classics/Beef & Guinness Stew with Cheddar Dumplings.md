# Beef & Guinness Stew with Cheddar Dumplings

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Black stout, brown beef, golden dumplings — the dumplings steam in the stew's last half hour and soak up everything worth having.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Beef shin or chuck — 1.3 kg, 4 cm chunks
- Plain flour — 40 g, plus 200 g dumplings
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Onions — 300 g, chunky
- Carrots — 300 g, thick coins
- Celery — 60 g, sliced
- Tomato purée — 30 g
- Guinness or dry stout — 500 ml
- Beef stock — 500 ml
- Dark brown sugar — 15 g
- Thyme — 4 sprigs; bay — 2
- Beef suet — 100 g
- Mature Cheddar — 80 g, grated
- Baking powder — 8 g; cold water — 110 ml
- Chives — 8 g; fine sea salt — 11 g

## Method
1. Toss beef in 40 g seasoned flour; brown hard in oil in batches; remove.
2. Colour onions, carrots and celery 8 minutes; add tomato purée, 1 minute.
3. Return beef; add stout, stock, sugar, thyme, bay. Braise covered at 150°C for 2–2.5 hours until tender.
4. Dumplings: rub 200 g flour, suet, baking powder, 3 g salt; toss in Cheddar and chives; bind with cold water to a soft dough. Shape 12 balls.
5. Check the stew's seasoning and consistency — loosen with stock if tight; the dumplings will drink.
6. Sit dumplings on the stew; cover and bake at 180°C for 20 minutes, then uncover 10 minutes to gild their tops.

## Plating & Service
Ladle into deep bowls, two dumplings apiece, thyme over — no sides needed beyond a spoon and bread for the shameless.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 200 g button mushrooms with the vegetables. No stout? Porter or brown ale, plus 5 ml balsamic at the end.
