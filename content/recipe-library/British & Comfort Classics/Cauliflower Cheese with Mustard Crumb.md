# Cauliflower Cheese with Mustard Crumb

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The trimming that fights the beef for attention — barely-blanched florets, a proper mature-Cheddar mornay, and a mustardy crumb for crunch.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Cauliflower — 1.2 kg, in large florets, leaves kept
- Whole milk — 600 ml
- Unsalted butter — 60 g (50 g roux, 10 g crumb)
- Plain flour — 50 g
- Mature Cheddar — 180 g, grated
- Gruyère — 60 g, grated
- English mustard — 12 g
- Freshly grated nutmeg — 0.5 g
- Panko breadcrumbs — 50 g
- Wholegrain mustard — 10 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g; white pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Blanch florets (and sliced leaves) in salted boiling water 3 minutes only; drain hard and steam-dry — waterlogged cauliflower ruins the sauce.
2. Roux: cook 50 g butter and flour 2 minutes; whisk in milk gradually. Simmer 4 minutes.
3. Off heat, melt in Cheddar and Gruyère; season with English mustard, nutmeg, salt and white pepper.
4. Fold florets through the mornay in a 2 L gratin dish.
5. Crumb: rub panko with 10 g melted butter and wholegrain mustard; scatter over.
6. Bake at 200°C for 25–30 minutes until blistered, bronzed and bubbling at the rim. Rest 5 minutes.

## Plating & Service
To the table in the dish — the corners with the caught, chewy sauce are currency; distribute them fairly.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add leeks or smoked bacon between layers. GF: cornflour roux and GF crumbs. Half broccoli makes it a two-tone gratin.
