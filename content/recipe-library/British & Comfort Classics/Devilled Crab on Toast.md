# Devilled Crab on Toast

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Brown crab meat gives the fire and depth, white meat the sweetness — grilled onto toast until blistered, done in ten minutes.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- White crab meat — 200 g
- Brown crab meat — 100 g
- Mayonnaise — 40 g
- English mustard — 8 g
- Worcestershire-style sauce — 8 ml
- Cayenne pepper — 1.5 g
- Spring onions — 30 g, finely sliced
- Lemon — 1, zest and 15 ml juice
- Unsalted butter — 30 g, softened
- Sourdough — 4 slices (200 g)
- Flat-leaf parsley — 8 g, chopped
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Preheat the grill to high.
2. Beat brown crab meat with mayonnaise, mustard, Worcestershire sauce, cayenne, lemon zest and juice.
3. Fold in white meat and spring onions gently, keeping the flakes intact. Check seasoning — it should bite back.
4. Toast the sourdough on one side under the grill; butter the untoasted side.
5. Pile the crab mixture onto the buttered side, right to the edges.
6. Grill 3–4 minutes until the top blisters and browns in spots.

## Plating & Service
Halve on the bias, stack casually, parsley and black pepper over, lemon on the side. Cold Chablis is non-negotiable.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: use GF bread. Lighter: crème fraîche instead of mayo. Add a pinch of curry powder for a nod to devilled tradition.
