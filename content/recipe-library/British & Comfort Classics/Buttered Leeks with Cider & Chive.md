# Buttered Leeks with Cider & Chive

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Leeks braised slowly in butter and a splash of dry cider collapse into silk — the quiet side that makes fish and chicken look better.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Leeks — 800 g, white and pale green, in 2 cm coins
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Dry cider — 80 ml
- Thyme — 2 sprigs
- Fine sea salt — 4 g
- White pepper — 1 g
- Chives — 8 g, sliced
- Crème fraîche — 40 g (optional)

## Method
1. Wash leek coins thoroughly in cold water; drain but leave damp.
2. Melt butter in a wide pan over low heat; add leeks, thyme and salt.
3. Cover with a paper cartouche and lid; sweat 12–14 minutes, stirring twice, until completely tender — zero colour.
4. Remove lid; add cider and raise heat, simmering 3–4 minutes until the liquid turns glossy.
5. Season with white pepper; fold in crème fraîche if using.
6. Finish with chives off the heat.

## Plating & Service
Spooned alongside roast fish, chicken or into a Sunday spread — the buttery liquor is sauce; do not drain it.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: olive oil and no crème fraîche. Add wholegrain mustard for gammon service; grated Cheddar and a grill turns them into a gratin.
