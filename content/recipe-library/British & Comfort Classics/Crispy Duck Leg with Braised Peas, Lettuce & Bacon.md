# Crispy Duck Leg with Braised Peas, Lettuce & Bacon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Slow-roasted duck legs over petits pois à la française — the bistro plate that makes a Tuesday feel like the Côte d'Azur.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Duck legs — 4 (about 1.1 kg)
- Fine sea salt — 8 g; black pepper — 2 g
- Thyme — 4 sprigs; garlic — 4 cloves (20 g), smashed
- Smoked bacon lardons — 120 g
- Baby gem lettuce — 2 heads (200 g), quartered
- Frozen petits pois — 400 g
- Pearl onions or small shallots — 150 g, peeled
- Chicken stock — 250 ml
- Unsalted butter — 30 g
- Cornflour — 5 g, slaked (optional)
- Mint — 6 g, torn

## Method
1. Salt duck legs 1 hour ahead (or overnight); pat dry, pepper, and lay skin-up in a snug tray with thyme and garlic.
2. Roast at 150°C for 2 hours until the fat has rendered and the meat gives; pour off (and keep) the fat as it collects.
3. Raise to 220°C for 12–15 minutes until the skin is glass-crisp. Rest 5 minutes.
4. Meanwhile render lardons in a wide pan 4 minutes; brown pearl onions in the fat 5 minutes.
5. Add stock; simmer 8 minutes until the onions are tender. Add peas and gem quarters; braise 3 minutes.
6. Finish with butter (and slaked cornflour if you want cling); season, add mint off the heat.

## Plating & Service
A ladle of braised peas and lettuce in each bowl, the duck leg riding on top, skin never touching the liquid.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The rendered fat is tomorrow's roast potatoes — label and keep. Confit route: submerge legs in duck fat at 130°C for 2.5 hours instead.
