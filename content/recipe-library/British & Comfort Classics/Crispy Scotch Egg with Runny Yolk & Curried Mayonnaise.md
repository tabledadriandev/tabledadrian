# Crispy Scotch Egg with Runny Yolk & Curried Mayonnaise

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The gastropub benchmark — molten centre, herbed sausage jacket, shattering crumb, and a curry mayo nodding to the picnic egg's spice heritage.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Free-range eggs — 4, plus 1 for pané
- Sausage meat — 400 g
- Sage — 4 g, chopped
- Thyme leaves — 2 g
- English mustard — 8 g
- Plain flour — 60 g
- Panko breadcrumbs — 120 g
- Mayonnaise — 120 g
- Mild curry powder — 6 g, toasted
- Lime juice — 10 ml
- Mango chutney — 20 g
- Neutral oil — 1.5 L, for frying
- Fine sea salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Boil 4 eggs exactly 5 minutes 30 seconds from boiling water; refresh in iced water 10 minutes, peel carefully.
2. Mix sausage meat with sage, thyme, mustard and 3 g salt; divide into 4 x 100 g portions.
3. Flatten each portion between cling film to a 12 cm disc; wrap around an egg, sealing seamlessly. Chill 20 minutes.
4. Whisk curry powder, lime juice and chutney into the mayonnaise; chill.
5. Pané: roll each ball in flour, beaten egg, then panko, pressing crumbs on firmly.
6. Heat oil to 160°C. Fry eggs 7–8 minutes, turning, until deep golden and the sausage reads 70°C just under the crumb.
7. Drain on a rack; salt immediately. Rest 2 minutes.

## Plating & Service
Halve with a sharp knife to reveal the flowing yolk; plate cut-side up over a swipe of curry mayo with dressed watercress.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Black pudding version: replace 100 g sausage with crumbled black pudding. Bake option: 190°C for 25 minutes (crumb less shattering).
