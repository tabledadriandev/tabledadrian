# Portobello & Halloumi Burger with Chimichurri

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Meaty roast portobellos stacked with squeaky golden halloumi and a sharp green chimichurri — the vegetarian burger nobody pities.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Portobello mushrooms — 8 large (500 g), stalks removed
- Halloumi — 250 g, in 8 slices
- Olive oil — 70 ml (30 ml mushrooms, 15 ml halloumi, 25 ml chimichurri)
- Garlic — 3 cloves (15 g); 2 grated for mushrooms, 1 minced for sauce
- Flat-leaf parsley — 25 g, finely chopped
- Oregano — 3 g, dried
- Red wine vinegar — 20 ml
- Chilli flakes — 1 g
- Seeded burger buns — 4 (340 g)
- Roasted red peppers (jarred) — 150 g, drained
- Rocket — 60 g
- Fine sea salt — 5 g; black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Chimichurri: stir parsley, minced garlic, oregano, vinegar, chilli flakes, 25 ml oil and 1 g salt; rest 15 minutes.
2. Heat oven to 210°C. Rub mushrooms with 30 ml oil, grated garlic, salt and pepper; roast gill-side up on a rack 12–14 minutes until juicy and collapsed. Drain briefly.
3. Pat halloumi dry; fry in 15 ml oil over medium-high, 90 seconds per side, until deep gold.
4. Toast the buns cut-side down in the halloumi pan.
5. Build: chimichurri on the base, rocket, two mushrooms, two halloumi slices, red peppers, more chimichurri, lid.
6. Press gently and skewer.

## Plating & Service
Serve with sweet potato fries and the remaining chimichurri — it improves everything it touches.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: swap halloumi for thick-cut smoked tofu, pressed and seared. GF: GF buns. Add a fried egg for brunch mode.
