# Minted Crushed Peas with Lemon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The five-minute side that belongs beside everything fried — barely cooked peas, crushed rough, singing with mint and lemon.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Frozen petits pois — 500 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 40 ml
- Mint leaves — 10 g, chopped
- Lemon — 1, zest and 20 ml juice
- Spring onion — 30 g, finely sliced
- Fine sea salt — 4 g
- Black pepper — 1 g
- Pinch of caster sugar — 2 g (only if the peas are dull)

## Method
1. Boil a large pan of salted water.
2. Cook peas 2–3 minutes only — they must stay bright and pop-sweet.
3. Drain thoroughly; return to the warm pan off the heat.
4. Crush with a masher or fork to a rough, chunky texture — half whole, half broken.
5. Fold in olive oil, mint, lemon zest and juice, spring onion, salt and pepper.
6. Taste: it should be vivid, sharp and barely warm-sweet; adjust with sugar only if needed.

## Plating & Service
A generous spoonful beside fish and chips, scampi or lamb — never smooth, never grey.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fold in crème fraîche for a richer version. Chilli flakes and feta turn it into a bruschetta topping for the crew.
