# Potted Brown Shrimp with Mace Butter & Toasted Crumpet

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Morecambe Bay in a ramekin — sweet brown shrimp sealed under spiced butter, made hours ahead and unmoulded at anchor.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Brown shrimp, peeled and cooked — 300 g
- Unsalted butter — 150 g
- Ground mace — 2 g
- Cayenne pepper — 1 g
- Freshly grated nutmeg — 0.5 g
- Lemon — 1, zest and 20 ml juice
- Anchovy fillet — 1 (4 g), mashed
- Crumpets — 4
- Watercress — 40 g
- Cornichons — 40 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Melt butter gently with mace, cayenne, nutmeg and anchovy; infuse 5 minutes at 60°C without boiling.
2. Stir in lemon zest and juice; fold in the shrimp off the heat, warming through 1 minute only.
3. Pack shrimp into four 100 ml ramekins with a slotted spoon, pressing lightly.
4. Pour the spiced butter over to cover completely; chill at least 2 hours until set.
5. Remove from the fridge 20 minutes before service — the butter should yield, not crack.
6. Toast crumpets until deep gold and crisp at the edges.

## Plating & Service
Ramekin on the plate, hot crumpet alongside, watercress and cornichons to cut the richness. Guests spread the shrimp and butter themselves.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No brown shrimp? Use chopped cooked North Atlantic prawns. GF: serve with toasted GF bread. Keeps 3 days sealed under butter.
