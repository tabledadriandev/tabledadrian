# Steak & Ale Pie with Suet Crust

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Chuck steak braised in dark ale until it collapses, under a suet lid that bakes into something between pastry and dumpling.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Beef chuck — 1.2 kg, in 4 cm chunks
- Plain flour — 40 g, plus 300 g for the crust
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Onions — 300 g, chunky dice
- Carrots — 200 g, thick coins
- Celery — 60 g, sliced
- Tomato purée — 30 g
- Dark ale — 500 ml
- Beef stock — 400 ml
- Worcestershire-style sauce — 15 ml
- Thyme — 4 sprigs; bay — 2
- Beef suet — 150 g
- Baking powder — 8 g
- Cold water — 150 ml
- Egg — 1, beaten; fine sea salt — 10 g

## Method
1. Toss beef in 40 g seasoned flour. Brown hard in oil in batches in a casserole; remove.
2. Soften onions, carrots and celery 8 minutes; add tomato purée for 1 minute.
3. Return beef; add ale, stock, Worcestershire sauce, thyme and bay. Cover; braise at 150°C for 2.5 hours until the beef yields completely.
4. Reduce the liquor on the hob if loose — it should coat a spoon. Season; cool (ideally overnight).
5. Crust: rub 300 g flour, suet, baking powder and 4 g salt; bind with cold water to a soft dough. Rest 15 minutes.
6. Fill a 1.5 L pie dish with the cold braise (a pie funnel helps). Roll crust to 8 mm; crimp onto the egg-washed rim. Cut a steam slit; egg-wash all over.
7. Bake at 190°C for 40–45 minutes until burnished and bubbling at the slit.

## Plating & Service
Bring the whole pie to the table and break the crust in front of guests; mash and buttered peas alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 150 g sautéed mushrooms or 100 g smoked bacon to the braise. Stout deepens it; porter sweetens it.
