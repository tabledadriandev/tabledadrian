# Salmon en Croute with Watercress Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A whole side wrapped in golden pastry over lemon-herb butter — the carving moment is pure charter theatre, and the peppery sauce keeps it fresh.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Salmon side, skinned, pin-boned — 900 g, halved crossways
- Unsalted butter — 100 g, softened
- Dill — 10 g; tarragon — 6 g, chopped
- Lemon — 1, zested
- Dijon mustard — 12 g
- Baby spinach — 200 g, wilted and squeezed bone-dry
- All-butter puff pastry — 500 g
- Egg yolks — 2, beaten
- Watercress — 150 g
- Crème fraîche — 200 g
- Fish or vegetable stock — 100 ml
- Fine sea salt — 7 g; black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Beat butter with dill, tarragon, lemon zest, mustard, 3 g salt and pepper.
2. Sandwich: spread herb butter over one salmon half, layer the dry spinach, top with the second half (thick end over thin for an even block). Chill 20 minutes.
3. Roll pastry to 3 mm in two sheets. Lay salmon on the smaller sheet, egg-wash the border, cap with the larger sheet and press out air. Trim to a 2 cm border; crimp with a fork.
4. Chill 30 minutes. Heat oven to 210°C with a heavy tray inside.
5. Egg-wash twice; score a herringbone pattern lightly.
6. Bake on the hot tray 28–32 minutes until deep gold; core should read 50°C. Rest 8 minutes.
7. Sauce: blanch watercress 30 seconds, refresh; blend with crème fraîche and warm stock until vivid green. Warm gently to 70°C; season.

## Plating & Service
Carve 3 cm slices at the table to show the pink-green-gold strata; sauce flooded alongside, buttered new potatoes to share.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap spinach for a mushroom duxelles for deeper flavour. Assemble in the morning, bake to order — pastry stays crisp.
