# Molten Chocolate Fondant with Sea Salt

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The dessert that made a thousand kitchen nightmares — timed to the minute so the centre pours; a flake of salt makes the chocolate louder.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Dark chocolate, 70% — 150 g, chopped
- Unsalted butter — 150 g, plus 20 g for moulds
- Cocoa powder — 20 g, for moulds
- Free-range eggs — 3, plus 2 yolks
- Caster sugar — 110 g
- Plain flour — 50 g
- Flaky sea salt — 2 g
- Vanilla ice cream — 4 scoops, to serve

## Method
1. Butter four 180 ml pudding moulds twice (chill between coats); dust thoroughly with cocoa.
2. Melt chocolate and 150 g butter over a bain-marie to 45°C; cool 5 minutes.
3. Whisk eggs, yolks and sugar 3–4 minutes until pale and doubled.
4. Fold the chocolate into the eggs, then sift in flour and fold until just combined.
5. Fill moulds to 1 cm below the rim; chill at least 1 hour (bake from cold — this is the safety margin). They hold 24 h.
6. Bake at 200°C for 12–14 minutes: risen, edges set, centre with a soft dime of wobble.
7. Rest 60 seconds; run a thin knife around and invert with conviction.

## Plating & Service
Fondant centre-plate, flaky salt on the crown, ice cream alongside — cut it open at the table or lose the moment.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: swap flour for cornflour. Bury a square of caramel chocolate in each for a double eruption. Test-bake one — ovens lie.
