# Shepherd's Pie with Braised Lamb Shoulder & Champ Topping

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Forget mince — slow-braised, hand-shredded lamb shoulder under a spring-onion champ crust is the shepherd's pie at its ceiling.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Lamb shoulder, boned — 1.2 kg, in 5 cm chunks
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Onions — 250 g, diced
- Carrots — 250 g, small dice
- Celery — 60 g, diced
- Tomato purée — 40 g
- Red wine — 250 ml
- Lamb or chicken stock — 500 ml
- Rosemary — 2 sprigs; thyme — 4 sprigs
- Worcestershire-style sauce — 15 ml (GF)
- Floury potatoes — 1.4 kg, peeled
- Spring onions — 80 g, sliced
- Whole milk — 120 ml; unsalted butter — 90 g
- Fine sea salt — 10 g; black pepper — 3 g

## Method
1. Brown lamb hard in oil in a casserole, in batches; remove.
2. Soften onions, carrots and celery 8 minutes; add tomato purée for 1 minute.
3. Return lamb; add wine, stock, herbs and Worcestershire sauce. Braise, covered, at 150°C for 2.5–3 hours until shreddable.
4. Shred the lamb into its liquor, discarding herb stems; reduce on the hob to a rich, spoon-coating gravy. Season.
5. Champ: boil potatoes 20 minutes; rice. Warm milk with spring onions 3 minutes; beat into potatoes with 70 g butter. Season.
6. Fill a 2 L dish with lamb; top with champ, furrowed with a fork, and dot with remaining 20 g butter.
7. Bake at 200°C for 25–30 minutes until bronzed and bubbling. Rest 10 minutes.

## Plating & Service
To the table in its dish with minted peas or buttered kale; the furrows catch and crisp — leave them ragged.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Beef shin makes it a cottage pie. DF: olive oil mash. Fold 50 g grated Cheddar into the champ for a gilded crust.
