# Welsh Rarebit Croquettes with Worcestershire Glaze

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Pub-food reinvention: the rarebit becomes a molten-centred croquette, the Worcestershire becomes a sticky reduction for dipping.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Whole milk — 300 ml
- Brown ale — 60 ml
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Plain flour — 80 g, plus 50 g for pané
- Mature Cheddar — 200 g, grated
- English mustard — 10 g
- Egg yolk — 1, plus 2 whole eggs for pané
- Panko breadcrumbs — 140 g
- Vegetarian Worcestershire-style sauce — 60 ml
- Dark brown sugar — 20 g
- Cider vinegar — 15 ml
- Neutral oil — 1.5 L, for frying
- Chives — 6 g
- Fine sea salt — 4 g

## Method
1. Make a stiff béchamel: cook butter and 80 g flour 2 minutes, whisk in milk and ale gradually; simmer 4 minutes until very thick.
2. Off heat, beat in Cheddar, mustard, yolk and salt. Spread 2 cm deep in a lined tray; chill 2 hours until firm.
3. Cut into 12 bars; roll each in flour, beaten egg, then panko. Double-crumb (egg and panko again) for a bombproof shell. Chill 20 minutes.
4. Reduce Worcestershire sauce, sugar and vinegar over low heat 4–5 minutes to a light glaze; it thickens as it cools.
5. Heat oil to 175°C; fry croquettes in two batches 3–4 minutes until deep amber.
6. Drain on a rack; rest 90 seconds — the centres are lava.

## Plating & Service
Three per plate over a streak of the glaze, chives scattered; serve with more glaze for dipping.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 40 g chopped leek sweated in butter to the béchamel. Bake-off option: 200°C for 15 minutes on a preheated tray.
