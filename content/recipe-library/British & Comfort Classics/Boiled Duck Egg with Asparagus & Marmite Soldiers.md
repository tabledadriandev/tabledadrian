# Boiled Duck Egg with Asparagus & Marmite Soldiers

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Nursery breakfast, charter edition — a big jammy duck egg with two kinds of soldiers: buttery Marmite toast and blanched asparagus spears.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Duck eggs — 4
- Asparagus spears — 300 g, trimmed
- White bloomer or sourdough — 4 slices (200 g)
- Unsalted butter — 60 g, softened
- Marmite — 10 g
- Flaky sea salt — 3 g
- Black pepper — 1 g
- Chives — 5 g, finely sliced
- Lemon — half, for the asparagus (15 ml juice)

## Method
1. Bring a pan of water to a rolling boil. Lower in duck eggs; cook exactly 7 minutes for a set white and molten yolk.
2. Meanwhile, blanch asparagus in salted boiling water 2 minutes; drain, toss with a little butter and lemon juice.
3. Beat remaining butter with Marmite until smooth.
4. Toast the bread until golden; spread with Marmite butter and cut into 2 cm soldiers.
5. Sit each egg in a cup, tap and slice off the top.
6. Season the exposed yolk with flaky salt, pepper and chives.

## Plating & Service
Egg in a cup centre-plate, toast soldiers stacked on one side, asparagus spears on the other — everything gets dipped.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Hen eggs: 6 minutes. GF: gluten-free toast. Marmite-free: anchovy butter or plain salted butter both work beautifully.
