# Rhubarb & Ginger Oat Crumble

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rhubarb and stem ginger under a gingery oat lid — sharper and racier than the apple original, and the pink juices stain the crust lace-red.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Rhubarb — 900 g, in 4 cm batons
- Caster sugar — 110 g
- Stem ginger in syrup — 50 g, chopped, plus 20 ml syrup
- Orange — 1, zest and 40 ml juice
- Cornflour — 12 g
- Plain flour — 160 g
- Jumbo oats — 80 g
- Ground ginger — 4 g
- Demerara sugar — 90 g
- Unsalted butter — 130 g, cold, diced
- Fine sea salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 190°C.
2. Toss rhubarb with caster sugar, stem ginger and its syrup, orange zest, juice and cornflour in a 2 L dish; rest 10 minutes to start the juices.
3. Crumble: rub flour, butter, ground ginger and salt to rubble; toss in oats and demerara, squeezing some clusters.
4. Scatter over the fruit loosely.
5. Bake 35–40 minutes until bronzed and bubbling pink at the corners.
6. Rest 10 minutes before serving.

## Plating & Service
With thick cold cream or warm custard — the cold-hot, sharp-sweet crossfire does the work.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: plant butter; serve with oat cream. Strawberries in the last month of rhubarb season make it a double act.
