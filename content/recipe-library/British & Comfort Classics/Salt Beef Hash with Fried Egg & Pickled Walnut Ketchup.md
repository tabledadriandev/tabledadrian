# Salt Beef Hash with Fried Egg & Pickled Walnut Ketchup

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Deli salt beef crisped into a mustardy potato hash, with a dark pickled-walnut ketchup that out-classes brown sauce.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Cooked salt beef (or corned beef) — 500 g, 2 cm chunks
- Waxy potatoes — 600 g, 2 cm dice
- Onion — 200 g, diced
- Olive oil — 45 ml
- English mustard — 10 g
- Worcestershire-style sauce — 10 ml
- Pickled walnuts — 80 g, plus 40 ml of their vinegar
- Date or tomato ketchup — 40 g
- Free-range eggs — 4
- Flat-leaf parsley — 12 g, chopped
- Spring onions — 30 g, sliced
- Fine sea salt — 4 g; black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Ketchup: blend pickled walnuts with their vinegar and the ketchup until smooth; loosen with water to a drizzling consistency.
2. Parboil potatoes 6 minutes; drain and steam-dry.
3. Fry potatoes in 30 ml oil over medium-high, 10–12 minutes, until crusted gold.
4. Add onion, 5 minutes; then salt beef, pressing the hash down to catch, 5 minutes — turn only twice for maximum crust.
5. Season with mustard, Worcestershire sauce and pepper (salt last — the beef carries it). Fold in parsley.
6. Fry eggs in 15 ml oil, basting, 2–3 minutes.

## Plating & Service
Hash in wide bowls, egg on top, walnut ketchup zigzagged over, spring onions scattered.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No pickled walnuts? Use HP-style brown sauce sharpened with 10 ml malt vinegar (contains gluten). Top with sauerkraut for a Reuben wink.
