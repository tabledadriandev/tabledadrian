# Roast Beetroot with Horseradish Creme Fraiche

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Foil-roasted beets in their skins keep every drop of sweetness — split hot over cold horseradish cream for the temperature clash.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Beetroot, mixed colours if possible — 800 g, scrubbed, similar size
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Thyme — 4 sprigs
- Red wine vinegar — 20 ml
- Crème fraîche — 150 g
- Fresh horseradish — 15 g, grated (or 25 g horseradish cream)
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Flaky sea salt — 5 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Dill — 5 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 200°C. Toss whole beets with 20 ml oil, thyme and fine salt; wrap in a loose foil parcel on a tray.
2. Roast 50–60 minutes until a knife slides through the largest.
3. Stir horseradish and lemon juice into crème fraîche; season lightly. Chill.
4. Rub the skins off the warm beets with a cloth (gloves preserve dignity).
5. Tear or cut into rough wedges; dress hot with remaining oil, vinegar, flaky salt and pepper.

## Plating & Service
Cold cream swiped on the plate, hot dressed beets over, dill and pepper — brilliant beside beef, mackerel or venison.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: whipped tahini-lemon instead of crème fraîche. Golden beets stain less on white uniforms.
