# Smoked Salmon Scrambled Eggs on Potato Farls

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Irish griddle breads doing the work of toast — soft, buttery scrambled eggs and cold-smoked salmon on top.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Floury potatoes — 500 g, peeled
- Plain flour — 100 g, plus dusting
- Baking powder — 3 g
- Unsalted butter — 90 g (30 g farls, 60 g eggs)
- Free-range eggs — 8
- Crème fraîche — 40 g
- Cold-smoked salmon — 200 g
- Chives — 10 g, sliced
- Lemon — 1, zested
- Fine sea salt — 5 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Boil potatoes in salted water 18 minutes until tender; drain, steam-dry, and rice while hot.
2. Work in 30 g butter, flour, baking powder and 3 g salt to a soft dough. Roll 1 cm thick on a floured surface; cut into 8 triangles.
3. Cook farls on a dry, medium-hot griddle 4 minutes per side until golden and puffed. Keep warm in a cloth.
4. Whisk eggs with a pinch of salt — no milk, no cream yet.
5. Melt 60 g butter in a cold saucepan, add eggs, and cook over medium-low, stirring constantly with a spatula.
6. When soft curds form (about 4 minutes), pull off the heat and stir in crème fraîche to stop the cooking.
7. Fold in half the chives; the eggs should be glossy and barely set.

## Plating & Service
Two farls per plate, eggs spooned over, salmon draped in loose ribbons, finished with lemon zest, remaining chives and black pepper.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: make farls with GF flour blend plus 2 g xanthan gum. Lighter: fold salmon through the eggs and skip half the butter.
