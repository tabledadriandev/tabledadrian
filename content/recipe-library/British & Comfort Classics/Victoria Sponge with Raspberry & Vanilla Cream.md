# Victoria Sponge with Raspberry & Vanilla Cream

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Equal weights, room-temperature everything, and restraint with the filling — the queen of sponges rises or falls on discipline.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Unsalted butter — 225 g, soft, plus 10 g for tins
- Caster sugar — 225 g, plus 10 g to finish
- Free-range eggs — 4 (about 225 g), room temperature
- Self-raising flour — 225 g, sifted
- Baking powder — 4 g
- Whole milk — 30 ml
- Vanilla extract — 5 ml
- Fine sea salt — 2 g
- Raspberry jam — 150 g
- Double cream — 250 ml
- Icing sugar — 20 g
- Fresh raspberries — 150 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 180°C; butter and line two 20 cm sandwich tins.
2. Beat butter and caster sugar 4–5 minutes until pale and mousse-like — this is the rise.
3. Add eggs one at a time, beating fully between; add a spoon of the flour if it curdles.
4. Fold in flour, baking powder and salt in two additions; loosen with milk and vanilla to a dropping consistency.
5. Divide evenly (weigh the tins); level gently. Bake 22–25 minutes until springy and shrinking from the sides.
6. Cool 10 minutes in tins, then fully on racks — warm cake murders whipped cream.
7. Whip cream with icing sugar to soft peaks. Spread jam on one sponge, cream over, raspberries pressed in; crown with the second sponge.

## Plating & Service
Caster sugar (never icing) over the top, per the WI rulebook; cut with a serrated knife wiped between slices.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Lemon curd and blueberry variant in summer. GF: GF self-raising blend plus 2 g xanthan — bake 3 minutes longer.
