# Triple-Cooked Chips with Rosemary Salt

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The definitive chip — boiled to the edge of collapse, twice fried, glass without and fondant within; rosemary salt is the only jewellery.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Floury potatoes (Maris Piper or Agria) — 1.2 kg
- Neutral oil — 2.5 L (or beef dripping for meat menus)
- Fine sea salt — 15 g (10 g blanching water)
- Flaky sea salt — 6 g
- Rosemary — 2 sprigs, leaves picked
- Malt vinegar — to the table

## Method
1. Cut potatoes into 2 x 2 cm batons; rinse under cold water until it runs clear.
2. Simmer in salted water from cold, 8–12 minutes, until nearly breaking — cracks on the surface are the goal.
3. Lift out delicately; steam-dry, then freeze on a rack 1 hour (or chill 3 hours).
4. Fry at 130°C for 8–10 minutes until a pale crust sets; drain and return to the rack. (Hold up to a day chilled.)
5. Blitz flaky salt with rosemary leaves to a green-flecked salt.
6. Final fry at 190°C, 4–5 minutes, to deep amber and audibly crisp.
7. Drain 30 seconds; shower with rosemary salt while glistening.

## Plating & Service
Stacked loosely in a warm bowl or on paper, never covered — steam is the enemy of the crust.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Beef dripping adds another dimension (no longer vegan). Thyme or seaweed salt variants both earn their place.
