# Cider Onion Soup with Cheddar Rarebit Crouton

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> French onion soup emigrates to Somerset — onions cooked to mahogany in cider and thyme, capped with a grilled rarebit raft.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Onions — 1 kg, thinly sliced
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Olive oil — 20 ml
- Thyme — 5 sprigs
- Bay leaf — 1
- Dry cider — 300 ml
- Vegetable stock — 900 ml
- Cider brandy or Calvados — 20 ml (optional)
- Baguette — 8 slices (160 g)
- Mature Cheddar — 140 g, grated
- English mustard — 6 g
- Worcestershire-style sauce (vegetarian) — 6 ml
- Egg yolk — 1
- Fine sea salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Cook onions with butter, oil and 3 g salt in a wide heavy pan over medium-low heat, stirring every few minutes, 40–45 minutes until deep mahogany — do not rush.
2. Add thyme and bay; deglaze with cider, scraping the fond, and reduce by half.
3. Add stock; simmer 20 minutes. Finish with cider brandy; season.
4. Beat Cheddar with mustard, Worcestershire sauce and yolk to a paste.
5. Toast baguette slices; spread thickly with the rarebit paste.
6. Ladle soup into ovenproof bowls, float two croutons per bowl, and grill 3–4 minutes until blistered and bronzed.

## Plating & Service
Straight from the grill on underplates with a warning — the bowls are volcanic. Thyme tips over the top.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Meat version: use beef stock and a splash of Madeira. GF: use GF baguette. The soup base freezes perfectly.
