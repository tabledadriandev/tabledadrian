# Venison & Port Pie with Thyme Pastry

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Game-season grandeur — venison shoulder, port and juniper under a thyme-flecked shortcrust; winter charter dinner at its most impressive.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Venison shoulder or haunch — 1.2 kg, 4 cm dice
- Plain flour — 40 g, plus 320 g for pastry
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Smoked bacon lardons — 150 g
- Shallots — 250 g, peeled, halved
- Carrots — 200 g, thick coins
- Juniper berries — 8, crushed
- Port — 250 ml
- Red wine — 200 ml
- Beef stock — 400 ml
- Redcurrant jelly — 25 g
- Thyme — 8 g leaves (3 g braise, 5 g pastry); bay — 2
- Unsalted butter — 160 g, cold, diced
- Egg — 1, plus 1 yolk; cold water — 60 ml
- Fine sea salt — 10 g

## Method
1. Toss venison in 40 g seasoned flour; brown fiercely in oil in batches. Remove.
2. Render lardons; add shallots and carrots, colouring 6 minutes.
3. Return venison with juniper, thyme (3 g), bay; deglaze with port and wine, reduce 5 minutes.
4. Add stock and jelly; braise covered at 150°C for 2.5 hours until tender. Reduce liquor to coat; season and cool completely.
5. Pastry: rub 320 g flour, butter, 5 g thyme and 4 g salt to crumbs; bind with yolk and water. Chill 30 minutes.
6. Line a 22 cm pie tin with two-thirds of the pastry; fill with the cold braise. Egg-wash the rim, lid with remaining pastry, crimp, slit and egg-wash.
7. Bake at 190°C for 45–50 minutes until deep amber. Rest 10 minutes before releasing.

## Plating & Service
Cut proper wedges at the table; serve with braised red cabbage and creamed potatoes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Beef cheek substitutes seamlessly. Add 100 g chestnuts for sweetness. The braise (like all of them) is better made a day ahead.
