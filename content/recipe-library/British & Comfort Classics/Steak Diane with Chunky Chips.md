# Steak Diane with Chunky Chips

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The trolley-side flambé classic — fillet medallions in a brandy-mustard-mushroom cream, executed in one pan with maximum theatre.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Beef fillet medallions — 8 x 90 g, lightly flattened to 2 cm
- Fine sea salt — 6 g; black pepper — 2 g
- Olive oil — 25 ml
- Unsalted butter — 50 g
- Shallots — 80 g, minced
- Garlic — 2 cloves (10 g), grated
- Button mushrooms — 250 g, sliced
- Brandy — 60 ml
- Dijon mustard — 15 g
- Worcestershire-style sauce — 12 ml
- Beef stock — 200 ml
- Double cream — 150 ml
- Flat-leaf parsley — 10 g, chopped
- Floury potatoes — 1 kg, in 3 cm chips; neutral oil — 2 L

## Method
1. Chips: simmer in salted water 8 minutes; steam-dry and chill. Fry at 140°C for 6 minutes; drain. Hold.
2. Season medallions. Sear in oil over high heat, 90 seconds per side for medium-rare; rest on a warm plate.
3. Lower heat; add butter, shallots and garlic, 2 minutes. Add mushrooms; cook 4 minutes until golden.
4. Off the flame, add brandy; return and flame (or simmer 1 minute). Reduce to a glaze.
5. Add mustard, Worcestershire sauce and stock; reduce by half. Add cream; simmer 2 minutes to coating. Season.
6. Final chip fry at 185°C, 4 minutes, to deep gold; salt hard.
7. Return medallions and juices to the sauce for 60 seconds only.

## Plating & Service
Two medallions per plate, sauce spooned over, parsley, chips stacked log-cabin style beside — finish the sauce at the table if the galley allows flames.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Sirloin or venison loin work; adjust sear by thickness. DF: swap cream for 80 ml extra stock mounted with olive oil.
