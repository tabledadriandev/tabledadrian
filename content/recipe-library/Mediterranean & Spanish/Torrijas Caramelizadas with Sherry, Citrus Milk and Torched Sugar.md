# Torrijas Caramelizadas with Sherry, Citrus Milk and Torched Sugar

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Holy Week french toast, upgraded — brioche soaked in citrus-cinnamon milk and oloroso, fried in butter and torched like a crème brûlée.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Brioche, day-old, 3 cm slices — 400 g (6)
- Whole milk — 500 ml
- Caster sugar — 80 g (soak) + 60 g (torch)
- Cinnamon stick — 4 g
- Orange peel — 8 g
- Lemon peel — 5 g
- Oloroso or cream sherry — 50 ml
- Eggs — 165 g (3)
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Honey — 20 g
- Orange zest, fresh — 2 g

## Method
1. Infuse milk with 80 g sugar, cinnamon and citrus peels at 85 °C for 10 minutes; strain, cool, add sherry.
2. Soak brioche slices 2 minutes per side until saturated but intact; drain on a rack 10 minutes.
3. Beat eggs; coat each soaked slice.
4. Fry in foaming butter over medium heat, 2–3 minutes per side, to deep gold.
5. Rest 5 minutes; sprinkle tops thickly with remaining sugar and torch to a hard amber shell.
6. Warm honey with a spoon of the soaking milk; serve as a light sauce with fresh zest over.

## Plating & Service
The brûlée crack over custardy bread is the moment; serve one thick slice per person, warm.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Alcohol-free without sherry, adding 5 ml vanilla; sturdy sourdough soaks longer and gives a tangier crumb.
