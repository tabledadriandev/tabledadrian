# Spanakopita Cigars with Three Greens and Brown Butter Phyllo

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Spanakopita rolled into crackling cigars — spinach, chard and wild rocket, phyllo brushed with nutty brown butter for extra toast.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Phyllo pastry sheets — 270 g (9 sheets)
- Spinach — 300 g
- Swiss chard, shredded — 150 g
- Rocket — 50 g
- Spring onions — 60 g
- Feta, crumbled — 180 g
- Dill — 12 g
- Egg — 55 g (1 egg)
- Unsalted butter — 120 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 20 ml
- Sesame seeds — 8 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Brown the butter over medium heat until nut-scented and hazel-coloured, about 4 minutes; strain and keep warm.
2. Wilt spinach, chard and rocket in oil over high heat 3 minutes; drain and squeeze very dry (target 120 ml liquid out), then chop.
3. Mix greens with spring onion, feta, dill, egg and pepper.
4. Lay one phyllo sheet, brush with brown butter, fold in half lengthwise, brush again.
5. Pipe 45 g filling along the short edge; roll into a tight cigar, tucking the ends.
6. Repeat for 9 cigars; arrange on a lined tray, brush tops, scatter sesame.
7. Bake at 190 °C for 22–25 minutes until deep gold and shattering.

## Plating & Service
Stack like firewood on a board, cut one diagonally to show the green core; serve warm with lemon.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 60 g crumbled walnuts to the filling; olive oil replaces butter for dairy-free (drop the feta or use plant feta).
