# Halibut Plaki Baked over Tomatoes, Leeks and Currants

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Plaki the old way — fish baked over slow vegetables with a Byzantine touch of currants; one dish, one oven, no stress at service.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Halibut or grouper fillets, thick — 700 g (4)
- Leeks, sliced — 300 g
- Ripe tomatoes, sliced — 500 g
- Red onion, sliced — 120 g
- Garlic, sliced — 10 g
- Currants — 25 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 80 ml
- Dry white wine — 100 ml
- Lemon — 120 g
- Dried oregano — 3 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 10 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Soften leeks, onion and garlic in 50 ml oil over medium heat, 10 minutes.
2. Add half the tomatoes, currants, wine and oregano; simmer 5 minutes; season.
3. Spread in a baking dish; lay remaining tomato slices over.
4. Salt the fish; nestle onto the bed, brushing with remaining oil, lemon slices on top.
5. Bake at 180 °C for 20–25 minutes until the fish flakes at 54–56 °C.
6. Rest 5 minutes; parsley and a squeeze of lemon to finish.

## Plating & Service
Serve from the dish, vegetables first as a bed, juices spooned over; room-temperature leftovers are tomorrow's lunch.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Any thick white fish plakis well; add 100 g potatoes (par-boiled) under the vegetables to make it heartier.
