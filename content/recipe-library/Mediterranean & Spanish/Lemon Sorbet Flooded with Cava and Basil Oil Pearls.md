# Lemon Sorbet Flooded with Cava and Basil Oil Pearls

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sorbete al cava — sharp lemon sorbet flooded tableside with cold cava, dotted with basil oil — the palate-reset dessert after a long seafood lunch.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Lemon juice, fresh — 300 ml
- Lemon zest — 6 g
- Water — 400 ml
- Caster sugar — 220 g
- Glucose syrup — 40 g
- Basil leaves — 12 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 50 ml
- Cava brut, ice-cold — 300 ml
- Fine sea salt — 1 g

## Method
1. Dissolve sugar and glucose in water at 60 °C; add zest, infuse 10 minutes, then chill.
2. Combine syrup with lemon juice and salt; strain.
3. Churn to a firm sorbet (or still-freeze, blitzing twice); mature in the freezer 2 hours.
4. Blanch basil 10 seconds; shock, dry, blend with oil and strain — a green oil.
5. Scoop sorbet into frozen coupes.
6. At the table: flood each coupe with 50 ml cava, dot with basil oil and serve with small spoons at once.

## Plating & Service
The pour is the service — cava foaming up around the sorbet; guests should drink the last of it from the glass.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Alcohol-free with chilled sparkling water and 5 ml elderflower; mint oil replaces basil in a heatwave.
