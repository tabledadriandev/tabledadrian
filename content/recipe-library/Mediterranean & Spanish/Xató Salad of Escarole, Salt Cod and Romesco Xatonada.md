# Xató Salad of Escarole, Salt Cod and Romesco Xatonada

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The winter salad of the Penedès coast — bitter escarole, salt cod and tuna under a rugged almond-hazelnut xatonada sauce.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Escarole, pale hearts — 300 g
- Salt cod, desalinated, shredded — 200 g
- Tuna confit in oil, drained — 120 g
- Anchovy fillets — 30 g
- Arbequina olives — 60 g
- Ñora peppers (or 8 g mild dried pepper flesh) — 12 g
- Tomato — 100 g
- Garlic — 6 g
- Blanched almonds — 40 g
- Hazelnuts, toasted — 40 g
- Stale bread, fried — 25 g
- Sherry vinegar — 20 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 110 ml
- Fine sea salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Soak ñora peppers in hot water 20 minutes; scrape out the flesh.
2. Roast tomato and garlic at 200 °C for 15 minutes until soft and lightly charred.
3. Blend ñora flesh, tomato, garlic, almonds, hazelnuts, fried bread, vinegar and salt; stream in 90 ml oil for a coarse, spoon-thick xatonada.
4. Wash and dry escarole thoroughly; tear into forkable lengths.
5. Toss escarole with two-thirds of the sauce.
6. Top with shredded salt cod, tuna, anchovies and olives; finish with remaining sauce and oil.

## Plating & Service
Serve family-style on a big platter, fish draped over the dressed leaves — traditionally eaten straight from the dish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF by omitting bread and adding 15 g extra almonds; the xatonada doubles as a sauce for grilled spring onions (calçots).
