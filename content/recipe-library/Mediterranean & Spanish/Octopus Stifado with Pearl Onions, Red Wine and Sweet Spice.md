# Octopus Stifado with Pearl Onions, Red Wine and Sweet Spice

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Octopus braised stifado-style — red wine, whole pearl onions, cinnamon and currants — winter-dark flavours that somehow belong on a summer deck.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Octopus, cleaned — 1500 g
- Pearl onions, peeled — 500 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 80 ml
- Garlic — 12 g
- Red wine — 300 ml
- Red wine vinegar — 30 ml
- Tomato paste — 40 g
- Chopped tomatoes — 300 g
- Cinnamon stick — 3 g
- Allspice berries — 2 g
- Bay leaves — 2 g
- Currants — 30 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 8 g

## Method
1. Cook the octopus in a dry covered pot over low heat 40 minutes in its own liquid; drain, reserving 200 ml, and cut into large pieces.
2. Brown pearl onions whole in 50 ml oil, 8 minutes, until burnished; remove.
3. Sauté garlic 1 minute; add tomato paste, then wine and vinegar, reducing by half.
4. Add tomatoes, reserved octopus liquid, cinnamon, allspice, bay and currants.
5. Return octopus and onions; braise covered at a bare simmer 45–60 minutes until both are meltingly tender.
6. Uncover for the last 15 minutes to thicken to a glossy, spoon-coating sauce; finish with pepper, parsley and remaining oil.

## Plating & Service
Deep plates over orzo, mash or crusty bread; the sauce is the treasure — do not strand it in the pot.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Classic beef stifado: same method, 1 kg chuck, 2.5 hours; no salt until the end — octopus brings its own.
