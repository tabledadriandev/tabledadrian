# Courgette, Pistachio and Herb Gemista Orzotto with Preserved Lemon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A green vegetarian main with real spine — orzo cooked risotto-style in courgette juice, finished with pistachio, mint and preserved lemon.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Orzo — 350 g
- Courgettes — 600 g
- Vegetable stock, hot — 800 ml
- Shallot, brunoise — 60 g
- Garlic — 8 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- Dry white wine — 80 ml
- Pistachios, toasted, chopped — 50 g
- Preserved lemon peel, brunoise — 15 g
- Mint — 8 g
- Dill — 8 g
- Graviera or pecorino, grated — 60 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Coarsely grate 400 g of the courgettes; salt lightly, rest 10 minutes, then squeeze, reserving the green juice into the stock.
2. Dice the remaining courgette 1 cm; sear golden in 20 ml oil, 3 minutes; reserve.
3. Sweat shallot and garlic in remaining oil 4 minutes; add orzo, toasting 2 minutes.
4. Add wine, then hot stock ladle by ladle, stirring, 12–14 minutes until al dente and creamy.
5. Fold in the grated courgette for the final 3 minutes.
6. Off heat: cheese, preserved lemon, herbs, seared courgette and pistachios; rest 2 minutes, loosen with stock to a flowing texture.

## Plating & Service
Wide bowls, a final flourish of pistachio and oil; it must ripple when the bowl tilts — never stiff.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan without cheese plus 10 g white miso; GF with short-grain rice, cooked 4 minutes longer.
