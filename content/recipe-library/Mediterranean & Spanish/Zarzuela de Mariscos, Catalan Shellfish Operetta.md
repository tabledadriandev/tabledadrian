# Zarzuela de Mariscos, Catalan Shellfish Operetta

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The 'operetta' of the Catalan coast — prawns, mussels, clams, squid and monkfish in a brandy-picada sauce; every element timed to its own aria.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Monkfish, 4 cm chunks — 400 g
- Large prawns, shell on — 400 g
- Squid, rings — 300 g
- Mussels — 500 g
- Clams — 300 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 80 ml
- Onion, brunoise — 150 g
- Ripe tomatoes, grated — 400 g
- Garlic — 15 g
- Brandy — 50 ml
- Dry white wine — 150 ml
- Fish fumet — 400 ml
- Blanched almonds, toasted — 40 g
- Fried bread — 20 g
- Saffron — 0.3 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 12 g
- Fine sea salt — 9 g

## Method
1. Sear prawns in 40 ml oil, 60 seconds per side; flambé with brandy; remove. Sear monkfish 2 minutes; remove. Sear squid 90 seconds; remove.
2. Soften onion in the same pan 8 minutes; add 10 g garlic and tomato, cooking 12 minutes to a dark sofrito.
3. Add wine, reduce by half; add fumet, simmer 10 minutes; season.
4. Pound almonds, fried bread, remaining garlic, saffron and half the parsley into a picada.
5. Return monkfish, 3 minutes; add squid, clams and mussels, cover 3–4 minutes until shells open.
6. Stir in the picada and prawns; simmer 2 final minutes until the sauce clings.
7. Rest 3 minutes; finish with parsley.

## Plating & Service
One magnificent pot at the table, shells up, sauce lava-thick; finger bowls and a mountain of bread.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Lobster halves seared first make it a zarzuela de gala; GF by swapping fried bread for 15 g extra almonds.
