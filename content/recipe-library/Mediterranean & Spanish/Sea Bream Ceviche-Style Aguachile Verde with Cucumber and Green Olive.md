# Sea Bream Ceviche-Style Aguachile Verde with Cucumber and Green Olive

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A Mediterranean aguachile — bream cured minutes, not hours, in a green olive, cucumber and jalapeño water; Spain meets the Pacific mid-Atlantic.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Sea bream fillets, skinned, sashimi-grade — 480 g
- Lime juice — 90 ml
- Cucumber — 300 g
- Green olives, pitted — 60 g
- Jalapeño or green guindilla — 15 g
- Coriander leaves — 12 g
- Green apple — 60 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 40 ml
- Red onion, shaved — 40 g
- Flaky sea salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Blend 60 ml lime juice with 150 g cucumber, half the olives, chilli, half the coriander and 3 g salt; strain — a pale-green aguachile water. Chill to 4 °C.
2. Slice bream against the grain, 5 mm; salt lightly and hold 5 minutes.
3. Slice remaining cucumber into thin half-moons; sliver remaining olives; matchstick the apple and toss with remaining lime.
4. Arrange fish in shallow bowls; pour the cold aguachile just to reach the fish.
5. Cure exactly 3–4 minutes — edges opaque, centres glassy.
6. Finish with cucumber, olives, apple, onion, coriander and olive oil.

## Plating & Service
Serve immediately at maximum chill — frozen bowls, cold forks; the fish continues curing on the plate.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Scallops or prawns (blanched 30 seconds) work; leave out chilli seeds to soften the burn for gentler tables.
