# Grilled Sardine Escabeche on Toast with Shaved Fennel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Charcoal-grilled sardines slipped warm into a carrot-saffron escabeche — better after an hour, superb the next day; sunshine preservation.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Fresh sardines, butterflied — 500 g (8 fish)
- Extra virgin olive oil — 100 ml
- Carrot, fine rounds — 80 g
- Shallot, sliced — 60 g
- Garlic, sliced — 8 g
- White wine vinegar — 60 ml
- Dry white wine — 60 ml
- Saffron — 0.2 g
- Bay leaf — 1 g
- Sourdough — 200 g (4 slices)
- Fennel bulb — 120 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 6 g
- Fine sea salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Warm 70 ml oil with garlic, shallot, carrot and bay over medium heat 4 minutes without colouring.
2. Add vinegar, wine and saffron; simmer 3 minutes, season, and keep the escabeche warm.
3. Salt sardines; grill skin-side down over high heat 2 minutes, flesh side 30 seconds.
4. Lay the hot sardines in a shallow dish; pour the warm escabeche over and rest at least 1 hour (or chill overnight).
5. Shave fennel paper-thin; toss with a spoonful of the escabeche liquid.
6. Grill bread, brush with remaining oil; top with fennel, sardines and spooned-over vegetables.

## Plating & Service
Serve at room temperature — two sardines per toast, escabeche jus drizzled around; a chilled vinho verde alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Mackerel fillets love this treatment; make it GF over grilled polenta instead of bread.
