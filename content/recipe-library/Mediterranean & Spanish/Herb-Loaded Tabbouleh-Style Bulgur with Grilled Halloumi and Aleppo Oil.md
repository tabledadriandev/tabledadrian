# Herb-Loaded Tabbouleh-Style Bulgur with Grilled Halloumi and Aleppo Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Parsley-forward bulgur — herbs as the salad, grain as the seasoning — topped with charred halloumi and warm Aleppo chilli oil.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Fine bulgur — 150 g
- Boiling water — 200 ml
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 60 g
- Mint, chopped — 15 g
- Ripe tomatoes, deseeded, diced — 300 g
- Spring onions — 50 g
- Lemon juice — 45 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 80 ml
- Aleppo pepper flakes — 4 g
- Halloumi — 250 g
- Fine sea salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Pour boiling water over bulgur with 2 g salt; cover 15 minutes, then fluff and cool.
2. Warm 30 ml oil to 60 °C with Aleppo flakes; infuse off-heat 10 minutes.
3. Dress the tomatoes with salt and half the lemon juice; rest 5 minutes.
4. Fold bulgur with parsley, mint, spring onion, tomatoes and their juices, remaining lemon and 40 ml oil — it should be green-flecked, not beige.
5. Slice halloumi 1 cm; sear in the remaining plain oil, 90 seconds per side, until mahogany.
6. Serve tabbouleh with hot halloumi on top and Aleppo oil spooned over.

## Plating & Service
Big communal bowl, halloumi added at the table so everyone hears the sizzle-to-plate moment.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF with quinoa; vegan with charred aubergine instead of halloumi.
