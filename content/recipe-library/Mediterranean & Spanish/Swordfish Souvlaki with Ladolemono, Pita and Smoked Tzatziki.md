# Swordfish Souvlaki with Ladolemono, Pita and Smoked Tzatziki

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Thick swordfish cubes marinated briefly (fish hates long acid), grilled hard and fast, folded into warm pita with a smoked-paprika tzatziki.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Swordfish loin, 3 cm cubes — 700 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 80 ml
- Lemon juice — 40 ml
- Dried oregano — 3 g
- Garlic, grated — 6 g
- Red pepper, 3 cm squares — 150 g
- Red onion, petals — 100 g
- Greek pita breads — 320 g (4)
- Greek yoghurt — 250 g
- Cucumber, grated, squeezed — 150 g
- Sweet smoked paprika — 3 g
- Dill — 6 g
- Fine sea salt — 7 g

## Method
1. Marinate swordfish in 40 ml oil, half the lemon, oregano and half the garlic — 20 minutes maximum, no longer.
2. Stir yoghurt with cucumber, remaining garlic, smoked paprika, dill and salt for the smoked tzatziki; chill.
3. Thread fish with pepper and onion onto skewers, not crowded.
4. Grill over high heat 8–10 minutes total, quarter-turning every 2–3 minutes, until charred outside and 52 °C within.
5. Whisk remaining oil and lemon into ladolemono; brush over the hot skewers.
6. Warm pitas on the grill 30 seconds per side; serve skewers over pita with tzatziki and extra ladolemono.

## Plating & Service
Skewers arrive on the pita, guests slide the fish off and build their own — casual food, precise cooking.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Tuna or monkfish skewer equally well; GF with rice instead of pita; the tzatziki loves 2 g chipotle if the boat leans smoky.
