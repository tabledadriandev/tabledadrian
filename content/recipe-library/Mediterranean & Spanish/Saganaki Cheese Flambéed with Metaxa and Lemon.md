# Saganaki Cheese Flambéed with Metaxa and Lemon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Kefalotyri seared in a black pan and flamed with Metaxa at the table — pure theatre, ready in six minutes.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Kefalotyri or graviera cheese, 1.5 cm slab — 320 g
- Plain flour — 40 g
- Cold water — 30 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 30 ml
- Metaxa brandy — 40 ml
- Lemon — 80 g (1 fruit)
- Dried oregano — 2 g
- Cracked black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Cut cheese into two slabs; dip briefly in cold water, then dredge all sides in flour.
2. Heat oil in a small cast-iron pan until shimmering, about 190 °C.
3. Lay in the cheese; fry 90 seconds per side until a deep golden crust forms but the centre only just slumps.
4. Remove pan from the burner, pour Metaxa over the cheese.
5. Ignite carefully with a long match; let flames die naturally, about 20 seconds.
6. Squeeze half the lemon over, scatter oregano and pepper, serve in the pan.

## Plating & Service
Take the pan flaming to the table if conditions allow; lemon wedge and crusty bread mandatory.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Use halloumi for a squeakier, more forgiving cheese; skip the flambé and deglaze with 20 ml lemon juice for alcohol-free.
