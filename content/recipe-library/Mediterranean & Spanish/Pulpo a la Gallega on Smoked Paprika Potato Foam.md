# Pulpo a la Gallega on Smoked Paprika Potato Foam

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Galician octopus with the potato reimagined as an airy foam — traditional soul, modern texture; a signature plate for tasting menus aboard.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Octopus, whole, frozen and thawed — 1200 g
- Bay leaf — 1 g
- Agria potatoes — 300 g
- Whole milk — 150 ml
- Unsalted butter — 40 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- Sweet smoked paprika — 6 g
- Hot smoked paprika — 1 g
- Flaky sea salt — 4 g
- Chives — 4 g

## Method
1. Bring a large pot of unsalted water to the boil with bay leaf; 'scare' the octopus by dipping it three times, then simmer at 90 °C for 50–60 minutes until a knife slides into the thickest tentacle.
2. Rest the octopus in its cooking water 15 minutes; reserve 100 ml of the liquid.
3. Cook peeled potatoes in salted water until tender; pass through a fine sieve.
4. Blend potato purée with hot milk, butter and 50 ml octopus water to a loose, pourable cream; load into a siphon with two charges, keep at 60 °C.
5. Cut tentacles into 2 cm coins; sear cut-side down in 30 ml olive oil over high heat for 90 seconds to caramelise.
6. Dispense potato foam into warm shallow bowls, arrange octopus on top.
7. Mix remaining oil with both paprikas; spoon around, finish with flaky salt and chives.

## Plating & Service
Foam first, octopus coins arranged in an arc, paprika oil flicked across; serve immediately while the foam holds.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No siphon: serve as a silky potato cream instead; swap milk and butter for oat cream to go dairy-free.
