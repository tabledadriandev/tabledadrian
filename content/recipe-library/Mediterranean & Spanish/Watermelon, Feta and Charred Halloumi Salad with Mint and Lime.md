# Watermelon, Feta and Charred Halloumi Salad with Mint and Lime

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Icy watermelon against two cheeses — cold crumbled feta and hot charred halloumi — a deck lunch that lands in under 25 minutes.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Watermelon, chilled — 800 g
- Halloumi — 200 g
- Feta — 100 g
- Cucumber — 150 g
- Red onion, shaved — 40 g
- Lime juice — 30 ml
- Lime zest — 2 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 50 ml
- Mint leaves — 10 g
- Nigella seeds — 3 g
- Flaky sea salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Cut watermelon into 3 cm chunks, discarding seeds; keep very cold.
2. Slice cucumber into ribbons with a peeler; soak onion in half the lime juice 10 minutes.
3. Whisk remaining lime juice, zest and 40 ml oil into a dressing.
4. Slice halloumi 1 cm thick; sear in remaining oil on high heat 90 seconds per side until deeply charred.
5. Toss watermelon, cucumber and onion with the dressing and most of the mint.
6. Top with hot halloumi, crumbled feta, nigella, remaining mint and flaky salt.

## Plating & Service
The hot-cold contrast is the dish — plate the cold base first, halloumi straight from the pan on top.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan with charred plant halloumi and smoked almonds; add rocket to stretch it for a crowd.
