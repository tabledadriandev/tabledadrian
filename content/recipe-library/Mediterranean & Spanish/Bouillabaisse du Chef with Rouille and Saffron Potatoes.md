# Bouillabaisse du Chef with Rouille and Saffron Potatoes

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A yacht-galley bouillabaisse: rockfish soup base mounted with fennel and pastis, fish poached à la minute, rouille whipped with the broth itself.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Mixed rockfish and fish bones — 1500 g
- Monkfish fillet — 400 g
- Red mullet fillets — 300 g
- Sea bass fillet — 300 g
- Mussels — 400 g
- Fennel bulb — 200 g
- Leek — 150 g
- Onion — 150 g
- Ripe tomatoes — 400 g
- Garlic — 30 g
- Orange peel — 8 g
- Pastis — 40 ml
- Saffron — 0.6 g
- Waxy potatoes — 400 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 120 ml
- Egg yolk — 20 g
- Cayenne — 1 g
- Fine sea salt — 12 g

## Method
1. Sweat sliced fennel, leek, onion and 20 g garlic in 60 ml oil for 10 minutes.
2. Add rockfish and bones; crush and cook 10 minutes. Add tomatoes, orange peel, pastis and 2500 ml water; simmer hard 40 minutes.
3. Blend the soup coarsely, bones and all, then pass firmly through a moulin or fine sieve; season and add half the saffron.
4. Simmer peeled potato rounds in 500 ml of the soup with remaining saffron, 15 minutes.
5. Rouille: whisk yolk with remaining grated garlic, cayenne and 20 ml warm soup; emulsify in 60 ml oil.
6. Poach monkfish in barely-simmering soup (85 °C) 6 minutes; add bass and mullet for 4 minutes, mussels until they open.
7. Serve fish and potatoes in warm bowls; ladle soup over at the table; rouille and extra broth alongside.

## Plating & Service
Two pours: broth first over the arranged fish, then a second ladle mid-course; croûtons and rouille passed around.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No rockfish? Double the bones and add 200 g prawn shells; the soup base freezes beautifully for two months.
