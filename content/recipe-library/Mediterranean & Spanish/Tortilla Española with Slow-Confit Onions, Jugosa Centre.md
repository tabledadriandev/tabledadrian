# Tortilla Española with Slow-Confit Onions, Jugosa Centre

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A properly juicy tortilla — potatoes confited in olive oil, centre just set at 62 °C; served barely warm it slices like custard.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Eggs — 360 g (7 eggs)
- Agria potatoes — 500 g
- Onion — 200 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 350 ml (most recovered)
- Fine sea salt — 7 g

## Method
1. Slice potatoes 3 mm thick and onion into thin julienne.
2. Confit both in olive oil at 130 °C for 25–30 minutes until completely tender but uncoloured; drain well, reserving oil.
3. Beat eggs with salt; fold in the warm potato-onion mix and rest 10 minutes so the eggs thicken slightly.
4. Heat a 22 cm non-stick pan with 15 ml reserved oil over medium-high heat.
5. Pour in the mix; cook 2 minutes, shaking, then run a spatula around the edge.
6. Flip with a flat plate and slide back; cook 90 seconds more — the centre should wobble (about 62 °C core).
7. Rest 10 minutes on a plate before slicing; the carryover sets the jugosa centre.

## Plating & Service
Slice into wedges or 3 cm cubes on picks for tapas service; a pinch of flaky salt on the cut face.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 60 g piquillo peppers or 40 g chorizo between the layers; cook 2 minutes longer for a fully set centre if preferred.
