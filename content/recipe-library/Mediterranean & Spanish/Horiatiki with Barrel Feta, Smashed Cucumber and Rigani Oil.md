# Horiatiki with Barrel Feta, Smashed Cucumber and Rigani Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The village salad, uncompromised — no lettuce, just peak tomatoes, smashed cucumber and a slab of barrel feta under oregano oil.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Ripe mixed tomatoes — 600 g
- Cucumber — 250 g
- Green pepper — 100 g
- Red onion — 80 g
- Barrel-aged feta, in one slab — 200 g
- Kalamata olives — 80 g
- Capers, rinsed — 15 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 80 ml
- Red wine vinegar — 15 ml
- Dried Greek oregano — 3 g
- Flaky sea salt — 4 g

## Method
1. Warm 30 ml olive oil to 50 °C, stir in oregano, and infuse off the heat 10 minutes.
2. Cut tomatoes into bold irregular wedges; season with flaky salt and rest 5 minutes to draw juices.
3. Smash cucumber lightly with the flat of a knife, then cut into chunks — the cracks drink the dressing.
4. Slice pepper into rings and onion into fine half-moons; soak onion in the vinegar 5 minutes.
5. Toss tomatoes, cucumber, pepper, onion (with its vinegar), olives and capers with remaining plain oil.
6. Lay the feta slab whole on top; spoon over the oregano oil.

## Plating & Service
Serve in a wide shallow bowl, feta unbroken until the table — guests break it in. Bread for the juices is non-negotiable.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Plant feta for vegan; add purslane or rocket in high summer.
