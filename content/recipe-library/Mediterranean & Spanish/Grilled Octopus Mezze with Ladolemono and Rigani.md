# Grilled Octopus Mezze with Ladolemono and Rigani

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Braise-then-char octopus glossed with ladolemono — the island taverna standard, executed with yacht-grill precision.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Octopus tentacles — 800 g
- Red wine vinegar — 30 ml
- Bay leaf — 1 g
- Black peppercorns — 2 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml
- Lemon juice — 35 ml
- Dried Greek oregano (rigani) — 3 g
- Garlic, grated — 3 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 6 g
- Flaky sea salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Place octopus in a dry heavy pot with vinegar, bay and peppercorns; cover, cook over the lowest heat (sub-simmer, about 90 °C) for 60–70 minutes in its own juices until tender.
2. Cool the octopus in its liquid 20 minutes, then drain and pat dry.
3. Whisk 75 ml olive oil with lemon juice, oregano and garlic into a thick ladolemono.
4. Brush tentacles with remaining oil; grill over very high heat 2–3 minutes per side until charred and crisp-edged.
5. Slice thick on the bias.
6. Dress generously with ladolemono, parsley and flaky salt while hot.

## Plating & Service
Serve on a warm platter with charred lemon halves; spoon extra ladolemono at the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The braising liquid, reduced, makes a stunning glaze for the octopus; serve over fava purée as a composed starter.
