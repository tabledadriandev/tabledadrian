# Rabbit a la Cazadora with Rosemary, Olives and Garnacha

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Hunter's rabbit braised with garnacha, rosemary and green olives — the inland Spain dish that converts rabbit sceptics by the second bite.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Rabbit, jointed — 1300 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- Onion, chopped — 150 g
- Carrot, rounds — 120 g
- Celery — 60 g
- Garlic, whole cloves — 20 g
- Garnacha or other red wine — 300 ml
- Chicken stock — 300 ml
- Tomato paste — 25 g
- Green olives, cracked — 100 g
- Rosemary — 4 g
- Bay leaf — 1 g
- Fine sea salt — 9 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Salt the rabbit 30 minutes ahead; brown hard in oil in a wide braiser, 4 minutes per side; remove.
2. Soften onion, carrot and celery 8 minutes; add garlic and tomato paste, 2 minutes.
3. Add wine; reduce by half, scraping the fond.
4. Return rabbit with stock, rosemary and bay; cover and braise at 150 °C for 50–60 minutes until the legs yield.
5. Uncover, add olives, and reduce on the hob 10 minutes until the sauce coats.
6. Rest 10 minutes; season with pepper and a thread of raw oil.

## Plating & Service
Rustic — the braiser at the table, saddle pieces to the guests, legs to whoever earned them; bread or saffron rice below.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken thighs substitute seamlessly; add 15 g dark chocolate at the end for a Catalan chocolate-thickened variant.
