# Moussaka with Aubergine Confit and Yoghurt-Bechamel Cloud

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Moussaka worth the labour — aubergines confited not fried, cinnamon-scented lamb ragù, and a yoghurt-lightened béchamel that soufflés at the edges.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Aubergines — 900 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 120 ml
- Lamb mince — 600 g
- Onion, chopped — 150 g
- Garlic — 10 g
- Red wine — 120 ml
- Chopped tomatoes — 400 g
- Tomato paste — 30 g
- Cinnamon, ground — 2 g
- Allspice — 1 g
- Waxy potatoes, 5 mm slices — 400 g
- Whole milk — 500 ml
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Plain flour — 60 g
- Greek yoghurt — 150 g
- Egg yolks — 40 g (2)
- Kefalotyri, grated — 80 g
- Fine sea salt — 12 g

## Method
1. Slice aubergines 1 cm; brush with 80 ml oil, salt, and bake at 200 °C for 25 minutes until golden and collapsed.
2. Brown lamb hard in 20 ml oil; add onion and garlic, 6 minutes; add wine, reduce.
3. Add tomatoes, paste, cinnamon, allspice and salt; simmer 30 minutes until thick and nearly dry.
4. Par-cook potato slices in salted water 6 minutes; drain.
5. Béchamel: cook butter and flour 2 minutes; whisk in milk, simmer 6 minutes. Off heat, whisk in yoghurt, yolks and half the cheese.
6. Layer in a deep dish: potatoes, half the aubergine, all the ragù, remaining aubergine.
7. Pour over the béchamel 2 cm deep; scatter remaining cheese.
8. Bake at 180 °C for 40–45 minutes until bronzed; rest 30 minutes — moussaka cuts clean only when settled.

## Plating & Service
Cut in confident squares; the layers should read like geological strata. Serve warm, not scalding, with a sharp green salad.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Beef or half-lentil ragù both work; vegetarian with double lentils and mushroom; the rest 30 minutes is non-negotiable.
