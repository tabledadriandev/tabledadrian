# Ajoblanco of Marcona Almonds with Green Grapes and Dill Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Málaga's snow-white almond gazpacho — pure, cold and elegant; the green grape and dill oil garnish keeps it modern.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Marcona almonds, blanched — 200 g
- Stale white bread, crustless — 60 g
- Cold water — 600 ml
- Garlic — 4 g
- Sherry vinegar — 18 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 100 ml
- Fine sea salt — 6 g
- Green grapes — 150 g
- Dill — 8 g
- Sunflower oil — 40 ml

## Method
1. Soak almonds and bread in the cold water 30 minutes.
2. Blend with garlic, vinegar and salt on high for 4 minutes until utterly smooth.
3. Stream in 90 ml olive oil to a silky, pourable cream; adjust water if needed.
4. Pass through a fine sieve; chill to 5 °C for at least 3 hours.
5. Blitz dill with sunflower oil and strain for dill oil.
6. Serve with halved grapes, dill oil droplets and a final trickle of olive oil.

## Plating & Service
Serve in small chilled coupes as a seated first course — it drinks like cold silk; garnish placed first, soup poured over.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF using 30 g extra almonds instead of bread; melon balls replace grapes beautifully in August.
