# Ensaladilla Rusa with Smoked Tuna Belly and Piparra

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Spain's beloved potato salad taken seriously — waxy potatoes dressed warm, proper homemade mayo, ribbons of smoked tuna belly.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Waxy potatoes (Monalisa) — 500 g
- Carrots — 120 g
- Peas, blanched — 80 g
- Eggs — 165 g (3 eggs)
- Egg yolk — 20 g
- Sunflower oil — 200 ml
- Sherry vinegar — 12 ml
- Dijon mustard — 5 g
- Smoked tuna belly (mojama-style or ventresca) — 100 g
- Piparra peppers — 40 g
- Chives — 5 g
- Fine sea salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Simmer whole potatoes and carrots in salted water until just tender: carrots 12 minutes, potatoes 20; peel and dice 1 cm while warm.
2. Boil eggs 9 minutes; cool, peel, chop two and reserve one for grating.
3. Dress the warm vegetables with half the vinegar and a pinch of salt; cool fully.
4. Make mayonnaise: whisk yolk with mustard, emulsify in oil, season with remaining vinegar.
5. Fold potatoes, carrots, peas and chopped egg with mayonnaise — gently, keeping the dice intact.
6. Chill 1 hour; top with tuna belly ribbons, piparras, grated egg and chives.

## Plating & Service
Press into a ring mould for a clean cylinder, toppings arranged like a still life; serve with picos or crisps.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Omit tuna for vegetarian; a few drops of the piparra brine in the mayo sharpen the whole dish.
