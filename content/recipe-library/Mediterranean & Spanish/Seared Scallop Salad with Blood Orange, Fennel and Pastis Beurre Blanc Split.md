# Seared Scallop Salad with Blood Orange, Fennel and Pastis Beurre Blanc Split

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Warm scallop salad where a pastis beurre blanc is deliberately split into golden beads over shaved fennel and citrus — technique as garnish.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Sea scallops, roe off — 360 g (12)
- Fennel bulb — 200 g
- Blood oranges — 300 g (2)
- Rocket — 60 g
- Shallot, minced — 30 g
- Dry white wine — 60 ml
- Pastis — 15 ml
- White wine vinegar — 10 ml
- Cold unsalted butter, cubed — 90 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 30 ml
- Fennel fronds — 4 g
- Fine sea salt — 4 g

## Method
1. Reduce shallot, wine, vinegar and pastis to 25 ml.
2. Whisk in cold butter cube by cube over low heat to a beurre blanc; season, then deliberately warm it to 70 °C so it just splits into golden beads in a clear whey; hold warm.
3. Shave fennel on a mandoline into iced water; drain and dry.
4. Segment the oranges, saving 20 ml juice; whisk juice with oil for the salad.
5. Pat scallops bone-dry, salt, and sear in a smoking pan 90 seconds on the first side, 30 on the second — deep crust, translucent centre.
6. Toss fennel, rocket and orange with the citrus oil; plate, set three scallops per portion, spoon the split pastis butter over and around; fronds to finish.

## Plating & Service
Serve immediately — crust up, split butter glistening like drops of amber; chilled Cassis blanc alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Keep the beurre blanc emulsified for a classic look; swap pastis for vermouth if aniseed divides the table.
