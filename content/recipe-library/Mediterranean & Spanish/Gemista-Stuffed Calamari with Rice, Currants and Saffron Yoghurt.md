# Gemista-Stuffed Calamari with Rice, Currants and Saffron Yoghurt

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Calamari tubes filled with a gemista-style rice of currants and pine-free crunch, braised in white wine, served over saffron yoghurt.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Calamari tubes and tentacles — 800 g (8 tubes)
- Short-grain rice — 150 g
- Onion, grated — 100 g
- Tomato, grated — 150 g
- Currants — 25 g
- Sunflower seeds, toasted — 25 g
- Dill — 10 g
- Mint — 5 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 80 ml
- Dry white wine — 150 ml
- Fish stock — 200 ml
- Greek yoghurt — 200 g
- Saffron — 0.2 g
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Fine sea salt — 7 g

## Method
1. Chop tentacles; sauté with onion in 30 ml oil, 5 minutes.
2. Add rice, tomato, currants and seeds; add 100 ml stock and simmer 5 minutes to half-cooked. Fold in dill, mint and salt; cool.
3. Bloom saffron in 10 ml hot water; stir into yoghurt with lemon juice and a pinch of salt.
4. Fill tubes two-thirds; secure with picks.
5. Brown tubes gently in 30 ml oil, 2 minutes per side.
6. Add wine and remaining stock; cover and braise at 90 °C for 35–40 minutes until tender and the rice is cooked.
7. Rest 5 minutes; slice thick and serve over the saffron yoghurt with the reduced pan juices and remaining oil.

## Plating & Service
Golden yoghurt beneath, white spirals above, jus bridging the two — a quietly impressive plate.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free with saffron-lemon olive oil sauce instead of yoghurt; the filling doubles as a stand-alone pilaf.
