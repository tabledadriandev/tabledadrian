# Classic Tzatziki with Cucumber Pressed in Ouzo Salt

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A tzatziki with real backbone — cucumber pressed with a whisper of ouzo and salt so the dip never weeps, garlic mellowed in lemon.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Greek yoghurt (10% fat), strained — 400 g
- Cucumber — 300 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g
- Ouzo — 10 ml
- Garlic, grated — 6 g
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 40 ml
- Dill, chopped — 8 g
- Mint leaves, sliced — 4 g

## Method
1. Coarsely grate the cucumber, skin on; toss with salt and ouzo and rest 15 minutes in a sieve.
2. Steep grated garlic in lemon juice 10 minutes to soften its bite.
3. Squeeze the cucumber hard in a clean cloth — you should extract at least 80 ml liquid.
4. Fold cucumber, garlic-lemon, dill and mint into the yoghurt with 25 ml olive oil.
5. Season, then rest 30 minutes in the fridge for the flavours to knit.
6. Serve cold with the remaining oil pooled on top.

## Plating & Service
A deep swirl in a chilled bowl, olive oil in the grooves, a dill sprig; flanked by warm bread and raw vegetables.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Coconut yoghurt makes a credible vegan version; skip ouzo and use plain salt if serving children.
