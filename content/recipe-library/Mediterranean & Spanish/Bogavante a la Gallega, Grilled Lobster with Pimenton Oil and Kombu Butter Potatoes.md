# Bogavante a la Gallega, Grilled Lobster with Pimenton Oil and Kombu Butter Potatoes

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Split lobster grilled shell-down and basted with warm pimentón oil, beside potatoes glazed in kombu butter — Galicia with a quiet umami upgrade.

## Ingredients (serves 2)
- Live lobster — 800 g (1 large)
- Extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- Sweet smoked paprika — 5 g
- Hot smoked paprika — 1 g
- Garlic, grated — 4 g
- Waxy potatoes — 400 g
- Unsalted butter — 50 g
- Dried kombu, powdered — 3 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Chives — 4 g
- Flaky sea salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Dispatch the lobster with a swift knife through the head cross; split lengthwise, crack claws, remove the vein.
2. Warm oil to 60 °C with both paprikas and garlic; infuse 10 minutes off-heat.
3. Simmer potatoes in salted water 18 minutes; drain and slice thick.
4. Melt butter with kombu powder; toss the potatoes to a glossy glaze, holding warm.
5. Grill lobster flesh-side down over high heat 2 minutes; flip shell-down, baste flesh continuously with pimentón oil, 6–8 minutes until the meat is just set (58 °C in the tail).
6. Grill claws 3 minutes longer than tails.
7. Rest 3 minutes; finish with lemon, salt, chives, and the last spoon of pimentón oil.

## Plating & Service
Half lobster per person, shell as the plate; potatoes fanned alongside, claw meat cracked and returned to the shell.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free with olive oil-kombu emulsion on the potatoes; langoustines take the same baste in half the time.
