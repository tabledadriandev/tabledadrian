# Pissaladière Squares with 3-Hour Onions and Niçois Olives

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Nice's onion tart — onions melted for three hours until jammy-sweet against salty anchovy lattice; cut into aperitif squares.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Strong bread flour — 300 g
- Water — 190 ml
- Fresh yeast — 6 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- Yellow onions, thinly sliced — 1500 g
- Fresh thyme — 4 g
- Bay leaf — 1 g
- Anchovy fillets — 60 g
- Niçois (Cailletier) olives — 80 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Mix flour, water, yeast, salt and 20 ml oil; knead 8 minutes to a smooth dough. Prove 90 minutes until doubled.
2. Meanwhile cook onions with 40 ml oil, thyme, bay and a pinch of salt in a wide pan over the lowest heat, covered for the first hour, then uncovered, stirring occasionally, for 3 hours total until deep amber and jammy with no liquid.
3. Cool onions; discard bay.
4. Press dough into an oiled 30 × 40 cm tray, 5 mm thick; prove 30 minutes.
5. Spread onions to the edges; lattice the anchovies and dot with olives.
6. Bake at 220 °C for 20–25 minutes until the base is crisp and deep gold underneath.
7. Brush the rim with remaining oil; rest 10 minutes, cut into 6 cm squares.

## Plating & Service
Serve barely warm on a board with rosé; the squares hold well for deck canapés.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Omit anchovy and add capers for vegetarian/vegan; the onion jam keeps 5 days and is superb under grilled fish.
