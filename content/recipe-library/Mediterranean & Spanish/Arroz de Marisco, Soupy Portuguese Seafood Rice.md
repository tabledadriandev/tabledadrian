# Arroz de Marisco, Soupy Portuguese Seafood Rice

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Portugal's brothy answer to paella — carolino rice rolling loose among prawns, clams and mussels, sharpened with lemon and a late hit of coriander.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Carolino or arborio rice — 320 g
- Prawns, shell on — 400 g
- Clams — 400 g
- Mussels — 400 g
- Fish fumet — 1300 ml
- Onion, chopped — 120 g
- Garlic — 12 g
- Ripe tomatoes, chopped — 350 g
- Red pepper, diced — 100 g
- White wine — 120 ml
- Piri-piri — 1.5 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- Coriander, chopped — 18 g
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Fine sea salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Peel prawns; simmer the shells in the fumet 15 minutes; strain and keep hot.
2. Steam clams and mussels open in the wine, 3 minutes; strain the liquor into the fumet, keep shellfish aside.
3. Soften onion, pepper and garlic in oil 7 minutes; add tomatoes and piri-piri, cooking 8 minutes.
4. Add rice, stir 1 minute; add all the hot fumet. Season.
5. Simmer 14 minutes, stirring occasionally — this rice should stay soupy, rolling in broth.
6. Add prawns for the final 3 minutes; fold in clams, mussels, lemon juice and coriander.
7. Rest 2 minutes and serve immediately — it thickens by the minute.

## Plating & Service
Deep bowls and spoons; ladle broth generously — malandrinho (naughty-loose) is the correct texture.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Half fish, half shellfish is common; add 100 g samphire at the end for a saline green edge.
