# Rizogalo Rice Pudding Brulee with Mastiha and Cherry Spoon Sweet

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Greek rice pudding perfumed with mastiha, chilled and brûléed, with glyko koutaliou cherries — island café dessert with a French crust.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Short-grain rice — 110 g
- Whole milk — 1000 ml
- Double cream — 100 ml
- Caster sugar — 100 g (pudding) + 50 g (brulee)
- Mastic crystals, ground with a pinch of sugar — 0.8 g
- Lemon peel — 5 g
- Cornflour — 12 g
- Cherries, pitted — 300 g
- Caster sugar (spoon sweet) — 150 g
- Lemon juice — 15 ml

## Method
1. Spoon sweet: simmer cherries, 150 g sugar and 50 ml water 20 minutes to a glossy syrup; add lemon juice; cool.
2. Simmer rice in the milk with lemon peel at the barest bubble, stirring often, 35–40 minutes until tender and creamy.
3. Whisk cornflour into the cream; stir in with 100 g sugar and cook 3 minutes.
4. Off heat, stir in ground mastic; remove peel.
5. Pour into shallow ramekins; chill 3 hours.
6. Sugar the tops and torch to amber glass; crown with a spoonful of cherry sweet.

## Plating & Service
The crack, then the cold mastic cream, then the winey cherry — instruct guests to dig one spoon through all three.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vanilla replaces mastic without shame; dairy-free with oat milk and coconut cream, 5 minutes longer on the simmer.
