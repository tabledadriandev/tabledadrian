# Salt Cod Croquetas with Saffron Aioli

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A silky béchamel croqueta that shatters then flows — the benchmark tapa; make the base a day ahead so service is only frying.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Salt cod, desalinated 24 h — 250 g
- Whole milk — 500 ml
- Unsalted butter — 60 g
- Plain flour — 70 g
- Shallot, brunoise — 40 g
- Nutmeg, grated — 0.5 g
- Plain flour for coating — 60 g
- Eggs, beaten — 100 g (2 eggs)
- Fine breadcrumbs (panko, blitzed) — 120 g
- Sunflower oil for frying — 1000 ml
- Egg yolk — 20 g
- Garlic, grated — 4 g
- Saffron threads, bloomed in 5 ml hot water — 0.2 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 120 ml
- Lemon juice — 10 ml

## Method
1. Poach the desalinated cod in the milk at 80 °C for 8 minutes; lift out, flake, and reserve the infused milk.
2. Sweat shallot in butter over low heat 4 minutes; add 70 g flour and cook the roux 3 minutes without colour.
3. Whisk in the warm cod milk in three additions; cook 8 minutes to a very thick béchamel, season with nutmeg.
4. Fold in flaked cod, spread 2 cm deep in a tray, press film to the surface and chill at least 4 hours.
5. For the aioli, whisk yolk, garlic and saffron water, then emulsify in olive oil drop by drop; season with lemon juice and salt.
6. Shape base into 25 g cylinders; coat in flour, egg, then breadcrumbs.
7. Fry at 180 °C in batches of six for 2–3 minutes until deep gold; drain on paper.
8. Rest 2 minutes so centres turn molten, then serve with the saffron aioli.

## Plating & Service
Stack croquetas on a warm plate with aioli in a small copper pot; a wedge of charred lemon alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap cod for wild mushrooms and use oat milk plus olive oil roux for dairy-free; GF breadcrumbs work well.
