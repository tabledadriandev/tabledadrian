# Courgette Carpaccio with Preserved Lemon, Pine Nuts and Pecorino

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Raw courgette shaved to translucence, dressed ahead so it softens like silk — a no-heat starter for galley-averse afternoons.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Courgettes, small and firm — 400 g
- Preserved lemon peel, brunoise — 15 g
- Lemon juice — 25 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- Pine nuts — 30 g
- Pecorino or aged sheep cheese — 40 g
- Mint leaves — 5 g
- Basil leaves — 5 g
- Flaky sea salt — 3 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Toast pine nuts in a dry pan over medium heat 2–3 minutes until golden; cool.
2. Shave courgettes lengthwise into ribbons on a mandoline, 1–2 mm.
3. Whisk lemon juice, preserved lemon and oil.
4. Toss ribbons with the dressing and salt; rest 10 minutes — they will relax and turn glossy.
5. Arrange in loose folds and waves on chilled plates, never flat.
6. Finish with pine nuts, shaved pecorino, torn herbs and pepper.

## Plating & Service
Height matters: the ribbons should stand in soft waves; serve cool with a crisp white.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan without cheese, adding 10 g white miso to the dressing; yellow courgettes make it two-tone.
