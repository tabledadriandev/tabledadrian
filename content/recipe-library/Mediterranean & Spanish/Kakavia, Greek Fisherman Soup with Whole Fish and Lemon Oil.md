# Kakavia, Greek Fisherman Soup with Whole Fish and Lemon Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Aegean's proto-bouillabaisse — whole small fish, waxy potatoes and torrents of olive oil boiled hard so the broth emulsifies milky-gold.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Mixed small whole fish (scorpionfish, bream), gutted — 1200 g
- Waxy potatoes, thick rounds — 500 g
- Onions, sliced — 200 g
- Celery, sliced — 80 g
- Carrots, rounds — 100 g
- Ripe tomatoes, grated — 300 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 150 ml
- Water — 1500 ml
- Lemon juice — 50 ml
- Lemon zest — 3 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 10 g
- Fine sea salt — 10 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Layer onions, celery, carrots and potatoes in a wide pot; pour over 120 ml of the oil and the water. Season hard.
2. Boil vigorously, uncovered, 20 minutes — the rolling boil emulsifies oil into the broth; this is the technique.
3. Add grated tomato; boil 5 minutes more.
4. Lay the whole fish over the vegetables; simmer 12–15 minutes until the eyes turn white and flesh lifts from the bone.
5. Lift fish out carefully; fillet quickly in the galley.
6. Whisk lemon juice with remaining oil and zest; return fish to bowls over vegetables, ladle broth, dress with the lemon oil and parsley.

## Plating & Service
Two stages like the islanders: broth with potatoes first if you like, fish after — or everything in one generous bowl.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Any small rockfish deepen the broth; strain and serve clear with the fish for a refined version.
