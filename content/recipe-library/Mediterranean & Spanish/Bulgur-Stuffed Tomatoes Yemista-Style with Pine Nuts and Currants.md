# Bulgur-Stuffed Tomatoes Yemista-Style with Pine Nuts and Currants

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Slow-baked Greek stuffed tomatoes — the filling cooked in the tomatoes' own juice, sweet-sour with currants; best an hour out of the oven.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Large ripe tomatoes — 1200 g (8)
- Coarse bulgur — 180 g
- Onion, grated — 120 g
- Garlic — 8 g
- Pine nuts — 30 g
- Currants — 30 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml
- Tomato passata — 100 g
- Dill — 10 g
- Mint — 6 g
- Caster sugar — 5 g
- Waxy potatoes, wedges — 300 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Slice lids off tomatoes; scoop flesh with a spoon, keeping shells intact. Blend the flesh; reserve 400 ml.
2. Salt and sugar the shell interiors; invert 15 minutes.
3. Sweat onion and garlic in 50 ml oil 6 minutes; add bulgur, pine nuts, currants and passata plus 200 ml tomato juice; simmer 5 minutes to half-cooked.
4. Off heat, fold in dill, mint and salt.
5. Fill shells three-quarters full (bulgur swells); replace lids.
6. Nestle in a baking dish with potato wedges; pour over remaining tomato juice and oil.
7. Bake at 180 °C for 55–60 minutes, basting once, until shells slump and the tops blister.
8. Rest 45–60 minutes — served warm, never hot, is the Greek way.

## Plating & Service
One tomato with potatoes per plate, pan juices spooned over; feta on the table for those who want it.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Rice replaces bulgur classically (GF); add 200 g minced lamb to the filling for a fuller version.
