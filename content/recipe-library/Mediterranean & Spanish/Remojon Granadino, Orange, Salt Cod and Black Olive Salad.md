# Remojon Granadino, Orange, Salt Cod and Black Olive Salad

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Andalusia's Moorish winter salad — flame-toasted salt cod torn over oranges with olives and egg; sweet, saline, bitter, complete.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Salt cod, desalinated — 300 g
- Oranges — 600 g (3)
- Black olives, dry-cured — 70 g
- Spring onions — 50 g
- Eggs — 110 g (2)
- Extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- Sweet smoked paprika — 2 g
- Sherry vinegar — 10 ml
- Flat-leaf parsley — 6 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Boil eggs 9 minutes; cool and quarter.
2. Toast the desalinated cod under a 250 °C grill for 8–10 minutes, turning, until the edges char and it flakes; cool and tear into ribbons.
3. Segment two oranges; squeeze the third for 50 ml juice.
4. Whisk orange juice, vinegar, paprika and oil into a dressing.
5. Layer orange segments, cod ribbons, olives and spring onion on a platter.
6. Dress generously; finish with egg quarters, parsley and pepper. Rest 10 minutes before serving.

## Plating & Service
Serve at room temperature, layers visible; this salad improves as it sits through lunch.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Blood oranges in season are spectacular; omit egg and it travels perfectly for beach picnics.
