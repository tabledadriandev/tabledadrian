# Anchoïade with Charred Baby Vegetables

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Provence's pungent anchovy dip smoothed with confit garlic, served warm with fire-kissed baby vegetables — the Riviera's answer to bagna cauda.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Anchovy fillets in oil, drained — 100 g
- Garlic cloves — 25 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 150 ml
- Red wine vinegar — 10 ml
- Dijon mustard — 8 g
- Baby carrots — 150 g
- Baby fennel, halved — 150 g
- Radishes with tops — 120 g
- Baby courgettes, halved — 150 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Confit garlic cloves in 50 ml olive oil at 120 °C for 8 minutes until soft and blond.
2. Blend anchovies, confit garlic and its oil, vinegar and mustard until smooth.
3. Stream in remaining oil with the blender running to a loose, warm emulsion; season with pepper only.
4. Blanch carrots and fennel 2 minutes in salted water; drain and dry.
5. Char all vegetables on a screaming-hot plancha 2–3 minutes, cut sides down, leaving them crisp within.
6. Serve the anchoïade warm (about 45 °C) with the vegetables arranged around it.

## Plating & Service
A warm stoneware pot of anchoïade centre-table, vegetables radiating out by colour; give guests small tongs.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Blend in 30 g black olives for a deeper, tapenade-leaning dip; raw vegetables only for a fully no-cook version.
