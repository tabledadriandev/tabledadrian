# Pan con Tomate, Aged Sherry Vinegar and Anchovy Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Catalonia's simplest luxury given a Riviera polish — the anchovy butter melts into hot bread before the tomato pulp goes on.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Rustic sourdough or pan de cristal — 360 g (6 slices)
- Ripe vine tomatoes — 400 g
- Unsalted butter, softened — 80 g
- Anchovy fillets, finely chopped — 20 g
- Garlic clove — 5 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 45 ml
- Aged sherry vinegar (PX) — 10 ml
- Flaky sea salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Halve tomatoes across the equator and grate flesh-side down on a box grater into a bowl; discard skins.
2. Season pulp with sherry vinegar, 15 ml olive oil and a pinch of salt; drain 10 minutes in a fine sieve to concentrate.
3. Beat softened butter with chopped anchovy until smooth.
4. Grill bread slices over high heat or in a 220 °C oven for 6–8 minutes until deeply golden and crisp.
5. Rub each hot slice once with the cut garlic clove, then spread thinly with anchovy butter.
6. Spoon over the drained tomato pulp, finish with remaining olive oil and flaky salt; serve immediately.

## Plating & Service
Serve on a wooden board cut into finger-width batons, tomato glistening; best passed around while still warm.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Omit anchovy butter and use only oil for a vegan version; gluten-free with a good GF baguette.
