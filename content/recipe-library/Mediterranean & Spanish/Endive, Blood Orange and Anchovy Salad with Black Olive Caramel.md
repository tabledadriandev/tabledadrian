# Endive, Blood Orange and Anchovy Salad with Black Olive Caramel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bitter leaves, sweet citrus, saline anchovy and a startling black-olive caramel — a cold-weather Riviera salad with real tension.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Belgian endives — 400 g
- Blood oranges — 400 g (2–3)
- Anchovy fillets — 30 g
- Black olives, pitted — 80 g
- Caster sugar — 40 g
- Water — 20 ml
- Sherry vinegar — 15 ml
- Dijon mustard — 5 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- Chervil or parsley — 5 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Dry olives, chop finely. Cook sugar and water to a light amber caramel (about 170 °C), stir in olives and 5 ml vinegar; spread on parchment, cool, then chop into shards.
2. Segment blood oranges over a bowl, keeping 30 ml juice.
3. Whisk the reserved juice with remaining vinegar, mustard and oil.
4. Halve endives, cut into long petals; toss gently with the dressing.
5. Arrange endive and orange segments with height; drape anchovies between.
6. Finish with olive caramel shards, chervil and pepper.

## Plating & Service
Plate individually — pale endive, ruby orange, jet-black caramel; serve immediately before the caramel softens.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skip anchovy and add shaved fennel for vegan; regular oranges plus 5 ml lemon work off-season.
