# Txangurro-Style Stuffed Crab Gratin with Brandy and Choricero

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> San Sebastián's baked spider-crab classic — sweet crab folded through a brandy-choricero sofrito, gratinéed in the shell.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- White and brown crab meat, picked — 500 g
- Onion, brunoise — 150 g
- Leek, brunoise — 80 g
- Garlic — 10 g
- Choricero pepper paste — 30 g
- Ripe tomatoes, grated — 250 g
- Brandy — 40 ml
- Dry white wine — 80 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 50 ml
- Unsalted butter — 30 g
- Fresh breadcrumbs — 40 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 8 g
- Espelette pepper — 1 g
- Fine sea salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Sweat onion and leek in oil over low heat 12 minutes until sweet; add garlic, 1 minute.
2. Add choricero paste and tomato; cook 10 minutes to a thick sofrito.
3. Add brandy, flame it off; add wine and reduce by half.
4. Fold in the crab (brown meat first for depth, white last for texture); warm through 3 minutes, season with salt and Espelette.
5. Fill cleaned crab shells or gratin dishes; top with breadcrumbs, parsley and dots of butter.
6. Grill at 240 °C for 5–7 minutes until bronzed and bubbling at the edges.
7. Rest 2 minutes; serve with lemon and small spoons.

## Plating & Service
In the shell if you have them — nothing plates better; a young white Rioja alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF with almond crumbs; tinned quality crab works at anchor — drain scrupulously and add 10 ml extra brandy.
