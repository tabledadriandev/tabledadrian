# Piquillo Peppers Stuffed with Brandade, Piquillo Coulis

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sweet fire-roasted piquillos filled with whipped salt cod brandade, napped with a coulis of their own trimmings — Basque comfort, elegant plate.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Piquillo peppers, roasted (jarred) — 300 g (12 peppers + trimmings)
- Salt cod, desalinated — 250 g
- Whole milk — 200 ml
- Garlic — 8 g
- Agria potato, cooked — 120 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml
- Double cream — 60 ml
- Sherry vinegar — 8 ml
- Chives — 5 g
- Fine sea salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Poach salt cod and garlic in milk at 80 °C for 8 minutes; drain, reserving 60 ml milk.
2. Beat warm cod with potato, then emulsify in 60 ml olive oil and enough reserved milk for a light, spoonable brandade; season.
3. Select the 12 best piquillos; blend any torn ones and trimmings with cream, sherry vinegar and 20 ml oil into a smooth coulis, season with salt.
4. Pipe brandade into each pepper until plump, arrange in an oiled baking dish.
5. Warm at 160 °C for 12–15 minutes until the filling reaches 65 °C.
6. Pool warm coulis on plates, set three peppers per portion on top, drizzle remaining oil and scatter chives.

## Plating & Service
Three glossy red peppers per plate on a crimson pool — dramatic against white porcelain; serve with toasted coca bread.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fill with wild-mushroom duxelles and cashew cream for a vegan version; the coulis is excellent on grilled fish too.
