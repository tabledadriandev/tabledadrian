# Charred Leeks with Romesco, Egg Mimosa and Hazelnut Picada

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Leeks burnt whole until the outer leaves are ash, then peeled to reveal silk — the calçot experience without waiting for February.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Leeks, medium — 800 g (8)
- Romesco sauce — 200 g
- Eggs — 110 g (2)
- Hazelnuts, toasted — 40 g
- Garlic, small clove — 3 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 8 g
- Lemon zest — 2 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- Sherry vinegar — 10 ml
- Flaky sea salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Boil eggs 10 minutes; cool, peel, and grate whole through a fine grater for the mimosa.
2. Char whole untrimmed leeks over a very hot grill or open flame 20–25 minutes, turning, until blackened right through the outer layers.
3. Rest wrapped in newspaper or foil 10 minutes to steam tender.
4. Chop hazelnuts, garlic, parsley and lemon zest to a coarse picada; loosen with 40 ml oil and vinegar.
5. Strip away charred outer layers to the pale, silky core; trim roots and slice in half lengthwise.
6. Warm romesco; swipe over plates, lay leeks over, dress with picada, egg mimosa, salt and remaining oil.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm with plenty of napkins; the reveal of the pale core from the black husk deserves a table audience.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan without the egg; spring onions or baby fennel take the same treatment in less time.
