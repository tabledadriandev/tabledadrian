# Paella de Marisco with Sofrito Reduction and Socarrat

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The benchmark seafood paella — a long-cooked sofrito, proper fumet, and a deliberate socarrat crust; the rice is the star, the seafood its crown.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Bomba rice — 500 g
- Prawn shells and fish bones for fumet — 600 g
- Water — 1800 ml
- Raw prawns, peeled (shells reserved) — 400 g
- Squid, cleaned, rings — 300 g
- Mussels — 500 g
- Ripe tomatoes, grated — 300 g
- Ñora pepper flesh — 10 g
- Garlic, chopped — 15 g
- Sweet smoked paprika — 6 g
- Saffron threads — 0.5 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml
- Fine sea salt — 10 g
- Lemon — 160 g (2)

## Method
1. Make fumet: brown prawn shells and bones in 20 ml oil, cover with water, simmer 30 minutes, strain — you need 1500 ml, seasoned assertively.
2. Toast saffron in a dry ladle 10 seconds; steep in 50 ml hot fumet.
3. In a 46 cm paella pan, sear squid in 40 ml oil over high heat 2 minutes; push aside.
4. Add remaining oil, garlic and ñora flesh 30 seconds, then grated tomato; cook the sofrito 12–15 minutes until brick-dark and jammy. Bloom paprika 15 seconds.
5. Add rice; toast in the sofrito 2 minutes until pearly.
6. Pour in hot fumet and saffron; spread rice evenly — do not stir again. Boil hard 8 minutes.
7. Reduce to a simmer; nestle in prawns and mussels hinge-down at minute 10; cook until liquid is gone, 16–18 minutes total.
8. Raise heat 60–90 seconds, listening for a dry crackle, to form the socarrat; rest under a cloth 5 minutes.

## Plating & Service
Serve the pan itself on a trivet at the centre of the table, lemon halves around the rim; guests eat traditionally from their sector.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Rabbit-and-artichoke turns it into paella de la huerta; never add chorizo. If the rice outruns the liquid, finish 5 minutes at 200 °C in the oven.
