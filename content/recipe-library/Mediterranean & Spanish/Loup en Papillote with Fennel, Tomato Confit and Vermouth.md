# Loup en Papillote with Fennel, Tomato Confit and Vermouth

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sea bass steamed in its own parchment tent with vermouth and fennel — zero-mess galley cooking that opens in a cloud of anise.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Sea bass fillets, skin on — 640 g (4)
- Fennel bulb, shaved — 200 g
- Cherry tomato confit — 160 g
- Leek, fine julienne — 100 g
- Celery, fine julienne — 50 g
- Dry vermouth — 60 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 50 ml
- Lemon — 120 g
- Tarragon — 4 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 200 °C. Cut four 40 cm parchment hearts.
2. Toss fennel, leek and celery with 20 ml oil and salt; divide over one half of each heart.
3. Season fillets; lay skin-up on the vegetables with tomato confit, a lemon slice and tarragon.
4. Spoon 15 ml vermouth and 7 ml oil over each; fold and crimp the edges into a tight seal.
5. Bake on a preheated tray 14–16 minutes — the parcels should balloon; fish reaches 54 °C.
6. Serve the parcels sealed; open at the table with scissors for the steam moment.

## Plating & Service
One puffed parcel per guest, scissors offered; the first cut releases anise, sea and vermouth.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Any flaky white fish works; swap vermouth for 20 ml orange juice for alcohol-free.
