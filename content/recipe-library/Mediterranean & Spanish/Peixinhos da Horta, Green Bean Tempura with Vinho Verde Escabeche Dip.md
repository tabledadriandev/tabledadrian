# Peixinhos da Horta, Green Bean Tempura with Vinho Verde Escabeche Dip

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Portugal's 'little garden fish' — the beignet that inspired Japanese tempura — with a sharp vinho verde escabeche dip.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Green beans, topped — 300 g
- Plain flour — 120 g
- Cornflour — 40 g
- Cold sparkling water — 200 ml
- Fine sea salt — 5 g
- Sweet paprika — 2 g
- Sunflower oil for frying — 1000 ml
- Vinho verde — 80 ml
- White wine vinegar — 30 ml
- Carrot, fine julienne — 40 g
- Garlic, sliced — 5 g
- Bay leaf — 1 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 30 ml

## Method
1. For the dip, warm olive oil with garlic and bay 2 minutes; add carrot, vinho verde and vinegar, simmer 3 minutes, season and cool — it should be sharp and aromatic.
2. Blanch beans 90 seconds in salted boiling water; refresh and dry completely.
3. Whisk flours, salt and paprika with sparkling water into a thin batter.
4. Heat oil to 180 °C.
5. Dip beans in small bundles of 3–4, fry 2 minutes until crisp and barely coloured.
6. Drain, salt, and serve upright in a glass with the escabeche dip alongside.

## Plating & Service
Serve standing in a tumbler like breadsticks, dip in a low bowl; eat within five minutes of frying.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Asparagus or courgette batons take the same batter; a dusting of piri-piri lifts the dip.
