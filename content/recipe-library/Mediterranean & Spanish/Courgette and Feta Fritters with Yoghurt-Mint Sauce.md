# Courgette and Feta Fritters with Yoghurt-Mint Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Kolokithokeftedes done properly — courgette wrung bone-dry so the fritters crisp instead of steam; herby, salty, irresistible.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Courgettes — 600 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g
- Feta, crumbled — 150 g
- Spring onions, sliced — 50 g
- Dill, chopped — 10 g
- Mint, chopped — 6 g
- Eggs — 55 g (1 egg)
- Plain flour — 80 g
- Baking powder — 3 g
- Sunflower oil for shallow frying — 300 ml
- Greek yoghurt — 200 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Mint, extra, sliced — 4 g

## Method
1. Coarsely grate courgettes, toss with salt, rest 15 minutes, then wring in a cloth to extract at least 150 ml liquid.
2. Mix courgette with feta, spring onion, dill, mint and egg.
3. Fold in flour and baking powder just until combined — do not overwork.
4. Stir yoghurt with lemon juice and extra mint for the sauce; chill.
5. Heat 1 cm oil to 175 °C in a wide pan.
6. Drop in 40 g spoonfuls, flatten slightly; fry 2–3 minutes per side to deep gold.
7. Drain on paper, salt lightly, serve hot with the yoghurt-mint sauce.

## Plating & Service
Stack in a loose pyramid with sauce in a bowl at the base; lemon wedges and picked dill over the top.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF flour blend works perfectly; bake at 200 °C for 18 minutes on an oiled tray for a lighter take.
