# Tuna Empedrat, White Bean and Tomato Salad from the Catalan Coast

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Catalonia's 'paved' bean salad — big white beans, tuna, tomato and olives, dressed hard and served cool; ballast for beach days.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Cooked large white beans (mongetes), drained — 500 g
- Tuna confit in olive oil, drained — 200 g
- Ripe tomatoes — 300 g
- Red onion — 60 g
- Green pepper — 60 g
- Black olives — 60 g
- Boiled eggs (optional) — 110 g (2)
- Sherry vinegar — 25 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- Flat-leaf parsley — 8 g
- Fine sea salt — 4 g

## Method
1. Rinse beans gently and drain well — they must be intact, not mushy.
2. Dice tomatoes and pepper 1 cm; sliver the onion and soak in the vinegar 10 minutes.
3. Whisk the onion-vinegar with oil and salt into a sharp dressing.
4. Fold beans, tomatoes and pepper with the dressing — gently, to keep the 'pavement' whole.
5. Flake tuna over in large pieces; add olives and quartered eggs.
6. Rest 20 minutes at cool room temperature; finish with parsley.

## Plating & Service
Serve on a wide platter at cellar temperature — cold kills the tomato; crusty bread on the side.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan without tuna and egg, adding capers and 100 g artichokes; salt-cod shreds make it an empedrat de bacallà.
