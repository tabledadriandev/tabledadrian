# Frisee, Fig and Duck Prosciutto Salad with Banyuls Dressing

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bitter frisée, ripe figs and rosy duck ham under a Banyuls-vinegar dressing — the Riviera's back-country answer to a bistro classic.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Frisée hearts, pale centres — 200 g
- Fresh figs — 320 g (4)
- Duck prosciutto, thin slices — 80 g
- Walnuts — 40 g
- Banyuls vinegar — 20 ml
- Dijon mustard — 6 g
- Honey — 5 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 55 ml
- Chives — 4 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Toast walnuts at 160 °C for 5 minutes; chop roughly.
2. Whisk vinegar, mustard and honey; emulsify in the oil.
3. Wash and thoroughly dry frisée; tear into forkable tufts.
4. Quarter the figs through the stem.
5. Toss frisée with two-thirds of the dressing; lift into loose nests on plates.
6. Tuck in figs and draped duck prosciutto; finish with walnuts, chives, pepper and remaining dressing.

## Plating & Service
Airy, tall plating — frisée should look like it landed, not was placed; serve promptly before the leaves wilt.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Jamón or bresaola substitute for duck; vegan with smoked tempeh ribbons and maple in the dressing.
