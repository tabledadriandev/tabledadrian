# Pimientos de Padrón with Smoked Salt and Idiazábal Shavings

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Blistered green peppers, Russian-roulette hot ones included — 8 minutes from galley to deck, the fastest crowd-pleaser aboard.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Padrón peppers — 300 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 30 ml
- Smoked flaky sea salt — 4 g
- Idiazábal cheese (or aged Manchego) — 40 g
- Lemon — 40 g (1/2 fruit)

## Method
1. Wash and thoroughly dry the peppers — water causes spitting.
2. Heat a heavy frying pan or plancha until smoking, around 240 °C.
3. Add oil, then peppers in a single layer; cook 5–7 minutes, tossing every minute, until blistered and collapsed on all sides.
4. Tip onto a warm plate and immediately season with smoked salt.
5. Shave Idiazábal over the hot peppers with a peeler so it softens slightly.
6. Serve with the lemon half for squeezing.

## Plating & Service
Pile high in a rustic bowl, cheese curls on top; eat with fingers by the stem while hot.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Omit cheese for vegan; toss with 5 ml sherry vinegar and toasted sesame for a different register.
