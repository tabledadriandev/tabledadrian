# Trempo Mallorquin Chopped Salad with Capers and Herbs

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Mallorca's chopped summer salad — pepper, tomato and onion in near-equal parts, cut small and dressed simply; the ultimate grilled-fish sidekick.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Ripe tomatoes — 400 g
- Green Italian peppers — 200 g
- White or new onion — 150 g
- Capers, rinsed — 20 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- White wine vinegar — 12 ml
- Purslane or flat-leaf parsley — 15 g
- Fine sea salt — 4 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Dice tomatoes, peppers and onion uniformly at 8 mm — the even cut is the identity of trempó.
2. Soak the diced onion in cold water 10 minutes to soften its bite; drain and dry.
3. Combine vegetables with capers in a wide bowl.
4. Season with salt and vinegar first, tossing well.
5. Add oil and toss again; rest 15 minutes at room temperature.
6. Fold through purslane or parsley and finish with pepper.

## Plating & Service
Serve in a shallow bowl next to anything grilled; spoon the juices over fish like a raw sauce.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add apricot or peach dice in July as the Mallorcans do; a tin of good sardines turns it into lunch.
