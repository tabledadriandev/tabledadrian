# Tomates Anciennes with Whipped Brousse, Olive Soil and Aged Balsamic

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Heirloom tomatoes at room temperature over whipped brousse, with a crunchy black-olive 'soil' — a study in how little perfect tomatoes need.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Heirloom tomatoes, mixed colours — 700 g
- Brousse or fresh ricotta — 200 g
- Double cream — 40 ml
- Lemon zest — 2 g
- Black olives, pitted — 100 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- Aged balsamic vinegar — 15 ml
- Basil, small leaves — 6 g
- Flaky sea salt — 4 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Dry the olives on paper, then dehydrate at 120 °C for 30 minutes until leathery; cool and chop to a coarse 'soil'.
2. Whip brousse with cream and lemon zest until light; season lightly.
3. Slice tomatoes in varied cuts — thick rounds, wedges, halves — and season with flaky salt 10 minutes ahead.
4. Swipe whipped brousse across each plate.
5. Arrange tomatoes with height and colour contrast; spoon over their salted juices.
6. Scatter olive soil, drizzle oil and balsamic, finish with basil and pepper.

## Plating & Service
Room-temperature tomatoes only — never fridge-cold; serve as a stand-alone course with grilled bread.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan with whipped almond ricotta; a few drops of orange blossom water in the tomato juices is a quiet Provençal trick.
