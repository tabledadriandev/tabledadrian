# Salmorejo Cordobés with Ibérico Ham and Grated Egg

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Thicker, richer cousin of gazpacho — Córdoba's tomato-bread cream, cold and dense enough to hold its classic ham-and-egg garnish.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Very ripe tomatoes — 1000 g
- Stale white bread (telera or baguette) — 150 g
- Garlic — 4 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 120 ml
- Sherry vinegar — 10 ml
- Fine sea salt — 8 g
- Eggs — 110 g (2 eggs)
- Ibérico ham, fine julienne — 60 g

## Method
1. Blend tomatoes on high 2 minutes; pass through a sieve to remove skin and seeds.
2. Add torn bread to the tomato juice; soak 15 minutes.
3. Blend with garlic, salt and vinegar for 3 minutes.
4. Stream in 100 ml olive oil until the cream turns pale coral and thick enough to coat a spoon heavily.
5. Chill to 6 °C, minimum 2 hours.
6. Boil eggs 10 minutes; cool and grate coarsely. Serve salmorejo topped with egg, ham and remaining oil.

## Plating & Service
Serve in shallow bowls — the garnish should sit proud on the surface, not sink; a Picual oil finish shines here.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Omit ham for vegetarian (as tagged) or replace with crisped capers and shaved fennel; never thin it — thickness is the identity.
