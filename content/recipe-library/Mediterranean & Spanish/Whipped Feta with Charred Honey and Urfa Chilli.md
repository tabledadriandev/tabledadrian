# Whipped Feta with Charred Honey and Urfa Chilli

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Silky whipped feta under a blistered honey drizzle — the mezze that anchors every Greek spread on deck.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Barrel-aged feta — 300 g
- Greek yoghurt, strained — 100 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 40 ml
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Thyme honey — 40 g
- Urfa chilli flakes — 2 g
- Fresh oregano leaves — 3 g
- Toasted sesame seeds — 5 g

## Method
1. Blend feta, yoghurt, 30 ml olive oil and lemon juice in a food processor for 2 minutes until completely smooth and mousse-like.
2. Chill 30 minutes to firm slightly.
3. Warm honey in a small pan over high heat until it bubbles and takes a light char at the edges, about 90 seconds.
4. Swoosh the whipped feta across a shallow bowl with the back of a spoon.
5. Pour the hot honey over in ribbons — it will sizzle against the cold feta.
6. Finish with Urfa chilli, oregano, sesame and remaining olive oil.

## Plating & Service
Serve with warm pita triangles and crudités; the hot-cold contrast is the point, so finish à la minute.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Use a firm plant-based feta and agave for vegan; swap Urfa for Aleppo pepper or crushed pink pepper.
