# Sea Bass Crudo with Gazpacho Water, Green Almonds and Basil Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Raw sea bass under a crystal-clear gazpacho water — all the flavour of Andalusia with none of the colour; a quiet showpiece.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Wild sea bass fillet, skinned, sashimi-grade — 320 g
- Ripe tomatoes — 500 g
- Cucumber — 100 g
- Green pepper — 40 g
- Sherry vinegar — 10 ml
- Fine sea salt — 8 g
- Green almonds or blanched marcona, slivered — 30 g
- Basil leaves — 15 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- Cherry tomatoes, quartered — 80 g
- Espelette pepper — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Blend tomatoes, cucumber, pepper, vinegar and 5 g salt; pour into a muslin-lined sieve over a bowl and let drip in the fridge 4 hours — never press. Collect the clear consommé.
2. Blanch basil 10 seconds, shock, squeeze; blend with oil and strain for a vivid basil oil.
3. Slice the bass against the grain into 5 mm tiles; season with remaining salt 5 minutes ahead.
4. Arrange fish in shallow chilled bowls with cherry tomatoes and almond slivers.
5. Pour 60–70 ml gazpacho water around each portion at the table.
6. Finish with basil oil droplets and Espelette.

## Plating & Service
Tableside pour is the moment — keep the consommé at 4 °C in a chilled jug; serve with cava.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Any pristine white fish or scallop works; the gazpacho water also makes a stunning vegan dish over burrata-style tofu.
