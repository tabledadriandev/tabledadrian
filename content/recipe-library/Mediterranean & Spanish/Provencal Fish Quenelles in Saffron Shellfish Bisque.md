# Provencal Fish Quenelles in Saffron Shellfish Bisque

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Featherlight pike-perch quenelles poached then gratinéed in a saffron shellfish bisque — Lyon technique gone to the coast.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Pike-perch or whiting fillet, iced — 400 g
- Egg whites — 60 g (2)
- Double cream, iced — 300 ml
- Nutmeg — 0.3 g
- Prawn shells — 300 g
- Brandy — 30 ml
- Tomato paste — 30 g
- Fish fumet — 600 ml
- Saffron — 0.3 g
- Unsalted butter — 40 g
- Plain flour — 20 g
- Gruyère, grated — 40 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 30 ml
- Fine sea salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Blitz iced fish with 5 g salt to a paste; add egg whites, then pulse in 250 ml iced cream. Pass through a sieve; chill 1 hour. Test-poach a spoonful — it should hold and float.
2. Roast prawn shells in oil over high heat 4 minutes; flambé with brandy.
3. Add tomato paste, then fumet; simmer 20 minutes, pressing shells; strain hard.
4. Make a blond roux with butter and flour; whisk in the bisque and saffron, simmer 5 minutes, finish with remaining cream. Season.
5. Shape quenelles with two hot wet spoons; poach in 90 °C salted water 8–10 minutes until doubled and firm.
6. Drain quenelles into a gratin dish, flood with bisque, top with Gruyère.
7. Bake at 220 °C for 8–10 minutes until puffed and bronzed. Serve bubbling.

## Plating & Service
Individual gratin dishes are worth the washing-up — quenelles soufflé in the oven and must be eaten within minutes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Salmon quenelles with dill bisque for a northern accent; the bisque alone, loosened, is a first-course soup.
