# Espardenyes-Style Squid and White Bean Cassoulet of the Costa Brava

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Mar i muntanya in a single pot — plancha-seared squid over creamy white beans in a picada-thickened jus; Costa Brava soul food.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Squid, cleaned, scored — 600 g
- Cooked white beans (santa pau or cannellini) — 500 g
- Chicken or fish stock — 300 ml
- Onion, brunoise — 100 g
- Tomato, grated — 150 g
- Garlic — 12 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- Blanched almonds, toasted — 25 g
- Fried bread — 15 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 10 g
- Dry white wine — 60 ml
- Celery leaf — 3 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Pound almonds, fried bread, 4 g garlic and half the parsley to a coarse picada.
2. Sweat onion in 30 ml oil 6 minutes; add remaining garlic and tomato, cooking 8 minutes to a sofrito.
3. Deglaze with wine; add beans and stock, simmering gently 10 minutes.
4. Stir in the picada; simmer 5 minutes until the jus coats a spoon. Season.
5. Score squid bodies in a fine diamond pattern; dry thoroughly.
6. Sear on a smoking plancha with remaining oil, 60–90 seconds per side, until curled and charred.
7. Serve squid over the beans, celery leaf and remaining parsley on top.

## Plating & Service
Shallow bowls: beans first, squid curls standing proud; a Costa Brava garnatxa alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> True espardenyes (sea cucumbers) if you find them — sear 60 seconds only; chickpeas swap for beans happily.
