# Loukoumades with Thyme Honey, Walnut Praline and Yoghurt Sorbet

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Feather-light honey doughnuts — a slack yeasted batter dropped by wet spoon — with walnut praline crunch and a sharp yoghurt sorbet against the sweetness.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Plain flour — 250 g
- Water, warm — 280 ml
- Fresh yeast — 10 g
- Caster sugar — 15 g (batter) + 100 g (praline)
- Fine sea salt — 4 g
- Sunflower oil for frying — 1500 ml
- Thyme honey — 120 g
- Walnuts, toasted — 80 g
- Greek yoghurt — 400 g
- Icing sugar — 80 g
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Ground cinnamon — 1 g

## Method
1. Sorbet: whisk yoghurt, icing sugar and lemon; churn (or still-freeze, whisking hourly for 3 hours).
2. Praline: cook 100 g sugar dry to amber caramel; stir in walnuts, pour onto parchment, cool and crush.
3. Whisk flour, yeast, 15 g sugar, salt and warm water into a thick batter; prove 45–60 minutes until bubbling.
4. Heat oil to 175 °C.
5. Drop walnut-sized spoonfuls with a wet spoon; fry 3–4 minutes, rolling, until golden and hollow.
6. Drain 30 seconds; toss immediately in warmed thyme honey.
7. Serve a pile per bowl with praline, cinnamon and a quenelle of yoghurt sorbet.

## Plating & Service
Hot, honeyed and disappearing fast — build bowls at the last second so the sorbet survives the introduction.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan with maple and coconut yoghurt sorbet; a grating of orange zest into the honey lifts everything.
