# Duck Breast a la Provencale with Lavender Honey, Figs and Panisse

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Crisp-skinned duck lacquered with lavender honey and thyme, roasted figs, and golden chickpea panisse batons — inland Provence for the last night of charter.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Duck breasts — 700 g (2 large)
- Lavender honey — 40 g
- Thyme — 3 g
- Sherry vinegar — 20 ml
- Fresh figs, halved — 300 g
- Chickpea flour — 150 g
- Water — 450 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- Duck jus or dark stock — 150 ml
- Lavender flowers, culinary — 0.5 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Panisse: whisk chickpea flour into cold water with 3 g salt and 20 ml oil; cook, stirring, 8–10 minutes to a very thick polenta texture. Spread 2 cm deep; chill 2 hours, cut into batons.
2. Score duck skin in a tight crosshatch; salt 30 minutes ahead.
3. Start duck skin-down in a cold pan over medium heat; render 8–10 minutes to glass-crisp, pouring off fat.
4. Flip 2 minutes, then rest; brush skin with honey warmed with thyme and vinegar; roast at 180 °C for 4–6 minutes to 54 °C core. Rest 8 minutes.
5. Fry panisse batons in duck fat and remaining oil at 180 °C until golden, 3 minutes; salt.
6. Roast figs cut-side up with a drop of honey at 180 °C for 6 minutes; warm the jus with lavender, strain after 60 seconds.
7. Slice duck; plate with figs, panisse and lavender jus, pepper over.

## Plating & Service
Fan the rosy slices, skin gleaming; panisse stacked like kindling — lavender should whisper, never shout.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap figs for roasted apricots in early summer; the panisse alone, with rouille, makes a superb apéro.
