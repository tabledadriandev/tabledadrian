# Atun Encebollado with PX Onions and Crushed White Beans

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Cádiz tuna smothered in onions melted with Pedro Ximénez — sweet-savoury depth over garlicky crushed beans; almadraba tradition, weeknight effort.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Tuna loin, 3 cm steaks — 700 g (4)
- Onions, thinly sliced — 600 g
- Pedro Ximénez sherry — 60 ml
- Sherry vinegar — 15 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 80 ml
- Garlic — 10 g
- Bay leaf — 1 g
- Sweet smoked paprika — 3 g
- Cooked white beans — 400 g
- Fish or vegetable stock — 100 ml
- Flat-leaf parsley — 8 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Cook onions with 40 ml oil, bay and a pinch of salt over low heat, covered 10 minutes, then uncovered 15 more, until deeply golden.
2. Add PX and vinegar; reduce to a glossy, jammy tangle. Bloom paprika through.
3. Warm beans with stock, 6 g sliced garlic and 20 ml oil; crush half coarsely with a fork; season and keep warm.
4. Salt tuna; sear in remaining oil over high heat 90 seconds per side — rosy centre, about 45 °C.
5. Rest tuna 3 minutes on the onions so juices mingle.
6. Plate beans, tuna, onions over; parsley and a thread of raw oil.

## Plating & Service
Rustic and generous — the onions should cascade off the tuna into the beans.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swordfish or bonito substitute; cook tuna through 2 minutes more for traditionalists, though rosy is kinder to it.
