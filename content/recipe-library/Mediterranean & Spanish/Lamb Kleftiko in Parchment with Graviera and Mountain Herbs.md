# Lamb Kleftiko in Parchment with Graviera and Mountain Herbs

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> 'Stolen' lamb — sealed in parchment with potatoes, graviera and oregano and left alone for 3.5 hours; the laziest spectacular main on the boat.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Lamb shoulder, bone-in, cut into 6 pieces — 1800 g
- Waxy potatoes, thick wedges — 800 g
- Red onions, quartered — 200 g
- Garlic cloves, whole — 30 g
- Graviera or kefalotyri, cubed — 150 g
- Ripe tomatoes, quartered — 300 g
- Lemon juice — 50 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 80 ml
- Dried oregano — 5 g
- Fresh rosemary — 4 g
- Bay leaves — 2 g
- Fine sea salt — 12 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Rub lamb with salt, pepper, oregano and half the lemon juice; rest 30 minutes (overnight is better).
2. Line a deep roasting dish with two large crossed sheets of parchment.
3. Layer potatoes, onions, tomatoes and garlic; nestle lamb on top with cheese cubes, rosemary and bay.
4. Pour over oil and remaining lemon; fold the parchment into a sealed parcel, tying with string.
5. Bake at 160 °C for 3–3.5 hours without peeking — the lamb should pull apart at a touch.
6. Open the parcel at the table; rest 10 minutes in its own steam before serving.

## Plating & Service
Bring the whole parcel out and open before the guests — steam, herbs and lamb perfume do the talking.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Goat shoulder is traditional in the islands; dairy-free without cheese, adding 100 g olives instead.
