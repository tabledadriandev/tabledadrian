# Tarte Tropezienne Petites with Orange Blossom Creme Mousseline

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Saint-Tropez's brioche-and-cream tart in individual form — pearl-sugar brioche split and filled with an orange-blossom mousseline; the Riviera's dessert.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Strong flour — 300 g
- Eggs — 165 g (3)
- Whole milk — 60 ml
- Fresh yeast — 12 g
- Caster sugar — 40 g (dough) + 120 g (cream)
- Fine sea salt — 5 g
- Unsalted butter — 90 g (dough) + 150 g (mousseline)
- Pearl sugar — 40 g
- Whole milk (cream) — 400 ml
- Egg yolks — 80 g (4)
- Cornflour — 35 g
- Orange blossom water — 8 ml
- Icing sugar — 15 g

## Method
1. Dough: mix flour, eggs, milk, yeast, 40 g sugar and salt; knead 8 minutes, then work in 90 g soft butter until silky. Prove 90 minutes; chill 2 hours.
2. Crème pâtissière: whisk yolks, 120 g sugar and cornflour; cook with milk heated to 85 °C, whisking, 2 minutes at a boil. Film and chill.
3. Shape 8 buns of 80 g; prove 60 minutes until pillowy; brush with egg wash (from reserved egg), crown with pearl sugar.
4. Bake at 180 °C for 18–20 minutes; cool completely.
5. Beat 150 g soft butter; whip in the smooth cold pastry cream in thirds, then orange blossom water — a light mousseline.
6. Split buns; pipe generous rosettes, cap, and dust with icing sugar. Chill 30 minutes before serving.

## Plating & Service
One petite tropézienne per guest, slightly cool; the pearl sugar top should crunch against the perfumed cream.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> A single 24 cm tart serves the table family-style; add crushed raspberries in the cream in July.
