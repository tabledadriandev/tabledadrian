# Burrata with Grilled Apricots, Lavender Honey and Pistachio Dukkah

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Creamy burrata against warm grilled apricots and a lavender-honey drizzle — high summer in Provence on one plate, six minutes of cooking.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Burrata — 250 g (2 balls)
- Apricots, firm-ripe — 400 g (6)
- Lavender honey — 30 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 45 ml
- Pistachios, toasted — 30 g
- Coriander seeds — 3 g
- Cumin seeds — 2 g
- Sesame seeds — 8 g
- Lemon zest — 2 g
- Flaky sea salt — 3 g
- Small basil leaves — 4 g

## Method
1. Toast coriander, cumin and sesame in a dry pan 90 seconds; crush coarsely with pistachios and a pinch of salt for the dukkah.
2. Bring burrata to room temperature — 20 minutes out of the fridge.
3. Halve and stone apricots; brush with 15 ml oil.
4. Grill flesh-side down over high heat 2–3 minutes until caramelised with clear marks.
5. Warm honey to 40 °C so it pours in threads.
6. Tear burrata onto plates, arrange apricots, drizzle honey and remaining oil, scatter dukkah, lemon zest, basil, salt.

## Plating & Service
Serve on individual plates while apricots are warm and cheese cool; grilled bread turns it into a light lunch.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Peaches or figs swap in seamlessly; nut-free dukkah with sunflower seeds.
