# Gambas al Ajillo with Fino Sherry and Lemon Confit

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sizzling garlic prawns finished with a splash of fino — arrives at the table still bubbling, the smell alone sells the next course.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Raw prawns, peeled, tail on — 400 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 100 ml
- Garlic, thinly sliced — 30 g
- Dried guindilla chilli, snipped — 2 g
- Fino sherry — 40 ml
- Lemon confit peel, brunoise — 10 g
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 8 g
- Fine sea salt — 4 g

## Method
1. Salt the prawns and rest 10 minutes at room temperature.
2. Warm olive oil in a terracotta cazuela or heavy pan over medium heat to about 140 °C.
3. Add garlic and chilli; cook 90 seconds until the garlic is pale gold and fragrant, never brown.
4. Raise heat to high, add prawns in one layer; cook 60 seconds per side until just opaque.
5. Deglaze with fino sherry, letting it bubble 30 seconds; stir in lemon confit.
6. Remove from heat, shower with parsley and serve in the cooking vessel.

## Plating & Service
Bring the cazuela to the table on a wooden trivet with bread for the oil; guests should hear it sizzle.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Use mushrooms and butter beans instead of prawns for a vegan cazuela; omit sherry and add 10 ml lemon juice for alcohol-free.
