# Sesame and Honey Pasteli Tart with Salted Chocolate and Orange

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Ancient Greek sesame-honey pasteli reimagined as a tart layer — chewy sesame caramel over crisp pastry under salted dark chocolate.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Plain flour — 180 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- Icing sugar — 30 g
- Cold water — 40 ml
- Fine sea salt — 3 g
- Sesame seeds — 180 g
- Thyme honey — 160 g
- Caster sugar — 40 g
- Orange zest — 4 g
- Dark chocolate 70% — 150 g
- Flaky sea salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Mix flour, icing sugar and 3 g salt with oil and water into a supple dough; press into a 24 cm tart ring, dock, chill 30 minutes.
2. Bake blind at 170 °C for 18–20 minutes until sandy gold; cool.
3. Toast sesame seeds in a dry pan until pale gold and popping, 4 minutes.
4. Cook honey and caster sugar to 121 °C; stir in sesame and orange zest.
5. Pour the pasteli over the base, spreading fast to the edges; cool 30 minutes until chewy-set.
6. Melt chocolate to 45 °C, cool to 31 °C, and spread a thin layer over; scatter flaky salt.
7. Set 1 hour at cool room temperature; cut with a hot knife.

## Plating & Service
Thin wedges — it is rich; the three strata (crisp, chew, snap) should show clean at the cut.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF base with almond flour and 20 g cornflour; a whisper of chilli in the chocolate is a fine trick.
