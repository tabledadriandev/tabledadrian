# Octopus, Potato and Green Olive Salad with Smoked Paprika Vinaigrette

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Warm octopus and waxy potato salad in the Portuguese manner — dressed while warm so the vinaigrette penetrates; picnic-proof.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Octopus tentacles, cooked tender — 500 g
- Waxy potatoes — 400 g
- Green olives, cracked — 80 g
- Red onion, shaved — 50 g
- Celery, thin slices — 60 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 80 ml
- Red wine vinegar — 25 ml
- Sweet smoked paprika — 4 g
- Garlic, grated — 3 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 10 g
- Fine sea salt — 4 g

## Method
1. If starting from raw, simmer octopus at 90 °C for 50–60 minutes until tender; cool in its liquid.
2. Simmer potatoes whole in salted water until tender, about 20 minutes; peel and slice 1 cm thick while hot.
3. Whisk oil, vinegar, paprika, garlic and salt into a rust-red vinaigrette.
4. Slice octopus into 2 cm coins; toss with the warm potatoes and half the vinaigrette; rest 15 minutes.
5. Fold in olives, onion and celery.
6. Finish with remaining vinaigrette and a heavy shower of parsley; serve just warm or at room temperature.

## Plating & Service
Mound casually on a platter — this is honest food; serve with lemon and a crisp white.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chickpeas replace potato for a higher-protein version; a chargrill pass on the cooked octopus adds smoke.
