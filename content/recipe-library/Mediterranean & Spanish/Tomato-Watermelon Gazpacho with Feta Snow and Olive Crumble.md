# Tomato-Watermelon Gazpacho with Feta Snow and Olive Crumble

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> August gazpacho cut with watermelon for sweetness, finished with frozen-grated feta 'snow' — cold, saline, faintly absurd, entirely delicious.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Ripe tomatoes — 700 g
- Watermelon flesh, deseeded — 400 g
- Cucumber — 100 g
- Red pepper — 80 g
- Sherry vinegar — 18 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- Fine sea salt — 7 g
- Feta, frozen solid — 80 g
- Black olives, pitted — 60 g
- Basil leaves — 5 g

## Method
1. Dry the olives; dehydrate at 120 °C for 30 minutes, then chop to a crumble.
2. Blend tomatoes, watermelon, cucumber, pepper, vinegar and salt for 3 minutes.
3. Stream in 60 ml oil; pass through a fine sieve.
4. Chill to 5 °C for at least 2 hours; check seasoning cold — cold mutes salt.
5. Pour into chilled bowls.
6. Grate the frozen feta over each like snow; finish with olive crumble, basil and remaining oil.

## Plating & Service
The feta snow melts into the pink soup within a minute — grate it at the table for the effect.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan with frozen plant feta or omit; a drop of rose water is a Sevillian summer trick.
