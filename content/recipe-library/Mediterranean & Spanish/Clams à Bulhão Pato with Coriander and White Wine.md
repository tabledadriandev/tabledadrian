# Clams à Bulhão Pato with Coriander and White Wine

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Lisbon's garlicky clams drowned in coriander and white wine — ten minutes of work, and the broth is the real prize.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Fresh clams (palourdes), purged — 1000 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 50 ml
- Garlic, thinly sliced — 15 g
- Dry white wine (vinho verde) — 120 ml
- Coriander, chopped, stems separated — 25 g
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Black pepper — 1 g
- Piri-piri or chilli flakes — 1 g

## Method
1. Scrub clams; discard any that stay open when tapped.
2. Warm oil in a wide lidded pan; cook garlic and coriander stems over medium heat 90 seconds without colouring.
3. Add chilli, then clams and wine; cover immediately.
4. Steam over high heat 3–5 minutes, shaking the pan, until the clams open.
5. Discard any that remain closed; off heat, add lemon juice and most of the coriander leaves.
6. Toss well and serve straight away with the broth.

## Plating & Service
A deep, wide bowl, broth ladled over, remaining coriander on top; bread mandatory, spoons welcome.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 100 g chopped chouriço with the garlic for a smoky version; mussels work identically.
