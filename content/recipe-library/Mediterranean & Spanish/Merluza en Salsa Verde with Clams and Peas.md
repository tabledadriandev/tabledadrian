# Merluza en Salsa Verde with Clams and Peas

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Basque hake in a trembling green sauce — the emulsion built by swirling the pan, never whisking; clams open into it, peas sweeten it.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Hake loins, skin on — 640 g (4)
- Clams, purged — 400 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml
- Garlic, thinly sliced — 15 g
- Plain flour — 10 g
- Dry white wine (txakoli) — 100 ml
- Fish fumet — 200 ml
- Peas, fresh or frozen — 120 g
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 25 g
- Guindilla chilli — 1 g
- Fine sea salt — 7 g

## Method
1. Salt the hake 15 minutes ahead; pat dry.
2. Warm oil with garlic and guindilla in a wide shallow pan over low heat until the garlic barely gilds.
3. Add flour, stirring 60 seconds without colour; add wine and fumet gradually, swirling the pan.
4. Simmer 3 minutes; add half the parsley.
5. Lay in the hake skin-up with the clams; cook at a gentle simmer 4 minutes, swirling the pan constantly in circles — the gelatine and oil emulsify into a pale-green cream.
6. Turn the fish, add peas, cook 3–4 minutes more, swirling, until clams open and hake reaches 52 °C.
7. Rain over the remaining parsley; serve straight from the pan.

## Plating & Service
The pan comes to the table; the sauce should tremble, not pour. Bread for every drop.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF by omitting flour and swirling longer — the fish gelatine will bind; monkfish or cod behave identically.
