# Taramasalata Blonde with Bottarga Snow

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A pale, mousse-light tarama — bread-based, whipped with grassy olive oil, finished with grated bottarga for a double hit of the sea.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- White cod or carp roe (tarama) — 100 g
- Stale white bread, crustless — 120 g
- Cold water — 100 ml
- Shallot, grated — 20 g
- Lemon juice — 45 ml
- Sunflower oil — 200 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 50 ml
- Bottarga — 15 g
- Chives — 4 g

## Method
1. Soak bread in cold water 5 minutes, then squeeze to a moist pulp.
2. Blend roe, bread, shallot and half the lemon juice for 1 minute.
3. With the motor running, stream in sunflower oil slowly as for mayonnaise until pale and mousse-like.
4. Loosen with 20–30 ml iced water to a soft, spoonable ribbon; season with remaining lemon juice.
5. Fold in 30 ml olive oil by hand to keep the emulsion light.
6. Chill 1 hour; serve topped with remaining oil, finely grated bottarga and chives.

## Plating & Service
A generous sweep in a shallow bowl, bottarga grated over like snow at the last second; warm pita and radishes alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF with cooked potato replacing bread; skip bottarga and add smoked paprika oil for a budget version.
