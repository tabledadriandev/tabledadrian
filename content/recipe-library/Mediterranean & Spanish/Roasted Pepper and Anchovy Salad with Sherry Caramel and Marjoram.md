# Roasted Pepper and Anchovy Salad with Sherry Caramel and Marjoram

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Fire-roasted peppers in ribbons under anchovy and a sherry-vinegar caramel — sweet, smoky, saline; keeps three days and improves daily.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Red peppers — 600 g (4)
- Yellow peppers — 300 g (2)
- Anchovy fillets — 40 g
- Garlic, sliced paper-thin — 6 g
- Caster sugar — 25 g
- Sherry vinegar — 30 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- Marjoram or oregano leaves — 3 g
- Flaky sea salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Roast whole peppers at 250 °C (or over flame) for 30–40 minutes, turning, until blackened all over.
2. Steam in a covered bowl 15 minutes; peel, deseed and tear into wide ribbons — never rinse, the juices are gold.
3. Strain and reserve the pepper juices.
4. Cook sugar to a light caramel; carefully add vinegar and pepper juices, reduce 2 minutes to a syrup.
5. Layer peppers with garlic, anchovy and half the oil; pour over the warm sherry caramel.
6. Rest 1 hour minimum; serve at room temperature with marjoram, remaining oil and flaky salt.

## Plating & Service
A shallow platter, ribbons glistening in the dark caramel; grilled bread or as a bed for grilled fish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan without anchovy, adding capers; keeps refrigerated 3 days under oil — flavour peaks on day two.
