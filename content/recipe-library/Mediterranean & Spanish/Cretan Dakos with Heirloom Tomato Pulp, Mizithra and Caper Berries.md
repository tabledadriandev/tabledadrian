# Cretan Dakos with Heirloom Tomato Pulp, Mizithra and Caper Berries

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Crete's barley-rusk 'bruschetta' — the rusk drinks tomato pulp and oil until it half-softens; assembled in minutes, eaten in fewer.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Barley rusks (paximadi) — 240 g (4)
- Very ripe heirloom tomatoes — 600 g
- Mizithra or fresh ricotta salata — 150 g
- Caper berries — 40 g
- Kalamata olives — 40 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 80 ml
- Red wine vinegar — 8 ml
- Dried oregano — 2 g
- Flaky sea salt — 3 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Grate tomatoes on a box grater into a bowl; discard skins. Season the pulp with salt and vinegar.
2. Rest the pulp 10 minutes; it should be juicy, not drained — the juice is the point.
3. Splash each rusk with 10 ml water and a spoon of tomato juice to begin softening.
4. Spoon the tomato pulp generously over the rusks.
5. Crumble mizithra over; add caper berries and olives.
6. Finish with oregano, pepper and a heavy pour of olive oil; rest 5 minutes before serving.

## Plating & Service
Serve on individual plates with knife and fork — the base should yield at the edge, stay crunchy at heart.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF rusks work identically; plant feta for vegan. Top with anchovy for a fisherman's version.
