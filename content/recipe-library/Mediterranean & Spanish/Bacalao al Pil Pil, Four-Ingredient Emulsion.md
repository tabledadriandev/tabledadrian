# Bacalao al Pil Pil, Four-Ingredient Emulsion

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Salt cod, olive oil, garlic, chilli — nothing else; the sauce is the cod's own gelatine whipped into the oil by patient rotation. Pure technique.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Salt cod loins, desalinated, skin on — 700 g (4 thick pieces)
- Extra virgin olive oil — 300 ml
- Garlic, sliced into coins — 25 g
- Dried guindilla chilli, rings — 2 g

## Method
1. Warm oil with garlic and chilli in a wide pan to 130 °C; fry until the garlic is pale gold. Lift out garlic and chilli; reserve.
2. Cool the oil to 60–70 °C — this is critical; hotter oil will never emulsify.
3. Lay in the cod skin-side up; confit 8–10 minutes at 65 °C, spooning oil over — white gelatine beads will weep from the fish.
4. Lift the cod out; pour off the oil, keeping the gelatinous juices in the pan.
5. Add oil back a ladle at a time, rotating the pan in steady circles (or whisking with a fine sieve) until each addition emulsifies into a thick ivory pil pil.
6. Return the cod to warm through 2 minutes; crown with the reserved garlic coins and chilli rings.

## Plating & Service
Napped thickly with the pil pil, garlic chips on top; a dish that earns silence at the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fresh cod works if brined 4 hours in 8% salt solution first; the sieve-whisk trick rescues any split sauce.
