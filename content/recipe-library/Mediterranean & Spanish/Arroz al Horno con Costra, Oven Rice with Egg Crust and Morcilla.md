# Arroz al Horno con Costra, Oven Rice with Egg Crust and Morcilla

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Alicante's oven-baked rice sealed under a soufflé-light egg crust — the cazuela goes to the table with the crust puffed and golden.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Bomba rice — 450 g
- Chicken stock, hot — 1000 ml
- Chicken thighs, boneless, chunks — 400 g
- Morcilla (blood sausage), sliced — 150 g
- Cooked chickpeas — 200 g
- Tomato, sliced — 150 g
- Garlic, whole head — 60 g
- Sweet smoked paprika — 5 g
- Saffron — 0.3 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- Eggs — 330 g (6)
- Fine sea salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 200 °C with a wide cazuela or 30 cm ovenproof pan inside.
2. Brown chicken in oil on the hob, 6 minutes; add morcilla 2 minutes; remove both.
3. Bloom paprika in the fat 15 seconds; add rice and toast 2 minutes.
4. Add hot saffron stock, chickpeas, chicken and morcilla; lay tomato slices and the whole garlic head in the centre. Season.
5. Bake uncovered 25 minutes until the liquid is absorbed and the surface dimples.
6. Beat eggs with a pinch of salt until frothy; pour evenly over the rice.
7. Return to the oven 8–10 minutes until the crust puffs golden. Serve at once — the crust falls like a soufflé.

## Plating & Service
Centre-table in the cazuela, crust intact; break it theatrically with the serving spoon.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian with mushrooms and smoked tofu replacing meats; the costra technique works over any leftover dry rice.
