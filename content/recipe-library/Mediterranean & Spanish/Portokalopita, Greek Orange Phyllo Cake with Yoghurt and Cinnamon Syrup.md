# Portokalopita, Greek Orange Phyllo Cake with Yoghurt and Cinnamon Syrup

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Torn dried phyllo soaked in orange-yoghurt batter, baked and drowned in cinnamon-orange syrup — the moistest cake in the Aegean.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Phyllo sheets, air-dried and torn — 250 g
- Eggs — 220 g (4)
- Caster sugar — 120 g (cake) + 200 g (syrup)
- Greek yoghurt — 250 g
- Sunflower oil — 150 ml
- Orange zest — 8 g
- Orange juice — 300 ml
- Baking powder — 8 g
- Vanilla — 2 g
- Water — 100 ml
- Cinnamon stick — 3 g
- Orange, sliced thin, for top — 100 g

## Method
1. Dry phyllo sheets spread out for 30 minutes until brittle; tear into small flakes.
2. Syrup: simmer 200 ml orange juice, water, 200 g sugar and cinnamon 6 minutes; cool.
3. Whisk eggs with 120 g sugar until fluffy; whisk in yoghurt, oil, zest, remaining 100 ml juice and vanilla.
4. Fold in baking powder, then phyllo flakes until fully coated.
5. Pour into an oiled 24 cm dish; lay orange slices on top.
6. Bake at 170 °C for 40–45 minutes until springy and golden.
7. Ladle the cool syrup over the hot cake in stages, letting each soak in.
8. Rest 1 hour before cutting.

## Plating & Service
Serve in squares with cold Greek yoghurt or vanilla ice cream — the contrast with the warm soaked cake is the point.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 2 g cardamom to the syrup; day two, pan-sear a square in butter and re-syrup it — chef's breakfast.
