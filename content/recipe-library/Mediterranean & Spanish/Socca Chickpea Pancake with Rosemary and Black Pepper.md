# Socca Chickpea Pancake with Rosemary and Black Pepper

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Nice's street-corner chickpea pancake, blistered in a ripping-hot pan — torn, not cut, and eaten with fingers and cold rosé.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chickpea (gram) flour — 150 g
- Water — 300 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 50 ml
- Fine sea salt — 4 g
- Rosemary, finely chopped — 2 g
- Black pepper, coarse — 2 g
- Lemon — 40 g (1/2 fruit)

## Method
1. Whisk chickpea flour, water, 20 ml oil and salt until smooth; rest the batter 30 minutes (or up to 12 hours chilled).
2. Heat the oven grill to maximum with a 26 cm cast-iron pan inside for 10 minutes.
3. Carefully add 15 ml oil to the smoking pan and pour in half the batter, 4–5 mm deep.
4. Grill 5–6 minutes until blistered, charred at the edges and set but creamy within.
5. Scatter rosemary and pepper in the final minute; repeat with remaining batter and oil.
6. Tear into ragged shards, squeeze lemon over, serve immediately.

## Plating & Service
Serve on parchment in a basket, unapologetically rustic; goes with tapenade or anchoïade for dipping.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Top with caramelised onions and olives before grilling for a heartier snack; batter keeps 2 days chilled.
