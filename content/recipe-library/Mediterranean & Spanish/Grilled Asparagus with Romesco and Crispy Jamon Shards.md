# Grilled Asparagus with Romesco and Crispy Jamon Shards

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Charred green asparagus over rugged romesco with glass-crisp jamón — the calçotada spirit, available all season.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Green asparagus, thick spears — 500 g
- Jamón serrano, thin slices — 60 g
- Roasted red pepper (jarred or home-roasted) — 150 g
- Tomato — 100 g
- Blanched almonds, toasted — 50 g
- Hazelnuts, toasted — 30 g
- Garlic — 6 g
- Sherry vinegar — 20 ml
- Sweet smoked paprika — 3 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 100 ml
- Fine sea salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Lay jamón flat on parchment; bake at 160 °C for 10–12 minutes until glass-crisp; drain and cool.
2. Roast tomato and garlic at 200 °C for 15 minutes.
3. Blend pepper, tomato, garlic, nuts, vinegar, paprika and salt; stream in 80 ml oil to a coarse romesco.
4. Snap the woody ends off the asparagus; toss with remaining oil and salt.
5. Grill on high heat 4–6 minutes, rolling, until charred but snappy.
6. Swipe romesco across the platter, pile asparagus over, snap jamón shards on top.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm; guests drag spears through the sauce — napkins, not cutlery.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan without jamón (smoked almonds add the salt-crunch); white asparagus version: peel and grill 2 minutes longer.
