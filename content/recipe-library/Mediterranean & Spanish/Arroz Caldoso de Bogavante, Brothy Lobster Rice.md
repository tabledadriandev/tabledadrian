# Arroz Caldoso de Bogavante, Brothy Lobster Rice

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The soupiest of Spanish rices — lobster split live into the pot, its coral thickening a brick-red broth; ladled, not plated.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Live lobster — 1000 g (1 large or 2 small)
- Bomba rice — 300 g
- Fish fumet — 1600 ml
- Brandy — 40 ml
- Onion, brunoise — 120 g
- Green pepper, brunoise — 60 g
- Tomato, grated — 250 g
- Garlic — 12 g
- Ñora flesh — 10 g
- Sweet smoked paprika — 4 g
- Saffron — 0.4 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- Flat-leaf parsley — 8 g
- Fine sea salt — 9 g

## Method
1. Dispatch the lobster with a firm knife cut through the head cross; split lengthwise, reserving coral and juices; crack claws, portion the tail.
2. Sear lobster pieces flesh-down in 40 ml oil over high heat 90 seconds; flambé with brandy, remove.
3. In the same pot, soften onion and pepper 8 minutes; add garlic and ñora, then tomato — cook 12 minutes to a dark sofrito; bloom paprika.
4. Add rice, toast 2 minutes; pour in all the hot fumet with saffron. Season.
5. Boil 8 minutes, then simmer; whisk the reserved coral with a ladle of broth and stir in at minute 10.
6. Return lobster at minute 12; cook to 17 minutes total — the rice must remain surrounded by loose broth.
7. Rest 3 minutes, shower with parsley, and serve immediately — caldoso waits for no one.

## Plating & Service
Deep bowls, lobster architecture on top, broth to the brim; bibs optional, spoons essential.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Half lobster, half prawns keeps the cost civil; a 10 g rice reduction (blended cooked rice) can thicken if your broth runs thin.
