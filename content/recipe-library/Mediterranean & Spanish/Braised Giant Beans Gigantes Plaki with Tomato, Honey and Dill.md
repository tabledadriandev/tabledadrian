# Braised Giant Beans Gigantes Plaki with Tomato, Honey and Dill

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Creamy gigantes baked slowly in a honey-touched tomato sauce until the top blisters — the vegan main that outsells the fish some nights.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Dried gigantes beans, soaked 12 h — 400 g
- Onion, chopped — 150 g
- Carrot, brunoise — 80 g
- Celery, brunoise — 60 g
- Garlic — 12 g
- Chopped tomatoes — 500 g
- Tomato paste — 30 g
- Honey or agave (for vegan) — 15 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 100 ml
- Dried oregano — 3 g
- Bay leaf — 1 g
- Dill, chopped — 12 g
- Fine sea salt — 9 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Simmer soaked beans in fresh unsalted water with bay for 45–50 minutes until tender but intact; drain, reserving 250 ml liquid.
2. Soften onion, carrot and celery in 60 ml oil, 8 minutes; add garlic, 1 minute.
3. Add paste, tomatoes, honey, oregano, salt and the reserved bean liquid; simmer 10 minutes.
4. Fold in beans; tip into a wide baking dish, surface levelled.
5. Bake at 180 °C for 35–40 minutes until the top blisters and the sauce thickens at the edges.
6. Rest 15 minutes; finish with dill, pepper and the remaining raw oil.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm (never hot) in the dish with bread, feta on the side for those who want it; improves overnight.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Butter beans from a jar cut the time to 40 minutes total; a top layer of crumbled feta before baking makes plaki saganaki.
