# Suquet de Peix with Potatoes, Allioli and Toasted Almond Picada

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Costa Brava fisherman's potato-and-fish stew — saffron broth thickened by waxy potatoes and a final almond picada; allioli stirred in at the table.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Monkfish or grouper, thick chunks — 600 g
- Large prawns, shell on — 300 g
- Waxy potatoes — 600 g
- Fish fumet — 900 ml
- Onion, grated — 100 g
- Ripe tomatoes, grated — 250 g
- Garlic — 18 g
- Saffron — 0.3 g
- Sweet paprika — 4 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 80 ml
- Blanched almonds, toasted — 35 g
- Fried bread — 15 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 10 g
- Allioli — 80 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Sear prawns in 30 ml oil 60 seconds per side; remove. Salt the fish.
2. Soften onion in the same oil 6 minutes; add 8 g garlic, then tomato — cook 10 minutes to a dark sofrito; bloom paprika.
3. 'Chascar' the potatoes: cut halfway and crack the rest so they release starch; add to the pot, coating in sofrito.
4. Add hot fumet and saffron; simmer 18–20 minutes until potatoes are nearly tender and the broth is thickening.
5. Pound almonds, fried bread, remaining garlic and half the parsley into a picada; stir in.
6. Add fish for 5 minutes, prawns for the final 2; the broth should coat a spoon.
7. Rest 5 minutes; parsley over, allioli passed at the table.

## Plating & Service
Shallow bowls, potatoes beneath, fish above, broth binding; each guest whisks in allioli to taste.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Any firm fish suits; clams added with the fish deepen the broth. GF confirmed if bread is omitted from picada (add 10 g almonds).
