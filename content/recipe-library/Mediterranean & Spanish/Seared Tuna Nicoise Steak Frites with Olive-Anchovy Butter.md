# Seared Tuna Nicoise Steak Frites with Olive-Anchovy Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Steak frites, Riviera dialect — thick tuna seared blue with a melting disc of tapenade butter over proper double-cooked frites.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Tuna loin steaks, 3 cm thick — 720 g (4)
- Agria potatoes — 800 g
- Sunflower oil for frying — 1500 ml
- Unsalted butter, softened — 100 g
- Black olives, minced — 40 g
- Anchovy fillets, minced — 15 g
- Lemon zest — 2 g
- Espelette pepper — 1 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 30 ml
- Rocket — 80 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Flaky sea salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Beat butter with olives, anchovy, lemon zest and Espelette; roll into a log in film, chill firm.
2. Cut potatoes into 1 cm frites; rinse, dry completely.
3. First fry at 140 °C for 6–7 minutes until tender and pale; rest on a rack 15 minutes.
4. Bring tuna to room temperature; salt all over, rub with olive oil.
5. Second fry at 185 °C for 3–4 minutes to deep gold; salt immediately.
6. Sear tuna on a smoking-hot plancha 60–75 seconds per side — deep crust, rare centre (about 40 °C).
7. Rest 3 minutes; top each steak with a thick disc of the butter; rocket dressed with lemon alongside the frites.

## Plating & Service
The butter should be mid-melt when plates land; stack frites high, no ramekins of ketchup in sight.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swordfish takes the same treatment one minute longer; dairy-free with tapenade whisked into warm olive oil instead of butter.
