# Rainbow Chard, Apricot and Manouri Salad with Warm Sherry-Bacon Vinaigrette

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Raw shredded chard tamed by a warm sherry-bacon vinaigrette, with grilled apricots and creamy manouri — sturdy enough to dress ahead.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Rainbow chard, stems separated — 400 g
- Apricots — 300 g (4–5)
- Manouri or firm ricotta salata — 150 g
- Smoked bacon or pancetta, lardons — 100 g
- Shallot, minced — 40 g
- Sherry vinegar — 30 ml
- Honey — 10 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 50 ml
- Sunflower seeds, toasted — 25 g
- Black pepper — 1 g
- Fine sea salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Shred chard leaves into ribbons; slice the colourful stems paper-thin and hold both in a large bowl with a pinch of salt, massaging 1 minute.
2. Halve apricots; grill flesh-side down 2–3 minutes until marked; slice.
3. Render lardons in a pan over medium heat 5–6 minutes until crisp; remove, keeping the fat.
4. Add shallot to the fat 1 minute; deglaze with vinegar and honey, whisk in olive oil off-heat.
5. Pour the warm vinaigrette over the chard; toss — the leaves will relax and gloss.
6. Fold in apricots and lardons; top with sliced manouri, sunflower seeds and pepper.

## Plating & Service
Serve slightly warm in a wide bowl — this salad holds 30 minutes without collapsing, ideal for buffets.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian with smoked almonds replacing bacon and 10 ml extra oil; peaches or figs swap per season.
