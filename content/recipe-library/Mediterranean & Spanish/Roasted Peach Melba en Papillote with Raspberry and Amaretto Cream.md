# Roasted Peach Melba en Papillote with Raspberry and Amaretto Cream

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Peach melba redirected — peaches roasted in a parchment parcel with raspberry and vanilla, opened over cold amaretto cream; hot fruit, cold cream.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Peaches, ripe but firm — 600 g (4)
- Raspberries — 200 g
- Caster sugar — 40 g
- Vanilla pod, split — 1 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Unsalted butter — 20 g
- Double cream — 200 ml
- Amaretto — 20 ml
- Icing sugar — 15 g
- Toasted flaked almonds — 25 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 200 °C; cut two large parchment hearts.
2. Halve and stone peaches; toss with sugar, lemon and vanilla seeds.
3. Divide peaches and half the raspberries between the parcels; dot with butter, add the vanilla pod halves.
4. Seal tightly; bake on a hot tray 13–15 minutes until the parcels balloon and the fruit yields.
5. Whip cream with icing sugar and amaretto to soft, floppy peaks; chill.
6. Open parcels at the table; crush in remaining raw raspberries, and serve hot fruit over cold cream, almonds last.

## Plating & Service
Parcels opened tableside over wide bowls of cream — steam, then perfume, then the pour of juices.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nectarines or apricots swap in; skip amaretto and use 2 g vanilla for the cream — still a knockout.
