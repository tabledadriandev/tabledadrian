# Grand Aioli of Poached Cod, Summer Vegetables and Whelks

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Provençal Friday feast — poached salt cod, whelks and market vegetables around a mortar of stiff garlic aioli; abundance as a technique.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Salt cod, desalinated — 800 g
- Whelks, cooked — 300 g
- Eggs — 330 g (6)
- New potatoes — 500 g
- Green beans — 300 g
- Baby carrots — 300 g
- Cauliflower florets — 300 g
- Baby artichokes, halved — 300 g
- Garlic — 25 g
- Egg yolks — 40 g (2)
- Extra virgin olive oil — 250 ml
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Bay leaf, thyme — 3 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Pound garlic with 3 g salt in a mortar to a smooth paste.
2. Work in the yolks, then olive oil drop by drop, then in a thread, until the aioli stands in stiff peaks; season with lemon juice. Keep cool.
3. Boil eggs 9 minutes; peel and halve.
4. Simmer potatoes 18 minutes; steam carrots 8, cauliflower 6, beans 4, artichokes 12 — each to just-tender, all kept warm.
5. Poach the cod in water with bay and thyme at 80 °C for 8–10 minutes until it flakes in large petals.
6. Warm the whelks 2 minutes in the cod water.
7. Arrange everything in generous groups around the aioli mortar; drizzle vegetables with any remaining oil.

## Plating & Service
One vast platter or wooden board, mortar centre-stage; a chilled Bandol rosé and long afternoon required.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Any firm fish poaches in place of cod (skip desalination); the vegetables alone make a stately vegan aioli with plant-based emulsion.
