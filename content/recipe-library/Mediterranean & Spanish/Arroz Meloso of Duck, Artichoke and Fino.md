# Arroz Meloso of Duck, Artichoke and Fino

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A creamy-loose 'meloso' rice of crisped duck and braised artichokes, deglazed with fino — inland Valencia for cooler evenings on deck.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Duck legs, boned, skin on, diced — 450 g
- Senia or carnaroli rice — 320 g
- Chicken stock, hot — 1300 ml
- Artichoke hearts, quartered — 300 g
- Onion, brunoise — 120 g
- Carrot, brunoise — 60 g
- Celery, brunoise — 40 g
- Tomato, grated — 150 g
- Fino sherry — 80 ml
- Sweet smoked paprika — 4 g
- Saffron — 0.3 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 30 ml
- Thyme — 2 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Render duck skin-side down in a cold wide pot brought to medium heat, 8–10 minutes, until deeply crisp; remove, pour off all but 30 ml fat.
2. Brown artichokes in the duck fat 4 minutes; remove.
3. Sweat onion, carrot and celery 8 minutes; add tomato and cook to a dark sofrito, 10 minutes; bloom paprika.
4. Deglaze with fino; reduce by half.
5. Add rice, toast 2 minutes; add hot stock and saffron. Season, add thyme.
6. Simmer 14 minutes, stirring twice (unlike paella, meloso welcomes a stir); return duck and artichokes for the final 4 minutes.
7. Rest 3 minutes; the texture should flow slowly off a spoon — add hot stock if it tightens.

## Plating & Service
Deep plates, crisp duck pieces visible on the surface; a chilled fino continues the theme in the glass.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Rabbit or chicken thigh versions are classic; mushrooms replace duck for a lighter, vegetarian meloso with vegetable stock.
