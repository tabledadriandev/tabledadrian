# Galaktoboureko with Crackling Phyllo and Lemon Syrup

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Semolina custard baked between shattering phyllo, drenched hot-into-cold with lemon syrup — Greece's greatest custard dessert, timing is all.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Whole milk — 1000 ml
- Fine semolina — 120 g
- Caster sugar — 150 g (custard) + 250 g (syrup)
- Eggs — 165 g (3)
- Vanilla pod, seeds — 1 g
- Lemon zest — 4 g
- Unsalted butter, melted — 180 g
- Phyllo sheets — 360 g (12)
- Water — 200 ml
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Honey — 30 g

## Method
1. Syrup first: boil water, 250 g sugar, honey and lemon zest 5 minutes; add lemon juice; cool completely.
2. Heat milk to 85 °C; rain in semolina, whisking, and cook 4 minutes until thick.
3. Whisk eggs with 150 g sugar and vanilla; temper into the semolina off-heat. The custard should ribbon.
4. Butter a 25 × 35 cm dish; layer 6 phyllo sheets, buttering each, edges overhanging.
5. Pour in custard; fold edges over, then layer remaining 6 sheets, buttering each; score the top through 2 layers into portions.
6. Bake at 170 °C for 45–50 minutes until deep gold.
7. Pour the COLD syrup over the HOT pastry the moment it exits the oven — listen for the crackle.
8. Rest 2 hours; the custard sets as the phyllo drinks.

## Plating & Service
Cut on the score lines; the cross-section — gold, cream, gold — should be clean and proud.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Orange-blossom syrup variant: swap lemon juice for 5 ml orange blossom water; never refrigerate the day you serve it.
