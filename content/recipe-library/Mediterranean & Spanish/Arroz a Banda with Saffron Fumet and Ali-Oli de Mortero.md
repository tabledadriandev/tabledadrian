# Arroz a Banda with Saffron Fumet and Ali-Oli de Mortero

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Fisherman's rice cooked 'apart' in an intense rockfish fumet — humble looking, outrageously deep; the aioli is pounded, not blended.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Senia or bomba rice — 350 g
- Rockfish or fish trimmings for fumet — 800 g
- Water — 1800 ml
- Onion — 100 g
- Tomato, grated — 200 g
- Garlic — 20 g
- Ñora peppers — 15 g
- Saffron — 0.4 g
- Sweet paprika — 4 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml
- Cuttlefish, diced — 300 g
- Fine sea salt — 10 g
- Lemon — 80 g

## Method
1. Fry whole ñoras in 20 ml oil 60 seconds until fragrant; reserve. In the same pot, brown fish trimmings and onion, add water, simmer 30 minutes; strain for 1200 ml fumet.
2. Pound 12 g garlic with salt in a mortar to a paste; work in 60 ml oil drop by drop to a dense, spoon-standing ali-oli.
3. Pound the fried ñora flesh with remaining garlic.
4. In the paella pan, sear cuttlefish in remaining oil 3 minutes; add ñora-garlic paste and tomato, cooking to a dark sofrito, 10 minutes; bloom paprika.
5. Toast rice in the sofrito 2 minutes; add hot fumet and saffron, season boldly.
6. Cook 17–18 minutes without stirring, high heat first 8 minutes; finish with a whispering socarrat and rest 5 minutes.

## Plating & Service
Traditionally served alone with lemon and ali-oli — the fish course was eaten separately; here the rice is the whole show.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Stir 40 g ali-oli through the hot rice tableside for arroz al ali-oli gratinado under a hot grill.
