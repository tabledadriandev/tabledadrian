# Bourride of Monkfish with Aioli-Mounted Broth and Fennel Confit

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bouillabaisse's blonde sister — the strained fish broth is emulsified with aioli into a pale velvet that naps the monkfish; discipline at 80 °C.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Monkfish fillet — 700 g
- Fish fumet — 900 ml
- Dry white wine — 150 ml
- Leek, white part, sliced — 120 g
- Fennel bulbs — 400 g
- Garlic — 20 g
- Egg yolks — 60 g (3)
- Extra virgin olive oil — 160 ml
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Bay leaf — 1 g
- Orange zest — 3 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Confit the fennel: quarter bulbs, submerge with 60 ml oil and 3 g salt in a covered pan at 110 °C for 35 minutes until silky.
2. Simmer fumet with wine, leek, bay and orange zest 15 minutes; strain and season.
3. Make a stiff aioli: pound garlic and salt, whisk with yolks, emulsify in 100 ml oil, finish with lemon.
4. Poach monkfish portions in the broth at 80 °C for 8–10 minutes to 52 °C core; keep warm.
5. Whisk half the aioli in a bowl with a ladle of hot broth, then stir back into the pot off the boil.
6. Stir over low heat to 78–80 °C until the broth coats a spoon like thin custard — never boil.
7. Plate fish over fennel confit; flood with the mounted broth.

## Plating & Service
Wide warm bowls, sauce poured to a shallow pool; remaining aioli served defiantly on the side.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Sea bass, hake or brill all bourride well; whisk in blanched spinach purée for a green spring version.
