# Esqueixada de Bacallà, Catalan Shredded Salt Cod Salad

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Catalonia's raw salt-cod salad — hand-shredded (esqueixar) cod cured just enough, with tomato, olives and a romesco-leaning dressing.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Salt cod loin, desalinated 36 h — 350 g
- Ripe tomatoes — 300 g
- Red pepper — 100 g
- Spring onions — 50 g
- Black Aragón olives — 60 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 80 ml
- Sherry vinegar — 15 ml
- Romesco sauce — 40 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 6 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Check the cod for salt: it should taste like the sea, not brine — soak longer if needed. Pat very dry.
2. Shred the raw cod by hand along its natural flakes into ribbons; never cut with a knife.
3. Grate half the tomatoes to a pulp; whisk with vinegar, romesco and 60 ml oil into a loose dressing.
4. Dice remaining tomatoes and pepper; slice spring onions finely.
5. Fold cod with the vegetables and half the dressing; marinate 15 minutes in the fridge.
6. Plate over the remaining dressing; finish with olives, parsley, pepper and the last oil.

## Plating & Service
Serve cool on flat plates with a swipe of dressing beneath — rustic dish, elegant plating; toasted coca alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> A confit-tuna version needs no desalination; add white beans to make it a fuller lunch.
