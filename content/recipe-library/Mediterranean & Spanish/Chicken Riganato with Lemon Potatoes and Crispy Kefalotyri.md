# Chicken Riganato with Lemon Potatoes and Crispy Kefalotyri

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> One-pan lemon-oregano chicken over potatoes that braise in the juices then crisp — with shards of pan-fried kefalotyri to finish.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chicken thighs, bone-in, skin-on — 1000 g (8)
- Waxy potatoes, thick wedges — 800 g
- Lemon juice — 80 ml
- Lemon zest — 4 g
- Garlic, grated — 12 g
- Dried Greek oregano — 6 g
- Dijon mustard — 10 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 80 ml
- Chicken stock — 250 ml
- Kefalotyri, 5 mm slices — 100 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 6 g
- Fine sea salt — 10 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Whisk lemon juice, zest, garlic, oregano, mustard, 60 ml oil, salt and pepper; massage half into the chicken, rest 30 minutes.
2. Toss potatoes with the remaining marinade in a large roasting tray; nestle chicken skin-up between the wedges.
3. Pour stock into the corners — never over the skin.
4. Roast at 200 °C for 50 minutes, basting the potatoes (not the skin) once.
5. Raise to 220 °C for 10–15 minutes until the skin is lacquered and the exposed potatoes catch; chicken should reach 78 °C.
6. Pan-fry kefalotyri slices in remaining oil, 60 seconds per side, until golden shards form; break over the tray with parsley.

## Plating & Service
Serve from the tray — burnished skin, lemony potatoes soaked in juices, molten cheese shards on top.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free without the cheese; swap chicken for thick cauliflower steaks (roast 35 minutes) for a vegan riganato.
