# Smoked Aubergine Melitzanosalata with Walnut and Pomegranate

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Aubergines burnt whole over flame until collapsing, folded with walnut and pomegranate — smoke, crunch and jewels in one bowl.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Aubergines — 800 g (2 large)
- Garlic, grated — 5 g
- Lemon juice — 25 ml
- Red wine vinegar — 8 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- Walnuts, toasted and chopped — 50 g
- Pomegranate seeds — 60 g
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 8 g
- Fine sea salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Char whole aubergines directly over a gas flame or under a 250 °C grill, turning, for 25–35 minutes until the skin is blackened and the flesh fully collapsed.
2. Rest in a covered bowl 10 minutes, then peel, keeping a few charred flecks for smokiness.
3. Drain the flesh in a sieve 10 minutes, then chop roughly — never blend, texture matters.
4. Fold in garlic, lemon juice, vinegar and 45 ml olive oil; season with salt.
5. Fold through half the walnuts and parsley.
6. Top with remaining walnuts, pomegranate seeds, parsley and the last of the oil.

## Plating & Service
Serve at room temperature in a wide bowl, pomegranate scattered like rubies; pita or grilled sourdough alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free with toasted pumpkin seeds; add 50 g strained yoghurt for a creamier, non-vegan version.
