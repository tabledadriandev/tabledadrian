# Marinated Kalamata Olives with Orange, Rosemary and Warm Fennel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Olives warmed gently in citrus-rosemary oil so their perfume blooms — the mezze equivalent of lighting a scented candle.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Kalamata olives, drained — 300 g
- Green Chalkidiki olives, drained — 200 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 100 ml
- Orange peel, wide strips — 12 g
- Lemon peel, wide strips — 6 g
- Rosemary sprigs — 4 g
- Fennel seeds — 3 g
- Coriander seeds, cracked — 2 g
- Dried chilli flakes — 1 g
- Fennel bulb, shaved — 80 g

## Method
1. Warm olive oil with citrus peels, rosemary, fennel seed, coriander and chilli at 70 °C for 5 minutes — infuse, never fry.
2. Add both olives and warm through gently 3 minutes at the same temperature.
3. Remove from heat and rest 30 minutes minimum (24 hours in the fridge is better).
4. Just before serving, rewarm to 45 °C.
5. Fold through raw shaved fennel for crunch.
6. Serve with a small fork and a bowl for stones.

## Plating & Service
A rustic warm bowl on the aperitivo table; the scented oil is excellent mopped with bread.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 60 g marcona almonds; the infused oil keeps a week and doubles as a fish-grilling baste.
