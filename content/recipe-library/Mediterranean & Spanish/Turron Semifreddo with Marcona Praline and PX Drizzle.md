# Turron Semifreddo with Marcona Praline and PX Drizzle

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Soft Jijona turrón folded into a no-churn semifreddo — Christmas nougat made a July dessert — with sherry poured over like espresso on affogato.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Soft turrón de Jijona — 250 g
- Double cream — 400 ml
- Egg yolks — 80 g (4)
- Caster sugar — 60 g (sabayon) + 80 g (praline)
- Whole milk — 60 ml
- Marcona almonds — 80 g
- Pedro Ximénez sherry — 60 ml
- Flaky sea salt — 1 g

## Method
1. Praline: dry caramel with 80 g sugar to amber; add almonds, pour out, cool, chop coarse.
2. Crumble turrón into warm milk; mash to a rough paste.
3. Whisk yolks and 60 g sugar over a bain-marie to 72 °C, then whisk cool to a thick sabayon.
4. Fold turrón paste into the sabayon.
5. Whip cream to soft peaks; fold through with half the praline.
6. Freeze in a lined terrine 6 hours.
7. Slice; scatter remaining praline and salt, and pour 7–8 ml chilled PX over each slice at the table.

## Plating & Service
Slices on frozen plates, PX poured from a small carafe tableside — the drizzle soaks in like dark honey.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Alcohol-free with pomegranate molasses thinned with orange juice; hazelnut turrón variant works identically.
