# Gilda Skewers with Smoked Anchovy and Piparra

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The original Basque pintxo, sharpened with smoked anchovy — a one-bite aperitivo that sets the tone for a deck sundowner.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Smoked anchovy fillets in oil — 90 g (12 fillets)
- Manzanilla olives, pitted — 120 g (24 olives)
- Piparra peppers (Basque guindillas) — 60 g (12 peppers)
- Extra virgin olive oil, arbequina — 30 ml
- Sherry vinegar — 5 ml
- Lemon zest — 2 g
- Espelette pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Drain anchovies and pat gently; halve each fillet lengthwise if very wide.
2. Trim piparra stems; fold each pepper into a tight S-shape.
3. Thread onto small skewers: olive, folded piparra, anchovy ribbon draped around, second olive.
4. Whisk olive oil with sherry vinegar, lemon zest and Espelette.
5. Arrange gildas on a chilled plate and spoon over the dressing just before service; rest 5 minutes so flavours marry.
6. Serve within 30 minutes, cool but not fridge-cold (around 14 °C).

## Plating & Service
Line up on a slate or chilled ceramic bar with cocktail picks upright; serve with ice-cold fino sherry or vermouth.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap anchovy for marinated white anchovy (boquerón) for a brighter, less salty bite; vegan version with smoked almond in place of fish.
