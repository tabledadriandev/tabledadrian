# Frozen Greek Yoghurt and Sour Cherry Swirl with Olive Oil Crumble

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Tart frozen yoghurt rippled with sour cherry, over a rubble of olive-oil crumble — a lighter finale after a long grilled-fish lunch.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Greek yoghurt, strained — 600 g
- Caster sugar — 130 g
- Glucose syrup — 40 g
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Sour cherries, pitted (fresh or jarred, drained) — 250 g
- Caster sugar (compote) — 60 g
- Cornflour — 5 g
- Plain flour — 100 g
- Ground almonds — 40 g
- Demerara sugar — 50 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- Fine sea salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Compote: simmer cherries and 60 g sugar 8 minutes; add cornflour slurried in 10 ml water, boil 1 minute; chill completely.
2. Whisk yoghurt with 130 g sugar, glucose and lemon until dissolved.
3. Churn to soft-serve texture (or still-freeze, whisking hourly for 3 hours).
4. Ripple the cold compote through in three folds — streaks, not blend.
5. Freeze 2 hours to scooping firmness.
6. Crumble: rub flour, almonds, demerara, salt and oil to clumps; bake at 170 °C for 12–15 minutes, stirring once, until golden. Cool.
7. Serve scoops over crumble with extra compote.

## Plating & Service
Build in stemmed glasses: crumble, scoop, ripple, crumble again; serve with long spoons on the shaded deck.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan with coconut yoghurt and maple; strawberries or apricots swap for cherries as the market dictates.
