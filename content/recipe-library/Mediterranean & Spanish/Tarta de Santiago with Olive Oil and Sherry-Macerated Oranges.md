# Tarta de Santiago with Olive Oil and Sherry-Macerated Oranges

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The pilgrim's almond torte — no flour, no butter, just almonds, eggs and citrus — with PX-macerated oranges to loosen it into a plated dessert.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Ground almonds — 300 g
- Caster sugar — 220 g
- Eggs — 275 g (5)
- Lemon zest — 5 g
- Orange zest — 5 g
- Ground cinnamon — 2 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 20 ml
- Oranges — 450 g (3)
- Pedro Ximénez sherry — 50 ml
- Icing sugar — 20 g

## Method
1. Whisk eggs with caster sugar 3 minutes until pale and doubled.
2. Fold in almonds, both zests and cinnamon; finish with olive oil.
3. Pour into an oiled, based-lined 22 cm tin.
4. Bake at 170 °C for 30–35 minutes until golden and a skewer comes out clean; cool in the tin.
5. Segment the oranges; macerate in PX 30 minutes.
6. Dust the cake with icing sugar over a St James cross template; serve slices with the oranges and their syrup.

## Plating & Service
A cross of white sugar on amber cake — history on the plate; the boozy oranges make it dinner-worthy.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 30 g pine nuts on top before baking; serve with yoghurt sorbet for a lighter finish.
