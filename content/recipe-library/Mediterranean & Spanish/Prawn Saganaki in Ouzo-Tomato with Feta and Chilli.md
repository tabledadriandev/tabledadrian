# Prawn Saganaki in Ouzo-Tomato with Feta and Chilli

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bubbling tomato, prawns and molten feta with an ouzo kick — a one-pan mezze that eats like a main and smells like an island taverna.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Raw prawns, peeled — 400 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 45 ml
- Onion, finely chopped — 80 g
- Garlic, sliced — 10 g
- Chilli flakes — 1.5 g
- Chopped tomatoes — 400 g
- Ouzo — 40 ml
- Feta — 120 g
- Dried oregano — 2 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 6 g
- Fine sea salt — 4 g

## Method
1. Sweat onion in olive oil over medium heat 5 minutes; add garlic and chilli for 1 minute more.
2. Add tomatoes and salt; simmer 10 minutes to a thick, glossy sauce.
3. Season prawns; stir into the sauce and cook 2 minutes.
4. Add ouzo, letting it bubble off for 60 seconds.
5. Crumble feta over in large nuggets; slide under a 250 °C grill for 3 minutes until the cheese blisters.
6. Finish with oregano, parsley and a thread of oil; serve in the pan.

## Plating & Service
Straight from grill to table with bread for the sauce; warn guests the pan is scorching.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap prawns for butter beans for vegetarian; skip ouzo and add fennel seed for the same anise note.
