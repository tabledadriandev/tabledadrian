# Sardinas Asadas with Escalivada and Smoked Salt

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Whole sardines grilled hard over coals on a bed of silky escalivada — burnt-sweet peppers and aubergine — coastal Spain at its most honest.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Fresh sardines, gutted — 800 g (12)
- Aubergine — 400 g
- Red peppers — 400 g
- Red onion — 150 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 80 ml
- Sherry vinegar — 15 ml
- Garlic, grated — 4 g
- Smoked flaky salt — 6 g
- Lemon — 120 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 6 g

## Method
1. Roast aubergine, peppers and onion whole over coals or at 250 °C, turning, 30–40 minutes until blackened and collapsed.
2. Rest covered 15 minutes; peel, deseed and tear into ribbons, saving the juices.
3. Dress the escalivada with its juices, 40 ml oil, vinegar and garlic; hold at room temperature.
4. Salt sardines 15 minutes ahead; brush with oil.
5. Grill over the hottest coals 2–3 minutes per side until the skin blisters and chars — do not move them until they release.
6. Bed the escalivada on a platter, lay sardines across, finish with smoked salt, remaining oil, lemon and parsley.

## Plating & Service
Eat with fingers, spine-pull encouraged; the escalivada catches the sardine juices below.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Mackerel fillets or red mullet work; escalivada keeps 3 days and improves — make double.
