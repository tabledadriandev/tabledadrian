# Fleurs de Courgette Beignets, Brousse and Lemon Zest

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Courgette flowers stuffed with lemony brousse and fried in a crackling tempura-light batter — the Riviera June market on a plate.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Courgette flowers — 12 flowers (about 120 g)
- Brousse or fresh ricotta — 200 g
- Lemon zest — 4 g
- Basil, sliced — 6 g
- Parmesan-style cheese, grated — 25 g
- Black pepper — 1 g
- Plain flour — 100 g
- Cornflour — 50 g
- Ice-cold sparkling water — 220 ml
- Fine sea salt — 5 g
- Sunflower oil for frying — 1200 ml
- Honey — 15 g

## Method
1. Drain brousse 30 minutes in a sieve; mix with lemon zest, basil, cheese, pepper and 2 g salt.
2. Open each flower gently, remove the stamen, pipe in 25 g filling, twist petals closed.
3. Whisk flour, cornflour and 3 g salt with sparkling water briefly — lumps are fine; keep over ice.
4. Heat oil to 180 °C.
5. Dip flowers in batter, letting excess run off; fry 3–4 at a time, 2–3 minutes, turning once, until pale gold and crisp.
6. Drain on a rack, salt immediately, thread with honey and serve within minutes.

## Plating & Service
Serve on crumpled parchment, three per person, honey barely visible — the crunch must stay audible across the deck.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan with silken-tofu-and-herb filling and no cheese; add 2 g saffron to the batter for colour and perfume.
