# Fideua de Gandia with Prawns, Monkfish and Burnt Aioli

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Valencian coast's noodle paella — thin fideos toasted deep gold before the fumet hits, so the finished dish tastes of the fire.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Fideos no. 2 (thin short noodles) — 500 g
- Fish fumet — 1300 ml
- Raw prawns, shell on — 500 g
- Monkfish, 3 cm chunks — 400 g
- Tomato, grated — 200 g
- Garlic, chopped — 12 g
- Ñora flesh or 5 g mild paprika — 8 g
- Saffron — 0.4 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 80 ml
- Egg yolk — 20 g
- Roasted garlic purée — 15 g
- Sunflower oil — 120 ml
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Fine sea salt — 9 g

## Method
1. Make the burnt aioli: whisk yolk with roasted garlic purée (from a whole head roasted at 200 °C for 35 minutes), emulsify in sunflower oil, season with lemon and salt.
2. Toast the dry fideos in 30 ml olive oil in the paella pan, stirring, 4–5 minutes to a deep toffee gold; set aside.
3. Sear prawns 60 seconds per side in 25 ml oil; remove. Sear monkfish 2 minutes; remove.
4. Build the sofrito: remaining oil, garlic, ñora, then tomato; 10 minutes to jammy.
5. Return noodles; add hot fumet, saffron and salt. Boil hard 5 minutes.
6. Nestle in monkfish, then prawns; simmer 6–8 minutes until the liquid is gone and noodle tips stand up.
7. Optional: 2 minutes under a hot grill so the tips crisp like wheat stubble. Rest 4 minutes.

## Plating & Service
Pan to table; the standing crisped noodle tips are the mark of a proper fideuà. Aioli passed separately.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF with corn-rice fideos; clams instead of monkfish for a brinier register.
