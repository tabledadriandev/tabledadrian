# Melon, Cured Ham and Basil with Cava Vinaigrette

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Charentais melon and hand-torn ham under a sparkling cava vinaigrette — the fastest elegant lunch on a hot day at anchor.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Charentais or cantaloupe melon, chilled — 800 g
- Cured ham (jamón or presunto), thin slices — 120 g
- Cava, chilled — 40 ml
- Cava vinegar or white wine vinegar — 10 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 40 ml
- Honey — 5 g
- Basil leaves, small — 6 g
- Black pepper, cracked — 1 g
- Flaky sea salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Halve and seed the melon; cut into thin crescent slices or scoop into large balls; keep at 8 °C.
2. Whisk vinegar, honey and oil; add the cava last so it keeps a gentle fizz.
3. Arrange melon on chilled plates.
4. Drape torn ham loosely between the melon — never flat, always with air.
5. Spoon over the cava vinaigrette just before serving.
6. Finish with basil, cracked pepper and a few salt flakes on the melon only.

## Plating & Service
Chilled plates are essential; serve within minutes while the vinaigrette still prickles.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan with ribbons of salt-baked kohlrabi instead of ham; white peach replaces melon late in summer.
