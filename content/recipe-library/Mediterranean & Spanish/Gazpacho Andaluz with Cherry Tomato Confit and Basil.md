# Gazpacho Andaluz with Cherry Tomato Confit and Basil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Silky Sevillian gazpacho blended to velvet — served properly cold at 6 °C with a confit-tomato and basil garnish.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Ripe vine tomatoes — 1000 g
- Cucumber, peeled — 150 g
- Green pepper — 80 g
- Garlic — 5 g
- Stale white bread — 50 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml
- Sherry vinegar — 20 ml
- Fine sea salt — 8 g
- Cherry tomatoes — 120 g
- Basil leaves — 5 g

## Method
1. Roughly chop tomatoes, cucumber, pepper and garlic; combine with torn bread, salt and vinegar; marinate 30 minutes.
2. Blend on high 3 minutes until completely smooth.
3. With the motor running, stream in 70 ml olive oil to emulsify — the colour turns salmon-orange.
4. Pass through a fine sieve; adjust salt and vinegar, then chill to 6 °C, at least 2 hours.
5. Meanwhile confit halved cherry tomatoes in remaining oil at 110 °C for 25 minutes.
6. Serve in chilled bowls, confit tomatoes and basil in the centre, a final thread of oil.

## Plating & Service
Pour from a jug at the table over the garnish; frozen bowls keep it properly cold in July heat.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF with almonds instead of bread; add 100 g strawberries or cherries in June for a fruit-inflected version.
