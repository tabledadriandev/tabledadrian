# Prawn Youvetsi with Orzo, Ouzo and Kefalotyri

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Baked orzo in a prawn-shell bisque-style tomato sauce, the prawns added at the last so they stay plump — taverna youvetsi, tightened up.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Raw prawns, shell on — 700 g
- Orzo (kritharaki) — 350 g
- Onion, brunoise — 120 g
- Garlic — 10 g
- Chopped tomatoes — 400 g
- Tomato paste — 30 g
- Ouzo — 50 ml
- Fish or light chicken stock — 900 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- Cinnamon stick — 2 g
- Chilli flakes — 1 g
- Kefalotyri, grated — 70 g
- Dill — 8 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Peel prawns; sauté the shells in 30 ml oil over high heat 3 minutes, add stock, simmer 15 minutes, strain — a fast prawn stock.
2. Soften onion in remaining oil 6 minutes; add garlic and tomato paste, 2 minutes.
3. Deglaze with ouzo; add tomatoes, cinnamon and chilli, simmering 8 minutes.
4. Stir in orzo and 800 ml hot prawn stock; transfer to a wide baking dish.
5. Bake at 190 °C for 20 minutes, stirring once, until the orzo is nearly tender and saucy.
6. Season prawns; push into the orzo, scatter half the cheese, bake 6–7 minutes more until prawns are just opaque.
7. Rest 5 minutes; finish with dill and remaining kefalotyri.

## Plating & Service
Serve in the baking dish, spoon straight to plates; the orzo should still flow slightly — add stock if it seizes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Classic youvetsi uses lamb (braise 2 hours before the orzo goes in); dairy-free without cheese, richer with 30 g extra oil.
