# Baklava Cigars with Pistachio, Mastic and Bitter Orange

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Baklava rolled into slim cigars — more crunch per bite — with mastic-scented syrup and bitter orange; cut sweetness, doubled perfume.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Phyllo sheets — 300 g (10)
- Pistachios — 200 g
- Walnuts — 100 g
- Caster sugar — 40 g (filling) + 250 g (syrup)
- Ground cinnamon — 3 g
- Unsalted butter, melted — 180 g
- Water — 200 ml
- Honey — 60 g
- Mastic crystals, ground with a pinch of sugar — 1 g
- Bitter orange peel (or orange peel + 5 ml lemon) — 8 g
- Lemon juice — 15 ml

## Method
1. Syrup: simmer water, 250 g sugar, honey and orange peel 6 minutes; off heat add lemon juice and ground mastic; cool completely.
2. Pulse pistachios and walnuts coarse; mix with 40 g sugar and cinnamon.
3. Halve each phyllo sheet crosswise; brush with butter.
4. Spoon 25 g nut mix along the short edge; roll into a slim cigar around a skewer, then scrunch slightly and slide the skewer out.
5. Pack cigars snugly in a buttered tray; brush tops generously.
6. Bake at 170 °C for 25–30 minutes to an even deep gold.
7. Pour the cold syrup over the hot cigars; rest 4 hours, turning once, until glossy and crisp-soaked.

## Plating & Service
Stack in a low pyramid, crushed pistachio over; serve with strong coffee — one cigar is never enough, plan for three.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> All-almond filling with orange blossom syrup is the island variant; skip mastic if unavailable — add 2 g vanilla.
