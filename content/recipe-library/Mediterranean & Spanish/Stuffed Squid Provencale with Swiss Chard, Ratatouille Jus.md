# Stuffed Squid Provencale with Swiss Chard, Ratatouille Jus

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Squid tubes packed with a chard-and-tentacle farce, braised until tender in a ratatouille-scented tomato jus — old Nice, refined.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Squid tubes and tentacles, cleaned — 800 g (8 tubes)
- Swiss chard, chopped — 300 g
- Onion, brunoise — 100 g
- Garlic — 12 g
- Stale breadcrumbs — 60 g
- Egg — 55 g (1)
- Extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- Ripe tomatoes, chopped — 400 g
- Courgette, brunoise — 100 g
- Red pepper, brunoise — 100 g
- Dry white wine — 120 ml
- Thyme, basil — 6 g
- Fine sea salt — 7 g

## Method
1. Chop tentacles finely; sauté with half the onion and 6 g garlic in 20 ml oil, 4 minutes.
2. Wilt chard in the same pan 3 minutes; squeeze dry, chop, and mix with tentacles, breadcrumbs, egg, thyme and 3 g salt.
3. Fill tubes two-thirds full (they shrink); close with toothpicks.
4. Brown the stuffed squid in 25 ml oil, 2 minutes per side; remove.
5. Soften remaining onion, courgette and pepper 6 minutes; add remaining garlic, tomatoes and wine.
6. Return squid; cover and braise at a bare simmer (90 °C) for 40–45 minutes until a knife pierces easily.
7. Rest 5 minutes; slice thick, spoon over the jus, tear basil over, finish with remaining oil.

## Plating & Service
Slice two tubes per plate to show the green spiral; jus around, good bread near.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF with cooked rice replacing breadcrumbs; a pinch of orange zest in the jus is old-Nice orthodoxy.
