# Greek Grain Bowl with Freekeh, Grilled Courgette and Avgotaraho Dressing

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A modern Aegean lunch bowl — smoky freekeh, charred courgette and a dressing whipped with grated avgotaraho (Greek bottarga).

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Freekeh — 250 g
- Vegetable stock — 600 ml
- Courgettes — 400 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 80 ml
- Lemon juice — 40 ml
- Avgotaraho or bottarga, grated — 15 g
- Garlic, grated — 3 g
- Cherry tomatoes, halved — 200 g
- Cucumber, diced — 150 g
- Spring onions — 40 g
- Dill — 10 g
- Mint — 6 g
- Fine sea salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Rinse freekeh; simmer in stock 20–25 minutes until tender with bite; drain and spread to cool slightly.
2. Slice courgettes lengthwise 1 cm thick; brush with 20 ml oil, salt, and grill on high heat 3 minutes per side until charred; cut into batons.
3. Whisk lemon juice, garlic and 50 ml oil; stir in two-thirds of the grated avgotaraho.
4. Dress the warm freekeh with half the dressing so it absorbs.
5. Fold in tomatoes, cucumber, spring onion, dill and mint.
6. Top with courgette, remaining dressing and a final grating of avgotaraho.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm or ambient in wide bowls; the roe should be grated over at the table for aroma.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan by swapping roe for 10 g white miso in the dressing; quinoa makes it GF.
