# Monkfish a la Plancha with Romesco Nero and Charred Baby Gem

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Monkfish 'scallops' blasted on the plancha over a black romesco darkened with squid ink — familiar sauce, midnight register.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Monkfish tail, trimmed, 3 cm medallions — 700 g
- Roasted red pepper — 150 g
- Tomato, roasted — 100 g
- Blanched almonds, toasted — 45 g
- Hazelnuts, toasted — 25 g
- Garlic, roasted — 8 g
- Squid ink — 4 g
- Sherry vinegar — 18 ml
- Sweet smoked paprika — 3 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 100 ml
- Baby gem lettuce, halved — 300 g
- Lemon — 80 g
- Flaky sea salt — 7 g

## Method
1. Blend pepper, tomato, nuts, garlic, ink, vinegar and paprika; stream in 70 ml oil for a thick, night-black romesco. Season and rest 30 minutes.
2. Salt monkfish 15 minutes ahead — it weeps; dry aggressively before cooking.
3. Brush baby gem with oil; char cut-side down on the plancha 2 minutes until blackened but crisp at the core; salt.
4. Heat the plancha to full; oil the medallions, not the plate.
5. Sear 2 minutes per side until burnished with a bouncy, just-opaque centre (54 °C).
6. Swipe romesco nero across plates; monkfish over, baby gem alongside, lemon and a final thread of oil.

## Plating & Service
The black sauce demands white plates; keep garnish spare — this one is graphic.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Classic red romesco if ink is a bridge too far; prawns and scallops love the same sauce.
