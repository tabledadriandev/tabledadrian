# Pork Secreto with Membrillo Jus, Charred Padron and Almond Migas

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The hidden Ibérico cut seared rare-pink like steak, with quince jus, blistered Padróns and crunchy almond migas — land food for the fish-weary.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Ibérico pork secreto — 700 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g
- Membrillo (quince paste) — 50 g
- Fino sherry — 60 ml
- Chicken jus or reduced stock — 150 ml
- Sherry vinegar — 10 ml
- Padrón peppers — 200 g
- Stale bread, torn small — 120 g
- Blanched almonds — 40 g
- Garlic, sliced — 8 g
- Sweet smoked paprika — 3 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml

## Method
1. Bring secreto to room temperature; salt 20 minutes ahead.
2. Migas: fry bread, almonds and garlic in 40 ml oil over medium heat, tossing 6–8 minutes until deep gold; dust with paprika; drain.
3. Jus: melt membrillo with fino, reduce 2 minutes; add jus and vinegar, simmering to a light coat; keep warm.
4. Sear secreto on a ripping plancha 3–4 minutes per side to 58–60 °C — it should stay blushing pink; rest 6 minutes.
5. Blister Padróns in the same pan with 15 ml oil, 4 minutes; salt.
6. Slice secreto thinly against the grain; plate over migas, peppers alongside, jus over the meat, remaining oil to finish.

## Plating & Service
Slice tableside if the galley allows — the pink interior against the mahogany crust sells the cut.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Presa or pluma cuts behave the same; GF migas with cooked chickpeas fried crisp instead of bread.
