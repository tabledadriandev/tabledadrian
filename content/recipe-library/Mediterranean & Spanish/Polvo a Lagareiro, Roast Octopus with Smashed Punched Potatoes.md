# Polvo a Lagareiro, Roast Octopus with Smashed Punched Potatoes

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Portugal's finest octopus dish — boiled tender, then roasted in torrents of garlic olive oil with 'punched' potatoes that drink the juices.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Octopus, cleaned — 1500 g
- Small potatoes, skin on — 700 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 150 ml
- Garlic cloves, smashed — 30 g
- Bay leaf — 2 g
- Onion, halved — 100 g
- Coarse sea salt — 15 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 10 g
- Black pepper — 1 g
- Lemon — 80 g

## Method
1. Simmer octopus with onion and bay at 90 °C for 50–60 minutes until a skewer slides through; drain and cut into whole-tentacle portions.
2. Boil potatoes in heavily salted water 20 minutes; drain, then 'punch' each with your palm to crack it open.
3. Lay potatoes and octopus in a roasting dish; scatter smashed garlic.
4. Pour over 130 ml olive oil — lagareiro means oil-press generous; season with pepper.
5. Roast at 220 °C for 20–25 minutes until tentacle tips crisp and potatoes soak golden.
6. Finish with parsley, remaining oil and lemon; serve in the roasting dish.

## Plating & Service
The dish goes to the table swimming — spoon the garlic oil over everything repeatedly during service.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Bacalhau a lagareiro: same method with thick desalted cod loins, 18 minutes roasting; turnip greens on the side are canonical.
