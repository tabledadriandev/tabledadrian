# Soutzoukakia Smyrneika, Cumin Meatballs in Wine-Tomato Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Smyrna's cumin-scented torpedo meatballs simmered in a red-wine tomato sauce — deep, aromatic comfort that reheats even better on day two.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Beef mince (or beef-lamb mix) — 600 g
- Stale bread, crusts off — 80 g
- Red wine — 180 ml
- Garlic, grated — 10 g
- Ground cumin — 6 g
- Egg — 55 g (1)
- Extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- Onion, grated — 100 g
- Chopped tomatoes — 500 g
- Tomato paste — 25 g
- Caster sugar — 4 g
- Cinnamon — 1 g
- Bay leaf — 1 g
- Fine sea salt — 9 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 8 g

## Method
1. Soak bread in 80 ml of the wine 5 minutes; squeeze lightly.
2. Knead mince with bread, garlic, cumin, egg and 5 g salt for 2 minutes; rest 15 minutes chilled.
3. Shape into 12 oval torpedoes; brown in 40 ml oil over medium-high heat, 2 minutes per side; remove.
4. Soften onion in the same pan 5 minutes; add paste, then remaining wine, reducing 2 minutes.
5. Add tomatoes, sugar, cinnamon, bay and remaining salt; simmer 10 minutes.
6. Return soutzoukakia; simmer gently, covered, 20 minutes, turning once.
7. Rest 10 minutes; finish with parsley and remaining oil.

## Plating & Service
Over rice pilaf, orzo or thick fried potatoes; the sauce should be dark, glossy and abundant.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF with rice instead of bread; the mixture grills beautifully as kebabs when the sauce feels like too much.
