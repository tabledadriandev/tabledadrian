# Pintxo of Seared Txistorra, Cider Apple and Sheep Cheese

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A Basque bar bite on a stick — hot txistorra sausage, sharp cider-glazed apple and a shard of aged sheep cheese; sweet, fatty, saline balance.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Txistorra (thin Basque chorizo) — 300 g
- Baguette — 180 g (12 slices)
- Apple (firm, sharp) — 160 g
- Dry cider — 60 ml
- Butter — 15 g
- Aged sheep cheese (Ossau-Iraty or Idiazábal) — 80 g
- Honey — 10 g
- Thyme leaves — 1 g

## Method
1. Cut txistorra into 12 pieces; sear in a dry pan over medium-high heat 4–5 minutes, rolling, until browned and cooked through.
2. Toast baguette slices until golden.
3. Cut apple into 12 wedges; caramelise in butter 2 minutes per side.
4. Add cider and honey; reduce 1 minute to glaze the apple.
5. Build: bread, apple wedge, txistorra, glaze spooned over.
6. Top each with a thin shard of cheese and thyme; skewer through and serve warm.

## Plating & Service
Line up on a wooden bar board — pintxos should look abundant and slightly chaotic; serve with cider poured from height.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Plant-based sausage works; pear replaces apple gracefully in autumn.
