# Dolmades of Chard with Herbed Rice and Avgolemono

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Swiss chard replaces vine leaves for a silkier wrap; the avgolemono is whisked to a satin foam — grandmother food, restaurant finish.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Swiss chard leaves, large — 18 leaves (about 400 g)
- Short-grain rice — 200 g
- Onion, brunoise — 120 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 80 ml
- Dill, chopped — 15 g
- Mint, chopped — 8 g
- Pine nuts, toasted — 30 g
- Currants — 25 g
- Lemon juice — 60 ml
- Vegetable stock — 500 ml
- Eggs — 110 g (2 eggs)
- Fine sea salt — 7 g

## Method
1. Blanch chard leaves 20 seconds in salted boiling water; refresh in iced water and trim thick stems flat.
2. Sweat onion in 50 ml oil 6 minutes; stir in rice 2 minutes, then add 150 ml stock and simmer 5 minutes to par-cook.
3. Off heat, fold in dill, mint, pine nuts, currants, 20 ml lemon juice and salt; cool.
4. Roll 25 g filling in each leaf: fold sides in, roll tight like a cigar.
5. Pack seam-down in a single layer in a wide pan; add remaining stock and oil, weight with a plate.
6. Simmer covered at 90 °C for 30–35 minutes; reserve 250 ml cooking liquid.
7. Whisk eggs with remaining lemon juice until frothy; temper in the hot liquid in a thin stream, whisking.
8. Return sauce to low heat, stirring to 78 °C until it coats a spoon — never boil.

## Plating & Service
Three dolmades per plate, avgolemono spooned over, dill and a grind of pepper; serve warm, not hot.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vine leaves work traditionally (brine-rinse first); vegan by finishing with lemon-oil emulsion instead of avgolemono.
