# Turbot with Saffron Beurre Blanc and Sea Fennel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Tronçon of turbot roasted on the bone, napped with a saffron beurre blanc — the reduction-to-butter ratio is the whole game: 60 ml to 150 g.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Turbot tronçons, on the bone — 900 g (4)
- Unsalted cold butter, cubed — 150 g
- Shallots, minced — 40 g
- Dry white wine — 100 ml
- White wine vinegar — 30 ml
- Saffron threads — 0.3 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 30 ml
- Sea fennel (or samphire) — 80 g
- Lemon juice — 8 ml
- Flaky sea salt — 6 g
- White pepper — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Salt the turbot 20 minutes ahead; dry well.
2. Reduce shallots, wine and vinegar with the saffron to exactly 60 ml of amber liquid.
3. Roast turbot: sear dark-skin down in oil in an ovenproof pan 3 minutes, then into a 190 °C oven for 10–12 minutes to 52 °C at the bone. Rest 4 minutes.
4. Blanch sea fennel 30 seconds; refresh briefly and warm in a corner of the fish pan.
5. Over the lowest heat, whisk the cold butter into the reduction cube by cube — 150 g into 60 ml — never letting it exceed 60 °C.
6. Season the beurre blanc with lemon juice and white pepper; strain if you like it couture, leave shallots if you like it honest.
7. Plate turbot, sauce boldly, sea fennel over.

## Plating & Service
On the bone always — flavour lives there; warn guests and offer a spoon for the gelatinous cheeks of the tronçon.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Brill or john dory take the same sauce; swap saffron for 10 g fresh tarragon at the end for a green variant.
