# Chorizo and Cider Cazuela, Charred Spring Onion

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Asturian bar classic — cooking chorizo braised in dry cider until the sauce turns brick-red and glossy; irresistible with crusty bread.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Fresh cooking chorizo — 400 g
- Dry Asturian cider (or brut cider) — 300 ml
- Bay leaf — 1 g
- Spring onions — 100 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 15 ml
- Honey — 10 g
- Sherry vinegar — 5 ml
- Flat-leaf parsley — 5 g

## Method
1. Cut chorizo into 3 cm chunks on the bias.
2. Sear in a dry cazuela over medium-high heat 3 minutes until the fat renders and edges caramelise.
3. Pour off excess fat, add cider and bay leaf; simmer uncovered 12–14 minutes until reduced to a glossy glaze.
4. Stir in honey and sherry vinegar; check seasoning.
5. Meanwhile brush spring onions with oil and char on a plancha 3 minutes until softened and striped.
6. Nestle the onions into the cazuela, scatter parsley and serve bubbling.

## Plating & Service
Straight to the table in the cazuela with toothpicks and bread for the cider glaze.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Use plant-based chorizo and a knob of smoked paprika butter alternative for vegetarian; reduce honey for lower sugar.
