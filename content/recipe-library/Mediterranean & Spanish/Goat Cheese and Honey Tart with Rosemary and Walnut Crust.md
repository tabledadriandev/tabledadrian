# Goat Cheese and Honey Tart with Rosemary and Walnut Crust

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A cheesecake-adjacent tart of fresh goat cheese and lavender honey in a walnut-rosemary crust — barely sweet, deeply Provençal, superb with dessert wine.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Plain flour — 180 g
- Walnuts, ground — 60 g
- Cold unsalted butter — 120 g
- Icing sugar — 40 g
- Rosemary, minced — 2 g
- Egg yolk — 20 g
- Fresh goat cheese, soft — 400 g
- Ricotta — 150 g
- Lavender honey — 110 g
- Eggs — 165 g (3)
- Lemon zest — 4 g
- Vanilla — 2 g
- Honey for glaze — 20 g

## Method
1. Rub butter into flour, walnuts, icing sugar and rosemary; bind with yolk and 15 ml cold water; chill 1 hour.
2. Line a 24 cm tart ring, 3 mm thick; freeze 15 minutes; blind-bake at 170 °C for 18 minutes, weights out for the last 5.
3. Beat goat cheese, ricotta and 110 g honey smooth; add eggs one by one, then zest and vanilla — do not aerate.
4. Pour into the warm shell.
5. Bake at 150 °C for 22–25 minutes until set at the edge with a 6 cm central wobble.
6. Cool fully, chill 2 hours; glaze with warmed honey before serving.

## Plating & Service
Thin slices with a few thyme flowers; pour a chilled Muscat or Sauternes and step back.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> A layer of fig jam under the filling is superb; GF crust with buckwheat flour 1:1 holds beautifully.
