# Crema Catalana with Burnt Sugar and Orange Blossom

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Catalonia's café-crème custard set on the stove, not the oven — citrus and cinnamon infused, torched to glass at the table.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Whole milk — 750 ml
- Orange peel, wide strips — 10 g
- Lemon peel — 6 g
- Cinnamon stick — 4 g
- Egg yolks — 120 g (6)
- Caster sugar — 110 g
- Cornflour — 30 g
- Orange blossom water — 3 ml
- Demerara sugar for burning — 60 g

## Method
1. Infuse milk with citrus peels and cinnamon: bring to 85 °C, cover, rest 20 minutes, strain.
2. Whisk yolks, caster sugar and cornflour until pale.
3. Re-warm milk; temper into the yolks, return to the pan.
4. Cook over medium-low, whisking constantly, to 84 °C until thick and just boiling at the edges, about 4 minutes.
5. Off heat, add orange blossom water; pour into shallow cazuelitas.
6. Chill 4 hours minimum, surface uncovered so it firms.
7. Just before serving, coat evenly with demerara and burn with a torch (or a traditional hot iron) to an amber glass.

## Plating & Service
Crack at the table — the tap of the spoon on the sugar is the dessert's overture; serve within 10 minutes of burning.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free with oat cream at 700 ml; infuse with lavender instead of cinnamon for a Provençal accent.
