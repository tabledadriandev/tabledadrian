# Salade Nicoise with Seared Rare Tuna and Anchovy Vinaigrette

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Niçoise reimagined with seared rare tuna loin — every element seasoned separately, the anchovy vinaigrette pulling it into one dish.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Tuna loin, sashimi-grade — 400 g
- Eggs — 220 g (4 eggs)
- Green beans — 200 g
- Baby gem lettuce — 200 g
- Cherry tomatoes — 250 g
- Niçois olives — 60 g
- Radishes — 80 g
- Spring onions — 40 g
- Anchovy fillets — 20 g
- Dijon mustard — 8 g
- Red wine vinegar — 20 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml
- Basil leaves — 6 g
- Fine sea salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Boil eggs 7 minutes for a fudgy yolk; cool in iced water, peel, halve.
2. Blanch beans 3 minutes in heavily salted water; refresh and dry.
3. Mash anchovies with mustard and vinegar; whisk in 80 ml oil for a punchy vinaigrette.
4. Season tuna, rub with remaining oil; sear on a smoking plancha 45 seconds per side — the core stays rare (about 38 °C).
5. Rest tuna 5 minutes, then slice 1 cm thick against the grain.
6. Dress each element separately in a little vinaigrette: leaves, beans, halved tomatoes, radishes.
7. Compose on a platter, tuna over, eggs, olives and spring onion last; final drizzle and basil.

## Plating & Service
Build on one grand platter for the table — height in the centre, tuna slices fanned rare-side up.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Classic version uses confit tuna belly from a jar; omit egg and use capers instead of anchovy in the dressing for a lighter plate.
