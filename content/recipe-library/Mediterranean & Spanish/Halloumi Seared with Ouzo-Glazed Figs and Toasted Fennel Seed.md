# Halloumi Seared with Ouzo-Glazed Figs and Toasted Fennel Seed

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Squeaky seared halloumi against warm ouzo-lacquered figs — sweet, saline, anise-scented; a five-ingredient showstopper.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Halloumi — 350 g
- Fresh figs — 240 g (4 figs)
- Ouzo — 40 ml
- Thyme honey — 30 g
- Fennel seeds — 3 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 25 ml
- Lemon zest — 2 g
- Rocket leaves — 40 g

## Method
1. Slice halloumi into 1.5 cm slabs; pat completely dry.
2. Toast fennel seeds in a dry pan 60 seconds; set aside.
3. Quarter figs; cook cut-side down in 10 ml oil over medium-high heat 2 minutes.
4. Add ouzo and honey; let it bubble to a glaze, 90 seconds, then remove figs and glaze.
5. Wipe the pan, add remaining oil, sear halloumi 2 minutes per side until deep golden.
6. Plate halloumi with figs, spoon over glaze, finish with fennel seeds, lemon zest and rocket.

## Plating & Service
Serve immediately — halloumi waits for no one; overlap cheese and figs casually, glaze pooling.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Ripe apricots or grilled peach work when figs are out of season; maple syrup replaces honey for strict vegetarians.
