# Red Mullet with Sauce Vierge and Crushed Ratte Potatoes

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Crisp-skinned rouget over warm crushed rattes, doused in a tomato-olive sauce vierge — the Riviera bistro plate, executed precisely.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Red mullet fillets, pin-boned, skin on — 480 g (8 fillets)
- Ratte or new potatoes — 500 g
- Ripe tomatoes, concassé — 250 g
- Black olives, chopped — 40 g
- Capers, rinsed — 15 g
- Shallot, brunoise — 30 g
- Basil, sliced — 8 g
- Coriander seeds, cracked — 2 g
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 110 ml
- Flaky sea salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Simmer potatoes in well-salted water 18–20 minutes until tender; drain.
2. Warm 60 ml oil to 50 °C with coriander seeds; add tomato concassé, shallot, olives, capers and lemon juice — the sauce vierge should be warm, never cooked.
3. Crush potatoes coarsely with a fork, folding in 30 ml oil and salt; keep warm.
4. Dry the mullet fillets; season the skin and press flat.
5. Sear skin-down in remaining oil over medium-high 2–3 minutes until the skin crackles; flip for 20 seconds only.
6. Stir basil into the sauce vierge at the last second; plate potatoes, fillets skin-up, sauce spooned generously around.

## Plating & Service
Skin up always — rouget skin is the flavour; serve on warm plates, sauce pooling into the crushed potatoes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Sea bream or john dory fillets substitute; swap basil for tarragon and add orange zest for a different vierge.
