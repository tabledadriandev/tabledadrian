# Cataplana de Marisco with Chourico, Prawns and Coriander

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Algarve's copper clamshell pot — prawns, clams and fish steamed over a chouriço-pepper base; the lid opens once, at the table.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Large prawns, shell on — 400 g
- Clams, purged — 600 g
- Firm white fish, chunks — 300 g
- Chouriço, sliced — 100 g
- Onion, sliced — 150 g
- Red pepper, strips — 150 g
- Ripe tomatoes, chopped — 400 g
- Garlic — 12 g
- White wine — 150 ml
- Piri-piri or chilli flakes — 2 g
- Sweet paprika — 4 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 50 ml
- Coriander, chopped — 20 g
- Bay leaf — 1 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Render chouriço in the oil in the cataplana (or wide lidded pot) 3 minutes; add onion and pepper, 6 minutes.
2. Add garlic, paprika and piri-piri, 1 minute; add tomatoes and bay, cooking 8 minutes.
3. Add wine; simmer 3 minutes and season.
4. Nestle fish into the sauce, then clams, then prawns on top with half the coriander.
5. Clamp the lid; cook over medium heat 8–10 minutes without opening — steam does the work.
6. Open at the table; shower with remaining coriander and check every clam has opened.

## Plating & Service
The hinged pot opens tableside in a rush of steam; serve with rice or fried bread and cold vinho verde.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skip chouriço and double the paprika for pescatarian purity; monkfish and lobster elevate it to celebration grade.
