# Fattoush-Style Riviera Salad with Crisped Socca and Sumac

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Levant-meets-Nice: fattoush crunch built on shards of crisped chickpea socca instead of pita — naturally gluten-free and twice as savoury.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chickpea flour — 100 g
- Water — 200 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- Baby gem lettuce — 200 g
- Ripe tomatoes — 300 g
- Cucumber — 200 g
- Radishes — 80 g
- Spring onions — 40 g
- Mint — 8 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 10 g
- Sumac — 5 g
- Lemon juice — 35 ml
- Pomegranate molasses — 15 g
- Tahini — 10 g
- Fine sea salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Whisk chickpea flour, water, 15 ml oil and 2 g salt; rest 20 minutes.
2. Pour thinly onto an oiled tray; bake at 220 °C for 10–12 minutes until crisp and deeply browned at the edges; cool and snap into shards.
3. Whisk lemon juice, pomegranate molasses, tahini, 45 ml oil and 3 g sumac into a creamy dressing.
4. Cut tomatoes into wedges, cucumber into half-moons, radishes into thin coins; tear lettuce.
5. Toss vegetables and herbs with the dressing.
6. Fold in socca shards at the last second; finish with remaining sumac and oil.

## Plating & Service
Serve immediately in a big shallow bowl while the socca still cracks; great beside grilled fish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Classic version with toasted pita if gluten is fine; add 150 g chickpeas to make it a complete lunch.
