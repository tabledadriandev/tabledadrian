# Grilled Whole Sea Bass Flambeed with Pastis and Dried Fennel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Loup de mer grilled over dried fennel branches and flamed with pastis at the table — the definitive Riviera fish, three ingredients from glory.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Whole sea bass, gutted, scaled — 1600 g (2 fish)
- Dried fennel branches — 30 g
- Fennel bulb, sliced — 150 g
- Lemon — 160 g (2)
- Extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- Pastis — 50 ml
- Flaky sea salt — 8 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Fresh thyme — 3 g

## Method
1. Score each fish three times per side to the bone; salt inside and out 20 minutes ahead.
2. Stuff cavities with fennel slices, lemon slices and thyme; brush skins with oil.
3. Lay half the dried fennel branches on the grill over medium-hot coals (or in a grill basket).
4. Grill fish on the branches 8–10 minutes per side, turning once with two spatulas, until 55 °C at the bone.
5. Transfer to a metal platter over the remaining fennel branches.
6. Warm pastis briefly, pour over the fish and ignite tableside; let flames die, then rest 4 minutes.
7. Fillet at the table; sauce with the flamed juices, remaining oil and lemon.

## Plating & Service
The flambé is the service — dim deck lights, warn the guests, pour with a straight arm.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Gilthead bream or dentex behave identically; no branches? Add 3 g fennel seed to the cavity and proceed.
