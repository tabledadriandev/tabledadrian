# Warm Lentil and Mackerel Salad with Xeres Dressing

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Puy lentils and seared mackerel under a sherry-vinegar dressing — cheap, fast, omega-rich; the crew lunch that guests always ask to share.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Puy lentils — 300 g
- Carrot, brunoise — 80 g
- Celery, brunoise — 60 g
- Shallot, brunoise — 60 g
- Bay leaf — 1 g
- Mackerel fillets, pin-boned — 400 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- Sherry (Xeres) vinegar — 25 ml
- Dijon mustard — 10 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 10 g
- Chives — 5 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Simmer lentils with bay in unsalted water for 20–25 minutes until tender but intact; drain, discard bay.
2. Sweat carrot, celery and half the shallot in 20 ml oil for 4 minutes; fold into the warm lentils.
3. Whisk vinegar, mustard, remaining raw shallot and 40 ml oil; dress the lentils warm and season.
4. Score mackerel skin; salt 5 minutes.
5. Sear skin-side down in remaining oil over high heat 3 minutes until crisp; flip for 30 seconds.
6. Serve mackerel over the lentils, herbs showered on top.

## Plating & Service
Shallow bowls, lentils loose and glossy, mackerel skin-up; a lemon cheek per plate.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Hot-smoked mackerel needs no cooking — just flake through; swap lentils for white beans for a softer texture.
