# Tapenade Noire with Confit Citrus and Marcona Almonds

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Coarse-chopped Nyons olive tapenade brightened with confit lemon — knife-cut, never blitzed to paste, so every element keeps its voice.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Black olives (Nyons or Kalamata), pitted — 250 g
- Capers, rinsed — 30 g
- Anchovy fillets (optional, omit for vegan) — 15 g
- Confit lemon peel, brunoise — 15 g
- Garlic, grated — 3 g
- Marcona almonds, chopped — 40 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- Fresh thyme leaves — 2 g
- Orange zest — 2 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Dry the olives well on paper — excess brine dulls the oil.
2. Chop olives, capers and anchovy by hand to a coarse 3–4 mm rubble.
3. Fold in confit lemon, garlic, thyme and orange zest.
4. Stir through olive oil to a spoonable, glistening texture.
5. Fold in almonds last to keep their crunch.
6. Rest 30 minutes at room temperature; check pepper — salt is rarely needed.

## Plating & Service
Serve in a shallow bowl with grilled fougasse, or quenelle onto seared tuna as an instant sauce.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Green olive version with basil and pistachio is summery; keeps 5 days chilled under a film of oil.
