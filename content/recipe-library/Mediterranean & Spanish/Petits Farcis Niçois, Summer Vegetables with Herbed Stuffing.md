# Petits Farcis Niçois, Summer Vegetables with Herbed Stuffing

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Nice's stuffed market vegetables in miniature — each vegetable's scooped flesh returns to its own stuffing, so nothing tastes generic.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Round baby tomatoes — 300 g (6)
- Baby courgettes, round — 300 g (6)
- Small sweet onions — 240 g (6)
- Sausage meat or minced pork — 300 g
- Stale bread, soaked in 60 ml milk or stock — 60 g
- Egg — 55 g (1 egg)
- Garlic, chopped — 8 g
- Basil, chopped — 10 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 8 g
- Parmesan-style hard cheese (optional) — 30 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- Dried breadcrumbs — 30 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Halve or behead vegetables; scoop flesh leaving 8 mm walls. Salt tomato shells and invert 15 minutes.
2. Blanch onion shells 5 minutes; chop all scooped flesh finely.
3. Sauté the chopped flesh with garlic in 20 ml oil for 6 minutes until dry; cool.
4. Mix with sausage meat, squeezed bread, egg, basil, parsley and cheese; season.
5. Fill shells generously in a domed mound; top with breadcrumbs and a thread of oil.
6. Bake at 180 °C for 40–50 minutes until burnished and the filling reaches 74 °C.
7. Rest 10 minutes — they are best warm, not hot.

## Plating & Service
One of each vegetable per plate with pan juices spooned over; equally good cold in a beach basket lunch.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian with cooked rice and chopped olives replacing meat; GF using rice and omitting breadcrumbs.
