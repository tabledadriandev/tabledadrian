# Arroz Negro with Squid Ink, Aioli and Chargrilled Baby Squid

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Jet-black rice, briny-sweet with squid ink, crowned with flash-charred baby squid and a sharp aioli to cut through.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Bomba or senia rice — 350 g
- Fish fumet — 1100 ml
- Baby squid, cleaned — 500 g
- Squid ink — 12 g
- Onion, brunoise — 120 g
- Green pepper, brunoise — 60 g
- Tomato, grated — 150 g
- Garlic — 12 g
- Sweet smoked paprika — 4 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 80 ml
- Egg yolk — 20 g
- Sunflower oil — 130 ml
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Fine sea salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Make aioli: pound 4 g garlic with salt, whisk with yolk, emulsify in sunflower oil, season with lemon juice; chill.
2. Separate squid tentacles; slice half the bodies into rings, keep the rest whole for grilling.
3. In a paella pan, soften onion and pepper in 50 ml oil, 8 minutes; add remaining garlic and squid rings, 2 minutes.
4. Add tomato; cook to a dark sofrito, 10 minutes. Bloom paprika 15 seconds.
5. Dissolve ink in 100 ml warm fumet; add to the pan with the rice, toasting 2 minutes.
6. Add remaining hot fumet, season, and cook without stirring: 8 minutes hard boil, then 8–10 gentle, until dry with a light socarrat.
7. Meanwhile toss whole squid and tentacles in remaining oil and salt; blast on a screaming plancha 90 seconds.
8. Rest rice 5 minutes; top with charred squid and quenelles of aioli.

## Plating & Service
Black rice, white aioli, blistered squid — monochrome drama; serve in the pan with lemon.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Use the ink sauce base for fideuà negra with GF noodles; prawns can replace half the squid.
