# Caldero Murciano, Grey Mullet Rice with Ñora and Garlic Fish Broth

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Mar Menor fisherman's pot — rice cooked in a ferociously reduced ñora-garlic fish broth, the fish served after, anointed with aioli.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Grey mullet or gilthead, whole, filleted, bones kept — 900 g
- Water — 2000 ml
- Ñora peppers — 20 g
- Garlic — 30 g
- Ripe tomatoes, grated — 250 g
- Bomba rice — 320 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml
- Sweet paprika — 4 g
- Saffron — 0.3 g
- Egg yolk — 20 g
- Sunflower oil — 120 ml
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Fine sea salt — 10 g

## Method
1. Fry whole ñoras in 30 ml olive oil 60 seconds; reserve. Fry 20 g garlic cloves until golden; reserve with the ñoras.
2. Brown the fish bones in the same oil; add water, simmer 25 minutes, strain.
3. Pound ñoras and fried garlic to a paste; return to the broth with tomato and paprika; simmer 20 minutes and season hard — this broth should taste almost too intense.
4. Make aioli with yolk, remaining raw garlic, sunflower oil and lemon.
5. Strain 1100 ml broth into a wide pot; bring to a rolling boil, add rice and saffron; cook 16–17 minutes to a loose, brothy finish.
6. Poach the fish fillets in the remaining broth at 70 °C for 6–8 minutes.
7. Serve the rice first, then the fish moistened with broth and crowned with aioli.

## Plating & Service
Two acts, one pot: rice course, then fish course — announce it like the Murcians do; aioli bridges both.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Any firm white fish works; the double-course service makes a 4-course dinner feel effortless.
