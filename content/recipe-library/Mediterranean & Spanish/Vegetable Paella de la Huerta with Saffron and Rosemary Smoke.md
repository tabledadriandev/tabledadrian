# Vegetable Paella de la Huerta with Saffron and Rosemary Smoke

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A garden paella that takes vegetables seriously — each cooked to its own point, with a rosemary branch smoked over the resting rice.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Bomba rice — 450 g
- Rich vegetable stock, hot — 1350 ml
- Artichoke hearts, quartered — 300 g
- Green beans (flat, romano) — 200 g
- Red pepper, strips — 150 g
- Butter beans, cooked — 200 g
- Tomato, grated — 250 g
- Garlic — 12 g
- Sweet smoked paprika — 6 g
- Saffron — 0.5 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml
- Rosemary, one woody branch — 5 g
- Lemon — 120 g
- Fine sea salt — 10 g

## Method
1. Sear artichokes in 40 ml oil in the paella pan until golden, 5 minutes; remove. Repeat with beans and pepper strips, 3 minutes; remove.
2. Add remaining oil and garlic 30 seconds, then tomato; cook the sofrito 12 minutes to brick-red. Bloom paprika 15 seconds.
3. Toast rice in the sofrito 2 minutes.
4. Add hot stock and saffron; season assertively (vegetable paellas die undersalted). Do not stir again.
5. Boil 8 minutes; arrange all vegetables and butter beans over the rice, simmer 8–10 minutes more until dry.
6. Create socarrat 60 seconds on high; kill the heat, lay the rosemary branch on the rice and cover with a cloth 5 minutes — the resting steam carries the resin.
7. Remove branch; serve with lemon.

## Plating & Service
The pan at the table, vegetables in deliberate sectors of colour; rosemary scent should arrive before the pan does.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add seared oyster mushrooms for umami weight; in spring, asparagus and peas replace beans.
