# Salt Cod Pastéis de Bacalhau with Lemon-Caper Mayonnaise

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Portugal's torpedo-shaped cod fritters, quenelled between two spoons and fried to a bronze crust — crisp outside, fluffy potato-cod within.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Salt cod, desalinated 24–36 h — 300 g
- Floury potatoes — 400 g
- Onion, very finely chopped — 60 g
- Garlic, grated — 5 g
- Flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 12 g
- Eggs — 110 g (2 eggs)
- Black pepper — 1 g
- Sunflower oil for frying — 1200 ml
- Egg yolk — 20 g
- Sunflower oil (mayo) — 130 ml
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Capers, chopped — 15 g

## Method
1. Simmer potatoes whole in their skins until tender, 25 minutes; peel and mash dry — no liquid added.
2. Poach cod in water at 80 °C for 8 minutes; flake finely, removing skin and bones, then shred with a fork to threads.
3. Beat cod into the warm mash with onion, garlic, parsley and pepper.
4. Beat in eggs one at a time to a firm, pipeable paste; chill 30 minutes.
5. Make the mayonnaise: whisk yolk, emulsify in oil, season with lemon juice and fold in capers.
6. Shape 40 g quenelles between two spoons.
7. Fry at 175 °C, six at a time, 3–4 minutes until deep bronze; drain and salt lightly.

## Plating & Service
Pile on a paper-lined dish with the caper mayonnaise for dipping; serve hot or at room temperature with drinks.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Bake at 210 °C for 18 minutes for a lighter version; smoked mackerel replaces cod in a pinch (skip desalination).
