# Daurade en Croute de Sel with Citrus Beurre Blanc

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Gilthead bream baked in an aromatic salt shell — cracked open at the table into clouds of steam — with a citrus beurre blanc.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Whole gilthead bream, gutted, not scaled — 1400 g (2 fish)
- Coarse sea salt — 2000 g
- Egg whites — 120 g (4)
- Lemon zest — 4 g
- Fennel seeds — 4 g
- Thyme sprigs — 5 g
- Orange — 150 g
- Shallot, minced — 30 g
- White wine — 60 ml
- White wine vinegar — 15 ml
- Cold unsalted butter, cubed — 150 g
- Chives — 4 g

## Method
1. Heat oven to 200 °C. Mix coarse salt with egg whites, lemon zest and fennel seeds to wet-sand texture.
2. Stuff fish cavities with thyme and two orange slices.
3. Bed 1 cm of salt mix on a tray; lay the fish, pack the remainder over to seal completely, leaving heads exposed.
4. Bake 25–30 minutes — 55 °C core through the shell (probe through the crust).
5. Rest 5 minutes; meanwhile reduce shallot, wine, vinegar and 30 ml orange juice to 25 ml.
6. Whisk in cold butter cube by cube over low heat; season and add chives — never boil.
7. Crack the crust at the table, brush away salt, lift the fillets; skin comes with the crust. Sauce alongside.

## Plating & Service
The crack is theatre: mallet or spoon-back, one firm rap along the spine; serve fillets on warm plates, sauce spooned over.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Sea bass or large red snapper love the shell; herbes de Provence in the salt for a different perfume. Dairy-free: sauce vierge instead.
