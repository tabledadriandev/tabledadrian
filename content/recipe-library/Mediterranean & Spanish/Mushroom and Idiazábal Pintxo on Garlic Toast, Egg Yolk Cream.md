# Mushroom and Idiazábal Pintxo on Garlic Toast, Egg Yolk Cream

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A tribute to the mushroom bars of Logroño — plancha-seared mushrooms stacked on garlic toast with a slow-cooked yolk cream binding it all.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Button or wild mushrooms — 500 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- Garlic, grated — 10 g
- Baguette — 180 g (12 slices)
- Egg yolks — 80 g (4 yolks)
- Unsalted butter, melted — 40 g
- Lemon juice — 5 ml
- Idiazábal cheese, grated — 50 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 6 g
- Fine sea salt — 4 g

## Method
1. Cook egg yolks in a bain-marie at 65 °C for 20 minutes, then whisk warm with melted butter and lemon juice to a glossy cream; hold warm.
2. Quarter mushrooms; sear in batches on a very hot plancha with 40 ml oil, 3 minutes per batch, until deeply browned; season.
3. Toss all mushrooms back with half the garlic for 30 seconds.
4. Rub toasted baguette slices with remaining garlic and brush with oil.
5. Stack mushrooms high on each toast, spoon over yolk cream.
6. Grate Idiazábal over and flash under a hot grill 60 seconds; finish with parsley.

## Plating & Service
Serve two per person on slate with skewers through the stack; the yolk cream should just start to run.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skip cheese and use olive-oil sabayon for dairy-free; add a drop of truffle oil for gala nights.
