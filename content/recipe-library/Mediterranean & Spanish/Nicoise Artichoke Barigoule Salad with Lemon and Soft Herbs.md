# Nicoise Artichoke Barigoule Salad with Lemon and Soft Herbs

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Baby artichokes braised barigoule-style in white wine and served just warm in their own broth-turned-vinaigrette.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Baby violet artichokes — 800 g (8)
- Lemons — 120 g (1.5 fruits)
- Extra virgin olive oil — 80 ml
- Carrot, thin rounds — 80 g
- Celery, sliced — 50 g
- Shallots, sliced — 60 g
- Garlic — 8 g
- Dry white wine — 150 ml
- Vegetable stock — 200 ml
- Thyme — 2 g
- Coriander seeds — 2 g
- Chervil, tarragon, parsley — 12 g
- Fine sea salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Turn the artichokes: strip dark leaves, trim stems, halve, and hold in water with half a lemon's juice.
2. Sweat shallot, carrot, celery and garlic in 40 ml oil for 4 minutes.
3. Add artichokes cut-side down; colour 3 minutes.
4. Add wine, reduce 2 minutes; add stock, thyme, coriander seeds and salt.
5. Cover with a paper cartouche; simmer at 90 °C for 18–20 minutes until a knife meets no resistance.
6. Lift out vegetables; reduce the liquid by half, then whisk in remaining oil and lemon juice off the heat.
7. Serve artichokes just warm, doused in the broth-vinaigrette, showered with soft herbs.

## Plating & Service
Shallow bowls, broth generously spooned — give guests spoons and bread; equally lovely cold the next day.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 100 g cooked white beans for lunch weight; a strip of orange zest in the braise is a Provençal whisper.
