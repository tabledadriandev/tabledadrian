# Espinacas con Garbanzos, Cumin and Fried Bread Picada

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Seville's Moorish-rooted chickpea and spinach stew, thickened with a fried bread and cumin picada — deeply savoury for something meat-free.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Cooked chickpeas, drained — 480 g
- Spinach, washed — 400 g
- Rustic bread — 60 g
- Garlic cloves — 15 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- Ground cumin — 4 g
- Sweet smoked paprika — 5 g
- Tomato, grated — 100 g
- Sherry vinegar — 12 ml
- Vegetable stock — 150 ml
- Tahini — 10 g
- Fine sea salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Fry bread and whole garlic cloves in 40 ml olive oil over medium heat until deep gold, about 4 minutes.
2. Pound or blitz the fried bread and garlic with cumin, vinegar and tahini into a coarse picada.
3. In the same pan, bloom paprika in remaining oil 15 seconds, add grated tomato and cook 4 minutes.
4. Add chickpeas and stock; simmer 10 minutes.
5. Wilt in the spinach in two additions, 3 minutes.
6. Stir through the picada, simmer 5 minutes until thick and spoon-coating; season with salt.

## Plating & Service
Serve in small earthenware bowls with a drizzle of raw olive oil; good warm or at room temperature on a mezze spread.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Gluten-free with GF bread or 30 g ground almonds in the picada; add a fried egg on top for a light lunch.
