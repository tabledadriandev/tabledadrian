# Arros Negre de Sepia amb Allioli d'Api, Cuttlefish Rice with Celery Aioli

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A thicker, cuttlefish-forward black rice from the Empordà, cut with a celery-leaf aioli that reads like green celery salt against the ink.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Senia rice — 320 g
- Cuttlefish with ink sac, cleaned — 600 g
- Fish fumet — 1000 ml
- Cuttlefish or squid ink — 8 g
- Onion, brunoise — 150 g
- Tomato, grated — 180 g
- Garlic — 10 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- Egg yolk — 20 g
- Celery leaves, blanched — 15 g
- Celery heart, brunoise — 40 g
- Sunflower oil — 120 ml
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Fine sea salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Blend blanched celery leaves with sunflower oil; whisk yolk with 4 g grated garlic and emulsify in the green oil; season with lemon — a pale-green aioli.
2. Dice cuttlefish 2 cm; dry well.
3. Sear cuttlefish in olive oil over high heat 3 minutes until golden at the edges.
4. Add onion, cook 8 minutes; add remaining garlic and tomato, reducing to a dark sofrito, 10 minutes.
5. Stir ink into 100 ml warm fumet; add with rice, toasting 2 minutes.
6. Add remaining hot fumet and celery brunoise; season and cook 16–17 minutes without stirring to a moist, just-clinging texture.
7. Rest 5 minutes; serve with the celery aioli.

## Plating & Service
Rustic bowls, one green quenelle each; the celery cuts the ink's iron sweetness cleanly.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Standard aioli if celery is divisive; peas folded in at minute 14 add sweetness and colour.
