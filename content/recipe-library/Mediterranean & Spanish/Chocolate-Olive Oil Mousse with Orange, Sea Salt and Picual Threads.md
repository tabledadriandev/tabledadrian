# Chocolate-Olive Oil Mousse with Orange, Sea Salt and Picual Threads

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> An intense dairy-free mousse where peppery Picual olive oil replaces cream — orange zest, salt flakes and a final raw-oil thread over the top.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Dark chocolate 70% — 200 g
- Eggs, separated — 220 g (4)
- Caster sugar — 60 g
- Picual extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml
- Orange zest — 4 g
- Orange juice — 30 ml
- Flaky sea salt — 2 g
- Candied orange peel, slivered — 20 g

## Method
1. Melt chocolate to 45 °C over a bain-marie; whisk in 70 ml oil, orange juice and zest — it will gloss.
2. Whisk yolks into the warm (not hot) chocolate one by one.
3. Whip whites to soft peaks; rain in sugar and whip to glossy medium peaks.
4. Fold a third of the whites into the chocolate to loosen, then fold in the rest in two additions, cutting cleanly.
5. Divide into 6 glasses; chill 3 hours.
6. Serve with candied orange, salt flakes and a visible thread of the remaining raw oil.

## Plating & Service
The oil thread on top is a statement, not a garnish — pour it at the table and name the olive variety.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Arbequina gives a milder mousse; a pinch of Espelette in the chocolate wakes the whole table up.
