# Whole Grilled Fagri with Ladolemono and Charred Lemon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Island taverna orthodoxy — whole pink sea bream over charcoal, basted and finished with whisked ladolemono; the fish, the lemon, nothing to hide behind.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Whole fagri or sea bream, gutted, scaled — 1600 g (2)
- Extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml
- Lemons — 240 g (3)
- Dried Greek oregano — 3 g
- Garlic, grated — 3 g
- Flaky sea salt — 8 g
- Black pepper — 1 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 5 g

## Method
1. Score fish twice per side; salt inside and out 30 minutes before grilling and leave uncovered — dry skin chars, wet skin sticks.
2. Whisk 60 ml oil with the juice of 1.5 lemons, oregano and garlic into an emulsified ladolemono; set half aside for serving.
3. Brush fish and grill grates with remaining oil.
4. Grill over medium-hot coals 8–10 minutes per side, turning once only, basting with ladolemono, to 55 °C at the bone.
5. Halve remaining lemons; char cut-side down on the grill 3 minutes.
6. Rest fish 4 minutes; fillet at the table, spoon over reserved ladolemono, parsley, charred lemon alongside.

## Plating & Service
Fillet tableside onto warm plates; the ladolemono is whisked again just before it hits the fish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Any whole white fish obeys this method; add 2 g dried thyme to the baste for hillside notes.
