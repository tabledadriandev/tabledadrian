# Jamón Croquetas de Ibérico with Membrillo Glaze

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Ibérico ham croquetas with a whisper-thin quince glaze — salty, sweet, molten; the tapa that disappears before the tray lands.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Ibérico ham, finely chopped — 120 g
- Ibérico ham fat or butter — 60 g
- Plain flour — 70 g
- Whole milk — 550 ml
- Onion, brunoise — 50 g
- Nutmeg — 0.5 g
- Membrillo (quince paste) — 60 g
- Water — 30 ml
- Plain flour for coating — 60 g
- Eggs, beaten — 100 g
- Fine breadcrumbs — 120 g
- Sunflower oil for frying — 1000 ml

## Method
1. Sweat onion in ham fat over low heat 5 minutes; add flour and cook 3 minutes to a blond roux.
2. Whisk in milk warmed to 60 °C in three stages; cook 8–10 minutes until thick and shining.
3. Fold in ham, season with nutmeg (salt rarely needed), spread 2 cm deep and chill 4 hours minimum.
4. Melt membrillo with water over low heat into a brushable glaze.
5. Roll base into 25 g balls; coat in flour, egg, breadcrumbs.
6. Fry at 180 °C for 2–3 minutes until mahogany; drain.
7. Brush each croqueta with the barest film of warm membrillo glaze and serve within minutes.

## Plating & Service
Pile on a warm slate, glaze catching the light; a few drops of glaze dotted on the plate.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chopped roasted chestnuts plus smoked paprika replace ham for vegetarian; skip glaze for classicists.
