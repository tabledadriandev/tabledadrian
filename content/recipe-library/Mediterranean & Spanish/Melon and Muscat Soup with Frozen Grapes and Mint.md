# Melon and Muscat Soup with Frozen Grapes and Mint

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Cavaillon melon blended with Muscat de Beaumes-de-Venise into a chilled dessert soup — frozen grapes as the ice cubes; summer's laziest elegance.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Ripe Cavaillon or charentais melon — 900 g flesh
- Muscat de Beaumes-de-Venise — 80 ml
- Lime juice — 20 ml
- Honey or agave — 15 g
- Fine sea salt — 0.5 g
- Green grapes, frozen solid — 150 g
- Mint leaves, small — 4 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 10 ml

## Method
1. Freeze the grapes at least 4 hours ahead — they stand in for ice without dilution.
2. Blend melon flesh with Muscat, lime, honey and salt until totally smooth.
3. Pass through a fine sieve for silk; chill to 4 °C, at least 2 hours.
4. Check balance cold: it should taste of melon first, wine second.
5. Pour into chilled shallow bowls.
6. Add frozen grapes, mint and three drops of olive oil per bowl; serve immediately.

## Plating & Service
Serve in glass so the pale-orange soup shows; the frozen grapes clink like ice and chill it as they thaw.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Alcohol-free with verjus (20 ml) replacing Muscat; white peach version blends in seamlessly.
