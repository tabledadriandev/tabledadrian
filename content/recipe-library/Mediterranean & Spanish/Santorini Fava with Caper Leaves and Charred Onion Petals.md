# Santorini Fava with Caper Leaves and Charred Onion Petals

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Velvet yellow split-pea purée from Santorini, topped with bittersweet charred onion — deceptively simple, quietly luxurious.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Yellow split peas (fava) — 300 g
- Water — 900 ml
- Onion — 150 g (plus 100 g for petals)
- Garlic — 8 g
- Bay leaf — 1 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 80 ml
- Lemon juice — 25 ml
- Caper leaves or capers, rinsed — 30 g
- Red onion, shaved — 40 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Rinse split peas until the water runs clear.
2. Simmer peas with 150 g chopped onion, garlic, bay and water at a gentle 95 °C, skimming, for 35–40 minutes until collapsing.
3. Discard bay; blend hot with 50 ml olive oil and lemon juice until totally smooth; season with salt.
4. Quarter the remaining onion into petals; char cut-side down in a dry pan over high heat 4 minutes until blackened at the edges.
5. Spread fava thickly in a shallow bowl while warm.
6. Top with charred onion petals, caper leaves, shaved red onion and the remaining olive oil.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm or at room temperature; the surface should be lacquered with oil. Pairs with grilled bread and assyrtiko.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Stir in 5 g smoked paprika for depth; top with grilled octopus to turn it into a starter.
