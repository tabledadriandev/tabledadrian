# Pan Bagnat, the Pressed Nicoise Sandwich

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Nice's 'bathed bread' — a Niçoise salad pressed inside a crusty roll overnight so the juices soak the crumb; built for tender lunches.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Round crusty rolls or small boules — 480 g (4)
- Tuna confit in olive oil, drained — 250 g
- Eggs, boiled 8 minutes — 220 g (4)
- Ripe tomatoes, sliced — 300 g
- Radishes, sliced — 60 g
- Green pepper, fine rings — 60 g
- Spring onions — 40 g
- Niçois olives, pitted — 50 g
- Anchovy fillets — 25 g
- Basil leaves — 8 g
- Red wine vinegar — 15 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Split rolls and pull out a third of the crumb to make room.
2. Whisk vinegar with olive oil; brush both cut faces generously — the bread must be truly 'bathed'.
3. Layer bottom halves: tomato (salted), tuna, sliced egg, pepper rings, radish, spring onion, olives, anchovy, basil.
4. Season with pepper, drizzle remaining vinaigrette, cap with tops.
5. Wrap each tightly in parchment and film; press under a board with a 2 kg weight, 2 hours minimum, overnight chilled is ideal.
6. Serve at cool room temperature, halved to show the strata.

## Plating & Service
Cut in half at the table so the compressed layers show; perfect for tenders, beach drops and lazy anchorages.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian with grilled artichokes and white beans replacing tuna and anchovy; day-old bread actually works better.
