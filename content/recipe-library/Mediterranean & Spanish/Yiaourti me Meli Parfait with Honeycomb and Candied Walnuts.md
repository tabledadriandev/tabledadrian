# Yiaourti me Meli Parfait with Honeycomb and Candied Walnuts

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Greece's simplest dessert reborn as a frozen parfait — strained yoghurt folded with honey sabayon, shards of honeycomb, candied walnuts.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Greek yoghurt, strained overnight — 500 g
- Thyme honey — 120 g
- Egg yolks — 60 g (3)
- Double cream — 200 ml
- Lemon zest — 3 g
- Caster sugar — 100 g
- Bicarbonate of soda — 5 g
- Walnuts — 80 g
- Icing sugar — 20 g
- Flaky sea salt — 1 g

## Method
1. Honeycomb: cook 100 g sugar with 20 g of the honey to 150 °C; whisk in bicarbonate, pour instantly onto parchment; cool and shatter.
2. Candied walnuts: toss walnuts with icing sugar and 10 ml water; toast at 170 °C for 8–10 minutes; cool.
3. Whisk yolks with remaining honey over a bain-marie to 72 °C until ribboned; whisk off-heat until cool.
4. Fold the sabayon into the yoghurt with lemon zest.
5. Whip cream to soft peaks; fold through.
6. Freeze in a lined loaf tin 5 hours; slice and serve with honeycomb, walnuts and salt flakes.

## Plating & Service
Thick slices on chilled plates, honeycomb standing in the parfait like quartz; serve before the sun softens it.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No-freeze version: serve as a mousse after 2 hours chilled; drizzle raw honey at the table for gloss.
