# Boquerones en Vinagre with Green Apple and Dill Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> House-cured white anchovies lifted with green apple matchsticks — bright, cold and clean; the tapa for guests who claim not to like anchovies.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Very fresh anchovies, butterflied — 400 g
- White wine vinegar — 200 ml
- Cold water — 100 ml
- Fine sea salt — 12 g
- Garlic, sliced paper-thin — 8 g
- Green apple (Granny Smith) — 80 g
- Dill fronds — 10 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml
- Lemon juice — 8 ml

## Method
1. Freeze butterflied anchovies at −20 °C for 24 hours (anisakis safety), then thaw in the fridge.
2. Combine vinegar, water and salt; submerge fillets skin-side down and cure 4 hours in the fridge until the flesh turns opaque white.
3. Blitz 8 g dill with 60 ml olive oil; strain through muslin for a vivid dill oil.
4. Drain fillets, pat dry, and lay in a shallow dish with garlic slices and remaining 30 ml olive oil for 30 minutes.
5. Cut apple into fine matchsticks and toss with lemon juice.
6. Arrange anchovies in rows, scatter apple, drizzle dill oil and finish with remaining dill fronds.

## Plating & Service
Serve on a chilled white platter in neat silver rows, dill oil pooling between; picks and crisp bread alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The cure works equally on sardine fillets; skip garlic for low-FODMAP guests.
