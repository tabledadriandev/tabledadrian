# Arroz con Leche Asturiano with Salted Caramel Skin

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Asturian rice pudding stirred to custard-thick over an hour, finished with a torched salted-caramel skin — patience made edible.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Short-grain rice (bomba) — 120 g
- Whole milk — 1200 ml
- Double cream — 100 ml
- Lemon peel, wide strip — 6 g
- Cinnamon stick — 4 g
- Caster sugar — 110 g (pudding) + 50 g (skin)
- Unsalted butter — 25 g
- Fine sea salt — 2 g
- Ground cinnamon — 1 g

## Method
1. Blanch rice 2 minutes in boiling water; drain — this steadies the starch release.
2. Simmer milk with lemon peel and cinnamon stick; add rice.
3. Cook at the barest simmer 50–60 minutes, stirring every few minutes, until the rice is fully soft and the milk is a thick cream.
4. Remove peel and stick; stir in 110 g sugar, cream, butter and salt; cook 5 final minutes.
5. Pour into shallow bowls; cool, then chill 2 hours.
6. Top each with remaining sugar and torch to a dark caramel skin; a whisper of ground cinnamon at the edge.

## Plating & Service
The contrast — cold silk pudding, warm crackling caramel — demands the torch happen minutes before service.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Infuse with orange peel and star anise for a different register; oat milk plus coconut cream for dairy-free.
