# Fig and Frangipane Galette with Anise and Pine Nuts

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A free-form galette of black figs over anise-scented frangipane — rustic enough for the crew mess, precise enough for the owner's table.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Plain flour — 220 g
- Cold unsalted butter — 140 g (pastry) + 90 g (frangipane)
- Iced water — 60 ml
- Fine sea salt — 3 g
- Caster sugar — 15 g (pastry) + 90 g (frangipane)
- Ground almonds — 100 g
- Egg — 55 g (1) + 20 g yolk for glaze
- Pastis or 2 g ground anise — 10 ml
- Fresh figs — 600 g
- Pine nuts — 25 g
- Honey — 20 g
- Demerara sugar — 15 g

## Method
1. Rub cold butter into flour, salt and 15 g sugar to rough pebbles; bind with iced water; chill 1 hour.
2. Beat 90 g soft butter with 90 g sugar; add egg, almonds and pastis for the frangipane.
3. Roll pastry to a 32 cm round, 3 mm; chill on parchment 15 minutes.
4. Spread frangipane leaving a 5 cm border; quarter figs and stand them snugly on top, cut faces up.
5. Fold the border over pleat by pleat; glaze the rim with yolk and dust with demerara; scatter pine nuts on the figs.
6. Bake at 190 °C for 40–45 minutes until the base is browned through (check underneath).
7. Brush the figs with warmed honey; rest 20 minutes.

## Plating & Service
Serve barely warm with crème fraîche or nothing; the cross-section of fig, frangipane and shattering base sells itself.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Apricots, plums or pears rotate through the season; GF pastry with a 1:1 blend behaves well at 3 mm.
