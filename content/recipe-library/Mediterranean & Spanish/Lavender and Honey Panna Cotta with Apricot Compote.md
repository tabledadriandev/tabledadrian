# Lavender and Honey Panna Cotta with Apricot Compote

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A barely-set panna cotta — 1.6 g gelatine per 100 ml — infused with lavender restraint, over a sharp apricot compote; wobble is everything.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Double cream — 500 ml
- Whole milk — 150 ml
- Lavender honey — 70 g
- Culinary lavender flowers — 1.5 g
- Vanilla pod, split — 1 g
- Gold gelatine leaves — 10 g
- Apricots, stoned, quartered — 400 g
- Caster sugar — 50 g
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Pistachios, chopped — 20 g

## Method
1. Soak gelatine in iced water 5 minutes.
2. Heat cream, milk, honey, lavender and vanilla to 80 °C; cover and infuse exactly 8 minutes — longer turns soapy.
3. Strain; whisk in the squeezed gelatine until dissolved.
4. Cool the base over ice to 30 °C (this stops the vanilla sinking); pour into 6 glasses or moulds.
5. Set 4 hours minimum in the fridge.
6. Simmer apricots, sugar and lemon 8–10 minutes to a chunky compote; chill.
7. Serve panna cotta with compote and pistachios; unmould by dipping moulds 3 seconds in hot water.

## Plating & Service
In glass, layered, for boat service (no unmoulding at anchor); the wobble should answer the sea's.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan with coconut cream and 2 g agar (boiled 2 minutes); swap apricot for roasted fig in September.
