# Chilled Cucumber and Yoghurt Soup with Ouzo, Dill and Prawn Tartare

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A drinkable tzatziki chilled to 5 °C, crowned with a raw-cooked prawn tartare — cooling first course for the hottest charter days.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Cucumbers — 500 g
- Greek yoghurt — 300 g
- Iced water — 100 ml
- Garlic, grated — 3 g
- Ouzo — 10 ml
- Lemon juice — 25 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 50 ml
- Dill — 12 g
- Raw prawns, very fresh — 150 g
- Espelette pepper — 1 g
- Fine sea salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Blanch prawns 45 seconds in boiling salted water; shock in iced water — barely cooked, translucent centres.
2. Dice prawns 5 mm; dress with 10 ml lemon juice, 15 ml oil, a little dill and salt; keep at 2 °C.
3. Peel and deseed 400 g of the cucumber; blend with yoghurt, iced water, garlic, ouzo, remaining lemon and 8 g dill until smooth.
4. Season, pass through a coarse sieve, and chill to 5 °C.
5. Brunoise the remaining cucumber for texture.
6. Serve soup with a spoon of prawn tartare and cucumber brunoise in the centre, dill and remaining oil over.

## Plating & Service
Pour tableside around the tartare island; frozen coupe glasses hold the chill for ten extra minutes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skip prawns and top with chopped pistachios for vegetarian; kefir replaces yoghurt for a lighter, tangier soup.
