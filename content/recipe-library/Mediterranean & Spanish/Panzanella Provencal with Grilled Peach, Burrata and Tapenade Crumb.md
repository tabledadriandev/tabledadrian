# Panzanella Provencal with Grilled Peach, Burrata and Tapenade Crumb

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A Riviera detour on panzanella — charred sourdough, grilled peach and burrata over tomato juices, with a dehydrated tapenade crumb.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Sourdough, day-old — 250 g
- Ripe tomatoes, mixed — 500 g
- Peaches, firm-ripe — 300 g (2)
- Burrata — 250 g (2 balls)
- Extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml
- Red wine vinegar — 20 ml
- Garlic — 4 g
- Black olive tapenade — 50 g
- Basil leaves — 10 g
- Flaky sea salt — 4 g

## Method
1. Spread tapenade thinly on parchment; dry in a 120 °C oven 40 minutes until brittle, then crush to a crumb.
2. Tear bread into rough chunks; toss with 30 ml oil and grill or bake at 220 °C for 8 minutes until charred at the edges.
3. Cut tomatoes into chunks, salt them, and rest 15 minutes; whisk their juices with vinegar, grated garlic and 50 ml oil.
4. Halve and stone peaches, brush with oil; grill flesh-side down 3 minutes until marked, then slice.
5. Toss bread with tomatoes and dressing; rest 10 minutes so the bread drinks but keeps some crunch.
6. Fold in peaches and basil; tear burrata over, finish with tapenade crumb, oil and salt.

## Plating & Service
Serve slightly warm from the grill in a wide bowl; the burrata cream should mingle with tomato juices.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan without burrata — add 80 g white beans; nectarines or figs swap for peach through the season.
