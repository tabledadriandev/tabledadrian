# Chickpea, Spinach and Chorizo Soup-Salad with Soft Egg

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Halfway between potaje and salad — warm chickpeas and wilted spinach in a chorizo-paprika broth, topped with a 6-minute egg; hearty light lunch for cooler passages.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Cooked chickpeas — 500 g
- Cooking chorizo, diced — 150 g
- Spinach — 250 g
- Onion, chopped — 100 g
- Garlic — 8 g
- Sweet smoked paprika — 4 g
- Chicken or vegetable stock — 400 ml
- Sherry vinegar — 15 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 40 ml
- Eggs — 220 g (4)
- Fine sea salt — 4 g

## Method
1. Boil eggs exactly 6 minutes; shock in iced water and peel — the yolks stay molten.
2. Render chorizo in a dry pot over medium heat 4 minutes; remove, leaving the red fat.
3. Sweat onion and garlic in the chorizo fat with 20 ml oil for 5 minutes; bloom paprika 20 seconds.
4. Add chickpeas and stock; simmer 10 minutes, crushing a few chickpeas to thicken.
5. Return chorizo, wilt in the spinach 2 minutes, season with vinegar and salt.
6. Serve in bowls with a halved soft egg on each, remaining oil drizzled over.

## Plating & Service
Wide bowls, egg halves yolk-up, a grind of pepper; bread or not, it stands alone.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan with smoked tofu and 2 g extra paprika, omitting egg; a pinch of cumin takes it toward Morocco.
