# Marmite du Pecheur, Saffron Fish Stew with Rouille Croutons

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The forgiving cousin of bouillabaisse — one pot, mixed fish and shellfish in a saffron-tomato broth, built for whatever the fisherman actually had.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Mixed firm fish fillets (bream, hake, monkfish) — 800 g
- Mussels — 500 g
- Prawns, shell on — 300 g
- Onion, sliced — 150 g
- Fennel, sliced — 150 g
- Garlic — 15 g
- Ripe tomatoes, chopped — 500 g
- Fish fumet — 800 ml
- Dry white wine — 150 ml
- Saffron — 0.4 g
- Orange zest — 3 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- Baguette — 150 g
- Rouille — 120 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 8 g
- Fine sea salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Sweat onion and fennel in 50 ml oil 8 minutes; add garlic 1 minute.
2. Add tomatoes, cook 8 minutes; add wine, reduce by half.
3. Add fumet, saffron and orange zest; simmer 15 minutes, season.
4. Slice and toast baguette; spread with rouille.
5. Add fish in order of thickness: monkfish first, 4 minutes; bream and hake, 3 minutes; prawns and mussels last, until shells open.
6. Rest off the heat 2 minutes; shower with parsley and remaining oil.
7. Serve from the pot with rouille croutons floated on each bowl.

## Plating & Service
The marmite goes to the table; ladle generously and let croutons soak — humble presentation, deep broth.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Use whatever the market gives — the method absorbs anything; GF croutons or none, with rouille spooned straight in.
