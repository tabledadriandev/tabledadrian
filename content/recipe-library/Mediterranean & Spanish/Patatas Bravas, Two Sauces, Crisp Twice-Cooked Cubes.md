# Patatas Bravas, Two Sauces, Crisp Twice-Cooked Cubes

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Twice-cooked agria potatoes with a proper smoked bravas sauce and lemon aioli — humble, but the first plate emptied every time.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Agria or Monalisa potatoes — 800 g
- Sunflower oil for frying — 1000 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- Onion, finely chopped — 80 g
- Garlic — 10 g
- Sweet smoked paprika — 8 g
- Hot smoked paprika — 3 g
- Tomato passata — 200 g
- Sherry vinegar — 10 ml
- Egg yolk — 20 g
- Sunflower oil (aioli) — 130 ml
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Fine sea salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Peel potatoes and cut into 3 cm irregular cubes; rinse and simmer in salted water at 90 °C for 8 minutes until just tender; drain and air-dry 15 minutes.
2. For bravas sauce, sweat onion and half the garlic in olive oil 6 minutes; add both paprikas off the heat, stir 20 seconds.
3. Add passata, simmer 12 minutes, season with sherry vinegar and salt, then blend smooth.
4. For aioli, whisk yolk with remaining grated garlic; emulsify in sunflower oil, finish with lemon juice and salt.
5. First fry: 150 °C for 5 minutes until blond and cooked through; drain and cool 10 minutes.
6. Second fry: 185 °C for 3–4 minutes until deeply crisp; season immediately with salt.
7. Toss into a bowl, zigzag both sauces generously over the top.

## Plating & Service
Serve in a shallow terracotta dish, bravas sauce first, aioli piped in bold stripes, picks on the side.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Roast at 210 °C with 40 ml oil instead of frying for a lighter version; vegan by replacing aioli with garlic soy-milk emulsion.
