# Shellfish Risotto with Prawn Stock and Charred Lemon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A butter-free seafood risotto mounted with an intense prawn-head oil — every grain tasting of the crustacean, finished with smoke-edged lemon.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- carnaroli rice — 320 g
- raw prawns, shell on — 600 g
- mussels — 400 g
- olive oil — 90 ml
- tomato paste — 20 g
- fennel, fine dice — 80 g
- shallot — 50 g
- garlic — 2 cloves
- dry white wine — 120 ml
- water or light fish stock — 1.3 L
- lemon — 1, halved
- parsley — 6 g
- chilli — 0.5 g
- fine salt — to taste

## Method
1. Peel prawns. Fry heads and shells hard in 40 ml oil, crushing, 5 minutes; add tomato paste, 2 minutes; add water/stock, simmer 20 minutes, strain, pressing. Skim the red oil off the top and reserve it.
2. Open mussels with a splash of wine; pick, strain liquor into the stock.
3. Char the lemon halves cut-side down in a dry pan until blackened; reserve.
4. Sweat shallot, fennel and garlic in 30 ml oil; toast rice 2 minutes; deglaze with remaining wine.
5. Cook with the prawn stock, stirring, 15 minutes.
6. Add raw prawns (halved) and mussels in the final 2 minutes.
7. Off heat, mantecare with the reserved prawn oil and remaining olive oil; salt, chilli, parsley, a squeeze of the charred lemon.

## Plating & Service
All'onda on flat plates, prawns on top, charred lemon quarter on the rim for guests to squeeze.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No cheese, no butter — the crustacean oil is the luxury. Squid ink (8 g) at step 5 makes the black-tie version.
