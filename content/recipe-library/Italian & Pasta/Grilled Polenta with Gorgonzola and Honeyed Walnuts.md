# Grilled Polenta with Gorgonzola and Honeyed Walnuts

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Set polenta cut into planks, char-marked on the plancha and topped with molten gorgonzola and honey-glossed walnuts — a side that steals from the main.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- cooked firm polenta, set overnight (from 250 g dry) — 900 g
- olive oil — 40 ml
- gorgonzola dolce — 150 g
- walnut halves — 50 g
- honey — 20 g
- rosemary, chopped — 1 g
- flaky salt and black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Cut the set polenta into 2 cm-thick planks or triangles; dry the surfaces.
2. Brush with oil; grill on a hot plancha 4–5 minutes per side without moving, until char-barred and crisp-crusted.
3. Warm honey with rosemary; toss the walnuts through it in a small pan, 2 minutes.
4. Top each hot plank with a slab of gorgonzola; let it slump 1 minute on the grill's edge.
5. Finish with the walnuts, flaky salt and pepper.

## Plating & Service
Overlapping on a warm platter, cheese still moving, honey threads pulled across — beside grilled meats or as aperitivo.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Taleggio for the gentler table; vegan with smoked almond cream. The planks also crisp beautifully in an air fryer at 200°C.
