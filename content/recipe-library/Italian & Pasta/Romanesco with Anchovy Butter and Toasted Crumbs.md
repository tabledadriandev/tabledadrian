# Romanesco with Anchovy Butter and Toasted Crumbs

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The fractal brassica roasted hard, glossed in anchovy butter, and finished with crunch — umami engineering disguised as a vegetable side.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- romanesco, medium florets — 700 g
- butter — 60 g
- anchovy fillets — 6
- garlic, minced — 4 g
- lemon juice and zest — of half a lemon
- coarse breadcrumbs — 30 g
- olive oil — 30 ml
- chilli flakes — 0.5 g
- black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Toss the florets with olive oil; roast at 220°C for 12–15 minutes until charred at the tips and just tender.
2. Toast the crumbs in a dry pan to gold; reserve.
3. Melt butter over low heat; add anchovies and garlic; crush until the anchovies dissolve into a sauce, 2 minutes.
4. Off heat, add lemon juice, zest and chilli.
5. Toss the hot florets through the anchovy butter.
6. Shower with crumbs and pepper at the last second so they stay loud.

## Plating & Service
A bronzed pile, crumbs raining over, lemon zest — beside lamb, turbot, or as the sleeper hit of the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cauliflower or broccoli behave identically. Vegetarian: swap anchovy for 15 g capers plus 5 g white miso in the butter.
