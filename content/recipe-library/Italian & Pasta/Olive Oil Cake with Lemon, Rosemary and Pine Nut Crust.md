# Olive Oil Cake with Lemon, Rosemary and Pine Nut Crust

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Ligurian pantry cake — fruity olive oil where butter would be, rosemary in the sugar, a pine-nut crust; stays moist for days at sea.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- plain flour — 280 g
- baking powder — 10 g
- fine salt — 3 g
- whole eggs — 3
- caster sugar — 220 g
- extra virgin olive oil, fruity — 180 ml
- whole milk or almond milk — 120 ml
- lemon zest — of 3 lemons
- lemon juice — 30 ml
- rosemary, minced very fine — 2 g
- pine nuts — 50 g
- icing sugar — for dusting

## Method
1. Rub the lemon zest and rosemary into the sugar until fragrant and damp.
2. Whisk eggs with the aromatic sugar 3 minutes to pale ribbons.
3. Stream in the olive oil, whisking; then the milk and lemon juice.
4. Fold in flour, baking powder and salt until just combined.
5. Pour into a lined 24 cm tin; carpet the surface with pine nuts.
6. Bake at 170°C for 40–45 minutes until a skewer exits clean and the crust is deep gold.
7. Cool in the tin 15 minutes; then on a rack.

## Plating & Service
Thick slices under icing sugar with espresso, or dressed up with the macerated berries and a spoon of crème fraîche for evening service.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> It improves on day two wrapped in paper. Blood-orange juice and zest in winter; the rosemary is quiet — double it only if the table is brave.
