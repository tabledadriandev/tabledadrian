# Tajarin with Butter and White Truffle

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Piedmont's 30-yolk pasta — impossibly thin golden strands needing nothing but mountain butter and shaved Alba truffle; luxury measured in grams.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- 00 flour — 300 g
- egg yolks — 14 (about 250 g)
- whole egg — 1
- fine salt — 2 g
- unsalted butter, best quality — 90 g
- light chicken or vegetable brodo — 60 ml
- Parmigiano Reggiano, grated — 40 g
- fresh white truffle (or black in season) — 20–30 g

## Method
1. Knead flour with yolks, egg and salt to a stiff, deep-yellow dough — it will feel dry at first; persist 12 minutes. Rest wrapped 1 hour.
2. Roll to 0.6 mm in batches. Dust well, roll into flat cylinders and hand-cut 2 mm strands with a long knife.
3. Loosen the nests immediately and dust with semolina.
4. Emulsify butter with hot brodo in a wide pan — do not brown; hold at a bare simmer.
5. Boil tajarin 60–75 seconds in generously salted water.
6. Drag the pasta into the butter emulsion with tongs, add Parmigiano, toss until every strand gleams.

## Plating & Service
Tall twirled nests on warm plates; shave truffle tableside in front of guests — 5–7 g per plate.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No truffle aboard? Finish with 5 ml truffle-infused butter and toasted hazelnuts for the Langhe spirit at a fraction of the cost.
