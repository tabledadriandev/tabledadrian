# Busiate alla Trapanese with Almond and Raw Tomato Pesto

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Western Sicily's corkscrew pasta with a no-cook pesto of almonds, raw tomato, garlic and basil — bright, cold-sauce freshness for hot deck lunches.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- fine semolina — 400 g
- warm water — 195 ml
- fine salt — 4 g
- ripe datterini or cherry tomatoes — 350 g
- blanched almonds, lightly toasted — 80 g
- basil leaves — 25 g
- garlic — 6 g
- extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- lemon juice — 5 ml
- black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Knead semolina dough; rest 20 minutes. Roll thin 20 cm ropes and twist each around a skewer to form spirals; slide off and dry 30 minutes.
2. Pound (or pulse) garlic and almonds to a coarse rubble.
3. Add tomatoes and basil; crush to a chunky, juicy pesto — do not purée smooth.
4. Stream in olive oil, season with salt, pepper, lemon juice. Rest 20 minutes at room temperature.
5. Boil busiate 7–8 minutes until al dente.
6. Toss the hot pasta with the raw pesto off the heat — it should warm, not cook.

## Plating & Service
Room-temperature-warm in wide bowls, basil tips, cracked almonds, a defiant thread of oil.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Classically finished with grated pecorino — add 30 g if dairy is welcome. Nut allergy: use toasted sunflower seeds.
