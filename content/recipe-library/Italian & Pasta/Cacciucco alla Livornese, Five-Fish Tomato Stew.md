# Cacciucco alla Livornese, Five-Fish Tomato Stew

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Livorno's harbour stew — tradition demands one fish per 'c' in the name; each species enters the red-wine tomato base by its own clock over garlic-rubbed bread.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- mixed firm fish (monkfish, gurnard, dogfish), chunks — 700 g
- octopus or cuttlefish, pieces — 300 g
- mussels — 400 g
- raw prawns, shell on — 8
- small squid, rings — 200 g
- tinned tomatoes — 500 g
- tomato paste — 30 g
- red wine — 150 ml
- fish stock (from the trimmings) — 600 ml
- garlic — 6 cloves
- chilli — 2 g
- sage — 3 g
- olive oil — 90 ml
- country bread, thick slices — 6
- parsley — 10 g
- fine salt and black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Make a quick stock from heads and bones, 25 minutes; strain.
2. Sizzle 4 garlic cloves, sage and chilli in 60 ml oil; add octopus/cuttlefish; stew 5 minutes.
3. Add wine; reduce. Add tomato paste, tomatoes and stock; simmer 25 minutes — the base must be rich before any fish enters.
4. Add monkfish-type chunks; simmer 8 minutes.
5. Add squid and prawns, 3 minutes; then mussels, lid on, until they open, 3 minutes. Never stir again — swirl the pot.
6. Grill the bread; rub hard with the remaining raw garlic; place one slice in each bowl.
7. Season the stew; rain parsley and the last raw oil.

## Plating & Service
Ladle over the garlic bread so it drowns, shells up, prawns crowning — bibs are not shameful.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The rule is variety over luxury: five humble species beat two noble ones. Prep every fish ahead; the assembly is a 15-minute sprint.
