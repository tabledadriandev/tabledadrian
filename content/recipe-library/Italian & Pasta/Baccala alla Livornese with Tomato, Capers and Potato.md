# Baccala alla Livornese with Tomato, Capers and Potato

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rehydrated salt cod floured and seared, then finished in a lively Livorno tomato sauce with capers — sturdy sailor's food that plates surprisingly elegantly.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- salt cod, soaked 48 h in changed water — 700 g (soaked weight)
- plain flour — 50 g
- olive oil — 80 ml
- onion, sliced — 150 g
- garlic — 2 cloves
- tinned tomatoes, crushed — 500 g
- capers, rinsed — 25 g
- waxy potatoes, 1 cm slices, parboiled — 300 g
- dry white wine — 80 ml
- chilli — 0.5 g
- parsley — 8 g
- black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Confirm the cod is fully desalinated (taste a corner raw); dry; cut into 8 pieces; flour lightly.
2. Sear in 50 ml oil, 2 minutes per side, to a golden crust; remove — it finishes later.
3. Soften onion in the same pan 8 minutes; add garlic and chilli, 1 minute; deglaze with wine.
4. Add tomatoes and capers; simmer 15 minutes; season carefully (cod and capers carry salt).
5. Shingle in the parboiled potatoes; nestle the cod on top; spoon sauce over.
6. Simmer lidded 8–10 minutes until the cod flakes; rest 5 minutes; parsley and raw oil to finish.

## Plating & Service
From the pan at the table, or one piece over potatoes with sauce pooled around — bread on standby.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fresh cod works with 10 g salt rubbed 1 hour ahead and rinsed. Black olives folded in push it toward the south.
