# Sicilian Fennel and Orange Salad with Red Onion and Olives

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The island's winter salad — shaved fennel, orange rounds, black olives and onion in a citrus-oil dressing; crunchy, juicy, and a perfect foil to fried food.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- fennel bulbs — 2 (about 500 g)
- oranges — 3
- red onion, shaved paper-thin — 60 g
- black olives — 60 g
- extra virgin olive oil — 50 ml
- lemon juice — 15 ml
- fennel fronds — 5 g
- flaky salt and black pepper — to taste
- chilli flakes (optional) — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Soak the shaved onion in iced water 15 minutes; dry.
2. Shave the fennel paper-thin on a mandoline into iced water; dry at service.
3. Peel oranges to the flesh; slice into 5 mm rounds over a bowl, saving juice.
4. Whisk 20 ml saved orange juice with lemon juice, oil, salt and pepper.
5. Assemble at the last minute: fennel, orange rounds, onion, olives, dressing, fronds.

## Plating & Service
Flat and generous on a chilled platter so every round shows, pepper and optional chilli over — beside fritto misto it is non-negotiable.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Blood oranges in season transform it. Add bottarga shavings or anchovy for a fuller antipasto plate.
