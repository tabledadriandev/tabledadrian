# Porcini Risotto with Aged Parmesan and Thyme

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Double mushroom depth — dried porcini in the stock, fresh porcini seared separately and folded in last so they stay meaty, not stewed.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- carnaroli rice — 320 g
- dried porcini — 30 g
- fresh porcini or king oyster, thick-sliced — 250 g
- vegetable stock, hot — 1.2 L
- shallot — 50 g
- butter — 80 g
- olive oil — 20 ml
- dry white wine — 80 ml
- Parmigiano Reggiano — 80 g
- thyme — 2 g
- fine salt and black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Soak dried porcini in 300 ml hot water 30 minutes; chop; add the strained liquor to the stock.
2. Sear fresh porcini hard in oil and 20 g butter until golden; season with thyme and salt; reserve.
3. Sweat shallot in 20 g butter; toast rice 2 minutes; deglaze with wine.
4. Add chopped soaked porcini, then cook with stock ladle by ladle, 15–16 minutes.
5. Off heat, rest 1 minute; mantecare with remaining cold butter and Parmigiano.
6. Fold in two-thirds of the seared porcini; loosen to a wave.

## Plating & Service
Best porcini slices arranged over, thyme flowers, one turn of pepper, a drop of aged balsamic if the guests run sweet.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> A 5 ml splash of soy in the stock deepens umami invisibly. Vegan: mantecare with 30 g cashew butter and nutritional yeast.
