# Focaccia di Recco with Stracchino

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Liguria's paper-thin unleavened focaccia — two translucent sheets around molten stracchino, blistered at maximum oven heat; the Riviera's greatest cheese delivery system.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- strong flour (W300+) — 400 g
- water — 200 ml
- extra virgin olive oil — 40 ml (plus 30 ml for finishing)
- fine salt — 8 g
- stracchino or crescenza cheese — 500 g
- flaky salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Knead flour, water, oil and salt 10 minutes to a smooth, elastic dough. Rest under a bowl 2 hours minimum.
2. Divide in two (one slightly larger). Oil a 30×40 cm shallow tray.
3. Stretch the larger piece over the backs of your hands like strudel dough until translucent — it should read newsprint. Drape over the tray with overhang.
4. Dot walnut-size lumps of stracchino every 5 cm.
5. Stretch the second sheet over the top; press to seal at the rim and trim. Pinch a few tears in the top sheet as steam vents.
6. Slick with olive oil and flaky salt.
7. Bake at the oven's absolute maximum (280–300°C, or 250°C fan on a preheated steel) 6–8 minutes until blistered and leoparded.

## Plating & Service
Cut in rough squares and serve scalding — the cheese must flow. No accompaniment needed except cold Vermentino.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Crescenza is canonical; a young taleggio-style without rind works. The stretch takes practice — tears in the bottom sheet, not the top, are the real failure.
