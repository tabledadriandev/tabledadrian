# Lemon and Olive Oil Panna Cotta with Basil Sugar

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A Riviera signature — cream infused with lemon zest, a delicate olive oil emulsified in for texture and perfume, finished with green basil sugar.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- double cream — 450 ml
- whole milk — 150 ml
- caster sugar — 90 g (plus 30 g for basil sugar)
- lemon zest — of 3 lemons
- gold gelatine — 6 g
- delicate extra virgin olive oil (ligurian taggiasca) — 40 ml (plus for finishing)
- basil leaves — 6 g
- lemon juice — 15 ml
- flaky salt — a few flakes

## Method
1. Soak gelatine. Warm cream, milk, 90 g sugar and zest to 80°C; infuse 15 minutes, covered; strain.
2. Dissolve the gelatine in the warm base; add lemon juice.
3. Cool to 35°C, then blend in the olive oil with a stick blender — 20 seconds to a full emulsion.
4. Pour into glasses; set 5 hours.
5. Basil sugar: blitz basil with 30 g sugar to a green frost; spread to dry 30 minutes.

## Plating & Service
In the glass: basil sugar in a thin sheet across the surface, three drops of oil, two flakes of salt — cream, citrus, grass in one spoon.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The oil must be a gentle one — a peppery Tuscan bully ruins it. Mandarin zest in winter; the basil sugar keeps a week airtight.
