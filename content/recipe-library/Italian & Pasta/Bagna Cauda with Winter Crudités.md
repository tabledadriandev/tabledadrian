# Bagna Cauda with Winter Crudités

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Piedmont farmhouse fondue — anchovy and garlic mellowed in milk then emulsified warm with oil and butter, served bubbling with raw winter vegetables.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- anchovy fillets in oil — 100 g
- garlic — 60 g (about 12 cloves)
- whole milk — 200 ml
- extra virgin olive oil — 150 ml
- butter — 40 g
- raw cardoon or celery hearts — 200 g
- peppers, thick batons — 200 g
- radicchio leaves — 100 g
- cauliflower florets, raw — 150 g
- baby carrots — 150 g
- cooked beetroot, wedges — 150 g

## Method
1. Simmer whole peeled garlic in the milk over the lowest heat 20 minutes until completely soft.
2. Mash the garlic into the milk; add anchovies and stir over low heat until they dissolve, 5 minutes.
3. Whisk in olive oil gradually, then the butter, keeping the sauce at a bare shudder — it must never fry.
4. Adjust with milk to a loose, dippable cream.
5. Prepare vegetables in generous, honest pieces — this is a communal dish.

## Plating & Service
Keep warm in a fujot (candle-warmed pot) or small fondue burner at the centre of the table, vegetables around like a still life.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Gentler version: double the milk step. Deck safety — use an enclosed candle warmer, never open flame in wind. Leftover sauce over roast peppers is tomorrow's lunch.
