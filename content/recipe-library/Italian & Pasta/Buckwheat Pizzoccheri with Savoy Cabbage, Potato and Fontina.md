# Buckwheat Pizzoccheri with Savoy Cabbage, Potato and Fontina

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Valtellina's alpine buckwheat ribbons baptised in garlic-sage butter with cabbage, potato and rivers of melting cheese — the ultimate cold-evening anchor dish.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- buckwheat flour — 240 g
- 00 flour — 120 g
- whole egg — 1
- warm water — about 130 ml
- savoy cabbage, 2 cm ribbons — 300 g
- waxy potatoes, 1.5 cm dice — 250 g
- fontina or Valtellina Casera, sliced thin — 200 g
- Parmigiano Reggiano — 60 g
- butter — 110 g
- garlic, sliced — 2 cloves
- sage — 10 leaves
- black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Knead flours, egg and water to a firm dough; rest 30 minutes.
2. Roll to 2 mm; cut ribbons 1 cm wide, 7 cm long.
3. Boil potatoes in salted water 6 minutes; add cabbage and pizzoccheri, cook 5 minutes more together.
4. Meanwhile take butter to golden-brown with garlic and sage.
5. In a warm gratin dish, layer drained pasta-and-vegetables with the cheeses in three strata.
6. Pour the sizzling butter over; rest 2 minutes under foil so the cheese rivers form. Do not stir — lift and fold once.

## Plating & Service
Serve from the dish at the table, spooned in shaggy layers, black pepper over — rustic by design.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Not for the light-lunch day. GF: use 100% buckwheat with 8 g psyllium and roll slightly thicker.
