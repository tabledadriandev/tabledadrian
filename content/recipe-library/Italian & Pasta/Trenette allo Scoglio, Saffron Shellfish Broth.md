# Trenette allo Scoglio, Saffron Shellfish Broth

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The 'rocky-shore' pasta done with discipline — each shellfish cooked to its own clock, united at the end in a saffron-touched broth of their juices.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- trenette or linguine — 400 g
- mussels — 500 g
- clams, purged — 500 g
- raw prawns, shell on — 8 (about 300 g)
- squid, cleaned, 1 cm rings — 200 g
- datterini tomatoes, halved — 200 g
- garlic — 3 cloves
- dry white wine — 120 ml
- saffron — 0.15 g
- extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- parsley — 8 g
- chilli — 0.5 g
- fish stock — 200 ml

## Method
1. Shell prawns; simmer heads and shells in fish stock 15 minutes; strain and bloom the saffron in it.
2. Open mussels and clams separately with a splash of wine, lids on, 2–3 minutes; pick half, strain and combine every drop of liquor.
3. Sizzle garlic and chilli in oil; add squid, 1 minute; add tomatoes and remaining wine, 2 minutes.
4. Add shellfish liquor and 150 ml saffron stock; simmer 3 minutes.
5. Boil trenette to 3 minutes under al dente; finish in the pan, feeding in stock, 3 minutes.
6. Add prawns for the final 2 minutes; then all shellfish and parsley for 30 seconds.

## Plating & Service
A crowded, glistening shoreline — shells up, prawns crossed on top, broth pooling, best oil to finish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The discipline: nothing overcooks because everything joins late. Scale down to two shellfish for a calmer prep on crossing days.
