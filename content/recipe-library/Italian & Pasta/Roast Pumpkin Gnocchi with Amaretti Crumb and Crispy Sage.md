# Roast Pumpkin Gnocchi with Amaretti Crumb and Crispy Sage

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Half potato, half oven-dried pumpkin for gnocchi that taste of autumn without turning gluey — finished with bitter-almond crumb, a Mantua wink.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- delica pumpkin or butternut — 600 g
- floury potatoes — 400 g
- 00 flour — 220 g
- egg yolk — 1
- Parmigiano Reggiano — 50 g
- fine salt — 7 g
- nutmeg — 0.3 g
- butter — 90 g
- sage — 12 leaves
- amaretti, crushed — 25 g
- black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Roast pumpkin wedges at 190°C for 45 minutes until dry-tender; bake potatoes whole alongside.
2. Rice both while hot; spread to steam-dry 5 minutes.
3. Work in salt, nutmeg, yolk, Parmigiano and flour to a soft dough — minimal handling.
4. Roll ropes, cut 2.5 cm pillows; ridge on a board.
5. Foam butter with sage to hazelnut stage.
6. Poach gnocchi in simmering salted water; lift 20 seconds after floating.
7. Glaze in the sage butter with a spoon of poaching water.

## Plating & Service
Bowls, crisp sage, amaretti crumb raining over, Parmigiano and pepper.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: gingersnap or toasted breadcrumb with a pinch of sugar mimics amaretti. Brown the butter deeply — pale butter wastes the dish.
