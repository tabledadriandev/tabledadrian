# Vegan Semolina Cavatelli with Slow-Roasted Tomato Sugo

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Proof that eggless pasta is a feature, not a compromise — chewy hand-rolled shells with a tomato sugo concentrated in the oven until it tastes sun-dried.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- fine semolina (rimacinata) — 400 g
- warm water — 200 ml
- fine salt — 4 g
- ripe plum tomatoes, halved — 800 g
- garlic, sliced — 3 cloves
- extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- dried oregano — 1 g
- caster sugar — 3 g
- basil — 12 g
- chilli flakes — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Roast tomato halves cut-side up with garlic, half the oil, sugar, oregano and salt at 140°C for 80 minutes until shrivelled and intense.
2. Knead semolina, water and salt 10 minutes; rest 20 minutes.
3. Roll 1.5 cm ropes, cut 2 cm pieces, drag each with two fingertips to curl into ridged shells.
4. Crush the roasted tomatoes coarsely with their juices in a wide pan; warm with the chilli.
5. Boil cavatelli 6–7 minutes until they float plus one minute.
6. Toss in the sugo with a ladle of pasta water and remaining oil until clinging; tear in basil.

## Plating & Service
Simple and generous, basil crown, best oil over. Almond 'parmesan' (blitzed toasted almonds and salt) for those who want it.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The sugo holds a week refrigerated under oil — make triple. Add capers and olives for a puttanesca-adjacent turn.
