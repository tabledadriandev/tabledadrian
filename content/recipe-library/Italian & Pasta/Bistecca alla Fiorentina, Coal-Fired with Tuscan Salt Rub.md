# Bistecca alla Fiorentina, Coal-Fired with Tuscan Salt Rub

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A 1.2 kg T-bone stood on its bone over real coals — Tuscan doctrine: no oil in the pan, no turning obsession, blood-rare inside a bitter char, salt only at the end.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- dry-aged T-bone or porterhouse, 5 cm thick — 1.2 kg
- coarse sea salt — 12 g
- black pepper, cracked — 3 g
- rosemary sprig — 1
- garlic, halved head — 1
- extra virgin olive oil, finest — 40 ml
- lemon — 1, halved (optional Tuscan garnish)

## Method
1. Bring the meat to full room temperature — 2 hours out of the fridge; dry the surface thoroughly.
2. Prepare embers (or preheat a heavy grill/plancha to maximum) — searing heat, no flame.
3. Grill without any oil: 5 minutes untouched per side for a true dark crust.
4. Stand the steak upright on the bone 5–7 minutes so heat drives to the centre; target core 48–50°C.
5. Rest 10 minutes on a warm board under loose foil with the rosemary and garlic laid on top.
6. Carve both muscles off the bone; slice 1.5 cm against the grain; reassemble at the bone.

## Plating & Service
On a warm board — never a cold plate — flaky salt, pepper and the raw oil over the cut faces only.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fiorentina is rare or it is roast beef — offer a separate finished-in-pan portion for well-done requests rather than sacrificing the steak.
