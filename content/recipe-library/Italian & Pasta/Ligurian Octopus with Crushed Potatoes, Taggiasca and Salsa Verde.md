# Ligurian Octopus with Crushed Potatoes, Taggiasca and Salsa Verde

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Riviera di Levante classic — silk-tender octopus over warm crushed potatoes with olives, tied together by a caper-anchovy salsa verde.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- octopus, cleaned — 1 kg
- waxy potatoes — 400 g
- taggiasca olives — 60 g
- parsley — 25 g
- capers — 20 g
- anchovy fillets — 3
- garlic — 1 small clove
- Dijon mustard — 5 g
- red wine vinegar — 15 ml
- extra virgin olive oil — 120 ml
- bay leaf, peppercorns — for the pot
- lemon — 1
- fine salt — to taste

## Method
1. Simmer the octopus with bay and peppercorns at 90°C for 50–70 minutes until a skewer slides into the thickest tentacle; cool 20 minutes in the liquid.
2. Boil potatoes in their skins in salted water; peel warm and crush coarsely with 30 ml oil, salt and a few olives.
3. Salsa verde: chop parsley, capers, anchovy and garlic fine; work in mustard, vinegar and 70 ml oil to a loose, spoonable sauce.
4. Separate tentacles; char them 2 minutes per side on a screaming grill or plancha with a little oil.
5. Warm the crushed potatoes.

## Plating & Service
A bed of crushed potato, one charred tentacle curling over it, olives scattered, salsa verde in bold strokes, lemon on the side.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pressure cooker: 18 minutes high, natural release — indistinguishable. Serve at room temperature as part of a spread with equal success.
