# Limoncello Tiramisu with Lemon Curd

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Amalfi rewrite — savoiardi soaked in limoncello syrup, mascarpone layered with sharp lemon curd; sunshine where the coffee used to be.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- egg yolks — 4
- caster sugar — 150 g (100 g cream, 50 g syrup)
- lemon juice — 120 ml
- lemon zest — of 3 lemons
- butter — 60 g
- mascarpone — 450 g
- double cream — 200 ml
- savoiardi — 300 g
- limoncello — 80 ml
- water — 150 ml
- white chocolate, grated — 20 g

## Method
1. Curd: whisk yolks, 100 g sugar, lemon juice and half the zest over a bain-marie to 78°C and thick; beat in butter; chill.
2. Syrup: dissolve 50 g sugar in the hot water; cool; add limoncello.
3. Beat mascarpone smooth; fold in half the curd; whip cream to soft peaks and fold in.
4. Dip savoiardi briefly in the syrup; layer in the dish.
5. Spread half the cream, ribbon in half the remaining curd; repeat biscuit and cream layers, finishing with curd swirled on top.
6. Chill 6 hours minimum.
7. Finish with white chocolate and the last zest.

## Plating & Service
Serve in coupes or cut squares, a zest cloud over — garnish with a tiny basil leaf for the Riviera wink.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Alcohol-free: replace limoncello with 40 ml more juice and 5 g zest in the syrup. The curd alone earns its place in the fridge doors of every charter.
