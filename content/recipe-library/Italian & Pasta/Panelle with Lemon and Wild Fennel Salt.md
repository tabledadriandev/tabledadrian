# Panelle with Lemon and Wild Fennel Salt

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Palermo's chickpea fritters — set like polenta, sliced thin, fried to a blistered crisp; naturally vegan, gluten-free, and dangerous by the dozen.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- chickpea flour — 250 g
- cold water — 750 ml
- fine salt — 6 g
- fennel seeds, toasted and ground — 2 g
- flaky salt — 3 g
- lemon — 1, in wedges
- parsley, chopped — 5 g
- black pepper — 1 g
- sunflower or olive oil for frying — 1 L

## Method
1. Whisk chickpea flour into cold water off the heat until lump-free; add fine salt and pepper.
2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, 8–10 minutes until very thick and pulling from the pan; stir in parsley.
3. Spread 5 mm thick on oiled trays (or fill oiled saucers, the traditional way); cool completely.
4. Cut into 8×5 cm rectangles or triangles.
5. Fry at 180°C for 2–3 minutes until puffed at the edges and gold; drain.
6. Season immediately with fennel salt.

## Plating & Service
Hot, stacked on paper, lemon wedges for squeezing — traditionally stuffed into a roll; aboard, serve as aperitivo with cold Grillo.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Batter keeps 2 days chilled, fry to order. Bake at 220°C brushed with oil for a lighter version at 70% of the crunch.
