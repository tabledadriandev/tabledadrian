# Tonnarelli Cacio e Pepe

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Three ingredients, zero hiding places — square-cut fresh tonnarelli and a pecorino emulsion built at exactly the right temperature so it never seizes.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- 00 flour — 200 g
- fine semolina — 100 g
- whole eggs — 3
- Pecorino Romano, finely grated — 160 g
- black peppercorns — 8 g
- fine salt — for the water only

## Method
1. Knead flours and eggs; rest 45 minutes. Roll to 2 mm and cut on the chitarra (or thick-cut setting) into square-section strands.
2. Toast peppercorns in a dry pan 2 minutes; crush coarsely. Return two-thirds to the pan.
3. Boil tonnarelli in barely salted water (the cheese is salty) 2 minutes.
4. Add 150 ml pasta water to the pepper pan; simmer 30 seconds.
5. Meanwhile whisk the pecorino with 80 ml cooler pasta water (about 60°C) to a smooth, thick cream.
6. Drag pasta into the pepper pan, toss 45 seconds, then pull OFF the heat for 30 seconds.
7. Add the cheese cream, tossing fast — the residual heat sets a silk emulsion. Adjust with drops of water.

## Plating & Service
Tight nest, remaining crushed pepper, nothing else. Serve in warm — never scorching — bowls.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Insurance for rough seas: blend 5 g cornstarch into the cheese; it cannot split. Half parmesan softens the salt for delicate palates.
