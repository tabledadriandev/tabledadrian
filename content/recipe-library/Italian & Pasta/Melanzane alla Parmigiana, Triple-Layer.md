# Melanzane alla Parmigiana, Triple-Layer

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The vegetarian secondo that outranks most meat — aubergine fried to cream, layered tight with tomato, basil and two cheeses, rested so it slices like terrine.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- aubergines, 8 mm lengthwise slices — 1.2 kg
- fine salt — for purging
- olive oil — 200 ml (for frying)
- tomato passata — 800 g
- garlic — 2 cloves
- basil — 20 g
- fior di latte mozzarella, drained well, torn — 300 g
- Parmigiano Reggiano, grated — 120 g
- eggs, beaten (optional, Neapolitan style) — 2
- black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Salt the aubergine slices 30 minutes; rinse, press dry with intent.
2. Fry in batches in 170°C oil until gold and supple; drain on racks. (Lighter: brush with oil, roast 220°C/15 min.)
3. Sauce: garlic in 30 ml oil, passata, salt, basil sprig; simmer 20 minutes.
4. Layer in a 25×30 cm dish: sauce, aubergine, sauce, mozzarella, Parmigiano, basil, (a thread of beaten egg) — four strata, ending with sauce and a thick Parmigiano lid.
5. Bake at 180°C fan 35–40 minutes until bronzed and bubbling at the walls.
6. Rest minimum 30 minutes — an hour is better; it must slice, not slide.

## Plating & Service
Clean squares, room-warm not scalding, basil tip, one thread of raw oil — pretend restraint while serving seconds.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Next-day cold parmigiana in focaccia is crew lore. Vegan: skip cheeses, layer with basil-cashew cream — different dish, still proud.
