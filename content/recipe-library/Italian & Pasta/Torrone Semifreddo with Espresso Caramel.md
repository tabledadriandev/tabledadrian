# Torrone Semifreddo with Espresso Caramel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Piedmont's nougat frozen into a no-churn parfait — Italian meringue keeps it sliceable straight from the freezer, espresso caramel keeps it honest.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- hard or soft torrone, chopped — 150 g
- toasted almonds and hazelnuts, chopped — 60 g
- egg whites — 3 (100 g)
- caster sugar — 160 g (120 g meringue, 40 g caramel)
- water — 40 ml
- honey — 20 g
- double cream — 400 ml
- vanilla — half a pod
- espresso — 60 ml
- fine salt — 1 pinch

## Method
1. Line a 24 cm loaf tin with film.
2. Italian meringue: boil 120 g sugar, honey and water to 118°C; stream onto whipping whites; whisk until glossy and cool.
3. Whip cream with vanilla to soft peaks.
4. Fold cream into meringue, then torrone and nuts.
5. Fill the tin; freeze 8 hours minimum.
6. Caramel: dry-caramelise 40 g sugar to amber; off heat, add hot espresso and salt carefully; reduce 1 minute; cool to a pourable syrup.

## Plating & Service
Unmould, slice 2.5 cm with a hot knife, caramel pulled across the plate, extra nut crumble — it holds 10 minutes on a warm deck, no more.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No ice-cream machine aboard? This IS the machine-free answer. Swap torrone for crushed amaretti and cherries in midsummer.
