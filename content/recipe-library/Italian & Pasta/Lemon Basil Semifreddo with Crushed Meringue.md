# Lemon Basil Semifreddo with Crushed Meringue

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Frozen lemon cloud threaded with basil and shattered meringue — the palate-resetting finale after a long tasting menu, sharp enough to wake the table.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- lemon juice — 100 ml
- lemon zest — of 3 lemons
- caster sugar — 180 g (120 g meringue syrup, 60 g curd)
- egg whites — 3
- water — 40 ml
- egg yolks — 3
- butter — 30 g
- basil leaves — 8 g
- double cream — 350 ml
- ready or homemade meringue, crushed — 60 g

## Method
1. Quick curd: yolks, 60 g sugar, juice and zest over a bain-marie to 78°C; beat in butter; chill.
2. Blanch basil 5 seconds; shock; blot; chop to a fine emerald mince.
3. Italian meringue: syrup at 118°C onto whipping whites; whisk cool.
4. Fold curd and basil into the meringue; then softly whipped cream; then crushed meringue last, in two folds.
5. Freeze in a lined tin or ring moulds 8 hours.

## Plating & Service
A slice on a frozen plate, extra meringue shards, basil tip, a few raw lemon segments — serve within 5 minutes of the freezer.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Lime-mint pivots it tropical; keep total citrus at 100 ml or it won't set clean. The crushed meringue must go in last or it dissolves.
