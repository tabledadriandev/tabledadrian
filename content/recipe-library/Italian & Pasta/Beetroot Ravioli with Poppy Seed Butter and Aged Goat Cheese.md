# Beetroot Ravioli with Poppy Seed Butter and Aged Goat Cheese

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Fuchsia casunziei-style parcels from the Dolomite playbook — sweet roasted beet against poppy-seed brown butter and a sharp goat cheese counterpoint.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- 00 flour — 300 g
- whole eggs — 3
- beetroot, roasted and peeled — 300 g
- ricotta, drained — 150 g
- Parmigiano Reggiano — 50 g
- dry breadcrumbs — 20 g
- fine salt and nutmeg — to taste
- butter — 90 g
- poppy seeds — 8 g
- aged goat cheese (or more Parmigiano) — 50 g
- red wine vinegar — 5 ml

## Method
1. Roast whole beets wrapped at 200°C for 60 minutes until tender; peel while warm.
2. Blitz 250 g beet to a fine purée; dry it in a pan 5 minutes. Beat with ricotta, Parmigiano, breadcrumbs, nutmeg, salt and vinegar. Chill firm.
3. Dice remaining beet 3 mm; reserve for garnish.
4. Make egg dough, rest 1 hour, roll to 0.7 mm.
5. Fill ravioli with 10 g each using a 6 cm round cutter; seal airtight.
6. Foam butter to light hazelnut, add poppy seeds off the heat.
7. Boil ravioli 3 minutes; glaze in the poppy butter with a spoon of pasta water.

## Plating & Service
Fuchsia rounds glazed and mirror-bright, beet dice, shaved goat cheese melting on contact.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Casunziei purists skip ricotta — add 50 g potato instead for a denser, sweeter filling. Vegan: cashew ricotta and olive-oil-poppy dressing.
