# Cannoli Siciliani with Sheep Ricotta and Candied Orange

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Shells fried blistered and hollow around drained sheep-ricotta cream — filled strictly at the last minute, because a soggy cannolo is a broken promise.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- plain flour — 250 g
- caster sugar — 25 g (shells) + 120 g (cream)
- cocoa powder — 8 g
- lard or butter, soft — 30 g
- Marsala — 60 ml
- white wine vinegar — 10 ml
- egg — 1 (plus white for sealing)
- sheep (or best cow) ricotta, drained 24 h — 600 g
- candied orange, fine dice — 50 g
- dark chocolate chips — 40 g
- pistachios, chopped — 40 g
- icing sugar — for dusting
- sunflower oil for frying — 1.5 L
- fine salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Dough: work flour, sugar, cocoa, salt, lard, egg, Marsala and vinegar to a firm dough; rest wrapped 1 hour.
2. Cream: pass the drained ricotta through a sieve twice with the 120 g sugar; fold in orange and chocolate; chill in a piping bag.
3. Roll dough to 1 mm; cut 10 cm discs; wrap diagonally around cannoli tubes, sealing the seam with egg white.
4. Fry at 180°C, 2 minutes, until blistered and deep bronze; drain; slide off tubes while warm.
5. Cool shells completely (they keep 3 days airtight).
6. AT SERVICE ONLY: pipe the cream from both ends; press pistachio into each face.

## Plating & Service
Two per plate on a smear of nothing, icing sugar through a fine sieve, extra pistachio — filled minutes before, stated proudly on the menu.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The vinegar and Marsala create the signature bubbles. No tubes? Cut cane sections or use foil-wrapped dowels. Ricotta must be genuinely dry — hang it overnight.
