# Lasagne Verdi with Ragù and Aged Parmesan Besciamella

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Spinach-emerald pasta sheets layered whisper-thin with ragù and nutmeg besciamella — baked shallow so every layer stays defined, Bologna trattoria style.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- 00 flour — 350 g
- spinach, blanched and squeezed very dry — 120 g
- whole eggs — 2
- beef and pork ragù (see tagliatelle recipe) — 900 g
- butter — 70 g
- plain flour for roux — 70 g
- whole milk — 900 ml
- nutmeg — 1 g
- Parmigiano Reggiano, grated — 150 g
- fine salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Purée spinach with eggs until completely smooth. Knead into flour to a firm green dough; rest 1 hour.
2. Besciamella: melt butter, cook flour 2 minutes, whisk in cold milk gradually. Simmer 8 minutes to nappe. Season with salt and nutmeg.
3. Roll dough to 0.8 mm, cut sheets to fit a 25×35 cm dish. Blanch 30 seconds in salted water, refresh in iced water, dry on cloths.
4. Loosen ragù with a ladle of stock so it spreads thinly.
5. Build 6 layers: a smear of besciamella on the base, then pasta, thin ragù, thin besciamella, Parmigiano. Repeat, finishing with besciamella and a heavy dusting of cheese.
6. Rest assembled lasagne 2 hours (or overnight, chilled) so layers set.
7. Bake at 180°C fan for 35 minutes until burnished and bubbling at the edges.
8. Rest 20 minutes before portioning — critical for clean slices.

## Plating & Service
Cut precise rectangles with a serrated knife, lift with a fish slice, serve on warm plates with nothing but a little extra sauce from the dish corners.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian: replace ragù with a slow-cooked mushroom-lentil ragù. Portions freeze beautifully for crew meals.
