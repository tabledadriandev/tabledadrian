# Caramelle of Taleggio and Pear with Hazelnut Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sweet-wrapper pasta twisted like candies around molten taleggio and caramelised pear — a northern cheese-course and pasta-course in one.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- 00 flour — 300 g
- whole eggs — 3
- taleggio, rind off — 180 g
- ricotta — 80 g
- firm pear (Abate), 5 mm dice — 150 g
- butter — 90 g (20 g for pears)
- caster sugar — 5 g
- thyme — 1 g
- hazelnuts, toasted and crushed — 30 g
- Parmigiano Reggiano — 40 g
- black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Caramelise pear dice in 20 g butter with sugar and thyme, 4 minutes; cool completely.
2. Beat taleggio with ricotta to a paste; fold in the pear. Chill firm in a piping bag.
3. Make dough; rest 1 hour. Roll to 0.7 mm; cut 8×6 cm rectangles.
4. Pipe 8 g filling across the centre, roll into a tube, then pinch and twist both ends like a bonbon wrapper.
5. Boil 3 minutes at a gentle simmer.
6. Brown remaining butter to hazelnut stage; add the crushed nuts off the heat.
7. Glaze caramelle in the nut butter with a spoon of pasta water.

## Plating & Service
Five candies angled across the plate, nut butter spooned over, Parmigiano and one crack of pepper.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Gorgonzola dolce for taleggio shifts it bluer; walnuts echo it. Serve as 3 pieces for an elegant starter or 7 as a main.
