# Pollo alla Cacciatora with Black Olives and Vin Santo

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Tuscan hunter's chicken — thighs braised on the bone with rosemary, tomato and black olives, deglazed with Vin Santo for a rounder, older-school sweetness.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- chicken thighs, bone-in, skin-on — 8 (about 1.4 kg)
- olive oil — 50 ml
- red onion, sliced — 150 g
- carrot and celery, fine dice — 120 g total
- garlic — 3 cloves
- rosemary — 3 g
- tinned tomatoes, crushed — 400 g
- Vin Santo or dry Marsala — 80 ml
- dry white wine — 100 ml
- black olives (leccino/kalamata) — 90 g
- chicken stock — 150 ml
- fine salt and black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Season thighs; brown skin-down in oil in a wide braiser until deeply golden, 8 minutes; flip 2 minutes; remove.
2. Sweat onion, carrot, celery 8 minutes in the fat; add garlic and rosemary, 1 minute.
3. Deglaze with Vin Santo then white wine; reduce by half.
4. Add tomatoes and stock; return the chicken skin-up, skin above the liquid line.
5. Braise uncovered at 180°C for 35–40 minutes until the sauce is thick and the skin re-crisped.
6. Add olives for the final 10 minutes; adjust seasoning.

## Plating & Service
Family-style in the braiser or two thighs per plate over wet polenta or white beans, sauce generously, rosemary flowers.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Rabbit is the older Tuscan version — same method, 10 minutes less. Peppers instead of tomato pushes it toward the Roman take.
