# Cime di Rapa with Garlic, Chilli and Colatura

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bitter turnip tops flashed with garlic and chilli, seasoned with a few drops of colatura instead of salt — the south's sharpest green.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- cime di rapa (or broccoli rabe) — 700 g
- extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- garlic, sliced — 3 cloves
- dried chilli — 1 g
- colatura di alici — 8 ml (or fine salt)
- lemon zest — of half a lemon

## Method
1. Trim the tough stalks; keep florets and tender leaves.
2. Blanch 2 minutes in boiling salted water; drain hard, pressing lightly.
3. Warm oil with garlic and chilli until the garlic just gilds.
4. Add the greens; sauté over high heat 4–5 minutes, letting some edges catch.
5. Off heat, season with colatura and lemon zest — taste before adding any salt.

## Plating & Service
A tangled dark-green mound beside fish or sausage, one thread of raw oil — or under burrata as an instant starter.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: swap colatura for salt and 5 g capers. Regular broccoli or kale submit to the same treatment honourably.
