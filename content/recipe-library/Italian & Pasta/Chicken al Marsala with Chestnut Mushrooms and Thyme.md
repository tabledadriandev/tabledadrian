# Chicken al Marsala with Chestnut Mushrooms and Thyme

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sicily's fortified wine doing what it does best — a fast pan sauce over sautéed chicken and mushrooms that drinks like something far slower.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- chicken breasts, halved horizontally — 4 (about 700 g)
- plain flour — 50 g
- chestnut mushrooms, thick slices — 300 g
- dry Marsala — 150 ml
- chicken stock — 150 ml
- butter, cold — 60 g
- olive oil — 30 ml
- shallot, minced — 40 g
- thyme — 3 g
- parsley — 5 g
- fine salt and black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Pound the chicken pieces to even 1.5 cm; season; flour lightly.
2. Sear in oil and 20 g butter, 2–3 minutes per side to gold and 68°C; remove.
3. Sear mushrooms hard in the same pan, undisturbed at first, 5 minutes; add shallot and thyme, 2 minutes.
4. Deglaze with Marsala — flame off first — and reduce by half.
5. Add stock; reduce to a light syrup; mount remaining cold butter.
6. Return chicken 1 minute, spooning over the glaze; parsley last.

## Plating & Service
Cutlets shingled with mushrooms cascading over, sauce ribboned, thyme flowers — potato purée or wilted spinach beneath.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dry (secco) Marsala only — sweet belongs in zabaione. Veal or pork tenderloin medallions swap in without changing a beat.
