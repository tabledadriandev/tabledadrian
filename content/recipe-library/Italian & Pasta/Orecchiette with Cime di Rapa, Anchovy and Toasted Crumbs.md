# Orecchiette with Cime di Rapa, Anchovy and Toasted Crumbs

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bari's thumb-dragged semolina ears with bitter greens, chilli and anchovy — finished with pangrattato instead of cheese, the cucina povera way.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- fine semolina (rimacinata) — 400 g
- warm water — 200 ml
- fine salt — 4 g
- cime di rapa (or broccoli rabe) — 500 g
- anchovy fillets in oil — 8 (30 g)
- garlic, sliced — 2 cloves
- dried chilli — 1 g
- extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- coarse dry breadcrumbs — 40 g

## Method
1. Knead semolina, water and salt 10 minutes to a firm dough; rest 20 minutes under a bowl.
2. Roll 1.5 cm ropes, cut 1 cm nubs, drag each across the board with a butter knife and invert over your thumb into little ears. Dry 30 minutes.
3. Toast breadcrumbs in 20 ml oil until deep gold; salt lightly; reserve.
4. Trim cime di rapa into florets and tender leaves.
5. Melt anchovies with garlic and chilli in remaining oil over low heat, 3 minutes.
6. Boil orecchiette 8–9 minutes, adding the greens for the final 3 minutes.
7. Drain into the anchovy oil with a ladle of cooking water; toss hard 1 minute until creamy.

## Plating & Service
Rustic bowls, a heavy rain of golden crumbs, one cracked chilli if guests like heat.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian/vegan: swap anchovy for 10 g capers and 5 g miso. The dried pasta version needs 11 minutes — start greens accordingly.
