# Fig, Gorgonzola and Walnut Crostini with Honey and Thyme

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Late-summer aperitivo in three bites — warm figs collapsing into gorgonzola dolce on charred bread, walnut for structure, thyme honey to bind the argument.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- ripe figs — 6
- gorgonzola dolce — 150 g
- walnut halves, toasted — 40 g
- baguette or country loaf, 1 cm slices — 12
- extra virgin olive oil — 30 ml
- honey — 25 g
- thyme leaves — 1 g
- black pepper — to taste
- balsamic vinegar — 5 ml

## Method
1. Warm honey with thyme gently 2 minutes; rest off the heat.
2. Toast the bread brushed with oil at 200°C until the edges catch, 5–6 minutes.
3. Quarter the figs; flash them cut-side down in a hot dry pan 60 seconds until caramel-edged.
4. Spread gorgonzola thickly on the warm toasts — it should start slumping.
5. Top with figs and walnuts; finish with thyme honey, 2 drops balsamic each and pepper.

## Plating & Service
On a dark board in one long line, honey pulled across all of them in a single ribbon at the last second.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Out of fig season: roasted grapes or pear. Milder table: substitute robiola; vegan: cashew blue-style cheese and maple.
