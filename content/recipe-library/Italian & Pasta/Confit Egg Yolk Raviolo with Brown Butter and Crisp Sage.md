# Confit Egg Yolk Raviolo with Brown Butter and Crisp Sage

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> One giant raviolo per guest hiding a molten 63°C yolk in a ricotta-spinach collar — the theatrical cut-and-flood moment charter guests film every time.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- 00 flour — 200 g
- whole eggs for dough — 2
- egg yolks — 4 (plus 2 spare)
- ricotta, drained overnight — 250 g
- spinach, blanched, squeezed, chopped — 100 g
- Parmigiano Reggiano, grated — 60 g
- nutmeg — 0.3 g
- butter — 90 g
- sage leaves — 12
- lemon juice — 5 ml
- fine salt — to taste

## Method
1. Confit yolks (optional but forgiving): hold yolks in 60 ml olive oil at 63°C for 25 minutes; or use impeccably fresh raw yolks.
2. Beat ricotta with spinach, 40 g Parmigiano, nutmeg and salt. Pipe 4 rings (7 cm diameter) onto 4 pasta squares rolled to 0.7 mm from the egg dough.
3. Slide one yolk into each ricotta ring. Season the yolk with a flake of salt.
4. Cap with a second square; seal from the yolk outwards, excluding all air. Cut with a 10 cm fluted cutter.
5. Brown the butter with sage until hazelnut-scented; add lemon juice; keep warm.
6. Poach ravioli in barely-simmering salted water (94°C) exactly 3 minutes — the pasta cooks, the yolk stays liquid.
7. Lift with a spider, drain on a cloth for 3 seconds.

## Plating & Service
One raviolo centered on a warm plate, brown butter spooned over, sage on top, Parmigiano snow. Instruct guests to cut immediately.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Make ahead: assembled ravioli hold 4 hours refrigerated on semolina. Swap spinach for nettle in spring.
