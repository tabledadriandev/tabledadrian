# Burrata with Grilled Peaches, Basil and Aged Balsamic

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Five components, no cooking beyond grill marks — the plate that sells a July lunch on deck the moment it lands.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- burrata — 2 balls (250 g)
- ripe but firm peaches — 3
- extra virgin olive oil — 40 ml
- aged balsamic vinegar (or reduction) — 15 ml
- basil, small leaves — 8 g
- flaky salt — 2 g
- black pepper — to taste
- sourdough, grilled (optional) — 4 slices

## Method
1. Take burrata out of the fridge 30 minutes ahead — cold kills it.
2. Halve and stone peaches; brush cut side with a little oil.
3. Grill cut-side down on a screaming plancha or grill 2–3 minutes, untouched, until caramel-marked; quarter.
4. Tear the burrata open onto plates rather than slicing.
5. Arrange peaches against the cream; dress with oil, balsamic, salt, pepper and basil.

## Plating & Service
Asymmetric — burrata off-centre, peaches leaning on it, dressing pooled in the cream's folds. Grilled bread on the side.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Winter version: roasted grapes and walnuts. Vegan: swap burrata for chilled whipped almond ricotta — different, still lovely.
