# Saffron Garganelli with Mussels and Courgette

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Golden saffron-dough quills rolled by hand, tossed with just-opened mussels and courgette ribbons in their own liquor — the colour of a Portofino sunset.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- 00 flour — 300 g
- whole eggs — 3
- saffron threads — 0.3 g, bloomed in 10 ml hot water
- mussels, cleaned — 1 kg
- dry white wine — 100 ml
- courgette — 200 g
- garlic — 2 cloves
- extra virgin olive oil — 50 ml
- parsley — 6 g
- black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Bloom saffron 15 minutes; knead into flour and eggs. Rest 1 hour. Roll 0.9 mm, cut 4 cm squares, roll into ridged quills on a comb.
2. Steam mussels with wine and 1 garlic clove in a covered pot 3 minutes until just open. Pick most, leave 12 in shell. Strain and reserve the liquor.
3. Ribbon the courgette on a mandoline; salt lightly.
4. Sizzle remaining garlic in oil; add 150 ml mussel liquor, reduce by a third.
5. Boil garganelli 2–3 minutes; transfer to the pan with courgette ribbons.
6. Toss 1 minute to emulsify; fold in picked mussels and parsley, warm 30 seconds.

## Plating & Service
Bowls crowned with the in-shell mussels, pepper, lemon oil if you like.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap mussels for clams for a finer broth. The saffron dough also makes stunning tagliatelle for butter-only service.
