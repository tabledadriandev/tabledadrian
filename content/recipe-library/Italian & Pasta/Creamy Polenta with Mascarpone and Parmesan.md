# Creamy Polenta with Mascarpone and Parmesan

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Polenta cooked slow in milk and water, finished with mascarpone so it pours like custard — the northern base for every braise on the menu.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- coarse-ground polenta (bramata) — 300 g
- water — 700 ml
- whole milk — 700 ml
- fine salt — 10 g
- mascarpone — 80 g
- Parmigiano Reggiano, grated — 80 g
- butter — 40 g
- black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Bring water, milk and salt to a simmer in a heavy pot.
2. Rain in the polenta, whisking constantly to prevent lumps.
3. Drop to the lowest heat; cook 45–50 minutes, stirring every 5 minutes with a wooden spoon, scraping the base.
4. It's done when it pulls from the walls and no grain crunches.
5. Off the heat, beat in butter, mascarpone and Parmigiano; adjust salt.
6. Loosen with hot milk to a slow-ribbon pour before serving.

## Plating & Service
Poured directly onto warm plates ahead of the braise, or presented in a copper pot at the table with pepper over.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Leftovers set overnight become tomorrow's grilled polenta. Vegan: olive oil and 15 g white miso instead of the dairies.
