# Pistachio Semifreddo with Warm Dark Chocolate Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Frozen pistachio mousse against a pour of hot 70% chocolate — the temperature clash is the dessert; everything made two days ahead.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- pure pistachio paste — 110 g
- egg yolks — 4
- caster sugar — 110 g
- water — 30 ml
- double cream — 400 ml
- pistachios, chopped — 50 g
- dark chocolate 70% — 150 g
- whole milk — 120 ml
- glucose or honey — 15 g
- flaky salt — 1 pinch

## Method
1. Pâte à bombe: whisk yolks; boil sugar and water to 118°C; stream onto the yolks, whisking to a thick, cool ribbon.
2. Beat in the pistachio paste.
3. Fold in cream whipped to soft peaks, then half the chopped pistachios.
4. Fill a lined loaf tin or 8 dariole moulds; freeze 8 hours.
5. Sauce at service: melt chocolate with hot milk and glucose to a glossy pour; keep at 45°C.

## Plating & Service
Slice or unmould onto cold plates, remaining pistachios and salt over, sauce poured at the table from a small copper pot.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Gianduia version: hazelnut paste and milk chocolate. The pâte à bombe pasteurises the yolks at 118°C — service-safe by design.
