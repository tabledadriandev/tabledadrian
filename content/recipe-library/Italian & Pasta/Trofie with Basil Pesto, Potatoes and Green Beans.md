# Trofie with Basil Pesto, Potatoes and Green Beans

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The full Ligurian ritual — hand-twisted trofie boiled with potato and beans, dressed in a mortar-bright pesto that stays emerald green.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- 00 flour — 300 g
- warm water — 150 ml
- fine salt — 3 g
- basil leaves, small Genovese type — 60 g
- pine nuts — 30 g
- garlic — 1 small clove (5 g)
- Parmigiano Reggiano — 40 g
- Pecorino Sardo — 20 g
- extra virgin olive oil, delicate Ligurian — 100 ml
- waxy potatoes, 1 cm dice — 200 g
- fine green beans, 3 cm lengths — 150 g
- coarse salt for the mortar — 2 g

## Method
1. Knead flour, water and salt; rest 30 minutes. Pinch hazelnut-size pieces and drag each across the board under your palm's edge to form tight spirals. Dry 30 minutes.
2. Chill the mortar. Pound garlic with coarse salt to cream, add pine nuts, pound smooth.
3. Add basil in three additions, grinding in circles — never bruising long. Work in cheeses, then oil in a stream. Keep cool.
4. Boil potatoes in well-salted water 5 minutes; add beans and trofie together for the final 4–5 minutes.
5. Reserve 100 ml starchy water; drain everything together.
6. Off the heat, fold pasta and vegetables through the pesto, loosening with the reserved water — pesto must never touch direct heat.

## Plating & Service
Loose and glossy in shallow bowls, a few whole basil tips, a final drop of Ligurian oil.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: replace pine nuts with 30 g sunflower seeds. Vegan: swap cheeses for 20 g white miso and 30 g more seeds.
