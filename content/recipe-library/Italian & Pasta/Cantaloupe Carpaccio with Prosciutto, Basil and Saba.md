# Cantaloupe Carpaccio with Prosciutto, Basil and Saba

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Prosciutto e melone reset — melon sliced paper-thin and cured for ten minutes with salt and saba so it eats like a delicate crudo under the ham.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- ripe cantaloupe melon — 1 small (about 800 g)
- prosciutto di Parma or San Daniele, sliced gossamer — 100 g
- saba or aged balsamic — 15 ml
- extra virgin olive oil — 30 ml
- lime juice — 10 ml
- flaky salt — 2 g
- basil, micro or torn — 5 g
- black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Peel and seed the melon; slice into 2 mm sheets on a mandoline or with a long knife.
2. Lay the sheets overlapping on chilled plates like a carpaccio.
3. Dress with lime juice, half the oil and flaky salt; rest 10 minutes — the melon turns translucent and intense.
4. Drape the ham loosely so it stands in waves, never flat.
5. Finish with saba, remaining oil, basil and one turn of pepper.

## Plating & Service
Serve within 20 minutes, plates cold, ham at room temperature — the contrast of cool fruit and silky fat is the dish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> White peaches or figs swap in seamlessly through August. Vegan: omit ham, add shaved fennel and smoked almonds.
