# Spring Risotto with Asparagus, Peas and Mint

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Primavera with a green-blitzed finish — asparagus stems become the stock, tips stay snappy, and a pea-mint purée turns the whole pan jade at the end.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- carnaroli rice — 320 g
- green asparagus — 400 g
- fresh or frozen peas — 200 g
- vegetable stock — 1.2 L
- shallot — 50 g
- butter — 70 g
- dry white wine — 70 ml
- Parmigiano Reggiano — 70 g
- mint leaves — 5 g
- lemon zest — of half a lemon
- fine salt and white pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Snap asparagus; simmer the woody stems in the stock 15 minutes; strain and keep hot. Slice spears, reserving tips whole.
2. Blanch 120 g peas with the mint 1 minute; blitz with 50 ml stock to a bright purée.
3. Sweat shallot in 30 g butter; toast rice; deglaze with wine.
4. Cook with the asparagus stock, stirring, 13 minutes; add sliced asparagus and remaining peas.
5. Cook 3 minutes more; add tips for the final minute.
6. Off heat, mantecare with remaining butter, Parmigiano and the pea-mint purée. Finish with lemon zest.

## Plating & Service
Vivid green and loose, tips pointing skyward, pea shoots or mint tips, one curl of Parmigiano.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fold in burrata at the table for indulgence, or keep it vegan with olive-oil mantecatura — the purée carries the creaminess.
