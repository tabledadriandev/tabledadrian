# Panzanella with Charred Peppers and Anchovy

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Tuscan bread salad rebuilt with discipline — stale bread soaked in a tomato-vinegar dressing rather than water, so nothing is ever soggy, only saturated with flavour.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- stale sourdough or Tuscan bread, 3 cm chunks — 300 g
- ripe mixed tomatoes — 500 g
- red pepper — 1 (about 180 g)
- cucumber, seeded, sliced — 150 g
- red onion, shaved — 60 g
- red wine vinegar — 40 ml
- extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml
- anchovy fillets — 6
- capers — 20 g
- basil — 12 g
- fine salt and black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Char the pepper over flame or under a hot grill until black; steam in a covered bowl, peel, and tear into ribbons.
2. Grate the two ripest tomatoes into a bowl; whisk with vinegar, 60 ml oil and salt — this is the soaking dressing.
3. Toast the bread chunks at 180°C for 8 minutes until dry and golden at the edges.
4. Toss bread through the dressing; rest 20 minutes, turning once.
5. Cut remaining tomatoes into irregular chunks; soak the shaved onion in cold water 10 minutes.
6. Fold everything together with cucumber, peppers, capers, anchovy and torn basil; finish with remaining oil.

## Plating & Service
Tumbled high in a wide bowl, the best tomato wedges and anchovies on top, black pepper and basil last. Serve within the hour.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: skip anchovy, add 60 g olives and more capers. Day-old focaccia makes a richer, Ligurian-leaning version.
