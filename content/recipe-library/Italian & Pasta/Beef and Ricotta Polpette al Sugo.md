# Beef and Ricotta Polpette al Sugo

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Meatballs kept cloud-soft with ricotta and milk-soaked bread, poached directly in tomato sugo — no frying, no dry centres, pure Sunday.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- beef mince (15% fat) — 500 g
- ricotta — 150 g
- white bread, crustless, milk-soaked — 80 g
- whole milk (for soaking) — 80 ml
- egg — 1
- Parmigiano Reggiano — 60 g
- garlic, minced — 4 g
- parsley — 10 g
- fine salt — 8 g
- black pepper and nutmeg — to taste
- tomato passata — 700 g
- olive oil — 50 ml
- basil — 8 g
- onion, halved — 1

## Method
1. Sugo: warm oil, add passata, the onion halves (to be removed), salt and half the basil; simmer 15 minutes.
2. Squeeze the soaked bread; mix with beef, ricotta, egg, Parmigiano, garlic, parsley, salt, pepper, nutmeg — mix only until combined.
3. Test-poach one mini ball; adjust seasoning.
4. Roll 50 g balls with wet hands.
5. Slide the polpette into the barely-simmering sugo; do not stir for 10 minutes.
6. Poach lidded 25 minutes total, swirling the pan occasionally; remove onion; finish with basil.

## Plating & Service
Four per bowl with plenty of sugo, torn basil, Parmigiano — bread or wet polenta below decks, tagliatelle if the guests insist.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Half pork mince deepens them. GF: use GF bread. The sugo, having poached the meat, is arguably the best part — never discard.
