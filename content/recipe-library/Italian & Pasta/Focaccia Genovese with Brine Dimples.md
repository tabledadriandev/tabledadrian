# Focaccia Genovese with Brine Dimples

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Genoa original — high hydration, a full cold ferment, and the defining move: dimples flooded with salt brine and oil before the bake, so every crater fries and steams at once.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- strong flour — 500 g
- water — 350 ml (plus 40 ml for brine)
- fresh yeast — 10 g (or 4 g dry)
- extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml (plus 40 ml for pan and brine)
- fine salt — 10 g (plus 4 g for brine)
- malt or honey-alternative syrup — 5 g
- flaky salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Mix flour, water, yeast, malt; rest 20 minutes. Add salt and 30 ml oil; knead to a smooth, slack dough.
2. Bulk 1 hour, folding once; then refrigerate 12–18 hours.
3. Oil a 30×40 cm tray generously. Stretch the dough to fill; prove 90 minutes at warm room temperature until pillowy.
4. Whisk the brine: 40 ml water, 20 ml oil, 4 g salt.
5. Dimple with straight fingers to the pan floor; flood with all the brine.
6. Prove 30 minutes more. Bake at 230°C for 20–25 minutes to deep gold with pale craters.
7. Brush with the last oil out of the oven; cool on a rack.

## Plating & Service
Cut in strips, eaten warm — in Genoa it's breakfast, dunked in cappuccino; aboard it anchors every aperitivo board.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Toppings stay minimal: olives pressed in, or paper-thin onion. The brine is not optional — it is the focaccia.
