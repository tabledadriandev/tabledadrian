# Carciofi alla Romana, Mint-Braised Artichokes

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Roman artichokes stuffed with mint and garlic, braised stem-up in oil and water until spoon-soft — a side with the presence of a course.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- globe or violet artichokes with stems — 4 large
- lemon — 1 (for the water)
- mint (mentuccia if possible) — 10 g
- parsley — 8 g
- garlic, minced — 6 g
- extra virgin olive oil — 100 ml
- dry white wine — 80 ml
- water — about 200 ml
- fine salt and black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Turn the artichokes: strip dark leaves, trim the tops by a third, peel stems, open the centre and remove the choke. Hold in lemon water.
2. Mix mint, parsley, garlic, salt and pepper with 20 ml oil; press this into the centres and between the leaves.
3. Stand the artichokes stem-UP in a snug pot.
4. Add remaining oil, wine and water to one-third up the heads.
5. Braise lidded at the gentlest simmer 30–35 minutes until a skewer meets no resistance.
6. Rest in the liquor 15 minutes; reduce the juices slightly to spoon over.

## Plating & Service
One per guest, stem proudly skyward, glossed with the emulsified braising juices — warm or room temperature.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> They keep 3 days submerged in their oil and improve. Frozen artichoke bottoms make a fine everyday version stuffed the same way.
