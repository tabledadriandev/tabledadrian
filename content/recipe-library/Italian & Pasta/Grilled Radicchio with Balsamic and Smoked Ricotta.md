# Grilled Radicchio with Balsamic and Smoked Ricotta

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Treviso-style bitter leaves charred until the edges frizzle, tamed with balsamic and a snowfall of smoked ricotta — bitterness as a feature, not a flaw.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- radicchio di Treviso or round chioggia — 2 heads (about 500 g)
- extra virgin olive oil — 50 ml
- balsamic vinegar — 25 ml
- honey — 8 g
- smoked ricotta or ricotta salata, grated — 40 g
- thyme — 1 g
- flaky salt and black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Quarter the radicchio through the core so the leaves hold together.
2. Whisk 15 ml balsamic, honey and 30 ml oil.
3. Brush the wedges with the remaining plain oil; salt.
4. Grill on high 2–3 minutes per cut face until the edges char and the core softens slightly.
5. Dress the hot wedges immediately with the balsamic mix — heat opens them to it.
6. Finish with grated smoked ricotta, thyme, pepper.

## Plating & Service
Wedges cut-face up on a warm platter, cheese melting into the folds — beside anything grilled, or with white beans as a lunch.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Roast at 220°C when there's no grill. Vegan: skip cheese, add toasted walnut crumb; sweet-toothed tables like 2 more grams of honey.
