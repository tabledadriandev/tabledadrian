# Mozzarella in Carrozza with Anchovy and Oregano

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The golden 'carriage' — mozzarella sealed between bread, double-dipped and fried until the middle pulls a metre; anchovy inside for grown-ups.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- white sandwich bread, crustless — 8 slices
- mozzarella (low-moisture or well-drained fior di latte) — 250 g
- anchovy fillets — 8
- dried oregano — 1 g
- plain flour — 80 g
- whole eggs, beaten — 3
- whole milk — 40 ml
- dry breadcrumbs — 120 g
- sunflower oil — 1 L
- fine salt — to taste
- lemon — 1, in wedges

## Method
1. Slice mozzarella 1 cm thick; press between paper towels 10 minutes — moisture is the saboteur.
2. Build sandwiches: mozzarella, 2 anchovy fillets, oregano; press the edges firmly; cut into triangles.
3. Seal the open edges by pressing them into the flour first.
4. Dredge fully in flour, then eggs beaten with milk and a little salt, then breadcrumbs; repeat egg and crumb for a double coat.
5. Rest 10 minutes so the crust sets.
6. Fry at 175°C, 2 minutes per side, to deep gold; drain and salt.

## Plating & Service
Cut one triangle open at the pass for the cheese-pull moment, lemon wedges alongside — serve in the first two minutes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skip anchovy and add basil for the children's table. Air fryer 200°C/8 minutes works with a generous oil spray.
