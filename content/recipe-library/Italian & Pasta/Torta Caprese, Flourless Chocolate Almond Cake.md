# Torta Caprese, Flourless Chocolate Almond Cake

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Capri's famous 'mistake' — no flour, just almonds, butter and dark chocolate; crackled crust outside, moist and fudgy at the heart, naturally gluten-free.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- dark chocolate 70% — 200 g
- butter — 180 g
- caster sugar — 160 g
- whole eggs, separated — 5
- almond flour (or blitzed blanched almonds) — 220 g
- cocoa powder — 15 g
- vanilla — half a pod
- fine salt — 2 g
- icing sugar — for dusting
- crème fraîche or unsweetened whipped cream — 200 g to serve

## Method
1. Melt chocolate and butter together at 50°C; cool slightly.
2. Whisk yolks with 100 g sugar and vanilla to ribbons; fold in the chocolate, then almond flour, cocoa and salt.
3. Whip whites with remaining 60 g sugar to soft, glossy peaks.
4. Fold whites in thirds into the heavy base — deflate as little as possible.
5. Pour into a buttered, based-lined 24 cm springform.
6. Bake at 170°C for 35–40 minutes: the edges set, the centre reads 92–94°C and still sighs. Do not overbake.
7. Cool fully in the tin — it settles into its signature crackle.

## Plating & Service
Thin wedges dusted white, a spoon of barely-sour cream — room temperature, never fridge-cold, ideally with an espresso alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Improves for 2 days wrapped. Add orange zest and 20 ml Grand-Marnier-style liqueur for gala, or serve warm with the pistachio semifreddo.
