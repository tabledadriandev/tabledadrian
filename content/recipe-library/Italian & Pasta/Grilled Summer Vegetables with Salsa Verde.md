# Grilled Summer Vegetables with Salsa Verde

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Verdure grigliate elevated by discipline — each vegetable cut to its own thickness and grilled to its own clock, united by a rough green sauce.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- courgettes, 8 mm lengthwise — 300 g
- aubergine, 1 cm rounds — 300 g
- red peppers, wide flats — 300 g
- spring onions, whole — 8
- extra virgin olive oil — 100 ml
- parsley — 30 g
- basil — 10 g
- capers — 20 g
- Dijon mustard — 5 g
- red wine vinegar — 15 ml
- garlic — 3 g
- flaky salt and black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Salt the aubergine rounds 15 minutes; pat dry. Brush all vegetables lightly with oil (about 40 ml total); season.
2. Grill each to its clock on high heat: peppers 4–5 minutes per side, aubergine 3–4, courgette 2–3, spring onions 2 — bar marks, no mush.
3. Salsa verde: hand-chop parsley, basil, capers and garlic; work in mustard, vinegar and 60 ml oil to a loose, coarse sauce.
4. Layer the vegetables on a platter as they come off, seasoning each layer.
5. Spoon salsa verde over while everything is warm; rest 10 minutes.

## Plating & Service
A composed abundance at room temperature, sauce zigzagged, extra in a bowl — it anchors the whole deck buffet.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fish-friendly upgrade: 2 anchovies in the salsa verde. Everything can be grilled hours ahead — this dish prefers patience.
