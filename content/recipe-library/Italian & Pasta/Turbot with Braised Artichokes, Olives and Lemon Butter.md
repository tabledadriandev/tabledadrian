# Turbot with Braised Artichokes, Olives and Lemon Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Thick turbot tranches roasted on the bone beside violet artichokes and taggiasca, tied by a beurre-blanc-adjacent lemon butter — the fish course for the final gala night.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- turbot tranches on the bone — 4 × 250 g
- violet artichokes — 6
- lemon — 2 (one for acidulated water, one juiced)
- taggiasca olives — 50 g
- dry white wine — 150 ml
- shallot, minced — 40 g
- cold butter, cubed — 120 g
- olive oil — 50 ml
- thyme — 2 g
- fish stock — 100 ml
- chives — 4 g
- fine salt and white pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Turn artichokes to hearts, halve, hold in lemon water; braise with 30 ml oil, thyme, half the wine and the stock, lidded, 12 minutes until tender; add olives; keep warm.
2. Dry the turbot; season; oil lightly.
3. Roast at 200°C, 10–12 minutes to core 50°C, basting once — on the bone means juicier and more forgiving.
4. Lemon butter: reduce remaining wine (75 ml) with shallot to 25 ml; whisk in cold butter cube by cube off the boil; finish with 15 ml lemon juice, salt, white pepper.
5. Rest fish 3 minutes; check the flesh lifts from the bone in pearly flakes.

## Plating & Service
Tranche centred, artichokes and olives leaning against it, sauce poured at the table, chives over.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Brill or john dory tranche substitutes. The butter holds 30 minutes at 50°C — never over 60°C or it splits.
