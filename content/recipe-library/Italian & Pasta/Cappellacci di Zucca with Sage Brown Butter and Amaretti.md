# Cappellacci di Zucca with Sage Brown Butter and Amaretti

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Ferrara's sweet-savoury pumpkin hats — roasted squash concentrated to a fudge-like filling, sharpened with Parmigiano and a whisper of amaretti.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- 00 flour — 300 g
- whole eggs — 3
- delica or butternut squash — 1 kg
- Parmigiano Reggiano, grated — 90 g
- amaretti biscuits, crushed — 30 g
- nutmeg — 0.5 g
- lemon zest — of 1 lemon
- fine salt — to taste
- butter — 100 g
- sage leaves — 16
- aged balsamic vinegar — 10 ml

## Method
1. Roast squash halves cut-side down at 190°C for 50 minutes until collapsing. Scoop flesh and dry it out in a pan over low heat, stirring, 10 minutes.
2. Pass squash through a sieve. Beat in Parmigiano (60 g), amaretti, nutmeg, lemon zest and salt. Chill until pipeable.
3. Make egg dough, rest 1 hour, roll to 0.7 mm.
4. Cut 7 cm squares, pipe 10 g filling, fold into triangles, seal excluding air, then join the two base corners around a finger into a 'little hat'.
5. Boil in salted water 3 minutes.
6. Meanwhile foam butter with sage over medium heat until nut-brown and the leaves crisp, 3–4 minutes.
7. Toss cappellacci gently in the brown butter with a spoon of pasta water to glaze.

## Plating & Service
Seven pieces per bowl, glazed and glossy, crisp sage on top, three drops of aged balsamic and the remaining Parmigiano.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: omit amaretti and add 15 g toasted breadcrumbs with a pinch of sugar. A drop of mostarda syrup in the filling is the classic Ferrarese twist.
