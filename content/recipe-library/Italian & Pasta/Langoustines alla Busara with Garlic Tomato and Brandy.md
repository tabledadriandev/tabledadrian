# Langoustines alla Busara with Garlic Tomato and Brandy

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Venetian-Adriatic scampi in their shells, flashed with garlic, brandy, tomato and a rain of peppery crumbs — messy, glorious, and gone in minutes.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- whole langoustines or large shell-on prawns — 16 (about 1 kg)
- extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- garlic, sliced — 4 cloves
- brandy or cognac — 40 ml
- dry white wine — 80 ml
- tomato passata — 250 g
- chilli — 1 g
- dry breadcrumbs, toasted — 30 g
- parsley — 10 g
- fine salt and black pepper — to taste
- lemon — 1, in wedges

## Method
1. Split the langoustines lengthwise through the back shell (or leave whole for full tradition); devein.
2. Heat oil in the widest pan; sear the langoustines flesh-down 60 seconds; remove.
3. Soften garlic and chilli in the same oil 1 minute.
4. Add brandy (flame off), then wine; reduce by half. Add passata and 50 ml water; simmer 5 minutes; season.
5. Return langoustines flesh-up; spoon sauce over; lid on 3–4 minutes until just set.
6. Finish with toasted crumbs, parsley and raw oil.

## Plating & Service
Straight from the pan at the table, shells up, sauce for dredging, lemon, finger bowls and a stack of bread — elegance through abandon.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF crumbs work; or skip crumbs for a cleaner sauce and toss the leftovers with spaghetti — the unofficial second course.
