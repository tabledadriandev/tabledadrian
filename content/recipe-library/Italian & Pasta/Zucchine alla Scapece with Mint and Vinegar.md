# Zucchine alla Scapece with Mint and Vinegar

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Naples' answer to escabeche — fried courgette coins soused warm in vinegar, garlic and mint; served cold, they cut through any rich main.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- courgettes, 5 mm coins — 600 g
- sunflower or olive oil for frying — 300 ml
- white wine vinegar — 50 ml
- garlic, sliced — 2 cloves
- mint leaves, torn — 8 g
- extra virgin olive oil — 20 ml
- caster sugar — 3 g
- fine salt — to taste

## Method
1. Salt the coins lightly; dry them 20 minutes on cloth — dry coins fry, wet coins stew.
2. Shallow-fry in 170°C oil in batches until golden on both faces, 3–4 minutes; drain.
3. Layer warm coins in a dish with garlic, mint and a pinch of salt as you go.
4. Warm the vinegar with sugar just to dissolve; pour over.
5. Add the raw olive oil; press gently so everything glistens.
6. Rest at least 2 hours — overnight is the true dish.

## Plating & Service
Room temperature in a shallow dish, more torn mint — beside grilled fish, frittata, or straight onto bruschetta.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Aubergine takes the same souse. Air-fried or grilled coins make the light version; the vinegar carries it either way.
