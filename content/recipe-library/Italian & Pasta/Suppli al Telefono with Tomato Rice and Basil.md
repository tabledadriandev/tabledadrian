# Suppli al Telefono with Tomato Rice and Basil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rome's answer to arancini — tomato-braised rice croquettes whose mozzarella centre stretches like old telephone wires; the name is the instruction.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- risotto rice — 350 g
- tomato passata — 400 g
- vegetable stock — 700 ml
- onion, fine — 60 g
- butter — 40 g
- Parmigiano Reggiano — 70 g
- basil — 8 g
- mozzarella, 1.5 cm batons, drained — 120 g
- plain flour — 60 g
- eggs, beaten — 2
- dry breadcrumbs — 180 g
- sunflower oil — 1.5 L
- fine salt and black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Soften onion in butter; add rice, toast 2 minutes; add passata and stock in stages, cooking 16 minutes to a thick, fully absorbed tomato risotto.
2. Beat in Parmigiano and torn basil; season; spread on a tray, press film on top, chill completely.
3. Form 80 g ovals around a baton of mozzarella, sealing rice thoroughly.
4. Roll in flour, egg, then crumbs.
5. Fry at 170°C for 4–5 minutes to mahogany; rest 2 minutes — the pull needs molten, not warm, cheese.

## Plating & Service
Halved with a slow pull for the table's benefit, flaky salt, nothing else needed.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> A spoon of ragù in the rice is the full Roman version. Bake-off option: 210°C/18 minutes on a rack, oil-sprayed.
