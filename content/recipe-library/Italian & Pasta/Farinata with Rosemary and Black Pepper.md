# Farinata with Rosemary and Black Pepper

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Genoa's chickpea flatbread — a thin batter rested for hours then blasted in a hot pan until custardy inside, crisp-edged, and smoky with olive oil.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- chickpea flour — 300 g
- water — 900 ml
- extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml
- fine salt — 8 g
- rosemary needles — 3 g
- black pepper, coarse — 2 g

## Method
1. Whisk chickpea flour into the water until smooth; rest 4–8 hours at room temperature, skimming any foam.
2. Stir in salt and 50 ml of the oil.
3. Heat the oven to 250°C (or its max) with a 32 cm shallow steel or copper pan inside.
4. Pour remaining oil into the scorching pan, then the batter — 8 mm deep, no more.
5. Scatter rosemary. Bake 12–15 minutes until deeply golden with crisp lace at the edges.
6. Rest 3 minutes; grind pepper over generously.

## Plating & Service
Cut in irregular wedges straight from the pan, eaten warm in hand with aperitivi.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Classic add-ins: paper-thin onion, or stracchino dabs post-bake. The long batter rest is what separates farinata from pancake — don't skip it.
