# Gnocco Fritto with Mortadella Mousse

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Emilian fried dough pillows — hollow, blistered, scalding — with a whipped mortadella mousse standing in for the classic cold-cut board; aperitivo theatre.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- plain flour — 350 g
- whole milk, warm — 160 ml
- fresh yeast — 8 g (or 3 g dry)
- lard or butter, soft — 30 g
- fine salt — 7 g
- sugar — 5 g
- mortadella — 200 g
- ricotta — 80 g
- mascarpone — 50 g
- pistachios, chopped (optional garnish) — 15 g
- sunflower oil or lard for frying — 1.5 L
- black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Dissolve yeast and sugar in the milk. Knead with flour, lard and salt 8 minutes to a soft dough. Prove 90 minutes until doubled.
2. Mousse: blitz mortadella to a paste; add ricotta and mascarpone; blend until whipped and airy; pepper; pipe into a bowl and chill.
3. Roll dough to 3 mm; cut 8×6 cm diamonds.
4. Fry at 180°C, a few at a time, ladling oil over — they should balloon in seconds; 60–90 seconds per side to pale gold.
5. Drain upright in a paper-lined bowl; salt lightly.

## Plating & Service
A tumble of hot pillows beside the chilled mousse, pistachio over the mousse — guests tear, dip, repeat.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The classic partner is simply sliced culatello or coppa if the mousse feels cheffy. Dough keeps overnight retarded in the fridge — fry to order.
