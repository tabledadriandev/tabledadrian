# Anelletti al Forno alla Palermitana

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Palermo's beach-picnic bake — little pasta rings set in meat ragù and caciocavallo, unmoulded like a cake and just as good at room temperature on deck.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- anelletti (or ditalini) — 400 g
- beef and pork mince — 400 g
- tomato passata — 500 g
- frozen peas — 120 g
- onion, fine — 100 g
- red wine — 80 ml
- aubergine, 1 cm dice — 250 g
- caciocavallo or provolone, diced — 150 g
- Parmigiano Reggiano — 60 g
- eggs, hard-boiled and sliced — 2
- olive oil — 80 ml
- dry breadcrumbs — 30 g
- fine salt, black pepper, bay — to taste

## Method
1. Fry aubergine dice in half the oil until bronzed; drain.
2. Soften onion in remaining oil; brown mince hard; deglaze with wine.
3. Add passata and bay; simmer 30 minutes; fold in peas; season.
4. Boil anelletti to 3 minutes under packet time; drain.
5. Mix pasta with ragù, aubergine, caciocavallo and half the Parmigiano.
6. Butter-and-crumb a 24 cm springform. Fill halfway, layer the egg slices, fill to the top; press. Crumb-and-cheese the surface.
7. Bake at 180°C fan 35 minutes until the crust is deep gold. Rest 20 minutes; unmould.

## Plating & Service
Present whole at the table, cut wedges like a cake — it should stand proud without slumping.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Palermo tradition insists on this at the beach — it travels and holds beautifully for tender lunches away from the yacht. Vegetarian: lentil-mushroom ragù.
