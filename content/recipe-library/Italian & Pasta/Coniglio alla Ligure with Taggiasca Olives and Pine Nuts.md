# Coniglio alla Ligure with Taggiasca Olives and Pine Nuts

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The western Riviera's signature rabbit — braised with rosemary, white wine, taggiasca olives and pine nuts until the sauce turns amber and clinging.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- rabbit, jointed — 1.4 kg
- extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- onion, fine — 120 g
- garlic — 2 cloves
- rosemary — 4 g
- thyme — 2 g
- dry white wine (Vermentino) — 250 ml
- chicken stock — 300 ml
- taggiasca olives — 80 g
- pine nuts — 30 g
- walnut-sized piece of butter (optional) — 20 g
- fine salt and black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Season the rabbit; brown in oil in a wide braiser to deep gold on all sides, 10 minutes; remove.
2. Soften onion 6 minutes; add garlic and herbs, 1 minute.
3. Return rabbit; add wine; reduce by half.
4. Add stock to halfway up the pieces; braise lidded at 160°C (or lowest flame) 50 minutes.
5. Add olives and pine nuts; cook uncovered 15 minutes more, turning, until the sauce reduces to a sticky amber glaze — loins will already be tender; legs need the full time.
6. Optional: mount the sauce with the butter.

## Plating & Service
Pieces glazed and stacked rustically, sauce and olives spooned over, rosemary flowers — with potatoes crushed in the same oil, or fresh trenette.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken thighs replicate it honestly for easier provisioning. Add 100 g fresh porcini in autumn for the Ponente Sunday version.
