# Lamb Scottadito with Mint, Lemon and Pecorino

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> 'Burnt fingers' chops — thin-cut lamb racks marinated fast, grilled ferociously and eaten by hand while too hot to hold; the Roman grill at its most primal.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- lamb rack chops, cut thin (1.5 cm) — 12 (about 900 g)
- extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- lemon juice and zest — of 1 lemon
- garlic, crushed — 2 cloves
- rosemary, chopped — 3 g
- mint, chopped — 6 g
- pecorino romano, finely grated — 30 g
- black pepper — 2 g
- flaky salt — to taste

## Method
1. Marinate the chops in oil, lemon juice, garlic, rosemary and pepper — 30 minutes, no longer, at room temperature.
2. Heat the grill or plancha to ferocious.
3. Drain and pat the chops (wet meat steams); lay them down and leave 2 minutes untouched.
4. Flip; 90 seconds more — target a pink 54–56°C with a dark rim of char on the fat.
5. Rest 3 minutes on a warm platter.
6. Shower with mint, lemon zest, pecorino and flaky salt while sizzling.

## Plating & Service
A messy pile on a shared board — bones as handles, lemon wedges, napkins mandatory; formality kills this dish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free without the pecorino, using extra mint and zest. Goat chops in spring are the shepherd's original.
