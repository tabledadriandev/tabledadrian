# Sea Bass Crudo with Blood Orange, Fennel and Green Olive

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sicilian insalata di arance re-imagined as crudo — sashimi-cut branzino, blood orange, shaved fennel and green olive; winter sunshine on a plate.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- sea bass fillet, skinned, sashimi-grade — 300 g
- blood oranges — 2
- fennel bulb, small — 1 (150 g)
- green olives (castelvetrano), slivered — 40 g
- extra virgin olive oil — 40 ml
- lemon juice — 10 ml
- flaky salt — 2 g
- pink peppercorns, crushed — 1 g
- fennel fronds or dill — 3 g

## Method
1. Chill plates. Slice the bass across the grain into 3 mm petals with a long, wet knife.
2. Segment one orange over a bowl, saving juice; slice the other into thin rounds.
3. Shave fennel paper-thin on a mandoline into iced water; drain and dry at service.
4. Whisk 20 ml blood-orange juice with lemon juice and oil to a quick citronette.
5. Assemble no more than 5 minutes before serving — the citrus should season, not cure.

## Plating & Service
Petals of fish and orange alternating, fennel tangle in the centre, olives and citronette dotted around, salt and pink pepper last.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Amberjack (ricciola) is even more Sicilian if the market offers it. In blood-orange off-season, use tarocco-style navels plus 3 drops of Campari in the dressing.
