# Affogato al Caffe with Hazelnut Brittle and Fior di Latte Gelato

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The two-minute dessert with one non-negotiable — the espresso must be pulled at the table over properly dense gelato; the brittle buys you texture and applause.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- fior di latte gelato or best vanilla ice cream — 400 g
- freshly pulled espresso — 4 double shots (120 ml)
- hazelnuts, toasted — 60 g
- caster sugar — 80 g
- flaky salt — 1 g
- dark chocolate, grated — 10 g
- Frangelico or amaretto (optional) — 20 ml

## Method
1. Brittle: dry-caramelise the sugar to amber; stir in hazelnuts and salt; pour onto paper; cool and crack into shards.
2. Chill four coupes or small tumblers in the freezer 20 minutes.
3. Scoop the gelato into tight, cold spheres; return to the freezer until the last second.
4. Pull the espresso AT service — crema intact, 92°C.
5. Place a scoop and a brittle shard in each frozen glass.

## Plating & Service
Espresso poured over at the table from small copper pots, chocolate grated across, optional liqueur offered — eaten in the ninety seconds it deserves.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Decaf pulls identically for late seatings. Gelato quality is 90% of this dessert — dense, low-overrun, not aerated supermarket foam.
