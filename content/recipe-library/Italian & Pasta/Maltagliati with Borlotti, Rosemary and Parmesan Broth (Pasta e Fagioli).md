# Maltagliati with Borlotti, Rosemary and Parmesan Broth (Pasta e Fagioli)

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> 'Badly-cut' pasta offcuts in a creamy borlotti pottage with a parmesan-rind broth — the frugal Emilian masterpiece every masterclass should end on.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- fresh egg pasta offcuts or dough — 250 g
- dried borlotti beans, soaked overnight — 250 g (or 500 g cooked)
- pancetta, fine dice — 60 g
- onion, carrot, celery brunoise — 150 g total
- garlic — 2 cloves
- rosemary — 3 g
- tomato paste — 25 g
- Parmigiano rind — 1 piece (40 g)
- olive oil — 40 ml
- Parmigiano Reggiano, grated — 40 g
- black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Simmer soaked borlotti with the parmesan rind in 1.5 L water until tender, 45–60 minutes; salt at the end. Keep the liquor.
2. Sweat pancetta and soffritto in oil 8 minutes; add garlic, rosemary and tomato paste, cook 2 minutes.
3. Add the beans with 800 ml of their liquor; simmer 10 minutes.
4. Blend one-third of the soup and return it — creamy, not smooth.
5. Roll dough to 1 mm and cut rough diamonds and ribbons — maltagliati means badly cut; embrace it.
6. Simmer pasta directly in the soup 3 minutes, adding liquor to a loose, spoonable consistency.

## Plating & Service
Deep bowls, a swirl of raw olive oil, black pepper, Parmigiano — serve with a spoon, not a fork.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: skip pancetta and rind; add 10 g miso and a bay leaf. Even better the next day — thin with water when reheating.
