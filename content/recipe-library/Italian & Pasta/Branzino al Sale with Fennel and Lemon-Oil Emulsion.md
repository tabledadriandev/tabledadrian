# Branzino al Sale with Fennel and Lemon-Oil Emulsion

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Whole sea bass baked in an aromatic salt crust — the fish steams in its own moisture at exactly the right salinity, cracked open tableside for maximum theatre.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- whole sea bass, gutted, scales ON — 1.4 kg
- coarse sea salt — 2 kg
- egg whites — 3 (about 100 g)
- fennel fronds and stalks — 60 g
- lemon — 2 (one sliced, one juiced)
- fresh bay — 3 leaves
- extra virgin olive oil — 80 ml
- black peppercorns — 5 g
- flat parsley — 5 g

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Stuff the cavity with lemon slices, fennel stalks, bay and peppercorns — do NOT salt the fish.
2. Mix coarse salt with egg whites and chopped fennel fronds to wet-sand texture.
3. Bed 1 cm of salt on a tray, lay the fish, pack the rest over leaving the tail exposed, pressing into a smooth dome.
4. Bake 25–30 minutes: rule of thumb 18 minutes per kg plus 5, or core 52–54°C probed through the crust.
5. Rest 8 minutes. Crack the crust with a spoon handle, brush away all salt, peel back the skin.
6. Whisk lemon juice into the olive oil with chopped parsley and a splash of the fish's own juices.

## Plating & Service
Fillet tableside onto warm plates, spoon over the lemon-oil emulsion, serve with braised fennel or simple greens.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The scales are the barrier — never use a scaled fish. Same crust suits daurade or a 2 kg dentice; adjust to core temperature, not time.
