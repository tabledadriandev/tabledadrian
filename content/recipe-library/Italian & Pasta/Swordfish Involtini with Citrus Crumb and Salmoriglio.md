# Swordfish Involtini with Citrus Crumb and Salmoriglio

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Messina-style swordfish rolls around a pine-nut, currant and citrus stuffing, grilled fast and dressed with oregano-lemon salmoriglio.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- swordfish, thin 5 mm slices — 12 (about 600 g)
- dry breadcrumbs — 100 g
- pine nuts, toasted — 25 g
- currants, soaked — 30 g
- capers, chopped — 15 g
- orange and lemon zest — of 1 each
- parsley — 8 g
- extra virgin olive oil — 100 ml
- lemon juice — 40 ml
- dried oregano — 2 g
- garlic, minced — 3 g
- bay leaves — 8
- fine salt and black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Have the fishmonger slice the swordfish thin, or freeze 30 minutes and slice yourself; pound gently to even 4 mm sheets.
2. Toast crumbs in 25 ml oil; mix with pine nuts, currants, capers, zests, parsley, salt and 15 ml oil to a moist stuffing.
3. Spread each slice with 15 g stuffing; roll snugly; skewer two rolls per stick with a bay leaf between.
4. Brush with oil; season.
5. Grill or plancha at high heat 2–3 minutes per side — swordfish dries fast; pull at 54°C.
6. Salmoriglio: whisk 60 ml oil, lemon juice, oregano, garlic and salt with 10 ml warm water until creamy.

## Plating & Service
Three rolls per plate over dressed rocket or grilled vegetables, salmoriglio spooned over the top, extra at the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: replace pine nuts with toasted sunflower seeds. The same stuffing and sauce serve for chicken paillard on mixed-table nights.
