# Anchovies al Verde, Piedmont Green Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Piedmont's merenda sinoira staple — salt-cured anchovies layered under a rough parsley-garlic-chilli sauce, improving in the fridge for days.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- best salt-packed or oil-packed anchovy fillets — 150 g
- flat-leaf parsley — 50 g
- garlic — 4 g
- red chilli — 4 g
- white bread crumb, soaked in vinegar — 30 g
- red wine vinegar — 25 ml (for soaking)
- extra virgin olive oil — 130 ml
- hard-boiled egg yolk (optional, classic) — 1
- fine salt — only if needed

## Method
1. If salt-packed: rinse anchovies, soak 10 minutes, debone and dry. Oil-packed: drain and pat.
2. Chop parsley, garlic and chilli to a fine rubble — by knife, for texture.
3. Squeeze the vinegar-soaked crumb; crumble it in, with the sieved yolk if using.
4. Work in the oil to a loose, spoonable green sauce.
5. Layer anchovies and sauce in a shallow dish or jar, finishing with sauce; rest at least 4 hours, ideally 24.

## Plating & Service
Straight from the dish onto warm crostini or grissini — a jar of these outperforms most canapés for effort-to-applause ratio.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: use GF crumb or 20 g cooked potato. The sauce doubles for bollito misto and grilled fish all week.
