# Spaghettone with Bottarga, Lemon and Toasted Crumb

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sicilian pantry luxury in eleven minutes — grated cured mullet roe emulsified with oil and pasta water, cut with lemon and crunch.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- spaghettoni — 400 g
- bottarga di muggine, whole piece — 60 g
- extra virgin olive oil — 80 ml
- garlic, bruised — 1 clove
- lemon zest and juice — of 1 lemon
- coarse breadcrumbs — 30 g
- flat-leaf parsley — 5 g
- black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Toast crumbs in 15 ml oil to deep gold; season and reserve.
2. Grate two-thirds of the bottarga finely; shave the rest into petals for finishing.
3. Warm remaining oil with garlic over the lowest heat 3 minutes; remove garlic.
4. Boil spaghetti in barely salted water; reserve 250 ml water.
5. Off the heat, whisk 150 ml pasta water into the oil, then the grated bottarga to a beige cream.
6. Toss in the pasta over low heat until glossy; finish with lemon juice, zest and parsley.

## Plating & Service
Twirled high, bottarga petals overlapping on the crown, crumbs and pepper last.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cheese never touches this plate. Stretch a small piece of bottarga further by adding 20 g toasted pine nuts — or keep it austere.
