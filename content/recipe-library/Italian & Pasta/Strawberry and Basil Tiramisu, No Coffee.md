# Strawberry and Basil Tiramisu, No Coffee

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The afternoon tiramisu — savoiardi soaked in a strawberry-basil consommé, layered with vanilla mascarpone; the one for guests who can't do espresso at midnight.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- strawberries — 600 g (400 g for soak, 200 g for layers)
- caster sugar — 130 g
- basil leaves — 8 g
- lemon juice — 20 ml
- egg yolks — 4
- mascarpone — 450 g
- double cream — 200 ml
- vanilla pod — 1
- savoiardi — 280 g
- white chocolate or freeze-dried strawberry — 15 g

## Method
1. Consommé: macerate 400 g quartered strawberries with 50 g sugar; warm over a bain-marie, covered, 30 minutes; strain without pressing; add lemon juice and torn basil, infuse 10 minutes, strain again; cool.
2. Sabayon yolks with 80 g sugar to 72°C; cool.
3. Fold in mascarpone with vanilla seeds, then softly whipped cream.
4. Slice remaining strawberries thin.
5. Dip savoiardi in the consommé; layer with cream and strawberry slices, two rounds.
6. Chill 6 hours.

## Plating & Service
Coupes show the rose-pink strata best — top with a strawberry fan, micro basil, and a dust of freeze-dried berry or white chocolate.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Raspberries or ripe peaches swap into the same architecture. The clear consommé is a pastry-section trick worth keeping for spritzes too.
