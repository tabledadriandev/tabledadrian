# Agnolotti del Plin with Roast Meat Filling and Brodo Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Pinched Piedmontese parcels filled with a three-meat roast reduced to essence — served 'al tovagliolo' simple, glazed in their own roasting-juice butter.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- 00 flour — 350 g
- egg yolks — 8
- whole egg — 1
- veal shoulder — 250 g
- pork neck — 150 g
- rabbit or chicken thigh — 150 g
- savoy cabbage leaves — 80 g
- onion, quartered — 100 g
- dry white wine — 100 ml
- Parmigiano Reggiano — 80 g
- whole egg for filling — 1
- butter — 80 g
- roasting juices/stock — 150 ml
- rosemary — 1 sprig
- fine salt, black pepper, nutmeg — to taste

## Method
1. Roast the three meats with onion and rosemary at 170°C, deglazing with wine, until deeply golden and tender, about 75 minutes. Reserve all juices.
2. Wilt cabbage in the roasting tray 5 minutes. Mince meats and cabbage finely (grinder, 4 mm plate). Bind with egg, Parmigiano, nutmeg, salt and a spoon of juices. Chill.
3. Make rich yolk dough; rest 1 hour. Roll to 0.7 mm in long bands.
4. Pipe a continuous rope of filling 1.5 cm from the bottom edge. Fold pasta over, seal, then pinch ('plin') at 2.5 cm intervals.
5. Cut between pinches with a fluted wheel, seam-side standing.
6. Reduce remaining roasting juices with stock to 80 ml; mount with butter to a glaze.
7. Boil agnolotti 2.5 minutes; toss delicately in the glaze.

## Plating & Service
Line plates in neat overlapping rows like fallen dominoes, glaze spooned along, Parmigiano only — no herbs.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The classic alternative service is in a linen napkin, naked, as antipasto. Filling freezes well; make double.
