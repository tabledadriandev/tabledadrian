# Amalfi Lemon Risotto with Basil and Mascarpone

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A pale, perfumed risotto where lemon enters three ways — zest in the toast, juice at the end, candied peel on top — kept airy with mascarpone.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- carnaroli rice — 320 g
- unwaxed lemons — 2 (zest and juice)
- vegetable stock, hot — 1.2 L
- shallot, fine — 50 g
- butter — 60 g
- mascarpone — 60 g
- Parmigiano Reggiano — 60 g
- dry white wine — 70 ml
- basil, torn — 8 g
- caster sugar (for peel) — 20 g
- fine salt and white pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Candy fine strips of half a lemon's zest: blanch twice, then simmer 5 minutes in 40 ml water with the sugar; drain.
2. Sweat shallot in 30 g butter; toast rice 2 minutes with the grated zest of one lemon.
3. Deglaze with wine; then cook with ladles of stock, stirring, 15 minutes.
4. At al dente, kill the heat; rest 1 minute.
5. Mantecare with remaining butter, mascarpone and Parmigiano; finish with 25 ml lemon juice, salt, white pepper.
6. Fold in torn basil last so it stays vivid.

## Plating & Service
Loose enough to find its own level; candied peel and micro basil on the surface, one drop of good oil.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add raw scampi carpaccio on top of the hot rice for a Riviera showstopper. Vegan: olive oil and oat crème fraîche mantecatura.
