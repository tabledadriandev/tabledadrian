# Scialatielli with Mussels, Pecorino and Black Pepper

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Amalfi's short, chewy milk-dough ribbons meet an impepata of mussels — sea brine against sharp pecorino, a pairing that shouldn't work and absolutely does.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- 00 flour — 350 g
- whole milk — 130 ml
- whole egg — 1
- pecorino, grated — 60 g (plus 20 g to finish)
- basil, chopped — 6 g
- olive oil — 50 ml
- mussels — 1.2 kg
- garlic — 2 cloves
- dry white wine — 80 ml
- black pepper, coarse — 5 g
- parsley — 6 g

## Method
1. Knead flour, milk, egg, 30 g pecorino, basil and 10 ml oil to a firm dough; rest 30 minutes.
2. Roll to 3 mm; cut ribbons 1 cm wide and 12 cm long — short and thick is correct.
3. Open mussels with garlic and wine, lidded, 3 minutes; pick two-thirds, strain the liquor.
4. Sizzle the pepper in remaining oil 30 seconds; add mussel liquor; reduce by a third.
5. Boil scialatielli 4–5 minutes; transfer to the pan.
6. Toss 2 minutes, feeding in pasta water; fold in all mussels, remaining pecorino and parsley off the heat.

## Plating & Service
Coiled in bowls with in-shell mussels around the rim, more pepper — the dish should bite back gently.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cheese-with-shellfish is the point here, sanctioned by the Amalfi coast itself. Courgette and its flowers replace mussels for a vegetarian sister dish.
