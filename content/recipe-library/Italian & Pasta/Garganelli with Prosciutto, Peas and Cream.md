# Garganelli with Prosciutto, Peas and Cream

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Hand-rolled ridged quills from Imola — the comb-textured tubes trap a silky prosciutto-and-pea cream that tastes like late spring in Emilia.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- 00 flour — 300 g
- whole eggs — 3
- prosciutto di Parma, 2 mm ribbons — 120 g
- fresh or frozen peas — 200 g
- double cream — 150 ml
- shallot, minced — 40 g
- butter — 25 g
- Parmigiano Reggiano, grated — 60 g
- black pepper — to taste
- fine salt — to taste

## Method
1. Make egg dough, rest 1 hour. Roll to 0.9 mm and cut 4 cm squares.
2. Roll each square corner-to-corner around a 6 mm dowel over a gnocchi comb, pressing to ridge and seal. Dry 30 minutes on semolina.
3. Sweat shallot in butter 3 minutes. Add half the prosciutto, warm 1 minute without crisping.
4. Add peas and cream; simmer 2 minutes. Season lightly (the ham carries salt).
5. Boil garganelli 2–3 minutes in salted water until al dente.
6. Toss pasta through the sauce with a splash of pasta water and the Parmigiano until it coats each quill.
7. Fold in remaining raw prosciutto ribbons off the heat.

## Plating & Service
Shallow bowls, sauce just clinging, a few pea shoots if aboard, coarse pepper over.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Lighter: replace cream with 100 ml pasta water emulsified with 40 g butter. Vegetarian: swap prosciutto for charred asparagus.
