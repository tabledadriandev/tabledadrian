# Spaghetti with Sea Urchin and Lemon Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sicilian ricci di mare folded raw into an emulsion at the last second — iodine, citrus and butter in a dish that must move from pan to guest in ninety seconds.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- spaghettoni — 360 g
- fresh sea urchin roe — 120 g
- unsalted butter, cold — 60 g
- extra virgin olive oil — 30 ml
- garlic, bruised — 1 clove
- lemon zest — of 1 lemon
- lemon juice — 10 ml
- dry white wine — 40 ml
- flat-leaf parsley, fine — 4 g
- espelette or mild chilli — 0.5 g
- fine salt — to taste

## Method
1. Reserve a third of the best urchin tongues whole for garnish; crush the rest to a paste with 10 ml oil.
2. Boil spaghetti in lightly salted water (urchin is saline) to 2 minutes under al dente.
3. Warm oil and garlic in a wide pan; remove garlic once golden. Add wine, reduce by half.
4. Transfer pasta with tongs plus 150 ml pasta water; cook 90 seconds, swirling constantly.
5. Off the heat: mount with cold butter, then the urchin paste, lemon zest and juice — the sauce must stay below 70°C or it grains.
6. Adjust with pasta water to a light cream; taste for salt.

## Plating & Service
Tight twirls, whole urchin tongues draped on top, parsley, a breath of chilli. Serve immediately in warm (not hot) bowls.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No urchin? The same method with 80 g crab and 20 g 'nduja is a worthy understudy. Never add cheese.
