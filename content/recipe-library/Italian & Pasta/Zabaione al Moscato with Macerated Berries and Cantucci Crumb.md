# Zabaione al Moscato with Macerated Berries and Cantucci Crumb

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The live-whisked dessert — warm Moscato foam beaten to the ribbon over the flame, poured over berries while it still breathes; pure tableside theatre.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- egg yolks — 6
- caster sugar — 90 g
- Moscato d'Asti (or dry Marsala for the classic) — 120 ml
- mixed berries — 300 g
- icing sugar — 15 g
- lemon juice — 10 ml
- cantucci or almond biscuits, crushed — 40 g
- lemon zest — of half a lemon

## Method
1. Macerate the berries with icing sugar and lemon juice, 30 minutes.
2. Set a round-bottomed bowl (copper if you have it) over barely-simmering water.
3. Whisk yolks and sugar to pale ribbons off the heat first, 2 minutes.
4. Add the Moscato; whisk over the bain-marie in figure-eights, 6–8 minutes, until tripled, foamy and 72°C — never scrambled at the walls.
5. Pull it the moment it holds a slowly-collapsing ribbon.

## Plating & Service
Berries in warm coupes, zabaione poured high in front of the guests, cantucci crumb and zest over — spoons in hands within the minute.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cold version: whisk over ice after cooking, fold in 100 ml whipped cream — holds 2 hours. The copper bowl is not vanity; it halves the whisking time.
