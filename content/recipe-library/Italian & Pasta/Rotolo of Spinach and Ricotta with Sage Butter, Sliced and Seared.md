# Rotolo of Spinach and Ricotta with Sage Butter, Sliced and Seared

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A whole pasta sheet rolled around ricotta and spinach, poached in a towel like a galantine, then sliced into pinwheels and seared in sage butter.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- 00 flour — 300 g
- whole eggs — 3
- ricotta, drained — 400 g
- spinach — 500 g
- Parmigiano Reggiano — 100 g
- egg yolk — 1
- nutmeg — 0.5 g
- butter — 100 g
- sage leaves — 14
- lemon zest — of half a lemon
- fine salt and black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Wilt spinach, squeeze very dry, chop. Beat with ricotta, 70 g Parmigiano, yolk, nutmeg, salt and pepper.
2. Make dough; rest 1 hour. Roll into one large sheet ~30×40 cm at 1 mm.
3. Blanch the sheet 45 seconds, refresh, dry on cloth.
4. Spread filling evenly leaving a 2 cm border; roll from the short side into a snug cylinder.
5. Wrap tightly in a clean cloth, tie the ends like a sausage.
6. Poach in salted water at 90°C for 30 minutes. Cool 15 minutes in the cloth, then unwrap.
7. Slice 2.5 cm pinwheels. Sear cut-side down in foaming sage butter until golden, 2 minutes per side.

## Plating & Service
Two seared pinwheels per plate, spiral facing up, sage butter and crisp leaves, Parmigiano dust.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Make the rotolo a day ahead — it slices cleaner cold. Swiss chard and toasted walnut in the filling is the winter variant.
