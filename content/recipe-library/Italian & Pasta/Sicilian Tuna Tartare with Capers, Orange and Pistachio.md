# Sicilian Tuna Tartare with Capers, Orange and Pistachio

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bluefin-style tartare seasoned like the island — capers, orange zest, Bronte pistachio and a whisper of colatura; clean knife-work, no cream, no waste.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- sashimi-grade tuna loin — 360 g
- capers, rinsed, chopped — 20 g
- orange zest — of 1 orange
- orange juice — 15 ml
- lemon juice — 10 ml
- colatura di alici (or 2 minced anchovies) — 5 ml
- extra virgin olive oil — 40 ml
- pistachios, toasted, chopped — 25 g
- spring onion, minced — 20 g
- espelette or mild chilli — 0.5 g
- flaky salt — to taste

## Method
1. Chill bowl and knives. Trim the tuna of every trace of sinew.
2. Dice by hand into precise 5 mm cubes — never chop to mush.
3. Whisk citrus juices, colatura, oil and chilli to a dressing.
4. Five minutes before service, fold tuna with capers, spring onion, zest and dressing. Taste — colatura and capers carry salt.
5. Check seasoning with one cube on a spoon before committing.

## Plating & Service
A loose quenelle or low ring, pistachio and zest over, one thread of oil, warm crostini or GF flax crackers alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap tuna for amberjack or swordfish per the market. Nut-free: toasted breadcrumbs (then not GF) or sunflower seeds.
