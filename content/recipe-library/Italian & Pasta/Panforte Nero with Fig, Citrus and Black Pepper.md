# Panforte Nero with Fig, Citrus and Black Pepper

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Siena's medieval spice slab — nuts, candied citrus and figs bound in honey-sugar syrup with pepper in the shadows; keeps a month, slices like quiet luxury.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- whole almonds, toasted — 200 g
- hazelnuts, toasted, skinned — 100 g
- candied orange and citron, diced — 180 g
- dried figs, chopped — 100 g
- plain flour — 90 g
- cocoa powder — 20 g
- cinnamon — 4 g
- coriander seed, ground — 1 g
- black pepper — 1.5 g
- nutmeg and cloves — 0.5 g each
- honey — 150 g
- caster sugar — 150 g
- water — 30 ml
- icing sugar — 30 g
- rice paper (ostia) — 1 sheet (optional, traditional)

## Method
1. Line a 22 cm ring with baking paper (or rice paper on the base, per tradition).
2. Toss nuts, candied fruit and figs with the flour, cocoa and all spices.
3. Boil honey, sugar and water to 116–118°C (soft ball).
4. Pour the syrup over the dry mix; work fast with an oiled spatula — it seizes quickly.
5. Press firmly into the ring, 2 cm thick, with wet hands.
6. Bake at 150°C for 30–35 minutes — it will still look soft; it sets on cooling.
7. Cool completely; unmould; snow heavily with icing sugar.

## Plating & Service
Thin wedges with espresso, vin santo or the cheese board — it is confection, spice cabinet and history in one slice.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: replace flour with rice flour. Wrapped in paper it keeps 4+ weeks — the ideal make-ahead for a long charter season.
