# Beef Carpaccio with Rocket, Parmesan and Lemon Emulsion

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Harry's Bar idea with a cleaner finish — beef pounded to translucency under a whipped lemon emulsion instead of heavy mayonnaise.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- beef fillet, centre-cut, trimmed — 350 g
- rocket — 50 g
- Parmigiano Reggiano, shaved — 40 g
- egg yolk — 1
- Dijon mustard — 3 g
- lemon juice — 20 ml
- extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml
- cold water — 15 ml
- capers, fried (optional) — 15 g
- flaky salt and black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Firm the fillet in the freezer 45 minutes; slice 3–4 mm rounds.
2. Pound each slice between oiled film to translucent 1 mm sheets.
3. Lay directly onto chilled plates in a single overlapping layer; film and refrigerate until service (max 2 hours).
4. Emulsion: whisk yolk, mustard and lemon; stream in oil; loosen with cold water to a drizzling cream; season.
5. At the pass: salt and pepper the meat, streak the emulsion, dress rocket with a drop of oil.

## Plating & Service
Meat covering the whole plate, emulsion in open zigzags, rocket in a loose centre pile, Parmesan shaved thin as paper, fried capers if using.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Sear-and-shave (30-second crust, then chill and slice) suits guests wary of raw. Tuna or beetroot carpaccio follow the same architecture.
