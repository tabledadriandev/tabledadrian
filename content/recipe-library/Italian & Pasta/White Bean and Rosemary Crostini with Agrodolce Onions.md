# White Bean and Rosemary Crostini with Agrodolce Onions

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Cannellini whipped with rosemary oil to a silk mousse, topped with sweet-sour onions — the vegan crostino that outcompetes the liver version at aperitivo hour.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- cooked cannellini beans — 400 g (or 2 tins, drained)
- extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml
- rosemary — 4 g
- garlic — 1 clove
- lemon juice — 20 ml
- red onions, sliced — 300 g
- red wine vinegar — 40 ml
- caster sugar — 25 g
- baguette or country bread, 1 cm slices — 18
- fine salt and black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Warm 60 ml oil with rosemary and the bruised garlic over the lowest heat 8 minutes; strain and cool.
2. Onions: soften in 20 ml oil 10 minutes; add vinegar and sugar; cook 10 minutes more to a glossy, jammy agrodolce.
3. Blitz beans with the rosemary oil, lemon juice, salt and 30–50 ml warm water until completely smooth and mousse-like.
4. Toast the bread brushed with remaining oil at 200°C to golden edges.
5. Assemble at the last minute: a thick swipe of bean cream, a curl of onions.

## Plating & Service
On a long board, rosemary flowers or fried needles over, black pepper — serves with anything cold and sparkling.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The bean cream doubles as a base for seared prawns or grilled radicchio. GF bread or polenta crisps work perfectly.
