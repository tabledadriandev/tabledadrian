# Tortellini in Capon Brodo

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Thumb-nail tortellini folded around a mortadella-prosciutto farce, served in a crystal-clear capon broth — pure Bologna discipline for a first course.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- 00 flour — 300 g
- whole eggs — 3 (165 g)
- pork loin, diced — 150 g
- mortadella — 100 g
- prosciutto di Parma — 80 g
- Parmigiano Reggiano, grated — 100 g
- whole egg for farce — 1
- nutmeg, grated — 1 g
- capon (or free-range chicken) — 1.2 kg pieces
- beef shin — 400 g
- onion, halved and charred — 1
- carrot — 1
- celery stalk — 1
- fine salt — to taste

## Method
1. Broth: cover capon, beef shin and vegetables with 3 L cold water. Bring to 95°C, never boiling; skim relentlessly. Hold 4 hours, strain through muslin, season lightly.
2. Farce: sear pork loin dice in a dry pan until golden and just cooked. Cool.
3. Blitz pork, mortadella and prosciutto to a fine paste. Beat in Parmigiano, egg and nutmeg. Rest overnight if possible.
4. Make egg dough (flour + eggs), knead 10 minutes, rest 1 hour wrapped.
5. Roll to 0.6 mm — you should read print through it. Cut 3.5 cm squares.
6. Pipe 2 g farce per square. Fold into triangles, press to seal, then wrap around a fingertip and pinch the points together.
7. Simmer tortellini directly in the broth 2–3 minutes until they float and the pasta is tender at the fold.
8. Taste broth, adjust salt.

## Plating & Service
Serve 12–14 tortellini per person in wide shallow bowls with 150 ml steaming broth; Parmigiano offered tableside only.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Faster: use good chicken stock clarified with an egg-white raft. The farce also fills cappelletti for a rustic look.
