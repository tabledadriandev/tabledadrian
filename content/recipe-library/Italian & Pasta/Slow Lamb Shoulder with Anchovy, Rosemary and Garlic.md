# Slow Lamb Shoulder with Anchovy, Rosemary and Garlic

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Anchovy dissolving into lamb fat is old Piedmont wisdom — larded shoulder roasted low until it shreds, the anchovy leaving only depth, never fish.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- lamb shoulder, bone-in — 1.8 kg
- anchovy fillets — 10
- garlic cloves, slivered — 6
- rosemary, in small tufts — 8 g
- extra virgin olive oil — 40 ml
- dry white wine — 250 ml
- chicken or lamb stock — 300 ml
- black pepper — 3 g
- fine salt — 12 g
- lemon — half, juiced

## Method
1. Pierce the shoulder 20 times; into each slit push a fold of anchovy, a garlic sliver and a rosemary tuft.
2. Rub with oil, salt and pepper; rest 1 hour (or overnight uncovered in the fridge).
3. Roast at 220°C for 20 minutes to colour.
4. Add wine and stock to the tray; cover tightly with foil; drop to 140°C for 4 hours, basting twice.
5. Uncover; 20 minutes more to re-crisp; the bone should pull out clean.
6. Skim the tray juices; sharpen with lemon; season.

## Plating & Service
Pull into generous shards at the table, juices over — with white beans, roast potatoes in the lamb fat, or a sharp leaf salad.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Anchovy is undetectable as fish — disclose for allergies only. Leftovers become the pappardelle ragù of the following day.
