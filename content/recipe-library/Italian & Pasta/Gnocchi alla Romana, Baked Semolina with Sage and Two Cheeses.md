# Gnocchi alla Romana, Baked Semolina with Sage and Two Cheeses

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rome's other gnocchi — semolina discs shingled like roof tiles, drenched in butter and baked until the edges crisp; comfort you can prep entirely ahead.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- coarse semolina — 250 g
- whole milk — 1 L
- butter — 100 g
- egg yolks — 2
- Parmigiano Reggiano — 100 g
- pecorino romano — 30 g
- sage leaves — 8
- nutmeg — 0.3 g
- fine salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Bring milk to a simmer with salt and nutmeg; rain in semolina, whisking hard.
2. Cook 8–10 minutes over low heat, stirring with a spatula, to a thick mass pulling from the pan.
3. Off heat, beat in yolks, 40 g butter and 60 g Parmigiano.
4. Spread 1.5 cm thick on an oiled tray; chill 1 hour until fully set.
5. Cut 6 cm discs. Shingle them overlapping in a buttered gratin dish.
6. Pour over the remaining butter melted with sage; top with both remaining cheeses.
7. Bake at 200°C fan 20–25 minutes until burnished; rest 5 minutes.

## Plating & Service
Serve from the gratin dish, crisp sage over — golden tiles, molten seams.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Offcuts re-melt into the next batch. Truffle butter between layers for gala nights; GF works with fine polenta at the same weight.
