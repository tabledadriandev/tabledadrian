# Porchetta-Spiced Pork Belly with Fennel and Salsa Verde

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rolled belly cured overnight with the porchetta trinity — wild fennel, garlic, rosemary — roasted low then blasted for glass crackling; carves like a galantine.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- pork belly, skin on, boneless — 2 kg
- fennel seeds, toasted, ground — 8 g
- wild fennel or fronds, chopped — 15 g
- rosemary, chopped — 6 g
- garlic, minced — 15 g
- black pepper — 4 g
- chilli flakes — 1 g
- lemon zest — of 1 lemon
- fine salt — 24 g (plus for skin)
- white wine — 150 ml
- parsley-caper salsa verde (parsley 25 g, capers 20 g, anchovy 2, mustard 5 g, vinegar 15 ml, oil 80 ml)

## Method
1. Score the flesh side in a crosshatch; rub with salt, fennel seed and fronds, rosemary, garlic, pepper, chilli and zest — flesh side only.
2. Roll tightly along the long side; tie at 3 cm intervals. Dry the skin; salt it; refrigerate uncovered overnight.
3. Roast at 140°C on a rack over a tray with the wine, 3 hours, to core 88–90°C.
4. Rest 20 minutes, then blast at 240°C (or under a fierce grill) 15–20 minutes, rotating, until the skin blisters to glass.
5. Rest 20 minutes more before carving.
6. Blitz or hand-chop the salsa verde ingredients loose and bright.

## Plating & Service
Carve 2 cm wheels with a serrated knife, crackling intact, salsa verde alongside — with roast fennel wedges or a bitter-leaf salad.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Day-two porchetta in focaccia with salsa verde is arguably the point of the exercise. Core temperature discipline beats every timing rule.
