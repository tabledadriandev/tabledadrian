# Zuppa Inglese with Alchermes and Vanilla Custard

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Emilia's crimson trifle — sponge drenched in spiced Alchermes liqueur, layered with proper crema pasticcera and chocolate custard; kitsch on paper, sublime in the glass.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- savoiardi or sponge — 250 g
- whole milk — 600 ml
- egg yolks — 6
- caster sugar — 140 g
- cornstarch — 45 g
- vanilla pod — 1
- dark chocolate 70% — 80 g
- Alchermes liqueur — 100 ml (or 60 ml rum + 40 ml grenadine-rose syrup for the colour)
- water — 60 ml
- dark chocolate shavings — 15 g
- fine salt — 1 pinch

## Method
1. Crema: whisk yolks, sugar, cornstarch and salt; temper with milk heated with the vanilla; cook, whisking, to a full boil for 60 seconds; split in half.
2. Beat the chocolate into one half. Press film onto both; cool fast.
3. Dilute the Alchermes with the water.
4. Dip savoiardi one second per side in the crimson bath.
5. Layer in glasses or a dish: biscuits, vanilla crema, biscuits, chocolate crema — two full rounds.
6. Chill 6 hours; finish with chocolate shavings.

## Plating & Service
Glass service shows the red-gold-brown strata — build the top layer in visible waves, shavings at the pass.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Alchermes is the soul (cochineal, rose, spice) — Italian delis on the coast stock it. Alcohol-free: rose syrup, orange juice and a drop of red colour.
