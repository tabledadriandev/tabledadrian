# Tagliatelle with Eight-Hour Beef and Pork Ragù

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A slow, milk-mounted Emilia-Romagna style ragù that clings to hand-cut egg tagliatelle — the dish that defines a Bologna-inspired pasta course on charter.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- 00 flour — 400 g
- whole eggs — 4 (about 220 g)
- beef chuck, hand-chopped 5 mm — 400 g
- pork shoulder, minced — 300 g
- pancetta, fine dice — 100 g
- onion, brunoise — 120 g
- carrot, brunoise — 100 g
- celery, brunoise — 80 g
- tomato passata — 200 ml
- dry white wine — 150 ml
- whole milk — 250 ml
- beef stock — 500 ml
- butter — 40 g
- Parmigiano Reggiano, grated — 80 g
- fine salt and black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Make the dough: mound flour, add eggs, knead 10 minutes to a smooth, firm ball. Wrap and rest 1 hour at room temperature.
2. Render pancetta in a heavy casserole over medium heat, 5 minutes. Add soffritto vegetables and sweat 12 minutes until translucent, never browned.
3. Raise heat, add beef and pork; cook until the liquid evaporates and the meat sizzles and catches lightly, about 15 minutes.
4. Deglaze with white wine, reduce to dry. Add passata and 300 ml stock. Bring to a bare simmer.
5. Braise uncovered at the gentlest blip (90–95°C surface) for 7 hours, adding stock as needed; the fat should sit in glossy pearls on top.
6. In the final hour, add milk in three additions, letting each absorb. Season.
7. Roll pasta to 0.8 mm (setting 7 of 9), dust with semolina, roll into loose cylinders and cut 8 mm ribbons. Nest and dry 20 minutes.
8. Boil tagliatelle in 1% salt water 90 seconds. Toss with ragù, butter and a splash of pasta water off the heat until emulsified.

## Plating & Service
Twirl into a low nest in warmed bowls, spoon extra ragù at the base, finish with Parmigiano and one turn of pepper.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: use a 400 g GF fresh-pasta blend with 4 eggs + 10 g xanthan-free psyllium. Lighter: swap milk for stock and finish with less butter.
