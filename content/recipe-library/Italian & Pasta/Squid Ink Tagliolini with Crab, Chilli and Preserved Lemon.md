# Squid Ink Tagliolini with Crab, Chilli and Preserved Lemon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Jet-black hand-cut ribbons against sweet white crab — a monochrome plate with a bright preserved-lemon pulse, made for evening service on deck.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- 00 flour — 300 g
- whole eggs — 2
- egg yolks — 2
- squid ink — 12 g
- white crab meat, picked — 250 g
- extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- garlic, minced — 5 g
- red chilli, brunoise — 6 g
- preserved lemon rind, brunoise — 15 g
- dry white wine — 50 ml
- fish or shellfish stock — 100 ml
- chives, sliced — 4 g
- fine salt — to taste

## Method
1. Whisk ink into eggs and yolks; knead into flour to a black, satin dough. Rest 1 hour.
2. Roll to 0.7 mm, cut 3 mm tagliolini by hand; nest on semolina.
3. Warm oil with garlic and chilli 2 minutes without colouring.
4. Add wine, reduce; add stock and bring to a lively simmer.
5. Boil tagliolini 60–75 seconds; drag into the pan with 100 ml pasta water.
6. Toss over high heat 1 minute to a glossy emulsion; the black strands should gleam.
7. Fold in crab and preserved lemon off the heat — just warmed through.

## Plating & Service
A black nest, crab tucked into the folds, chives, one thread of your best oil.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Egg-allergy table: use semolina-water dough with 10 g ink. Bottarga grated over instead of crab makes a faster variant.
