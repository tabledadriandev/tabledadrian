# Vitello Tonnato with Crisp Capers and Anchovy Mayo

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Piedmont's rosé-pink veal under a tuna-anchovy emulsion made like a proper mayonnaise — silky, saline, and better after a night in the fridge.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- veal eye of round (girello) — 800 g
- dry white wine — 200 ml
- carrot, celery, onion — 200 g total
- bay leaf and cloves — 1 + 2
- tinned tuna in olive oil, drained — 160 g
- anchovy fillets — 4
- egg yolks — 2
- Dijon mustard — 5 g
- sunflower oil — 150 ml
- extra virgin olive oil — 30 ml
- lemon juice — 20 ml
- capers — 30 g (half fried crisp)
- fine salt and white pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Poach the veal: submerge in wine, 1 L water, vegetables and aromatics; hold at 80°C until the core reaches 58°C (about 45–60 minutes). Cool completely in its liquid overnight.
2. Mayonnaise: whisk yolks with mustard and lemon juice; stream in sunflower oil, then olive oil, to a thick emulsion.
3. Blitz tuna, anchovies and 50 ml of the cold poaching liquid; fold into the mayonnaise. Season; loosen to a coating cream.
4. Fry half the capers in 1 cm of hot oil (170°C) for 60 seconds until they blossom; drain.
5. Slice the cold veal 2 mm thin across the grain.

## Plating & Service
Shingle slices barely overlapping across a chilled plate, nappe with sauce leaving a pink border visible, scatter both capers, one turn of white pepper.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Sous-vide 58°C/3h makes it foolproof. Turkey breast at 62°C is a lighter, halal-friendly stand-in with the same sauce.
