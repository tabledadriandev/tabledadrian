# Piadina Romagnola with Squacquerone, Rocket and Parma Ham

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Romagna flatbread cooked on a dry pan in minutes — folded warm around melting squacquerone, peppery rocket and prosciutto; beach food with a pedigree.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- plain flour — 400 g
- lard or soft butter — 70 g (or 60 ml olive oil)
- whole milk or water — 170 ml
- baking soda — 2 g
- fine salt — 8 g
- squacquerone or stracchino — 250 g
- prosciutto di Parma — 120 g
- rocket — 60 g
- extra virgin olive oil — 10 ml
- black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Rub lard into flour, salt and soda; knead with milk 5 minutes to a smooth dough. Rest 30 minutes.
2. Divide into 4; roll each to a 24 cm round, 2–3 mm thick.
3. Heat a dry cast-iron pan or testo to medium-high.
4. Cook each piadina 90 seconds per side, pricking bubbles, until leopard-spotted but still pliable.
5. Immediately spread with squacquerone, lay ham and rocket dressed with oil and pepper, fold in half.

## Plating & Service
Halved on a board, cut faces showing the cream and green — serve while the bread still steams.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Grilled vegetables and stracchino for the vegetarian fold. The dough holds 2 days chilled; cook to order in 3 minutes flat.
