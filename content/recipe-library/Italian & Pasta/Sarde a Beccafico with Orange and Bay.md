# Sarde a Beccafico with Orange and Bay

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Butterflied sardines rolled around a pine-nut, currant and citrus crumb — Palermo's poor-man's songbird, all Sicilian sweet-savoury tension.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- fresh sardines, butterflied, heads off — 18 (about 700 g)
- dry breadcrumbs — 120 g
- pine nuts, toasted — 30 g
- currants or sultanas, soaked — 40 g
- anchovy fillets, minced — 3
- orange juice and zest — of 1 orange
- lemon juice — 20 ml
- caster sugar — 10 g
- olive oil — 70 ml
- bay leaves — 12
- parsley — 8 g
- fine salt and black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Toast breadcrumbs in 30 ml oil until golden; cool.
2. Mix crumbs with pine nuts, drained currants, anchovy, parsley, half the orange zest, salt and pepper; moisten with 20 ml oil.
3. Lay sardines skin-down; spread 15 g filling on each; roll from head end, tail pointing up like the bird they mimic.
4. Pack rolls snugly in an oiled dish, a bay leaf between each.
5. Whisk orange juice, lemon juice, sugar and remaining oil; pour over.
6. Bake at 180°C for 12–14 minutes; rest 10 minutes.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm or at room temperature, tails skyward, pan juices spooned over, remaining zest grated across.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF crumbs work perfectly. The same farce stuffs butterflied fresh anchovies for a daintier canapé version.
