# Squid Ink Risotto with Seared Cuttlefish and Gremolata

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Venetian nero done Riviera-style — jet-black rice, ivory ribbons of flash-seared cuttlefish, and a lemon gremolata that cuts the brine.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- carnaroli rice — 320 g
- cuttlefish with ink sacs (or squid + 12 g ink) — 500 g
- fish stock, hot — 1.2 L
- shallot — 50 g
- garlic — 2 cloves
- olive oil — 70 ml
- dry white wine — 100 ml
- tomato paste — 15 g
- lemon zest — of 1 lemon
- parsley, fine — 8 g
- chilli — 0.5 g
- fine salt — to taste

## Method
1. Clean cuttlefish; score the bodies and cut into 4 cm pieces; dice the wings and tentacles small.
2. Sweat shallot and 1 garlic clove in 40 ml oil; add the diced trim, 2 minutes.
3. Toast rice 2 minutes; deglaze with wine; stir in ink and tomato paste.
4. Cook with hot stock, stirring, 15–16 minutes to glossy black al dente.
5. Meanwhile sear the scored pieces 60 seconds per side in a screaming pan with 20 ml oil; salt.
6. Gremolata: parsley, lemon zest, minced remaining garlic, chilli, 10 ml oil.
7. Mantecare the rice with the last oil; check salt (ink is saline).

## Plating & Service
Black mirror of rice, ivory curls arranged on top, gremolata scattered — the contrast is the plate.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Butter-and-parmesan mantecatura is Venetian orthodoxy if dairy is welcome. Warn guests about dramatic smiles.
