# Braised Cavolo Nero with Garlic and Lemon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Tuscan black kale cooked twice — blanched, then braised in garlic oil until silky with crisp edges — the darkest, deepest green on the menu.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- cavolo nero — 600 g
- extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- garlic, sliced — 4 cloves
- chilli flakes — 0.5 g
- vegetable stock or water — 100 ml
- lemon juice and zest — of half a lemon
- flaky salt — to taste

## Method
1. Strip leaves from the ribs; tear into wide ribbons.
2. Blanch 3 minutes in boiling salted water; drain, pressing lightly.
3. Warm the oil with garlic and chilli until the garlic gilds.
4. Add the kale; toss to coat; add stock.
5. Braise uncovered 10–12 minutes until the liquid is gone and edges catch and crisp.
6. Finish with lemon juice, zest and flaky salt.

## Plating & Service
A dark, glossy tangle beside anything from bistecca to white fish — or under a fried egg for crew breakfast.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> A spoon of the braise chopped fine becomes ribollita's soul tomorrow. Anchovy melted in the oil is the non-vegan upgrade.
