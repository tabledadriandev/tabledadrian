# Fried Courgette Flowers with Ricotta, Lemon and Anchovy

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Flowers stuffed with lemon ricotta (one hidden anchovy for those who dare), in a cold sparkling-water batter that fries to glass.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- courgette flowers, stamens removed — 12
- ricotta, drained — 200 g
- Parmigiano Reggiano — 40 g
- lemon zest — of 1 lemon
- anchovy fillets, halved — 6 (optional)
- mint, chopped — 3 g
- plain flour — 100 g
- cornstarch — 40 g
- ice-cold sparkling water — 200 ml
- egg yolk — 1
- sunflower oil — 1.5 L
- fine salt — to taste

## Method
1. Beat ricotta with Parmigiano, zest, mint and a little salt; pipe into a bag.
2. Open each flower gently; slip in half an anchovy if using, then pipe 15 g ricotta; twist the petal tips closed.
3. Whisk yolk into the sparkling water, then loosely into the flours — lumps are fine, cold is critical.
4. Heat oil to 175°C.
5. Dip each flower, letting excess run off; fry 2–3 minutes, turning once, to pale gold.
6. Drain on a rack; salt immediately.

## Plating & Service
Three per plate on a smear of nothing — just lemon wedge and flaky salt; the batter should shatter audibly.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: cashew ricotta and no yolk in the batter. The batter also loves sage leaves and thin lemon slices for a fritto vegetale.
