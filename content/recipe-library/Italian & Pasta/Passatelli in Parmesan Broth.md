# Passatelli in Parmesan Broth

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rustic Romagna comfort — bread-and-Parmigiano strands extruded straight into hot broth; ready in minutes yet tastes of nonna's kitchen.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- fine dry breadcrumbs — 150 g
- Parmigiano Reggiano, grated — 150 g
- whole eggs — 3
- lemon zest — of 1 lemon
- nutmeg, grated — 0.5 g
- plain flour — 15 g
- vegetable or chicken broth, excellent quality — 1.4 L
- flat-leaf parsley, chopped — 5 g
- fine salt — to taste

## Method
1. Beat eggs, then work in breadcrumbs, Parmigiano, flour, zest and nutmeg to a firm, slightly tacky dough. Rest 15 minutes.
2. Bring broth to a gentle simmer and season precisely.
3. Press dough through a potato ricer with large holes (5 mm) directly over the broth, cutting 5–6 cm strands with a knife.
4. Poach 2–3 minutes until the passatelli float and swell.
5. Rest off the heat 1 minute so they firm slightly.

## Plating & Service
Ladle into warm bowls with plenty of broth, shower with parsley and a final grating of Parmigiano.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> For a Riviera spin, poach in a light fish broth and finish with lemon oil. GF: use GF breadcrumbs and 10 g rice flour.
