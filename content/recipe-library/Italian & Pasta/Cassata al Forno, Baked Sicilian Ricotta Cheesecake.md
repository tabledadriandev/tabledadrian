# Cassata al Forno, Baked Sicilian Ricotta Cheesecake

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The baked, sober sister of the baroque cassata — sheep ricotta and chocolate chips inside a short lemon pastry; humble outside, silk within.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- plain flour — 300 g
- butter, cold — 150 g
- caster sugar — 210 g (90 g pastry, 120 g filling)
- egg yolks — 3 (pastry) plus whole eggs — 2 (filling)
- lemon zest — of 2 lemons
- sheep or best cow ricotta, drained 24 h — 800 g
- dark chocolate chips — 80 g
- candied orange, fine dice — 50 g
- cinnamon — 1 g
- icing sugar — for dusting
- fine salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Pastry: rub butter into flour, 90 g sugar and salt; bind with yolks and half the zest; rest 1 hour chilled.
2. Sieve the drained ricotta twice with 120 g sugar until silky; beat in the whole eggs, remaining zest, cinnamon; fold in chocolate and candied orange.
3. Line a 24 cm tin with two-thirds of the pastry rolled to 3 mm.
4. Fill; cover with the remaining pastry as a lid, sealing the rim; vent the centre.
5. Bake at 170°C for 55–60 minutes until pale gold and just set at the middle.
6. Cool completely in the tin, then chill 4 hours — it slices clean only cold.

## Plating & Service
Room-temperature wedges under icing sugar — with an espresso it needs no further argument.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dry ricotta is the whole game — hang it a full day. Skip candied fruit for purists; add 30 g pistachio for Palermo flair.
