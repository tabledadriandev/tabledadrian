# Brasato al Barolo with Creamy Polenta

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Beef braised in an entire bottle of Nebbiolo until the sauce turns to garnet lacquer — Piedmont's Sunday centrepiece, better made the day before.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- beef chuck or cheek, whole piece — 1.5 kg
- Barolo or Nebbiolo — 750 ml
- onion, carrot, celery, rough cut — 350 g total
- garlic — 3 cloves
- juniper berries — 5
- cloves — 2
- bay leaves — 2
- cinnamon — 1 small piece (2 g)
- tomato paste — 30 g
- olive oil — 50 ml
- butter — 30 g
- beef stock — 300 ml
- coarse polenta — 250 g
- whole milk and water — 500 ml each
- Parmigiano Reggiano — 60 g
- fine salt and black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Marinate the beef overnight in the wine with vegetables and spices. Drain; dry the meat completely; reserve everything.
2. Sear the beef in oil on all faces to a hard mahogany crust, 12 minutes total.
3. Remove; brown the drained vegetables 8 minutes; add tomato paste, 2 minutes.
4. Return beef with the marinade wine and stock; bring to a shudder.
5. Braise lidded at 150°C for 3–3.5 hours, turning hourly, until a fork twists in easily.
6. Rest the meat; strain and reduce the sauce to a glossy coat; mount with butter; season.
7. Polenta: rain into simmering milk-water with salt; cook 40 minutes, stirring; finish with Parmigiano.

## Plating & Service
Slice 2 cm thick across the grain, shingle over soft polenta, lacquer with sauce — one juniper berry as a warning shot.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chill overnight before slicing for perfect portions, then reheat in the sauce. Any serious Nebbiolo works; the dish forgives no thin wine.
