# Peperonata with Capers and Basil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Peppers stewed to silk in olive oil with onion, tomato and a caper lift — the do-everything Italian condiment: side, crostini topping, or bed for fish.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- mixed red and yellow peppers, 2 cm strips — 800 g
- red onion, sliced — 200 g
- datterini or tinned tomatoes — 250 g
- garlic — 2 cloves
- extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- red wine vinegar — 20 ml
- capers, rinsed — 25 g
- caster sugar — 5 g
- basil — 10 g
- fine salt and black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Soften the onion in oil with a pinch of salt, 8 minutes, without colour.
2. Add garlic, then the peppers; stew over medium heat, lidded, 15 minutes, stirring now and then.
3. Add tomatoes, sugar and vinegar; cook uncovered 15 minutes until thick, glossy and completely tender.
4. Fold in capers; season.
5. Rest 20 minutes minimum; tear in basil at the end.

## Plating & Service
Warm or room temperature, pooled beside grilled swordfish, sausage or eggs — or over crostini with ricotta.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> It keeps a week under oil and improves daily. A pinch of chilli and oregano leans it Calabrian; saffron leans it toward Liguria's condiglione.
