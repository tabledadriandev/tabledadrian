# Vanilla Panna Cotta with Macerated Berries, Minimal Gelatine

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The wobble test — just enough gelatine that it barely stands, collapsing into cream at the first spoon; Piedmont's dessert judged entirely on restraint.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- double cream — 500 ml
- whole milk — 150 ml
- caster sugar — 80 g
- vanilla pod, split and scraped — 1
- gold gelatine leaves — 6 g (about 3.5 leaves)
- mixed berries — 250 g
- icing sugar — 20 g
- lemon juice — 10 ml
- mint — a few tips

## Method
1. Soak gelatine in iced water 5 minutes.
2. Warm cream, milk, sugar and vanilla (pod and seeds) to 80°C — never boil; infuse 10 minutes off the heat.
3. Remove the pod; squeeze the gelatine and dissolve it in the warm cream.
4. Cool the base over ice, stirring, until it just thickens (this stops the vanilla sinking).
5. Pour into 6 lightly-oiled 120 ml moulds or serving glasses; chill 5 hours.
6. Macerate the berries with icing sugar and lemon juice 30 minutes.

## Plating & Service
Dip moulds 3 seconds in hot water and unmould onto chilled plates — it should shiver visibly — berries and juices around, one mint tip.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> In-glass service is safer under way and needs only 5 g gelatine. Infuse the cream with espresso, saffron or fig leaf as the season argues.
