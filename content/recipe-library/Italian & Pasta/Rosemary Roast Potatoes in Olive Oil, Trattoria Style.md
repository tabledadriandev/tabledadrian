# Rosemary Roast Potatoes in Olive Oil, Trattoria Style

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Par-steamed, rough-shaken potatoes roasted in generous olive oil with whole garlic and rosemary — the crisp-creamy standard every secondo deserves.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- yellow waxy-floury potatoes (agata/marabel), 4 cm chunks — 1.2 kg
- extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml
- garlic cloves, skin on, crushed — 6
- rosemary sprigs — 3
- flaky salt — 6 g
- black pepper — to taste
- lemon — half (optional, to finish)

## Method
1. Steam or parboil the chunks 8 minutes; drain and shake the pan hard to rough the edges; steam-dry 5 minutes.
2. Heat the oil in the roasting tray at 210°C for 5 minutes.
3. Turn the potatoes into the hot oil carefully; coat every face; season lightly.
4. Roast 25 minutes untouched; turn once; add garlic and rosemary.
5. Roast 15–20 minutes more to deep gold and audible crunch.
6. Drain a moment on a rack; flaky salt, pepper, and a small squeeze of lemon if serving with fish.

## Plating & Service
A rough golden pile, the confit garlic cloves scattered for those who know, rosemary crisped on top.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Duck fat instead of oil beside the brasato; oregano and lemon instead of rosemary beside grilled fish. Never crowd the tray.
