# Seared Tuna with Sicilian Agrodolce Onions

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Tonno con cipollata — thick tuna kept rare against a mountain of slow onions collapsed into sweet-sour vinegar; the island's proof that two components suffice.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- tuna loin steaks, 3 cm thick — 4 × 170 g
- white or red onions, sliced 5 mm — 600 g
- extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- red wine vinegar — 60 ml
- caster sugar — 25 g
- bay leaves — 2
- dried oregano — 1 g
- mint leaves — 4 g
- fine salt and black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Sweat the onions with bay and a pinch of salt in 50 ml oil over low heat, lidded, 15 minutes until totally soft, no colour.
2. Add sugar and vinegar; cook uncovered 8 minutes to a glossy, spoonable agrodolce. Rest off the heat.
3. Dry the tuna; season; oil lightly.
4. Sear on a violent plancha 60–90 seconds per side — deep crust, rare 40–45°C centre.
5. Rest 3 minutes; slice each steak into thick batons against the grain.

## Plating & Service
A warm bed of agrodolce onions, tuna batons cut-face up showing the ruby centre, oregano, torn mint, one thread of oil.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The onions improve for 3 days and love grilled mackerel or pork equally. For guests avoiding rare fish, cook to 54°C and slice thinner.
