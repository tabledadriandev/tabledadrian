# Gluten-Free Fresh Tagliatelle with Lemon Butter and Basil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A rice-buckwheat-psyllium dough that rolls, folds and bites like true egg pasta — the recipe that lets coeliac guests join the masterclass table.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- fine rice flour — 180 g
- buckwheat flour — 60 g
- tapioca starch — 60 g
- psyllium husk powder — 8 g
- xanthan gum — 3 g
- whole eggs — 3
- egg yolk — 1
- olive oil — 10 ml
- fine salt — 3 g
- butter — 80 g
- lemon zest and juice — of 1 lemon
- basil — 10 g
- Parmigiano Reggiano — 50 g

## Method
1. Whisk dry ingredients thoroughly. Add eggs, yolk and oil; knead 5 minutes to a clay-like, slightly tacky dough. Rest 30 minutes wrapped — psyllium needs time to hydrate.
2. Roll between parchment sheets to 1 mm (GF dough tears on open rollers when thinner).
3. Dust with rice flour, roll into a loose cylinder, cut 8 mm ribbons and unfurl gently.
4. Boil in well-salted water 90 seconds — GF pasta overcooks fast.
5. Meanwhile melt butter with 40 ml pasta water to an emulsion; add lemon juice and zest.
6. Transfer tagliatelle with a spider straight into the butter; toss delicately with cheese and torn basil.

## Plating & Service
Loose nests, extra zest, basil oil dots if you have it.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The dough also makes sturdy GF lasagne sheets. Dairy-free: finish with olive oil, lemon and toasted GF crumbs instead.
