# Sea Bass Braised with Fennel, Olives and Vermentino

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> One pan, one bottle open — fennel melted in olive oil, fillets braised on top with taggiasca olives and a glass of the same Vermentino you're pouring.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- sea bass fillets, skin on — 4 × 160 g
- fennel bulbs, 1 cm wedges — 2 (about 500 g)
- taggiasca or niçoise olives — 60 g
- Vermentino — 150 ml
- extra virgin olive oil — 60 ml
- garlic, sliced — 2 cloves
- fennel seeds — 2 g
- orange zest — of half an orange
- fish or vegetable stock — 100 ml
- fennel fronds — 5 g
- fine salt and black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Brown the fennel wedges cut-side down in 40 ml oil, 5 minutes, to caramel edges.
2. Add garlic and fennel seeds, 1 minute; deglaze with the wine; reduce by half.
3. Add stock, olives and orange zest; lid on; braise 12 minutes until the fennel yields.
4. Season the fillets; lay skin-up on the fennel; lid ajar.
5. Braise 6–8 minutes to 52°C core; rest 2 minutes off the heat.
6. Finish with fronds and the remaining raw oil.

## Plating & Service
Fennel bedded in shallow bowls, fillet on top, olives and juices around — with nothing but bread and the rest of the bottle.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Daurade, cod or halibut braise identically. Add a pinch of saffron to the stock for a bouillabaisse-adjacent whisper.
