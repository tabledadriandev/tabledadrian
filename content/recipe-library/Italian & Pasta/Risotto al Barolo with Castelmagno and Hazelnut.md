# Risotto al Barolo with Castelmagno and Hazelnut

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Piedmont in one bowl — rice cooked in reduced Barolo until garnet-dark, sharpened with Castelmagno and scattered with Langhe hazelnuts.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- carnaroli rice — 320 g
- Barolo or Nebbiolo — 400 ml
- vegetable or light beef stock, hot — 1 L
- shallot, fine — 50 g
- butter — 80 g
- Castelmagno or aged toma, crumbled — 70 g
- Parmigiano Reggiano — 40 g
- hazelnuts, toasted, crushed — 30 g
- fine salt and black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Reduce 300 ml of the wine by half in a small pan; keep warm.
2. Sweat shallot in 30 g butter; toast the rice 2 minutes.
3. Deglaze with the remaining 100 ml raw wine; absorb.
4. Cook with alternating ladles of stock and reduced wine, stirring, 15–16 minutes to a deep ruby.
5. Off the heat, rest 1 minute, then mantecare with the remaining cold butter, Castelmagno and Parmigiano.
6. Season; loosen to a slow wave with the last of the stock.

## Plating & Service
Flat plates, hazelnuts scattered, a few crumbs of Castelmagno melting on the surface, coarse pepper.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Any structured Nebbiolo works — never cooking-grade wine, the dish is the wine. Vegan: mantecare with 40 g cashew butter and miso.
