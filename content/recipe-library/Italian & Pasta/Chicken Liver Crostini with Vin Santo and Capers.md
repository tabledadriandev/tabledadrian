# Chicken Liver Crostini with Vin Santo and Capers

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The obligatory opening of every Tuscan table — warm, coarse liver paste sharpened with capers and anchovy, still blushing inside, on oil-rubbed crostini.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- chicken livers, cleaned — 400 g
- shallot, sliced — 80 g
- butter — 50 g
- olive oil — 30 ml
- Vin Santo or dry Marsala — 80 ml
- capers, rinsed — 30 g
- anchovy fillets — 3
- sage leaves — 4
- chicken stock — 60 ml
- baguette or Tuscan bread, 1 cm slices — 18
- fine salt and black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Soften shallot in oil and half the butter with the sage, 5 minutes.
2. Raise the heat; add the livers, seasoned; sear 3 minutes — they must stay rosy inside.
3. Deglaze with Vin Santo; reduce by half.
4. Add capers, anchovies and stock; simmer 3 minutes.
5. Pulse-chop or hand-chop to a coarse, rustic paste; mount with remaining butter. Adjust salt and pepper.
6. Toast bread slices brushed with oil at 200°C until gold at the edges.

## Plating & Service
Spread warm and generous, one caper on each, a drop of Vin Santo syrup if you're feeling baroque.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Hold the topping warm over a bain-marie — never re-boil or it greys. GF bread or polenta squares carry it just as well.
