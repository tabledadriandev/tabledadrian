# Pistachio Tiramisu with Bronte Cream and Salted Crumble

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sicily hijacks the Veneto — pistachio paste through the mascarpone, espresso kept for depth, and a salted pistachio crumble where the cocoa used to be.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- egg yolks — 5
- caster sugar — 110 g
- mascarpone — 450 g
- pure pistachio paste — 90 g
- double cream — 150 ml
- savoiardi — 280 g
- espresso, cooled — 300 ml
- amaretto or pistachio liqueur (optional) — 20 ml
- pistachios, chopped — 60 g
- butter — 25 g
- plain flour — 25 g
- brown sugar — 25 g
- flaky salt — 1 g

## Method
1. Crumble: rub butter, flour, brown sugar, 30 g pistachios and salt; bake at 170°C for 10–12 minutes; cool.
2. Sabayon the yolks and sugar over a bain-marie to 72°C; cool.
3. Fold in mascarpone beaten with the pistachio paste; then softly whipped cream.
4. Dip savoiardi fast in espresso (plus liqueur).
5. Layer biscuits and green cream twice in the dish; smooth.
6. Chill 6 hours.
7. Top with crumble and remaining pistachios only at service.

## Plating & Service
Squares or coupes, the crumble applied at the pass so it thunders, one pinch more flaky salt.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Buy paste that is 100% pistachio — sweetened spreads collapse the balance. Hazelnut paste turns it Piedmontese gianduia-style.
