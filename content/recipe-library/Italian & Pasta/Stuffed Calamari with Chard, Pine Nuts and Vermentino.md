# Stuffed Calamari with Chard, Pine Nuts and Vermentino

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Ligurian totani ripieni — squid tubes filled with their own chopped tentacles, chard and pine nuts, braised gently in white wine so they stay silk, never rubber.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- medium squid, cleaned, tubes + tentacles — 8 (about 900 g)
- Swiss chard, wilted, squeezed, chopped — 200 g
- dry breadcrumbs — 60 g
- pine nuts, toasted — 25 g
- garlic — 3 cloves
- parsley — 10 g
- anchovy fillets — 2
- egg — 1
- extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- Vermentino or dry white — 200 ml
- datterini tomatoes, halved — 200 g
- fine salt, black pepper, chilli — to taste

## Method
1. Chop the tentacles fine; sauté 2 minutes in 20 ml oil with 1 garlic clove and the anchovy.
2. Mix with chard, breadcrumbs, pine nuts, parsley, egg, salt and pepper to a moist farce.
3. Fill tubes two-thirds only (they shrink); close with a toothpick.
4. Brown the tubes in 30 ml oil 2 minutes; add remaining sliced garlic and chilli.
5. Add wine; reduce by half; add tomatoes and 100 ml water.
6. Braise lidded at a bare simmer 25 minutes, turning once — tender means either 3 minutes or 25; nothing between.
7. Rest 5 minutes; finish with parsley and raw oil.

## Plating & Service
Slice each tube into 3 thick wheels showing the green spiral, sauce beneath, pine nuts over.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: use GF crumbs. The same farce stuffs onions or tomatoes for a vegetarian pan alongside.
