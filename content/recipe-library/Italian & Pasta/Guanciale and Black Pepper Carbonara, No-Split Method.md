# Guanciale and Black Pepper Carbonara, No-Split Method

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Roman carbonara with a temperature-controlled sabayon finish — glossy, never scrambled, even when the pass is moving.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- spaghetti or fresh tonnarelli — 400 g
- guanciale, 1 cm lardons — 180 g
- egg yolks — 6
- whole egg — 1
- Pecorino Romano, grated — 90 g
- black pepper, coarsely ground — 5 g
- fine salt — for the water

## Method
1. Render guanciale from a cold pan over medium-low heat until glassy and golden, 8 minutes. Keep fat and lardons separate off the heat.
2. Whisk yolks, egg, pecorino and pepper to a thick paste.
3. Boil pasta in lightly salted water to al dente; reserve 300 ml water.
4. Whisk 100 ml hot pasta water into the egg paste to temper it.
5. Toss pasta in the guanciale pan (off heat) with the fat and a splash of water.
6. Add the egg cream and stir vigorously over the lowest flame — or just the pan's residual heat — until it thickens to 65°C and coats like custard.
7. Fold in lardons; adjust with water to a flowing gloss.

## Plating & Service
Deep bowls, extra pecorino and a violent crack of pepper over the top.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No guanciale? Pancetta works; smoked bacon changes the dish. Never cream, never garlic — offer a vegetarian version with fried shiitake 'lardons'.
