# Pansotti with Walnut Sauce and Preboggion Greens

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Liguria's pot-bellied triangles stuffed with wild-style greens and ricotta, napped in a milk-soaked walnut cream — a Riviera dish for your Riviera guests.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- 00 flour — 300 g
- whole eggs — 2
- dry white wine — 30 ml
- chard, spinach and rocket mix (for preboggion) — 350 g
- ricotta — 200 g
- Parmigiano Reggiano, grated — 70 g
- marjoram leaves — 2 g
- walnut halves — 150 g
- white bread, crustless — 30 g
- whole milk — 120 ml
- garlic — 3 g
- extra virgin olive oil — 40 ml
- fine salt and nutmeg — to taste

## Method
1. Blanch the walnuts 2 minutes; rub off any loose skins for a sweeter, paler sauce.
2. Blend walnuts with milk-soaked bread, garlic, 30 g Parmigiano, oil and salt to a coating cream. Loosen with more milk if needed.
3. Wilt greens 2 minutes, squeeze hard, chop fine. Beat with ricotta, remaining Parmigiano, marjoram, nutmeg, salt.
4. Knead flour, eggs and wine to a supple dough; rest 45 minutes.
5. Roll to 0.8 mm, cut 6 cm squares, fill with 8 g each, fold into fat triangles, sealing well.
6. Boil 3 minutes in salted water.
7. Warm the walnut sauce gently with a ladle of pasta water — never boil — and coat the pansotti.

## Plating & Service
Five per plate on a pool of sauce, cracked walnut, marjoram tips, one drop of oil.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: cashew-tofu filling and omit cheese from the sauce with 10 g white miso. The wine in the dough is traditional — it keeps it tender.
