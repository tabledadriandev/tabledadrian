# Tagliata di Manzo with Rocket, Parmesan and Rosemary Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The sliced steak that feeds four from two ribeyes — hard sear, long rest, fanned over rocket so the juices become the dressing; charter lunch royalty.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- ribeye or sirloin, 3.5 cm thick — 2 × 400 g
- rocket — 80 g
- Parmigiano Reggiano, shaved — 50 g
- extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- rosemary — 2 sprigs
- garlic, bruised — 2 cloves
- lemon juice — 15 ml
- aged balsamic — 10 ml
- flaky salt and black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Warm 50 ml oil with rosemary and garlic at 60°C for 15 minutes; strain — this is the finishing oil.
2. Bring steaks to room temperature; dry; season with salt only.
3. Sear on a ripping plancha or pan: 3 minutes, flip, 3 minutes, targeting 50–52°C core.
4. Rest a full 8 minutes on a warm board — the rest IS the recipe.
5. Slice 1 cm against the grain, capturing every drop of juice.
6. Dress rocket with lemon juice and a little rosemary oil.

## Plating & Service
Rocket first, slices fanned over, board juices and rosemary oil spooned across, Parmesan shavings, balsamic in five drops, pepper last.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Porcini sautéed hard replace rocket in autumn. Dairy-free: skip cheese, add toasted hazelnut crumb (then not nut-free).
