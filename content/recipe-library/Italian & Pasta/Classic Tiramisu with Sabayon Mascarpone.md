# Classic Tiramisu with Sabayon Mascarpone

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The benchmark, built properly — yolks cooked to a marsala sabayon over the bain-marie so the cream is silk-stable and food-safe for charter service.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- egg yolks — 6
- caster sugar — 120 g
- dry Marsala — 40 ml
- mascarpone, room temperature — 500 g
- double cream — 150 ml
- savoiardi (ladyfingers) — 300 g
- strong espresso, cooled — 350 ml
- coffee liqueur (optional) — 20 ml
- cocoa powder, unsweetened — 20 g
- dark chocolate, grated — 15 g
- fine salt — 1 pinch

## Method
1. Whisk yolks, sugar and Marsala in a bowl over barely-simmering water until 72°C and ribbon-thick, 6–8 minutes; cool, whisking, to room temperature.
2. Loosen the mascarpone with a spatula; fold into the sabayon with the salt.
3. Whip cream to soft peaks; fold in.
4. Mix espresso and liqueur. Dip each savoiardo 1–2 seconds per side — soaked outside, dry heart.
5. Layer in a 25×20 cm dish: biscuits, half the cream, biscuits, remaining cream. Smooth the top.
6. Refrigerate minimum 6 hours, ideally overnight.
7. Dust with cocoa and grated chocolate only at service.

## Plating & Service
Cut clean squares with a hot knife or scoop generously — cocoa dusted at the last second so it never dampens.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> For glass-coupe service, pipe the cream in layers — sets faster and travels better in a seaway. Decaf espresso for the evening-sensitive.
