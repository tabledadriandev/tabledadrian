# Fritto Misto di Mare with Lemon and Fried Herbs

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The eternal harbour fry — squid, prawns and small fish in a whisper of semolina-flour crust, with fried sage and lemon slices in the mix, Ligurian style.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- squid, cleaned, rings and tentacles — 300 g
- raw prawns, shell on tail — 250 g
- fresh anchovies or small red mullet — 200 g
- plain flour — 120 g
- fine semolina — 80 g
- sparkling cold water — for a light dip (100 ml)
- lemon — 2 (one sliced 3 mm for frying, one in wedges)
- sage leaves — 8
- parsley sprigs — 4
- sunflower oil — 2 L
- fine salt — to taste

## Method
1. Dry every piece of seafood obsessively — moisture is the enemy of crunch.
2. Mix flour and semolina. Dip seafood briefly in sparkling water, then dredge, shaking off every excess gram.
3. Heat oil to 180°C. Fry in small batches, squid first (90 seconds), then fish (2 minutes), prawns last (2 minutes) — never crowding.
4. Fry the lemon slices, sage and parsley 30–45 seconds until glassy.
5. Drain everything on a rack, not paper; salt from height immediately.

## Plating & Service
Piled loose and tall in a paper cone or on a warm platter, fried lemon and herbs woven through, wedges alongside. Serve within 3 minutes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: rice flour + cornstarch 50/50 fries even crisper. Add ribbons of courgette to stretch it for aperitivo hour.
