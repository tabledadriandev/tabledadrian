# Veal Scaloppine al Limone with Capers

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The trattoria standard executed precisely — a two-minute veal sauté finished in a lemon-butter pan sauce that must emulsify, not split; weeknight speed, hotel finish.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- veal escalopes, pounded 5 mm — 8 × 70 g
- plain flour — 50 g
- butter, cold, cubed — 80 g
- olive oil — 25 ml
- dry white wine — 80 ml
- chicken stock — 100 ml
- lemon juice — 30 ml
- lemon, sliced paper-thin — 6 slices
- capers, rinsed — 20 g
- parsley — 6 g
- fine salt and white pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Season the veal; flour both sides, shaking off every excess gram — thick flour makes gluey sauce.
2. Sear in oil and 20 g butter over high heat, 60 seconds per side, in two rounds; remove.
3. Deglaze with wine; reduce by half. Add stock and lemon slices; simmer 2 minutes.
4. Add lemon juice and capers; then mount the remaining cold butter, swirling off the boil, to a glossy nappe.
5. Return the veal 30 seconds, spooning sauce over; parsley last.

## Plating & Service
Two scaloppine per plate, one glazed lemon slice each, sauce ribboned over — with spinach or a potato purée.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Identical method carries chicken, turkey or pork loin. If the sauce splits, one spoon of cold stock and a swirl rescues it.
