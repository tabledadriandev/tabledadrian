# Lemon Granita with Whipped Cream and Warm Brioche Notes

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sicilian breakfast logic served as dessert — coarse, flaky lemon ice against soft unsweetened cream; scrape-frozen by hand, no machine aboard required.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- water — 600 ml
- caster sugar — 160 g
- lemon juice, fresh — 220 ml
- lemon zest — of 2 lemons
- double cream (optional, to serve) — 150 ml
- candied lemon or fresh zest — for finishing
- small brioche buns, warmed (optional, the full Catania rite) — 6

## Method
1. Dissolve sugar in 200 ml of the water over gentle heat with the zest; cool; add remaining water and the lemon juice.
2. Strain into a wide shallow metal tray — surface area is speed.
3. Freeze; after 45 minutes, drag a fork through the crystals. Repeat every 30 minutes, 4–5 times, until the whole tray is glittering flakes.
4. Whip the cream, unsweetened, to soft folds.
5. Hold the granita at -12 to -10°C if possible — colder turns it to a brick.

## Plating & Service
Piled high in frozen glasses, cream spooned over (never under), zest across — a warm brioche alongside for the full Sicilian morning-after.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Note the diet tags: dairy-free as served without cream. Coffee, almond-milk or mulberry granita follow the same 160 g/L sugar logic — sugar ratio IS the texture.
