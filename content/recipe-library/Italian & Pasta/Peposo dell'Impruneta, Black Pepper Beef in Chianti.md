# Peposo dell'Impruneta, Black Pepper Beef in Chianti

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Tuscan kiln-workers' stew — beef, whole garlic, Chianti and an unapologetic fistful of pepper, sealed and forgotten for four hours; five ingredients, zero technique, total depth.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- beef shin or chuck, 4 cm cubes — 1.5 kg
- Chianti or Sangiovese — 750 ml
- garlic cloves, peeled, whole — 10
- black peppercorns, half crushed, half whole — 12 g
- fine salt — 12 g
- tomato paste (modern but useful) — 30 g
- olive oil — 30 ml
- rustic bread for serving — 300 g

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 140°C. No browning — this is doctrine.
2. Pack the beef into a heavy casserole with garlic, all the pepper, salt, tomato paste and oil.
3. Pour over wine to barely cover.
4. Lid on; into the oven for 4 hours, stirring at hour 2 and 3.
5. Uncover for the final 30 minutes to reduce to a dark, peppery gloss; the beef should collapse under a spoon.
6. Rest 15 minutes; taste — it should genuinely bite with pepper.

## Plating & Service
In deep bowls over thick grilled bread that drowns slowly, or beside white beans — nothing refined, everything right.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> This dish was invented by terracotta kiln workers who cooked at the kiln mouth; low, slow, and untouched is the whole recipe. Cheek version: 3.5 hours.
