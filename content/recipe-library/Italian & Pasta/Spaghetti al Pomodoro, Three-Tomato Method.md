# Spaghetti al Pomodoro, Three-Tomato Method

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The simplest plate in the canon, engineered — cooked passata for body, confit datterini for sweetness, raw grated tomato for brightness, one basil infusion.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- spaghetti — 400 g
- tomato passata — 400 g
- datterini tomatoes — 250 g
- large ripe vine tomato — 1 (about 150 g)
- garlic, bruised — 2 cloves
- extra virgin olive oil — 80 ml
- basil, whole sprigs — 15 g
- caster sugar — 2 g if needed
- fine salt — to taste

## Method
1. Confit datterini: halve, toss with 30 ml oil and salt, roast at 150°C for 25 minutes.
2. Warm 40 ml oil with garlic until fragrant; add passata, one basil sprig, simmer 20 minutes; season, balance with sugar; remove garlic and basil.
3. Grate the raw tomato on a box grater; discard skin; salt lightly.
4. Boil spaghetti to 2 minutes under al dente.
5. Finish pasta in the passata with a ladle of water, 2 minutes, until it clings.
6. Off heat: fold in confit datterini, the raw tomato pulp, remaining oil and torn basil.

## Plating & Service
A tall rotating twirl set dead centre, sauce ring around, one perfect basil tip. Parmigiano offered, never presumed.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> This is the dish to calibrate new galley olive oil — it hides nothing. In deep winter use all-passata plus 10 g butter.
