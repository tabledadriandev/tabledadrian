# Doppio Raviolo of Ricotta and Slow Tomato, Basil Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Two fillings in one sealed pasta — a white ricotta chamber beside a red tomato-jam chamber, so every bite is caprese engineered in pasta form.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- 00 flour — 300 g
- whole eggs — 3
- ricotta, drained overnight — 220 g
- Parmigiano Reggiano — 60 g
- datterini tomatoes — 400 g
- tomato paste — 15 g
- caster sugar — 5 g
- red wine vinegar — 5 ml
- basil — 15 g
- butter — 80 g
- fine salt and white pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Tomato chamber: halve datterini, cook down with paste, sugar, vinegar and salt over low heat 40 minutes to a thick, pipeable jam. Chill.
2. White chamber: beat ricotta, 40 g Parmigiano, salt, white pepper. Chill in a piping bag.
3. Make dough; rest 1 hour. Roll to 0.7 mm bands.
4. Pipe pairs of side-by-side mounds (6 g each, 1 cm apart) down one band. Cap with a second band, sealing around and BETWEEN each pair to create twin chambers.
5. Cut 9×5 cm rectangles with a fluted wheel; check every internal seal.
6. Boil 3 minutes at a gentle simmer.
7. Melt butter with torn basil and a spoon of pasta water; glaze the ravioli.

## Plating & Service
Three doppi per plate, seam ridge visible, basil butter spooned over, micro basil on the tomato half.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Discipline on filling moisture is everything — if the tomato jam smears, chill it firmer. A third chamber of olive tapenade turns it into a tricolore showpiece.
