# Potato Gnocchi with Gorgonzola Cream and Walnut Gremolata

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Feather gnocchi from baked — never boiled — potatoes, sauced with a dolcelatte cream that stays light, and cut by a walnut-parsley crunch.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- floury potatoes (agria/russet) — 1 kg
- 00 flour — 250 g (plus dusting)
- egg yolk — 1
- fine salt — 8 g
- nutmeg — 0.3 g
- gorgonzola dolce — 150 g
- double cream — 100 ml
- whole milk — 50 ml
- walnuts, toasted, chopped — 40 g
- parsley — 5 g
- lemon zest — of half a lemon
- black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Bake whole potatoes on salt at 200°C for 60–70 minutes until completely tender. Halve, scoop, and rice while scalding hot.
2. Spread the riced potato to steam dry 5 minutes — dryness is the whole game.
3. Gather with salt, nutmeg, yolk and the minimum flour to a soft, barely-bound dough; do not knead more than 30 seconds.
4. Roll 2 cm ropes, cut 2.5 cm pillows, roll over a gnocchi board if you want ridges.
5. Melt gorgonzola with cream and milk over low heat to a pourable sauce; pepper generously.
6. Poach gnocchi in simmering salted water; lift 20 seconds after they float.
7. Slide into the sauce; shake, don't stir.

## Plating & Service
Shallow bowls, sauce pooling, walnut-parsley-lemon gremolata over — the crunch makes the dish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Piquant version: half gorgonzola piccante. GF: 180 g rice flour + 20 g potato starch instead of 00, handle even more gently.
