# Vincisgrassi with Porcini, Prosciutto and Truffle Besciamella

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Marche's aristocratic answer to lasagne — layers scented with porcini, prosciutto and truffle rather than tomato; opulent, pale gold, unforgettable.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- 00 flour — 350 g
- whole eggs — 3
- egg yolk — 1
- dried porcini — 40 g
- fresh mushrooms, sliced — 300 g
- prosciutto di Parma, torn — 150 g
- chicken livers (optional, classic), cleaned — 100 g
- butter — 120 g
- plain flour for roux — 70 g
- whole milk — 900 ml
- truffle paste or minced black truffle — 20 g
- Parmigiano Reggiano — 140 g
- dry Marsala — 40 ml
- fine salt, black pepper, nutmeg — to taste

## Method
1. Soak porcini in 300 ml warm water 30 minutes; strain, keep the liquor, chop.
2. Sauté fresh mushrooms and porcini in 40 g butter until dry and golden; add livers if using, sear 2 minutes, then Marsala; reduce. Chop everything to a coarse rubble.
3. Besciamella: 80 g butter, flour, milk plus 150 ml porcini liquor; simmer 8 minutes; season with nutmeg, salt; stir in truffle.
4. Make pasta; roll to 0.9 mm; cut sheets, blanch 30 seconds, refresh, dry.
5. Layer in a buttered 25×35 cm dish: besciamella, pasta, mushroom mix, prosciutto, Parmigiano — six layers, ending with besciamella and cheese.
6. Rest 1 hour. Bake at 175°C fan for 40 minutes until blistered gold.
7. Rest 20 minutes before cutting.

## Plating & Service
Clean rectangles, a little extra warm besciamella, shaved truffle over the pass if the budget smiles.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skip livers for a lighter modern read. Vegetarian: drop prosciutto, double the porcini, add 5 ml soy to the besciamella liquor.
