# Caponata with Toasted Pine Nuts and Cocoa Nib

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sicily's sweet-sour aubergine stew, rested a full day so the agrodolce settles — a whisper of cocoa nib nods to the island's baroque monsù kitchens.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- aubergines, 2.5 cm dice — 700 g
- celery hearts, 1 cm slices — 150 g
- onion, sliced — 150 g
- tomato passata — 200 g
- green olives, pitted — 80 g
- capers, rinsed — 40 g
- red wine vinegar — 60 ml
- caster sugar — 35 g
- pine nuts, toasted — 30 g
- cocoa nibs — 8 g
- olive oil — 120 ml
- basil — 10 g
- fine salt — to taste

## Method
1. Fry aubergine in batches in 90 ml oil until bronzed and creamy; drain on paper.
2. Blanch celery 2 minutes; drain.
3. Soften onion in remaining oil 8 minutes; add celery, passata, olives and capers; simmer 10 minutes.
4. Dissolve sugar in the vinegar; add to the pan; cook 5 minutes until glossy agrodolce.
5. Fold in aubergine gently; simmer 5 minutes only — it should hold its shape.
6. Cool, then rest 24 hours refrigerated. Serve at room temperature with pine nuts, cocoa nibs and basil.

## Plating & Service
A generous spoonful on rustic plates or crostini, nibs and nuts scattered, basil tip — never serve fridge-cold.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: swap pine nuts for toasted sunflower seeds. Swordfish chunks seared and folded in make it a light secondo.
