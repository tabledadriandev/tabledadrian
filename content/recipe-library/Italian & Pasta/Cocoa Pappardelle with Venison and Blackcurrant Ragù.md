# Cocoa Pappardelle with Venison and Blackcurrant Ragù

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bitter cocoa in the dough — an old Veneto trick — against a red-wine venison ragù lifted with blackcurrant; dramatic, dark and built for winter charters.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- 00 flour — 280 g
- cocoa powder, unsweetened — 25 g
- whole eggs — 3
- venison shoulder, 1.5 cm dice — 600 g
- red wine — 300 ml
- blackcurrant jam or crème de cassis — 25 g
- onion, brunoise — 100 g
- carrot, brunoise — 80 g
- celery, brunoise — 60 g
- juniper berries — 4
- bay leaf — 1
- tomato paste — 30 g
- olive oil — 50 ml
- game or beef stock — 400 ml
- fine salt and black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Brown venison hard in oil in batches; set aside.
2. Sweat soffritto 10 minutes; add tomato paste, cook 2 minutes.
3. Return meat with wine, juniper and bay; reduce by half.
4. Add stock; braise covered at 150°C for 2 hours until spoon-tender. Shred coarsely, reduce the sauce to cling, finish with blackcurrant, salt and pepper.
5. Knead flour, cocoa and eggs to a dark dough; rest 1 hour.
6. Roll to 0.9 mm, cut 20 mm pappardelle.
7. Boil 2 minutes; toss through the ragù with a ladle of pasta water.

## Plating & Service
Dark ribbons folded tall, sauce beneath, a few fresh currants or a parsley leaf for relief.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Beef cheek substitutes seamlessly. The cocoa dough is savoury — do not sweeten; it exists to echo the game's darkness.
