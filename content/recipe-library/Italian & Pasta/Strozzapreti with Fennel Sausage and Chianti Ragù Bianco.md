# Strozzapreti with Fennel Sausage and Chianti Ragù Bianco

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Eggless hand-twisted 'priest-stranglers' with a tomato-free sausage ragù — rosemary, fennel seed and red wine reduced to a glossy cling.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- 00 flour — 250 g
- fine semolina — 50 g
- warm water — 150 ml
- Italian pork sausage, skinned — 350 g
- fennel seeds, cracked — 3 g
- rosemary, chopped — 2 g
- garlic, sliced — 2 cloves
- Chianti or other Sangiovese — 150 ml
- chicken stock — 250 ml
- butter — 30 g
- Parmigiano Reggiano — 50 g
- olive oil — 20 ml
- fine salt and black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Knead flours and water 8 minutes to a supple eggless dough; rest 30 minutes.
2. Roll to 2 mm, cut 1.5 cm strips, then roll each between your palms into rough twists and tear into 8 cm lengths.
3. Brown crumbled sausage hard in olive oil, 8 minutes, breaking to small nuggets.
4. Add garlic, fennel seed and rosemary; cook 1 minute.
5. Deglaze with wine, reduce to a syrup. Add stock and simmer 20 minutes until sticky.
6. Boil strozzapreti 4–5 minutes in salted water.
7. Toss pasta into the ragù with butter, cheese and pasta water; emulsify 1 minute over high heat.

## Plating & Service
Rustic mounds in warm bowls, extra cheese, a thread of grassy olive oil.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The eggless dough is naturally vegan up to the sauce — for a vegan bowl, use mushroom-walnut crumble instead of sausage and miso for depth.
