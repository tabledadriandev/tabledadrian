# Spinach with Brown Butter, Pine Nuts and Sultanas

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Spinaci alla genovese — leaves barely wilted then rolled through nutty butter with pine nuts and plumped sultanas; sweet, saline, three minutes of work.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- spinach, large leaf — 600 g
- butter — 50 g
- pine nuts — 25 g
- sultanas, soaked 10 minutes in warm water — 30 g
- garlic, bruised — 1 clove
- nutmeg — a scrape
- fine salt and black pepper — to taste
- lemon — a small squeeze

## Method
1. Wash the spinach; wilt it in a dry pan with only its washing water, 2 minutes; drain and press lightly.
2. Brown the butter with the garlic clove and pine nuts until both turn gold and the butter smells of hazelnut; remove garlic.
3. Add drained sultanas; sizzle 30 seconds.
4. Fold in the spinach; toss 1 minute to coat and heat through.
5. Season with salt, pepper, nutmeg and the smallest squeeze of lemon.

## Plating & Service
A neat warm mound beside fish or veal, the pine nuts and sultanas surfacing — a saucer of elegance in ten minutes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chard, escarole or tender kale all take the treatment. Vegan: olive oil instead of butter, plus 3 extra minutes of patience for the nuts.
