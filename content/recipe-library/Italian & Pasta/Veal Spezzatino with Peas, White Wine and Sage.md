# Veal Spezzatino with Peas, White Wine and Sage

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The gentle white stew — veal shoulder in a blond, tomato-free braise finished with sweet peas; proof that Italian braising isn't always red.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- veal shoulder, 3 cm cubes — 900 g
- plain flour — 30 g
- butter — 40 g
- olive oil — 30 ml
- onion, fine — 120 g
- celery, fine — 60 g
- garlic — 2 cloves
- dry white wine — 200 ml
- chicken stock — 500 ml
- sage — 4 leaves
- lemon zest — of half a lemon
- peas, fresh or frozen — 250 g
- parsley — 8 g
- fine salt and white pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Season and lightly flour the veal; brown in oil and half the butter in batches to pale gold only — this is a blond stew.
2. Soften onion and celery in the pot 6 minutes; add garlic and sage, 1 minute.
3. Return meat; add wine; reduce by half.
4. Add stock to barely cover; braise lidded at a whisper (or 150°C oven) 70–80 minutes until fork-tender.
5. Add peas; simmer uncovered 8 minutes while the sauce tightens to a light velouté.
6. Finish with remaining butter, lemon zest, parsley, white pepper.

## Plating & Service
Deep plates over potato purée, soft polenta or steamed rice — the sauce should just flow, never glue.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Artichokes replace peas in spring. Pork shoulder version for crew is indistinguishable by candlelight.
