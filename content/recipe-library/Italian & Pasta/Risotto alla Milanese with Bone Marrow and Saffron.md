# Risotto alla Milanese with Bone Marrow and Saffron

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The golden Lombard benchmark — marrow in the soffritto, saffron bloomed in stock, and a violent off-heat mantecatura that makes it ripple like silk.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- carnaroli rice — 320 g
- bone marrow, diced — 40 g
- butter — 70 g
- onion, very fine brunoise — 60 g
- dry white wine — 80 ml
- light beef or chicken stock, hot — 1.3 L
- saffron threads — 0.3 g
- Parmigiano Reggiano, grated — 80 g
- fine salt — to taste

## Method
1. Bloom saffron in 100 ml hot stock for 15 minutes.
2. Melt marrow with 20 g butter; sweat onion 5 minutes without colour.
3. Toast rice 2 minutes until the grains click and turn pearlescent at the edges.
4. Deglaze with wine; let it vanish.
5. Feed in hot stock a ladle at a time over lively heat, stirring, 14 minutes; add the saffron stock at minute 10.
6. At 16–17 minutes the rice should be al dente in a wave-loose base ('all'onda'). Kill the heat.
7. Rest 1 minute, then beat in cold butter and Parmigiano violently until glossy. Adjust salt and looseness.

## Plating & Service
Pour onto flat plates and slap the base so it spreads into a perfect gold disc. Nothing on top — maybe one saffron thread.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Serve with ossobuco for the full Milanese rite. No marrow? All butter works; 5 g dried porcini powder is a modern cheat for depth.
