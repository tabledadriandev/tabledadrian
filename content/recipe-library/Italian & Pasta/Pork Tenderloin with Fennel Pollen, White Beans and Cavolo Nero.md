# Pork Tenderloin with Fennel Pollen, White Beans and Cavolo Nero

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Tuscan butcher's flavours in a fast-roast format — fennel-pollen-crusted tenderloin, creamy cannellini and garlicky black kale; earthy, lean, quietly luxurious.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- pork tenderloins, trimmed — 2 × 400 g
- fennel pollen (or ground toasted fennel seed) — 4 g
- fine salt — 10 g
- black pepper — 2 g
- olive oil — 70 ml
- cooked cannellini beans — 400 g
- garlic — 4 cloves
- sage — 4 leaves
- chicken stock — 150 ml
- cavolo nero, stripped — 300 g
- chilli — 0.5 g
- lemon — half, juiced
- dry white wine — 80 ml

## Method
1. Rub the tenderloins with salt, pepper and fennel pollen; rest 30 minutes.
2. Sear in 30 ml oil on all faces, 6 minutes total; roast at 190°C to core 58–60°C, about 8–10 minutes. Rest 8 minutes.
3. Beans: warm 2 crushed garlic cloves and sage in 20 ml oil; add beans and stock; simmer 8 minutes; crush a third of them to cream the rest; season.
4. Blanch cavolo nero 3 minutes; sauté with remaining oil, sliced garlic and chilli; finish with lemon.
5. Deglaze the roasting pan with wine for a spoonful of jus.

## Plating & Service
Beans as the base, sliced pork blushing at 1.5 cm, kale to the side, jus and a dust more pollen over.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fennel pollen is the porchetta soul in powder form — a little jar lasts a season. Chicken breast at 62°C works identically.
