# Bonet Piemontese, Chocolate Amaretti Caramel Flan

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Piedmont's ancient dark flan — cocoa, amaretti and a whisper of rum set in caramel-lined moulds; the langhe grandmother's dessert with a bain-marie discipline.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- caster sugar — 200 g (100 g caramel, 100 g custard)
- whole eggs — 4
- egg yolk — 1
- whole milk — 500 ml
- double cream — 100 ml
- cocoa powder — 25 g
- amaretti, finely crushed — 80 g (plus 8 for garnish)
- dark rum — 20 ml
- espresso — 30 ml
- fine salt — 1 pinch

## Method
1. Dry-caramelise 100 g sugar to deep amber; divide instantly among 8 dariole moulds (or one 20 cm ring), tilting to coat the bases.
2. Warm milk and cream to 80°C.
3. Whisk eggs, yolk, remaining sugar, cocoa and salt; temper in the hot milk; add crushed amaretti, rum and espresso.
4. Rest the mix 10 minutes so the amaretti hydrate; stir and pour over the set caramel.
5. Bake in a bain-marie at 150°C for 40–50 minutes to a firm tremble (85°C core).
6. Chill overnight — non-negotiable for clean release.

## Plating & Service
Run a knife around, invert onto plates with lips (caramel floods), one amaretto and a few crushed crumbs on top.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Alcohol-free: swap rum for more espresso. GF depends on your amaretti — most pure almond ones qualify; check the label.
