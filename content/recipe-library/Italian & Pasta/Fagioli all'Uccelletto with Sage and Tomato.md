# Fagioli all'Uccelletto with Sage and Tomato

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Tuscany's 'little bird style' beans — cannellini simmered with garlic, sage and tomato until creamy; the eternal companion to grilled meat and sausage.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- cooked cannellini beans (from 300 g dried, or 3 tins) — 750 g
- extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- garlic, crushed — 4 cloves
- sage leaves — 8
- tomato passata — 250 g
- bean cooking liquor or water — 150 ml
- fine salt and black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Warm the oil with garlic and sage over low heat until the garlic gilds and the sage crisps at the edges, 4 minutes.
2. Add the passata; simmer 10 minutes to a thick sauce.
3. Fold in the beans with their liquor.
4. Simmer gently 15 minutes, crushing a few beans against the wall so the sauce turns creamy.
5. Season boldly with salt and pepper; rest 10 minutes — they improve as they sit.

## Plating & Service
A generous spoonful beside sausage, lamb or bistecca, raw oil over — or on grilled bread as a standalone lunch.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> From dried: simmer 300 g soaked beans with sage and garlic 60–90 minutes first, and use that liquor. Day two, they're even better.
