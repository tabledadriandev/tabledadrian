# Pappardelle with Wild Boar and Juniper Ragù

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Broad silk ribbons carrying a red-wine boar ragù scented with juniper and bay — Tuscany's hunting-lodge classic refined for the saloon table.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- 00 flour — 400 g
- whole eggs — 4
- wild boar shoulder, 1 cm dice — 700 g
- red wine (Sangiovese) — 400 ml
- juniper berries, crushed — 6
- bay leaves — 2
- onion, brunoise — 120 g
- carrot, brunoise — 100 g
- celery, brunoise — 80 g
- tomato paste — 40 g
- tinned tomatoes — 300 g
- olive oil — 50 ml
- dark chocolate 85% — 10 g
- fine salt and black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Marinate boar overnight in the wine with juniper and bay. Drain, reserving wine; pat the meat completely dry.
2. Brown boar in olive oil in batches until crusted; set aside.
3. Sweat soffritto in the same pot 10 minutes. Add tomato paste, cook 2 minutes.
4. Return meat, add reserved wine; reduce by half. Add tomatoes and water to barely cover.
5. Braise covered at 150°C for 3 hours until the boar shreds under a spoon. Shred coarsely into the sauce, reduce to cling, and round it off with the chocolate.
6. Make egg dough, rest, roll to 0.9 mm, cut 20 mm ribbons.
7. Boil pappardelle 2 minutes; toss through the ragù with pasta water until glossy.

## Plating & Service
Wide bowls, ribbons folded like drapery, sauce pooling beneath, one fried sage leaf per plate.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No boar? Pork cheek with 2 extra juniper berries gets remarkably close. Finish with orange zest for a Maremma accent.
