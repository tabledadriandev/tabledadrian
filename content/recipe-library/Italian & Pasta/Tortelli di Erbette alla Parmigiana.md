# Tortelli di Erbette alla Parmigiana

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Parma's midsummer ritual — chard-and-ricotta tortelli traditionally eaten on St John's Eve, dressed only with butter and a snowfall of Parmigiano.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- 00 flour — 300 g
- whole eggs — 3
- Swiss chard leaves (no stems) — 400 g
- ricotta, drained — 300 g
- Parmigiano Reggiano — 120 g
- egg yolk — 1
- nutmeg — 0.4 g
- butter — 90 g
- fine salt — to taste

## Method
1. Wilt chard 2 minutes; squeeze until nearly dry; chop very fine.
2. Beat with ricotta, 80 g Parmigiano, yolk, nutmeg and salt; chill.
3. Make egg dough; rest 1 hour; roll to 0.7 mm bands.
4. Pipe 12 g mounds; fold the band over and seal into large rectangles (8×5 cm) with a fluted wheel — Parma tortelli are generous.
5. Boil 3–4 minutes at a gentle simmer.
6. Melt butter to just foaming; layer tortelli and butter and remaining Parmigiano in a warm dish — traditionally never tossed, always layered.

## Plating & Service
Overlapping in warm shallow bowls, butter pooling in the pleats, a final uncompromising snow of Parmigiano.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nettle or spinach substitute for chard by season. Some Parma families add a scrape of lemon zest — heresy elsewhere, delicious everywhere.
