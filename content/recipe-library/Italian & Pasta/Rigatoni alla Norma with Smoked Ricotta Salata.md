# Rigatoni alla Norma with Smoked Ricotta Salata

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Catania's opera in a bowl — bronze-fried aubergine folded into basil tomato sauce with salted ricotta shaved like parmesan snow.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- rigatoni or fresh maccheroni — 400 g
- aubergines — 500 g
- San Marzano tinned tomatoes — 500 g
- garlic, crushed — 2 cloves
- basil — 15 g
- extra virgin olive oil — 100 ml (partly for frying)
- ricotta salata (smoked if possible) — 80 g
- fine salt and black pepper — to taste
- sugar — 2 g if needed

## Method
1. Cut aubergine into 2 cm batons, salt 20 minutes, pat dry.
2. Fry aubergine in 80 ml oil in batches at 180°C until mahogany and creamy inside; drain on paper.
3. Sizzle garlic in the remaining oil, add tomatoes, simmer 20 minutes; season, add half the basil.
4. Boil pasta to 1 minute under al dente in salted water.
5. Finish pasta in the sauce with a ladle of pasta water, 1 minute.
6. Fold in two-thirds of the aubergine gently.

## Plating & Service
Top with remaining aubergine, generous shavings of ricotta salata, basil crown.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Lighter: roast the aubergine at 220°C with 30 ml oil instead of frying. Vegan: swap ricotta salata for almond 'parmesan'.
