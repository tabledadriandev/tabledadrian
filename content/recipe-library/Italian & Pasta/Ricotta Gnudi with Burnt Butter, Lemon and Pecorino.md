# Ricotta Gnudi with Burnt Butter, Lemon and Pecorino

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> 'Nude' ravioli — pure ricotta clouds rolled in semolina overnight so they self-skin, then poached and rolled in nutty butter. Two-ingredient magic, all technique.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- ricotta, drained overnight in muslin — 500 g
- Parmigiano Reggiano, grated — 80 g
- egg yolk — 1
- fine semolina — 200 g (for coating)
- 00 flour — 20 g
- nutmeg — 0.3 g
- fine salt and white pepper — to taste
- butter — 90 g
- lemon zest and juice — of 1 lemon
- pecorino toscano, shaved — 40 g

## Method
1. Beat drained ricotta with Parmigiano, yolk, flour, nutmeg, salt and white pepper — the mix should just hold a shape.
2. Roll into 25 g balls with wet hands.
3. Bury the balls in a tray of semolina; refrigerate uncovered 12–24 hours, turning once — the semolina forms a delicate skin.
4. Poach in barely-simmering salted water; they are done 60 seconds after floating (about 3–4 minutes).
5. Take butter to hazelnut brown; stop it with lemon juice.
6. Roll gnudi gently through the butter.

## Plating & Service
Five per plate, burnt butter spooned over, lemon zest, pecorino shavings, one turn of white pepper.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The overnight semolina rest is non-negotiable — rushed gnudi dissolve. Spinach folded in (150 g, squeezed) gives the classic Tuscan green version.
