# Roasted Fennel with Orange, Olives and Pernod-Free Anise Glaze

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Fennel wedges roasted to caramel and glazed with reduced orange juice and crushed anise seed — Sicilian flavours, no liquor cabinet required.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- fennel bulbs, 8 wedges each — 3 (about 750 g)
- extra virgin olive oil — 50 ml
- orange juice — 150 ml
- orange zest — of 1 orange
- anise or fennel seeds, crushed — 2 g
- black olives — 50 g
- fine salt and black pepper — to taste
- fennel fronds — for garnish

## Method
1. Toss the wedges with oil, salt and pepper on a roasting tray, cut faces down.
2. Roast at 210°C for 25 minutes, turning once, until deeply caramelised at the edges.
3. Meanwhile reduce orange juice with the anise seed to a light syrup, about 40 ml.
4. Add olives and glaze to the tray; roast 5 minutes more, tossing to lacquer.
5. Finish with zest and fronds.

## Plating & Service
Glossy wedges shingled on a warm platter, olives scattered, pan syrup spooned over — brilliant beside branzino or pork.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> A splash of Pernod in the reduction for the grown-up version. Serve room temperature as part of an antipasti spread — it holds all afternoon.
