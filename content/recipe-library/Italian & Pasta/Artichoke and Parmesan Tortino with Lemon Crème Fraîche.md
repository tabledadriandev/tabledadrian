# Artichoke and Parmesan Tortino with Lemon Crème Fraîche

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A delicate baked artichoke custard-cake — braised violets folded into parmesan royale, set in a water bath, warm and trembling under a citrus cream.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- violet artichokes — 6 (or 400 g cleaned hearts)
- lemon — 1 (for acidulated water and zest)
- olive oil — 40 ml
- garlic — 1 clove
- dry white wine — 60 ml
- whole eggs — 3
- double cream — 200 ml
- Parmigiano Reggiano — 80 g
- crème fraîche — 80 g
- thyme — 2 g
- fine salt, white pepper, nutmeg — to taste
- butter for moulds — 15 g

## Method
1. Turn artichokes to the hearts; hold in lemon water. Slice 5 mm.
2. Braise with oil, garlic, thyme and wine, lidded, 12 minutes until tender; season; cool and discard garlic.
3. Whisk eggs, cream, Parmigiano, salt, white pepper and nutmeg to a royale.
4. Butter four 150 ml ramekins; layer in artichokes, pour over royale.
5. Bake in a bain-marie at 150°C for 22–25 minutes until just set with a slight tremble.
6. Stir lemon zest and a pinch of salt into the crème fraîche.

## Plating & Service
Unmould onto warm plates (or serve in ramekins on rough seas), a spoon of lemon cream alongside, thyme tips, one braised artichoke quarter on top.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Frozen artichoke hearts are honest in winter. The royale base takes asparagus or porcini by season — same ratios.
