# Linguine alle Vongole in Bianco

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Clams, wine, garlic, parsley — the trick is a double emulsion of clam liquor and starch so the linguine drink the sea without a drop of cream.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- linguine — 400 g
- palourde clams (vongole veraci), purged — 1.2 kg
- extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- garlic, sliced paper-thin — 3 cloves
- dry white wine — 100 ml
- flat-leaf parsley, stems and leaves separate — 10 g
- dried chilli — 0.5 g
- lemon zest — 1 pinch
- black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Purge clams 2 hours in 3% salt water; scrub and drain.
2. Open clams: hot wide pan, half the oil, parsley stems, wine, then clams; lid on 2–3 minutes. Pull each clam the second it opens.
3. Strain the liquor through muslin. Pick half the clams, keep half in shell.
4. Sizzle garlic and chilli gently in remaining oil; add the strained liquor.
5. Boil linguine to 3 minutes under al dente in barely salted water; transfer to the pan.
6. Finish the pasta in the clam liquor, adding pasta water in splashes, 3 minutes until creamy and al dente.
7. Fold in all clams and chopped parsley; toss 30 seconds with a final thread of raw oil.

## Plating & Service
Shells arranged upward, sauce pearlescent at the bowl's base, lemon zest barely there.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Rosso version: add 150 g datterini at step 4. Never cheese. If clams are sandy, double-purge — grit ends careers.
