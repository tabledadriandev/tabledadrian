# Cantucci di Prato with Vin Santo Service

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Twice-baked almond biscuits, hard on purpose — built to be drowned in a small glass of vin santo; the Tuscan full stop to any long dinner.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- plain flour — 350 g
- caster sugar — 200 g
- whole eggs — 2 plus 1 yolk (plus 1 beaten egg for glaze)
- baking powder — 6 g
- fine salt — 2 g
- whole almonds, skin on, lightly toasted — 200 g
- orange zest — of 1 orange
- vanilla — half a pod
- honey — 15 g
- vin santo — 375 ml bottle, to serve

## Method
1. Whisk eggs, yolk, sugar, honey, vanilla and zest 2 minutes.
2. Fold in flour, baking powder and salt; then the whole almonds — the dough is stiff and sticky.
3. Shape two logs (30 × 5 cm) on a lined tray with wet hands; glaze with beaten egg.
4. Bake at 180°C for 25 minutes until gold and just firm.
5. Cool 10 minutes — no longer; slice on a sharp diagonal, 1.5 cm, with a serrated knife.
6. Lay the slices flat; second bake at 150°C for 15–18 minutes, turning once, until dry and blond-gold.
7. Cool completely — they harden to their final snap.

## Plating & Service
Five per guest standing in a small glass, the vin santo poured 60 ml alongside — instruct the table: dunk, hold three seconds, no apologies.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> They keep a month airtight — bake a double batch at season start. Pistachio-and-lemon or chocolate-hazelnut variants rotate well; the second bake sets the shelf life.
