# Carne Cruda all'Albese with Hazelnut and Egg Yolk

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Piedmont's knife-cut raw veal seasoned with nothing but lemon, oil and salt — dressed table-side with yolk and toasted hazelnut for Langhe authenticity.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- veal or beef fillet, sinew-free, very fresh — 400 g
- lemon juice — 25 ml
- extra virgin olive oil, delicate — 50 ml
- fine salt — 4 g
- white pepper — 0.5 g
- egg yolks, freshest — 4
- hazelnuts, toasted, halved — 30 g
- rocket, small leaves — 20 g
- garlic — half a clove, for the bowl only

## Method
1. Chill the meat, knives and bowl 30 minutes in the freezer.
2. Rub the mixing bowl with the cut garlic; discard it.
3. Hand-chop the meat into 4 mm dice — never a machine; it must fall loose, not paste.
4. Ten minutes before serving, fold through lemon juice, oil, salt and white pepper.
5. Taste; the seasoning should whisper, not shout.

## Plating & Service
Press loosely into a ring, crown with a raw yolk in a half-shell or directly on top, hazelnuts and rocket around, final thread of oil.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Shaved white truffle in season is the canonical upgrade. Egg-free: replace yolk with 10 g stracciatella per plate — unorthodox, excellent.
