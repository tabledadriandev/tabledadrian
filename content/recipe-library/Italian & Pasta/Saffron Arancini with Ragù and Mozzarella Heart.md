# Saffron Arancini with Ragù and Mozzarella Heart

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Palermo street royalty — saffron rice conetti with a molten centre of ragù and mozzarella, double-crumbed for a shattering shell.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- carnaroli or originario rice — 400 g
- chicken stock — 1 L
- saffron — 0.2 g
- butter — 50 g
- Parmigiano Reggiano — 60 g
- beef ragù, thick and cold — 250 g
- peas, cooked — 60 g
- mozzarella, 1 cm dice, drained — 100 g
- plain flour — 80 g
- cold water — 120 ml
- dry breadcrumbs — 200 g
- sunflower oil for frying — 1.5 L
- fine salt — to taste

## Method
1. Cook the rice risotto-style but without stirring pride — absorb the saffron stock fully, about 16 minutes; beat in butter and Parmigiano; season.
2. Spread 2 cm deep on a tray, press film onto the surface, chill completely (ideally overnight).
3. With wet hands, flatten 90 g rice on your palm, cup it, fill with 20 g ragù, a few peas and 2 dice of mozzarella; close into a cone or sphere, sealing rice over rice.
4. Whisk flour and water to a smooth batter ('lega'). Roll each arancino in it, then in breadcrumbs, pressing.
5. Fry at 170°C for 5–6 minutes to deep amber; drain, salt, rest 3 minutes — the centre needs the carryover.

## Plating & Service
Stack on paper on a board, points up, lemon wedges — meant for hands on the aft deck.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Butter-and-ham (al burro) and pistachio-mortadella fillings rotate well. Air-fryer at 200°C works at 80% glory for lighter days.
