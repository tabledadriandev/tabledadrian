# Herb-Laminated Silk Handkerchiefs with Ricotta and Lemon Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Whole herb leaves pressed between translucent pasta sheets like botanical glass — fazzoletti draped over lemon ricotta for a masterclass-showpiece starter.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- 00 flour — 250 g
- whole eggs — 2
- egg yolk — 1
- flat parsley, chervil and small basil leaves — 20 g
- ricotta, drained — 250 g
- lemon zest — of 2 lemons
- extra virgin olive oil — 50 ml
- Parmigiano Reggiano — 40 g
- butter — 40 g
- fine salt and white pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Make egg dough; rest 1 hour. Roll a band to 0.9 mm.
2. Lay whole herb leaves over one half, spacing them like pressed flowers. Fold the other half over and re-roll to 0.8 mm — the leaves stretch into ghostly silhouettes.
3. Cut 12 cm squares; rest on semolina.
4. Beat ricotta with half the lemon zest, 20 ml oil, salt and white pepper until mousse-like.
5. Boil handkerchiefs 60–90 seconds; slip into a pan of melted butter and 30 ml pasta water; glaze gently.
6. Warm the ricotta to room temperature — never hot.

## Plating & Service
A spoon of ricotta, one handkerchief draped in soft folds over it, second sheet offset, lemon oil, zest and Parmigiano dust.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap ricotta for burrata cream in summer. The laminated sheets also cut into striking maltagliati for broth.
