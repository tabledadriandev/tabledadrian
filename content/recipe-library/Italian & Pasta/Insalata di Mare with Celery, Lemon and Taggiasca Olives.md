# Insalata di Mare with Celery, Lemon and Taggiasca Olives

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The seafood salad done properly — octopus simmered to silk, squid and prawns each cooked to their own second, married warm into the lemon dressing so they drink it in.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- octopus, cleaned — 600 g
- squid, cleaned — 300 g
- raw prawns — 300 g
- mussels — 400 g
- celery heart with leaves, fine slices — 100 g
- taggiasca olives — 60 g
- extra virgin olive oil — 90 ml
- lemon juice — 45 ml
- garlic — 1 clove, bruised
- parsley — 10 g
- chilli — 0.5 g
- bay leaf and peppercorns — for the pot
- fine salt — to taste

## Method
1. Simmer octopus with bay and peppercorns at a bare shudder 40–50 minutes until a knife slides in; cool 15 minutes in its liquid; slice thick.
2. In the same liquid, poach squid rings 45 seconds, then prawns 2 minutes; lift each promptly.
3. Steam mussels open separately; pick.
4. Whisk oil, lemon, garlic, chilli and salt; remove garlic after 10 minutes.
5. Dress all the seafood while still warm; fold in celery, olives and parsley.
6. Rest 1–2 hours refrigerated; return towards room temperature to serve.

## Plating & Service
A loose, glistening tumble in a shallow bowl, celery leaves and one last thread of oil — no lettuce, ever.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Warm-dressing absorption is the entire secret. Swap prawns for cannellini beans for an earthier Ligurian read.
