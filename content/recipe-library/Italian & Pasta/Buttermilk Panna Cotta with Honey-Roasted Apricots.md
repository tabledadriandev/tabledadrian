# Buttermilk Panna Cotta with Honey-Roasted Apricots

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A tangy, lighter panna cotta — buttermilk folded in cold keeps the culture alive and the finish lactic and fresh against warm honeyed apricots.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- double cream — 350 ml
- caster sugar — 75 g
- vanilla pod — 1
- gold gelatine — 5.5 g
- buttermilk — 300 ml
- apricots, halved — 8
- honey — 40 g
- thyme — 1 g
- lemon juice — 5 ml
- pistachios or almonds, sliced (optional) — 15 g

## Method
1. Soak gelatine. Warm cream, sugar and vanilla to 80°C; infuse 10 minutes; dissolve the gelatine in it.
2. Cool to below 35°C, then whisk in the cold buttermilk (heat would grain it).
3. Pour into 6 glasses or moulds; set 5 hours.
4. Roast apricot halves cut-up with honey and thyme at 200°C for 12–15 minutes until caramel-edged; sharpen the tray juices with lemon.
5. Serve the apricots warm or ambient over or beside the set cream.

## Plating & Service
One trembling dome or glass, two apricot halves, tray syrup spooned over, thyme flowers, optional nuts for texture.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Winter version: roasted plums or blood orange. The buttermilk trick works in any panna cotta — subtract cream, add culture, never reheat.
