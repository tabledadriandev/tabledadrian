# Erbazzone Reggiano, Chard and Parmesan Pie

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Reggio Emilia's thin double-crusted greens pie — an olive-oil dough rolled crackling thin around chard, spring onion and a serious dose of Parmigiano.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- plain flour — 300 g
- cold water — 130 ml
- olive oil — 40 ml (plus 20 ml for brushing)
- fine salt — 5 g
- Swiss chard — 800 g
- spring onions, sliced — 100 g
- garlic — 1 clove
- pancetta, fine dice (optional, classic) — 60 g
- Parmigiano Reggiano, grated — 150 g
- dry breadcrumbs — 20 g
- black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Knead flour, water, oil and salt to a smooth dough; rest 30 minutes wrapped.
2. Wilt the chard; squeeze very dry; chop fine.
3. Render pancetta (or warm 20 ml oil); soften spring onion and garlic 4 minutes; fold in chard, 2 minutes; cool.
4. Beat in Parmigiano, breadcrumbs, pepper — the filling should be dense, almost stiff.
5. Roll two-thirds of the dough to 2 mm; line an oiled 28 cm tray with overhang. Spread the filling 1.5 cm deep.
6. Roll the rest for a lid; seal, crimp, dock all over with a fork, brush with oil.
7. Bake at 190°C fan 40–45 minutes until deep gold and crackled.

## Plating & Service
Cut small squares, warm or room temperature — perfect aperitivo, picnic tender, or crew standby.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan without pancetta and with 40 g toasted pine nuts + 15 g miso for the cheese's depth. Keeps 3 days, revives at 160°C.
