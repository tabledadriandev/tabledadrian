# Chestnut Tagliatelle with Porcini, Pancetta and Rosemary

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sweet chestnut-flour ribbons from the Apennine tradition, matched with porcini and crisp pancetta — an autumn charter dish with mountain soul.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- 00 flour — 220 g
- chestnut flour — 90 g
- whole eggs — 3
- fresh porcini (or 30 g dried, soaked) — 300 g
- pancetta, lardons — 100 g
- garlic — 2 cloves
- rosemary — 2 g
- butter — 50 g
- dry Marsala or white wine — 40 ml
- Parmigiano Reggiano — 50 g
- black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Knead both flours with eggs to a smooth mocha-toned dough; rest 1 hour.
2. Roll to 0.9 mm, cut 8 mm ribbons, nest on semolina.
3. Crisp pancetta in a dry pan; lift out, leaving the fat.
4. Sear thick-sliced porcini hard in the fat with butter, 4 minutes, until golden. Add garlic and rosemary for the last minute.
5. Deglaze with Marsala; add 100 ml pasta water.
6. Boil tagliatelle 90 seconds; finish in the pan 1 minute with the cheese, tossing to gloss.
7. Return pancetta; one turn of pepper.

## Plating & Service
Autumnal and tumbled, the best porcini caps reserved for the top, rosemary flowers if aboard.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian: skip pancetta, brown the mushrooms in butter and add 5 ml soy for depth. Chestnut flour dulls quickly — buy small, keep frozen.
