# Sea Bream all'Acqua Pazza with Datterini and Wild Fennel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> 'Crazy water' — fillets poached in a fast broth of tomatoes, garlic, wine and their own gelatine; ten honest minutes to one of Italy's great fish dishes.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- sea bream fillets, skin on — 4 × 160 g
- datterini tomatoes, halved — 300 g
- garlic, sliced — 3 cloves
- dry white wine — 120 ml
- water or light fish stock — 200 ml
- extra virgin olive oil — 70 ml
- chilli — 1 g
- wild fennel or fennel fronds — 6 g
- parsley — 6 g
- fine salt and black pepper — to taste

## Method
1. Warm 50 ml oil with garlic and chilli in a wide sauté pan until fragrant, never brown.
2. Add tomatoes cut-side down; blister 3 minutes, crushing a few.
3. Pour in wine; reduce by half. Add water/stock; simmer 5 minutes to a loose, brothy sauce; season.
4. Nestle in the fillets skin-up; spoon broth over; lid ajar.
5. Poach at a gentle simmer 6–8 minutes until the flesh flakes at 52°C.
6. Rest 2 minutes; finish with fennel, parsley and remaining raw oil.

## Plating & Service
Shallow bowls, fillet centred, broth and tomatoes around, bread mandatory for the juices.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Works with any white fish or even whole small fish (add 6 minutes). A handful of olives and capers pushes it toward the Aeolians.
