# Bucatini all'Amatriciana

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Lazio benchmark — guanciale fat, tomato and pecorino sharpened with a splash of wine vinegar, sauce clinging inside the hollow strands.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- bucatini — 400 g
- guanciale, 1 cm strips — 150 g
- San Marzano tinned tomatoes, hand-crushed — 500 g
- Pecorino Romano, grated — 70 g
- dried chilli — 1 g
- white wine vinegar — 10 ml
- fine salt — to taste

## Method
1. Render guanciale from cold over medium heat until crisp-edged, 8 minutes. Lift out half for garnish.
2. Add chilli to the fat, then vinegar; let it flash off.
3. Add tomatoes; simmer 20 minutes until the fat re-emerges glossy on the surface. Season carefully.
4. Boil bucatini to 2 minutes under the packet time in salted water.
5. Finish the pasta in the sauce with a ladle of its water, tossing until it grips.
6. Off the heat, rain in the pecorino, tossing to a rosy cream.

## Plating & Service
High twirl, reserved crisp guanciale on top, more pecorino at the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Onion and garlic are hotly debated in Amatrice — omit both for orthodoxy. Turkey guanciale exists for halal tables; or smoked aubergine for vegetarians.
