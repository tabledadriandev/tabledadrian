# Roasted Grape and Taleggio Toast, Vin Santo Air

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Molten taleggio and blistered grapes on charred sourdough, under a boozy Vin Santo foam — rustic Italy with one modernist flourish.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Sourdough baguette, 1 cm slices — 12
- Olive oil — 25 ml
- Taleggio — 150 g
- Red grapes — 200 g
- Balsamic vinegar — 10 ml
- Thyme — 2 g
- Vin Santo — 80 ml
- Water — 80 ml
- Caster sugar — 8 g
- Soy lecithin — 1 g (0.6% of the Vin Santo base)
- Black pepper — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Roast grapes with balsamic, thyme and a little oil at 200 °C for 12 minutes until blistered.
2. Brush bread with oil; grill hard on one side.
3. Top each slice with 12 g taleggio; melt under the salamander 2 minutes.
4. Warm Vin Santo, water and sugar to 45 °C; add lecithin (0.6%).
5. Froth with a stick blender at the surface until a coarse-bubbled air holds; rest 60 seconds.
6. Crown each toast with 2 roasted grapes and their juices.
7. Spoon Vin Santo air over; crack pepper.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm on olive-wood boards, foam still trembling. Pour the rest of the Vin Santo for the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: cashew "taleggio" and the same air (lecithin is soy). Figs replace grapes beautifully in late August.
