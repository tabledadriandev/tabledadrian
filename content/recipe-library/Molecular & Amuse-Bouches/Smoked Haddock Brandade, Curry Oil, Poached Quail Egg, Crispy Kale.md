# Smoked Haddock Brandade, Curry Oil, Poached Quail Egg, Crispy Kale

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Kedgeree's soul in fine-dining clothes — warm smoked haddock brandade, split curry oil, a barely-poached quail egg and shattering kale.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Smoked haddock, undyed — 300 g
- Whole milk — 300 ml
- Bay leaf — 1
- Potato, cooked, riced — 180 g
- Olive oil — 50 ml
- Double cream — 40 ml
- Madras curry powder — 6 g
- Grapeseed oil — 80 ml
- Quail eggs — 8
- White vinegar — 20 ml
- Kale leaves, stripped — 60 g
- Fine salt — 4 g

## Method
1. Poach haddock in milk with bay at 80 °C for 8 minutes; drain, flake.
2. Beat warm fish with potato, olive oil, cream to a loose brandade; season; hold at 60 °C.
3. Toast curry powder 60 seconds; steep in 60 °C grapeseed oil 15 minutes; strain to a golden split oil.
4. Toss kale with a film of oil and salt; bake at 150 °C for 10–12 minutes until glass-crisp.
5. Poach quail eggs 65 seconds in simmering vinegared water; trim.
6. Spoon brandade into warm bowls; nest 2 eggs per serving.
7. Spoon curry oil around; stand kale shards upright.

## Plating & Service
Serve hot; the yolk and curry oil marble into the brandade as guests break in. Comfort, sharpened.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free: oat milk poach and olive-oil-only brandade. Smoked trout or cod work; add a mango-lime fluid gel (0.85% agar) for lift.
