# Comté Custard Profiterole, Vin Jaune Glaze

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Jura in a bite: molten 24-month Comté cream inside choux, lacquered with a nutty vin jaune agar glaze.

## Ingredients (serves 14)
- Choux paste — 250 g (water 125 ml, butter 50 g, flour 75 g, eggs 2)
- Comté 24 months, grated — 150 g
- Whole milk — 150 ml
- Double cream — 80 ml
- Egg yolks — 2 (36 g)
- Cornflour — 6 g
- Fine salt — 1.5 g
- Vin jaune (or dry oloroso) — 90 ml
- Honey — 10 g
- Agar-agar — 0.8 g (0.8% of the wine glaze)
- Walnut halves, toasted, chopped — 20 g

## Method
1. Pipe and bake 14 choux at 180 °C for 20–22 minutes; cool.
2. Heat milk and cream to 80 °C; melt in Comté off heat; blend smooth.
3. Whisk yolks with cornflour; temper with the cheese cream; cook to 82 °C to a thick custard; season; chill.
4. Warm vin jaune and honey; add agar (0.8%); boil 45 seconds; hold at 45 °C.
5. Pierce choux bases; pipe the Comté custard full.
6. Dip tops in the warm glaze; it sets satin in 60 seconds.
7. Press chopped walnuts into the glaze rim.

## Plating & Service
Serve at room temperature within the hour; a 20-second flash at 180 °C revives the choux if humid.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free without walnuts. Gruyère + fino works; GF choux with rice flour holds the custard fine.
