# Watermelon Nigiri, Rice Vinegar Air

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Compressed, slow-baked watermelon turns dense and tuna-like — a vegan nigiri that fools the eye and delights at the raw bar.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Watermelon, seedless block — 600 g
- Tamari — 25 ml
- Toasted sesame oil — 8 ml
- Rice vinegar — 100 ml
- Water — 100 ml
- Caster sugar — 10 g
- Soy lecithin — 1.2 g (0.6% of vinegar liquid)
- Sushi rice, cooked and seasoned — 240 g
- Wasabi paste — 8 g
- Black sesame — 4 g

## Method
1. Cut watermelon into a 3 cm slab; vacuum-compress with tamari and sesame oil (or press weighted 2 h).
2. Bake at 110 °C for 2 h, turning once, until the slab is dense, translucent and sashimi-textured; chill.
3. Slice against the grain into 12 nigiri tops, 6 × 3 cm.
4. Warm vinegar, water, sugar to 45 °C; add lecithin (0.6%), blitz surface to a fine air; rest 1 minute.
5. Form 20 g rice pillows with wet hands; a dot of wasabi on each.
6. Drape watermelon over rice; press gently.
7. Spoon vinegar air over one end; scatter black sesame.

## Plating & Service
Serve on a chilled slab, two per guest at the raw-bar station; air collapses in ~3 minutes, so foam à la minute.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Low-carb: serve watermelon "sashimi" alone over daikon threads. Swap air for a yuzu-ponzu fluid gel (0.9% agar) in windy deck service.
