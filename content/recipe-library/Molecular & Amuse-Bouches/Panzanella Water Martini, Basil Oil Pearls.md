# Panzanella Water Martini, Basil Oil Pearls

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Tuscan salad clarified into a crystal martini with sunken basil-oil pearls and a crouton rim — drink your salad, elegantly.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Ripe tomatoes — 800 g
- Cucumber — 150 g
- Red pepper — 100 g
- Red wine vinegar — 20 ml
- Fine salt — 8 g
- Stale sourdough — 60 g
- Olive oil — 20 ml
- Basil leaves — 25 g
- Grapeseed oil — 80 ml
- Agar-agar — 1.4 g (0.9% of the water phase for pearls: 100 ml tomato water + oil)
- Extra basil water — 60 ml

## Method
1. Blend tomatoes, cucumber, pepper, vinegar, salt; hang in muslin overnight — collect ~500 ml clear panzanella water.
2. Blitz basil with warm (60 °C) grapeseed oil; strain through a coffee filter to a green oil.
3. Boil 100 ml of the clear water with agar (0.9%); whisk in 40 ml basil oil off heat; drip from a syringe into 4 °C cold oil for emerald pearls; strain, rinse.
4. Toast sourdough cubes in olive oil until deep gold; blitz to a coarse crumb with salt.
5. Wet martini-glass rims; dip in crouton crumb.
6. Chill panzanella water to 4 °C; adjust salt and vinegar.
7. Pour 45 ml per glass; sink a spoonful of basil pearls into each.

## Plating & Service
Serve as a "first sip" course. The pearls burst under the tongue as the crumb rim delivers the missing bread.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF crumb rim. Add a whisper of vodka for an actual bloody-Mary-adjacent apéritif; celery salt rim variant.
