# Cured Duck Breast, Cherry Fluid Gel, Pistachio Soil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Quick-cured, sous-vide then seared duck sliced thin as charcuterie, with a port-cherry gel and green pistachio soil — a starter that eats like a landscape.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Duck breast, skin on — 1 large (350 g)
- Fine salt — 10 g
- Caster sugar — 6 g
- Juniper, crushed — 2 g
- Orange zest — 2 g
- Cherries, pitted — 200 g
- Ruby port — 50 ml
- Caster sugar — 15 g
- Agar-agar — 1.9 g (0.85% of the cherry liquid)
- Pistachios — 60 g
- Panko or GF crumbs — 20 g
- Olive oil — 10 ml
- Watercress — 30 g

## Method
1. Cure duck with salt, 6 g sugar, juniper, zest for 2 h; rinse, dry.
2. Vacuum-seal; cook sous-vide at 57.0 °C for 90 minutes; chill.
3. Simmer cherries, port, 15 g sugar 8 minutes; blend, strain (~220 ml); boil with agar (0.85%) 60 seconds; set; blend to fluid gel.
4. Blitz pistachios with crumbs, oil, pinch of salt; toast at 150 °C for 10 minutes to a green soil.
5. Sear duck skin-side in a dry pan 3–4 minutes to render and crisp; rest 5.
6. Slice 3 mm, arrange 5 slices per plate.
7. Pipe cherry gel commas, scatter pistachio soil, dress watercress.

## Plating & Service
Serve just warm. The gel replaces a jus — nothing runs, everything clings; ideal for deck dining.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free soil: pumpkin seed. Winter: blackberry-red wine gel at the same 0.85% agar. GF confirmed with GF crumbs.
