# Universal Fluid Gel Matrix — Savoury and Sweet

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> One technique, every plate: boil, set, blend — a sauce that holds a drawn line on a moving yacht and never weeps.

## Ingredients (serves 20)
- Flavoured liquid (juice, stock, purée thinned pourable) — 500 ml
- Agar-agar — 4.25 g (0.85% — the house default)
- Fine salt or sugar — to season
- Acid (lemon/vinegar) — to finish, added late
- Optional: locust bean gum — 0.5 g (0.1% for elasticity, anti-syneresis)
- Optional gloss: neutral oil — 10 ml

## Method
1. Season the liquid fully first — gelling mutes salt and acid slightly.
2. Whisk in agar (0.85%); bring to a full boil for 60–90 seconds (agar must reach 90 °C+ to hydrate).
3. Very acidic liquids (pH < 3): boil the agar in a neutral portion, then whisk in the acid off heat.
4. Cast into a shallow tray; set 20–30 minutes at 4 °C to a firm slab.
5. Blend the slab 60–90 seconds to a glossy, thick sauce; add a splash of base liquid to adjust flow.
6. Pass through a fine sieve — non-negotiable for a lacquer finish.
7. Store bottled up to 5 days; serves cold or warm to 60 °C (agar is heat-stable there).

## Plating & Service
Pipe dots, pull lines, hide under proteins. Ratio ladder: 0.6% = loose nappe, 0.85% = piping gel, 1.1% = firm dots that stand.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy liquids: same ratios. For a shiny "glaze" over frozen items, dip at 1.0% agar held at 55–60 °C.
