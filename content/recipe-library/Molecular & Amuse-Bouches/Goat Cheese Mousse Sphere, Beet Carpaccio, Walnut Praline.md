# Goat Cheese Mousse Sphere, Beet Carpaccio, Walnut Praline

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A trembling reverse-spherified goat cheese heart on rose-windows of beet carpaccio — the classic salad, staged as jewellery.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Fresh goat cheese — 180 g
- Double cream — 70 ml
- Honey — 10 g
- Fine salt — 2.5 g
- Calcium lactate gluconate — 5.2 g (2.0% of the cheese base)
- Sodium alginate — 5 g (0.5% bath in 1000 ml low-calcium water)
- Beetroots, mixed colours — 3 (450 g)
- Sherry vinegar — 20 ml
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Walnuts — 60 g
- Caster sugar — 50 g
- Baby chard — 20 g

## Method
1. Blend goat cheese, cream, honey, salt to a thick pourable base; shear in calcium lactate gluconate (2.0%); rest 1 h.
2. Rest the 0.5% alginate bath overnight.
3. Roast beets in foil at 190 °C for 50–60 minutes; peel; shave 1.5 mm rounds on a mandoline; dress with vinegar, oil, salt.
4. Freeze cheese base in 35 mm hemispheres.
5. Caramelise sugar to 175 °C; add walnuts; cool; chop to praline.
6. Bathe frozen domes 3.5 minutes at 20 °C, spooning bath over; rinse; rest 15 minutes to thaw to liquid centre.
7. Fan beet carpaccio; centre one sphere; scatter praline and chard; finish with oil.

## Plating & Service
Warn the table: pierce the sphere and drag beet through the flowing cheese. Serve at cool room temperature.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: sunflower praline. Sheep's curd or gorgonzola dolce (thinned) both sphere at the same 2.0%/0.5% ratios.
