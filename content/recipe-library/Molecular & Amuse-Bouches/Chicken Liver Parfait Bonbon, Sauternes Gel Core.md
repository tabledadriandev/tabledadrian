# Chicken Liver Parfait Bonbon, Sauternes Gel Core

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A chocolate-glossy bonbon that turns out to be silken parfait with a liquid Sauternes heart — the best kind of deception.

## Ingredients (serves 14)
- Chicken livers, cleaned — 250 g
- Eggs — 2
- Butter, melted warm — 200 g
- Port — 40 ml
- Brandy — 20 ml
- Shallot, sliced — 40 g
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Sauternes — 100 ml
- Agar-agar — 0.6 g (0.6% — a tender, spoon-soft gel)
- Cocoa butter — 100 g
- Dark chocolate (85%) — 40 g

## Method
1. Reduce port, brandy, shallot to a syrup (~20 ml); cool.
2. Blend livers, eggs, reduction, salt; stream in warm butter; pass twice through a chinois.
3. Cook in a terrine sous-vide at 64 °C for 45 minutes (or bain-marie to 64 °C core); chill overnight.
4. Boil Sauternes with agar (0.6%) 60 seconds; set in a tray, then blend to a thick fluid gel; freeze in 10 mm sphere moulds.
5. Whip the parfait smooth; enrobe each frozen Sauternes core in 20 g parfait, roll into balls; freeze 1 h.
6. Melt cocoa butter with chocolate to 40 °C.
7. Dip frozen bonbons on a fork; the shell snaps set in seconds. Temper 2 h in the fridge before serving (core thaws liquid).

## Plating & Service
Serve on a gold-leaf-flecked stone, one each, at cellar temperature — the shell cracks to warm parfait and a rush of sweet wine.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Kosher-style/halal adaptation: duck-fat-free fig gel core and no fortified wine (verjus + honey gel at 0.6% agar).
