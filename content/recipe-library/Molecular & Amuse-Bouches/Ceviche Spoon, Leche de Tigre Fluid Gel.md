# Ceviche Spoon, Leche de Tigre Fluid Gel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Classic ceviche where the tiger's milk has been gelled to cling to every cube — no drips on deck, all the acid-punch.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Sea bass fillet, skinless — 240 g
- Lime juice — 120 ml
- Fish trim (from the bass) — 40 g
- Celery — 20 g
- Red onion — 30 g
- Fresh ginger — 5 g
- Ají amarillo paste — 12 g
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Agar-agar — 1.3 g (0.8% of the strained leche de tigre)
- Red onion, shaved — 20 g
- Sweet potato, cooked, 5 mm dice — 80 g
- Coriander leaves — 12

## Method
1. Blend lime juice, fish trim, celery, 30 g onion, ginger, ají amarillo, salt with 3 ice cubes; strain — about 160 ml leche de tigre.
2. Boil the liquid with agar (0.8%) for 45 seconds only (preserve acidity); set 30 minutes at 4 °C.
3. Blend the set gel to a glossy fluid gel; sieve; chill.
4. Dice bass into 1.5 cm cubes; salt lightly 5 minutes.
5. Fold fish through the fluid gel until every face is coated.
6. Spoon 2 cubes onto each chilled spoon with a cube of sweet potato.
7. Garnish with shaved onion and coriander.

## Plating & Service
Serve on crushed ice within 15 minutes of dressing — the fish should still taste raw-fresh at the centre.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Scallop or amberjack sub perfectly. For heat lovers, a dot of rocoto paste under the fish; keep the gel at 0.8% agar regardless.
