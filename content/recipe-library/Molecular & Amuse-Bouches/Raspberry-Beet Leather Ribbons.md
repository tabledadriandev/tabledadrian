# Raspberry-Beet Leather Ribbons

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Glossy garnet ribbons that tie, twist and bow — a fruit-vegetable leather engineered pliable for wrapping mousses and crowning desserts.

## Ingredients (serves 14)
- Raspberries — 250 g
- Cooked beetroot — 150 g
- Caster sugar — 50 g
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Glucose syrup — 20 g (the pliability agent)
- Fine salt — 0.5 g
- Cornflour — 3 g

## Method
1. Blend raspberries, beetroot, sugar, lemon, glucose, salt until totally smooth.
2. Pass through a fine sieve to remove every seed.
3. Whisk in cornflour; warm to 80 °C for 2 minutes (light bind).
4. Spread 1.5 mm thin on silicone with an offset palette.
5. Dehydrate at 60 °C for 5–6 h — it should peel cleanly but still bend without cracking.
6. Cut ribbons 2 × 20 cm with a pizza wheel while warm.
7. Store rolled in parchment, airtight; re-soften 30 seconds over steam if they stiffen.

## Plating & Service
Tie around goat-cheese mousse "gifts", weave over panna cotta, or stand in loops on a chocolate crémeux.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Mango-carrot ribbons (same glucose trick) for golden plates. Savoury: tomato-strawberry leather with 1 g salt — startling with burrata.
