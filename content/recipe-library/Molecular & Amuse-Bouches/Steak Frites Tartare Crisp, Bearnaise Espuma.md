# Steak Frites Tartare Crisp, Béarnaise Espuma

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Steak frites flattened into one bite — beef tartare on a potato-glass crisp with warm siphon béarnaise; bistro nostalgia, tasting-menu manners.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Beef fillet — 200 g
- Potato, large — 1 (250 g)
- Clarified butter — 30 g
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Shallot brunoise — 20 g
- Capers, chopped — 12 g
- Dijon — 6 g
- Chives — 4 g
- Egg yolks — 3 (55 g)
- Tarragon vinegar reduction (vinegar 40 ml, shallot 15 g, tarragon stalks, reduced to 15 ml) — 15 ml
- Butter, melted warm — 150 g
- Tarragon leaves, chopped — 3 g

## Method
1. Slice potato lengthwise 1.5 mm; lay strips overlapping on a buttered tray, brush with clarified butter, salt; press with a second tray.
2. Bake at 170 °C for 18–22 minutes to amber glass; cut into 12 rectangles while warm.
3. Whisk yolks with the reduction over a bain-marie to 72 °C ribbon stage.
4. Stream in warm melted butter; season; fold in tarragon.
5. Load a 0.5 L siphon, one charge; hold at 55 °C.
6. Hand-cut beef 4 mm; dress with shallot, capers, Dijon, chives, salt.
7. Mound 15 g tartare on each crisp; siphon a béarnaise cloud alongside on the plate.

## Plating & Service
Assemble at the pass; the crisp holds 5 minutes under tartare. Serve with the béarnaise still steaming.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-light: olive-oil sabayon instead of butter béarnaise. The potato-glass technique carries any tartare — tuna, veal, beet.
