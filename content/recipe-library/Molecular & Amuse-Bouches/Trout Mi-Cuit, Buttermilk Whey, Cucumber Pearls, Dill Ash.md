# Trout Mi-Cuit, Buttermilk Whey, Cucumber Pearls, Dill Ash

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> 45 °C trout that reads raw but flakes cooked, in a pool of lemon buttermilk whey with basic-spherified cucumber caviar and a black dill ash rim.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Sea trout fillet, skinless — 400 g
- Fine salt — 8 g
- Olive oil — 20 ml
- Buttermilk — 300 ml
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Cucumber juice, strained — 150 ml
- Sodium alginate — 1.5 g (1.0% of cucumber juice)
- Calcium chloride — 5 g in 1000 ml water (0.5% bath)
- Dill — 40 g
- Dill fronds — 8
- Trout roe — 40 g

## Method
1. Salt trout portions 15 minutes; rinse, dry; oil and vacuum-seal.
2. Cook sous-vide at 45.0 °C for 20–25 minutes (mi-cuit); rest 3 minutes.
3. Warm buttermilk with lemon and 2 g salt to 70 °C until it splits; strain — keep the clear whey; chill.
4. Blend cucumber juice with alginate (1.0%); rest 2 h; drip into the 0.5% calcium bath 60 seconds; rinse.
5. Dehydrate half the dill at 70 °C for 1 h; burn the rest in a dry pan; blitz both to a green-black ash.
6. Break trout into large warm flakes in shallow bowls; rim one side with dill ash.
7. Pour 60 ml chilled whey around; spoon cucumber pearls and roe over; dill fronds.

## Plating & Service
Warm fish, icy whey — serve the moment the pour finishes. The pearls should drift like green roe.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Arctic char and salmon take identical timing. Dairy-free: substitute clarified cucumber-kefir water or a light dashi.
