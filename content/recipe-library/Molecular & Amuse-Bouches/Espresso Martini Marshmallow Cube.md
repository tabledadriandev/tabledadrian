# Espresso Martini Marshmallow Cube

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The after-dinner cocktail as a pillowy cube — vodka-coffee marshmallow rolled in cocoa-espresso dust; serve with the actual martini for maximum effect.

## Ingredients (serves 16)
- Fresh espresso — 100 ml
- Coffee liqueur — 40 ml
- Vodka — 25 ml
- Caster sugar — 160 g
- Glucose syrup — 40 g
- Water — 40 ml
- Gelatine leaves (gold) — 14 g (about 5% of total liquid — firm marshmallow set)
- Cocoa powder — 15 g
- Finely ground espresso beans — 5 g
- Cornflour — 15 g

## Method
1. Bloom gelatine in iced water.
2. Boil sugar, glucose and the 40 ml water to 118 °C.
3. Pour the syrup slowly into the espresso in a stand mixer on low; add squeezed gelatine; then liqueur and vodka.
4. Whip on high 8–10 minutes to a thick, glossy, barely-warm mousse.
5. Spread 2.5 cm deep in an oiled, clingfilmed frame; set 4 h at room temperature.
6. Mix cocoa, ground espresso, cornflour; cut marshmallow into cubes with an oiled knife.
7. Roll cubes in the dust; shake off excess; hold airtight up to 24 h.

## Plating & Service
Serve one cube on a saucer beside espresso or the cocktail itself; it dissolves into a boozy coffee mousse mid-palate.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Zero-proof: replace liqueur/vodka with more espresso + 5 ml vanilla. Vegan set: aquafaba whip with 1.0% agar syrup — softer, serve chilled.
