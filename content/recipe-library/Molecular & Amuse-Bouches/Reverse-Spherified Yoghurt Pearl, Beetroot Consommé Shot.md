# Reverse-Spherified Yoghurt Pearl, Beetroot Consommé Shot

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A giant yoghurt sphere that stays liquid inside indefinitely (reverse spherification never over-sets) submerged in ruby-clear beetroot consommé — theatre in a shot glass.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Greek yoghurt (10% fat) — 250 g
- Double cream — 50 ml
- Calcium lactate gluconate — 6 g (2.0% of yoghurt base)
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Sodium alginate — 5 g (0.5% bath)
- Still mineral water (low calcium) — 1000 ml
- Raw beetroot, juiced — 500 ml
- Sherry vinegar — 10 ml
- Agar-agar — 2.5 g (0.5% for clarification)
- Fresh dill tips — 10 sprigs

## Method
1. Whisk yoghurt, cream, salt and calcium lactate gluconate (2.0%) until smooth; rest 1 h chilled.
2. Blend alginate into the water (0.5% bath), rest 4 h or overnight to de-aerate.
3. Clarify the beetroot: bring juice + vinegar to 90 °C with agar (0.5%), set in fridge, then freeze, and thaw overnight through muslin — collect crystal-clear consommé.
4. Using a 30 ml hemisphere spoon, lower yoghurt portions gently into the alginate bath; bathe 3 minutes, spooning bath over the top.
5. Lift each sphere, rinse in clean water 30 seconds; hold submerged in a little consommé (spheres keep 24 h).
6. Season consommé, chill to 6 °C, pour 40 ml into each shot glass.
7. Slide one sphere into each glass; garnish with a dill tip.

## Plating & Service
Instruct guests to take it in one go — the sphere ruptures into the earthy-sweet consommé. Serve at 6 °C.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: use coconut yoghurt (add 2.0% calcium lactate gluconate as it contains none). Swap beetroot for clarified tomato water in summer.
