# Tomato Tonnato, Basil Air, Caper Crumble

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Vitello tonnato with slow-roasted tomato standing in for the veal — the anchovy-tuna cream and a basil air make it lighter, stranger, better.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Plum tomatoes — 6 (600 g)
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Caster sugar — 6 g
- Tinned tuna in oil, drained — 120 g
- Anchovy fillets — 4 (16 g)
- Egg yolk — 1 (18 g)
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Grapeseed oil — 100 ml
- Capers, fried crisp — 30 g
- Basil — 20 g
- Water — 150 ml
- Soy lecithin — 0.9 g (0.6% of basil water)

## Method
1. Halve tomatoes; season with salt, sugar, olive oil; roast at 120 °C for 50–60 minutes to concentrated petals.
2. Blend tuna, anchovy, yolk, lemon; stream in grapeseed oil to a spoonable tonnato; season.
3. Fry capers at 180 °C for 30 seconds until they flower; drain.
4. Blanch basil 10 seconds; blend with the 150 ml water; strain.
5. Add lecithin (0.6%); froth cold with a stick blender; rest 1 minute.
6. Swipe tonnato across each plate; arrange warm tomato petals.
7. Spoon basil air over; scatter crisp capers.

## Plating & Service
Serve the tomatoes warm against cool cream. A starter that converts tonnato skeptics in one bite.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Classic: add seared veal slices. Egg-free: 0.3% xanthan emulsion instead of yolk mayo-style base.
