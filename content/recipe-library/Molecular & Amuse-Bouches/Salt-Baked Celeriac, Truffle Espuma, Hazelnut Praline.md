# Salt-Baked Celeriac, Truffle Espuma, Hazelnut Praline

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A whole celeriac baked in a salt shell, carved tableside like a roast, with black-truffle siphon foam — vegetable cookery with main-course swagger.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Celeriac, whole — 1 (800 g)
- Coarse salt — 1000 g
- Egg whites — 100 g
- Whole milk — 200 ml
- Double cream — 100 ml
- Butter — 30 g
- Black truffle, chopped — 12 g
- Fine salt — 3 g
- Gelatine leaf — 1.5 g (0.45% of the foam base)
- Hazelnuts — 50 g
- Caster sugar — 40 g
- Chives — 4 g

## Method
1. Mix coarse salt with egg whites; encase the scrubbed celeriac completely.
2. Bake at 200 °C for 90–100 minutes until a skewer meets no resistance; rest 15 minutes.
3. Warm milk, cream, butter with truffle and fine salt; steep 15 minutes; blend, strain.
4. Dissolve bloomed gelatine (0.45%) in the warm base; siphon, 2 charges; hold 60 °C.
5. Caramelise sugar to 175 °C; stir in hazelnuts; cool on silicone; chop to praline.
6. Crack the salt crust at the table; carve 2 cm wedges.
7. Plate wedges, siphon truffle espuma over, scatter praline and chives.

## Plating & Service
The crust-crack is the show — do it in the saloon, plate at the sideboard. Serve piping hot.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: oat cream foam with 0.3% xanthan, no gelatine. Kohlrabi or beetroot take the same salt shell (reduce to 70 min).
