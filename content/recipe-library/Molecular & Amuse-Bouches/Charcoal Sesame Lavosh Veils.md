# Charcoal Sesame Lavosh Veils

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Jet-black, paper-thin cracker veils that curve like torn silk — rolled to translucency and baked over crumpled foil for movement.

## Ingredients (serves 14)
- Strong flour — 150 g
- Water — 70 ml
- Olive oil — 20 ml
- Fine salt — 3 g
- Activated charcoal powder — 4 g
- Caster sugar — 3 g
- White sesame seeds — 10 g
- Sea salt flakes — 2 g

## Method
1. Knead flour, water, oil, fine salt, sugar, charcoal 5 minutes to a smooth black dough; rest 30 minutes wrapped.
2. Roll through a pasta machine to setting 8 — you should read print through it.
3. Lay sheets over crumpled foil hills on a tray (this bakes in the waves).
4. Mist with water; scatter sesame and salt flakes.
5. Bake at 160 °C for 8–10 minutes until matte and rigid.
6. Cool on the foil; snap into dramatic torn shards.
7. Store airtight 5 days with silica.

## Plating & Service
Lean over tartares, dips and cheese mousses for instant height; the black frames any bright purée.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: rice + tapioca flour blend (add 2 g psyllium). Beet powder (6 g) instead of charcoal for a magenta version.
