# Tomato Water Veil Sheets

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Ghost-thin, transparent gel sheets that drape over crudo or vegetables like silk — the veil ratio that flexes without tearing.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Clear tomato water (from 1 kg tomatoes, hung overnight) — 400 ml
- Fine salt — 3 g
- Caster sugar — 2 g
- Agar-agar — 3.8 g (0.95%)
- Locust bean gum — 0.4 g (0.1% — the flex agent; without it veils crack)
- Sherry vinegar — 4 ml

## Method
1. Season the tomato water with salt, sugar, vinegar.
2. Whisk in agar (0.95%) and LBG (0.1%); boil 90 seconds.
3. Skim any foam — a veil shows every flaw.
4. Pour onto a dead-level flat tray to 1.5 mm depth (use a heat gun to pop bubbles).
5. Set 15 minutes at room temperature, then 15 chilled.
6. Cut squares or circles with a wet knife or cutter; lift with an offset palette knife.
7. Drape over the dish; the veil should hug every contour. Holds 24 h layered between oiled parchment.

## Plating & Service
Over scallop crudo, burrata, or a whole tomato salad — serve backlit; the guest lifts the veil and the aroma escapes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Any clear liquid works at 0.95% + 0.1%: dashi, consommé, beet juice, elderflower. For a warm dish, veils hold to ~60 °C.
