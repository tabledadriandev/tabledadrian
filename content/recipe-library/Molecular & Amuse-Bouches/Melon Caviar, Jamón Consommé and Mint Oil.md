# Melon Caviar, Jamón Consommé and Mint Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Melón con jamón inverted — the ham becomes a crystal broth, the melon becomes caviar; familiar flavours, alien textures.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Cantaloupe juice, strained — 200 ml
- Sodium alginate — 2 g (1.0% of melon juice)
- Calcium chloride — 5 g in 1000 ml water (0.5% bath)
- Jamón serrano offcuts — 150 g
- Water — 600 ml
- Gelatine leaf — 3 g (for freeze-thaw clarification, 0.5%)
- Mint leaves — 20 g
- Grapeseed oil — 60 ml
- Sea salt flakes — 1 g

## Method
1. Simmer jamón in the water at 85 °C for 40 minutes; strain — do not boil (fat clouds it).
2. Dissolve bloomed gelatine (0.5%) in the warm broth; freeze solid, then thaw through muslin in the fridge overnight for a perfectly clear consommé.
3. Blend melon juice with alginate (1.0%); rest 2 h.
4. Drip into the 0.5% calcium bath, 60–75 seconds; rinse.
5. Blitz mint with 60 °C oil for 45 seconds; strain through coffee filter for a green oil.
6. Chill consommé to 6 °C; check seasoning (the ham salts it).
7. Pour 40 ml consommé per glass, spoon in melon caviar, finish with 4 drops mint oil and a salt flake.

## Plating & Service
Serve icy in cocktail coupes — pink-amber broth, orange pearls, emerald beads of oil.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian: smoked kombu-shiitake "ham" broth clarified the same way. Watermelon caviar (1.0% alginate) for high summer.
