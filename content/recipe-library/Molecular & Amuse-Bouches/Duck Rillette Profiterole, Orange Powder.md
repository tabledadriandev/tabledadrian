# Duck Rillette Profiterole, Orange Powder

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Duck à l'orange in canapé form: silky rillettes in warm choux with a maltodextrin orange-oil powder that melts back to sauce.

## Ingredients (serves 14)
- Choux paste — 250 g (water 125 ml, butter 50 g, flour 75 g, eggs 2)
- Duck leg confit, picked — 250 g
- Duck fat, soft — 60 g
- Orange zest — 3 g
- Sherry vinegar — 8 ml
- Black pepper — 1 g
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Orange oil (zest of 2 oranges infused in 50 ml grapeseed oil at 60 °C, strained) — 50 g
- Tapioca maltodextrin — 30 g (60% of the oil weight)
- Icing sugar — 5 g
- Chervil — 14 pluches

## Method
1. Pipe and bake 14 small choux at 180 °C for 20–22 minutes; cool.
2. Beat picked confit with soft duck fat, zest, vinegar, salt, pepper to a rough rillette.
3. Warm the orange oil gently to 30 °C.
4. Whisk maltodextrin (60% of oil weight) with icing sugar; work in the oil to a silky powder; sieve.
5. Split choux; pipe rillettes into the bases.
6. Flash in a 180 °C oven 3 minutes to warm through; cap.
7. Spoon a dune of orange powder beside each; chervil on top.

## Plating & Service
Instruct guests to drag the warm profiterole through the powder — it liquefies into orange oil on contact with the tongue.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Works with rabbit or pork rillettes; GF choux with rice flour. The powder technique fits any aromatic oil in the pantry.
