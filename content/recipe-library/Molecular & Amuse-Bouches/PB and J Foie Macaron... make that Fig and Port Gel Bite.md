# Roquefort Cheesecake Cube, Fig-Port Gel Sheet

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A savoury cheesecake cube wrapped in a glass-thin fig and port gel veil — the cheese course compressed into a single canapé.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Roquefort — 100 g
- Cream cheese — 150 g
- Double cream — 50 ml
- Gelatine leaves (gold) — 3 g (1.0% of the cheese mass)
- Walnut praline, chopped — 30 g
- Fig purée — 150 g
- Ruby port — 50 ml
- Caster sugar — 10 g
- Agar-agar — 1.9 g (0.95% of fig-port liquid — flexible veil with the fruit pectin)
- Micro thyme — 12 sprigs

## Method
1. Beat Roquefort and cream cheese smooth; warm the cream to 60 °C, dissolve bloomed gelatine (1.0%), beat in.
2. Set in a 2 cm-deep frame lined with cling film, 4 h at 4 °C; cut into 12 cubes.
3. Warm fig purée, port, sugar; add agar (0.95%); boil 60 seconds.
4. Pour paper-thin (1.5 mm) onto a flat tray; set 15 minutes.
5. Cut gel sheets into squares just larger than the cubes.
6. Drape one veil over each cube, tucking the corners under.
7. Press a pinch of walnut praline on top; finish with micro thyme.

## Plating & Service
Serve at cool room temperature with a glass of the same port. The veil should glow backlit on a bright plate.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: seed brittle. Swap Roquefort for aged goat cheese and the veil for membrillo-sherry (same 0.95% agar).
