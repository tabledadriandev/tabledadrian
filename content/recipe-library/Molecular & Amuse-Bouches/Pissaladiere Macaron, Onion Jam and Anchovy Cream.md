# Pissaladière Macaron, Onion Jam and Anchovy Cream

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Nice's onion tart disguised as a patisserie macaron — savoury olive shells, slow onion jam, whipped anchovy cream; a Riviera in-joke.

## Ingredients (serves 14)
- Egg whites — 100 g
- Caster sugar — 90 g
- Icing sugar — 100 g
- Ground sunflower seeds (sifted fine, nut-free "tant pour tant") — 110 g
- Black olive powder (dehydrated tapenade, blitzed) — 8 g
- Onions, thinly sliced — 500 g
- Butter — 30 g
- Thyme — 2 g
- Sherry vinegar — 10 ml
- Anchovy fillets — 25 g
- Cream cheese — 120 g
- Double cream — 40 ml

## Method
1. Dehydrate 60 g tapenade at 70 °C for 6 h; blitz to powder.
2. Whip whites, adding caster sugar gradually, to firm peaks; fold in icing sugar, ground seeds, olive powder; macaronner to a slow ribbon.
3. Pipe 3 cm shells; rest 30 minutes until skinned; bake at 145 °C for 14 minutes; cool on the mat.
4. Cook onions with butter, thyme and a pinch of salt over low heat 45–50 minutes to a deep amber jam; finish with vinegar; cool.
5. Blend anchovies into cream cheese; whip with cream to piping texture.
6. Pipe a ring of anchovy cream on half the shells; fill centres with onion jam.
7. Cap, twist gently, rest 2 h in the fridge; serve at room temperature.

## Plating & Service
Serve on a patisserie stand so guests expect sweet — the umami surprise is the dish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Classic almond shells if nuts are cleared. Vegetarian: swap anchovy for miso + capers in the cream.
