# Olive Oil Caviar on Tomato Bread Crouton

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Pan con tomate rebuilt as a canapé — cold-oil agar pearls of grassy Ligurian oil that pop like caviar over intense tomato bread.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Extra virgin olive oil (for pearls) — 100 ml
- Water — 100 ml
- Agar-agar — 1.8 g (0.9% of the water phase)
- Fine salt — 1 g
- Extra virgin olive oil, chilled to 4 °C (setting column) — 500 ml
- Baguette, 5 mm slices — 12 pieces
- Ripe tomatoes, halved — 300 g
- Garlic clove — 1 (5 g)
- Sea salt flakes — 2 g
- Basil, micro — 12 leaves

## Method
1. Chill the 500 ml setting oil in a tall container in the freezer to 2–4 °C (must be viscous-cold).
2. Boil the 100 ml water with salt and agar (0.9%) for 2 minutes; whisk in the 100 ml olive oil off heat to a warm emulsion (~55 °C).
3. Drip the emulsion from a syringe into the cold oil column; droplets sink and set into pearls before reaching the bottom.
4. Strain pearls, keep chilled (they hold 48 h — agar pearls are thermo-irreversible below 80 °C).
5. Toast baguette slices at 180 °C for 6 minutes until golden.
6. Rub croutons with garlic, then crush tomato halves over, cut-side down; season with sea salt.
7. Spoon a generous quenelle of oil caviar on each crouton; finish with micro basil.

## Plating & Service
Line up on a slate with the pearls glistening; serve within 15 minutes so croutons stay crisp.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: use toasted GF sourdough. Infuse the pearl oil with rosemary or Espelette for variation; a smoked oil version is superb with anchovy.
