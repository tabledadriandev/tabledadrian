# Bloody Mary Gazpacho Sphere, Celery Salt Rim

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Brunch cocktail meets Andalusia: a spiced tomato sphere served in a celery-salt-rimmed spoon with a vodka mist at the table.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Ripe tomatoes — 500 g
- Red pepper — 80 g
- Cucumber — 60 g
- Sherry vinegar — 12 ml
- Tabasco — 2 ml
- Worcestershire-style vegan sauce — 6 ml
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Calcium lactate gluconate — 6 g (2.0% of the strained gazpacho)
- Xanthan gum — 0.6 g (0.2%)
- Sodium alginate — 5 g (0.5% bath in 1000 ml water)
- Celery salt — 5 g
- Vodka in an atomiser — 30 ml

## Method
1. Blend vegetables with vinegar, Tabasco, vegan Worcestershire, salt; strain to a smooth 300 g liquid.
2. Shear in xanthan (0.2%), then calcium lactate gluconate (2.0%); rest 2 h chilled.
3. Rest the alginate bath (0.5%) overnight.
4. Freeze gazpacho in 25 mm hemisphere moulds.
5. Bathe frozen domes 3 minutes at 20 °C, turning once; rinse.
6. Wet spoon rims, dip in celery salt.
7. Seat one sphere per spoon; at the table, one spritz of vodka over each.

## Plating & Service
The vodka atomiser moment is the show — spray from 20 cm just before guests lift the spoons.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Alcohol-free: mist with pickle brine. For heat-lovers, a dot of fermented chilli paste under the sphere.
