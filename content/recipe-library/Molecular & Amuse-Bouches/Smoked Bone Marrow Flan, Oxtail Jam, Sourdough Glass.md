# Smoked Bone Marrow Flan, Oxtail Jam, Sourdough Glass

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A trembling smoked-marrow flan under sticky oxtail, capped with a see-through sourdough crisp — beef on beef on bread, engineered light.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Oxtail — 600 g
- Red wine — 300 ml
- Beef stock — 500 ml
- Carrot, onion, celery mirepoix — 150 g
- Bone marrow, soaked — 120 g
- Whole eggs — 3 (165 g)
- Double cream — 150 ml
- Beef stock (for flan) — 150 ml
- Fine salt — 8 g
- Sourdough, crustless — 100 g
- Water — 150 ml
- Sherry vinegar — 10 ml
- Chives — 4 g

## Method
1. Brown oxtail; braise with wine, 500 ml stock, mirepoix at 140 °C for 4 h; pick meat; reduce liquor to a glaze; fold together with vinegar — the "jam".
2. Cold-smoke marrow 20 minutes (or use 2 g smoked salt); melt at 70 °C.
3. Blend eggs, cream, 150 ml stock, marrow fat, salt; strain twice.
4. Pour 60 g into each buttered ramekin; bake in a bain-marie at 130 °C for 25–30 minutes to a shiver.
5. Blend sourdough with the water to a paste; spread 1.5 mm; dehydrate at 70 °C for 6 h; fry shards 8 seconds at 200 °C — glass.
6. Warm oxtail jam; spoon over each flan.
7. Cap with a sourdough glass shard and chives.

## Plating & Service
Serve in the ramekin, hot. Guests crack the glass into the jam — build, crunch, collapse.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Short rib replaces oxtail. GF glass from rice congee dried the same way (60 °C, 8 h).
