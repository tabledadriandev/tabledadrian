# Melon and Ibérico Textures, Fino Fluid Gel, Marcona Crumble

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Melón con jamón, drawn with a ruler — compressed melon batons, 24-month ibérico, a bone-dry fino gel and salted Marcona gravel.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Charentais melon — 1 (900 g)
- Jamón ibérico, hand-sliced — 80 g
- Fino sherry — 150 ml
- Caster sugar — 12 g
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Agar-agar — 1.4 g (0.85% of the fino liquid)
- Marcona almonds — 50 g
- Olive oil — 10 ml
- Basil tips — 8
- Black pepper — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Cut melon into 8 neat batons (7 × 2 cm); vacuum-compress with 1 g salt (or press weighted 1 h) for glassy density.
2. Warm fino with sugar to burn off a little alcohol, 2 minutes; boil with agar (0.85%) 45 seconds; set; blend to a fluid gel; sieve.
3. Chop Marconas coarse; toast at 150 °C for 8 minutes; salt lightly.
4. Bring jamón to room temperature 20 minutes (fat must glisten).
5. Plate 2 melon batons per plate; drape jamón in loose waves.
6. Pipe fino gel dots along the batons.
7. Scatter almond crumble; finish with basil, oil, one grind of pepper.

## Plating & Service
Serve at cool room temperature — never fridge-cold, or the ham fat seizes and the melon mutes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: toasted pine nut alternative (technically seed). Vegetarian: swap jamón for smoked, pressed watermelon ribbon.
