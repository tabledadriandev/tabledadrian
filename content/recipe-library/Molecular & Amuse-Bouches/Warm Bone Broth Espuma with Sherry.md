# Warm Bone Broth Espuma with Sherry

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A deeply reduced broth aerated to a warm foam — meat-course gravitas at amuse weight, and the collagen does the stabilising.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Rich beef or chicken bone broth (well-gelled when cold) — 500 ml
- Dry oloroso sherry — 25 ml
- Sherry vinegar — 5 ml
- Fine salt — 3 g
- Gelatine leaf — 2 g (0.4% — top-up; the broth's own collagen carries the rest)
- Chives, minced — 3 g

## Method
1. Reduce the broth by a quarter to concentrate; skim relentlessly — fat kills foam.
2. Season with sherry, vinegar, salt; the flavour should be a notch too intense (foam dilutes).
3. Dissolve the bloomed gelatine (0.4%) in the warm broth.
4. Strain through a chinois into a 0.5 L siphon.
5. Charge twice; shake 10 times; hold in a 60 °C bath.
6. Test-dispense: it should stand in soft peaks for 3 minutes.
7. Dispense into warm espresso cups; chives on top.

## Plating & Service
Serve as a between-course warmer, or foam it over roasted roots and pithiviers as a "gravy cloud".

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan analogue: shiitake-kombu broth + 0.3% xanthan and 0.3% methylcellulose. Madeira replaces oloroso for a sweeter register.
