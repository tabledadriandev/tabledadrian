# Frozen Lime Marshmallow of Margarita

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A margarita whipped to a marshmallow and frozen — salt-rim dust outside, cold tequila cloud inside; the ultimate sundowner opener.

## Ingredients (serves 16)
- Lime juice — 120 ml
- Water — 80 ml
- Tequila blanco — 50 ml
- Triple sec — 20 ml
- Caster sugar — 90 g
- Gelatine leaves (gold) — 11 g (4.1% of total liquid — firm whip)
- Egg white powder — 6 g
- Fine salt — 1 g
- Sea salt flakes, ground fine — 4 g
- Lime zest, dried — 2 g

## Method
1. Bloom gelatine in iced water 5 minutes.
2. Heat lime juice, water, sugar, salt to 60 °C; dissolve squeezed gelatine.
3. Whisk in albumin, then tequila and triple sec (liquid at ~35 °C).
4. Whip in a stand mixer 8–10 minutes to a thick, ribboning mousse.
5. Pipe into 3 cm silicone domes; freeze 4 h at −18 °C.
6. Grind sea salt with dried lime zest.
7. Unmould frozen, dust with lime salt, hold at −18 °C; serve straight from the freezer.

## Plating & Service
Pass on a frozen slate during golden hour; they soften from ice cream to marshmallow over 3 minutes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Zero-proof: replace spirits with 70 ml verjus + orange blossom water. Vegan: aquafaba + 1.0% agar version, served chilled not frozen.
