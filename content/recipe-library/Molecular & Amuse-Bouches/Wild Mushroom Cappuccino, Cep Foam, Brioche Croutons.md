# Wild Mushroom Cappuccino, Cep Foam, Brioche Croutons

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A double-mushroom trick — deep roasted-mushroom soup below, a pale cep-milk foam above — served in cappuccino cups with brioche "biscotti".

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Mixed wild mushrooms — 400 g
- Shallots, sliced — 60 g
- Butter — 40 g
- Madeira — 40 ml
- Chicken or vegetable stock — 600 ml
- Double cream — 80 ml
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Dried ceps — 15 g
- Whole milk — 250 ml
- Soy lecithin — 1.6 g (0.6% of the cep milk)
- Brioche, 1 cm cubes — 80 g
- Chives — 3 g

## Method
1. Roast mushrooms hard in half the butter until mahogany, 10–12 minutes.
2. Add shallots and remaining butter, 3 minutes; deglaze with Madeira.
3. Add stock; simmer 15 minutes; blend with cream and salt; strain; hold at 75 °C.
4. Steep dried ceps in the milk at 80 °C for 15 minutes; strain; season lightly.
5. Toast brioche cubes at 160 °C for 8 minutes until golden.
6. Add lecithin (0.6%) to the warm cep milk; froth with a stick blender, tilted.
7. Fill cups two-thirds with soup; spoon cep foam over like crema; chives; croutons on the saucer.

## Plating & Service
Serve in proper cappuccino cups, saucer and all — guests stir the foam down like sugar.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: oat cream + oat milk (lecithin unchanged at 0.6%), olive oil roast. A drop of truffle oil in the foam for gala nights.
