# Chilled Cucumber-Buttermilk Soup, Dill Oil Caviar, Rye Dust

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Ice-cold lactic soup scattered with emerald dill-oil pearls that burst herbaceous against toasted rye — a Nordic summer in a bowl.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Cucumber, peeled, deseeded — 400 g
- Buttermilk — 300 ml
- Crème fraîche — 60 g
- Fine salt — 5 g
- White wine vinegar — 8 ml
- Dill — 40 g
- Grapeseed oil — 100 ml
- Water — 100 ml
- Agar-agar — 0.9 g (0.9% of the water phase)
- Dark rye bread — 80 g
- Butter — 15 g
- Dill fronds — 8

## Method
1. Blend cucumber, buttermilk, crème fraîche, salt, vinegar; strain; chill to 4 °C.
2. Blitz dill with 60 °C grapeseed oil 45 seconds; filter through coffee paper — a deep green oil.
3. Boil the 100 ml water with agar (0.9%) and a pinch of salt; whisk in 40 ml dill oil off heat to a warm emulsion.
4. Drip from a syringe into a tall column of 4 °C oil; strain the green pearls; rinse gently.
5. Toast rye crumbs in butter until dark and crisp; blitz to a coarse dust with salt.
6. Pour soup into chilled shallow bowls.
7. Spoon dill caviar across the surface; rye dust in one crescent; finish with fronds and drops of dill oil.

## Plating & Service
Serve at 4–6 °C; the pearls should sit half-sunken like beads on the pale soup.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: pumpernickel-style GF crumbs. Kefir replaces buttermilk for extra tang; a few brown shrimp turn it coastal.
