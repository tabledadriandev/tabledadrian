# Coconut-Lime Cloud Meringue Shards, Aquafaba Base

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Vegan meringue that outperforms egg white in humidity — aquafaba whipped with xanthan insurance, baked into aerodynamic shards.

## Ingredients (serves 14)
- Aquafaba (chickpea water, reduced by a third) — 120 g
- Caster sugar — 110 g
- Cream of tartar — 1 g
- Xanthan gum — 0.5 g (0.4% of the aquafaba — structure and weep-proofing)
- Coconut milk powder — 15 g
- Lime zest, fine — 3 g
- Fine salt — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Reduce aquafaba gently to concentrate proteins; chill fully.
2. Whip with cream of tartar to soft peaks, 4–5 minutes.
3. Rain in sugar over 3 minutes; whip to stiff gloss.
4. Whisk xanthan (0.4%) with coconut milk powder and salt; fold in with the lime zest.
5. Spread in rough 2 mm waves on silicone with an offset palette.
6. Dehydrate at 80 °C for 2–2.5 h until released and hollow-crisp.
7. Snap into shards; store airtight with silica, 4 days.

## Plating & Service
Plant in sorbets, top fruit carpaccios, or sandwich with coconut namelaka — a dessert-course texture that costs cents.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Savoury direction: swap sugar to 40 g + 4 g nutritional yeast, dust with tomato powder. Freeze leftover shards, re-crisp 20 min at 80 °C.
