# Chestnut Velouté Sphere on Porcini Sablé

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A liquid chestnut soup captured in a sphere, sitting on a crumbly porcini shortbread — autumn's soup-and-bread course in one mouthful.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Cooked chestnuts — 200 g
- Vegetable stock — 250 ml
- Double cream — 50 ml
- Fine salt — 3.5 g
- Calcium lactate gluconate — 9 g (2.0% of the finished velouté)
- Xanthan gum — 0.9 g (0.2%)
- Sodium alginate — 5 g (0.5% bath in 1000 ml water)
- Butter, cold — 90 g
- Flour — 110 g
- Dried porcini, powdered — 8 g
- Egg yolk — 1
- Sage leaves, fried — 12

## Method
1. Simmer chestnuts in stock 15 minutes; blend with cream and salt to a pourable velouté (~450 g); strain.
2. Shear in xanthan (0.2%) and calcium lactate gluconate (2.0%); chill 2 h.
3. Freeze in 25 mm hemispheres.
4. Rub butter, flour, porcini powder, a pinch of salt; bind with yolk; rest 30 minutes; roll 4 mm, cut 4 cm discs.
5. Bake sablés at 165 °C for 12–14 minutes; cool.
6. Bathe frozen domes in the 0.5% alginate bath 3 minutes at 20 °C; rinse; rest 15 minutes to thaw liquid.
7. Seat one sphere per sablé; top with a fried sage leaf.

## Plating & Service
Serve the moment the core turns liquid (test one). Guests get bread, soup and crunch in a single bite.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: oat cream velouté, butter-free sablé with olive oil (harder dough — chill well). Jerusalem artichoke velouté is a killer swap.
