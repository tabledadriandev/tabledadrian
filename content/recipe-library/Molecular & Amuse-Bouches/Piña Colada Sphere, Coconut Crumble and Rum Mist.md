# Piña Colada Sphere, Coconut Crumble and Rum Mist

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Pool-bar kitsch refined: a liquid piña colada sphere on toasted coconut sand, finished with an aromatic rum spray at the table.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Pineapple juice, strained — 200 ml
- Coconut cream — 100 ml
- Sugar syrup 1:1 — 20 ml
- Fine salt — 1 g
- Calcium lactate gluconate — 6.5 g (2.0% of the colada base)
- Xanthan gum — 0.65 g (0.2%)
- Sodium alginate — 5 g (0.5% bath, 1000 ml low-calcium water)
- Desiccated coconut — 50 g
- Demerara sugar — 15 g
- Aged rum in an atomiser — 30 ml
- Pineapple sage or mint tips — 10

## Method
1. Whisk pineapple juice, coconut cream, syrup, salt; shear in xanthan (0.2%) and calcium lactate gluconate (2.0%); rest 2 h chilled.
2. Rest the 0.5% alginate bath overnight.
3. Freeze the base in 30 mm hemispheres.
4. Toast desiccated coconut with demerara at 150 °C for 8–10 minutes, stirring, to a golden crumble; cool.
5. Bathe frozen domes 3 minutes at 20 °C; rinse.
6. Bed each spoon with coconut sand; seat a sphere once its core has re-liquefied (~10 minutes).
7. At the pass: one rum spritz over each from 20 cm; garnish with a sage tip.

## Plating & Service
Serve poolside at sunset. The mist means the rum hits the nose, not the recipe — 0.3 ml per guest.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Zero-proof mist: toasted-coconut water. Mango-passion colada variant uses the same 2.0%/0.5% reverse-sphere ratios.
