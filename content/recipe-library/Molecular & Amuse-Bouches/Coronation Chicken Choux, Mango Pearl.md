# Coronation Chicken Choux, Mango Pearl

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The retro sandwich filling reborn: curried chicken mousse in a crisp craquelin choux, topped by a single mango caviar pearl.

## Ingredients (serves 14)
- Choux paste — 250 g (water 125 ml, butter 50 g, flour 75 g, eggs 2)
- Craquelin (butter 40 g, demerara 50 g, flour 50 g) — 140 g
- Poached chicken breast, minced fine — 180 g
- Mayonnaise — 60 g
- Greek yoghurt — 40 g
- Madras curry powder, toasted — 4 g
- Mango chutney, blended smooth — 25 g
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Mango juice — 100 ml
- Sodium alginate — 1 g (1.0% of mango juice)
- Calcium chloride — 5 g in 1000 ml water (0.5% bath)
- Coriander cress — 14

## Method
1. Pipe 3 cm choux; top with 2 mm craquelin discs; bake at 180 °C for 22–25 minutes without opening the door; cool.
2. Blend mango juice with alginate (1.0%); rest 2 h.
3. Drip large pearls into the 0.5% calcium bath, 60 seconds; rinse.
4. Fold chicken with mayonnaise, yoghurt, curry powder, chutney, salt.
5. Pierce choux bases; pipe full with the coronation mousse.
6. Cut a small cap from each top.
7. Seat one mango pearl in the opening; finish with coriander cress.

## Plating & Service
Serve within the hour on a tiered stand — a knowing nod for British charter guests at tea time.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF choux with fine rice flour. Swap chicken for chickpea + cashew for vegetarian (adds nuts).
