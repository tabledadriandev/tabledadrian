# Nitrogen-Free Instant Herb Oil Snow

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Green "snow" without LN2 — herb oil frozen against maltodextrin, grated to order; melts to basil oil the instant it lands on warm food.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Basil (or parsley/dill) leaves — 60 g
- Grapeseed oil — 150 ml
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Tapioca maltodextrin — 95 g (about 60–65% of the finished oil weight)

## Method
1. Blanch herbs 10 seconds; shock; squeeze bone-dry.
2. Blitz with 60 °C oil for 45 seconds; hang through a coffee filter — a saturated green oil (~140 g).
3. Whisk maltodextrin with salt; work in the cooled oil to a damp powder (60–65%).
4. Pack the powder into a shallow container; press flat.
5. Freeze solid, 3 h minimum — the malto matrix freezes grateable, pure oil never would.
6. Grate on a microplane directly over the dish at the pass.
7. Return the block to the freezer between tables; it keeps 2 weeks.

## Plating & Service
Snow over warm burrata, tomato salads, grilled fish — it melts into glossy herb oil in ~20 seconds, scenting the plate as it goes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Brown-butter snow (dairy) and chorizo-oil snow follow the same 60–65% rule. For white "parmesan snow", freeze-grate the cheese itself instead.
