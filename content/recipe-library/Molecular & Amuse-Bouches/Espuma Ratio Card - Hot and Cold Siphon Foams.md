# Espuma Ratio Card — Hot and Cold Siphon Foams

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The four stabiliser systems for the iSi, with exact percentages — never a collapsed foam at the pass again.

## Ingredients (serves 16)
- Base liquid, strained dead-smooth — 500 ml
- System A (cold, dairy-rich): the fat is the stabiliser — cream ≥30% of base
- System B (cold or hot, lean): gelatine — 2.5–5 g (0.5–1.0%)
- System C (vegan/hot): xanthan — 1–1.5 g (0.2–0.3%)
- System D (hot, eggy): whole eggs/yolks cooked to 82 °C, no other stabiliser
- Fine salt/sugar — season 10% stronger than a sauce
- N2O charges — 2 per 0.5 L

## Method
1. Choose the system: cream bases foam alone; lean juices need B or C; sabayons are D.
2. Gelatine route: bloom, dissolve at 60 °C, chill the base to below 25 °C before charging (set-then-shake gives the tightest foam).
3. Xanthan route: shear in 0.2–0.3%, rest 20 minutes to de-bubble.
4. Strain EVERYTHING through a chinois into the siphon — one fibre blocks the valve mid-service.
5. Fill max ⅔; charge twice; shake 8–10 times.
6. Cold espumas: rest 1 h chilled. Hot: hold in a 55–62 °C bath, never hotter with dairy/eggs.
7. Test-dispense before every service; purge upside down.

## Plating & Service
Dispense onto the plate last, walk fast. A foam's life is 3–5 minutes — build the pass around it.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan hot foam: 0.3% xanthan + 0.3% methylcellulose holds heat impressively. Note ratios per dish in the siphon log.
