# Everything Bagel Tuile Cigars

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A brittle tuile cigar with the full everything-bagel hit — the house vessel for whipped cream cheese fillings and smoked fish mousses.

## Ingredients (serves 14)
- Butter, melted — 50 g
- Egg white — 50 g
- Flour — 55 g
- Icing sugar — 20 g
- Fine salt — 2 g
- White sesame — 8 g
- Black sesame — 6 g
- Poppy seeds — 6 g
- Dried onion flakes — 8 g
- Dried garlic granules — 4 g

## Method
1. Whisk butter, egg white, flour, icing sugar, salt to a smooth tuile paste; rest 30 minutes chilled.
2. Mix the everything blend: sesames, poppy, onion, garlic.
3. Spread paste through a 10 × 6 cm stencil onto silicone, 1 mm thin.
4. Scatter each rectangle generously with the blend.
5. Bake at 165 °C for 6–7 minutes to an even gold.
6. Working one at a time (keep the tray on the oven door), roll hot around a 1 cm dowel, seam down.
7. Cool; store airtight with silica up to 3 days.

## Plating & Service
Pipe whipped cream cheese + chive inside, cap with trout roe — a two-bite "bagel" for the canapé tray.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: rice flour 1:1 works. Savoury-parmesan variant: replace sugar with 20 g fine parmesan.
