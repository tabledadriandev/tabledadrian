# Cucumber-Elderflower Granita Oyster

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A Gillardeau under a pale-green flurry of cucumber-elderflower granita — the ice melts into a ready-made mignonette in the shell.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Oysters No.2 — 12
- Cucumber juice, strained — 250 ml
- Elderflower cordial — 40 ml
- Champagne vinegar — 20 ml
- Fine salt — 1.5 g
- Xanthan gum — 0.3 g (0.1% — slows ice-crystal coarsening)
- Shallot, super-fine brunoise — 15 g
- Lemon — 1
- Borage flowers — 12
- Crushed ice + seaweed for the tray

## Method
1. Whisk cucumber juice, cordial, vinegar, salt; shear in xanthan (0.1%).
2. Freeze in a shallow tray, scraping with a fork every 30 minutes for 2.5–3 h to a fine granita.
3. Shuck oysters, keeping every drop of liquor; loosen the muscle.
4. Strain the liquor back over each oyster.
5. Add a pinch of shallot to each.
6. Cap each oyster with a generous fork of granita.
7. Finish with a borage flower; lemon wedge alongside.

## Plating & Service
Serve immediately on ice and bladderwrack; 60 seconds later the granita is a chilled sauce — both moments are correct.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No elderflower: use verjus + a drop of jasmine. Same granita is superb over raw scallop or tomato hearts (vegan route).
