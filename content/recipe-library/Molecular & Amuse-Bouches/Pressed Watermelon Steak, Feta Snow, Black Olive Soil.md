# Pressed Watermelon Steak, Feta Snow, Black Olive Soil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Greek salad reassembled — dense seared watermelon "steak", frozen feta grated to snow, and a bitter-sweet olive soil.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Watermelon, 4 cm slab — 800 g
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Sherry vinegar — 15 ml
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Feta — 150 g
- Double cream — 30 ml
- Kalamata olives, pitted — 120 g
- Panko or GF crumbs — 25 g
- Caster sugar — 8 g
- Oregano, fresh — 2 g
- Basil tips — 8

## Method
1. Vacuum-compress watermelon blocks (10 × 5 × 4 cm) twice at 99% (or press weighted overnight) — flesh turns garnet and dense.
2. Blend feta with cream; freeze solid in a shallow tray, 3 h.
3. Dehydrate olives at 90 °C for 2–2.5 h until leathery-dry; blitz with crumbs and sugar to a dark soil.
4. Sear watermelon blocks in oil, 90 seconds per face, in a screaming pan; season with salt and vinegar.
5. Rest 2 minutes; slice each block once on the bias.
6. Plate over a line of olive soil; grate feta snow generously at the pass.
7. Finish with oregano, basil and olive oil.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm-over-cold immediately — the snow half-melting on the seared melon is the dish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: frozen-grated almond feta (adds nuts). Add jamón shards for carnivores; the soil also loves tomato dishes.
