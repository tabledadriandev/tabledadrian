# Charred Leek Terrine, Romesco Fluid Gel, Almond Air

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Calçotada in terrine form — pressed charred leeks sliced like agate, with romesco as a piped gel and a floating almond air.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Leeks, medium — 10 (1.2 kg)
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Fine salt — 8 g
- Vegetable stock — 150 ml
- Agar-agar — 1.4 g (0.9% of the stock — terrine binder)
- Roasted red peppers — 200 g
- Tomato, roasted — 100 g
- Almonds, toasted — 40 g
- Garlic, roasted — 8 g
- Sherry vinegar — 15 ml
- Agar-agar — 2.9 g (0.85% of the romesco, loosened with 60 ml water)
- Almond milk — 200 ml
- Soy lecithin — 1.2 g (0.6%)

## Method
1. Char whole leeks under a hot grill, turning, 15–20 minutes until blackened outside; steam in foil 10 minutes; strip to the tender hearts.
2. Season hearts; lay lengthwise in a lined terrine mould in tight layers.
3. Boil stock with agar (0.9%); pour over the leeks layer by layer; press lightly; set 4 h at 4 °C.
4. Blend peppers, tomato, almonds, garlic, vinegar, oil, salt to romesco; loosen with the water; boil with agar (0.85%) 60 seconds; set; blend to a piping fluid gel.
5. Season almond milk; add lecithin (0.6%); froth cold.
6. Slice terrine 2 cm with a serrated knife.
7. Plate a slice, pipe romesco gel dots, crown with almond air.

## Plating & Service
The cross-section shows tree-ring circles of leek. Serve cool with warm grilled bread for the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: sunflower romesco and oat-milk air. A few grilled langoustines convert it to a surf-and-turf starter.
