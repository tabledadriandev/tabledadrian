# Kombu-Cured Vegetable Sashimi Components

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Kombujime for vegetables — pressing kohlrabi, beets and turnip between kelp sheets until they eat like raw fish; the plant-based raw bar's backbone.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Kombu sheets, wiped — 6 large
- Kohlrabi, peeled — 1 (300 g)
- Golden beetroot, peeled — 1 (200 g)
- Small turnips — 2 (150 g)
- Sake — 30 ml
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Tamari — 15 ml
- Toasted sesame oil — 5 ml
- Yuzu juice — 8 ml

## Method
1. Slice all vegetables 2 mm on a mandoline; salt lightly 10 minutes; pat dry.
2. Brush kombu with sake to soften and awaken glutamates.
3. Layer vegetables in single sheets between kombu; wrap tight in cling film; weight lightly.
4. Cure 4–12 h at 4 °C — longer = deeper umami, softer texture (kohlrabi takes 12 h well; turnip 4).
5. Unwrap; the slices should be translucent and supple like cured fish.
6. Cut into sashimi rectangles or keep as carpaccio rounds.
7. Dress at service with tamari, sesame oil, yuzu.

## Plating & Service
Fan like a sashimi moriawase over crushed ice with real wasabi; or use as the wrapper for vegetable "nigiri".

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Scallops and bream take the same cure at 2–3 h. Spent kombu: simmer into dashi, then dry and fry as chips — three lives, one kelp.
