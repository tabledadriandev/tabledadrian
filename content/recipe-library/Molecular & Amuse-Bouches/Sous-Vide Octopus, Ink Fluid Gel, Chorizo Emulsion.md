# Sous-Vide Octopus, Ink Fluid Gel, Chorizo Emulsion

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Five-hour 77 °C octopus — cut-with-a-spoon tender, then charred — over a jet-black ink gel and a warm chorizo-fat emulsion.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Octopus tentacles, large — 4 (600 g)
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Fish stock — 200 ml
- Squid ink — 12 g
- Sherry vinegar — 10 ml
- Agar-agar — 1.8 g (0.85% of the ink liquid)
- Chorizo, diced — 100 g
- Egg yolk — 1 (18 g)
- Lemon juice — 8 ml
- Grapeseed oil — 60 ml
- Smoked paprika — 1 g
- Parsley oil — 15 ml

## Method
1. Vacuum-seal tentacles with 15 ml olive oil; cook sous-vide at 77.0 °C for 5 h; ice-shock; refrigerate (holds 3 days).
2. Warm fish stock with ink and vinegar; boil with agar (0.85%) 60 seconds; set; blend to a lacquer-black fluid gel.
3. Render chorizo gently 8 minutes; strain — keep fat and crisp pieces separately.
4. Blend yolk and lemon; emulsify with warm chorizo fat topped up with grapeseed oil; season with paprika.
5. Dry tentacles well; char in a smoking-hot pan or over coals, 2 minutes, until blistered.
6. Swipe ink gel across warm plates; slice tentacles thick on the bias.
7. Spoon chorizo emulsion, scatter crisp chorizo, dot parsley oil.

## Plating & Service
Serve hot. Black gel, coral octopus, brick-red emulsion — the plate looks like volcanic coastline.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No sous-vide: simmer at 90 °C for 75 minutes. Halal: replace chorizo with smoked-paprika butterbean emulsion.
