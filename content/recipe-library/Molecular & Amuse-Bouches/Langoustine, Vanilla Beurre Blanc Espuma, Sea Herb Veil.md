# Langoustine, Vanilla Beurre Blanc Espuma, Sea Herb Veil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Butter-poached langoustine under a translucent sea-lettuce gel veil and a vanilla beurre blanc blown to weightlessness — luxury with a light hand.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Langoustine tails, shelled — 8 (240 g)
- Butter — 120 g
- Dry white wine — 100 ml
- White wine vinegar — 30 ml
- Shallot, sliced — 40 g
- Vanilla pod, split — ½
- Double cream — 40 ml
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Sea lettuce / kombu stock — 200 ml
- Agar-agar — 1.9 g (0.95% of the veil liquid)
- Locust bean gum — 0.2 g (0.1%)
- Samphire, blanched — 40 g

## Method
1. Reduce wine, vinegar, shallot to 30 ml; add vanilla seeds and cream.
2. Mount with 90 g cold butter cubes over low heat; season; strain — a slightly loose beurre blanc.
3. Load into a 0.5 L siphon, 1 charge; hold at 55 °C strictly (butter splits hotter).
4. Boil the sea-lettuce stock with agar (0.95%) and LBG (0.1%) 60 seconds; pour 1.5 mm thin on a flat tray; set 15 minutes; cut 10 cm squares.
5. Poach langoustines in the remaining 30 g butter with a spoon of water at 60 °C for 4–5 minutes until pearly.
6. Plate two tails per plate; drape a sea veil over each pair.
7. Siphon vanilla beurre blanc alongside; finish with samphire.

## Plating & Service
The veil should read as a green glass sheet over the tails. Serve instantly — foam and veil both live short lives.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Scallops or monkfish cheek sub well. Dairy-free: olive oil sabayon foam (yolk-based) replaces beurre blanc.
