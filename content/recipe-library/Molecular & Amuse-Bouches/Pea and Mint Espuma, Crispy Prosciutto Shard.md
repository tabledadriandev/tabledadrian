# Pea and Mint Espuma, Crispy Prosciutto Shard

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Electric-green siphon foam that tastes of June, speared with a glass-crisp prosciutto sail — three textures, ninety seconds of prep at the pass.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Frozen petits pois — 300 g
- Vegetable stock — 200 ml
- Double cream — 80 ml
- Mint leaves — 8 g
- Fine salt — 3 g
- Gelatine leaf (gold) — 2 g (0.35% of the liquid — light siphon stabiliser)
- Prosciutto slices — 6 (90 g)
- Lemon zest — 1 g
- Pea shoots — 12

## Method
1. Lay prosciutto between two baking sheets; bake at 160 °C for 12–14 minutes until flat and crisp; drain, snap into shards.
2. Boil stock; add peas, cook 3 minutes; add mint for the last 20 seconds.
3. Blend with cream and salt; pass through a chinois (essential for the siphon).
4. Bloom gelatine, dissolve into the warm base (~60 °C).
5. Load a 0.5 L siphon, charge twice, shake 10 times; hold at 55 °C in a bath.
6. Siphon into warmed glasses to two-thirds.
7. Finish with lemon zest, a pea shoot, and a prosciutto shard planted upright.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm immediately; the foam holds ~4 minutes. Beautiful against white porcelain.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian/vegan: omit prosciutto (use a kale crisp), swap cream for oat cream and gelatine for 0.15% xanthan sheared in.
