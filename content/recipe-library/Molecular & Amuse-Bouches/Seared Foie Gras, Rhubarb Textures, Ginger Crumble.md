# Seared Foie Gras, Rhubarb Textures, Ginger Crumble

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Caramel-crusted foie against rhubarb as gel, pickle and crisp — acid engineering to carry the richness through three more bites than usual.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Foie gras escalopes, 80 g each — 4
- Fine salt — 3 g
- Rhubarb — 400 g
- Caster sugar — 60 g
- Grenadine — 8 ml
- Agar-agar — 1.7 g (0.85% of the rhubarb juice for the gel)
- White wine vinegar — 40 ml
- Water — 40 ml
- Flour — 50 g
- Butter — 40 g
- Demerara — 30 g
- Ground ginger — 3 g

## Method
1. Juice 250 g rhubarb with 30 g sugar (simmer 8 min, strain — 200 ml); add grenadine; boil with agar (0.85%) 60 seconds; set, blend to fluid gel.
2. Pickle: bring vinegar, water, 30 g sugar to boil; pour over 80 g rhubarb batons; cool.
3. Slice remaining rhubarb into ribbons; dehydrate at 70 °C for 2 h to crisps.
4. Rub flour, butter, demerara, ginger; bake crumble at 165 °C for 12 minutes; cool.
5. Score foie, salt; sear in a dry, very hot pan 45–60 seconds per side to a mahogany crust; rest on paper 1 minute.
6. Swipe rhubarb gel; seat the foie.
7. Arrange pickled batons, crisps and ginger crumble around.

## Plating & Service
Serve immediately, foie still trembling. A small glass of off-dry chenin alongside settles the argument.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No foie policy: thick-seared scallops love the identical garnish. GF crumble with rice flour and oats.
