# Ash-Rolled Chèvre, Compressed Pear, Chicory and Honey Tuile

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Leek-ash chèvre logs with vacuum-glassy pear and a shattering honey tuile — a cheese course masquerading as a starter, gone in four bites.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Fresh goat cheese log — 200 g
- Leek-top ash (charred, dried, blitzed) — 10 g
- Williams pear, firm — 2
- Verjus — 50 ml
- Honey — 40 g
- Butter — 20 g
- Flour — 20 g
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Red chicory — 1 head (120 g)
- Olive oil — 20 ml
- Sherry vinegar — 8 ml
- Thyme leaves — 1 g

## Method
1. Cut chèvre into 8 pucks; roll edges through leek ash for a black band.
2. Slice pears into 5 mm wedges; vacuum-compress with verjus (or press weighted 2 h) until translucent.
3. Melt honey, butter, salt; stir in flour; spread thin on silicone; bake at 170 °C for 6–7 minutes to an amber lace; snap into shards.
4. Separate chicory leaves; dress with oil, vinegar, salt.
5. Warm the chèvre pucks 3 minutes at 100 °C — surface soft, centre cool.
6. Compose pucks, pear, chicory on each plate.
7. Plant honey tuile shards; scatter thyme.

## Plating & Service
Serve at once — warm cheese, icy pear, bitter leaf, sweet glass. Autumn's whole argument on one plate.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF tuile with rice flour. Swap pear for compressed quince poached first; vegan with ash-rolled almond chèvre (adds nuts).
