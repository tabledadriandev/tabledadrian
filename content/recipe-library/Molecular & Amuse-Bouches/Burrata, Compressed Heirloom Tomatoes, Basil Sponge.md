# Burrata, Compressed Heirloom Tomatoes, Basil Sponge

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Caprese at 2.0 — vacuum-compressed tomatoes like stained glass, a microwave basil sponge, and burrata dressed only with its own cream.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Heirloom tomatoes, mixed colours — 400 g
- White balsamic — 20 ml
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Burrata — 2 (250 g)
- Basil leaves — 40 g
- Eggs — 2
- Flour — 30 g
- Caster sugar — 8 g
- Olive oil — 25 ml
- Tomato water (from trim) — 60 ml
- Xanthan gum — 0.12 g (0.2% of tomato water — light nappe dressing)
- Basil cress — 8

## Method
1. Cut tomatoes into large petals; vacuum-compress with white balsamic and 2 g salt (or press weighted 2 h).
2. Blend basil, eggs, flour, sugar, 15 ml oil, 2 g salt; strain into a siphon; 2 charges.
3. Siphon into paper cups (⅓ full); microwave each 40 seconds at 900 W; cool inverted; tear into clouds.
4. Blend reserved tomato trim; strain; shear xanthan (0.2%) into 60 ml of the water for a glossy dressing.
5. Tear burrata into 4; season the cut faces.
6. Compose tomatoes, burrata, torn green sponge.
7. Spoon the tomato-water nappe around; finish with oil and cress.

## Plating & Service
Room-temperature tomatoes, cool burrata, plate in under 2 minutes. Colour does the marketing.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: cashew burrata. GF sponge with rice flour works in the siphon. Peaches replace half the tomatoes in August.
