# Crab Raviolo in Clarified Tom Kha Broth

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> One huge raviolo of crab and lemongrass in a crystal-clear coconut-galangal broth — Thai flavour with consommé discipline via agar clarification.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- 00 pasta dough (flour 150 g, egg 1, yolks 2) — 250 g
- White crab meat — 160 g
- Brown crab meat — 40 g
- Lemongrass, minced — 8 g
- Lime zest — 2 g
- Coconut milk — 400 ml
- Chicken or fish stock — 300 ml
- Galangal, sliced — 20 g
- Kaffir lime leaves — 4
- Fish sauce — 15 ml
- Lime juice — 20 ml
- Agar-agar — 3.5 g (0.5% of the broth for freeze-thaw clarification)
- Coriander cress + red chilli threads — to garnish

## Method
1. Simmer coconut milk, stock, galangal, lime leaves 15 minutes; season with fish sauce and lime — a full tom kha.
2. Clarify: whisk in agar (0.5%), boil 60 seconds, set in a shallow tray, freeze solid, then thaw overnight in muslin over a bowl — collect a clear, fragrant broth.
3. Mix both crab meats, lemongrass, zest; season.
4. Roll pasta to setting 8 (near-transparent); cut 8 discs of 12 cm.
5. Form 4 large ravioli with 45 g filling; seal with water, exclude all air.
6. Simmer ravioli 3 minutes in salted water.
7. Bowl each raviolo; pour 120 ml hot clarified tom kha at the table; garnish cress and chilli threads.

## Plating & Service
The broth must be poured in front of the guest — clear as tea, tasting like a storm. Serve at 80 °C.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Prawn or lobster filling works. GF: rice-paper "raviolo" folded around the filling and steamed 4 minutes.
