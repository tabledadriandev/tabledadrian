# Master Reverse Spherification — Dairy, Alcohol, Purées

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The bulletproof method for anything basic spherification can't touch — yoghurt, cocktails, thick purées — with the frozen-mould trick for perfect spheres every time.

## Ingredients (serves 20)
- Flavour base (dairy, purée, cocktail ≤20% ABV) — 500 g
- Calcium lactate gluconate — 10 g (2.0% of the base; skip or halve if base is already dairy-rich)
- Xanthan gum — 1 g (0.2% — body so thin bases hold shape)
- Sodium alginate — 5 g (0.5% bath)
- Still low-calcium water — 1000 ml
- Clean water (rinse) — 500 ml
- Holding liquid (matching juice/oil) — 200 ml

## Method
1. Blend the base with xanthan (0.2%) then calcium lactate gluconate (2.0%); rest 2 h chilled to de-aerate.
2. Blend the alginate bath (0.5%) the day before; rest overnight — it must be glass-clear and bubble-free.
3. For spheres: freeze the base in hemisphere or sphere moulds until solid.
4. Drop frozen shapes into the bath at 18–22 °C; bathe 3 minutes, turning at half-time; skin thickness tracks bathing time.
5. Rinse 30 seconds; rest in holding liquid.
6. Spheres keep 24–48 h — reverse spherification stops gelling once rinsed (the calcium is inside, the alginate outside).
7. Temper 10–15 minutes before serving so the core returns to liquid.

## Plating & Service
This is the method behind liquid croquetas, cocktail spheres, yoghurt pearls and burrata bombes — one master ratio, infinite menus.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Above 20% ABV, dilute the cocktail or it won't gel. For firmer standing spheres, bathe 4 minutes; for near-invisible skins, 2.
