# Cauliflower Panna Cotta, Caviar and Gold

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The EMP-school pairing of nutty cauliflower cream and oscietra — a barely-set savoury panna cotta that lets the caviar do the talking.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Cauliflower florets — 300 g
- Whole milk — 250 ml
- Double cream — 200 ml
- Butter — 20 g
- Fine salt — 3.5 g
- Gelatine leaves (gold) — 5 g (0.9% of the set liquid — delicate tremble)
- Oscietra caviar — 30 g
- Chives, minced — 3 g
- Crème fraîche — 40 g
- Gold leaf — 2 sheets

## Method
1. Sweat cauliflower in butter 5 minutes without colour.
2. Add milk and cream; simmer gently 15 minutes until soft.
3. Blend with salt until velvet; pass through a chinois — you need ~550 g.
4. Bloom gelatine; dissolve into the warm base at 60 °C.
5. Cast 50 g into each chilled glass at a tilt (set on an angled tray); set 3 h at 4 °C.
6. Loosen crème fraîche with a few drops of water; spoon a thin layer over the set cream.
7. Finish with 3 g caviar, chives and a whisper of gold leaf.

## Plating & Service
Serve at 8 °C on a caviar tin lid or mother-of-pearl spoon alongside. Pure charter-night flex.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Budget version: smoked trout roe. Vegan: oat cream base set with 0.8% agar + 0.1% LBG, kelp "caviar" pearls (1.0% alginate, basic method).
