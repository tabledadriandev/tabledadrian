# Tomato Tartare Cone with Basil Fluid Gel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A sesame cornet filled with concentrated tomato "tartare" and a slick of basil gel — the vegan answer to the classic tuna cone.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Plum tomatoes — 600 g
- Tamari — 10 ml
- Sherry vinegar — 8 ml
- Smoked paprika — 1 g
- Shallot, brunoise — 25 g
- Capers, chopped — 15 g
- Feuille de brick — 3 sheets
- Olive oil — 20 ml
- Black and white sesame — 10 g
- Basil leaves — 30 g
- Spinach — 20 g
- Water — 150 ml
- Agar-agar — 1.4 g (0.8% of basil liquid)

## Method
1. Blanch, peel, quarter and deseed tomatoes; dry petals at 90 °C for 1 h to concentrate.
2. Dice petals 5 mm; dress with tamari, vinegar, paprika, shallot, capers.
3. Cut brick sheets into 12 quarter-circles; brush with oil, scatter sesame, roll around cone moulds.
4. Bake cones at 170 °C for 7–8 minutes until golden; cool on the moulds.
5. Blanch basil and spinach 10 seconds, shock; blend with the water; strain.
6. Boil basil liquid with agar (0.8%) 60 seconds; set, blend to fluid gel, sieve into a piping bag.
7. Pipe gel inside each cone, fill with tartare, finish with a dot of gel on top.

## Plating & Service
Stand cones in a holed cedar block. Fill max 10 minutes before service.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: use rice-paper cones (brush, no bake — dry at 60 °C). Non-vegan: fold in 40 g diced raw tuna for a 50/50 tartare.
