# Scallop Crudo, Clarified Gazpacho and Tomato Pearls

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Raw diver scallops floating in water-clear gazpacho with bursting tomato caviar — all the Andalusian flavour, none of the colour, until the pearls pop red.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Diver scallops, XL — 6 (300 g)
- Ripe tomatoes — 700 g
- Cucumber — 100 g
- Red pepper — 80 g
- Sherry vinegar — 15 ml
- Fine salt — 7 g
- Tomato passata, strained — 100 ml
- Sodium alginate — 1 g (1.0% of the passata)
- Calcium chloride — 5 g in 1000 ml water (0.5% bath)
- Extra virgin olive oil — 20 ml
- Basil oil — 10 ml
- Micro basil — 8 leaves

## Method
1. Blend tomatoes, cucumber, pepper, vinegar, 5 g salt; hang in muslin overnight at 4 °C — collect ~350 ml clear gazpacho water; season.
2. Blend passata with alginate (1.0%); rest 2 h.
3. Drip into the 0.5% calcium bath 60 seconds; rinse — red pearls in a clear pool later.
4. Slice scallops horizontally into 3 coins each; salt lightly 2 minutes.
5. Arrange 4–5 coins per chilled bowl, slightly overlapping.
6. Pour 80 ml clarified gazpacho around at the table.
7. Finish with tomato pearls, both oils, micro basil.

## Plating & Service
Pour the clear liquid tableside from a small carafe — the reveal (it tastes screaming red) is the course.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: silken tofu coins or compressed watermelon instead of scallop. Add a green chilli thread for heat.
