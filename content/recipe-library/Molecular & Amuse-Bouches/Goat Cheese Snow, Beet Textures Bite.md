# Goat Cheese Snow, Beet Textures Bite

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Beetroot three ways — roast cube, fluid gel, glass crisp — under a frozen goat-cheese snow; a study dish that eats like a garden.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Beetroot, medium — 3 (450 g)
- Olive oil — 15 ml
- Sherry vinegar — 15 ml
- Caster sugar — 6 g
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Agar-agar — 1.7 g (0.85% of the beet juice for the gel)
- Isomalt — 20 g
- Fresh goat cheese log — 150 g
- Double cream — 50 ml
- Baby red-vein sorrel — 12 leaves

## Method
1. Roast 2 beets whole in foil with oil and salt at 190 °C for 50–60 minutes; peel, cut 1.5 cm cubes, dress with half the vinegar.
2. Juice the raw beet: you need 200 ml. Boil with sugar, remaining vinegar and agar (0.85%) for 60 seconds; set, blend to fluid gel, sieve.
3. For crisps: reduce 100 ml beet juice with isomalt to a syrup, brush paper-thin on silicone, dehydrate at 70 °C for 3 h; snap into shards.
4. Blend goat cheese with cream; spread 1 cm deep in a tray; freeze solid.
5. Chill 12 small plates.
6. Set a beet cube on each, pipe a comma of fluid gel, plant a crisp.
7. At the pass, grate frozen goat cheese over generously; finish with sorrel.

## Plating & Service
Serve immediately — the snow softens to a cream as it hits the room-temperature beet.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: frozen-grate a cashew-miso cream (adds nuts). Golden beets + orange gel (same 0.85% agar) make a sunnier version.
