# Mango-Passion Caviar on Coconut Panna Cotta Spoon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bright tropical caviar bursting over a barely-set coconut base — a two-texture opener that reads luxurious but costs pennies per spoon.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Mango purée (sieved) — 150 g
- Passion fruit juice, strained — 50 ml
- Sodium alginate — 2 g (1.0% of flavour base)
- Calcium chloride — 5 g (0.5% bath)
- Still mineral water (low calcium) — 1000 ml
- Coconut milk (full fat) — 200 ml
- Caster sugar — 12 g
- Agar-agar — 1.8 g (0.9% of coconut base)
- Lime zest — 2 g
- Micro coriander — 12 leaves

## Method
1. Blend mango purée, passion juice and alginate at high speed; rest 2 h in fridge to de-aerate fully (bubbles ruin spheres).
2. Dissolve calcium chloride in the 1000 ml water for the setting bath; hold at 18–20 °C.
3. For the base: warm coconut milk with sugar, whisk in agar (0.9%), boil 90 seconds to hydrate, cast 15 g into each tasting spoon; set 20 min at 4 °C.
4. Drop the mango base from a 10 ml syringe held 4 cm above the bath, droplet by droplet; basic spherification gels on contact.
5. Bathe pearls 60–90 seconds — no longer, or they gel through.
6. Lift with a perforated spoon, rinse 20 seconds in clean water.
7. Spoon 8–10 pearls over each set coconut disc, grate lime zest over, crown with micro coriander.

## Plating & Service
Serve on chilled ceramic spoons within 30 minutes; the caviar must still burst. One-bite service at the gangway with a chilled towel.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap mango for beetroot juice + orange for a savoury version; use yuzu juice (cut 50/50 with water, alginate still 1.0%) for a sharper hit.
