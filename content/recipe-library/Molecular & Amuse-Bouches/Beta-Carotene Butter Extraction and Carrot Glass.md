# Beta-Carotene Butter Extraction and Carrot Glass

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Carotenoids are fat-soluble — so we pull carrot's colour and sweetness into butter, then turn the spent juice into amber glass; one vegetable, two weapons.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Carrot juice, fresh — 500 ml
- Unsalted butter — 250 g
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Isomalt — 40 g
- Glucose — 10 g
- Citric acid — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Simmer carrot juice, reducing to 200 ml — flavour and carotene concentrate.
2. Whisk in butter cube by cube at a bare simmer; then hold at 90 °C for 30 minutes — the fat strips the beta-carotene; the butter turns traffic-cone orange.
3. Rest 10 minutes; separate: the orange carotene butter rises; ladle it off and chill (keeps 2 weeks; finishes sauces, bastes fish, mounts purées).
4. Reserve the pale spent juice below — about 80 ml.
5. Boil spent juice with isomalt and glucose to 155 °C.
6. Pour thin on silicone; tilt; cool to a translucent amber carrot glass; snap into shards.
7. Store butter chilled, glass airtight with silica.

## Plating & Service
Carotene butter over 62 °C eggs, langoustine or agnolotti; the glass plants upright in carrot purée for a tone-on-tone garnish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Same extraction works with red pepper juice (capsanthin) and tomato (lycopene — go 45 min). Vegan: extract into refined coconut oil.
