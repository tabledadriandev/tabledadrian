# Sungold Tomato Bombe, Burrata Cream Core

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Looks like a whole Sungold tomato; bites like a caprese — a tomato-gel shell moulded around liquid burrata cream, stem and all.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Sungold/yellow tomato juice, strained — 300 ml
- Fine salt — 2.5 g
- Caster sugar — 4 g
- Agar-agar — 3 g (1.0% of the juice — self-supporting shell)
- Locust bean gum — 0.3 g (0.1% — anti-crack elasticity)
- Burrata — 150 g
- Double cream — 40 ml
- Basil oil — 15 ml
- Real tomato stems/calyxes, washed — 12
- Maldon salt — 1 g

## Method
1. Blend burrata with cream and a pinch of salt to a thick pourable cream; freeze in 15 mm sphere moulds.
2. Season tomato juice with salt and sugar; whisk in agar (1.0%) and LBG (0.1%); boil 90 seconds; hold at 50 °C.
3. Spear each frozen burrata core; dip into the tomato gel 2 seconds; rest 30 seconds; dip again for a 2 mm shell.
4. Rest bombes on parchment; refrigerate 1 h (cores thaw back to liquid cream).
5. Brush each bombe lightly with basil oil for gloss.
6. Seat a real calyx on top of each.
7. Plate on a smear of basil oil with one Maldon flake beside.

## Plating & Service
Serve whole with a knife-less instruction: one bite. The reveal of white liquid inside orange "tomato" is the dish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: cashew cream core (adds nuts) or whipped silken tofu. Red tomato juice shell for classic looks; ratios unchanged.
