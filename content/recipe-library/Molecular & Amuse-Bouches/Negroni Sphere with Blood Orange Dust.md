# Negroni Sphere with Blood Orange Dust

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The apéritif becomes the first bite — a liquid negroni in a reverse-spherified skin, dusted with freeze-dried blood orange; pure cocktail-hour theatre.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Gin — 60 ml
- Sweet red vermouth — 60 ml
- Bitter red aperitivo — 60 ml
- Sugar syrup (1:1) — 20 ml
- Calcium lactate gluconate — 5.5 g (2.0% of cocktail base)
- Xanthan gum — 0.55 g (0.2%, for droplet cohesion)
- Sodium alginate — 5 g (0.5% bath)
- Still mineral water (low calcium) — 1000 ml
- Freeze-dried blood orange, powdered — 8 g
- Orange zest strips — 10

## Method
1. Stir gin, vermouth, aperitivo and syrup; note alcohol above ~20% ABV resists gelling, so the syrup dilution matters — final ABV ~19%.
2. Shear in xanthan (0.2%) and calcium lactate gluconate (2.0%) with a stick blender; rest 2 h chilled to de-aerate.
3. Blend alginate bath (0.5%) the day before; rest overnight.
4. Freeze the negroni base in 25 mm hemisphere moulds until just solid (frozen reverse spherification gives perfect spheres).
5. Drop frozen hemispheres into the bath at 20 °C; bathe 3 minutes, turning at 90 seconds.
6. Rinse 30 seconds in clean water; hold spheres in a splash of vermouth, chilled.
7. Serve each sphere on a frozen ceramic spoon; dust with blood orange powder, lay a zest strip alongside.

## Plating & Service
Serve at the start of dinner in place of the apéritif, on a tray of crushed ice. One bite, eyes closed.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Zero-proof: replace spirits with bitter orange soda + verjus (calcium still 2.0%). A white negroni (gin, bianco, gentian) works with lemon dust.
