# Dehydrated Vegetable Skin Crisps and Powders

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Trim-bin alchemy — skins and pulps become glass crisps and neon powders; zero-waste garnish stock for a week of plates.

## Ingredients (serves 16)
- Beetroot skins and trim — 200 g
- Tomato skins (from blanching) — 100 g
- Carrot pulp (from juicing) — 150 g
- Mushroom stems — 100 g
- Olive oil — 10 ml
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Caster sugar — 6 g
- Vinegar powder or citric acid — 1 g

## Method
1. Tomato skins: lay flat on silicone; dehydrate at 60 °C for 6 h; blitz to a red powder with citric acid.
2. Beet trim: simmer 10 minutes; blend with 2 g sugar to a paste; spread 2 mm; dehydrate at 60 °C for 8 h into snappable leather-glass shards.
3. Carrot pulp: season with oil, salt, 4 g sugar; press thin between mats; dehydrate at 60 °C for 8 h to lace crisps.
4. Mushroom stems: dry whole at 70 °C for 6 h; blitz to an umami dust with salt.
5. Condition all crisps 10 minutes at room temp; if they bend, return to the dehydrator.
6. Blitz any broken crisps into their matching powders — nothing wasted twice.
7. Store each airtight with silica, labelled and dated.

## Plating & Service
Powders rim plates and dust foams; shards give height. A pinch of tomato powder on a lecithin air is a two-second upgrade.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fruit versions (raspberry, mango) at 55 °C protect colour. Log dehydrator loads overnight — 8 h at 60 °C is the house default.
