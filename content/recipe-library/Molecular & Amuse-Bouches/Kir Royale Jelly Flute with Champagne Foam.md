# Kir Royale Jelly Flute with Champagne Foam

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The welcome drink you eat with a long spoon — cassis jelly floor, champagne foam roof, one flute, zero effort at service time.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Crème de cassis — 100 ml
- Water — 100 ml
- Gelatine leaves (gold) — 4 g (2.0% of the cassis layer — clean set in a flute)
- Champagne or crémant — 300 ml
- Caster sugar — 20 g
- Gelatine leaves (gold) — 3 g (0.9% of the foam base)
- Lemon juice — 5 ml
- Blackberries — 10
- Gold leaf — 1 sheet

## Method
1. Warm cassis and water to 55 °C; dissolve 4 g bloomed gelatine (2.0%); pour 20 ml into each flute; set upright 2 h at 4 °C.
2. Warm 100 ml of the champagne with sugar to 55 °C; dissolve 3 g bloomed gelatine.
3. Whisk in remaining champagne and lemon; chill until just starting to thicken (~45 min).
4. Whip the syrupy base with electric beaters to a stable mousse-foam, 3–4 minutes.
5. Spoon or pipe the foam over the set cassis layer, leaving strata visible.
6. Chill 30 minutes more.
7. Crown with one blackberry and a fleck of gold.

## Plating & Service
Line the flutes on the welcome table; serve with long-handled spoons as guests step aboard.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: agar at 0.5% + 0.1% LBG in the cassis; aquafaba foam whipped with 0.3% xanthan. Zero-proof: blackcurrant cordial + sparkling verjus.
