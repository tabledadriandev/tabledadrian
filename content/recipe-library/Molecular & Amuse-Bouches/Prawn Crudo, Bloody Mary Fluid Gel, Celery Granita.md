# Prawn Crudo, Bloody Mary Fluid Gel, Celery Granita

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Raw carabineros against a spiced tomato gel and a fluffy celery ice — the cocktail-bar starter that stays razor-fresh in Riviera heat.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Carabinero or red prawns, sashimi-fresh — 8 (280 g)
- Tomato juice, strained — 200 ml
- Vodka — 15 ml
- Worcestershire sauce — 5 ml
- Tabasco — 1.5 ml
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Agar-agar — 1.9 g (0.85% of the seasoned tomato liquid)
- Celery juice, strained — 250 ml
- Caster sugar — 8 g
- Xanthan gum — 0.25 g (0.1% — fine granita crystals)
- Celery leaves + olive oil — to finish

## Method
1. Season celery juice with sugar and a pinch of salt; shear in xanthan (0.1%); freeze in a tray, forking every 30 minutes for 2.5 h.
2. Season tomato juice with vodka, Worcestershire, Tabasco, lemon, salt; boil with agar (0.85%) 45 seconds; set; blend to a fluid gel.
3. Peel prawns, keep heads for stock; butterfly tails; chill hard.
4. Slick each plate with a bold comma of Bloody Mary gel.
5. Season prawns with salt and a thread of olive oil; arrange over the gel.
6. Fork the granita loose.
7. Spoon celery granita beside the prawns; celery leaves on top.

## Plating & Service
Serve on frozen plates within 3 minutes. The granita must smoke slightly as it hits the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Alcohol-free: drop vodka, add 5 ml pickle brine. Scallop or tuna versions work; vegan with charred baby gem hearts.
