# Truffled Celeriac Velouté Shot, Hazelnut Air

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Winter in 50 ml — silky celeriac with black truffle under a toasted-hazelnut foam that smells like a forest floor.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Celeriac, peeled, diced — 400 g
- Shallot, sliced — 40 g
- Butter — 25 g
- Vegetable stock — 500 ml
- Double cream — 80 ml
- Black truffle, chopped — 8 g
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Hazelnut milk (blanched hazelnuts 60 g blended with 240 ml water, strained) — 250 ml
- Soy lecithin — 1.5 g (0.6% of hazelnut milk)
- Hazelnuts, toasted, crushed — 15 g

## Method
1. Sweat shallot in butter 3 minutes without colour; add celeriac, sweat 5 more.
2. Add stock; simmer 20 minutes until completely tender.
3. Blend with cream, truffle and salt until absolutely smooth; pass through chinois.
4. Hold velouté at 70 °C.
5. Warm hazelnut milk to 45 °C with a pinch of salt; add lecithin (0.6%); tilt-blend the surface into a stable air, rest 60 seconds.
6. Pour 45 ml velouté per warmed shot glass.
7. Crown with hazelnut air and a few crushed hazelnuts.

## Plating & Service
Serve on a tray as guests move to the table — warm, aromatic, gone in two sips.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut allergy: replace hazelnut air with brown-butter milk air (same 0.6% lecithin). Vegan: oat cream, olive oil instead of butter.
