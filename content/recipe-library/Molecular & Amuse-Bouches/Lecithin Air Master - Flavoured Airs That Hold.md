# Lecithin Air Master — Flavoured Airs That Hold

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The 0.6% rule and the tilt-blend technique — a stable air from any watery liquid in ninety seconds, from parmesan milk to yuzu.

## Ingredients (serves 16)
- Flavoured liquid (thin, water-based, low-fat) — 300 ml
- Soy lecithin powder — 1.8 g (0.6%; range 0.3–1.0%)
- Fine salt or sugar — season boldly; air dilutes flavour perception
- Optional: 0.1% xanthan (0.3 g) for longer-lasting bubbles

## Method
1. The liquid must be low-fat — fat is the enemy of lecithin airs; skim or split first.
2. Season hard; the guest receives mostly bubbles.
3. Whisk in lecithin (0.6%); it dissolves best at 40–50 °C.
4. Pour into a wide, shallow container; more surface = more air.
5. Tilt the container 20°; run a stick blender half-submerged at the surface, drawing air down, 60–90 seconds.
6. Rest 45–60 seconds so heavy bubbles drain back; harvest only the standing top layer.
7. Spoon onto plates immediately; the air survives 2–4 minutes (longer with the xanthan assist).

## Plating & Service
An air on the plate signals technique in one glance. Beat it fresh per table; the base can hold for days chilled.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Citrus airs: keep below 40 °C to hold aroma. Sunflower lecithin swaps 1:1 for soy-allergic guests.
