# Lobster Roll Éclair, Bisque Glaze

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> New England meets Paris: a mini éclair filled with lemon-tarragon lobster, glazed with a reduced bisque "fondant" set with agar.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Choux paste — 300 g (water 125 ml, butter 55 g, flour 80 g, eggs 2–3)
- Cooked lobster meat, diced — 220 g
- Mayonnaise — 50 g
- Crème fraîche — 30 g
- Lemon juice — 8 ml
- Tarragon, chopped — 3 g
- Celery, fine brunoise — 25 g
- Lobster bisque, well reduced — 150 ml
- Agar-agar — 1.2 g (0.8% of the bisque)
- Butter, cold — 20 g
- Chive tips — 12

## Method
1. Pipe 12 éclairs of 6 cm; bake at 185 °C for 20 minutes, then 165 °C for 15 until hollow and dry; cool.
2. Reduce bisque to an intense 150 ml; whisk in agar (0.8%); boil 60 seconds; mount with cold butter.
3. Hold the glaze at 45 °C (agar sets below ~38 °C — work warm).
4. Fold lobster with mayonnaise, crème fraîche, lemon, tarragon, celery; season.
5. Split éclairs along one side; pipe the lobster filling generously.
6. Dip each éclair top into the warm bisque glaze; it sets to a satin skin in a minute.
7. Finish with a chive tip laid along the glaze.

## Plating & Service
Serve within 30 minutes, cool but not cold. Napkins mandatory; seconds inevitable.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Crab or prawn versions work identically. GF choux with rice flour; lighter filling with yoghurt replacing mayo.
