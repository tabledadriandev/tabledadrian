# Foie Gras Snow, Smoked Maldon and Brioche Soil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Frozen-grated foie melts on the tongue like savoury snow over sweet brioche crumbs — maximum luxury from 15 g per guest.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Foie gras terrine (cooked, firm) — 180 g
- Brioche — 120 g
- Butter — 20 g
- Demerara sugar — 8 g
- Fine salt — 1 g
- Tawny port — 30 ml
- Caster sugar — 5 g
- Agar-agar — 0.3 g (0.9% of the port)
- Smoked Maldon salt — 3 g
- Chervil pluches — 12

## Method
1. Freeze the foie terrine solid, minimum 4 h at −18 °C.
2. Blitz brioche to coarse crumbs; toss with melted butter, demerara, salt.
3. Toast at 160 °C for 10–12 minutes, stirring twice, to a golden "soil"; cool.
4. Boil port + sugar with agar (0.9%) 60 seconds; set, then blend to a fluid gel and load a squeeze bottle.
5. Spoon 10 g brioche soil into each frozen shot glass; add 3 dots of port gel.
6. At the pass, grate frozen foie over each glass on a microplane into a tall snowdrift.
7. Finish with smoked Maldon flakes and a chervil pluche.

## Plating & Service
Serve instantly — the snow starts melting in 90 seconds, which is exactly the point.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Halal-friendly luxury: frozen-grate a chickpea–miso "faux gras" torchon. GF: gluten-free brioche or buckwheat crumble soil.
