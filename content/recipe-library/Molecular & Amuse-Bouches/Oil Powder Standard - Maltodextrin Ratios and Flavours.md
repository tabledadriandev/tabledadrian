# Oil Powder Standard — Maltodextrin Ratios and Flavours

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Any fat, made snow: the 60% rule, the sieve discipline, and six house flavours that melt back to liquid oil on the tongue.

## Ingredients (serves 16)
- Flavoured oil or rendered fat — 100 g
- Tapioca maltodextrin — 60 g (start at 60% of fat weight; adjust to 80% for softer fats)
- Fine salt — 1 g (or icing sugar 5 g for sweet powders)
- House flavours: olive, brown butter, pancetta fat, chorizo oil, basil oil, toasted sesame

## Method
1. The fat must be liquid but cool — 20–30 °C; hot fat clumps maltodextrin.
2. Whisk maltodextrin with salt (or sugar) in a dry bowl.
3. Add the fat in three additions, whisking to a crumbly mass.
4. Test: pinch — it should powder, not smear. Greasy? Add malto 10 g at a time.
5. Pass through a drum sieve for a fine snow (twice for service-grade).
6. Store airtight with a silica sachet — humidity collapses it within hours at sea.
7. Plate with a dry spoon over dry elements only; contact with moisture returns it to oil (which is the trick — use it).

## Plating & Service
Dust over eggs, seared fish, salads or desserts at the very last second. A dune, not a blanket: 3–4 g per plate.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nutella and peanut butter powder at 50% malto for petit fours (adds nuts). Never inhale-adjacent plating — it's featherlight.
