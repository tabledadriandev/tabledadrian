# Aperol Spritz Gummy Wedge

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The spritz as a glowing orange wedge you chew — firm gelatine-agar hybrid gel with real prosecco bubbles trapped inside.

## Ingredients (serves 20)
- Aperol — 100 ml
- Prosecco, cold — 150 ml
- Blood orange juice — 100 ml
- Caster sugar — 45 g
- Agar-agar — 1.8 g (0.5% of total liquid — brittle structure)
- Gelatine leaves (gold) — 14 g (4.0% — chew and elasticity; the hybrid prevents cracking)
- Fine salt — 0.5 g
- Orange, for zest — 1
- Sea salt flakes — 2 g

## Method
1. Bloom gelatine in iced water.
2. Boil orange juice, Aperol, sugar, salt with agar (0.5%) for 60 seconds.
3. Cool to 60 °C; dissolve the gelatine (4.0%).
4. Cool further to 35 °C — just above set — then gently stir in the cold prosecco to keep some sparkle.
5. Pour immediately into a 2 cm-deep tray; refrigerate 3 h.
6. Cut into orange-wedge triangles with a hot knife.
7. Finish each wedge with grated zest and one salt flake.

## Plating & Service
Pile on crushed ice in an oversized coupe at aperitivo hour; they hold 2 h chilled.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: all-agar version at 0.9% + 0.15% LBG (less chew, cleaner snap). Negroni or Hugo spritz take the same hybrid ratios.
