# Aged Comté Royale, Onion Consommé, Chive Oil Pearls

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> French onion soup, inverted — the cheese becomes a silken baked custard, the broth arrives crystal-clear over it, chive pearls burst green.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Onions, thinly sliced — 800 g
- Butter — 40 g
- Dry white wine — 100 ml
- Water — 900 ml
- Fine salt — 8 g
- Gelatine leaves — 4.5 g (0.5% for freeze-thaw clarification)
- Comté 30 months, grated — 120 g
- Whole milk — 150 ml
- Double cream — 100 ml
- Whole eggs — 2 (110 g)
- Chives — 30 g
- Grapeseed oil — 70 ml
- Agar-agar — 0.9 g (0.9% of the water phase for pearls)

## Method
1. Caramelise onions in butter, low and slow, 45–50 minutes to deep bronze; deglaze with wine; add water; simmer 30 minutes; strain, season.
2. Clarify: dissolve bloomed gelatine (0.5%); freeze; thaw through muslin 24 h — an amber, transparent onion consommé.
3. Melt Comté into hot milk and cream; blend with eggs and 2 g salt; strain.
4. Bake 55 g royale per bowl in a bain-marie at 130 °C for 22–25 minutes to a shiver.
5. Blitz chives with 60 °C oil; filter. Boil 100 ml water with agar (0.9%); whisk in 30 ml chive oil; drip into cold oil for pearls; strain.
6. Reheat consommé to 85 °C.
7. Pour consommé over each royale tableside; float chive pearls.

## Plating & Service
Serve in glass bowls if you have them — amber over ivory, green beads on top. Spoons only, silence guaranteed.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Gruyère works. Add a 5 mm brioche crouton raft for the classical echo (adds gluten).
