# Charred Leek Ash Goat Curd Truffle

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Coal-black spheres of whipped goat curd rolled in leek ash — dramatic, dead simple, and a vegetarian anchor for any canapé tray.

## Ingredients (serves 14)
- Leek tops (green only) — 200 g
- Goat curd — 250 g
- Double cream — 40 ml
- Fine salt — 3 g
- Lemon zest — 2 g
- Thyme leaves — 1 g
- Honey — 8 g
- Black pepper — 0.5 g
- Chive batons — 14

## Method
1. Wash leek tops, pat dry; burn directly over gas flame or under a hot grill until fully blackened and brittle, 8–10 minutes.
2. Dry the charred leek at 120 °C for 20 minutes to guarantee crispness.
3. Blitz to a fine ash; sieve; season the ash with a pinch of salt.
4. Beat goat curd with cream, salt, lemon zest, thyme and honey to a pipeable paste; chill 1 h until scoopable.
5. Scoop 20 g balls with a small ice cream scoop; roll quickly between gloved palms.
6. Roll each ball through the leek ash for full coverage.
7. Rest 15 minutes in the fridge; pierce each with a chive baton.

## Plating & Service
Serve on a bed of black pebbles for the full "truffle" illusion. Cool but not fridge-cold — 10 minutes out before service.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: whipped cashew-coconut curd (adds nuts). Ash works with onion tops or aubergine skin too — keep a jar in the dry store.
