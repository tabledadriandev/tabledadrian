# Sous-Vide Confit Egg Yolk Sauce Batch

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Yolks held at 64 °C become a self-made sauce — jammy, pipeable, emulsion-stable; batch a dozen and the week's plates gain a golden gear.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Egg yolks, intact — 12
- Olive oil — 200 ml
- Fine salt — 3 g
- Options per batch of 4: smoked salt 2 g / white soy 8 ml / truffle trim 5 g

## Method
1. Set the circulator to 64.0 °C exactly — 63 °C is looser, 65 °C sets fudgy; know your target dish.
2. Submerge yolks in the oil in a small container (or bag them with oil, 4 per bag).
3. Cook 45–60 minutes; the yolk proteins thicken to a dense, glossy jam.
4. Season variants in their bags for the last 15 minutes.
5. Lift yolks with a slotted spoon; they hold shape and pipe like curd.
6. For sauce: whisk 2 yolks with 20 ml warm water — an instant carbonara-grade emulsion that won't scramble.
7. Hold at 50 °C up to 2 h, or chill 3 days and re-temper at 55 °C for 10 minutes.

## Plating & Service
One yolk over asparagus, tartare or potato espuma; pierced tableside it sauces the dish itself.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The confit oil becomes a bonus: eggy, faintly rich — use it in mayonnaise. Duck yolks (same temp, 75 min) are gala-night decadence.
