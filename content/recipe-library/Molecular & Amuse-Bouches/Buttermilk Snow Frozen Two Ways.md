# Buttermilk Snow, Frozen Two Ways

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Lactic, cooling "snow" that lifts rich or oily plates — one version grated, one scraped granita-style, both from the same two-minute base.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Buttermilk — 400 ml
- Double cream — 50 ml
- Caster sugar — 20 g
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Xanthan gum — 0.45 g (0.1% — finer, slower-melting crystals)

## Method
1. Whisk everything; shear in xanthan (0.1%); taste — it should be bright, barely sweet, clearly salted.
2. Way one (microplane snow): freeze in a shallow block 4 h; grate over dishes at the pass in drifts.
3. Way two (granita): freeze in a tray, forking every 30 minutes for 2.5 h to a fluffy scrape.
4. Hold either at −18 °C; refresh the grated snow per table (it compacts).
5. Never portion ahead — snow's life on a plate is 90 seconds.
6. Pair with fat (smoked fish, foie, fried items) or sugar (berries, honey cake).
7. Base keeps 5 days chilled before freezing; re-shear before use.

## Plating & Service
Snow over beet-cured salmon or strawberries at the table; granita into oyster shells or over melon.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free: coconut kefir + lime at the same 0.1% xanthan. Savoury umami version: add 4 ml white soy.
