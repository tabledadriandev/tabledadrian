# Fennel Pollen Cured Sea Bass, Bergamot Gel Ribbon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A 40-minute cure with fennel pollen, then ribbons of bergamot fluid gel laid like calligraphy over the translucent bass — Riviera minimalism.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Wild sea bass fillet, skinned — 300 g
- Fine salt — 12 g
- Caster sugar — 8 g
- Fennel pollen — 1.5 g
- Bergamot juice (or lemon + 2 drops bergamot oil) — 80 ml
- Water — 40 ml
- Caster sugar — 12 g
- Agar-agar — 1.1 g (0.9% of the bergamot liquid)
- Fennel bulb, shaved paper-thin — 60 g
- Olive oil — 20 ml
- Fennel fronds — 12

## Method
1. Mix salt, 8 g sugar, fennel pollen; cure the bass 40 minutes; rinse fast, dry.
2. Slice the bass 3 mm on the bias; lay on chilled plates.
3. Boil bergamot juice, water, 12 g sugar with agar (0.9%) 45 seconds; set 30 minutes.
4. Blend the set gel silky; sieve; pipe ribbons across the fish through a 3 mm tip.
5. Dress shaved fennel with olive oil and a pinch of salt.
6. Nest a little fennel salad beside the fish.
7. Finish with fronds and 3 drops of oil on the bass.

## Plating & Service
Serve at 10 °C within 20 minutes of slicing. White plate, one green line of frond — restraint sells it.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cure works on bream and amberjack. Yuzu or Meyer lemon gels (same 0.9% agar) rotate through the seasons.
