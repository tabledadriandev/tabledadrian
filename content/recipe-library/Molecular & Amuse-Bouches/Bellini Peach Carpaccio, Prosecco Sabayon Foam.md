# Bellini Peach Carpaccio, Prosecco Sabayon Foam

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Harry's Bar drunk from a plate — shaved white peach under a warm prosecco zabaione blown through a siphon.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- White peaches, ripe but firm — 3 (450 g)
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Caster sugar — 10 g
- Prosecco — 150 ml
- Egg yolks — 4 (72 g)
- Caster sugar — 40 g
- Peach liqueur — 15 ml
- Xanthan gum — 0.25 g (0.1% — foam stability in the siphon)
- Raspberries — 12
- Small basil leaves — 12

## Method
1. Freeze peaches 40 minutes (firmness for shaving); slice paper-thin on a mandoline.
2. Fan slices on chilled plates; brush with lemon and the 10 g sugar.
3. Whisk yolks, 40 g sugar, prosecco, liqueur in a bain-marie to 74 °C, whisking constantly to a thick sabayon.
4. Shear in xanthan (0.1%).
5. Load into a 0.5 L siphon; one charge; keep at 55 °C.
6. Siphon a warm cloud over the cold peaches at the pass.
7. Finish with a raspberry and basil leaf per plate.

## Plating & Service
The warm foam collapsing over icy peach is a 90-second window — plate, foam, walk.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Zero-proof: white grape juice + verjus sabayon. Nectarines or apricots take the same treatment through the summer.
