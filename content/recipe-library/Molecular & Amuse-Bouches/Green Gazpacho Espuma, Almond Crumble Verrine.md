# Green Gazpacho Espuma, Almond Crumble Verrine

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Cold cucumber-herb gazpacho whipped through a siphon into a green cloud over toasted almond gravel — a two-minute pass with a chef's polish.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Cucumber, peeled — 300 g
- Green pepper — 80 g
- Spring onion — 30 g
- Basil — 10 g
- Flat parsley — 10 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 40 ml
- Sherry vinegar — 15 ml
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Xanthan gum — 1 g (0.2% of the strained soup — siphon body without dairy)
- Marcona almonds — 60 g
- Smoked paprika — 1 g

## Method
1. Blend vegetables and herbs with oil, vinegar, salt for 2 minutes.
2. Strain through a chinois — you want ~480 g of smooth liquid.
3. Shear in xanthan (0.2%); chill fully to 4 °C.
4. Load a 0.5 L siphon; charge twice; shake well; rest chilled 30 minutes.
5. Chop almonds; toast at 160 °C for 8 minutes; season with paprika and salt.
6. Spoon almond crumble into 12 small verrines.
7. Siphon the espuma over to a soft peak; 3 drops of olive oil on each.

## Plating & Service
Serve immediately at 6 °C, spoon standing in the foam. Bright green against clear glass — no garnish needed.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: toasted sunflower seed crumble. Add a dice of raw scallop at the bottom for pescatarian menus.
