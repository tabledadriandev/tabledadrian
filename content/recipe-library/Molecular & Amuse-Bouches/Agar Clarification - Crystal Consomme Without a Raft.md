# Agar Clarification — Crystal Consommé Without a Raft

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Freeze-thaw clarification: all the clarity of a classical raft, none of the flavour loss — works on stocks, juices, even gazpacho.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Flavoured liquid (stock, vegetable juice, broth) — 1000 ml
- Agar-agar — 2 g (0.2% — the clarification dose, NOT a setting dose)
- Fine salt — to season after clarifying
- Muslin cloth + perforated tray — equipment

## Method
1. Season the liquid lightly (you can adjust after).
2. Whisk in agar (0.2%); bring to a full boil 60 seconds; the low dose sets a fragile gel.
3. Pour into a shallow container; set at 4 °C for 1 h.
4. Freeze the gel solid — minimum 6 h; ice crystals shred the gel network.
5. Thaw the frozen block in muslin over a bowl in the fridge, 24–48 h.
6. The gel web traps every particle; crystal liquid drips through with full flavour and colour.
7. Season the collected consommé; use within 4 days or freeze.

## Plating & Service
Expect 70–80% yield. This is the engine behind clear gazpacho, tom kha consommé and see-through "bloody mary" — one technique, a dozen dishes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Gelatine variant (0.5%) gives slightly rounder mouthfeel but isn't vegan and needs a colder room. For fatty broths, chill and skim before gelling.
