# Snail Croustillant, Parsley-Garlic Emulsion, Pastis Air

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Escargot without the pincers: crisp kadaif-wrapped snails over persillade cream with an anise air — Burgundy re-cut for canapé service.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Cooked Burgundy snails — 24
- Kadaif pastry — 80 g
- Butter, melted — 40 g
- Flat parsley — 40 g
- Garlic, blanched 3 times — 10 g
- Double cream — 100 ml
- Fine salt — 3 g
- Water — 150 ml
- Pastis — 15 ml
- Soy lecithin — 1 g (0.6% of the pastis liquid)
- Espelette pepper — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Pat snails dry; season. Wrap each pair in a loose band of buttered kadaif.
2. Bake at 190 °C for 10–12 minutes until the nest is deep gold.
3. Blanch parsley 15 seconds; shock; squeeze.
4. Blend parsley, garlic, cream, salt to a bright green emulsion; warm gently to 60 °C (no boil).
5. Warm water and pastis to 45 °C; add lecithin (0.6%); froth with a stick blender.
6. Spoon persillade emulsion into 12 small warm bowls.
7. Seat a croustillant on each, crown with pastis air, dust Espelette.

## Plating & Service
Serve immediately — the kadaif has a 10-minute crispness window over the warm cream.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Snail-averse guests: swap in roasted king oyster mushroom pieces, same wrap and bake. GF: rice vermicelli nest instead of kadaif.
