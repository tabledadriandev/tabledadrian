# Beef Carpaccio, Parmesan Espuma, Rocket Fluid Gel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Harry's Bar classic with its garnishes re-engineered: parmesan as warm foam, rocket as a peppery gel line, capers as crisp gravel.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Beef fillet, centre cut — 300 g
- Olive oil — 20 ml
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Parmesan, grated — 100 g
- Whole milk — 200 ml
- Double cream — 50 ml
- Gelatine leaf (gold) — 1.5 g (0.4% — siphon stabiliser)
- Rocket — 80 g
- Spinach — 20 g
- Water — 120 ml
- Agar-agar — 1.5 g (0.85% of rocket liquid)
- Capers, fried — 25 g

## Method
1. Wrap the fillet tight in cling film to a cylinder; freeze 90 minutes until firm.
2. Slice 2 mm rounds; flatten gently between oiled film; lay onto 4 chilled plates; salt and oil.
3. Steep parmesan in milk + cream at 80 °C for 15 minutes; strain; dissolve bloomed gelatine; load siphon, 2 charges; hold 55 °C.
4. Blanch rocket and spinach 15 seconds; blend with the water; strain.
5. Boil rocket liquid with agar (0.85%) 45 seconds; set; blend to a fluid gel; sieve.
6. Pipe gel lines across the carpaccio.
7. Siphon three parmesan clouds per plate; scatter fried capers.

## Plating & Service
Serve within 5 minutes — warm foam over cool beef. Cover the whole plate with meat; no china should show beneath.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Tuna or beet carpaccio take the same garnish set. Skip gelatine and serve parmesan milk as an air (0.6% lecithin) if no siphon aboard.
