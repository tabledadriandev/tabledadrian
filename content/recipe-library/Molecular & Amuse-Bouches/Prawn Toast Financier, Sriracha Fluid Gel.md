# Prawn Toast Financier, Sriracha Fluid Gel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The dim-sum classic rebuilt as a one-bite financier — prawn mousse baked into sesame sponge with a hot-sweet gel stripe.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Raw prawns, peeled — 200 g
- Egg white — 60 g
- Ice-cold water — 20 ml
- Fine salt — 3 g
- Cornflour — 10 g
- Sesame oil — 5 ml
- Spring onion, minced — 15 g
- White sesame seeds — 15 g
- Panko — 30 g
- Sriracha — 60 g
- Rice vinegar — 20 ml
- Honey — 15 g
- Agar-agar — 0.9 g (0.9% of the sriracha liquid)
- Coriander cress — 12 sprigs

## Method
1. Blitz prawns, egg white, iced water, salt, cornflour, sesame oil to a tight mousse; fold in spring onion.
2. Pipe into 12 oiled mini-financier moulds; top with mixed sesame and panko.
3. Bake at 180 °C for 10–12 minutes to 62 °C core; rest 2 minutes; unmould.
4. Whisk sriracha, vinegar, honey with 20 ml water; boil with agar (0.9%) 60 seconds.
5. Set the gel 20 minutes, blend smooth, sieve, load a piping bag.
6. Flash financiers 2 minutes at 200 °C for crust just before serving.
7. Pipe a neat gel stripe on each; crown with coriander cress.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm on a bamboo tray. The crisp sesame top against the bouncy prawn interior is the point.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: rice crumbs + GF cornflour check. Swap sriracha gel for yuzu-kosho gel (same 0.9% agar) for a sharper profile.
