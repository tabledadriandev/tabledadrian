# Octopus a la Gallega, Paprika Oil Powder, Potato Espuma

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Pulpo a la gallega with its olive-oil-paprika turned to powder that melts back on the warm octopus — Galicia through a maltodextrin lens.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Octopus tentacles, cooked (or raw, see step 1) — 500 g
- Bay leaf — 1
- Ratte potatoes — 300 g
- Whole milk — 120 ml
- Double cream — 80 ml
- Butter — 30 g
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 60 g
- Sweet smoked paprika — 4 g
- Hot paprika — 1 g
- Tapioca maltodextrin — 38 g (about 60% of the oil weight)
- Parsley, chopped — 4 g

## Method
1. If raw: simmer octopus with bay at a bare 90 °C for 60–75 minutes until a knife slides in; rest in its liquid 15 minutes.
2. Cook potatoes; tamis; beat with hot milk, cream, butter, 4 g salt to a pourable purée; sieve into a siphon; 2 charges; hold 60 °C.
3. Warm olive oil to 30 °C with both paprikas; steep 10 minutes; strain.
4. Whisk maltodextrin (60% of oil weight) with 2 g salt; work in the paprika oil to a red-gold powder; sieve.
5. Slice tentacles 1 cm on the bias; sear hard in a drop of oil 90 seconds for colour.
6. Siphon potato espuma into warm bowls; lean octopus against it.
7. Dust generously with paprika powder; finish with parsley.

## Plating & Service
Serve hot; the powder liquefies into smoky oil as it lands. Wooden plates nod to the tradition.


> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free: olive-oil-mounted potato foam (add 0.3% xanthan for siphon body). Charred hispi cabbage replaces octopus for vegetarian.
