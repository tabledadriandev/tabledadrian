# Gin Botanical Air for Oysters and Crudo

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A juniper-citrus air that lands the aroma of a martini over raw shellfish with only a whisper of alcohol — the raw bar's secret weapon.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Water — 250 ml
- Gin — 40 ml
- Juniper berries, crushed — 3 g
- Coriander seed, cracked — 1.5 g
- Lemon peel (no pith) — 4 g
- Cucumber peel — 10 g
- Caster sugar — 6 g
- Fine salt — 1.5 g
- Soy lecithin — 1.8 g (0.6% of the finished infusion)

## Method
1. Simmer water with juniper, coriander, lemon peel and sugar for 5 minutes.
2. Off heat, add cucumber peel; steep 10 minutes; strain.
3. Add gin and salt; the base should taste like a strong, cold martini's first sniff.
4. Cool to 40–45 °C; whisk in lecithin (0.6%).
5. Tilt the container; blitz the surface with a stick blender 60–90 seconds.
6. Rest 60 seconds; harvest only the standing foam.
7. Base keeps 3 days chilled; re-froth per service.

## Plating & Service
One teaspoon of air per oyster, scallop crudo or cucumber gazpacho — aroma first, brine second. Foam à la minute at the raw bar.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Zero-proof: double juniper, drop gin. Sunflower lecithin for soy-free trays. A pastis version (fennel + peel) suits sea bass crudo.
