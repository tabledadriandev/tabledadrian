# White Asparagus, Sauce Gribiche Espuma, Hazelnut Crumble

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sous-vide white asparagus in brown butter with gribiche lightened into a siphon foam — the classic's richness, half the weight.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- White asparagus, peeled — 12 spears (500 g)
- Butter — 60 g
- Caster sugar — 5 g
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Eggs, soft-boiled 6 min — 2
- Dijon mustard — 10 g
- Crème fraîche — 100 g
- Whole milk — 60 ml
- Cornichons, brunoise — 25 g
- Capers — 15 g
- Tarragon + chervil, chopped — 6 g
- Hazelnuts, toasted, crushed — 40 g

## Method
1. Vacuum-seal asparagus with 30 g butter, sugar, 3 g salt; cook sous-vide at 85 °C for 25–35 minutes until just yielding.
2. Blend soft eggs, mustard, crème fraîche, milk, 3 g salt until smooth; strain.
3. Load a 0.5 L siphon, 2 charges; hold at 50 °C (a warm gribiche base, eggs already cooked).
4. Brown the remaining 30 g butter to noisette.
5. Drain spears; glaze in the noisette 1 minute.
6. Plate 3 spears per plate; siphon a generous gribiche cloud alongside.
7. Scatter cornichons, capers, herbs and hazelnut crumble over the foam.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm, immediately. The foam carries all the gribiche sharpness without drowning the asparagus.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: toasted breadcrumbs (adds gluten). Green asparagus: 85 °C for 12 minutes only. Vegan foam: silken tofu + mustard + 0.2% xanthan.
