# Miso-Glazed Aubergine Cube, Bonito Air

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Nasu dengaku miniaturised into lacquered cubes under a smoky bonito foam that waves in the breeze — umami stacked on umami.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Aubergine — 2 large (600 g)
- Grapeseed oil — 30 ml
- White miso — 60 g
- Mirin — 30 ml
- Sake — 20 ml
- Caster sugar — 15 g
- Water — 250 ml
- Katsuobushi — 8 g
- Soy lecithin — 1.5 g (0.6% of the bonito infusion)
- Toasted sesame — 4 g
- Spring onion, fine rings — 8 g

## Method
1. Cut aubergine into 12 cubes (3 cm); score the tops in a crosshatch.
2. Pan-fry in oil over medium heat, all faces, 8–10 minutes until creamy inside.
3. Simmer miso, mirin, sake, sugar 2 minutes to a glaze.
4. Brush cubes generously; grill at 230 °C for 3–4 minutes until blistered and lacquered.
5. Bring water to 80 °C, off heat add katsuobushi; steep 4 minutes, strain.
6. Add lecithin (0.6%) to the warm infusion; froth with a stick blender, tilted; rest 1 minute.
7. Plate each cube on a spoon, crown with bonito air, sesame and spring onion.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm; the air should tremble. Dark stoneware makes the lacquer glow.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: smoked kombu-shiitake infusion for the air (same 0.6% lecithin). Works with courgette barrels in high summer.
