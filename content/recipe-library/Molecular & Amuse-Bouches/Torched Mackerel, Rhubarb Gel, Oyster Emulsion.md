# Torched Mackerel, Rhubarb Gel, Oyster Emulsion

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Blowtorched mackerel against sharp rhubarb fluid gel and a briny oyster cream — fat, acid and ocean in a three-point landing.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Mackerel fillets, pin-boned — 3 (330 g)
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Caster sugar — 5 g
- Rhubarb, chopped — 250 g
- Caster sugar — 25 g
- Grenadine (for colour) — 5 ml
- Agar-agar — 1.7 g (0.85% of the strained rhubarb juice)
- Oysters, shucked, with liquor — 3
- Egg yolk — 1 (18 g)
- Dijon mustard — 3 g
- Grapeseed oil — 90 ml
- Lemon juice — 5 ml
- Sea purslane or samphire — 30 g

## Method
1. Cure mackerel with the salt-sugar mix 20 minutes; rinse, dry; portion each fillet in four.
2. Simmer rhubarb with sugar and 50 ml water 8 minutes; strain — collect 200 ml juice; add grenadine.
3. Boil juice with agar (0.85%) 60 seconds; set; blend to fluid gel; sieve.
4. Blend oysters and liquor with yolk, mustard, lemon; emulsify with oil to a soft cream; season.
5. Blanch purslane 10 seconds; refresh.
6. Torch mackerel skin-side until blistered and just warm beneath, ~40 seconds per piece.
7. Plate: swipe of oyster emulsion, mackerel on top, rhubarb gel dots, purslane.

## Plating & Service
Serve slightly warm from the torch on cold plates. The rhubarb gel must be sharp enough to make you blink.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Egg-free emulsion: blend oysters with 0.3% xanthan and stream in oil. Gooseberry replaces rhubarb midsummer (same 0.85% agar).
