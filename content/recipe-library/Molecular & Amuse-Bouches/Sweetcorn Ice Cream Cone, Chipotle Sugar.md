# Sweetcorn Ice Cream Cone, Chipotle Sugar

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A savoury-sweet corn ice cream in a corn-husk-charred mini cone with smoky chipotle sugar — street-food memory, fine-dining execution.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Sweetcorn kernels (fresh) — 300 g
- Whole milk — 250 ml
- Double cream — 150 ml
- Egg yolks — 3 (55 g)
- Caster sugar — 60 g
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Xanthan gum — 0.35 g (0.05% — anti-ice-crystal insurance)
- Corn tortillas — 4
- Grapeseed oil — 20 ml
- Chipotle powder — 2 g
- Demerara sugar — 15 g
- Lime zest — 2 g

## Method
1. Char half the corn in a dry pan until spotted; simmer all corn in milk and cream 10 minutes; blend, strain hard.
2. Whisk yolks with sugar; temper with the hot corn cream; cook to 83 °C, stirring.
3. Add salt; shear in xanthan (0.05%); chill overnight; churn.
4. Cut tortillas into quarter-circles, brush with oil, roll around mini cone moulds; bake at 170 °C for 8 minutes; cool.
5. Mix chipotle powder with demerara and lime zest.
6. Rim each cone edge with a little water then chipotle sugar.
7. Pipe or quenelle a small scoop of corn ice cream into each cone at the pass.

## Plating & Service
Serve immediately, cone in a shot glass of corn kernels. The hot-smoky rim against the cold cream is the trick.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: coconut cream + 0.4% (of liquid) locust bean gum + churn. Brown-butter powder on top pushes it toward dessert.
