# Black Olive and Rye Edible Soil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The dark, salty-bitter "earth" that grounds any garden plate — batch it monthly, it keeps for weeks and makes vegetables look freshly dug.

## Ingredients (serves 16)
- Kalamata olives, pitted — 200 g
- Dark rye bread — 150 g
- Olive oil — 25 ml
- Demerara sugar — 15 g
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Dried porcini, powdered — 6 g
- Instant coffee — 2 g (depth, not flavour)

## Method
1. Rinse olives; dry at 90 °C for 2–2.5 h until leathery and matte.
2. Tear rye; toss with oil; toast at 150 °C for 20–25 minutes until fully dry.
3. Blitz olives to coarse rubble; blitz rye separately to mixed fine-and-coarse crumbs.
4. Combine with demerara, salt, porcini powder, coffee.
5. Pulse once or twice — the mix should look like fresh loam: uneven, dark, slightly glossy.
6. Re-dry the whole mix at 90 °C for 20 minutes (insurance against clumping).
7. Store airtight up to 3 weeks; refresh 5 minutes at 120 °C before big services.

## Plating & Service
Bed for salt-baked roots, goat cheese "truffles", tomato gardens; pipe herb emulsions straight onto it.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: buckwheat + GF crumbs. Sweet soil variant: cocoa, chocolate biscuit, muscovado, same two-stage dry.
