# Carrot-Ginger Sphere on Coconut Tapioca

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A glowing amber sphere of juiced carrot and ginger over creamy coconut pearls — vivid, dairy-free, and quietly anti-inflammatory.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Carrot juice, fresh — 250 ml
- Ginger juice — 15 ml
- Lime juice — 10 ml
- Fine salt — 1.5 g
- Calcium lactate gluconate — 5.5 g (2.0% of juice blend)
- Xanthan gum — 0.55 g (0.2%)
- Sodium alginate — 5 g (0.5% bath, 1000 ml water)
- Small tapioca pearls — 60 g
- Coconut milk — 200 ml
- Coriander cress — 10 sprigs

## Method
1. Blend carrot, ginger, lime, salt with xanthan (0.2%) and calcium lactate gluconate (2.0%); rest 2 h chilled.
2. Prepare the alginate bath (0.5%) ahead; rest overnight.
3. Boil tapioca in water 12–15 minutes until translucent; rinse cold.
4. Warm coconut milk with a pinch of salt; fold in tapioca; keep at room temp.
5. Freeze carrot base in 25 mm hemispheres (cleanest spheres) 3 h.
6. Bathe frozen domes 3 minutes at 20 °C; rinse 30 seconds.
7. Spoon 25 g coconut tapioca into glasses; seat one sphere on top; garnish coriander cress.

## Plating & Service
Chilled glass, espresso spoon, one dramatic burst. Holds 4 h submerged in carrot juice if prepped ahead.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap carrot for mandarin + turmeric in winter. For low-FODMAP guests, halve the coconut milk with lactose-free milk alternative.
