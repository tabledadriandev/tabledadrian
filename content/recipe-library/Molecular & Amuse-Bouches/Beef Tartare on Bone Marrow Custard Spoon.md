# Beef Tartare on Bone Marrow Custard Spoon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Hand-cut fillet on a warm, wobbling marrow royale — the custard replaces the raw yolk and doubles the beefiness.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Beef fillet, trimmed — 240 g
- Bone marrow, soaked and drained — 80 g
- Whole eggs — 2 (110 g)
- Beef stock, warm — 180 ml
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Shallot, brunoise — 25 g
- Cornichons, brunoise — 20 g
- Dijon mustard — 8 g
- Sherry vinegar — 6 ml
- Tabasco — 1 ml
- Chives — 5 g
- Crispy capers (fried 30 s at 180 °C) — 20 g

## Method
1. Melt marrow gently at 70 °C; blend with eggs, warm stock and half the salt; strain twice.
2. Pour 25 g royale into each deep ceramic spoon; steam at 84 °C for 11–13 minutes to a fragile set; hold warm.
3. Keep the fillet ice-cold; dice by hand into 4 mm cubes — never mince.
4. Fold beef with shallot, cornichon, mustard, vinegar, Tabasco, chives, remaining salt.
5. Check seasoning aggressively (cold mutes salt).
6. Quenelle 18 g tartare onto each warm custard.
7. Scatter crispy capers over.

## Plating & Service
Warm spoon, cool tartare — serve within 5 minutes of assembly so the temperature contrast survives.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No marrow? Brown-butter royale (same egg:liquid ratio 1:1.6). Venison or tuna tartare both sit happily on the custard.
