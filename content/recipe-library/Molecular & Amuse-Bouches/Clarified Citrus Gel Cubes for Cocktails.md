# Clarified Citrus Gel Cubes for Cocktails

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Crystal cubes of clarified grapefruit and lime that chill and garnish a drink at once — cocktail-food crossover for the bartender's station.

## Ingredients (serves 16)
- Pink grapefruit juice — 400 ml
- Lime juice — 100 ml
- Caster sugar — 50 g
- Fine salt — 0.5 g
- Agar-agar (clarification) — 1 g (0.2% of the juice)
- Agar-agar (setting) — 4 g (1.0% of the clarified liquid)
- Locust bean gum — 0.4 g (0.1%)
- Micro mint — 16 tips

## Method
1. Clarify: boil the mixed juices with 0.2% agar 60 seconds; set, freeze solid, thaw through muslin 24 h — a pale rosé, perfectly clear liquid (~400 ml).
2. Season the clarified juice with sugar and salt.
3. Take a third of it; boil with the setting agar (1.0%) and LBG (0.1%) 60 seconds.
4. Whisk back into the remaining two-thirds off heat (protects fresh aroma).
5. Pour into a 2 cm tray; set 1 h at 4 °C.
6. Cut precise 2 cm cubes with a hot wet knife.
7. Hold chilled, covered, 48 h.

## Plating & Service
Two cubes in a coupe, spirits poured over tableside: the drink stays cold without dilution, then the guest eats the garnish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Yuzu-sake cubes for the Japanese menu; blood orange for negronis. The 0.2%-clarify-then-1.0%-set pattern is a reusable house standard.
