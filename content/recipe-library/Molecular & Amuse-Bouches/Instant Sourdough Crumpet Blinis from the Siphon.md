# Instant Sourdough Crumpet Blinis from the Siphon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Siphon-aerated sourdough-discard batter griddled into featherweight blinis, holes and all — the caviar vehicle that uses up the starter.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Sourdough starter/discard (100% hydration) — 150 g
- Flour — 100 g
- Whole milk — 150 ml
- Whole egg — 1 (55 g)
- Caster sugar — 8 g
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Bicarbonate of soda — 2 g
- Butter, for the pan — 20 g
- N2O charges — 2

## Method
1. Whisk starter, flour, milk, egg, sugar, salt to a smooth, pourable batter; rest 30 minutes.
2. Whisk in the bicarb; strain into a 1 L siphon.
3. Charge twice; shake; rest 10 minutes.
4. Heat a non-stick pan over medium-low with a film of butter.
5. Dispense 4 cm rounds; the foam batter sets riddled with crumpet holes.
6. Cook 90 seconds until the surface dulls; flip 30 seconds.
7. Cool on a rack; re-warm 2 minutes at 160 °C for service.

## Plating & Service
Crème fraîche + caviar, smoked salmon + dill, or confit yolk + pancetta powder — the holes hold the garnish like a crumpet should.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: buckwheat + rice flour with 2 g psyllium. No starter aboard? 5 g yoghurt + 30 min rest fakes the tang.
