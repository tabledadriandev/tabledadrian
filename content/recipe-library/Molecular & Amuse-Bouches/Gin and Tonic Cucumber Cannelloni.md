# Gin and Tonic Cucumber Cannelloni

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A ribbon of raw cucumber rolled around a gin–tonic fluid gel with juniper crumble — the G&T you can pick up with two fingers.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Cucumber, long — 2 (600 g)
- Tonic water (flat) — 200 ml
- Gin — 40 ml
- Lime juice — 15 ml
- Caster sugar — 10 g
- Agar-agar — 2.2 g (0.85% of the G&T liquid)
- Fine salt — 1 g
- Juniper berries, toasted and crushed — 4 g
- Panko, toasted golden (or GF crumbs) — 20 g
- Borage or basil flowers — 12

## Method
1. Slice cucumbers lengthwise on a mandoline at 1.5 mm; salt lightly, rest 10 minutes, pat dry.
2. Boil tonic, lime, sugar, salt with agar (0.85%) for 90 seconds; stir in gin off heat (keeps aroma, drops ABV slightly).
3. Set in a shallow tray at 4 °C for 30 minutes to a firm slab.
4. Blend the slab to a glossy fluid gel; pass through a fine sieve; load into a piping bag.
5. Pipe a 10 g line of gel across each cucumber ribbon; roll into tight cannelloni.
6. Mix juniper with toasted crumbs.
7. Dip one end of each cannelloni into the juniper crumble; top with a flower.

## Plating & Service
Stand upright on a chilled stone, crumble end up. Serve within 20 minutes at 6 °C.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Zero-proof: replace gin with juniper-infused water. Swap tonic for yuzu soda and finish with togarashi crumbs for a Japanese accent.
