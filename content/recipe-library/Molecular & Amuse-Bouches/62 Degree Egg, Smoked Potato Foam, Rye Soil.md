# 62-Degree Egg, Smoked Potato Foam, Rye Soil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The comfort trinity — egg, potato, bread — rebuilt with a sous-vide egg, hay-smoked potato espuma and dark rye soil; humble, precise, deeply satisfying.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Eggs — 4
- Ratte potatoes — 300 g
- Whole milk — 150 ml
- Double cream — 100 ml
- Butter — 40 g
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Smoked salt — 2 g
- Dark rye bread — 120 g
- Butter (for soil) — 20 g
- Chives — 6 g
- Pickled shallot rings — 30 g

## Method
1. Cook eggs sous-vide at 62.0 °C for 60 minutes; hold at 55 °C.
2. Simmer potatoes in salted water; drain, dry, pass through a tamis.
3. Beat in hot milk, cream, butter, smoked salt to a pourable purée; sieve again.
4. Load a 0.5 L siphon, 2 charges; hold at 60 °C.
5. Blitz rye to crumbs; toast with butter and a pinch of salt at 160 °C for 12 minutes to a dark soil.
6. Crack each egg into a warm shallow bowl (white barely set, yolk fluid).
7. Siphon smoked potato foam to cover the egg; ring with rye soil, shallots, chives.

## Plating & Service
Serve immediately with a spoon only. Guests break the hidden yolk into the foam — sauce makes itself.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: buckwheat soil. Add trout roe or crisped pancetta on top; vegan version with confit egg-yolk-shaped carrot purée sphere is a fun cover.
