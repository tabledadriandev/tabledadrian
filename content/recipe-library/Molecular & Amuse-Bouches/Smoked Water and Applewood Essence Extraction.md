# Smoked Water and Applewood Essence Extraction

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> House-made smoked water — the invisible seasoning that puts campfire under sauces, gels and cocktails without a smoker on deck at service.

## Ingredients (serves 20)
- Still water, in a wide tray — 500 ml
- Applewood chips — 60 g
- Ice — 200 g
- Fine salt — 1 g
- Optional carriers: neutral oil 100 ml (for smoked oil in parallel)

## Method
1. Chill the water tray over the ice bed — cold liquid absorbs smoke compounds far better.
2. Light the chips in a stovetop smoker or smoking gun setup.
3. Smoke the covered tray in 3 rounds of 8 minutes, re-charging smoke each round and stirring between.
4. Taste after each round: target a clean bonfire note with zero ashtray bitterness (over-smoking is unfixable).
5. Season with the salt; bottle.
6. Run the oil tray alongside for smoked oil (same rounds).
7. Keeps 2 weeks chilled; freeze in ice-cube portions for dosing.

## Plating & Service
Dose 5–10% smoked water into tomato consommé, béarnaise, a margarita, or the alginate bath itself for smoked spheres.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cherry wood reads sweeter; oak heavier. No gun? Cold-smoke the tray in a kettle BBQ with a snake of 5 coals — 30 minutes, lid cracked.
