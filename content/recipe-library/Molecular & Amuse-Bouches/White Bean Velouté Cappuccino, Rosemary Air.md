# White Bean Velouté Cappuccino, Rosemary Air

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A humble bean soup dressed as a cappuccino — creamy base, aromatic rosemary foam "crema", and a dusting of porcini "cocoa".

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Cooked cannellini beans — 400 g
- Vegetable stock — 500 ml
- Celery, diced — 40 g
- Garlic, sliced — 8 g
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Rosemary sprigs — 6 g
- Water — 250 ml
- Soy lecithin — 1.5 g (0.6% of rosemary infusion)
- Dried porcini, powdered — 4 g

## Method
1. Sweat celery and garlic in oil 4 minutes; add beans and stock; simmer 15 minutes.
2. Blend until totally smooth with salt; pass; hold at 70 °C.
3. Simmer rosemary in the 250 ml water 5 minutes; steep 10 more; strain.
4. Season infusion lightly; add lecithin (0.6%).
5. Froth the infusion at 45 °C with a stick blender, tilted, until a fine-bubbled air forms; rest 60 seconds.
6. Pour velouté into espresso cups to two-thirds.
7. Spoon rosemary air on top; dust with porcini powder through a fine sieve.

## Plating & Service
Serve on saucers with tiny spoons — the cappuccino visual gets a smile every time.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add crispy pancetta dust for meat menus. Low-FODMAP: use canned lentils' firm cousin — go chickpea-free, infuse oil with garlic instead.
