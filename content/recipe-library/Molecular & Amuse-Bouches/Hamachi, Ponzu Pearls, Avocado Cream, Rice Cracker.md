# Hamachi, Ponzu Pearls, Avocado Cream, Rice Cracker

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sashimi-grade yellowtail with ponzu frozen-reverse-spheres that burst like citrus roe, silky avocado and a black rice crisp — raw-bar theatre, plated.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Hamachi loin, sashimi grade — 280 g
- Ponzu — 100 ml
- Water — 20 ml
- Calcium lactate gluconate — 2.4 g (2.0% of ponzu blend)
- Sodium alginate — 5 g (0.5% bath in 1000 ml water)
- Avocado, ripe — 1 (170 g flesh)
- Lime juice — 12 ml
- Fine salt — 3 g
- Cooked black rice — 80 g
- Grapeseed oil (frying) — 300 ml
- Radish, shaved — 40 g
- Shiso leaves — 4

## Method
1. Whisk ponzu, water, calcium lactate gluconate (2.0%); freeze in 12 mm hemisphere moulds.
2. Rest the 0.5% alginate bath overnight; bathe frozen ponzu domes 2 minutes; rinse; hold chilled (they thaw to liquid pearls).
3. Blend warm black rice; spread 2 mm; dehydrate at 65 °C for 8 h; fry shards at 200 °C for 5 seconds; salt.
4. Blend avocado, lime, salt to a tight cream; pipe-ready.
5. Slice hamachi 4 mm on the bias; season with a whisper of salt.
6. Plate: avocado dots, draped hamachi, 4–5 ponzu pearls per plate.
7. Plant rice crackers, shaved radish, torn shiso.

## Plating & Service
Cold plates, 4-minute assembly. Pearls burst soy-citrus over each slice — no sauce pot needed.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Salmon or sea bream sub cleanly. Vegan: king oyster "scallops" (60 °C sous-vide 30 min, then seared) with the same ponzu pearls.
