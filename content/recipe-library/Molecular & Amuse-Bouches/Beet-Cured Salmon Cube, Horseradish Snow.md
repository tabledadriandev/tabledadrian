# Beet-Cured Salmon Cube, Horseradish Snow

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Jewel-toned cubes of 24-hour beet-cured salmon under a drift of frozen horseradish cream — Nordic colour-blocking in one bite.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Salmon fillet, skinless, pin-boned — 300 g
- Fine salt — 30 g
- Caster sugar — 30 g
- Raw beetroot, grated — 150 g
- Gin — 15 ml
- Dill, chopped — 8 g
- Double cream — 100 ml
- Fresh horseradish, grated — 15 g
- Lemon juice — 5 ml
- Dill fronds — 12

## Method
1. Mix salt, sugar, beetroot, gin and chopped dill; pack around the salmon; cure 24 h at 4 °C, turning once.
2. Rinse the fillet 10 seconds, pat dry; the outer 3 mm should be magenta.
3. Steep horseradish in cream 30 minutes; strain, season with lemon and a pinch of salt.
4. Freeze the horseradish cream solid in a shallow tray, 3 h minimum.
5. Cut salmon into 12 neat cubes (2.5 cm), magenta edge up.
6. Set each cube on a chilled spoon.
7. At the pass, grate the frozen cream over each cube in a fine snow; finish with a dill frond.

## Plating & Service
Serve on frozen spoons within 2 minutes of snowing — the melt onto cool salmon is the sensation.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free: freeze oat crème fraîche with horseradish. The cure works on trout and mackerel; add juniper for a wilder profile.
