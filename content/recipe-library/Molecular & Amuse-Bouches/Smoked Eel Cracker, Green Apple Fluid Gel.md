# Smoked Eel Cracker, Green Apple Fluid Gel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Smoke, fat, acid, crunch in one bite — a puffed tapioca-eel cracker cut by razor-sharp Granny Smith gel.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Tapioca pearls, small — 100 g
- Smoked eel trimmings — 60 g
- Water — 500 ml
- Fine salt — 3 g
- Smoked eel fillet, 1 cm batons — 120 g
- Granny Smith juice (juiced, with 0.5 g ascorbic acid) — 250 ml
- Caster sugar — 8 g
- Agar-agar — 2.1 g (0.85% of apple juice)
- Crème de raifort (horseradish) — 15 g
- Chive tips — 12

## Method
1. Simmer tapioca in the water with eel trimmings and salt for ~35 minutes until translucent; discard trimmings.
2. Blend briefly, spread 2 mm thin on a silicone mat; dehydrate at 60 °C for 8–10 h (or oven 70 °C fan, door ajar).
3. Snap into shards; fry at 200 °C for 5 seconds until puffed; drain, season.
4. Boil apple juice and sugar with agar (0.85%) 90 seconds; set at 4 °C, then blend to a smooth fluid gel and sieve.
5. Warm eel batons gently at 50 °C for 4 minutes (just to release fat, not cook).
6. Pipe 3 dots of apple gel on each cracker; add a dab of horseradish.
7. Lay a warm eel baton across; finish with a chive tip.

## Plating & Service
Serve on a smoked-glass tray immediately — crackers soften within 10 minutes of saucing.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap eel for hot-smoked trout; GF works naturally if crumbs avoided (tapioca is GF — check soy sauce absent). Apple gel also loves oysters.
