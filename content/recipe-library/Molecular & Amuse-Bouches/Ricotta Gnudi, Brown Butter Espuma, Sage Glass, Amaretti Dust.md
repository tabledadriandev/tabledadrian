# Ricotta Gnudi, Brown Butter Espuma, Sage Glass, Amaretti Dust

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Pillowy gnudi under a noisette-butter siphon foam with a stained-glass sage tuile — nonna's flavours through a pastry-section lens.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Ricotta, drained overnight — 350 g
- Parmesan, grated — 60 g
- Egg yolk — 1 (18 g)
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Nutmeg — 0.3 g
- Semolina — 120 g
- Butter — 150 g
- Whole milk — 150 ml
- Gelatine leaf — 1.2 g (0.4% of the foam base)
- Isomalt — 60 g
- Sage leaves, small — 12
- Amaretti — 30 g

## Method
1. Mix ricotta, parmesan, yolk, salt, nutmeg; roll 20 g balls; bury in semolina 24 h at 4 °C (they self-skin).
2. Brown the butter to deep noisette; strain.
3. Whisk 100 g noisette into the warm milk; season; dissolve bloomed gelatine (0.4%); siphon, 2 charges; hold 55 °C.
4. Melt isomalt to 165 °C; spoon thin pools on silicone; press a sage leaf into each; a second drizzle over; cool to glass.
5. Poach gnudi in barely-simmering salted water 3–4 minutes until they float plus 1 minute.
6. Roll poached gnudi in the remaining noisette.
7. Plate 4 gnudi, siphon brown-butter foam, plant sage glass, dust with crushed amaretti.

## Plating & Service
Serve instantly — the foam slumps in 3 minutes and the glass hates steam. Warn the galley: no lids.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: skip amaretti, use toasted breadcrumbs with sugar. Pumpkin gnudi in October; same 0.4% gelatine foam.
