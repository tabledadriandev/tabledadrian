# Oyster Leaf, Cucumber Air and Mignonette Pearls

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The vegan oyster: the uncanny sea flavour of oyster leaf with cold cucumber foam and popping shallot-vinegar caviar, served on the half shell.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Oyster leaves (Mertensia maritima) — 12
- Cucumber juice, strained — 250 ml
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Soy lecithin — 1.5 g (0.6% of cucumber juice)
- Red wine vinegar — 80 ml
- Water — 20 ml
- Shallot, brunoise — 20 g
- Caster sugar — 5 g
- Sodium alginate — 1 g (1.0% of vinegar base)
- Calcium chloride — 5 g in 1000 ml water (0.5% bath)

## Method
1. Simmer vinegar, water, sugar 1 minute; cool, add shallot; strain off 100 g liquid for pearls (keep shallot).
2. Blend the vinegar liquid with alginate (1.0%); rest 2 h.
3. Drip into the 0.5% calcium bath, 60 seconds; rinse — you want 5–6 pearls per serving.
4. Season cucumber juice with salt; add lecithin (0.6%) and froth cold with a stick blender.
5. Chill 12 cleaned oyster shells on crushed ice.
6. Lay an oyster leaf in each shell, a pinch of drained shallot, then pearls.
7. Crown with cucumber air at the last second.

## Plating & Service
Present on an ice tray exactly like a plateau de fruits de mer. Guests slurp from the shell.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> If oyster leaf is unavailable, use a nori-brushed baby gem heart. Non-vegan: lay a real oyster beneath — the pearls replace mignonette.
