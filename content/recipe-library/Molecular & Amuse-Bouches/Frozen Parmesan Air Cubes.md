# Frozen Parmesan Air Cubes

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A lecithin air, frozen — weightless umami cubes that dissolve on hot pasta or soup in seconds, leaving only parmesan behind.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Parmesan, grated — 150 g
- Whole milk — 300 ml
- Water — 150 ml
- Fine salt — 1 g
- Soy lecithin — 2.7 g (0.6% of the strained liquid)

## Method
1. Steep parmesan in milk and water at 80 °C for 20 minutes; do not boil.
2. Strain without pressing; skim any surface fat (fat collapses the air).
3. Season with salt; cool to 45 °C.
4. Whisk in lecithin (0.6%); tilt-blitz the surface to a dense foam.
5. Spoon the foam into small silicone cube moulds, layering as it builds.
6. Freeze solid, 3 h; unmould into a lidded box; hold at −18 °C up to 1 week.
7. Serve frozen — placed on hot food, a cube collapses into an instant parmesan cloud, then a sauce.

## Plating & Service
One cube over hand-rolled pasta, risotto or onion consommé, set down in front of the guest so they watch it die beautifully.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pecorino or comté steep the same. Vegan: white miso + nutritional yeast in oat milk, same 0.6% lecithin, freezes identically.
