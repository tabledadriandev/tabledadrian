# Aerated Chocolate-Miso Bite, Buckwheat Praline

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A savoury-leaning petit chocolate: siphon-aerated dark chocolate set under vacuum for a moussey, cavernous crumb with salty miso depth.

## Ingredients (serves 14)
- Dark chocolate 70% — 200 g
- Cocoa butter — 30 g
- White miso — 25 g
- Grapeseed oil — 20 ml
- Buckwheat groats — 40 g
- Caster sugar — 40 g
- Water — 15 ml
- Sea salt flakes — 1 g
- Black sesame — 5 g

## Method
1. Toast buckwheat in a dry pan 4 minutes until nutty.
2. Cook sugar and water to 165 °C; stir in buckwheat and sesame; pour on silicone, cool, chop to praline rubble.
3. Melt chocolate and cocoa butter to 45 °C; blend in miso and oil until smooth.
4. Pour into a 0.5 L siphon at 40–45 °C; double-charge with N2O.
5. Dispense into a vacuum canister (or deep container in a chamber sealer); pull vacuum so the mousse expands, then set the container in the fridge under vacuum 2 h. No chamber? Siphon into cups and freeze fast — still airy, less dramatic.
6. Break the aerated slab into rough 3 cm shards.
7. Press one edge of each into praline; finish with a salt flake.

## Plating & Service
Serve as the bridge to dessert or with coffee; the vacuum bubbles glint like volcanic rock.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut praline (hazelnut) for classic direction; keep vegan by checking chocolate. Add 2 g yuzu zest to the melt for lift.
