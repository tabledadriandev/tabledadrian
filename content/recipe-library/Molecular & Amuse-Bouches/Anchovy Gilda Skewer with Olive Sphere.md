# Anchovy Gilda Skewer with Olive Sphere

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Basque gilda, upgraded: the olive is a liquid manzanilla sphere that explodes against salty anchovy and hot guindilla.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Manzanilla olive brine + blended olives, strained — 150 ml
- Water — 50 ml
- Calcium lactate gluconate — 3 g (1.5% — brine already carries calcium)
- Xanthan gum — 0.4 g (0.2%)
- Sodium alginate — 5 g (0.5% bath in 1000 ml water)
- Cantabrian anchovy fillets — 12
- Guindilla peppers — 12
- Extra virgin olive oil — 30 ml
- Lemon zest — 1 g

## Method
1. Blend olives with their brine and water; strain hard through muslin to a green "olive juice" (200 g).
2. Shear in xanthan (0.2%) and calcium lactate gluconate (1.5%); rest 2 h chilled.
3. Rest the 0.5% alginate bath overnight.
4. Scoop 10 g portions with a round measuring spoon into the bath; bathe 2.5 minutes; rinse.
5. Hold spheres in olive oil with lemon zest.
6. Fold each anchovy in a wave onto a pick with a guindilla.
7. Seat one olive sphere at the tip of each skewer just before service.

## Plating & Service
Serve with ice-cold fino or a dirty martini; instruct: whole skewer, one bite.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan gilda: swap anchovy for smoked aubergine ribbon. The olive sphere technique works with kalamata for Greek menus.
