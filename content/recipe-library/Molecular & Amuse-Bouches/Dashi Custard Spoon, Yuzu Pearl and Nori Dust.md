# Dashi Custard Spoon, Yuzu Pearl and Nori Dust

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Chawanmushi shrunk to one perfect spoonful, lifted by a bursting yuzu pearl — silk, umami, citrus in three seconds.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Kombu — 5 g
- Katsuobushi — 10 g
- Water — 400 ml
- Whole eggs — 2 (110 g)
- Light soy sauce (GF tamari) — 8 ml
- Mirin — 8 ml
- Yuzu juice — 60 ml
- Water (for pearl base) — 40 ml
- Sodium alginate — 1 g (1.0% of yuzu base)
- Calcium chloride bath — 5 g in 1000 ml water (0.5%)
- Nori sheet — 1 (3 g)

## Method
1. Cold-steep kombu in water 30 minutes, bring to 60 °C, remove kombu; add katsuobushi off heat, steep 5 minutes, strain — you need 300 ml dashi.
2. Whisk eggs with dashi (ratio 1:3), tamari, mirin; strain twice for silk.
3. Fill 10 deep ceramic spoons with 30 g custard; steam at 85 °C for 10–12 minutes until just set with a tremble.
4. Blend yuzu juice + water with alginate (1.0%); rest 2 h to de-aerate.
5. Drip into the 0.5% calcium bath at 20 °C, 60 seconds; rinse.
6. Toast nori 30 seconds over flame; blitz to dust.
7. Place one yuzu pearl on each warm custard; dust one edge with nori.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm (55 °C) within minutes of steaming; the pearl must burst against the warm custard.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian: shiitake–kombu dashi only. Add a cube of sous-vide 62 °C lobster tail beneath the custard for charter upgrades.
