# Sake-Steamed Clam, Dashi Gelée and Shiso Air

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A single palourde in its shell under trembling dashi jelly and green shiso foam — the sea, suspended.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Palourde clams, large — 12
- Sake — 80 ml
- Kombu — 4 g
- Katsuobushi — 8 g
- Water — 350 ml
- Light soy — 6 ml
- Mirin — 6 ml
- Gelatine leaves (gold) — 5 g (1.4% of the dashi — soft, spoonable gelée)
- Shiso leaves — 10
- Water (for air) — 150 ml
- Soy lecithin — 0.9 g (0.6% of shiso water)

## Method
1. Steam clams with sake, covered, 2–3 minutes until they open; reserve meat in shells; strain and keep the nectar.
2. Make dashi: kombu in water to 60 °C, remove; katsuobushi off heat 4 minutes; strain.
3. Combine 250 ml dashi with clam nectar, soy, mirin; dissolve bloomed gelatine (1.4%) at 60 °C.
4. Spoon gelée base over each clam in its shell; set 2 h at 4 °C.
5. Blanch shiso 5 seconds; blend with the 150 ml water; strain.
6. Add lecithin (0.6%); froth cold with a stick blender.
7. Crown each set clam with shiso air just before service.

## Plating & Service
Serve on crushed ice with seaweed; one slurp per shell. The gelée must tremble, not bounce.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan-adjacent: swap clam for compressed cucumber "shell", kombu-shiitake dashi, agar at 0.45% + 0.1% LBG instead of gelatine.
