# Parmesan Air on Warm Tomato Water Geleé

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Caprese as vapour and glass — a barely-set warm tomato gel under a weightless parmesan foam; umami with almost no weight on the palate.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Ripe tomatoes — 800 g
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Agar-agar — 1.6 g (0.55% of tomato water — soft, warm-stable gel)
- Locust bean gum — 0.3 g (0.1%, for elasticity)
- Parmesan, finely grated — 120 g
- Whole milk — 250 ml
- Water — 100 ml
- Soy lecithin — 2.6 g (0.6% of parmesan liquid)
- Basil oil — 20 ml
- Micro basil — 10 leaves

## Method
1. Blitz tomatoes with salt; hang in muslin overnight at 4 °C to collect ~300 ml clear tomato water.
2. Heat tomato water to 95 °C with agar (0.55%) and LBG (0.1%); cast 25 g into each small glass; set at 4 °C for 30 min. Agar sets firm but this low dose stays tender and holds warm service to 60 °C.
3. Steep parmesan in milk + water at 80 °C for 20 minutes; strain without pressing.
4. Season the parmesan milk, add lecithin (0.6%), and froth with a stick blender at the surface, tilting the pan to draw air.
5. Let the foam stabilise 60 seconds; spoon only the air, not the liquid.
6. Warm the gel glasses 3 minutes at 55 °C in the oven before service.
7. Crown each with parmesan air, 3 drops basil oil and a micro basil leaf.

## Plating & Service
Serve immediately in small stemless glasses — the contrast of warm gel and cool air lasts about 2 minutes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: nutritional-yeast + miso "parmesan" milk (lecithin still 0.6%). Add a drop of 25-year balsamic under the gel for a sweet floor.
