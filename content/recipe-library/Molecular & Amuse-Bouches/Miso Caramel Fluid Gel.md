# Miso Caramel Fluid Gel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Salted caramel with umami undertow, gelled to pipe in perfect beads — bridges desserts, roasted roots and seared scallops alike.

## Ingredients (serves 14)
- Caster sugar — 150 g
- Double cream, warm — 150 ml
- Butter — 30 g
- White miso — 35 g
- Water — 100 ml
- Fine salt — 0.5 g
- Agar-agar — 3.5 g (0.85% of the finished caramel liquid)

## Method
1. Dry-caramelise the sugar to deep amber (185 °C by eye: wisping smoke, mahogany edge).
2. Off heat, whisk in warm cream carefully, then butter.
3. Blend in miso and salt; loosen with the water to a pourable sauce (~410 g).
4. Whisk in agar (0.85%); boil 60 seconds.
5. Set in a shallow tray 30 minutes at 4 °C.
6. Blend to a glossy fluid gel; sieve; bottle.
7. Keeps 7 days chilled; serves cold or warmed to 50 °C.

## Plating & Service
Beads beside chocolate desserts, a stripe under roasted pumpkin, or dotted on a scallop crudo with lime.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: coconut cream + refined coconut oil; ratios unchanged. Red miso is stronger — cut to 25 g.
