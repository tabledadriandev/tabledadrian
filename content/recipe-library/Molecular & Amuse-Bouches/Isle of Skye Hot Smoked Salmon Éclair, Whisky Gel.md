# Hot-Smoked Salmon Éclair, Whisky Gel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Smoked salmon mousse in crisp choux with a peaty whisky fluid gel — a Scottish dram you can pass on a tray.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Choux paste — 300 g (water 125 ml, butter 55 g, flour 80 g, eggs 2–3)
- Hot-smoked salmon — 200 g
- Cream cheese — 100 g
- Crème fraîche — 50 g
- Lemon juice — 8 ml
- Dill, chopped — 4 g
- Black pepper — 1 g
- Islay whisky — 40 ml
- Water — 60 ml
- Honey — 12 g
- Agar-agar — 0.85 g (0.85% of the whisky liquid)
- Salmon roe — 30 g

## Method
1. Pipe 12 mini éclairs; bake at 185 °C for 20 minutes, then 165 °C for 12–15; cool.
2. Blend salmon, cream cheese, crème fraîche, lemon, pepper; fold in dill; load a piping bag.
3. Warm water and honey; add agar (0.85%); boil 60 seconds; add whisky off heat.
4. Set 30 minutes; blend to a fluid gel; sieve into a squeeze bottle.
5. Split éclairs; pipe the salmon mousse in waves.
6. Dot whisky gel along the mousse.
7. Finish with salmon roe and dill tips; cap at an angle.

## Plating & Service
Serve cool within 45 minutes. Offer the same whisky as a 10 ml pour alongside for the full echo.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Trout + fino sherry gel (same 0.85%) for Spanish menus; GF choux works; skip roe for pregnancy-safe trays.
