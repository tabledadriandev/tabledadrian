# Lobster, Sweetcorn Espuma, Popcorn Powder, Lime Gel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Butter-poached lobster with corn three ways — warm foam, buttered-popcorn maltodextrin powder, charred kernels — cut by a sharp lime gel.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Lobster tails, raw, shelled — 4 (320 g)
- Butter — 100 g
- Sweetcorn kernels — 400 g
- Whole milk — 200 ml
- Double cream — 60 ml
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Popcorn, popped in butter — 30 g
- Melted butter (for powder) — 40 g
- Tapioca maltodextrin — 26 g (about 60% of the butter weight)
- Lime juice — 80 ml
- Water — 40 ml
- Caster sugar — 15 g
- Agar-agar — 1.2 g (0.9% of the lime liquid)
- Coriander cress — 8

## Method
1. Simmer 300 g corn in milk 12 minutes; blend with cream and salt; strain; siphon with 2 charges; hold 60 °C.
2. Char remaining kernels in a dry pan until spotted black.
3. Blitz popcorn to dust; whisk maltodextrin (60% of the melted-butter weight) with the popcorn dust and a pinch of salt; work in the butter to a powder; sieve.
4. Boil lime juice, water, sugar with agar (0.9%) 45 seconds; set; blend to fluid gel.
5. Poach lobster in the 100 g butter with 30 ml water at 58–60 °C for 6–8 minutes, basting.
6. Slice each tail in three; plate over corn espuma.
7. Scatter charred corn, dunes of popcorn powder, lime gel dots; finish with cress.

## Plating & Service
Serve immediately in warm shallow bowls; powder last so it stays dry. Tastes like a fairground on the Riviera.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Monkfish or scallops sub for lobster; vegan version stars grilled corn ribs with olive-oil powder instead of butter.
