# Compressed Nashi, Blue Cheese Mousse, Walnut Soil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Vacuum-compressed nashi turns to glassy, juice-saturated tiles — the perfect cool plinth for piped Roquefort cream and bitter walnut crumble.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Nashi pear — 2 (400 g)
- Verjus — 60 ml
- Honey — 10 g
- Roquefort — 90 g
- Crème fraîche — 60 g
- Double cream — 60 ml
- Walnuts — 60 g
- Demerara sugar — 15 g
- Fine salt — 1 g
- Celery leaves, tiny — 12

## Method
1. Cut nashi into 2 cm cubes; vacuum-seal flat with verjus and honey at 99% compression; rest 30 minutes (the flesh turns translucent).
2. Toast walnuts at 160 °C for 8 minutes; pulse with sugar and salt to a coarse soil; re-toast 4 minutes.
3. Blend Roquefort with crème fraîche until smooth.
4. Whip cream to soft peaks; fold into the cheese; load a piping bag with a small star tip; chill 30 minutes.
5. Drain nashi cubes, pat dry.
6. Pipe a rosette of mousse on each cube.
7. Dip mousse tops into walnut soil; finish with a celery leaf.

## Plating & Service
Line up on a cool slate; serve within 30 minutes at 8–10 °C. Pairs stupidly well with Sauternes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: pumpkin-seed soil. No vacuum machine: compress under a weighted tray 2 h — softer effect, still lovely.
