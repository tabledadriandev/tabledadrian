# Beetroot Meringue Kiss, Whipped Goat Cheese

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A shocking-pink vegetable meringue sandwiching lactic goat cream — crunchy, then vanishing; the colour alone sells the tray.

## Ingredients (serves 15)
- Beetroot juice, reduced from 300 ml to 100 ml — 100 g
- Caster sugar — 40 g
- Egg white powder (albumin) — 8 g
- Xanthan gum — 0.3 g (0.3% of the reduced juice)
- Fine salt — 1 g
- Fresh goat cheese — 120 g
- Double cream — 40 ml
- Honey — 8 g
- Thyme leaves — 1 g
- Freeze-dried raspberry powder — 3 g

## Method
1. Reduce beetroot juice to 100 g; cool to room temperature.
2. Whisk in albumin, sugar, salt and xanthan (0.3% stabilises the foam against weeping); whip in a stand mixer 8–10 minutes to stiff peaks.
3. Pipe 30 small kisses (2.5 cm) onto a lined tray.
4. Dehydrate at 70 °C for 2 h (or 80 °C oven, door cracked) until dry and hollow-sounding.
5. Whip goat cheese with cream and honey to piping consistency; fold in thyme.
6. Sandwich pairs of kisses with 8 g goat cream.
7. Dust one edge with raspberry powder just before service.

## Plating & Service
Stack on a chilled coupe like petit-four jewels; serve within 45 minutes — humidity is the enemy at sea.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: aquafaba version (100 g aquafaba, 0.4% xanthan, same method). Swap beet for carrot juice + orange for an autumn palette.
