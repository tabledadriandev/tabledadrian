# Chilled Almond Ajo Blanco Sphere, Grape and Dill

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> White gazpacho as a trembling reverse sphere over shaved frozen grapes — Andalusian soul, avant-garde body.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Blanched almonds — 150 g
- Water, iced — 300 ml
- Garlic, blanched twice — 4 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 40 ml
- Sherry vinegar — 12 ml
- Fine salt — 3.5 g
- Calcium lactate gluconate — 9 g (2.0% of the finished soup)
- Xanthan gum — 0.9 g (0.2%)
- Sodium alginate — 5 g (0.5% bath in 1000 ml low-calcium water)
- Muscat grapes — 150 g
- Dill tips — 10

## Method
1. Blend almonds, water, garlic 3 minutes; rest 1 h; blend again with oil, vinegar, salt; strain — you need ~450 g velvet soup.
2. Shear in xanthan (0.2%) then calcium lactate gluconate (2.0%); chill 2 h to de-aerate.
3. Rest the 0.5% alginate bath overnight.
4. Freeze soup in 30 mm hemisphere moulds until solid.
5. Freeze grapes; shave on a microplane just before service into a snow.
6. Bathe frozen domes 3 minutes at 20 °C; rinse 30 seconds.
7. Bed each glass with grape snow, seat the sphere, top with a dill tip and 2 drops of olive oil.

## Plating & Service
Serve within 5 minutes of shaving grapes; the sphere thaws liquid inside in ~8 minutes — time the pass.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: sunflower-seed ajo blanco (same ratios). Classic garnish swap: melon pearls instead of grape snow.
