# Niçoise en Gelée, Tuna Confit, Olive Powder

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The salade niçoise set in a crystal tomato aspic terrine — every slice a stained-glass window of its ingredients, with black olive powder for thunder.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Tomato water (from 1.2 kg tomatoes, hung overnight) — 500 ml
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Agar-agar — 2 g (0.4%)
- Gelatine leaves (gold) — 5 g (1.0% — the agar-gelatine pair slices cleanly yet melts in the mouth)
- Tuna loin — 250 g
- Olive oil — 300 ml
- Green beans, blanched — 100 g
- Quail eggs, boiled 2 min 30, peeled — 6
- Cherry tomatoes, confit — 12 halves
- Anchovy fillets — 6
- Kalamata olives, pitted — 80 g
- Basil leaves — 12

## Method
1. Confit tuna in 60 °C olive oil for 25 minutes; flake into large petals.
2. Dehydrate olives at 90 °C for 2.5 h; blitz to powder.
3. Season tomato water; boil a third of it with agar (0.4%) 60 seconds; off heat, dissolve bloomed gelatine (1.0%); whisk back into the rest, keep at 35 °C.
4. In a lined terrine mould, layer tuna, beans, halved quail eggs, confit tomatoes, anchovies, basil.
5. Pour the tepid gel base to cover; tap out air; set 6 h at 4 °C.
6. Slice 2 cm with a hot thin knife.
7. Plate each slice; dust one edge with olive powder; dress with oil drops.

## Plating & Service
Serve at 8 °C with grilled sourdough for the table. The slice must stand clean-edged — chill the plates.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian: omit tuna/anchovy, double eggs and add artichoke confit. The 0.4% agar + 1.0% gelatine aspic ratio is a house standard — reuse it.
