# Duck Consommé, Foie Royale, Orange Air

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Duck à l'orange as a clear ritual: gelatine-filtered consommé poured over a silken foie royale, with a bitter-orange air riding the steam.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Duck stock, well-made — 1000 ml
- Gelatine leaves — 5 g (0.5% for freeze-thaw clarification)
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Foie gras, cleaned — 80 g
- Whole egg — 1 (55 g)
- Double cream — 80 ml
- Duck consommé (from above) — 60 ml
- Orange juice — 120 ml
- Water — 80 ml
- Campari (optional) — 10 ml
- Soy lecithin — 1.3 g (0.6% of the orange liquid)
- Chervil — 8 pluches

## Method
1. Clarify: dissolve bloomed gelatine (0.5%) in hot stock; freeze solid in a shallow tray; thaw in muslin over a bowl at 4 °C, 24 h — crystal consommé, full flavour (no raft, no loss).
2. Blend foie, egg, cream, the 60 ml consommé, 2 g salt; pass twice.
3. Pour 50 g royale into each warmed bowl; steam at 84 °C for 14–16 minutes to a delicate set.
4. Season the remaining consommé; hold at 85 °C.
5. Warm orange juice, water, Campari to 45 °C; add lecithin (0.6%); froth.
6. Pour 150 ml consommé gently down the bowl's side over each royale at the table.
7. Float a spoon of orange air; finish with chervil.

## Plating & Service
Tableside pour, then silence — the royale should barely survive the spoon. Serve with nothing else.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken-liver royale for a gentler cost. Vegetarian: roasted-mushroom consommé (same 0.5% gelatine freeze-thaw) with a parmesan royale.
