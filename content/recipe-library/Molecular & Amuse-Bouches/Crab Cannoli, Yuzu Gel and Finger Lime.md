# Crab Cannoli, Yuzu Gel and Finger Lime

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A shatter-crisp brick tube of dressed white crab, dotted with yuzu fluid gel and citrus pearls that pop like roe — Riviera meets Kyoto.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Feuille de brick — 4 sheets
- Egg white — 20 g
- White crab meat, picked — 240 g
- Kewpie-style mayonnaise — 40 g
- Yuzu juice — 50 ml
- Water — 50 ml
- Caster sugar — 8 g
- Agar-agar — 0.85 g (0.85% of yuzu liquid)
- Finger limes — 2
- Shiso leaves, fine chiffonade — 4
- Fine salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Cut brick into 12 rectangles 12 × 8 cm; brush edges with egg white; roll around 1.5 cm steel tubes.
2. Bake at 175 °C for 8–9 minutes until deep gold; cool, slide off tubes.
3. Boil yuzu juice, water, sugar with agar (0.85%) 60 seconds; set, blend to fluid gel, sieve.
4. Fold crab with mayonnaise, salt and half the shiso; check seasoning.
5. Pipe crab into tubes from both ends (a wide plain tip).
6. Dot 3 points of yuzu gel along the top of each cannoli.
7. Halve finger limes, squeeze the citrus pearls over; finish with remaining shiso.

## Plating & Service
Rest on a ceramic cradle so they cannot roll. Fill maximum 15 minutes ahead — crispness is everything.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: rice-paper tubes dried at 60 °C then flash-fried. Lobster or spanner crab both work; vegan version with hearts of palm + vegan mayo.
