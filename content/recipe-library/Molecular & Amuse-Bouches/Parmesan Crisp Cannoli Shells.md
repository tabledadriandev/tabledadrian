# Parmesan Crisp Cannoli Shells

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> One-ingredient tubes: lacy fried parmesan rolled hot into cannoli shells — the keto vessel that upgrades any mousse into a canapé.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Parmesan, aged 24 months, finely grated — 240 g
- Black pepper — 0.5 g (optional)
- Fennel seeds, cracked — 1 g (optional)

## Method
1. Heat a good non-stick pan over medium (or oven at 180 °C on silicone).
2. Sprinkle 20 g parmesan into a thin 9 cm round per shell; season if using.
3. Cook 2–3 minutes until the fat renders, bubbling slows and the lace turns gold at the edge.
4. Lift with a palette knife; count 3 seconds; roll around a 2 cm dowel while pliable.
5. Hold 20 seconds until it sets crisp; slide off.
6. Repeat; regrease nothing — the cheese self-oils.
7. Cool completely; store airtight with silica up to 48 h.

## Plating & Service
Pipe with whipped ricotta-lemon, tomato tartare, or duck rillette. Also serves flat as a crisp or moulded over a cup as a tuile basket.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Grana padano is cheaper for staff-meal testing; pecorino runs saltier — cut with 20% mild alpine cheese. Keep shells away from any steam.
