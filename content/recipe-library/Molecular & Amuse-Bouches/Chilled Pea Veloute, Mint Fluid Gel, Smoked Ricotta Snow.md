# Chilled Pea Velouté, Mint Fluid Gel, Smoked Ricotta Snow

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A cold, electric-green soup with a formal mint gel line and frozen smoked ricotta grated at the table — spring served at 6 °C.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Petits pois, frozen — 500 g
- Vegetable stock — 500 ml
- Crème fraîche — 60 g
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Mint leaves — 25 g
- Water — 150 ml
- Caster sugar — 6 g
- Agar-agar — 1.3 g (0.85% of the mint liquid)
- Ricotta salata (or ricotta, cold-smoked 20 min) — 100 g
- Olive oil — 15 ml
- Pea shoots — 12

## Method
1. Boil stock; add peas; cook 3 minutes; blend at once with crème fraîche and salt.
2. Pass through a chinois over ice (speed preserves colour); chill.
3. Blanch mint 10 seconds; blend with the 150 ml water and sugar; strain.
4. Boil mint liquid with agar (0.85%) 45 seconds; set; blend to fluid gel; sieve.
5. Freeze the smoked ricotta 2 h until grateable.
6. Pipe a straight line of mint gel across each chilled bowl; pour velouté around at the table.
7. Grate ricotta snow over; finish with oil and pea shoots.

## Plating & Service
Tableside pour keeps the gel line crisp. Everything except the pour holds 24 h — a perfect prep-ahead starter.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: oat crème fraîche; frozen-grated almond ricotta. Courgette-basil version runs the same ratios (0.85% agar gel).
