# Confit Egg Yolk, Potato Espuma, Chorizo Crumb Verrine

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Huevos rotos in a glass — a 64 °C yolk buried in warm potato foam with crisp chorizo rubble; comfort food with tweezers.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Egg yolks — 10
- Olive oil (for confit) — 200 ml
- Ratte potatoes, peeled — 400 g
- Whole milk — 150 ml
- Double cream — 100 ml
- Butter — 40 g
- Fine salt — 5 g
- iSi cream charger siphon — 1 (2 charges)
- Chorizo, finely diced — 80 g
- Chives, sliced — 5 g

## Method
1. Confit yolks in 64 °C oil for 45 minutes (sous-vide bath or careful pan with thermometer).
2. Simmer potatoes in salted water until tender; drain and dry 2 minutes over heat.
3. Pass through a fine tamis; beat in hot milk, cream, butter — the purée must be pourable.
4. Sieve again, load into a 0.5 L siphon, charge twice, shake; hold at 62 °C in a bath.
5. Render chorizo in a dry pan over medium heat until crisp, 6–8 minutes; drain on paper.
6. Warm 10 verrines; add a spoon of chorizo oil to each.
7. Siphon potato espuma to half-fill, nest a confit yolk, cover with more espuma; top with chorizo crumb and chives.

## Plating & Service
Serve with an espresso spoon and the instruction to dig to the bottom. Ideal for cool-evening deck service.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian: smoked almond crumb instead of chorizo (adds nuts). Lighter: olive-oil-only espuma, milk instead of cream.
