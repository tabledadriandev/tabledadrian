# Fermented Honey Fluid Gel and Bee Pollen Crumble

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A sweet-sour honey gel (quick lacto shortcut) with a crunchy pollen-seed crumble — the bridge component between cheese courses and desserts.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Honey — 150 g
- Water — 150 ml
- Live yoghurt whey (strained from yoghurt) — 20 ml
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Fine salt — 1 g
- Agar-agar — 2.7 g (0.85% of the honey liquid)
- Bee pollen — 30 g
- Sunflower seeds, toasted — 40 g
- Demerara sugar — 15 g

## Method
1. Whisk honey with the water and whey; cover loosely; hold at 28–30 °C for 24–36 h until it tastes gently sour and fizzes faintly.
2. (Short on time: skip fermentation, add 10 ml extra lemon.)
3. Add lemon and salt; boil with agar (0.85%) for 60 seconds — this also pasteurises.
4. Set 30 minutes; blend to a glossy fluid gel; bottle.
5. Pulse sunflower seeds with demerara to coarse rubble; toast 6 minutes at 150 °C; cool.
6. Fold in bee pollen off heat (heat kills its perfume).
7. Store gel 1 week chilled, crumble airtight 2 weeks.

## Plating & Service
Gel dots + crumble beside aged comté, roast figs, or a yoghurt panna cotta — instant sweet-course architecture.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: maple syrup version, ferment with 5 g raw sauerkraut brine. Pollen-allergic guests: puffed quinoa crumble instead.
