# Brown Crab Custard Tart, Seaweed Cracker Lid

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The whole crab in two layers — a deeply savoury brown-meat custard under a snapping nori-rice cracker holding dressed white meat.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Brown crab meat — 120 g
- Whole eggs — 2 (110 g)
- Dashi or light fish stock — 180 ml
- Light soy — 8 ml
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Cooked sushi rice — 100 g
- Nori, blitzed — 4 g
- White crab meat — 120 g
- Lime juice + zest — 8 ml + 1 g
- Grapeseed oil for frying — 300 ml
- Coriander cress — 12

## Method
1. Blend brown meat, eggs, stock, soy, salt; strain twice.
2. Pour 25 g into each small ramekin; steam at 85 °C for 12 minutes to a soft set; keep warm.
3. Blend warm rice with nori; spread 2 mm thin on silicone; dehydrate at 65 °C for 8 h.
4. Snap into rounds slightly wider than the ramekins; fry at 200 °C for 5–8 seconds until puffed; salt.
5. Dress white crab with lime juice, zest, a little oil, salt.
6. Cap each warm custard with a puffed cracker.
7. Pile dressed white meat on the lid; finish with cress.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm; guests crack through the lid into the custard. A dish that eats bigger than its size.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF naturally (check soy = tamari). Lobster or hand-picked shrimp on top both work; vegetarian version with shiitake custard.
