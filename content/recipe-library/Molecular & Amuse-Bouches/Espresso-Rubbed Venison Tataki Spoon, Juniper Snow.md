# Espresso-Rubbed Venison Tataki Spoon, Juniper Snow

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sous-vide venison loin kissed with espresso and seared black, sliced onto spoons under a freezing juniper-gin "snow" grated at the pass.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Venison loin, trimmed — 300 g
- Finely ground espresso — 6 g
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Black pepper — 1 g
- Grapeseed oil — 15 ml
- Gin — 50 ml
- Water — 100 ml
- Sugar — 15 g
- Juniper berries, crushed — 3 g
- Redcurrants — 36
- Micro red mustard — 12 leaves

## Method
1. Rub venison with espresso, salt, pepper; vacuum-seal.
2. Cook sous-vide at 54.0 °C for 90 minutes; ice-shock 10 minutes.
3. Simmer water, sugar, juniper 5 minutes; strain, cool, add gin; freeze the mix solid in a shallow tray (alcohol keeps it grateable, not rock hard).
4. Sear the loin in smoking oil 30 seconds per face; rest 5 minutes.
5. Slice 2–3 mm; fold one slice per chilled spoon.
6. Add 3 redcurrants and a mustard leaf per spoon.
7. At the pass, grate the juniper-gin ice over each spoon like snow.

## Plating & Service
Serve instantly — hot-seared meat, melting alpine snow. Winter charters, fireplace lounge.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Beef fillet works at the same 54 °C/90 min. Alcohol-free snow: juniper tea + 20% sugar syrup, scraped granita-style every 30 min while freezing.
