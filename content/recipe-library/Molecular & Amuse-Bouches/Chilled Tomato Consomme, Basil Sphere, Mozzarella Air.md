# Chilled Tomato Consommé, Basil Sphere, Mozzarella Air

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Caprese as three phases of matter — clear tomato liquid, a bursting basil sphere, and mozzarella as fog. The table goes quiet.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Ripe tomatoes — 1200 g
- Fine salt — 10 g
- Basil leaves — 50 g
- Water — 150 ml
- Caster sugar — 4 g
- Sodium alginate — 1.6 g (1.0% of basil base)
- Calcium chloride — 5 g in 1000 ml water (0.5% bath)
- Buffalo mozzarella — 200 g
- Whole milk — 200 ml
- Soy lecithin — 2.2 g (0.6% of mozzarella milk)
- Olive oil — 20 ml

## Method
1. Blend tomatoes with salt; hang in muslin overnight — collect ~500 ml clear consommé; season, chill to 5 °C.
2. Blanch basil 10 seconds; blend with the water and sugar; strain to 160 ml.
3. Blend basil liquid with alginate (1.0%); rest 2 h; drop large spoonfuls into the 0.5% calcium bath 90 seconds — 4 ravioli-sized spheres; rinse.
4. Tear mozzarella into the milk; warm to 60 °C 10 minutes; blend, strain.
5. Add lecithin (0.6%); froth the mozzarella milk with a stick blender.
6. Place one basil sphere in each chilled coupe; pour 100 ml consommé around at the table.
7. Crown with mozzarella air and 3 drops of olive oil.

## Plating & Service
Instruct: one spoon through all three layers. Serve at 6 °C in stemmed glass so the clarity shows.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: oat-milk "mozzarella" air with 2 g white miso. The spheres hold 30 minutes submerged in consommé if pre-service crunch demands.
