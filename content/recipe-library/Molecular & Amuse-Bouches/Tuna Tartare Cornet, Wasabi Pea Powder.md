# Tuna Tartare Cornet, Wasabi Pea Powder

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A sesame tuile cone of yellowfin dressed in soy-lime, finished with a shocking green powder of freeze-dried wasabi peas — crunch, heat, silk.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Yellowfin tuna, sashimi grade — 240 g
- Soy sauce — 15 ml
- Lime juice — 10 ml
- Toasted sesame oil — 6 ml
- Chives, minced — 6 g
- Espelette pepper — 0.5 g
- Flour — 30 g
- Egg white — 30 g
- Icing sugar — 25 g
- Butter, melted — 25 g (or neutral oil for dairy-free)
- Black sesame — 8 g
- Wasabi peas — 40 g

## Method
1. Whisk flour, egg white, icing sugar, melted butter to a tuile paste; rest 30 minutes.
2. Spread through a triangle stencil on silicone; scatter black sesame; bake at 165 °C for 6–7 minutes.
3. Working fast, roll each hot triangle around a cone mould; cool.
4. Blitz wasabi peas to a fine powder; sieve twice.
5. Dice tuna 4 mm with a wet knife; keep on a bowl over ice.
6. Dress tuna with soy, lime, sesame oil, chives, Espelette — no earlier than 10 minutes before service.
7. Fill cones, dip the exposed tartare into wasabi pea powder.

## Plating & Service
Serve upright in a stone cone-holder within 10 minutes of filling. The powder must stay dry and electric-green.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fully dairy-free with oil-based tuile. Salmon or amberjack sub cleanly; vegan version with dashi-marinated tomato petals.
