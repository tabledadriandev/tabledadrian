# XO Scallop Tartlet, Shaoxing Pearl

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Raw diver scallop in a lacy tartlet with fierce house XO and a bursting Shaoxing pearl — a one-bite argument for Chinese flavours on fine-dining trays.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Wonton wrappers — 12
- Grapeseed oil — 20 ml
- Diver scallops, diced 5 mm — 200 g
- XO sauce, finely chopped — 40 g
- Lime juice — 8 ml
- Sesame oil — 4 ml
- Chives — 4 g
- Shaoxing wine — 80 ml
- Light soy — 10 ml
- Honey — 8 g
- Sodium alginate — 1 g (1.0% of the Shaoxing base)
- Calcium chloride — 5 g in 1000 ml water (0.5% bath)

## Method
1. Press wonton wrappers into oiled mini tart moulds; bake at 175 °C for 8–9 minutes until golden; cool.
2. Warm Shaoxing, soy, honey to 40 °C (drives off a little alcohol, helps gelling); cool.
3. Blend with alginate (1.0%); rest 2 h.
4. Drip pearls into the 0.5% calcium bath, 60 seconds; rinse.
5. Fold scallop with XO, lime, sesame oil, chives — dress no more than 10 minutes ahead.
6. Fill each tartlet with 18 g scallop mix.
7. Crown with one Shaoxing pearl and a chive tip.

## Plating & Service
Serve on a dark lacquer tray immediately; the pearl bursts wine over the sweet scallop.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: rice-paper tart shells. Cooked prawn version for pregnant guests; vegetarian XO (shiitake-chilli) is worth the batch.
