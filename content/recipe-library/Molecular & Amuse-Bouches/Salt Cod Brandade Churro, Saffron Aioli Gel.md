# Salt Cod Brandade Churro, Saffron Aioli Gel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Brandade piped and fried like a churro, ridges and all, with a saffron aioli that has been gelled so it clings in a perfect golden stripe.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Salt cod, desalinated 24 h — 200 g
- Whole milk — 200 ml
- Potato, cooked, riced — 150 g
- Garlic confit — 15 g
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Choux paste (water 100 ml, butter 40 g, flour 60 g, egg 1) — 200 g
- Egg yolk — 1 (18 g)
- Lemon juice — 8 ml
- Saffron threads — 0.2 g
- Grapeseed oil — 90 ml
- Agar-agar — 1 g (0.8% of the finished aioli loosened with 30 ml water)
- Fine salt — to balance

## Method
1. Poach cod in milk at 80 °C for 8 minutes; drain, flake.
2. Beat cod with potato, garlic confit and olive oil to a rough brandade.
3. Make choux; beat the brandade into the warm choux 2:1; load a piping bag with a star tip.
4. Bloom saffron in 10 ml hot water. Emulsify yolk, lemon, saffron water with grapeseed oil to a loose aioli; whisk in 30 ml water.
5. Boil the loosened aioli briefly with agar (0.8%); set 30 minutes; blend to a piping gel. (Yes — you can boil aioli when it's for a gel.)
6. Pipe 8 cm churros directly into 180 °C oil; fry 2–3 minutes to deep gold; drain, salt.
7. Pipe a saffron gel stripe down each ridge line.

## Plating & Service
Serve upright in a glass of folded paper, gel stripe facing the guest. Eat in two bites, hot.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF choux with rice flour works. Swap cod for smoked haddock; vegan brandade of butter beans + nori also fries beautifully.
