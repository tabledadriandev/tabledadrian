# Mojito Pipette Melon Ball

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A chilled melon sphere skewered with a pipette of rum-lime-mint — guests squeeze the cocktail through the fruit as they bite; instant party.

## Ingredients (serves 16)
- Galia melon — 1 (about 900 g)
- White rum — 80 ml
- Lime juice — 60 ml
- Sugar syrup 1:1 — 50 ml
- Mint leaves — 12 g
- Xanthan gum — 0.4 g (0.2% of cocktail — keeps it in suspension in the pipette)
- Lime zest — 2 g
- Sea salt flakes — 1 g
- Mint tips — 16
- Disposable 4 ml pipettes — 16

## Method
1. Scoop 16 melon balls (3 cm) with a parisienne cutter; chill on a cloth-lined tray.
2. Muddle mint with sugar syrup; rest 10 minutes; strain.
3. Whisk rum, lime juice and mint syrup; shear in xanthan (0.2%) — a light nappe so it doesn't leak.
4. Load pipettes with 3.5 ml cocktail each.
5. Spear each melon ball with a pipette stem, bulb up.
6. Dust melon with lime zest and one salt flake.
7. Crown each with a mint tip; hold at 4 °C max 1 h.

## Plating & Service
Stand in a bed of crushed ice; brief the crew to explain the squeeze-and-bite. Sunset slot, upper deck.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Virgin: verjus replaces rum. Watermelon + tequila + Tajín rim turns it into a paloma bite.
