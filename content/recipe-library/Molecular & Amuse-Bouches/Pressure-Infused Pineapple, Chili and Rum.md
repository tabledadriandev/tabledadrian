# Pressure-Infused Pineapple, Chilli and Rum

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The iSi as an infusion machine: rum, lime and chilli forced into pineapple cells in ninety seconds — a cocktail-food crossover garnish with a bite.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Pineapple, ripe, 2 cm chunks — 400 g
- Aged rum — 60 ml
- Lime juice — 25 ml
- Sugar syrup 1:1 — 25 ml
- Red chilli, sliced thin — 6 g
- Fine salt — 1 g
- Mint sprig — 1
- N2O charges — 2

## Method
1. Load pineapple loosely into a 1 L siphon.
2. Pour over rum, lime, syrup, salt; add chilli and mint.
3. Seal; charge twice, swirling between charges.
4. Rest 90 seconds under pressure — the gas drives liquid into the fruit.
5. VENT FAST, pointing away: the rapid decompression flash-infuses as cells burst open.
6. Open; drain the chunks (keep the liquor — it's a shot or a glaze).
7. Serve within 2 h; the sparkle-tingle texture fades after that.

## Plating & Service
Skewer as a canapé, dice over ceviche, or drop one cube into a daiquiri as an edible garnish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Zero-proof: verjus + vanilla. Same trick carbonates grapes (champagne, 1 charge of CO2) and infuses cucumber with gin for instant "G&T melons".
