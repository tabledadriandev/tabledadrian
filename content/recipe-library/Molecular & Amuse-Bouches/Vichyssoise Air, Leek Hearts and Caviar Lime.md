# Vichyssoise Air, Leek Hearts and Caviar Lime

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Deconstructed vichyssoise: butter-braised baby leek hearts under a cold potato-leek air, sparked with finger-lime pearls.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Baby leeks — 6
- Butter — 30 g
- White wine — 30 ml
- Potato, peeled — 150 g
- Leek whites, sliced — 100 g
- Whole milk — 300 ml
- Double cream — 80 ml
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Soy lecithin — 3 g (0.6% of the strained soup)
- Finger limes — 2
- Chive oil — 15 ml

## Method
1. Halve baby leeks; braise cut-side down with butter, wine, pinch of salt at 160 °C, covered, 20 minutes; keep warm.
2. Simmer potato and leek whites in milk 18 minutes until collapsing.
3. Blend with cream and salt; strain to a thin, silky soup; chill.
4. Add lecithin (0.6%) to the cold soup; froth with a stick blender, tilted, to a fine air.
5. Lay one warm leek heart per small plate.
6. Spoon the cold vichyssoise air over generously.
7. Dot with finger-lime pearls and chive oil.

## Plating & Service
Warm leek, cold air — serve the second the foam stands. A study in temperature contrast for the first course.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: oat milk + olive oil braise; lecithin unchanged at 0.6%. Add smoked eel flakes for a non-veg upgrade.
