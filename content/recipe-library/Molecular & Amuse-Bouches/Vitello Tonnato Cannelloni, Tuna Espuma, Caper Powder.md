# Vitello Tonnato Cannelloni, Tuna Espuma, Caper Powder

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sous-vide rose veal rolled into cannelloni around its own sauce — the tonnato aerated to a mousse — with an electric caper dust.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Veal loin — 400 g
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Olive oil — 20 ml
- Tinned tuna, drained — 140 g
- Anchovies — 3 (12 g)
- Egg yolk — 1 (18 g)
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Grapeseed oil — 90 ml
- Double cream — 60 ml
- Capers — 60 g
- Rocket — 30 g
- Lemon zest — 1 g

## Method
1. Salt veal; vacuum-seal with olive oil; cook sous-vide at 58.0 °C for 90 minutes; ice-shock; chill thoroughly.
2. Blend tuna, anchovy, yolk, lemon; stream in grapeseed oil; loosen with cream to a siphon-able base; strain.
3. Load a 0.5 L siphon, 2 charges; chill.
4. Rinse capers; dehydrate at 90 °C for 3 h until crisp; blitz to powder.
5. Slice cold veal 2 mm on a slicer; lay pairs overlapping into 12 sheets.
6. Siphon tonnato mousse along each sheet; roll into cannelloni.
7. Plate 3 per portion; dust caper powder; finish with rocket and zest.

## Plating & Service
Serve at 10–12 °C. The powder line should run the plate's full length — one dramatic stroke.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Turkey breast (62 °C, 2 h) for a lighter version. Egg-free espuma: lean on the cream + 0.2% xanthan.
