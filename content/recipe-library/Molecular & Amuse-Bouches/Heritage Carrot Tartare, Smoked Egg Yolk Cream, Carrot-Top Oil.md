# Heritage Carrot Tartare, Smoked Egg Yolk Cream, Carrot-Top Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Salt-baked carrots diced like steak tartare with all the classic garnishes, bound by a smoked confit-yolk cream — the vegetarian tartare that meat eaters order twice.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Heritage carrots, mixed — 500 g
- Coarse salt (baking crust) — 600 g
- Egg yolks — 4
- Olive oil — 150 ml
- Smoked salt — 3 g
- Dijon mustard — 8 g
- Shallot, brunoise — 30 g
- Cornichons, brunoise — 25 g
- Capers, chopped — 15 g
- Carrot tops (greens) — 30 g
- Grapeseed oil — 80 ml
- Rye crisps or GF seed crackers — 8

## Method
1. Bake carrots buried in coarse salt at 180 °C for 40–45 minutes until tender; brush clean, peel, cool.
2. Confit yolks in the olive oil at 64 °C for 40 minutes; cold-smoke 15 minutes (or add smoked salt).
3. Blend 3 yolks with 40 ml of their oil, mustard, smoked salt to a thick cream.
4. Blanch carrot tops 10 seconds; blitz with 60 °C grapeseed oil; filter to a green oil.
5. Dice carrots 4 mm; fold with shallot, cornichon, capers and 2 tbsp yolk cream.
6. Press tartare into a ring on each plate; paint the top with more cream.
7. Dot carrot-top oil around; crown with the last crumbled yolk; crisps alongside.

## Plating & Service
Serve cool, not cold, with the crackers upright in the tartare. Reads as meat until the first bite — let the menu stay coy.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: swap yolk cream for smoked cashew aioli. Beetroot tartare runs identically (bake 60 min).
