# Pogácsa — Hungarian Cheese & Crackling Butter Scones

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The tender, layered little scones of every Hungarian gathering — laminated with butter and sour cream, capped with cheese; dangerous by the basketful.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Strong white flour — 500 g
- Cold butter — 200 g
- Sour cream — 200 g
- Fresh yeast — 20 g (or 7 g dry)
- Milk, lukewarm — 60 ml
- Sugar — 5 g
- Egg yolks — 2 (40 g)
- Salt — 12 g
- Gruyère or trappista, grated — 150 g
- Egg, for glazing — 1 (55 g)
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Wake the yeast in milk with sugar, 10 minutes.
2. Rub cold butter into flour and salt to coarse flakes; add sour cream, yolks and the yeast milk; bring together gently — do not overwork.
3. Rest 30 minutes chilled. Roll to 2 cm; letter-fold in thirds. Rest 20 minutes; repeat the fold twice more (three folds total for the layers).
4. Roll to 2.5 cm; score the surface in a fine diamond crosshatch — the pogácsa signature.
5. Cut with a 5 cm round cutter, pressing straight down without twisting; place close together on a tray.
6. Glaze with beaten egg; crown generously with cheese and pepper.
7. Bake at 200°C for 20–25 minutes to tall, golden, flaky towers.

## Plating & Service
Piled warm in a cloth-lined basket with drinks — they vanish at the rate of conversation.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fold 100 g pork cracklings (tepertő) into the dough for the legendary tepertős pogácsa; dill or caraway vary the crown.
