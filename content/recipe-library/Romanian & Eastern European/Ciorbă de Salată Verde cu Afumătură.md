# Ciorbă de Salată Verde cu Afumătură

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Transylvania's cult soup: whole lettuce simmered with smoked bacon in a creamy, vinegar-lifted broth — improbable and addictive.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Romaine or butterhead lettuce — 2 heads (500 g)
- Smoked bacon lardons — 120 g
- Onion — 100 g
- Garlic — 15 g
- Sour cream — 200 g
- Egg yolks — 2 (40 g)
- White wine vinegar — 45 ml
- Water or light chicken stock — 1.4 L
- Salt — 9 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Dill — 8 g

## Method
1. Render lardons in a dry pot over medium heat until crisp, 5 minutes; remove half for garnish.
2. Sweat diced onion in the fat 4 minutes; add sliced garlic for 1 minute.
3. Add stock, bring to 95°C. Add lettuce cut into 3 cm ribbons; simmer 10 minutes.
4. Whisk yolks with sour cream, temper with hot broth, stir in below 80°C.
5. Season with salt, pepper and vinegar; simmer gently 2 minutes without boiling.
6. Finish with chopped dill and reserved crisp lardons.

## Plating & Service
Shallow bowls, lardons and dill on top; a poached egg turns it into lunch.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian: smoked paprika + 20 g butter replaces bacon; the vinegar-cream balance is the soul, keep it.
