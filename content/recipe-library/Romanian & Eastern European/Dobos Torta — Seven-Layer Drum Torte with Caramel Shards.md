# Dobos Torta — Seven-Layer Drum Torte with Caramel Shards

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Budapest 1885, still undefeated: seven thin sponge drums, chocolate buttercream, and the glassy caramel lid cut into angled sails.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Eggs, separated — 8 (440 g)
- Caster sugar — 300 g
- Plain flour — 160 g
- Butter, melted (sponge) — 40 g
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Vanilla — 5 ml
- Dark chocolate 60% — 200 g
- Butter, soft (cream) — 250 g
- Icing sugar — 80 g
- Egg (for the cream, pasteurised) — 1 (55 g)
- Cocoa powder — 15 g
- Sugar (caramel) — 180 g
- Lemon juice (caramel) — 5 ml

## Method
1. Sponge: whip yolks with 100 g sugar to ribbon; whip whites with 100 g sugar to firm peaks. Fold together with flour, salt, vanilla, melted butter.
2. Draw seven 22 cm circles on parchment; spread 90 g batter thinly on each. Bake in rotation at 200°C, 6–7 minutes per sheet, to pale gold. Trim while warm using a cake ring.
3. Buttercream: melt chocolate, cool to 30°C. Beat butter with icing sugar 5 minutes; beat in egg, then chocolate and cocoa to a silky, spreadable cream.
4. Stack six layers with thin, even cream (about 90 g per layer); mask the sides. Chill 1 hour. Reserve the best layer for the lid.
5. Caramel: dry-melt sugar with lemon juice to deep amber (175°C); pour quickly over the reserved layer on oiled parchment, spreading thin. Before it sets, cut into 10 wedges with an oiled knife.
6. Pipe 10 small cream rosettes on the torte's rim; lean each caramel sail against one at the canonical angle.
7. Rest 2 hours chilled; slice with a hot knife along the sail lines.

## Plating & Service
Tall, precise slices showing seven strata and the amber sail — serve with strong coffee and a straight face.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The layers can bake a day ahead (stack with parchment); classic Dobos cream uses no cream — butter and chocolate only, keep it that way.
