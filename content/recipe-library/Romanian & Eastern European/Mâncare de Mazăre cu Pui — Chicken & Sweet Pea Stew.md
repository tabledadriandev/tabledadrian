# Mâncare de Mazăre cu Pui — Chicken & Sweet Pea Stew

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The gentle midweek stew every Romanian child knows: chicken and sweet peas in a light tomato-dill sauce — humble, and quietly perfect.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chicken thighs, boneless, halved — 600 g
- Sweet peas (fresh or frozen) — 500 g
- Onion, fine dice — 150 g
- Carrot, small dice — 100 g
- Sunflower oil — 30 ml
- Tomato passata — 250 g
- Chicken stock — 300 ml
- Sweet paprika — 4 g
- Sugar — 5 g
- Fresh dill — 12 g
- Salt — 11 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml

## Method
1. Brown chicken lightly in oil, 5 minutes; set aside.
2. Soften onion and carrot in the same pot, 7 minutes; add paprika off the heat.
3. Add passata, stock and sugar; return chicken and simmer, covered, 20 minutes.
4. Add peas; simmer uncovered 10–12 minutes until the sauce lightly thickens.
5. Season with salt, pepper and a corrective squeeze of lemon.
6. Finish with plenty of chopped dill.

## Plating & Service
Shallow bowls with bread for the sauce; a summer dish that asks for nothing else.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Meatless 'de post' version is classic — same pot, no chicken, a little extra oil; new potatoes turn it into a one-pot dinner.
