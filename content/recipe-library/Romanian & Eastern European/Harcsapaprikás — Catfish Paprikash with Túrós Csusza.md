# Harcsapaprikás — Catfish Paprikash with Túrós Csusza

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Hungary's river-fish masterpiece: catfish in paprika-sour cream sauce over túrós csusza — noodles with curd cheese and bacon; the Tisza river on a plate.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Catfish fillets, skinless — 600 g
- Lard or sunflower oil — 40 g
- Onion, fine dice — 200 g
- Sweet Hungarian paprika — 16 g
- Hungarian wax pepper — 100 g
- Tomato — 100 g
- Fish or chicken stock — 250 ml
- Sour cream — 300 g
- Cornflour — 10 g
- Salt — 13 g
- Fresh pasta squares or wide egg noodles — 320 g
- Twaróg or curd cheese — 200 g
- Smoked bacon, diced — 120 g
- Dill — 8 g

## Method
1. Cut catfish into 4 cm chunks; salt 15 minutes, pat dry.
2. Soften onion in lard, 10 minutes; off heat add paprika, then chopped tomato, sliced pepper and stock; simmer 10 minutes.
3. Slake cornflour into 250 g sour cream; temper into the sauce below a boil; season.
4. Poach the catfish in the sauce, 8–10 minutes at a bare simmer — do not stir; shake the pan.
5. Csusza: boil noodles; render bacon crisp. Toss noodles in the bacon fat, then with crumbled curd cheese and half the bacon.
6. Rest the fish 3 minutes.

## Plating & Service
Csusza as the bed, catfish and blushing sauce over, remaining bacon and dill on top, the reserved 50 g sour cream in a cool spoonful.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Monkfish stands in respectably at sea; the csusza alone, with extra bacon, is a legitimate Hungarian supper.
