# Pasulj — Serbian Smoked Bean Soup-Stew

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Army canteens, ski lodges and grandmothers agree on pasulj: beans cooked twice with smoked meat, finished with a paprika roux (zaprška).

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Dried white or pinto beans, soaked overnight — 400 g
- Smoked pork ribs or ham hock — 600 g
- Onion — 200 g
- Carrot — 120 g
- Garlic — 20 g
- Sunflower oil — 40 ml
- Plain flour — 25 g
- Sweet paprika — 12 g
- Bay leaves — 2
- Salt — 8 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Parsley — 10 g

## Method
1. Boil drained beans in fresh water 10 minutes; drain (the Serbian 'first water' ritual).
2. Combine beans, smoked meat, bay, half the onion (whole) and 2.4 L water; simmer 100–120 minutes until beans are creamy and meat pulls from the bone.
3. Remove meat; chunk it and return. Discard the onion.
4. Zaprška: fry remaining diced onion in oil 8 minutes, add grated carrot 3 minutes, flour 2 minutes, then paprika off the heat.
5. Stir the roux into the pot; simmer 15 minutes until lightly thickened. Season carefully — the meat carries salt.
6. Crush in garlic, rest 10 minutes, finish with parsley.

## Plating & Service
Thick enough to mound slightly; serve with raw onion wedges and country bread.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Prebranac (baked bean cousin) uses the same base layered and baked. GF: thicken with mashed beans instead of flour.
