# Salam de Biscuiți — Chocolate Biscuit Salami with Rum & Walnuts

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The no-bake log of every Romanian childhood, tightened up: dark cocoa mass, crushed biscuits, walnuts, rum and rahat, sliced like charcuterie.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Plain biscuits (petit beurre) — 400 g
- Butter — 150 g
- Caster sugar — 120 g
- Whole milk — 120 ml
- Cocoa powder — 50 g
- Dark chocolate 55% — 80 g
- Dark rum — 30 ml
- Vanilla — 5 ml
- Walnuts, toasted, chopped — 100 g
- Turkish delight (rahat), diced — 80 g
- Icing sugar, for rolling — 30 g
- Fine salt — 1 g

## Method
1. Break biscuits into rough 1 cm rubble — uneven pieces make the 'fat' of the salami.
2. Melt butter, sugar, milk, cocoa and chocolate together over low heat to a glossy sauce; off heat add rum, vanilla, salt.
3. Cool 10 minutes; fold through biscuits, walnuts and rahat until every piece is coated.
4. Divide onto two sheets of parchment; roll into tight 6 cm logs, twisting the ends like candy wrappers.
5. Chill 4 hours minimum until sliceable.
6. Roll the naked logs in icing sugar for the authentic 'salami bloom'.

## Plating & Service
Slice 1 cm rounds on a board, charcuterie-style, with coffee — the mosaic cross-section is the charm.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Espresso replaces milk for adults; GF biscuits work identically. Keeps a week chilled, improves for three days.
