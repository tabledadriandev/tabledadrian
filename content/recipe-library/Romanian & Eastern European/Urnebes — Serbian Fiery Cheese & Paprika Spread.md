# Urnebes — Serbian Fiery Cheese & Paprika Spread

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> 'Chaos' in Serbian: ripe white cheese beaten with hot paprika and garlic into a trembling orange spread — the loudest voice on a meze board.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Full-fat feta or Serbian white cheese — 400 g
- Kajmak or crème fraîche — 100 g
- Hot paprika — 8 g
- Sweet paprika — 6 g
- Garlic — 12 g
- Sunflower oil — 40 ml
- Hard-boiled egg (optional, traditional in Niš) — 1
- White wine vinegar — 10 ml
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Bring cheese to room temperature; mash with a fork to a coarse paste.
2. Beat in kajmak until creamy but not smooth — texture matters.
3. Bloom both paprikas in the oil warmed to 60°C for 2 minutes; cool slightly.
4. Beat the paprika oil, crushed garlic, vinegar and pepper into the cheese.
5. Grate in the egg if using; adjust heat with more hot paprika — it should genuinely bite.
6. Rest 1 hour in the fridge for the flavours to fuse.

## Plating & Service
Mound in a bowl, rake the surface with a fork, drizzle oil into the grooves; serve with warm somun or lepinja bread and rakija courage.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Tame it with more kajmak for gentle palates; a spoonful under grilled peppers is an instant composed starter.
