# Tochitură of Iberico Pluma, Polenta Cream & 64° Egg

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Tochitură's garlic-wine-paprika DNA on premium pork: rosy pluma, crisp belly nuggets, silky polenta cream and a slow egg replacing the fried one.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Iberico pluma or pork neck fillet — 500 g
- Pork belly, cooked and pressed — 200 g
- Garlic — 25 g
- Dry white wine — 150 ml
- Rich pork or chicken jus — 200 ml
- Sweet paprika — 6 g
- Smoked paprika — 2 g
- Dried thyme — 2 g
- Fine cornmeal — 100 g
- Milk — 300 ml
- Chicken stock — 300 ml
- Butter — 50 g
- Aged sheep's cheese, grated — 60 g
- Eggs — 4
- Salt — 10 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Sunflower oil — 30 ml

## Method
1. Cook eggs in a 64°C bath for 60 minutes; hold warm at 55°C.
2. Polenta cream: simmer cornmeal in milk and stock 25 minutes; blend with butter and cheese, pass; hold at 60°C — pourable, not set.
3. Sauce: reduce wine by half with crushed garlic; add jus, both paprikas and thyme; reduce to a glossy nappe. Mount with 10 g butter.
4. Cut pressed belly into 2 cm cubes; crisp all sides in oil, 5 minutes.
5. Season pluma; sear hard in a screaming pan, 2 minutes per side, rest to 58°C core, slice thick.
6. Warm everything; season the sauce finally.

## Plating & Service
A pool of polenta cream, sliced pluma and belly cubes arranged across, the 64° egg nested to one side, sauce spooned over the meat only — cut the egg at the table into the polenta.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The classic smoked sausage can replace belly; beef fillet works but loses the pork-garlic romance.
