# Kotlet Schabowy with Buttered Young Potatoes & Mizeria

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Poland's Sunday cutlet: pork loin pounded thin, breaded, fried in butter-and-lard until blistered gold, with dill potatoes and cooling cucumber mizeria.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Pork loin, boneless — 4 × 160 g
- Plain flour — 80 g
- Eggs — 2 (110 g)
- Fine breadcrumbs — 180 g
- Lard — 60 g
- Butter — 60 g
- Salt — 12 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- New potatoes — 700 g
- Fresh dill — 12 g
- Cucumber — 400 g
- Sour cream — 150 g
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Sugar — 4 g

## Method
1. Mizeria first: slice cucumber 2 mm, salt lightly, drain 20 minutes; squeeze gently, dress with sour cream, lemon, sugar, pepper and half the dill. Chill.
2. Boil potatoes in salted water until tender; toss with 30 g butter and remaining dill.
3. Pound the pork between film to 8 mm; season.
4. Bread: flour, beaten egg, crumbs — pressed on lightly, never compacted.
5. Fry in lard + 30 g butter at 170°C, 3 minutes per side, spooning fat over, until the crust puffs and blisters — the wavy crust is the schabowy signature.
6. Rest 2 minutes on a rack; salt while hot.

## Plating & Service
Cutlet overlapping the dill potatoes, mizeria in a separate small bowl, lemon wedge — Sunday in Kraków on a plate.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken or veal work; soaking the loin in milk for 2 hours beforehand is the grandmother's tenderness trick.
