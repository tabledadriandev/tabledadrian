# Chilled Beet Kvass Borscht, Smoked Crème Fraîche & Dill Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A summer-service borscht: fermented beet base served ice-cold and crystal, with smoked crème fraîche and a vivid dill oil — chlodnik meets fine dining.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Beetroot — 700 g
- Beet kvass (fermented 5 days, as for barszcz) — 400 ml
- Cucumber — 200 g
- Radishes — 80 g
- Apple — 100 g
- Crème fraîche — 150 g
- Smoked salt — 3 g
- Dill — 40 g
- Grapeseed oil — 100 ml
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Honey — 8 g
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Espelette pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Juice 500 g raw beets (or blend with 200 ml water and strain); combine with kvass, lemon juice, honey and salt. Chill to 2°C.
2. Dice remaining 200 g beets, cook sous-vide at 85°C for 1 hour (or steam 25 minutes) until tender; dice 5 mm, chill.
3. Dill oil: blitz dill with oil at 60°C for 4 minutes, hang in muslin, chill.
4. Fold smoked salt into crème fraîche; whip to soft peaks, load into a piping bag.
5. Cut cucumber and radish into 5 mm dice; brunoise the apple, holding it in lemon water.
6. Check the base seasoning cold — chilled soups need 10–15% more seasoning.

## Plating & Service
Arrange a tight mosaic of beet, cucumber, radish and apple dice in chilled bowls, pipe one crème fraîche dome, pour the ruby soup at table, finish with dill oil pearls and Espelette.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: whipped coconut yoghurt with smoked salt; add a 6-minute quail egg for a chlodnik nod.
