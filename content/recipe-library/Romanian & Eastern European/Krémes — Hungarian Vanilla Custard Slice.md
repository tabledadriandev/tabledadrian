# Krémes — Hungarian Vanilla Custard Slice

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The café-house cube of Budapest and Oradea both claim: a thick trembling vanilla custard lightened with whipped egg whites between crackling puff pastry lids.

## Ingredients (serves 9)
- All-butter puff pastry — 500 g
- Whole milk — 750 ml
- Vanilla pods — 2
- Egg yolks — 8 (160 g)
- Egg whites — 4 (120 g)
- Caster sugar — 220 g
- Cornflour — 70 g
- Plain flour — 20 g
- Butter — 40 g
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Icing sugar — 30 g
- Gelatine leaves (insurance, optional) — 3 g

## Method
1. Roll pastry into two 24 × 24 cm sheets; dock thoroughly. Bake between trays at 200°C for 25 minutes to flat, deep gold; cool. Trim to fit a 22 cm square frame.
2. Custard: infuse milk with split vanilla pods. Whisk yolks, 150 g sugar, cornflour and flour; temper with hot milk; cook, whisking hard, to a thick boil for 90 seconds. Off heat: butter, salt (and bloomed gelatine if service demands cuttable insurance).
3. Immediately whip whites with 70 g sugar to glossy soft peaks.
4. Fold the meringue into the hot custard in thirds — the heat sets the whites; work decisively.
5. Frame the bottom pastry; pour in the full 5 cm of cream; level. Lay the top pastry on, pressing gently.
6. Chill 4 hours minimum.
7. Cut with a serrated knife dipped in hot water — clean vertical strokes, wiping between; dust with icing sugar.

## Plating & Service
A tall cube per plate, strata visible and proud; café service means coffee, sparkling water and unhurried time.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> French crème pâtissière alone makes it a mille-feuille — the folded hot meringue is what makes it krémes; eat within 24 hours while the pastry snaps.
