# Pączki — Polish Rose-Jam Doughnuts with Candied Orange Glaze

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Fat Thursday's reason to exist: feather-light yeast doughnuts with the pale ring of a perfect fry, filled with rose petal jam and glazed with orange.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Strong white flour — 500 g
- Whole milk, lukewarm — 220 ml
- Fresh yeast — 30 g
- Caster sugar — 80 g
- Egg yolks — 5 (100 g)
- Butter, melted, cooled — 80 g
- Spiritus or vodka — 20 ml
- Vanilla — 5 ml
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Rose petal jam (or thick raspberry) — 250 g
- Sunflower oil, for frying — 1.5 L
- Icing sugar — 150 g
- Orange juice and zest — 40 ml / 4 g
- Candied orange peel — 40 g

## Method
1. Sponge: yeast, 50 ml milk, 10 g sugar, 50 g flour; 20 minutes until lively.
2. Whip yolks with remaining sugar to pale ribbon — the yolk-whip is the lightness secret.
3. Knead flour, milk, sponge, yolk ribbon, vanilla, salt and vodka (it blocks oil absorption) 10 minutes; add butter gradually; knead to a soft, glossy dough. Prove 90 minutes.
4. Roll to 2 cm; cut 8 cm rounds; ball them tight, seam down. Prove 40 minutes on floured cloth until pillowy.
5. Fry at 170°C, 2.5–3 minutes per side, lidded for the first side (the rise), open for the second — the pale waistline ring is the badge of a proper prove.
6. Cool 10 minutes; pipe 25 g rose jam into each through a side incision.
7. Glaze: icing sugar whisked with orange juice; dip the crowns, scatter candied peel and zest.

## Plating & Service
Piled on a platter while the glaze is tacky; count them before the crew walks past, because you will not afterwards.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Plum powidła or vanilla custard fill the other classic pączki; day-old ones revive with 8 seconds of microwave and lowered expectations.
