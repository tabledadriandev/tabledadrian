# Mămăliga de Aur — Classic Pot Polenta

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The golden staple under half this library: coarse cornmeal, water, salt, patience — and the string-cut wedges of tradition.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Coarse yellow cornmeal — 400 g
- Water — 1.6 L
- Fine salt — 14 g
- Sunflower oil or butter (optional) — 20 g

## Method
1. Bring salted water to a rolling boil in a heavy pot (the classic ceaun).
2. Rain in the cornmeal in a slow stream, whisking constantly to prevent lumps.
3. Drop to the lowest heat; switch to a strong wooden spoon and stir every 2–3 minutes.
4. Cook 30–35 minutes until thick, glossy, and pulling cleanly from the pot's walls.
5. Optionally beat in the fat for a suppler crumb.
6. Turn out onto a wooden board in one dome; rest 5 minutes.

## Plating & Service
Cut wedges with taut kitchen string or a wet knife, never sawed with a dry blade; serve steaming beside anything with sauce.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Firm (this ratio) for slicing; add 200 ml more water for a soft, spoonable mămăligă under stews. Leftovers grill beautifully.
