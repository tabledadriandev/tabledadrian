# Székelykáposzta — Pork & Sauerkraut Goulash with Dill Cream

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Transylvanian-Hungarian marriage of pörkölt and sauerkraut, mellowed with dill-flecked sour cream — the definition of cold-weather food.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Pork shoulder, diced 3 cm — 800 g
- Sauerkraut — 800 g
- Onions, diced — 250 g
- Lard or sunflower oil — 50 g
- Sweet Hungarian paprika — 20 g
- Caraway, ground — 3 g
- Garlic — 15 g
- Tomato paste — 30 g
- Chicken or pork stock — 400 ml
- Sour cream — 300 g
- Fresh dill — 12 g
- Salt — 8 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Gold the onions in lard, 12 minutes. Off heat, stir in paprika; add pork, caraway and 100 ml stock.
2. Braise covered at the lowest simmer 60 minutes, stirring occasionally.
3. Rinse kraut briefly, squeeze; add with tomato paste, garlic and remaining stock.
4. Simmer covered 45–60 minutes more until pork and kraut melt together; season carefully.
5. Stir in two-thirds of the sour cream and half the dill; warm through below a boil.
6. Rest 10 minutes.

## Plating & Service
Deep bowls, the remaining sour cream spooned on top, dill scattered; boiled potatoes or dark bread alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Smoked pork ribs added at the start deepen it; day-two székely is famously superior — build it ahead for charter.
