# Piftie de Curcan — Turkey Aspic with Garlic & Pickled Carrot

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The winter-feast aspic, done with turkey wings for cleaner flavour — garlicky, crystal-set, served icy with mustard; a textural adventure for the curious guest.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Turkey wings and drumsticks — 1.5 kg
- Pig's trotter (for gelatine) — 500 g
- Onion — 150 g
- Carrots — 200 g
- Garlic — 40 g
- Black peppercorns — 4 g
- Bay leaves — 2
- Salt — 16 g
- White wine vinegar — 30 ml
- Pickled baby carrots, sliced — 80 g
- Parsley leaves — 8 g
- Dijon or Romanian mustard, to serve — 60 g

## Method
1. Cover meats with 3 L cold water; bring slowly to 90°C, skimming — never boil, clarity is everything.
2. Add onion, whole carrots, peppercorns, bay; simmer 3–3.5 hours until the liquid is sticky between two fingers.
3. Strain through muslin; season with salt and vinegar. Test set: 2 tablespoons on a cold plate, 10 minutes in the fridge — it must set firmly.
4. Pull turkey meat into neat pieces, discarding skin and bone.
5. Crush garlic with a little broth and stir into the strained liquid; rest 10 minutes, then pass again.
6. Arrange meat, cooked carrot coins, pickled carrot and parsley leaves in bowls or a terrine; ladle broth over.
7. Chill 6 hours or overnight until glass-firm; unmould or serve in the bowl.

## Plating & Service
Serve well chilled with sharp mustard and rye toast; scrape off any surface fat for a clean presentation.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> All-pork piftie is the classic; a refined route is to clarify the broth with egg whites for museum-grade transparency.
