# Alivenci Moldovenești — Warm Cheese & Cornmeal Pudding Cake

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Moldova's rustic soufflé-cake: fresh cheese, cornmeal and dill-optional batter baked until golden, served warm with cold sour cream and honey.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Fresh cow's cheese or ricotta — 500 g
- Fine cornmeal — 150 g
- Sour cream — 200 g (plus 150 g to serve)
- Eggs, separated — 4 (220 g)
- Caster sugar — 100 g
- Butter, melted — 60 g
- Vanilla — 5 ml
- Lemon zest — 4 g
- Baking powder — 6 g
- Fine salt — 3 g
- Honey, to serve — 60 g

## Method
1. Beat cheese with yolks, sugar, 200 g sour cream, melted butter, vanilla and zest.
2. Fold in cornmeal, baking powder and salt; rest 15 minutes so the cornmeal drinks.
3. Whip whites to soft peaks; fold in thirds into the batter.
4. Pour into a buttered 26 cm dish; smooth.
5. Bake at 175°C for 45–50 minutes until golden, risen and barely wobbling at the centre.
6. Rest 15 minutes — it settles from soufflé to pudding-cake, as intended.

## Plating & Service
Warm generous squares with cold sour cream and a ribbon of honey — the hot-cold, sweet-lactic seesaw is the dish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The savoury ancestral version adds dill and skips sugar — serve it beside ciorbă; either way it reheats gently and well.
