# Rădăuțeană Velouté, Sous-Vide Chicken & Smoked Paprika Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Ciorbă rădăuțeană's garlic-vinegar-cream trinity as a plated velouté: burnished chicken, pickled celeriac, and paprika oil for warmth.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Chicken breasts, skin on — 3 × 180 g
- Intense chicken stock — 900 ml
- Whipping cream — 250 ml
- Egg yolks — 2 (40 g)
- Garlic confit (cloves cooked in oil at 90°C, 40 min) — 40 g
- White wine vinegar — 35 ml
- Celeriac — 150 g
- Pickling liquid (100 ml vinegar, 100 ml water, 20 g sugar, 5 g salt) — 225 ml
- Smoked sweet paprika — 6 g
- Grapeseed oil — 80 ml
- Salt — 8 g
- Chives — 8 g

## Method
1. Shave celeriac into 1 mm ribbons; bring pickling liquid to 80°C, pour over, hold 1 hour minimum.
2. Paprika oil: warm oil to 70°C, whisk in paprika, infuse 30 minutes off heat, pass through coffee filter.
3. Season chicken, vacuum-seal, cook at 62°C for 75 minutes. Chill bags in ice if working ahead.
4. Velouté: reduce stock by a third; add cream and garlic confit, blend smooth, pass. Whisk yolks with vinegar, temper, incorporate below 80°C; season. It should coat a spoon lightly.
5. Pat chicken dry; crisp skin-side down in a dry pan 3 minutes until mahogany, rest, slice into thick coins.
6. Drain celeriac ribbons and fold into loose rosettes.

## Plating & Service
Three chicken coins per shallow bowl with a celeriac rosette; pour velouté at the table and spiral paprika oil over — the orange-on-ivory contrast is the dish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Guinea fowl breast upgrades it for gala nights; skip yolks and mount with 30 g butter for a simpler kitchen flow.
