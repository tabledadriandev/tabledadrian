# Mămăligă Gnocchi, Telemea Cream & Sage Brown Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Leftover-mămăligă thinking with Italian technique: golden polenta gnocchi over a telemea cream, dressed in sage-scented beurre noisette.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Coarse cornmeal — 200 g
- Water — 800 ml
- Salt — 10 g
- Egg yolks — 2 (40 g)
- Plain flour — 80 g
- Parmesan or aged cașcaval, grated — 60 g
- Telemea or feta — 150 g
- Whipping cream — 150 ml
- Butter — 100 g
- Sage leaves — 16
- Lemon juice — 8 ml
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Nutmeg — 0.3 g

## Method
1. Cook a stiff mămăligă (cornmeal into boiling salted water, 30 minutes); spread 2 cm deep, chill fully.
2. Crumble the cold mămăligă; knead briefly with yolks, flour, grated cheese and nutmeg to a soft dough.
3. Roll ropes; cut 2.5 cm pillows; press lightly with a fork. Hold on semolina.
4. Telemea cream: blend telemea with warm cream to silk; pass; hold at 60°C — salty, pourable.
5. Poach gnocchi in simmering salted water; lift 60 seconds after floating. Then crisp in a film of butter, 2 minutes per side.
6. Brown remaining butter with sage to noisette; stop with lemon juice off heat.

## Plating & Service
A pool of telemea cream, gnocchi piled loosely, sage butter and crisp leaves spooned over, coarse pepper — rustic materials, refined manners.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF flour blends bind equally well; smoked telemea in the cream echoes the shepherd's fire.
