# Punjena Pljeskavica — Cheese-Stuffed Balkan Burger

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Serbia's flying-saucer burger, stuffed with kajmak and smoked cheese that erupts on the grill's first press — leskovačka street food, plated with pride.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Beef chuck, 20% fat, minced twice — 800 g
- Smoked bacon, very fine dice — 80 g
- Sparkling water, ice-cold — 60 ml
- Bicarbonate of soda — 3 g
- Salt — 14 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Hot paprika — 3 g
- Kajmak or mascarpone + feta (2:1) — 120 g
- Smoked cheese, grated — 80 g
- Lepinja flatbreads — 4
- Urnebes or ajvar — 120 g
- Red onion rings — 80 g

## Method
1. Knead beef with salt until sticky, 3 minutes; add bacon, spices, bicarbonate and sparkling water; knead 2 minutes. Rest overnight refrigerated.
2. Divide into 8 thin patties (about 120 g each), pressed to 12 cm between film.
3. Mix kajmak with smoked cheese; centre 50 g on four patties, cap with the others, seal edges thoroughly and re-press gently to 2 cm.
4. Chill 30 minutes to set the seal.
5. Grill or plancha at 230°C: 4 minutes, flip once, 4 minutes — 72°C at the meat (the core cheese will be molten).
6. Rest 3 minutes; the pause stops the eruption happening on the flip.

## Plating & Service
On warm lepinja with urnebes, onion rings, and nothing so misguided as ketchup; knife and fork, or brave hands.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Unstuffed and thin is the classic pljeskavica; a fried egg on top is the 'uvijač' late-night move.
