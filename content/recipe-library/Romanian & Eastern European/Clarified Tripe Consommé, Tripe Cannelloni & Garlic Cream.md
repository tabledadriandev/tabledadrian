# Clarified Tripe Consommé, Tripe Cannelloni & Garlic Cream

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Ciorbă de burtă rebuilt for a tasting menu: crystal consommé, a cannelloni of braised tripe, and the garlic-sour cream flavours as precise dots.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Cooked honeycomb tripe (from 800 g raw, braised soft) — 500 g
- Rich beef broth from the braise — 1.5 L
- Egg whites — 120 g
- Minced lean beef — 200 g
- Carrot, mirepoix — 80 g
- Celeriac, mirepoix — 60 g
- Garlic — 50 g
- Whipping cream — 150 ml
- Sour cream — 100 g
- Sherry vinegar — 25 ml
- Salt — 10 g
- Chives — 8 g
- Espelette pepper — 1 g
- Gelatine leaf — 2 g

## Method
1. Braise tripe in beef broth 3–3.5 hours until completely tender (as for classic ciorbă); reserve broth.
2. Clarify: whisk egg whites with minced beef and mirepoix, add to cold broth, bring slowly to 90°C, let the raft form; ladle through muslin. Season, add 10 ml vinegar.
3. Lay the best tripe sheet flat, honeycomb out; roll tightly around a 12 mm core of finely chopped seasoned tripe bound with 2 g bloomed gelatine. Wrap in film, poach 10 minutes at 80°C, chill, slice into 4 cm cylinders.
4. Garlic cream: simmer garlic cloves in cream 15 minutes until soft, blend smooth, season, load into a squeeze bottle.
5. Whisk sour cream with remaining vinegar and a pinch of salt; second bottle.
6. To serve: reheat cannelloni in a little consommé at 80°C for 4 minutes.

## Plating & Service
One cannelloni upright per warm bowl, three dots each of garlic cream and soured cream, chives and Espelette; pour consommé table-side.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The same architecture works with braised chicken thigh for guests who fear tripe — keep the garlic and vinegar accents identical.
