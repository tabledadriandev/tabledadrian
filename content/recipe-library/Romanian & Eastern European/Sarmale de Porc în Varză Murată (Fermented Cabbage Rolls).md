# Sarmale de Porc în Varză Murată (Fermented Cabbage Rolls)

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The national dish, done properly: hand-rolled pork parcels in sour fermented cabbage, layered with smoked bacon and cooked low for hours — better on day two, best on day three.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Whole fermented sour cabbage leaves — 1.2 kg (about 24 leaves)
- Pork shoulder, minced — 800 g
- Smoked bacon, diced — 150 g
- Short-grain rice — 100 g
- Onions — 300 g
- Sunflower oil — 40 ml
- Tomato passata — 300 g
- Sweet paprika — 8 g
- Dried thyme — 3 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Bay leaves — 3
- Smoked pork ribs — 300 g
- Sauerkraut, shredded (from trimmings) — 400 g
- Sour cream and mămăligă, to serve — 300 g

## Method
1. Rinse cabbage leaves if very sour; trim thick central ribs flat. Reserve and shred all trimmings.
2. Sweat diced onion in oil 10 minutes; cool. Mix with pork, rice, paprika, thyme, pepper and 100 g passata. (No added salt if cabbage is well fermented — taste a leaf first.)
3. Roll: 60 g filling near the stem end, fold sides in, roll into a tight cigar; tuck the end in with a finger.
4. Layer a heavy 6 L pot: shredded cabbage, bay and half the bacon on the bottom, sarmale in tight concentric circles, ribs and remaining bacon between layers, shredded cabbage on top.
5. Pour over remaining passata and water to barely cover; weight with a plate.
6. Simmer at the barest blip — 150°C oven or lowest flame — for 3–3.5 hours. Do not stir, ever; shake the pot gently instead.
7. Rest at least 30 minutes; reheating tomorrow only improves them.

## Plating & Service
Serve 3 rolls per person with hot mămăligă, a spoon of sour cream and a hot pickled chilli — the holy Romanian quartet.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Half pork, half beef tightens the texture; the fasting version (mushroom-rice) is elsewhere in this library. Freeze brilliantly.
