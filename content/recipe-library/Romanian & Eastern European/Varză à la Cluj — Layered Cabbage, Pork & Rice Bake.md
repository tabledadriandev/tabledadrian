# Varză à la Cluj — Layered Cabbage, Pork & Rice Bake

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Deconstructed sarmale for the impatient: sauerkraut, paprika pork and rice layered like a lasagne under sour cream — Transylvania's great casserole.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Sauerkraut, shredded — 1 kg
- Pork shoulder, minced — 700 g
- Short-grain rice, par-cooked 10 min — 150 g
- Onions, diced — 250 g
- Sunflower oil or lard — 60 g
- Sweet paprika — 10 g
- Dried thyme — 3 g
- Tomato passata — 200 g
- Smoked bacon, diced — 120 g
- Sour cream — 300 g
- Salt — 8 g
- Black pepper — 3 g

## Method
1. Rinse sauerkraut if aggressive; squeeze. Sauté half the oil with the kraut over medium heat 10 minutes; set aside.
2. Brown bacon, then onion in remaining oil, 8 minutes; add pork, breaking it up, until no longer pink, 8 minutes. Season with paprika, thyme, pepper, salt; stir in passata and par-cooked rice.
3. Layer a deep 28 cm baking dish: kraut, meat-rice, kraut, meat-rice, finishing with kraut.
4. Spread 200 g sour cream over the top.
5. Bake at 180°C, covered with foil, 45 minutes; uncover for 25 more until burnished at the edges.
6. Rest 15 minutes — it slices into proper portions once settled.

## Plating & Service
Cut generous squares, extra cold sour cream on top, black pepper; a dish that feeds a hungry crew in one tray.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Turkey mince lightens it; fully assembled trays freeze and bake from thawed with 10 extra minutes.
