# Körözött — Paprika-Caraway Sheep's Cheese Spread

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Hungary's Liptauer: sheep's curd whipped with paprika, caraway and onion — the spread that turns a glass of wine on deck into a moment.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Sheep's milk curd or quark — 300 g
- Soft butter — 100 g
- Sour cream — 50 g
- Sweet Hungarian paprika — 10 g
- Caraway seeds, lightly crushed — 3 g
- Shallot, minced — 40 g
- Dijon mustard — 10 g
- Capers, chopped — 15 g
- Chives — 10 g
- Salt — 5 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Beat butter until light, 2 minutes; add curd and sour cream and beat just to combine — over-beating makes it runny.
2. Fold in paprika, caraway, shallot, mustard, capers, salt and pepper.
3. Rest refrigerated 2 hours minimum; the caraway and paprika need time to bloom.
4. Re-season after resting — cold mutes salt.
5. Bring to cool room temperature 20 minutes before serving; fold in half the chives.

## Plating & Service
A rustic mound raked with a fork, remaining chives and a dusting of paprika over, radishes and dark bread around.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 20 g anchovy for the Viennese Liptauer lineage; quark + ricotta 50/50 mimics sheep's curd well.
