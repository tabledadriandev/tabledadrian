# Fasole Bătută with Caramelised Onion & Smoked Paprika

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> 'Beaten beans' — a garlicky white-bean mousse crowned with sweet caramelised onions, the dish that proves Romanian fasting food outshines most mains.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Dried white beans, soaked overnight — 400 g
- Garlic — 25 g
- Sunflower oil — 100 ml
- Onions, sliced — 400 g
- Tomato paste — 25 g
- Smoked paprika — 4 g
- Salt — 14 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Bay leaf — 1
- Lemon juice — 15 ml

## Method
1. Simmer drained beans with bay in fresh water 75–90 minutes until completely soft; drain, reserving 200 ml cooking liquid.
2. Meanwhile caramelise sliced onions in 40 ml oil over medium-low heat, 25–30 minutes to deep amber; stir in tomato paste and paprika for the final 2 minutes.
3. Blend hot beans with garlic, 8 g salt and 60 ml oil, adding cooking liquid until the texture of thick hummus — or beat by hand for rustic body.
4. Season with lemon juice and pepper; it should taste boldly of garlic.
5. Spread into a shallow bowl while warm.

## Plating & Service
Crown with the hot caramelised onions and their paprika oil; serve warm with pickles and bread.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fold in 5 g chopped rosemary for a Transylvanian accent; leftover bătută makes a superb sandwich layer under roast vegetables.
