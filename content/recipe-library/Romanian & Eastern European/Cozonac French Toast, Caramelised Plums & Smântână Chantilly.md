# Cozonac French Toast, Caramelised Plums & Smântână Chantilly

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Day-two cozonac's highest calling: thick slices soaked in vanilla custard, seared in butter, with rum-caramel plums and a sour-cream chantilly.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Cozonac cu nucă, thick slices — 4 × 3 cm (400 g)
- Eggs — 3 (165 g)
- Whole milk — 200 ml
- Double cream — 100 ml
- Caster sugar — 70 g
- Vanilla — 5 ml
- Butter — 60 g
- Plums, halved and stoned — 400 g
- Dark rum — 30 ml
- Honey — 20 g
- Sour cream — 150 g
- Whipping cream — 100 ml
- Icing sugar — 15 g
- Cinnamon — 1 g

## Method
1. Whisk eggs, milk, cream, 30 g sugar and vanilla; soak cozonac slices 2 minutes per side.
2. Chantilly: whip cream with icing sugar to soft peaks; fold in sour cream. Chill.
3. Caramelise plums: foam 30 g butter with 40 g sugar; add plums cut-side down, 4 minutes to mahogany; flash with rum and honey, 1 minute.
4. Sear soaked slices in remaining butter over medium heat, 3 minutes per side, to a custardy centre and caramelised crust.
5. Dust the slices with cinnamon.

## Plating & Service
A slice, three plum halves and their rum caramel, a cloud of smântână chantilly — brunch or dessert, both defensible.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Apricots in July, quince in November; panettone stands in when the cozonac tin is empty (tell no one).
