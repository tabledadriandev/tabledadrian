# Lapte de Pasăre — Floating Islands with Vanilla Crème Anglaise

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> 'Bird's milk' — clouds of poached meringue drifting on cold vanilla custard; the dessert Romanian grandmothers deploy when words fail.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Whole milk — 1 L
- Egg whites — 4 (120 g)
- Caster sugar — 180 g
- Fine salt — 1 g
- Egg yolks — 6 (120 g)
- Vanilla pod — 1 (or 10 ml extract)
- Cornflour — 8 g
- Lemon zest — 2 g

## Method
1. Bring milk to 85°C with the split vanilla pod in a wide pan.
2. Whip whites with salt to soft peaks; rain in 80 g sugar, whipping to glossy firm peaks.
3. Poach islands: drop large spoonfuls onto the barely trembling milk; 90 seconds per side. Lift onto a tray; repeat.
4. Custard: whisk yolks with 100 g sugar and cornflour; temper with the strained hot poaching milk; return to the pan and stir at 82–84°C until it coats a spoon.
5. Chill the custard fully, zest folded in.
6. Chill the islands separately, uncovered, 1 hour.

## Plating & Service
A deep pool of cold custard, three islands afloat, a thread of caramel (40 g sugar, dry method) zig-zagged over if you want the pâtisserie finish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The cornflour is the Romanian home cook's anti-splitting policy; a coffee custard variant is quietly magnificent.
