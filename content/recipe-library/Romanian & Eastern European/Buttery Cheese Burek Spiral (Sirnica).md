# Buttery Cheese Burek Spiral (Sirnica)

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Hand-stretched dough pulled tissue-thin, filled with tangy cheese and coiled into the Balkan breakfast of champions — technically pita sirnica, and worth every stretch.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Strong white flour — 500 g
- Warm water — 300 ml
- Salt — 10 g
- Sunflower oil — 120 ml
- Feta or Balkan white cheese — 400 g
- Full-fat cottage cheese or urda — 200 g
- Eggs — 2 (110 g)
- Butter, melted — 60 g
- Sparkling water — 40 ml

## Method
1. Knead flour, water, salt and 20 ml oil for 8 minutes to a soft, supple dough. Divide into 3 balls, coat in oil, rest under a bowl 60 minutes — the rest is what allows the stretch.
2. Filling: crumble cheeses with eggs; do not add salt until tasted (feta varies).
3. On a large oiled tablecloth, flatten one ball, then stretch from underneath with the backs of your hands until translucent, roughly 80 × 60 cm; trim thick edges.
4. Drizzle with oil, scatter a third of the filling in a line along one long edge, and use the cloth to roll into a loose rope.
5. Coil the rope into the centre of an oiled 28 cm round tin; repeat, continuing the spiral outward with the remaining ropes.
6. Brush with melted butter; bake at 200°C for 35–40 minutes to deep gold.
7. Out of the oven, sprinkle with sparkling water and cover with a cloth for 10 minutes — the Bosnian trick for a tender crust.

## Plating & Service
Cut in wedges, warm, with a glass of drinkable yoghurt — nothing else needed.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Filo (12 sheets, buttered in pairs) is an honest shortcut; spinach + cheese makes it zeljanica.
