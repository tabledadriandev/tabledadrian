# Crispy Polenta Batons with Aged Cheese & Rosemary Salt

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Mămăligă chips for the aperitivo hour: set polenta cut into batons, fried to a glass crust, showered with aged cheese and rosemary salt.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Coarse cornmeal — 300 g
- Water — 1.2 L
- Whole milk — 200 ml
- Salt — 14 g
- Butter — 40 g
- Aged cașcaval or parmesan, finely grated — 80 g
- Fresh rosemary — 6 g
- Flaky salt — 5 g
- Sunflower oil, for frying — 700 ml
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Sour cream, for dipping — 150 g

## Method
1. Cook cornmeal in water, milk and salt, stirring, 30 minutes; beat in butter and half the cheese.
2. Pour 3 cm deep into an oiled tray; chill 3 hours until completely firm.
3. Cut into 10 × 2.5 cm batons; pat surfaces dry.
4. Rosemary salt: chop rosemary fine, rub into flaky salt.
5. Fry batons in 180°C oil in batches, 4–5 minutes, until deep gold with a shattering shell; drain on a rack.
6. Immediately dust with remaining cheese, rosemary salt and pepper.

## Plating & Service
Stacked log-cabin style with sour cream (or garlicky mujdei-cream) for dipping — deck-drinks food with mountain roots.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Bake at 220°C for 30 minutes, turning, for the lighter route; smoked paprika in the salt is a fine detour.
