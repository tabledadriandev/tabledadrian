# Lovage Consommé with Poultry Quenelles (Perișoare Rethought)

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Ciorbă de perișoare as a clear, elegant first course: mousseline quenelles instead of boiled meatballs, lovage oil instead of chopped herbs.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Chicken breast, very cold — 350 g
- Whipping cream, cold — 200 ml
- Egg white — 30 g
- Clear chicken consommé — 1.2 L
- Fermented borș, reduced by half — 150 ml
- Fresh lovage — 40 g
- Grapeseed oil — 100 ml
- Carrot, brunoise — 60 g
- Celeriac, brunoise — 60 g
- Salt — 9 g
- White pepper — 1 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml

## Method
1. Lovage oil: blitz 30 g lovage with oil at 60°C for 4 minutes; hang in muslin over a bowl, chill.
2. Mousseline: blitz diced chicken with salt and egg white until smooth; pulse in cold cream in three additions. Pass through a tamis; keep below 8°C throughout.
3. Test-poach a teaspoon at 80°C; adjust seasoning with salt, white pepper, lemon.
4. Shape quenelles between two dessert spoons (35 g each); poach at 80°C for 8 minutes.
5. Blanch brunoise 90 seconds; refresh.
6. Combine consommé with reduced borș; bring to 90°C, season — it should be gently sour, not aggressive.

## Plating & Service
Two quenelles per bowl, brunoise scattered, consommé poured at table, 5 drops of lovage oil pearling on the surface, one small lovage leaf.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pike or pike-perch mousseline is a stunning Delta-inspired variant; keep cream ratios identical.
