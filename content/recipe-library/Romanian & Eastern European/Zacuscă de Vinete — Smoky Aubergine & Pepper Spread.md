# Zacuscă de Vinete — Smoky Aubergine & Pepper Spread

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Romania's autumn ritual in a jar — fire-roasted aubergine and gogoșari peppers cooked down slowly with onion and tomato until spoonably rich.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Aubergines — 1.5 kg
- Red gogoșari or romano peppers — 1 kg
- Onions — 400 g
- Tomato passata — 400 g
- Sunflower oil — 150 ml
- Bay leaves — 2
- Salt — 18 g
- Black pepper, ground — 3 g
- Sugar — 10 g

## Method
1. Char aubergines and peppers whole over open flame or under a 250°C grill, turning, until skins are black and flesh collapses — 15–20 minutes.
2. Steam in a covered bowl 15 minutes; peel (never rinse), deseed the peppers. Drain aubergine flesh in a colander 30 minutes.
3. Chop aubergine with a wooden or plastic knife (metal oxidises it); chop peppers into 1 cm pieces.
4. Sweat finely diced onion in the oil over medium heat 12 minutes until translucent and sweet.
5. Add peppers; cook 10 minutes. Add aubergine, passata, bay, salt, pepper, sugar.
6. Cook uncovered at a low blip, stirring every few minutes, 45–60 minutes, until the oil rises to the surface and a spoon dragged through leaves a clean trail.
7. Jar hot into sterilised jars or chill for service within a week.

## Plating & Service
Serve at room temperature in a shallow bowl, a swirl on top with good oil, toasted sourdough or GF crackers alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 200 g sautéed mushrooms for zacuscă de ciuperci; a pinch of smoked paprika deepens it if you roasted without flame.
