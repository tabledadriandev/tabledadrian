# Halászlé — Fisherman's Soup with Carp & Hot Paprika

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Baja-style river fish soup: a double-strength stock from heads and frames, stained crimson with paprika, fillets poached in at the end.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Whole carp, filleted, head and frame reserved — 1.5 kg
- Onion — 300 g
- Sweet Hungarian paprika — 30 g
- Hot paprika — 5 g
- Tomato — 150 g
- Green pepper — 100 g
- Salt — 15 g
- Water — 2.5 L
- Dry white wine — 100 ml

## Method
1. Cut fillets into 4 cm pieces, salt lightly, refrigerate.
2. Simmer head, frame, skin and finely chopped onion in water at 95°C for 60 minutes, mashing occasionally.
3. Pass everything through a food mill or coarse sieve, pressing hard — the onion body is essential; discard bones.
4. Return the milled base to the pot with chopped tomato, pepper and wine; bring to a lively simmer.
5. Rain in both paprikas, whisk, and boil 5 minutes — halászlé is the one Hungarian dish where paprika hits rolling liquid.
6. Lower in the fillets; poach 8–10 minutes without stirring (shake the pot instead). Season.

## Plating & Service
Ladle carefully to keep fillets whole; hot cherry-pepper rings on the side. Traditionally followed by túrós csusza.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Riviera adaptation: scorpion fish and gurnard frames, monkfish to finish — closer to a paprika bouillabaisse.
