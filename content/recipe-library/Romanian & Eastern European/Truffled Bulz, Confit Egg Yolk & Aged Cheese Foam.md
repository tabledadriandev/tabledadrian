# Truffled Bulz, Confit Egg Yolk & Aged Cheese Foam

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The shepherd's cheese-hearted polenta ball crossed with Piedmont: truffle in the core, a 64° yolk on top, and the sheep's cheese returning as a warm foam.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Coarse cornmeal — 250 g
- Water — 1 L
- Whole milk — 200 ml
- Salt — 10 g
- Butter — 60 g
- Smoked scamorza — 120 g
- Black truffle, chopped (or 10 g truffle paste) — 15 g
- Egg yolks — 4
- Grapeseed oil (for confit) — 200 ml
- Aged sheep's cheese, grated — 120 g
- Whipping cream — 150 ml
- Chives — 6 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Cook cornmeal in water, milk and salt, stirring, 30 minutes to a stiff mămăligă; beat in 40 g butter. Cool 10 minutes.
2. Mix chopped scamorza with truffle. Form four 140 g bulz spheres around 35 g of the truffled cheese each.
3. Confit yolks: hold yolks in 64°C oil for 40 minutes (or use a water bath at 64°C for whole eggs, 60 minutes, and separate).
4. Cheese foam: melt sheep's cheese into warm cream at 70°C, blend, pass, season; froth with a stick blender before serving.
5. Brush bulz with remaining butter; roast at 220°C for 15 minutes to a golden crust.
6. Rest 3 minutes — molten centre protocol applies.

## Plating & Service
Bulz centred, yolk seated in a small dimple on top, foam spooned around, chives and pepper; break the yolk table-side into the truffle lava.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Porcini duxelles replaces truffle honourably; for vegans, cashew-miso core and no yolk — different dish, still compelling.
