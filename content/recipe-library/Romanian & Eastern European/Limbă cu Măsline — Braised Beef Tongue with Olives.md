# Limbă cu Măsline — Braised Beef Tongue with Olives

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The old-Bucharest bistro dish: silky beef tongue in a tomato-wine sauce studded with green olives — offal at its most elegant and approachable.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Beef tongue — 1.2 kg
- Onion — 150 g
- Carrot — 100 g
- Bay leaves — 2
- Black peppercorns — 4 g
- Sunflower oil — 40 ml
- Onions, diced (sauce) — 200 g
- Tomato passata — 400 g
- Dry white wine — 150 ml
- Green olives, pitted — 200 g
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Sugar — 5 g
- Dried thyme — 2 g
- Salt — 12 g
- Parsley — 10 g

## Method
1. Simmer tongue with onion, carrot, bay and peppercorns in water at 90°C for 2.5–3 hours until a skewer slides in easily.
2. Peel the skin while warm (it lifts cleanly only when hot); slice 8 mm on the bias. Reserve 400 ml strained broth.
3. Blanch olives 2 minutes to tame the brine.
4. Sauce: soften diced onion in oil 8 minutes; add passata, wine, broth, thyme, sugar; simmer 15 minutes.
5. Add tongue slices and olives; simmer gently 15 minutes until the sauce glosses and clings.
6. Balance with lemon juice and salt; rest 10 minutes; parsley over.

## Plating & Service
Shingled slices under generous sauce with mashed potato; garnish with a few whole olives and parsley.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pressure cooker cuts the tongue to 70 minutes; the same sauce flatters braised veal cheeks for tongue-shy guests.
