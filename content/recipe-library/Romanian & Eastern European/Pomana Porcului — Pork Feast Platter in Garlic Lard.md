# Pomana Porcului — Pork Feast Platter in Garlic Lard

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The pig-slaughter thanksgiving: cuts of pork fried together in their own lard with garlic and wine, eaten hot from the pan — primal, generous, unforgettable.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Pork neck, cubed 3 cm — 500 g
- Pork belly, cubed — 300 g
- Pork tenderloin, thick coins — 300 g
- Pork liver, cubed — 200 g
- Smoked sausage, thick slices — 200 g
- Pork lard — 60 g
- Garlic — 40 g
- Dry white wine — 150 ml
- Sweet paprika — 6 g
- Dried thyme — 3 g
- Salt — 14 g
- Black pepper — 4 g
- Pickled vegetables, to serve — 300 g
- Mămăligă, to serve — 600 g

## Method
1. Melt lard in a wide, heavy pan; fry the belly first over medium-high heat, 8 minutes, to render and crisp.
2. Add neck cubes; fry 15 minutes, turning, until bronzed.
3. Add tenderloin and sausage; 6 minutes more.
4. Add liver last — 4 minutes only, so it stays blushing inside.
5. Deglaze with wine; add crushed garlic, paprika, thyme, salt, pepper; toss 3 minutes until the pan juices turn to a garlicky glaze.
6. Rest 5 minutes in the pan.

## Plating & Service
Bring the pan to the table over a trivet; serve with mămăligă, pickles and shots of țuică — ceremony included.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The mix of cuts is the point — adjust to what the butcher gives you, but keep the liver-last timing sacred.
