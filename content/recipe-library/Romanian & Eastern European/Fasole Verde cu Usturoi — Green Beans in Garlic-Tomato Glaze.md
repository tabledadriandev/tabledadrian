# Fasole Verde cu Usturoi — Green Beans in Garlic-Tomato Glaze

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The summer garden side: snappy green beans in a light tomato sauce hit with raw garlic and dill at the end — hot or room temperature, both correct.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Green beans, trimmed — 600 g
- Onion, fine dice — 100 g
- Garlic — 20 g
- Sunflower oil — 40 ml
- Tomatoes, grated, or passata — 300 g
- Sugar — 4 g
- Salt — 10 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Fresh dill — 10 g
- Lemon juice — 8 ml

## Method
1. Blanch beans in well-salted boiling water 4 minutes; refresh briefly — they must stay bright and snappy.
2. Soften onion in oil, 6 minutes.
3. Add tomato and sugar; simmer 8 minutes to a light sauce.
4. Fold in the beans; simmer 5 minutes to marry.
5. Off the heat: crushed raw garlic, salt, pepper, lemon.
6. Dill through; rest 5 minutes.

## Plating & Service
Warm beside grilled meat or fish — or chilled as part of a summer spread with telemea and bread.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> A spoon of sour cream stirred in makes the Transylvanian creamy version; yellow wax beans are the August classic.
