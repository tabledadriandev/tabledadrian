# Tarhana Soup with Smoked Butter & Sheep's Cheese

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Fermented grain-and-yoghurt tarhana whisked into a rustic, tangy porridge-soup — the Balkans' original instant soup, finished with foaming smoked butter.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Tarhana (fermented dried yoghurt-flour granules) — 160 g
- Vegetable or chicken stock — 1.6 L
- Tomato paste — 40 g
- Butter — 60 g
- Smoked salt — 3 g
- Garlic — 10 g
- Sweet paprika — 5 g
- Aged sheep's cheese, grated — 80 g
- Dried mint — 3 g
- Salt — 8 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Whisk tarhana into 300 ml cold stock; rest 15 minutes to soften (lump insurance).
2. Bring remaining stock to 90°C with tomato paste and crushed garlic.
3. Whisk in the tarhana slurry; simmer 15–18 minutes, whisking often, until the soup thickens to a light porridge and loses any raw-flour taste.
4. Season with salt and pepper.
5. Foam butter in a small pan to beurre noisette, 3 minutes; off heat add paprika, dried mint and smoked salt.
6. Ladle soup, spoon the sizzling butter over each bowl.

## Plating & Service
Rustic bowls showered with grated sheep's cheese; the paprika-mint butter should still be audibly sizzling as it lands.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Buy tarhana from Balkan/Turkish grocers or ferment your own (yoghurt + flour + grated vegetables, dried over 5 days). Vegan: olive oil instead of butter, omit cheese.
