# Lamb Sweetbread 'Drob', Spring Herb Emulsion & Pickled Ramson

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Easter drob reimagined: crisped lamb sweetbreads over a bright emulsion of the drob's herb garden — dill, parsley, lovage — with pickled wild garlic.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Lamb sweetbreads — 500 g
- Milk, for soaking — 300 ml
- Plain flour or rice flour — 40 g
- Clarified butter — 50 g
- Dill — 20 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 20 g
- Lovage — 8 g
- Spinach — 50 g
- Egg yolk — 1 (20 g)
- Dijon mustard — 8 g
- Grapeseed oil — 120 ml
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Pickled ramson (wild garlic) buds or capers — 40 g
- Salt — 8 g
- White pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Soak sweetbreads in milk 4 hours; blanch from cold salted water to 85°C, hold 3 minutes, shock in ice.
2. Peel membranes; press between two trays with a light weight, 2 hours in the fridge.
3. Herb emulsion: blanch herbs and spinach 20 seconds, shock, squeeze; blend with yolk, mustard and lemon, then emulsify with oil in a thread. Season; pass. Keep below 10°C.
4. Portion sweetbreads into 40 g nuggets; season, dust with flour.
5. Sauté in clarified butter at high heat, 2 minutes per side, to a deep-gold crust and creamy centre.
6. Rest 1 minute on a rack; season again lightly.

## Plating & Service
A comma of herb emulsion, three sweetbread nuggets, pickled ramson buds between, one lovage leaf — serve immediately while the crust crackles.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken oysters or firm white fish cheeks work for guests avoiding offal; the emulsion doubles as a stunning sauce for the classic drob.
