# Halászlé Essence, Pike-Perch & Paprika Beurre Blanc

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Fisherman's soup for white tablecloths: a clarified carp-paprika essence poured over a pearly pike-perch fillet with a blushing paprika beurre blanc.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Carp or freshwater fish frames and heads — 1.2 kg
- Onion — 250 g
- Sweet Hungarian paprika — 22 g
- Hot paprika — 3 g
- Tomato — 120 g
- Egg whites (clarification) — 90 g
- Pike-perch fillets, skin on — 6 × 130 g
- Dry white wine — 100 ml
- Shallots — 60 g
- White wine vinegar — 20 ml
- Cold butter, diced — 150 g
- Salt — 12 g
- Chive batons — 10 g

## Method
1. Make halászlé base: simmer frames with chopped onion and tomato in 2.2 L water 60 minutes; mill and strain. Whisk in paprikas, boil 5 minutes, then strain again through muslin.
2. Clarify 1.2 L of base with whisked egg whites: bring slowly to 90°C, let the raft work 20 minutes, ladle through muslin. Season; it should be a translucent brick-amber.
3. Beurre blanc: reduce wine, vinegar and minced shallot to 50 ml; whisk in cold butter cube by cube at 60–70°C; season, then whisk in 3 g sweet paprika. Hold at 50°C.
4. Score the fillet skin; season. Pan-roast skin-down in a film of oil over medium-high 4 minutes to crisp, flip 60 seconds — core 52°C.
5. Warm essence to 85°C in a pouring jug.

## Plating & Service
Fillet skin-up in a deep plate, beurre blanc brushed over, chive batons; the clear paprika essence poured around at the table — soup and fish course in one.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Also superb with sea bass on the Riviera; dairy-free version replaces beurre blanc with a paprika–olive oil sabayon.
