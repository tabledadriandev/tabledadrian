# Karađorđeva Šnicla — Rolled Veal Cutlet with Kajmak Core

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Serbia's 'maiden's dream': pounded veal rolled around kajmak, breaded and fried to a golden log that floods cream when cut — kitsch, decadent, unbeatable.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Veal escalopes — 4 × 150 g
- Kajmak (or mascarpone beaten with 30% feta) — 160 g
- Plain flour — 80 g
- Eggs — 3 (165 g)
- Fine breadcrumbs — 200 g
- Sunflower oil, for frying — 700 ml
- Salt — 10 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Lemon — 1
- Tartar sauce: mayonnaise — 120 g
- Cornichons, chopped — 40 g
- Capers, chopped — 15 g
- Parsley — 8 g

## Method
1. Pound each escalope between film to 4–5 mm, keeping an even rectangle; season.
2. Pipe 40 g cold kajmak along one short edge; fold in the sides and roll into a tight cylinder. Chill 30 minutes — cold and tight or it leaks.
3. Bread twice: flour, egg, crumbs, then egg and crumbs again — the double jacket is the leak insurance.
4. Chill 15 more minutes.
5. Deep-fry at 170°C for 6–7 minutes, turning, to deep gold; finish 5 minutes at 180°C oven if the centre needs it (core 65°C).
6. Rest 3 minutes; mix the tartar ingredients.

## Plating & Service
Sliced once on the bias so the kajmak oozes, tartar sauce and lemon alongside, pommes frites if the gym is closed tomorrow.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pork loin is the everyday version; a thin slice of ham rolled with the kajmak steadies the filling for service under pressure.
