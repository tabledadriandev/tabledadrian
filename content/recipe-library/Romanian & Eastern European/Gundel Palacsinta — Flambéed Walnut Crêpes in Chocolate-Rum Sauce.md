# Gundel Palacsinta — Flambéed Walnut Crêpes in Chocolate-Rum Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Budapest's most famous dessert: crêpes around a rum-raisin walnut cream, cloaked in dark chocolate sauce and flamed at the table — restaurant theatre from 1910 that still works.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Plain flour — 180 g
- Eggs — 3 (165 g)
- Whole milk — 400 ml
- Sparkling water — 50 ml
- Butter, melted — 40 g
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Walnuts, ground — 200 g
- Whipping cream — 250 ml
- Caster sugar — 110 g
- Raisins — 60 g
- Dark rum — 80 ml
- Orange zest — 4 g
- Cinnamon — 1 g
- Dark chocolate 60% — 180 g
- Whole milk (sauce) — 120 ml
- Egg yolk (sauce) — 1 (20 g)
- Butter (finishing) — 20 g

## Method
1. Crêpes: whisk flour, eggs, milk, salt, half the butter; rest 30 minutes; add sparkling water. Fry 12 thin crêpes.
2. Filling: boil 150 ml cream with 80 g sugar; stir into ground walnuts with raisins (soaked in 30 ml rum), zest and cinnamon — a thick, spreadable paste. Cool slightly.
3. Sauce: melt chocolate with milk and 100 ml cream; whisk a ladle into the yolk with 30 g sugar, return, and thicken gently at 80°C; keep warm.
4. Spread each crêpe with 40 g filling; fold into quarters (the classic fan).
5. Crisp the folded crêpes briefly in the remaining butter, 1 minute per side.
6. At the table: sauce over, then warmed rum (50 ml) ignited and spooned flaming across.

## Plating & Service
Two fans per plate in a pool of chocolate sauce, the blue flame dying as the plate lands — allow the drama its moment.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skip the flambé below deck and add the rum to the sauce instead; candied orange in the filling is the Gundel restaurant's own flourish.
