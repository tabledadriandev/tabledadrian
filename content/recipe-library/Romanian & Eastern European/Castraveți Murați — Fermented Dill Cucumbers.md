# Castraveți Murați — Fermented Dill Cucumbers

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Half-sour to full-sour garlic-dill cucumbers in living brine — the pickle that judges every Romanian meal, and the easiest ferment to master.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Small pickling cucumbers — 1.5 kg
- Water — 1.5 L
- Coarse non-iodised salt — 45 g
- Garlic cloves, smashed — 30 g
- Dill stems with flower heads — 30 g
- Fresh horseradish root or leaves — 40 g
- Sour cherry or vine leaves (tannin, optional) — 6
- Black peppercorns — 4 g
- Coriander seeds — 3 g

## Method
1. Scrub cucumbers; trim the blossom end 2 mm (it harbours softening enzymes). Soak in ice water 1 hour for maximum crunch.
2. Dissolve salt in the water (3% brine) — no heat needed if your water is clean.
3. Pack a sterilised jar: leaves and horseradish at the bottom, cucumbers vertical and tight, garlic, dill and spices between.
4. Submerge completely under brine; weight down.
5. Ferment at 18–22°C: half-sour and electric-green at day 3–4; fully sour and olive-toned at day 10–14. Cloudy brine is success, not failure.
6. Refrigerate at your preferred sourness.

## Plating & Service
Cold, whole, alongside anything rich — or split lengthwise with grilled meat; the brine seasons potato salads and ciorbă.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Summer 'salted cucumbers' (2% brine, 2 days on the counter) are the fresh-eating cousin; never use iodised salt or chlorinated water.
