# Bulz Ciobănesc — Grilled Polenta Bombs with Cheese Heart

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Shepherd's bulz: mămăligă balls hiding molten smoked cheese, grilled until crusted — mountain food that plates like a treasure hunt.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Coarse cornmeal — 350 g
- Water — 1.4 L
- Salt — 12 g
- Smoked scamorza or cașcaval afumat — 200 g
- Telemea or feta — 100 g
- Butter — 60 g
- Sour cream — 200 g
- Eggs (optional crown) — 6
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Make a stiff mămăligă: rain cornmeal into boiling salted water, whisking, then stir with a wooden spoon over low heat 30 minutes until very thick and pulling cleanly from the pot.
2. Cool 10 minutes until handleable. With buttered hands, flatten 150 g portions into discs.
3. Centre 30 g smoked cheese and 15 g crumbled telemea on each; close into tight balls.
4. Brush with melted butter; grill on a plancha or roast at 220°C for 15–18 minutes, turning, until golden-crusted.
5. Optionally top each with a fried egg.
6. Rest 3 minutes — the centre is lava.

## Plating & Service
One bulz per wooden board, split table-side so the cheese flows, sour cream and cracked pepper alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Stir 50 g butter into the mămăligă for a richer crumb; bacon-wrapped bulz is the carnivore upgrade.
