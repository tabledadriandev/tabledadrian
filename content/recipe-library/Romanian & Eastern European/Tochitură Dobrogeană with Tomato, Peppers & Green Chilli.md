# Tochitură Dobrogeană with Tomato, Peppers & Green Chilli

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Black Sea coast's saucier take: pork and peppers stewed with tomato and a live edge of green chilli — looser, brighter, made for mopping with mămăligă.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Pork shoulder, diced 3 cm — 700 g
- Pork liver (optional, classic) — 150 g
- Sunflower oil — 40 ml
- Onions, sliced — 250 g
- Red bell peppers — 250 g
- Green chilli — 25 g
- Tomatoes, grated, or passata — 400 g
- Garlic — 25 g
- Dry white wine — 100 ml
- Sweet paprika — 6 g
- Dried thyme — 3 g
- Salt — 12 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Eggs (optional crown) — 6

## Method
1. Brown the pork in oil over high heat in two batches, 6 minutes each; set aside.
2. Soften onion in the same pot 8 minutes; add sliced peppers and chilli, 5 minutes.
3. Return pork, add wine; reduce 2 minutes. Add tomato, paprika, thyme, salt, pepper.
4. Cover and stew at a low simmer 45 minutes until the pork yields.
5. Add diced liver (if using) and crushed garlic; cook 8 minutes more, uncovered, to a rich, spoonable sauce.
6. Rest 10 minutes; check salt and chilli heat.

## Plating & Service
Over mămăligă or with bread, fried egg optional; scatter raw green chilli rings for the brave.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fish tochitură (catfish, same sauce, 12 minutes) is the Danube Delta cousin worth trying on charter.
