# Clătite cu Brânză Dulce și Stafide — Baked Cheese Crêpe Roll-Ups

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Thin crêpes rolled around vanilla-lemon sweet cheese and raisins, baked under sour cream until just gratinated — the Romanian answer to blintzes.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Plain flour — 200 g
- Eggs — 3 (165 g)
- Whole milk — 450 ml
- Sparkling water — 50 ml
- Butter, melted — 40 g
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Fresh cow's cheese or ricotta — 500 g
- Caster sugar — 90 g
- Egg yolk — 1 (20 g)
- Vanilla — 5 ml
- Lemon zest — 5 g
- Raisins, rum-soaked — 80 g
- Sour cream — 250 g
- Icing sugar — 20 g

## Method
1. Batter: whisk flour, eggs, milk, salt and half the butter; rest 30 minutes; loosen with sparkling water.
2. Fry thin crêpes in a buttered 22 cm pan, 60 seconds per side; stack (makes about 12).
3. Filling: beat cheese with sugar, yolk, vanilla and zest; fold in drained raisins.
4. Roll each crêpe around 45 g filling, tucking the ends, blintz-style.
5. Arrange seam-down in a buttered dish; blanket with sour cream.
6. Bake at 180°C for 20 minutes until the cream trembles and the edges catch colour.
7. Rest 5 minutes; dust with icing sugar.

## Plating & Service
Two per plate with the baked sour cream spooned over — a dessert that doubles as a legendary charter breakfast.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Serve unbaked with just jam for the street-stand version; apricot preserve under the sour cream is the Banat refinement.
