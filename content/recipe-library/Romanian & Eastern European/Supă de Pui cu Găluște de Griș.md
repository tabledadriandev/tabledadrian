# Supă de Pui cu Găluște de Griș

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Crystal-clear chicken soup with cloud-light semolina dumplings — the test of a Romanian bunica and of any commis.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Whole chicken, jointed — 1.2 kg
- Onion — 150 g
- Carrot — 200 g
- Celeriac — 100 g
- Parsnip — 100 g
- Black peppercorns — 3 g
- Salt — 14 g
- Eggs — 2 (110 g)
- Fine semolina — 90 g
- Sunflower oil — 10 ml
- Cold sparkling water — 20 ml
- Parsley — 10 g

## Method
1. Cover chicken with 3 L cold water; bring to 92°C, skim relentlessly for a clear broth.
2. Add whole vegetables and peppercorns; simmer uncovered 70 minutes, never boiling.
3. Strain; keep the carrot, slice into coins. Season broth with salt and return to 95°C.
4. For găluște: whisk eggs with oil and a pinch of salt, fold in semolina and sparkling water; rest 10 minutes to hydrate.
5. Drop tablespoon-sized dumplings into barely simmering broth (90°C); cover and poach 15 minutes without lifting the lid — they should double in size.
6. Add carrot coins back, check seasoning, finish with parsley.

## Plating & Service
One large dumpling per bowl, carrot coins around, broth poured at the table from a jug if you want theatre.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The sparkling water is the fluffiness insurance; gluten-free semolina (corn) works with 5 minutes' extra rest.
