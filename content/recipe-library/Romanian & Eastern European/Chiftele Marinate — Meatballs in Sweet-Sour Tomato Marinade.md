# Chiftele Marinate — Meatballs in Sweet-Sour Tomato Marinade

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Fried garlicky meatballs re-simmered in a bay-and-thyme tomato 'marinade' with a sweet-sour spike — canteen nostalgia elevated by care.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Pork and beef mince — 700 g
- Potato, raw, grated and squeezed — 120 g
- Onion, grated — 100 g
- Garlic — 20 g
- Egg — 1 (55 g)
- Dill and parsley, chopped — 15 g
- Plain flour — 60 g
- Sunflower oil — 200 ml
- Tomato passata — 600 g
- Sugar — 12 g
- Red wine vinegar — 25 ml
- Bay leaves — 2
- Dried thyme — 3 g
- Salt — 14 g
- Black pepper — 3 g

## Method
1. Knead meats with potato, onion, half the garlic, egg, herbs, 10 g salt and pepper; rest 15 minutes.
2. Roll 45 g balls, flatten slightly, dust with flour; shallow-fry at 170°C, 3 minutes per side, to golden. Drain.
3. Marinade: simmer passata with 250 ml water, sugar, vinegar, bay, thyme, remaining salt for 10 minutes.
4. Add remaining crushed garlic; slide in the meatballs.
5. Simmer gently, uncovered, 15 minutes, turning once, until the sauce clings.
6. Rest 10 minutes — they improve as they drink the sauce.

## Plating & Service
Over mashed potatoes or mămăligă, sauce spooned generously, dill on top.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Bake the chiftele instead (200°C, 20 minutes) for a lighter route; GF with rice-flour dusting.
