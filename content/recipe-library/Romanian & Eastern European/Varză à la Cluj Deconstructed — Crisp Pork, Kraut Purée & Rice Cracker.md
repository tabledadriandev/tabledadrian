# Varză à la Cluj Deconstructed — Crisp Pork, Kraut Purée & Rice Cracker

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The casserole exploded into textures: pressed paprika pork, a silky sauerkraut purée, puffed rice cracker for the rice layer, and sour cream as a warm foam.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Pork shoulder — 600 g
- Sweet paprika — 10 g
- Dried thyme — 3 g
- Sauerkraut — 500 g
- Butter — 60 g
- Whipping cream — 100 ml
- Short-grain rice — 100 g
- Sunflower oil, for puffing — 400 ml
- Sour cream — 200 g
- Whole milk — 80 ml
- Soy lecithin (optional) — 2 g
- Chicken stock — 400 ml
- Salt — 10 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Chives — 8 g

## Method
1. Rub pork with paprika, thyme, 6 g salt; braise with stock, covered, at 150°C for 2.5 hours. Shred, season, press 2 cm deep between trays; chill overnight. Cut 4 rectangles.
2. Rice cracker: overcook rice to a paste (30 minutes), blend, spread 2 mm on a mat, dry at 70°C for 4 hours; break into shards and puff in 200°C oil, 5 seconds. Salt.
3. Kraut purée: rinse kraut once, squeeze; stew in 40 g butter with a splash of stock 25 minutes; blend with cream to silk, pass, season.
4. Sour cream foam: warm sour cream with milk to 60°C, season, add lecithin and froth with a stick blender (or simply loosen and spoon).
5. Crisp the pork rectangles in a film of oil, pressing, 3 minutes per side until lacquered.
6. Warm the purée; check all seasonings.

## Plating & Service
A swoosh of kraut purée, the crisp pork slab on top, foam spooned at one end, rice cracker leaning across, chives — all the casserole's flavours, none of its gravity.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The pressed-pork technique holds three days, making this a genuine service-friendly showpiece; smoked pork lifts it further.
