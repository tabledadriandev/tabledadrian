# Mămăligă în Straturi — Baked Polenta, Cheese & Smoked Bacon Layers

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Polenta lasagne from the Carpathians: mămăligă layered with two cheeses, smoked bacon and sour cream, baked until bubbling — crew-meal royalty.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Coarse cornmeal — 400 g
- Water — 1.6 L
- Salt — 14 g
- Butter — 60 g
- Telemea or feta, crumbled — 250 g
- Cașcaval or gruyère, grated — 200 g
- Smoked bacon, diced — 180 g
- Sour cream — 300 g
- Eggs — 2 (110 g)
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Chives — 8 g

## Method
1. Cook a medium-firm mămăligă: cornmeal into boiling salted water, stir 25 minutes; beat in 40 g butter.
2. Render bacon until just crisp, 6 minutes.
3. Butter a deep 26 cm dish. Layer: a third of the mămăligă, half the telemea, half the bacon, a third of the cașcaval; repeat; finish with mămăligă.
4. Whisk sour cream with eggs and pepper; pour over, letting it seep at the edges. Top with remaining cașcaval.
5. Bake at 190°C, 35–40 minutes, until burnished and puffed at the rim.
6. Rest 15 minutes to set the slices.

## Plating & Service
Cut wedges that show the strata; a sharp salată de varză alongside cuts the richness perfectly.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian: swap bacon for 250 g sautéed mushrooms; assembled trays hold 2 days chilled, bake to order.
