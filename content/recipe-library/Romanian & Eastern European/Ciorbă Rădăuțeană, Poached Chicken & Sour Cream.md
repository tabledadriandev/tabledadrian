# Ciorbă Rădăuțeană, Poached Chicken & Sour Cream

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rădăuți's famous answer to tripe soup — the same creamy, garlicky, vinegar-bright profile built on tender chicken instead.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Chicken legs, bone-in — 900 g
- Chicken breast — 300 g
- Carrot — 150 g
- Celeriac — 100 g
- Parsnip — 80 g
- Onion — 120 g
- Garlic — 40 g
- Egg yolks — 3 (60 g)
- Sour cream 24% — 250 g
- White wine vinegar — 50 ml
- Salt — 14 g
- Black pepper, ground — 2 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 15 g
- Hot chillies in vinegar, to serve — 60 g

## Method
1. Cover chicken with 2.8 L cold water, bring to 95°C, skim. Add whole peeled vegetables and simmer 45 minutes.
2. Add the breast for the final 20 minutes. Remove all chicken; strain broth and discard vegetables except the carrot.
3. Shred the meat off the bones into bite-size pieces; slice the carrot into thin coins.
4. Return broth to the pot with meat and carrot; season with salt and pepper, hold at 85°C.
5. Crush garlic to a paste with a pinch of salt, dilute with 50 ml broth, stir in off the heat.
6. Whisk yolks with sour cream, temper with 250 ml hot broth, then blend into the soup below 80°C.
7. Sharpen with vinegar, rest 5 minutes, shower with chopped parsley.

## Plating & Service
Ladle generously, making sure each bowl gets breast and leg meat; serve chillies and extra sour cream alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> For a lighter version use Greek yoghurt stabilised with 5 g cornflour instead of the yolk–sour cream liaison.
