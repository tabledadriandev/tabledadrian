# Murături Asortate — Mixed Brine-Fermented Winter Pickles

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Romanian cellar in a jar: green tomatoes, cauliflower, carrots and peppers fermented in pure brine with horseradish keeping everything defiantly crunchy.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Green tomatoes (gogonele) — 600 g
- Cauliflower florets — 400 g
- Carrots, thick coins — 300 g
- Red bell peppers, quartered — 300 g
- Small cucumbers — 300 g
- Fresh horseradish root, batons — 60 g
- Garlic — 40 g
- Celery stalks with leaves — 80 g
- Dried dill stems with heads — 20 g
- Black peppercorns — 5 g
- Mustard seeds — 8 g
- Bay leaves — 3
- Coarse salt, non-iodised — 60 g
- Water — 3 L

## Method
1. Dissolve salt in the water (2% brine), bringing a third of it briefly to the boil with the peppercorns, mustard seeds and bay; cool completely and recombine.
2. Pack vegetables snugly into sterilised jars, layering horseradish, garlic, celery and dill throughout — horseradish is the crunch insurance.
3. Pour brine to submerge fully; weight with a small plate or fermentation weight.
4. Cover loosely; ferment at 18–20°C. 'Pull the air' every 2 days: pour brine off and back, or shake — the old ritual that equalises the ferment.
5. Taste from day 7; they hit stride at 2–3 weeks, turning sour, effervescent and golden.
6. Refrigerate once you love them; they hold for months.

## Plating & Service
A rainbow bowl straight from the jar beside fatty mains and winter feasts; the brine itself is a prized hangover tonic.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No vinegar, ever — this is lacto-fermentation. Add hot chillies for fire; keep everything submerged and cool, and the jar keeps giving.
