# Dry-Aged Beef Mici, Smoked Bone Marrow & Charred Bread

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Mici with a steakhouse pedigree: dry-aged trim in the grind, smoked marrow basting, mustard emulsion, and charred sourdough standing in for the street-corner slice.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Dry-aged beef trim and chuck, minced twice — 800 g
- Beef bone marrow — 100 g
- Rich beef stock, ice cold — 100 ml
- Garlic — 25 g
- Bicarbonate of soda — 4 g
- Sweet paprika — 5 g
- Dried savory — 2 g
- Salt — 14 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Dijon mustard — 60 g
- Egg yolk — 1 (20 g)
- Grapeseed oil — 100 ml
- Sourdough, thick slices — 6
- Pickled green chillies — 60 g

## Method
1. Blitz garlic with cold stock; strain. Melt half the marrow, cool to liquid-warm.
2. Knead beef with salt 3 minutes to a sticky paste; work in bicarbonate, spices, garlic stock and melted marrow. Rest 24 hours.
3. Mustard emulsion: whisk yolk with mustard, emulsify with oil to a loose mayonnaise; season, sharpen with pickle brine.
4. Shape 80 g rolls with wet hands. Smoke remaining marrow cubes under cloche 3 minutes (or use plain).
5. Grill mici over high heat 3–4 minutes per side to 72°C, basting with the smoked marrow as it melts.
6. Char the sourdough on the grill, rub lightly with garlic.

## Plating & Service
Three mici per plank on charred bread, a confident swipe of mustard emulsion, pickled chillies; the marrow gloss should catch the light.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The classic ratio beef:lamb 80:20 also works aged; without a smoker, a drop of liquid smoke in the marrow is honest enough at sea.
