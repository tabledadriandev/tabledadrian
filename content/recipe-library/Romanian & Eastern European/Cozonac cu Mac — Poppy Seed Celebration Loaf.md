# Cozonac cu Mac — Poppy Seed Celebration Loaf

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The other great cozonac: the same silky brioche twisted around a dark, honeyed poppy-seed paste — the Banat and Transylvanian houses' loyalty test.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Strong white flour — 600 g
- Whole milk, lukewarm — 250 ml
- Fresh yeast — 30 g
- Caster sugar — 140 g
- Egg yolks — 4 (80 g)
- Butter, soft — 120 g
- Fine salt — 8 g
- Vanilla — 8 ml
- Orange zest — 6 g
- Poppy seeds, ground — 300 g
- Milk (filling) — 200 ml
- Honey — 60 g
- Sugar (filling) — 80 g
- Raisins — 60 g
- Lemon zest (filling) — 4 g
- Egg, for glazing — 1 (55 g)

## Method
1. Make the enriched dough exactly as for cozonac cu nucă: sponge, long knead, butter in stages, 90-minute warm prove.
2. Filling: simmer ground poppy seeds in milk with sugar and honey, stirring, 10 minutes to a thick paste; add raisins and lemon zest; cool completely.
3. Divide dough in two; roll each to 40 × 30 cm; spread the cool poppy paste to 1 cm of the edges.
4. Roll into ropes; twist together and settle into a lined 30 cm loaf tin.
5. Prove 45 minutes; glaze with beaten egg.
6. Bake at 170°C for 45–50 minutes to a 94°C core, tenting after 25 minutes.
7. Cool on its side, rotating — poppy filling is heavy and honest about shortcuts.

## Plating & Service
Slices that show the black spiral against gold crumb; serve with tea, or toasted with butter on day three.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Grinding the poppy seeds fresh (or twice through the grinder) is the flavour unlock; the paste freezes for the next bake.
