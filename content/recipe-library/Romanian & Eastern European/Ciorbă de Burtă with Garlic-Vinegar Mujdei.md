# Ciorbă de Burtă with Garlic-Vinegar Mujdei

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The benchmark Romanian tripe soup — silky, garlicky, sharpened with vinegar; a soul-restoring first course after a long charter night.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Honeycomb beef tripe, cleaned — 1200 g
- Beef knuckle bones — 800 g
- Carrot — 200 g
- Celeriac — 150 g
- Onion, whole — 200 g
- Garlic — 60 g
- Egg yolks — 4 (80 g)
- Sour cream 24% (smântână) — 300 g
- White wine vinegar — 60 ml
- Fine salt — 18 g
- Black peppercorns — 4 g
- Bay leaves — 2
- Hot pickled chillies, to serve — 80 g
- Sunflower oil — 20 ml

## Method
1. Blanch tripe from cold water, drain, rinse. Cover tripe and bones with 4 L cold water, bring to 95°C and skim thoroughly.
2. Add whole onion, carrot, celeriac, bay and peppercorns; simmer uncovered at a bare tremble for 3–3.5 hours until tripe is completely tender.
3. Lift out tripe, cool slightly, slice into 5 mm × 40 mm ribbons. Strain broth; you need about 2.5 L.
4. Return broth to 90°C with the tripe ribbons; season with 14 g salt and simmer 10 minutes.
5. Crush garlic with 4 g salt and 20 ml oil into a paste (mujdei); loosen with 50 ml hot broth and stir into the pot off the heat.
6. Whisk yolks with sour cream, temper with 200 ml hot broth added gradually, then stir the liaison into the soup — do not exceed 80°C afterwards.
7. Finish with vinegar to taste (start with 40 ml); adjust salt. Rest 10 minutes before serving.

## Plating & Service
Serve steaming in deep bowls with extra vinegar, hot pickled chillies and a spoon of sour cream on the side — guests season their own.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken-breast ribbons poached in the broth replace tripe for a milder 'ciorbă falsă de burtă'; skip the yolk liaison and use lactose-free cream for dairy-light.
