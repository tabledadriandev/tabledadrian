# Papanași Prăjiți — Fried Cheese Doughnuts, Sour Cherry & Smântână

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Romania's most beloved dessert and its most photographed: hot cheese doughnuts wearing their own little hats, buried in sour cream and sour cherry jam.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Fresh cow's cheese or drained ricotta — 500 g
- Eggs — 2 (110 g)
- Caster sugar — 60 g
- Vanilla extract — 5 ml
- Lemon zest — 4 g
- Fine semolina — 40 g
- Plain flour — 220 g
- Baking powder — 8 g
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Sunflower oil, for frying — 1 L
- Sour cream — 300 g
- Sour cherry jam (dulceață de vișine) — 250 g
- Icing sugar — 20 g

## Method
1. Beat cheese with eggs, sugar, vanilla and zest; fold in semolina, then flour, baking powder and salt to a soft, slightly tacky dough. Rest 20 minutes.
2. With floured hands, divide into 6 large balls plus 6 small (the hats).
3. Shape each large ball into a ring: flatten to 3 cm, punch a hole with a floured finger and widen gently. Roll the hats round.
4. Heat oil to 160–165°C — hotter and they brown before cooking through.
5. Fry rings 3–4 minutes per side, hats 3 minutes total, to deep gold; drain on a rack.
6. Serve within minutes: ring down, sour cream over, jam cascading, hat on top, icing sugar snow.

## Plating & Service
Assembled tall and served immediately — the contrast of hot doughnut, cold smântână and sharp vișine is the entire argument.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Blueberry jam is the mountain-resort alternate; the dough hates waiting, so fry to order.
