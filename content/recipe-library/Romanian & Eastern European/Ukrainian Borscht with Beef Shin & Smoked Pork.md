# Ukrainian Borscht with Beef Shin & Smoked Pork

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Deep garnet borscht built on beef shin and a whisper of smoked pork — beets sautéed with vinegar so the colour never fades.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Beef shin, bone-in — 800 g
- Smoked pork ribs — 300 g
- Beetroot — 500 g
- White cabbage — 300 g
- Potato — 300 g
- Carrot — 150 g
- Onion — 150 g
- Tomato paste — 60 g
- Garlic — 20 g
- White wine vinegar — 40 ml
- Sugar — 8 g
- Bay leaves — 2
- Salt — 15 g
- Sour cream, to serve — 200 g
- Dill — 15 g

## Method
1. Cover beef and ribs with 3.2 L cold water; bring to 95°C, skim, add bay and simmer 90 minutes.
2. Julienne beets; sauté in a wide pan with 20 ml oil skimmed from the broth, tomato paste, vinegar and sugar for 10 minutes over medium heat — this locks the colour.
3. Remove meats; strain broth. Pull meat from bones in chunks and return with broth to the pot.
4. Add diced potato; simmer 10 minutes. Add shredded cabbage, grated carrot and diced onion; 10 minutes more.
5. Fold in the beet base; simmer 10 minutes only — longer dulls the colour. Season with salt.
6. Crush garlic into the pot off the heat; rest, covered, 20 minutes (borscht improves with a nap).

## Plating & Service
Serve with a heaped spoon of sour cream, torn dill, and garlic rubbed on dark bread if you have it.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan borscht: replace meats with 400 g white beans and 8 g smoked paprika; finish with cashew cream.
