# Kürtőskalács — Chimney Cake with Caramelised Walnut Crust

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Székely fairground spiral: sweet dough wound around a cylinder, rolled in sugar, and roasted until the outside is caramel armour and the inside is steam-soft ribbon.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Strong white flour — 500 g
- Whole milk, lukewarm — 220 ml
- Fresh yeast — 25 g
- Caster sugar — 160 g
- Egg yolks — 2 (40 g)
- Butter, melted — 90 g
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Vanilla — 5 ml
- Walnuts, finely chopped — 100 g
- Cinnamon — 4 g

## Method
1. Wake yeast in milk with 20 g sugar. Knead flour, yolks, 40 g sugar, salt, vanilla, yeast milk and 40 g butter for 8 minutes to a soft dough; prove 60 minutes to double.
2. Wrap thick wooden rolling pins (or cannoli-style tubes) in foil; butter well.
3. Divide dough into 6; roll each into a long 1 cm rope. Wind spirally around the cylinder, edges touching; roll on the bench to fuse and flatten.
4. Brush with melted butter; roll firmly through caster sugar.
5. Roast over the grill's indirect heat or at 200°C fan, rotating every 4 minutes, 20–25 minutes, until deep caramel-gold all around.
6. Roll the hot cake immediately through the walnut-cinnamon-sugar mix (remaining sugar, walnuts, cinnamon); slide off the cylinder.

## Plating & Service
Stand the hollow chimney upright on a board; guests unwind their own warm ribbon — the theatre is half the dessert.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Coconut or cocoa-sugar crusts are the fair-stand standards; the rotisserie attachment on a yacht grill was born for this.
