# Ciorbă de Lobodă, Spring Greens & Young Garlic

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A pale-pink spring soup of orache (or chard) and young garlic, brightened with borș — Romania's gentlest ciorbă.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Red orache or rainbow chard leaves — 300 g
- Spring onions — 80 g
- Young garlic — 30 g
- Carrot — 100 g
- Rice — 40 g
- Fermented borș or lemon juice — 250 ml / 35 ml
- Egg — 1 (55 g)
- Sour cream — 100 g
- Vegetable stock — 1.5 L
- Salt — 10 g
- Fresh lovage — 8 g
- Sunflower oil — 15 ml

## Method
1. Sweat sliced spring onion, garlic and diced carrot in oil for 4 minutes over medium heat.
2. Add stock and rice; simmer 12 minutes at 95°C.
3. Add roughly chopped orache; cook 5 minutes until wilted but still vivid.
4. Boil the borș separately, skim, and add; season with salt.
5. Whisk egg with sour cream, temper with hot soup and stir in off the heat below 80°C.
6. Finish with chopped lovage.

## Plating & Service
Serve in shallow bowls so the pink-green leaves show; a drizzle of sour cream on top.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: skip the liaison and mount with 30 ml cold-pressed rapeseed oil; spinach substitutes cleanly for orache.
