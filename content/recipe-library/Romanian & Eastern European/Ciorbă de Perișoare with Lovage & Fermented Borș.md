# Ciorbă de Perișoare with Lovage & Fermented Borș

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Herb-flecked pork-and-rice meatballs poached in a vegetable broth soured with fermented wheat-bran borș — lovage is non-negotiable.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Pork shoulder, minced — 500 g
- Short-grain rice, rinsed — 60 g
- Egg — 1 (55 g)
- Onion, finely grated — 80 g
- Carrot — 150 g
- Celeriac — 100 g
- Red bell pepper — 100 g
- Tomato passata — 150 g
- Fermented borș (or 40 ml lemon juice) — 400 ml
- Fresh lovage — 12 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 10 g
- Salt — 14 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Sour cream, to serve — 120 g

## Method
1. Sweat diced carrot, celeriac and pepper in a dry 5 L pot with a splash of water for 5 minutes; add 2.2 L water and simmer 15 minutes at 95°C.
2. Mix pork, rice, egg, grated onion, 6 g salt, pepper and half the chopped parsley; knead 2 minutes until tacky.
3. With wet hands roll 30 g meatballs (about 22 pieces).
4. Add passata to the broth, then lower in the meatballs one by one; poach at 90°C for 25 minutes without boiling.
5. Bring borș to the boil separately, skim, and add to the soup; simmer 5 more minutes and season with remaining salt.
6. Off the heat, add chopped lovage and parsley; rest 10 minutes.

## Plating & Service
Serve with a quenelle of sour cream melting into the broth and extra lovage on top.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap pork for turkey thigh for leaner bowls; use cooked quinoa instead of rice and it stays gluten-free if your borș is certified GF.
