# Fasole cu Cârnați — Baked Beans with Smoked Sausage

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Romania's Army Day dish: creamy beans and smoked sausage in paprika-tomato sauce — humble, thunderously good, and even better reheated.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Dried white beans, soaked overnight — 500 g
- Smoked pork sausages (cârnați afumați) — 500 g
- Onions, diced — 250 g
- Carrot, grated — 120 g
- Sunflower oil — 40 ml
- Tomato passata — 350 g
- Sweet paprika — 10 g
- Dried thyme — 3 g
- Bay leaves — 2
- Salt — 12 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Parsley — 10 g

## Method
1. Boil drained beans 10 minutes in fresh water; drain again. Cover with 1.8 L fresh water, add bay, simmer 60–80 minutes until tender.
2. Brown sausages whole in oil, 6 minutes; remove. Sweat onion in the same fat 8 minutes; add carrot 3 minutes, then paprika off heat and passata.
3. Combine sauce with the beans and 300 ml of their liquor; season with thyme, salt, pepper.
4. Slice sausages thick on the bias; nestle into the beans.
5. Bake uncovered at 180°C for 30 minutes until the top blisters and the sauce thickens.
6. Rest 10 minutes; scatter parsley.

## Plating & Service
Straight from the dish with raw onion crescents and pickled cucumbers — spoon food, gloriously so.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: smoked tofu chunks and 3 g extra smoked paprika; a spoon of mustard stirred in at the end is a Transylvanian touch.
