# Papanași Reimagined — Cheese Beignet, Yogurt Sorbet & Sour Cherry Gel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The national dessert plated for a tasting menu: an airy cheese beignet, its cold sour-cream half reborn as yogurt sorbet, the jam as a mirror-bright vișine gel.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Fresh cheese or drained ricotta — 300 g
- Eggs — 2 (110 g)
- Caster sugar — 130 g
- Plain flour — 130 g
- Baking powder — 5 g
- Lemon zest — 3 g
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Greek yogurt — 400 g
- Glucose syrup — 60 g
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Sour cherries (frozen fine) — 300 g
- Agar-agar — 2 g
- Sunflower oil, for frying — 1 L
- Icing sugar — 15 g
- Micro lemon balm — 6 g

## Method
1. Sorbet: warm 100 ml water with 70 g sugar and glucose to dissolve; cool, whisk into yogurt with lemon juice; churn and freeze. (No machine: freeze and blitz twice.)
2. Cherry gel: simmer cherries with 40 g sugar 10 minutes; blend, pass. Weigh 300 g of purée, whisk in agar, boil 90 seconds, set chilled, then blend to a glossy fluid gel; load into a bottle.
3. Beignet batter: whisk cheese, eggs, 20 g sugar, zest and salt; fold in flour and baking powder. Rest 20 minutes.
4. Fry small quenelles (30 g) of batter at 165°C, turning, 4 minutes to puffed deep gold; drain.
5. Dust beignets lightly with icing sugar.
6. Plate fast — sorbet melts, beignets deflate slowly; both punish hesitation.

## Plating & Service
Three beignets in a curve, gel dots and one bold swipe, a quenelle of yogurt sorbet on a crumb of the fried batter, lemon balm — the classic's flavours, choreographed.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The gel and sorbet hold for days, making this an efficient à-la-carte dessert; blueberry gel swaps in seamlessly.
