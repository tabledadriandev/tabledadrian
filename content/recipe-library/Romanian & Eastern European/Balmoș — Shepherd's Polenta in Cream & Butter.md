# Balmoș — Shepherd's Polenta in Cream & Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Mămăligă's decadent alpine cousin: cornmeal cooked directly in cream and butter with sheep's cheese folded through — the richest dish in the Carpathians.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Coarse cornmeal — 300 g
- Double cream — 500 ml
- Whole milk — 500 ml
- Butter — 100 g
- Sour cream — 150 g
- Urda or fresh ricotta — 150 g
- Telemea or salted feta — 150 g
- Salt — 8 g
- Chives or spring onion — 10 g

## Method
1. Bring cream, milk and 60 g butter to a simmer with salt in a heavy pot.
2. Rain in cornmeal, whisking constantly; drop to the lowest heat.
3. Stir with a wooden spoon 30–35 minutes — balmoș is stirred far more than mămăligă; it should ripple like thick custard and pull from the sides.
4. Fold in urda and half the telemea; cook 3 minutes more.
5. Beat in remaining butter and the sour cream off the heat — glossy, trembling, barely holding shape.
6. Adjust salt (the cheeses carry plenty).

## Plating & Service
Spoon into warm shallow bowls, crumble remaining telemea over, chives, and a final knob of butter melting in the centre.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> A fried egg or crisp bacon turns it into brunch; leftover balmoș sets into slabs that pan-fry beautifully.
