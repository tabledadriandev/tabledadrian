# Baklava cu Nucă — Balkan Walnut Baklava with Citrus Honey Syrup

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Balkan family baklava — all walnut, honeyed, lemon-bright — hot pan, cold syrup: the one physics rule that decides crispness.

## Ingredients (serves 12)
- Filo pastry sheets — 500 g (about 24 sheets)
- Walnuts, finely chopped — 500 g
- Caster sugar — 80 g
- Cinnamon — 5 g
- Butter, clarified — 300 g
- Sugar (syrup) — 400 g
- Water — 350 ml
- Honey — 120 g
- Lemon juice and zest — 30 ml / 4 g
- Orange zest — 3 g
- Cloves — 4

## Method
1. Syrup first: boil sugar, water, honey, citrus zests, cloves and lemon juice 10 minutes to light syrup; chill completely.
2. Mix walnuts with sugar and cinnamon.
3. Butter a 30 × 25 cm tray. Layer 8 filo sheets, brushing each with clarified butter.
4. Scatter half the walnuts; 4 more buttered sheets; remaining walnuts; finish with 8 buttered sheets, the top one immaculate.
5. Chill 20 minutes, then cut through completely into diamonds with a sharp knife — pre-cutting is non-negotiable.
6. Bake at 170°C for 45–50 minutes to even deep gold.
7. Pour the cold syrup slowly over the hot baklava, aiming for the cut lines; it will hiss. Rest at least 4 hours, ideally overnight, uncovered.

## Plating & Service
One diamond per plate with espresso, or pyramided on a brass tray for the full effect; it keeps a week and improves for two days.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Half pistachio drifts it Ottoman-luxe; syrup-hot-pastry-cold is the other valid school — pick one physics and never mix them.
