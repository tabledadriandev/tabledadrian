# Duck Breast, Sauerkraut Velouté & Juniper Jus

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rață pe varză in evening dress: rosy duck breast over a silky sauerkraut velouté, caramelised cabbage wedge, and a juniper-dark jus.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Duck breasts — 4 × 180 g
- Sauerkraut — 400 g
- Whipping cream — 150 ml
- Butter — 50 g
- Duck or chicken jus — 250 ml
- Juniper berries, crushed — 5
- Dry white wine — 100 ml
- Hispi or sweetheart cabbage — 1 small (400 g)
- Sweet paprika — 3 g
- Honey — 10 g
- Sherry vinegar — 10 ml
- Salt — 9 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Thyme — 3 sprigs

## Method
1. Velouté: rinse kraut once, squeeze, stew in 30 g butter 20 minutes with a splash of water; blend with cream to complete silk, pass twice, season — it should whisper sour, not shout.
2. Jus: reduce wine with juniper and thyme by half; add jus, reduce to nappe, mount with 10 g butter, balance with honey and vinegar.
3. Cut cabbage into 4 wedges through the core; sear cut-face down in 10 g butter until deeply caramelised, 6 minutes; season, dust faintly with paprika, finish in a 180°C oven 8 minutes.
4. Score duck skin finely; start skin-down in a cold pan, render over medium 8–10 minutes to crisp, flip 2 minutes; rest to 54°C core.
5. Slice each breast into three thick pieces.

## Plating & Service
A pool of pale velouté, duck slices skin up, the burnished cabbage wedge, jus around the duck only — the plate reads autumn immediately.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Goose breast for winter charters; the velouté also flatters roast pork belly and grilled sausages from the classic canon.
