# Sernik — Polish Baked Cheesecake with Sour Cream Glaze

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The twaróg cheesecake of Polish Sundays: dense yet light, vanilla-lemon scented, on a butter-crumb base under a barely-sweet sour cream glaze.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Twaróg (or ricotta pressed dry + cream cheese 2:1) — 900 g
- Caster sugar — 200 g
- Eggs — 5 (275 g)
- Butter, soft — 100 g
- Potato starch or cornflour — 40 g
- Vanilla pod — 1
- Lemon zest — 6 g
- Fine salt — 3 g
- Butter biscuits, crushed — 180 g
- Butter, melted (base) — 80 g
- Sour cream — 300 g
- Icing sugar — 30 g
- Raisins (optional) — 80 g

## Method
1. Base: press biscuit crumbs mixed with melted butter into a lined 24 cm springform; chill.
2. Pass twaróg twice through a mincer or sieve — the fluff step that separates sernik from brick.
3. Beat soft butter with sugar; add cheese, then eggs one at a time on low speed (air is the enemy of crack-free cheesecake).
4. Fold in starch, vanilla seeds, zest, salt and optional raisins.
5. Pour over the base; bake at 160°C for 60–70 minutes until the edges set and the centre wobbles like a held breath. Oven off, door ajar, 30 minutes.
6. Glaze: whisk sour cream with icing sugar; spread over the warm cake; return to the cooling oven 10 minutes.
7. Chill overnight — sernik is a next-day cake, constitutionally.

## Plating & Service
Clean wedges (hot knife) with nothing but coffee; Poles will inspect the texture, so pass the twaróg twice.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The 'królewski' version adds a cocoa-crumb lattice; frozen slices thaw impeccably for charter provisioning.
