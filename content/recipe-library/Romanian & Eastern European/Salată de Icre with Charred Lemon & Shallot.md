# Salată de Icre with Charred Lemon & Shallot

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Carp roe whipped with oil into an ivory mousse — Romania's taramasalata, lighter and brighter, here with charred lemon for depth.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Salted carp or pike roe (icre) — 150 g
- Sunflower oil — 350 ml
- Semolina, fine — 30 g
- Water — 90 ml
- Lemons — 2 (one juiced, one halved and charred)
- Shallot, minced — 50 g
- Sparkling water — 30 ml
- White pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Simmer semolina in the 90 ml water 3 minutes to a thick porridge; cool completely (this is the traditional stabiliser and stretcher).
2. Char the halved lemon cut-side down in a dry pan 4 minutes until caramelised; juice it.
3. Whisk roe with the cooled semolina 1 minute to loosen.
4. Add oil in a thin stream, whisking constantly (or electric mixer, medium), until thick, pale and doubled in volume — 6–8 minutes.
5. Season with both lemon juices and white pepper; adjust with sparkling water to a soft, scoopable mousse.
6. Fold in minced shallot; chill 1 hour.

## Plating & Service
Swoosh across a plate, a few drops of oil, black olives and warm blistered flatbread — or elegant on endive leaves for canapés.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No semolina (GF): use 60 g soaked stale GF bread or simply more oil and patience. Smoked cod roe gives a Nordic accent.
