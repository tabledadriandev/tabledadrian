# Saramură de Crap — Fire-Grilled Carp in Hot Brine

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Danube grill masterpiece: charred carp doused in a boiling chilli-garlic brine that becomes the sauce — primitive technique, electric result.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Carp fillets, skin on (or whole butterflied) — 800 g
- Coarse salt — 15 g
- Sunflower oil — 20 ml
- Water — 400 ml
- Garlic — 30 g
- Hot chilli — 20 g
- Sweet paprika — 5 g
- Cherry tomatoes — 150 g
- Lemon juice — 25 ml
- Black peppercorns, cracked — 3 g
- Thyme sprigs — 4
- Lovage or parsley — 10 g

## Method
1. Score the skin, salt the fish with 10 g salt, rest 20 minutes; pat dry, oil lightly.
2. Grill over high heat (or plancha 240°C): skin-side 6–7 minutes to char and crisp, flesh-side 3 minutes.
3. Meanwhile boil water with remaining salt, crushed garlic, sliced chilli, paprika, cracked pepper, thyme and halved cherry tomatoes for 5 minutes.
4. Lay the grilled fish in a deep dish; pour the boiling brine over — it should hiss.
5. Add lemon juice; rest 5 minutes as the brine pulls char from the skin and becomes the sauce.
6. Scatter lovage.

## Plating & Service
Serve in the dish, brine and all, with mămăligă to drink up the liquid — spoons mandatory.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Works with sea bass, gurnard or mackerel at sea; the boiling-brine-over-char is the transferable trick.
