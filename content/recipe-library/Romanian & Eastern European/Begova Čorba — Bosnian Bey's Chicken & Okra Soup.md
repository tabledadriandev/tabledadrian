# Begova Čorba — Bosnian Bey's Chicken & Okra Soup

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sarajevo's velvety 'Bey's soup' — chicken and okra in a lemon-cream broth; the Ottoman thread in the Balkan tapestry.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Chicken thighs, bone-in — 700 g
- Dried baby okra (or fresh) — 60 g / 200 g
- Carrot — 120 g
- Parsnip — 80 g
- Celeriac — 60 g
- Onion — 100 g
- Butter — 30 g
- Sour cream or kajmak — 200 g
- Egg yolk — 1 (20 g)
- Lemon juice — 40 ml
- Salt — 12 g
- White pepper — 1 g
- Parsley — 10 g

## Method
1. Simmer chicken in 2.2 L water at 92°C, skimmed, 50 minutes. Strain; shred meat.
2. If using dried okra, soak in warm water with 10 ml lemon juice 30 minutes; rinse.
3. Sweat finely diced onion, carrot, parsnip and celeriac in butter 8 minutes without colour.
4. Add broth, shredded chicken and okra; simmer 20 minutes (fresh okra: 12).
5. Whisk sour cream with yolk, temper with hot broth, stir in below 80°C.
6. Season, finish with lemon juice and parsley — silky, gently sour, pale gold.

## Plating & Service
Serve in small bowls as an opener with lemon wedges; in Sarajevo it precedes every wedding meal.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Courgette batons stand in for okra with no soaking; keep the lemon-cream finish.
