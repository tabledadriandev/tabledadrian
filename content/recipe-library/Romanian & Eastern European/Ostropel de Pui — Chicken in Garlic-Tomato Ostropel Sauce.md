# Ostropel de Pui — Chicken in Garlic-Tomato Ostropel Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Oltenia's weeknight hero: bronzed chicken simmered in a garlic-loaded tomato sauce with a vinegar snap — the garlic goes in twice.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Chicken thighs and drumsticks, bone-in — 1.2 kg
- Sunflower oil — 40 ml
- Onion, diced — 150 g
- Garlic — 40 g
- Tomato passata — 500 g
- Dry white wine — 100 ml
- Red wine vinegar — 20 ml
- Sweet paprika — 6 g
- Dried thyme — 3 g
- Bay leaf — 1
- Salt — 12 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Parsley — 10 g

## Method
1. Season chicken; brown skin-side first in oil over medium-high heat, 8 minutes total. Set aside.
2. Soften onion in the same pan 6 minutes; add half the garlic, crushed, for 1 minute.
3. Add paprika off the heat, then passata, wine, thyme, bay, salt and pepper.
4. Return chicken, skin up; simmer, partly covered, 35 minutes until tender and the sauce has thickened to coat.
5. Stir in remaining garlic (crushed) and the vinegar; 3 final minutes — the raw-garlic finish is the ostropel signature.
6. Rest 5 minutes; scatter parsley.

## Plating & Service
Serve over mămăligă or with crusty bread; the sauce is the star, so be generous with it.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Duck legs (add 20 minutes) are magnificent here; meatball ostropel is the school-canteen classic Romanians secretly crave.
