# Varză Roșie Călită — Braised Red Cabbage with Apple & Caraway

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Winter's glossy purple side: red cabbage collapsed slowly with apple, red wine and caraway — the duck and pork whisperer.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Red cabbage, cored, shredded — 1 kg
- Onion, sliced — 150 g
- Tart apples, grated — 250 g
- Sunflower oil or duck fat — 40 g
- Dry red wine — 150 ml
- Red wine vinegar — 40 ml
- Honey or sugar — 25 g
- Caraway seeds — 3 g
- Bay leaf — 1
- Salt — 11 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Soften onion in the fat over medium heat, 6 minutes.
2. Add cabbage in handfuls, turning to coat and wilt; 8 minutes.
3. Add apple, wine, vinegar, honey, caraway, bay, salt.
4. Cover; braise at the gentlest simmer 45 minutes, stirring occasionally, until yielding but not mushy.
5. Uncover for 5 final minutes to gloss and reduce.
6. Balance: it should ring sweet-sour in equal measure; adjust vinegar or honey.

## Plating & Service
A deep-purple mound beside duck, goose, pork or sausages; it reheats better than it debuts.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> A grated raw potato in the last 10 minutes binds the juices, Transylvanian style; star anise instead of caraway drifts it festive.
