# Zamă de Găină — Moldovan Sour Chicken Soup with Homemade Noodles

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Moldova's cure-all: free-range chicken broth with hand-cut noodles, soured with borș and perfumed with lovage — thinner and gentler than Romanian ciorbă.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Chicken legs and wings — 1 kg
- Plain flour — 150 g
- Egg — 1 (55 g)
- Onion — 120 g
- Carrot — 150 g
- Parsnip — 80 g
- Fermented borș — 400 ml
- Lovage — 12 g
- Parsley — 10 g
- Salt — 13 g
- Black peppercorns — 3 g
- Sour cream, to serve — 120 g

## Method
1. Simmer chicken in 2.6 L water at 92°C, skimmed, with peppercorns for 50 minutes.
2. Noodles: knead flour, egg and 2 g salt (add water by the teaspoon if needed) 5 minutes; rest 20 minutes. Roll to 1.5 mm, flour, roll up and slice into 3 mm ribbons.
3. Add diced onion, carrot and parsnip to the broth; simmer 15 minutes.
4. Remove chicken; strip meat into pieces and return.
5. Boil borș separately, add to the pot, season.
6. Drop in noodles; cook 4 minutes. Finish with lovage and parsley off the heat.

## Plating & Service
Country bowls with sour cream and green chilli; traditionally served to guests as the first act of any Moldovan feast.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Zamă must stay light — do not thicken. GF: rice noodles added for the final 3 minutes.
