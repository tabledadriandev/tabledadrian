# Sarajevo Meat Burek with Onion & Black Pepper

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> In Sarajevo only the meat one earns the name burek — a coiled pie of hand-stretched dough and peppery beef-onion filling, brushed with pan juices.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Strong white flour — 500 g
- Warm water — 300 ml
- Salt — 12 g
- Sunflower oil — 130 ml
- Beef chuck, coarsely minced — 600 g
- Onions, finely diced — 300 g
- Black pepper, coarsely ground — 4 g
- Sweet paprika — 3 g
- Beef stock — 100 ml

## Method
1. Make and rest the stretched dough exactly as for cheese burek (three oiled balls, 60 minutes).
2. Filling: mix raw minced beef with onion, 8 g salt, pepper and paprika — raw filling steams inside the coil, keeping it juicy.
3. Stretch each ball tissue-thin on an oiled cloth; oil the surface, lay a third of the filling in a crumbled line and roll into a rope with the cloth.
4. Coil into an oiled 30 cm tin, snail-fashion.
5. Bake at 210°C for 40–45 minutes until bronzed.
6. Boil the stock and brush or drizzle it over the hot burek; rest covered 10 minutes so the juices redistribute.

## Plating & Service
Wedges straight from the tin with kefir or plain yoghurt; the crackle-then-juice contrast is the point.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Lamb mince with 2 g cumin nods to the Ottoman root; day-old burek revives perfectly in a 180°C oven for 8 minutes.
