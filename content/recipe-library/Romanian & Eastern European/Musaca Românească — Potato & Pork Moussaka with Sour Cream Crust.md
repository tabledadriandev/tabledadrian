# Musaca Românească — Potato & Pork Moussaka with Sour Cream Crust

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Romanian musaca is potato-based, tomato-sweet and crowned with a sour cream-egg custard — comfort engineering across three neat layers.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Potatoes, waxy — 1.2 kg
- Pork and beef mince, mixed — 700 g
- Onions, diced — 200 g
- Sunflower oil — 60 ml
- Tomato passata — 300 g
- Garlic — 15 g
- Sweet paprika — 6 g
- Dried thyme — 3 g
- Salt — 14 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Sour cream — 250 g
- Eggs — 2 (110 g)
- Parsley — 10 g
- Tomatoes, sliced — 200 g

## Method
1. Slice potatoes 7 mm; par-fry in batches in oil until blond, 4 minutes per batch (or toss with oil and roast at 220°C for 15 minutes). Season.
2. Brown mince with onion, 10 minutes; add garlic, paprika, thyme, 200 g passata, salt, pepper; simmer 10 minutes to a thick ragù. Fold in half the parsley.
3. Layer an oiled 28 cm dish: potatoes, all the meat, remaining potatoes; pour remaining passata over.
4. Bake at 180°C, covered, 30 minutes.
5. Whisk sour cream with eggs and a pinch of salt; pour over, add tomato slices, and bake uncovered 25 minutes until the custard is golden and set.
6. Rest 15 minutes before cutting.

## Plating & Service
Square portions showing the strata, remaining parsley over, a sharp tomato salad alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Courgette or aubergine can replace half the potato; lamb mince with cumin drifts it toward the Balkan musaka.
