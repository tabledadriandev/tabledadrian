# Crap Prăjit cu Mujdei și Mămăligă — Fried Carp, Garlic Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Danube trinity on one plate: cornmeal-crusted carp fried crisp, pounded raw-garlic mujdei, and hot mămăligă — Friday food across half of Romania.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Carp fillets, skin on, pin-boned — 4 × 180 g
- Coarse cornmeal (for crust) — 100 g
- Salt — 12 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Sunflower oil — 200 ml
- Garlic — 40 g
- Cold water or fish stock — 80 ml
- Sunflower oil (mujdei) — 40 ml
- Lemon — 1
- Coarse cornmeal (mămăligă) — 250 g
- Water — 1 L
- Parsley — 8 g

## Method
1. Mămăligă: rain 250 g cornmeal into 1 L boiling salted water, whisk, then stir over low heat 25 minutes until it pulls from the pot; rest covered.
2. Mujdei: pound garlic with 4 g salt to a paste; work in 40 ml oil drop by drop, then loosen with cold water to a pourable, fiercely garlicky sauce.
3. Score the carp skin; season, and press the flesh side into cornmeal for a thin crust.
4. Shallow-fry in 180°C oil: skin-side 5 minutes until deeply crisp, flip 2–3 minutes; the cornmeal should crackle.
5. Drain on a rack; salt hot.
6. Slice mămăligă with a string or wet knife.

## Plating & Service
Fillet over a slab of mămăligă, mujdei spooned boldly across, lemon and parsley — eat immediately, crust audible.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Any firm freshwater or coastal fish suits; mujdei with sour cream folded in is the softer Moldovan road.
