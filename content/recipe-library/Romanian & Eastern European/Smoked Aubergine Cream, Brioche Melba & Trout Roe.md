# Smoked Aubergine Cream, Brioche Melba & Trout Roe

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Salată de vinete for the tasting menu: an ethereal smoked aubergine cream siphon-light, shards of brioche melba, and trout roe standing in for the tomato's acidity-sweetness.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Aubergines — 1.2 kg
- Whipping cream — 150 ml
- Sunflower oil — 60 ml
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Salt — 8 g
- Brioche loaf — 200 g
- Butter, melted — 40 g
- Trout roe — 60 g
- Pickled shallot rings — 40 g
- Chive oil — 20 ml
- White pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Char aubergines over flame until collapsed and profoundly smoky; peel, drain 45 minutes, then blend with oil, lemon, salt and pepper until silk-smooth; pass through a fine tamis.
2. Fold in cream whipped to soft peaks (or load the base with cream into a siphon, 2 charges, chill 2 hours).
3. Freeze the brioche 30 minutes; slice 2 mm on a meat slicer, brush with butter, and bake between two trays at 160°C for 8–10 minutes to glassy gold melba shards.
4. Warm nothing — this dish is served cool, not cold: 12–14°C for the cream.
5. Check seasoning at serving temperature.

## Plating & Service
A cloud of aubergine cream in a shallow bowl, trout roe scattered like dew, pickled shallot rings, chive oil drops, and melba shards standing vertically.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: omit roe and cream, aerate the base with 60 ml aquafaba in the siphon; finish with capers for the saline pop.
