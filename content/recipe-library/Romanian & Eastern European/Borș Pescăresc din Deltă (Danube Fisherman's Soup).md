# Borș Pescăresc din Deltă (Danube Fisherman's Soup)

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Delta fishermen boil the broth from small fish, then poach prime cuts in it — served with mujdei-spiked broth on the side.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Small whole freshwater fish (perch, roach), gutted — 800 g
- Carp or pike-perch steaks — 700 g
- Onion — 150 g
- Carrot — 150 g
- Celeriac — 80 g
- Tomatoes, grated — 300 g
- Green chilli — 20 g
- Fermented borș — 500 ml
- Garlic — 40 g
- Salt — 15 g
- Lovage and parsley — 15 g
- Black peppercorns — 3 g
- Sunflower oil — 15 ml

## Method
1. Simmer the small fish with onion, carrot, celeriac and peppercorns in 2.5 L water at 95°C for 30 minutes.
2. Strain firmly through a fine sieve, pressing; discard solids. You want ~2.2 L intense broth.
3. Return broth to the pot with grated tomato and sliced chilli; simmer 10 minutes.
4. Boil borș separately and add; season with salt.
5. Lower in the carp steaks; poach at 88°C for 10–12 minutes until just flaking.
6. Crush garlic with salt and oil into mujdei, loosen with 100 ml hot broth.
7. Finish soup with lovage and parsley.

## Plating & Service
Tradition: fish lifted onto a plate with mujdei, broth served in bowls alongside — do both and let guests assemble.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Sea-friendly Riviera version: gurnard and scorpion fish frames for the broth, monkfish cheeks poached to serve.
