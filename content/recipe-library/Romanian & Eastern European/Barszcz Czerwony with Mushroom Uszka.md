# Barszcz Czerwony with Mushroom Uszka

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Poland's Christmas Eve jewel — clear fermented beet broth with tiny wild-mushroom 'ear' dumplings; make the beet ferment 5 days ahead.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Beetroot — 900 g (600 g for kwas, 300 g for broth)
- Water for kwas — 1 L
- Garlic — 30 g
- Rye bread crust — 30 g
- Dried porcini — 30 g
- Carrot — 100 g
- Celeriac — 80 g
- Onion — 120 g
- Allspice berries — 4
- Bay leaves — 2
- Plain flour — 200 g
- Egg — 1 (55 g)
- Shallot — 60 g
- Butter — 20 g
- Salt — 12 g
- Sugar — 5 g
- Marjoram — 2 g

## Method
1. Kwas (day −5): slice 600 g beets into a jar with garlic, bread crust and 1 L cooled boiled water + 8 g salt; weight down, cover, ferment 5 days at 20–22°C. Strain.
2. Soak porcini in 300 ml warm water 30 minutes; reserve liquor.
3. Simmer 300 g sliced beets, carrot, celeriac, onion, allspice and bay in 1.2 L water + porcini liquor for 40 minutes at 92°C; strain.
4. Uszka filling: chop soaked porcini finely, sauté with minced shallot in butter 6 minutes, season, cool.
5. Dough: knead flour, egg, 60 ml water and 2 g salt for 6 minutes; rest 30 minutes. Roll to 1 mm, cut 5 cm squares, fill with 3 g each, fold into triangles and pinch the corners together.
6. Boil uszka in salted water 2 minutes after they float.
7. Combine strained broth with 350–450 ml kwas to taste; season with salt, sugar, marjoram. Heat to 85°C — never boil, or the colour and sourness die.

## Plating & Service
Five uszka per shallow bowl, glass-clear crimson broth poured over, one marjoram pinch.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Quick kwas cheat: 40 ml beet juice + 25 ml apple cider vinegar. Vegan: bind uszka dough with water only.
