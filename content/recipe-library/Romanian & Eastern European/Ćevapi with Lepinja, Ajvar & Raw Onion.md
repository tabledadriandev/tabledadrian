# Ćevapi with Lepinja, Ajvar & Raw Onion

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Balkan grill's ten little kings: seasoned beef-lamb fingers, rested overnight for their signature bounce, stuffed into steam-softened lepinja with ajvar.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Beef chuck, 20% fat, minced twice — 700 g
- Lamb shoulder, minced — 300 g
- Garlic, very finely grated — 15 g
- Bicarbonate of soda — 3 g
- Sparkling water, ice-cold — 80 ml
- Salt — 16 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Sweet paprika — 4 g
- Lepinja or somun flatbreads — 6
- Ajvar — 200 g
- White onion, fine dice — 150 g

## Method
1. Knead meats with salt 3 minutes until tacky and paste-like.
2. Add garlic, pepper, paprika, bicarbonate and sparkling water in two additions; knead 2 minutes more.
3. Rest covered 12–24 hours in the fridge — the rest builds the springy bite that defines ćevapi.
4. Roll finger-size pieces: 30 g, 7 cm long. (A piping bag with a 2 cm nozzle is the cheat.)
5. Grill over high heat 8–9 minutes total, turning to mark all sides, to 72°C core.
6. Halve the lepinja, steam 30 seconds over the grill (or brush with meat juices and warm).

## Plating & Service
Ten ćevapi tucked into the bread pocket with a snowbank of raw onion and a generous smear of ajvar — kajmak on the side if dairy's welcome.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> All-beef is Sarajevo standard; do not add breadcrumbs, egg or herbs — ćevapi purity is enforceable by law in three countries.
