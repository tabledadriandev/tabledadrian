# Salată de Boeuf — Celebration Salad in a Vegetable Mosaic

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Romania's every-holiday centrepiece: diced beef, root vegetables and pickles bound in homemade mayonnaise, decorated like a mosaic — nostalgia engineering.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Beef brisket or veal — 400 g
- Potatoes — 400 g
- Carrots — 250 g
- Parsnip — 100 g
- Peas, cooked — 150 g
- Cornichons (castraveciori) — 200 g
- Pickled red pepper (gogoșar) — 150 g
- Egg yolks, for mayonnaise — 3 (60 g)
- Sunflower oil — 300 ml
- Dijon mustard — 20 g
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Salt — 12 g
- Black olives, to decorate — 40 g

## Method
1. Simmer beef in salted water at 90°C until fork-tender, 60–90 minutes; cool in the broth.
2. Boil potatoes, carrots and parsnip whole in skins until just tender; cool completely (warm vegetables make gluey salad).
3. Dice everything — beef, vegetables, cornichons, most of the gogoșar — into neat 6–8 mm cubes; fold with peas in a large bowl.
4. Mayonnaise: whisk yolks with mustard and a pinch of salt; add oil drop by drop then in a thread; season with lemon.
5. Fold three-quarters of the mayonnaise through the salad; season and taste — it needs the pickles' acidity to sing.
6. Shape into a dome or terrine, mask with remaining mayonnaise, and decorate with olive and gogoșar mosaics. Chill 2 hours minimum.

## Plating & Service
Present whole so guests see the decoration, then portion; serve cold with crusty bread.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken breast is the lighter standard; 'de post' fasting version binds with soaked-bread-and-oil vegan mayo.
