# Pierogi with Braised Beef & Wild Mushroom

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Meat pierogi worth the hours: shin braised to collapse, ground with porcini and caramelised onion, sauced with the reduced braise itself.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Beef shin — 700 g
- Dried porcini — 25 g
- Onions — 250 g
- Carrot — 100 g
- Beef stock — 700 ml
- Butter — 80 g
- Plain flour — 500 g
- Egg — 1 (55 g)
- Warm water — 220 ml
- Salt — 14 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Marjoram — 3 g
- Sour cream — 150 g
- Parsley — 10 g

## Method
1. Brown the shin; braise with carrot, 100 g onion, soaked porcini (liquor included) and stock, covered, at 160°C for 2.5 hours until collapsing.
2. Caramelise remaining onion in 30 g butter, 15 minutes.
3. Shred beef; pulse with porcini, caramelised onion, marjoram, 150 ml reduced braising liquid, salt and pepper to a moist, spoonable farce. Chill.
4. Dough: knead flour, egg, water and 6 g salt, 8 minutes; rest 30 minutes. Roll 2 mm, cut 8 cm rounds.
5. Fill (22 g), seal with a crimped rope edge; boil in salted water 2 minutes past floating.
6. Reduce remaining braise to a glossy jus; foam in 50 g butter.

## Plating & Service
Six per plate, jus spooned over, a streak of sour cream, parsley; serve pan-crisped for texture on the second night.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Duck confit + porcini is the luxe variant; the farce freezes, so batch it on prep day.
