# Lángos with Garlic Butter, Sour Cream & Cheese

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Budapest market-hall royalty: pillow-soft fried bread painted with garlic and buried under sour cream and cheese — irresistible beach-day charter food.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Strong white flour — 500 g
- Milk, lukewarm — 200 ml
- Water, lukewarm — 100 ml
- Fresh yeast — 15 g (or 5 g dry)
- Sugar — 5 g
- Salt — 10 g
- Potato, boiled and riced — 100 g
- Sunflower oil, for frying — 1 L
- Garlic — 30 g
- Butter, melted — 50 g
- Sour cream — 300 g
- Semi-hard cheese (trappista or gruyère), grated — 200 g

## Method
1. Dissolve yeast and sugar in the milk; rest 10 minutes until foamy.
2. Knead flour, riced potato, salt, yeast milk and water 8 minutes to a soft, slightly sticky dough; prove covered at 26°C for 60 minutes to double.
3. Divide into 6; ball, rest 15 minutes on oiled trays.
4. Heat oil to 175°C. Stretch each ball by hand into a 20 cm disc, slightly thinner in the middle.
5. Fry one at a time: 90 seconds per side to puffed deep gold; drain on a rack.
6. Stir crushed garlic into melted butter; brush each hot lángos generously.

## Plating & Service
Serve immediately, painted with garlic butter, a thick layer of sour cream and a snowfall of cheese — eaten by hand, folded, dripping.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The potato in the dough is the softness secret; toppings go anywhere — ajvar and feta, or gravlax and dill crème for yacht service.
