# Đuveč Rice — Serbian Baked Vegetable Rice

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Balkan grill-house's essential bed: rice baked with peppers, tomato and paprika until every grain is stained sunset-orange.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Long-grain rice — 300 g
- Red bell peppers, diced — 250 g
- Onion, diced — 150 g
- Carrot, small dice — 100 g
- Peas — 100 g
- Tomatoes, grated, or passata — 300 g
- Sunflower oil — 50 ml
- Sweet paprika — 10 g
- Hot paprika — 2 g
- Vegetable stock, hot — 650 ml
- Salt — 11 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Parsley — 8 g

## Method
1. Soften onion in oil in an ovenproof sauté pan, 6 minutes; add peppers and carrot, 5 minutes.
2. Off heat stir in both paprikas, then rice, coating every grain, 1 minute back on the heat.
3. Add tomato, hot stock, salt and pepper; stir once only.
4. Scatter peas; cover and bake at 180°C for 25 minutes.
5. Rest, covered, 10 minutes — no peeking, no stirring.
6. Fork through gently; parsley over.

## Plating & Service
A generous spoon under or beside ćevapi, pljeskavica and anything charred — it drinks meat juices gratefully.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 150 g smoked sausage before baking for a standalone supper; the leftover đuveč fries into excellent crispy rice.
