# Somlói Galuska — Hungarian Trifle of Three Sponges

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Hungary's gloriously excessive restaurant trifle: plain, walnut and cocoa sponges soaked in rum syrup, layered with vanilla custard, raisins, walnuts, chocolate sauce and cream.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Eggs, separated — 6 (330 g)
- Caster sugar — 260 g
- Plain flour — 150 g
- Ground walnuts — 40 g
- Cocoa powder — 30 g
- Whole milk — 500 ml
- Egg yolks (custard) — 4 (80 g)
- Cornflour — 30 g
- Vanilla pod — 1
- Water — 200 ml
- Dark rum — 60 ml
- Orange zest — 4 g
- Raisins — 80 g
- Walnuts, chopped — 80 g
- Dark chocolate 60% — 150 g
- Whipping cream — 400 ml

## Method
1. One sponge batter, three ways: whip yolks with 100 g sugar; whip whites with 60 g sugar; fold together with flour. Divide in three — fold walnuts into one third, cocoa into another. Bake each in a lined 20 cm frame at 180°C, 10–12 minutes.
2. Custard: heat milk with the split vanilla pod; whisk yolks, 80 g sugar and cornflour; temper, thicken at 84°C, press film to the surface, cool.
3. Syrup: boil water with 20 g sugar and zest; cool, add rum; soak the raisins in a spoonful.
4. Chocolate sauce: melt chocolate with 100 ml cream to a pourable glaze.
5. Build in a glass dish or individual coupes: plain sponge, syrup, custard, raisins and walnuts, cocoa sponge, syrup, custard, walnut sponge, syrup. Chill 4 hours.
6. Serve: scoop rough galuska (dumplings) of the trifle, whipped cream (300 ml, soft peaks) over, warm chocolate sauce poured last.

## Plating & Service
Served in coupes, chocolate sauce flowing down the cream — architectural neatness is against the spirit of the dish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> It must be built a day ahead; the scooped, slightly collapsed serving style is authentic Gundel-era presentation.
