# Mizeria — Polish Cucumber & Sour Cream Salad

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The coolest thing on a Polish table: paper-thin cucumbers, briefly salted, folded in sour cream with dill and a lemon lift — schabowy's eternal partner.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Cucumbers — 600 g
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Sour cream — 180 g
- Lemon juice or white vinegar — 15 ml
- Sugar — 5 g
- Fresh dill — 12 g
- Chives — 6 g
- White pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Slice cucumbers 1–2 mm on a mandoline (peel if the skin is tough).
2. Salt, toss, and drain in a colander 20 minutes; press gently — this is the crunch step.
3. Whisk sour cream with lemon, sugar and white pepper.
4. Fold in cucumbers, dill and chives just before serving.
5. Chill 10 minutes; serve the same hour — mizeria waits for no one.

## Plating & Service
A small cold bowl beside anything breaded, fried or paprika-sauced; it resets the palate between bites.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Kefir or Greek yoghurt lighten it; a grated garlic clove turns it toward the Balkan tarator family.
