# Chifteluțe cu Usturoi — Crisp Garlic-Herb Meatball Fritters

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Flattened, garlicky pork-and-potato meatballs with a shattering crust — served hot or cold, they vanish faster than anything else on a Romanian table.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Pork shoulder, minced — 500 g
- Potato, raw, finely grated and squeezed — 150 g
- Onion, grated — 80 g
- Garlic — 20 g
- Eggs — 1 (55 g)
- Dill, chopped — 8 g
- Parsley, chopped — 8 g
- Dried thyme — 2 g
- Salt — 10 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Plain flour, for dredging — 60 g
- Sunflower oil, for shallow frying — 250 ml

## Method
1. Squeeze grated potato and onion firmly dry in a cloth.
2. Knead pork with potato, onion, crushed garlic, egg, herbs, thyme, salt and pepper for 2 minutes until cohesive; rest 15 minutes in the fridge.
3. Shape into 50 g balls, flatten to 2 cm patties.
4. Dredge lightly in flour, shaking off excess.
5. Shallow-fry in 170°C oil, 3–4 minutes per side to deep golden with a 72°C core.
6. Drain on a rack, salt lightly while hot.

## Plating & Service
Pile on a platter with mujdei or mustard and bread-and-butter pickles; equally good cold in tomorrow's sandwich.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Bake at 200°C for 22 minutes for a lighter version; 50/50 pork-beef gives firmer bite. GF: rice flour dredge.
