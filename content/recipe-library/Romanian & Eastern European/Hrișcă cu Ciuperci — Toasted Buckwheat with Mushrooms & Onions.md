# Hrișcă cu Ciuperci — Toasted Buckwheat with Mushrooms & Onions

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Kasha as Eastern Europe actually eats it: toasted buckwheat steamed to separate grains, folded with browned mushrooms and sweet onions.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Toasted buckwheat groats (kasha) — 300 g
- Water — 540 ml
- Chestnut mushrooms, sliced — 300 g
- Onions, diced — 200 g
- Sunflower oil — 50 ml
- Garlic — 10 g
- Salt — 10 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Dill or parsley — 10 g
- Butter (optional) — 20 g

## Method
1. Rinse groats; toast dry in the pan 3 minutes until nutty.
2. Add water (1:1.8) and 6 g salt; boil, then cover on the lowest heat 15 minutes. Rest 10 minutes off heat, untouched.
3. Meanwhile brown mushrooms in half the oil over high heat, undisturbed in a single layer, 6 minutes; season.
4. Soften onions in remaining oil, 10 minutes to gold; add garlic 1 minute.
5. Fork the buckwheat loose; fold in mushrooms, onions and optional butter.
6. Herbs through; check seasoning.

## Plating & Service
Steaming beside cutlets and stews, or under a fried egg as the classic solo supper.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken stock instead of water deepens it; a spoon of sour cream on top is the Ukrainian table default.
