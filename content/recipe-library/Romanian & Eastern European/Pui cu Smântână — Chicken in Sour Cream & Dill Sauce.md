# Pui cu Smântână — Chicken in Sour Cream & Dill Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Grandmother's white sauce chicken: gentle, dill-flecked, sour-cream-silky — the dish Romanians order when they need to feel looked after.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Chicken thighs, bone-in — 1.2 kg
- Butter — 40 g
- Onion, finely diced — 150 g
- Garlic — 15 g
- Chicken stock — 300 ml
- Sour cream 24% — 400 g
- Cornflour — 12 g
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Fresh dill — 20 g
- Salt — 12 g
- White pepper — 2 g
- Sweet paprika — 3 g

## Method
1. Season and brown chicken in butter, skin-side first, 8 minutes; set aside.
2. Sweat onion in the same pan 6 minutes; add garlic 1 minute, then paprika off the heat.
3. Add stock; return chicken skin-up, cover and simmer gently 30 minutes.
4. Slake cornflour with 30 ml cold water into the sour cream; temper with pan liquid.
5. Stir the cream into the pan; simmer below boiling 5 minutes until the sauce naps a spoon.
6. Finish with lemon, salt, white pepper and masses of chopped dill.

## Plating & Service
With mămăligă or buttered noodles, extra dill over; the pale sauce wants a colourful pickle alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Rabbit legs are the old-country version; Greek yoghurt (stabilised with the cornflour) lightens it without losing the tang.
