# Plăcinte cu Brânză și Mărar — Moldovan Cheese & Dill Pies

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Moldova's griddled pies: thin stretched dough folded around salty cheese and dill, cooked on the plancha until leopard-spotted — eaten warm, in hand.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Plain flour — 500 g
- Warm water — 260 ml
- Sunflower oil — 60 ml
- Salt — 9 g
- Telemea or feta — 300 g
- Twaróg or ricotta — 200 g
- Egg — 1 (55 g)
- Fresh dill — 20 g
- Spring onion — 60 g
- Butter, melted — 40 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Sour cream, to serve — 200 g

## Method
1. Knead flour, water, 30 ml oil and salt 8 minutes to a soft dough; divide into 6, oil, rest 45 minutes.
2. Filling: crumble cheeses with egg, dill, sliced spring onion and pepper — salt only if the telemea allows.
3. Roll then hand-stretch each ball on an oiled surface to a thin 30 cm round.
4. Centre 90 g filling; fold the edges over in pleats to a flat square parcel, pressing gently.
5. Cook on a dry plancha or heavy pan at medium heat, 5–6 minutes per side, until spotted deep brown and the dough cooks through.
6. Brush hot pies with melted butter; rest 3 minutes under a cloth.

## Plating & Service
Halved diagonally, steam escaping, sour cream for dipping — best eaten standing in the galley, honestly.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Potato-onion and sour-cherry fillings complete the Moldovan trilogy; leftover pies revive on the plancha in 2 minutes.
