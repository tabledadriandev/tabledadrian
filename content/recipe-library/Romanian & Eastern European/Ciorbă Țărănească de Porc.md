# Ciorbă Țărănească de Porc

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Peasant-style pork ciorbă crowded with root vegetables — hearty, honest, and better the next day.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Pork shoulder, diced 2.5 cm — 600 g
- Onion — 150 g
- Carrot — 150 g
- Parsnip — 100 g
- Celeriac — 80 g
- Potato — 250 g
- Red bell pepper — 100 g
- Green beans — 100 g
- Tomatoes, grated — 250 g
- Fermented borș — 400 ml
- Lovage — 10 g
- Salt — 13 g
- Sunflower oil — 20 ml
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Sear pork in oil in a 5 L pot until light gold, 6 minutes; add 2.3 L water, bring to 95°C and skim.
2. Simmer 50 minutes, then add diced onion, carrot, parsnip and celeriac for 15 minutes.
3. Add potato, pepper and beans; cook 15 minutes.
4. Stir in grated tomato; simmer 10 minutes.
5. Boil borș separately, add, season, and simmer 5 final minutes.
6. Finish off-heat with chopped lovage and rest 10 minutes.

## Plating & Service
Big rustic bowls, raw green chilli and country bread alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Turkey thigh works for a lighter pot; a spoon of sour cream per bowl if dairy is welcome.
