# Șnițel de Pui cu Salată de Cartofi

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Romanian party șnițel — garlic worked into the egg wash — with a mustardy warm potato salad; simple, but every detail matters.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chicken breasts — 2 large (600 g)
- Plain flour — 80 g
- Eggs — 3 (165 g)
- Garlic — 15 g
- Fine breadcrumbs — 180 g
- Sunflower oil — 300 ml
- Salt — 12 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Waxy potatoes — 800 g
- Red onion, fine dice — 80 g
- Dijon or Romanian mustard — 25 g
- White wine vinegar — 30 ml
- Cold-pressed sunflower oil — 50 ml
- Parsley — 10 g

## Method
1. Butterfly and gently pound chicken to 1 cm; season both sides.
2. Whisk eggs with crushed garlic and a pinch of salt — this garlic egg wash is the Romanian signature.
3. Bread: flour, garlicky egg, crumbs, pressing lightly; rest 10 minutes so the crust sets.
4. Boil potatoes in skins until tender, 20 minutes; peel warm, slice thick.
5. Dress warm potatoes with mustard, vinegar, cold-pressed oil, onion, salt and parsley — warm potatoes drink the dressing.
6. Shallow-fry șnițel at 170°C, 3–4 minutes per side to deep gold, 72°C core; drain on a rack, salt while hot.

## Plating & Service
Șnițel overlapping the potato salad, lemon wedge, extra mustard — the cold-salad-hot-crust contrast is everything.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pork loin is the picnic classic; GF with cornflake or GF crumbs. The salad only improves after an hour.
