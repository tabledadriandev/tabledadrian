# Pork Cheek Sarmale, Smoked Cabbage Jus & Mămăligă Espuma

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sarmale for the tasting menu: one perfect roll of braised pork cheek in fermented cabbage, glazed with a smoked cabbage jus, over an airy mămăligă espuma.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Pork cheeks — 800 g
- Fermented sour cabbage leaves, large — 12
- Onion, diced — 150 g
- Smoked bacon — 80 g
- Chicken stock — 800 ml
- Dry white wine — 150 ml
- Tomato paste — 30 g
- Sweet paprika — 6 g
- Fine cornmeal — 120 g
- Whole milk — 350 ml
- Butter — 60 g
- Whipping cream — 150 ml
- Sauerkraut juice — 100 ml
- Salt — 10 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Thyme — 4 sprigs

## Method
1. Braise pork cheeks with onion, bacon, wine, tomato paste, paprika, thyme and 600 ml stock, covered, at 150°C for 3 hours until gelatinous. Shred; fold with enough reduced braise to bind richly.
2. Trim 12 sour cabbage leaves to neat squares; roll 60 g cheek filling in each into tight, identical cylinders. Wrap sets of film-rolled sarmale; steam 20 minutes at 90°C to set. Hold.
3. Jus: char 200 g cabbage trim on the plancha until blackened at the edges; simmer with remaining braising liquid 20 minutes; strain, reduce to a glossy jus, finish with 20 g butter and a teaspoon of sauerkraut juice.
4. Espuma: cook cornmeal in milk and 200 ml stock 25 minutes; blend with cream and 40 g butter, pass through a chinois, season, load into a warm siphon (2 charges), hold at 60°C.
5. To serve: glaze sarmale in the jus over low heat, rolling to lacquer, 4 minutes.

## Plating & Service
A cushion of espuma, two lacquered sarmale on top, jus pulled around the plate; one fried sage-size cabbage crisp for height.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The espuma without a siphon becomes a soft mămăligă cream — whip in the cream at the end. Beef short rib replaces cheek 1:1.
