# Ajvar — Fire-Roasted Pepper & Aubergine Relish

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Balkans' red gold: roasted red peppers milled and slow-fried in oil until jammy — the obligatory partner to ćevapi and everything grilled.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Red peppers (roga or romano) — 2 kg
- Aubergine — 400 g
- Sunflower oil — 150 ml
- White wine vinegar — 25 ml
- Salt — 15 g
- Sugar — 8 g
- Hot chilli (optional) — 15 g

## Method
1. Roast peppers and aubergine at 250°C (or over flame) until blackened all over, about 20 minutes, turning once.
2. Steam covered 15 minutes; peel and deseed peppers, peel aubergine. Drain both in a colander 1 hour — dry flesh is what makes ajvar, not soup.
3. Pass through a food mill on the coarse plate (or pulse-chop; never purée smooth).
4. Heat oil in a wide heavy pan; add the pulp and fry at a gentle sputter, stirring every 3–4 minutes with a wooden spoon, 50–70 minutes, until thick, brick-red and the oil weeps at the edges.
5. Season with salt, sugar and vinegar (and minced chilli for ljuti ajvar); cook 5 final minutes.
6. Jar hot into sterilised jars; it matures beautifully over a week.

## Plating & Service
Serve in a shallow bowl at room temperature with grilled meats, or simply with warm bread and white cheese.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pure-pepper ajvar omits aubergine for a sweeter, brighter jar; roasting over wood fire is the flavour ceiling.
