# Ciorbă de Văcuță with Summer Vegetables

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The everyday beef ciorbă of Romanian homes — light, vegetable-packed and lemon-sharp; ideal light lunch on deck.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Beef shin, diced 2 cm — 600 g
- Onion — 120 g
- Carrot — 150 g
- Celeriac — 80 g
- Red bell pepper — 100 g
- Green beans — 120 g
- Potato — 200 g
- Tomatoes, grated — 250 g
- Peas — 80 g
- Lemon juice — 50 ml
- Fresh lovage — 10 g
- Parsley — 10 g
- Salt — 13 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Cover beef with 2.5 L cold water, bring slowly to 95°C, skim thoroughly, and simmer 75 minutes.
2. Add diced onion, carrot and celeriac; simmer 15 minutes.
3. Add potato, pepper and green beans; simmer 15 minutes more.
4. Stir in grated tomato and peas; cook 10 minutes and season.
5. Sharpen with lemon juice off the heat; finish with chopped lovage and parsley.

## Plating & Service
Ladle into wide bowls making sure every serving gets beef and a colourful mix of vegetables.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Sour with fermented borș (400 ml, replacing that much water) for deeper authenticity; add a spoon of sour cream table-side if dairy is welcome.
