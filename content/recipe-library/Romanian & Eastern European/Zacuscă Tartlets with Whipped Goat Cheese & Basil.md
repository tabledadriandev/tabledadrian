# Zacuscă Tartlets with Whipped Goat Cheese & Basil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Autumn's jar meets canapé hour: crisp buckwheat-rye tartlets, a layer of reduced zacuscă, and a piped cloud of whipped goat cheese.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Plain flour — 150 g
- Buckwheat flour — 50 g
- Cold butter — 100 g
- Ice water — 40 ml
- Salt — 4 g
- Zacuscă (see house recipe), reduced — 300 g
- Fresh goat cheese — 200 g
- Whipping cream — 80 ml
- Honey — 8 g
- Lemon zest — 2 g
- Small basil leaves — 12
- Espelette pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Rub flours, salt and butter to coarse sand; bind with ice water, press flat, chill 30 minutes.
2. Roll to 2.5 mm; line eight 7 cm tartlet rings, dock, freeze 15 minutes.
3. Blind-bake at 170°C with baking beans 15 minutes, then 8 minutes uncovered to even gold; cool.
4. Dry the zacuscă further in a pan over medium heat, 8–10 minutes, until paste-thick (wet zacuscă means soggy pastry).
5. Whip goat cheese with cream, honey, lemon zest and a pinch of salt to piping consistency.
6. Assemble to order: 30 g zacuscă per shell, goat cheese piped in peaks.

## Plating & Service
Three peaks of goat cheese per tartlet, one basil leaf per peak, a breath of Espelette; serve within 30 minutes of filling.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Full-vegan build: cashew-miso cream instead of goat cheese; the tart shells hold 3 days airtight, so only assembly is à la minute.
