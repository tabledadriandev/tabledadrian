# Cartofi Țărănești — Peasant Potatoes with Smoked Paprika & Onions

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Skillet potatoes the Romanian village way: par-steamed, then crusted in a pan with slow onions, smoked paprika and dill.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Waxy potatoes — 1 kg
- Onions, sliced — 250 g
- Sunflower oil — 60 ml
- Smoked paprika — 6 g
- Sweet paprika — 4 g
- Garlic — 15 g
- Salt — 12 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Fresh dill — 10 g
- Butter (optional) — 20 g

## Method
1. Steam or boil potatoes in skins until just tender, 18 minutes; cool 10 minutes, peel if you like, cut into thick wedges.
2. Soften the onions in half the oil over medium heat, 12 minutes, to pale gold; set aside.
3. Raise the heat; crust the potatoes in remaining oil in a single layer, undisturbed 4 minutes per face, until bronzed.
4. Return onions with crushed garlic; toss 2 minutes.
5. Off the heat: both paprikas (they scorch fast), salt, pepper, optional butter.
6. Dill through at the last second.

## Plating & Service
Straight from the skillet beside șnițel, sausages or fried eggs — the corners and crusty bits go to whoever plates.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> A splash of pickle brine at the end is a cook's secret; leftover boiled potatoes from yesterday make the best version.
