# Green Borscht with Sorrel & Six-Minute Egg

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Spring's sour answer to red borscht — sorrel wilted into chicken broth with potato, capped with a jammy egg.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Chicken thighs, bone-in — 600 g
- Sorrel leaves — 250 g
- Potato — 300 g
- Carrot — 100 g
- Onion — 120 g
- Spring onions — 40 g
- Eggs — 6
- Sour cream — 180 g
- Salt — 12 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Dill — 10 g
- Sunflower oil — 15 ml

## Method
1. Simmer chicken in 2.2 L water at 92°C, skimmed, for 45 minutes; strain, shred meat, return meat to broth.
2. Sweat diced onion and grated carrot in oil 5 minutes; add to the pot with diced potato; simmer 12 minutes.
3. Boil eggs 6 minutes 30 seconds from boiling water; refresh in ice, peel, halve.
4. Chiffonade sorrel; stir into the soup and cook only 3 minutes — it turns khaki, that is correct and the flavour is the point.
5. Season; sorrel is sour so taste before adding any acid.
6. Finish with sliced spring onion and dill.

## Plating & Service
One halved egg per bowl, yolk facing up, sour cream alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No sorrel? Spinach plus 35 ml lemon juice mimics it; young nettles (blanched) are the foragers' version.
