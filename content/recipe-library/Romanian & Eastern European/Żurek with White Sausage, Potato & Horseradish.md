# Żurek with White Sausage, Potato & Horseradish

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Poland's fermented rye soup — sour, smoky, creamy — ladled over white sausage and egg; the starter (zakwas) needs 4 days, so plan ahead.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Rye flour (for zakwas) — 100 g
- Water for zakwas — 400 ml
- Garlic — 25 g
- Fresh white sausage (biała kiełbasa) — 500 g
- Smoked bacon — 150 g
- Potato — 400 g
- Onion — 120 g
- Chicken or pork stock — 1.6 L
- Dried marjoram — 4 g
- Fresh horseradish, grated — 20 g
- Double cream — 150 ml
- Eggs, boiled 8 min — 4
- Bay leaf and allspice berries — 2 + 4
- Salt — 10 g

## Method
1. Zakwas (day −4): whisk rye flour with lukewarm water in a jar, add 2 crushed garlic cloves and a bay leaf; cover with muslin, keep at 22–25°C for 4 days, stirring daily, until pleasantly sour.
2. Simmer sausages in stock with bay and allspice at 85°C for 15 minutes; remove, slice thick.
3. Render diced bacon; sweat diced onion in the fat 5 minutes; add to the stock with diced potato; simmer 15 minutes.
4. Stir the zakwas well and pour in 300–400 ml through a sieve; simmer 10 minutes — taste as you go, it should be assertively sour.
5. Add cream, marjoram, grated horseradish and crushed remaining garlic; season and return sausage slices to warm through.
6. Rest 5 minutes; the soup should coat a spoon lightly.

## Plating & Service
Serve in deep bowls (or hollowed bread loaves for rustic drama) with egg halves and extra marjoram.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Shortcut zakwas: 60 ml live rye kvass concentrate or buttermilk + 20 ml lemon. GF option: ferment buckwheat flour instead of rye.
