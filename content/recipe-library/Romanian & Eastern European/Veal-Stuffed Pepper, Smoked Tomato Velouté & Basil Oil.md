# Veal-Stuffed Pepper, Smoked Tomato Velouté & Basil Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Ardei umpluți with a saucier's hand: a single roasted romano pepper packed with veal and dill, floating on a smoked tomato velouté with basil oil.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Romano peppers — 4 large
- Veal shoulder, minced — 450 g
- Cooked short-grain rice — 80 g
- Shallot, minced — 80 g
- Butter — 30 g
- Fresh dill — 12 g
- Salt — 11 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Ripe tomatoes — 700 g
- Smoked paprika — 3 g
- Whipping cream — 100 ml
- Basil — 25 g
- Grapeseed oil — 80 ml
- Sugar — 6 g
- Sherry vinegar — 8 ml
- Sour cream — 80 g

## Method
1. Basil oil: blitz basil with oil at 60°C, 4 minutes; hang through muslin; chill.
2. Velouté: halve tomatoes, roast at 200°C for 30 minutes with sugar and a pinch of salt; blend with smoked paprika and cream, pass, balance with vinegar; hold at 70°C.
3. Soften shallot in butter; mix with veal, rice, dill, 8 g salt, pepper.
4. Slit peppers lengthwise, deseed through the slit, keep stems on; pipe in the filling snugly.
5. Roast slit-up at 180°C for 35–40 minutes to a 72°C core, skins blistering.
6. Rest 5 minutes; loosen sour cream to drizzling consistency.

## Plating & Service
Velouté mirror, the whole pepper laid across it, sour cream thread, basil oil pearls at the rim — one pepper, one plate, zero clutter.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Lamb-mint filling drifts it Balkan; the velouté doubles as a superb sauce for the classic chiftele.
