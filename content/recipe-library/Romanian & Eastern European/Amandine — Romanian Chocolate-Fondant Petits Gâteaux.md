# Amandine — Romanian Chocolate-Fondant Petits Gâteaux

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The cocoa-glazed confectionery-shop cube of every Romanian childhood: rum-soaked chocolate sponge, chocolate-amandine cream, fondant top.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Eggs — 6 (330 g)
- Caster sugar — 220 g
- Plain flour — 150 g
- Cocoa powder — 45 g
- Sunflower oil — 40 ml
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Butter, soft — 200 g
- Icing sugar — 100 g
- Dark chocolate 55%, melted — 120 g
- Ground almonds — 40 g
- Water — 150 ml
- Dark rum — 40 ml
- Fondant or glaze: sugar — 150 g
- Cocoa (glaze) — 25 g
- Cream (glaze) — 80 ml
- Almond halves — 8

## Method
1. Sponge: whisk eggs with 180 g sugar to full ribbon, 8 minutes; fold in sieved flour, cocoa and salt, then oil. Bake in a lined 24 cm square at 170°C for 25–28 minutes; cool, split into two layers.
2. Syrup: boil water with 40 g sugar; cool, add rum.
3. Cream: beat butter with icing sugar 4 minutes; beat in cooled melted chocolate and ground almonds.
4. Soak both sponge layers generously with syrup — amandine must verge on wet.
5. Layer: sponge, all the cream, sponge; press lightly, chill 2 hours.
6. Glaze: warm cream with sugar and cocoa to a smooth pourable glaze (or use softened fondant); spread over the top, let it set.
7. Trim edges hot-knifed; cut 8 precise cubes, wiping the blade between cuts.

## Plating & Service
Each cube crowned with an almond half, served on small plates with espresso — cofetărie nostalgia, executed cleanly.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> A teaspoon of instant coffee in the syrup is the modern lift; the cubes hold three days chilled and taste best on day two.
