# White Bean Velouté, Crispy Ham Hock & Tarragon Vinegar

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Ciorbă de fasole cu ciolan, plated: silk-smooth bean velouté, a croustillant of pulled hock, and the tarragon-vinegar top note as a gastrique.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Dried white beans (coco or cannellini), soaked — 300 g
- Smoked ham hock — 700 g
- Onion — 120 g
- Carrot — 100 g
- Bay leaves — 2
- Butter — 40 g
- Whipping cream — 100 ml
- White wine vinegar — 60 ml
- Sugar — 20 g
- Fresh tarragon — 10 g
- Salt — 8 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Grapeseed oil — 20 ml

## Method
1. Simmer hock with bay in 2 L water for 2 hours until falling apart; strain, keep 800 ml broth. Pull meat, discard skin and bone.
2. Cook drained beans in the hock broth with onion and carrot for 60–75 minutes until collapsing.
3. Discard carrot; blend beans with enough broth to a pourable velvet, add cream and butter, blend again, pass through a chinois. Season.
4. Gastrique: reduce vinegar and sugar to a light syrup (about 3 minutes); off heat add chopped tarragon.
5. Press pulled hock between parchment into a 1 cm slab; chill, cut into 6 batons, crisp all sides in oil over medium-high heat.
6. Reheat velouté to 80°C, loosening with broth to a just-pourable consistency.

## Plating & Service
One hock baton off-centre in each bowl, velouté poured around, gastrique zig-zagged over the surface, one tarragon leaf.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: smoked paprika + miso replaces the hock, olive oil for butter — the gastrique stays.
