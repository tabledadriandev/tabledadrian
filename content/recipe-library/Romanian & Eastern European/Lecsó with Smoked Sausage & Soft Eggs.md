# Lecsó with Smoked Sausage & Soft Eggs

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Hungary's pepper-tomato stew — summer in a pan — beefed up with smoked sausage and eggs folded through at the end; breakfast, lunch or midnight food.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Hungarian wax peppers (or mixed sweet peppers) — 700 g
- Ripe tomatoes — 500 g
- Onions, sliced — 200 g
- Smoked sausage (debreceni or kolbász) — 250 g
- Lard or sunflower oil — 40 g
- Sweet Hungarian paprika — 12 g
- Sugar — 5 g
- Salt — 10 g
- Eggs — 4
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Render sliced sausage in lard 4 minutes; set aside, keeping the paprika-stained fat.
2. Soften onion in the fat 8 minutes; add peppers sliced into 1 cm rings, 10 minutes.
3. Off heat stir in paprika; add chopped tomatoes, sugar, salt.
4. Simmer uncovered 15–20 minutes until saucy but not collapsed — peppers should keep gentle bite.
5. Return sausage; crack eggs into hollows (or stir them through for the creamy version).
6. Cover 4 minutes until whites set, yolks soft.

## Plating & Service
Straight from the pan with crusty white bread; paprika oil pooling at the edges is the mark of a proper lecsó.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian: skip sausage, finish with 100 g grated cheese; jarred lecsó base is Hungary's pantry gold — make double and preserve.
