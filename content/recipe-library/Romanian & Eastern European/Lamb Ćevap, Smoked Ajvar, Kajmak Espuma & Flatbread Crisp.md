# Lamb Ćevap, Smoked Ajvar, Kajmak Espuma & Flatbread Crisp

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Ćevapi's flavour triangle — char, red pepper, cream — rebuilt with plated precision: a single long lamb ćevap, smoked ajvar, and kajmak as a warm cloud.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Lamb shoulder, 20% fat, minced twice — 600 g
- Beef chuck, minced — 200 g
- Garlic — 15 g
- Bicarbonate of soda — 3 g
- Ice-cold sparkling water — 60 ml
- Salt — 13 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Ajvar — 250 g
- Smoked paprika — 2 g
- Kajmak or mascarpone + feta (2:1) — 180 g
- Whole milk — 60 ml
- Lepinja or pita, split thin — 2
- Butter, melted — 30 g
- Pickled red onion — 60 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 6 g

## Method
1. Knead meats with salt to a paste; add garlic, pepper, bicarbonate and sparkling water; rest 24 hours.
2. Shape four 25 cm-long ćevapi (150 g each) around flat skewers; chill.
3. Smoke the ajvar under cloche 4 minutes (or warm with smoked paprika); hold at 55°C.
4. Kajmak espuma: warm kajmak with milk to 55°C, blend, pass, siphon with 1 charge (or whip loosely); keep warm.
5. Brush split bread with butter; bake at 180°C for 8 minutes between trays into flat crisps.
6. Grill the ćevapi over high heat, turning, 8–9 minutes to 72°C with confident char.

## Plating & Service
A stripe of smoked ajvar the length of the plate, the ćevap on top, espuma piped at one end, flatbread crisp leaning, pickled onion and parsley — street flavour, gala posture.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The same build serves as a canapé with 30 g mini-ćevapi; beef-only keeps it halal-friendly with beef kajmak alternatives.
