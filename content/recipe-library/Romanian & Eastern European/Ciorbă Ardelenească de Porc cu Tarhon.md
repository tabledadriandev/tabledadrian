# Ciorbă Ardelenească de Porc cu Tarhon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Transylvanian pork soup where fresh tarragon and cream replace lovage and borș — a Hungarian-Saxon border child.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Pork shoulder, diced — 600 g
- Onion — 120 g
- Carrot — 150 g
- Celeriac — 80 g
- Potato — 250 g
- Sour cream — 250 g
- Plain flour — 15 g
- Fresh tarragon — 12 g
- White wine vinegar — 45 ml
- Salt — 13 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Sunflower oil — 20 ml
- Sweet paprika — 4 g

## Method
1. Sear pork in oil, 6 minutes; add 2.2 L water, bring to 95°C and skim. Simmer 50 minutes.
2. Add diced onion, carrot, celeriac; simmer 15 minutes. Add potato; 15 minutes more.
3. Slake flour with sour cream and 100 ml cold water until smooth.
4. Temper the cream mix with hot broth, stir into the soup and simmer 5 minutes to cook out — keep below a boil after adding.
5. Season with salt, pepper and paprika; sharpen with vinegar.
6. Finish generously with chopped tarragon off the heat.

## Plating & Service
Serve with extra tarragon leaves and a swirl of sour cream.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cornflour keeps it gluten-free; beef chuck or smoked tofu both take the tarragon-cream treatment well.
