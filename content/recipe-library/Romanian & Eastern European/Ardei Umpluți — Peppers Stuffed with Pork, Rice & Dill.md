# Ardei Umpluți — Peppers Stuffed with Pork, Rice & Dill

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Summer's sarmale: sweet peppers filled with dill-scented pork and rice, simmered in tomato sauce and served with cold sour cream against the heat.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Bell peppers or Romanian white peppers — 6 large (900 g)
- Pork shoulder, minced — 600 g
- Short-grain rice — 80 g
- Onion, grated — 150 g
- Carrot, grated — 80 g
- Sunflower oil — 40 ml
- Tomato passata — 600 g
- Sugar — 8 g
- Fresh dill — 15 g
- Dried thyme — 2 g
- Salt — 14 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Sour cream, to serve — 200 g
- Tomato slices (lids) — 6

## Method
1. Cut lids off peppers, deseed; keep cavities intact.
2. Sweat onion and carrot in oil 8 minutes; cool. Mix with pork, rice, dill, thyme, 10 g salt and pepper.
3. Fill peppers loosely to 1 cm below the rim — rice swells. Cap each with a tomato slice.
4. Stand peppers snugly in a deep pot; pour passata mixed with 400 ml water, sugar and remaining salt around them.
5. Simmer, covered, at a gentle blip 60–70 minutes (or bake at 170°C), until rice is cooked and peppers sag softly.
6. Rest 15 minutes in the sauce.

## Plating & Service
One pepper per bowl, generous sauce, a cold spoon of sour cream on top and dill over — the hot-cold contrast is the dish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: mushroom-rice-walnut filling from the fasting sarmale works perfectly here; tomato-braised all the same.
