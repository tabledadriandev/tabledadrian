# Ciulama de Ciuperci — Wild Mushrooms in Velvet Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Romania's white velouté stew: mixed mushrooms folded into a nutmeg-scented cream sauce, always over mămăligă — monastic food with French bones.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Mixed mushrooms (chestnut, oyster, porcini) — 700 g
- Butter — 60 g
- Onion, fine dice — 120 g
- Garlic — 10 g
- Plain flour — 40 g
- Whole milk — 400 ml
- Vegetable stock — 200 ml
- Sour cream — 150 g
- Nutmeg, grated — 0.5 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Salt — 10 g
- White pepper — 2 g
- Parsley — 8 g
- Mămăligă, to serve — 600 g

## Method
1. Tear or slice mushrooms; sauté in 30 g butter over high heat in two batches until golden and dry, 6 minutes each. Season lightly.
2. Soften onion in remaining butter, 6 minutes; add garlic 1 minute.
3. Add flour; cook the roux 2 minutes without colour.
4. Whisk in milk then stock gradually; simmer 8 minutes to a smooth, coating sauce.
5. Fold in mushrooms and sour cream; warm below a boil 5 minutes. Season with nutmeg, salt, white pepper, lemon.
6. Rest 5 minutes; parsley through.

## Plating & Service
A ladle over soft mămăligă, the sauce settling into the polenta — pale, silky, quietly luxurious.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken ciulama (poached, shredded) is the meaty sibling; GF with rice-flour roux. A few drops of truffle oil are anachronistic but welcome.
