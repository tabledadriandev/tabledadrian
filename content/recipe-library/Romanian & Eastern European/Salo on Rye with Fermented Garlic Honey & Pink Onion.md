# Salo on Rye with Fermented Garlic Honey & Pink Onion

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Ukraine's beloved cured pork fat, shaved translucent over hot rye toast — the garlic-honey glaze bridges tradition and the modern table.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Cured salo or lardo, well chilled — 200 g
- Dark rye bread — 6 slices (300 g)
- Garlic — 15 g
- Honey — 30 g
- Apple cider vinegar — 10 ml
- Red onion — 80 g
- Ice water — 200 ml
- Dill fronds — 6 g
- Black pepper, coarse — 2 g
- Flaky salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Slice red onion into fine rings; crisp in ice water 15 minutes, drain and pat dry.
2. Warm honey to 50°C with grated garlic and vinegar; infuse 10 minutes off heat, strain.
3. Toast rye slices in a dry pan or at 220°C until the edges char slightly, 4–5 minutes.
4. Shave the chilled salo at 1 mm on a slicer or with a long knife — it must be translucent.
5. Brush hot toast lightly with the garlic honey; drape salo immediately so it turns pearly at the edges.

## Plating & Service
Halve the toasts, top with onion rings, dill, pepper and a few salt flakes; serve while the bread is still warm enough to just soften the salo.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Lardo di Colonnata is the Riviera-legal stand-in; for a smoked accent, flash the salo with a smoking gun under cloche at the pass.
