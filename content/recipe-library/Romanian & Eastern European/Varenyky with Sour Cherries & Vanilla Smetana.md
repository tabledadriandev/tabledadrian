# Varenyky with Sour Cherries & Vanilla Smetana

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Summer-kitchen Ukraine: tender dumplings bursting with sugared sour cherries, dressed in melted butter and vanilla-sweetened smetana.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Plain flour — 450 g
- Kefir or buttermilk, lukewarm — 250 ml
- Bicarbonate of soda — 3 g
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Sunflower oil — 15 ml
- Sour cherries, pitted (fresh or frozen) — 600 g
- Caster sugar — 140 g
- Cornflour — 15 g
- Butter, melted — 60 g
- Sour cream (smetana) — 250 g
- Vanilla — 5 ml
- Icing sugar — 20 g

## Method
1. Toss cherries with 100 g sugar; drain 30 minutes (keep the juice); toss the fruit with cornflour — the anti-burst measure.
2. Kefir dough: mix flour, kefir, bicarbonate, salt and oil to a soft dough; knead 4 minutes; rest 30 minutes covered — kefir dough stays cloud-tender.
3. Roll to 2 mm; cut 8 cm rounds. Fill with 4–5 cherries each; seal into half-moons with a firm rope crimp.
4. Boil in batches in barely simmering salted water; lift 90 seconds after floating — a hard boil bursts them.
5. Toss gently with melted butter on a warm platter.
6. Reduce the reserved cherry juice with remaining sugar to a light syrup, 4 minutes; whisk vanilla and icing sugar into the smetana.

## Plating & Service
Six per bowl, cherry syrup zig-zagged, vanilla smetana in a generous cool spoonful — eat before they eat each other.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Frozen cherries straight from the freezer are actually easier to seal; blueberries or plums follow the same law of cornflour.
