# Papricaș de Pui cu Găluște — Chicken Paprikash with Dumplings

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Banat borderland's paprikash: chicken in a blushing paprika-sour cream sauce with pinched flour dumplings — Romania and Hungary agreeing for once.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Chicken thighs, bone-in — 1.2 kg
- Lard or sunflower oil — 40 g
- Onions, finely diced — 250 g
- Sweet Hungarian paprika — 18 g
- Tomato paste — 20 g
- Chicken stock — 400 ml
- Sour cream — 300 g
- Plain flour — 220 g (200 g dumplings + 20 g sauce)
- Eggs — 2 (110 g)
- Garlic — 10 g
- Salt — 15 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Parsley — 10 g

## Method
1. Brown seasoned chicken in lard, 8 minutes; set aside. Sweat onion in the fat 10 minutes to gold.
2. Off heat, stir in paprika and tomato paste; add stock and garlic, return chicken, simmer covered 35 minutes.
3. Dumplings (găluște): beat 200 g flour with eggs, 4 g salt and 60 ml water to a thick sticky batter; rest 10 minutes.
4. Push small nubs of batter off a wet spoon into boiling salted water; simmer 4 minutes after they rise, drain.
5. Slake 20 g flour into the sour cream; temper with sauce, stir into the pot, simmer below boiling 5 minutes.
6. Fold dumplings into the sauce; rest 5 minutes; parsley over.

## Plating & Service
Chicken over a nest of dumplings, blushing sauce covering everything, cold cucumber salad alongside for relief.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF: cornflour-based dumplings or serve on rice; the paprika must be fresh this year — stale paprika ruins paprikash.
