# Śledź w Oleju — Herring with Allium Three Ways & Apple

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Polish vodka-table herring elevated: matjes fillets in cold oil with raw, pickled and fried onion plus tart apple — briny, sweet, crunchy, essential.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Matjes herring fillets — 400 g
- Cold-pressed rapeseed oil — 150 ml
- Red onion — 80 g
- Shallots — 80 g
- White onion — 80 g
- White wine vinegar — 60 ml
- Sugar — 15 g
- Tart apple (Granny Smith) — 120 g
- Allspice berries, cracked — 3
- Bay leaf — 1
- Black pepper, coarse — 2 g
- Chives — 8 g

## Method
1. Soak herring in cold water 30–60 minutes if very salty; pat dry, cut into 3 cm pieces.
2. Quick-pickle red onion: rings in vinegar, sugar and 30 ml water, 30 minutes minimum.
3. Fry sliced shallots in 30 ml of the oil until deep gold and crisp, 6–8 minutes; drain.
4. Slice white onion paper-thin; julienne the apple at the last minute.
5. Layer herring with raw onion, apple, cracked allspice, bay and pepper in a jar or dish; cover with remaining oil. Marinate 2+ hours (overnight is better).
6. To serve, lift out with some oil, top with pickled onion and crisp shallots.

## Plating & Service
Serve cold on small plates with the three alliums distinct, chives over, dark rye alongside — and frozen vodka if the flag allows.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> A spoon of sour cream folded through 10 minutes before serving makes śledź w śmietanie; beetroot dice makes it a šuba nod.
