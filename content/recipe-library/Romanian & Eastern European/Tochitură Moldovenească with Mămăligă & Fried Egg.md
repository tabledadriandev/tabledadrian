# Tochitură Moldovenească with Mămăligă & Fried Egg

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Moldova's iron-pan feast: pork simmered then fried in its own fat with garlic and wine, served over mămăligă under a sunny egg and grated cheese.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Pork shoulder, diced 3 cm — 800 g
- Smoked sausage (cârnați afumați) — 200 g
- Pork lard or sunflower oil — 40 g
- Garlic — 30 g
- Dry white wine — 150 ml
- Sweet paprika — 6 g
- Dried thyme — 3 g
- Salt — 12 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Bay leaf — 1
- Eggs — 6
- Telemea or salted sheep's cheese, grated — 150 g
- Cornmeal (for mămăligă) — 300 g
- Water — 1.2 L

## Method
1. Simmer pork with bay in barely covering salted water until the water evaporates entirely, about 45 minutes — this tenderises before frying.
2. Add lard to the pot; fry the pork in its rendered fat over medium-high heat until bronzed, 10 minutes. Add sliced sausage for the last 5.
3. Deglaze with wine; add paprika, thyme, pepper and crushed garlic. Simmer 10 minutes to a glossy, scant sauce. Adjust salt.
4. Mămăligă: rain cornmeal into 1.2 L boiling salted water, whisking; cook 25 minutes over low heat, stirring, until it pulls from the pot.
5. Fry eggs in a little lard, basting, keeping yolks liquid.
6. Rest the tochitură 5 minutes; it should glisten, not swim.

## Plating & Service
A dome of mămăligă, tochitură beside, the fried egg on top, grated telemea snowing over everything — served in the pan for rustic drama if the table allows.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Beef-and-kidney versions exist; the non-negotiables are garlic, wine and the egg-cheese crown.
