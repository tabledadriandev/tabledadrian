# Venison Bigos with Prunes, Juniper & Dark Chocolate

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Hunter's bigos taken literally: venison shoulder and smoked sausage in slow-collapsed two-cabbage stew, deepened with prune, juniper and a closing shave of dark chocolate.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Venison shoulder, diced 3 cm — 700 g
- Smoked kiełbasa — 250 g
- Smoked bacon — 120 g
- Sauerkraut — 700 g
- White cabbage, shredded — 500 g
- Onions — 250 g
- Dried porcini — 25 g
- Prunes — 120 g
- Dry red wine — 250 ml
- Juniper berries, crushed — 8
- Bay leaves — 3
- Allspice — 4 berries
- Marjoram — 4 g
- Dark chocolate 70% — 15 g
- Salt — 10 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Sunflower oil — 30 ml

## Method
1. Soak porcini in 300 ml warm water. Render bacon; brown venison hard in batches in the fat with a little oil.
2. Soften onion in the pot; return meat with kraut (rinsed once), fresh cabbage, wine, porcini and liquor, juniper, bay, allspice and 300 ml water.
3. Simmer at the lowest blip, covered, 2.5 hours, stirring from the base occasionally.
4. Add sliced kiełbasa, prunes and marjoram; uncover and simmer 30–40 minutes to a thick, mahogany stew.
5. Finish: grate in the chocolate off the heat — depth, not sweetness; season.
6. Best rested overnight and reheated; skim before serving.

## Plating & Service
Deep bowls, a piece of everything in each ladle; dark rye and something Burgundian to drink.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Boar shoulder is the other honest hunter's route; chocolate can become 10 g plum butter (powidła) for the orthodox.
