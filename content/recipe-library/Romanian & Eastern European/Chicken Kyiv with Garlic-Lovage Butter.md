# Chicken Kyiv with Garlic-Lovage Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The classic done seriously — a whole breast around a frozen log of garlic butter, here perfumed with lovage as an Eastern European wink; the pour of butter at the cut is the show.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Chicken breasts, skin off, fillet attached — 4 × 200 g
- Butter, soft — 150 g
- Garlic — 20 g
- Fresh lovage or parsley — 12 g
- Lemon zest — 3 g
- Salt — 12 g
- White pepper — 2 g
- Plain flour — 80 g
- Eggs — 3 (165 g)
- Fine breadcrumbs — 220 g
- Sunflower oil, for frying — 700 ml

## Method
1. Beat butter with crushed garlic, chopped lovage, zest, 4 g salt; roll into 4 logs (35 g each) in film; freeze solid.
2. Butterfly each breast without cutting through; lay the inner fillet aside, and pound all to 6 mm between film.
3. Season; wrap each frozen butter log in the small fillet, then the breast around it, sealing completely. Wrap tightly in film into torpedoes; chill 30 minutes.
4. Double-bread: flour, egg, crumbs, egg, crumbs. Chill 15 minutes.
5. Fry at 165°C for 8 minutes, turning, then 180°C oven for 6–8 minutes to a 68°C core against the butter cavity.
6. Rest 3 minutes so the butter settles from boiling to flowing.

## Plating & Service
Whole on the plate with buttered peas or braised greens; the tableside cut and golden gush is mandatory theatre.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dill butter is the Odesa mood; keep every chilling step — warm butter and lazy seals are the only ways this dish fails.
