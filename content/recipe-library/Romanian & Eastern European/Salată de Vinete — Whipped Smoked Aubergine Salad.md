# Salată de Vinete — Whipped Smoked Aubergine Salad

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The purest expression of smoked aubergine: whipped with sunflower oil like a mayonnaise, sharpened with raw onion — every Romanian summer in one bite.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Aubergines — 1.5 kg
- Sunflower oil — 120 ml
- White onion, very finely diced — 80 g
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Salt — 10 g
- Egg yolk (optional, for sheen) — 1 (20 g)
- Tomatoes, for garnish — 150 g
- Flat-leaf parsley — 5 g

## Method
1. Char aubergines whole over flame until collapsed and deeply smoky, about 18 minutes, turning often.
2. Rest covered 10 minutes; peel meticulously and drain flesh in a tilted colander for 30–45 minutes — watery salată is the cardinal sin.
3. Chop the flesh to a fine pulp with a non-metal knife.
4. Whip with a wooden spoon (or paddle attachment, low speed), adding oil in a thin stream as for mayonnaise, with the yolk if using — 3–4 minutes until pale and glossy.
5. Season with salt and lemon; fold in the raw onion just before serving so it stays crunchy.

## Plating & Service
A generous quenelle on a chilled plate, tomato wedges and parsley alongside, thick bread for scooping.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan by simply omitting the yolk; garlic-lovers swap onion for 8 g crushed garlic (then it flirts with melitzanosalata).
