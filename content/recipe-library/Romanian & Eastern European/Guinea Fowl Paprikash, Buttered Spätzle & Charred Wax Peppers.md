# Guinea Fowl Paprikash, Buttered Spätzle & Charred Wax Peppers

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Paprikash refined without betrayal: guinea fowl supremes over hand-shaved spätzle, the sauce built on roasted carcass jus and finished with crème fraîche.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Guinea fowl supremes, skin on — 4 × 170 g
- Guinea fowl or chicken carcass jus — 400 ml
- Onion, fine dice — 150 g
- Butter — 90 g
- Sweet Hungarian paprika — 15 g
- Tomato paste — 20 g
- Crème fraîche — 150 g
- Dry white wine — 100 ml
- Plain flour — 250 g
- Eggs — 3 (165 g)
- Whole milk — 80 ml
- Hungarian wax peppers — 200 g
- Salt — 13 g
- White pepper — 2 g
- Chives — 8 g

## Method
1. Spätzle: beat flour, eggs, milk and 4 g salt to a thick elastic batter; rest 20 minutes. Scrape thin ribbons off a board into boiling salted water; lift when floating, shock, oil lightly.
2. Sauce: soften onion in 30 g butter, 10 minutes; off heat add paprika and tomato paste; deglaze with wine, add jus, simmer 15 minutes, blend, pass. Whisk in crème fraîche below a boil; season.
3. Char whole wax peppers on the plancha until blistered but still snappy; salt.
4. Roast the supremes: skin-down in 30 g butter over medium heat 6 minutes to crisp, flip, baste, rest to 62°C core.
5. Toss spätzle in remaining butter in a hot pan until edges catch gold, 3 minutes; chives through.
6. Nap the sauce onto warm plates.

## Plating & Service
Sauce mirror, spätzle nest, the supreme sliced once on the bias, charred pepper alongside — colour like a Budapest café ceiling.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken supremes are the honest weekday version; the sauce base (no cream) freezes, so bank it.
