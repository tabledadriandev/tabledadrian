# Ciorbă de Fasole cu Ciolan Afumat

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Smoked ham hock and creamy beans in a tarragon-vinegar broth — the soup Romanian mountain lodges are judged by.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Dried white beans, soaked overnight — 400 g
- Smoked ham hock (ciolan) — 900 g
- Onion — 150 g
- Carrot — 150 g
- Celeriac — 100 g
- Red bell pepper — 120 g
- Tomato passata — 200 g
- Sunflower oil — 30 ml
- Dried tarragon — 3 g
- White wine vinegar — 50 ml
- Bay leaves — 2
- Salt — 10 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Fresh parsley — 10 g

## Method
1. Cover the hock with 3 L cold water, bring to 95°C, skim, and simmer 60 minutes with bay leaves.
2. Drain the soaked beans, add to the pot and simmer 60–75 minutes more until tender.
3. Meanwhile sweat diced onion, carrot, celeriac and pepper in oil over medium heat for 8 minutes; stir in passata and cook 3 minutes.
4. Lift out the hock; pull the meat into chunks, discarding skin and bone, and return meat to the pot.
5. Add the vegetable base to the soup, simmer 15 minutes, then season — the hock is salty, so taste before adding salt.
6. Finish with tarragon and vinegar; rest 10 minutes and top with parsley.

## Plating & Service
Serve rustic in wide bowls with raw onion quarters and rye bread on the side (bread optional for GF service).

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan route: replace hock with 15 g smoked paprika and 40 g sun-dried tomatoes, using 30 g extra oil for body.
