# Drob de Miel — Herbed Lamb Terrine in Caul Fat

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Easter terrine of lamb offal and mountains of green herbs, wrapped in lacy caul fat with hidden boiled eggs — a slice is spring on a plate.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Lamb offal mix (liver, heart, lungs or shoulder) — 700 g
- Spring onions — 150 g
- Fresh dill — 30 g
- Fresh parsley — 30 g
- Fresh lovage — 10 g
- Eggs, raw — 3 (165 g)
- Eggs, boiled 8 min — 3
- Bread, white, soaked in milk — 80 g
- Garlic — 15 g
- Salt — 12 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Caul fat, soaked in vinegared water — 200 g
- Sunflower oil — 15 ml

## Method
1. Simmer offal in salted water 25 minutes; cool and chop finely by hand (a grinder makes it pasty — hand-chopped is the texture of a good drob).
2. Soften sliced spring onions in oil 4 minutes; cool.
3. Mix chopped offal with onions, all chopped herbs, squeezed bread, raw eggs, crushed garlic, salt and pepper.
4. Line a 25 cm loaf tin with caul fat, leaving overhang.
5. Pack in half the mixture, lay the peeled boiled eggs end-to-end down the centre, cover with the rest; fold the caul over.
6. Bake at 180°C, 60–70 minutes to a 74°C core and bronzed lace top.
7. Cool 20 minutes before slicing, or chill overnight for clean cold slices.

## Plating & Service
Thick slices showing the egg bullseye, with radishes, spring onions and horseradish cream.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> All-shoulder drob suits offal-shy guests; line the tin with blanched chard instead of caul for a lighter look.
