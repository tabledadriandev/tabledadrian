# Sfeclă cu Hrean — Beetroot & Horseradish Relish Salad

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The crimson jolt beside every Romanian roast and aspic: sweet baked beets grated with fresh horseradish, vinegar and a whisper of caraway.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Beetroot, whole, similar size — 800 g
- Fresh horseradish root — 60 g
- White wine vinegar — 60 ml
- Sunflower oil — 30 ml
- Sugar or honey — 12 g
- Salt — 8 g
- Caraway seeds, toasted (optional) — 2 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml

## Method
1. Bake beets whole in skins, wrapped in foil, at 190°C for 60–75 minutes until a knife slides through; the oven concentrates what boiling dilutes.
2. Cool until handleable; slip off the skins.
3. Grate coarsely (gloves save your manicure and your reputation).
4. Peel and finely grate the horseradish — work fast, near an open window, and mix immediately with the vinegar to fix its bite.
5. Fold beets with the horseradish-vinegar, oil, sugar, salt, caraway and lemon.
6. Mature 2 hours refrigerated; it improves for days.

## Plating & Service
A small vivid mound beside piftie, roast pork, sausages or fried fish; it cuts richness like a blade.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Polish ćwikła is this exact salad's twin; add grated apple for a softer Ukrainian accent. Keeps a week chilled.
