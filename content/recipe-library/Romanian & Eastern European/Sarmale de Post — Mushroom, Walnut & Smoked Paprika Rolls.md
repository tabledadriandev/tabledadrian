# Sarmale de Post — Mushroom, Walnut & Smoked Paprika Rolls

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Fasting-day sarmale that no one experiences as a compromise: caramelised mushrooms, walnuts and smoked paprika in sour cabbage — deeply savoury without a gram of meat.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Fermented sour cabbage leaves — 1 kg (about 20 leaves)
- Chestnut mushrooms — 600 g
- Walnuts, toasted, chopped — 100 g
- Short-grain rice — 120 g
- Onions — 250 g
- Carrot, grated — 100 g
- Sunflower oil — 60 ml
- Smoked paprika — 8 g
- Dried thyme — 3 g
- Tomato passata — 300 g
- Bay leaves — 2
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Dill, chopped — 10 g
- Shredded sauerkraut — 300 g

## Method
1. Chop mushrooms to a coarse rubble; sauté in 40 ml oil over high heat in batches until browned and dry, 8 minutes per batch.
2. Sweat diced onion and carrot in remaining oil 8 minutes; combine with mushrooms, rice, walnuts, paprika, thyme, pepper and dill.
3. Trim cabbage leaves; roll 55 g filling per leaf into tight parcels.
4. Layer the pot with shredded sauerkraut, sarmale in circles, bay leaves; pour passata and water to barely cover.
5. Weight with a plate; simmer at a bare blip 2 hours.
6. Rest 30 minutes; like all sarmale, they peak the next day.

## Plating & Service
Serve with mămăligă and a drizzle of the paprika-stained pot liquor; hot pickled peppers alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: replace walnuts with 80 g cooked buckwheat for the same earthy chew; a splash of soy sauce (not traditional) deepens the umami further.
