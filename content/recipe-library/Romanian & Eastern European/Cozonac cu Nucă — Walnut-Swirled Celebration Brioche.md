# Cozonac cu Nucă — Walnut-Swirled Celebration Brioche

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Christmas and Easter loaf on which every Romanian household is judged: a rich, slow-risen brioche twisted around dark walnut-cocoa filling.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Strong white flour — 600 g
- Whole milk, lukewarm — 250 ml
- Fresh yeast — 30 g (or 10 g dry)
- Caster sugar — 150 g
- Egg yolks — 4 (80 g)
- Butter, soft — 120 g
- Sunflower oil — 30 ml
- Fine salt — 8 g
- Vanilla — 8 ml
- Lemon zest — 6 g
- Rum — 15 ml
- Walnuts, ground — 300 g
- Cocoa powder — 25 g
- Milk (filling) — 80 ml
- Sugar (filling) — 100 g
- Egg white — 1 (30 g)
- Egg, for glazing — 1 (55 g)

## Method
1. Sponge: whisk yeast with 50 ml milk, 10 g sugar and 50 g flour; rest 20 minutes until bubbling.
2. Knead flour, remaining milk, sugar, yolks, sponge, salt, vanilla, zest and rum 10 minutes; add soft butter in stages, then oil, kneading until silky and pulling from the bowl — traditional cozonac is kneaded until the arm despairs, a mixer forgives.
3. Prove, covered, at 26–28°C for 90 minutes to double. Draughts are the enemy: cozonac superstition exists for thermodynamic reasons.
4. Filling: warm milk with sugar; stir into walnuts and cocoa; cool, then loosen with whipped egg white.
5. Divide dough in two; roll each to 40 × 30 cm, spread filling, roll into ropes. Twist the two ropes together; lay into a lined 30 cm loaf tin.
6. Prove 45 minutes; glaze with beaten egg.
7. Bake at 170°C for 45–50 minutes (94°C core); tent with foil after 25. Cool on its side, turning occasionally — the old trick against collapse.

## Plating & Service
Thick slices showing the walnut spiral, with coffee — or French-toasted tomorrow, if any survives.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Rahat (Turkish delight) cubes or raisins join the swirl in many houses; the loaf freezes whole, glazed and baked.
