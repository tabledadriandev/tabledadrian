# Mici — Grilled Garlic Beef Rolls with Bicarbonate Spring

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Romania's grill anthem: skinless beef-lamb rolls, springy from bicarbonate and broth, breathing garlic — the smell of every Romanian summer, built for the yacht plancha.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Beef chuck, 20% fat, minced twice — 700 g
- Lamb shoulder, minced — 200 g
- Pork fat back, minced (optional but classic) — 100 g
- Beef stock, ice cold — 120 ml
- Garlic — 30 g
- Bicarbonate of soda — 4 g
- Sweet paprika — 5 g
- Dried thyme — 2 g
- Dried savory — 1 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Salt — 14 g
- Mustard and crusty bread, to serve — 80 g

## Method
1. Blitz garlic with the cold stock; strain, pressing — you want garlic water, not pulp.
2. Knead all meats with salt for 3 minutes until sticky and paste-like (the bind comes from worked myosin, not egg).
3. Add bicarbonate, spices, and the garlic stock in three additions, kneading each in — the mix should be soft, almost mousse-like.
4. Rest, covered, 12–24 hours refrigerated: non-negotiable for the signature spring.
5. With wet hands (or a piping bag, 3 cm nozzle), shape 80 g rolls, 8 cm long.
6. Grill over high heat or plancha at 230°C: 3–4 minutes per side, turning once, to 72°C core — juicy, bouncy, charred in stripes.

## Plating & Service
Straight from the grill in threes, with sharp mustard, white bread and cold beer; never plate them fancy — mici are democracy.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> All-beef works; the lamb and fat back are the flavour insurance. Rest mixture two days for even better texture.
