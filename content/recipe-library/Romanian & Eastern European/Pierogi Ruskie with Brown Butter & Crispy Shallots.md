# Pierogi Ruskie with Brown Butter & Crispy Shallots

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The canonical potato-and-cheese pierogi: supple dough, tangy twaróg filling, brown butter and crisp shallots — comfort with technique behind it.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Plain flour — 500 g
- Warm water — 250 ml
- Sunflower oil — 20 ml
- Salt — 8 g
- Floury potatoes — 600 g
- Twaróg or ricotta + feta (2:1) — 300 g
- Onion, fine dice — 150 g
- Butter — 120 g
- Shallots, sliced — 100 g
- White pepper — 2 g
- Sour cream, to serve — 200 g
- Chives — 10 g

## Method
1. Dough: knead flour, warm water, oil and 4 g salt for 8 minutes until smooth and soft as an earlobe; rest under a bowl 30 minutes.
2. Filling: boil potatoes, rice them dry; brown the diced onion in 30 g butter; combine with crumbled twaróg, 4 g salt and white pepper. Cool.
3. Roll dough to 2 mm; cut 8 cm circles. Fill with 20 g each, fold, pinch a tight rope seam — no air pockets.
4. Boil in well-salted water; lift 90 seconds after they float. Drain on a tray, filmed with butter.
5. Crisp the shallots in 30 g butter until deep gold; lift out. Brown the remaining butter to noisette.
6. Optional finish: pan-fry boiled pierogi in the noisette 2 minutes per side for blistered edges.

## Plating & Service
Seven per plate, brown butter spooned over, crispy shallots and chives, sour cream on the side.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Freeze raw on trays and boil from frozen (+90 seconds); sauerkraut-mushroom filling is the Christmas Eve sibling.
