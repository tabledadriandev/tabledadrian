# Rață pe Varză Călită — Duck Legs on Braised Sauerkraut

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A winter Sunday in one pan: duck legs rendered crisp, braised until sliding off the bone over sauerkraut cooked in the duck fat with paprika and thyme.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Duck legs — 4 (about 1.2 kg)
- Sauerkraut — 1 kg
- Onions, sliced — 200 g
- Sweet paprika — 8 g
- Dried thyme — 4 g
- Bay leaves — 2
- Black peppercorns, cracked — 3 g
- Dry white wine — 150 ml
- Tomato passata — 100 g
- Salt — 6 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Score duck skin, season; start skin-down in a cold heavy casserole, render over medium-low 15 minutes until deeply golden. Set legs aside; keep 40 g fat in the pot.
2. Soften onion in duck fat 8 minutes. Rinse kraut lightly, squeeze, add with paprika, thyme, bay, cracked pepper; sauté 10 minutes.
3. Add wine and passata; nestle duck legs skin-up, kraut around not over them.
4. Braise uncovered at 160°C for 2 hours, until the skin is lacquered and the meat gives at a touch.
5. Skim excess fat; taste kraut — it should be mellow-sour, savoury, stained brick.
6. Rest 10 minutes.

## Plating & Service
One leg per plate on a mound of kraut, pan juices spooned over; boiled potatoes or mămăligă alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Goose legs for December feasts; add 60 g smoked bacon to the kraut for extra depth. The reheat next day is arguably better.
