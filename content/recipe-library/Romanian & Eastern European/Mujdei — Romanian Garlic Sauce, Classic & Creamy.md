# Mujdei — Romanian Garlic Sauce, Classic & Creamy

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Romania's fiercest condiment: raw garlic pounded with salt and beaten with oil and water — plus the mellow sour-cream variant for the cautious.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Garlic, hard and fresh — 60 g
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Sunflower oil — 80 ml
- Cold water or light stock — 100 ml
- Lemon juice or vinegar — 10 ml
- Sour cream (creamy variant, optional) — 100 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Peel and de-germ the garlic (the green core is the bitterness culprit).
2. Pound to an absolute paste with the salt in a mortar — 3 patient minutes; a blender bruises, a mortar persuades.
3. Beat in the oil drop by drop as for aioli until thick and pale.
4. Loosen with cold water to a pourable sauce; brighten with lemon and pepper.
5. For creamy mujdei, fold half the base into sour cream instead of water.
6. Rest 15 minutes; the heat rounds slightly. Use within a day — raw garlic waits for no one.

## Plating & Service
Spoon over grilled fish, fried carp, mămăligă, mici or roast chicken; a little bowl on the table disappears entirely.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Roast half the garlic first for a sweet, halved-strength mujdei; a spoon of tomato passata makes the red Oltenian version.
