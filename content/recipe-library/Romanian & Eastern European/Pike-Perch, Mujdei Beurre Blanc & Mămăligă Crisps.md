# Pike-Perch, Mujdei Beurre Blanc & Mămăligă Crisps

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Șalău — the Danube's noblest fish — with Romania's garlic sauce reborn as a beurre blanc, and mămăligă turned into golden glass.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Pike-perch fillets, skin on — 4 × 150 g
- Fine cornmeal — 100 g
- Water — 400 ml
- Salt — 9 g
- Garlic — 30 g
- Whole milk — 100 ml
- Dry white wine — 80 ml
- White wine vinegar — 20 ml
- Shallot, minced — 40 g
- Cold butter, diced — 150 g
- Sunflower oil — 40 ml
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Chervil or parsley — 6 g
- White pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Crisps: cook cornmeal in salted water 20 minutes; spread 3 mm on a mat, chill until set, cut into shards, and bake between trays at 170°C for 20–25 minutes to deep-gold glass.
2. Tame the garlic: simmer cloves in milk 10 minutes; drain, crush to a purée.
3. Beurre blanc: reduce wine, vinegar and shallot to 30 ml; whisk in cold butter cube by cube at 60–70°C; blend in the garlic purée; season with salt, white pepper, lemon. Hold at 50°C.
4. Dry the fillets; score the skin. Pan-roast skin-down in oil over medium-high, pressing flat first 30 seconds, 4 minutes to crisp; flip for 45 seconds to a 52°C core.
5. Rest 1 minute on a rack, skin up.

## Plating & Service
Fillet skin-up on a generous spoonful of mujdei beurre blanc, two mămăligă shards standing against it, chervil — the Delta on porcelain.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Sea bass or John Dory adapt seamlessly on the Riviera; the milk-simmered garlic keeps the sauce round, never raw-hot.
