# Gołąbki in Tomato Cream — Polish Cabbage Rolls

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Poland's 'little pigeons': sweet cabbage leaves around pork and rice, baked in a tomato-cream sauce — rounder and gentler than their sour Romanian cousins.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- White cabbage — 1 large head (1.5 kg)
- Pork shoulder, minced — 600 g
- Beef mince — 200 g
- Long-grain rice, par-cooked — 120 g
- Onion, diced — 200 g
- Butter — 50 g
- Tomato passata — 500 g
- Beef stock — 300 ml
- Double cream — 150 ml
- Marjoram — 4 g
- Salt — 14 g
- Black pepper — 3 g
- Bay leaf — 1
- Parsley — 10 g

## Method
1. Core the cabbage; submerge in boiling salted water and peel leaves off as they soften, 2 minutes each. Shave down thick ribs.
2. Soften onion in half the butter; mix with meats, rice, marjoram, salt and pepper.
3. Roll 80 g filling per leaf: sides in, rolled tight.
4. Line a casserole with torn leaves; pack rolls seam-down.
5. Whisk passata, stock, bay and a pinch of sugar; pour over. Cover; bake at 170°C for 75 minutes.
6. Lift rolls; blend cream into the sauce, reduce 5 minutes, season, return rolls for 10 final minutes.

## Plating & Service
Two rolls per plate under a generous blanket of the blush sauce, parsley over; mashed potatoes are the natural bed.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Buckwheat replaces rice in the eastern-Polish version; freeze cooked rolls in their sauce for effortless crew meals.
