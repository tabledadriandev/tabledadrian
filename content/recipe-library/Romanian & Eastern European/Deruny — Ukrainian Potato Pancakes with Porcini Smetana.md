# Deruny — Ukrainian Potato Pancakes with Porcini Smetana

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Lacy-edged potato pancakes with a wild mushroom-sour cream sauce — Ukrainian dacha food that plates like a bistro dish.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Floury potatoes — 800 g
- Onion — 120 g
- Eggs — 2 (110 g)
- Plain flour — 40 g
- Salt — 10 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Sunflower oil — 150 ml
- Dried porcini — 20 g
- Chestnut mushrooms, sliced — 250 g
- Butter — 30 g
- Sour cream (smetana) — 250 g
- Garlic — 10 g
- Dill — 10 g

## Method
1. Soak porcini in 200 ml warm water 30 minutes; chop, keep the liquor.
2. Sauce: sauté chestnut mushrooms in butter over high heat until browned, 6 minutes; add porcini, garlic and liquor, reduce by half; stir in sour cream, warm below a boil, season, add half the dill.
3. Grate potatoes and onion on the fine side; squeeze very dry in a cloth, keeping the settled potato starch from the liquid and adding it back.
4. Mix with eggs, flour, salt, pepper — a thick, drippy batter.
5. Fry heaped spoonfuls in 5 mm of 175°C oil, pressing thin: 3 minutes per side to deep gold and lacy edges. Drain on a rack.
6. Salt while hot; work in batches, holding at 100°C oven.

## Plating & Service
Three pancakes overlapping, porcini smetana spooned across the middle, dill over — edges must stay audible.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The onion in the batter stops browning-oxidation and adds perfume; GF with buckwheat flour. Apple-sauce and sugar is the sweet nursery version.
