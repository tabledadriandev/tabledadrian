# Salată de Varză Albă cu Mărar — White Cabbage & Dill Slaw

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The obligatory Romanian grill companion: finely shaved cabbage massaged with salt until silky, dressed only with vinegar, oil and dill.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- White cabbage — 800 g
- Fine salt — 10 g
- White wine vinegar — 40 ml
- Cold-pressed sunflower oil — 40 ml
- Sugar — 5 g
- Fresh dill — 15 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Carrot (optional colour) — 80 g

## Method
1. Shave the cabbage as finely as your knife or mandoline allows — thickness is the difference between slaw and salad.
2. Massage firmly with the salt for 2 full minutes until it glistens and softens; rest 15 minutes.
3. Squeeze gently, discarding only excess brine.
4. Dress with vinegar, sugar and oil; grate in carrot if using.
5. Fold through chopped dill and pepper; rest 10 minutes and taste — it should crunch, then melt.

## Plating & Service
A tall tangle beside mici, șnițel or anything fried; it cuts fat like a squeeze of lemon.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> A grated apple sweetens it for children; leftover slaw brightens tomorrow's sandwiches.
