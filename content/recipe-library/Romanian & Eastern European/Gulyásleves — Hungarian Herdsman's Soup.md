# Gulyásleves — Hungarian Herdsman's Soup

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The original goulash is this paprika-bronzed soup, not a stew — beef, peppers and pinched csipetke dumplings (leave them out for GF).

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Beef chuck, diced 2 cm — 700 g
- Onion — 250 g
- Lard or sunflower oil — 40 g
- Sweet Hungarian paprika — 25 g
- Hot paprika — 3 g
- Caraway seeds, ground — 3 g
- Garlic — 20 g
- Tomatoes — 200 g
- Hungarian wax peppers or green bell — 200 g
- Potato — 400 g
- Carrot — 150 g
- Salt — 14 g
- Water — 2.2 L
- Flat-leaf parsley — 10 g

## Method
1. Melt lard in a heavy 5 L pot; caramelise finely diced onion slowly, 15 minutes to deep gold.
2. Off the heat stir in both paprikas and caraway (paprika burns above 130°C); immediately add beef and 100 ml water.
3. Season, cover, and braise gently 60 minutes in its own juices, stirring, adding splashes of water as needed.
4. Add chopped tomato, sliced peppers, crushed garlic and the remaining water; simmer 30 minutes.
5. Add diced potato and carrot; simmer 25 minutes until beef and potato are tender.
6. Adjust salt; a soup-consistency broth, brick red with beads of paprika fat.

## Plating & Service
Serve in deep bowls with green wax pepper rings and crusty bread; csipetke (flour-egg pinched dumplings) simmered in for the classic finish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Csipetke: 100 g flour + 1 egg + salt, pinched into the soup for the final 5 minutes (adds gluten/egg allergens).
