# Papanași Fierți — Boiled Cheese Dumplings in Toasted Breadcrumb Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The quieter northern sibling of fried papanași: poached cheese dumplings rolled through sugared brown-butter breadcrumbs — nursery food elevated by browning.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Fresh cow's cheese or drained ricotta — 500 g
- Eggs — 2 (110 g)
- Fine semolina — 80 g
- Plain flour — 60 g
- Caster sugar — 50 g
- Lemon zest — 4 g
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Butter — 80 g
- Fine breadcrumbs — 120 g
- Icing sugar — 25 g
- Sour cream — 200 g
- Sour cherry jam (optional) — 150 g

## Method
1. Mix cheese, eggs, semolina, flour, 30 g sugar, zest and salt to a soft dough; rest 20 minutes (semolina must hydrate).
2. With wet hands roll 50 g dumplings.
3. Poach in barely simmering salted water 8–10 minutes after they rise; lift gently with a slotted spoon.
4. Brown the butter to noisette; toast breadcrumbs in it with remaining sugar 3–4 minutes to deep gold.
5. Roll the hot dumplings through the crumbs until completely coated.
6. Dust with icing sugar.

## Plating & Service
Three per bowl with cold sour cream and, if you like, a spoon of jam — humble-looking, disappears instantly.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cinnamon in the crumbs is the Bukovina touch; these reheat gently in the crumb pan, unlike their fried siblings.
