# Choux Puffs with Whipped Icre & Pickled Cucumber

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Salată de icre piped into crisp choux — a one-bite Black Sea éclair with cucumber pickle cutting the richness; a canapé that outworks its size.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Water — 125 ml
- Milk — 125 ml
- Butter — 100 g
- Plain flour — 150 g
- Eggs — 4 (220 g)
- Salt — 4 g
- Salted carp or pike roe — 100 g
- Sunflower oil — 220 ml
- Cooked fine semolina, cooled — 25 g
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Cucumber — 150 g
- White wine vinegar — 50 ml
- Sugar — 12 g
- Dill fronds — 6 g

## Method
1. Choux: boil water, milk, butter and salt; add flour off heat, beat, then dry the panade over medium heat 2 minutes. Cool to 60°C; beat in eggs gradually to a glossy V-drop consistency.
2. Pipe 3 cm domes on parchment; bake at 190°C for 25 minutes, then 160°C for 10 — do not open the oven early. Cool on a rack.
3. Whip the roe with cooled semolina, then oil in a thread, to a thick pale mousse; season with lemon. Chill in a piping bag.
4. Pickle: 1 mm cucumber coins in vinegar, sugar, a pinch of salt — 30 minutes.
5. To serve, pierce each puff from the base and fill generously with roe mousse (about 15 g).
6. Top with one cucumber coin and a dill frond, anchored with a dot of mousse.

## Plating & Service
Serve on a stone slab, six per plate for a canapé round, within 20 minutes of filling so the choux stays crisp.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Smoked cod roe deepens it; for gala service, crown with a pinch of ossetra instead of cucumber.
