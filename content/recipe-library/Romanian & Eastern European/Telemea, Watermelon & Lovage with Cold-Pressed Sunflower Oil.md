# Telemea, Watermelon & Lovage with Cold-Pressed Sunflower Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Dobrogea summer duo — salty sheep's telemea and iced watermelon — plated with lovage and toasted seeds; four ingredients doing the work of twenty.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Watermelon, seedless — 900 g
- Telemea or aged feta in brine — 250 g
- Fresh lovage leaves — 6 g
- Cold-pressed sunflower oil — 40 ml
- Lime juice — 15 ml
- Sunflower seeds, toasted — 30 g
- Black pepper, coarse — 1 g
- Watermelon pickle or pickled rind (optional) — 60 g

## Method
1. Chill watermelon to 4°C; cut into clean 3 cm cubes, discarding any pale flesh.
2. Slice telemea into 5 mm shards; if very salty, rinse briefly and pat dry.
3. Toast sunflower seeds in a dry pan 3 minutes until fragrant.
4. Dress melon cubes with lime juice and a pinch of pepper just before plating.
5. Tear lovage at the last second — it bruises fast and its celery-anise perfume is the dish's signature.

## Plating & Service
Alternate melon cubes and telemea shards, scatter seeds and torn lovage, finish with a confident pour of sunflower oil; serve on chilled plates within minutes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: substitute pressed almond feta. Grill half the melon cubes 60 seconds per side for a smoky register.
