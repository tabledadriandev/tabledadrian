# Plachie de Pește — Baked Fish on Melted Onions & Tomato

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A Delta classic: fish baked over a deep bed of slow-melted onions with tomato, wine and lemon — the onions, cooked to sweetness, are half the dish.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- White fish fillets or steaks (carp, pike-perch, sea bass) — 700 g
- Onions, sliced — 600 g
- Sunflower oil or olive oil — 60 ml
- Tomatoes, sliced — 300 g
- Tomato passata — 150 g
- Dry white wine — 150 ml
- Garlic — 20 g
- Lemon — 1 (sliced)
- Bay leaves — 2
- Sweet paprika — 4 g
- Salt — 12 g
- Black pepper — 2 g
- Parsley — 10 g

## Method
1. Melt the onions in oil over medium-low heat, 25 minutes, stirring, to soft gold — do not rush this.
2. Add garlic, paprika, passata and wine; simmer 5 minutes; season.
3. Spread the onion bed in a baking dish; lay seasoned fish on top.
4. Shingle tomato and lemon slices over the fish; tuck in bay leaves.
5. Bake at 180°C for 25–30 minutes until the fish flakes at 55°C core.
6. Rest 5 minutes; parsley over.

## Plating & Service
Bring the dish to the table; serve each portion with a deep spoonful of the onion stew beneath.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 50 g raisins and 30 g olives for the Greek-influenced sud version; superb at room temperature next day.
