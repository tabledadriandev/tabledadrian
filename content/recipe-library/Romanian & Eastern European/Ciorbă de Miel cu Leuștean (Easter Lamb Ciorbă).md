# Ciorbă de Miel cu Leuștean (Easter Lamb Ciorbă)

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The Easter table's opener: young lamb, spring onion and masses of lovage, finished with a yolk-cream liaison.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Lamb shoulder and neck, diced — 800 g
- Spring onions — 120 g
- Carrot — 150 g
- Celeriac — 80 g
- Rice — 50 g
- Tomatoes, grated — 200 g
- Fermented borș or lemon juice — 350 ml / 45 ml
- Egg yolks — 2 (40 g)
- Sour cream — 150 g
- Lovage — 15 g
- Parsley — 10 g
- Salt — 13 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Cover lamb with 2.5 L cold water, bring to 95°C, skim well; simmer 55 minutes.
2. Add sliced spring onion, diced carrot and celeriac; simmer 15 minutes.
3. Add rice and grated tomato; cook 15 minutes.
4. Boil borș separately and add; season.
5. Whisk yolks with sour cream, temper, stir in below 80°C.
6. Finish with chopped lovage and parsley; rest 10 minutes.

## Plating & Service
Serve with green chilli and country bread; the lovage aroma should hit before the bowl lands.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Goat kid is the shepherd's version; skip liaison and it is dairy-free and lighter.
