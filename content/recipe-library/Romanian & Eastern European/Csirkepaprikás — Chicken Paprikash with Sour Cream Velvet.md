# Csirkepaprikás — Chicken Paprikash with Sour Cream Velvet

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Hungary's most-loved sauce: chicken braised in paprika-onion base finished with a sour cream liaison — the colour of a Budapest sunset.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Chicken legs, jointed — 1.4 kg
- Lard or sunflower oil — 40 g
- Onions, finely diced — 300 g
- Sweet Hungarian paprika — 22 g
- Tomato — 100 g
- Hungarian wax pepper — 100 g
- Chicken stock — 300 ml
- Sour cream — 350 g
- Cornflour — 12 g
- Garlic — 10 g
- Salt — 13 g
- Black pepper — 2 g

## Method
1. Brown seasoned chicken in lard, skin first, 8 minutes; set aside.
2. Cook onion in the fat, low heat, 12 minutes to gold. Off heat, stir in paprika.
3. Add chopped tomato, sliced pepper, garlic and stock; return chicken skin-up. Simmer covered 35 minutes.
4. Remove chicken; whisk cornflour into sour cream, temper with hot sauce, return to the pan below a boil.
5. Simmer gently 4 minutes to a velvet that coats a spoon; adjust salt.
6. Return chicken to the sauce for 2 minutes.

## Plating & Service
Classic partner is nokedli or buttered tagliatelle; a dollop of untouched sour cream and a wax pepper ring on top.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Never let the sauce boil after the cream, and never use smoked paprika here — sweet Hungarian only.
