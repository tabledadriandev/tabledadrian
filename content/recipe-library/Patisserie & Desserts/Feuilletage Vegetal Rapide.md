# Feuilletage Vegetal Rapide

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rough-puff with 80%-fat plant butter and cider vinegar — 4 quick folds deliver honest flake for vegan tatins and palmiers in under 2 hours.

## Ingredients (serves 900 g dough)
- T55 flour — 500 g (100%)
- Plant butter block 80% fat, frozen 15 min and cubed — 375 g (75%)
- Ice water — 200 g (40%)
- Cider vinegar — 10 g (2%)
- Fine salt — 10 g (2%)
- Sugar — 15 g (3%)

## Method
1. Toss cold fat cubes through flour, salt and sugar; leave the cubes whole.
2. Add water and vinegar; bring together into a shaggy rectangle — visible fat is correct.
3. Roll to 1 cm and give 2 double tours back to back, flouring lightly.
4. Rest 30 min at 4 °C; give 2 more double tours.
5. Rest 1 h; roll to 3 mm and bake at 180 °C fan — plant fats brown slower, judge by lift and crispness, ~25 min.

## Plating & Service
Deploy for vegan guest weeks: tartes fines, cheese-free twists with za'atar, caramelized palmiers.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Choose a plant block that stays plastic at 13 °C (coconut-shea blends work); avoid soft tub margarine — it smears the layers.
