# Entremets Opera Contemporain

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Seven strata of joconde, coffee butter cream, ganache and coffee syrup, squared to the millimetre and mirrored in dark glaze — the Opéra, kept classical, cut modern.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Biscuit joconde sheets — 3 x (20 x 20 cm)
- Coffee imbibage: espresso 150 g, sugar 60 g, coffee extract 5 g
- Coffee French buttercream: yolks 80 g, sugar 140 g, water 45 g, butter 250 g, coffee extract 12 g
- Ganache: dark 64% 150 g, cream 150 g
- Chablonnage: dark couverture 60 g
- Glaçage miroir cacao — 250 g
- Gold leaf — 2 flakes

## Method
1. Chablonner one joconde sheet with melted couverture; flip, this is the base.
2. Imbibe generously; layer: buttercream 4 mm, joconde, imbibage, ganache 4 mm, joconde, imbibage, buttercream.
3. Press flat under a light board; freeze 2 h.
4. Glaze the top with mirror glaze at 32 °C, or spread a final ganache coat and comb it.
5. Trim all four edges with a hot knife for glass-clean strata; cut 10 x (2.5 x 8 cm) bars.
6. Temper to 14 °C for 30 min; gold on each bar.

## Plating & Service
Serve with nothing — the Opéra is a complete argument; espresso on the side at most.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Baileys-spiked ganache for gala; matcha opéra (matcha buttercream, white ganache) as a second colourway.
