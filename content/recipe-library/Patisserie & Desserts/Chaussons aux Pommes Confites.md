# Chaussons aux Pommes Confites

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Inverse puff turnovers around vanilla-confited apple compote — scored, gilded, and baked to a lacquered scallop shell.

## Ingredients (serves 10 chaussons)
- Feuilletage inversé — 900 g
- Apple compote: apples 600 g, butter 30 g, sugar 60 g, vanilla 1 pod, lemon 10 g, calvados 10 g
- Apple dice, sautéed — 150 g
- Gilding: yolk 40 g, cream 20 g
- Syrup 30°B — 30 g

## Method
1. Cook compote low and slow 20 min to thick; fold in the sautéed dice; chill completely.
2. Roll puff to 3 mm; cut 13 cm ovals with a fluted cutter.
3. Roll each oval slightly longer; 40 g filling on one half, edges egg-washed, fold, press — never crimp with a fork.
4. Chill 30 min; flip over, gild twice, score a fan pattern with a paring knife tip; pierce a vent.
5. Bake 180 °C fan 28–30 min; syrup-brush at the oven door for lacquer.
6. Cool 15 min.

## Plating & Service
Warm at breakfast or with cider in the afternoon; the filling must be cold at build or the puff dies.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pear-chocolate chaussons; the confite compote keeps a week and fills crêpes besides.
