# Sorbet Chocolat Noir, Sans Lait

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Water-based 70% chocolate sorbet — darker-tasting than any ice cream because nothing dilutes the cocoa; secretly vegan.

## Ingredients (serves 1 kg (10 portions))
- Water — 600 g
- Dark couverture 70% (dairy-free) — 200 g
- Cocoa powder — 30 g
- Sucrose — 110 g
- Dextrose — 50 g
- Glucose powder — 30 g
- Sorbet stabilizer — 4 g
- Salt — 1.5 g

## Method
1. Dry-blend stabilizer with 30 g sucrose.
2. Boil water with sugars and cocoa, whisking, 2 min; add stabilizer blend at 45 °C on the way up.
3. Pour over the couverture; blend 2 min to a glossy emulsion.
4. Blast-chill; mature 12 h — it thickens like a pudding; normal.
5. Churn; extract at −6 °C; serve at −11 °C.

## Plating & Service
With olive oil and fleur de sel, or beside raspberry sorbet in a black-red coupe.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Spike with 20 g rum or 2 g espresso; the fat-free base carries infusions (mint, orange zest) exceptionally well.
