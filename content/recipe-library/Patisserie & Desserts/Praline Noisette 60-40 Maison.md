# Praline Noisette 60-40 Maison

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> House praliné: 60% deeply roasted Piedmont hazelnuts, 40% dry caramel, ground just past grainy — the flavour signature of half the trolley.

## Ingredients (serves 700 g paste)
- Whole hazelnuts — 420 g (60%)
- Sugar — 280 g (40%)
- Fleur de sel — 2 g
- Vanilla bean, spent pod — 1 (optional, in the caramel)

## Method
1. Roast hazelnuts at 160 °C for 15–18 min to a full mahogany core — pale nuts make flat praliné.
2. Dry caramel: melt the sugar in stages to 185 °C, a true dark amber.
3. Pour caramel over the warm nuts on a mat; cool completely.
4. Break up, grind in a robot-coupe: powder (1 min), paste (4 min), fluid paste (8 min) — scrape often, rest the motor.
5. Stop when it flows off a spoon in a ribbon with fine grain; add salt at the end.
6. Store airtight at room temperature 1 month; stir before use as oil separates.

## Plating & Service
The base of mousseline, croustillant, and bonbons; a spoon over vanilla ice cream is a legitimate dessert.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> 50/50 almond-hazelnut for classic French; pecan praliné with 5 g espresso powder for an American accent.
