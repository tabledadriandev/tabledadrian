# Pains au Chocolat, Batons 60 pour Cent

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Croissant dough wrapped around two 60% batons — square, sharp-layered and rolled tight so the chocolate never leaks.

## Ingredients (serves 12 pieces)
- Pâte à croissant, laminated — 1.1 kg
- Chocolate batons 60% — 24
- Gilding: yolk 40 g, cream 20 g

## Method
1. Roll dough to 3.5 mm; cut 8 x 14 cm rectangles (~90 g).
2. Baton at the short edge, one tight roll, second baton, continue rolling; seam dead-centre underneath.
3. Proof 2.5 h at 26 °C until wobbling.
4. Double gild; bake 175 °C fan 16–17 min.
5. Cool 15 min — molten chocolate burns; guests will not wait, warn them.

## Plating & Service
The croissant's serious sibling; count two per teenager aboard.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> A pinch of fleur de sel on the batons before rolling; bicolour cocoa-laminated version for gala breakfasts.
