# Tarte Abricot-Romarin, Frangipane

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Apricot halves roasted into frangipane with rosemary sugar — a rustic-looking tart with strict technique underneath.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Pâte sucrée, raw-lined 22 cm ring — 300 g
- Frangipane — 350 g
- Ripe apricots, halved — 550 g
- Rosemary sugar: sugar 30 g + fresh rosemary 2 g, blitzed
- Apricot jam — 40 g
- Flaked almonds — 25 g
- Rosemary flowers — to garnish

## Method
1. Pipe frangipane 10 mm into the raw shell.
2. Press apricot halves cut-side up in concentric circles, pushing them halfway in.
3. Scatter rosemary sugar and flaked almonds.
4. Bake at 170 °C fan 40–45 min until frangipane is set and the fruit edges char slightly.
5. Glaze warm with loosened apricot jam; cool to just-warm before cutting.

## Plating & Service
Best 30 min out of the oven with crème fraîche ice cream; the fruit should still sigh steam.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Plums with star anise sugar in September; GF with the rice-almond shell and 15 g rice flour in the frangipane.
