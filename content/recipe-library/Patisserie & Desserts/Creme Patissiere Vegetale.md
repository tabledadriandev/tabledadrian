# Creme Patissiere Vegetale

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Oat-soy pastry cream thickened with starch and enriched with cocoa butter — pipes, bakes and slices like the classic, ready for vegan éclairs and tarts.

## Ingredients (serves 900 g base)
- Oat drink (barista) — 600 g
- Soy drink — 200 g
- Sugar — 140 g
- Cornstarch — 70 g
- Cocoa butter — 40 g
- Plant butter — 40 g
- Vanilla pods — 1.5
- Turmeric — 0.2 g (colour whisper)
- Salt — 1 g

## Method
1. Infuse vanilla in the milks at 85 °C, 10 min.
2. Whisk sugar, starch and turmeric; slurry with a ladle of the liquid.
3. Combine and boil 90 s, whisking hard — full gelatinization or it weeps.
4. Off heat, blend in cocoa butter, plant butter and salt.
5. Film-contact; blast-chill; paddle smooth before use.
6. Holds piping structure 24 h; freezes poorly — make fresh.

## Plating & Service
Drops into every recipe calling for crème pât: vegan mille-feuille, éclairs, pain suisse.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Diplomate version: fold in 300 g whipped coconut cream stabilized with 2 g agar-boiled base.
