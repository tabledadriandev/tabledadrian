# Genoise a Chaud

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Whole-egg foam sponge warmed over a bain-marie — the fraisier and moka base that wants syrup and rewards patience in folding.

## Ingredients (serves 1 x 22 cm cake or 1 sheet)
- Whole eggs — 250 g
- Sugar — 155 g (62% of egg)
- T55 flour — 155 g (62%)
- Melted butter — 40 g (16%)
- Vanilla extract — 3 g

## Method
1. Whisk eggs and sugar over a bain-marie to 45 °C.
2. Transfer to the mixer, whip 10 min to full ribbon — the batter should hold a figure-eight for 5 s.
3. Fold in sifted flour in three passes with a maryse, cutting to the base of the bowl.
4. Sacrifice a spoon of batter into the melted butter, then fold back in.
5. Bake 25 min at 170 °C in a buttered 22 cm ring; done at gentle spring-back.
6. Cool inverted on a rack; slice into 3 layers next day — day-old génoise slices cleaner.

## Plating & Service
Always imbibe: 30 °B syrup flavoured with kirsch (fraisier) or coffee (moka), ~150 g per cake.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cocoa génoise: 130 g flour + 25 g cocoa; sheet version bakes 8 min at 200 °C for roulades.
