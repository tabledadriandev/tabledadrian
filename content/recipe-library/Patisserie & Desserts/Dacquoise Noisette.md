# Dacquoise Noisette

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Crisp-shelled, marshmallow-hearted hazelnut meringue biscuit — the GF base layer for praliné entremets.

## Ingredients (serves 3 x 20 cm discs)
- Egg whites — 200 g
- Sugar — 70 g
- Roasted hazelnut flour — 170 g
- Icing sugar — 150 g
- Cornstarch — 20 g
- Chopped roasted hazelnuts — 40 g

## Method
1. Sift hazelnut flour, icing sugar and cornstarch together.
2. Whip whites, raining in the sugar from soft peaks, to a firm glossy meringue.
3. Fold in the dry mix in three passes — stop the second it is homogeneous.
4. Pipe 18 cm spirals with a 12 mm tip; scatter chopped hazelnuts, dust icing sugar twice, 5 min apart, for the pearl crust.
5. Bake 20–22 min at 165 °C fan; the centre should stay supple.

## Plating & Service
Base for Paris-Brest revisité and praliné entremets; also delicious alone with chantilly and roasted figs.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Almond or pistachio flour 1:1; coconut dacquoise (dessicated coconut) for exotic entremets.
