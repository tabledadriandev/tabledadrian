# Canneles Bordelais

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rum-vanilla batter aged 48 hours, baked in beeswaxed copper to a black-crusted, custard-hearted cannelé — patience in fluted form.

## Ingredients (serves 16 canneles)
- Whole milk — 500 g
- Butter — 25 g
- Vanilla pod — 1
- T55 flour — 100 g
- Sugar — 225 g
- Egg yolks — 60 g
- Whole egg — 50 g
- Dark rum — 40 g
- Beeswax + butter (moulds) — 50 g/50 g

## Method
1. Boil milk, butter and vanilla; cool to 60 °C.
2. Whisk flour and sugar; add eggs and yolks, then the warm milk in a stream — a crêpe-like batter.
3. Add rum; rest 48 h at 4 °C, stirring once daily.
4. Coat copper moulds with the 50/50 beeswax-butter at 80 °C, drained upside down.
5. Stir the batter (never whip); fill moulds to 5 mm from the rim, batter at 20 °C.
6. Bake 230 °C for 12 min, then 180 °C for 48–52 min; unmould hot — mahogany-black crust, wobbling heart.

## Plating & Service
Serve within 5 h of baking; day-old cannelés revive 5 min at 180 °C.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No copper? Heavy carbon-steel moulds beat silicone every time; orange-Grand Marnier variant swaps the rum.
