# Pralines Anciennes aux Noisettes

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sugar-crusted hazelnuts sablé-ed and re-caramelized in the pan — the rose-tinted fairground praline, done in copper, eaten by the fistful.

## Ingredients (serves 400 g pralines)
- Whole hazelnuts, raw — 250 g
- Sugar — 200 g
- Water — 65 g
- Vanilla pod — 1/2
- Beet powder (optional rose tint) — 2 g
- Fleur de sel — 1 g

## Method
1. Cook sugar, water and vanilla to 117 °C in a wide pan.
2. Add nuts; stir off heat until the syrup sables — turns to white sand coating every nut.
3. Return to medium heat, stirring always: the sand re-melts into caramel layer by layer, 8–10 min.
4. Stop at three-quarters caramelized for the classic crusted look; salt, tint if using.
5. Pour onto silicone, separate with two forks; cool fully.
6. Store airtight 3 weeks.

## Plating & Service
Crush over ice cream, bag as guest gifts, or eat warm off the mat (chef's tax).

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Almonds or pecans identically; cinnamon-dusted Christmas version.
