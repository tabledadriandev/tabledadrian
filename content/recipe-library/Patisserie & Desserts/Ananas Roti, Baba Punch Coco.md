# Ananas Roti, Baba Punch Coco

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Vanilla-roasted pineapple beside a coconut-punch-soaked mini baba and coconut sorbet — dairy-free without announcing it.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Pineapple, peeled, cored — 1 (800 g)
- Roasting glaze: honey 60 g, vanilla 1 pod, lime zest 1, rum 20 g
- Mini babas — 6
- Coconut punch syrup: coconut milk 200 g, sugar 120 g, water 200 g, lime juice 20 g, rum 30 g
- Coconut sorbet — 300 g
- Toasted coconut — 20 g
- Lime — 1

## Method
1. Roast the whole pineapple at 200 °C for 30 min, basting with the glaze every 8 min, until bronzed.
2. Warm the punch syrup to 60 °C; soak babas 6 min; drain.
3. Slice the pineapple into thick wedges, keeping the caramelized bark.
4. Plate: pineapple wedge, punch baba, sorbet quenelle on toasted coconut.
5. Spoon roasting juices over the fruit; microplane lime across everything.

## Plating & Service
A pour of warm roasting caramel at the table finishes it; serve immediately.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Alcohol-free: replace rum with 10 g extra lime and vanilla; GF babas from the rice-flour dough.
