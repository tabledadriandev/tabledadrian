# Madeleines au Miel de Chataignier

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Chestnut-honey madeleines with the obligatory hump — batter rested overnight, baked to order, eaten inside four minutes.

## Ingredients (serves 24 madeleines)
- T55 flour — 150 g (100%)
- Sugar — 110 g (73%)
- Chestnut honey — 40 g (27%)
- Whole eggs — 150 g (100%)
- Butter, beurre noisette, cooled — 150 g (100%)
- Baking powder — 6 g (4%)
- Lemon zest — 1
- Salt — 1 g

## Method
1. Whisk eggs, sugar and honey 2 min; add zest.
2. Fold in flour, powder and salt; stream in the cooled beurre noisette.
3. Rest the batter 12 h at 4 °C — thermal shock builds the hump.
4. Pipe into buttered-floured shells 80% full; chill trays 15 min.
5. Bake at 210 °C for 4 min, drop to 170 °C for 7–8 min until the hump peaks golden.
6. Unmould immediately onto linen; serve within minutes.

## Plating & Service
Bake during coffee service so they land warm; a lemon-glaze dip (icing sugar + juice) for the trolley version.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Brown-butter-sage savoury version for aperitif; GF: 90 g rice flour + 60 g almond flour, same method.
