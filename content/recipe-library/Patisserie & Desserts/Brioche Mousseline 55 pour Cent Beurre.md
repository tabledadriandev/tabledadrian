# Brioche Mousseline 55 pour Cent Beurre

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Silk-crumbed high-butter brioche on an overnight cold bulk — the base for pain suisse, tropézienne and toasted breakfast slices.

## Ingredients (serves 2 loaves (500 g each))
- T45 gruau flour — 500 g (100%)
- Whole eggs, cold — 300 g (60%)
- Sugar — 60 g (12%)
- Fine salt — 10 g (2%)
- Fresh yeast — 20 g (4%)
- Butter 82%, 15 °C — 275 g (55%)
- Whole milk — 30 g (6%)
- Egg yolk + 10 g milk for gilding

## Method
1. Mix flour, eggs, milk, sugar, salt and yeast on speed 1 for 5 min, then speed 2 until windowpane, 8–10 min; dough at max 24 °C.
2. Add butter in 4 stages; knead until glossy and fully elastic, ~8 min.
3. Bulk 45 min at room temperature, fold, then 12 h at 4 °C.
4. Divide 8 x 60 g balls per Nanterre tin; proof 2.5 h at 26 °C until doubled.
5. Gild twice, bake at 160 °C fan 25–28 min to 93 °C core.
6. Cool on wire fully before slicing — the crumb sets as it cools.

## Plating & Service
Serve barely warm with cultured butter and citrus marmalade at breakfast; day-two loaves become pain perdu.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Reduce butter to 40% for a sturdier bun dough; enrich with 1 vanilla bean and orange zest for tropézienne.
