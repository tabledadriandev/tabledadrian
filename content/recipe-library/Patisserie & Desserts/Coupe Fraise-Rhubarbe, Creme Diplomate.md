# Coupe Fraise-Rhubarbe, Creme Diplomate

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Layered coupe of roasted rhubarb, strawberry sorbet, diplomate and sablé crumble — a sundae with French table manners.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Rhubarb — 400 g
- Sugar — 80 g
- Grenadine or raspberry juice — 30 g
- Strawberry sorbet — 360 g
- Crème diplomate — 250 g
- Sablé diamant crumble — 90 g
- Fresh strawberries — 150 g
- Elderflower cordial — 20 g

## Method
1. Roast rhubarb batons with sugar and grenadine at 180 °C for 12–14 min — tender, intact, jewel-pink; chill in juices.
2. Macerate quartered strawberries in elderflower 20 min.
3. Chill coupe glasses.
4. Layer: rhubarb and juices, crumble, diplomate, strawberries.
5. Crown with a sorbet quenelle and a last scatter of crumble; serve immediately.

## Plating & Service
Build in front of guests for the lunch table — three minutes of theatre, zero pass pressure.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Autumn coupe: roasted plums, vanilla ice cream, streusel; GF crumble swaps in directly.
