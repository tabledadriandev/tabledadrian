# Bonbons Moules, Coeur Caramel-Vanille

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Polished polycarbonate-moulded bonbons: tempered dark shell, flowing vanilla caramel heart, mirror shine — box-of-chocolates engineering.

## Ingredients (serves 42 bonbons)
- Dark couverture 66%, tempered — 600 g (shell + cap)
- Caramel filling: sugar 180 g, glucose 30 g, cream 180 g, butter 70 g, vanilla 1 pod, salt 2 g — cooked to 106 °C
- Cocoa butter, coloured (optional) — 30 g
- Cotton gloves and a heat gun — non-negotiable

## Method
1. Polish moulds with cotton; optionally flick coloured cocoa butter at 30 °C for décor.
2. Temper couverture 45 → 27 → 31–32 °C; fill cavities, vibrate, invert and tap out; scrape clean — a 1 mm shell.
3. Set 20 min at 16 °C.
4. Cool the caramel to 28 °C; pipe to 1.5 mm below the rim.
5. Crust 2 h at 16 °C; cap with tempered couverture, scrape flat.
6. Set 12 h at 16 °C; the shells release with a click when contraction is complete.

## Plating & Service
A clean release means proper temper — shine you can read by; box at 16–18 °C, 60% humidity max.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Praliné or passion-ganache fillings on the same shell; on a humid quay, run the AC before demoulding.
