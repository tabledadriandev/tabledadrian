# Croquant Amandes Salees au Beurre

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Buttery almond toffee cooked to 148 °C and snapped into shards — the crunch element for ice creams, and dangerous on its own.

## Ingredients (serves 500 g shards)
- Sugar — 250 g
- Glucose — 60 g
- Water — 50 g
- Salted butter — 100 g
- Flaked almonds, toasted — 150 g
- Fleur de sel — 2 g
- Vanilla — 1/4 pod

## Method
1. Cook sugar, glucose and water to 118 °C; add the butter in pieces.
2. Cook, stirring, to 148 °C — pale amber, hard-crack.
3. Off heat, fold in warm almonds, salt and vanilla.
4. Pour thin on silicone; lay a second mat over and roll to 3 mm.
5. Cool fully; snap into shards.
6. Airtight with silica; sea humidity is its assassin.

## Plating & Service
Stand shards in sorbets, crush over sundaes, or bag as crew treats.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Sesame croquant (150 g toasted sesame) for plated exotic desserts; dark-chocolate-drizzled shards for the trolley.
