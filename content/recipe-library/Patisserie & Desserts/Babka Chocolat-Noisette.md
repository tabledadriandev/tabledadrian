# Babka Chocolat-Noisette

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Brioche twisted around gianduja-cocoa smear and roasted hazelnuts, syruped out of the oven — the Central-European guest at the French breakfast table.

## Ingredients (serves 2 loaves)
- Brioche dough (40% butter), cold — 800 g
- Filling: gianduja 180 g, butter 40 g, cocoa 15 g, icing sugar 30 g
- Roasted hazelnuts, chopped — 80 g
- Syrup 30°B — 80 g
- Gilding: yolk 20 g, milk 10 g

## Method
1. Beat the filling spreadable at 25 °C.
2. Roll each dough half to 3 mm (25 x 35 cm); spread filling edge to edge; scatter hazelnuts.
3. Roll into tight logs; freeze 15 min; split each log lengthwise.
4. Twist the two halves cut-face UP around each other; into buttered tins.
5. Proof 2 h at 26 °C; gild the exposed dough.
6. Bake 160 °C fan 35–40 min to 92 °C core; soak with syrup immediately.

## Plating & Service
Slice thick once fully cool; toast day-two slices in butter.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pistachio-white chocolate babka; cinnamon-walnut for a NYC accent; freezes whole for a month.
