# Caramel Beurre Sale de Guerande

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Dry caramel to 185 °C, deglazed with hot cream, mounted with salted butter — pourable at 20 °C, pipeable at 4 °C; one recipe, every consistency.

## Ingredients (serves 600 g sauce (12 portions))
- Sugar — 250 g
- Glucose — 40 g
- Cream 35%, hot — 250 g
- Salted butter — 90 g
- Fleur de sel de Guérande — 3 g
- Vanilla bean — 1/2

## Method
1. Dry-melt sugar and glucose in a wide pan in stages to a deep amber, 183–185 °C — paler is just sweet, darker is bitter.
2. Off heat, add hot cream in three careful additions (it storms).
3. Return to 105 °C for a sauce, 108 °C for a piping caramel.
4. At 60 °C blend in butter, salt and vanilla seeds.
5. Jar and cool; keeps 3 weeks at 4 °C.

## Plating & Service
The sauce for profiteroles and tatin; at 108 °C it becomes the insert of choux and entremets.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Miso caramel: 15 g white miso instead of salt; dairy-free: full-fat coconut milk and plant butter — cook to 107 °C to offset the extra water.
