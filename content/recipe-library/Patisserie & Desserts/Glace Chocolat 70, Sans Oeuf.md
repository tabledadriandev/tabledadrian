# Glace Chocolat 70, Sans Oeuf

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Egg-free dark chocolate ice cream — cocoa butter is the texturizer, dextrose keeps it scoopable despite 70% couverture's snap.

## Ingredients (serves 1 kg (10 portions))
- Whole milk — 620 g
- Cream 35% — 120 g
- Dark couverture 70% — 160 g
- Cocoa powder — 15 g
- Sucrose — 90 g
- Dextrose — 55 g
- Milk powder 0% — 30 g
- Stabilizer blend — 4 g
- Salt — 1.5 g

## Method
1. Dry-blend stabilizer with 30 g sucrose; whisk all powders and sugars into cold milk and cream.
2. Heat to 85 °C, 2 min, whisking the base smooth.
3. Pour over the couverture; blend 2 min to a full emulsion — chocolate ice cream lives or dies on this step.
4. Blast-chill; mature 12 h.
5. Churn; extraction at −6 °C; the base is thick — trust it, do not thin.
6. Serve at −11 °C, one degree warmer than vanilla; chocolate serves harder.

## Plating & Service
Pairs with olive oil, fleur de sel and toasted brioche as a complete plated dessert.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free: oat drink + 40 g extra couverture; milk-chocolate version: 220 g of 40%, cut sucrose to 70 g.
