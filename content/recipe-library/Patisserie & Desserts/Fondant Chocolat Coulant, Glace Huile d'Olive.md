# Fondant Chocolat Coulant, Glace Huile d'Olive

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A 12-minute molten centre timed to the second, against olive-oil ice cream and fleur de sel — the plated classic with a Riviera accent.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Dark couverture 70% — 200 g
- Butter — 180 g
- Whole eggs — 200 g
- Egg yolks — 60 g
- Sugar — 130 g
- T55 flour — 70 g
- Fleur de sel — 1 g
- Olive-oil ice cream (base blanche + 60 g olive oil) — 360 g
- Cocoa nibs — 15 g
- Finishing olive oil — 12 g

## Method
1. Melt chocolate and butter to 45 °C.
2. Whisk eggs, yolks and sugar just to combine — no ribbon; air makes cake, not lava.
3. Fold in the chocolate, then flour and salt.
4. Pipe 110 g into buttered-cocoa'd 7 cm rings on trays; rest 4 h at 4 °C (or freeze).
5. Bake from fridge at 190 °C fan exactly 11–12 min — edges set, a 2 cm centre wobble.
6. Rest 1 min, unring onto plates.

## Plating & Service
Quenelle of olive-oil ice cream on nibs beside it, thread of oil and three salt flakes over the fondant; serve inside 90 seconds.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> From frozen: 14 min; GF: swap flour for 50 g almond flour + 20 g cornstarch — arguably better.
