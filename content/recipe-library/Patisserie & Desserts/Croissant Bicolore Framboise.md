# Croissant Bicolore Framboise

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A raspberry-tinted lamination layer over classic croissant dough — striped pink spirals with a raspberry insert; patisserie-counter theatre.

## Ingredients (serves 12 croissants)
- Pâte à croissant, laminated through 2 tours — 1 kg
- Colour dough: détrempe 150 g + raspberry powder 12 g + beet powder 2 g
- Raspberry confit (pectin NH, pipeable) — 180 g
- Gilding: cream only — 30 g
- Freeze-dried raspberry — 5 g

## Method
1. Sheet the coloured dough to 2 mm, chill.
2. After the final tour, lay the pink sheet on the dough block, roll together to 3.5 mm — the colour rides the surface.
3. Cut triangles pink-side out; score the pink layer lightly with a lame in fine lines (it opens into stripes).
4. Roll; proof 2.5 h at 26 °C; gild with cream only — egg browns and kills the pink.
5. Bake 165 °C fan 17–18 min, watching colour.
6. Cool; inject 15 g raspberry confit through the base; dust one tip with berry powder.

## Plating & Service
Stack in a pink-striped pyramid at brunch; they disappear before the plain ones.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Matcha-white chocolate or cocoa-praliné colourways on the same method.
