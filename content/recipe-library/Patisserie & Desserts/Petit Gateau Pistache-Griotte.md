# Petit Gateau Pistache-Griotte

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Pistachio bavaroise cubes with griotte confit centre, pistachio glaze and a kirsch-soaked crumb — Sicily and the Black Forest shake hands.

## Ingredients (serves 8 individual)
- Financier pistache base squares, 5 cm — 8
- Griotte insert: griotte purée 140 g, sugar 35 g, pectin NH 3 g, kirsch 6 g
- Pistachio bavaroise: milk 180 g, pistachio paste 50 g, yolks 50 g, sugar 45 g, gelatin 5 g, cream whipped 260 g
- Pistachio glaze: white couverture 150 g, cream 75 g, pistachio paste 25 g, nappage 60 g
- Griottes in kirsch — 8
- Chopped pistachios — 15 g

## Method
1. Cast griotte insert into small cubes; freeze.
2. Pistachio anglaise to 83 °C, gelatin, cool to 24 °C, fold cream.
3. Mould 5.5 cm cubes with inserts; freeze 5 h.
4. Glaze at 32 °C; seat on financier squares.
5. Thaw 2 h; one kirsch griotte and pistachio shards on top.
6. Serve at 12 °C.

## Plating & Service
Square plates, single cube, a drop of griotte syrup — restraint sells it.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Almond-amarena version; skip kirsch for family charters.
