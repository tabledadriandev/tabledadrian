# Macaron Framboise Pleine Chair

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Deep pink shells, a raspberry pectin confit that stays sharp, and half a fresh raspberry hidden in each centre.

## Ingredients (serves 20 macarons)
- Macaron shells, raspberry pink — 40
- Raspberry purée — 200 g
- Sugar — 60 g
- Pectin NH — 4 g
- Lemon juice — 8 g
- Fresh raspberries — 10, halved

## Method
1. Heat purée to 45 °C; rain in pectin dry-blended with the sugar.
2. Boil 90 s whisking; add lemon; chill to a firm confit.
3. Beat smooth; pipe a ring on half the shells.
4. Seat a raspberry half in each centre, cap and press to the rim.
5. Mature 24 h at 4 °C; serve at 15 °C.

## Plating & Service
The fruit-forward anchor of any macaron box — its sharpness makes the chocolate ones taste better.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cassis or apricot on the same pectin ratios; add 4 g rose water for an Ispahan macaron.
