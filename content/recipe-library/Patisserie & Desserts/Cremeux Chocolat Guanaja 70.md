# Cremeux Chocolat Guanaja 70

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A crème anglaise emulsified into 70% couverture — dense, glossy, spoon-coating; the textural anchor of plated chocolate desserts.

## Ingredients (serves 10 quenelles or 1 entremets insert)
- Whole milk — 250 g
- Cream 35% — 250 g
- Egg yolks — 100 g
- Sugar — 50 g
- Dark couverture 70% — 220 g
- Fleur de sel — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Cook milk, cream, yolks and sugar to 83 °C, stirring in a figure-eight — a nappe anglaise.
2. Pour through a chinois onto the couverture in three additions, creating a tight elastic core with a maryse.
3. Blend 1 min with an immersion blender, blade fully submerged — no air.
4. Add salt; pour into a shallow tray, film-contact.
5. Set 6 h at 4 °C; rest 10 min at room temperature before quenelling at 12 °C.

## Plating & Service
Quenelle onto a streusel base with cocoa nib tuile; or pipe as entremets inserts at the tray stage.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Milk-chocolate version: 300 g of 40% couverture + 2 g gelatin. Dairy-free: oat milk and coconut cream, same anglaise.
