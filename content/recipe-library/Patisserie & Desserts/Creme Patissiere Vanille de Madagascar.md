# Creme Patissiere Vanille de Madagascar

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The mother cream — cornstarch-only for a clean set, finished with cold butter for shine; ratio holds at any scale.

## Ingredients (serves 1.4 kg base)
- Whole milk — 1000 g (100%)
- Sugar — 200 g (20%)
- Egg yolks — 160 g (16%)
- Cornstarch — 80 g (8%)
- Butter, cold — 100 g (10%)
- Vanilla beans, split — 2
- Fine salt — 1 g

## Method
1. Infuse split vanilla in milk, brought to 85 °C; lid on, 15 min.
2. Whisk yolks, sugar and cornstarch to a smooth blanchi.
3. Temper with a third of the hot milk, return all to the pan.
4. Boil 1 full minute at a rolling bubble, whisking hard — cornstarch must gelatinize fully or it thins overnight.
5. Off heat, beat in cold butter and salt.
6. Film-contact, blast-chill to 4 °C within 30 min; smooth with a paddle before use.

## Plating & Service
Base for éclairs, flans, diplomate and mousseline; keeps 48 h at 4 °C.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pistachio: whisk 80 g pure paste into 1 kg warm cream. GF by nature; dairy-free: oat milk + 20% coconut cream, drop the butter.
