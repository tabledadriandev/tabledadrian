# Citron Destructure, Sorbet Basilic

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The lemon tart exploded on the plate: curd, crumbled sucrée, torched meringue shards and basil sorbet — same flavours, new geometry.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Lemon curd de Menton — 300 g
- Pâte sucrée, baked and crumbled — 150 g
- Swiss meringue shards: whites 60 g, sugar 120 g, baked
- Sorbet citron-basilic — 360 g
- Candied lemon — 30 g
- Basil oil: basil 20 g blanched, grapeseed oil 60 g
- Micro basil — 12 tips

## Method
1. Bake meringue spread thin at 90 °C for 2 h; snap into shards.
2. Blend basil oil; strain through muslin.
3. Pipe three curd bulbs off-centre on each chilled plate.
4. Scatter a crescent of sucrée crumble; anchor the sorbet quenelle on it.
5. Stand meringue shards between bulbs; dot candied lemon and basil oil.
6. Finish with micro basil; serve immediately.

## Plating & Service
All components hold 48 h; plating takes 90 seconds — the perfect finale for a 12-cover night.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Yuzu curd and shiso sorbet version; the crumble base can be GF sucrée for a fully GF plate.
