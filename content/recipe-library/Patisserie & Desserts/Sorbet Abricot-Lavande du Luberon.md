# Sorbet Abricot-Lavande du Luberon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Roasted-apricot sorbet with a two-minute lavender infusion — Provence in a quenelle; roasting concentrates fruit that travels unripe.

## Ingredients (serves 1 kg (10 portions))
- Ripe apricots, halved — 700 g
- Sucrose (for roasting) — 40 g
- Sorbet syrup 60°Brix — 330 g
- Water — 60 g
- Dried lavender flowers — 1.5 g
- Lemon juice — 12 g

## Method
1. Roast apricots cut-side up with the sugar at 190 °C for 12 min until edges caramelize.
2. Infuse lavender in the hot syrup portion 2 min only — over-infused lavender is soap; strain.
3. Blend apricots (skins on), syrup, water and lemon; pass through a chinois.
4. Check 30°Brix; mature 4 h.
5. Churn, extract at −6 °C, harden 3 h.

## Plating & Service
Beside an almond financier with a drop of apricot-kernel oil — the noyau echo is the chef's wink.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skip lavender and add 1 vanilla pod for a purer fruit line; peach or nectarine roast identically.
