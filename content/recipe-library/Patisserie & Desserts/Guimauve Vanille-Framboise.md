# Guimauve Vanille-Framboise

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Two-tone marshmallow cubes — vanilla and raspberry layers on gelatin and Italian meringue, tossed in starch-icing sugar; cloud engineering.

## Ingredients (serves 50 cubes)
- Sugar — 300 g
- Glucose — 50 g
- Water — 100 g
- Honey — 50 g
- Gelatin 200 bloom — 20 g + 120 g cold water
- Egg whites — 90 g
- Vanilla pod — 1
- Raspberry purée, reduced by half — 60 g
- Coating: icing sugar 60 g + cornstarch 60 g

## Method
1. Bloom gelatin; melt at 50 °C.
2. Cook sugar, glucose, water and honey to 125 °C; pour onto soft-peak whites; add melted gelatin; whip to warm-thick, 8 min.
3. Split the mass: vanilla seeds in half, reduced raspberry folded into the other.
4. Frame the vanilla layer 1.5 cm in an oiled 20 cm square; pipe the raspberry layer on top; smooth.
5. Set 6 h uncovered at 18 °C.
6. Cut with an oiled knife; roll cubes in the starch-sugar coat.

## Plating & Service
Skewer over the beach-BBQ embers or box with the caramels for cabins.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Passion, violet or coffee layers; halal/kosher-style: fish or bovine gelatin per guest requirements — retag accordingly.
