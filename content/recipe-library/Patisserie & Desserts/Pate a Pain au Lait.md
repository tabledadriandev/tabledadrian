# Pate a Pain au Lait

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Soft milk-bread dough with tangzhong for a cottony crumb that stays fresh 3 days — the base for navettes, buns and pain suisse.

## Ingredients (serves 16 navettes)
- T45 flour — 500 g (100%)
- Tangzhong: 50 g of the flour + 150 g milk, cooked
- Whole milk, cold — 175 g (35%)
- Whole egg — 50 g (10%)
- Sugar — 70 g (14%)
- Salt — 9 g (1.8%)
- Fresh yeast — 18 g (3.6%)
- Butter — 100 g (20%)

## Method
1. Cook tangzhong to 65 °C whisking constantly; cool.
2. Mix everything except butter to moderate development, 8 min; add butter, knead to windowpane.
3. Bulk 1 h at 25 °C with one fold.
4. Divide 60 g, shape navettes, proof 1.5 h at 27 °C.
5. Gild, score lengthwise with scissors, bake 160 °C fan 12–14 min — keep them pale gold.

## Plating & Service
Split warm with salted butter and dark chocolate shavings for a crew-favourite goûter.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 80 g pearl sugar pre-bake; swap 20% flour for wholegrain T80 with +5% milk for a breakfast version.
