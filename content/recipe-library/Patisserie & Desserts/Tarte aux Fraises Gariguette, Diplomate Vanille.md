# Tarte aux Fraises Gariguette, Diplomate Vanille

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Gariguettes stood whole on a vanilla diplomate over a hidden strawberry compotée — the June tart that needs perfect fruit and nothing else.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Pâte sucrée, baked 22 cm shell — 280 g
- Crème d'amande, baked in shell — 180 g
- Strawberry compotée: strawberries 150 g, sugar 25 g, pectin NH 3 g, lemon 5 g
- Crème diplomate — 350 g
- Gariguette strawberries — 500 g
- Nappage neutre — 40 g
- Basil micro-leaves — 8
- Pistachios, chopped — 15 g

## Method
1. Bake the shell blind 15 min, pipe in crème d'amande, bake 165 °C until golden, ~18 min; cool.
2. Cook compotée: fruit and lemon to 60 °C, rain in pectin-sugar, boil 1 min; spread 3 mm over the almond layer.
3. Pipe diplomate in a smooth dome-less layer to the rim.
4. Halve strawberries lengthwise; stand them tip-up in tight concentric circles.
5. Brush warm nappage on the cut faces only; scatter pistachio and basil.
6. Chill 1 h; eat the same day.

## Plating & Service
The knife test of a pastry chef: every slice must show shell, almond, red compotée, cream, fruit in clean strata.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap in raspberries + lemon-zested diplomate in August; wild strawberries unhalved for gala service.
