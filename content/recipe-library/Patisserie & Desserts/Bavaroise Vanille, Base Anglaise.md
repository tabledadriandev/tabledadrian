# Bavaroise Vanille, Base Anglaise

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The classical moulded cream — anglaise, gelatin, cream — that every charlotte and doomed entremets interior depends on; melts at exactly body temperature.

## Ingredients (serves 1 charlotte or 10 moulded inserts)
- Whole milk — 250 g
- Egg yolks — 80 g
- Sugar — 70 g
- Vanilla bean — 1
- Gelatin 200 bloom — 6 g + 36 g water
- Cream 35%, whipped soft — 300 g

## Method
1. Cook an anglaise: milk, yolks, sugar, vanilla to 83 °C.
2. Strain, add bloomed gelatin, cool over ice to 22 °C, stirring so it stays fluid.
3. At the first sign of thickening, fold in the soft-whipped cream in two passes.
4. Mould immediately; it sets fast — have rings lined and inserts ready before you start.
5. Set 4 h at 4 °C or freeze for entremets assembly.

## Plating & Service
Unmould onto biscuit cuillère with macerated raspberries for a 30-minute charlotte.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Flavour the anglaise with 60 g praliné or 100 g fruit purée (reduce milk by 80 g); keep total gelatin at 1.1% of the mass.
