# Nougat de Montelimar aux Amandes et Pistaches

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Honey-lavender nougat whipped to 135 °C, loaded 45% with roasted almonds and pistachios, pressed between wafer sheets — the Provençal confection benchmark.

## Ingredients (serves 50 pieces)
- Lavender honey — 200 g
- Sugar — 300 g
- Glucose — 80 g
- Water — 80 g
- Egg whites — 60 g
- Roasted almonds, warm — 300 g
- Roasted pistachios, warm — 100 g
- Candied orange dice — 50 g
- Wafer paper sheets — 2
- Vanilla — 1/2 pod

## Method
1. Boil honey to 121 °C in one pan; sugar, glucose and water to 145 °C in another — run both burners.
2. Whip whites to soft peaks; stream in the honey, then the sugar syrup, whipping.
3. Whip-dry the mass over low flame (or bain-marie) to 135 °C mass temperature — a ball in cold water should hold firm.
4. Fold in warm nuts, orange and vanilla with an oiled spatula.
5. Press 2 cm thick between wafer sheets in a frame; weight overnight at 18 °C.
6. Cut bars with a serrated knife, oiled.

## Plating & Service
Wrap bars in glassine; the white nougat should chew clean, never stick to teeth — that is the 135 °C test.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Soft nougat: stop the mass at 128 °C; honey defines it — buy the best lavender honey on the quay.
