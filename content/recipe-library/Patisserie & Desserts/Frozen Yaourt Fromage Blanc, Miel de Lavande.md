# Frozen Yaourt Fromage Blanc, Miel de Lavande

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Fromage blanc frozen yogurt with lavender honey — high lactic freshness, low sweetness, the health-leaning dessert guests re-order.

## Ingredients (serves 900 g (9 portions))
- Fromage blanc 40% — 500 g
- Whole milk — 200 g
- Lavender honey — 90 g
- Sucrose — 60 g
- Dextrose — 30 g
- Stabilizer blend — 3 g
- Lemon juice — 12 g

## Method
1. Pasteurize milk with sugars and dry-blended stabilizer at 85 °C, 1 min; chill.
2. Whisk into the cold fromage blanc with honey and lemon.
3. Mature 4 h; the honey aroma blooms in the cold base.
4. Churn; extract at −6 °C; the lactic base freezes quickly.
5. Serve at −12 °C within 3 days — freshness is the point.

## Plating & Service
With a warm madeleine and fresh figs; or under a drizzle of the same honey and bee pollen.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Greek-yogurt version 1:1; diabetic-leaning: replace sucrose with more dextrose and halve the honey (retag accordingly).
