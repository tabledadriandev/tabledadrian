# Tarte Chocolat-Framboise Vegetale

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Vegan sucrée shell, raspberry confit floor and a coconut-cream dark ganache poured to a mirror — the vegan showpiece tart that outsells its dairy twin.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Vegan pâte sucrée shell, baked 20 cm — 270 g
- Raspberry confit: purée 150 g, sugar 30 g, pectin NH 3 g
- Ganache: dark couverture 64% (dairy-free) — 220 g
- Coconut cream — 220 g
- Agave syrup — 20 g
- Plant butter — 30 g
- Fresh raspberries — 200 g
- Cocoa powder — 3 g

## Method
1. Boil the confit 1 min; spread 2 mm across the shell floor; set 20 min.
2. Heat coconut cream and agave to 80 °C; emulsify onto the couverture in thirds.
3. Blend in plant butter at 38 °C.
4. Pour into the shell at 34 °C over the confit; self-level.
5. Set 3 h at 16 °C — not the fridge.
6. Ring the rim with fresh raspberries, three of them dusted cocoa.

## Plating & Service
Room-temperature service; the coconut reads as texture, not flavour, under 64% chocolate.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Passion confit floor instead of raspberry; GF-vegan with the rice-flour plant-butter shell — the full-inclusion tart.
