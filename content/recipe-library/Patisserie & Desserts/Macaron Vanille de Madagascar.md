# Macaron Vanille de Madagascar

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Ivory shells around a two-day-matured vanilla ganache montée — the white macaron by which the whole box is judged.

## Ingredients (serves 20 macarons)
- Macaron shells, uncoloured — 40
- White couverture 35% — 120 g
- Cream 35%, hot — 110 g
- Cream 35%, cold — 110 g
- Vanilla pods — 1.5
- Vanilla powder — 1 g

## Method
1. Infuse pods in the hot cream 15 min; rebuild to 110 g; emulsify onto the couverture.
2. Add cold cream and seeds; mature 12 h at 4 °C.
3. Whip to piping firmness; load a 10 mm plain tip.
4. Pipe a full ring on half the shells, cap with partners, pressing to the edge.
5. Mature 24 h at 4 °C in a closed box; bring to 16 °C for 20 min before service.
6. Dust a crescent of vanilla powder on each dome.

## Plating & Service
The reference: crisp shell, chewy interior, cold silk centre — check one from each batch yourself.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Tonka or coffee infusion on the same ganache frame; shelf life 4 days at 4 °C.
