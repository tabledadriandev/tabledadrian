# Tarte Chocolat Grand Cru, Fleur de Sel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Hazelnut sablée, a whisper of praliné crunch, and a barely-set 70% ganache poured so gently it self-mirrors — served at room temperature, always.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Pâte sablée noisette, baked 20 cm shell — 270 g
- Croustillant praliné feuilletine — 90 g
- Ganache: dark couverture 70% — 210 g
- Cream 35% — 210 g
- Glucose — 25 g
- Butter — 40 g
- Fleur de sel — 1 g + flakes to finish
- Cocoa nibs, caramelized — 15 g

## Method
1. Press the croustillant in a thin even layer into the baked shell; chill 15 min.
2. Heat cream and glucose to 80 °C; emulsify onto the couverture in thirds.
3. At 38 °C add butter and salt; blend without aerating.
4. Pour into the shell from low height at 34 °C; the surface self-levels to a mirror.
5. Set 3 h at 16 °C — never the fridge; refrigeration dulls the shine and hardens the wax.
6. Finish with 5 flakes of fleur de sel and a line of caramelized nibs.

## Plating & Service
Slice with a hot knife wiped between cuts; serve 18–20 °C where the ganache melts at first pressure.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add an under-layer of 60 g passion crémeux for a Mogador accent; GF with the rice-almond shell.
