# Tarte Citron de Menton, Meringue Torchee

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A thin sucrée shell, sharp yuzu-lemon crémeux and torched Italian meringue kisses — the Riviera classic tightened to restaurant precision.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Pâte sucrée, baked 22 cm shell — 280 g
- Crémeux yuzu-citron — 550 g
- Italian meringue: egg whites — 100 g
- Sugar — 180 g
- Water — 55 g
- Lemon zest — 1
- Candied lemon dice — 40 g
- Silver leaf or lemon balm tips — to garnish

## Method
1. Yolk-seal the baked shell; cool.
2. Cast the crémeux at 35 °C into the shell to 2 mm below the rim; set 4 h at 4 °C.
3. Cook sugar and water to 121 °C; pour onto soft-peak whites; whip to 35 °C, firm and glossy.
4. Pipe meringue kisses of three sizes across one half of the tart.
5. Torch the meringue amber; scatter candied lemon and zest between the kisses.
6. Serve within 6 h at 12 °C — the shell is crispest before hour eight.

## Plating & Service
Cut with a hot dry knife; a 30 g quenelle of crème fraîche on the plate balances the sugar.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF shell (rice-almond sucrée) works identically; skip meringue and lattice with torched lemon segments for a lighter finish.
