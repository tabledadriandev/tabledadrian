# Streusel Amande-Fleur de Sel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Equal-parts crunch: the 25/25/25/25 crumble that seasons every petit gâteau base and plated dessert with salt and roast.

## Ingredients (serves 600 g crumble)
- Cold butter, cubes — 150 g (100%)
- Cassonade — 150 g (100%)
- Almond flour — 150 g (100%)
- T55 flour — 150 g (100%)
- Fleur de sel — 3 g (2%)

## Method
1. Sablage everything to a coarse crumb; do not let it ball up.
2. Press through a 6 mm grid (or crumble by hand) onto a tray; freeze 20 min.
3. Bake from frozen at 155 °C fan 16–18 min, stirring once, to an even gold.
4. Cool completely; store airtight with silica up to 1 week.

## Plating & Service
Scatter under quenelles for instant contrast, or bind warm with 10% melted milk chocolate and press into rings as an entremets base.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cocoa streusel: 20 g cocoa replacing flour; GF: buckwheat flour 1:1 — its nuttiness is even better.
