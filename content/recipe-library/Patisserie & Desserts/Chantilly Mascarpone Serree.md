# Chantilly Mascarpone Serree

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Cream cut with 25% mascarpone whips tighter, holds 8 hours and survives a warm deck — the only chantilly worth piping on charter.

## Ingredients (serves garnishes 10 desserts)
- Cream 35%, very cold — 400 g
- Mascarpone — 100 g
- Icing sugar — 45 g
- Vanilla bean — 1, seeds
- Vanilla extract — 3 g

## Method
1. Loosen mascarpone with 50 g of the cream until smooth — lumps never whip out later.
2. Add remaining cream, sugar, vanilla seeds and extract.
3. Whip on medium (never high) to firm peaks, ~3 min; medium speed builds smaller, stabler bubbles.
4. Pipe or quenelle; holds shape 8 h at 4 °C.

## Plating & Service
Quenelle beside anything warm — tatin, fondant, clafoutis; the mascarpone keeps it from weeping on a hot plate.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Lighter: 10% mascarpone; coffee chantilly: dissolve 4 g instant espresso in the cream first.
