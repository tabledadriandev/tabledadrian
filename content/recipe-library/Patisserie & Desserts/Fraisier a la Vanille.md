# Fraisier a la Vanille

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Kirsch-imbibed génoise, a wall of standing strawberries and vanilla mousseline — the June entremets that rewards knife discipline.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Génoise, 2 discs 18 cm x 1 cm — 300 g
- Kirsch syrup: 30°B syrup 120 g + kirsch 15 g
- Mousseline vanille: crème pât 500 g, butter 250 g, vanilla 1 pod
- Strawberries, even-sized — 700 g
- Strawberry compotée — 100 g
- Marzipan 33%, rolled 2 mm — 120 g (or nappage + fruit)
- Pistachio, chopped — 10 g

## Method
1. Line an 18 cm ring (4.5 cm tall) with rhodoïd; seat one imbibed génoise disc.
2. Halve strawberries; stand cut-face against the ring, tips up, shoulder to shoulder.
3. Pipe mousseline behind and between every berry with no air pockets — the unmoulding is decided here.
4. Fill the centre with quartered berries and compotée; pipe mousseline, seat the second imbibed disc.
5. Skim mousseline flat; chill 4 h; cap with marzipan or glaze.
6. Unring, peel rhodoïd, dress the top with berries and pistachio.

## Plating & Service
Cut with a tall thin knife dipped in hot water — every slice must show the berry palisade.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Framboisier with raspberry rows; lighter build: diplomate instead of mousseline, serve same-day.
