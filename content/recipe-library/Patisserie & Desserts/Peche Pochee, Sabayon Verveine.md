# Peche Pochee, Sabayon Verveine

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> White peach poached in verbena syrup with a warm whipped sabayon torched at the pass — high summer on the aft deck.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- White peaches — 6
- Poaching: water 800 g, sugar 300 g, white wine 200 g, fresh verbena 15 g, lemon 1/2
- Sabayon: yolks 100 g, poaching syrup 100 g, white wine 30 g
- Cream, softly whipped — 60 g
- Fresh verbena tips — 6
- Crushed amaretti or GF almond crumble — 40 g

## Method
1. Poach whole peaches in barely-simmering (85 °C) syrup 8–12 min; skins slip off after an ice-bath second.
2. Cool peaches in the syrup 4 h; they drink the verbena.
3. Sabayon: whisk yolks, syrup and wine over a bain-marie to 72 °C and tripled — thick ribbons.
4. Off heat, whisk 1 min; fold in the whipped cream.
5. Seat each peach in a shallow bowl, 20 g crumble beneath; coat with sabayon.
6. Torch the sabayon lightly; verbena tip on top.

## Plating & Service
Serve just-warm sabayon over cellar-cool peach — the temperature gap is the dish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Apricots or poached pears off-season; skip cream for a classic sharper sabayon.
