# Eclair Citron-Yuzu Meringue

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Citrus crémeux-filled éclair topped not with fondant but torched meringue ridges — glaze-free, sharper, alive.

## Ingredients (serves 12 eclairs)
- Pâte à choux — 550 g
- Crémeux yuzu-citron — 650 g
- Italian meringue: whites 90 g, sugar 160 g, water 50 g
- Candied lemon zest — 30 g
- Lime zest, fresh — 1

## Method
1. Bake 13 cm craquelin-free éclairs at 165 °C fan 38 min — the top will be meringued, imperfections vanish.
2. Fill with citrus crémeux at 14 °C, 55 g per shell.
3. Whip Italian meringue to 35 °C, firm.
4. Pipe a tight zigzag ridge of meringue down each éclair with a 10 mm star tip.
5. Torch amber; dress candied zest along the ridge, microplane lime over.
6. Serve within 4 h; meringue softens by hour six.

## Plating & Service
The bright option beside chocolate éclairs on the trolley; sells to the 'nothing too sweet' guest.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Passion-mango crémeux under the same meringue; GF choux version identical weights.
