# Petit Gateau Vanille-Pecan

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Individual domes of vanilla mousse over maple-pecan praliné insert and streusel base, in white velvet — the plated-lunch entremets.

## Ingredients (serves 8 individual)
- Streusel base discs, 6 cm — 8
- Pecan praliné insert: pecan praliné 160 g, cream 40 g, gelatin 1 g
- Vanilla mousse: milk 150 g, vanilla 1.5 pods, white couverture 160 g, gelatin 5 g + 30 g water, cream whipped 280 g
- Velvet spray white — 150 g
- Maple-caramelized pecan halves — 16
- Vanilla powder — 1 g

## Method
1. Cast praliné insert into 3 cm demi-sphere moulds; freeze.
2. Vanilla mousse: infuse, strain, gelatin, emulsify on couverture, fold cream at 28 °C.
3. Fill 6.5 cm dome moulds two-thirds; press in inserts; close with mousse; freeze 5 h.
4. Unmould; velvet-spray white while frozen.
5. Seat each dome on a streusel disc; thaw 2 h at 4 °C.
6. Two maple pecans and a breath of vanilla powder per dome.

## Plating & Service
Lunch dessert in under a minute at the pass: dome, sauce dot, done.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chocolate-tonka mousse over the same insert; batch and hold frozen unmoulded 2 weeks.
