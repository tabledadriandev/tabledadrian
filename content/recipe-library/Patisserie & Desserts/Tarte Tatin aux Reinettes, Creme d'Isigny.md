# Tarte Tatin aux Reinettes, Creme d'Isigny

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Reinette quarters cooked twice in a blond caramel — once on the stove, once under puff — for a tatin that stands upright in deep amber varnish.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Reinette apples (or Braeburn) — 1.4 kg
- Sugar — 180 g
- Butter — 90 g
- Vanilla bean — 1
- Pâte feuilletée, 3 mm disc 26 cm — 300 g
- Fleur de sel — 1 g
- Crème fraîche d'Isigny — 200 g

## Method
1. Peel and quarter apples; dry uncovered at 4 °C for 12 h — dehydration is the anti-collapse insurance.
2. Dry caramel with the sugar in a 24 cm tatin pan to amber; off heat, mount the butter, add vanilla seeds and salt.
3. Stand apple quarters upright in tight rosettes; cook on medium heat 20 min, basting, until the caramel runs thick.
4. Cool 15 min; cap with docked puff, tucking edges down inside.
5. Bake at 185 °C fan 40 min; rest 10 min exactly, then flip in one commitment onto the platter.
6. Serve warm with cold crème d'Isigny.

## Plating & Service
Flip in front of guests if nerves allow — the reveal is half the dessert.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Quince-and-apple tatin in November; a splash of cider vinegar in the caramel cuts richness.
