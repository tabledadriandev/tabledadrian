# Croissants Vegetaux

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Vegan lamination with an 80%-fat plant block and soy-milk détrempe — honest honeycomb and shatter, gilded with maple-soy glaze.

## Ingredients (serves 10 croissants)
- T45 flour — 500 g (100%)
- Soy drink — 230 g (46%)
- Sugar — 60 g (12%)
- Salt — 10 g (2%)
- Fresh yeast — 20 g (4%)
- Plant butter, soft (détrempe) — 40 g (8%)
- Plant butter block 80% fat (tourage) — 250 g (50%)
- Glaze: soy drink 30 g + maple syrup 15 g

## Method
1. Mix the détrempe 8 min; bulk 30 min; chill flat 12 h.
2. Condition the plant block to 13 °C — plant fats break colder than butter; err warm.
3. Lock in; three single tours, 40 min freezer-shortened rests between.
4. Roll 3.5 mm; cut and roll croissants; proof at 25–26 °C max, 2.5–3 h.
5. Glaze with maple-soy; bake 170 °C fan 17–18 min.
6. Second glaze pass out of the oven for shine.

## Plating & Service
Serve in the same basket as the classics; most guests cannot pick them out.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pain au chocolat with dairy-free batons; choose a plant block designed for lamination — tub margarine will smear and fail.
