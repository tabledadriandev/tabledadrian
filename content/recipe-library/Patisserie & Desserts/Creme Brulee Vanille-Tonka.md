# Creme Brulee Vanille-Tonka

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> All-cream custard baked low, burnt to order with a double sugar pass — the crack is the recipe; tonka is the signature.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Cream 35% — 600 g
- Whole milk — 100 g
- Egg yolks — 130 g
- Sugar — 90 g
- Vanilla pod — 1
- Tonka bean, grated — 1/3
- Cassonade for burning — 60 g

## Method
1. Infuse vanilla and tonka in cream and milk at 85 °C, 15 min; strain.
2. Whisk yolks and sugar without aerating; blend in the infusion.
3. Rest 30 min; skim every bubble.
4. Fill shallow 12 cm dishes; bake at 100 °C fan (or 130 °C static bain-marie) 45–50 min to a uniform tremble.
5. Chill 4 h minimum.
6. At service: blot the surface, cassonade pass one, torch; pass two, torch to dark amber glass.

## Plating & Service
Serve within 5 minutes of burning — the contrast of hot glass over cold cream is the entire dessert.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Coffee, verbena or lavender infusions on the same base; the shallow dish maximizes crust-to-cream ratio.
