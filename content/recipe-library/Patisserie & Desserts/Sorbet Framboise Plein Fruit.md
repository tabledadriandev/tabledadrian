# Sorbet Framboise Plein Fruit

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> 62% raspberry sorbet at 30°Brix — pure fruit slap, stabilized for a fine crystal that survives the freezer week.

## Ingredients (serves 1 kg (10 portions))
- Raspberry purée (unsweetened) — 620 g
- Sorbet syrup 60°Brix — 340 g
- Water — 30 g
- Lemon juice — 10 g

## Method
1. Blend purée, syrup, water and lemon cold; check 29–31°Brix, adjust with water or syrup.
2. Mature 4 h at 4 °C.
3. Churn; extract at −6 °C — fruit sorbets extract fast, do not over-churn to ice.
4. Blast-freeze; serve at −12 °C.
5. Texture check: a quenelle should curl glossy, not flake.

## Plating & Service
With warm chocolate fondant or solo in a chilled coupe with fresh berries and a crack of pepper.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Strawberry: same ratio +5 g lemon; cassis: raise syrup to 700 g per kg purée — its acidity demands it.
