# Orangettes au Chocolat Noir

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Candied orange batons confited in stages over three days, dried and dipped in tempered 70% — bitter, bright, secretly vegan.

## Ingredients (serves 60 pieces)
- Orange peel, thick-skinned — 6 oranges
- Blanching water — as needed
- Syrup day 1: water 500 g, sugar 500 g
- Sugar additions days 2–3 — 150 g + 150 g
- Glucose — 50 g
- Dark couverture 70% (dairy-free), tempered — 400 g

## Method
1. Cut peel (with 3 mm pith) into batons; blanch from cold three times, refreshing the water.
2. Day 1: simmer 10 min in the syrup with glucose; cool in syrup overnight.
3. Days 2 and 3: add sugar, re-simmer 10 min, rest overnight — staged confit keeps them supple, never crystallized.
4. Drain on racks 24 h until tacky-dry.
5. Temper the couverture; dip each baton two-thirds, leaving an orange tip.
6. Set on silicone at 16 °C; store airtight 3 weeks.

## Plating & Service
Coffee-service classic; the naked orange tip is the mark of a hand-dipped batch.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Grapefruit or citron peel identically; roll half the batch in crystal sugar instead of chocolate for contrast.
