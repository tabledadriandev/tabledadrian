# Entremets Exotique Coco-Mangue-Passion

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Coconut mousse, mango-passion crémeux insert, coconut dacquoise — the white-and-gold entremets built GF by nature for deck dinners.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Coconut dacquoise disc (GF), 16 cm — 1
- Crémeux insert: mango purée 140 g, passion purée 60 g, yolks 60 g, sugar 50 g, gelatin 3 g, butter 80 g
- Coconut mousse: coconut purée 250 g, white couverture 120 g, gelatin 6 g + 36 g water, cream whipped 280 g
- Velvet spray: white couverture 100 g + cocoa butter 100 g
- Fresh mango — 100 g
- Passion fruit — 1
- Toasted coconut — 15 g

## Method
1. Cook the exotic crémeux to 83 °C, butter at 40 °C; cast 14 cm; freeze.
2. Coconut mousse: warm purée with gelatin, emulsify onto couverture, fold cream at 28 °C.
3. Invert-build in 18 cm: mousse, insert, mousse, dacquoise; freeze 6 h.
4. Velvet-spray the frozen cake at 45 °C gun temperature for a matte white skin.
5. Thaw 3 h at 4 °C; dress a crescent of mango cubes, passion seeds and toasted coconut.
6. Serve at 12 °C.

## Plating & Service
The finale for tropical-themed dinners; velvet finish photographs better than mirror in sunlight.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dairy-free: coconut cream whipped with 2 g stabilizer replaces cream; the couverture swaps to oat-based.
