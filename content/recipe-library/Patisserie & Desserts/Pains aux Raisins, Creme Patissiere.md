# Pains aux Raisins, Creme Patissiere

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Lamination spiraled around rum-soaked raisins and crème pâtissière, glazed with apricot — the underrated third of the viennoiserie trinity.

## Ingredients (serves 12 spirals)
- Pâte à croissant, laminated — 1 kg
- Crème pâtissière, smooth — 300 g
- Raisins — 180 g, soaked overnight in 40 g rum + 40 g hot water
- Cassonade — 20 g
- Apricot glaze — 60 g
- Gilding: yolk 30 g, cream 15 g

## Method
1. Roll dough to a 3.5 mm rectangle, 40 x 35 cm.
2. Spread crème pâtissière thin to 2 cm from one long edge; scatter drained raisins and cassonade.
3. Roll from the far edge into a snug log; seal the bare edge; freeze 20 min.
4. Slice 3 cm spirals; tray cut-side up, tucking the tail underneath.
5. Proof 2 h at 26 °C; gild; bake 170 °C fan 16–18 min.
6. Brush warm with apricot glaze.

## Plating & Service
Glaze while hot so it soaks the spiral seams; serve the same morning.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pistachio cream and griotte version; pecan-maple with maple sugar for transatlantic guests.
