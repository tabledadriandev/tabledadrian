# Creme Mousseline Pralinee

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Crème pât beaten with butter and 60/40 praliné — the Paris-Brest engine; the emulsion holds piping ridges for hours in a warm saloon.

## Ingredients (serves fills 1 Paris-Brest (8 pers))
- Crème pâtissière, smooth, at 22 °C — 500 g
- Butter, 20 °C pommade — 220 g
- Praliné noisette 60/40 — 180 g
- Fleur de sel — 1 g

## Method
1. Both masses must be 20–22 °C — measure, do not guess; a 5 °C gap splits the emulsion.
2. Whip butter 3 min until pale and mousseux.
3. Add crème pâtissière one spoon at a time on medium speed, scraping between additions.
4. Fold in praliné and salt; whip 1 final minute until light.
5. Pipe immediately with an 18 mm star tip; set 1 h at 4 °C before cutting.

## Plating & Service
Serve at 12–14 °C, never fridge-cold — butter creams go waxy and mute below 10 °C.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> If it splits, warm the bowl edge with a torch and re-whip; pistachio version: swap praliné for 150 g pistachio paste + 30 g sugar.
