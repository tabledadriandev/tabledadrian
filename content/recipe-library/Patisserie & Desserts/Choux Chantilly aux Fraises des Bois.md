# Choux Chantilly aux Fraises des Bois

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Craquelin choux split and overfilled with mascarpone chantilly and wild strawberries — the simplest thing on the trolley and the first to go.

## Ingredients (serves 12 choux)
- Pâte à choux — 450 g
- Craquelin discs — 12
- Chantilly mascarpone serrée — 500 g
- Wild strawberries (or quartered gariguettes) — 250 g
- Strawberry compotée — 100 g
- Icing sugar — 15 g

## Method
1. Pipe 5 cm choux, cap with craquelin; bake 165 °C fan 35 min; cool.
2. Cut off the top third of each choux with a serrated knife.
3. Spoon 8 g compotée into each base.
4. Pipe a tall chantilly rosette; press berries into the cream.
5. Replace lids at an angle; dust with icing sugar.
6. Serve within 3 h.

## Plating & Service
Pile on a stand for tea service; guests take them by hand.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Peach and verbena in August; fold lemon zest into the chantilly to cut the cream on hot days.
