# Caramel Vegetal au Coco, Trois Textures

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> One coconut caramel, three stop-points: 104 °C sauce, 112 °C piping caramel, 121 °C cutting caramels — the vegan caramel masterclass in a single pan.

## Ingredients (serves 500 g (sauce, souple, bonbon))
- Sugar — 300 g
- Glucose — 80 g
- Coconut cream (30% fat) — 300 g
- Plant butter — 60 g
- Vanilla — 1/2 pod
- Fleur de sel — 2.5 g
- Lime zest (for the bonbons) — 1/2

## Method
1. Warm coconut cream with vanilla.
2. Dry caramel with sugar and glucose to 178 °C amber.
3. Deglaze in stages; the coconut spits — long sleeves.
4. Sauce: draw off a third at 104 °C. Souple: a third at 112 °C for éclair and tart fillings.
5. Bonbons: cook the rest to 121 °C (coconut carries more water — 3 °C above dairy targets); frame, set, cut, salt, zest.
6. Blend plant butter into the sauce and souple portions at 60 °C.

## Plating & Service
One session stocks the vegan pastry station for a week: sundae sauce, tart filling, cabin sweets.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Miso-coconut caramel (12 g white miso in the sauce); store all three airtight below 20 °C.
