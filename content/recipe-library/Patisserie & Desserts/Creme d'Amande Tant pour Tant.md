# Creme d'Amande Tant pour Tant

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Equal weights butter, sugar, almond, egg — never whipped, only emulsified, so it bakes dense and moist without soufflé-ing out of the tart.

## Ingredients (serves 800 g (2 tarts))
- Butter, pommade — 200 g (100%)
- Icing sugar — 200 g (100%)
- Almond flour — 200 g (100%)
- Whole eggs, room temp — 200 g (100%)
- T55 flour — 20 g (10%)
- Dark rum — 20 g (10%)
- Salt — 1 g

## Method
1. Paddle butter and icing sugar smooth — do not aerate; air makes it puff and crack in the oven.
2. Add almond flour and salt; mix low.
3. Add eggs in 3 stages, fully emulsified each time; finish with flour and rum.
4. Rest 12 h at 4 °C — resting hydrates the almond and stops oven-boil.
5. Pipe 8 mm into raw tart shells; bake with the tart at 160–170 °C until set and golden.

## Plating & Service
The layer under every baked fruit tart — apricot, pear, fig; it drinks the fruit juices and stays sliceable.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Frangipane: fold 250 g crème pâtissière into 500 g of this. Pistachio cream: swap 80 g almond for pistachio paste.
