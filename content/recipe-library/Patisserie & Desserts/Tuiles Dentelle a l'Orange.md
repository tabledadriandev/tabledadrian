# Tuiles Dentelle a l'Orange

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Lace tuiles of caramelized orange butter — the shattering vertical element for plated desserts and coupe glacées.

## Ingredients (serves 30 tuiles)
- Butter, melted — 75 g
- Sugar — 150 g
- Orange juice — 50 g
- Orange zest — 1
- T55 flour — 40 g
- Glucose — 20 g

## Method
1. Whisk sugar, flour and zest; add juice, glucose and melted butter to a smooth paste.
2. Rest 2 h at 4 °C.
3. Spoon 8 g dots far apart on silicone mats; they quadruple in spread.
4. Bake 175 °C fan 7–8 min to an even amber lace.
5. Shape warm over a rolling pin (arcs) or leave flat; set 1 min.
6. Store airtight with silica; they die in sea air within the hour if left out.

## Plating & Service
Stand upright in ice creams and mousses at the last second before the plate leaves the pass.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cocoa-nib tuiles: 20 g nibs, drop the zest; GF: rice flour 1:1 — the lace is even finer.
