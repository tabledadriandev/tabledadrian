# Textures de Fraise, Estragon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Strawberry five ways — raw, confit, sorbet, espuma, chip — with a tarragon whisper; a single fruit given a full vocabulary.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Gariguette strawberries — 500 g
- Strawberry sorbet — 300 g
- Espuma: strawberry purée 200 g, cream 100 g, sugar 30 g, gelatin 3 g (siphon, 1 charge)
- Confit: strawberries 150 g, sugar 30 g, pectin 2 g
- Strawberry chips: 6 thin slices dried at 70 °C for 3 h
- Tarragon leaves — 8
- Olive oil — 10 g
- Strawberry consommé: 200 g berries, 20 g sugar, steamed 1 h and strained

## Method
1. Make the consommé sous-vide or over a bain-marie; chill crystal-clear.
2. Cook confit 1 min with pectin; chill.
3. Load and chill the siphon 3 h.
4. Quarter raw berries; dress with oil and torn tarragon.
5. Build in chilled bowls: confit base, raw berries, sorbet quenelle, espuma cloud, chip upright.
6. Pour cold consommé at the table.

## Plating & Service
June only, peak fruit only — the dish has nowhere to hide.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Raspberry or apricot builds identically; swap tarragon for basil or elderflower.
