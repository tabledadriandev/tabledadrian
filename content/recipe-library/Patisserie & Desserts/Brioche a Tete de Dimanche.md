# Brioche a Tete de Dimanche

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The fluted parisienne with its perched head — a technical exercise in shaping that turns Sunday breakfast into an event.

## Ingredients (serves 10 brioches)
- Brioche mousseline dough, cold-proofed 12 h — 700 g
- Butter for moulds — 20 g
- Gilding: yolk 40 g, cream 20 g

## Method
1. Divide cold dough: 10 x 55 g bodies, 10 x 15 g heads.
2. Ball everything tight; rest 10 min.
3. Bore a finger-hole through each body; shape heads into teardrops; seat tail-down through the hole — the classic trick that never pops.
4. Into buttered fluted moulds; proof 2 h at 26 °C.
5. Gild carefully around, never gluing head to body; snip 4 cuts around the collar.
6. Bake 165 °C fan 14–15 min; unmould onto wire at once.

## Plating & Service
One per plate with jam trio and beurre de baratte — Sunday protocol.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Same dough as loaves or tressée; saffron-infused milk version for a golden crumb.
