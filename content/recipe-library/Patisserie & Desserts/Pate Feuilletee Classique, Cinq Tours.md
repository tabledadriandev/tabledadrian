# Pate Feuilletee Classique, Cinq Tours

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Classic five-fold puff with a vinegar détrempe for whiteness and extensibility — 160 layers for millefeuille and chaussons.

## Ingredients (serves 1.2 kg dough)
- T55 flour — 500 g (100%)
- Cold water — 235 g (47%)
- Fine salt — 11 g (2.2%)
- Melted butter, cooled (détrempe) — 60 g (12%)
- White vinegar — 8 g (1.6%)
- Dry butter 84% (beurre de tourage) — 400 g (80%)

## Method
1. Mix détrempe ingredients just to a rough ball; wrap and rest 2 h at 4 °C.
2. Beat tourage butter into a 20 x 20 cm square at 13–15 °C — dough and butter must match in plasticity.
3. Roll détrempe to 20 x 40 cm, enclose butter, seal seams.
4. Give a double tour (book fold); rest 1 h at 4 °C.
5. Give a single tour; rest 1 h. Repeat: double, rest, single — 5 tours total.
6. Rest 12 h before use; roll to 2 mm for millefeuille, bake between trays at 175 °C fan ~35 min, then caramelize with icing sugar at 210 °C for 3 min.

## Plating & Service
Bake weighted between two trays for dead-flat, glassy caramelized sheets; trim edges with a serrated knife while warm.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Inverse method (butter outside) gives even more rise; vegan: 84%-fat plant tourage margarine, identical folds.
