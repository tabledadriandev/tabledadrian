# Caramels Mous Fleur de Sel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Cut-and-wrap soft caramels cooked to 118 °C exactly — chew without tooth-pull, salt without stunt; the guest-cabin turndown sweet.

## Ingredients (serves 60 caramels)
- Sugar — 300 g
- Glucose — 150 g
- Cream 35% — 300 g
- Butter — 80 g
- Salted butter — 40 g
- Fleur de sel — 3 g
- Vanilla pod — 1

## Method
1. Warm cream with vanilla; hold hot.
2. Cook sugar and glucose to a light amber (175 °C).
3. Deglaze with hot cream in stages; add both butters.
4. Cook to 118 °C exactly, stirring the base constantly — 116 °C smears, 121 °C pulls teeth.
5. Add salt; pour into a 20 cm frame on silicone; set 12 h at 18 °C.
6. Cut 2 x 2 cm; wrap in cellophane twists.

## Plating & Service
Two per cabin at turndown; keeps 3 weeks airtight below 20 °C.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Passion caramels: replace 150 g cream with purée, cook to 119 °C; chocolate caramels: 60 g cocoa mass at the end.
