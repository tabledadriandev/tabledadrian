# Coques de Macaron, Meringue Italienne

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The production macaron shell on Italian meringue — more forgiving than French method at sea, with a reliable foot in any humidity.

## Ingredients (serves 80 shells (40 macarons))
- Almond flour, extra-fine — 250 g (100%)
- Icing sugar — 250 g (100%)
- Egg whites (for the tant-pour-tant) — 90 g (36%)
- Egg whites (for the meringue) — 90 g (36%)
- Sugar — 250 g (100%)
- Water — 75 g (30%)
- Powdered colour — as needed

## Method
1. Sift almond flour and icing sugar twice; paste with the first 90 g whites and any colour.
2. Cook sugar and water to 118 °C; pour onto the second whites at soft peaks; whip to 45 °C, firm and glossy.
3. Macaronage: fold meringue into the paste in thirds, then work with the scraper until the batter flows in a slow, continuous lava ribbon.
4. Pipe 3.5 cm rounds on templates; rap trays twice; pop bubbles with a toothpick.
5. Croûtage 20–30 min until the surface no longer sticks to a fingertip.
6. Bake 145 °C fan, one tray at a time, 13–14 min; shells should twist off clean when cool.

## Plating & Service
Mature filled macarons 24 h at 4 °C before serving — day-two texture is the entire point of a macaron.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cocoa shells: replace 25 g almond with cocoa; shells freeze unfilled 1 month between papers.
