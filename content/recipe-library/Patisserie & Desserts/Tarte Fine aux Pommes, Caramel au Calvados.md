# Tarte Fine aux Pommes, Caramel au Calvados

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A 2 mm puff disc shingled with paper-thin apples, baked hard and lacquered with Calvados caramel — 30 minutes from order to table.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Pâte feuilletée, 2 mm disc 24 cm — 280 g
- Royal Gala apples — 3 (about 450 g)
- Butter, melted — 30 g
- Sugar — 40 g
- Caramel beurre salé — 80 g
- Calvados — 15 g
- Vanilla ice cream — to serve

## Method
1. Dock the puff disc heavily; chill 15 min.
2. Slice apples 1.5 mm on the mandoline; shingle in overlapping rings to the edge — no border.
3. Brush with butter, sift sugar evenly over.
4. Bake at 190 °C fan 25–30 min until the rim is deep brown and the base fully crisp — check by lifting.
5. Warm the caramel with the Calvados; brush over the hot tart.
6. Serve within 15 min, warm.

## Plating & Service
Slide whole onto a board, cut at the table, scoop of vanilla melting into the caramel.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: plant puff + coconut caramel; pears with a grind of black pepper in October.
