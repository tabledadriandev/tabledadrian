# Eclair Cafe-Noisette

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Espresso crème pât lightened with hazelnut praliné, a coffee glaze and one line of caramelized hazelnuts down the spine.

## Ingredients (serves 12 eclairs)
- Pâte à choux — 550 g
- Coffee crème pâtissière: base 600 g + espresso 40 g + instant coffee 4 g
- Praliné noisette — 90 g
- Glaze: blond fondant 250 g, coffee extract 8 g, glucose 15 g
- Caramelized hazelnut halves — 36
- Gold dust — trace

## Method
1. Pipe and bake 13 cm éclairs at 165 °C fan, 38 min.
2. Beat espresso and instant coffee into the warm crème pât; cool; fold in the praliné.
3. Fill each shell with 55 g of coffee-praliné cream.
4. Warm fondant with glucose and coffee extract to exactly 35 °C — hotter kills the shine.
5. Glaze the tops; set 10 min.
6. Seat three caramelized hazelnut halves in a line along each éclair.

## Plating & Service
With a macchiato at 16:00 — the éclair replaces both biscuit and sugar.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap praliné for gianduja in winter; decaf build for evening service.
