# Brioche Sans Gluten au Psyllium

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A GF brioche where psyllium gel plays the gluten — batter-shaped, twice-proofed, with real pull-apart crumb and a butter crown.

## Ingredients (serves 1 loaf + 6 buns)
- Fine rice flour — 250 g (56%)
- Tapioca starch — 120 g (27%)
- Buckwheat flour — 75 g (17%)
- Psyllium husk — 18 g + 180 g warm water (the gel)
- Whole eggs — 150 g
- Whole milk, warm — 150 g
- Sugar — 55 g
- Fresh yeast — 15 g
- Butter, soft — 120 g
- Salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Whisk psyllium into warm water; gel 5 min.
2. Paddle everything except butter 4 min — it is a thick batter, not a dough; trust it.
3. Add soft butter; beat 3 min glossy.
4. Proof in the bowl 1 h at 26 °C; fold once with a wet spatula.
5. Scoop into a buttered loaf tin and bun moulds; proof 1 h; gild gently.
6. Bake 165 °C fan — buns 18 min, loaf 30 min to 94 °C core; cool fully before cutting.

## Plating & Service
Toasts brilliantly for GF breakfast service; the psyllium crumb tears in sheets like the real thing.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Burger-bun version with sesame; sweet version +20 g sugar and orange zest for GF pain perdu.
