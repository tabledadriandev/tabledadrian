# Chouquettes au Sucre Perle

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Unfilled choux studded with pearl sugar — baked in ten minutes' work, gone in five; the goûter of every French childhood.

## Ingredients (serves 40 pieces)
- Pâte à choux — 500 g
- Pearl sugar C40 — 120 g
- Gilding: egg yolk 20 g + milk 10 g
- Butter for trays — 10 g

## Method
1. Pipe 3 cm domes with a 12 mm plain tip on buttered trays.
2. Gild lightly; press a generous pinch of pearl sugar onto each — more than seems reasonable.
3. Tip trays to shed loose sugar.
4. Bake 165 °C fan 28–30 min until hollow, golden and dry.
5. Cool on wire; eat within 4 h — chouquettes do not wait.

## Plating & Service
A warm paper bag of them at children's teatime, or beside coffee for crew families.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fold 60 g chocolate chips into the dough; fill day-olds with diplomate to reincarnate as mini choux à la crème.
