# Tarte Bourdaloue Poire-Amande

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Vanilla-poached pear halves fanned into almond cream — the 19th-century Paris classic executed with a modern crisp shell and honest poach.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Pâte sucrée, raw-lined 22 cm ring — 300 g
- Crème d'amande — 320 g
- Williams pears — 4
- Poaching: water 1 L, sugar 400 g, vanilla pod 1, lemon 1/2
- Flaked almonds — 30 g
- Apricot nappage — 40 g
- Icing sugar — 10 g

## Method
1. Poach peeled, halved, cored pears in the syrup at 85 °C until a knife slides free, 15–20 min; cool in the syrup overnight.
2. Pipe crème d'amande into the raw shell.
3. Drain pears well, slice crosswise 3 mm keeping the shape, fan 8 halves stem-in.
4. Scatter flaked almonds on the exposed cream; bake 170 °C fan 45–50 min.
5. Glaze the fruit with warm nappage; dust the rim with icing sugar.
6. Serve just warm or room temperature.

## Plating & Service
A 20 g pour of the reduced poaching syrup on the plate ties fruit to cream.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Poach in Poire Williams-spiked syrup for gala; quince bourdaloue at Christmas.
