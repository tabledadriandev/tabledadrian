# Glacage Miroir Cacao

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The black mirror: cocoa-cream glaze at 32% sugar syrup that sets shine-side-perfect on a frozen entremets at exactly 32 °C.

## Ingredients (serves glazes 2 entremets (18 cm))
- Water — 120 g
- Sugar — 220 g
- Glucose — 80 g
- Cream 35% — 145 g
- Cocoa powder, sifted — 75 g
- Gelatin 200 bloom — 12 g + 72 g water

## Method
1. Boil water, sugar and glucose to 106 °C.
2. Whisk in cream, then cocoa; return to a simmer 1 min, whisking smooth.
3. Off heat at 60 °C, add bloomed gelatin; blend 2 min, blade fully submerged — zero bubbles.
4. Pass through a chinois, film-contact, mature 12 h at 4 °C.
5. Re-melt to 45 °C, cool to 31–32 °C; pour in one confident pass over the entremets frozen to −18 °C.
6. Let drip 2 min, clean the base with a hot palette, transfer to the board.

## Plating & Service
One pour, no touch-ups — reheated second coats always show; excess glaze re-melts twice before losing shine.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> White/coloured mirror: 150 g condensed milk + 150 g white couverture instead of cream and cocoa, gelatin identical.
