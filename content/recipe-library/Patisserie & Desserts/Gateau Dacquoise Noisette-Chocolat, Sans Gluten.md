# Gateau Dacquoise Noisette-Chocolat, Sans Gluten

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Three hazelnut dacquoise discs layered with dark ganache montée and praliné — a naturally gluten-free gâteau that concedes nothing.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Dacquoise noisette discs, 18 cm — 3
- Ganache montée chocolat: dark 64% 140 g, hot cream 140 g, cold cream 280 g
- Praliné noisette, fluid — 100 g
- Caramelized hazelnuts — 60 g
- Cocoa powder — 5 g
- Gold leaf

## Method
1. Mature the chocolate ganache montée 12 h; whip to soft-firm.
2. Anchor one disc; pipe ganache bulbs around the rim, fill the centre; drizzle 50 g praliné.
3. Second disc; repeat.
4. Third disc flat-side up; pipe a crown of bulbs; centre filled with crushed caramelized hazelnuts.
5. Chill 2 h; cocoa on one arc, gold on one bulb; serve at 14 °C.

## Plating & Service
Cut with a serrated knife through the crisp dacquoise shell; announce the GF status — it wins loyalty.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Coffee ganache version; freeze the naked layered cake 2 weeks, finish on board.
