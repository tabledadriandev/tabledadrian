# Pate Sablee Noisette

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A richer, more fragile crumb than sucrée thanks to roasted hazelnut flour and yolk only — for chocolate and praliné tarts.

## Ingredients (serves 3 x 20 cm tart rings)
- T55 flour — 225 g (100%)
- Roasted hazelnut flour — 60 g (27%)
- Butter, cold — 150 g (67%)
- Icing sugar — 90 g (40%)
- Egg yolk — 40 g (18%)
- Fleur de sel — 2.5 g (1.1%)

## Method
1. Roast hazelnut flour at 150 °C for 8 min; cool completely.
2. Sablage butter with both flours and salt until sandy.
3. Add icing sugar, then yolks; press together without kneading.
4. Rest 12 h at 4 °C, roll to 3 mm, line rings, freeze 20 min.
5. Bake at 155 °C fan for 20–22 min until deep gold; the extra fat colours fast, watch the last 3 min.

## Plating & Service
Best under dark chocolate ganache tarts; the hazelnut echoes any praliné layer.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Replace hazelnut flour with almond for a neutral version; brown the butter first (then re-chill) for a noisette-on-noisette effect.
