# Tarte Citron Vegetale, Meringue Aquafaba

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Vegan lemon tart with a starch-set citrus cream and torched aquafaba meringue — indistinguishable theatre, zero animal products.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Vegan pâte sucrée shell (plant butter), baked 22 cm — 280 g
- Lemon cream: lemon juice 200 g, water 100 g, sugar 160 g, cornstarch 35 g, cocoa butter 40 g, plant butter 80 g, zest 2 lemons, turmeric 0.3 g
- Aquafaba meringue: reduced aquafaba 120 g, sugar 180 g, tartar 1 g
- Candied lemon — 30 g

## Method
1. Boil juice, water, sugar, starch and zest 90 s to a thick, glossy cream.
2. Blend in cocoa butter and plant butter at 45 °C; cast into the shell; set 4 h at 4 °C.
3. Whip aquafaba to firm gloss with sugar and tartar (10 min — it takes longer than egg white).
4. Pipe meringue kisses across the tart; torch gently — aquafaba torches faster than egg, feather the flame.
5. Candied lemon between the kisses; serve within 4 h.

## Plating & Service
Meringue is best within hours — build for the meal, not the fridge.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Lime-coconut variant with coconut cream folded into the citrus base; GF vegan: rice-flour shell with plant butter.
