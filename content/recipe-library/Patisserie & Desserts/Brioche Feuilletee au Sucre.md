# Brioche Feuilletee au Sucre

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Brioche dough laminated like a croissant and rolled into swirled loaves — shreddable ribbons of butter crumb under a pearl-sugar crown.

## Ingredients (serves 2 loaves)
- Brioche dough (40% butter version), chilled — 800 g
- Dry butter (tourage) — 200 g (25% of dough)
- Pearl sugar — 60 g
- Gilding: yolk 30 g, cream 15 g
- Syrup 30°B for brushing — 40 g

## Method
1. Lock the tourage butter into the cold brioche dough.
2. Two single tours with 30 min freezer rests — brioche dough is soft; work cold and fast.
3. Roll to 4 mm; cut three long strips per loaf, braid loosely or roll as a trio of swirls into tins.
4. Proof 2 h at 26 °C to the tin rim.
5. Gild, crown with pearl sugar; bake 160 °C fan 32–35 min to 92 °C core.
6. Brush lightly with syrup out of the oven.

## Plating & Service
Tear, never slice, at the table; the ribbons are the show.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cinnamon-sugar in the folds for a Scandinavian accent; praliné-swirled version for the trolley.
