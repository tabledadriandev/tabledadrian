# Dome Chocolat-Tonka, Coeur Cassis

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Tonka-infused dark mousse domes with a blackcurrant heart on cocoa streusel — bitter, floral, sharp; a study in tension.

## Ingredients (serves 8 individual)
- Cocoa streusel discs, 6 cm — 8
- Cassis insert: blackcurrant purée 160 g, sugar 45 g, pectin NH 3 g
- Tonka mousse: milk 160 g, tonka bean 1 grated, dark 64% 210 g, gelatin 3 g, cream whipped 300 g
- Glaçage miroir cacao — 300 g
- Blackcurrants or cassis pearls — 24
- Silver leaf

## Method
1. Boil cassis insert 1 min with pectin; cast demi-spheres; freeze.
2. Infuse grated tonka in hot milk 10 min; strain hard; gelatin, emulsify on chocolate, fold cream at 35 °C.
3. Mould domes with cassis hearts; freeze 5 h.
4. Mirror-glaze at 32 °C over the frozen domes on a rack.
5. Seat on streusel discs; thaw 2 h at 4 °C.
6. Three cassis berries and silver on each; serve 12 °C.

## Plating & Service
Dinner finale with a pour of cassis coulis at the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Tonka is potent: one bean per 160 g milk, never more; vanilla for a gentler dome.
