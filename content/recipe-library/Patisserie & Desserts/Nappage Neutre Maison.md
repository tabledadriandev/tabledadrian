# Nappage Neutre Maison

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Pectin-set neutral glaze brushed warm over fruit tarts — shine that holds 24 h without the gummy commercial texture.

## Ingredients (serves 500 g glaze)
- Water — 300 g
- Sugar — 180 g
- Glucose — 40 g
- Pectin NH — 8 g
- Lemon juice — 8 g

## Method
1. Mix pectin with 40 g of the sugar (dry-blend prevents lumps).
2. Heat water, remaining sugar and glucose to 45 °C; rain in the pectin mix whisking.
3. Boil 1 full minute; add lemon juice off heat.
4. Cool; it sets to a gel. Rewarm to 60 °C and brush or spray at 45–50 °C over fruit.
5. Keeps 2 weeks at 4 °C; re-boil to reuse.

## Plating & Service
A whisper-thin coat: fruit should look wet, not lacquered.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Infuse with vanilla pod or verbena during the boil; tint with fruit purée (20%) for a coloured tart glaze.
