# Entremets Palet Or, Chocolat-Caramel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Dark mousse over a liquid-edge caramel insert, hazelnut croustillant and dacquoise, sealed in black mirror glaze — the dinner-party chocolate bomb, engineered.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Dacquoise noisette disc, 16 cm — 1
- Croustillant praliné — 130 g
- Caramel insert: caramel beurre salé cooked to 106 °C — 180 g, gelatin 2 g
- Chocolate mousse: dark 64% 220 g, milk 160 g, gelatin 3 g + 18 g water, cream whipped 320 g
- Glaçage miroir cacao — 350 g
- Caramelized hazelnuts — 10
- Gold leaf

## Method
1. Cast the caramel insert with gelatin into a 14 cm ring; freeze solid.
2. Spread croustillant on the dacquoise; freeze.
3. Mousse: boil milk, dissolve gelatin, emulsify onto chocolate; at 38 °C fold in the whipped cream.
4. In an 18 cm ring build upside down: mousse, frozen caramel insert, mousse, dacquoise-croustillant lid; freeze 6 h at −18 °C.
5. Unmould, glaze at 32 °C on the frozen entremets; rest 3 h at 4 °C to thaw.
6. Crown with hazelnuts and gold; serve at 12 °C.

## Plating & Service
Cut with a hot dry knife; the caramel layer should bow, not run.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Individual 7 cm domes glaze the same; milk-chocolate-passion version keeps every ratio.
