# Pate Sucree Vanille, Ferrandi Base

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The house sweet dough: sablage method for a crisp, sandy shell that holds blind-baking without shrinking — the backbone of every tart on board.

## Ingredients (serves 2 x 24 cm tart rings)
- T55 flour — 250 g (100%)
- Cold butter 82%, cubed — 150 g (60%)
- Icing sugar — 95 g (38%)
- Almond flour — 30 g (12%)
- Whole egg — 50 g (20%)
- Fine sea salt — 2 g (0.8%)
- Vanilla bean, seeds only — 1/2 pod

## Method
1. Paddle flour, salt and cold butter on speed 1 until a fine sand forms, 3–4 min; no visible butter lumps.
2. Add icing sugar, almond flour and vanilla seeds; mix 30 s.
3. Add egg, mix just until the dough gathers — do not develop gluten.
4. Fraiser once with the palm on the bench, flatten to 2 cm, wrap; rest 12 h at 4 °C.
5. Roll to 2.5 mm between guitar sheets, line buttered rings, dock; freeze 20 min.
6. Blind-bake at 160 °C fan for 18–22 min to an even hazelnut blond; brush inside with beaten yolk and return 2 min to seal.

## Plating & Service
Store baked shells in an airtight box with silica up to 48 h; fill à la minute for a crack on the fork.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap 30 g flour for cocoa for a chocolate shell; for nut-free, replace almond flour with rice flour at 100%.
