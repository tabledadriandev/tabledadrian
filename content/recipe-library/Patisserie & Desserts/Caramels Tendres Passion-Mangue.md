# Caramels Tendres Passion-Mangue

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Fruit caramels where purée replaces half the cream — sharp tropical acidity inside a butterscotch chew; cooked one degree higher to offset the fruit water.

## Ingredients (serves 60 caramels)
- Sugar — 300 g
- Glucose — 160 g
- Cream 35% — 150 g
- Passion fruit purée — 100 g
- Mango purée — 60 g
- Butter — 90 g
- Fleur de sel — 2 g

## Method
1. Warm cream and purées together to 60 °C.
2. Cook sugar and glucose to 175 °C amber.
3. Deglaze carefully — fruit spits harder than cream.
4. Cook to 119 °C, stirring constantly; fruit caramels catch at the base.
5. Butter and salt in off heat; frame, set 12 h at 18 °C.
6. Cut and wrap; store below 20 °C.

## Plating & Service
The bright counterpoint in the caramel box; label the flavours for the steward.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Raspberry-yuzu on the same ratio; vegan: coconut cream, plant butter, 120 °C target.
