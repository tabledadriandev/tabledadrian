# Biscuit Joconde

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The flexible almond sponge of the Opéra — thin, moist, rollable, and it drinks imbibage without dissolving.

## Ingredients (serves 2 x 40x30 cm sheets)
- Whole eggs — 200 g
- Icing sugar — 150 g
- Almond flour — 150 g
- T55 flour — 40 g
- Egg whites — 130 g
- Sugar — 25 g
- Melted butter — 30 g

## Method
1. Whip whole eggs, icing sugar and almond flour 8 min to a thick ribbon.
2. Whip whites with sugar to a soft bec d'oiseau.
3. Fold flour into the almond mass, then the meringue in two passes, then the melted butter.
4. Spread 550 g per 40 x 30 cm mat at exactly 4 mm with an offset palette.
5. Bake 6–7 min at 210 °C — fast and hot keeps it flexible; it is done when it springs back.
6. Flip onto sugared paper immediately and cool covered.

## Plating & Service
Cut to entremets frames; sheets freeze flat for a month between papers.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cocoa joconde: replace the 40 g flour with 30 g flour + 20 g cocoa; pistachio: swap 50 g almond for pistachio flour.
