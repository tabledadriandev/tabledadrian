# Souffle Chaud au Grand Marnier

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The classic hot soufflé on a pastry-cream base — rises straight, holds two minutes, and forgives nothing; timing is the ingredient.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Crème pâtissière, warm — 300 g
- Grand Marnier — 45 g
- Orange zest — 1
- Egg whites — 240 g
- Sugar — 90 g
- Butter (moulds) — 30 g
- Sugar (moulds) — 40 g
- Icing sugar — 10 g
- Candied orange dice — 30 g

## Method
1. Butter 6 ramekins in upward strokes; sugar-coat fully; chill.
2. Whisk Grand Marnier and zest into the warm (40 °C) crème pâtissière; fold in candied orange.
3. Whip whites, raining in sugar from soft peaks, to a firm bird's beak.
4. Fold a third into the base vigorously, the rest gently in two passes.
5. Fill to the rim, skim flat, thumb the inner edge clean — this trench makes the straight rise.
6. Bake at 185 °C static 14–16 min, no door; dust and run.

## Plating & Service
March them to the table the moment they emerge — announce the dessert 20 minutes ahead and make the guests wait, never the soufflé.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Base takes chocolate (80 g melted 70% instead of alcohol) or raspberry (100 g reduced purée); prepped moulds and base hold 4 h ahead.
