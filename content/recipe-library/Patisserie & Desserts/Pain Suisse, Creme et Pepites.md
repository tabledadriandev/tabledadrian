# Pain Suisse, Creme et Pepites

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Laminated rectangles carrying crème pâtissière and dark chips — the boulangerie's quiet best-seller, worth doing properly.

## Ingredients (serves 10 pieces)
- Pâte à croissant, laminated — 900 g
- Crème pâtissière, firm — 280 g
- Dark chocolate chips — 120 g
- Gilding: yolk 30 g, cream 15 g
- Syrup 30°B — 30 g

## Method
1. Roll dough to 3.5 mm, a 32 x 40 cm sheet.
2. Spread crème pâtissière over half (lengthwise); rain chips into the cream.
3. Fold the bare half over; press gently; freeze 15 min.
4. Cut 4 x 16 cm bars; tray, gild.
5. Proof 2 h at 26 °C; gild again; bake 170 °C fan 16–18 min.
6. Syrup-flash at the oven door.

## Plating & Service
Breakfast or goûter; the cream keeps the crumb moist into the afternoon.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap chips for griottes; matcha crème pât with white chips for colour.
