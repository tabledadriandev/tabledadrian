# Millefeuille Vanille a la Minute

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Caramelized inverse-puff sheets and vanilla diplomate assembled to order — the crispest sixty seconds in pâtisserie, plated on its side.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Feuilletage inversé, baked caramelized sheets — 3 x (30 x 12 cm)
- Crème diplomate vanille (firm, 8 g gelatin base) — 600 g
- Icing sugar — 15 g
- Vanilla powder — 1 g
- Caramel shards — 20 g

## Method
1. Bake puff between trays at 175 °C fan 35 min; dust icing sugar, caramelize at 210 °C 3 min; cool flat.
2. Trim sheets with a serrated knife to identical rectangles; cut portion widths BEFORE assembly.
3. Pipe diplomate bulbs in offset rows on the first layer; stack the second; repeat; cap with the third, caramel-side up.
4. Assemble maximum 30 min before service.
5. Plate each portion standing on its side — the modern cut that keeps layers intact under the fork.
6. Dust one edge with vanilla powder; caramel shard on top.

## Plating & Service
The pastry-section exam: if the fork crushes the layers, the puff was under-caramelized.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Praliné mousseline version; strawberry slices between bulbs in June.
