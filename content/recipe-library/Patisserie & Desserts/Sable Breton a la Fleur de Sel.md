# Sable Breton a la Fleur de Sel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Thick, baking-powder-lifted salted butter sablé baked inside the ring — the sturdy, crumbly pedestal for tartes modernes and petits gâteaux.

## Ingredients (serves 2 x 22 cm discs or 12 rings)
- T55 flour — 235 g (100%)
- Salted butter, soft — 170 g (72%)
- Sugar — 150 g (64%)
- Egg yolks — 70 g (30%)
- Baking powder — 10 g (4.3%)
- Fleur de sel — 2 g (0.9%)

## Method
1. Cream butter and sugar 2 min; add yolks, emulsify.
2. Add sifted flour, baking powder and fleur de sel; mix to just combined.
3. Roll to 5 mm between sheets; chill 2 h; cut discs to fit rings.
4. Bake inside buttered rings at 160 °C fan 18–20 min — the ring is essential, it spreads without it.
5. Cool 10 min before unmoulding; the crumb is fragile warm.

## Plating & Service
Top with a piped ring of ganache montée and fresh berries for a 5-minute assembled tarte moderne.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Brown the butter and re-solidify for noisette depth; press 40 g crushed pistachios into the raw disc.
