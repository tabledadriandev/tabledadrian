# Galette des Rois, Frangipane Noisette

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Inverse puff, hazelnut frangipane, hidden fève and a chevron glaze scored freehand — January's ritual, built to rise straight and shine like furniture.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Feuilletage inversé, 2 discs 24 cm x 2.5 mm — 550 g
- Frangipane noisette (hazelnut version) — 450 g
- Fève — 1
- Gilding: yolk 60 g, cream 20 g, pinch salt
- Syrup 30°B — 40 g

## Method
1. Pipe frangipane in a spiral on disc one, 3 cm border; bury the fève off-centre.
2. Water-brush the border; cap with disc two; press out air from centre outward; seal without stretching.
3. Chill 1 h; flip the galette (flatter face up); trim to a clean circle, scallop the edge with knife-backs.
4. Gild; chill 30 min; gild again; score chevrons or rosace with a paring knife tip; pierce 5 vents.
5. Bake 175 °C fan 40–45 min on a heavy tray; syrup-flash at the door for mirror shine.
6. Rest 20 min; serve warm.

## Plating & Service
Youngest guest under the table calls the slices — the ritual travels well to a yacht saloon.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Apple-frangipane layer for the fruit camp; pistachio-griotte galette as the modern option.
