# Macaron Chocolat-Passion

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Cocoa shells hiding a milk-chocolate passion ganache — the tropical-cacao alliance that converts macaron sceptics.

## Ingredients (serves 20 macarons)
- Macaron shells, cocoa — 40
- Milk couverture 40% — 160 g
- Passion fruit purée — 90 g
- Glucose — 10 g
- Butter — 30 g
- Cocoa powder — 2 g

## Method
1. Warm passion purée with glucose to 60 °C.
2. Emulsify onto the melted milk couverture in thirds — the acid base splits if rushed.
3. Blend in butter at 38 °C; crystallize at 16 °C to piping texture.
4. Pipe, cap, press to the rim.
5. Mature 24 h at 4 °C; dust one edge with cocoa before boxing.

## Plating & Service
Serve mid-box: after fruit, before caramel — its acidity bridges both.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Yuzu-dark chocolate on the same ratio (purée 80 g to 70% couverture 140 g + 20 g more butter).
