# Choux Sans Gluten, Riz et Mais

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Gluten-free choux on rice flour and cornstarch that puffs hollow and crisp — the platform for GF éclairs and profiteroles nobody suspects.

## Ingredients (serves 30 choux)
- Water — 125 g
- Oat drink (or milk) — 125 g
- Plant butter (or butter) — 100 g
- Salt — 3 g
- Sugar — 5 g
- Rice flour, fine — 90 g
- Cornstarch — 55 g
- Psyllium powder — 2 g
- Whole eggs — 250 g

## Method
1. Boil liquids, fat, salt and sugar.
2. Add the combined flours, starch and psyllium in one go; dry the panade 3 min to 75 °C — GF panade needs the full dry.
3. Paddle 2 min to cool; add eggs in four stages to the slow-ribbon stage.
4. Pipe 4 cm choux; rest 15 min (GF batter relaxes into smoother domes).
5. Bake 165 °C fan 35–38 min without opening; dry 6 min at 150 °C.
6. Cool; fill as any choux.

## Plating & Service
Run GF profiteroles for coeliac guests in the same service as the classics — same sauce, same ceremony.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Craquelin (rice-flour version) hides any surface flaws; dairy-free as written with plant fats.
