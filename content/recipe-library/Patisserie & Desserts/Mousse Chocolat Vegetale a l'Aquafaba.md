# Mousse Chocolat Vegetale a l'Aquafaba

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Chickpea-water meringue folded into 70% chocolate — a vegan mousse with genuine airiness that no one clocks as plant-based.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Aquafaba (chickpea water), reduced by a third — 150 g
- Sugar — 60 g
- Cream of tartar — 1 g
- Dark couverture 70% (dairy-free) — 200 g
- Plant milk (oat) — 60 g
- Vanilla — 1/2 pod
- Fleur de sel — 0.5 g
- Cocoa nibs — 15 g

## Method
1. Reduce aquafaba to concentrate protein; chill completely.
2. Melt couverture with oat milk and vanilla to 45 °C; cool to 35 °C — the folding window.
3. Whip aquafaba with tartar to soft peaks; rain in sugar; whip to firm gloss, 6–8 min.
4. Fold a third into the chocolate briskly, the rest in two gentle passes.
5. Pot into glasses; set 4 h at 4 °C.
6. Salt flakes and nibs to serve.

## Plating & Service
Serve in the same coupe as the dairy version — parity of presentation is the point.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Praliné vegan mousse: 60 g praliné into the chocolate; the aquafaba must be firm-peak or the mousse slumps.
