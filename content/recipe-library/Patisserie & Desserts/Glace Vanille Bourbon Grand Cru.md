# Glace Vanille Bourbon Grand Cru

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Three-pod vanilla ice cream on a custard base at 0.35% stabilizer — dense chew, slow melt, the benchmark scoop beside every tatin.

## Ingredients (serves 1 kg (10 portions))
- Whole milk — 600 g
- Cream 35% — 220 g
- Egg yolks — 80 g
- Sucrose — 100 g
- Dextrose — 45 g
- Milk powder 0% — 35 g
- Stabilizer (LBG/guar 2:1) — 3.5 g
- Bourbon vanilla pods — 3, split
- Salt — 1 g

## Method
1. Infuse split pods in milk and cream at 85 °C, 20 min lid on; scrape seeds back in.
2. Dry-blend stabilizer with 30 g sucrose.
3. Rebuild to 85 °C whisking in yolks, sugars, milk powder, then the stabilizer blend at 45 °C on the way up.
4. Hold 85 °C for 2 min; blend, chinois, blast-chill.
5. Mature 12 h; churn; extract at −6 °C into a chilled bac.
6. Harden at −18 °C 4 h before service.

## Plating & Service
Quenelle at −12 °C between two spoons dipped in warm water; pods rinse, dry and go to the vanilla-sugar jar.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fold in 80 g crushed praliné at extraction; lighter: milk to 700 g, cream to 120 g, add 10 g milk powder.
