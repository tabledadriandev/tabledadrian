# Ganache d'Enrobage 70 pour Cent

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The 1:1 dark ganache with glucose and butter — pourable at 35 °C, truffle-firm at 16 °C; one recipe, three uses.

## Ingredients (serves covers 1 entremets or 40 truffles)
- Dark couverture 70% — 300 g
- Cream 35% — 300 g
- Glucose syrup — 30 g
- Butter — 45 g
- Fleur de sel — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Heat cream and glucose to 80 °C.
2. Pour over chopped couverture in thirds, stirring tight circles from the centre until glossy and elastic.
3. At 38 °C, blend in butter and salt.
4. Use at 35 °C to glaze, 28 °C to pipe, or set 12 h at 16 °C to hand-roll truffles.
5. Never refrigerate uncovered — condensation dulls the surface.

## Plating & Service
Poured over an entremets on a rack it self-levels to a soft-shine coat 2 mm thick.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Infuse the cream with rosemary or espresso; for a firmer frame filling, shift to 2:1 chocolate:cream.
