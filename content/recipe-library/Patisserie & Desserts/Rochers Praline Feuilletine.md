# Rochers Praline Feuilletine

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Milk-chocolate praliné mass folded with feuilletine and roasted nibs, scooped and dipped in tempered dark — the two-bite trolley crunch.

## Ingredients (serves 30 rochers)
- Praliné noisette — 200 g
- Milk couverture 40%, melted — 120 g
- Cocoa butter — 15 g
- Feuilletine — 80 g
- Roasted chopped hazelnuts — 50 g
- Dark couverture for dipping, tempered — 300 g
- Fleur de sel — 1 g

## Method
1. Mix praliné, milk couverture and cocoa butter at 40 °C; cool to 24 °C, stirring — it must pre-crystallize.
2. Fold in feuilletine, nuts and salt.
3. Scoop 15 g mounds onto silicone; chill 20 min until handleable.
4. Temper the dark couverture (45 → 27 → 31 °C).
5. Fork-dip each rocher; tap off excess; set at 16 °C for 20 min.
6. Store at 16–18 °C, never the fridge.

## Plating & Service
Coffee-tray or petit-four course; a proper snap then praliné melt is the quality check.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> White-chocolate dip with freeze-dried raspberry; GF certified if the feuilletine is (or swap to crisped rice).
