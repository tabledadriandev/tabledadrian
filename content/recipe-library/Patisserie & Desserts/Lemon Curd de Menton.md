# Lemon Curd de Menton

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Whole-egg curd cooked direct on the flame to 84 °C, butter-blended — brighter and lighter than the crémeux, no gelatin, spoon-ready.

## Ingredients (serves 550 g (2 tarts or 10 verrines))
- Lemon juice — 160 g
- Lemon zest — 3 lemons
- Whole eggs — 150 g
- Sugar — 160 g
- Butter, cold — 120 g
- Salt — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Rub zest into sugar 1 min.
2. Whisk with eggs and juice in a saucepan; cook on medium, whisking without pause, to 84 °C and thick.
3. Pass through a chinois onto the cold butter; blend 1 min.
4. Film-contact; chill 3 h.
5. Use within 5 days.

## Plating & Service
Verrines with crushed meringue and basil; or the fast filling when there is no time to set a crémeux.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Bergamot or yuzu for 30% of the juice; vegan: 25 g cornstarch replaces eggs, boil 1 min, plant butter at the end.
