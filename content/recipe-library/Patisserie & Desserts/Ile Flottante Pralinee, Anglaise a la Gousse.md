# Ile Flottante Pralinee, Anglaise a la Gousse

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Poached meringue island on cold vanilla anglaise, roofed in caramel threads and praliné — the grandmother dessert with sharpened technique.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Egg whites — 200 g
- Sugar — 100 g
- Crème anglaise: milk 500 g, yolks 120 g, sugar 90 g, vanilla 2 pods
- Caramel for threads: sugar 150 g, glucose 30 g
- Praliné noisette, fluid — 60 g
- Caramelized almonds, crushed — 30 g

## Method
1. Anglaise to 83 °C; strain, chill fully.
2. Whip whites with sugar to firm gloss; pipe into buttered 8 cm dome moulds.
3. Steam-poach at 90 °C (oven, bain-marie, covered) 8–10 min until just set and bouncy; cool in moulds.
4. Cook caramel to 160 °C; rest 2 min, then draw threads over an oiled rolling pin into nests.
5. Pour anglaise into shallow bowls; float an island in each.
6. Drizzle fluid praliné over the domes; crown with caramel nest and crushed almonds.

## Plating & Service
Assembled in 3 minutes at the pass; anglaise and islands hold separately 24 h.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pistachio-praliné variant; microwave-poach islands 25 s at 600 W in a pinch — honestly, it works.
