# Pates de Fruits Framboise

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Raspberry fruit jellies on yellow pectin, cooked to 75°Brix and cast in a frame — stained-glass squares that hold a year of summer.

## Ingredients (serves 60 squares)
- Raspberry purée — 500 g
- Sugar (1) — 50 g + yellow pectin 12 g
- Sugar (2) — 480 g
- Glucose — 100 g
- Tartaric acid solution 50% — 8 g
- Crystal sugar for coating — 100 g

## Method
1. Warm purée to 45 °C; whisk in pectin dry-blended with sugar (1).
2. Boil; add sugar (2) in thirds, then glucose.
3. Cook to 106–107 °C (75°Brix on the refractometer), stirring the base always.
4. Off heat, stir in tartaric acid; pour immediately into a 20 cm frame — it sets in minutes.
5. Cure 12 h at 18 °C uncovered.
6. Cut 2.5 cm squares; roll all faces in crystal sugar.

## Plating & Service
Petit-four trays and guest gift boxes; keeps 4 weeks airtight, unsugared faces re-coated as needed.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Apricot, cassis or quince identically (adjust acid: sweeter fruits need the full 8 g); passion fruit cut with 20% apple purée for pectin support.
