# Biscuit Cuillere

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Separated-egg piped sponge with a pearl-sugar crust — the charlotte wall that must be light yet stand structural duty.

## Ingredients (serves 40 ladyfingers or 2 charlotte cartouches)
- Egg whites — 180 g
- Sugar — 150 g
- Egg yolks — 100 g
- T55 flour — 90 g
- Cornstarch — 60 g
- Icing sugar for dusting — 40 g

## Method
1. Whip whites to soft peaks, add sugar in three rains, whip to firm gloss.
2. Fold in yolks briefly — 5 turns maximum.
3. Fold in sifted flour and starch in two light passes.
4. Pipe 10 cm fingers or joined bands with a 14 mm tip on parchment.
5. Double-dust icing sugar; bake 10–12 min at 180 °C until pale gold and dry-crusted.
6. Slide paper off the tray immediately to stop carry-over drying.

## Plating & Service
Line charlotte moulds with joined bands, imbibed side in; leftovers become tiramisu on crew night.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chocolate: swap 20 g starch for cocoa; GF: rice flour + cornstarch 1:1 with 5 g extra starch.
