# Tarte Passion-Coco

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Coconut dacquoise hidden in a sucrée shell under passion crémeux, coconut chantilly overhead — a tropical tart with French posture.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Pâte sucrée, baked 20 cm shell — 260 g
- Coconut dacquoise disc, 18 cm — 90 g
- Passion crémeux: passion purée 180 g, eggs 150 g, sugar 130 g, gelatin 3 g + 18 g water, butter 160 g
- Coconut chantilly: cream 200 g, coconut purée 80 g, mascarpone 40 g, icing sugar 20 g
- Toasted coconut shavings — 20 g
- Fresh passion fruit — 1

## Method
1. Cook the passion crémeux like a curd to 84 °C, gelatin in, butter blended at 40 °C.
2. Seat the dacquoise disc in the baked shell; cast crémeux over to the rim; set 4 h.
3. Whip the coconut chantilly to firm peaks.
4. Pipe a cloud of chantilly spheres over half the surface.
5. Dress toasted coconut and fresh passion seeds across the exposed crémeux.
6. Serve at 12 °C the same day.

## Plating & Service
For deck lunches: the acidity carries in the heat where chocolate desserts die.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Mango-lime crémeux variation; dairy-free chantilly with whipped coconut cream 30% fat.
