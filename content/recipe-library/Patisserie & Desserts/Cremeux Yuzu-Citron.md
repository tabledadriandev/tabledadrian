# Cremeux Yuzu-Citron

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A butter-mounted citrus curd blended at 40 °C for a mirror texture — sharper than lemon alone thanks to 30% yuzu.

## Ingredients (serves 2 tart fillings or 12 inserts)
- Lemon juice — 120 g
- Yuzu juice — 50 g
- Whole eggs — 170 g
- Sugar — 150 g
- Lemon zest — 2 lemons
- Gelatin 200 bloom — 3 g + 18 g water
- Butter, cold cubes — 200 g

## Method
1. Rub zest into sugar to release oils.
2. Whisk eggs, sugar and juices; cook over medium heat to 84 °C, whisking constantly, until thick.
3. Off heat, add bloomed gelatin; cool to 40 °C — the critical temperature.
4. Blend in cold butter cube by cube for a full 2 min: the long shear makes the shine.
5. Cast into shells or moulds; set 4 h at 4 °C.

## Plating & Service
Pipe into a sucrée shell, blowtorch a meringue crown, finish with fresh yuzu zest.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> All-lime with kaffir leaf infusion for an exotic entremets insert; vegan: 2 g agar boiled in the juice, plant butter at 40 °C.
