# Paris-Brest Praline, Coeur Coulant

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A crown of eight choux with craquelin, praliné mousseline and a liquid praliné heart in every bump — the wheel that wins every trolley.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Pâte à choux — 500 g
- Craquelin discs, 4 cm — 8
- Flaked almonds — 20 g
- Mousseline pralinée — 850 g
- Pure praliné noisette (fluid) — 120 g
- Icing sugar — 10 g
- Caramelized hazelnuts, halved — 16

## Method
1. Pipe eight 5 cm choux in a touching 20 cm ring; cap each with craquelin, scatter flaked almonds between.
2. Bake 165 °C fan 45 min — rings need longer than éclairs; dry 8 min oven ajar.
3. Split horizontally with a serrated knife; the top comes off as a crown.
4. Pipe a rosette of mousseline into each bump; inject 15 g fluid praliné into the centre of each rosette.
5. Pipe a second layer of mousseline peaks; replace the crown lid.
6. Chill 1 h; dust icing sugar; seat hazelnut halves in the seams.

## Plating & Service
Slice at the table into the eight natural portions; each cut releases the liquid centre.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Individual Paris-Brest: 8 cm rings, 12 min less baking; pecan-maple praliné for variety.
