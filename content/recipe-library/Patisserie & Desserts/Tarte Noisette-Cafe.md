# Tarte Noisette-Cafe

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Hazelnut sablée, coffee-caramel layer, whole roasted hazelnuts and a coffee ganache montée — the afternoon-espresso tart.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Pâte sablée noisette, baked 20 cm shell — 270 g
- Coffee caramel: sugar 120 g, cream 120 g, butter 50 g, espresso 30 g, salt 1 g
- Whole roasted hazelnuts — 120 g
- Coffee ganache montée: white couverture 130 g, hot cream 130 g + 30 g coffee beans, cold cream 260 g
- Espresso powder — 1 g
- Gold leaf — 1 flake

## Method
1. Infuse crushed coffee beans in the hot cream 15 min; strain, rebuild weight; make the ganache montée; mature 12 h.
2. Cook the coffee caramel to 106 °C; cool to 35 °C.
3. Fold the hazelnuts through the caramel; fill the shell level.
4. Set 1 h at 4 °C.
5. Whip the coffee ganache; pipe overlapping waves across the tart with a 20 mm flat tip.
6. Dust a whisper of espresso powder; place the gold on one wave crest.

## Plating & Service
Serve at 14 °C with a ristretto — the tart is the sugar course of the coffee itself.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Walnut-maple in autumn (maple syrup replaces 40 g sugar in the caramel); decaf beans for evening charters.
