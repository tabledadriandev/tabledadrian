# Sorbet Mangue-Passion

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Alphonso mango with 15% passion purée for the aromatic top note — creamy from fruit fibre alone, the exotic-plate workhorse.

## Ingredients (serves 1 kg (10 portions))
- Mango purée — 520 g
- Passion fruit purée — 90 g
- Sorbet syrup 60°Brix — 300 g
- Water — 80 g
- Lime juice — 10 g

## Method
1. Blend all cold; target 30°Brix — mango is sweet, hence the extra water.
2. Mature 4 h.
3. Churn to a soft-serve texture at −5.5 °C extraction; mango's fibre gives natural chew.
4. Harden 3 h at −18 °C.
5. Serve at −12 °C with fresh lime zest microplaned over.

## Plating & Service
The centre of an exotic vacherin, or quenelled onto a coconut dacquoise.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 2 g kaffir lime leaf infusion to the syrup; peach-verbena follows identical ratios in summer.
