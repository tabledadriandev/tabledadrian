# Frangipane de Galette

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Two-thirds almond cream, one-third crème pât — softer and custardier than plain crème d'amande, the true galette des rois interior.

## Ingredients (serves fills 1 x 24 cm galette)
- Crème d'amande — 400 g
- Crème pâtissière, smooth — 200 g
- Dark rum — 15 g
- Bitter almond extract — 2 drops
- Orange zest — 1/2 orange

## Method
1. Paddle the crème pâtissière smooth on its own first.
2. Add crème d'amande, rum, extract and zest; mix just to homogeneous.
3. Pipe in a spiral onto the puff base leaving a 3 cm border; press in the fève.
4. Chill 30 min before lidding the galette — a cold filling will not steam-split the seam.
5. Bakes inside the galette at 175 °C fan, ~40 min.

## Plating & Service
Also the filling for pithiviers, jésuites and amandines.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Hazelnut frangipane with 20 g cocoa for a January variation; keeps 3 days at 4 °C.
