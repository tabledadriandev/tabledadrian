# Pate Brisee Fine au Beurre

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> An unsweetened, flaky lining dough for flans and fruit tarts where the filling carries the sugar — stays crisp under wet custards.

## Ingredients (serves 2 x 24 cm tarts)
- T55 flour — 250 g (100%)
- Cold butter — 125 g (50%)
- Fine salt — 5 g (2%)
- Sugar — 10 g (4%)
- Cold water — 60 g (24%)
- Egg yolk — 20 g (8%)

## Method
1. Sand butter into flour, salt and sugar, leaving pea-sized flakes for flake structure.
2. Whisk water with yolk, add in one go, gather with a scraper — max 20 s of mixing.
3. Give one gentle fold, flatten, wrap; rest 4 h minimum at 4 °C.
4. Roll to 2.5 mm, line, dock and freeze before baking.
5. Blind-bake at 170 °C fan 20 min with baking beans, then 5 min empty.

## Plating & Service
The workhorse for flan parisien and tourte bases; keeps 3 days raw at 4 °C or 1 month frozen in sheets.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Use 50/50 butter and cold lard for extra flake (not vegetarian); GF: 60% rice flour, 40% starch plus 3 g psyllium.
