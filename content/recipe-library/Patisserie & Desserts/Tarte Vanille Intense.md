# Tarte Vanille Intense

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Three vanillas in three textures — ganache, whipped ganache, vanilla powder — in one blond shell; deceptive simplicity, maximum haunting.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Pâte sucrée, baked 20 cm shell — 260 g
- Vanilla dark-blond ganache: white couverture 35% — 180 g
- Cream 35% — 160 g
- Vanilla pods — 2
- Ganache montée vanille, matured — 350 g
- Vanilla powder (dried ground pods) — 2 g
- Nappage neutre — 20 g

## Method
1. Infuse pods in hot cream 15 min; emulsify onto the couverture; add scraped seeds.
2. Pour the ganache into the sealed shell at 32 °C; set 2 h at 4 °C.
3. Whip the matured ganache montée to soft-firm peaks.
4. Pipe fat petals with a Saint-Honoré tip in concentric rings over the set ganache.
5. Dust one crescent edge with vanilla powder; dot nappage pearls on three petals.
6. Rest 1 h at 4 °C; serve at 13 °C.

## Plating & Service
Serve as the trolley's quiet centrepiece — guests expecting 'plain vanilla' get converted.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Spike the base ganache with 10 g dark rum; tonka-vanilla for winter charters.
