# Pate de Cacao Sablés Viennois

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Piped W-shaped Viennese sablés in deep cocoa, half-dipped in dark chocolate — tender enough to need no filling.

## Ingredients (serves 30 biscuits)
- Butter, pommade 20 °C — 190 g (100%)
- Icing sugar — 75 g (39%)
- Egg white, room temp — 30 g (16%)
- T55 flour — 200 g (105%)
- Cocoa powder — 25 g (13%)
- Salt — 1.5 g
- Dark couverture, tempered — 150 g
- Vanilla — 1/2 pod

## Method
1. Cream butter, icing sugar, salt and vanilla until light.
2. Add egg white; beat smooth.
3. Fold in sifted flour and cocoa; the dough must stay soft enough to pipe — work fast.
4. Pipe 6 cm W shapes with a 12 mm star tip.
5. Bake 165 °C fan 13–15 min; cool completely.
6. Half-dip each sablé in tempered couverture; set on paper at 16 °C.

## Plating & Service
Coffee-tray royalty; box them for departing guests as the yacht's signature biscuit.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Plain vanilla viennois (drop cocoa, +25 g flour); dip in milk chocolate and crushed hazelnuts.
