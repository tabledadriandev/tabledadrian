# Glace Creme Fraiche d'Isigny

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Cultured-cream ice cream with a lactic bite — the pastry section's secret weapon against very sweet plates.

## Ingredients (serves 900 g (9 portions))
- Whole milk — 450 g
- Crème fraîche d'Isigny — 300 g
- Sucrose — 90 g
- Dextrose — 40 g
- Milk powder 0% — 30 g
- Stabilizer blend — 3.5 g
- Lemon juice — 10 g
- Salt — 1 g

## Method
1. Pasteurize milk with sugars, milk powder and stabilizer (dry-blended) at 85 °C for 2 min.
2. Blast-chill to 4 °C.
3. Blend in crème fraîche, lemon and salt cold — never cook the crème fraîche; its culture is the flavour.
4. Mature 4 h only (the acidity keeps working).
5. Churn, extract at −6 °C, harden.

## Plating & Service
Beside warm apple tatin or roasted apricots — the acidity resets the palate every spoon.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Goat-fromage-frais version for a plated cheese-course dessert; add 40 g honey (minus 30 g sucrose) for tang-and-honey.
