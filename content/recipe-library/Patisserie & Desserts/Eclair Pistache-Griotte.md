# Eclair Pistache-Griotte

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Pistachio mousseline around a hidden griotte compote core, glazed pale green with white chocolate — the Sicily-meets-Paris éclair.

## Ingredients (serves 12 eclairs)
- Pâte à choux — 550 g
- Pistachio mousseline: crème pât 450 g, butter 180 g, pistachio paste 80 g
- Griotte compote: griottes 200 g, sugar 30 g, pectin NH 3 g, kirsch 8 g
- Glaze: white couverture 180 g, cream 90 g, pistachio paste 20 g, nappage 40 g
- Chopped pistachios — 25 g
- Freeze-dried cherry powder — 3 g

## Method
1. Bake éclair shells; cool completely.
2. Cook the griotte compote, boil 1 min with pectin; cool; load into a 6 mm-tip bag.
3. Pipe a core line of compote down each shell first, then surround with mousseline to full weight (60 g total).
4. Warm glaze to 33 °C; dip tops.
5. Dress a line of chopped pistachio down one edge; dust cherry powder on the opposite tip.
6. Hold at 4 °C; serve at 13 °C within 8 h.

## Plating & Service
Cut one open for the trolley display — the red core in green cream sells the batch.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Raspberry core in summer; amarena syrup (10 g) folded into the mousseline for a softer cherry note.
