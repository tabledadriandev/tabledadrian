# Pate a Choux Maitre, Ratio 2-1-1-2

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The master choux: half milk for colour, full drying of the panade, eggs by hydration feel — pipes éclairs, choux and Paris-Brest alike.

## Ingredients (serves approx 40 choux or 24 eclairs)
- Whole milk — 125 g
- Water — 125 g
- Butter — 110 g
- Fine salt — 3 g
- Sugar — 5 g
- T55 flour — 140 g
- Whole eggs — 240–260 g (about 5)

## Method
1. Bring milk, water, butter, salt and sugar to a rolling boil — butter fully melted before the boil.
2. Off heat, add flour in one go; stir, then dry the panade over medium heat 2–3 min until a film forms on the pan and dough reaches 75 °C.
3. Transfer to a mixer, paddle 1 min to release steam.
4. Add eggs in 4 additions; stop at the 'V' ribbon — the dough should close a trench slowly.
5. Pipe on perforated mats: 13 cm éclairs with a 14 mm star tip, or 4 cm choux.
6. Bake at 165 °C fan, no steam valve open, 35–40 min; never open the oven before 30 min. Dry 5 min at 150 °C.

## Plating & Service
Pipe and freeze raw choux on trays; bake from frozen (+5 min) for à la minute service on charter.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> For craquelin tops: 50 g butter, 60 g cassonade, 60 g flour rolled at 1.5 mm. GF: swap flour for 90 g rice flour + 50 g cornstarch.
