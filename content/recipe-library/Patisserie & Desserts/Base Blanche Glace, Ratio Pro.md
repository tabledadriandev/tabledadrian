# Base Blanche Glace, Ratio Pro

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The balanced white ice-cream base: 10% fat, 10.5% MSNF, 18% sugars split for scoopability at −14 °C, stabilizer at 0.4% — flavour it any direction.

## Ingredients (serves 1.1 kg base (12 scoops))
- Whole milk — 640 g
- Cream 35% — 200 g
- Milk powder 0% — 40 g
- Sucrose — 110 g
- Dextrose — 40 g
- Glucose powder DE38 — 30 g
- Egg yolks — 60 g
- Stabilizer blend (LBG/guar) — 4 g (0.4%)
- Salt — 1 g

## Method
1. Dry-blend stabilizer with 30 g of the sucrose.
2. Heat milk, cream and yolks; at 40 °C whisk in all powders and sugars, stabilizer blend last.
3. Pasteurize at 85 °C for 2 min, whisking.
4. Blend 1 min; blast-chill to 4 °C.
5. Mature 12 h at 4 °C — hydration of the stabilizer is where texture is made.
6. Churn to −6 °C extraction; blast-freeze to −18 °C; serve at −12 to −14 °C.

## Plating & Service
The canvas: infuse, swap sugars, add pastes — totals must stay near 37% solids.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vanilla: 2 pods in the infusion. Egg-free: replace yolks with 20 g milk powder + 10 g cream. Never skip maturation.
