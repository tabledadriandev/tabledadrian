# Tarte Figue-Miel de Lavande, Chevre Frais

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Late-summer figs on an almond base with whipped honey-chèvre — the dessert that bridges the cheese course on Provençal charters.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Pâte brisée fine, raw-lined 22 cm — 280 g
- Crème d'amande — 250 g
- Fresh figs, quartered — 600 g
- Lavender honey — 50 g
- Fresh goat cheese — 120 g
- Cream 35% — 60 g
- Thyme leaves — 1 g
- Black pepper — 2 turns

## Method
1. Pipe crème d'amande into the raw shell; arrange fig quarters cut-side up, densely.
2. Drizzle 30 g of the honey; bake at 170 °C fan 38–40 min.
3. Whip chèvre with cream and 20 g honey to soft peaks.
4. Cool tart to warm; quenelle or pipe chèvre cream between the figs.
5. Finish with thyme, pepper and a last thread of honey.

## Plating & Service
Serve slightly warm as dessert or in place of cheese; a glass of Rivesaltes beside it.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: plant butter almond cream and whipped coconut-cashew cream with the same honey weight in maple.
