# Croustillant Praline Feuilletine

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The crackling layer: praliné bound with just enough chocolate to set, feuilletine folded last so every flake stays crisp.

## Ingredients (serves 2 entremets bases (18 cm))
- Praliné noisette 60/40 — 200 g
- Milk couverture 40% — 80 g
- Butter — 20 g
- Feuilletine (crêpes dentelle crumbs) — 100 g
- Fleur de sel — 1 g

## Method
1. Melt couverture and butter to 40 °C.
2. Stir into the praliné with the salt until smooth.
3. Fold in feuilletine gently — over-mixing crushes the flake and kills the crunch.
4. Spread 4 mm inside rings on the biscuit layer; set 20 min at 4 °C.
5. Assemble the entremets on top while the croustillant is firm.

## Plating & Service
The layer guests always ask about; it must be thin — over 5 mm it turns tooth-cracking when frozen.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Keep away from moist creams with a thin chocolate chablonnage brushed on top; GF: use GF corn flakes crushed fine.
