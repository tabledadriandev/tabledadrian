# Sorbet Citron-Basilic

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Menton lemon sorbet blitzed with fresh basil at churning — a pre-dessert that resets eight courses in one spoon.

## Ingredients (serves 900 g (9 portions))
- Lemon juice, fresh — 300 g
- Water — 320 g
- Sorbet syrup 60°Brix — 360 g
- Lemon zest — 2 lemons
- Fresh basil leaves — 12 g
- Egg-white powder (optional, for lightness) — 2 g

## Method
1. Warm 100 g of the water with zest to 60 °C; infuse 10 min, strain into the mix.
2. Blend juice, water, syrup (and egg-white powder); target 28–29°Brix — lemon needs the higher sugar to stay scoopable.
3. Mature 4 h.
4. Blanch basil 5 s, shock, squeeze; blend into the cold base just before churning to keep it electric green.
5. Churn, extract, harden briefly; serve within 48 h while the basil is vivid.

## Plating & Service
One small quenelle in a frozen coupe, olive-oil thread, single basil bud — pre-dessert done.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Lime-shiso or grapefruit-tarragon on the same skeleton; Prosecco float for aperitif service.
