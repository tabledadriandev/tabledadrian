# Sirop de Sorbet 60 Brix

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The standing sorbet syrup: sucrose, glucose powder and stabilizer at 60°Brix — cut it with any fruit purée and churn the same day.

## Ingredients (serves 1.5 kg syrup base)
- Water — 600 g
- Sucrose — 620 g
- Glucose powder DE38 — 220 g
- Dextrose — 60 g
- Sorbet stabilizer (LBG-based) — 9 g
- Lemon juice — 15 g

## Method
1. Dry-blend stabilizer with 100 g of the sucrose.
2. Heat water to 40 °C; whisk in all sugars, then the stabilizer blend.
3. Boil 2 min; add lemon off heat.
4. Blast-chill; mature 12 h at 4 °C.
5. Keeps 3 weeks refrigerated — the section's daily sorbet engine.

## Plating & Service
Standard cut: 550–650 g syrup per 1 kg fruit purée depending on the fruit's own sugar; target 28–32°Brix on the refractometer.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> For very sweet fruit (mango, banana) drop to 450 g syrup + 50 g water; for acidic fruit (cassis, lemon) rise to 700 g.
