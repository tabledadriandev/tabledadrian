# Gelato Pistache de Bronte

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sicilian-style low-overrun pistachio gelato: 8% pure paste, milk-forward, served warmer and softer than French glace.

## Ingredients (serves 1 kg (10 portions))
- Whole milk — 680 g
- Cream 35% — 130 g
- Pure pistachio paste (100%) — 80 g
- Sucrose — 105 g
- Dextrose — 40 g
- Milk powder 0% — 40 g
- Stabilizer blend — 4 g
- Salt — 1.5 g

## Method
1. Dry-blend stabilizer with part of the sucrose.
2. Pasteurize milk, cream, sugars and powders at 85 °C, 2 min.
3. Blend the hot base onto the pistachio paste with the salt, 90 s.
4. Blast-chill, mature 12 h; the paste's oils bind overnight.
5. Churn low-speed if possible (target 25–30% overrun); extract at −5 °C.
6. Serve at −11 °C for the true gelato pull.

## Plating & Service
Serve in a coupe with crushed roasted pistachios and a thread of olive oil.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cut with 20 g almond paste to stretch expensive pistachio honestly; never use green-tinted sweetened pastes.
