# Namelaka Chocolat au Lait

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The 'ultra-creamy' Japanese-French cream: milk, gelatin and a heavy cold-cream charge — softer than crémeux, denser than mousse, pipes in ribbons.

## Ingredients (serves 12 plated portions)
- Milk couverture 40% — 250 g
- Whole milk — 130 g
- Glucose — 7 g
- Gelatin 200 bloom — 2.5 g + 15 g water
- Cream 35%, cold — 260 g

## Method
1. Heat milk and glucose to 80 °C; dissolve the bloomed gelatin.
2. Emulsify onto the melted couverture in three additions.
3. Blend in the cold cream, blade submerged, 1 min.
4. Cast into a piping bag or tray; mature 12 h at 4 °C — it sets to its final texture overnight.
5. Pipe straight from the bag: it holds a soft ridge without whipping.

## Plating & Service
Ribbon it across a plate against roasted banana and a buckwheat crumble; it reads luxurious with zero last-minute work.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dark version: 200 g of 64%, milk to 150 g; yuzu namelaka: replace 50 g milk with yuzu juice added cold.
