# Panna Cotta Vanille Vegetale, Coulis d'Abricot

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Coconut-almond panna cotta set with agar to a true tremble (not a bounce), under a sharp apricot coulis — the vegan set-cream done to restaurant wobble.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Coconut cream — 400 g
- Almond drink (barista) — 300 g
- Sugar — 70 g
- Agar-agar — 2.2 g (0.3% — the tremble ratio)
- Vanilla pods — 1.5
- Salt — 0.5 g
- Coulis: apricot purée 200 g, sugar 20 g, lemon 10 g
- Toasted almond flakes — 15 g

## Method
1. Whisk agar into the cold liquids with sugar and vanilla seeds and pods.
2. Bring to a full boil for 90 s — under-boiled agar never sets.
3. Rest 5 min; remove pods; pour into glasses slightly tilted for the diagonal line.
4. Set 3 h at 4 °C.
5. Boil the coulis 1 min; chill; pour the second layer level.
6. Almond flakes at service.

## Plating & Service
The 0.3% agar ratio is the difference between silk and rubber — weigh to the decigram.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Verbena infusion instead of vanilla; berry coulis in June; for gelatin service use 8 g gelatin per kg instead.
