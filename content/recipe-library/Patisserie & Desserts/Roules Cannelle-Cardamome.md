# Roules Cannelle-Cardamome

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Pain-au-lait dough spiraled with brown-butter cinnamon-cardamom sugar, knotted Scandinavian-style and syrup-glossed.

## Ingredients (serves 12 rolls)
- Pain au lait dough, cold — 900 g
- Filling: butter 120 g (beurre noisette, re-cooled), cassonade 110 g, cinnamon 8 g, ground cardamom 4 g
- Gilding: egg 30 g, milk 15 g
- Cardamom syrup: sugar 60 g, water 60 g, 4 pods crushed
- Pearl sugar — 30 g

## Method
1. Beat the filling to a spreadable paste.
2. Roll dough to 3 mm (30 x 45 cm); spread filling fully; letter-fold in three.
3. Cut 2 cm strips; twist and knot each around two fingers, tail tucked through the centre.
4. Proof 1.5 h at 26 °C; gild.
5. Bake 170 °C fan 16–18 min; brush hot with cardamom syrup; pearl sugar on top.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm with coffee at elevenses; the knot shape guarantees filling in every bite.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Saffron dough for Lucia season; orange-zest-anise filling as a southern variation.
