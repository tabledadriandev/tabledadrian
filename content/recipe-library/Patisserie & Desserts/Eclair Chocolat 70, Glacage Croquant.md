# Eclair Chocolat 70, Glacage Croquant

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Dark chocolate crémeux filling with a thin snapping chocolate-nib glaze instead of fondant — an éclair for people who find éclairs too sweet.

## Ingredients (serves 12 eclairs)
- Pâte à choux — 550 g
- Crémeux chocolat 70% — 650 g
- Croquant glaze: dark couverture 200 g, grapeseed oil 30 g, caramelized cocoa nibs 30 g
- Cocoa powder — 5 g
- Fleur de sel — pinch per éclair

## Method
1. Bake 13 cm éclairs at 165 °C fan 38–40 min; cool.
2. Pipe crémeux at 14 °C into the shells, 55 g each — it should resist slightly.
3. Melt couverture with oil to 40 °C, stir in nibs, cool to 32 °C.
4. Dip tops for a 1.5 mm coat that sets to a snap in 5 min at 16 °C.
5. Salt each éclair with three flakes; dust one tip with cocoa.

## Plating & Service
The glaze cracks audibly under the fork — serve at 14 °C, never colder or the crémeux stiffens.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Milk-chocolate crémeux with the dark shell glaze for contrast; GF choux with rice flour works with the same weights.
