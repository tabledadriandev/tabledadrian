# Pavlova aux Agrumes d'Hiver

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Mallow-hearted meringue crown with mascarpone chantilly and five winter citrus — January's answer to berry season.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Egg whites — 150 g
- Sugar — 150 g
- Icing sugar — 150 g
- Cornstarch — 10 g
- White vinegar — 5 g
- Chantilly mascarpone — 400 g
- Citrus: orange 2, blood orange 2, pink grapefruit 1, mandarin 2, kumquat 6
- Citrus gel: juice 150 g, sugar 30 g, agar 1.5 g
- Pistachios — 20 g
- Mint tips — 8

## Method
1. Whip whites, rain in sugar to a dense gloss; fold in icing sugar, starch and vinegar.
2. Mound into a 20 cm crown with a shallow crater; bake 100 °C for 2 h, cool in the oven overnight.
3. Boil the citrus gel; set, blend to a fluid gel.
4. Segment all citrus, halve kumquats; drain on paper.
5. Fill the crater: gel dots, chantilly cloud, citrus mosaic by colour.
6. Pistachio and mint to finish; serve within 2 h.

## Plating & Service
Assemble at the last moment — meringue and citrus are enemies on the clock.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Summer version with berries and elderflower; the shell keeps 3 days airtight.
