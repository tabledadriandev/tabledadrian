# Tarte Framboise-Pistache de Sicile

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Pistachio cream baked into the shell, raspberries filled with pistachio praliné, a green-on-red geometry that eats as good as it photographs.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Pâte sucrée, raw-lined 22 cm ring — 300 g
- Pistachio cream: butter 90 g, sugar 90 g, pistachio flour 60 g, almond flour 30 g, egg 90 g
- Raspberry compotée with pectin NH — 120 g
- Fresh raspberries — 400 g
- Pistachio praliné (fluid) — 80 g
- Chopped Sicilian pistachios — 20 g
- Nappage neutre — 25 g

## Method
1. Bake shell with the pistachio cream at 165 °C, 25 min; cool.
2. Spread the compotée 2 mm over the cooled cream.
3. Stand raspberries hole-up in tight rings covering the tart.
4. Pipe pistachio praliné into every raspberry cavity — the hidden payoff.
5. Nappage the fruit lightly; rim the tart edge with chopped pistachio.
6. Chill 45 min; serve day-of.

## Plating & Service
Cut with a serrated blade, gentle strokes — clean slices sell the geometry.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Strawberry-pistachio in May; a few drops of rose water in the compotée for an Ispahan echo.
