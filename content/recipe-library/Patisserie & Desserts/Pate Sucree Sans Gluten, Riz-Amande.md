# Pate Sucree Sans Gluten, Riz-Amande

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A GF sweet dough engineered with rice flour, starch and psyllium so it rolls, lines and blind-bakes exactly like classic sucrée.

## Ingredients (serves 2 x 22 cm tart rings)
- Fine rice flour — 150 g (60%)
- Cornstarch — 70 g (28%)
- Almond flour — 60 g (24%)
- Psyllium husk powder — 4 g (1.6%)
- Icing sugar — 90 g (36%)
- Cold butter — 140 g (56%)
- Whole egg — 55 g (22%)
- Salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Blend all dry ingredients; sablage with cold butter to fine sand.
2. Add egg, press to a dough; the psyllium needs 5 min to hydrate — rest 10 min, then knead 20 s until pliable.
3. Rest 2 h at 4 °C (not longer — it dries).
4. Roll to 3 mm between sheets; line rings; freeze 30 min — freezing is non-negotiable for GF dough.
5. Blind-bake from frozen at 155 °C fan 22–25 min; yolk-seal and bake 2 min more.

## Plating & Service
Indistinguishable from wheat sucrée under citrus or chocolate tarts — announce it proudly to GF guests.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: replace almond flour with more rice flour +10 g butter; add 8 g cocoa (minus 8 g starch) for chocolate shells.
