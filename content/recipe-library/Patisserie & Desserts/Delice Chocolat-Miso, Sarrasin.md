# Delice Chocolat-Miso, Sarrasin

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Namelaka ribbon, miso caramel and toasted buckwheat on a single dark plate — umami pâtisserie in three strokes.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Namelaka chocolat au lait — 300 g
- Miso caramel: sugar 120 g, cream 120 g, butter 40 g, white miso 15 g
- Buckwheat, toasted (kasha) — 40 g
- Cocoa streusel — 90 g
- Dark chocolate tuile shards — 6
- Buckwheat ice cream (base blanche infused with 50 g kasha) — 300 g

## Method
1. Infuse toasted buckwheat in the warm ice-cream base 20 min; strain, churn.
2. Cook the miso caramel to 106 °C; cool to piping texture.
3. Ribbon namelaka in a long S across each plate with a flat 20 mm tip.
4. Pipe miso-caramel dots along the ribbon; scatter streusel and kasha in a crescent.
5. Quenelle the ice cream on the streusel; stand a tuile shard against it.
6. Serve at once — namelaka at 12 °C, plate cold.

## Plating & Service
For guests who claim dessert is 'too sweet' — the miso and buckwheat argue otherwise.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Sesame variant: black sesame praliné dots and sesame ice cream; GF with buckwheat streusel.
