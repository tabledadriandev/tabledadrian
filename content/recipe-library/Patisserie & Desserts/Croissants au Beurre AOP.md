# Croissants au Beurre AOP

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The full croissant protocol from lamination to bake — honeycomb interior, shattering crust, and a proof schedule built around guest breakfast.

## Ingredients (serves 12 croissants)
- Pâte à croissant (laminated, 3 single tours) — 1.1 kg
- Gilding: egg yolk 40 g, cream 20 g, pinch salt

## Method
1. Roll the laminated dough to 3.5 mm, resting 10 min mid-roll if it fights back.
2. Cut 9 x 27 cm triangles (~85 g); notch the base, roll without stretching, tip tucked under.
3. Tray on parchment, 6 per tray, gild lightly.
4. Proof at 26–27 °C, 75% humidity, 2.5–3 h — a gentle shake shows a jelly wobble when ready.
5. Gild again; bake at 175 °C fan dropping to 165 °C, 17–18 min to deep amber.
6. Cool 20 min on wire; serve within the hour.

## Plating & Service
Line the breakfast basket with linen, croissants seam-down; never stack them warm.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Day-two croissants become almond croissants: syrup dip, frangipane, flaked almonds, 12 min at 170 °C.
