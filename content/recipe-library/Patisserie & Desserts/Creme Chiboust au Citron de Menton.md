# Creme Chiboust au Citron de Menton

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Lemon pastry cream collapsed into hot Italian meringue — the ethereal, torch-able crown of a Saint-Honoré.

## Ingredients (serves tops 2 Saint-Honore (12 pers))
- Lemon juice (Menton) — 150 g
- Whole milk — 100 g
- Egg yolks — 80 g
- Sugar (base) — 50 g
- Cornstarch — 22 g
- Gelatin 200 bloom — 5 g + 30 g water
- Lemon zest — 2 lemons
- Italian meringue: egg whites 120 g, sugar 200 g, water 60 g

## Method
1. Cook a pastry cream with lemon juice, milk, yolks, base sugar, cornstarch and zest; boil 1 min.
2. Beat in bloomed gelatin off heat; hold the base warm at 45 °C.
3. Cook sugar and water to 121 °C; pour over whites at soft peaks; whip to 40 °C, not colder.
4. Fold a third of the warm meringue into the warm base to loosen, then the rest, gently — both masses warm is the whole secret.
5. Pipe or mould immediately; set 3 h at 4 °C. Torch the surface at service.

## Plating & Service
Caramel-glazed choux around a chiboust dome: the classic Saint-Honoré, best assembled under 4 h before service.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Passion fruit chiboust: replace lemon juice 1:1; the cream must be eaten same-day — meringue weeps by hour 12.
