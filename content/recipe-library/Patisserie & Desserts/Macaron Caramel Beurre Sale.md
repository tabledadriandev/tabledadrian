# Macaron Caramel Beurre Sale

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Toffee-coloured shells around a caramel filling cooked to 108 °C so it chews without escaping — the box's inevitable favourite.

## Ingredients (serves 20 macarons)
- Macaron shells, caramel-tinted — 40
- Sugar — 150 g
- Glucose — 25 g
- Cream 35%, hot — 150 g
- Salted butter — 110 g
- Fleur de sel — 1.5 g
- Vanilla — 1/4 pod

## Method
1. Dry caramel to 182 °C; deglaze with hot cream in three additions.
2. Cook back to 108 °C exactly — the pipe-but-not-run temperature.
3. Cool to 40 °C; blend in butter, salt, vanilla; crystallize 4 h at 16 °C.
4. Beat smooth; pipe generous domes, cap and press.
5. Mature 24 h; a few salt flakes on each dome before service.

## Plating & Service
Always the first flavour to sell out — batch it double.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Miso-caramel (12 g white miso, drop the salt); coffee-caramel with 3 g espresso in the cream.
