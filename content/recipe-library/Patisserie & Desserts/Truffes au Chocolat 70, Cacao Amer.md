# Truffes au Chocolat 70, Cacao Amer

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Hand-rolled bitter-dusted truffles on an infusion-ready ganache — deliberately irregular, dusted dark, gone by midnight.

## Ingredients (serves 40 truffles)
- Dark couverture 70% — 300 g
- Cream 35% — 250 g
- Glucose — 25 g
- Butter — 50 g
- Dark rum or nothing — 15 g
- Bitter cocoa powder — 80 g
- Fleur de sel — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Boil cream and glucose; emulsify onto the couverture in thirds.
2. Butter, salt (and rum) in at 38 °C; blend briefly.
3. Set in a shallow tray 12 h at 16 °C.
4. Scoop 12 g balls; roll fast between cool palms — irregular is correct.
5. Roll through sifted cocoa; shake off excess in a sieve.
6. Store at 16 °C; bring to 18 °C to serve.

## Plating & Service
With coffee and digestifs; a copper bowl of them beats any plated mignardise for atmosphere.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Infuse the cream: earl grey (8 g), tonka (1/2 bean) or piment d'Espelette (1 g); roll in praliné grains instead of cocoa.
