# Gianduja Lait Maison

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Hazelnut paste and milk chocolate ground silky and set into a block — slice it, melt it, pipe it; the Turin-Lyon axis in one jar.

## Ingredients (serves 600 g block)
- Roasted hazelnuts — 250 g
- Icing sugar — 150 g
- Milk couverture 40%, melted — 200 g
- Cocoa butter — 20 g
- Fleur de sel — 1 g

## Method
1. Grind warm roasted hazelnuts with icing sugar to a fluid paste, 6–8 min.
2. Add melted couverture and cocoa butter at 40 °C; grind 2 min more to full smoothness.
3. Temper the mass down to 24 °C over a cold bain-marie, stirring (seeding the sugar keeps it simple).
4. Cast into a framed tray 2 cm deep; set 12 h at 16 °C.
5. Cut cubes for the trolley or re-melt at 40 °C into creams.

## Plating & Service
Shave over warm crêpes, cube onto petit-four trays, or melt into namelaka for a gianduja cream.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dark gianduja: 64% couverture and +30 g icing sugar; vegan with oat-milk couverture.
