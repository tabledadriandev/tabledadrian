# Madeleines Sans Gluten, Miel-Citron

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rice-and-almond madeleines with the same overnight rest and thermal-shock hump — moist longer than wheat versions thanks to the almond.

## Ingredients (serves 20 madeleines)
- Fine rice flour — 90 g
- Almond flour — 60 g
- Cornstarch — 20 g
- Sugar — 100 g
- Honey — 35 g
- Whole eggs — 150 g
- Beurre noisette, cooled — 140 g
- Baking powder (GF) — 6 g
- Lemon zest — 1
- Salt — 1 g

## Method
1. Whisk eggs, sugar, honey and zest 2 min.
2. Fold in the dry mix; stream in the beurre noisette.
3. Rest 12 h at 4 °C — non-negotiable for the hump.
4. Pipe into buttered shells; chill trays 15 min.
5. Bake 210 °C for 4 min, then 170 °C for 7–8 min.
6. Unmould hot; serve within the hour.

## Plating & Service
Tea-time proof that GF baking can be a pleasure, not a penalty.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Orange-blossom instead of lemon; dip humps in tempered dark chocolate once cool.
