# Clafoutis d'Abricot Tiede, Sorbet Lait d'Amande

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Individual apricot clafoutis with an almond-milk sorbet melting into the warm custard — the June dessert that costs nothing and pleases everyone.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Ripe apricots — 500 g
- Whole eggs — 150 g
- Sugar — 100 g
- T55 flour — 50 g
- Almond flour — 30 g
- Whole milk — 200 g
- Cream 35% — 100 g
- Butter + sugar for moulds — 20 g/20 g
- Amaretto — 10 g
- Almond-milk sorbet: almond milk 500 g, sugar 90 g, dextrose 40 g, stabilizer 2.5 g — 300 g needed

## Method
1. Butter-sugar six 10 cm gratin dishes; seat apricot halves cut-up, snugly.
2. Blend eggs, sugars, flours, milk, cream and amaretto to a smooth batter; rest 30 min.
3. Pour around the fruit to two-thirds height.
4. Bake 180 °C fan 25–28 min — puffed, golden, centre just set.
5. Rest 10 min; serve warm in the dish, sorbet quenelled on at the table.

## Plating & Service
The custard must tremble; over-baked clafoutis is an omelette with fruit.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cherry clafoutis, stones in, in season; GF: replace flour with 40 g rice flour + 10 g cornstarch.
