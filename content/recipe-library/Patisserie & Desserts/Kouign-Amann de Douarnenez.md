# Kouign-Amann de Douarnenez

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bread dough laminated with salted butter AND sugar — the Breton caramel-crusted flake that out-indulges every croissant.

## Ingredients (serves 12 individual)
- T55 flour — 400 g (100%)
- Water — 240 g (60%)
- Fresh yeast — 8 g (2%)
- Salt — 8 g (2%)
- Salted butter (tourage) — 300 g (75%)
- Sugar (lamination) — 250 g (63%)
- Sugar + butter for moulds — 60 g/40 g

## Method
1. Mix a lean dough; bulk 1 h; chill 1 h.
2. Lock in the butter block; give one single tour plain.
3. Second tour: shower a third of the sugar across the dough before folding; repeat for tours three and four — sugar goes IN the folds.
4. Roll to 4 mm; cut 10 cm squares; fold corners to centre; seat in buttered-sugared muffin rings.
5. Proof 45 min at 26 °C only — sugar dough over-proofs fast.
6. Bake 180 °C fan 30–35 min until lacquered mahogany; unmould IMMEDIATELY onto silicone or they weld.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm, caramel-side up; the crackle should be audible across the saloon.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Apple slivers folded into the centre; day-old ones revive 4 min at 180 °C — better than fresh, some say.
