# Entremets Rose-Framboise-Litchi

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rose mousse around a raspberry-lychee confit heart on almond dacquoise — a florist's entremets that stays on the right side of perfume.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Dacquoise amande disc, 16 cm — 1
- Confit insert: raspberry purée 150 g, lychee purée 100 g, sugar 40 g, pectin NH 5 g
- Rose mousse: white couverture 180 g, milk 130 g, gelatin 5 g + 30 g water, rose water 12 g, cream whipped 300 g
- Glaze: neutral mirror tinted powder-pink — 300 g
- Fresh raspberries — 12
- Lychees — 4
- Rose petals, unsprayed — 6

## Method
1. Boil confit 90 s with pectin; cast in a 14 cm ring; freeze.
2. Mousse: hot milk and gelatin onto white couverture, rose water at 30 °C, fold in cream at 28 °C.
3. Build inverted in an 18 cm ring: mousse, confit insert, mousse, dacquoise; freeze 6 h.
4. Glaze pink at 30 °C on the frozen cake.
5. Thaw 3 h at 4 °C; crown with raspberries (some filled with confit), lychee petals and rose.
6. Serve at 12 °C.

## Plating & Service
For anniversaries and proposals — the yacht's romance dessert; keep rose at 12 g, never more.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap rose for verbena for a subtler garden note; individual heart moulds for Valentine charters.
