# Pate a Croissant, Levure et Tourage 50

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Laminated yeasted dough at 50% tourage butter with 3 single folds — honeycomb crumb, glass crust, the yacht breakfast benchmark.

## Ingredients (serves 12 croissants)
- T45 gruau flour — 500 g (100%)
- Cold water — 115 g (23%)
- Cold whole milk — 115 g (23%)
- Sugar — 60 g (12%)
- Fine salt — 10 g (2%)
- Fresh yeast — 20 g (4%)
- Soft butter (détrempe) — 40 g (8%)
- Dry butter 84% (tourage) — 250 g (50%)

## Method
1. Mix all détrempe ingredients 4 min speed 1 + 4 min speed 2 — under-developed on purpose; dough at 24 °C.
2. Bulk 30 min, flatten to a 25 x 25 cm square, freeze 20 min then 12 h at 4 °C.
3. Beat tourage butter to 17 x 25 cm at 14 °C; lock in with dough edges sealed.
4. Three single tours with 45 min at 4 °C between each; keep the seam consistent.
5. Roll to 3.5 mm; cut 9 x 27 cm triangles (~85 g), roll without tension.
6. Proof 2.5 h at 26–27 °C (never above 28 °C or the butter bleeds); gild, bake 165 °C fan 17–18 min.

## Plating & Service
Bake for 08:00 service; croissants peak 20 minutes out of the oven — plan proofing backwards from guest wake-up.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Same dough makes pains au chocolat (8 x 14 cm rectangles, two batons) and bicolour versions with 10% cocoa-tinted lamination layer.
