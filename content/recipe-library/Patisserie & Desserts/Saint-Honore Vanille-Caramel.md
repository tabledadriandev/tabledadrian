# Saint-Honore Vanille-Caramel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Puff base, caramel-dipped choux and Saint-Honoré-tip chiboust and chantilly waves — the technical summit of choux pastry, built in strict order.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Feuilletage inversé, 3 mm disc 22 cm — 260 g
- Pâte à choux — 300 g
- Crème pâtissière — 300 g
- Dry caramel: sugar 300 g, glucose 60 g, water 90 g
- Chantilly mascarpone — 350 g
- Crème chiboust or diplomate vanille — 400 g
- Vanilla bean — 1

## Method
1. Dock the puff disc; pipe a choux spiral on it and 14 x 3 cm choux beside; bake 170 °C fan ~40 min.
2. Fill the small choux with crème pâtissière.
3. Cook caramel to 165 °C; dip choux tops, set caramel-side down in dome moulds for a glass finish; then dip bases and glue around the disc rim.
4. Fill the centre with vanilla diplomate or chiboust, doming slightly.
5. Pipe alternating chantilly waves with the Saint-Honoré tip from centre to rim.
6. Assemble under 4 h before service; caramel is the countdown clock.

## Plating & Service
Present whole; portion with a caramel-cracking confidence at the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Rose-raspberry Saint-Honoré (rose chantilly, raspberry inserts in choux); humidity is the enemy — never build on a rainy quay.
