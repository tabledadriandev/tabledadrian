# Choux Craquelin Mangue-Passion

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Domed choux with exotic crémeux core, mango cubes and a passion glaze — the sunshine choux for deck service.

## Ingredients (serves 12 choux)
- Pâte à choux — 450 g
- Yellow craquelin (butter 50 g, cassonade 60 g, flour 60 g, turmeric 0.5 g) — 12 discs
- Passion-mango crémeux: purée mix 200 g, eggs 150 g, sugar 120 g, gelatin 3 g, butter 150 g
- Fresh mango brunoise — 120 g
- Passion glaze: passion purée 60 g, nappage 120 g
- Coconut shavings, toasted — 15 g

## Method
1. Bake craquelin choux 165 °C fan 35 min; cool.
2. Cook the exotic crémeux to 84 °C, butter in at 40 °C; chill 4 h.
3. Cut choux lids; pipe crémeux, fold mango brunoise into the centre of each.
4. Pipe a smooth crémeux dome above the rim; freeze 20 min.
5. Warm passion glaze to 30 °C; dip each dome for a bright mirror; replace lids ajar.
6. Finish with toasted coconut; serve at 12 °C.

## Plating & Service
Line up on a slate with lime zest grated across the whole row at the pass.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Raspberry-lychee winter version; dairy-free crémeux with plant butter, same 84 °C cook.
