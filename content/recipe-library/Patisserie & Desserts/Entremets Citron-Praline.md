# Entremets Citron-Praline

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Lemon mousse with a praliné coulant core over sablé breton — acid opening, roasted-nut finish; the sleeper hit of the repertoire.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Sablé breton disc, baked in 16 cm ring — 1
- Praliné coulant insert: praliné 150 g, cream 50 g, gelatin 1 g
- Lemon mousse: lemon curd base 300 g, gelatin 5 g + 30 g water, italian meringue 100 g, cream whipped 220 g
- Nappage neutre, yellow-tinted — 200 g
- Candied lemon zest — 20 g
- Caramelized hazelnut halves — 10

## Method
1. Warm cream and gelatin into the praliné; cast a 12 cm disc; freeze.
2. Lemon mousse: melt gelatin into warm curd, fold in meringue then cream at 26 °C.
3. Invert-build in a 16 cm ring: mousse, praliné insert, mousse; freeze 5 h; the breton stays outside the mould.
4. Glaze the frozen mousse with yellow nappage at 35 °C; seat on the sablé breton.
5. Thaw 3 h at 4 °C; crown with candied zest and hazelnuts.
6. Serve at 12 °C; the praliné core should flow when cut.

## Plating & Service
Cut at the table for the lava reveal; the breton base must be assembled under 8 h before service or it softens.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Yuzu-sesame version (black sesame praliné) for a Japanese accent.
