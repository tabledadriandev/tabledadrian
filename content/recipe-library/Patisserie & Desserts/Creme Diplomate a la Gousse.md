# Creme Diplomate a la Gousse

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Gelatin-stabilized crème pât lightened with cream — cuts clean in fruit tarts and holds a dome in a display cabinet at sea.

## Ingredients (serves 1 kg (2 tarts or 30 choux))
- Crème pâtissière, warm (35 °C) — 600 g
- Gelatin 200 bloom — 6 g, bloomed in 36 g cold water
- Cream 35%, softly whipped — 400 g
- Vanilla bean — 1/2, seeds

## Method
1. Melt bloomed gelatin at 50 °C; whisk into the 35 °C crème pâtissière with vanilla seeds.
2. Cool the base to 25–28 °C — warmer melts the cream, colder sets lumps.
3. Fold in the soft-whipped cream in two additions with a whisk, then finish with a maryse.
4. Use within 20 min: pipe into tarts or choux, then set 2 h at 4 °C.

## Plating & Service
The strawberry-tart cream: pipe a 1.5 cm bed, set, then shingle fruit — slices stay architectural.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fold in 100 g crushed raspberries for a fruit diplomate; agar version (1.6 g, boiled into the base) for gelatin-free service.
