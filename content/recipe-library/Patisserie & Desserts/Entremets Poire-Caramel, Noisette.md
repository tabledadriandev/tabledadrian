# Entremets Poire-Caramel, Noisette

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Caramel bavaroise, roasted-pear insert, hazelnut dacquoise and croustillant — autumn's entremets in bronze velvet.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Dacquoise noisette disc 16 cm + croustillant praliné 120 g
- Pear insert: roasted pear dice 200 g, pear purée 100 g, sugar 20 g, pectin NH 4 g, vanilla 1/2 pod
- Caramel bavaroise: sugar 120 g (dry caramel), cream 120 g, milk 150 g, yolks 60 g, gelatin 6 g, cream whipped 300 g
- Velvet spray caramel-tinted: milk couverture 100 g + cocoa butter 100 g
- Caramelized hazelnuts — 12
- Dried pear chip — 5

## Method
1. Roast pear dice at 200 °C 12 min; boil with purée, sugar-pectin, vanilla 1 min; cast 14 cm; freeze.
2. Dry caramel to 180 °C, deglaze with cream, build an anglaise with milk and yolks to 83 °C; gelatin in; cool to 24 °C; fold whipped cream.
3. Invert-build: bavaroise, pear insert, bavaroise, dacquoise-croustillant; freeze 6 h.
4. Velvet-spray bronze on the frozen cake.
5. Thaw 3 h; dress hazelnuts and pear chips along one arc.
6. Serve 12 °C with a jug of warm caramel on the side.

## Plating & Service
October to December headliner; pairs with a 20-year tawny.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Apple-calvados insert variant; the bavaroise takes coffee (8 g) beautifully.
