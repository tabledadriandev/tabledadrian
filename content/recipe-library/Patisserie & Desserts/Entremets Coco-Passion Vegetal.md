# Entremets Coco-Passion Vegetal

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A fully vegan entremets: coconut mousse on agar-kappa, passion gel insert, almond-free coconut dacquoise on aquafaba — mirror-glazed and proud.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Vegan coconut dacquoise: aquafaba 120 g, sugar 100 g, dessicated coconut 90 g, rice flour 30 g — 1 disc 16 cm
- Passion insert: purée 200 g, sugar 40 g, agar 2.5 g
- Coconut mousse: coconut cream 400 g, sugar 70 g, agar 3 g, kappa carrageenan 1 g, whipped coconut cream 200 g
- Vegan mirror glaze: water 100 g, sugar 200 g, glucose 100 g, coconut condensed milk 100 g, white vegan couverture 150 g, agar 4 g — yellow tint
- Fresh passion fruit — 2
- Toasted coconut — 15 g

## Method
1. Bake the vegan dacquoise 25 min at 160 °C; cool.
2. Boil passion insert with agar 90 s; cast 14 cm; chill set, then freeze.
3. Boil 200 g coconut cream with sugar, agar and kappa 90 s; cool to 35 °C; fold in whipped coconut cream fast — agar sets at 38 °C, work quickly.
4. Invert-build: mousse, insert, mousse, dacquoise; freeze 6 h.
5. Boil glaze components 2 min; blend; use at 35 °C on the frozen cake (agar glazes run warmer).
6. Thaw 3 h at 4 °C; passion seeds and coconut to finish.

## Plating & Service
Serve at 10 °C; agar textures firm faster than gelatin — slightly warmer service flatters them.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Mango-lime colourway; the kappa is optional but gives the gelatin-like elasticity back.
