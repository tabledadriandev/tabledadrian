# Religieuse Caramel Beurre Sale

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Two-tier choux nun filled with salted-caramel diplomate, dressed in caramel fondant with piped butter-cream collerette — a Left-Bank classic done straight.

## Ingredients (serves 10 pieces)
- Pâte à choux — 600 g
- Craquelin — 10 large + 10 small discs
- Caramel diplomate: crème pât 500 g, caramel beurre salé 150 g, gelatin 4 g + 24 g water, cream whipped 300 g
- Caramel fondant: fondant 300 g, caramel beurre salé 50 g, glucose 15 g
- French buttercream — 120 g
- Fleur de sel — pinch each

## Method
1. Pipe 10 x 6 cm and 10 x 3 cm choux with craquelin; bake 165 °C fan (40 min large, 30 min small).
2. Whisk caramel and melted gelatin into warm crème pât; cool to 25 °C; fold in whipped cream.
3. Fill both sizes generously through the base.
4. Glaze both in 35 °C caramel fondant; while tacky, seat the small choux on the large.
5. Pipe a buttercream collerette of vertical teardrops around the joint.
6. Chill 1 h; salt-flake the crown; serve at 13 °C same day.

## Plating & Service
A dessert with presence — one per guest, no plate clutter, just a fork.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chocolate or coffee religieuse from the same architecture; mini versions (4 cm + 2 cm) for petit-four service.
