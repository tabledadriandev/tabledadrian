# Flan Parisien a la Vanille

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Tall Parisian flan: brisée shell, vanilla custard at exactly 8% starch, burnished top — served cold the next day, never day-of.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Pâte brisée fine, lining a 18 cm x 4.5 cm ring — 320 g
- Whole milk — 700 g
- Cream 35% — 300 g
- Egg yolks — 140 g
- Sugar — 180 g
- Cornstarch — 80 g
- Vanilla pods — 2
- Butter — 30 g

## Method
1. Line the tall ring, dock, freeze 30 min; blind-bake 15 min at 170 °C.
2. Infuse vanilla in milk and cream at 85 °C, 15 min.
3. Whisk yolks, sugar, starch; temper and cook the custard, boiling 90 s — it must be thick and glossy.
4. Beat in butter; pour hot into the hot shell to 5 mm below the rim.
5. Bake at 200 °C fan 30–35 min until the top blisters mahogany but the centre still trembles.
6. Cool 2 h, then 12 h at 4 °C before unringing — patience is the recipe.

## Plating & Service
Cut cold in tall wedges; serve at 10 °C when the custard is a dense silk.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Coconut flan: half the milk as coconut milk; chocolate flan: whisk 120 g dark couverture into the hot custard.
