# Feuilletage Inverse au Beurre Charentais

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Inverse puff — the butter wraps the détrempe — for the shattering, melt-first texture on galettes and Saint-Honoré bases.

## Ingredients (serves 1.3 kg dough)
- Beurre manié: dry butter 84% — 375 g
- Beurre manié: T55 flour — 150 g
- Détrempe: T55 flour — 350 g
- Détrempe: cold water — 145 g
- Détrempe: salt — 11 g
- Détrempe: soft butter — 110 g
- Détrempe: white vinegar — 5 g

## Method
1. Paddle beurre manié ingredients smooth; shape 20 x 30 cm, chill to 13 °C.
2. Mix détrempe to a smooth ball, shape 20 x 15 cm, rest 2 h at 4 °C.
3. Wrap the détrempe inside the beurre manié like an envelope.
4. Double tour, rest 1.5 h; double tour, rest 1.5 h; final single tour.
5. Rest 12 h at 4 °C. Roll at 15 °C dough temperature — inverse puff tears if too cold.
6. Bake at 170 °C fan; expect 8–9x rise on a 2 mm sheet.

## Plating & Service
Reserve for showpieces: galette des rois, vol-au-vent, caramelized bases — the flake is audibly crisper than classic puff.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> If Charentais dry butter is unavailable, freeze standard 82% butter sheets 10 min between tours to compensate.
