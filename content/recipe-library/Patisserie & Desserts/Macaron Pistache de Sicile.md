# Macaron Pistache de Sicile

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Green-gold shells with a pistachio ganache built on pure paste — no almond extract shortcuts, just roasted Bronte depth.

## Ingredients (serves 20 macarons)
- Macaron shells, pistachio green — 40
- White couverture 35% — 130 g
- Cream 35% — 130 g
- Pure pistachio paste — 45 g
- Butter — 20 g
- Chopped pistachios — 15 g
- Fleur de sel — 0.3 g

## Method
1. Boil cream; emulsify onto couverture; blend in pistachio paste, butter and salt at 38 °C.
2. Mature at 16 °C to piping texture, ~3 h.
3. Pipe domes on half the shells; rim with chopped pistachio so the edge shows green.
4. Cap and press; mature 24 h at 4 °C.
5. Serve at 15 °C — cold mutes pistachio badly.

## Plating & Service
Pairs in the box opposite framboise: eat pink, then green, in that order.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fold 10 g griotte confit into the centre; sesame-praliné version swaps paste 1:1.
