# Torte Chocolat Sans Farine, Creme Crue

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The flourless chocolate torte: 70% chocolate, butter, eggs, sugar, nothing else — cracked top, truffle heart, unwhipped cold cream over each slice.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Dark couverture 70% — 250 g
- Butter — 200 g
- Whole eggs — 250 g
- Sugar — 180 g
- Cocoa powder — 20 g
- Fleur de sel — 1.5 g
- Cream 35%, raw and cold — 200 g

## Method
1. Melt chocolate and butter to 45 °C.
2. Whisk eggs and sugar to a light ribbon, 4 min.
3. Fold the masses together with cocoa and salt.
4. Pour into a buttered, cocoa-dusted 20 cm ring on silicone.
5. Bake 160 °C fan 30–35 min — set rim, quivering 6 cm centre; it firms as it cools.
6. Cool 2 h; serve at room temperature, never chilled.

## Plating & Service
A wedge, a pour of icy unwhipped cream, three salt flakes — restraint as luxury.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Espresso (10 g) in the melt; keeps 3 days and improves by day two.
