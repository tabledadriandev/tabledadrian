# Ganache Montee Vanille Ivoire

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A 1:3 white-chocolate-to-cream overnight ganache whipped to a satin mousse — the modern pâtisserie topping that pipes like chantilly and tastes like crème brûlée.

## Ingredients (serves tops 2 tarts or 24 choux)
- White couverture 35% — 150 g
- Cream 35% (hot) — 150 g
- Cream 35% (cold) — 300 g
- Vanilla beans — 1.5, split and scraped
- Gelatin 200 bloom — 2 g + 12 g water (optional, for warm climates)

## Method
1. Infuse vanilla in the hot cream at 85 °C, 10 min; re-weigh and top up to 150 g.
2. Pour over the couverture (and melted gelatin), wait 1 min, emulsify from the centre.
3. Blend in the cold cream; film-contact and mature 12 h at 4 °C — non-negotiable for crystallization.
4. Whip cold on medium speed to soft-firm peaks: 2–3 min; it over-whips fast, stop early.
5. Pipe with a Saint-Honoré tip; holds 6 h refrigerated.

## Plating & Service
The vanilla tart topper: pipe petals over a ganache-filled shell, dust one edge with vanilla powder.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Infuse the hot cream with coffee beans, earl grey or tonka instead of vanilla; ratio stays 1:1:2.
