# Pates de Fruits Mangue-Passion

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Tropical pâtes de fruits balancing low-pectin mango with sharp passion — cooked to 107 °C for the clean bite that snaps then melts.

## Ingredients (serves 60 squares)
- Mango purée — 350 g
- Passion fruit purée — 150 g
- Sugar (1) — 50 g + yellow pectin 13 g
- Sugar (2) — 470 g
- Glucose — 100 g
- Tartaric acid solution 50% — 7 g
- Crystal sugar — 100 g

## Method
1. Warm the purées to 45 °C; rain in the pectin blend whisking hard.
2. Boil; add sugar (2) in three additions, then glucose.
3. Cook to 107 °C stirring constantly — tropical purées scorch faster than red fruit.
4. Acid in off heat; cast into the frame at once.
5. Cure 12 h; cut; sugar all faces.

## Plating & Service
Pair one red, one gold jelly per petit-four plate for the colour story.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Coconut-lime variant (coconut purée + 15% apple); store dry — humidity weeps them at sea, box with silica.
