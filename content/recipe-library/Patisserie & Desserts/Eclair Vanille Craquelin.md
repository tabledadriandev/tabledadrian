# Eclair Vanille Craquelin

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Craquelin-topped éclairs for a perfectly even shell, filled with double-vanilla diplomate and glazed with white-chocolate vanilla fondant.

## Ingredients (serves 12 eclairs)
- Pâte à choux — 550 g
- Craquelin: butter 50 g, cassonade 60 g, T55 flour 60 g
- Crème diplomate vanille — 700 g
- Glaze: white couverture 150 g, cream 75 g, nappage 50 g, vanilla powder 1 g
- Vanilla bean, dried, ground — for dusting

## Method
1. Roll craquelin 1.5 mm; freeze; cut 13 x 3 cm bands.
2. Pipe 13 cm éclairs with a 14 mm star tip; lay craquelin bands on top.
3. Bake 165 °C fan 38–40 min, no door opening; dry 5 min at 150 °C; cool on wire.
4. Pierce three holes underneath; fill each éclair with 55–60 g diplomate until heavy in the hand.
5. Warm glaze to 32 °C; dip the tops in one steady drag, clean edges with a finger.
6. Set 15 min; dust one end with vanilla powder.

## Plating & Service
Serve within 6 h at 12 °C; the craquelin keeps the top crisp far longer than fondant alone.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Same shell, chocolate diplomate + dark glaze; coffee version with 8 g espresso in the cream.
