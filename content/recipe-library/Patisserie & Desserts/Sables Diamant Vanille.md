# Sables Diamant Vanille

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Slice-and-bake vanilla shortbread rolled in crystal sugar — the diamond edge glitters, the crumb dissolves; the tin that fills itself.

## Ingredients (serves 40 biscuits)
- Butter, pommade — 190 g (95%)
- T55 flour — 200 g (100%)
- Cornstarch — 25 g (12%)
- Icing sugar — 80 g (40%)
- Vanilla seeds — 1 pod
- Fleur de sel — 1.5 g
- Crystal sugar (coating) — 60 g
- Egg white for brushing — 15 g

## Method
1. Cream butter, icing sugar, vanilla and salt — smooth, not aerated.
2. Add flour and starch; mix to just-combined.
3. Roll into 3 cm logs; chill 3 h firm.
4. Brush logs with white; roll in crystal sugar to a full diamond coat.
5. Slice 1 cm coins; bake 160 °C fan 16–18 min, pale gold edges only.
6. Cool on the tray 5 min — they set fragile.

## Plating & Service
Coffee-tray staple; stores 10 days airtight, dough logs freeze a month.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cocoa diamants (25 g cocoa for 25 g flour); citrus zest or espresso versions from the same log.
