# Financiers Noisette Brulee

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Beurre noisette pushed to the edge of dark, hazelnut flour in place of half the almond — a financier with the flavour of the pan.

## Ingredients (serves 20 financiers)
- Egg whites — 180 g (100%)
- Icing sugar — 180 g (100%)
- Almond flour — 70 g (39%)
- Roasted hazelnut flour — 70 g (39%)
- T55 flour — 60 g (33%)
- Beurre noisette, strained, 45 °C — 160 g (89%)
- Honey — 15 g
- Salt — 1.5 g

## Method
1. Cook butter to a deep hazelnut brown, solids toasted; strain and cool to 45 °C.
2. Whisk whites just to loosen (no foam) with icing sugar, flours, salt.
3. Stream in the warm beurre noisette and honey; rest 2 h at 4 °C.
4. Pipe into buttered financier moulds; press a hazelnut half into each.
5. Bake 175 °C fan 13–15 min to golden with a moist heart.
6. Cool 5 min; unmould warm.

## Plating & Service
With coffee, or as the pedestal for roasted apricots and crème fraîche ice cream as a plated dessert.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pistachio financiers (pistachio flour + 20 g paste); GF: replace flour with rice flour 1:1.
