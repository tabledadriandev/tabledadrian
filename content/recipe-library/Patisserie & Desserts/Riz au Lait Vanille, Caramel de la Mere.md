# Riz au Lait Vanille, Caramel de la Mere

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Round-grain rice cooked slowly in vanilla milk to a loose cream, served barely warm under a sheet of dark caramel — bistro comfort, palace execution.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Round rice (Arborio or dessert rice) — 160 g
- Whole milk — 1000 g
- Cream 35% — 150 g
- Sugar — 90 g
- Vanilla pods — 2
- Salt — 1 g
- Caramel: sugar 120 g, water 30 g, cream 90 g, salted butter 25 g
- Whipped cream, soft — 100 g

## Method
1. Blanch rice 2 min in water; drain.
2. Simmer rice in milk, vanilla and salt at a lazy bubble 35–40 min, stirring often, until tender but flowing.
3. Off heat, stir in sugar and the 150 g cream; the rice keeps drinking — keep it loose.
4. Cool to 40 °C; fold in the soft whipped cream.
5. Caramel to deep amber; deglaze with cream and butter.
6. Serve in shallow bowls, caramel poured in a full sheet on top at the table.

## Plating & Service
Barely warm, never fridge-cold; the caramel sheet cracks under the first spoon.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Coconut-milk version (dairy-free) with mango; a spent vanilla pod in the sugar jar perfumes the next batch.
