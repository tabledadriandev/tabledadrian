# Charlotte aux Framboises et a la Violette

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Biscuit cuillère palisade, raspberry bavaroise, a whisper of violet, crowned with a kilo of fresh fruit — the grand old lady, dressed young.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Biscuit cuillère bands + 2 discs — 350 g
- Framboise imbibage: syrup 80 g, raspberry eau-de-vie 10 g
- Raspberry bavaroise: purée 300 g, sugar 60 g, gelatin 8 g + 48 g water, violet extract 2 drops, cream whipped 350 g
- Fresh raspberries — 400 g
- Nappage neutre — 30 g
- Crystallized violets — 8

## Method
1. Line an 18 cm charlotte ring with the biscuit band, sugar-crust out; seat one imbibed disc.
2. Bavaroise: warm a third of the purée, dissolve gelatin, mix back, add violet; at 24 °C fold the cream.
3. Pour half the bavaroise; sink 150 g raspberries; second imbibed disc; remaining bavaroise.
4. Set 5 h at 4 °C.
5. Unring; pile fresh raspberries in concentric rings on top; nappage lightly.
6. Finish with crystallized violets; tie with a ribbon for the table.

## Plating & Service
Serve at 10 °C, cut with a serrated knife through the biscuit wall first.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pear-caramel charlotte in winter; violet is optional — two drops or none.
