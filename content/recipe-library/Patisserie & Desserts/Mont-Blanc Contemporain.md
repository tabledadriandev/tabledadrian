# Mont-Blanc Contemporain

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Crisp meringue, chantilly heart, and chestnut vermicelli piped in a full sphere — the Mont-Blanc rebuilt light with a cassis surprise.

## Ingredients (serves 8 individual)
- French meringue discs 6 cm: whites 100 g, sugar 100 g, icing sugar 100 g
- Cassis gel: purée 100 g, sugar 20 g, pectin 2 g
- Chantilly mascarpone — 300 g
- Chestnut vermicelli: chestnut paste 250 g, chestnut cream 150 g, butter 60 g, rum 15 g
- Icing sugar — 10 g
- Candied chestnut pieces — 40 g

## Method
1. Bake meringue discs at 90 °C for 90 min — dry, not coloured.
2. Boil the cassis gel; chill; load a fine-tip bag.
3. On each meringue: a dot of cassis gel, a tall chantilly dome, candied chestnut shards.
4. Beat vermicelli paste smooth; pipe with a multi-hole vermicelli tip round and round into a full nest covering the cream.
5. Dust one flank with icing sugar for the snow line.
6. Serve within 4 h at 10 °C.

## Plating & Service
Autumn's signature; the cassis cuts the chestnut's sweetness — non-negotiable.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Matcha vermicelli version; GF by nature — announce it.
