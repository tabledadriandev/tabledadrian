# Financiers, Almond & Orange Blossom

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Dense, moist brown-butter almond cakes with a whisper of orange blossom — five minutes of work, days of praise.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Butter — 150 g
- Ground almonds — 100 g
- Icing sugar — 150 g
- Plain flour — 60 g
- Egg whites — 150 g (5)
- Orange blossom water — 8 ml
- Orange zest — 3 g
- Honey — 15 g
- Flaked almonds — 20 g
- Fine salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Cook the butter to a deep hazelnut noisette; strain and cool to 45°C.
2. Whisk ground almonds, icing sugar, flour and salt.
3. Stir in the unwhipped egg whites, then honey, orange blossom and zest.
4. Add the brown butter in a thread, mixing to a glossy batter; rest 1 hour at 4°C.
5. Pipe into buttered financier moulds; scatter flaked almonds.
6. Bake at 190°C for 12–15 minutes until golden with darker edges; cool 5 minutes and unmould.

## Plating & Service
Stack warm on a linen napkin beside coffee; they stay moist 3 days in a tin — ideal mid-charter ammunition.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Brown-butter + hazelnut flour is the deeper variant; raspberry pressed into each before baking adds a sour jewel.
