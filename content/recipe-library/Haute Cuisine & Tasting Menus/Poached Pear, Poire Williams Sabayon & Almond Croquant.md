# Poached Pear, Poire Williams Sabayon & Almond Croquant

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A whole vanilla-poached pear under warm eau-de-vie sabayon with shattering almond glass.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Williams pears, firm — 6
- Water — 1 l
- Caster sugar — 250 g (syrup) + 60 g (sabayon) + 100 g (croquant)
- Vanilla pod — 1
- Lemon — 1
- Egg yolks — 90 g (5)
- Poire Williams eau-de-vie — 50 ml
- Flaked almonds — 80 g
- Butter — 10 g
- Creme fraiche — 60 g

## Method
1. Peel pears whole, leaving stems; rub with lemon.
2. Poach in syrup (water, 250 g sugar, vanilla, lemon peel) at 85°C for 20–30 minutes until a skewer glides; cool in the syrup.
3. Croquant: caramelise 100 g sugar to amber, stir in almonds and butter, pour thin on paper, cool and crack into shards.
4. Sabayon: whisk yolks, 60 g sugar, 60 ml poaching syrup and the eau-de-vie over a 75°C bain-marie to a thick, warm ribbon, 5 minutes.
5. Drain pears and stand each in a warm shallow bowl.
6. Coat with sabayon, plant croquant shards, and add one cold spoon of creme fraiche.

## Plating & Service
The pear must stand upright and glossy, sabayon poured to a skirt — serve while the foam is warm.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Alcohol-free: use pear juice reduced by half in the sabayon; hazelnut croquant is the autumn swap.
