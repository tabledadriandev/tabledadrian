# King Crab, Kohlrabi & Green Apple Remoulade

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sweet crab bound in a bright remoulade under crisp kohlrabi veils — clean, cold and elegant.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- King crab leg meat, cooked — 300 g
- Kohlrabi — 1 (300 g)
- Granny Smith apple — 1 (150 g)
- Egg yolk — 18 g
- Dijon mustard — 8 g
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Grapeseed oil — 90 ml
- Dill, chopped — 6 g
- Radish, fine slices — 30 g
- Trout roe (optional) — 20 g
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Espelette pepper — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Make a lemon mayonnaise: whisk yolk, mustard and 10 ml lemon juice, then thread in oil; season.
2. Pick through the crab for shell; fold with two-thirds of the mayonnaise, dill and Espelette.
3. Shave kohlrabi into 1 mm discs on a mandoline; brine 10 minutes in 2% salt water, then dry — they should be pliable and glassy.
4. Julienne the apple and toss with the remaining lemon juice.
5. Press crab into a 6 cm ring on each chilled plate; top with apple julienne.
6. Shingle kohlrabi discs over the crab like scales, brushing with a little mayonnaise to gloss.
7. Garnish with radish, optional roe and a few dill fronds.

## Plating & Service
The kohlrabi dome should look like a pale green rose; serve with a chilled Chablis within 10 minutes of building.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Poached lobster or white crab from brown legs both work; vegan mayo converts it fully dairy-and-egg-free.
