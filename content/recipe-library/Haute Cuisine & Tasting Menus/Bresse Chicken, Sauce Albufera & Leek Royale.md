# Bresse Chicken, Sauce Albufera & Leek Royale

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Poached-then-glazed Bresse breast with the ivory foie-enriched Albufera — old Escoffier gold, plated modern.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Bresse chicken breasts, skin on — 4 (720 g)
- Chicken stock, superior — 1 l
- Double cream — 150 ml
- Foie gras — 60 g
- Madeira — 40 ml
- Butter — 70 g
- Leeks — 300 g
- Whole eggs — 100 g (2)
- Whole milk — 100 ml
- White pepper — 1 g
- Fine salt — 8 g
- Chervil — 8 sprigs

## Method
1. Leek royale: stew sliced leeks in 20 g butter until melting; blend with eggs, milk and salt, pass, pour into buttered moulds, steam at 90°C for 15 minutes until just set.
2. Poach the breasts in stock at 68°C for 35–40 minutes to a core of 62°C.
3. Reduce 300 ml of the poaching stock with Madeira to 100 ml; add cream, simmer to coating.
4. Blend the sauce with foie gras and 30 g butter; pass — sauce Albufera must be pale ivory and lush. Season.
5. Crisp the breast skin: dry, then colour skin-down in 20 g butter, 2 minutes.
6. Unmould the royales; slice each breast in two on the bias.
7. Plate breast, royale, Albufera poured generously; chervil to finish.

## Plating & Service
Poultry cooked this gently needs a warm room and warmer plates; carve to show the pearl-white interior.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Any exceptional free-range chicken applies; skip foie and mount with butter for a lighter supreme sauce.
