# Anjou Pigeon, Beetroot, Blackberry & Jus of its Bones

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Squab roasted on the crown, with dark fruit and earth — a game course of blood-deep colours and precision.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Anjou pigeons — 4 (500 g each)
- Beetroots, medium — 3 (450 g)
- Blackberries — 150 g
- Red wine — 200 ml
- Port — 50 ml
- Shallot, sliced — 60 g
- Butter or duck fat — 50 g
- Thyme — 5 sprigs
- Juniper berries, crushed — 3
- Red wine vinegar — 15 ml
- Fine salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Remove legs and wishbones; keep crowns whole. Chop legs and carcass trim for the jus.
2. Brown the trimmings in half the fat; add shallot, juniper and thyme, deglaze with wine and port, cover with water and simmer 40 minutes; strain and reduce to 120 ml.
3. Roast whole beets wrapped at 190°C for 45 minutes; peel and cut into wedges.
4. Warm half the blackberries in the jus; crush lightly, strain back in, and sharpen with vinegar.
5. Colour the crowns on all sides in the remaining fat, 4 minutes; roast at 200°C for 6–8 minutes to a breast core of 54°C. Rest 10 minutes.
6. Glaze beet wedges in a spoon of jus.
7. Carve breasts off the crown, season the cut side; plate with beets, fresh blackberries and sauce.

## Plating & Service
Serve the breasts blushing claret-red with the jus poured at the table; offer the roasted legs as a rustic second service.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Duck breast scales the idea up; cherry and pinot noir replace blackberry and port in early summer.
