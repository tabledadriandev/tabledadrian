# Fillet of Beef Rossini Revisited, Perigueux Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The decadent classic, tightened: seared foie gras on aged fillet with black-truffle Madeira sauce — no toast, no apology.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Beef fillet, centre-cut, aged — 4 x 160 g
- Foie gras slices — 4 x 50 g
- Madeira — 120 ml
- Truffle jus — 40 ml
- Veal or beef jus, reduced — 200 ml
- Black truffle, chopped — 15 g
- Shallot, brunoise — 40 g
- Butter — 60 g
- Grapeseed oil — 25 ml
- Potato puree, silky — 300 g
- Thyme — 4 sprigs
- Fleur de sel — 5 g

## Method
1. Temper the fillets 45 minutes; season heavily.
2. Sear in smoking oil 90 seconds per face; add 40 g butter and thyme, baste 2 minutes, then finish at 180°C to 50°C core for medium-rare. Rest 8 minutes.
3. Sauce: sweat shallot in the beef pan, deglaze with Madeira, reduce by two-thirds; add jus and truffle jus, reduce to coating; finish with chopped truffle and 20 g butter.
4. Score the foie gras slices and salt them.
5. Sear foie in a dry, very hot pan 30–40 seconds per side until mahogany with a molten centre; drain on paper.
6. Warm the potato puree to a gloss.
7. Stack: puree, rested fillet, foie gras; spoon Perigueux sauce over and around.

## Plating & Service
Serve on hot plates with the sauce poured table-side; a watercress bouquet is the only permitted freshness.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skip foie and crown with roasted cep for 'Rossini du maquis'; sherry replaces Madeira at sea.
