# Apricot Souffle, Noyau Ice Cream

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A tall, trembling apricot souffle with almond-kernel ice cream melting into its heart.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Apricots, ripe — 500 g
- Caster sugar — 160 g
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Cornflour — 18 g
- Egg whites — 180 g (6)
- Butter, soft — 30 g
- Caster sugar (moulds) — 30 g
- Whole milk — 250 ml
- Double cream — 150 ml
- Egg yolks — 4
- Apricot kernels or 2 g almond extract — 8
- Flaked almonds, toasted — 20 g

## Method
1. Ice cream: infuse milk and cream with crushed kernels 30 minutes; make an anglaise with yolks and 60 g sugar to 83°C; strain, chill, churn.
2. Cook apricots with 60 g sugar and lemon to a thick puree; slake cornflour with 20 ml water, boil into the puree 1 minute; cool to room temperature.
3. Butter six ramekins upward-stroked; coat with caster sugar.
4. Whip whites with remaining 40 g sugar to firm, glossy peaks.
5. Whisk a third of the whites into the apricot base, then fold the rest in two additions.
6. Fill to the rim, level, and thumb the edge clean.
7. Bake at 190°C on a preheated tray for 12–14 minutes until risen 4 cm with a moist centre.
8. Serve immediately; crack the top and drop in a spoon of noyau ice cream at the table, almonds alongside.

## Plating & Service
The maitre d' carries, the chef watches the clock — a souffle forgives nobody. Straight from oven to table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Raspberry or passion fruit purees follow the same cornflour math; skip kernels for a nut-free vanilla ice cream.
