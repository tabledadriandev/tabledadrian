# Suckling Pig Belly, Langoustine & Sauce Ravigote

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Crackled suckling pig with sweet langoustine and a sharp herb ravigote — surf-and-turf without the cliche.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Suckling pig belly, boneless — 800 g
- Fine salt — 10 g
- Langoustine tails — 8 (250 g)
- Butter — 40 g
- Shallot, brunoise — 40 g
- Capers, chopped — 20 g
- Cornichons, chopped — 30 g
- Chervil, tarragon, parsley, chopped — 15 g
- Dijon mustard — 10 g
- Red wine vinegar — 20 ml
- Grapeseed oil — 60 ml
- Egg, hard-boiled, chopped — 1
- Apple, brunoise — 60 g

## Method
1. Salt the belly and roast skin-up on a rack: 150°C for 2 hours, then 230°C for 15–20 minutes until the skin blisters to glass. Rest 15 minutes.
2. Whisk vinegar, mustard and oil; fold in shallot, capers, cornichons, herbs and egg — the ravigote should be sharp enough to make you blink.
3. Press the rested belly lightly and cut into 4 neat rectangles.
4. Season langoustine tails; sear in foaming butter 45 seconds per side — barely opaque.
5. Re-crisp the belly portions skin-down in the langoustine pan, 2 minutes.
6. Plate belly and langoustine side by side; spoon ravigote between them and scatter apple brunoise.

## Plating & Service
Crackling must shatter under the spoon at the table — test one corner in the kitchen and re-blast if it sighs.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pork belly (non-suckling) needs an extra hour at 150°C; scallops substitute for langoustine gracefully.
