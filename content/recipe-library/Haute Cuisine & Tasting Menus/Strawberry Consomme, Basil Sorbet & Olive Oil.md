# Strawberry Consomme, Basil Sorbet & Olive Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Crystal-red strawberry water with iced basil — a pre-dessert that tastes like July at 8 p.m.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Strawberries, ripe — 800 g
- Caster sugar — 90 g
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Basil — 50 g
- Water — 200 ml
- Glucose — 25 g
- Extra virgin olive oil — 15 ml
- Small strawberries, quartered — 100 g
- Black pepper — 0.3 g

## Method
1. Hull 800 g strawberries, toss with 50 g sugar, and seal in a bowl over a bain-marie (or vacuum bag at 60°C) for 45 minutes.
2. Strain through muslin without pressing — collect a perfectly clear crimson consomme; chill and season with lemon.
3. Sorbet: dissolve remaining sugar and glucose in water at 60°C; chill, blend with blanched basil, and churn to soft-serve; freeze.
4. Season quartered strawberries with a crack of pepper.
5. Pool 60 ml consomme in chilled bowls with the strawberry quarters.
6. Quenelle basil sorbet in the centre and finish with 3 drops of olive oil per bowl.

## Plating & Service
Serve fiercely cold; the oil beads on the red mirror like stained glass — do not stir before the guests see it.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The spent pulp becomes tomorrow's breakfast coulis; tarragon replaces basil for a licorice register.
