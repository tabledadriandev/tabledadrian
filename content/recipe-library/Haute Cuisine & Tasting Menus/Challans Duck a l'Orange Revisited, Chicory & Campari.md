# Challans Duck a l'Orange Revisited, Chicory & Campari

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The maligned classic rebuilt with bitter chicory and a Campari edge — sharp, bittersweet, adult.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Duck breasts — 2 large (700 g)
- Oranges — 3
- Campari — 30 ml
- White wine vinegar — 25 ml
- Caster sugar — 40 g
- Duck jus — 200 ml
- Red chicory (Treviso) — 2 heads
- Olive oil — 20 ml
- Honey — 15 g
- Star anise — 1
- Butter — 20 g
- Fine salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Make a gastrique: caramelise sugar to amber, arrest with vinegar and the juice of 2 oranges; add star anise and reduce by half.
2. Add duck jus and Campari; reduce to coating and strain. Season.
3. Segment the remaining orange into supremes.
4. Score, salt and render the duck breasts from a cold pan, 10–12 minutes skin-down; flip 2 minutes; rest to a 54°C core.
5. Halve chicory, brush with oil and honey, and char cut-down 3 minutes; season — the bitterness must survive.
6. Warm the supremes in the sauce with the butter.
7. Slice the duck, plate with chicory, supremes and the Campari-orange sauce.

## Plating & Service
Bitter leaves against lacquered duck — plate on warm dark ceramic and pour the sauce last.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skip Campari for a classic bigarade; blood orange in winter is non-negotiable if you can get it.
