# Carabinero Prawn, Fregola & Shellfish Bisque

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Scarlet Mediterranean prawns over toasted fregola drinking a head-juice bisque — primal and polished at once.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Carabinero prawns, XL — 8 (800 g)
- Fregola sarda — 240 g
- Olive oil — 70 ml
- Shallot, brunoise — 60 g
- Garlic — 2 cloves
- Tomato paste — 30 g
- Brandy — 30 ml
- Fish stock — 900 ml
- Celery, brunoise — 40 g
- Lemon — 1
- Parsley, chopped — 8 g
- Espelette pepper — 1 g
- Fine salt — 7 g

## Method
1. Separate heads from tails; peel tails leaving the last segment. Reserve everything.
2. Roast heads and shells in 30 ml oil, pressing to release the coral; add tomato paste, flambe with brandy, add stock and simmer 30 minutes; strain, pressing the heads hard. Reduce to 500 ml.
3. Toast fregola in 20 ml oil with shallot, garlic and celery for 3 minutes until nutty.
4. Cook the fregola like risotto, ladling in hot bisque over 12–14 minutes until al dente and creamy.
5. Season the tails; sear in 20 ml smoking oil 60 seconds per side — centres must stay translucent.
6. Finish fregola with lemon zest, parsley and Espelette; loosen with bisque to a wave.
7. Plate fregola, prawns on top, remaining bisque spooned over; serve the crisped heads alongside.

## Plating & Service
Insist guests squeeze the heads over the fregola — that juice is the dish. Finger bowls mandatory.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Gambero rosso or large tiger prawns substitute; GF orzo of rice makes a coeliac version.
