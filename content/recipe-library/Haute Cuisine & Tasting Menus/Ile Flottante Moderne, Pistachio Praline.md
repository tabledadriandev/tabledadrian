# Ile Flottante Moderne, Pistachio Praline

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A poached meringue island on cold anglaise, re-armed with pistachio crunch and a needle of caramel.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Egg whites — 180 g (6)
- Caster sugar — 100 g (meringue) + 90 g (anglaise) + 120 g (caramel)
- Whole milk — 500 ml
- Double cream — 100 ml
- Egg yolks — 108 g (6)
- Vanilla pod — 1
- Pistachios, peeled — 80 g
- Fleur de sel — 1 g

## Method
1. Anglaise: heat milk, cream and vanilla; whisk yolks with 90 g sugar; combine and stir to 83°C; strain and chill fully.
2. Praline: caramelise 60 g of the caramel sugar, stir in pistachios and salt, cool on paper, chop coarsely.
3. Whip whites, raining in 100 g sugar, to a firm gloss.
4. Fill dome moulds (or shape large quenelles) and steam at 90°C for 8 minutes (or microwave 30 seconds at 600 W) until springy.
5. Cook remaining 60 g sugar to amber and pull thin caramel needles on paper.
6. Pool cold anglaise, float the island, cover with praline and stab with caramel shards.

## Plating & Service
Cold sauce, cloud-warm island, hot caramel — assemble in the last ninety seconds before the plates walk.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Classic almonds work; a coffee anglaise variant turns it after-dinner serious.
