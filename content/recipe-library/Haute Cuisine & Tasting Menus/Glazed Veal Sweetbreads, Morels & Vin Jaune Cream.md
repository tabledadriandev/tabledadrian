# Glazed Veal Sweetbreads, Morels & Vin Jaune Cream

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The grand Jura pairing as a main course — caramelised sweetbreads, wild morels and oxidative wine cream.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Veal sweetbreads, heart — 700 g
- Fresh morels — 200 g (or 40 g dried)
- Vin jaune — 80 ml
- Double cream — 200 ml
- Butter — 100 g
- Chicken jus — 100 ml
- Shallot, brunoise — 40 g
- White wine vinegar — 30 ml
- Baby spinach — 100 g
- Lemon juice — 8 ml
- Fine salt — 7 g

## Method
1. Soak sweetbreads 4 hours in ice water; blanch from cold with vinegar to 85°C, ice, peel and press 2 hours.
2. Wash morels three times; dry. (If dried: soak 30 minutes, keep the liquor, strain through muslin.)
3. Sweat shallot in 20 g butter; add morels 4 minutes, then vin jaune, reduce by half.
4. Add cream (and morel liquor), simmer to coating consistency; season and hold.
5. Portion sweetbreads into 4; dry, season, and roast in foaming butter 4 minutes per side, basting, to a golden crust.
6. Add chicken jus and glaze, rolling, to a 68°C core and a mahogany lacquer.
7. Wilt spinach with a nut of butter and lemon.
8. Plate spinach, sweetbreads, morels and vin jaune cream spooned over.

## Plating & Service
This dish wants deep plates and a Savagnin in the glass — announce the vintage as it lands.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dry amontillado sherry mimics vin jaune; chicken oysters make a humbler, still-lovely version.
