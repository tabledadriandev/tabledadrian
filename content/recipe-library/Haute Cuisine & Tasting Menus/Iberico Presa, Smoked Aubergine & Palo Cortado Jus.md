# Iberico Presa, Smoked Aubergine & Palo Cortado Jus

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Acorn-fed pork cooked like steak — juicy pink — over smoky aubergine cream with a sherry-deepened jus.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Iberico presa — 700 g
- Aubergines — 2 (600 g)
- Olive oil — 60 ml
- Garlic — 2 cloves
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Palo cortado sherry — 80 ml
- Pork or chicken jus — 200 ml
- Shallot, brunoise — 40 g
- Honey — 10 g
- Smoked paprika — 1 g
- Padron peppers — 12
- Fine salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Burn whole aubergines directly over flame or plancha, turning, 15 minutes until collapsed and charred through.
2. Scoop the flesh, drain 10 minutes, and blend with 30 ml oil, garlic (blanched), lemon and salt to a smoky cream.
3. Temper the presa 30 minutes; season.
4. Sear in 20 ml smoking oil, 2–3 minutes per side, then rest and finish at 160°C to a core of 58–60°C — pink is correct for this cut. Rest 8 minutes.
5. Sweat shallot in the pan, deglaze with palo cortado, reduce by half; add jus and honey, reduce to coating, season with paprika.
6. Blister padrons in the remaining oil with salt.
7. Slice the presa across the grain; plate over aubergine cream with padrons and jus.

## Plating & Service
Slice thick and defiant — the marbling should glisten. Serve with the same sherry, lightly chilled.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pork neck (coppa) works for crew night; oloroso or madeira replace palo cortado.
