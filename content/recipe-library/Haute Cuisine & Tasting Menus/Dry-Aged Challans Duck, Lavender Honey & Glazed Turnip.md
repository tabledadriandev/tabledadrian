# Dry-Aged Challans Duck, Lavender Honey & Glazed Turnip

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Crisp-skinned aged duck lacquered with Provencal lavender honey — perfumed, not floral soap; the line is thin and this walks it.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Challans duck breasts, dry-aged — 2 large (700 g)
- Lavender honey — 40 g
- Dried lavender flowers — 0.5 g
- Red wine vinegar — 25 ml
- Duck jus, reduced — 150 ml
- Baby turnips — 12 (400 g)
- Duck fat — 30 g
- Chicken stock — 150 ml
- Thyme — 4 sprigs
- Orange zest — 3 g
- Fleur de sel — 5 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Score the duck skin in a fine diamond pattern without touching the flesh; salt and rest 30 minutes.
2. Warm honey with lavender and orange zest at 60°C for 10 minutes; strain, then whisk in vinegar for a gastrique.
3. Start breasts skin-down in a cold pan; render over medium-low 10–12 minutes, pouring off fat, until the skin is glass.
4. Flip 2 minutes, then rest; return to a 180°C oven for 3–4 minutes to a core of 54°C. Rest again 8 minutes.
5. Glaze turnips in duck fat, stock and thyme, rolling, 10 minutes until tender and lacquered.
6. Warm the jus with half the gastrique; brush the duck skin with the rest and flash under the salamander 30 seconds.
7. Slice each breast into 6, season the cut faces, plate with turnips and sauce.

## Plating & Service
Serve rosy slices fanned skin-up, jus mirrored, one thyme tip; the lavender must whisper.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No aged duck? Standard breasts, skin dried overnight uncovered in the fridge. Honey-rosemary swaps for lavender.
