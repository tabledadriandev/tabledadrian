# Skate Wing, XO Butter & Charred Baby Gem

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Ribbed skate glazed in a punchy XO butter with smoky charred lettuce — texture, umami and speed.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Skate wings, skinned — 4 x 250 g
- XO sauce — 50 g
- Butter — 100 g
- Baby gem lettuce — 4 heads
- Grapeseed oil — 30 ml
- Rice flour — 40 g
- Lime — 1
- Spring onion, sliced — 20 g
- Coriander — 8 g
- Fine salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Dust the skate in rice flour; season.
2. Halve baby gems, oil, and char cut-face down on a plancha 2–3 minutes; season and dress with lime juice.
3. Fry skate in oil and 30 g butter, 4 minutes on the thick side, 2 on the reverse, to 56°C at the bone; rest.
4. Wipe the pan; melt remaining 70 g butter with XO sauce until foaming.
5. Return skate briefly and spoon the XO butter over for 60 seconds to lacquer the ribs.
6. Plate with charred gems, remaining butter spooned over, spring onion and coriander scattered.

## Plating & Service
Serve on the bone — the ribs guide the fork. The XO butter should pool amber in the ribs' valleys.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Ray, brill or halibut take the butter; vegetarian 'XO' of fermented mushrooms and chilli covers meat-free guests.
