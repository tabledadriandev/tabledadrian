# Guinea Fowl Ballotine, Black Truffle Farce & Suprême Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A truffled mosaic ballotine poached to silk, glazed with a classic supreme sauce — charcuterie skill meets saucier craft.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Guinea fowl breasts, skin on — 4 (600 g)
- Guinea fowl or chicken thigh meat — 200 g
- Egg white — 20 g
- Double cream — 120 ml
- Black truffle, chopped — 12 g
- Pistachios — 0 g (omit for nut-free)
- Chicken stock — 700 ml
- Butter — 60 g
- Dry vermouth — 40 ml
- Tarragon — 6 g
- Savoy cabbage — 200 g
- Fine salt — 8 g
- White pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Blend chilled thigh meat with egg white and 3 g salt; beat in 80 ml cold cream over ice; fold in truffle.
2. Butterfly each breast slightly, season, spread with 50 g farce, and roll tightly in cling film into 5 cm ballotines; tie the ends.
3. Poach at 64°C for 40–45 minutes to a core of 62°C.
4. Reduce 300 ml poaching stock with vermouth to 80 ml; add remaining 40 ml cream and mount with 30 g butter; season, add chopped tarragon.
5. Braise ribboned cabbage in 30 g butter and a ladle of stock until glossy, 8 minutes.
6. Unwrap ballotines, dry, and colour the skin briefly in a hot pan for a golden finish.
7. Slice thick to show the truffled centre; plate on cabbage with the supreme sauce.

## Plating & Service
The cross-section sells the work — perfect rings of farce inside pearl-white breast. Sauce poured, never flooded.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken breast substitutes; morels in the farce swap for truffle in spring at equal glory.
