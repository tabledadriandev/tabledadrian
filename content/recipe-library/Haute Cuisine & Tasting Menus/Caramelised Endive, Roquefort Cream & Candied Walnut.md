# Caramelised Endive, Roquefort Cream & Candied Walnut

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bitter chicory glazed to amber against blue-cheese silk — a three-element plate of perfect tension.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- White endives — 4 (600 g)
- Butter — 50 g
- Caster sugar — 20 g
- Orange juice — 100 ml
- Roquefort — 100 g
- Double cream — 100 ml
- Walnut halves — 60 g
- Icing sugar — 20 g
- Sherry vinegar — 10 ml
- Chervil — 8 sprigs
- Fine salt — 4 g

## Method
1. Halve endives lengthwise, keeping the root intact.
2. Colour cut-side down in butter and caster sugar over medium heat, 6–8 minutes to deep amber.
3. Add orange juice, cover, and braise gently 15 minutes until a knife slips through the core; reduce the juices to a glaze with vinegar.
4. Warm cream to 70°C and whisk in crumbled Roquefort until just smooth; do not boil.
5. Toss walnuts with icing sugar and 5 ml water; toast at 170°C for 8 minutes until lacquered.
6. Plate one glazed half per guest (plus a trimmed second half), pour Roquefort cream alongside, finish with walnuts and chervil.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm, glaze side up and shining; the blue cheese cream should pool, not coat.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Gorgonzola dolce is gentler; skip cheese and finish with tahini-honey for a dairy-free variant (adds sesame).
