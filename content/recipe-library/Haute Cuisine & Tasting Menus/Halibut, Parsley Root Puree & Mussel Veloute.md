# Halibut, Parsley Root Puree & Mussel Veloute

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Snow-white halibut with sweet parsley root and a briny mussel cream — northern flavours, southern precision.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Halibut portions — 4 x 170 g
- Mussels — 500 g
- Dry white wine — 100 ml
- Shallot, sliced — 50 g
- Parsley root (or parsnip) — 400 g
- Whole milk — 250 ml
- Butter — 90 g
- Double cream — 100 ml
- Parsley leaves — 15 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Fine salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Steam mussels with wine and shallot in a lidded pan 3 minutes; strain and reserve the liquor, pick the meat.
2. Simmer parsley root in milk with salt 18 minutes; blend with 40 g butter to a tight puree.
3. Reduce mussel liquor to 80 ml, add cream, simmer 3 minutes, season with lemon — hold at 70°C.
4. Brine halibut 10 minutes in 5% salt water; dry.
5. Butter-baste in a medium pan with 50 g butter, 2 minutes per side, to a core of 50°C; rest 3 minutes.
6. Warm mussels in the veloute 60 seconds; blend in blanched parsley for a pale green fleck if desired.
7. Plate puree, halibut, mussels and veloute spooned around.

## Plating & Service
The halibut should flake into thick pearlescent leaves — undercook rather than over. Deep warm bowls.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cod or hake take the same treatment; smoked mussels in the veloute add a campfire register.
