# Skrei Cod, Smoked Butter Emulsion & Cockle Jus

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Winter cod at its peak, flaking into a smoky butter sauce studded with sweet cockles.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Skrei cod loin — 4 x 170 g
- Cockles or vongole — 400 g
- Dry white wine — 100 ml
- Smoked butter — 100 g
- Shallot, brunoise — 40 g
- Double cream — 30 ml
- Leek, julienne — 100 g
- Grapeseed oil — 20 ml
- Chives — 6 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Fine salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Brine cod 15 minutes in 6% salt water — this sets the flakes glossy; dry.
2. Steam cockles open with wine and shallot, 2 minutes; strain the liquor and pick most of the meat, keeping 8 in shell.
3. Reduce the liquor to 60 ml, add cream, then whisk in the smoked butter cube by cube; season with lemon.
4. Fry leek julienne in oil at 160°C until golden and crisp; salt.
5. Roast the cod in a lightly oiled pan, presentation-side first, 3 minutes, then flip and finish to 50°C core.
6. Warm cockle meat in the emulsion 30 seconds.
7. Plate cod, spoon over the cockle emulsion, top with crisp leek and chives.

## Plating & Service
Break one flake at the pass to check the pearly sheen; the crisp leek gives the dish its height and rustle.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Regular cod or hake work all year; unsmoked butter plus 2 g smoked salt fakes the smokehouse honestly.
