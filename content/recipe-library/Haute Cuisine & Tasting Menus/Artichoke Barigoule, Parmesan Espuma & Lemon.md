# Artichoke Barigoule, Parmesan Espuma & Lemon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Provencal braised artichokes lifted by an airy Parmesan cloud — the Riviera's vegetable soul, plated fine.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Violet artichokes — 8 small (800 g)
- Carrot, sliced — 80 g
- Celery, sliced — 40 g
- Shallot, sliced — 60 g
- White wine — 150 ml
- Olive oil — 60 ml
- Thyme — 4 sprigs
- Coriander seeds — 2 g
- Parmesan — 100 g
- Double cream — 200 ml
- Whole milk — 100 ml
- Lemon — 1
- Chervil — 8 sprigs
- Fine salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Turn the artichokes to the tender heart, rubbing with lemon as you go; halve them.
2. Sweat carrot, celery and shallot in olive oil 4 minutes; add artichokes cut-side down and colour lightly.
3. Add wine, thyme, coriander seeds, 150 ml water and salt; braise lidded at a simmer for 18–20 minutes until tender.
4. Reduce the braising liquor to a glaze and roll the artichokes in it.
5. Heat cream and milk to 80°C, whisk in finely grated Parmesan off the heat, steep 10 minutes, strain.
6. Load the Parmesan cream into a siphon, charge twice, hold at 55°C.
7. Plate artichokes with vegetables and glaze; crown with espuma, lemon zest and chervil.

## Plating & Service
Serve in shallow bowls — glossy braised hearts half-buried in a snowdrift of Parmesan foam.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: skip the espuma and finish with almond cream (nut allergen); pearl onions and pancetta make a heartier version.
