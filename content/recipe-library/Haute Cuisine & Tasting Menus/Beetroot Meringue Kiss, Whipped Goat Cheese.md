# Beetroot Meringue Kiss, Whipped Goat Cheese

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A shocking-pink crisp shell that dissolves into cool goat cheese — pure theatre in one bite.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Beetroot juice — 200 ml
- Egg white — 60 g (2)
- Caster sugar — 60 g
- Freeze-dried raspberry powder — 5 g
- Fresh goat cheese — 150 g
- Double cream — 50 ml
- Honey — 10 g
- Lemon zest — 2 g
- White balsamic vinegar — 5 ml
- Fine salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Reduce beetroot juice to 40 ml of syrup at a bare simmer, about 20 minutes; cool completely.
2. Whip egg whites to soft peaks, rain in sugar, then whip to stiff gloss; fold in 20 ml beet syrup.
3. Pipe 3 cm domes onto a silicone mat and dehydrate at 90°C for 2 hours until crisp and hollow.
4. Whip goat cheese, cream, honey, lemon zest, vinegar and salt to piping consistency; chill.
5. Pierce the base of each meringue and pipe in 8 g of goat cheese cream.
6. Pair shells into kisses, dust one cheek with raspberry powder and serve within 30 minutes.

## Plating & Service
Present on a bed of black pebbles; instruct guests to take it whole so shell and cream collapse together.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Aquafaba (60 g) replaces egg white and whipped cashew cream the cheese for a vegan version.
