# Razor Clams, Sea Lettuce Butter & Finger Lime

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Razor clams flashed open and grilled in seaweed butter — served in the shell, tasting of the tide.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Razor clams, live — 12
- Dry white wine — 100 ml
- Butter, soft — 120 g
- Sea lettuce or wakame, chopped — 15 g
- Shallot, brunoise — 30 g
- Garlic — 1 clove
- Lemon zest — 3 g
- Finger lime — 2 (30 g pearls)
- Chives — 6 g
- Espelette pepper — 1 g
- Sourdough crumb, toasted — 0 g (omit) or GF crumb — 30 g

## Method
1. Purge clams 1 hour in cold salted water.
2. Steam with wine in a lidded pan at full heat for 60–90 seconds, pulling each clam the instant it opens.
3. Remove meat, discard the dark sac, slice the white flesh into 1 cm pieces; clean and keep 12 shells.
4. Beat butter with sea lettuce, shallot, garlic, lemon zest and Espelette.
5. Refill shells with the sliced clam and 8 g seaweed butter each.
6. Grill under a hot salamander for 2–3 minutes until bubbling; scatter GF crumb if using.
7. Finish with finger lime pearls and chives.

## Plating & Service
Three shells per guest on hot salt, butter still foaming — squeeze-of-the-sea in every bite.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Mussels or scallops in half-shells take the same butter; the seaweed butter freezes beautifully in logs.
