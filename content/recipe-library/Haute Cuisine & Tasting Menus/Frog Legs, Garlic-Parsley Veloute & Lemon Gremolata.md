# Frog Legs, Garlic-Parsley Veloute & Lemon Gremolata

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A nod to Burgundy — crisp frog legs riding a vivid green garlic veloute, brightened with gremolata.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Frog legs, paired — 16 (500 g)
- Plain flour — 50 g
- Butter — 90 g
- Garlic — 6 cloves
- Flat parsley — 80 g
- Spinach — 50 g
- Chicken stock — 300 ml
- Double cream — 80 ml
- Lemon — 1
- Panko, toasted — 20 g
- Espelette pepper — 1 g
- Fine salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Blanch 4 garlic cloves three times from cold water to tame them.
2. Simmer blanched garlic in stock 10 minutes; add cream, then blend with blanched parsley leaves and spinach; pass — the veloute must be racing green.
3. Make gremolata: toasted panko, lemon zest, 1 raw grated garlic clove, chopped parsley stems.
4. Season and flour the frog legs, shaking off the excess.
5. Pan-fry in foaming butter with the last crushed garlic clove, 3 minutes per side, until golden and just cooked.
6. Finish the legs with a squeeze of lemon and Espelette.
7. Pool warm veloute, stack the legs, and scatter gremolata generously.

## Plating & Service
Serve with finger bowls and no apology — these are meant to be picked up once the veloute is mopped.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken wings mid-joints work the same way for squeamish tables; GF flour and crumbs convert it cleanly.
