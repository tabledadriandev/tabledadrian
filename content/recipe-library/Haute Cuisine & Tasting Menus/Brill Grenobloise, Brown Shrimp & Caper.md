# Brill Grenobloise, Brown Shrimp & Caper

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Roasted brill with the sharpest of classic garnishes — brown butter, capers, lemon and croutons, plus sweet grey shrimp.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Brill fillets, skin off — 4 x 170 g
- Butter — 130 g
- Grapeseed oil — 20 ml
- Brown shrimp, peeled — 100 g
- Capers, rinsed — 30 g
- Lemons — 2
- White bread, 5 mm dice — 60 g
- Parsley, chopped — 10 g
- Plain flour — 30 g
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Cut one lemon into skinless segments, then dice; juice the other.
2. Fry bread dice in 30 g butter to golden croutons; drain.
3. Dust brill in flour; cook in oil and 40 g butter, 3 minutes presentation side, 2 minutes reverse, to 52°C core; rest.
4. Wipe the pan, cook remaining 60 g butter to noisette.
5. Add capers, shrimp and lemon dice; swirl 45 seconds, then add juice and parsley off the heat.
6. Plate the brill and spoon the sizzling grenobloise over; scatter croutons last so they stay crunchy.

## Plating & Service
Serve on hot plates within 60 seconds of saucing — grenobloise waits for no one. Buttered spinach alongside.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skate wing is the classic alternative; GF bread and flour convert it fully for coeliac guests.
