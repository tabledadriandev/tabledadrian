# Sea Trout, Charred Cucumber & Oscietra Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Blush-pink sea trout with smoky cucumber and a beurre blanc folded with caviar at the pass.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Sea trout fillet, skin on — 4 x 160 g
- Cucumbers — 2 (600 g)
- Dill — 10 g
- White wine — 80 ml
- White wine vinegar — 20 ml
- Shallot, brunoise — 40 g
- Cold butter, diced — 130 g
- Double cream — 20 ml
- Oscietra caviar — 40 g
- Grapeseed oil — 25 ml
- Fleur de sel — 4 g

## Method
1. Halve one cucumber lengthwise, deseed, and char cut-side down on a dry plancha at full heat 4 minutes; slice into 3 cm chevrons and season.
2. Juice the second cucumber; reduce the juice by half.
3. Reduce wine, vinegar and shallot to 25 ml; add cream and the cucumber reduction.
4. Mount with cold butter below 80°C; season lightly — the caviar brings salt.
5. Cook the trout skin-side down in oil over medium heat 4–5 minutes until the skin crisps and the flesh turns opaque two-thirds up; flip for 20 seconds. Core 48°C.
6. Fold the caviar into the sauce off the heat at the very last moment.
7. Plate chevrons, trout skin-up, and spoon the caviar sauce alongside; finish with dill and fleur de sel.

## Plating & Service
Never let the sauce boil after the caviar goes in — the eggs must pop, not poach. Pale plates, green accents.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Salmon substitutes seamlessly; keep it dairy-free with an olive-oil sabayon and the same caviar finish.
