# Duck Rillette Choux Puff, Pickled Cherry

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A warm miniature choux hiding cool, silky duck rillette and one sharp cherry — bistro soul, palace polish.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Confit duck legs, picked — 250 g
- Duck fat — 60 g
- Dijon mustard — 10 g
- Orange zest — 3 g
- Water — 125 ml
- Butter — 55 g
- Plain flour — 75 g
- Whole eggs — 100 g (2)
- Griottine or fresh cherries — 10
- Red wine vinegar — 60 ml
- Caster sugar — 30 g
- Fine salt — 4 g

## Method
1. Pit cherries and pickle in warmed vinegar and sugar for at least 2 hours.
2. Beat picked duck with soft duck fat, mustard, orange zest and salt to a rough, spreadable rillette; chill.
3. Make choux: boil water, butter and salt; beat in flour, dry 2 minutes, then beat in eggs gradually.
4. Pipe 2.5 cm puffs and bake at 185°C for 22 minutes until golden and hollow; cool.
5. Split puffs, pipe in 20 g rillette, and seat half a pickled cherry inside each.
6. Warm assembled puffs at 140°C for 3 minutes so pastry is warm but rillette stays cool.

## Plating & Service
Serve two per guest on a wooden board with the remaining cherries; pairs beautifully with chilled Beaujolais.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Confit chicken or mushroom duxelles fills the same puff; GF choux works with a rice-flour panade.
