# Navarin of Lamb, Spring Vegetables & Rosemary

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The classic spring stew clarified and refined — lamb shoulder braised to a wobble, vegetables cooked apart and glazed.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Lamb shoulder, 4 cm dice — 1.2 kg
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Onion, mirepoix — 150 g
- Carrot, mirepoix — 100 g
- Celery — 50 g
- Tomato paste — 40 g
- White wine — 200 ml
- Chicken stock — 900 ml
- Rosemary — 2 sprigs
- Baby carrots — 12
- Baby turnips — 8
- Peas — 100 g
- Pearl onions — 12
- Fine salt — 10 g

## Method
1. Season and sear the lamb hard in oil in batches until deeply browned.
2. Add mirepoix, cook 6 minutes; add tomato paste, cook 2 minutes; deglaze with wine.
3. Cover with stock, add rosemary, and braise lidded at 150°C for 2 hours until yielding.
4. Lift the meat; strain the sauce, skim thoroughly, and reduce to a glossy coating consistency.
5. Cook the garnish vegetables separately in salted water to just-tender (peas 60 s, turnips and carrots 4 min, onions 6 min).
6. Return lamb to the sauce; glaze the vegetables in a ladle of it.
7. Serve lamb and vegetables in wide bowls with the sauce spooned over.

## Plating & Service
Stew, but plated like a tasting course — meat stacked, vegetables placed, sauce poured at the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pressure cooker: 45 minutes. Swap lamb for monkfish cheeks (reduce braise to 10 minutes) for a sea-navarin.
