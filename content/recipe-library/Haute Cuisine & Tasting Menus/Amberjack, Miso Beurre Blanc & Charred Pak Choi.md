# Amberjack, Miso Beurre Blanc & Charred Pak Choi

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Firm ricciola with a white-miso butter sauce — a Franco-Japanese handshake perfectly at home in the Med.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Amberjack loin — 4 x 160 g
- Pak choi — 4 heads (400 g)
- White miso — 35 g
- Sake or dry white wine — 60 ml
- Rice vinegar — 15 ml
- Shallot, brunoise — 30 g
- Cold butter, diced — 120 g
- Ginger, julienne — 10 g
- Grapeseed oil — 30 ml
- Sesame seeds, toasted — 4 g
- Lime — 1

## Method
1. Reduce sake, vinegar and shallot to 20 ml.
2. Whisk in cold butter cube by cube below 80°C, then whisk in miso until glossy; sharpen with lime juice. Hold at 60°C.
3. Halve pak choi, oil the cut faces, and char cut-side down on a hot plancha 3 minutes; season and steam-finish 1 minute with a splash of water.
4. Fry ginger julienne in 15 ml oil until crisp.
5. Sear amberjack in remaining oil, 2 minutes per side to a rosy 50°C core; rest 3 minutes.
6. Slice thickly, plate on pak choi, coat with miso beurre blanc, finish with ginger and sesame.

## Plating & Service
Sauce spooned to a mirror, sesame scattered from height; the fish centre should blush like tuna.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swordfish or tuna work; certified GF miso keeps it coeliac-safe — check the label, not the vibe.
