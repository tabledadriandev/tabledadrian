# Charred Hispi Cabbage, Miso Brown Butter & Buckwheat

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A quartered sweetheart cabbage burnt, basted and glazed until meaty — umami cooking with almost no ingredients.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Hispi (sweetheart) cabbages — 2
- Butter — 120 g
- White miso — 30 g
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Vegetable stock — 100 ml
- Buckwheat groats — 40 g
- Grapeseed oil — 30 ml
- Spring onion, sliced — 20 g
- Chilli flakes — 1 g
- Fine salt — 4 g

## Method
1. Toast buckwheat in a dry pan 4–5 minutes until biscuit-brown and crackling; salt lightly.
2. Quarter the cabbages through the core; oil and salt the cut faces.
3. Char cut-down in a dry cast-iron pan or plancha, 4–5 minutes per face, until properly blackened at the edges.
4. Add stock, cover, and steam 6–8 minutes until the cores yield.
5. Cook the butter to noisette; whisk in miso and lemon off the heat.
6. Baste the quarters generously with miso brown butter and roast at 200°C for 8 minutes, basting twice.
7. Plate with more butter spooned over, buckwheat, spring onion and chilli.

## Plating & Service
Serve the quarter whole, burnt tips up — knife-and-fork vegetable cookery with steak confidence.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan with olive oil + miso; a poached egg or shaved cured yolk turns it into lunch for six.
