# Miniature Apple Tatin, Creme Normande

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> One-bite upside-down apples in deep caramel with a Calvados cream — the grand tatin distilled to petit-four size.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Braeburn or Reinette apples — 4 (600 g)
- Caster sugar — 120 g
- Butter — 60 g
- Vanilla pod — 0.5
- All-butter puff pastry — 250 g
- Creme fraiche — 100 g
- Double cream — 50 ml
- Calvados — 15 ml
- Icing sugar — 10 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Fine salt — 1 g

## Method
1. Cook sugar dry to a deep amber; whisk in butter, vanilla seeds, salt and lemon juice; divide between 8 muffin-tin cups.
2. Peel apples and cut into thick wedges; pack tightly into the caramel, rounded side down.
3. Bake apples alone at 180°C for 20 minutes to start collapsing.
4. Cap each cup with a 7 cm puff disc, tucking the edge inside.
5. Bake at 200°C for 15–18 minutes until the pastry is deep gold; rest 3 minutes only.
6. Whip creme fraiche, cream, icing sugar and Calvados to soft peaks.
7. Flip the tatins out caramel-up and serve warm with the creme normande.

## Plating & Service
Serve two per guest on a long slate, cream piped between; the caramel must still slowly flow.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pears in October, quince in November; alcohol-free cream with vanilla for dry service.
