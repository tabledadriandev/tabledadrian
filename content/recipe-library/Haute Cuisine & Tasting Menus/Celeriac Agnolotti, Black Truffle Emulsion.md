# Celeriac Agnolotti, Black Truffle Emulsion

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Silk pasta pillows of roasted celeriac under a glossy truffle butter — pasta masterclass technique, palace finish.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- 00 flour — 200 g
- Egg yolks — 108 g (6)
- Whole egg — 50 g (1)
- Celeriac — 500 g
- Butter — 110 g
- Whole milk — 100 ml
- Parmesan, grated — 60 g
- Black truffle — 15 g
- Truffle jus — 25 ml
- White vegetable stock — 150 ml
- Lemon juice — 8 ml
- Fine salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Knead flour, yolks and whole egg 8 minutes to a smooth dough; rest wrapped 1 hour.
2. Roast half the celeriac, diced, in 30 g butter at 180°C for 25 minutes until caramelised; simmer the rest in milk until soft.
3. Blend both celeriacs with 20 g Parmesan and salt to a thick farce; pipe-chill 1 hour.
4. Sheet pasta to setting 8 (about 0.8 mm); pipe a rope of farce and fold into agnolotti, pinching 3 cm pockets; cut with a fluted wheel.
5. Make the emulsion: reduce stock and truffle jus by half, then mount with 80 g cold butter; add chopped truffle and lemon.
6. Cook agnolotti in well-salted water at a gentle boil for 2.5 minutes.
7. Glaze the pasta in the emulsion 1 minute, loosening with pasta water to a mirror shine.
8. Plate 7 per person, sauce spooned over, remaining Parmesan and truffle shaved on top.

## Plating & Service
Serve in shallow warm bowls, agnolotti in one strict row — the gloss of the emulsion is the quality test.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Farce variations: Jerusalem artichoke, pumpkin-amaretti, or pea-mint in spring; the emulsion loves a drop of vin jaune.
