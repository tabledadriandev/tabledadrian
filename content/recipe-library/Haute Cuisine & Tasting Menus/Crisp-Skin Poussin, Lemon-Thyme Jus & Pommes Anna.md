# Crisp-Skin Poussin, Lemon-Thyme Jus & Pommes Anna

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Spatchcocked baby chicken pressed to glass-crisp skin, with a bright lemon-thyme jus and a buttery potato crown.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Poussins — 2 (900 g)
- Butter, clarified — 120 g
- Agria potatoes — 500 g
- Chicken jus — 200 ml
- Lemons — 2
- Thyme — 8 sprigs
- Garlic, crushed — 2 cloves
- White wine — 60 ml
- Butter, cold — 30 g
- Fine salt — 8 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Spatchcock the poussins, dry, and salt; rest uncovered at 4°C for 2 hours if time allows.
2. Pommes Anna: slice potatoes 2 mm, toss in 80 g clarified butter and salt, and shingle in a 16 cm pan in tight rosette layers.
3. Cook the potato on the stove 5 minutes, then bake at 180°C for 35–40 minutes, pressing twice; flip out golden.
4. Cook poussins skin-down in 40 g clarified butter with garlic and thyme under a weighted pan, 12–14 minutes, until the skin is amber glass.
5. Flip, baste 4–5 minutes to a thigh core of 72°C; rest 8 minutes.
6. Deglaze with wine, add jus and lemon juice, reduce to coating, mount with cold butter, and add thyme leaves and zest.
7. Halve the birds; serve with wedges of pommes Anna and the lemon-thyme jus.

## Plating & Service
Skin up, potato rosette showing its rings, jus poured at the table — humble bird, immaculate execution.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Quail (2 per guest, 8 minutes) makes it a tasting course; swap potato for celeriac Anna in winter.
