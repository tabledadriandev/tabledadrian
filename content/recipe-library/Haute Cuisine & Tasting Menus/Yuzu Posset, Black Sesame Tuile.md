# Yuzu Posset, Black Sesame Tuile

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Two-ingredient chemistry — cream set by citrus alone — under a dramatic black sesame crisp.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Double cream — 500 ml
- Caster sugar — 120 g
- Yuzu juice — 70 ml
- Lemon juice — 20 ml
- Yuzu or lemon zest — 3 g
- Black sesame seeds — 30 g
- Egg white — 30 g
- Icing sugar — 30 g
- Butter, melted — 20 g
- Plain flour — 0 g (use 20 g rice flour)

## Method
1. Boil cream and caster sugar for exactly 3 minutes, stirring.
2. Off the heat, whisk in yuzu and lemon juices; the mixture will visibly thicken.
3. Pour through a fine sieve into six glasses; set at 3°C for at least 3 hours.
4. Tuile: whisk egg white, icing sugar, rice flour and melted butter; rest 20 minutes.
5. Spread thinly into 8 cm strips, scatter heavily with black sesame, and bake at 160°C for 6–7 minutes; curl warm over a rolling pin.
6. Serve each posset with zest grated over and a tuile balanced across the rim.

## Plating & Service
The posset should shiver, not wobble; the jet-black tuile against pale cream is the whole design.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Lime and kaffir leaf make an equally sharp posset; the tuile keeps 3 days in a dry box.
