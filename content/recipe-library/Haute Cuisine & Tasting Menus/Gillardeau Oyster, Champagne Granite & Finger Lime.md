# Gillardeau Oyster, Champagne Granite & Finger Lime

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> An ice-cold, saline one-bite opener that sets a luxurious tone the moment guests sit down.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Gillardeau oysters No. 3 — 8 pieces
- Brut Champagne — 200 ml
- Rice vinegar — 15 ml
- Caster sugar — 8 g
- Shallot, brunoise — 20 g
- Finger lime — 2 pieces (about 30 g pearls)
- Chervil leaves — 8 small
- Crushed ice — 500 g
- Fleur de sel — 1 g

## Method
1. Whisk Champagne, vinegar and sugar until dissolved; pour into a shallow tray and freeze at -18°C.
2. Scrape with a fork every 30 minutes for 2 hours until you have fine, dry flakes of granite.
3. Shuck the oysters, strain and reserve their liquor, and release the muscle; chill at 2°C.
4. Rinse each oyster briefly in its own strained liquor to remove grit, then return to the deep shell.
5. Macerate the shallot brunoise in 10 ml of the oyster liquor for 10 minutes.
6. Halve the finger limes and press out the citrus pearls.
7. Top each oyster with 5 g shallot, a generous spoon of granite, finger-lime pearls, chervil and a whisper of fleur de sel.

## Plating & Service
Seat each shell on a bed of crushed ice and seaweed; serve within 3 minutes so the granite stays frosty.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Swap Champagne for non-alcoholic sparkling tea for a dry charter; a drop of yuzu juice gives a Japanese accent.
