# Buttermilk Sherbet, Honeycomb & Bee Pollen

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Tangy frozen buttermilk with shards of homemade honeycomb — a pre-dessert bridging savoury and sweet.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Buttermilk — 500 ml
- Double cream — 100 ml
- Caster sugar — 110 g
- Glucose — 30 g
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Honey — 25 g
- Caster sugar (honeycomb) — 100 g
- Golden syrup or glucose (honeycomb) — 40 g
- Bicarbonate of soda — 6 g
- Bee pollen — 8 g

## Method
1. Warm cream with 110 g sugar, glucose and honey to 60°C; chill, then whisk into buttermilk with lemon.
2. Churn to soft-serve and freeze 2 hours.
3. Honeycomb: cook 100 g sugar and golden syrup to 150°C amber.
4. Whisk in bicarbonate — it will erupt — and pour instantly onto paper; do not touch until cold.
5. Snap honeycomb into rough shards; keep bone-dry.
6. Quenelle sherbet, plant two shards, and dust bee pollen at the pass.

## Plating & Service
Serve in chilled stone bowls; the honeycomb hisses softly as it meets the sherbet — let guests hear it.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Kefir sharpens it further; the honeycomb stales in humidity, so store with silica or make daily at sea.
