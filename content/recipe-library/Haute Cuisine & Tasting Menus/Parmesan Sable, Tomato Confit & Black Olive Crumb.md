# Parmesan Sable, Tomato Confit & Black Olive Crumb

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A crisp, savoury shortbread carrying the sun of the Riviera — intense tomato against salty olive soil.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Parmesan, finely grated — 100 g
- Plain flour — 100 g
- Cold butter, diced — 80 g
- Egg yolk — 18 g (1)
- Cherry tomatoes — 250 g
- Thyme — 4 sprigs
- Garlic, sliced — 8 g
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Kalamata olives, pitted — 80 g
- Basil, micro — 8 leaves
- Icing sugar — 5 g
- Fine salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Halve tomatoes, season with salt, icing sugar, thyme, garlic and olive oil; dry at 110°C for 90 minutes until intense but supple.
2. Dry the olives at 90°C for 60 minutes, then chop to a coarse black crumb.
3. Rub flour, Parmesan and butter to sand; bind with yolk and salt, rest 30 minutes at 4°C.
4. Roll to 4 mm, cut 4 cm discs and bake at 160°C for 12 minutes until deep gold; cool on the tray.
5. Assemble no more than 15 minutes before service: sable, one tomato petal, a pinch of olive crumb.
6. Finish each with a micro basil leaf and a drop of the tomato confit oil.

## Plating & Service
Line up on a warm stone so the sable stays crisp; one elegant bite per guest with the aperitif.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Use a gluten-free flour blend with 2 g xanthan for coeliac guests; a sliver of anchovy converts it for pescatarians who want more punch.
