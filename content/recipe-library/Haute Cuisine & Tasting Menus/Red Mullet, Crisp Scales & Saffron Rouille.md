# Red Mullet, Crisp Scales & Saffron Rouille

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rouget cooked a l'ecaille — scales crisped like armour — with a garlicky saffron rouille and its own liver sauce.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Red mullet, 300 g each, scales on — 4
- Olive oil — 150 ml
- Potato, cooked — 60 g
- Egg yolk — 18 g
- Garlic — 2 cloves
- Saffron — 0.2 g
- Cayenne — 0.3 g
- Fish stock — 200 ml
- Tomato, concasse — 120 g
- Basil — 8 leaves
- Baguette croutons — 40 g
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml

## Method
1. Fillet the mullet leaving scales intact; reserve livers. Pin-bone carefully.
2. Bloom saffron in 10 ml hot fish stock.
3. Pound garlic, potato, yolk, saffron and cayenne; emulsify with 100 ml olive oil into a stiff rouille; season with lemon.
4. Reduce fish stock to 60 ml, whisk in the sieved livers off the heat and 20 ml olive oil; keep at 60°C.
5. Heat 30 ml oil in a non-stick pan until hazing; lay fillets scale-side down, pressing 30 seconds.
6. Fry 3 minutes without moving until the scales stand crisp like sequins; kiss the flesh side 15 seconds.
7. Plate scale-side up with warm tomato concasse, liver jus, rouille-crowned croutons and basil.

## Plating & Service
The scales must audibly crackle; serve immediately with the rouille croutons balanced on the fillet.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> If scaling intimidates, skip to crisp-skin method; gurnard is the honest substitute.
