# Gougeres with Aged Comte & Espelette

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Warm, cheese-laced choux puffs straight from the oven — the most irresistible welcome on any charter.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Water — 125 ml
- Whole milk — 125 ml
- Butter — 100 g
- Plain flour — 150 g
- Whole eggs — 200 g (4)
- Comte 24 months, grated — 120 g
- Espelette pepper — 2 g
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Nutmeg, grated — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Bring water, milk, butter and salt to a full boil; add flour off the heat in one go and beat smooth.
2. Dry the panade over medium heat for 2 minutes until it films the pan base.
3. Transfer to a mixer; beat in eggs one at a time until the paste falls in a slow V from the paddle.
4. Fold in 90 g Comte, nutmeg and half the Espelette.
5. Pipe 3 cm domes onto baking paper, top with remaining Comte and Espelette.
6. Bake at 180°C for 25–30 minutes without opening the door, until deep golden and hollow-sounding.
7. Rest 3 minutes and serve blood-warm.

## Plating & Service
Pile into a linen-lined copper pan and pass around while still steaming; they vanish in seconds.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Fold in 30 g chopped ham for carnivores, or swap Comte for a firm goat cheese and smoked paprika for variety.
