# Wagyu Striploin, Bone Marrow Crust & Charred Shallot

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A restrained wagyu course — small portion, huge flavour — under a sizzling marrow-herb crust.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Wagyu striploin (BMS 6+) — 500 g
- Bone marrow, soaked — 100 g
- Panko — 40 g
- Parsley, chopped — 10 g
- Shallots, banana — 4
- Red wine — 150 ml
- Beef jus — 150 ml
- Grapeseed oil — 20 ml
- Thyme — 4 sprigs
- Sherry vinegar — 10 ml
- Fleur de sel — 5 g
- Black pepper, crushed — 2 g

## Method
1. Beat soaked marrow with panko, parsley, pepper and 2 g salt; roll 3 mm thick between papers and freeze.
2. Halve shallots in their skins; char cut-down in a dry pan 6 minutes, then roast at 180°C for 15 minutes until soft.
3. Reduce wine to 40 ml; add jus, reduce to coating, sharpen with vinegar.
4. Sear the tempered, seasoned wagyu in oil, 90 seconds per side with thyme — wagyu renders itself; pour off excess fat.
5. Rest to 48°C core, then top with a cut sheet of marrow crust.
6. Flash under a hot salamander 60–90 seconds until the crust bubbles gold; core 52°C.
7. Slice 1 cm thick; plate with shallot petals and the red wine jus.

## Plating & Service
Serve 100–120 g per guest, no more — richness is the portion control. Crust must arrive audibly sizzling.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The marrow crust upgrades any aged sirloin; GF panko converts it. Leftover crust freezes for a month.
