# Nougat Glace, Honey & Candied Fruit

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A frozen honey-nougat parfait studded with caramelised nuts and candied fruit — Provence's iced answer to nougat de Montelimar.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Egg whites — 90 g (3)
- Lavender honey — 120 g
- Caster sugar — 60 g
- Double cream — 400 ml
- Almonds and pistachios — 100 g
- Caster sugar (nuts) — 60 g
- Candied orange and melon, diced — 80 g
- Kirsch — 15 ml
- Raspberries — 150 g
- Caster sugar (coulis) — 25 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml

## Method
1. Caramelise 60 g sugar, stir in the nuts, cool on paper and chop — a coarse nut brittle.
2. Macerate candied fruit in kirsch.
3. Boil honey and 60 g sugar to 121°C; pour onto whipping whites and beat to a cool, glossy Italian meringue.
4. Whip cream to soft peaks; fold into the meringue with brittle and fruit.
5. Fill a lined terrine or individual moulds; freeze at least 6 hours.
6. Coulis: blend raspberries, 25 g sugar and lemon; strain.
7. Slice the nougat glace with a hot knife and serve on the coulis.

## Plating & Service
Slices should show the mosaic of fruit and nuts; serve within 4 minutes of the freezer on chilled plates.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Any honey works but lavender says Provence; dried apricot and cranberry replace candied fruit for less sweetness.
