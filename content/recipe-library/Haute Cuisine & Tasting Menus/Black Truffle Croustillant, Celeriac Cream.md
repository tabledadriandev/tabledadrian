# Black Truffle Croustillant, Celeriac Cream

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A shattering brik cigar of celeriac silk and Perigord truffle — winter luxury in two bites.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Feuilles de brick — 4 sheets
- Clarified butter — 60 g
- Celeriac, peeled and diced — 300 g
- Whole milk — 200 ml
- Butter — 30 g
- Fresh black truffle — 12 g
- Truffle jus — 15 ml
- Egg white — 15 g
- Chives — 4 g
- Fine salt — 4 g

## Method
1. Simmer celeriac in milk with salt at 90°C for 18 minutes until completely tender.
2. Drain, then blend with butter and truffle jus to a thick, pipeable puree; cool to room temperature.
3. Fold in 6 g chopped truffle and the chives.
4. Cut brick sheets into 12 x 8 cm rectangles, brush with clarified butter, and pipe a 1 cm line of puree along one edge.
5. Roll into tight cigars, sealing the seam with egg white.
6. Fry seam-down in clarified butter at 170°C for 2 minutes, turning, until uniformly golden; drain on paper.
7. Shave the remaining truffle over the warm cigars just before serving.

## Plating & Service
Stand the cigars upright in a stone vessel of black quinoa 'soil'; serve warm within 5 minutes of frying.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Bake at 200°C for 8 minutes instead of frying for a lighter canape; parsnip replaces celeriac in a pinch.
