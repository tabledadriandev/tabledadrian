# Crispy Chicken Skin, Sweetcorn Puree & Espelette

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Glassy, salty chicken crackling as a spoon for sweet corn silk — high-low luxury guests adore.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Chicken skin, scraped clean — 200 g
- Sweetcorn kernels — 300 g
- Butter — 30 g
- Double cream — 60 ml
- Lime juice — 8 ml
- Espelette pepper — 1.5 g
- Chives — 4 g
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Popcorn shoots or micro coriander — 8 tips

## Method
1. Scrape all fat from the chicken skins and lay them flat between two papered trays.
2. Salt lightly and bake pressed at 170°C for 35–40 minutes until deep amber and glassy; drain and cool.
3. Cook corn in foaming butter for 5 minutes, add cream, simmer 3 minutes.
4. Blend on high for 2 minutes, pass through a chinois, season with salt and lime juice.
5. Snap skins into 8 elegant shards.
6. Pipe a bead of warm corn puree along each shard, dust with Espelette and finish with chives and shoots.

## Plating & Service
Serve propped on a napkin-lined pebble tray so the shards stay dry and audible — the crunch is the point.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> For a vegetarian take, use crisped kale leaves as the vehicle; smoked paprika substitutes Espelette.
