# Pink Grapefruit & Champagne Granite, Tarragon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The palate cleanser: bitter grapefruit and cold Champagne scraped to sparkling frost with a shock of tarragon.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Pink grapefruit juice, fresh — 400 ml
- Brut Champagne — 200 ml
- Caster sugar — 70 g
- Glucose — 20 g
- Tarragon — 6 g
- Grapefruit segments — 12
- Fleur de sel — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Warm 100 ml of the juice with sugar and glucose to 45°C until dissolved.
2. Steep tarragon in the warm syrup for 10 minutes; strain and chill.
3. Combine syrup, remaining juice, Champagne and salt; pour 2 cm deep into a tray.
4. Freeze at -18°C, scraping with a fork every 30 minutes for 2–3 hours into dry, glittering flakes.
5. Trim grapefruit segments and chill them in coupe glasses.
6. Pile granite high over the segments and serve within 2 minutes with a tarragon tip.

## Plating & Service
Frozen coupes, tall spoonfuls, no garnish beyond one leaf — it must vanish in five spoonfuls between courses.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Alcohol-free: sparkling white tea. Ruby port drizzled over turns it into a proper small dessert.
