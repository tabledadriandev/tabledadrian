# Heirloom Tomato Tartare, Burrata Cream & Basil Sorbet

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Tomato treated like beef fillet, iced basil against warm-weather burrata — the Riviera on one plate.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Mixed heirloom tomatoes — 600 g
- Sun-dried tomatoes, chopped — 40 g
- Shallot, brunoise — 30 g
- Capers, chopped — 15 g
- Sherry vinegar — 12 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 50 ml
- Burrata — 200 g
- Double cream — 40 ml
- Basil — 60 g
- Water — 150 ml
- Caster sugar — 45 g
- Glucose — 15 g
- Fine salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Make sorbet base: dissolve sugar and glucose in water at 60°C, chill, blend with blanched basil and 2 g salt, churn and freeze.
2. Blanch, peel and deseed the tomatoes; dice the flesh into 5 mm tartare.
3. Drain the dice in a sieve for 20 minutes — watery tartare is a failure.
4. Fold tomato with sun-dried tomato, shallot, capers, vinegar, 30 ml olive oil and salt.
5. Blend burrata with cream and a pinch of salt to a thick, pourable cream.
6. Press tartare into a 7 cm ring on each chilled plate; pour burrata cream in a moat.
7. Crown with a basil sorbet quenelle and the remaining olive oil at the last second.

## Plating & Service
Serve immediately — the sorbet should just begin melting into the burrata as the plate lands.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: silken tofu cream replaces burrata. Add anchovy to the tartare for non-vegetarians who want depth.
