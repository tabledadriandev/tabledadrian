# Caviar Tartelette, Creme Fraiche & Chive

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The classic opener owners expect: a crisp buckwheat shell, cool cream and a glistening dome of Oscietra.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Oscietra caviar — 60 g
- Buckwheat flour — 60 g
- Plain flour — 60 g
- Cold butter — 60 g
- Egg yolk — 18 g
- Creme fraiche — 120 g
- Chives, finely sliced — 8 g
- Lemon zest — 2 g
- Fine salt — 3 g
- Gold leaf — 2 small flakes (optional)

## Method
1. Rub both flours, butter and salt to fine sand; bind with yolk, rest 30 minutes at 4°C.
2. Roll to 2 mm and line eight 4 cm tartlet rings; freeze 15 minutes.
3. Bake blind at 165°C for 12 minutes until nut-brown; cool in the rings.
4. Season creme fraiche with chives, lemon zest and a pinch of salt; pipe a rosette into each shell.
5. Using a mother-of-pearl spoon, crown each tartlette with 7 g caviar.
6. Finish with a chive tip and, for gala nights, a whisper of gold leaf.

## Plating & Service
Serve on crushed ice with mother-of-pearl spoons alongside; never let caviar touch metal.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Avruga or smoked herring roe keeps the look at a fraction of cost; dairy-free creme works seamlessly.
