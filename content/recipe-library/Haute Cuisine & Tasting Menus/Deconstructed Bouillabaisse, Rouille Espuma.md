# Deconstructed Bouillabaisse, Rouille Espuma

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Three fish cooked precisely apart, reunited in a burnished saffron broth with an airy rouille cloud.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Monkfish — 200 g
- Red mullet fillets — 2 (160 g)
- Sea bass fillet — 200 g
- Mussels — 300 g
- Rockfish or fish bones — 700 g
- Fennel — 150 g
- Onion — 100 g
- Tomatoes — 300 g
- Garlic — 4 cloves
- Saffron — 0.4 g
- Pastis — 25 ml
- Olive oil — 90 ml
- Potato, cooked — 60 g
- Egg yolk — 18 g
- Cayenne — 0.4 g
- Orange zest — 3 g
- Fine salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Sweat fennel, onion and 2 garlic cloves in 40 ml oil; add bones, tomatoes, saffron, orange zest and pastis, cover with 1.2 l water, boil hard 25 minutes, then pass through a mouli and reduce to 600 ml. Season assertively.
2. Rouille: pound potato, yolk, 2 garlic cloves, cayenne and saffron-stained broth (20 ml); emulsify with 50 ml oil.
3. Loosen the rouille with 60 ml warm broth, load into a siphon with one charge; hold at 55°C.
4. Steam mussels open in a splash of broth; keep warm.
5. Cook each fish to its own point: monkfish roasted to 54°C, bass crisp-skinned to 52°C, mullet flashed skin-down 2 minutes.
6. Arrange the fish and mussels in wide bowls; pour boiling broth at the table and crown with rouille espuma.

## Plating & Service
The table-side pour releases the saffron perfume; serve with nothing but a spoon and loyalty.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No siphon? Serve classic rouille croutons. Any three sustainable rock fish keep it honest.
