# Rhubarb & Custard, Three Textures

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Poached batons, sharp sorbet and a set vanilla cream — the nursery pairing given tasting-menu discipline.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Rhubarb, forced pink — 600 g
- Caster sugar — 180 g
- Grenadine — 20 ml
- Orange juice — 100 ml
- Double cream — 300 ml
- Whole milk — 100 ml
- Vanilla pod — 1
- Egg yolks — 72 g (4)
- Gelatine — 3 g
- Glucose — 25 g
- Water — 100 ml
- Micro red-vein sorrel — 12 leaves

## Method
1. Set custard: make an anglaise (cream, milk, vanilla, yolks, 60 g sugar) to 83°C; add bloomed gelatine, pour 60 g into each bowl, set 3 hours.
2. Poach: bring orange juice, 60 g sugar, grenadine and 100 ml water to 85°C; submerge 5 cm rhubarb batons 3–4 minutes until just tender; cool in the liquor.
3. Sorbet: simmer remaining rhubarb trimmings with 60 g sugar, glucose and a splash of poaching liquor 10 minutes; blend, strain, churn.
4. Reduce 150 ml of the poaching liquor to a glossy pink syrup.
5. Arrange batons over the set custard, brushing with syrup.
6. Finish with a sorbet quenelle and sorrel leaves.

## Plating & Service
Pink on cream in a white bowl — resist all other colours. Serve the sorbet at the last second.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Green outdoor rhubarb needs 30% more sugar; gooseberry runs the same play in midsummer.
