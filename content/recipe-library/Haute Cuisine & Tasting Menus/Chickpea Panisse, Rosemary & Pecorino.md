# Chickpea Panisse, Rosemary & Pecorino

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Nice's street-corner fritter tightened up for the salon — creamy chickpea centres, crisp golden crusts.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Chickpea (gram) flour — 250 g
- Water — 750 ml
- Olive oil — 40 ml plus 300 ml to fry
- Rosemary, chopped — 3 g
- Pecorino, finely grated — 40 g
- Lemon zest — 3 g
- Black pepper — 1 g
- Fine salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Whisk gram flour into cold water with salt and 40 ml olive oil until lump-free.
2. Cook over medium heat, whisking constantly, for 8–10 minutes until very thick and pulling from the pan.
3. Beat in rosemary, then spread 2 cm deep in an oiled tray; chill 2 hours until firm.
4. Cut into 6 x 1.5 cm batons.
5. Shallow-fry at 180°C for 3 minutes, turning, until deeply golden; drain and salt.
6. Shower immediately with pecorino, lemon zest and pepper.

## Plating & Service
Pile in a paper-lined copper pot, cheese still melting — serve with sundowners while the fryer is hot.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skip pecorino and dust with smoked paprika for a vegan snack; a harissa mayo dip suits spice-lovers.
