# Green Pea Tortellini in Iberico Ham Consomme

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bright pea parcels floating in a crystal broth built from Iberico trimmings — humble and grand at once.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- 00 flour — 200 g
- Egg yolks — 108 g (6)
- Whole egg — 50 g
- Frozen peas — 300 g
- Ricotta — 80 g
- Parmesan — 40 g
- Mint — 6 g
- Iberico ham trimmings and bone — 300 g
- Chicken stock, clear — 1 l
- Egg whites (to clarify) — 60 g
- Pea shoots — 12
- Fine salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Simmer ham trimmings in chicken stock at 90°C for 45 minutes; chill.
2. Clarify: whisk egg whites into the cold broth, bring slowly to 85°C without stirring, and ladle the clear liquid through muslin.
3. Blanch peas 90 seconds, ice, and blend with ricotta, Parmesan, mint and salt to a stiff farce; pipe-chill.
4. Make pasta with flour, yolks and egg; rest 1 hour, then sheet to 0.8 mm.
5. Cut 6 cm squares, pipe 6 g farce, fold to triangles and wrap into tortellini, sealing with a water-brushed edge.
6. Season the consomme delicately — the ham should carry the salt.
7. Boil tortellini 2.5 minutes in salted water; place 6 in each warm bowl and pour 120 ml consomme at the table; finish with pea shoots.

## Plating & Service
Table-side broth pour over the green parcels — steam, perfume, silence. Serve with chilled fino sherry.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegetarian: build the consomme from roasted mushrooms and kombu instead of ham; the pea farce is unchanged.
