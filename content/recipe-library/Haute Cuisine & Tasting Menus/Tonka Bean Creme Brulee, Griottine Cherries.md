# Tonka Bean Creme Brulee, Griottine Cherries

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The perfect brulee — barely set, glass-topped — perfumed with tonka and hiding boozy cherries below.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Double cream — 500 ml
- Whole milk — 100 ml
- Tonka bean, grated — 1 (about 1 g)
- Egg yolks — 108 g (6)
- Caster sugar — 80 g
- Griottine cherries — 90 g
- Demerara sugar — 40 g
- Fine salt — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Heat cream, milk and grated tonka to 80°C; steep 15 minutes.
2. Whisk yolks with caster sugar and salt; combine with the warm cream and strain.
3. Place 5 griottines in each shallow dish; pour the custard over.
4. Bake in a bain-marie at 120°C for 35–45 minutes until set with a central shiver.
5. Chill at least 4 hours, uncovered, so the surface dries.
6. Sift an even 6 g demerara over each; torch in circles to a dark amber glass. Rest 2 minutes and serve.

## Plating & Service
The crack must be audible across the table — torch at the last possible moment, never ahead.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vanilla or coffee replace tonka; skip cherries and it becomes the purest possible brulee.
