# Glazed Veal Sweetbread Nuggets, Caper-Raisin Emulsion

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Crisp-glazed sweetbreads over a sweet-sharp caper-raisin puree — an offal dish that converts sceptics instantly.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Veal sweetbreads, heart — 500 g
- White wine vinegar — 30 ml
- Golden raisins — 80 g
- Capers, rinsed — 50 g
- Water — 120 ml
- Butter — 80 g
- Chicken jus, reduced — 60 ml
- Cauliflower, small florets — 150 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Thyme — 4 sprigs
- Fine salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Soak sweetbreads in ice water 4 hours, changing the water twice.
2. Blanch from cold with vinegar to 85°C, hold 3 minutes, ice, then peel away membrane and press under a light weight for 2 hours.
3. Simmer raisins in the water 10 minutes, add capers, blend to a smooth emulsion and season with lemon.
4. Portion sweetbreads into 40 g nuggets and dry thoroughly; season.
5. Roast in foaming butter with thyme, 3–4 minutes per side, basting to a deep golden crust.
6. Add chicken jus and glaze, rolling, 2 minutes; core temperature 68°C.
7. Caramelise cauliflower florets in the same pan.
8. Swipe the caper-raisin emulsion, seat the glazed nuggets and cauliflower, and spoon over the pan glaze.

## Plating & Service
Serve on warm plates the moment the glaze sets — the crust-to-cream contrast is everything.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> The caper-raisin emulsion is magnificent with roasted cauliflower alone for a vegetarian course.
