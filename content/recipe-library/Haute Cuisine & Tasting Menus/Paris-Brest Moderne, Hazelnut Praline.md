# Paris-Brest Moderne, Hazelnut Praline

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Individual craquelin-topped choux rings with a lightened praline mousseline — the cyclist's cake in couture.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Water — 125 ml
- Whole milk — 125 ml
- Butter — 100 g (choux) + 150 g (mousseline)
- Plain flour — 150 g (choux) + 60 g (craquelin)
- Whole eggs — 200 g (4)
- Demerara sugar — 60 g
- Hazelnuts — 150 g
- Caster sugar — 120 g (praline) + 60 g (creme pat)
- Whole milk (creme pat) — 300 ml
- Egg yolks — 54 g (3)
- Cornflour — 25 g
- Icing sugar — 10 g
- Fine salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Craquelin: beat 50 g butter, demerara and 60 g flour; roll 2 mm, freeze, cut 7 cm rings.
2. Choux: boil water, milk, 100 g butter, salt; beat in flour, dry 2 minutes, then beat in eggs to a slow V.
3. Pipe 7 cm rings, cap with craquelin rings, bake at 175°C for 35–40 minutes — door shut.
4. Praline: roast hazelnuts at 160°C for 12 minutes; cook 120 g sugar to amber caramel, pour over nuts, cool, blitz to a fluid paste.
5. Creme patissiere: boil milk; whisk yolks, 60 g sugar, cornflour; combine and boil 90 seconds; chill flat.
6. Mousseline: beat 100 g soft butter into smooth creme pat with 100 g praline.
7. Split the rings; pipe tall mousseline rosettes, add a praline core in each, cap and dust with icing sugar.

## Plating & Service
Serve within 2 hours of assembly; the contrast of crisp shell and silk filling defines the pastry section's honour.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pistachio praline is a glorious variant; unfilled rings freeze perfectly for two weeks.
