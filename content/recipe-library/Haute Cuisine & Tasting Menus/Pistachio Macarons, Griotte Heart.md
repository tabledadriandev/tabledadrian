# Pistachio Macarons, Griotte Heart

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Smooth-domed pistachio shells around a sour-cherry secret — the petit four that tests the oven and the cook.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Ground almonds — 125 g
- Icing sugar — 125 g
- Egg whites, aged — 90 g (in two 45 g parts)
- Caster sugar — 110 g
- Water — 30 ml
- Pistachio paste — 40 g
- Green colour (optional) — 1 drop
- Butter, soft — 80 g
- White chocolate — 60 g
- Griottine cherries — 20
- Fine salt — 1 g

## Method
1. Sift almonds and icing sugar; mix to a paste with 45 g whites, pistachio paste and colour.
2. Italian meringue: boil caster sugar and water to 118°C; pour onto the other 45 g whipping whites; beat to 40°C, glossy.
3. Macaronage: fold meringue into the paste until it flows like slow lava — ribbon dissolving in 20 seconds.
4. Pipe 3.5 cm rounds on silicone mats; rap the trays, rest 30 minutes until skinned.
5. Bake at 145°C for 14–16 minutes; cool fully before lifting.
6. Filling: melt white chocolate, beat into soft butter with salt.
7. Pipe a ring of filling, seat a drained griottine in each centre, and cap; mature 24 hours at 4°C. Temper before serving.

## Plating & Service
Serve at room temperature after 24 hours' maturing — a fresh macaron is a crime of impatience.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Raspberry centres replace griottines; store shells frozen and fill on demand during long charters.
