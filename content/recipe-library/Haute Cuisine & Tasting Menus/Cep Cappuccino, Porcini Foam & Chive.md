# Cep Cappuccino, Porcini Foam & Chive

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A demitasse of deep forest-floor umami under a frothy milk cap — comfort dressed in black tie.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Fresh ceps (or 30 g dried porcini) — 300 g
- Button mushrooms — 200 g
- Shallot, sliced — 60 g
- Butter — 40 g
- White vegetable stock — 700 ml
- Double cream — 150 ml
- Whole milk — 150 ml
- Madeira — 30 ml
- Chives — 5 g
- Fine salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Sweat shallot in butter 3 minutes; add sliced mushrooms and cook hard for 6 minutes to drive off water.
2. Deglaze with Madeira and reduce to a glaze.
3. Add stock, simmer 15 minutes, then add cream and return to 90°C.
4. Blend 2 minutes on high, pass through a chinois and season; hold at 80°C.
5. Warm milk to 65°C and froth with a bamix or steam wand to a fine foam.
6. Pour soup into warmed espresso cups, cap with milk foam and dust with chives.

## Plating & Service
Serve on saucers with a demitasse spoon, like coffee — the visual joke lands every time.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Oat milk foams beautifully for dairy-free; a drop of white truffle oil at the pass upgrades gala dinners.
