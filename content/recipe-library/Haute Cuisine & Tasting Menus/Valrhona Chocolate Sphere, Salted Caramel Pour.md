# Valrhona Chocolate Sphere, Salted Caramel Pour

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The table-side showstopper: a hot caramel stream collapses a tempered shell onto cold chocolate cremeux.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Valrhona Guanaja 70% — 300 g (shells)
- Double cream — 250 ml (cremeux)
- Whole milk — 100 ml
- Egg yolks — 4 (72 g)
- Caster sugar — 60 g (cremeux) + 150 g (caramel)
- Guanaja 70% — 120 g (cremeux)
- Double cream (caramel) — 180 ml
- Butter — 30 g
- Fleur de sel — 3 g
- Cocoa nibs — 20 g
- Vanilla ice cream — 300 g

## Method
1. Temper 300 g chocolate: melt to 50°C, cool to 28°C, lift to 31.5°C.
2. Coat 8 cm hemisphere moulds twice; set, unmould, and seal pairs into spheres with a warm tray edge.
3. Cremeux: make an anglaise (milk, cream, yolks, 60 g sugar) to 83°C; pour over 120 g chocolate, blend, set 4 hours.
4. Caramel: dry-cook 150 g sugar to deep amber; add warm cream carefully, then butter and fleur de sel. Keep at 75°C.
5. Build plates: a quenelle of cremeux, a scoop of ice cream, cocoa nibs — sphere placed over everything.
6. At the table, pour 40 ml hot caramel over each sphere and let it fold open.

## Plating & Service
Silence, pour, collapse, applause. Brief the service team on the pour height — 10 cm, dead centre.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Dark-milk 64% is friendlier for kids; the spheres hold a week in a cool dry box, so batch them in port.
