# Earl Grey Chocolate Truffles, Cocoa Dust

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Bergamot-perfumed ganache rolled rough and dusted dark — handmade imperfection as the final flourish.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Dark chocolate 70% — 300 g
- Double cream — 250 ml
- Earl Grey tea, loose leaf — 8 g
- Butter, soft — 40 g
- Honey — 15 g
- Cocoa powder — 60 g
- Fleur de sel — 1 g

## Method
1. Heat cream to 85°C; steep the tea 5 minutes exactly, then strain, pressing, and re-weigh to 220 ml (top up with cream).
2. Reheat to 80°C and pour over the chopped chocolate with honey; emulsify from the centre.
3. At 35°C, beat in soft butter and salt; pour into a tray and set 3 hours at 16–18°C.
4. Scoop 12 g portions and roll quickly between cool palms — irregular is correct.
5. Roll through sifted cocoa powder; shake off the excess in a sieve.
6. Store at 16°C; serve within a week.

## Plating & Service
Pile in a rough pyramid on slate with coffee — offer seconds; there are never leftovers.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Jasmine tea or fresh mint steep the same way; oat cream converts it vegan with 20 g less butter.
