# Duck Liver Parfait, Port Gel & Brioche Feuille

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A mirror-smooth parfait with ruby port glaze — make-ahead luxury that holds three days on charter.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Duck livers, cleaned — 400 g
- Whole eggs — 100 g (2)
- Butter, melted — 200 g
- Shallot, sliced — 60 g
- Garlic — 1 clove
- Madeira — 60 ml
- Brandy — 30 ml
- Double cream — 60 ml
- Ruby port — 200 ml
- Gelatine leaves — 4 g (2)
- Brioche — 6 thin slices
- Fine salt — 8 g
- Pink curing salt (optional) — 1 g

## Method
1. Reduce shallot, garlic, Madeira and brandy to a syrup; cool.
2. Blend livers, eggs, reduction, cream and salts at 40°C until silky; stream in 50°C melted butter.
3. Pass twice through a fine chinois into a terrine mould.
4. Bake in a bain-marie at 110°C to a core of 64°C, about 35–40 minutes; chill overnight.
5. Reduce port to 100 ml, dissolve in bloomed gelatine, cool to 25°C and pour a 2 mm mirror over the parfait; set 1 hour.
6. Bake brioche slices pressed between trays at 170°C for 8 minutes into crisp leaves.
7. Scoop hot-spoon quenelles or cut clean slices; serve with brioche feuilles and a pinch of fleur de sel.

## Plating & Service
The port mirror must be flawless — pour it in one motion. Cornichons and pickled shallot cut the richness table-side.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken livers substitute exactly; for GF service use seeded crackers and this becomes gluten-free.
