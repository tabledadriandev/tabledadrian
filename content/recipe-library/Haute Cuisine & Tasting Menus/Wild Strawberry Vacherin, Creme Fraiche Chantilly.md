# Wild Strawberry Vacherin, Creme Fraiche Chantilly

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Crisp meringue, tangy chantilly and fraises des bois — the lightest grand dessert in the repertoire.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Egg whites — 120 g (4)
- Caster sugar — 120 g
- Icing sugar — 120 g
- Wild strawberries (fraises des bois) — 250 g
- Strawberries — 200 g
- Caster sugar (coulis) — 30 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Creme fraiche — 200 g
- Double cream — 200 ml
- Vanilla pod — 1
- Icing sugar (chantilly) — 25 g

## Method
1. Whip whites to soft peaks; rain in caster sugar to a stiff gloss, then fold in sifted icing sugar.
2. Pipe six 8 cm nests and extra shards; dry at 90°C for 2 hours until crisp through; cool in the oven.
3. Coulis: blend 200 g strawberries with 30 g sugar and lemon; strain.
4. Chantilly: whip cream, creme fraiche, icing sugar and vanilla seeds to soft peaks.
5. Fill each nest with chantilly, pressing wild strawberries into the cream.
6. Crown with more berries, meringue shards and coulis poured at the table.

## Plating & Service
Assemble within 30 minutes of service — vacherin softens beautifully and then fatally.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Any peak-season berry works; passion fruit curd inside the nest is the winter escape route.
