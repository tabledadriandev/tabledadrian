# Salt-Baked Beetroot Terrine, Smoked Creme Fraiche & Horseradish

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A stained-glass terrine of salt-baked beets with cool smoke and heat — a vegetarian centrepiece that outshines meat.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Mixed beetroots (red, golden, candy) — 1.2 kg
- Coarse sea salt — 800 g
- Egg white — 60 g
- Thyme — 5 g
- Gelatine leaves — 8 g (4)
- Beetroot juice — 200 ml
- Sherry vinegar — 25 ml
- Creme fraiche — 150 g
- Smoked oil or 2 g smoked salt — 10 ml
- Fresh horseradish — 15 g
- Baby red-vein sorrel — 12 leaves
- Fine salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Mix coarse salt, egg white and thyme; encase beets by colour and bake at 180°C for 75–90 minutes until tender; cool, crack, peel.
2. Slice beets into 3 mm sheets on a mandoline, keeping colours separate.
3. Warm beet juice with vinegar to 60°C, season, and dissolve bloomed gelatine into it.
4. Layer beet sheets in a lined terrine mould, painting every layer with the gelatine juice, alternating colours.
5. Press overnight at 3°C under a 1 kg weight.
6. Whip creme fraiche with smoked oil and salt; grate in half the horseradish.
7. Slice the terrine 2 cm thick with a hot knife; plate with smoked cream, fresh horseradish and sorrel.

## Plating & Service
The cross-section is the show — serve the slice upright, cream swept alongside, nothing else crowding the plate.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Coconut yoghurt makes it vegan (use agar, 3 g, instead of gelatine); a scatter of candied walnuts adds crunch (nut allergen).
