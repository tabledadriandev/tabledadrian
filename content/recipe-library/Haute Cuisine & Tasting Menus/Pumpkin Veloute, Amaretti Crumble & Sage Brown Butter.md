# Pumpkin Veloute, Amaretti Crumble & Sage Brown Butter

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Silky roasted pumpkin soup with the Lombard trick of crushed amaretti — sweet, bitter, nutty, complete.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Delica or butternut pumpkin — 900 g
- Shallot, sliced — 80 g
- Butter — 90 g
- Vegetable stock — 700 ml
- Double cream — 100 ml
- Amaretti biscuits — 50 g
- Sage leaves — 12
- Nutmeg — 0.5 g
- Pumpkin seed oil — 15 ml
- Pumpkin seeds, toasted — 20 g
- Fine salt — 7 g

## Method
1. Roast pumpkin wedges at 190°C for 30 minutes until caramelised at the edges; scoop the flesh.
2. Sweat shallot in 30 g butter 4 minutes; add pumpkin and stock, simmer 10 minutes.
3. Blend on high with cream, nutmeg and salt; pass through a chinois — it should nap a spoon.
4. Cook remaining 60 g butter to noisette; fry sage leaves 30 seconds until crisp, reserve both.
5. Crush amaretti with toasted pumpkin seeds into a coarse crumble.
6. Pour veloute into warm bowls; finish with amaretti crumble, sage, brown butter and pumpkin seed oil.

## Plating & Service
Pour at the table over the garnishes so the crumble stays crisp; the seed oil ring gives the dark green contrast.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: use gingerbread crumb instead of amaretti. Coconut cream converts it dairy-free with a Thai squash accent.
