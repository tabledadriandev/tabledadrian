# White Truffle Risotto, Aged Carnaroli & Parmesan

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The November ritual: acquerello rice, good broth, and Alba truffle shaved until someone says stop.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Aged Carnaroli rice — 280 g
- White vegetable or light chicken stock — 1.2 l
- White wine — 80 ml
- Shallot, brunoise — 50 g
- Butter — 100 g
- Parmesan, 30 months, grated — 90 g
- White Alba truffle — 15–20 g
- Celery heart, brunoise — 30 g
- Fine salt — 6 g
- White pepper — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Hold the stock at a bare simmer; warm four flat bowls.
2. Sweat shallot and celery in 30 g butter until translucent, 4 minutes.
3. Toast the rice 2 minutes until the grains click; deglaze with wine and let it vanish.
4. Ladle in hot stock one addition at a time, stirring, for 16–17 minutes — the grain should be al dente with a flowing wave.
5. Rest off the heat 1 minute; mantecatura: beat in remaining 70 g cold butter and Parmesan hard until glossy.
6. Season, loosen with a final splash of stock — all'onda, it must ripple.
7. Plate flat, tap the bowl, and shave white truffle over each at the table.

## Plating & Service
Shave the truffle in front of the guest, never in the kitchen — the perfume is 80% of the price.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Off-season: finish with lemon zest and marjoram instead; never use truffle oil, ever.
