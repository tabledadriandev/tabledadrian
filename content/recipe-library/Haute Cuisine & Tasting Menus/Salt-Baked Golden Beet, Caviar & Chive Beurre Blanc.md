# Salt-Baked Golden Beet, Caviar & Chive Beurre Blanc

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A vegetable treated like a fish course — sweet golden beet under classic butter sauce and a coin of caviar.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Golden beetroots, medium — 4 (600 g)
- Coarse sea salt — 600 g
- Egg white — 50 g
- White wine — 100 ml
- White wine vinegar — 30 ml
- Shallot, brunoise — 40 g
- Cold butter, diced — 150 g
- Double cream — 20 ml
- Oscietra caviar — 30 g
- Chives — 8 g
- Lemon juice — 5 ml

## Method
1. Encase beets in the salt-egg white crust and bake at 180°C for 70–80 minutes until a skewer meets no resistance.
2. Rest 10 minutes, crack the crust, peel, and trim each beet into a thick 5 cm 'medallion'.
3. Reduce wine, vinegar and shallot to 30 ml.
4. Add cream, then whisk in cold butter cube by cube over low heat — never above 80°C.
5. Strain the beurre blanc, season with lemon and fold in chives.
6. Warm the beet medallions in a spoonful of the sauce, glazing all faces.
7. Plate one glazed beet per guest, sauce pooled around, 7 g caviar seated on top.

## Plating & Service
Minimal and confident: one golden disc, ivory sauce, black caviar. Warm shallow bowls only.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skip caviar and add crisped capers for a fully vegetarian plate; red beets work but will blush the sauce.
