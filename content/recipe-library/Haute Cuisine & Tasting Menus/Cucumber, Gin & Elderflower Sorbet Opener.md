# Cucumber, Gin & Elderflower Sorbet Opener

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A frozen gin-and-tonic disguised as a first course — dangerously refreshing at anchor in July.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Cucumber, unpeeled — 500 g
- Elderflower cordial — 90 ml
- Gin — 40 ml
- Lime juice — 30 ml
- Caster sugar — 40 g
- Glucose powder — 20 g
- Fine salt — 1 g
- Borage flowers — 8
- Cucumber ribbons — 8 thin strips

## Method
1. Juice the cucumbers and strain to yield about 350 ml of vivid green juice.
2. Warm 100 ml of the juice with sugar and glucose to 45°C until dissolved; chill.
3. Combine all liquids with salt; the gin must not exceed 40 ml or the sorbet will not freeze.
4. Churn in an ice-cream machine to a soft-serve texture, then blast-freeze 30 minutes.
5. Curl a cucumber ribbon inside each frozen coupe.
6. Quenelle the sorbet into the coupes and crown with a borage flower.

## Plating & Service
Serve on crushed ice between canape rounds or as course zero; it must arrive frostier than the Champagne.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Replace gin with cucumber water for zero-alcohol charters; a pinch of wasabi in the base adds a grown-up edge.
