# Marinated Sea Bream, Fennel Pollen & Blood Orange

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Riviera-caught bream cured minutes before serving, with anise perfume and bitter-sweet citrus.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Sea bream fillets, sashimi grade — 320 g
- Blood oranges — 2
- Fennel bulb, small — 1 (200 g)
- Fennel pollen — 1 g
- Lemon juice — 25 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 50 ml
- Pink peppercorns — 1 g
- Fennel fronds — 8 sprigs
- Fleur de sel — 3 g
- Caster sugar — 2 g

## Method
1. Pin-bone and skin the bream; slice on the bias into 3 mm sashimi and lay flat on chilled plates.
2. Segment one blood orange into supremes; juice the other (about 60 ml).
3. Whisk blood orange juice, lemon juice, sugar, fleur de sel and olive oil.
4. Shave fennel paper-thin and crisp in ice water 10 minutes; drain and dry.
5. Five minutes before serving, spoon the citrus dressing over the fish — it should barely opalesce, not cook through.
6. Dress with fennel shavings, orange supremes, crushed pink pepper, fennel pollen and fronds.

## Plating & Service
Serve on chilled white plates so the blood orange bleeds visibly into the dressing; eat within 10 minutes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Regular oranges plus 5 ml grenadine mimic blood orange off-season; amberjack or bass substitute freely.
