# Smoked Eel Tartlet, Granny Smith & Wasabi

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rich smoked eel cut by sharp apple and a controlled sting of wasabi — a sommelier's favourite with Riesling.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Smoked eel fillet — 160 g
- All-butter puff pastry — 200 g
- Egg yolk, for glaze — 18 g
- Granny Smith apple — 1 (150 g)
- Creme fraiche — 80 g
- Fresh wasabi, grated — 4 g
- Lime juice — 10 ml
- Chives, sliced — 5 g
- Baby shiso — 8 leaves
- Fine salt — 2 g

## Method
1. Roll puff to 3 mm, dock, glaze with yolk and bake between two trays at 190°C for 15 minutes until lacquered and flat.
2. Cut the warm pastry into 8 rectangles of 3 x 6 cm.
3. Whip creme fraiche with wasabi, lime juice and salt to firm ribbons; transfer to a piping bag.
4. Slice eel into 20 g batons, trimming any dark bloodline.
5. Cut the apple into 2 mm julienne and hold in water with a squeeze of lime.
6. Pipe wasabi cream along each pastry base, seat the eel on top, and finish with apple, chives and shiso.

## Plating & Service
Serve on a chilled slate within 10 minutes of building so the pastry stays shattering-crisp.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Hot-smoked trout stands in for eel; use a spelt or GF pastry and dairy-free creme for allergies.
