# Breakfast Radishes, Seaweed Butter & Fleur de Sel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The simplest, chicest thing on the table: crunchy radishes dragged through iodine-rich whipped butter.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Breakfast radishes with tops — 24 pieces
- Beurre d'Isigny, soft — 150 g
- Nori sheet, toasted — 3 g
- Dried wakame, rehydrated and chopped — 10 g
- Lemon juice — 8 ml
- Fleur de sel — 4 g
- Espelette pepper — 1 g
- Ice water — 500 ml

## Method
1. Trim radishes leaving 2 cm of the prettiest leaves; crisp in ice water 15 minutes and dry.
2. Blitz toasted nori to a fine powder.
3. Whip butter 3 minutes until pale; fold in nori powder, wakame, lemon juice and Espelette.
4. Pipe or quenelle the butter into a chilled bowl and rake the surface with a fork.
5. Arrange radishes leaves-up around the butter, over crushed ice.
6. Serve fleur de sel in a tiny pinch-pot alongside.

## Plating & Service
Rustic-luxe: a swoop of green-flecked butter, standing radishes, salt on the side — let guests dip and season themselves.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Whipped miso-cashew cream makes it vegan (adds nuts, soy); the seaweed butter is also superb on grilled fish.
