# Roasted Quail Breast, Grape & Verjus Salad

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rosy quail with burst muscat grapes and the gentle acid of verjus — an autumn starter that eats like a small main.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Quail crowns — 4 (about 480 g)
- Muscat grapes — 200 g
- Verjus — 60 ml
- Chicken jus, reduced — 80 ml
- Frisee hearts — 60 g
- Hazelnut oil — 0 ml (omit for nut-free) or grapeseed oil — 20 ml
- Shallot, brunoise — 20 g
- Thyme — 4 sprigs
- Duck fat or olive oil — 30 g
- Fleur de sel — 3 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Season the quail crowns and rest at room temperature 20 minutes.
2. Colour the crowns in duck fat with thyme, 2 minutes per side, basting constantly.
3. Roast at 190°C for 5–6 minutes to a breast core of 58°C; rest 6 minutes breast-down.
4. Halve half the grapes; blister the rest whole in the quail pan for 90 seconds.
5. Deglaze with verjus, reduce by half, add chicken jus and shallot; season to a light, sharp sauce.
6. Dress frisee and halved grapes with grapeseed oil and a spoon of the sauce.
7. Carve breasts off the crown, season the cut face with fleur de sel, and plate with salad, blistered grapes and sauce.

## Plating & Service
One crown per guest, breasts fanned, salad standing tall — the sauce should be brushed, not flooded.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pigeon breast doubles the luxury; in summer use cherries and a splash of red-wine vinegar instead of grapes and verjus.
