# Chestnut Veloute, Foie Gras Royale & Sherry

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Winter in a bowl: silky chestnut soup poured over a trembling foie gras custard at the table.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Cooked peeled chestnuts — 400 g
- Shallot, sliced — 60 g
- Celery, sliced — 40 g
- Butter — 40 g
- Dry sherry — 50 ml
- Chicken or vegetable stock — 800 ml
- Double cream — 150 ml
- Foie gras — 100 g
- Whole egg — 50 g
- Egg yolk — 18 g
- Whole milk — 100 ml
- Fine salt — 7 g
- Chives — 4 g

## Method
1. Make the royale: blend foie gras, egg, yolk, warm milk and 2 g salt; pass, pour 30 g into each warmed bowl.
2. Steam the bowls, covered, at 90°C for 12–14 minutes until just set with a central tremble; hold warm.
3. Sweat shallot and celery in butter 5 minutes; add chestnuts and sherry, reduce 2 minutes.
4. Add stock, simmer 20 minutes; add cream and return to 90°C.
5. Blend on high 2 minutes, pass through a fine chinois, and season — the texture must be double-cream thick.
6. At the table, pour 120 ml veloute around (not over) each royale and finish with chives.

## Plating & Service
Table-side pour from a warmed silver jug; the royale should stay visible as an island in the veloute.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skip the royale and add sherry-glazed chestnuts for an easier build; parsnip replaces chestnut nicely in January.
