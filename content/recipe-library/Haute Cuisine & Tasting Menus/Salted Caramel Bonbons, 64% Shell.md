# Salted Caramel Bonbons, 64% Shell

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Glossy moulded chocolates with a flowing salted caramel heart — the last word of the meal, engineered to shine.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Dark chocolate 64%, tempered — 400 g
- Caster sugar — 150 g
- Glucose — 30 g
- Double cream, warm — 150 ml
- Butter — 40 g
- Fleur de sel — 3 g
- Vanilla pod — 0.5
- Cocoa butter, coloured (optional) — 10 g

## Method
1. Polish the moulds; flick with coloured cocoa butter if painting.
2. Temper the chocolate: melt to 50°C, table to 28°C, lift to 31.5°C.
3. Shell the moulds: fill, tap out bubbles, invert and drain; scrape clean; set 20 minutes.
4. Caramel: cook sugar and glucose to deep amber; whisk in warm cream, vanilla and butter; add fleur de sel and cool to 28°C.
5. Pipe caramel to 2 mm below the shell rim; let skin 1 hour.
6. Cap with tempered chocolate, scrape flat, set 2 hours at 16–18°C and unmould with a confident twist.

## Plating & Service
Serve at 18–20°C with coffee — never fridge-cold; the shell snap then the caramel flow is the finale of the whole menu.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Passion fruit caramel (replace half the cream with puree) is the summer variant; keep 3 weeks in a cool dry box.
