# Charred Octopus, Smoked Paprika Emulsion & Confit Potato

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Blistered octopus over saffron-gold potatoes — Galicia refined for white tablecloths.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Octopus, double-sucker tentacles — 600 g
- Bay leaves — 2
- Ratte potatoes — 300 g
- Olive oil — 250 ml
- Garlic — 3 cloves
- Saffron — 0.2 g
- Egg yolk — 18 g
- Smoked paprika — 4 g
- Sherry vinegar — 10 ml
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Parsley, chopped — 6 g
- Fine salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Simmer the octopus with bay in unsalted water at 85–90°C for 60–75 minutes until a knife slides into the thickest part; cool in the liquid.
2. Confit peeled potatoes in 200 ml olive oil with garlic and saffron at 90°C for 35 minutes; season.
3. Make the emulsion: whisk yolk with vinegar, paprika and salt, then thread in 50 ml of the warm confit oil; loosen with lemon juice.
4. Dry the tentacles thoroughly and oil lightly.
5. Char on a screaming-hot plancha or grill, 2 minutes per side, until blistered and crisp-edged.
6. Slice potatoes into thick coins, swipe the paprika emulsion, arrange potato and octopus, and finish with parsley and confit oil.

## Plating & Service
Serve on warm stoneware; the octopus should arrive crackling from the plancha with visible char.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pressure-cook the octopus 18 minutes to save time; the emulsion doubles as a dip for crudites.
