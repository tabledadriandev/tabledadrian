# Tropical Pavlova, Mango, Lime Leaf & Passion

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Marshmallow-hearted meringue under sharp tropical fruit and kaffir-scented cream — sunshine engineering.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Egg whites — 150 g (5)
- Caster sugar — 250 g
- Cornflour — 10 g
- White wine vinegar — 5 ml
- Double cream — 300 ml
- Kaffir lime leaves — 3
- Icing sugar — 30 g
- Ripe mango — 1 (300 g)
- Passion fruits — 4
- Lime — 1
- Mint tips — 8

## Method
1. Whip whites to soft peaks; rain in caster sugar over 5 minutes to a dense gloss; fold in cornflour and vinegar.
2. Shape six 8 cm discs with swooped sides; bake at 110°C for 75–90 minutes, then cool in the oven — crisp shell, mallow core.
3. Infuse warmed cream with bruised lime leaves 20 minutes; chill thoroughly.
4. Whip the infused cream with icing sugar to soft peaks.
5. Dice mango into 1 cm jewels; halve passion fruits.
6. Crown each pavlova with cream, mango, passion pulp, lime zest and mint.

## Plating & Service
Assemble at the pass, serve within 20 minutes; the collapse of shell into mallow into cream is the pleasure.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Coconut cream whips for a dairy-free version (chill the tin overnight); berries convert it to the classic.
