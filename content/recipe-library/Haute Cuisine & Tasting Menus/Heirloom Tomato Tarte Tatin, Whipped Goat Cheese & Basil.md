# Heirloom Tomato Tarte Tatin, Whipped Goat Cheese & Basil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Caramelised tomatoes baked under puff and flipped glossy-side up — a savoury tatin that owns a summer lunch.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Mixed heirloom tomatoes, small — 700 g
- Caster sugar — 40 g
- Sherry vinegar — 25 ml
- Butter — 30 g
- Thyme — 4 sprigs
- Puff pastry — 300 g
- Goat cheese, fresh — 150 g
- Double cream — 40 ml
- Basil — 12 leaves
- Olive oil — 20 ml
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Black pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Halve tomatoes and roast cut-up at 140°C for 25 minutes to drive off water; season.
2. In a 24 cm ovenproof pan, cook sugar to amber; add vinegar, butter and thyme for a sharp caramel.
3. Pack the tomatoes cut-down into the caramel, tightly — shrinkage is real.
4. Cap with a 26 cm puff disc, tucking the edges down the sides; vent once.
5. Bake at 200°C for 25–28 minutes until deep gold; rest 5 minutes only.
6. Flip decisively onto a board — one motion, no fear.
7. Whip goat cheese with cream and salt; serve wedges warm with the cheese, basil and olive oil.

## Plating & Service
Serve within 15 minutes of flipping while the caramel is still lacquer, with a bitter-leaf salad.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: olive-oil pastry and whipped tofu-lemon cream; shallot tatin follows the same math in winter.
