# Blue Lobster Salad, Vanilla Vinaigrette & Baby Gem

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Poached blue lobster with a whisper of vanilla — the classic pairing that still silences a table.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Blue lobster, live — 1 (700 g)
- Court-bouillon — 3 l
- Baby gem lettuce — 2 heads
- Mango, brunoise — 60 g
- Avocado — 1 (150 g)
- Vanilla pod — 1
- White balsamic — 20 ml
- Dijon mustard — 5 g
- Grapeseed oil — 60 ml
- Creme fraiche — 30 g
- Chervil — 8 sprigs
- Fine salt — 4 g

## Method
1. Dispatch the lobster, then blanch in boiling court-bouillon: 4 minutes for the tail, 6 for the claws; ice immediately.
2. Extract tail and claw meat whole; slice the tail into 8 medallions and chill.
3. Split the vanilla pod and whisk seeds with balsamic, mustard and salt; emulsify with grapeseed oil.
4. Crush avocado with creme fraiche and salt to a coarse cream.
5. Dress baby gem leaves and mango brunoise lightly in the vinaigrette.
6. Compose: avocado cream at the base, leaves standing, medallions and claw meat glazed with vinaigrette.
7. Finish with chervil and a final thread of vanilla vinaigrette around the plate.

## Plating & Service
Serve well-chilled but not refrigerator-cold — 10 minutes at room temperature lets the vanilla speak.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Prawns or crab keep the idea affordable; swap creme fraiche for coconut cream to lose dairy.
