# Dover Sole, Brown Butter, Grapes & Verjus

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Whole sole cooked on the bone in noisette butter, sharpened with verjus — Veronique without the nostalgia.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Dover soles, skinned, 450 g each — 4
- Butter — 160 g
- Grapeseed oil — 20 ml
- Muscat grapes, halved — 200 g
- Verjus — 60 ml
- Capers, rinsed — 20 g
- Parsley, chopped — 8 g
- Lemon — 1
- Rice flour — 40 g
- Fleur de sel — 5 g
- White pepper — 1 g

## Method
1. Trim fins and dust the soles lightly with rice flour; season.
2. In two large pans, heat oil with 40 g butter each to a fast foam.
3. Cook the soles 4 minutes on the presentation side, basting, then 3 minutes on the reverse; core 54°C.
4. Rest the fish; wipe one pan, add remaining 80 g butter and cook to hazelnut brown.
5. Add grapes, capers and verjus — stand back — and swirl 60 seconds.
6. Finish with parsley, a squeeze of lemon and salt.
7. Lift the top fillets or serve whole on the bone, spooning the grape noisette over generously.

## Plating & Service
Fillet table-side for full effect: four strokes, bones lifted whole. Steamed ratte potatoes are the only side allowed.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Lemon sole works midweek; peeled white grapes and a spoon of cream turn it classically Veronique.
