# Roasted Monkfish, Vadouvan Butter & Heritage Carrots

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Meaty monkfish tail with a gently spiced French-curry butter — exotic warmth without losing its Riviera manners.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Monkfish tail, trimmed — 700 g
- Vadouvan spice blend — 8 g
- Butter — 120 g
- Heritage carrots — 400 g
- Carrot juice — 200 ml
- Orange juice — 50 ml
- Cumin seeds — 1 g
- Coriander, fresh — 8 g
- Lime — 1
- Grapeseed oil — 20 ml
- Fine salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Brine monkfish in 5% salt water 15 minutes; dry and portion into 4 medallion sets.
2. Simmer carrot juice with orange juice and cumin, reducing to 60 ml; blend with 200 g cooked carrot and salt into a satin puree.
3. Roll remaining carrots in oil and roast at 190°C for 18–20 minutes until blistered.
4. Melt butter with vadouvan at 60°C and infuse 10 minutes.
5. Sear monkfish in a hot pan with oil, 2 minutes per face; then baste with the vadouvan butter 4–5 minutes to a core of 54°C.
6. Rest 4 minutes, basting once more.
7. Plate puree, roasted carrots and sliced monkfish; spoon over the spiced butter, finish with coriander and lime zest.

## Plating & Service
Slice the monkfish thick to show the pearly interior; the butter should freckle the plate with spice.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Ghee makes it lactose-light; the vadouvan butter is equally good on roasted cauliflower for a veg main.
