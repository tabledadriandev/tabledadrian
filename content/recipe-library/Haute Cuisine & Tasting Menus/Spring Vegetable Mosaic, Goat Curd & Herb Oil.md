# Spring Vegetable Mosaic, Goat Curd & Herb Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Two dozen pieces of perfect spring produce arranged like a stained-glass window over whipped goat curd.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Baby carrots, mixed — 8 (150 g)
- Asparagus tips — 8 (100 g)
- Fresh peas — 80 g
- Broad beans, double-podded — 80 g
- Radishes — 4
- Baby courgette — 1 (80 g)
- Baby turnips — 4 (100 g)
- Goat curd — 160 g
- Double cream — 30 ml
- Mixed soft herbs (chervil, tarragon, chive, basil) — 40 g
- Grapeseed oil — 100 ml
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Fleur de sel — 4 g

## Method
1. Blend half the herbs with grapeseed oil at 60°C for 4 minutes; strain through muslin over ice.
2. Whip goat curd with cream, lemon juice and salt to soft peaks; chill in a piping bag.
3. Blanch each vegetable separately in heavily salted water to just-tender — peas 60 s, asparagus 2 min, carrots and turnips 3–4 min — icing each batch.
4. Slice courgette and radishes raw on the mandoline, 1 mm.
5. Dry every element meticulously and dress in a bowl with herb oil and fleur de sel.
6. Pipe goat curd in a low disc, then build the mosaic: cooked and raw vegetables standing, leaning, overlapping.
7. Finish with remaining herb leaves and a final ring of herb oil.

## Plating & Service
Every plate should look grown, not arranged — vary heights and angles. Serve cool, not cold.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Whipped tofu with white miso replaces curd for vegans (adds soy); winter version uses roasted roots and the same oil.
