# Line-Caught Sea Bass, Fennel Confit & Pastis Emulsion

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Crisp-skinned bass over melting fennel with an anise-scented olive oil emulsion — the taste of the Esterel coast.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Sea bass fillets, skin on — 4 x 170 g
- Fennel bulbs — 2 (500 g)
- Olive oil — 120 ml
- Garlic, sliced — 8 g
- Thyme — 4 sprigs
- Orange — 1
- Pastis — 25 ml
- Fish stock — 150 ml
- Lecithin (optional) — 2 g
- Fennel fronds — 8
- Black olives, dried and crushed — 20 g
- Fine salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Slice fennel 5 mm thick and confit gently in 80 ml olive oil with garlic, thyme, orange zest and salt, lidded, 30 minutes until silky.
2. Reduce fish stock with pastis and the juice of half the orange to 80 ml.
3. Blend the reduction with 40 ml olive oil (and lecithin if using) into a loose emulsion; keep at 60°C.
4. Score the bass skin, salt 10 minutes, then dry hard.
5. Cook skin-side down in a cold-start non-stick pan, pressing flat, 5–6 minutes until 80% cooked; flip for 30 seconds. Core 52°C.
6. Plate fennel confit, bass skin-up, froth and spoon the pastis emulsion, finish with olive crumb and fronds.

## Plating & Service
Skin like glass, sauce like sea foam. Serve with nothing starchy — let the fennel be the comfort.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Ouzo or a fennel-seed infusion replaces pastis; the fennel confit keeps a week under its oil.
