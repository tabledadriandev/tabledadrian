# Milk-Fed Veal Loin, Girolles & Tarragon Jus

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Pale, tender veal with apricot-scented girolles — a gentle, aristocratic main that lets summer produce speak.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Veal loin, trimmed — 700 g
- Girolles, cleaned — 250 g
- Butter — 80 g
- Shallot, brunoise — 40 g
- White wine — 80 ml
- Veal jus — 200 ml
- Tarragon — 8 g
- Baby gem or chard — 200 g
- Grapeseed oil — 20 ml
- Lemon juice — 8 ml
- Fine salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Temper the loin 30 minutes; season.
2. Sear in oil and 30 g butter on all faces, 5 minutes total, then roast at 170°C to a core of 56°C, about 15–18 minutes. Rest 10 minutes.
3. Dry-fry girolles in a hot pan 2 minutes to expel water; add 30 g butter and shallot, cook 3 minutes, season with lemon.
4. Deglaze the roasting pan with wine; add jus, reduce to coating, and infuse tarragon stems 5 minutes; strain and finish with chopped leaves and 20 g butter.
5. Wilt the greens with the last nut of butter.
6. Carve pink slices, season the faces; plate with girolles, greens and tarragon jus.

## Plating & Service
Rosy veal, golden mushrooms, dark jus — a quiet plate that must be served hot and fast.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Pork tenderloin is the honest weekday version; trumpet mushrooms work when girolles finish.
