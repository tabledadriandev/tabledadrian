# Yellowtail, Ponzu Beurre Blanc & Pickled Kohlrabi

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Torched amberjack where a Japanese citrus sauce meets French butter craft — the Riviera's east-facing plate.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Yellowtail (hamachi) loin, sashimi grade — 320 g
- Kohlrabi — 150 g
- Rice vinegar — 60 ml
- Caster sugar — 25 g
- Ponzu — 40 ml
- White wine — 60 ml
- Shallot, brunoise — 30 g
- Cold butter, diced — 100 g
- Grapeseed oil — 10 ml
- Shiso or coriander cress — 12 leaves
- Black sesame — 2 g
- Fine salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Pickle 1 mm kohlrabi slices in warmed rice vinegar, sugar and a pinch of salt for at least 30 minutes.
2. Slice yellowtail into 5 mm sashimi and arrange on oiled plates.
3. Blowtorch the slices briefly until the edges blister and the fat scents the air; season lightly.
4. Reduce wine with shallot to 15 ml; whisk in cold butter cube by cube below 80°C.
5. Finish the beurre blanc with ponzu off the heat; do not re-boil.
6. Sauce around the fish, fold pickled kohlrabi into waves between slices, garnish with cress and sesame.

## Plating & Service
Serve immediately after torching, sauce barely warm — the fish should remain essentially raw at the centre.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Salmon belly is a lush substitute; tamari keeps it gluten-free if the ponzu is certified GF.
