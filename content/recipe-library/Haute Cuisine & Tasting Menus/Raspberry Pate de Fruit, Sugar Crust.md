# Raspberry Pate de Fruit, Sugar Crust

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Jewel-bright fruit jellies with a true set and clean snap of acidity — the benchmark petit four.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Raspberry puree, sieved — 500 g
- Caster sugar — 450 g + 60 g (coating)
- Glucose syrup — 90 g
- Yellow pectin — 12 g
- Citric acid solution (1:1 with water) — 8 g
- Lemon juice — 10 ml

## Method
1. Whisk pectin with 50 g of the sugar (dry) — this prevents lumps.
2. Warm the puree to 40°C; rain in the pectin-sugar, whisking, and bring to a boil.
3. Add remaining 400 g sugar in three additions, then glucose; cook, stirring, to 106–107°C (75° Brix).
4. Off the heat, stir in citric acid solution and lemon juice; pour immediately into a 20 cm frame, 1.5 cm deep.
5. Set 4 hours at room temperature — never in the fridge.
6. Cut 2.5 cm cubes with an oiled knife and roll in caster sugar just before service.

## Plating & Service
Serve in a single sugar-frosted row on dark slate with coffee; they hold two weeks in an airtight box, uncoated.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Passion fruit, apricot or blackcurrant purees follow identical numbers; halve citric acid for low-acid fruits.
