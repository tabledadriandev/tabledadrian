# Chocolate & Olive Oil Cremeux, Fleur de Sel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Dark chocolate silk finished with grassy oil and salt crystals — five ingredients, total sophistication.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Dark chocolate 70% — 200 g
- Whole milk — 200 ml
- Double cream — 200 ml
- Egg yolks — 72 g (4)
- Caster sugar — 50 g
- Extra virgin olive oil, peppery — 30 ml
- Fleur de sel — 2 g
- Cocoa nibs — 15 g
- Orange zest — 2 g

## Method
1. Make an anglaise: heat milk and cream; whisk yolks with sugar, combine, and stir to 83°C.
2. Pour hot anglaise over the chopped chocolate in three additions, emulsifying from the centre.
3. Blend 30 seconds with a hand blender, adding 15 ml olive oil in a thread.
4. Pour into shallow bowls or a single dish; set at 3°C for 4 hours.
5. Temper 20 minutes at room temperature before serving — cold kills the perfume.
6. Finish each with olive oil pooled in a thumb-press, fleur de sel, cocoa nibs and a breath of orange zest.

## Plating & Service
Serve with a warm spoon and unhurried confidence; the oil pool should sit like a green-gold eye.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Oat cream converts it dairy-free; a rosemary-infused oil variation is spectacular with the same chocolate.
