# Nicoise 'Fine' — Confit Tuna, Quail Egg & Tapenade

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The harbour salad rebuilt with olive-oil-poached tuna and precise garnishes — Nice, tightened to a fine point.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Yellowfin tuna loin — 320 g
- Olive oil — 400 ml
- Thyme — 4 sprigs
- Quail eggs — 8
- Fine green beans — 120 g
- Datterini tomatoes — 120 g
- Nicoise olives, pitted — 60 g
- Anchovy fillets — 4 (16 g)
- Capers — 10 g
- Red wine vinegar — 15 ml
- Basil — 8 leaves
- Fleur de sel — 3 g

## Method
1. Cure the tuna with fleur de sel for 20 minutes; rinse and dry.
2. Poach the loin in 60°C olive oil with thyme for 20–25 minutes to a rosy 45°C core; rest in the oil.
3. Boil quail eggs 2 minutes 20 seconds, ice, peel and halve — yolks must be fudgy.
4. Blanch beans 3 minutes, ice, and dress with 15 ml poaching oil and vinegar.
5. Blitz olives, anchovies and capers with 20 ml oil to a coarse tapenade.
6. Quarter the tomatoes and season with fleur de sel.
7. Flake the tuna into large petals; compose with beans, tomatoes, eggs, tapenade dots and basil.

## Plating & Service
Compose loosely but deliberately — this should look effortless. Serve barely warm with chilled rose.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Omit anchovy for a milder tapenade; leftover confit oil is liquid gold for vinaigrettes all week.
