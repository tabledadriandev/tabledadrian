# Peach Melba Revisited, Raspberry & Amaretti

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Escoffier's peach rebuilt: vanilla-poached peach, raspberry coulis, iced vanilla and amaretti crunch.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Yellow peaches, ripe — 6
- Water — 800 ml
- Caster sugar — 200 g (syrup) + 30 g (coulis)
- Vanilla pods — 2
- Raspberries — 300 g
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Whole milk — 250 ml
- Double cream — 250 ml
- Egg yolks — 90 g (5)
- Caster sugar (anglaise) — 80 g
- Amaretti, crushed — 60 g
- Raspberries, fresh — 100 g

## Method
1. Ice cream: anglaise of milk, cream, one vanilla pod, yolks and 80 g sugar to 83°C; chill, churn, freeze.
2. Poach peaches whole in syrup (water, 200 g sugar, one vanilla pod) at 85°C for 8–12 minutes; cool, slip off the skins.
3. Coulis: blend 300 g raspberries with 30 g sugar and lemon; strain seeds out.
4. Halve and stone the peaches, keeping the shape.
5. Build in coupes: amaretti base, peach half, ice cream quenelle, second peach half leaning.
6. Pour coulis at the table and finish with fresh raspberries.

## Plating & Service
Serve in chilled silver coupes if the boat has them — this dessert deserves its history. Coulis always table-side.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: replace amaretti with sable crumb; nectarines poach identically in high summer.
