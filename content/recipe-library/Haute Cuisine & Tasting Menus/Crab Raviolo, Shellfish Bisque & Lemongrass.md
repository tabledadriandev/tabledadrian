# Crab Raviolo, Shellfish Bisque & Lemongrass

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> One grand raviolo per guest, flooded with a lemongrass-scented bisque — a signature-dish candidate.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- 00 flour — 150 g
- Egg yolks — 90 g (5)
- White crab meat — 200 g
- Brown crab meat — 40 g
- Creme fraiche — 30 g
- Lime zest — 2 g
- Crab or prawn shells — 400 g
- Tomato paste — 30 g
- Cognac — 30 ml
- Fish stock — 700 ml
- Double cream — 100 ml
- Lemongrass, bruised — 2 stalks
- Butter — 40 g
- Fine salt — 7 g

## Method
1. Roast the shells with 20 g butter at 200°C for 20 minutes; flambe with cognac in a pan.
2. Add tomato paste, fish stock and lemongrass; simmer 35 minutes, pressing the shells, then strain hard.
3. Reduce to 250 ml, add cream, season — the bisque should coat a spoon lightly.
4. Fold white crab with brown crab, creme fraiche, lime zest and salt; chill the farce.
5. Sheet the pasta (flour + yolks, rested 1 hour) to 0.8 mm; cut eight 12 cm discs.
6. Mound 60 g farce on four discs, cap with the others, and seal from the centre out, excluding all air.
7. Poach ravioli 3.5 minutes in salted water at a bare boil; glaze with the remaining butter.
8. Centre one raviolo per warm bowl and pour the bisque around at the table.

## Plating & Service
The raviolo must sit proud of the bisque; a lemongrass baton laid across the rim signals the aromatics.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Lobster or prawn farce works identically; finish with a coriander-oil ring for colour.
