# Loup en Croute, Sauce Choron

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A whole sea bass baked in golden pastry scales with a tomato-blushed bearnaise — Bocuse-era theatre, worth every minute.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Sea bass, 1.2 kg, filleted with skin off — 2 sides
- Puff pastry — 600 g
- Scallop meat — 150 g
- Egg white — 20 g
- Double cream — 100 ml
- Tarragon — 12 g
- Egg yolks (sauce) — 3
- Butter, clarified — 180 g
- White wine vinegar — 30 ml
- Shallot, brunoise — 30 g
- Tomato paste — 25 g
- Egg wash — 1 yolk + 10 ml cream
- Fine salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Blend scallop, egg white and 2 g salt; beat in cold cream over ice; fold in chopped tarragon for a green-flecked mousse.
2. Sandwich the mousse between the two seasoned bass sides, rebuilding the fish shape.
3. Roll pastry into two sheets, 3 mm; encase the fish, sealing edges with egg wash, and trim to a fish silhouette.
4. Score scales with an upturned piping tip, add a pastry eye and fins; egg-wash twice, resting 20 minutes between coats.
5. Bake at 200°C for 30–35 minutes to a core of 54°C; rest 8 minutes.
6. Sauce: reduce vinegar, shallot and tarragon stems to 15 ml; whisk yolks over 70°C bain-marie to ribbons, stream in clarified butter, finish with tomato paste and chopped tarragon.
7. Carve in 4 cm bands at the table and sauce each slice.

## Plating & Service
Carry it whole to the table — the scale-scored crust is the applause moment. Carve confidently, sauce warm.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Salmon inside works equally; leftover choron is outrageous on grilled asparagus next day.
