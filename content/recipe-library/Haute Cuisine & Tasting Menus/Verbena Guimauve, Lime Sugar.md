# Verbena Guimauve, Lime Sugar

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Cloud-soft herbal marshmallows tossed in citrus sugar — a light, perfumed last touch that surprises everyone.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Caster sugar — 300 g + 60 g (coating)
- Glucose — 60 g
- Water — 100 ml
- Gelatine leaves — 16 g (8)
- Egg whites — 60 g (2)
- Fresh lemon verbena — 12 g
- Lime zest — 6 g
- Cornflour — 30 g
- Icing sugar — 30 g

## Method
1. Steep verbena in the 100 ml of hot water 15 minutes; strain, keeping 90 ml of infusion.
2. Boil infusion, 300 g sugar and glucose to 125°C.
3. Whip whites to soft peaks; pour on the syrup in a thread, then add squeezed gelatine dissolved in the hot pan.
4. Whip 8–10 minutes until thick, glossy and barely warm.
5. Spread 3 cm deep in a tray dusted with sieved cornflour-icing sugar; set 4 hours uncovered.
6. Cut into cubes with oiled scissors; toss in caster sugar blitzed with lime zest.

## Plating & Service
Serve stacked like soft dice beside the coffee tray; they bounce — encourage guests to squeeze one first.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Rose water or mint infusions swap in; vegan version needs agar and aquafaba with a denser result.
