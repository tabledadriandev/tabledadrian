# Grilled Langoustines, Kombu Butter & Cauliflower Textures

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Split langoustines grilled under umami butter with cauliflower as puree, roasted and raw — a study in quiet luxury.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- XL langoustines — 12 (1 kg)
- Butter, soft — 140 g
- Kombu, powdered — 6 g
- Lemon zest — 3 g
- Cauliflower — 500 g
- Whole milk — 200 ml
- Double cream — 50 ml
- Grapeseed oil — 20 ml
- Chives — 6 g
- Lemon juice — 12 ml
- Fleur de sel — 5 g

## Method
1. Beat butter with kombu powder, lemon zest and 2 g salt.
2. Split langoustines lengthwise, clean the heads, and chill flesh-up on a tray.
3. Simmer 300 g cauliflower in milk and cream 15 minutes; blend to a gloss and season.
4. Slice 100 g raw cauliflower into 1 mm petals; dress with lemon juice and oil.
5. Roast remaining florets in a hot pan with a nut of kombu butter until golden.
6. Spread 8 g kombu butter on each langoustine half; grill flesh-up under a hot salamander 2–3 minutes until just set.
7. Plate puree, roasted and raw cauliflower, then the langoustines; spoon over the pan butter and chives.

## Plating & Service
Serve heads-on in a sweeping arc — guests must be encouraged to suck the heads; that is where the sea lives.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Split king prawns take the butter equally well; the kombu butter transforms grilled corn for crew meals.
