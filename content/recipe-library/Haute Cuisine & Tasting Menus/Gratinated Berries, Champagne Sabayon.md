# Gratinated Berries, Champagne Sabayon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Warm-cold theatre: cool berries under a blowtorched cloud of Champagne sabayon, built in four minutes flat.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Raspberries — 150 g
- Strawberries, quartered — 150 g
- Blueberries — 100 g
- Egg yolks — 4 (72 g)
- Caster sugar — 60 g
- Champagne — 80 ml
- Lemon zest — 2 g
- Icing sugar — 10 g
- Mint tips — 4

## Method
1. Arrange the mixed berries in shallow gratin dishes or wide bowls; chill.
2. Whisk yolks, sugar and Champagne in a round-bottomed bowl over a 75°C bain-marie.
3. Whisk hard and constantly for 4–6 minutes until tripled, thick and holding a 3-second ribbon at 65°C.
4. Add lemon zest; spoon the sabayon generously over the cold berries.
5. Torch (or flash under a hot salamander, 40 seconds) to a leopard-spotted gold.
6. Dust with icing sugar, crown with mint, serve instantly.

## Plating & Service
From torch to table in under a minute — the contrast of hot foam and cold fruit is the entire dish.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Sauternes or Muscat make a richer sabayon; peaches and apricots gratinate beautifully in August.
