# Lobster Bisque, Cognac Chantilly & Tarragon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A deep, roasted-shell bisque under a cold cognac cream — old-school grandeur executed to the gram.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Lobster shells and heads — 800 g
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Carrot, mirepoix — 80 g
- Onion, mirepoix — 100 g
- Celery, mirepoix — 50 g
- Tomato paste — 40 g
- Cognac — 60 ml
- White wine — 150 ml
- Fish stock — 1.2 l
- Double cream — 250 ml
- Rice — 30 g
- Tarragon — 8 g
- Cayenne — 0.5 g
- Fine salt — 8 g

## Method
1. Roast crushed shells in oil at 200°C for 25 minutes until deeply toasted.
2. Transfer to a pot, add mirepoix, cook 6 minutes; add tomato paste and cook 2 minutes more.
3. Flambe with 40 ml cognac; add wine and reduce by half.
4. Cover with fish stock, add rice and half the tarragon; simmer 40 minutes.
5. Blend everything — shells included — for 2 minutes, then pass through a chinois and again through muslin.
6. Add 150 ml cream, season with salt and cayenne, and hold at 80°C.
7. Whip remaining 100 ml cream with 20 ml cognac and a pinch of salt to soft peaks.
8. Serve the bisque with a floating cognac chantilly and picked tarragon.

## Plating & Service
Pour at the table around the chantilly island; a single tarragon leaf on the cream, nothing more.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Prawn or crab shells substitute freely; skip cream in the soup and it turns into a superb consomme-style shot.
