# Cep Tartlet, Aged Comte & Walnut

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Autumn forest in a crisp shell — roasted ceps, mountain cheese cream and bitter walnut crunch.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- All-butter puff pastry — 250 g
- Egg yolk — 18 g
- Fresh ceps — 400 g
- Butter — 50 g
- Shallot, brunoise — 40 g
- Garlic — 1 clove
- Comte 24 months — 80 g
- Double cream — 120 ml
- Walnuts, toasted — 40 g
- Madeira — 20 ml
- Parsley, chopped — 5 g
- Fine salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Roll puff to 3 mm, cut four 10 cm discs, dock, glaze with yolk and bake between trays at 190°C for 18 minutes; keep flat and crisp.
2. Warm cream to 80°C, whisk in 60 g grated Comte until smooth; hold warm.
3. Dice cep stems; slice the caps 5 mm thick.
4. Saute caps hard in butter 3 minutes until golden; add stems, shallot and garlic, cook 3 minutes more.
5. Deglaze with Madeira, reduce to nothing, season and fold in parsley.
6. Spread Comte cream on each pastry disc, pile on the ceps, scatter walnuts and shave the remaining Comte over.

## Plating & Service
Serve warm as the cheese cream slackens — a peppery leaf salad on the side is all it needs.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chestnut mushrooms with 5 g dried porcini deliver off-season; skip walnuts and add crisp buckwheat for nut allergies.
