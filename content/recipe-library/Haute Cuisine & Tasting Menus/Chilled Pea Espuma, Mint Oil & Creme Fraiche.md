# Chilled Pea Espuma, Mint Oil & Creme Fraiche

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A verrine of vivid green sweetness — the taste of spring in one chilled spoonful, siphon-light.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Frozen petits pois — 400 g
- Vegetable stock, white — 250 ml
- Banana shallot, sliced — 40 g
- Butter — 20 g
- Creme fraiche — 80 g
- Gelatine leaf — 2 g (1 leaf)
- Mint leaves — 20 g
- Grapeseed oil — 100 ml
- Pea shoots — 8 tips
- Fine salt — 4 g

## Method
1. Blitz mint and grapeseed oil at 60°C for 4 minutes, then strain through muslin over ice; reserve the vivid oil.
2. Sweat shallot in butter for 3 minutes without colour; add stock and bring to 90°C.
3. Add peas, cook exactly 90 seconds, then blend on high for 2 minutes with bloomed gelatine and salt.
4. Pass through a fine chinois over an ice bath to lock in the colour.
5. Fold in 40 g creme fraiche, load into a 0.5 l siphon, charge twice, and chill 2 hours at 3°C.
6. Dispense espuma into chilled glasses, spoon on remaining creme fraiche, dot with mint oil and crown with a pea shoot.

## Plating & Service
Serve in frosted verrines on a slate with the mint oil pooling into emerald beads on the surface.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Replace creme fraiche with coconut yoghurt and use agar instead of gelatine for a vegan verrine.
