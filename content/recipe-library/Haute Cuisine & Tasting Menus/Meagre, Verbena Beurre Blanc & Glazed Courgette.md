# Meagre, Verbena Beurre Blanc & Glazed Courgette

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Underrated maigre with a lemon-verbena butter sauce — herbal, bright and quietly original.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Meagre (maigre) fillets, skin on — 4 x 170 g
- Fresh lemon verbena — 8 g (or 3 g dried)
- White wine — 80 ml
- White wine vinegar — 15 ml
- Shallot, brunoise — 40 g
- Cold butter, diced — 130 g
- Courgettes — 2 (400 g)
- Vegetable stock — 80 ml
- Olive oil — 30 ml
- Lemon — 1
- Fleur de sel — 4 g

## Method
1. Reduce wine, vinegar, shallot and half the verbena to 25 ml; strain.
2. Turn courgettes into 4 cm barrels; colour in olive oil, then glaze with stock and a nut of butter until tender and shining, 6 minutes.
3. Salt the fish skin 10 minutes; dry hard.
4. Cook skin-side down over medium heat 4–5 minutes until crisp and nearly cooked through; flip 20 seconds. Core 52°C.
5. Mount the reduction with cold butter below 80°C; infuse remaining verbena 2 minutes, strain again, season with lemon.
6. Plate courgette barrels, fish skin-up, verbena beurre blanc pooled around; zest lemon over at the pass.

## Plating & Service
A pale, aromatic plate — serve immediately so the herbal top notes survive the walk to the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Sea bass or corvina substitute; lemon thyme replaces verbena honestly in winter.
