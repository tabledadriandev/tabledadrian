# Crystal-Clear Tomato Consomme, Basil Oil Pearls

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Water-clear, yet more intensely tomato than any tomato — quiet molecular technique doing loud work.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Ripe Marmande tomatoes — 1.5 kg
- Basil — 30 g plus 8 tips
- Celery — 50 g
- Sherry vinegar — 10 ml
- Caster sugar — 5 g
- Fine salt — 8 g
- Grapeseed oil — 80 ml
- Cold-pressed olive oil — 20 ml

## Method
1. Blitz tomatoes, celery, 20 g basil, salt, sugar and vinegar for 60 seconds — coarse, not fine.
2. Hang the pulp in muslin over a bowl at 3°C for 12 hours; never press, only let it drip.
3. Meanwhile blend remaining basil with grapeseed oil at 60°C, strain through muslin over ice.
4. Taste the clear consomme and adjust with salt and a drop of vinegar; chill to 4°C.
5. Pour 60 ml into chilled glasses; pipette 6–8 beads of basil oil onto the surface with the olive oil.
6. Garnish each with one perfect basil tip and serve at once.

## Plating & Service
Serve in stemmed shot glasses so the clarity shows; instruct guests to drink it in two slow sips.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Freeze the pulp leftover for sauce vierge; add 5 g white soy for extra depth (adds soy allergen).
