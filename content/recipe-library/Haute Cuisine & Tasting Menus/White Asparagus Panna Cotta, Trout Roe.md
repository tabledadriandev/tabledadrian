# White Asparagus Panna Cotta, Trout Roe

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Barely-set spring asparagus cream with bursts of amber roe — a study in gentle bitterness and pop.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- White asparagus, peeled — 350 g
- Whole milk — 200 ml
- Double cream — 150 ml
- Gelatine leaves — 4 g (2)
- Trout roe — 50 g
- Lemon zest — 2 g
- White pepper — 0.5 g
- Fine salt — 4 g
- Chervil — 8 sprigs
- Hazelnut oil — 8 ml

## Method
1. Simmer chopped asparagus in milk and cream at 90°C for 12 minutes until soft.
2. Blend 2 minutes, pass through a fine chinois, and season with salt, white pepper and lemon zest.
3. Bloom gelatine in ice water, dissolve into the warm (60°C) cream, and weigh 55 g into each chilled glass.
4. Set at 3°C for at least 3 hours until just trembling.
5. Spoon 6 g trout roe onto each panna cotta.
6. Finish with a drop of hazelnut oil and a chervil sprig at the pass.

## Plating & Service
Serve the glasses on a linen runner with espresso spoons; the wobble should be barely legal.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Omit roe and add crisped capers for vegetarians; agar (1.2 g) replaces gelatine but set it slightly looser.
