# Rabbit Saddle, Prune & Armagnac, Dijon Jus

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Boned rabbit saddle rolled around Armagnac prunes with a mustard-sharpened jus — Gascony refined.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Rabbit saddles, boned — 2 (600 g)
- Prunes, pitted — 100 g
- Armagnac — 50 ml
- Dijon mustard — 25 g
- Chicken jus — 200 ml
- Double cream — 40 ml
- Butter — 50 g
- Shallot, brunoise — 40 g
- Savoy cabbage leaves — 8
- Thyme — 4 sprigs
- Grapeseed oil — 20 ml
- Fine salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Soak prunes in warm Armagnac 30 minutes; chop half, keep half whole.
2. Lay each boned saddle skin-down, season, spread with chopped prunes, and roll tight; wrap in cling film into 5 cm cylinders.
3. Poach at 64°C for 35 minutes to a core of 60°C.
4. Blanch cabbage leaves 90 seconds; butter and season them.
5. Sweat shallot in 20 g butter; add the prune-Armagnac liquor and flame, then jus and cream; reduce to coating and whisk in mustard off the boil.
6. Unwrap saddles, dry, and colour on all sides in oil and remaining butter with thyme, 3 minutes.
7. Slice thickly; plate on cabbage with whole prunes and the Dijon jus.

## Plating & Service
The spiral of prune inside pale rabbit is the money cut — slice with a long, hot knife.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken thighs rolled the same way for easier provisioning; dried apricots swap for prunes.
