# Baba au Rhum, Chantilly & Lime

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A featherweight yeast baba drowned at the table in warm rum syrup — the trolley moment of any tasting menu.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Strong flour — 250 g
- Fresh yeast — 12 g
- Whole milk, warm — 60 ml
- Whole eggs — 150 g (3)
- Caster sugar — 20 g (dough) + 300 g (syrup)
- Butter, soft — 90 g
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Water — 600 ml
- Dark rum — 120 ml
- Orange — 1
- Vanilla pod — 1
- Double cream — 250 ml
- Icing sugar — 25 g
- Lime — 1

## Method
1. Dissolve yeast in warm milk; mix with flour, eggs, 20 g sugar and salt; beat 8 minutes to an elastic, glossy dough.
2. Beat in soft butter until incorporated; pipe into buttered baba or savarin moulds, half full.
3. Prove 40 minutes until doubled; bake at 185°C for 18–20 minutes to deep gold; unmould and dry 1 hour (day-old babas drink better).
4. Syrup: boil water, 300 g sugar, orange zest and vanilla; cool to 60°C and add 80 ml rum.
5. Submerge babas 3–4 minutes per side until saturated but intact; drain on a rack.
6. Whip cream with icing sugar to soft peaks.
7. Serve each baba with chantilly, lime zest, and remaining rum in a pipette or poured at the table.

## Plating & Service
Warm syrup, cold cream, and the rum finished in front of the guest — measure generously, announce the vintage.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Grand Marnier or aged rhum agricole rotate happily; alcohol-free syrup with orange blossom for dry tables.
