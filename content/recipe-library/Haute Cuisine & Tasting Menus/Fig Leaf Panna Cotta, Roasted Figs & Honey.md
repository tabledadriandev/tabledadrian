# Fig Leaf Panna Cotta, Roasted Figs & Honey

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Coconut-scented fig leaf infusion in a barely-set cream, with jammy roasted figs — late-summer poetry.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Double cream — 500 ml
- Whole milk — 150 ml
- Caster sugar — 90 g
- Fig leaves, fresh — 4
- Gelatine leaves — 7 g (3.5)
- Black figs — 9
- Honey — 50 g
- Thyme — 3 sprigs
- Lemon juice — 10 ml
- Olive oil — 10 ml

## Method
1. Toast fig leaves briefly over a flame until fragrant (10 seconds a side).
2. Heat cream, milk and sugar to 80°C; steep the leaves 30 minutes, then strain and reheat to 60°C.
3. Add bloomed gelatine; pour into six glasses or moulds and set 4 hours at 3°C.
4. Halve the figs; roast cut-up with honey, thyme and lemon at 200°C for 10–12 minutes until bubbling.
5. Cool the figs to warm; loosen the pan juices with a drop of water.
6. Serve panna cotta with three fig halves, pan syrup and a thread of olive oil.

## Plating & Service
If unmoulding, one-second dip in hot water and pray — otherwise glasses are elegant and safe at sea.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No fig leaves? Infuse with toasted coconut flakes. Agar (1.6 g) replaces gelatine for vegetarians who avoid it.
