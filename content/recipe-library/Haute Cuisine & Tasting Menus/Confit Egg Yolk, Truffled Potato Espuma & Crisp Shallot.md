# Confit Egg Yolk, Truffled Potato Espuma & Crisp Shallot

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A 64°C yolk bleeding gold into truffled potato cloud — humble ingredients, hypnotic result.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Free-range eggs — 4
- Ratte potatoes, peeled — 300 g
- Whole milk — 150 ml
- Double cream — 100 ml
- Butter — 60 g
- Black truffle, chopped — 10 g
- Truffle jus — 15 ml
- Banana shallots — 2 (80 g)
- Grapeseed oil — 300 ml
- Chives — 5 g
- Fine salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Cook the whole eggs in a 64°C water bath for exactly 60 minutes; hold at 55°C.
2. Simmer potatoes in salted milk and cream at 90°C for 20 minutes until collapsing.
3. Blend potato with its liquid, butter, truffle jus and salt; pass twice, load into a siphon with 2 charges; hold at 60°C.
4. Slice shallots into 1 mm rings, dust lightly and fry at 150°C for 3–4 minutes until amber; drain and salt.
5. Crack each egg carefully, discard the white, and cradle the intact yolk in a warm bowl.
6. Dispense espuma around the yolk, scatter chopped truffle, chives and crisp shallots.
7. Instruct guests to break the yolk through the foam before the first spoonful.

## Plating & Service
Serve in deep warm bowls; the reveal of the molten yolk under the foam is the moment — no garnish should hide it.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Sub Jerusalem artichoke for potato in winter; skip truffle and finish with brown butter and sage for a simpler night.
