# Compressed Watermelon, Aged Feta & Basil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Vacuum-compressed melon that eats like tuna — ruby-glass cubes against salty feta snow.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Seedless watermelon — 600 g
- Lime juice — 20 ml
- White balsamic — 15 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 20 ml
- Barrel-aged feta, frozen — 80 g
- Basil, micro — 12 leaves
- Black pepper, cracked — 1 g
- Fleur de sel — 2 g

## Method
1. Cut watermelon into precise 3 cm cubes, discarding all rind and pale flesh.
2. Vacuum-compress the cubes with lime juice and white balsamic at 99%; rest 30 minutes at 3°C (or press under a weighted tray for 2 hours).
3. Drain the cubes and pat dry — they should be translucent and jewel-like.
4. Grate the frozen feta on a microplane into fine snow; keep frozen.
5. Place one cube per spoon, brush with olive oil, and season with fleur de sel and pepper.
6. Shower with feta snow and finish with a micro basil leaf.

## Plating & Service
Serve on black spoons immediately, before the feta snow melts into the glossy cube.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skip feta and add a drop of ponzu for a vegan bite; a sliver of preserved lemon adds Riviera brightness.
