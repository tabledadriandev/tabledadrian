# Salsify, Brown Butter Crumb & Perigord Truffle

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The forgotten root given the full treatment — glazed batons, nutty crumbs and black truffle shavings.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Salsify — 600 g
- Lemon — 1
- Whole milk — 300 ml
- Butter — 120 g
- Panko — 60 g
- Parmesan, grated — 30 g
- Vegetable stock — 150 ml
- Black truffle — 12 g
- Parsley, chopped — 5 g
- Sherry vinegar — 8 ml
- Fine salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Peel salsify into 8 cm batons, holding them in lemon water; simmer in milk, water and salt for 12–15 minutes until tender.
2. Cook 60 g butter to a hazelnut-brown noisette; toast panko in it 3 minutes, then drain on paper and mix with Parmesan and parsley.
3. Drain the salsify and dry well.
4. Caramelise batons in 30 g butter for 5 minutes until golden on all faces.
5. Add stock and remaining 30 g butter; reduce, rolling, until the batons wear a shining glaze; sharpen with vinegar.
6. Plate batons in a raft, cover generously with brown-butter crumb, and shave truffle over at the table.

## Plating & Service
Table-side truffle shaving over warm salsify releases the aroma — time the plates so guests smell it landing.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No truffle? Finish with a poached egg and it becomes a perfect lunch; GF panko keeps coeliacs on board.
