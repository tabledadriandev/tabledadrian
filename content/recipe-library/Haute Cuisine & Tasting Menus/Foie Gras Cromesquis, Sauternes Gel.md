# Foie Gras Cromesquis, Sauternes Gel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A molten cube of foie gras that explodes warm in the mouth — warn your guests, then watch their faces.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Foie gras, deveined — 250 g
- Chicken jus, reduced — 100 ml
- Gelatine leaves — 6 g (3)
- Plain flour — 80 g
- Whole eggs, beaten — 100 g
- Fine panko — 150 g
- Sauternes — 150 ml
- Caster sugar — 10 g
- Agar — 1.5 g
- Fine salt — 5 g
- Grapeseed oil, to fry — 1 l

## Method
1. Melt foie gras gently into warm chicken jus at 70°C, blend smooth, season, and add bloomed gelatine.
2. Pour into a 2 cm-deep tray and set overnight at 3°C, then cut into 2 cm cubes and freeze solid.
3. Boil Sauternes with sugar and agar for 1 minute, set in a thin tray, then blend to a shining gel; bag it.
4. Pane the frozen cubes twice: flour, egg, panko, then egg and panko again — no gaps or they will leak.
5. Hold breaded cubes at -18°C until service.
6. Fry from frozen at 180°C for 90–120 seconds until mahogany; rest 60 seconds so the centre turns liquid.
7. Dot each plate with Sauternes gel and seat the cromesquis alongside.

## Plating & Service
Serve on a small warm plate with a single instruction: one bite, mouth closed. The liquid centre is 70°C.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Chicken liver parfait works for a gentler cost; a cep duxelles centre makes an excellent vegetarian cromesquis.
