# Frozen Lemon Verbena Parfait, Meringue & Lemon Curd

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> An iced herbal parfait with sharp curd and crisp meringue — pre-dessert scale, patisserie discipline.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Double cream — 300 ml
- Egg yolks — 4 (72 g)
- Caster sugar — 90 g
- Water — 30 ml
- Fresh lemon verbena — 10 g
- Lemon juice — 90 ml
- Whole eggs (curd) — 100 g
- Caster sugar (curd) — 80 g
- Butter (curd) — 100 g
- Egg whites — 60 g
- Caster sugar (meringue) — 60 g
- Verbena tips — 6

## Method
1. Infuse warmed cream with verbena 20 minutes; strain, chill, and whip to soft peaks.
2. Pate a bombe: boil sugar and water to 118°C, pour onto whipping yolks, and beat to a cold, thick ribbon.
3. Fold cream into the bombe; pipe into 4 cm ring moulds and freeze 4 hours.
4. Curd: whisk lemon juice, whole eggs and sugar over a bain-marie to 82°C; blend in cold butter; chill.
5. Meringue: whip whites, rain in sugar, pipe small kisses and dry at 90°C for 90 minutes.
6. Unmould parfaits onto chilled plates; pipe curd dots, plant meringue kisses, finish with verbena tips.

## Plating & Service
Serve half-frozen — 3 minutes out of the freezer — so the parfait shears like ice cream but melts like mousse.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Mint or basil infuse the same way; the components hold 3 days, making this the charter workhorse pre-dessert.
