# Scallop Ceviche Spoon, Leche de Tigre

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Diver scallop cured in a sharp, aromatic tiger's milk — bracing, cold and gone in one swallow.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Diver scallops, sashimi grade — 240 g
- Lime juice — 80 ml
- Celery, chopped — 30 g
- Ginger — 8 g
- Red chilli, deseeded — 6 g
- Coriander stems — 10 g
- Red onion, shaved — 30 g
- Sweet potato, brunoise, blanched — 60 g
- Coriander leaves — 8
- Fine salt — 4 g

## Method
1. Blend lime juice, celery, ginger, half the chilli, coriander stems and salt; strain — this is the leche de tigre; chill to 2°C.
2. Slice each scallop horizontally into 3 coins; keep on ice.
3. Soak shaved onion in ice water 10 minutes; drain.
4. Three minutes before serving, bathe the scallop coins in the leche de tigre.
5. Build spoons: 3 scallop coins, 5 ml of the milk, sweet potato brunoise, onion.
6. Finish with sliced chilli and a coriander leaf.

## Plating & Service
Serve on frozen ceramic spoons in a single flowing line down the table; 3 minutes of cure, no more.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Sea bass or amberjack replace scallop; omit chilli and add cucumber juice for a gentler, child-friendly bite.
