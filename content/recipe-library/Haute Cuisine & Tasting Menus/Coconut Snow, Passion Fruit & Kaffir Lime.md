# Coconut Snow, Passion Fruit & Kaffir Lime

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Frozen coconut shaved to powder-snow over sharp passion fruit — a tropical cleanser with zero heaviness.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Coconut milk, full fat — 400 ml
- Coconut cream — 100 ml
- Icing sugar — 60 g
- Lime juice — 20 ml
- Kaffir lime leaves — 4
- Passion fruits — 6
- Caster sugar — 20 g
- Coriander cress or micro mint — 12 tips

## Method
1. Warm coconut milk and cream with icing sugar and bruised kaffir leaves to 60°C; steep 15 minutes, strain, add lime juice.
2. Freeze the mixture solid in a shallow tray, at least 4 hours.
3. Scrape the frozen block with a heavy fork (or blitz in bursts) into fine dry snow; return to the freezer.
4. Halve the passion fruits; loosen the pulp with caster sugar.
5. Spoon passion pulp into frozen coupes.
6. Pile coconut snow into a high drift over the pulp and finish with cress.

## Plating & Service
Work fast with frozen tools; the snow should stand in a peak for the 40-second walk to the table.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Mango coulis can replace passion fruit for gentler acidity; a spoon of dark rum under the snow for the night crowd.
