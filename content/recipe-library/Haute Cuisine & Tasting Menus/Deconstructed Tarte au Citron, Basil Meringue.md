# Deconstructed Tarte au Citron, Basil Meringue

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Every element of the classic — curd, sable, meringue — rebuilt as a freeform landscape with a basil twist.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Lemon juice — 150 ml
- Whole eggs — 200 g (4)
- Caster sugar — 140 g (curd) + 80 g (meringue)
- Butter — 180 g (curd) + 90 g (sable)
- Lemon zest — 6 g
- Plain flour — 150 g
- Icing sugar — 50 g
- Egg yolk — 18 g
- Egg whites — 80 g
- Basil — 12 g
- Creme fraiche — 60 g
- Candied lemon zest — 20 g

## Method
1. Curd: whisk lemon juice, eggs, 140 g sugar and zest over a bain-marie to 82°C; blend in 180 g cold butter; pipe-chill 4 hours.
2. Sable: rub flour, icing sugar and 90 g butter to sand; bind with yolk, chill, roll 3 mm, bake at 160°C for 14 minutes; break into shards and crumb.
3. Meringue: whip whites, rain in 80 g sugar to stiff gloss; fold in 6 g finely blitzed basil.
4. Pipe meringue kisses; dry half at 90°C for 90 minutes, keep half fresh for torching.
5. On each plate: sable crumb path, curd domes, creme fraiche dots.
6. Add crisp and torched meringues, sable shards standing, candied zest and small basil leaves.

## Plating & Service
Built a la minute in under 90 seconds per plate — mise en place is the dish; every element must be ready at hand.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF flour converts the sable; thyme or rosemary swap for basil in cooler months.
