# Mille-Feuille, Tahitian Vanilla & Salted Butter Caramel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Three sheets of caramelised puff, vanilla diplomat and a ribbon of salted caramel — the crispest dessert in France.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- All-butter puff pastry — 500 g
- Icing sugar — 40 g
- Whole milk — 400 ml
- Tahitian vanilla pods — 2
- Egg yolks — 90 g (5)
- Caster sugar — 90 g (creme) + 120 g (caramel)
- Cornflour — 35 g
- Butter — 30 g
- Double cream — 150 ml (diplomat) + 120 ml (caramel)
- Gelatine — 2 g
- Fleur de sel — 2 g

## Method
1. Roll puff to 2.5 mm; bake between heavy trays at 190°C for 25 minutes.
2. Dust with icing sugar and flash at 220°C for 3–4 minutes to a glass-like caramel glaze; cut into 4 x 10 cm rectangles while warm.
3. Creme pat: boil milk with vanilla; whisk yolks, 90 g sugar, cornflour; combine, boil 90 seconds, add gelatine; chill flat.
4. Caramel: dry-cook 120 g sugar to amber; add warm 120 ml cream, butter and fleur de sel; cool to piping thickness.
5. Diplomat: beat creme pat smooth; fold in 150 ml softly whipped cream.
6. Pipe diplomat bulbs on two pastry layers with caramel lines between; stack and cap with the third.
7. Serve upright or fashionably on the side; last-second assembly only.

## Plating & Service
Assembled to order and served within 15 minutes — a bent mille-feuille is a confession of a slow pass.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Coffee or praline diplomat variants rotate weekly; trimmings become tomorrow's crumble base.
