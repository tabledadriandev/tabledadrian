# Langoustine Tartare Spoon, Yuzu Kosho & Ginger

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sweet raw langoustine barely dressed, with a slow-burning citrus heat — clean, precise, unforgettable.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Langoustine tails, sashimi grade — 240 g
- Yuzu kosho — 4 g
- Yuzu juice — 15 ml
- Ginger, finely grated — 4 g
- Toasted sesame oil — 5 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 20 ml
- Chives — 5 g
- Radish, 1 mm slices — 30 g
- Fleur de sel — 2 g
- Micro coriander — 8 sprigs

## Method
1. Peel and de-vein the langoustines over ice; keep everything below 4°C throughout.
2. Dice the tails into precise 5 mm cubes with a wet knife.
3. Whisk yuzu juice, yuzu kosho, ginger, sesame oil and olive oil into a dressing.
4. No more than 5 minutes before service, fold tartare, dressing, chives and fleur de sel together.
5. Spoon 30 g into chilled tasting spoons and lean a radish slice against each mound.
6. Crown with micro coriander and serve immediately.

## Plating & Service
Present the spoons on a frozen granite slab; the tartare must read ice-cold against the warm heat of the kosho.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Scallop or line-caught sea bass replaces langoustine; omit sesame oil and add lime zest for a sesame-free table.
