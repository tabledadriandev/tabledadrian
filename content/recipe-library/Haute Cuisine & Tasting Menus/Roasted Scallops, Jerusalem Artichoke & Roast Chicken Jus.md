# Roasted Scallops, Jerusalem Artichoke & Roast Chicken Jus

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Caramelised scallops with earthy sunchoke and a savoury chicken jus — the land-and-sea pairing that never misses.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- XL scallops, roe off — 12 (480 g)
- Jerusalem artichokes — 500 g
- Whole milk — 250 ml
- Butter — 90 g
- Chicken wings, chopped — 400 g
- Shallot, sliced — 50 g
- Thyme — 4 sprigs
- Sherry vinegar — 10 ml
- Grapeseed oil — 30 ml
- Hazelnut oil — 0 ml (omit) or chive oil — 10 ml
- Fine salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Roast chicken wings in 15 ml oil at 210°C for 25 minutes until mahogany; simmer with shallot, thyme and 500 ml water for 30 minutes, strain and reduce to 100 ml; finish with vinegar and 20 g butter.
2. Simmer 350 g peeled artichokes in milk and salt 20 minutes; blend with 30 g butter to a silken puree.
3. Slice remaining artichokes 3 mm thin and fry at 160°C until golden chips; salt.
4. Dry scallops thoroughly; season just before the pan.
5. Sear in smoking oil 90 seconds until deep gold, flip, add 40 g butter and baste 45 seconds; core 48–50°C.
6. Plate puree, three scallops per guest, jus around and artichoke crisps standing between.

## Plating & Service
Colour is the flavour — the scallop crust must be chestnut brown while the centre stays translucent.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cauliflower replaces sunchoke for sensitive stomachs; a veal jus works if wings aren't available.
