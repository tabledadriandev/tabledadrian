# Caneles de Bordeaux, Rum & Vanilla

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Mahogany-crusted, custard-hearted caneles — the petit four that proves a pastry section's patience.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Whole milk — 500 ml
- Butter — 25 g
- Vanilla pod — 1
- Plain flour — 100 g
- Icing sugar — 240 g
- Egg yolks — 36 g (2)
- Whole egg — 50 g (1)
- Dark rum — 40 ml
- Beeswax + butter (moulds) — 30 g + 30 g
- Fine salt — 1 g

## Method
1. Boil milk with butter and split vanilla; cool to 60°C.
2. Whisk flour, icing sugar and salt; add yolks and egg, then the warm milk in stages to a thin, lump-free batter.
3. Add rum; rest the batter 24–48 hours at 4°C — non-negotiable for the crumb.
4. Brush copper (or heavy steel) moulds with melted beeswax-butter; freeze the moulds.
5. Stir the batter gently, fill moulds to 5 mm below the rim.
6. Bake at 230°C for 15 minutes, then 180°C for 45–50 minutes until nearly black-brown.
7. Unmould hot onto a rack; serve within 5 hours, crust crackling, heart custardy.

## Plating & Service
Serve with coffee as the final act — one per guest, still faintly warm, stacked in a pyramid.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No beeswax? Butter alone works with a softer crust. Orange blossom water replaces rum for dry charters.
