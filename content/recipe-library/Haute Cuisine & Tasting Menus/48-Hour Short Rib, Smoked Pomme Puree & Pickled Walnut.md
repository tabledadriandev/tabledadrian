# 48-Hour Short Rib, Smoked Pomme Puree & Pickled Walnut

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sous-vide short rib that slices like fillet but eats like braise, with smoky potato silk and dark pickled-walnut relief.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Beef short rib, boneless — 900 g
- Fine salt — 12 g
- Red wine — 250 ml
- Beef jus — 200 ml
- Black peppercorns — 3 g
- Ratte potatoes — 500 g
- Whole milk, lightly smoked — 150 ml
- Butter — 120 g
- Pickled walnuts — 40 g
- Watercress — 40 g
- Sherry vinegar — 10 ml

## Method
1. Salt the short rib, vacuum-seal, and cook at 62°C for 48 hours; ice-shock if working ahead.
2. Reduce wine with peppercorns to 60 ml; add jus and any bag juices (skimmed), reduce to a lacquer, finish with vinegar.
3. Smoke the milk lightly (10 minutes under a cloche) or use 2 g smoked salt.
4. Boil potatoes, dry well, and pass through a ricer; beat in hot smoked milk and 100 g butter for a loose, glossy puree.
5. Portion the rib into 4 blocks; sear all faces in 20 g butter until crusted, 3 minutes, and brush with the glaze.
6. Chop pickled walnuts to a rough relish.
7. Plate puree, glazed rib, walnut relish and dressed watercress.

## Plating & Service
Square edges, mirror glaze, one sweep of puree — braise flavours with fillet geometry.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No circulator? Braise at 140°C for 3.5 hours, pressed overnight. Prunes replace pickled walnuts for nut-free.
