# Veal Cheek Blanquette, Winter Vegetables & Lemon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Nursery comfort with palace finish — collapsing veal cheeks in an ivory velvet sauce, brightened with lemon.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Veal cheeks, trimmed — 1.2 kg
- White wine — 200 ml
- Chicken stock — 1 l
- Onion, clouted with 2 cloves — 1
- Carrot — 150 g
- Celery — 60 g
- Bouquet garni — 1
- Button mushrooms — 200 g
- Pearl onions — 12
- Double cream — 150 ml
- Egg yolks — 2 (36 g)
- Lemon — 1
- Butter — 40 g
- Fine salt — 10 g

## Method
1. Blanch the cheeks from cold water; refresh and rinse — a blanquette is never browned.
2. Simmer cheeks with wine, stock, clouted onion, carrot, celery and bouquet at 90°C, lidded, for 2.5–3 hours until spoon-soft.
3. Glaze pearl onions and quartered mushrooms in butter with a ladle of broth.
4. Strain the broth and reduce to 400 ml.
5. Liaise: whisk yolks with cream, temper with hot broth, and return below 80°C, stirring to a coating velvet. Season with lemon juice and salt.
6. Return cheeks and garnish to the sauce; warm through without boiling.
7. Serve with steamed rice or just the vegetables, lemon zest grated over.

## Plating & Service
Ivory on ivory: white bowls, glossy sauce, a single bay leaf as punctuation. Never let it boil after the liaison.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Classic veal shoulder works in 90 minutes; a spoon of vin jaune in the sauce is a quiet flex.
