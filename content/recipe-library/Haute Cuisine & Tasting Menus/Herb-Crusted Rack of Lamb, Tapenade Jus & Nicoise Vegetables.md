# Herb-Crusted Rack of Lamb, Tapenade Jus & Nicoise Vegetables

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rose-pink lamb under a green herb crust with an olive-deepened jus — Provence's answer to Sunday roast.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Lamb racks, French-trimmed — 2 (8 bones, 900 g)
- Panko — 60 g
- Parsley — 20 g
- Basil — 10 g
- Dijon mustard — 30 g
- Butter, melted — 40 g
- Black olive tapenade — 30 g
- Lamb jus — 200 ml
- Datterini tomatoes — 150 g
- Baby courgettes — 2
- Red pepper, roasted and peeled — 1
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Fine salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Blitz panko, parsley, basil and a pinch of salt to a green crumb; mix with melted butter.
2. Sear the seasoned racks fat-side down until golden, 4 minutes; cool 5 minutes.
3. Brush the fat with mustard and press on the herb crust.
4. Roast at 190°C for 15–18 minutes to a core of 54°C; rest 10 minutes loosely tented.
5. Halve courgettes and blister with tomatoes and pepper strips in olive oil; season.
6. Warm the jus and whisk in tapenade off the boil — do not reduce further or it turns salty.
7. Carve into double chops, season the cut faces, plate with vegetables and the tapenade jus.

## Plating & Service
Carve at the table if the sea is calm — the green crust against pink lamb is the shot guests photograph.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> GF panko converts the crust; anchovy in the jus (fish allergen) deepens it for the brave.
