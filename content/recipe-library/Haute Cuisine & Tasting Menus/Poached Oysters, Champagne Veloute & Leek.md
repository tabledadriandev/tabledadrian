# Poached Oysters, Champagne Veloute & Leek

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Warm oysters just set in their own liquor under a Champagne cream — decadence for cold-weather cruising.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Speciales de Claire No. 2 — 12
- Leek, whites only — 200 g
- Butter — 60 g
- Champagne — 150 ml
- Fish stock — 150 ml
- Double cream — 150 ml
- Egg yolk — 18 g
- Lemon juice — 8 ml
- Chives — 5 g
- Oscietra caviar (optional) — 20 g
- White pepper — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Shuck the oysters, straining and reserving every drop of liquor; clean and warm the deep shells.
2. Slice leek whites into fine rings; stew gently in 30 g butter with 2 tablespoons water, lidded, 10 minutes until melting.
3. Reduce Champagne by two-thirds; add fish stock and strained oyster liquor, reduce by half again.
4. Add cream, simmer to light-coating consistency, then liaise with the yolk off the heat; season with lemon and white pepper.
5. Slide oysters into the veloute at 65°C for 60–90 seconds — edges just ruffled, centres barely warm.
6. Spoon leek fondue into each shell, seat an oyster on top, nap with veloute.
7. Finish with chives and, for gala service, a small spoon of caviar.

## Plating & Service
Serve three shells per guest on hot coarse salt; the sauce must tremble, never boil, after the yolk goes in.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skip the yolk liaison for a lighter sauce; sparkling Vouvray replaces Champagne without shame.
