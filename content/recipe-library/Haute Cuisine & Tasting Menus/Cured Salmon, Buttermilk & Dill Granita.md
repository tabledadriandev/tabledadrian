# Cured Salmon, Buttermilk & Dill Granita

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Beet-blushed cured salmon against frozen dill snow — a Scandinavian whisper on a Mediterranean table.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Salmon fillet, skinless — 400 g
- Fine salt (cure) — 40 g
- Caster sugar (cure) — 40 g
- Beetroot, grated — 100 g
- Buttermilk — 250 ml
- Dill — 30 g
- Caster sugar (granita) — 15 g
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Cucumber — 100 g
- Mustard seeds, pickled — 10 g
- Dill fronds — 8

## Method
1. Pack salmon with salt, sugar and grated beetroot; cure 12 hours at 3°C, then rinse and dry.
2. Blend buttermilk, blanched dill, sugar, lemon juice and a pinch of salt; freeze in a tray.
3. Scrape the frozen buttermilk with a fork every 30 minutes until you have a fine green-white granita.
4. Slice the cured salmon into 3 mm leaves — the beet ring should glow at the edge.
5. Slice cucumber into ribbons and season lightly.
6. Drape salmon on chilled plates with cucumber, pickled mustard seeds and dill; spoon granita over at the last second.

## Plating & Service
Speed matters — the granita has 90 seconds of perfection on the plate. Serve with aquavit or dry Riesling.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Trout cures identically in 8 hours; coconut yoghurt thinned with lime replaces buttermilk for dairy-free.
