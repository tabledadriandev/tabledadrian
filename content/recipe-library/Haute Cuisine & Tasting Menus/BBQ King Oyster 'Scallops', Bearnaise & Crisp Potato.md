# BBQ King Oyster 'Scallops', Bearnaise & Crisp Potato

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Thick-cut king oyster mushrooms scored, basted and grilled to a scallop-like bite — with a proper bearnaise.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- King oyster mushrooms, thick — 600 g
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Soy-free mushroom glaze: veg jus 80 ml + maple 10 g
- Butter, clarified — 150 g
- Egg yolks — 3 (54 g)
- Tarragon — 10 g
- White wine vinegar — 30 ml
- Shallot, brunoise — 30 g
- Agria potatoes — 300 g
- Grapeseed oil, to fry — 400 ml
- Chives — 5 g
- Fine salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Cut mushroom stems into 3 cm 'scallops'; score both faces in a crosshatch.
2. Brine 10 minutes in 4% salt water; dry hard.
3. Sear in olive oil 3 minutes per face until golden, then baste with the warm vegetable glaze until lacquered.
4. Bearnaise: reduce vinegar, shallot and tarragon stems to 15 ml; whisk yolks with the reduction over a 70°C bain-marie to ribbons; stream in clarified butter; season and add chopped tarragon.
5. Cut potatoes into fine straw, rinse, dry, and fry at 170°C for 3–4 minutes to a golden nest; salt.
6. Plate three 'scallops' per guest, bearnaise spooned beside, potato nest on top, chives to finish.

## Plating & Service
The crosshatch caramelises like scallop coral — serve hot and glossy with the bearnaise barely warm.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan bearnaise works with soy milk emulsion (adds soy); the glaze loves a drop of smoked oil.
