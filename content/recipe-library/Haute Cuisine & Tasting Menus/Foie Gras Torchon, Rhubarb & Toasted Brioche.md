# Foie Gras Torchon, Rhubarb & Toasted Brioche

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Rose-pink torchon with sharp rhubarb cutting the richness — a benchmark of classical craft.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Foie gras lobe, grade A — 500 g
- Fine salt — 6 g
- Caster sugar — 3 g
- White port — 30 ml
- Pink peppercorns, crushed — 1 g
- Rhubarb — 300 g
- Grenadine — 20 ml
- Caster sugar (rhubarb) — 60 g
- Water — 100 ml
- Brioche loaf — 6 slices (240 g)
- Fleur de sel — 2 g

## Method
1. Soak the lobe in ice water with 10 g salt for 2 hours; devein meticulously at room temperature.
2. Season with salt, sugar, pepper and port; cure flat, covered, 12 hours at 3°C.
3. Roll tightly in muslin into a 6 cm cylinder, tying the ends hard; poach at 68°C for 90 seconds.
4. Ice-shock, re-tighten the torchon, and mature 24 hours at 3°C.
5. Bring sugar, water and grenadine to 85°C; drop in 4 cm rhubarb batons, cover, and infuse off the heat 15 minutes — they must stay intact.
6. Reduce the poaching syrup to a glaze; dice half the rhubarb into brunoise and keep the batons whole.
7. Slice torchon into 80 g rounds with a hot knife; toast brioche at the last minute.
8. Plate torchon with fleur de sel, rhubarb batons, brunoise and glaze; brioche alongside.

## Plating & Service
The torchon slice must be flawless — hot dry knife, one stroke. Serve with Sauternes or a late-harvest Riesling.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Roasted apricot replaces rhubarb in summer; gluten-free seed toast works for coeliac guests.
