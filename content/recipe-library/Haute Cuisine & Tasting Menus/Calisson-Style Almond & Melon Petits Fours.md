# Calisson-Style Almond & Melon Petits Fours

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Aix-en-Provence's marzipan-melon lozenge, made in-house with royal icing sheen — regional soul at petit-four scale.

## Ingredients (serves 10)
- Ground almonds, fine — 200 g
- Candied melon — 150 g
- Candied orange — 50 g
- Icing sugar — 150 g (paste) + 100 g (icing)
- Orange blossom water — 10 ml
- Apricot jam — 20 g
- Egg white — 20 g
- Rice paper sheets — 2
- Lemon juice — 3 ml

## Method
1. Blitz candied melon and orange to a smooth paste with orange blossom and jam.
2. Add ground almonds and 150 g icing sugar; work to a firm, clay-like paste.
3. Roll 1 cm thick between sheets, with rice paper beneath; rest overnight, uncovered, to firm.
4. Royal icing: whisk egg white with 100 g icing sugar and lemon juice to a thick ribbon.
5. Spread a thin icing veil over the slab; dry 20 minutes.
6. Cut into 5 cm lozenges (almond-shape) through paste and rice paper; dry 12 hours at room temperature.

## Plating & Service
Serve upright in a row with espresso; the matte white icing over ivory paste needs no decoration.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No candied melon? Use candied apricot. The paste freezes; ice and cut on demand.
