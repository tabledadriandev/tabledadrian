# Warm Madeleines, Brown Butter & Honey

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Humped, golden madeleines baked to order and rushed to the table in a linen fold — the warmest goodbye.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Butter — 120 g
- Honey — 25 g
- Whole eggs — 150 g (3)
- Caster sugar — 100 g
- Plain flour — 130 g
- Baking powder — 5 g
- Lemon zest — 4 g
- Whole milk — 20 ml
- Fine salt — 2 g
- Icing sugar — 10 g

## Method
1. Cook the butter to a hazelnut-brown noisette; stir in honey and cool to 45°C.
2. Whisk eggs with sugar 3 minutes to a light ribbon; add lemon zest.
3. Fold in sifted flour, baking powder and salt, then the brown butter and milk.
4. Rest the batter at 4°C for at least 2 hours (overnight is better) — the chill makes the hump.
5. Pipe into buttered, floured madeleine moulds, three-quarters full.
6. Bake at 210°C for 4 minutes, then 180°C for 5–6 minutes until the humps rise proud and edges brown.
7. Unmould at once, dust with icing sugar, and serve within 10 minutes.

## Plating & Service
Batter holds 3 days — bake a tray at the coffee order, never before. Guests should burn their fingers slightly.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Add 10 g matcha or orange blossom for variety; GF blend plus 2 g xanthan works with a denser crumb.
