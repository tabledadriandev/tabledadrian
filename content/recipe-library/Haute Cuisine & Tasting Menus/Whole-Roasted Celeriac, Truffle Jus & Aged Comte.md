# Whole-Roasted Celeriac, Truffle Jus & Aged Comte

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A celeriac roasted for hours and carved like a joint — the vegetable main that convinces committed carnivores.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Celeriac, large — 1 (1.2 kg)
- Butter — 120 g
- Coarse sea salt — 30 g
- Thyme — 6 sprigs
- Garlic, halved head — 1
- Vegetable jus, reduced — 200 ml
- Truffle jus — 30 ml
- Black truffle — 10 g
- Aged Comte — 60 g
- Hazelnut oil — 0 ml (omit) or olive oil — 15 ml
- Chives — 6 g
- Sherry vinegar — 10 ml

## Method
1. Scrub the celeriac; rub with 40 g butter and coarse salt.
2. Roast at 160°C in a lidded cocotte with thyme and garlic for 2 hours.
3. Uncover, add remaining butter, and baste every 10 minutes for another 30–40 minutes until mahogany and knife-tender to the core.
4. Rest 10 minutes, then carve into thick 'steaks'.
5. Combine vegetable jus and truffle jus; reduce to a light coating, sharpen with vinegar, and add chopped truffle.
6. Baste the steaks with pan butter and a spoon of jus.
7. Plate with the truffle jus, shaved Comte, chives and a thread of oil.

## Plating & Service
Carve at the table exactly like a roast — the reveal of the golden interior is the argument.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan: baste with olive oil and finish with almond shavings (nut allergen) instead of Comte.
