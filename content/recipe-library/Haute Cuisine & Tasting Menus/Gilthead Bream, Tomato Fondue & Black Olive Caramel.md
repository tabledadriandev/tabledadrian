# Gilthead Bream, Tomato Fondue & Black Olive Caramel

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Crisp daurade over slow tomato silk with a bittersweet olive caramel — the Cote d'Azur in high definition.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Gilthead bream fillets, skin on — 4 x 160 g
- Ripe tomatoes — 600 g
- Olive oil — 80 ml
- Garlic, sliced — 10 g
- Thyme — 4 sprigs
- Saffron — 0.1 g
- Kalamata olives, pitted — 100 g
- Honey — 25 g
- Sherry vinegar — 20 ml
- Basil — 12 leaves
- Fine salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Blanch, peel, deseed and chop tomatoes; stew with 40 ml oil, garlic, thyme, saffron and salt for 30 minutes to a thick, sweet fondue.
2. Blend olives, honey and vinegar; reduce in a small pan, stirring, 6–8 minutes to a glossy pourable caramel.
3. Score the bream skin, salt for 10 minutes, and dry hard.
4. Press-fry skin-down in 30 ml hot oil for 4 minutes until 80% cooked, glassy-skinned; flip 20 seconds. Core 52°C.
5. Warm the fondue and check seasoning; tear in half the basil.
6. Plate a bed of tomato fondue, bream skin-up, olive caramel in brush-strokes, remaining basil and a thread of olive oil.

## Plating & Service
Skin to the sky, caramel painted not pooled; serve with chilled Bandol rose without asking.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Sea bass or red snapper substitute; the olive caramel keeps two weeks and transforms grilled lamb too.
