# Smoked Eel, Beetroot Glaze & Apple Ketchup

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Lacquered smoked eel over sweet-sharp apple — earth, smoke and orchard in three precise elements.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Smoked eel fillet — 280 g
- Beetroot juice — 250 ml
- Maple syrup — 20 ml
- Sherry vinegar — 15 ml
- Bramley apples — 300 g
- Cider vinegar — 30 ml
- Caster sugar — 30 g
- Star anise — 1
- Celery, fine dice — 40 g
- Watercress — 30 g
- Rapeseed oil — 15 ml
- Fine salt — 3 g

## Method
1. Reduce beetroot juice with maple and sherry vinegar to 60 ml of glossy glaze, about 20 minutes at a simmer.
2. Cook apples with cider vinegar, sugar and star anise, lidded, 15 minutes; remove anise, blend smooth, season — this is the ketchup.
3. Portion the eel into 70 g blocks, checking for pin bones.
4. Warm the eel skin-side down in a dry non-stick pan at low heat 2 minutes, brushing repeatedly with beet glaze until lacquered.
5. Dress celery dice and watercress with rapeseed oil and salt.
6. Swipe apple ketchup, seat the glazed eel on it, and finish with the celery-watercress salad.

## Plating & Service
Dark plates flatter the mahogany lacquer; serve just warm, not hot, so the smoke stays elegant.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Hot-smoked mackerel is a fine substitute; a horseradish creme (adds dairy) rounds it for colder months.
