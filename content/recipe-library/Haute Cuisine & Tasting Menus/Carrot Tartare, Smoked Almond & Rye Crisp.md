# Carrot Tartare, Smoked Almond & Rye Crisp

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Whole-roasted carrots chopped and seasoned like steak tartare — a plant-forward statement course.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Large sand carrots — 600 g
- Coarse sea salt bed — 400 g
- Smoked almonds, chopped — 40 g
- Cornichons, brunoise — 30 g
- Shallot, brunoise — 30 g
- Capers, chopped — 20 g
- Dijon mustard — 10 g
- Tomato paste — 15 g
- Smoked paprika — 2 g
- Olive oil — 40 ml
- Rye bread, frozen — 120 g
- Chervil — 8 sprigs

## Method
1. Roast whole unpeeled carrots on the salt bed at 200°C for 50–60 minutes until deeply wrinkled and concentrated.
2. Slice frozen rye paper-thin, brush with 10 ml oil and bake flat at 160°C for 10 minutes until crisp.
3. Peel the warm carrots and chop the flesh to a 4 mm tartare — knife only, never a processor.
4. Fold in shallot, cornichons, capers, mustard, tomato paste, paprika and remaining olive oil; season boldly.
5. Rest the tartare 15 minutes for the flavours to marry.
6. Press into an 8 cm ring on each plate, scatter smoked almonds and chervil, and stand rye shards upright.

## Plating & Service
Serve at cool room temperature with the rye crisps as scoops — it must taste seasoned enough to convince a carnivore.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Nut-free: use toasted sunflower seeds. An egg-yolk-style dot of smoked aioli on top dresses it for non-vegans.
