# John Dory, Stuffed Courgette Flower & Bouillabaisse Jus

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Saint-Pierre with a mousse-filled courgette flower and an intense saffron shellfish jus — Marseille in evening dress.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- John Dory fillets — 4 x 160 g
- Courgette flowers — 4
- Scallop meat — 100 g
- Double cream — 80 ml
- Egg white — 15 g
- Fish bones and prawn shells — 500 g
- Fennel, sliced — 100 g
- Tomato, chopped — 200 g
- Pastis — 20 ml
- Saffron — 0.3 g
- Olive oil — 60 ml
- Orange zest — 3 g
- Baby fennel — 4
- Fine salt — 7 g

## Method
1. Make the jus: roast bones and shells in 30 ml oil, add fennel, tomato, pastis, saffron and orange zest, cover with water, simmer 40 minutes, strain and reduce to 200 ml.
2. Blend chilled scallop with egg white and 2 g salt; beat in cold cream over ice for a mousse.
3. Pipe 30 g mousse into each flower, twisting the petals closed.
4. Steam the flowers at 90°C for 6–7 minutes until the mousse is set (58°C core).
5. Blanch baby fennel 3 minutes and glaze in a little jus.
6. Sear the Dory skin-side down in remaining oil 3 minutes; turn off and let carry to 52°C core.
7. Plate fish, flower and fennel; froth the jus with a hand blender and spoon around.

## Plating & Service
The saffron jus should be poured glossy and just-frothed; a drop of good olive oil on the fish, nothing else.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> No flowers? Wrap the mousse in blanched courgette ribbons. Rouille croutons on the side feed the hungry.
