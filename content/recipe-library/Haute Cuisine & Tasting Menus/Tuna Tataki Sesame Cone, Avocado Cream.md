# Tuna Tataki Sesame Cone, Avocado Cream

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A tiny nori-sesame cone piled with seared tuna — hand-held sushi glamour for the aft deck.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Yellowfin tuna loin, sashimi grade — 200 g
- Plain flour — 40 g
- Egg white — 30 g
- Icing sugar — 30 g
- Melted butter — 30 g
- Black and white sesame seeds — 20 g
- Nori powder — 4 g
- Avocado — 1 (150 g flesh)
- Lime juice — 15 ml
- Soy sauce — 15 ml
- Mirin — 10 ml
- Micro shiso — 8 leaves

## Method
1. Whisk flour, egg white, icing sugar and melted butter to a smooth tuile batter; rest 30 minutes.
2. Spread through a 7 cm triangle stencil, scatter with sesame and nori powder, bake at 165°C for 6 minutes.
3. Working fast, roll each warm triangle around a cone mould; cool until crisp.
4. Sear the tuna 20 seconds per face in a smoking-hot dry pan, then ice, dry, and cut into 1 cm dice.
5. Dress tuna with soy and mirin; blend avocado with lime juice and salt to a smooth cream.
6. Pipe avocado cream into each cone, top with tuna and crown with micro shiso.

## Plating & Service
Stand cones in a perforated stone block; serve within 10 minutes — a soggy cone is a firing offence.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Butter can be swapped for olive oil in the tuile; cooked shredded crab replaces tuna for guests avoiding raw fish.
