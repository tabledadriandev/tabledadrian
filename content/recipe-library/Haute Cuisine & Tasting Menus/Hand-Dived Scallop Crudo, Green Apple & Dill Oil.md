# Hand-Dived Scallop Crudo, Green Apple & Dill Oil

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sweet raw scallop, tart apple and grassy dill — a cold, crystalline start that flatters any white Burgundy.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Hand-dived scallops, XL — 8 (about 320 g)
- Granny Smith apple — 1 (150 g)
- Apple juice, clear — 100 ml
- Lime juice — 20 ml
- Dill — 25 g
- Grapeseed oil — 100 ml
- Cucumber, brunoise — 60 g
- Horseradish, finely grated — 3 g
- Fleur de sel — 3 g

## Method
1. Blend dill and grapeseed oil at 60°C for 4 minutes; strain through muslin over ice for a vivid green oil.
2. Whisk apple juice, lime juice and horseradish; chill this dressing to 2°C.
3. Slice each scallop into 4 coins and arrange on chilled plates in a loose ring.
4. Cut apple into fine julienne, holding it in acidulated water.
5. Spoon the apple-horseradish dressing over the scallops and season with fleur de sel.
6. Scatter cucumber brunoise and drained apple julienne; pool dill oil into the dressing.

## Plating & Service
Serve within 5 minutes of dressing on deeply chilled plates; the contrast of green oil in pale juice is the visual hook.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Sea bream works equally well; add 5 g trout roe per plate (fish allergen) for salt-pop and colour.
