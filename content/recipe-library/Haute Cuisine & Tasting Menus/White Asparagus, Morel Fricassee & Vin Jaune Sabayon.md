# White Asparagus, Morel Fricassee & Vin Jaune Sabayon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Two spring aristocrats — fat white spears and wild morels — under a nutty Jura wine sabayon.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- White asparagus, XL — 12 spears (700 g)
- Fresh morels — 150 g
- Shallot, brunoise — 30 g
- Butter — 70 g
- Double cream — 60 ml
- Vin jaune (or dry sherry) — 60 ml
- Egg yolks — 4 (72 g)
- Lemon juice — 8 ml
- Caster sugar — 5 g
- Chervil — 8 sprigs
- Fine salt — 6 g

## Method
1. Peel asparagus twice from tip to base; simmer in salted water with sugar and 20 g butter at 95°C for 8–12 minutes until a knife slides through.
2. Halve and wash morels three times in tepid water; dry thoroughly.
3. Sweat shallot in 30 g butter, add morels and cook 4 minutes; add cream and 20 ml vin jaune, simmer to coating consistency, season.
4. Whisk yolks with 40 ml vin jaune and 20 ml asparagus water over a 70°C bain-marie to thick ribbons, 5–6 minutes.
5. Whisk in remaining 20 g butter in pieces; season with lemon and salt — hold no more than 15 minutes.
6. Lay 3 spears per warm plate, spoon morel fricassee across the middle.
7. Sauce generously with sabayon and flash under a hot salamander for 20 seconds until gold-tipped; finish with chervil.

## Plating & Service
Serve on long warm plates, spears aligned; the sabayon should hold a soft peak as it glazes.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Green asparagus needs only 4 minutes; dried morels (30 g, rehydrated) work off-season — save the soaking liquor for the sabayon.
