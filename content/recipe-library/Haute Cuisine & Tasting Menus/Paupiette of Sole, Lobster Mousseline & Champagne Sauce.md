# Paupiette of Sole, Lobster Mousseline & Champagne Sauce

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Sole fillets rolled around a coral lobster mousse, poached and cloaked in Champagne cream — pure palace technique.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Sole fillets, skinned — 8 (600 g)
- Raw lobster meat — 200 g
- Egg white — 30 g
- Double cream — 200 ml
- Fish stock — 400 ml
- Champagne — 200 ml
- Shallot, brunoise — 40 g
- Butter — 60 g
- Trout roe — 30 g
- Chervil — 8 sprigs
- Cayenne — 0.2 g
- Fine salt — 7 g

## Method
1. Chill the blender jug. Blend lobster with egg white and 3 g salt to a paste.
2. Work in 120 ml cold cream over ice in three additions; test-poach a nugget — it must be light yet sliceable. Season with cayenne.
3. Lay fillets skin-side up, spread 40 g mousseline on each, and roll into paupiettes; wrap each tightly in cling film.
4. Poach at 62°C for 18–20 minutes to a 56°C core.
5. Reduce stock, Champagne and shallot to 100 ml; add remaining 80 ml cream and reduce to coating; mount with butter.
6. Unwrap paupiettes, dry gently, and trim the ends for a clean spiral.
7. Sauce generously, crown with trout roe and chervil.

## Plating & Service
Slice one paupiette per plate to show the spiral, one whole beside it; the sauce should coat like double silk.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Scallop mousseline is a fine economy; a saffron thread in the sauce turns it sunset gold.
