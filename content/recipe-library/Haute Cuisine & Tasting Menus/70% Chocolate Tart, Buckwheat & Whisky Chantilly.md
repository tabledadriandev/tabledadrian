# 70% Chocolate Tart, Buckwheat & Whisky Chantilly

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> A thin, intense chocolate tart with toasted buckwheat crunch and a smoky whisky cream — after-dark elegance.

## Ingredients (serves 8)
- Plain flour — 180 g
- Cocoa powder — 20 g
- Icing sugar — 60 g
- Butter, cold — 100 g
- Egg yolk — 18 g
- Dark chocolate 70% — 250 g
- Double cream — 250 ml (ganache) + 150 ml (chantilly)
- Whole milk — 80 ml
- Whole egg — 50 g
- Buckwheat groats — 30 g
- Peaty whisky — 20 ml
- Icing sugar (chantilly) — 15 g
- Fleur de sel — 1 g

## Method
1. Sable: rub flour, cocoa, icing sugar and butter to sand; bind with yolk and 15 ml cold water; rest 1 hour.
2. Line a 22 cm ring at 2.5 mm; blind-bake at 160°C for 18 minutes until dry and biscuit-crisp.
3. Toast buckwheat in a dry pan until nutty; scatter half over the baked base.
4. Ganache: heat 250 ml cream and milk to 80°C; pour over chocolate, emulsify, then blend in the egg.
5. Fill the case and bake at 120°C for 15–18 minutes until set with a central tremble; cool 2 hours, never refrigerated.
6. Whip 150 ml cream with icing sugar and whisky to soft peaks.
7. Serve thin wedges with whisky chantilly, remaining buckwheat and a few grains of fleur de sel.

## Plating & Service
Cut with a hot dry knife for mirror edges; room temperature service is non-negotiable for shine and snap.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skip whisky for vanilla chantilly; GF flour plus 2 g xanthan converts the sable honourably.
