# Beetroot en Croute, Sauce au Poivre Vert

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Salt-baked beetroot wrapped in mushroom duxelles and golden pastry — a vegetarian 'Wellington' with a peppercorn cream.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Beetroots, medium — 4 (600 g)
- Coarse salt crust: flour 200 g + salt 100 g + water 90 ml
- Chestnut mushrooms — 400 g
- Shallot, brunoise — 60 g
- Butter — 50 g
- Thyme — 4 sprigs
- Puff pastry — 500 g
- Egg wash — 1 yolk + 10 ml cream
- Green peppercorns in brine — 20 g
- Brandy — 20 ml
- Double cream — 150 ml
- Vegetable stock — 100 ml
- Fine salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Bake beets in the simple salt dough at 180°C for 60 minutes; cool, peel, and trim into neat barrels.
2. Blitz mushrooms; cook with shallot, thyme and butter over high heat 10–12 minutes until completely dry. Season and cool.
3. Wrap each beet in a 5 mm coat of duxelles, then in 3 mm puff pastry; seal seams underneath.
4. Egg-wash twice, score a spiral, and rest 20 minutes at 4°C.
5. Bake at 200°C for 22–25 minutes until deep gold.
6. Sauce: reduce brandy and stock by half; add cream and crushed green peppercorns, simmer to coating; season.
7. Rest 5 minutes; halve one croute per plate and sauce alongside.

## Plating & Service
The cross-section — crimson beet, black duxelles ring, gold crust — should be shown cut-face up.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Vegan pastry and oat cream convert it fully; celeriac barrels work identically.
