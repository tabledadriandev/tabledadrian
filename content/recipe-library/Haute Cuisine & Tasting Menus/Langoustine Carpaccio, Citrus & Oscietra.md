# Langoustine Carpaccio, Citrus & Oscietra

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Raw langoustine pounded to a translucent veil, dressed with citrus and caviar — the definition of restraint.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Langoustine tails, XL sashimi grade — 8 (about 320 g)
- Oscietra caviar — 30 g
- Pink grapefruit — 1
- Orange — 1
- Lemon juice — 15 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil — 40 ml
- Creme fraiche — 40 g
- Chive tips — 12
- Baby sorrel — 12 leaves
- Fleur de sel — 2 g
- Espelette pepper — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Peel and de-vein the langoustines over ice; butterfly each tail open.
2. Place 2 tails between oiled parchment sheets and pound gently with a flat mallet into a 12 cm disc; repeat, then chill flat.
3. Segment grapefruit and orange over a bowl, saving the juices; trim segments to neat 1 cm supremes.
4. Whisk 30 ml citrus juice with lemon juice, olive oil and fleur de sel into a bright emulsion.
5. Invert one langoustine disc onto the centre of each frozen plate and peel away the paper.
6. Brush generously with the citrus dressing and season with Espelette.
7. Arrange citrus supremes, dots of creme fraiche, 7 g caviar, chives and sorrel across each veil.

## Plating & Service
Frozen plates are non-negotiable; the carpaccio must arrive glistening and near-transparent, caviar catching the light.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Scallops pound the same way at lower cost; swap creme fraiche for avocado puree to lose the dairy.
