# Squid 'Tagliatelle', Ink Emulsion & Lemon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Raw squid knife-cut into ribbons and barely warmed — pasta with no pasta, dressed in its own ink.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Large squid tubes, super-fresh — 600 g
- Squid ink — 8 g
- Fish stock — 150 ml
- Olive oil — 80 ml
- Garlic, sliced — 6 g
- Chilli, sliced — 4 g
- Lemons — 2
- Datterini tomatoes, halved — 150 g
- Parsley, chopped — 8 g
- Fine salt — 5 g

## Method
1. Clean the tubes, split them open and score the inside in a fine crosshatch.
2. Freeze 30 minutes until firm, then knife-cut into 4 mm ribbons the length of the tube; hold at 2°C.
3. Reduce fish stock to 60 ml, whisk in the ink and 30 ml olive oil to a black satin emulsion; season and hold at 60°C.
4. Warm 30 ml oil with garlic and chilli until fragrant; add tomatoes for 2 minutes.
5. Add squid ribbons and toss exactly 45–60 seconds at high heat — they curl like fresh pasta and must stay tender.
6. Off the heat: lemon juice, zest, parsley and remaining oil.
7. Twirl into nests with a carving fork, sauce the black emulsion around.

## Plating & Service
Serve in hot bowls with a lemon cheek; overcooking by 30 seconds turns silk into rubber — count aloud if you must.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Cuttlefish works cut thinner; the ink emulsion doubles as a dramatic base for seared scallops.
