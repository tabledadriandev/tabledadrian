# Green Apple Sorbet, Fennel Sugar & Chartreuse

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Electric-green acidity with anise crunch and one cold thread of Chartreuse — a cleanser that wakes the room.

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- Granny Smith apples — 6 (900 g)
- Caster sugar — 100 g
- Glucose — 30 g
- Water — 100 ml
- Ascorbic acid or lemon juice — 3 g / 20 ml
- Fennel seeds — 4 g
- Demerara sugar — 20 g
- Green Chartreuse — 30 ml
- Apple, brunoise — 60 g

## Method
1. Dissolve caster sugar and glucose in water at 60°C; chill this syrup.
2. Juice the apples straight into the ascorbic acid (or lemon) to hold the green; strain.
3. Combine 400 ml apple juice with the syrup; churn immediately and freeze.
4. Toast fennel seeds 2 minutes; grind coarsely with demerara for fennel sugar.
5. Dress apple brunoise with a few drops of Chartreuse.
6. Quenelle sorbet into frozen bowls over the brunoise, dust with fennel sugar, and spoon 5 ml Chartreuse around each.

## Plating & Service
The sorbet must be genuinely green — speed from juicer to churn is the trick. Frozen bowls, always.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Skip Chartreuse and add elderflower for alcohol-free service; pear works with the same acid discipline.
