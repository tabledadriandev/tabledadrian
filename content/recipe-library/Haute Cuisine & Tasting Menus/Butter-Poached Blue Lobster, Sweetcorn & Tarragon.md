# Butter-Poached Blue Lobster, Sweetcorn & Tarragon

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> Lobster cooked in an emulsified butter bath, with sweetcorn two ways and anise-fresh tarragon.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Blue lobsters, live, 600 g each — 2
- Butter — 250 g
- Water — 40 ml
- Sweetcorn kernels — 400 g
- Double cream — 80 ml
- Lime juice — 10 ml
- Tarragon — 10 g
- Shallot, brunoise — 30 g
- Espelette pepper — 1 g
- Fleur de sel — 5 g

## Method
1. Dispatch and blanch lobsters 60 seconds; extract raw tail and claw meat carefully.
2. Make beurre monte: bring water to a simmer and whisk in butter piece by piece; hold at 60°C.
3. Poach tails 5–6 minutes and claws 8 minutes in the beurre monte to an opaque, tender 55°C.
4. Cook 300 g corn with cream 5 minutes, blend and pass to a silken puree; season with lime.
5. Char remaining corn in a dry pan until spotted; fold with shallot and chopped tarragon.
6. Slice tails, glaze all meat with poaching butter and season with fleur de sel and Espelette.
7. Plate puree, charred corn, lobster; finish with tarragon tips and a spoon of the poaching butter.

## Plating & Service
The lobster must gleam — brush with butter at the pass. Serve with the same Chardonnay used in the kitchen glass.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Corn swaps to celeriac in winter; use the shells for tomorrow's bisque without exception.
