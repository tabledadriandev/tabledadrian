# Wild Turbot, Champagne Beurre Blanc & Sea Herbs

> [!tip] Chef's angle
> The king of flatfish poached on the bone, sauced with Champagne butter — the definitive luxury fish course.

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- Turbot tranches, on the bone — 4 x 220 g
- Fish stock — 500 ml
- Champagne — 250 ml
- Shallot, brunoise — 50 g
- Cold butter, diced — 180 g
- Double cream — 25 ml
- Champagne vinegar — 15 ml
- Sea aster, samphire and oyster leaf — 80 g
- Lemon juice — 8 ml
- Fleur de sel — 4 g
- White pepper — 0.5 g

## Method
1. Brine the turbot tranches in 5% salt water for 10 minutes; rinse and dry.
2. Reduce 150 ml Champagne with shallot and vinegar to 30 ml.
3. Poach turbot in fish stock and remaining Champagne at 62–65°C for 12–15 minutes to a core of 52°C.
4. Meanwhile add cream to the reduction, then mount with cold butter over low heat, never above 80°C.
5. Season the beurre blanc with lemon, salt and white pepper; keep at 60°C.
6. Blanch samphire 30 seconds; warm sea aster in a spoon of the sauce.
7. Peel the turbot skin, lift each tranche onto a hot plate, glaze with sauce and dress with sea herbs.
8. Serve extra beurre blanc in a warm sauceboat.

## Plating & Service
White-on-white plating: pearl fish, ivory sauce, green herbs. The bone stays in — flavour and drama.

> [!note] Variations & dietary swaps
> Brill halves the price; a splash of yuzu in the sauce swings it Japanese. Trimmings make tomorrow's fumet.
