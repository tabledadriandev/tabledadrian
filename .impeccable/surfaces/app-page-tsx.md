# Home

Mode: Persuade
Audience: people booking a private chef for a table in London or Europe. The Riviera and Monaco feeling is atmosphere only. Published claims stay the ones already on the site.
Job: decide to book. Action: Book your experience.
Proof: real plates from the gallery, the published prices, the four existing testimonials, EHL, Stanford, fifteen years.
Constraints: Sola copy stays word for word on its own cream and deep-green sub-brand. No invented clients, dishes, or testimonials. Reduced motion is fades. WebGL stays lazy.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A private table, composed. The page refuses the restaurant template of a full-bleed photo, a kicker, and a row of service cards.
OWN-WORLD: Warm ivory, near-black ink, one champagne gold. Bodoni Moda at display scale, Hanken Grotesk for UI, hairline rules, a gold inset rim on photographs. Sola is the only cream-and-green room.
STORY: The visitor sees the plate and the sentence, believes the cooking is written for their table, and books.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop is a pinned split. Left, ink, the headline "Dinner, composed around your table." at roughly 8vw with "your" in italic gold, the lede, the light booking button, and one fact line. Right, the burrata plate full height, gold rim, a live gold-dust shader over the photograph. Mobile stacks the plate, then the same type.
FORM: Brief-pinned maison plate. Concept seed was not rolled; the brief fixed the world. Signature interaction: the shader on the hero plate, then a pinned service title and a horizontal plate gallery.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
