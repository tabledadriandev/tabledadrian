# Home

Mode: Persuade
Audience: people booking a private chef for a table on the Côte d'Azur and in Monaco.
Job: decide to book. Action: Enquire about a date.
Proof: real plates from the gallery, fees on request, the four existing testimonials, Swiss-trained (EHL), Nutrition certificate, Stanford, 10+ years.
Constraints: Sola copy stays word for word on its own cream and deep-green sub-brand. No invented clients, dishes, or testimonials. Reduced motion is a static cloche and a still strip. WebGL stays lazy and self-hosted.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Dinner, composed around your table. The page refuses the restaurant template of a full-bleed photo, a kicker row of equal cards, and a luxury-dark default that ignores the room.
OWN-WORLD: Ink with a champagne glow in dark, ivory with ink and champagne in light. Italic Bodoni, frosted-glass pill nav, frosted offer cards, a gold cloche on an ivory plate.
STORY: The visitor sees the table and the sentence, believes the cooking is written for their kitchen, and enquires.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A frosted pill across the top. Left, the italic line "Dinner, composed around your table.", the Riviera lede, an inverted enquire pill, and the credential chip. Right, the cloche.
FORM: Brief-pinned approved hybrid comp (no concept roll; the attached dark and light comps are the contract). Signature interaction: the lazy three.js cloche, then three sticky offer panels and a moving strip of the kitchen's photographs.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
